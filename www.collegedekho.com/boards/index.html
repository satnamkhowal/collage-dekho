    List of School Boards in India - National and State Wise List of Boards

    Board Exams in India are conducted for Classes 10 and 12 every year at the National and State level. There are various boards that are responsible for conducting the board exams. The national level board exams include the CBSE and CISCE boards which are also known as two of the major boards in India. Along with this, there are various state-level boards such as the UP board, Rajasthan Board, Haryana Board, Punjab Board, etc. There is a separate education board for almost every state in India responsible for conducting board exams in that particular state. The state boards are responsible for conducting exams in schools that function under and are recognized by the state government.

    These national level and state level boards are responsible for conducting the class 10th and 12th board examinations. Not only this but the syllabus, exam pattern, result, question papers, etc. are also managed by these boards every year. The boards release their separate timetable for Class 10th and 12th on their respective web portals. The candidates can check this page to get more information on Board Exams in India.

CBSE has released the syllabus, previous year's question paper, exam pattern, sample papers, and other study material on the official website.
