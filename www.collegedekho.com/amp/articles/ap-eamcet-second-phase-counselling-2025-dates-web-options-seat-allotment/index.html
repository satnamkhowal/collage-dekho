AP EAMCET Second Phase Counselling 2025:The seat allotment result has been finally released on August 14, after a 10-day delay following the by the High Court orders. As per the revised schedule, those who have have allotted a seat in the second/ final round of counselling must report to the colleges till August 20, 2025. The registration for the AP EAMCET second/final phase counselling 2025 concluded on July 30, 2025. On the other hand, candidates were allowed to exercise the web options till July 31, 2025.

The AP EAMCET final phase counselling 2025 comprises steps like registration, fee payment, web options, seat allotment, and self-reporting. The candidates who were not able to secure a seat in the first phase of counselling or who wanted to secure a different seat have to go through the second round of counselling. Check the complete details related to AP EACMET second-phase counselling 2025, like the schedule, web options instructions and more in the article.

Self-Reporting After AP EAMCET Second Phase Seat Allotment 2025

The AP EAMCET self-reporting for the phase 2 counselling is being conducted from August 14 to 20, 2025. Check the steps for AP EAMCET self-reporting below:

  • To check the seat allotment result and download the seat allotment letter, candidates will have to log in to their registered accounts

  • On the seat allotment portal, complete the AP EAMCET online self-reporting

  • After completing the online reporting, candidates will have to do the physical reporting to the allotted colleges for admission

  • Candidates who are allotted a seat in the AP EAMCET Final Phase counselling 2025 and fail to do the self-reporting online and physically lose their seat

Who Is Eligible for AP EAMCET Second Phase Web Options 2025?

The eligibility criteria for the AP EAMCET second-phase web options 2025 have been provided below:

  • Candidates who did not get a seat in the first round of AP EAMCET counselling 2025 are eligible to exercise the web options

  • Candidates who qualified for the AP EAMCET/EAPCET exam but did not register for the round 1 counselling are eligible for the second-phase web options.

  • Candidates who paid the fees for the counselling but were able to participate in the first round of counselling can exercise the second-phase web options for the AP EAMCET exam.

  • Candidates who were allotted seats in the phase 1 counselling but want to upgrade their allotted seats are eligible.

  • Candidates who cancelled their allotted seats during the first phase of counselling can participate in the second phase of AP EAMCET counselling 2025.

Instructions for AP EAMCET Second Phase Seat Allotment 2025

Candidates must go through the instructions for the second phase of the AP EAMCET seat allotment 2025 to make the process hassle-free for themselves:

  • The second phase of the counselling process will comprise 5 steps, which are registration & fee payment, document verification, web options (choice filling), seat allotment, and self-reporting & college admission

  • The AP EAMCET seat allotment 2025 will be based on the ranks secured in the exam, category/reservation, seat availability, and the choices filled by candidates during web options.

  • To access the AP EAMCET seat allotment letter, registered candidates must log in to their accounts using their login credentials.

  • Candidates will be allotted seats that remained vacant after the phase one counselling in the second round of AP EAMCET seat allotment.

  • If someone wants to upgrade from their allotted seat during the first round of seat allotment, they will have to participate in the second round of web options. However, the previous round of seat allotment will be cancelled if a new seat is allotted in the second phase.

Important Instructions for Candidates Exercising AP EAMCET Second Phase Web Options 2025

Candidates who are going to exercise the AP EAMCET Phase 2 web options must check the important instructions below:

  • While exercising the AP EAMCET second-phase web option, candidates should prefer the less preferred colleges. This will increase the chances of admission; therefore, candidates are advised to go through the seat matrix for the AP EAMCET 2025 counselling

  • If the candidate fails to report after the second phase of seat allotment, then their candidature or the seat will be cancelled

  • Candidates must go through the AP EAMCET counselling dates regularly so that they do not miss any event

  • Reserve category candidates should ensure updated certificates are uploaded (issued after Jan 1, 2025, for income certificates).

FAQs

If I have qualified for the AP EAMCET exam but have not participated in the first phase of counselling. Am I eligible for the second round of AP EAMCET Phase 2 counselling 2025?

Though you have not participated in the first phase of counselling, you are still eligible for the second round of AP EAMCET counselling 2025. You have to register for the AP EAMCET final phase counselling 2025 between July 27 and 30, 2025, to participate in the AP EAMCET second phase counselling 2025.

When will the seat allotment results be released for AP EAMCET Round 2 Counselling?

The seat allotment result for the AP EAMCET round 2 counselling will be released on August 4, 2025. To check the AP EAMCET 2025 seat allotment results, candidates will have to log in to their registered accounts using their login credentials.

What are the registration dates for AP EAMCET Final Phase Counselling 2025?

The registration dates for the AP EAMCET final/second phase counselling 2025 are between July 27, 2025 and July 30, 2025.&nbsp;
