ICAR UG Counselling 2025: ICAR, or the Indian Council of Agricultural Research, has released the counselling schedule for ICAR UG 2025 counselling. The exam authority will display the seat matrix for the counselling process on October 14, 2025, and the students will be able to register for counselling, exercise choice filling, make payment of counselling fee and upload documents by October 17, 2025. Based on the choices filled by the students, the authorities will release the round 1 seat allotment on October 21, 2025, following which the last date to report to the allotted colleges will be October 24, 2025. You can check the complete ICAR UG counselling schedule here.
Earlier, the ICAR Merit ranks/ rank cards for all registered candidates were released on September 12, 2025, followed by the ICAR UG seat matrix 2025 for all participating colleges on September 22, 2025. Refer to the official website of ICAR counselling @icarcounseling.comto find the direct link to the ICAR UG counselling registration 2025.

Under the information brochure section, students will be able to find important details like eligibility criteria, reservation policy, online counselling process, and more. Students can download the ICAR CUET UG counselling brochure and ICAR UG seat matrix by clicking on the link given below:

CUET ICAR UG Counselling Information Brochure 2025- PDF Download (REVISED)
ICAR UG Seat Matrix 2025 (Updated)- PDF Download

Since 2023, the ICAR UG exam has shifted under theCUET UG exam, which is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). However, ICAR UG admission 2025, along with the counselling and choice filling process, is still supervised by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) only. Students who wish to take admission to UG courses in the Agricultural and Allied Science stream at any ICAR-affiliated college/agricultural university must register for the ICAR AIEEA 2025 counselling process.

Students can find the latest information and updates on ICAR UG counselling 2025, counselling schedule, eligibility requirements, documents required, details on seat allotment and more, here.

ICAR UG Counselling Eligibility

To take part in the counselling process for ICAR admission 2025, students must qualify for the ICAR counselling eligibility criteria. Have a look at the detailed eligibility requirements for ICAR counselling rounds 2025 given below:

  • Nationality - Must be an Indian citizen.
  • Age - Must be less than 16 years old (as of August 31, 2025).
  • Academic Qualifications - Must have completed Class 12th with a minimum of 50% marks in the Science stream with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Agriculture/Mathematics as their compulsory subjects (40% for SC/ST/PwD).
  • Exam Qualifications - Must have appeared for CUET UG and qualified in the relevant subjects as per their course and college preferences.

Note: Check out the official website of the agricultural college/university you want to take admission in to have a detailed idea of the eligibility requirements.

ICAR UG Counselling Process 2025

To register for the ICAR counselling for UG courses, students can follow this step-by-step guide provided below to be eligible for ICAR admission 2025:

  • To apply for the ICAR counselling process for UG courses, students will be required to visit the official website of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research at icaradmission.in.
  • On the homepage, you will be easily able to spot the registration link for ICAR UG 2025 counselling. The ICAR counselling form will open up on your screen after you click on it.
  • Fill in the necessary information, such as personal and academic details etc. Upload the required documents and preview the form before hitting submit.
  • Once you’ve made the final edits to your ICAR counselling application form, you can hit submit.
  • A payment gateway will be displayed on your dashboard. To successfully register for the ICAR UG counselling rounds, make a payment of INR 500 through any online payment method - Credit Card / Debit Card / Net Banking / UPI.
  • Once you have paid for the ICAR counselling fees, you will receive your login credentials on your registered email ID or via SMS.
  • Save and use these login details to access the ICAR counselling platform at the official portal of ICAR.

ICAR UG Choice Filling and Locking

As a part of the ICAR counselling process, students will be required to participate in the ICAR AIEEA choice filling process too. Here is a detailed guide to participating in the ICAR choice filling process 2025:

  • Use the login credentials provided to you at the time of ICAR counselling registration and log in to the official portal @icaradmission.in.
  • Once you have logged in to your account, you will be able to access the choice filling section where you can choose your courses and colleges, in order of preference.
  • While choosing your courses/colleges, you must prioritise your choices by marking them with numbers. (1 for the most desired, 2 for the next category, and so on).
  • Till the ICAR choice filling deadline, students can make modifications to their choice filling form.
  • On the last date of the choice filling process, students must make their final choice. After this date, students will not be allowed to make any changes to their choices.
  • Download and save these choices for future reference.

ICAR UG Seat Allotment Process 2025

Students can get a detailed insight into the seat allotment process for ICAR 2025 admissions as given below. Mentioned below are the aspects that are taken into consideration by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research while allotting seats:

  • Ranks and Merit: ICAR authorities will release a merit list based on the scores obtained by students in the CUET UG exam. This merit list/rank list will act as a basis for the seat allotment process.
  • Reservation Criteria: Certain seats are reserved for different categories in the CUET UG exam. A separate merit list is prepared for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, and PwD categories based on the percentage of seats reserved for them. The authorities will then allot seats to these reserved categories based on this merit list.
  • Students’ Preferences: Based on the choices and their respective priorities selected by the students, the authorities will prepare the seat allocation lists after every round of ICAR counselling.

During the ICAR seat allotment process, students can choose float/slide/freeze options in the following situations:

  • Students can choose the “float” option if they are not completely satisfied with the current allotment and would like to explore better options.
  • Students can choose the “slide” option if they are satisfied with the college but would like to get another course in the same institution.
  • Students can choose the “freeze” option if they are satisfied with both their course and college and would like to finalise their admission, thus ending the scope for participating in the subsequent rounds.

ICAR UG College Reporting

Once the students have been allotted seats after the ICAR counselling rounds, they will be required to show up at their respective colleges along with their provisional admission letter and other required documents. Here is the detailed procedure for ICAR admission 2025 after the seat allotment process:

  • Provisional Admission Letter/Cancellation Letter: If a student’s seat is confirmed after the document verification process, then a provisional admission letter will be issued by the authorities. However, in case of seat cancellations, the ICAR authority will issue a cancellation letter.
  • Physical Document Verification Process: Once a student has downloaded the provisional admission letter, they will be required to report to the college along with their original documents, ID proofs for their physical verification.
  • Seat Acceptance Fee Payment: After reporting to the allocated college, students will be required to pay INR 10,000 to confirm their admission. In some cases, students may be required to pay the entire first-year fee. ICAR Admission 2025 will be confirmed after the successful fee payment.

How Many Rounds Will Be Conducted For ICAR UG Counselling?

In total, the ICAR counselling UG 2025 will take place in four rounds. Students who are unable to get a seat in these four rounds can take part in the ICAR UG Mop-Up Counselling Round. The Mop-Up Round will be conducted if there are still any vacant seats after the 4 rounds. This round will help students to secure a seat who were unable to attend any of the previous counselling rounds.

FAQs

What if I reject the seat during ICAR UG counselling?

If you reject a seat during ICAR UG counselling process, then you will not be eligible for that seat in the mop-up round. Additionally, you will not receive a refund of the seat allotment fee.

Why is my name not in the ICAR UG seat allotment?

If your name is not present in the ICAR UG seat allotment, then you might not have met the eligibility requirements or cleared the CUET exam. Other reasons could include issues with the registration or choice-filling process.

Where will the ICAR UG counselling 2025 schedule be released?

Students should keep tracking the official website of the National Testing Agency at exams.nta.ac.in/ICAR to download ICAR UG 2025 counselling brochure once it is released.

What if I miss an ICAR UG counselling round?

If you miss an ICAR UG counselling round, then you might not be eligible to participate in the subsequent counselling rounds. However, you can still apply in the later / mop-up rounds.

When will ICAR UG counselling 2025 start?

The ICAR UG counselling 2025 is expected to start in July 2025. Keep tracking the latest updates on ICAR UG counselling dates 2025 at NTA&rsquo;s official website.
