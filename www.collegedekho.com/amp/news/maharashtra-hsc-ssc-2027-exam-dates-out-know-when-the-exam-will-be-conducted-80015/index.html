Maharashtra HSC & SSC 2027 Exam Dates Result:The Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary has announced the official dates for the Maharashtra HSC and SSC 2027 exam dates. As per the official announcement, the Maharashtra HSC 2027 written exam will be held fromFebruary 8 to March 16, 2027, whereas the SSC written exam will be conducted fromFebruary 15 to March 11, 2027. Along with the HSC and SSC written exam dates, the Board has also released the Maharashtra HSC and SSC practical exam dates. The HSC Class 12 and SSC Class 10 Practical/ Oral/ internal evaluation dates areJanuary 8 to February 6, 2027,andJanuary 28 to February 13, 2027,respectively. The advance announcement regarding the exam dates is useful to keep the candidates aware, and they can take the exam accordingly.
