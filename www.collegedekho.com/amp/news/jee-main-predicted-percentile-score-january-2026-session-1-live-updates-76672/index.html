Since JEE is being conducted in multiple shifts, the difficulty level usually varies each shift. Considering that the paper can be either easy, moderate, or tough, the percentile vs. score analysis has been presented accordingly for all obtainable percentiles.

While evaluating a candidate's performance, NTA usesJEE normalization methodto adjust raw scores from every exam’s shift to account for varying difficulty levels. In other words, to determine the candidate’s percentile, NTA first calculates what the overall difficulty level of the paper was - easy, moderate, or tough. Then, accordingly, the percentile is decided for all candidates who took the examination. As a result, our subject expert has also analyzed percentile vs score for all varied difficulty levels across all shifts collectively.
