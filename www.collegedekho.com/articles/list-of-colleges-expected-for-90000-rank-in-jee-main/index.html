FAQs

How to check the JEE Main 2025 rank?

To check your JEE Main 2025 rank, go to the official website i.e.,  jeemain.nta.nic.in, log in with your JEE Main 2025 application number and password/date of birth, and download your JEE Main 2025 rank card. 

Which NIT is considered to be the best?

Based on the NIRF rankings, NIT Tiruchirappalli (NIT Trichy) has consistently ranked as the top NITs in India. Other highly reputed NITs comprise NIT Rourkelal, NIT Surathakal, and NIT Warangal. 

What is the ranking system in the JEE Mains 2025 exam?

JEE Main Rank is the position of a candidate on the basis of the marks they have secured in the JEE Main 2025 exam. It is a numerical value assigned to a candidate based upon their performance in the JEE Main 2025 exam. The JEE Main 2025 rank is calculated based on the total number of candidates who appeared for the JEE Main 2025 exam and their scores.

How to predict college by the JEE Main college predictor 2025?

The JEE Main 2025 college predictor tool makes use of historical data, comprising cutoffs from the previous years, to analyze and predict the possible colleges a candidate may get admission to based upon their JEE Main 2025 rank. Candidates input their JEE Main 2025 rank, category, and other important details to derive a list of colleges suitable for their rank.

Which college can I get with 70 percentile in JEE Main 2025?

With a 70 percentile in JEE Main 2025, you have a fair chance of securing admission into some of the top engineering colleges in India such as National Institute of Technology (NITs), Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs), and other state-level engineering institutes.

Can I get admission into IIIT with 93 percentile in JEE Main 2025?

It is extremely challenging for the general category candidates to get seat in NIT or IIIT with 93 percentile in JEE Main 2025. However, candidates can opt for GFTI as well as state college counselling.

Can I get admission in any IIIT with 87 percentile in JEE Main 2025?

Scoring 87 percentile in JEE Main 2025 is not considered to be good for securing admission across the IIITs (Indian Institutes of Information Technology) or NITs (National Institutes of Technology) in the B. Tech courses, specifically for candidates belonging to the General category.

What is the minimum rank to get admission into NIT?

Candidates’ rank should be below 10,000 to secure admission into NITs. However, the newer NITs offer admission to candidates with a rank above 70,000 in JEE Main 2025.

Can I get admission in NITs with 1.5 lakh rank in JEE Main 2025?

You can get admission into the CSE course offered by the colleges such as NIT Sikkim, NIT Meghalaya, NIT Puducherry, NIT Goa, etc. with 1.5 lakh rank in JEE Main 2025.

Can I get admission in any NIT with 90,000 rank in JEE Main 2025?

With a rank of 90,000 in JEE Main 2025 exam, it is difficult for the candidates to get admission into the NIT colleges. Some of the low rank NIT colleges offer admission to the candidates with 90,000 rank in JEE Main 2025.
