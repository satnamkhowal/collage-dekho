There are over 900 engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu, including 60+ government colleges and 550+ private institutions, offering BTech and MTech programs. These courses are available across a wide range of disciplines such as Computer Science Engineering (CSE), Information Technology (IT), Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Mechanical Engineering (ME), and more. Admission to BTech programs is primarily based on scores obtained in JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and CUET, while MTech admissions are conducted through GATE and CUET PG scores. Additionally, some colleges offer direct admissions based on Class 12 merit through Tamil Nadu Engineering Admission (TNEA) counselling. The cutoff for engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu varies greatly with the type of college. Tier-1 colleges usually have a cutoff above 95 percentile whereas cutoff for tier-2 colleges fluctuates between 85-95 percentile in JEE Mains. For government and aided colleges, cutoffs are determined through TNEA counselling based on Class 12 PCM marks, with top branches like CSE and AIML having higher cutoffs followed by ECE and ME. The following article talks about the top engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu classifying them based on their NIRF ranks, highest and average packages, fees, overall ROI and other factors.
