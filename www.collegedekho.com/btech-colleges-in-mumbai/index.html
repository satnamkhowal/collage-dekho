Mumbai has more than 90 BTech colleges, including government institutes, autonomous engineering colleges, deemed universities, and private institutions. The city’s engineering education system is led by institutions such as IIT Bombay, Institute of Chemical Technology Mumbai, Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute, Sardar Patel Institute of Technology, and K. J. Somaiya College of Engineering. Admissions in most Mumbai engineering colleges are conducted through MHT CET and Maharashtra CAP counselling, while IIT Bombay admits students through JEE Advanced and JoSAA.

Fee structures vary widely across colleges. Government institutions such as VJTI and ICT Mumbai have comparatively lower BTech fees, generally between INR 2–4 lakhs for the full programme. IIT Bombay’s total BTech fee is around INR 9.2 lakhs. Private colleges such as SPIT, DJSCE, and KJSCE typically have total BTech fees ranging from INR 4–10 lakhs depending on the branch and category.

Placement data also differs by institution and specialisation. IIT Bombay reported an average BTech package of around INR 23.5 LPA, with technology, consulting, and finance companies among the largest recruiters. VJTI Mumbai reported average engineering packages near INR 12 LPA, while SPIT Mumbai recorded average packages around INR 14.5 LPA. KJSCE reported packages crossing INR 1 crore internationally in recent placement cycles. ICT Mumbai continues to remain focused on chemical, polymer, and pharmaceutical engineering placements, with recruiters from sectors such as oil & gas, FMCG, and speciality chemicals.

Top 10 BTech Colleges in Mumbai — NIRF Rankings 2025

Mumbai is India's most distinctive BTech city: it has IIT Bombay (#3 NIRF nationally) and ICT Mumbai (#41 Engineering) — two of India's top 45 engineering institutions — alongside a deep network of private autonomous colleges (SPIT, KJSCE, DJSCE, TSEC, Fr. CRCE) that consistently deliver placement packages of INR 12–20+ LPA. No other Indian city has this combination of an IIT, a specialised national institute, and multiple top-performing private engineering colleges within the same metro.

VJTI Mumbai (Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute, est. 1887) is the oldest engineering institution in Mumbai — predating IIT Bombay (1958) by 71 years. Established under the reign of Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad of Baroda in 1887, VJTI is one of India's oldest continuously operating technical institutions. Its MHT CET Computer Engineering cutoff of rank 112 (percentile 99.961) reflects the calibre of students it attracts — comparable to top NIT cutoffs nationally. VJTI's avg INR 12 LPA at INR 3.37 L total fee gives a 359% ROI — the highest of any Mumbai government BTech college.

Top 10 Government BTech Colleges in Mumbai

VJTI and ICT Mumbai both use CAPAI (Centralised Process to Autonomous Institutes) within the Maharashtra CAP framework — a specific sub-process for autonomous institutions. Students targeting VJTI or ICT Mumbai should verify registration requirements at dtemaharashtra.gov.in during the counselling window, as CAPAI has a slightly different documentation process than the general CAP for SPPU-affiliated colleges.

Top 10 Private BTech Colleges in Mumbai

Mumbai's private BTech landscape is dominated by SPPU autonomous colleges in the western suburbs (Andheri, Vile Parle, Bandra) and a few private universities (NMIMS, Somaiya Vidyavihar). SPIT, KJSCE, DJSCE, Fr. CRCE, and TSEC are among Mumbai's most consistently sought-after private engineering colleges with MHT CET cutoffs above 99.5 percentile for CSE.

Best BTech College in Mumbai for CSE

IIT Bombay is the best BTech CSE college in Mumbai (and #3 nationally) — CSE 258/277 placed (93%+), avg IIT Bombay UG INR 23.50 LPA, highest INR 1.68 Cr. For MHT CET students: VJTI's Computer Engineering (MHT CET rank 112 / 99.961 percentile) is the next best, followed by SPIT (99.5+), KJSCE (avg INR 20 LPA), DJSCE, and Fr. CRCE.

The MHT CET students who score this high typically have JEE Main scores in the 98th–99.5th percentile range nationally — meaning VJTI Computer Engineering attracts a peer cohort comparable to lower-ranked NIT CSE branches.

What MHT CET percentile is needed for top Mumbai BTech colleges?

Mumbai's top BTech colleges (VJTI, SPIT, KJSCE) are among the most competitive MHT CET destinations in Maharashtra — requiring 99.5–99.961 percentile for Computer Engineering. The 2025 confirmed VJTI cutoff data shows precisely how selective these institutions are.

Entrance Exams Accepted for BTech Colleges in Mumbai

Mumbai BTech admission has two entirely separate systems. Only IIT Bombay uses JEE Advanced + JoSAA. Every other Mumbai BTech college uses MHT CET + Maharashtra CAP. This is the most critical planning point for Mumbai BTech applicants.

Placements & ROI at Top BTech Colleges in Mumbai 

IIT Bombay dominates Mumbai BTech placements nationally — avg INR 23.50 LPA, 65 international offers, CSE 258/277 placed. KJSCE leads private colleges by average (INR 20 LPA). SPIT leads accessible private colleges at INR 14.50 LPA. Mumbai's placement ecosystem is uniquely anchored in finance-tech (J.P. Morgan, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley all recruit from Mumbai engineering campuses — rare for non-IIT colleges nationally).

How many BTech colleges are there in Mumbai?

Mumbai has over 93 BTech colleges — approximately 17 government and 76 private (promilo.com data). Colleges are affiliated with the University of Mumbai or operate as SPPU autonomous, deemed university, or independent university institutions. IIT Bombay is a central government Institute of National Importance. ICT Mumbai and VJTI are state government autonomous institutions. Most private colleges are SPPU-affiliated autonomous institutions in the western suburbs (Andheri, Vile Parle, Bandra) or Navi Mumbai.

Is IIT Bombay the best BTech college in India?

IIT Bombay is ranked #3 Engineering and #3 Overall in NIRF 2025, making it India's third-best BTech institution after IIT Madras (#1) and IIT Delhi (#2). Globally, IIT Bombay ranks 47th in Engineering and Technology in QS World University Rankings by Subject (2023) and is one of India's only engineering institutions in THE World University Rankings Top 500. For BTech CSE specifically, IIT Bombay's CSE avg INR 53.2 LPA (Adarsh Barnwal data) is among the highest in India. IIT Bombay is also NIRF Innovation #1 — India's most innovative university.
