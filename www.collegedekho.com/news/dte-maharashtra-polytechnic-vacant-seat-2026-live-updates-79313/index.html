CAP Round 1 Option Filling Process

Candidates should keep the following points in mind while filling their college preferences:

  • Arrange colleges in your genuine order of preference.
  • Keep your dream college at the top, followed by realistic and safe options.
  • Include a sufficient number of colleges to improve allotment chances.
  • Review the previous year's closing cutoffs before locking choices.
  • Verify every selected college and branch before final submission.
  • Submit and confirm the option form before the deadline to become eligible for CAP Round 1 allotment

What Happens After the CAP Round 1?

After the announcement of the CAP Round 1 allocation, the allocated candidates must complete the online confirmation process and report to the Admission Reporting Centre (ARC) and the allotted institution as per the schedule. In case any candidate is unhappy with his/her allotment, he/she can join further CAP rounds, where new vacant seats that occur after Round 1 are declared for the next option filling procedure.
