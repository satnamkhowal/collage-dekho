TG CPGET 2026 Phase 1 Seat Allotment LIVE Updates

TG CPGET 2026 Phase 1 Seat Allotment LIVE Updates: The Telangana Council of Higher Education will release the TG CPGET 2026 Phase 1 seat allotment result on September 17, 2026, on the official website, cpgetadmissions.com . The authority has yet to announce the time for releasing the TG CPGET 2026 Phase 1 seat allotment. Based on previous years’ trends, the TG CPGET Phase 1 seat allotment result is likely to be released in the afternoon or evening.

Before releasing the Phase 1 seat allotment result, candidates are advised to keep ready the login credentials such as the TG CPGET hall ticket number/ registration number, which need to be entered to check the result. Seat allottees should accept the allotted seat, pay the fees, and complete self-reporting through the website on or before September 21, 2026.

TG CPGET 2026 Phase 1 Seat Acceptance Process

Candidates who will receive a seat via the TG CPGET Phase 1 seat allotment round will have to complete the prescribed seat acceptance procedure on or by September 18, 2026. Depending on the counselling instructions, this may involve paying the required admission or seat confirmation fee through the designated online facility. Check out the following steps for accepting the allotted seats:

  • Visit the official TG CPGET counselling website and open the Phase 1 seat allotment link.
  • Log in using your credentials and check the allotted college and postgraduate course carefully in the seat allotment result.
  • Download the allotment order and verify details including candidate name, course, college, category, and reporting instructions.
  • Pay the prescribed seat acceptance/admission fee through the available online payment facility before the deadline.
  • Download and save the fee payment receipt for future reference. Candidates should ensure that the transaction status is successful.
  • Complete self-reporting via the official website. Candidates should follow the instructions mentioned in the allotment order.
  • Report to the allotted college within the specified period if physical reporting is required. Carry the allotment order, fee receipt, and required original documents.
  • Complete document verification at the allotted institution. Candidates should provide the certificates and other documents requested by the college.
  • Confirm admission within the deadline. Failure to pay the required fee or complete reporting formalities on time may result in cancellation of the allotted seat.

Candidates should not assume that receiving an allotment automatically completes admission. They must follow every instruction mentioned in the allotment order. Failure to pay the prescribed fee or complete the required process within the deadline may result in cancellation of the allotted seat.
