How to Access the Choice Filling Link Once Activated

When KEA toggles the link live on the portal, candidates can complete their option entry using the following steps:

  • Navigate to cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea.
  • Click on the link titled "KCET 2026 Round 2 Option Entry" on the admissions dashboard.
  • Enter your CET Application Number and Secret Key / Password assigned during document verification.
  • Re-order your priority list, remove unwanted institutes, or add new course-college combinations based on the newly published vacancy matrix.
  • Frequently save your updates and keep a printed copy of the locked worksheet displaying the submission timestamp for future reference.

Key Guidelines for Round 2 Option Filling

Choice 2 Upgradation Policy: Candidates with a seat allocation from Choice 2 during Round 1 will keep their seat allocation on a provisional basis. The seat will be automatically voided and reallocated to some other candidate in the merit list if the higher option allocated in Round 2 gets seat allocation.

Check the Seat Vacancy Matrix: Candidates should first review the seat vacancy matrix of each government, aided, and private engineering college according to branches and categories before filling the options.

No Limit on Options: Candidates can enter an extensive list of safety options to increase the possibility of getting admission in this round.

Auto-Locking Process: All options left on the website by the deadline will get auto-locked; still, candidates are encouraged to save the options well in advance.
