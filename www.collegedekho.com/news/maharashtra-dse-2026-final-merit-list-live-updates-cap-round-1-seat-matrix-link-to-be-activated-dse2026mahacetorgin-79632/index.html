Maharashtra DSE 2026 Final Merit List LIVE Updates

Maharashtra DSE 2026 Final Merit List LIVE Updates: The State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra, will release the DSE final merit list for CAP Round 1 today, on July 30, 2026 . Along with the final merit list, the authority will also release the provisional category-wise seat matrix on the official website: dse2026.mahacet.org.in . Candidates whose names will be displayed on the final merit list can participate in the CAP Round 1 option entry process, which will begin on July 31, 2026. Note that once the final merit list is released, the candidates will not be able to submit any grievances under any circumstances. Before exercising the CAP Round 1 options, the candidates should refer to the seat matrix to know the college-wise available vacant seats.

After releasing the Maharashtra DSE 2026 final merit list and before participating in the CAP Round 1 option entry process, the candidates should refer to the following instructions:

  • Review the Seat Matrix First: Before filling the CAP Round 1 preferences between July 31 and August 2, candidates should analyse the Seat Matrix PDF to identify available vacant seats across institutes for branch and category.
