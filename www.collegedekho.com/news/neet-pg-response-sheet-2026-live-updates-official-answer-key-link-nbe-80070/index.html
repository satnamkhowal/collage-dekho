How to Calculate Probable Marks via NBEMS Marking Scheme?

Once candidates access the official response key link, they can calculate their raw estimated score using the standard formula:

\[\text{Estimated Score} = (\text{Total Correct Answers} \times 4) - (\text{Total Incorrect Answers} \times 1)\]

  • Correct Option: +4 Marks

  • Incorrect Option: -1 Mark

  • Unattempted Question: 0 Marks

If any incorrect options or question discrepancies are spotted in the master answer sheet, NBEMS will activate a limited online challenge portal for candidate submissions. Aspirants are advised to keep their candidate login credentials handy for instant updates.
