Start with the branch you want, then compare the department—not just the institution name.

Choose the branch before the college

Computer Science, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil and other branches lead to different coursework and practical requirements. Compare curriculum, labs, faculty exposure and internships for the specific department.

Admission route

B.Tech admission commonly depends on Class 12 subject eligibility and may involve JEE Main, state counselling or institution-specific processes. The exact route can change, so check the current official admission notice before applying.
