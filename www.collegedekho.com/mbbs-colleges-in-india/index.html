Updated on - Sep 21, 2026 12:14 PM IST

There are 344 M.B.B.S. (Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery) colleges in India. Out of these, 82 are government/public colleges and 183 are privately owned. The fees for top M.B.B.S.
