Top Medical Colleges in India Admissions

Most of the Medical colleges in India take admission on the basis of an entrance exam followed by a personal interview and group discussion round. The detailed eligibility criteria might vary from college to college but the basic eligibility for admission has been listed below:

  • Candidates must have passed 12th or equivalent from a recognised board.

Selection Process for Medical Colleges in India

Admission to top Medical colleges in India is done either through an entrance exam or merit in the entrance exam. Most of the top medical colleges in India offer admissions based on entrance exams. However, some private colleges in India offer direct admissions based on merit or medical quota. Usually, the selection process for the best Medical colleges in India goes through the 3 stages as listed below:

Through Entrance Exam:

  • Written Admission Test (WAT): The initial step in the selection process for top Medical colleges in India is to qualify for the written test. Entrance exams like TNEA, AIPMT, AP EAMCET, NEET, AIIMS PG, COMEDK UGET, FMGE, etc. are accepted for admission to medical colleges in India.
  • Group Discussion (GD): Candidates who qualify for the written test will have to appear for the group discussion round. Here a topic will be given to a group of students to speak upon.
  • Personal Interview (PI): The final round of selection for the best Medical colleges in India is the personal interview round. Candidates who qualify for this round will be selected for final admission.

Direct Admission:

Step 1: Check eligibility criteria, fee structure, and placement records for the college you wish to apply at.

Step 2: Fill out the admission form in online or offline mode.

Step 3: Submit required documents like graduation, 10th, and 12th mark sheets, transfer certificate, etc.

Step 4: If your application is accepted, you will be notified of the admission.

Step 5: Report to the allotted college for document verification, pay the required fees, and complete the admission formalities.

*Note: These are the general steps in the admission process and the actual selection process might vary from college to college.

Entrance Exams Accepted Top Medical Colleges in India 2025

Most of the top Medical colleges in India require candidates to qualify for an entrance exam. Now, students must be wondering which entrance exams they need to appear for to get admission to Medical colleges in India. There are various exams accepted for admission such as TNEA, AIPMT, AP EAMCET, NEET, AIIMS PG, COMEDK UGET, FMGE, etc..

AIIMS MBBS

AIIMS MBBS is a national level entrance exam for admission to MBBS course at the AIIMS (All India Institute of Medical Sciences). Candidates who have passed 12th with PCB are eligible for AIIMS exam. The selection is based on AIIMS scores and subsequent GD-PI rounds.

NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test)

NEET is the single largest medical entrance examination in India. It's conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to undergraduate medical courses (MBBS, BDS, etc.) in government and private medical colleges across India.

COMEDK UGET

COMEDK UGET (Undergraduate Entrance Test) is a state-level engineering entrance exam conducted by the Consortium of Medical, Engineering, and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK). While it's primarily for engineering admissions, it also offers admissions to medical and dental courses in participating colleges in Karnataka.

FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Examination)

The Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) is a licensure examination conducted by the National Board of Examinations (NBE)  in India. It's mandatory for Indian citizens who have completed their primary medical qualification from a foreign medical institution to practice medicine in India.

AIPMT

The All India Pre-Medical Test (AIPMT) is a national-level entrance examination conducted by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) for admission to MBBS and BDS courses in government medical colleges across India.
