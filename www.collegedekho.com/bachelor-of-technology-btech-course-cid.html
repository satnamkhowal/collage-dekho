BTech course is a four-year undergraduate degree awarded to candidates interested in engineering and technology. The BTech full form is Bachelor of Technology and this program is either studied as a general degree or with specialisations like Electrical Engineering (EE), Mechanical Engineering (ME), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Civil Engineering (CE), and many more. Candidates can get admission to the top BTech colleges either through merit or entrance exams like JEE Main, JEE Advanced, MHT CET, TS EAMCET, etc.

BTech Course Overview

BTech Course, also known as Bachelor of Technology, includes comprehensive learning in the field of Engineering and technology. It is a four-year undergraduate degree course that has seen a jump in recent years. It is because of the rise in technology-related innovations, software engineering, machine learning, AI, and more. Candidates can choose any specialisation of their choice in the BTech course, such as chemical engineering, civil engineering, mechanical engineering, electronics and communication engineering, robotics engineering, etc.

BTech course eligibility requirements are graduates of Class 12 in the Science stream with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. They must have scored above 50%- 60% aggregate. Additionally, the top BTech colleges in India like the Indian Institute of Technology (IITs), National Institute of Technology (NITs), VIT Vellore, and many more accept national, state, or college-level entrance exams. The most popular BTech entrance exams for students are JEE Main, JEE Advanced, TS EAMCET, VITEEE, etc.

While the BTech course fees range from INR 2,00,000 to INR 20,00,000, the salary of a BTech individual starts from INR 2,50,000 per annum. The scope and opportunities for this course are vast with profiles like Data Scientist, Web Developer, Software Engineer, etc., in esteemed job-providing institutions like Wipro, Infosys, Tata Consultancy, Accenture Services, IBM Global Services, and HCL Technologies.

BTech Course Eligibility Criteria

The eligibility for BTech after 12th is as follows:

  • Minimum Qualification Required: Candidates have to pass 10+2 in the Science stream (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics) from a recognised board or equivalent.
  • Minimum Marks Required (Aggregate): Candidates have to secure 50% marks in aggregate. The maximum marks can be as high as 70% depending on college-to-college.
  • Age Limit: Some colleges and entrance exams can have a restriction on age for all the aspirants who aspire to take admission in BTech courses. Candidates should be at least 18 years of age and not be more than 24 years of age counted from the date of the examination.
  • Entrance Exams: The JEE Main and JEE Advanced are considered the two national-level entrance examinations through which BTech admissions are granted in IITs, NITs, and CFTIs. Other than this, state-level entrance tests can also be conducted and some BTech entrance tests are separately organized by the universities themselves.

Note: Original copies of all necessary documents and mark sheets have to be presented during the time of admission at the respective college or university.

On the other hand, if a student seeks BTech course 2nd-year admission via the method of lateral entry then students with a diploma must fulfill the subsequent BTech lateral entry eligibility criteria:

  • Diploma in engineering from an AICTE-approved institute
  • Minimum marks in diploma to be decided by the university
  • JEE Main or Entrance (if any), accepted for Lateral entry by the University

BTech Course Entrance Exams

There are numerous entrance exams in India, divided into national, state level, and university levels. The national level top BTech entrance exams are JEE Main and JEE Advanced state-level BTech exams include WBJEE, MHT CET; And university level exams include BITSAT, VITEEE, and SRMJEEE. To know more about the entrance exam for BTech after 12th, read the following.

JEE Main

The Joint Entrance Examination is conducted by the National Testing Agency or NTA. It is conducted for admissions in NIT, IIITs, GFTIs, and other institutions all over India.

JEE Advanced

The JEE Advanced is conducted for admissions to IITs. Candidates who come under the ranks of 2.5 Lakhs are eligible to apply and sit for JEE Advanced. 

WBJEE

WBJEE is conducted by the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination Board. It is conducted for admissions in undergraduate BTech courses.

MHT CET

MHT CET is conducted by the state common entrance test cell of Maharashtra. The exam is conducted based on class 11 and class 12 syllabi.  

BITSAT

BITSAT is conducted by the Birla Institute of Technology & Science, Pilani for students who are interested in taking admissions in the Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad campuses. 

VITEEE

VITEEE is conducted by the Vellore Institute of Technology for candidates who are interested in taking admissions for the Vellore and the Chennai campuses.

SRMJEEE

SRMJEEE is conducted by SRM University for candidates willing to take admissions in BTech for various campuses. Admissions are taken based on entrance exams and merit, and the seats are allotted on the choice of filling and merit.

How to Get Admission to a BTech Course?

If you want to know how to get admission to BTech course in India, below is the sequence of steps that have to be followed by the candidates before applying for BTech courses.

  • Candidates to be admitted to BTech have to appear for an entrance exam
  • There are various national-level entrance exams like JEE Main, which is accepted by NITs, GFTIs, and JEE Advanced which is accepted exclusively by IITs
  • Other than the exams mentioned above, there are other state-level exams that are accepted by many colleges like WBJEE, AP EAMCET, TS EAMCET, KEAM, and MHT CET. other university-based entrance exams such as VITEEE, SRMJEEE, BITSAT, UPESEAT, etc
  • The entrance exams are conducted mostly in April, May, and June every year
  • After the declaration of the results, it is followed by counselling, and the seat allotment process is conducted by the concerned authorities. During the counselling process, candidates will have to choose the college and courses of their choice based on the rank acquired by them.
  • After the college is chosen, candidates will have to visit the campus of the respective college to execute the admission formalities.

Can I pursue BTech course without maths?

Yes, students can pursue BTech without maths according to AICTE. It means students who have studied Physics, Chemistry, and Biology can also do BTech, especially the CSE specialisation.
