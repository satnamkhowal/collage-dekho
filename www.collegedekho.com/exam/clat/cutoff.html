CLAT 2026 Cutoff

CLAT 2026 Cutoff will be released for round 3 has been released on February 25, 2026. Round 1 cutoff of CLAT 2026 has been released in the online mode on January 7, 2026 and round 2 on January 22, 2026. One of the most important factors for applicants hoping to gain admission to India's top National Law Universities (NLUs) is CLAT Cutoff 2026. The Consortium of National Law Universities (NLUs) administers the Common Law Admission Test, a national entrance exam for undergraduate (UG) and graduate (PG) legal programs. Each participating NLU provides its own CLAT cutoff scores and rankings following the results announcement, depending on a number of variables such exam difficulty, category, and seat availability. 

The minimum rank or score needed for an applicant to be admitted to a specific NLU is known as the CLAT cutoff. Every year and across categories like General, OBC, SC, ST, and PwD, it changes. Additionally, the five-year integrated LL.B. program (UG) and the LL.M. program (PG) have different cutoffs. Because of its repute and small number of seats, elite NLUs like NLSIU Bengaluru, NALSAR Hyderabad, and WBNUJS Kolkata typically have the highest cutoffs. 

You must be aware of CLAT cutoff 2026 in order to evaluate your chances of being accepted into the law schools of their choice during the counseling and seat allocation process. Setting reasonable preparation targets is made easier by examining past year cutoffs, which also offer insightful information about anticipated patterns.

Factors Determining CLAT Cutoff 2026

On the basis of the CLAT 2026 Cutoff, you will be offered admission into the colleges accepting CLAT scores/ ranks. After the release of the CLAT 2026 Results, those who have appeared in the entrance exam must check the CLAT 2026 Cutoff, which is based on different parameters. The factors that determine CLAT 2026 Cutoff are listed below.

  • Number of seats available
  • Number of candidates who appeared in CLAT 2026
  • Cut-off of the previous year
  • Number of qualifying candidates
  • The difficulty level of the entrance exam

Types of CLAT Cutoff 2026

The CLAT cutoffs are released in multiple stages during the admission process:

  • Expected Cutoff: After the test, experts and coaching centers provide predictions based on student input and the degree of difficulty.
  • Official Cutoff: The final closing ranks for each NLU is released by the Consortium of NLUs following the counseling rounds.
  • Opening Rank: The rank of the first candidate who got admission in an NLU.
  • Closing Rank: The rank of the last candidate who secured a seat in that NLU.

Importance of CLAT 2026 Cutoff

Given below is the importance of the CLAT 2026 Cutoff 

  • It can give you an idea as to what rank is needed to secure admission.
  • The CLAT 2026 Cutoff is category and course specific, so one needs to refer to the programme they are applying for admission.
  • Since CLAT 2026 Cutoff is variable and depends on factors such as category and seat intake, it is important to keep a check on these factors as well.

What is a Good Score in CLAT 2026?

According to the CLAT exam pattern being revised in 2024, the total number of questions has been reduced to 120 from 150. This change has impacted the predicted good score in CLAT 2026 as compared to the exams before 2024. The revised exam pattern has also altered the overall CLAT cutoff score. As per the exam analysis, a good score in CLAT 2026 will be around 85 - 90 marks. The general category students should aim to score 90+, which is a good score to get into the top 3 NLUs. Similarly, a good score for those belonging to the SC/ ST category will be 60-70. 

A moderately easy paper indicates that a good score in CLAT 2026 would be 80 - 90 marks to get into the top 10 NLUs. Tier-2 and tier-3 NLUs will have lower cutoffs than the top-ranked NLUs. However, the difference in cutoff marks of the top 5-6 NLUs is not as vast as it may appear if we compare it with the previous years' scores. The cutoffs will increase with tougher competition and a higher number of test-takers.

FAQs about CLAT Cut Off

How much should I score in the English section of CLAT to clear the cutoff?

The CLAT cutoff for the English section is tentatively 30. If you score equal to or above this, you will be able to clear the CLAT cutoff easily.

What is the expected cutoff of CLAT for the GK section?

The expected CLAT cutoff for the General Knowledge and Current section is around 30-35.

Is there a section-wise cutoff of CLAT?

Yes, there is a defined section-wise CLAT cutoff every year. Candidates must make sure that they qualify the cutoff of each section of CLAT.

What are the NLUs demanding high CLAT cutoff?

NLSIU Bangalore, NALSAR Hyderabad, NLU New Delhi, WBNUJS Kolkata are among the top NLUs which have the highest CLAT cutoff every year.

Can I apply for admission to a college whose CLAT cutoff I do not qualify?

No, you cannot apply for admission to a law college whose CLAT cutoff criteria you fail to meet. The college will not consider your application and will not send you the call letter for the counselling process.

Why is CLAT cutoff important?

The CLAT cutoff reveals to the candidates the colleges in which they can apply for admission and the colleges for admission to which they are not eligible.

How is CLAT cutoff determined?

The CLAT cutoff is determined on the basis of several factors. Some of them are: number of seats available, number of candidates who appeared for the exam, previous year CLAT cutoff, number of qualifying candidates, difficulty level of the exam, etc.

Is CLAT cutoff for all the colleges same?

No, the CLAT cutoff is different for all the colleges that participate in the entrance examination.

Is CLAT cutoff the same for every category?

No, different cutoff lists of CLAT are released for all the categories. The Consortium publishes category-wise CLAT cutoff separately.

When is CLAT cutoff declared?

The cutoff of CLAT is released after the CLAT results have been announced.
