About BHU UET 2022

The BHU UET 2022 exam date is expected to be released in May and the candidates can start filling the application form filling once the notification is released. The Banaras Hindu University (BHU) conducts BHU UET every year for admission into various Undergraduate courses (UG) offered by the University and its affiliated colleges.

Every year, BHU conducts this entrance examination to give admission to all the eligible candidates. The BHU UET entrance examination is conducted at the university level only and on the basis of BHU UET scores, candidates are offered admission into the university.  All the candidates who are interested to take admission into various courses offered by the Banaras Hindu University (BHU) can check out all the relevant information about BHU UET 2022 application form, admit card, eligibility criteria, the pattern of examination, result, counselling details, etc before applying for the entrance examination here.

Is BHU UET Tough?

Banaras Hindu University is one of the creative and innovative universities of India, which was founded by Pandit Madan Mohan Malviya. BHU UET is an undergraduate entrance of Banaras Hindu University. BHU UET is conducted by the National Testing Agency. BHU UET is held every year for admission to Under Graduation programmes offered at BHU. The varsity offers a number of courses in UG.

BHU UET examination is not very difficult to crack, provided the preparation and the basics of the concepts are strong. The preparation strategy should be good, covering all the aspects of preparation like topics, revision, writing mock tests… etc. The smart, studious, and sharp students do prepare for the examination in advance. The preparation, which is started early, gives good results. BHU UET examination is not tough at all. Let`s know the analysis of BHU UET for the last two years. 

In the year 2021 BHU UET examination, the difficulty level of the examination is easy to moderate. Most of the topics were easy except Mathematics because questions were tricky. 

According to the students who have appeared for BHU UET 2020, the overall difficulty of the examination was moderate to difficult.

According to the analysis of BHU UET 2019 Overall difficulty level of the examination is easy but a few sections like General Knowledge, Current Affairs, and Legal Aptitude were a little difficult.

Banaras Hindu University has given consent to the National Testing Agency to conduct under graduation entrance test form this year, which means the aspirants of BHU UET must appear for CUCET 2022  in order to take admissions in BHU for undergraduate courses (Central Universities Common Entrance Test) from this year onwards.  BHU UET is merged with CUCET.

BHU UET Conducting Body

Banaras Hindu University was founded by Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviyaji in 1916. The university offers various undergraduate, postgraduate and diploma courses. BHU is constantly working towards promoting learning and research in the fields of Arts and Sciences. There are 6 institutes, 14 faculties (streams) and more than 132 departments functioning under BHU. With over 20,000 students, BHU is one of the largest residential universities in Asia. It has many colleges such as IIT-BHU, FMS-BHU, IAS-BHU, IMS-BHU, ESD-BHU that are considered among the best in the country.

BHU UET Application Process 2022 - Important Dates

The application process of BHU UET 2022 will begin once the official notification for the exam is released by Banaras Hindu University (BHU). To apply for BHU UET 2022, candidates will have to fill the complete BHU UET 2022 application form in online mode. While filling the application form of BHU UET 2022, candidates must ensure that they fill in only the correct information and before the final submission, they should verify all the details entered by them in the BHU UET 2022 application form.

BHU UET 2021 Eligibility Criteria

Before proceeding to fill BHU UET application form 2022, it is advisable to candidates to meet the BHU UET 2022 eligibility criteria. It is mandatory to fulfil all the mentioned eligibility norms in order to proceed with the BHU UET 2022 application form filling process.

With the help of BHU UET 2022 eligibility criteria, candidates will know about the detailed eligibility norms including educational parameters, nationality, age limit, reservation criteria etc which is required to appear for the entrance examination.

BHU UET 2022 Pattern of Examination

Candidates must take a look at BHU UET 2022 exam pattern, before beginning their preparation for BHU UET 2022. The exam pattern comprises the type of questions asked in the entrance exam duration of the test, marking scheme, etc.

Candidates must take note that since BHU UET is held for various UG courses, therefore, the pattern of examination will be different for each course. The question paper will be objective type in nature and the time duration of attempting the question paper of BHU UET will be different for each course. BHU UET 2022 Admit Card

Banaras Hindu University (BHU) will release the admit card of Banaras Hindu University (BHU)  in online mode for all the candidates who have successfully registered for the entrance examination. To access the admit card of BHU UET 2022, candidates will have to download the admit card by using their login credentials created during registration.

The admit card of BHU UET is an important document which candidates must carry to the exam centre and it will have all the details like the candidate’s name, roll number of the candidate, address of exam centre, photograph and signature of the candidate etc. Candidates must ensure that they check all the information that is mentioned in the  BHU UET 2022 admit card before downloading it.

FAQs about BHU UET

How many times in a year is BHU UET held?

BHU UET is held only once a year for all the undergraduate courses.

Is BHU UET a university-level exam?

Yes, BHU UET is a university-level exam.

How much is charged as an application fee for BHU UET?

Rs. 600 is charged as an application fee for one course for the Unreserved category. However, SC/ST candidates are required to pay just Rs. 300 for one course. For more than one course, Rs. 400 is charged per course from the Unreserved category and Rs. 200 is charged from the SC/ST candidates.

Are calculators allowed in the BHU UET?

No, calculators and other electronic devices or gadgets are strictly prohibited in the exam centre.

How many students apply for the BHU UET exam?

2-3 lakh students apply for the BHU UET exam every year.

What type of questions are asked in the BHU UET exam?

All the objective type questions are asked in the BHU UET exam.

What is the medium of instruction for BHU UET?

The medium of instruction for BHU UET is English and Hindi.

Does BHU UET offer admission to the B.Ed programme?

Yes, through BHU UET an individual can secure admission in a B.Ed programme.

Which courses are offered for admission through BHU UET?

BHU UET is short for the Undergraduate Entrance Test. It is held to offer admission to all the undergraduate courses provided by Banaras Hindu University.

Who is the conducting body of BHU UET?

The conducting body of BHU UET is Banaras Hindu University.
