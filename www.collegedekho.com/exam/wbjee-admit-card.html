WBJEE 2026 Exam Day Guidelines

Here are the important instructions that the applicants need to follow on the exam day of WBJEE 2026 -

  • You have to visit the WBJEE exam center 2026 at least 30 minutes before the reporting time

  • Do not forget to bring a valid ID card along with your WBJEE admit card 2026.

  • No participant will be allowed to carry any written or printed material, calculator, pen, log table, wristwatch, any communication device

  • Entry into the exam hall is strictly prohibited after the commencement of the exam

  • Bring a black/ blue ballpoint pen on the exam day

  • Applicants should take their seats at least 15 minutes before the commencement of the test

  • Applicants should put their signatures on the top of the question booklet

  • It is advisable to read the instructions given on the OMR and the cover page of the question booklet

  • While filling the OMR sheet, candidates should write their names in BLOCK LETTERS

  • Rough work should be done only in the space provided in the question booklet

  • Any participant who is found to be impersonating will be handed over to the police & candidature of the original candidate will be canceled

Problems while Downloading the WBJEE 2026 Admit Card

You might face the following problems while they are trying to download the admit card for WBJEE 2026: 

  • Registration is Incomplete: Applicants who have not completed the registration process or have not uploaded the documents/photographs in the specified format by WBJEEB will not be issued the WBJEE admit card. Therefore, students had to maintain utmost caution while filling out the application form and completing the registration formalities.

  • Incorrect Login Credentials: Students might tend to forget their login credentials while downloading the WBJEE hall ticket 2026. Therefore it is advised to write down the credentials like the application number and password in a personal diary and keep it safe. Applicants will not be able to download the hall ticket of WBJEE 2026 if the login credentials are incorrect. In case of, loss of the login credentials students must recover it using his/her registered mail ID or phone number.

Slow Internet Access: Applicants must ensure to have proper internet connectivity at home or elsewhere from where he/she can download the WBJEE 2026 hall ticket and preferably take a colored printout of the same to carry along on the day of the exam.

How to Correct Date of Birth on WBJEE Admit Card 2026?

To correct the date of birth on your WBJEE admit card, you should contact the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board (WBJEEB) as soon as possible. They will be able to guide you through the process of submitting the necessary documentation to correct. It is important to act promptly to ensure that your admit card reflects the correct information before the exam.

The WBJEEB is likely to ask for supporting documentation to verify the correct date of birth. Commonly accepted documents include a birth certificate, passport, Aadhaar card, or any other government-issued identification document that displays your correct date of birth.

After submitting the necessary documentation, you may need to follow up with the WBJEEB to ensure that they have received your request and that the correction is being processed. You can inquire about the status of your application and ask for an updated admit card reflecting the correct date of birth.

Steps to Take If WBJEE Admit Card 2026 Is Lost

Losing your admit card can be stressful, but there are steps you can take to address this situation. Here's what you can do - 

1. Contact the exam conducting authority or the institution where you need to submit the admit card for document verification. Explain your situation and ask if there are alternative methods for verifying your identity and participation in the exam.

2. If possible, provide any other forms of identification or documentation that you have to prove your identity and participation in the exam. This could include a copy of the admit card if you have one, along with other government-issued identification.

3. If the above steps are not feasible, inquire about the possibility of obtaining a duplicate admit card or a written confirmation from the exam conducting authority or institution to fulfill the document verification requirement.

What to do if No Photo on WBJEE Admit Card 2026?

The photograph and signature on the WBJEE admit card serve as an additional means of identification and help prevent impersonation. The photo helps authorities verify the identity of the applicant, while the name and date on the photo provide further confirmation and prevent any confusion. 

In case of a missing photograph in the WBJEE 2026 hall ticket, participants must reach out to the conducting authority, i.e., WBJEEB helpdesk number, to report the issue immediately. Failure to do so may result in complications or even disqualification from the examination.

How to Apply for a duplicate WBJEE Admit Card 2026?

In case you misplace or lose your admit card for the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination (WBJEE) 2026, you can obtain a duplicate copy from the Board. However, it is important to note that the request for a duplicate admit card can only be made until the end of counselling or 60 days after the declaration of the result, whichever is earlier. To apply for a duplicate admit card, you need to write an application to the Board, along with a fee of INR 500. This fee should be paid through a bank draft drawn in favour of the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination Board and payable in Kolkata.

You need to carry your admit cards to the examination hall on the day of the WBJEE 2026 examination. The admit card contains important information such as the name, roll number, examination centre, date and time of the exam, etc. Therefore, you are advised to keep your admit cards safe and secure till the completion of the examination process.

WBJEE Exam Centres 2026

The conducting authorities have announced the WBJEE Exam Centres 2026 along with the release of the WBJEE notification. The exam centres are spread across different cities in West Bengal, and you can choose their preferred exam centre when filling out the WBJEE 2026 application form. 

Based on the preferences filled at the time of registration, the WBJEEB will allot the exam centre. It is worth noting that the authorities make every effort to allocate the first choice of exam centre to each student. The exam centre will be mentioned on their admit card, which they will need to carry with them on the day of the exam.
