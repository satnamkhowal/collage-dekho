TISSNET 2024 Conducting Body

Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS) was started as the Sir Dorabji Tata Graduate School of Social Work in the year 1936. In 1944, the institute got its current name. Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS) organizes a National Entrance Test (NET) under the name of TISSNET for admission to all Masters programmes offered at TISS campuses at Mumbai, Tuljapur, Hyderabad, Guwahati, IMHST Chennai, MHAT Calicut, and G-SET Ranchi. TISS aims to provide education to students by bringing together high-quality scholars and practitioners from different fields such as Social, Economic, Political, Physical, Habitat, Engineering, Health, and Environmental Sciences.

TISSNET Eligibility Criteria

Following are the complete TISSNET 2024 Eligibility Criteria:

  • Qualification: Candidates should have completed 15 years of formal education with any pattern of study from any recognized board.

  • The candidates must complete a Bachelor's Degree of a minimum of 3 or 4 years or equivalent from UGC Recognized University.

  • Age Limit: There is no upper age limit for TISSNET 2024.

  • Appearing: Candidates appearing in the final examination of the required qualification can also apply for the examination, but they must complete it before June 2023.

TISSNET 2024 Selection Process

Tata Institute of Social Sciences is responsible for governing the TISSNET Selection Process to shortlist the candidates for admission to their M.A programmes. The selection procedure consists of the three steps mentioned below:

  1. TISSNET Exam (TISS National Entrance Test)

  2. TISS Programme Aptitude Test (TISSPAT): It will be a 45 minutes aptitude test conducted for the shortlisted candidates for TISSNET examination. Through TISSPAT exam, candidates will be shortlisted for appearing in the Online Personal Interview (OPI).

  3. OA (ONLINE Assessment) will consist of 2 components: Extempore Reflection on a given topic and Online Personal Interview (OPI)

The final scores for admission will be calculated from the candidates scores in NET, PAT and OCI. 30% of TISSNET score, 40% of TISSPAT score and 30% of OCI will be considered for admission.

TISSNET 2024 Merit List

The official authorities will release the TISSNET 2024 Merit List through online mode. Candidates securing higher marks in the entrance exam than the minimum qualifying exam will be shortlisted for the TISSNET 2024 Merit List. Based on the first TISSNET 2024 Merit List, the official authorities will invite the shortlisted candidates to appear for the Pre – Interview and Group Discussion round. Then later on after the completion of the Pre – Interview and Group Discussion round, the official authorities will prepare a final TISSNET 2024 Merit List.

TISSNET 2024 Changes in MA (HRM) and MA (ODCL)

Given below are some of the key changes in TISSNET for MA (HRM) and MA (ODCL) for 2024-26.

  • TISSNET will be an online exam with a duration of 100 minutes.

  • Candidates can apply for a maximum of 3 MA programs. Barring MA (HR) and MA (ODCL), candidates can choose one more MA program.

  • The shortlisting will be done based on TISSNET scores for the Online PI round.

  • Once the shortlisting is done, candidates have to appear in TISSMAT/ TISSPAT before the online personal interview. However, due to the cancellation of these exams, the revised admission process announced by TISS will be applicable.

  • The Online Assessment and Online Personal Interviews will be conducted for all candidates. No offline process is defined for the same.

  • TISSMAT is a common test only for MA (HRM) and MA (ODCL) programs and it should not be taken separately for each of the two programs.

  • Final Admission will be based on TISSNET, TISSMAT, and OPI.

TISSNET 2024 Key Changes for Other MA Programmes

Listed below are some key changes for TISSNET other MA programmes

  • Candidates can apply for a maximum of 3 programmes.
  • Take Online 100 Minutes TISSNET Entrance Exam
  • The time duration for each TISSPAT is 45 minutes. For example, if you apply for 2 different MA programmes, you will spend 90 minutes on two TISSPAT exams.
  • The shortlisting will be done on the basis of TISSNET for online PI.
  • After shortlisting, it is mandatory to appear in TISSPAT before online PI for each MA programme separately.
  • PI for TIS admission 2024 will be held online only.
  • TISSNET 2024 weightage has been reduced and for TISSPAT, it has increased compared to last year.
  • Final admission will be done on the basis of TISSNET, TISSPAT, and performance in PI.

FAQs about TISSNET

Does TISS accept CAT scores?

No, TISS does not accept CAT scores. This is because the university conducts its separate entrance exam for admission to MA programmes in the School of Management and Labour Studies. The CAT exam, on the other hand, is the mandatory entrance exam for MBA admission to IIMs. If a student has given the CAT exam, they have to take TISSNET 2023 separately for admission to the Tata Institute of Social Sciences.

What is TISSMAT?

The TISSMAT or TISS Management Aptitude Test is a national-level entrance examination taken by candidates who are particularly interested in applying for MA (HRM & LR) and/or MA (ODCL) programmes. Only those applicants who take the TISSNET exam are eligible for the TISSMAT. The test has been designed to analyse the applicant's business and social aptitude. The ability to comprehend and interpret data about management literature, economy, market, trade, and business predicaments is expected of prospective TISSMAT applicants.

What is the exam pattern of the TISSNET 2024?

The exam pattern of the TISSNET 2024 includes three sections Quantitative Skills and Logical Reasoning, English Language Comprehension and Usage and Current Affairs and Understanding of Social Issues. It will have 100 multiple-choice questions, each for one mark. The exam lasts one hour and forty minutes and is administered in 100 test centres throughout India.

What are the TISSNET 2024 eligibility criteria?

The following are the TISSNET 2024 eligibility criteria:

  • The applicant must hold a bachelor's degree from an accredited university or the equivalent.

  • Students may also apply if they will have earned their bachelor's degree or its equivalent before June 2024.

  • When filling out the TISSNET 2024 application, applicants pursuing a three-year bachelor's degree should have cleared all four semesters, while those pursuing a four-year bachelor's degree must have passed all subjects through the sixth semester.

What are the TISSNET 2024 exam centres?

The following are the TISSNET 2024 exam centres in different regions of India:

Eastern: Silchar, Ranchi, Raipur, Patna, Kolkata, Jamshedpur, Guwahati, Dimapur, and Bhubaneswar.

Western: Thane, Solapur, Pune, Navi Mumbai, Nagpur, Mumbai, Indore, Gwalior, Bhopal, Aurangabad, and Ahmedabad.

Northern: Varanasi, Greater Noida, Mohali, Lucknow, Jaipur, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Delhi, Dehradun, and Chandigarh.

Southern: Visakhapatnam, Thiruvananthapuram, Madurai, Kozhikode, Hyderabad, Cochin, Chennai, and Bengaluru.

Is TISSNET conducted every year?

Yes, the TISSNET is conducted every year or it can also be said that it is conducted once a year. As per the official sources, the TISSNET 2024 will take place in February 2024. After taking the CAT 2023 and XAT 2023 exams, the TISSNET 2024 exam in February provides an excellent chance to secure admission to the highly coveted M.A. programmes offered by TISS at its Mumbai campus and other campuses like Tuljapur, Hyderabad, and Guwahati with effortless preparation.

What are some important TISSNET 2024 preparation tips?

The following are some important TISSNET 2024 preparation tips which students must follow to receive a decent score on the exam:

  • Examine your level of preparation by leveraging the TISSNET 2024 mock tests, previous year's question papers and sample papers.

  • Review the TISSNET 2024 exam pattern and syllabus thoroughly.

  • Join study groups on social media platforms to discuss problems and doubts.

  • Identify your stronger areas and revise them regularly while simultaneously concentrating more on weaker areas.

When will the TISSNET 2024 exam be conducted?

The TISSNET 2024 exam will be conducted in February 2024. Conducted by the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), TISSNET is a computer-based national entrance examination for admission to all of its master's degree (MA) programmes at its Mumbai campus as well as its other three campuses in Guwahati, Hyderabad, and Tuljapur. It consists of objective multiple-choice questions, so someone with a basic understanding of computers can easily take the TISSNET 2024 exam.

When will the TISSNET 2024 admit card be released?

The TISSNET 2024 admit card will be released in the third week of February 2024. Along with the original ID proof, it is one of the most important documents to be carried by the applicant on exam day. If a student fails to bring this document, they will not be allowed to sit for the exam. Candidates can download the TISSNET 2024 admit card from the official website. The TISSNET admit card can only be downloaded online by registered applicants who have paid the registration fee.

What is the full form of TISSNET?

The full form of TISSNET is the TISS National Entrance Test or Tata Institute of Social Sciences National Entrance Test. It is a university-level entrance exam for the School of Management and Labour Studies' MA programmes. The postgraduate programme in human resources management and labour relations provided at TISS campuses correspondences to the management programmes provided by top MBA institutes in India. English Proficiency, Mathematical Skills & Logical Reasoning, and General Awareness questions are included in the TISSNET entrance exam paper, which is conducted in CBT format.
