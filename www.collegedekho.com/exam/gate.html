GATE Conducting Body

The 7 prestigious IITs and IISc Bangalore jointly administer the national-level Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) entrance exam every year. It follows an 8-year rotation pattern in which each institute conducts the GATE exam at regular intervals. The GATE zonal office of the specific institute, one of the eight zones where the GATE exam will be held, is in charge of overseeing GATE exam-related activities, from registration, admit card release to exam conduct, followed by result declaration. The conducting body for GATE exam 2026 this year is IIT Guwahati.

FAQs about GATE

How many attempts for GATE?

There is no limit on the number of attempts in the GATE exam. GATE is conducted annually, so you can appear only once a year. If you want, you can reapply for the GATE exam next year. 

When can I apply for GATE 2026?

The GATE 2026 application form will likely be released in August 2025. You can apply on the GATE 2026 GOAPS portal. 

Is GATE tougher than JEE?

GATE is generally considered to be tougher than JEE Main. GATE exam syllabus is at per undergraduate level, whereas JEE Main syllabus is as per Class 11th and 12th. The GATE exam requires a deeper understanding of the concepts. 

How to prepare for the GATE exam in 2026?

If you are aiming for the GATE 2026 exam, you must begin your preparation at least 8 to 9 months before the exam. Make a proper GATE study timetable, add all the topics and chapters in it. Study as per the plan, understand the concept, and join a good coaching centre if needed. Solve previous year papers, mock tests, and sample papers regularly. Revise the syllabus timely manner. 

What is the GATE cut off for 2026?

GATE 2026 cut off will be released along with the GATE result 2026 in the month of March 2026. The GATE qualifying cutoff marks are the minimum marks that you must score to pass the exam. GATE admission cutoff will be released by the respective college during the GATE counselling. 

Who will conduct the GATE exam in 2026?

The GATE 2026 exam will be conducted by IIT Guwahati in the month of February 2026.

Do I need to fill two GATE application forms 2026 to appear in two papers?

No, you dont need to fill two GATE registration forms to appear in two papers. You can choose two paper combination from the set and fill out the SINGLE GATE 2026 application form.

What is the qualifying marks for GATE?

For General students, the expected qualifying mark is 35.5. OBC students are expected to achieve a minimum of 31.9 marks, while SC/ST/PWD students have a lower threshold of 23.6 marks.

Which branch is best in GATE?

There is no specific best branch in GATE 2026. The exam conducting body conducts GATE exam papers in various branches so that students interested in different engineering specializations can get admission to top MTech colleges. However, many students find Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) to be the best branch of GATE.

Which branch is toughest in GATE?

Electronics and communication engineering, mining, physics, and electrical are the most difficult branches in the GATE exam.

Is GATE conducted 2 times a year?

No, the GATE exam is conducted only one time a year.

What is the GAT-B exam?

Graduate Aptitude Test - Biotechnology (GAT-B) all-India entrance test conducted annually to offer admissions to different courses in the department of biotechnology. 

Can I participate in any of the sessions for a paper that is being held in more than one session?

NO. Each candidate will only be assigned with one of the many sessions of a specific paper.

How many GATE papers may I apply for?

A student may submit an application for ONE or TWO of the 30 papers listed in the GATE INFORMATION BROCHURE or on the GATE website (choosing from the list of combinations provided under Two Paper Combinations). The student is in charge of selecting the right paper. 

The following suggestions offer some principles in this regard:

  • The students are required to participate in paper(s) related to the field of their qualifying degree, up to two depending on the combinations listed in Two Paper Combinations.
  • However, the students are allowed to select any other paper in accordance with their admission strategy, keeping in mind the requirements of the institutions they desire to apply to for admission.

How can I register for GATE?

Students must complete and submit the GATE online application form in order to register for the entrance exam. For further information on how to complete and submit a GATE application, students can refer to the information above.

How can I pay the fee for the GATE exam?

After completing the online application form on the GATE website, the payment option will be displayed. Any of the listed payment alternatives are available to the student. The student is required to pay any additional fees, such as service fees, processing fees, or bank fees.

Is there a maximum age to take the GATE exam?

No, there is no upper age limit for GATE participation.

Who conducts the GATE exam every year?

GATE examination is conducted on a rotational basis by all the seven IITs and IISc Bangalore every year. GATE 2026 will be conducted by IIT Guwahati.

Can a GATE qualified student apply for M.Tech/M.E admission in institutes of national importance only?

No, in addition to the institutes of national importance, GATE qualified students can apply for admissions in any institute offering M.Tech/M.E/MS/Direct PhD admissions.
