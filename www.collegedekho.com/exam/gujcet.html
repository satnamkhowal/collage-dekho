About GUJCET 2026 Exam (Exam Over, Answer Key Soon)

The GUJCET 2026 exam has been conducted on March 29, 2026. It is predicted that the answer key for GUJCET 2026 may be out by April 1 to 8, 2026. The GUJCET Results 2026 are likely to be published between May 8 and 12, 2026. The Gujarat Common Entrance Test, commonly known as GUJCET, is a state-level entrance exam for admission to the undergraduate courses B.Tech and B.Pharma. The GUJCET exam is conducted by the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB). The exam is conducted in the pen-and-paper-based mode (OMR) with MCQs covering Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology. The total number of questions is 120, with 40 from each subject. On the other hand, the counselling process for the GUJCET is carried out by the Admission Committee for Professional Courses (ACPC). Check the complete details related to the GUJCET 2026 exam on the page.

GUJCET Exam Pattern 2026

GUJCET 2026 Exam Pattern is released by GSEB in a detailed format along with the information brochure to make students aware of the current exam trend, question pattern, marks distribution, marking scheme, exam duration etc. Aspirants must go through the complete GUJCET Exam Pattern 2026 to gain an understanding of how the paper is conducted and also to plan their preparation strategy accordingly. Here is an overview of the GUJCET 2026 Exam Pattern for reference -

  1. Mode of Exam: Offline (Pen and Paper Based)
  2. Duration of GUJCET Exam 2026: 3 hours  
  3. Paper I (Physics & Chemistry): 120 minutes  
  4. Paper II (Mathematics): 60 minutes  
  5. Language Mode: English, Hindi, and Gujarati  
  6. Sections:  
  • Physics & Chemistry  
  • Mathematics/Biology (for B.Pharma)  
  1. Type of Question: Multiple-choice questions (MCQ)  
  2. Number of Questions:  
  • Physics: 40 questions  
  • Chemistry: 40 questions  
  • Mathematics/Biology: 40 questions  
  1. Total Questions: 120  
  2. Marking Scheme:
  • Each correct response: 1 mark  
  • Negative marking: -0.25 for an incorrect attempt

GUJCET Answer Key 2026

GUJCET 2026 Answer Key will be released in April 2026. GSEB will publish the answer key for GUJCET 2026 in two phases. First, the preliminary answer key was released along with the GUJCET 2026 Response Sheet. Candidates will be able to check their responses and verify the same with the official answer keys provided by the authorities. They can accordingly calculate their marks and credit their GUJCET 2026 scores. If students come across any error or discrepancy in the answer keys, they may raise objections against the same by submitting a certain amount as a fee. The exam authorities will review and resolve the objections and publish the final GUJCET Answer Key 2026 ahead of the results.

GUJCET Merit List 2026

GUJCET Merit List 2026 will be released by ACPC based on the marks obtained by candidates in the exam. Candidates will be able to check their GUJCET rank mentioned in the merit list once it is published on the official website - gujacpc.nic.in. Students must note that the final merit list of GUJCET 2026 qualifiers will be released after taking into account the challenges raised by candidates against the provisional merit list.

The details about the GUJCET 2026 Merit List can be checked below -

  • The first merit list will include the names and ranks of candidates appearing in the GUJCET exam. The list will be prepared giving 60% weightage to marks obtained in PCM and 40% weightage to the percentile marks obtained in the GUJCET entrance exam.

  • The second GUJCET merit list will be prepared for candidates taking the JEE Main exam. The merit will be prepared giving 60% weightage to marks obtained in PCM and 40% weightage to the percentile marks obtained in JEE Main.

GUJCET Counselling 2026

The Admission Committee for Professional Courses (ACPC) is the organising body responsible for conducting GUJCET Counselling. ACPC Gujarat will commence the counselling registration in March 2026. The GUJCET 2026 Counselling will include steps such as online registration, document verification, choice filling, seat allotment, acceptance fee payment, and finally, reporting to the allotted colleges. Aspirants willing to take part in the GUJCET 2026 Counselling will have to complete the registration process and get the documents verified first. The allocation of seats will be done based on the candidate's preferences, rank, and the availability of seats. Those who will be assigned seats during the GUJCET 2026 Seat Allotment will have to pay the required fees to confirm their admission.

GUJCET Cutoff 2026

GUJCET Cutoff 2026 will be released online on the official website by ACPC. GUJCET 2026 Cutoff refers to the minimum qualifying marks that a candidate must obtain to be eligible for admission to the various participating institutes. Candidates securing the higher cutoff scores thus have better chances of getting admission to their desired colleges and courses. ACPC takes into account several factors, such as the total number of applicants, the difficulty level of the exam in the given year, the total number of seats available, the previous cutoff trends etc. to determine the GUJCET qualifying cutoff. 

GUJCET Participating Institutes 2026

GUJCET Participating Institutes 2026 are the colleges/institutes offering admission to qualified candidates based on their ranks through the counselling process of GUJCET. Candidates taking part in GUJCET Counselling 2026 will be allotted seats in any of the participating institutes based on their merit and preferences. The GUJCET 2026 Participating Colleges include a list of some of the finest and reputed institutes where one can get admission based on their scores. ACPC will release the official list of colleges participating in the upcoming counselling session of GUJCET 2026. GUJCET Eligibility Criteria 2026

GUJCET 2026 Eligibility Criteria is released by GSEB Gujarat on the official website. Candidates are advised to review the detailed eligibility criteria before applying for the GUJCET 2026 Exam. The eligibility requirements for GUJCET are determined along multiple parameters, such as the candidate's age limit, nationality, domicile, educational qualifications, qualifying marks, etc. Applicants failing to meet any of the conditions laid down by the conducting body shall be considered ineligible for GUJCET 2026 Registration. Check the GUJCET Eligibility Criteria 2026 mentioned below. 

  • Educational Qualification: Students must have completed their 12th Intermediate or equivalent exam from a recognized board to apply for the test.
  • Minimum Marks: Applicants in the general category must score at least 45% (40% for candidates in the reserved category) to be eligible.
  • Subjects Required: Candidates must pass class 12th intermediate or equivalent exam with PCM/PCB for pharmacy courses and PCM for engineering courses.
  • Appearing: Candidates who will be sitting in their final year will also be able to apply for the GUJCET 2026 test.

FAQs about GUJCET

How is GUJCET Merit List 2026 Prepared?

The ACPC will prepare the GUJCET 2026 merit list by considering both the Senior Secondary Class scores and GUJCET exam results. The merit list will include all qualified candidates, who will then be contacted for further admission steps. The scoring system allocates 60% weightage to Class XII marks and 40% to GUJCET 2026 scores. To account for different question paper difficulties, a normalization method will be used, resulting in the final score calculated as follows: Applicant’s Final Score = (0.60 x Class XII marks) + (0.40 x GUJCET 2026 Marks). Candidates can check the merit list online.

GUJCET is for which course?

Gujarat Common Entrance Test (GUJCET), is an entrance examination for admission to Engineering and Pharmacy courses across the top colleges in Gujarat.

How many times is GUJCET taken in a year?

GUJCET is conducted once a year. 

Where can I download the GUJCET admit card?

You can download the admit card of GUJCET from the official website gujcet.gseb.org 2025 once it is released.

Is NCERT enough for cracking the GUJCET entrance exam?

Yes NCERT books are enough for cracking the GUJCET exam. As per the latest GUJCET topper interviews, NCERT books serve the basic and the maximum purpose for effective preparation for the GUJCET exam. Apart from the NCERT books, candidates can refer to other reference books by HC Verma, Halliday/ Resnick/ Walker, Amit Rastogi, Morrison Boyd, RC Mukherji, etc. 

How many seats in private engineering are filled through GUJCET?

70% of seats in private engineering colleges are filled through GUJCET

Is JEE Main acceptable for admission in B.Tech instead of GUJCET?

Candidates with a valid score in JEE Main can directly participate in ACPC admission process.

Is GUJCET score mandatory for admission in B.Pharma?

GUJCET score is mandatory for admission in B.Pharma.

Are students from other states eligible to appear for GUJCET?

Students from other states are eligible to appear for GUJCET. However, they will be eligible only for 5% seats in government engineering colleges under state quota.

What is GUJCET Exam?

Gujrat Common Entrance Test (GUJCET) is a state-level entrance examination conducted for candidates seeking admission into Diploma/Degree courses in Engineering & Technology and Pharmacy courses. Basis their performance in the entrance, candidates can apply in the Government, Autonomous, Government-in-Aid and Self-Financing Institutes in the state of Gujarat.

What is ACPC Gujarat?

ACPC is Admission Committee for Professional Courses, which conducts admission process for B.E/ B.Tech admissions in Gujarat.
