SET Eligibility Criteria 2026

The points given below state the SET 2026 eligibility criteria that students need to follow to qualify for the SET exam 2026:

  • The candidate must have passed Class 12 or similar exams in the relevant stream from a recognizable committee or college with a minimum score of 50% (45% for SC/ST students).
  • Applicants in Class 12 or waiting to receive Class 12 results are also qualified to participate but must provide a passing certificate.
  • An applicant who has passed the Class 12 or equivalent examination from an international board must acquire an adjacency certificate from the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

SET Admit Card 2026

The SET (Symbiosis Entrance Test) Admit Card is one of the most important documents required by candidates appearing for the examination conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed University). The SET admit card not only verifies a candidate’s identity but also ensures the smooth administration of the test. Candidates must download it from the official SET website using their registered login credentials, as the admit card is not sent via email or post. After downloading, it is crucial to check all details carefully and ensure that the photograph and signature are clearly visible. Any discrepancy should be reported immediately to the SET authorities for correction. 

How to Download SET 2026 Admit Card? – Step-by-Step Guide

The Symbiosis International University releases the SET admit card online on its official website. Candidates who have successfully registered for the exam can download it using their login credentials. Follow the steps below:

Step 1: Visit the Official SET Website - Go to the official Symbiosis Entrance Test portal at set-test.org

Step 2: Click on “Admit Card” Link - On the homepage, look for the “Download Admit Card” or “Admit Card for SET” link and click on it.

Step 3: Login to Your Account - 

Enter the following credentials:

  • SET ID / Email ID

  • Password

  • Captcha code (if any)

Then click on Login.

Step 4: Select the Test (SET, SLAT, or SITEEE) - If you applied for multiple tests, choose the correct one for which you want to download the admit card.

Step 5: Download the Admit Card -

Once the admit card appears on the screen:

  • Check all details carefully

  • Click on “Download” or “Print Admit Card”

Step 6: Print the Admit Card - Print the admit card in color (recommended) and keep multiple copies for safety.

SET Exam Day Instructions 2026

SET 2026 will be conducted in the computer-based test mode. Candidates appearing for the exam must not only be prepared for the exam but also for the exam day. Mentioned below are some of the exam-day instructions that the candidates must follow:

  • Do not forget to carry the colored printout of the SET admit card. Digital or soft copies of the admit cards will not be accepted.
  • Reach the exam center at least an hour before the commencement of the exam.
  • Do not carry electronic gadgets or articles which are prohibited in the exam hall.
  • Carry a valid ID proof issued by the Government of India such as an Aadhar Card, Driving License, PAN Card, Voter ID or Passport.
  • Always wear a mask and follow the COVID-19 protocols.

SET Preparation Strategy

The Symbiosis Entrance Test (SET) is one of the most competitive undergraduate entrance examinations in India, opening the gateway to prestigious programs offered by Symbiosis International University (SIU). Every year, thousands of aspirants compete for limited seats across disciplines such as Management, Liberal Arts, Computer Studies, Economics, Mass Communication, and more. With competition rising steadily, a well-planned and disciplined SET preparation strategy becomes essential for securing a top score and gaining admission to the desired program. 

It takes a clever combination of conceptual understanding, ongoing practice, time management, and familiarity with the exam format to prepare for SET. It goes beyond simply memorizing the material. Students must take a methodical and balanced approach because SET measures a variety of skills, including General English, Quantitative Aptitude, Analytical & Logical Reasoning, and General Awareness. Students are better able to prioritize their efforts when they are aware of the marking scheme, topic weighting, and difficulty level of each section.

SET Admission Process 2026

The SET exam 2026 consists of three steps: filling out the application form, the SET 2026 exam, and a personal interview/written aptitude test.

STEP 1: Completing the SET registration form

Applicants must apply for the SET written test by completing the SET 2026 registration form, which is available on the official website. They must sign up as new participants on the SET website. Applicants will gain access to the application form after successfully registering. The enrollment fee of INR 2,250 must be paid online only via net banking or credit/debit card.

STEP 2: Get your SET admit card

Only enrolled students will be provided SET hall tickets. Applicants can obtain their separate hall tickets by visiting the official website.

STEP 3: Take the SET exam

The SET exam 2026 will be held in computer-based test (CBT) mode at the planned time at various exam centers across the nation. Applicants must bring their hall tickets and one form of identification to the assessment test (Driving license, Aadhar Card, Voter ID, Passport).

STEP 4: Verify the SET result

The SET result will be available on the website. Chosen applicants will be invited to participate in the WAT/PI round. Each institute published its own SET cut-off. Applicants are encouraged to visit individual sites.

STEP 5: Attend SET WAT/PI.

To the WAT/PI round, the candidate must bring all relevant documents (original and photocopy). The performance evaluation of an applicant in SET and WAT/PI will be used to decide enrollment. Last year, WAT was held on the day of the SET exam. Applicants were given 30 minutes to complete the WAT.

SET Result 2026

The SET 2026 result determines a candidate’s eligibility for the next stages of the selection process, such as Personal Interaction (PI), Writing Ability Test (WAT), or Studio Test, depending on the chosen course. The SET result reflects a candidate’s performance through detailed section-wise scores, total marks, and qualifying status. As the examination evaluates candidates across areas like General English, Quantitative Aptitude, Analytical Reasoning, and General Awareness, the result provides a clear picture of their strengths and weaknesses. The transparency and accessibility of the SET result make it easier for students to gauge their readiness for admission and plan their next steps accordingly. 

How to Check Result of SET 2026?

Step 1: Visit the Official Website -  Go to the official SET exam portal:

Step 2: Click on “SET Result” Link - On the homepage, look for the link labeled “SET Result” or “Download Scorecard”

Step 3: Login with Credentials - Enter the following details like SET ID / Registration Number and Password

Step 4: View the Result - After logging in your SET scorecard will appear on the screen. It includes your section-wise scores, overall score, and qualifying status.

FAQs about SET

The SET exam is conducted across how many exam centres?

The SET exam is conducted in 85 cities across the nation.

What is the frequency of the SET exam?

The SET exam is conducted once a year.

Is it essential to meet the eligibility criteria to sit for the SET exam?

In order to sit for the SET exam, it is of utmost importance to fulfil the prescribed eligibility criteria of the exam. The criteria differ for SEED, SIT EEE, SLAT and SET.

How much is the negative marking for incorrect answers in the SET exam?

There is no negative marking for wrong answers in the SET exam.

What is the mode of the SET exam?

The SET exam is held in online mode.

Is there a separate entrance exam for Design conducted by the Symbiosis Institute?

Yes, a separate exam called as SEED is conducted by the Symbiosis University for admission to the Design course.

SIT EEE stands for which exam?

SIT EEE stands for Symbiosis Institute of Technology Engineering Entrance Examination. The exam offers admission to the engineering courses provided by Symbiosis University.

SLAT exam is conducted for admission to which courses?

SLAT exam is the Symbiosis Law Admission Test conducted by the Symbiosis University which provides admission to BBA LLB (Hons), BA LLB (Hons), BBA LLB and BA LLB.

What is the conducting body of the SET exam?

The conducting body of SET is Symbiosis International University.

What is the full form of SET?

The full form of SET is Symbiosis Entrance Test. It is the entrance exam that is conducted to shortlist students for admission to the Symbiosis International University (SIU), Pune.
