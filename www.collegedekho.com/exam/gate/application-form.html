GATE 2026 Registration Process

In order to apply for GATE 2026, students have to follow certain steps if they want to complete the entire process in a systematic and correct manner. The registration process for GATE 2026 involves the following stages: Registration, GOAPS Password, GOAPS Enrolment ID, Application Form Filling and Uploading Images and Supporting Documents, Online Fee Payment etc. 

Step-by-Step Guide to GATE 2026 Registration Process

The steps to register and fill out the GATE registration form 2026 have been explained below - 

Step 1: GATE 2026 Registration through GOAPS

  • Visit the official website of GATE, gate2026.iitg.ac.in.

  • Register as a “New User” and proceed as mentioned in the guide

  • After successful registration, they will get access to GOAPS with the credentials they entered, such as name, a valid email ID, and mobile number.

  • International students must note that any contact with the student will be done only through the provided e-mail ID.

*Note: The email ID and mobile number submitted must be valid in nature because all communication related to the examination will be sent through this medium.

  • After providing all details like a valid email address, mobile number, and a GOAPS password of their choice.

GOAPS Password:

  • While creating the password, you must note that it should be a password that cannot be guessed easily.

  • You shouldn't use your name, date of birth, or an easily guessable string of letters or numbers.

  • Try creating a password that has numbers, letters, and special characters.

  • Confirm the registration.

GOAPS Enrolment ID:

  • After the successful registration, you will receive an email that contains the GOAPS Enrolment ID.

  • The Enrolment ID is necessary to log in to fill out the online application form for GATE 2026.

How Do I Make Changes to GATE 2026 Paper?

Students change the papers in the GATE 2026 application form within a certain time frame. Students must first pick their two paper combinations: the first paper and the second paper. Follow the steps given below to make changes to the GATE 2026 papers:

Step 1: Visit the official website of the GOAP.

Step 2: Log in with your GOAPS enrollment ID or email address, and password.

Step 3: Once logged in, you can change the options for GATE 2026.

Step 4: After selecting your favorite subject, click the 'Submit' button, and you will be brought to the payment page. Entering the captcha takes you to the payment page. 

Step 5: Pay the required amount for the GATE 2026 application correction and print the amended application form.

How to Change GATE 2026 Exam City/ Centre?

Some of the important points about the process of changing the GATE exam city can be checked below -

  • Students who wish to change the exam city in the GATE application form will have the option to do the same.

  • The students need to log in to the GOAPS portal with their email ID and password to change the exam city.

  • The students can choose any 3 exam centres.

  • This can be done once the application form is completed and processed for further proceedings.

  • A requisite amount is also to be paid along with the desired correction. 

  • Therefore, students who wish to make changes must not wait further, as the window once closed will not be opened again. No further changes will then be entertained concerning exam centres.

How to Add GATE Second Paper?

Students who wish to add the GATE secondary paper from the two-paper combination can follow these steps:

Step 1: Visit the official website of IIT Guwahati, gate2026.iitg.ac.in.

Step 2: Go to the application section and click the "Apply Online" option.

Step 3: Log in with your enrollment ID/email address and password. 

Step 4: After logging in, the GATE application form status will appear on the screen. 

Step 5: If you submitted your GATE application with one test paper. You can add paper using the option located below.

Step 6: Select "GATE Paper II" and submit.

Step 7: Follow the instructions and proceed to make the payment. 

Step 8: Keep the receipt for future reference once you've completed the payment.

How to Check GATE 2026 Application Status?

IIT Guwahati will open the window for checking the GATE application status in 2026. Students will be able to visit the GOAPs portal to check their GATE 2026 application form status. How to Retrieve Forgotten GATE 2026 Enrollment ID & Password?

In case students have forgotten their GATE 2026 registration or enrollment ID, they can access it by following the steps given below. 

Step 1: Visit the GOAPS page.

Step 2: Then, go to the ‘Forgot GATE 2026 Enrolment ID or Registration ID’ and click on the link. 

Step 3: If your email address is still valid but you cannot remember your enrollment or registration IDs, click "Request for an OTP" to receive a 'One Time Password'.

Step 4: To change the password if you already have an OTP, click "Already have an OTP?"

Step 5: Then reset your password, and you will be able to get your GATE 2026 registration ID.

FAQs about GATE Application Form

What are the documents required for GATE application 2026?

The GATE documents required to fill out the application form are degree certificates, passing marksheets, a Valid Photo ID such as an Aadhaar Card/Passport/Driving License, and a caste certificate, etc. 

Will my GATE 2026 application be rejected if I don't provide a photograph that meets the specifications?

Your GATE application will be tagged as DEFECTIVE. You will be notified via email, SMS, or phone call from your Zone to RECTIFY your DEFECT (by uploading a fresh photograph). You will be offered opportunities for rectification up until the deadline, beyond which your GATE application form 2026 will be declared PROVISIONAL or REJECTED.

What should I do if the power or Internet connection fails during an online GATE application fee payment?

When you can come back online, go to the GATE 2026 website and verify the progress of your payment. If GATE has received your money, you can proceed to print the GATE 2026 application form. If your money was not received by GATE 2026, you may initiate a new payment. If any fee amount has been debited (deducted) from your bank account but you have not received an acknowledgment of payment from GATE 2026, the money will be reimbursed back to your account. However, this could take a week or longer.

Why should I choose THREE examination cities in the GATE 2026 application form?

Students will most likely be assigned a center in the GATE 2026 exam city of their first preference. Only in circumstances where too many students choose a specific city as their first preference do the other two options become significant. Students are required to enter their choices of cities to sit in the test, but should remember that due of operational limits, the GATE committee reserves the right to add a new city or remove an existing one, and assign a city that may not be one of the options.

Can I use one e-mail address to fill multiple GATE 2026 application forms?

NO. A single e-mail address can be used to submit only one GATE application form 2026.

How to register for GATE 2026?

To register for the GATE 2026 exam. visit the GOAPs portal at gate2026.iitg.ac.in and click on registration link. First complete the registration and generate password and enrollment ID. Fill out the application form, upload documents and pay the fees.

What is the registration fee for 2026 GATE?

The GATE application fee 2026 for female, SC/ST students is INR 1000/-. And for the extended period, the GATE fee is INR 1500/-/ For all other students, including foreign nationals the GATE 2025 application fee is INR 2000/- and for the extended period is INR 2500/-.

Is GATE 2026 registration started?

 The GATE 2026 registration process will start on August 25, 2025 at gate2026.iitg.ac.in.

Can students make edits in the application form of GATE 2026?

Yes, students will be given the facility to make corrections in their GATE application forms 2026, dates for which will be announced soon.

Where to register for GATE 2026 exam?

Students are required to enrol through the GATE Online Application Processing System (GOAPS) at gate2026.iitg.ac.in login to fill up the GATE 2026 Registration form. 

When to apply for GATE 2026?

The GATE 2026 registration will start on August 25, 2025.

What is the last date to fill the GATE 2026 application form?

GATE application form 2026 last date without late fees is September 25, 2025. The GATE 2026 application form last date with late fees October 6, 2025.

Who is eligible to apply for GATE 2026?

Students who have a graduation degree or are in the final year or in the last semester are eligible to apply for GATE 2026 exam.

I have chosen category as OBC while filling GATE application, but there is no option to upload the certificate. What to do?

The OBC Certificate is not required to be uploaded. OBC certificate is required only while taking admission using the GATE score.

Will the authority provide a "magnified question paper" for visual impairment?

Visually disabled students are requested to contact the GATE Institute Representative as soon as they enter the examination centre. The representative and the invigilator would arrange for the magnified question paper for the visually handicapped students. 

I am facing difficulty while uploading my signature. I have followed all the guidelines defined by the GATE authorities. What should I do now?

Check the format in which you are trying to upload the document. Also, check the size and also do not resize the file.

For how many GATE papers can I apply?

A student can apply for a maximum of two papers of the 30 papers listed on the official website of GATE 2026. The choice of the appropriate paper is the responsibility of the student. Some guidelines in this respect are suggested below.

1. The student is expected to appear in a paper appropriate to the discipline of his/her qualifying degree.

2. The student is, however, free to choose any paper according to his/her admission plan, keeping in mind the eligibility criteria of the institutions in which he/she is seeking admission.
