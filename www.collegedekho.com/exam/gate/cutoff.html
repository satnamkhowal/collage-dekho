How to Check GATE Cutoff 2026?

GATE 2026 cut off marks for all 30 papers in online mode on the official website. To check the GATE 2026 cutoff marks, you can follow the steps given below. 

Step / Aspect

Detailed Explanation

1. Visit the Official GATE 2026 Website

Begin by going to the official GATE 2026 website hosted by the conducting IIT. During the result period, the homepage will display a link labeled “GATE 2026 Cutoff” or “Qualifying Marks” under the announcements or results section. Since multiple unofficial websites circulate unverified data, candidates should ensure they are browsing the authentic portal before checking cutoff scores.

2. Access the GOAPS Candidate Login (If Required)

In many cases, the cutoff details are accessible through public links. However, for paper-specific qualifying cutoffs and personalized details, you may need to log in through the GOAPS portal using your registered email ID/enrolment number and password. Logging in provides access to the candidate dashboard, where your marks and the official qualifying cutoff for your specific paper are displayed.

3. Locate the Cutoff PDF or Cutoff Chart

After accessing the cutoff section, a detailed table or downloadable PDF will appear, containing cutoff scores categorized by GATE paper (e.g., CE, ME, CS, EE) and reservation category (General, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, EWS). This chart helps you compare your performance with the minimum marks required to qualify. Most cutoff PDFs also provide additional context about the number of test takers and normalization details.

4. Understand the Qualifying vs. Admission Cutoff

GATE cutoffs are of two types, and understanding the difference is crucial:

(a) Qualifying Cutoff: Minimum score needed to qualify the exam. Released by GATE authorities.

(b) Admission Cutoff: Minimum score required for admission to IITs, NITs, IIITs, GFTIs, and universities. These are released separately by individual institutes or by counselling platforms like COAP (for IITs) and CCMT (for NITs). The admission cutoff is usually higher than the qualifying cutoff because it depends on seat availability, competition, and demand for the specialization.

5. Check Institute-Specific Cutoffs Separately

After confirming the official qualifying cutoff, candidates who aim for admission must check institute-wise cutoffs. For IITs, cutoffs are accessed through COAP, where round-by-round scores are published. For NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, CCMT releases opening and closing GATE scores for each course and category. Checking these helps you shortlist colleges realistically and plan your counselling choices.

6. Review Previous Years’ Cutoff Trends for Better Prediction

Looking at previous years’ cutoff trends allows candidates to understand how cutoffs fluctuate based on exam difficulty, competition levels, and the popularity of specializations. If your GATE score is close to the previous year’s cutoff range, you can estimate your likelihood of securing admission in similar institutes.

7. Interpret the Cutoff Properly and Plan Next Steps

To interpret cutoffs effectively, compare your normalized GATE score (out of 1000) with the admission cutoffs and your raw marks with qualifying cutoffs. Based on this comparison, you can begin preparing your counselling strategy, identifying dream, safe, and backup institutes. This planning helps optimize your chances during COAP and CCMT rounds.

8. Keep Monitoring Official Updates

Cutoffs may get updated during counselling rounds, especially when seat vacancies fluctuate. Continuous monitoring of the official GATE portal, COAP, and CCMT websites ensures that you don’t miss any revised cutoffs, deadlines, or announcements. Staying updated helps you respond quickly during the admission process.

Tech, they continue to study for the GATE examination scheduled next year. The PSU recruiting process includes a legitimate GATE score, a group discussion, and a personal interview (PI). FAQs about GATE Cut Off

If I score GATE qualifying marks 2026, will I get admission in IITs and NITs?

You need to score the GATE admission cutoff 2026 to get MTech seats at IITs and NITs. By scoring the GATE qualifying marks 2026 you will become eligible to take part in the GATE counselling process; CCMT and COAP. You need to appear in CCMT and COAP counselling during which GATE 2026 cutoff IIT and NIT are different. If you scored the GATE cutoff marks for IIT and NIT, then only you will get M.Tech admission.

When will the GATE 2026 cut off marks be released?

The GATE 2026 cut off marks will be released in March 2026; gate2026.iitg.ac.in.

When will the GATE 2026 cutoff IIT NIT be released?

The GATE cutoff marks for IIT and NIT will be released via COAP and CCMT counselling respectively. The IIT GATE cutoff 2026 will be released after COAP counselling is over on the respective IIT official website. The NIT GATE 2026 cutoff will be released during the CCMT counselling after each round of seat allotment. 

Can I get IIT with 40 marks in GATE?

40 marks is very less to get IIT admission through GATE 2025. You must have GATE score between 700 to 850 if you belong to general category to get IIT admission. The IIT GATE cutoff for OBC ranges between 450 to 650 and for SC/ST between 300 to 500.

Will the GATE 2026 cut offs remain the same as the previous year?

No, the GATE 2026 cutoff will be different compared to the previous year's GATE cutoff. IIT officials update the GATE admission cutoff annually. You cannot pass the exam relying on the previous year's GATE cut off.

What is the difference between the GATE qualifying and admission cutoffs?

GATE qualifying cutoff is the passing marks required in GATE to be eligible for IIT and NIT admissions. GATE admission cutoff, on the other hand, is the cutoff or closing ranks at which admission is granted for MTech programs at NITs, IITs and other institutions. 

Is 65 a good score in GATE 2026?

Yes, 65 marks in GATE are considered good marks as your rank will be under 200.

What is a safe score for the GATE exam?

93+ is considered an excellent score in the GATE 2025 exam. The Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering has a maximum score of 100 marks. Candidates scoring above 90, are considered decent scores, and above 93+, are considered excellent scores. 

Is 15 a good score in GATE 2026?

15 is not a good score in GATE 2026. With 15 marks in GATE 2026 You may only be admitted to state-run or private colleges. 

Is 40 marks good in GATE 2025?

Yes, 40 marks are good in GATE 2025. Having 40 marks in the gate indicates that you have already passed and are among the top 5000 of the nation's engineering graduates.

What is the cut-off for GATE 2025?

The expected GATE 2025 cutoff is 35.5 for General, 31.9 for OBC, and 23.6 for SC/ST/PWD. The student's category and the GATE paper will determine their GATE 2025 cut off marks. 

Is GATE cut off 2025 different for all papers?

There is a separate GATE qualifying cutoff for all papers, and the same will be released along with the results. 

How is GATE cutoff determined for M.Tech admission in NIT?

CCMT, which is the official body for conducting M.Tech admission in NITs through GATE will release institute-wise cutoff in the form of closing ranks or minimum score. The closing rank is the rank of the last rank of the student securing admission in the respective NIT.

What are the factors deciding the GATE 2025 cutoff?

GATE cutoff is determined on the basis of several factors such as the total number of students appeared and cleared the entrance exam, previous years' cutoff etc.
