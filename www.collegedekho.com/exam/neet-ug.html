NEET 2026 Exam Pattern

The NEET 2026 exam pattern will remain the same as last year. In 2025, the NTA modified the exam pattern by removing Section B with optional questions in the exam paper. The exam duration was also reduced to 3 hours from 3 hours and 20 minutes. All of the 180 questions in the exam are now mandatory to answer. There will be a positive marking of 4 marks for every correct answer. On the other hand, 1 mark will be deducted for every incorrect response. There is no negative marking for unattempted questions. 

The entrance exam will be conducted in offline mode. According to the NEET 2026 exam pattern, students can appear for the test in 13 language mediums. These include Hindi, English, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Gujarati, Oriya, Punjabi, Tamil, Marathi, Assamese, and Urdu. The question paper will be divided into 4 sections for Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology. Each section will comprise 45 multiple-choice questions. For more information, refer to the NEET 2026 Exam Pattern.

Preparation Tips for NEET UG 2026 Exam

The NEET 2026 Exam Preparation Tips focuses on the strategies to help students secure a good score in the test. Some of the tips and tricks to follow are:

  1. Learn the Difficult Topics: Build your preparation strategy by focusing on the difficult chapters from Biology and either Physics or Chemistry simultaneously.
  2. Refer to the NCERT Books: A significant portion of the NEET syllabus is derived from Classes 11th and 12th NCERT books. Thus, ensure to follow them thoroughly. 
  3. Use Diagrams for Clarity: Incorporate diagrams into your study routine for a better understanding. Diagrams promote active engagement, aid in conceptual understanding, and improve problem-solving skills, especially in Physics.
  4. Enhance Memorisation Techniques: Memorisation is essential for mastering chemical equations, diagrams, and formulas required for NEET 2025. Regular review of these notes will aid in effective memorisation over time.

NEET 2026 Admit Card

The NEET 2026 Admit Card will be released on the official NTA website a few days before the exam day. The admit card will be released in a PDF format, typically 5-7 days prior to the exam date. It is essential to note that only students who have completed their exam registration following the eligibility guidelines will be eligible to download their NEET 2026 admit card. To download the PDF, you must visit the official website, click on the direct admit card link, and log in to the exam portal by using your registration number and password. 

After downloading the NEET 2026 admit card PDF, students must print out the document and affix a recent postcard-sized photograph (4"x6") in the designated space. It must be signed by the Student's parent/guardian, with the candidate leaving their signature blank. It is mandatory to carry the admit card to the designated exam centre for entry. Without it, no student will be allowed entry to the exam hall. The NEET 2026 admit card PDF also serves as an important document for verification during the NEET counselling process.

NEET Answer Key 2026

The NEET 2026 Answer Key will be released shortly after the entrance exam has been conducted. The document will be released in PDF format online. The NTA will release the NEET 2026 Answer Key in two formats, Provisional and Final. Once the Provisional NEET 2026 Answer Key is released, students will be given a timeframe to raise objections against it. A nominal fee of INR 200 per challenge/objection needs to be paid to raise their query. The NTA will analyse the objections and publish the Final NEET 2026 Answer Key with the revised answers. The NEET 2026 Response Sheet will be released along with the Final NEET 2026 Answer Key.

How to Calculate Final Score using NEET Answer Key?

Candidates can follow the below-given steps to calculate the final score using the NEET Answer Key listed below. 

  • Step 1: Download the official NEET answer key from the main website.

  • Step 2: Cross-check the marked answers and calculate the total number of right responses and wrong answers.

  • Step 3: Assign +4 for the correct answer and deduct -1 for wrong answers.

  • Step 4: Subtract the total marks calculated from the maximum marks, i.e., 720.

  • Step 5: Calculate NEET UG score 2026 = (4 x no. of correct answers) – (1 x no. of incorrect answers)

NEET 2026 Merit List

The NEET 2026 merit list will be released online in a PDF format. The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) will release the NEET 2026 Merit List for the 15% All India Quota seats on behalf of the NTA. The state-wise NEET 2026 Merit Lists for the 85% State Quota seats will be released by the respective state counselling bodies on behalf of the MCC. The NEET 2026 Merit List will feature the names of students who have secured high scores in the entrance exam. Based on the merit list, the MCC and state authorities will conduct the NEET counselling process. 

The All India Quota (AIQ) merit list consists of the names of the students, their All India Rank, Scores Obtained and gender. It does not consider the student’s category as the merit list is AIQ. It also displays the number of students who registered vs the number of students who have appeared for the NEET UG exam. On the contrary, the state-wise NEET merit lists will feature the student’s category and personal details along with their NEET score and state-wise toppers. Students can either visit the official website to download the merit list or refer to the direct download link to be provided on this page.

The NEET 2026 Counselling will be conducted in 4 rounds primarily. If seats remain vacant after that, a special stray vacancy will be conducted. The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) conducts the NEET counselling for the 15% AIQ seats. The respective state authorities will conduct the state-wise NEET counselling for the 85% State Quota seats. Students who are featured on the merit list must register for the counselling sessions online and submit the required documents for verification. A counselling fee must be paid to confirm their participation.

NEET UG 2026 Choice Filling

The NEET UG 2026 Choice Filling is a part of the counselling process. During this stage, students are required to enter their list of desired colleges for admission. The options are to be submitted in order of preference and seats will be allocated accordingly. In the NEET 2026 Choice Filling process, students can submit multiple options based on the seat availability and course. This process will be conducted separately for each round of counselling.

NEET UG 2026 Seat Allotment

The NEET 2026 Seat Allotment process is an important step in the Counselling process. The NEET 2026 Seat Allotment List PDF is released by the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) on behalf of the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) to allocate seats across the top medical colleges to eligible students. The NEET MBBS Seat Allotment List is prepared based on the preferences filled in by the candidates during the choice-filling round. The choices filled during the choice-filling rounds for NEET UG Seat Allotment 2026, along with the student's NEET All India Rank, play a pivotal role in determining the student's seat allotment results. The DGHS and MCC carefully analyze the students’ ranks and preferences to ensure a systematic and transparent allocation of seats.

Transparency is a crucial aspect of the MBBS and BDS seat allocation through the NEET Counselling 2026 process. Once the NEET 2026 seat allotment list is finalized, it is officially published on the NEET website. Students can view this list to ascertain the medical college and course they have been allocated. By providing comprehensive details of the allotted seats, the NEET 2026 Seat Allotment process allows students to make informed decisions about their future. Moreover, the NEET Seat Allotment 2026 process is designed to offer equal opportunities to all students in the field of medical science. It operates with principles of equity, ensuring that deserving students are granted admission to their preferred medical colleges. By considering both merit and personal choices, the process maximizes the chances of students securing seats in institutions that align with their aspirations.

FAQs about NEET

What is the NEET Exam Date 2026?

The NTA NEET UG 2026 exam date is expected to be in the 1st Sunday of May, i.e., May 3, 2026. Students will be able to access the admit card for the same 3-5 days prior to the test. Aspirants must visit the exam center carrying the admit card and 1 ID proof to secure entry into the premises. Failure to do so will result in instant dismissal from the test.

What is the safest score in the NEET exam 2026?

Candidates should aim to score at least 600+ or 650+ in the test to get a shot at bagging admission at topmost colleges. Anything lower than this will put you in a tough spot as most renowned institutions' cutoffs are above 650. So, the range of 600-700 can be deemed as the safest score in the NEET test.

Will there be an application form correction window?

Yes, once the official registration and application form filling process of NEET 2026 is completed, aspirants are allowed to make some changes. However, when the form correction window opens, many areas are fixed and can’t be changed. Only a few fields are up for rectification. So, ensure that you fill all the details correctly in the first go itself.

When will NEET application form 2026 be released?

The NEET 2026 application form is released on February 8, 2026, online. 

How many applicants are expected to appear for NEET 2026 exam?

Previously, in NEET 2025, 20 lakh+ students applied for the test. Based on the trend, it is expected that the NEET 2026 exam will receive 23 lakh+ applicants.

How to prepare for NEET 2026?

To ace the NEET 2026 exam, candidates should learn concepts in-depth, refer to the right books, know the exam pattern, study with novel practices, solve mock tests, practice regularly, eat healthily, take timely leisure breaks, and give their best in the exams. Most importantly, be confident in what you know and success shall come to you!

Will the NEET 2026 syllabus be reduced?

Yes, there has been an official announcement by NTA regarding the NEET 2026 syllabus reduction.

What is the mode of the NEET 2026 exam?

NEET exam 2026 will be based on a pen-paper test model, which is conducted in offline mode.

Are there any limits to attempting the NEET exam in 2026?

No, there is no upper age limit to appear for NEET 2026 exam.

What is the full form of the NEET exam?

The full form of the NEET 2026 exam is signified as the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test.

When will the NEET 2026 Admit Card be released?

The NEET 2026 admit card is likely to be published by the last week of April 2026.

What is the difficulty level of NEET?

The difficulty level of the NEET exam can range anywhere from moderate to tough. Generally, physics is considered to be the toughest section while Biology and Chemistry are the easier ones. However, with a well-structured plan, you can ace all the subjects.

Apart from MBBS, which other courses am I eligible to take admission in using the NEET 2026 scores?

NEET 2026 scores allow candidates to take admission in BDS, Veterinary, AYUSH, BSc (Hons) Nursing, and other paramedical as well as medical courses.

How can I prepare for NEET at home?

Students can prepare for the NEET exam 2026 at home with NCERT books. You can also refer to other study material, solve last year’s papers, attempt mock tests, and revise daily to score higher marks in the medical entrance exam.

Can I get 720 marks in NEET?

Yes, it is possible to get 720 marks in the NEET exam. In 2021, 3 toppers, Mrinal Kutteri, Karthika G Nair, and Tanmay Gupta secured AIR 1 with a NEET score of 720. If you possess the same determination and willpower, you can also score 720/ 720 in the NEET-UG exams. However, do not tire yourself in the process as your mental health is more important than any other exam. You can consider taking a drop for a year or two to prepare better for the NEET exam.

Is there a negative marking in NEET 2026?

Yes, there is a provision for negative marking in NEET 2026. For every wrong answer, there will be a deduction of 1 mark in your exam paper. However, for unattempted questions, there will be no negative marking in NEET 2026.
