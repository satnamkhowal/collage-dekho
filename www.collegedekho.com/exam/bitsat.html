BITSAT Eligibility Criteria 2026

The BITSAT 2026 eligibility criteria differ based on the courses offered. Before registering for the entrance exam, you must go through the complete BITSAT 2026 eligibility criteria. In a recent official announcement, BITS has tightened the rules for repeaters and droppers. Please know that if you fail to possess the required criteria, you won’t be eligible to apply for the exam. Find the detailed BITSAT eligibility criteria below: -

Specifications

Details

For First Degree programmes except B.Pharm. (Hons)

  • You must have qualified for your 12th standard exam from a recognised central or state board or its equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Proficiency in English is required.
  • A minimum aggregate of 75% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics subjects with at least 60% marks in each subject is necessary.

For admission to B.Pharm (Hons)

  • You should have passed the 12th standard examination from a recognized Central or State board or its equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. Proficiency in English is mandatory. PCMB students can also apply.
  • A minimum aggregate of 75% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (and Mathematics, if applicable for PCMB students) with at least 60% marks in each subject is required.
Year of Passing
  • Students passing in 2025 and 2026 are only eligible to sit for the entrance exam.
  • If you are a retaker or repeater of the Class 12 board exam (in all subjects), you cannot apply.
  • Those who were in the passout batch in 2025 and wish to improve their scores in specific subjects in 2026 are eligible to apply.

Additional Criteria

  • You will be eligible to apply only if you are appearing for the 12th standard exam in 2026 or qualified for Class 12 in 2022.
  • If you have appeared in the 12th or its equivalent exams multiple times, then only the latest attempt will be considered.
  • You will not be eligible if you are currently studying on any BITS campus currently.
  • Admissions are purely merit-based. Merit is determined by the BITSAT 2026 exam score. However, eligibility is subject to meeting minimum marks requirements in the Class 12 Board exam.

Nationality

  • Students with Indian citizenship are eligible to apply for BITSAT 2026 under the regular quota.
  • Students with a valid passport from any foreign country and SAT scores can apply for BITSAT 2026 under the International Student Admission Scheme. 

Age Limit

  • There is no age limit to appear for the BITSAT exam.

BITSAT 2026 Exam Day Guidelines and Instructions

A day before the BITSAT exam, you are advised to go through the exam day guidelines. These are important instructions that need to be followed by all on the day of the examination. Failing to abide by the BITSAT 2026 exam day instructions may lead to disqualification or barring from taking the test if deemed unfit by the authorities. Listed below are the general BITSAT 2026 guidelines to be followed for taking the exam: -

  • You need to carry the BITSAT admit card along with valid ID proof and a passport-size photo to the exam centre.
  • You are instructed to arrive at the allotted BITSAT exam centre in 2026 at least 1 hour before the commencement of the test. 
  • You are strictly prohibited from carrying calculators, logbooks, notebooks, watches, mobile phones, Bluetooth devices, electronic gadgets, etc. inside the exam hall.

  • You must sign the attendance sheet only in the presence of the invigilator

The exam pattern of BITSAT 2026 will be released by BITS Pilani on its website along with the official brochure. The questions about Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, in addition to Logical Reasoning and English, will be asked in the exam. Students attempting the B.Pharm paper have to attempt Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, and Logical Reasoning. You can find more details related to BITSAT 2026 exam pattern in the table below: -

  • All questions will be multiple choice, and the mode of instruction will be English.
  • The exam will consist of 130 MCQs in total.
  • The question paper will be divided into five sections.
  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology, English proficiency, and Logical Thinking of the candidates will be tested.

  • For an incorrect answer, -1 (negative marking present)

BITSAT 2026 Answer Key

BITS Pilani officially does not release any answer key officially for the exam. However, there are various coaching institutes that publish the memory-based answer key on their websites. Through the BITSAT answer key 2026, you will be able to evaluate the total number of correct responses and calculate marks according to the BITSAT marking scheme. The BITSAT exam 2026 is conducted for a total of 390 marks. To calculate scores using the unofficial BITSAT 2026 answer key, you must follow these steps: -

  • Count the total number of questions answered correctly out of 130 questions and multiply the sum by 3. 
  • Count the total number of questions answered incorrectly and multiply the sum by 1.
  • Find the difference between the total number of correct responses and incorrect responses.
  • The result obtained will be the estimated BITSAT scores.

BITSAT Counselling 2026

BITSAT counselling is being conducted in six rounds. Only those who pass the BITSAT 2026 exam will be considered eligible to participate in BITSAT Counselling 2026. Moreover, the counselling will include the procedures such as the following:-

  • Online Registration: BITS Pilani will conduct the BITSAT 2026 counselling registration in online mode. You must visit the official website, click on the counselling registration link, and provide all the required information.
  • Marks Submission: You must upload information on subject-wise marks acquired in the Class 12 examination. Those awaiting results must download the BITSAT counselling application form 2026 and submit it to the authorities once their Class 12 results are out. They must put "Original form already submitted, 12th standard marks being submitted now" at the top of the application form.

FAQs about BITSAT

When will the BITSAT exam 2026 be held?

The BITSAT 2026 exam will be conducted tentatively in May & June 2025 for session 1 & 2 respectively.

Is BITSAT tougher than JEE?

No, as per the analysis, questions in the BITSAT exam are easier as compared to JEE.

What is the registration fee for BITSAT 2026?

The BITSAT registration fee for male students is INR 3,400/-, and for female students is INR 2,900/-.

What is the pattern of the BITSAT exam 2026?

The BITSAT exam 2026 will be held in online CBT mode for 3 hours. The paper will have 130 MCQ-type questions. BITSAT 2026 exam will have 5 sections: Physics, Chemistry, English and Logical Reasoning, Mathematics/Biology.

How to prepare for BITSAT 2026?

To prepare for the BITSAT 2026 exam, you should prepare a timetable, study the complete syllabus, focus on important topics, do timely revision, work on your mistakes, etc. Solving previous year papers and mock tests is also important to score good marks in the exam.  

Is 200 a good score in BITSAT?

A good score in BITSAT is typically above 300 out of a total of 390 marks.

Are there multiple attempts for BITSAT 2026?

There are only two attempts in BITSAT 2026. If you have already enrolled at any of the BITS campuses, then you are ineligible to take the test. Admissions are made solely on merit, which is further determined by your performance in the examination.

I am open to attending any of the three BITS campuses. Do I have to apply for each campus separately online?

No, you do not need to apply for each of the three campuses separately. The BITSAT 2026 application form will be available for all three campuses.

Can I prepare for BITSAT 2026 at home?

Yes, you can prepare for the BITSAT 2026 exam at home. It might seem challenging in the first few phases without coaching, but eventually, with hard work and dedication, you can complete the syllabus of BITSAT 2026 a month before the BITSAT entrance exam.

Will the 75% criteria be applied to the BITSAT 2026 exam?

Yes, as per the previous exam pattern, the 75% criteria will be applicable to the BITSAT 2026 exam. It states that candidates with 75% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology will be eligible to apply for the exam.

Who is eligible for BITS 2026?

Students who should have passed the 12th examination from a recognized Central or State board or its equivalent with at least 75% marks in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, or Biology and adequate proficiency in English are eligible for BITSAT 2026 exam.

Is there a provision for direct B.Tech admission without BITSAT score/ rank?

First rank holders of all national and state boards are eligible for direct admission in B.Tech course offered by BITS. 

Is BITSAT score mandatory for B.Pharma admission at BITS campuses?

If you are aspiring for B.Pharma admission in any of the campuses of BITS, you will have to clear BITSAT exam.
