IBPS PO Syllabus 2026

IBPS PO Syllabus 2026: Candidates planning for the IBPS PO 2026 exam must understand the IBPS PO Syllabus 2026 thoroughly, considering the fact that the level of competition will be very high with respect to state-level exams. Aspirants must start their IBPS PO preparation for the Preliminary and to start with, they must be handy with the Preliminary syllabus and IBPS PO exam pattern 2026.

IBPS PO 2026 Syllabus defines the subjects and topics covered in the examination. Candidates appearing for the examination must know the syllabus of IBPS PO. It is important to have a clear understanding of the syllabus in order to prepare for the exam in an effective way. The syllabus for IBPS PO Mains is slightly different from that of the IBPS Preliminary examination.

Data Analysis and Interpretation Reasoning and Computer Aptitude are added along with the English Language and General Awareness in the IBPS PO Mains Syllabus 2026. Whereas, the IBPS PO Prelims Syllabus 2026 includes English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability. IBPS PO Mains Syllabus: Descriptive Test

In order to excel in the essay writing component, aspirants must practice writing essays on current affairs and trending topics as part of the IBPS PO syllabus 2026. As for the letter-writing component, candidates should practice both formal and informal letter-writing. IBPS PO Interview Syllabus 2026

The IBPS PO syllabus 2026 for the Interview stage is not fixed to a certain number of topics and can range to a vast among of subjects spanning different areas like banking, finance, economics, etc. The questions might vary and be based on the Governors, the Headquarters, the sorts of cards, the bank accounts, or the KYC requirements. Candidates must be informed about current events, including questions on the national budget, economic reforms, GST, etc. The interview round carries a maximum score of 100. 

IBPS PO Interview Questions

The IBPS PO Interview questions are also not limited to a specific number and questions can come from random areas and subjects. What is the IBPS PO mains syllabus 2026?

The IBPS PO mains syllabus 2026 includes topics like Blood Relations, Order and Ranking, Alphanumeric Series, Distance and Direction, Verbal Reasoning, Seating Arrangements, Puzzles, Sentence Correction, Para Jumbles, Fill in the Blanks, Para/ Sentence Completion, Cloze Test, Reading Comprehension, Number Series, Data Interpretation, Simplification/ Approximation, Quadratic Equation, Data Sufficiency, Mensuration, Current Affairs, Banking Awareness, GK Updates, Currencies, Important Places, Books and Authors, Awards, and more.

What is the IBPS PO prelims syllabus 2026?

The IBPS PO prelims syllabus 2026 includes topics like Seating Arrangements, Puzzles, Input-Output, Data Sufficiency, Blood Relations, Order and Ranking, Alphanumeric Series, Cloze Test, Reading Comprehension, Spotting Errors, Sentence Improvement, Sentence Correction, Para Jumbles, Fill in the Blanks, Para/Sentence Completion, Number Series, Data Interpretation, Simplification/ Approximation, Quadratic Equation, Data Sufficiency, Mensuration, and more.

What sections are there in the IBPS PO exam 2026?

The sections in IBPS PO preliminary examination are  English Language, Quantitative Ability, and Reasoning Ability. IBPS PO main exams 2026 will have questions on sections like Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude, English Language, Data Interpretation & Analysis, and Genera/ Economy/ Banking Awareness.

What is the IBPS PO syllabus 2026?

The IBPS PO syllabus 2026 for the preliminary and mains exam is slightly different. While the IBPS PO prelims 2026 syllabus comprises the English Language, Quantitative Ability, and Reasoning Ability, the IBPS PO mains syllabus 2026 includes Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude, English Language, Data Interpretation & Analysis, and Genera/ Economy/ Banking Awareness.
