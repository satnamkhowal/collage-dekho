CUET 2026 Syllabus

CUET UG Syllabus 2026 has been revised by the National testing Agency. CUET Syllabus 2026 includes three prominent sections which are Domain Subjects, Languages and General Aptitude Test. CUET Syllabus 2026 for undergraduate courses has been released by NTA for 23 domain subjects, 13 languages and General Aptitude tests. You can select up to 5 CUET subjects and appear for the same on the given date and time. CUET UG Exam 2026 has been scheduled between May 11 and 31, 2026 in CBT Mode.

Students preparing through CUET UG Syllabus also must know that NTA has recorded 1,492,764 CUET registration 2026 as compared to 1,354,699 registration last year which will make the competition more tough. The maximum number of regustration came from five states namely Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Delhi and rajasthan. Some of the popular subjects that were preferred by the students while filling the CUET application form include English, Ohysics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and General Aptitude Test. 

CUET UG Syllabus is completely based on their Class XIIth NCERT curriculum and one must prepare accordingly. CUET Syllabus has been revised and students from Science, Arts & Commerce streams must review the syllabus and the subjects that they have studied in Class XII. Students willing to appear for CUET UG Exam 2026 must go through the detailed CUET syllabus online so that they can crack the exam. As per the CUET Exam Pattern 2026, there will be 50 multiple choice questions for each domain subject.

CUET 2026 Syllabus for UG Programmes

CUET syllabus 2026 is quite vast and in-depth, however, it includes the topics from the 12th class. CUET UG syllabus 2026 is divided into three sections - Section 1 (1A and 1B), Section II, and Section III. Sections 1A and 1B include questions from languages and Section II has questions that are asked from domain-specific. Section III will have questions for General Test and Ability. We have given CUET UG 2026 syllabus for all three sections below. The table given below will take you through the different sections of CUET syllabus 2026 that you need to prepare for CUET exam 2026.

FAQs about CUET Syllabus

Is JEE harder than CUET?

Indeed, CUET is simpler than JEE Mains based on the syllabus. Compared to CUET, the JEE has a far higher difficulty level. Without having to study from the class 11 syllabus, candidates can easily pass the CUET exam by adhering to the syllabus for class 12.

Does the CUET ask for the class 11 syllabus?

No, the CUET syllabus does not include the class 11th syllabus. Candidates preparing for the CUET exam should only refer to the NCERT syllabus for class 12.

What is the general syllabus for the CUET?

CUET 2026 General Test syllabus includes sections on current affairs, general knowledge, quantitative reasoning, logical and analytical reasoning, general mental ability, and numerical ability. 

What is the CUET exam 2026 syllabus?

The CUET 2026 syllabus includes the following sections:

  • Part A: General awareness, general English, quantitative aptitude, and logical reasoning
  • Section 3: General test, which includes general knowledge, current affairs, general mental ability, numerical ability, and reasoning
  • Language test: Reading comprehension, which tests students' fluency in the language
  • Domain subject: Based on the NCERT model syllabus for the subject students choose while filling out their application form

What will be the mode to conduct the CUET 2026 exam?

The CUET exam 2026 will be conducted in online mode through computer-based test.

What is the pattern of questions asked in the CUET 2026 exam?

The CUET 2026 questions are objective type questions and carry multiple choice questions (MCQs).

From where can the candidates download the CUET 2026 syllabus?

The candidates can download the CUET syllabus 2026 for various subjects in PDF format from our collegedekho page as well as from the official website. 

Does the CUET 2026 syllabus incorporate questions from Class 11 apart from Class 12?

No, the CUET 2026 syllabus is made only from the Class 12 syllabus and it has no topics or chapters from the Class 11 syllabus.

What is the level of difficulty of the CUET 2026 syllabus?

The CUET syllabus 2026 is mostly aligned with the syllabus of the Class 12 board. Most of the topics are extracted from the Class 12 board syllabus and the level of questions is usually easy. 

What topics are part of CUET 2026 English syllabus for appearing in the CUET UG entrance test?

The CUET syllabus 2026 for English will consist of MCQ-based questions and the topics will be asked from reading comprehension based on different types of passages–factual, literary aptitude, literary and narrative, and vocabulary.

Why is it important for the students to check CUET syllabus 2026?

Candidates must go through the detailed CUET syllabus to get an idea about all the topics so that they can prepare well for the entrance test.
