About FMGE 2025 December Session (Pass Certificate from till Apr 14)

The FMGE 2025 December session pass certificate is being distributed by the NBEMS authorities till April 14, 2026. Qualified students must physically collect their certificates from the NBEMS office located in New Delhi. The FMGE 2025 results for the December session were released online on January 29, 2026, on the official webpage. The FMGE 2025 exam was held on January 17, 2025, at the exam centres. The Part A examination was from 9 am to 11.30 pm, while the Part B exam was held from 2 pm to 4.30 pm. The FMGE 2025 December session Paper 1 was moderate to solve for students. On the other hand, the FMGE 2025 June paper was of moderate difficulty.

Students who have secured a medical degree from a foreign country, such as the USA, UK, Australia, or New Zealand, are exempt from appearing for the FMGE 2025 Exam. The National Board of Examinations for Medical Sciences conducts the foreign medical entrance exam twice annually, in June and December Sessions. Once the exam results are announced, the NBEMS will issue the pass certificate to the eligible students for practising foreign medicine in India. So, students eager to know more about the FMGE 2025 December Session exam dates can go through the registration process, admit card download process, etc.

    FMGE 2025 Selection Process

    FMGE 2025 exam is a screening test for Medical and Dental students who have been certified as having passed in their UD Medical or Dental Degrees. Students can check out the FMGE 2025 selection process stated below. 

    1. FMGE 2025 Application Form Filling: To qualify for this screening test, candidates must first fill out the correct details in application form.
    2. FMGE 2025 Admit Card for December Session: The admit card for the FMGE 2025 exam is released 5 to 6 days before the exam day. It is mandatory to carry the admit card along with ID proof to the FMGE exam centre.
    3. FMGE 2025 Exam Day: Applicants must reach the exam centre 30 or 45 minutes before exam time.
    4. FMGE 2025 Result Declaration: The result is released a month after the exam is conducted. To pass this screening test, it is mandatory to score a minimum qualifying mark.
    5. Issuance and Collection of Pass Certification: Those who score above or equal to the minimum qualifying percentile have to collect a Pass Certificate from the NBE's office in New Delhi.

    FAQs about FMGE

    Is there any negative marking in the FMGE exam?

    No, there will be no negative marking in the FMGE 2025 exam. 

    In which language is the FMGE exam held?

    The FMGE exam 2025 is held in English language only. 

    Is there any age limit for students applying for the FMGE exam 2025?

    As per the FMGE 2025 exam guidelines, there is no age limit for the students applying for FMGE 2025 exam.

    What is FMGE?

    FMGE is conducted as per the guidelines of the Medical Council of India. It is a license examination conducted by the National Board of Examination (NBE). FMGE exam is mandatory for all the medical graduates from international institutes who have completed their primary medical degree, i.e., MBBS course outside India.

    What is the full form of FMGE?

    The full form of FMGE is Foreign Medical Graduates Examination.

    Do I need to submit Class 12 mark sheet in the FMGE 2025 Application form?

    Yes, aspirants need to submit the Class 12 mark sheet to become eligible to fill the FMGE 2025 Application form.

    What type of questions will be asked in FMGE 2025 question paper?

    The FMGE 2025 question paper will have 300 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) where Part A and Part B will contain 150 questions each.

    Is there any negative marking in FMGE 2025?

    No, there are no negative markings for answers in FMGE 2025.

    Who can appear in the FMGE 2025 Exam?

    Aspirants with Primary medical qualifications who fulfill the FMGE eligibility criteria can appear for the FMGE 2025 exam. 

    What should the dimensions of the image must be for the FMGE admit card?

    The dimension for the image should be a passport size photograph affixed to the admit card and should be 35 * 45 mm with at least 75% coverage of the candidate's face and head.

    Can I take E-Aadhaar to the FMGE Exam Centre?

    A printout of your E-Aadhaar can be carried with the Aadhaar Number printed on it. However, there should be a clear picture in the E-Aadhaar. Only then candidates can enter the Exam Hall.

    I forgot my Login ID, so how do I download my FMGE 2025 Admit Card?

    You can click the 'Forgot Password' button in the Applicant Login section of the FMGE website. After that, you need to enter all the details in the specified column and retrieve your new login ID and password.

    What type of questions will there be in the FMGE 2025 exam?

    In the FMGE 2025 Exam, a total of 300 multiple-choice questions will be asked of the students. The questions covered the entire FMGE 2025 Syllabus. 

    If I don't clear FMGE Exam I can study in India?

    No, it is mandatory to clear FMGE exam if you have completed a medical degree from outside India. However, candidates with a medical degree from New Zealand, Australia, Canada, the United States of America or the United Kingdom are excluded from the FMGE examination.

    How many attempts do you get for FMGE?

    A candidate has a maximum of three attempts to pass the examination. The FMGE is conducted twice a year in June & December and a candidate has to score a minimum of 50% marks in each of the papers to be declared as passed.
