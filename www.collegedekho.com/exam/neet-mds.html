NEET MDS (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for Master of Dental Surgery) is conducted for admission into postgraduate dental surgery courses across India. It is a national-level entrance exam conducted annually by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS). The NEET MDS 2026 exam is to be held on May 2, 2026, this year.

About NEET MDS 2026 Exam

The NEET MDS exam 2026 date has been announced by the NBEMS on its official website. The national-level entrance test will be conducted on May 2, 2026. NBEMS has released the NEET MDS city intimation slip 2026 at natboard.edu.in on April 22, 2026. Students can check and download the exam city intimation slip from the official website. Shortly after, NEET MDS 2026 admit card will be released tentatively on April 29, 2026. The registration and application process was conducted from March 14 to 30, 2026.

NEET MDS Eligibility Criteria 2026

Before appearing for the NEET MDS 2026 exam, you must remember that you will have to meet all the eligibility criteria as discussed by the official authority. The NEET MDS 2026 Eligibility Criteria suggests that it is mandatory to complete a 1-year internship period before appearing for the test. The Dental Council of India (DCI) curated the eligibility criteria for NEET MDS 2026 as per the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)’s approval. Refer to the table below to learn in detail about the eligibility criteria of NEET MDS 2026. 

  • You should hold a BDS degree from a recognised and renowned institute to be eligible for the NEET MDS 2026 Exam.

  • You must have a permanent/provisional registration certificate with DCI or the State Dental Council (SDC) by June 30, 2026.

  • One must complete their one-year compulsory rotatory internship by June 30 of the academic year.

NEET MDS 2026 Application Process

NEET MDS 2026 exam will be conducted in online mode. The registration process for NEET MDS 2026 was open from February till March 2026. Students have to participate in the NEET MDS 2026 Registration, and they have to meet all the eligibility criteria as per the guidelines. The application fee for the NEET MDS 2026 is INR 3,000 for general, and INR 2,750 for reserved candidates. Paying for the NEET MDS 2026 application is simple. Use Net banking, debit cards, or credit cards. After a successful registration, download your NEET MDS 2026 admit card. Follow these steps to fill out the application.

  1. Visit the official National Board of Examinations (NBE) website.

  2. Click on NEET MDS registration for 2026.

  3. Provide details like name, parents’ information, email, contact details, and answer security questions.

  4. Upon registration, you'll receive a login ID and password.

  5. Sign in with these credentials.

  6. Begin filling out the NEET MDS 2026 application form.

  7. Input personal and academic information.

  8. Upload necessary documents.

  9. Choose your preferred exam centers.

  10. Pay the NEET MDS 2026 application fee.

  11. Print a copy of the NEET MDS application form for 2026.

Following these steps ensures a smooth completion of the NEET MDS 2026 application process.

NEET MDS 2026 Result

NEET MDS result 2026 will be announced on the official website of NBE in the form of a PDF. The NEET MDS 2026 result is expected to be released in May 2026. It comprises the qualifying status of students, the scores they have obtained, the roll no, and the overall rank obtained. Here is how to download the result of NEET MDS 2026 given below.

To download your NEET MDS Result 2026 and scorecard, follow these simple steps:

NEET MDS Result 2026:

  1. Visit the official website at nbe.edu.in.

  2. Navigate to the NEET MDS section.

  3. Click on the "Result" button under the NEET MDS 2026 tab.

  4. The NEET MDS 2026 result PDF will open on the screen.

  5. To check your result, use Ctrl+F keys and input your roll number.

  6. Verify the roll number and view the marks scored out of 960, along with your NEET MDS 2026 rank.

  7. Download the NEET MDS 2026 result PDF by clicking on the download option.

  8. Take a printout for future reference.

NEET MDS 2026 Scorecard:

  1. Visit nbe.edu.in.

  2. Select the login window.

  3. Enter your assigned login credentials, including the application ID and password.

  4. The NEET MDS 2026 scorecard details will appear on the screen.

  5. Check your roll number, NEET MDS 2026 rank, marks scored out of 960, 50% AIQ rank, and category rank.

  6. Take a printout of the NEET MDS 2026 scorecard.

NEET MDS 2026 Counselling

The NEET MDS 2026 Counselling process will be conducted in online mode. A total of 3 rounds will be conducted. In case of any vacant seats, an extra stray vacancy round is conducted. The Medical Council Committee (MCC) will oversee the counselling process. Refer to the table below to learn more about counselling process of NEET MDS Counselling 2026.

Conducting Authority

MCC (Medical Counselling Committee) is responsible for NEET MDS counselling 2026

Seat Allocation

MCC handles 50% All India quota seats, ESIC and AFMC seats, and deemed/central university seats. Jammu and Kashmir aspirants participate in state-specific counselling

Counselling Rounds

There are three main rounds of NEET MDS 2026 counselling with a potential stray vacancy round. A separate mop-up round is conducted for central, deemed, and ESIC colleges

Eligibility

Qualifiers must meet the minimum qualifying percentile and clear the exam results to participate in NEET MDS counselling

Registration

Qualified candidates register online, providing preferences for colleges and courses

Seat Allotment

Allocation depends on choices, seat availability, NEET MDS rank, reservations, and other factors

FAQs about NEET MDS

Who is the exam conducting authority for NEET MDS 2026 exam?

NEET MDS 2026 exam is held by the National Board of Examinations (NBE). It is the official body that is in charge of conducting the test on a national scale. NBE releases all relevant information on its official website - nbe.edu.in. 

In total, how many seats are offered via NEET MDS 2026 entrance test?

More than 6,500 seats are made available for candidates who wish to secure admission through NEET MDS 2026 exam. Government and private institutions grant admission to all students based on their merit strictly. Since the exam is held at national level, the competition is quite tough for every seat.

What is the NEET MDS 2026 lower age criteria?

Basis the NEET MDS eligibility criteria 2026, students must not be less than 20 years of age while applying for the entrance exam. One has to be above the given age threshold to appear for the test. Official authorites hold the right to disqualify a candidate if any information found is incorrect.

Am I allowed submit NEET MDS 2026 application form offline?

No, students are not allowed to send in their application form of NEET MDS 2026 offline. They have to fill in the registration forms online. Students need to enter the details within the given stipulated timeline, post which, no registrations are accepted.

Is there any negative marking given for NEET MDS 2026 exam?

Yes, as per the NEET MDS 2026 marking scheme, -1 mark is deducted for every wrong answer. Similarly, for every correct answer, +4 marks are awarded, and no marks are assigned for unanswered questions. So, students remain aware while attempting the exam paper.

In total, how many questions are asked in NEET MDS 2026 exam?

Basis the NEET MDS 2026 exam paper pattern, a total of 240 multiple choice questions are asked in the exam. Students are given 3 hours to answer the questions. Based on the choices filled, aspirants will be assigned marks and results will be declared.

In total how many cities will the NEET MDS 2026 exam be conducted?

NEET MDS 2026 will be held in a total of 72 cities nationwide. Aspirants are requested to choose the exam centre closest to them to ensure they reach the exam hall on time. Any late entries are not allowed, and aspirants will have to wait for another year to appear for the NEET MDS exam.
