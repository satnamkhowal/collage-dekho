XAT 2026 Cutoff (Out)

The XAT cutoff 2026 for XLRI PGDM BM and HRM courses has been released by the official authorities. The XAT total cutoff for PGDM HRM was 92 marks for Engg male, 90 marks for Non-Engg male, and 97 marks for Engg female. Further, for PGDM HRM courses, it was 84 marks for Non-Engg female, 87 marks for Engg transgender and 84 marks for Non-Engg Transgender students. In case of PGDM BM courses, the total cutoff scores were 94 marks for Engg male, 94 for Non-Engg male, 88 marks for Engg female, Non-Engg female, Engg transgender, & Non Engg transgender examinees. 

The XAT 2026 cutoff is the minimum mark candidates must score to get shortlisted for the XAT participating colleges for various postgraduate management courses. Based on the XAT cutoff 2026, XLRI Jamshedpur will shortlist qualified candidates for the GD/PI round, which will be conducted in February/March 2026, and send the schedule for the same to their registered email addresses. Students eager to know more about the XAT 2026 cutoff can go through the expected cutoff, steps to check the XAT cutoff scores, etc.

How to Check XAT 2026 Cutoff?

To check the XAT cutoff 2026 list, candidates will need to follow the given steps:

  • Visit the official website of XLRI Jamshedpur at xlri.ac.in.

  • If you wish to check the cutoff for other participating colleges, visit the official website for the same.

  • On the official website, select the program you wish to apply for and click on the link for ‘XAT Cut-Off Marks’ from the drop-down menu.

  • You will be redirected to a new page to check out the XAT cutoff marks for that particular MBA program.

  • Now you can check the XAT cut off for your preferred management course.

  • Take a printout of the XAT cutoff marks or save it in PDF format for future reference.

Factors Determining XAT Cutoff 2026

Different parameters decide the cutoff marks for any examination. TLet’s look at the factors determining the XAT cutoff 2026.

  • Total Number of Candidates: A large applicant pool results in greater competition and a higher overall cutoff, whereas a small pool results in less competition and a lower cutoff. Therefore, when the number of applicants is higher, candidates can expect the cutoff to be higher as well.

  • Difficulty Level of the Exam: If the XAT 2026 questions are more challenging, the cutoff score is likely to be lowered and vice versa. The difficulty level is a crucial component that should be considered when figuring out the XAT 2026 cutoff.

  • Total Number of Seats: The number of seats each institute offers for admission to its MBA and PGDM programs has an impact on the XAT sectional cutoff. More demand and a higher cutoff are associated with fewer seats, whereas fewer seats and a lower cutoff are associated with more seats.

  • Total Qualified Candidates: If more candidates qualify for the XAT exam, it increases the number of college applications and the level of competition. This leads XAT participating institutes to increase their XAT cutoffs to select only the best students out of the entire applicant pool.

  • College Ranking: It is a well-known fact that the best MBA colleges are also the toughest to get into. Since the top-ranked B-schools receive a huge amount of applications every year, the cutoff for these institutes is also high to only allow the best candidates to secure admission.

  • Previous Years' Cutoff: XAT cutoff trends from previous years are also a determining factor for institutes. Typically the cutoff follows a similar pattern as that of last year’s cutoff, both overall and sectional. This is why being aware of the previous year’s cutoff trends can be helpful for candidates who wish to seek admission in the current academic year.

What After XAT 2026 Cut Off?

Once the XAT 2026 cutoff is announced, XLRI Jamshedpur along with other XAT participating colleges will release the list of candidates who have been shortlisted for selection rounds including Group Discussion (GD), Personal Interview (PI), and Essay Writing. The XAT accepting colleges will send the GD/PI call letters to students via email. Aspirants who wish to participate in the XAT selection process of these colleges must fill out the online registration form for the XAT selection process for their preferred XAT participating institute. Candidates must apply to each of the colleges separately since there is no standardized XAT selection process. The GD/PI rounds at XLRI Jamshedpur are conducted in Delhi, Kolkata, Jamshedpur and Bangalore. Candidates must check the official website of their preferred colleges to know how the XAT selection process will be conducted for those institutes.

FAQs about XAT Cut Off

What is the XAT sectional cutoff?

The minimum percentile that applicants must achieve for each section of the exam is known as the "sectional cutoff" for the XAT. A candidate must have achieved at least 85 percentile in the XAT's Quantitative Aptitude part in order to be shortlisted for a group discussion and personal interview, for instance, if the test's cutoff is 85 percentile.

What is the importance of XAT cutoff during the XLRI PI selection?

When choosing candidates for the personal interview stage at XLRI Jamshedpur, the XAT cutoff is the most crucial factor. People who don't make the cutoff aren't even considered for PI shortlisting. Class 10, 12, and graduation grades, as well as the XAT cutoff, are the two primary factors used to choose candidates for the PI round.

What is XAT cutoff?

The XAT cutoff percentile is the minimum score that candidates must achieve to qualify for the XAT 2023 examination. It is the basis on which XLRI will shortlist the candidates for the next rounds of the selection process. Only the candidates who will score above the XAT cutoff will be eligible for admission to XLRI. 

Can I get admission to XLRI without clearing the XAT cutoff?

No, you cannot get admission to XLRI without clearing the XAT cutoff. Also clearing the XAT cutoff does not guarantee admission to the institute. You will have to outperform the other candidates in group discussions and personal interview rounds.

When will the XAT cutoff 2026 be announced?

The XAT cutoff 2026 will be announced by XLRI in January 2026, on the website. The XAT 2026 results will be announced in January 2026.

What factors determine the XAT cutoff 2026?

The factors determining the XAT cutoff 2026 are the difficulty level of the XAT 2026 question paper, the number of candidates appearing for the exam, the total number of seats available for admission, the previous year’s cutoff and the rankings of the participating colleges. 

What is the expected XAT 2026 sectional cutoff?

The expected XAT 2026 sectional cutoff is 8-8.5 for Verbal Ability and Logical Reasoning, 8.5-9 for Decision Making, and 9-11 for Quantitative Ability and Data Interpretation. The expected overall XAT cutoff 2026 for business management courses is between 32.5 and 33.5, and for human resource management is between 30 and 31.

Is the XAT cutoff set by XLRI accepted by other MBA colleges?

No, the XAT cutoff set by XLRI is not accepted by other MBA colleges. It is valid only for admission to XLRI. The other business schools set their own XAT cutoff to conduct the selection process of the candidates. 

Is the XAT sectional cutoff also considered for admission to institutes?

No, the XAT sectional cutoff is not considered for admission to institutes. Only the overall cutoff determines whether one will get admission or not. However, candidates must aspire to maintain the sectional cutoff too so that they can be easily eligible for admission. 

Is the XAT cutoff 2026 the same for all participating institutes?

No, the XAT cutoff 2026 is not the same for all participating institutes. The colleges fix their percentile for admission based on the criteria determined by the admission committee. The XAT cutoff of participating institutes ranges between the 75 percentile and 95 percentile.
