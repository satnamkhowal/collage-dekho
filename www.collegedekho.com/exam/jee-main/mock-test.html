​

  • Step 3: The next window will pop up with a Username and Password filled in. Candidates need not use their credentials to access the JEE Main 2026 Mock Test.

​

  • Step 5: After going through the instructions carefully, candidates must proceed to click on "Accept Terms & Conditions".

​

  • Step 6: The JEE Main 2026 Mock Test will begin.

​JEE Main Mock Test 2026: How to Appear For Mock Tests At Practice Centres?

The facility to appear for the JEE Main 2026 mock testonline at practice centres will be open for select locations. Candidates need to register to be able to take the JEE Mains free mock test at the test practice centres. NTA has built a network of test centres across India to assist JEE applicants in preparing for the exam and growing accustomed to the Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode of the exam. Applicants can go to these test preparation facilities and take a free mock test for JEE Main 2026 there.

Follow the steps mentioned below to take the JEE Main 2026 mock test series at the practice centre - 

  1. Locate a Practice Centre: Visit the official website of NTA nta.ac.in to locate a practice centre near you
  2. Register Online: Once you have located a practice centre, you need to register online for the mock test. Go to the NTA's official website and click on the link for JEE Main 2026 Mock Test FREEregistration. Follow the instructions to complete the registration process. You will receive a confirmation email once your registration is complete.
  3. Visit the Practice Centre: On the day of the JEE Main mock test 2026, visit the practice centre at the scheduled time. Carry a valid photo ID proof, such as an Aadhar card, PAN card, or passport, along with your confirmation email.
  4. Appear for the JEE Main 2026 Mock Test: Once you reach the practice centre, you will be directed to a computer terminal where you can appear for the mock test. The onlinemock test for JEE Main 2026 will be conducted in the same format as the actual JEE Main exam.
  5. Receive Your Scores: After you complete the JEE Main 2026 mock test, you will receive your scores immediately.

Benefits of Attempting Online JEE Main Mock Test Series

  • Overall Performance Analysis: After each JEE Main mock test attempt, candidates can get a detailed analysis report of their performance. This helps students assess their strengths and shortcomings and pushes them for improvement. 

  • Revision: Attempting the online JEE Mains free mock test assists in the daily revision of all important topics, concepts and formulas. By taking 2-3 mock exams a day, students can get well-acquainted with the actual test pattern. 

  • Helps Improve Accuracy and Speed: The mock test series for JEE Mains 2025 helps candidates improve their speed and accuracy as they become well-adept with the question type and nature, exam duration, etc.

  • Boosts Self-Confidence: By attempting the JEE Main mock questions, students become confident in their abilities after each session.

FAQs about JEE Main Mock Test

Who can attempt the JEE Main Session 1 2025 mock test?

The NTA JEE Main mock test 2025 can be attempted by anyone. No login credentials are required to access the test. To take the JEE Main 2025 mock test, candidates need to register at the NTA website.

How attempting the JEE Main 2025 mock test will benefit me?

By attempting JEE Main mock tests 2025 you will be able to check your preparation so far, improve your time management skills, work on your shortcomings and perform well in the real-time JEE Main 2025 exam. 

Will JEE Main 2025 have 4 attempts?

No, JEE Main 2025 will only allow you two tries. The JEE Main 2025 test will be administered by NTA in two sessions, in January and April. Therefore, the candidate should keep on studying for JEE 2025 from top books, NCERT, attempting JEE Main mock tests, previous year papers, doing revision, and working on their time accuracy. 

Is 4 Months enough for JEE Main 2025 preparation?

Yes, by developing and rigorously adhering to a disciplined approach, candidates can successfully complete JEE Main in 4 months. Candidates can further their preparations by completing JEE main 2025 mock test, studying from the best study materials, and regularly revising the whole syllabus. 

Will JEE Main 2025 be tough?

JEE has a reputation for shocking applicants regarding their difficulty level. Whatever the difficulty of the questions, you should be psychologically ready. As a JEE candidate, it's not a good idea to worry about these things when preparing for JEE 2025, which might be difficult or simple.

Does JEE 2025 require 75%?

Yes, 75% is required for JEE 2025 as less than 75% in the class 12th board exam would exclude candidates from taking the JEE. This guideline is relevant to both droppers and pickers. The person who originally registered for the JEE in 2023 and subsequently dropped out in 2025 will need to achieve this 75% requirement.
