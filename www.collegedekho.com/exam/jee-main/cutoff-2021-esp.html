JEE Main Cutoff 2021

The Joint Entrance Examination - Main (JEE Main) 2021 cutoff scores, announced by the National Testing Agency (NTA), saw a noticeable decrease compared to the previous year. This change in cut-offs impacted eligibility and competition for admission to prestigious engineering colleges in India.

Here's a breakdown of the key facts:

  • General Category: The qualifying cutoff, required for appearing in JEE Advanced and securing B.Tech/B.Arch seats in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, stood at 87.8992241 (out of 360 marks). This was a dip from 90.3765335 in 2020.
  • Other Categories: Similar downward trends were observed for other reserved categories. OBC-NCL witnessed a cutoff of 68.0234447, SC at 46.8825338, and ST at 34.6728999.
  • Reasons for Dip: Several factors contributed to the lower cut-offs, including increased exam attempts (four sessions in 2021 instead of two), change in exam pattern (introduction of percentile scores), and perceived difficulty level variations across sessions.

  • The cutoff was significantly impacted by the complexity of the JEE Main 2021 exam questions.
