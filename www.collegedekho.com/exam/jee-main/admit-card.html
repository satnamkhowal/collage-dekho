About JEE Main Admit Card 2026 (Out)

The JEE Main admit card 2026 session 2 for Paper 1 has been released on March 29, 2026. The hall ticket for Paper 2 will be available shortly. The NTA usually releases the hall ticket 2-3 days prior to the date of the exam. The JEE Main session 2 admit card is published online, in a PDF format, on the official website of JEE Mains 2026 at jeemain.nta.nic.in. It is important to note that all students are required to carry their admit cards to their allotted exam centres on the exam date without fail, or they will be denied entry into the exam hall. The JEE Main 2026 Session 2 exam is set to be conducted on April 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 2026 (Paper 1 - B.Tech). What to do in Case of a Forgotten JEE Main Login Password?

Candidates can retrieve the JEE Main login password for JEE Mains hall ticket download 2026from the official website. Check the steps to retrieve the forgotten JEE Main login password below:

  • Go to the official website: jeemain.nta.nic.in 
  • Click on the JEE Main Admit Card 2026 download link 
  • The login window will appear on the screen 
  • Click on the "Forgot Password" link available below the login window
  • Answer the security question that was selected while filling out the JEE Main Application Form 2026
  • The Code will be sent through SMS to the registered mobile number
  • A password reset link will be provided to the registered email
  • Through the link, reset the JEE Main 2026 login password
  • Log in to the registered account for the exam to download the JEE Main Hall Ticket 2026 

Discrepancies in JEE Main 2026 Admit Card

There are chances that students might find discrepancies in details after the JEE Mains hall ticket download 2026. In case of any discrepancies or inconsistencies in the JEE Main Admit Card 2026 for the January session, the candidate should contact the NTA's Helpline Number between 10 a.m. and 5.00 p.m. They can also follow the steps to resolve in case they come across any discrepancy in the JEE Main 2026 admit card. In this situation, the individual would show up before the examination using the previously downloaded JEE Main admit card. Nonetheless, NTA will take the required steps to rectify the record later.

NTA Help Line Number: 011-40759000 (10.00 A.M. to 05.00 P.M)

JEE Main 2026 Admit Card: List of Items Allowed & Banned

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has prescribed the list of items that are allowed and banned at the JEE Main 2026 exam centre on the JEE Mains hall ticket 2026. Candidates are advised to check the list of these items to avoid any last-minute hassle at the exam centre. 

Items Allowed at JEE Main 2026 Exam Centre

Check the list of items that NTA has allowed inside the JEE Main exam center as per the JEE Mains hall ticket 2026:

  • JEE Main Admit Card 2026 (hard copy) 
  • Passport-size photograph (2 copies, as uploaded on the JEE Main application form)
  • Black/Blue ballpoint pen 
  • Valid government photo-identity proof 
  • PwD certificate (if applicable)
  • Self-Declaration Form

Items Prohibited at JEE Main 2026 Exam Centre

The list of items that are prohibited/banned at the JEE Main exam centre are mentioned below as featured on the JEE Mains hall ticket 2026 download:

  • Electronic Devices (Mobile, Calculator, Watches, etc.)
  • Papers, chits, bits of paper, etc.

JEE Main 2026 Exam Day Guidelines

The following JEE Main 2026 exam day instructionsas mentioned on the JEE Main 2026 Hall Ticket must be followed by the candidates on the exam day - 

  • Candidates are required to reach the test centre before the scheduled time to avoid last-minute hurries. Since there will be security checks, candidates should make sure they reach the JEE Main exam centres 2026 early.
  • Candidates should submit the JEE Main admit card 2026 and the six blank papers for rough work (supplied by the centre) before leaving the examination hall at the appropriate drop box. Failure to do so may result in the answer sheets not being evaluated.
  • JEE Mains admit card 2026 along with valid ID proof of the candidate has to be presented at the exam centre.

Note: Valid ID proofs include Aadhar, Passport, Voter ID, School ID/ PAN/ Driving License etc.

  • Candidates are not required to carry any blank papers to the exam hall. Blank paper or rough sheets will be provided to the candidates inside the exam hall.
  • Candidates suffering from diabetes will not be allowed to carry packed food such as chocolate, sandwiches, etc. inside the examination hall. They will be allowed to carry eatables inside the exam hall such as sugar tablets/fruits and transparent water bottles.
  • No electronic devices will be allowed in the JEE Main exam hall 

Note: Gadgets such as calculators, pagers, mobile phones, and more will not be allowed inside the examination centre

  • Candidates will not be allowed to enter the exam centre after the examination commences or leave before it concludes.
  • PwD certificate for the candidates claiming relaxation (if any)
  • As per the JEE Main Dress Code 2026, shoes or footwear with heavy soles, and dresses with huge buttons are not allowed in the exam hall. However, clothing with full sleeves is permitted.

FAQs about JEE Main Admit Card

Is the JEE Main 2025 City Intimation Slip for Session 2 Released?

Yes, the JEE Main City Intimation Slip has been released on the official website on March 20, 2025, for Session 2. Candidates can access the JEE Main City Slip 2025  by logging into their registered accounts using their application number and date of birth. 

Is the JEE Main Session 2 admit card 2025 released?

Yes, the JEE Main Admit Card 2025 for the Session 2 has been released for the exam scheduled on April 2, 3, and 4, 2025 on March 28, 2025.  Candidates can download the JEE Main 2025 hall ticket using the application number and password/date of birth on the official website: jeemain.nta.nic.in. 

How to check my JEE Main exam centre 2025?

JEE Main 2025 exam centre details is available on the JEE Main 2025 advanced city intimation slip as well as the JEE Main admit card 2025. Candidates can visit jeemain.nta.nic.in to download the JEE Main 2025 admit card

What is the helpline number for JEE Mains admit card 2025?

NTA Students' Helpline Number is 1800 112211 or 1800 425 3800.

What is the official website for downloading the JEE Main Admit Card 2025?

The JEE Main admit card 2025 download official website is jeemain.nta.nic.in.

How to download JEE Mains admit card 2025?

Students can download the IIT JEE Main 2025 admit card by visiting the JEE Main 2025 official website jeemain.nta.nic.in and entering the JEE Main 2025 login credentials: application number and password.

What are the documents to be carried along with the admit card of JEE Main 2025?

Candidates will have to carry any of the following documents as their ID proof along with the JEE Main 2025 admit card. 

  • Aadhar Card
  • Ration Card
  • Driving License
  • Employee ID
  • PAN Card
  • College ID
  • Any Government-issued ID Proof
  • Voter ID
  • Passport
  • Bank Passbook
  • ID proof authorized by Gazetted Officer

What should I do if the spelling of my name on the JEE Main admit card is incorrect?

Candidates who notice an inaccuracy or a problem with their JEE Main 2025 admit card should contact the NTA at the hotline number to get it addressed.

Is it mandatory to carry the colour printout of JEE Main admit card?

There is no such rule. If the image of the candidate is not clear in the black and white printout of the admit card, it is advisable to take the colour printout of the same.

What are the details required for downloading the JEE Main 2025 Admit Card?

For downloading and taking the printout of the admit card, you will have to enter the 'JEE Main Application Number', 'Password' and 'Security Pin'.

What if a candidate's signature appears blurred in the JEE Main 2025 Admit Card?

If the signature of a candidate appears blurred in the JEE Main 2025 admit card, he/she must raise the issue with the NTA officials to avoid facing any issues in the future.
