LPUNEST Conducting Body

LPUNEST is conducted by Lovely Professional University for admission to its various programs. Lovely Professional University (LPU) is a private university situated in Jalandhar, Punjab, India. The university was established in 2005 by Lovely International Trust, under The Lovely Professional University Act, 2005 (Punjab Act 25 of 2005), and started operation in 2006. With 24,000 students in 2010, it claims to be the largest single-campus private university in India. Lovely Professional University is NAAC A ++ Grade University which is also ranked 50th on the country's list of government and private universities.

LPUNEST Exam Pattern 2026

LPUNEST 2026 exam pattern comprises the type of questions, number of questions, marking scheme, exam pattern, and other factors. Acquiring knowledge of, the LPUNEST 2026 exam pattern can aid candidates in improving their exam performance. For physics, chemistry, and mathematics, the total number of questions (25) will be divided into 2 categories: 20 questions will be in MCQ format, and 5 questions will be in the form of fill-in-the-blanks. The examination will be conducted online and has a total duration of 2 hours and 30 minutes. It will be available in both English and Hindi, consisting of five sections: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology, and English, each containing 25 questions, resulting in a total of 120 questions across the paper. Importantly, there will be no negative marking in the marking scheme.

FAQs about LPUNEST

What is LPUNEST 2025 Exam Dates?

LPUNEST Exam Dates 2025 have been set as from January 10, 2025, to February 5, 2025, for remotely proctored exams and January 20, 2025, to February 5, 2025, for LPU NEST 2025 test centre-based exams.

What is LPUNEST Eligibility Criteria 2025?

Indian citizens are eligible to apply for the LPUNEST B.Tech course. To qualify, candidates from the general category must have completed their 10+2 or an equivalent examination with a minimum aggregate of 60% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Additionally, applicants must have successfully passed either the LPUNEST 2025 exam or the JEE Main 2025 exam.

Suggest some LPUNEST preparation strategy 2025 for students.

To effectively prepare for your LPUNEST 2025 exams, start by familiarizing yourself with the syllabus and understanding the exam paper pattern. It's important to gather study guides that can aid in your preparation. Creating a study schedule will help you organize your time efficiently, ensuring you cover all necessary material. Additionally, practising with last year's LPUNEST exam papers can give you valuable insight into the types of questions to expect. Finally, make sure to allocate sufficient time for revision to reinforce your understanding and boost your confidence.

Which are the best books for LPUNEST 2025?

For those seeking to strengthen their understanding of LPUNEST 2025 physics, "Concepts of Physics Vol I and II" by H.C. Verma is highly recommended, along with "Problems in General Physics" by I.E. Irodov and "Objective Physics Vol 1 or 2" by DC Pandey and Arihant team. LPUNEST 2025 Chemistry candidates can benefit from the NCERT textbooks for Classes XI and XII, "Organic Chemistry" by O.P. Tandon, and "Modern Approach to Chemical Calculations" by R.C. Mukherjee. In the domain of LPUNEST 2025 mathematics, "Maths XI & XII" by R.D. Sharma is a solid choice, complemented by "Higher Algebra" by Hall and Knight and "Problems in Calculus of One Variable" by I.A. Maron. Each of these books offers valuable insights and practice for students striving for excellence in their respective subjects.

What is LPUNEST Exam Pattern 2025?

LPUNEST 2025 examination will be conducted online and lasts for 2 hours and 30 minutes, available in both English and Hindi. It consists of five sections with a total of 120 questions, including 25 questions each from Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology, and English. Notably, there is no negative marking for incorrect answers.

The qualifying score for LPUNEST is above 40 per cent.

How many times is LPUNEST conducted in a year?

Is there any negative marking in LPUNEST?

No, there is no negative marking in LPUNEST.

What is the duration of LPUNEST?

The duration of LPUNEST is 2 hours and 30 minutes.

Is LPUNEST an online exam?

Yes, LPUNEST is a computer-based online test.

Is it difficult to crack LPUNEST?

No, LPUNEST does not come in the category of very difficult exams. If you prepare well and cover the entire syllabus, you can crack the exam easily.

Is it mandatory to appear for LPUNEST for admission to LPU?

Yes, admission to almost all the courses of LPU is done through LPUNEST. However, you can also be exempt from giving the exam depending upon the course in which you seek admission.
