Why XAT 2026 Exam is Held Every Year?

The XAT full form is Xavier Aptitude Test and it is a national-level entrance exam conducted by XLRI, Jamshedpur for the selection of candidates from across the country for admission to MBA/PGDM courses offered by XLRI and 10 other XAMI member institutes. Additionally, over 250 B-schools across India accept XAT exam 2026 scores for admission to various postgraduate courses. The XAT exam has been conducted by XLRI for more than 75 years and it is one of the most popular MBA entrance exams among aspirants who wish to pursue higher education in the field of management.

XAT 2026 Conducting Body

XLRI was founded in 1949 by the Society of Jesus (Jesuits) and is based in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, India. XLRI began by offering courses for management and trade unions in 1949 before setting up a two-year, full-time program in industrial welfare in 1953. In 1955, it took on the name Xavier Labor Relations Institute and became India's oldest business management school.

XAT 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Meeting the eligibility criteria for the XAT exam is vital for candidates who want to pursue an MBA course at any college or university that accepts XAT scores. Students who do not meet the eligibility criteria for XAT are bound to be rejected for admission during further assessment rounds. The detailed XAT 2026 eligibility criteria are given below.

  • Candidates must hold a bachelor’s degree with a minimum duration of three years or an equivalent degree in any discipline from a recognized university.

  • Final-year students can also apply for the exam. A providential certificate or a previous year's mark sheet is mandatory for registration in such cases.

  • Individuals with a professional degree like CA/CS are also eligible for the XAT exam 2026.

  • Candidates are not required to meet a minimum aggregate marks/percentage criteria in their bachelor’s degree for the XAT exam.

  • There is no upper age limit for candidates to apply for XAT.

  • There is no limit to the number of attempts a candidate can take for the XAT exam.

  • Candidates who are foreign nationals can also apply for the XAT exam, based on their GMAT scores.

XAT Exam Day Guidelines 2026

Check out the exam day guidelines for XAT 2026 that must be followed by all candidates to ensure a smooth examination experience:

  • Make sure to print the XAT 2026 admit card on an A4 size paper and bring your admit card and original ID proofs as specified in the admit card. Check if the photo and the signature of the candidate are properly printed. Keep the digital admit card on your mobile phone. You must affix your photograph as required, there is an assigned space in the admit card where the photo has to be pasted with glue before handing it over for verification at the XAT exam 2026 test centre.

  • Reach the examination center at least 90 minutes before the exam begins to avoid last minute hassles. Verify the seating arrangements once you reach the exam center. Also verify your documents with the verification personnel.

  • Use the writing pad provided to complete all rough work. The writing pad with all pages intact will have to be returned along with the pen after completion of the examination to the hall invigilator. Do not waste writing pad pages as no extra writing pad will be given to the candidates.

  • Use the washroom beforehand.

  • Do not apply henna or mehndi on the palm side. This instruction is mainly given to girls attempting the examination. This is suggested as applying mehndi on the palm side will cause trouble in capturing the candidate’s biometric fingerprint which is mandatory for entering the examination lab.

  • Do not use the keyboard ever during the examination; this will lock your computer system.

  • Any kind of gadget is prohibited in the exam lab. Do not carry them with you. Otherwise, the examination will be cancelled for the candidate.

XAT 2026 Selection Process

The XAT 2026 selection process begins after the XAT results are declared. Typically, the selection process begins in the month of February. The selection process consists of three rounds, Group Discussion, Written Aptitude Test/Essay Writing, and Personal Interview. Candidates who have successfully passed the cutoff requirements for the XAT exam will be eligible for the selection process. It includes the following stages.

  • Appearance in the XAT 2026 exam.

  • Shortlisting of candidates based on the cutoff requirements of participating institutes.

  • Further selection rounds involve Group Discussion, Written Aptitude Test/Essay Writing, and Personal Interview.

  • Final selection is done on the basis of the candidate's performance in the prior selection rounds and considering other factors.

    • Candidate Profile

    • Previous Academic Scores

    • Work Experience (if applicable)

XAT 2026 Exam Preparation Tips

A good preparation plan should be complemented with XAT 2026 exam preparation tips and guidelines while preparing for XAT. Check out the best tips for XAT preparation mentioned below:

  • Candidates must fully understand the format of the XAT exam 2026, which includes the number of sections, question types, and time constraints. Additionally, candidates ought to allow enough time to acquaint themselves with the syllabus and identify the subjects on which they possess a basic comprehension.
  • Create a thorough study library with excellent study materials, such as XAT books, online resources, and video lectures. Find a mentor to assist you with your studies and look for XAT 2026 coaching centers that offer crash courses.
  • Candidates are required to take four to five mock exams per week in order to improve their practice and comprehension of the questions that will be asked on the XAT exam 2026. Examine your errors and confirm that you comprehend the fundamental ideas after each practice test.
  • Make sure you assess your performance thoroughly after each mock exam so you can identify your areas of weakness and strengthen them. You should also focus on answering questions quickly by identifying the ones that require too much time. 
  • Given the limited time allotted for the XAT exam 2026, consider time management immediately. Set a timer and complete practice questions as if they were part of the actual exam to increase your accuracy and speed.
  • Focus on building a strong conceptual foundation for the reasoning and quantitative sections rather than just memorizing shortcuts. You will be able to respond to a variety of questions if your concepts are clear.
  • Read articles from newspapers, magazines, and the internet to broaden your vocabulary as you prepare for the VARC section. Regular reading will help you become more proficient at reading comprehension.
  • Consider carefully going over and evaluating the main concepts, calculations, and techniques from the last few weeks. Try to limit your revisions to the subjects you know a lot about or are proficient in. Do not pick up new subjects at this time.

FAQs about XAT

Who can apply for XAT 2026?

Students who have completed a three-year bachelor's degree from any recognized institute of higher learning in India are eligible to apply for XAT 2026 by filling out the application form. The final-year students can also apply for the same provided they get their provisional results while the admission process is conducted. 

Is there any limit on the number of XAT attempts?

No, there is no limit on the number of XAT attempts. Students can take the XAT exams as many times as they want with the exam being held once a year mostly in the month of January. The candidates only have to fulfil the eligibility criteria to appear for the XAT exam.

Does any IIM accept XAT score?

No, the IIMs do not accept the XAT scores for admission to its MBA programmes. All the IIMs accept the scores of the CAT examination to conduct its selection process. Candidates will have to score well in the CAT examination to get admission at any IIM.

When will XAT 2026 be conducted?

The XAT 2026 exam will be conducted on January 4, 2026. It is a computer-based online test for MBA and PGDM admissions that will be held in a single session. The admit card for the exams will be released soon, and students must download it as soon as possible.

Who is the conducting body of XAT 2026?

Xavier Labour Relations Institute (XLRI), Jamshedpur is the conducting body of XAT 2026. It is one of the most prestigious and reputed MBA entrance examinations that is accepted by XLRI Jamshedpur and other XLRI institutes. More than 1000 business schools across India accept the XAT scores. 

How many candidates appear for XAT?

Around 90,000 to 1 lakh students appear for XAT every year. Among them, around 75,000 to 80,000 students are expected to take the exam.

Does XLRI accept CAT score?

No, XLRI Jamshedpur and the 10 other XLRI institutes do not accept CAT scores. You will have to appear for the XAT exam instead of the CAT to get admission to XLRI. CAT scores are accepted at only IIMs and other business schools. 

Why is XAT conducted?

XAT is a common admission test conducted for admission in the MBA/ PGDM Program offered at XLRI and other top MBA colleges. The exam is being held for the past 72 years to access the eligibility of the students to study various MBA and PGDM programmes. 

Is XAT tougher than CAT?

Many candidates consider XAT to be a more difficult exam than CAT. The Quantitative Aptitude section of XAT is very difficult to attempt as compared to CAT as per experts as well as students who appear for the exam. XAT also has decision-making and general knowledge sections which require candidates to prepare separately.
