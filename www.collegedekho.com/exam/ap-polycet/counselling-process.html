AP POLYCET Counselling Process 2026

Candidates must check the list of documents required for AP POLYCET counselling. The detailed counselling process for AP POLYCET 2026 explains to the candidates the important steps, which will ensure that the candidates do not get confused about what to do once they are called up to participate:

Step 1: Online Registration

To participate in the AP POLYCET web-based counselling, the candidates have to register themselves for the same. For registration purposes, the candidates have to visit the official AP Polycet website and go through all the instructions before initiating the process of online registration. In this step, the candidates are required to create a password for all future activities. Through the account created by the candidates in this step, the candidates are informed regarding the seat allotment and other important things by the authorities. 

Step 2: Uploading of Certificates

This year, the candidates have to upload the certificates online. The candidates must upload the scanned PDF of the following documents:

  • EWS certificate valid for the year 2026-27 for eligible OC candidates

  • Caste certificate submission for BC/SC/ST candidates 

  • Income certificate 

  • Region of study details(AU, SVU etc)

Step 3: Entry of Options/Option Entry

This is the most important step in the AP Polycet counselling process. In this step, the candidates will have to fill out their choices via the available online method. The candidates will be required to enter their choices in decreasing order of priority. The candidates can also find the previous year's rank list data on the same page which will assist them during the process of choice filling. It is mandatory for the candidates to take a printout of the option filling form after submitting their preferences. 

Step 4: Seat Allotment

The seat allotment process will begin as per the schedule mentioned on the official website of AP POLYCET. Various factors such as the category of the candidates, choices filled by the candidates, and the position of the candidates in the merit list will be taken into consideration by the concerned authorities before they finalise the seat allotment. The candidates can check the seat allotment status of the AP POLYCET 2026 by logging into the student’s account page. After the seat allotment is over, the candidates will be issued with provisional allotment letters which must be downloaded by the candidates for future use. 

Step 5: Reporting & Fee Payment

After the candidates are allotted seats, they have to download the provisional allotment letter within the stipulated time period and have to report to the Institute where they have been allocated seats. The candidates have to, first of all, report online and then physically report at the Institute allocated to them. The candidates must be aware of the fact that they need to report both online and physically at the allocated Institute, failing to do so will lead to the cancellation of the seat allocated to them.

How to Download AP POLYCET 2026 Provisional Allotment Letter

Here are the steps that the candidates must follow in order to download the AP Polycet 2026 provisional allotment letter:

  • The Provisional Seat Allotment letter for AP Polycet 2026 will be updated on the official AP Polycet website.

  • The candidates have to enter the valid details on the student's login page such as Hall Ticket number, ICR Form Number, Date of Birth and Password and press the Submit button.

  • The Provisional Allotment Letter will be displayed on the computer screen.

  • The candidates have to download the same and carry a coloured printout of the same for the purpose of admission.

AP POLYCET Spot Round Seat Allotment Process 2026

The spot round seat allotment process will be conducted by the authorities if a certain number of seats remain to be filled after the Web-Based counselling rounds conclude. The candidates must fulfil the following eligibility criteria in order to be considered for the AP POLYCET 2026 spot round process:

  • Should have passed the AP POLYCET 2026 entrance exam 

  • Should not have joined any Polytechnic Institute via the Convenor AP Polycet Admissions

  • Did not participate in the process of documents verification

  • Did not qualify the AP POLYCET 2026 exam

  • Did not appear in the AP POLYCET 2026 entrance exam

Process to Surrender/Cancel the Allocated AP POLYCET Seats

Candidates can undertake these steps if he/she wishes to surrender or cancel the allotted seat: 

  • Step 1: Check for dates of surrender on the AP POLYCET official website
  • Step 2: Log in to the application form and click on the “Surrender Tab”
  • Step 3: Submit the surrender form in online mode and take a printout of the same

AP POLYCET Seat Allotment 2026

AP POLYCET seat allotment 2026 will be released across multiple rounds via online mode at appolycet.nic.in. Candidates will be allotted seats across the AP POLYCET participating colleges 2026 based upon their AP POLYCET 2026 rank, caste category, choices filled by them and the availability of seats at the respective institutes. Candidates accepting the seats allotted to them will have to self-report online and finally report to the allotted institute to complete the admission procedure. Candidates will also be required to submit the allotment fee of INR 800/- in order to get their seat allotment letter.

FAQs about AP POLYCET Counselling Process

How to select a polytechnic college?

To select a college through AP Polycet, candidates must go through the detailed information related to the college, like infrastructure, libraries, facilities, transportation, etc. Therefore, candidates are advised to fill out the choice filling accordingly to get the best college based on the secured rank.

What is a good rank in AP POLYCET?

A rank between 5000 and 20000 is considered a good rank, whereas a rank between 1 to 5000 is considered an excellent rank in AP Polycet. 

Where can I register for the AP Polycet counselling?

The registration for the AP Polycet counselling process can be done on the official website: polycetap.nic.in

What is the counselling registration fee for AP Polycet?

AP Polycet counselling for the unreserved/general/OBC category is INR 900 and INR 500 for the SC/ST category. 

How do I pay my AP POLYCET Counselling fee?

Candidates can pay the AP POLYCET 2025 counselling fee from the official website of SBTET - appolycet.nic.in and click on 'Pay Fee Online'. Further, pay the AP POLYCET 2025 Hall ticket number and rank and pay the fee through net banking/credit card/debit card.
