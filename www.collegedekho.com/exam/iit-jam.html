What is IIT JAM Exam 2026?

IIT JAM exam 2026 is a national-level entrance exam administered annually by various IITs. The Joint Admission Test for M.Sc. (JAM) is a rotational examination administered throughout India by the IITs and IISc. Premier institutes across the country use IIT JAM exam score to admit students to various postgraduate programmes such as MSc, MSc (Tech), MS (Research), MSc - M.Tech, Dual Degree, Joint MSc - PhD, and MSc - PhD Dual Degree across multiple disciplines.

IIT JAM 2026 Application Form

IIT JAM 2026 Application form was released by IIT Bombay on September 5, 2025. To fill out the IIT JAM online application form, the students have to register on the JAM Online Application Processing System (JOAPS). The last date to register for the IIT JAM 2026 exam is October 12, 2025.

How to Apply for IIT JAM 2026

 IIT JAM application form is divided into four sections-  i) Registration on the JOAPS (JAM Online Application Processing System),  ii) Filling Out the IIT Application Form, iii) Documents Upload, and iv) Application Payment. Check the steps to fill out the IIT JAM online application form below:

Step 1: Register on JOAPS @jam2026.iitb.ac.in 

The first step to filling out the IIT JAM application form is registration on the JOAPS (JAM Online Application Processing System). Candidates need to register themselves on the JOAPS website online. The steps to make the registration are given below:

  • Go to the official website- jam2026.iitb.ac.in
  • Click on the JOAPS link available on the screen or Click on the candidate portal available on  the screen 
  • The registration form will appear on the screen 
  • Fill out the personal details like name, email address, mobile number
  • Create a password for the candidate’s login 
  • Confirm the password 
  • Answer the arithmetic equation as CAPTCHA code
  • Click on the ‘check-in’ box
  • Click on the ‘Submit’ button
  • Verify the mobile number and email ID and move forward with the IIT JAM application form 

Step 2: Fill out the IIT JAM 2026 Application Form

The second step to filling out the IIT JAM application form is to fill in the details in the application form. Check the details in the IIT JAM application form below:

  • Fill out the exam details in the application form like the name of the test paper(s). Candidates who want to appear for the two test papers must ensure that both papers are conducted in two different shifts. 
  • Select the test centres as per the order of preference in the second step in the application form
  • Fill out the personal details in the next step like the father’s name, mother’s name, gender, category, date of birth, etc. The candidate's name, mobile number, and email ID will be fetched from the registration form
  • Fill in the correspondence and permanent address
  • In the next step, the candidate has to fill out the educational details like name of the qualifying degree, year of passing, marks obtained, etc

Step 3: Upload Documents to IIT JAM Application Form 2026

The candidates have to upload the photograph, signature, and tenth mark sheet PDF as documents in the IIT JAM form 2026. The steps to upload documents in the IIT JAM application form are given below:

  • Go to the official website: jam2026.iitb.ac.in
  • Click on the ‘Apply Online’ button 
  • Log in to the existing account or create the account for filling out the IIT JAM application form 
  • Move forward to the ‘Upload Document’ tab 

  • Click on the photograph/signature/tenth mark sheet tab
  • Browse the photograph/signature/tenth mark sheet on the computer 
  • Select the photograph/signature/mark sheet
  • Click on the Open button (if the specifications are correct according to the prescribed format)
  • The photograph/signature/mark sheet will be uploaded to the IIT JAM 2026 application form

Step 4: Pay the IIT JAM 2026 Application Fee

In the last stage of the IIT JAM application process, the candidates have to pay the application fees for the IIT JAM exam.

  • Pay the IIT JAM 2026 registration fees by using any payment mode like- UPI, internet banking, credit card, debit card, etc.

IIT JAM 2026 eligibility criteria are listed below by category:

Categories

Common Eligibility Criteria

General/OBC

Applicants have to get a 55% aggregate score on the qualifying exam, or 5.5 out of 10 CGPA.

If a student gets the promotion during the qualifying exam without providing a mark sheet, they must present the promotion certificate or another document proving their promotion, which should include the subjects they took and their marks for the semester or year.

To validate the minimum educational requirements (MEQ), the promotion certificate or document must bear the signature of the institute's head.

SC/ST/PWD

Students must pass the qualifying exam with a minimum of 50% or a CGPA of 5 out of 10.

If an applicant is promoted in the qualifying exam without a mark sheet, he or she must have a promotion certificate or another certificate or document as proof of promotion, which must include the names of the subjects taken and the marks earned in those subjects during the semester/year.

To be considered for the minimum educational requirements (MEQ), the promotion certificate or document must be signed by the institute's head.

FAQs about IIT JAM

When will IIT JAM exam be conducted in 2026?

IIT JAM 2026 will be conducted on February 15, 2026. This is mentioned in the unofficial schedule released by IIT Bombay on its official website. 

Which IIT will conduct IIT JAM 2026?

IIT JAM 2026 will be conducted by IIT Bombay. The organizing IIT has announced the unofficial notification.

Does IIT JAM happen twice a year?

No, IIT JAM is conducted only once a year. It is usually held in the month of February.

When to apply for IIT JAM 2026?

The applications for IIT JAM 2026 are expected to start from September 05, 2025, onwards. This is just according to a tentative schedule. The exact date will be given in the official advertisement.

What is the IIT JAM 2026 age limit?

There is no maximum age limit for applying to IIT JAM. The only requirement is that the candidate hold an undergraduate degree in their field of study. However, IITs participating in JAM may have age-specific admission requirements.

What is the fee for IIT JAM 2026?

Can I apply for multiple subjects in IIT JAM?

Yes, you can apply for up to two test papers, but they must be in separate sessions.

Is there a specific eligibility criteria for IIT JAM 2026?

Yes, the eligibility criteria vary based on the chosen subject. Generally, candidates need a bachelor's degree in a relevant field or a related discipline.

What subjects are offered at IIT JAM 2026?

IIT JAM offers a range of subjects like Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biotechnology, Geology, and more for postgraduate admissions.

Is there any negative marking in the IIT JAM 2026 exam?

Yes, there is a provision for negative marking in the IIT JAM 2026 exam. For the multiple-choice questions (MCQs), an incorrect answer will lead to a deduction of 1/3 mark, and for the multiple-select questions (MSQs), there will be no negative marking. However, there is no negative marking for the numerical answer type (NAT) questions.

What is the IIT JAM 2026 exam pattern?

According to the IIT JAM exam pattern, the exam is a computer-based test (CBT). The duration of the exam is 3 hours with multiple-choice questions (MCQ), multiple-select questions (MSQ), and numerical answer type (NAT) questions. 

Is a biological science subject included in IIT JAM?

The Biological Science paper has been discontinued from 2020 in IIT JAM.

How many papers are there in the IIT JAM entrance exam?

IIT JAM is conducted for biotechnology, chemistry, geology, mathematics, mathematical statistics and physics.

What is IIT JAM?

IIT JAM is a national level entrance exam conducted for admission in M.Sc, Joint M.Sc.-Ph.D., M.Sc.-Ph.D. Dual Degree and other Post-Bachelor's Degree programmes in IITs and Integrated PhD programmes at IISc.
