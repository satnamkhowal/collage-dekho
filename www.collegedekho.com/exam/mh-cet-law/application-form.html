MH CET Law 2026 Application Form

Maharashtra CET Cell has issued a notice regarding the release of the "Exam City Intimation Slip PDF" before the final hall tickets. The MH CET 2026 official notification was announced on November 21, 2025, and the application form was released on January 8, 2026, for the 3-year LLB course. The deadline to submit the 3-year LLB application form was March 3, 2026 (closed). MHT CET Cell has now opened the application form correction window from March 9 to 11, 2026.

For the 5-year LLB course, the application form was released on January 9, 2026, and the deadline for applicants to submit the form has been extended till March 31, 2026. The correction window for the 5-year LLB will be declared after the registration process. 

Maharashtra state CET cell issues the MH CET Law 2026 'Exam City Intimation Slip' on March 17, 2026, as advance information to you for making a travel plan before you appear for the exam. This intimation specifically informs you of the city in which your examination centre will be located. This slip is not your final admit card. The final official hall ticket will have details of your exam venue and will be available in your candidate login around three to four days before your examination date. You are advised to download the intimation slip at the earliest to confirm your allotted district and keep visiting the official website.

You are advised to check the MH CET Law 2026 eligibility criteria before filling out the online form. The main stages of the MH CET Law 2026 application process include registering for the entrance examination, filling out the online form, fee payment, form submission and taking its printout.

Applicants are advised to keep a copy of the submitted form for any further reference. Additionally, those who seek to make any corrections to the MH CET Law 2026 application form can do so during the form editing window. Individuals will be allowed to correct information in fields like name, date of birth, photograph, signature, gender, etc.

Step by Step Application Process of MH CET Law 2026

Here is the step-by-step application process for MH CET Law 2026 -

Step 1: Registration

To start applying for MH CET Law, you will first be required to visit the official website of the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra and click on the “New Registration” tab. After that, you will have to enter their details such as their Name, email ID, Mobile number and other contact details. You will be sent a provisional registration number, which they will have to save and keep for future reference. Afterwards, you will be sent their Application number as well as Password to the email ID and registered Mobile number.

Step 2: Filling out the MH CET Law Application Form

The next step involves filling out the MH CET Law application form, which requires providing various details like their father’s / husband’s name, correspondence address, as well as educational details such as certificates/mark sheets for SSC / HSC / Convocation / Degree or Equivalent degree.

Applicants should go to https://cetcell.mahacet.org/ to fill out the online application form. They should select the option of "Apply Online," which will take them to a new screen to fill out their form - 

  • The applicants must go to the "Click here for New Registration" link and provide their name, contact information, and email address to register an application. The system will generate a Provisional Registration Number and Password and display them on the screen.
  • They should write down and save their Provisional Registration Number and Password in a notebook.
  • The applicants will also get an email and SMS with the Provisional Registration Number and Password.
  • If the applicants cannot finish the application form in one sitting, they can store the information by selecting the "Save and Next" tab.
  • Before submitting the online application, applicants are urged to use the "Save and Next" feature to verify and edit the details in the online application form.
  • Applicants are recommended to carefully fill out and verify the information provided in the online application, as no changes will be permitted or entertained after pressing the Final Submission button.
  • The names of the applicants and their guardians should be input as it is on their SSC, HSC, or equivalent certificates.
  • By clicking the 'Validate Your Details' and 'Save & Next' buttons, applicants can validate their filled-in details and save their filled-in application.

Step 3: Upload of Photograph & Signature

FAQs about MH CET LAW Application Form

What details are required in the MH CET Law application form?

Applicants must provide personal information such as name, date of birth, category, domicile status, contact details, educational qualifications, and preferred examination centre. All details must match official documents to avoid rejection during verification.

How can candidates fill the MH CET Law application form?

Candidates can fill the application form online by visiting the official CET Cell website. The process includes registration using a valid email ID and mobile number, filling personal and academic details, uploading scanned documents, and completing the payment process.

Is the MH CET Law application form common for both 3-Year and 5-Year LL.B?

No, separate application forms are released for MH CET Law 3-Year LL.B and MH CET Law 5-Year LL.B. Candidates must apply separately if they wish to appear for both examinations and pay the respective application fees.

What are the eligibility criteria for filling the MH CET Law application form?

For the 5-Year LL.B course, candidates must have passed or be appearing in Class 12 or equivalent examination. For the 3-Year LL.B course, candidates must hold a bachelor’s degree in any discipline. Minimum qualifying marks vary by category and must be carefully checked before applying.

Who can apply through the MH CET Law application form?

Any candidate who meets the eligibility criteria specified by the CET Cell can apply. This includes candidates from Maharashtra as well as outside the state, subject to reservation rules. Eligibility differs for 3-Year and 5-Year LL.B courses, particularly with respect to educational qualification and minimum marks.
