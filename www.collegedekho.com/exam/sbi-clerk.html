SBI Clerk Exam Application Process 2025

The SBI Clerk 2025 application form was released by the State Bank of India on August 6, 2025. Students can fill out the SBI Clerk registration form 2025 till August 26, 2025. SBI Clerk 2025 Language Proficiency Test Exam Pattern

The SBI Language Proficiency Test is the last stage in the SBI Clerk examination. Students who clear the SBI Mains examination get to sit for the Language Proficiency Test. However, this test is not compulsory for all applicants. Only those applicants who are unable to provide evidence of their language proficiency need to appear for this test. However, if you can prove your proficiency in the said local language of the state you are applying from, then you need not sit for this test. A student’s Class 10th and 12th marksheets or passing certificates will act as proofs in this case.

SBI Clerk 2025 Preparation Plan

SBI Clerk is gaining the name of one of the most competitive banking exams in India. Lakhs of applicants prepare for the SBI Clerk exam every year and only a few thousands get the opportunity to become a banker in India's most reputed Public Sector Bank. It is important for the aspirants to prepare for the SBI Clerk 2025 exam with full devotion to ace the exam. 

We have put our sincere efforts into presenting before you a credible Preparation plan for the different stages of SBI Clerk 2025. Please have a look and benefited from the SBI Clerk 2025 Preparation Plan.

Sections To Be Covered For SBI Clerk 2025

  1. English Language
  2. Quantitative Aptitude
  3. Reasoning Ability
  4. General & Financial Knowledge

Preparation Strategy for English Language 

Go through the following preparation strategy for English Language to score well in this section:

  • The aspirants with a strong English base can score high easily in this section.
  • Make a habit of reading English Newspapers every day and take a full-length mock test.
  • Self-assess the mock tests, so that you can figure out the strong and weak areas and can score well. 
  • Study Wren and Martin’s grammar book thoroughly to strength your basic Grammar skill.
  • It is not possible to build Vocabulary overnight, rather it will take a period of time to make strong your Vocabulary skills. To improve their Vocabulary skill, the aspirants should read Newspapers, magazines, advertisements, or novels. It will in turn help you to make a sentence using idioms and phrases.
  • If you come across any new word, search for the meaning of that word right away and learn it.

    Preparation Strategy for Quantitative Aptitude

    If your concepts are clear and have strong calculation skills, then the section on Numerical Ability can turn out to be one of the most scoring sections for you. Go through the following preparation tips for the Numerical Ability section:

    • Work on accuracy and speed, so that the candidates can complete the section very fast and get a high score.
    • First, clear your basic concepts and then attempt the questions of those topics, which you have learned. 
    • Take 3 days time to prepare each topic and solve maximum questions on the topic. 
    • The aspirants should appear for a mock test daily. First, identify the sections, in which you are weak and try to attempt those sections. 
    • Keep 3-4 hours daily for Numerical Ability preparation. 
    • Time management is the basic aspect of the SBI clerk exam. Appearing for the mock test will assist the candidates to prepare themselves for timing strategy.
    • Practice previous year's papers to know what type of questions are asked in the exam.

    Preparation Strategy for Reasoning Ability

    You can easily crack this section if you clarity of concepts related to this section. You will be given evaluated in this area based on your logical and reasoning skills. Go through the following section to know about the preparation strategy of Reasoning Ability:

    • Most of the questions of the Reasoning Ability section cover the Seating Arrangement/ Puzzles solving. Hence, you should have a good command of the Seating Arrangement and Puzzles solving.
    • There is no trick to solving the Reasoning section, hence, practice is the only way to get a good score in the Reasoning Ability section. 
    • You should also practice the miscellaneous questions such as Direction Sense, Coding-Decoding, Ranking and Order, Data Sufficiency, Input-Output, Syllogisms, Assumptions, Effect, and Cause, Conclusions, etc. If you are not confident in solving the Puzzles and Arrangements, then you should focus on solving the Miscellaneous part.
    • For the Syllogisms section, you should practice the traditional Venn Diagram method or the A, E, I, O rules process. 
    • For the Computer Aptitude section, you should not practice more on Computer Awareness. Instead, this section will include sections like logic, diagrams, and flowcharts.

    Preparation Strategy for General & Financial Knowledge

    General & Financial Knowledge is only asked in SBI Clerk Mains exam. This section is all about the general and economic sense of a person. The questions asked are related to the recent developments in the banking sector, questions related to banking Policies, questions related to RBI, etc. The given below points will help the aspirants out to prepare and ace the  General & Financial Knowledge section.

    • You are strictly advised to read the newspaper daily and to have an eye on the Economic section of the newspaper.
    • A lot of Banking coaches release the GA capsules and magazines. You can follow any of such magazine and prepare for the banking awareness.
    • We all know that the more you study the general knowledge the less it feels to be understood. The fact that GK is endless may persist but you are advised to study GK from reference books to build up your general concepts.

      FAQs about SBI Clerk

      What is the duration of the SBI Clerk exam?

      The duration of the SBI Prelims exam is 1 hour, and the SBI Mains exam is 2 hours and 40 minutes long. Both these exams will be held in online mode.

      Does the SBI Clerk exam have an interview round?

      No, there is no interview round for the SBI Clerk exam.

      What is the last date to register for the SBI Clerk exam in 2025?

      The last date to register for the SBI Clerk 2025 exam will be released shortly by the State Bank of India.

      What is the application form fee for the SBI Clerk exam in 2025?

      The application form fee for the SBI Clerk exam in 2025 is INR 750 for applicants belonging to the General and OBC categories. Applicants belonging to other categories are not required to pay any amount for registration.

      How often is the SBI Clerk exam conducted?

      The State Bank of India conducts the SBI Clerk examination on an annual basis.

      What is the age limit for the SBI Clerk exam?

      The minimum age limit for the SBI Clerk exam is 20 years and the maximum age limit is 28 years for the unreserved category. Age relaxation is provided to the reserved category as per the rules.

      Is there any negative marking in SBI Clerk exam?

      Yes, there is a negative marking in SBI Clerk exam. 1/4th marks are deducted for wrong answers. 

      Can Class 12 pass candidates appear in SBI Clerk?

      No, candidates after Class 12 are not eligible to apply for the SBI Clerk exam. Graduation in any discipline is compulsory to apply for the exam.

      Is there any interview in SBI Clerk?

      No, there is no interview in SBI Clerk. Candidates are selected based on the mains exam subject to their qualifying the local language test.

      What is the SBI Clerk job?

      SBI Clerk job profile includes documentation and back-office work such as balance tallying, data entry, and more. The job also entails marketing financial products such as processing loans, schemes, deposits, funds to potential customers.

      Is SBI Clerk is a Gazzeted post?

      No, SBI Clerk is a non-gazzated clerical job.
