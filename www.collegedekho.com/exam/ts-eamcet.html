Nationality

Students for the TS EAMCET 2026 exam must hold an Indian National/ Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) / Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Card.

OR

  • Students must have completed or appeared in the final year of the Engineering Diploma examination conducted by the State Board of Technical Education and Training, Telangana / Andhra Pradesh, or any other examination deemed equivalent by the Board with a minimum of 45% marks (40% for reserved category students).

  • For B.Tech. (Agricultural Engineering /Dairy Technology /Food Science and Technology) and B.Sc. courses, students should be at least 17 years old by December 31 of the admission year, with an upper age limit of 22 years for all students and 25 years for SC/ST students.

How to Fill Out the TS EAMCET Application Form 2026?

The detailed step-by-step process to fill out the TS EAMCET application form is provided here. You must carefully complete the TS EAMCET 2026 registration to ensure you are successfully registered for the exam with the right subjects. You can follow the instructions given below for TS EAMCET 2026 registration:

Step 1: Fee Payment

The first step of TS EAMCET registration is fee payment. Visit the TS EAMCET official website and click on the fee payment link. You can pay the TS EAMCET application fees through TS Online, AP Online, E-Seva, MEE-Seva, or Debit or Credit Card.

Step 2: Registration and Form Filling

After completing the fee payment, students can proceed with the TS EAMCET registrations. During the TS EAMCET 2026 registration process, students must provide their personal details, qualification details, category, and local area status. 

  • Enter your personal information, including name, gender, date of birth, nationality, category, and contact details. Students must ensure to provide authentic information matching the details on their supporting documents.
  • Provide details about your educational qualifications, including the examination you have passed or are appearing for, the year of passing, the name of the board or university, and any other relevant information.
  • From the list of available options, select your preferred exam centre. Students should ideally pick the test centre nearest to their location to avoid hassles on the exam day. 

Step 3: Upload necessary supporting documents

Upload the important documents such as your photograph, signature, and other required certificates as specified by the conducting authority. These include the marksheet of the qualifying examination, certificates, and a scanned copy of your photograph and signature. 

Step 4: Review and submit the application form

Before submitting the TS EAMCET 2026 application form, thoroughly review all the information provided. It is crucial to ensure that there are no errors or inconsistencies. Once you have confirmed the accuracy of the details, proceed with submitting the application form.

Step 5: Print a copy of the application form

Upon successful submission of the form, students must take a printout of the application form for the record. Additionally, they should retain a copy of both the completed application form for the TS EAMCET exam and the payment receipt for any potential future communication.

FAQs about TS EAMCET

Can I write eamcet again next year?

Yes, you can write the TS EAMCET exam next year, if you fail in your first attempt. 

How many times TS EAMCET is conducted in a year?

The TS EAMCET exam is conducted only once a year by JNTU Hyderabad for admission into various professional courses offered in Telangana University / Private Colleges. 

Which books are best for EAMCET preparation 2026?

Some of the best books for TS EAMCET preparation 2026 are NCERT books of Class 11th and 12th, EAMCET Physics (Andhra Pradesh and Telangana) by Arihant Experts, Fundamentals of Inorganic Chemistry by Ananya Ganguly, Problems Plus In IIT Mathematics by A. Dasgupta, etc.

How to apply for TS EAMCET 2026?

To apply for TS EAMCET 2026 visit the official website at eapcet.tsche.ac.in and click on the fee payment link. First, complete the fee payment then proceed with the registration. Provide the required details and upload the list of documents. Then submit the application form.

What is the TS EAMCET exam date 2026?

The TS EAMCET 2026 exam dates are May 9, May 10, and May 11, 2026.

When will the TS EAMCET application form 2026 released?

The TS EAMCET 2026 application form will be released in February 2026. 

Is EAMCET harder than jee?

No, TS EAMCET is not harder than JEE Main. The overall difficulty level of the JEE Main is higher as many students participate which raises the cutoff marks. However, scoring the TS EAMCET cutoff is not easy. In 2024, the TS EAMCET pass percentage was 74.98358394%, which indicates that many students failed also. You should not take the TS EAMCET exam lightly and study well to score the cutoff marks. 

Does EAMCET have minus marks?

No, there will be negative markings in the TS EAMCET 2026 exam for incorrect answers.

What is the syllabus of EAMCET 2026?

The TS EAMCET syllabus 2026 for Engineering includes Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics subject. The TS EAMCET 2026 syllabus of Agriculture includes Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and, Zoology. 

What is the official website of TS EAMCET 2026?

The official website of TS EAMCET 2026 is eamcet.tsche.ac.in.

How to prepare for EAMCET 2026?

To prepare for TS EAMCET 2026 begin your preparation early. Study the complete TS EAMCET syllabus, do a timely revision, solve mock tests, previous year papers, sample papers, etc.

What is the exam pattern of EAMCET 2026?

The TS EAMCET 2026 exam will be held in CBT mode. According to the TS EAMCET exam pattern 2026, the question paper will include 160 objective-type questions in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry. TS EAMCET 2026 test will last three hours.

Will IPE marks be added in TS EAMCET 2026?

Only the group subject marks, i.e., Physics, Mathematics/Botany/Zoology, and Chemistry, will be considered while determining the rank, with 75% weightage given to the TS EAMCET 2026 score and 25% given to the IPE score.

Is TS EAMCET rank valid for Deemed Universities in Telangana?

TS EAMCET rank is valid for admission in B.Tech and B.Pharma courses offered by deemed universities in Telangana.

Is TS EAMCET rank valid for B.F.Sc admission in Telangana?

Students aspiring for B.F.Sc admission in Telangana must appear for TS EAMCET exam. 

Is TS EAMCET rank valid for B.Sc Agriculture admission in Telangana?

TS EAMCET score or rank is valid for B.Sc Agriculture admission in Telangana. Student must clear this exam for admission in UG agriculture courses.
