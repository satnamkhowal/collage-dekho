What is CMAT?

CMAT full form is Common Management Admission Test. It is a national-level MBA entrance exam conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) in online computer-based test mode for 3 hours. The syllabus, crafted by NTA with AICTE's input, covers Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation, Logical Reasoning, Language Comprehension, General Awareness, and Innovation & Entrepreneurship. Each section comprises 20 questions, totaling 400 marks. Correct answers earn 4 marks, with a 1-mark penalty for incorrect ones, while unanswered questions don't carry negative marks. 

Over 1,300 AICTE-approved MBA institutions accept CMAT scores, making it one of India's most popular MBA entrance exams after CAT and XAT. Around 70,000 candidates take the test annually. CMAT is known for its relatively lower difficulty than CAT and XAT, making it a preferred choice among management aspirants. Qualified candidates can apply to MBA colleges in India, including GIM Goa, BIMTECH, Great Lakes Institute of Management, K J Somaiya Mumbai, PUMBA, and Welingkar Mumbai.

CMAT 2026 Eligibility Criteria

The key eligibility criteria pointers for CMAT 2026 are as follows:

  • You must be a citizen of India.
  • You must hold a Graduation degree or an equivalent degree in any discipline from a recognised university. NTA doesn't mention any minimum percentage to apply for CMAT. 
  • Students who are currently in their final year of bachelor's degrees and whose results will be declared before the commencement of admission for the academic year 2026-27 are also eligible to apply for CMAT 2026.
  • There is no upper age limit to apply for the CMAT exam. However, the minimum age to apply for the exam is 20 years.

If an applicant doesn't fulfil the CMAT 2026 eligibility criteria, they will not be able to fill out the application form and appear for the exam.

CMAT 2026 Application & Registration Process

CMAT registration includes two steps: applicant registration and completing the application form. Check the major steps for filling out the CMAT form.

  • Step 1: Begin by creating a login on the official website of CMAT 2026 @cmat.nta.nic.in / www.nta.ac.in. Click on the "New User" button and provide the necessary details like your name, email ID, and mobile number to establish your login credentials. Once the registration is completed, the Application Number will automatically be generated.
  • Step 2: With your login or registration credentials, proceed to fill out the CMAT application form, which includes sections for personal information, academic history, and work experience if applicable. Ensure all fields are accurately completed and upload the required documents.
  • Step 3: Following form completion, upload the required scanned image of a recent photograph and necessary documents like the candidate's signature and PwD certificate, adhering to the guidelines regarding size, format, and resolution provided on the website.
  • Step 4: Complete the registration by paying the CMAT fee, which can be done online using debit/credit cards, net banking, or UPI. The fee is anticipated to be approximately INR 2,500 for General/ EWS/ OBC-NCL (Male) and INR 1,250 for Females, Third Gender and Reserved category students.
  • Step 5: Upon finishing the registration and payment, review the CMAT 2026 registration summary, saving the filled form for future reference. It's advisable to print a copy of the form and keep it safe until after the exam.
  • Step 6: After submission, aspirants get the opportunity to edit the CMAT form once the window opens. Make sure to do the necessary corrections within the prescribed timeline. Please note that you are not allowed to modify the entire application, but only selective fields of the form.

CMAT Exam Day Guidelines

If you're taking the CMAT exam in 2026, it's important to know what to do on the day of the exam to avoid any issues. Here are some important CMAT exam day guidelines to keep in mind:

  • Print out your CMAT 2026 admit card twice. You can't use a digital or scanned copy as proof.
  • You'll need to bring a secondary photo ID along with your CMAT 2026 admit card. You can use any of the following documents for ID: Aadhaar card, PAN card, Voter ID card, Passport, Driver's license, or College/University ID card.
  • Arrive at the CMAT exam centre at the reporting time listed on your admit card. The gates will close 15 minutes before the exam starts, so don't be late. 
  • Don't bring any electronic items with you, including phones, watches, calculators, or iPads. It's also recommended that you don't bring any valuables.
  • Bring a black or blue ballpoint pen to sign the attendance sheet, as well as two passport-sized photos to attach to the admit card and attendance sheet.
  • You can't bring bags or any other items into the exam centre besides the documents and a pen. There may not be any place to store your bags.

How to Prepare for CMAT 2026?

When preparing for CMAT, it's essential to have a strong foundation of the important concepts from the syllabus to crack the exam. Focus on building a strong foundation of the important concepts, take as many mock tests and practice the previous year's question papers to boost your chances of success. Here’s what you need to do for a foolproof CMAT 2026 preparation.

Cover the CMAT Syllabus

To ensure a comprehensive understanding of the subject, it is important to cover the entire syllabus thoroughly. This involves carefully going through every topic and sub-topic outlined in the syllabus to gain a complete grasp of the material. By meticulously studying each section of the syllabus, you can feel confident in your ability to excel in the subject and achieve your academic goals. CMAT 2026 Answer Key

The question-wise official answers and options selected by you will be provided in the CMAT answer key and response sheet. The provisional CMAT answer key is made available on the official website 3-4 days after the exam, and the final CMAT answer key is released along with the result. If you feel it has a discrepancy, you can challenge the CMAT answer key by filing objections against it as per the timeline provided.

To challenge every question, you will have to pay the specified fee. A panel of experts are responsible for examining the objections received, and if any of the objections are found to be valid, the provisional CMAT answer key is revised. After the analysis of objections and revision of the provisional answer key, NTA will release the final answer key. 

Follow the steps below to access the CMAT answer key.

  • Visit the official website (exams.nta.ac.in/CMAT).
  • Click on the 'Display Question Papers and Responses' link. 
  • Enter your Application No., Date of Birth or Password, and Security Pin.
  • Click on 'Sign In'.
  • The CMAT answer key is displayed on the screen.
  • Take a printout of the answer key for future reference.

The scorecard has a validity of one year and contains a candidate’s personal details, overall & sectional score/ percentile, qualifying status, rank, and more. Test-takers will be able to download the CMAT result 2026 by following these steps.

  • Visit the CMAT official website i.e. exams.nta.ac.in/CMAT.
  • Click on the ' View CMAT Result 2026' link available on the home page.
  • Log in by entering the Application Number and Password or Date of Birth.
  • After that, click on the 'Submit' Tab.
  • The CMAT scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
  • Download and save the CMAT scorecard 2026.
  • Take a few printouts for further reference.

FAQs about CMAT

What is CMAT exam date 2026?

The CMAT 2026 exam will be conducted in the last week of January 2026 in two shifts:

  • Shift 1: 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM
  • Shift 2: 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM.

The exam will be held in a computer-based test (CBT) mode.

What is the maximum age limit for CMAT?

There is no age limit to apply for the Common Management Admission Test (CMAT). Candidates who have completed their graduation or are in the final year of their degree course can apply for the CMAT exam.

Will CMAT 2026 be conducted online or offline?

The CMAT 2026 exam will be conducted in the last week of January 2026 in computer-based mode at specified testing centres. Candidates must punch in their answers using the given computer system's keypad and mouse. As specified on the CMAT application form, candidates are assigned an examination centre in the city of their choice.

What is the maximum number of attempts in CMAT 2026?

There is no limit on the maximum number of CMAT attempts. Candidates can appear for the exam any number of times as long as they are eligible. It must be noted, however, that only your latest attempt will be counted when applying for admission.

Do government MBA colleges in India accept CMAT?

Yes, government MBA colleges in India accept CMAT. Some of the top government MBA colleges accepting CMAT scores include IIITM Gwalior, MNIT Jaipur, NIT Rourkela, PUMBA Pune, University School of Management Studies, GGS IP University, Delhi, SIMSREE Mumbai, Jammu University, etc.

Do IIMs accept CMAT scores?

CMAT scores are not accepted by the IIMs (Indian Institute of Management). Admission to IIMs can only be taken through the CAT entrance exam. Therefore, if you want to get admission in IIMs better appear for CAT exam

What is the CMAT 2026 registration date?

The CMAT 2026 registrations will commence from the second week of November 2025 on the official website. The last date to fill out the CMAT 2026 application form is in the last week of December 2025. 

How many times is the CMAT exam conducted?

The CMAT exam is held once a year, in April or May. The CMAT is organized in various cities across India at different exam centres. You can select the CMAT test city based on your preferences and convenience.

Is it mandatory to take the CMAT exam for MBA admissions?

No, the CMAT is not mandatory for admission to every MBA college in India. However, if you want to gain admission at AICTE-approved institutes, you must sit the CMAT exam for admission to Management programmes. You can also take alternative MBA admission examinations that your college accepts. Some colleges even provide direct admission without requiring an entrance exam.

Who is the conducting body of CMAT?

CMAT is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). The CMAT is a three-hour computer-based online test that assesses candidates' abilities in areas such as quantitative techniques, logical reasoning, language comprehension, general awareness, and innovation and entrepreneurship.

What is CMAT Exam?

The Common Management Admission Test (CMAT) is a National Level Entrance Examination for admission to the country's Management Programme(s). This Test enables AICTE-affiliated participating institutions to choose suitable graduate candidates for admission to Management Courses. Every year, approximately 70,000 kids participate. Some of India's leading business schools accept CMAT scores, including SIMSREE, Great Lakes Institute of Management, JBIMS, K J Somaiya Mumbai, Welingkar Mumbai, and PUMBA.
