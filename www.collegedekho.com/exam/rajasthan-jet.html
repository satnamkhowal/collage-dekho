What is Rajasthan JET Exam?

Rajasthan JET is conducted every year for admission to the BSc Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, Food Nutrition & Dietetics, Community Science/ Home Science, B.F.Sc, B.Tech Dairy Technology, and B.Tech Food Technology courses. JET is administered by one of the participating Agriculture universities, and all colleges affiliated with the universities accept the Rajasthan JET exam score for admission to the previously mentioned courses. In 2026, the Rajasthan JET will be conducted by Swami Keshwanand Rajasthan Agricultural University (SKRAU), Bikaner. JET Agriculture is another name for the Rajasthan Joint Entrance Test. Candidates from other states may also apply for the examination and submit their application before the deadline for the JET form date 2026. Such candidates, however, are only permitted to enrol in private universities. Rajasthan JET 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Before applying for the Rajasthan JET 2026 exam, candidates should confirm that they meet the prerequisite criteria. They must fulfil the following Rajasthan JET 2026 eligibility criteria before beginning the registration process.

  • All students who are permanent residents of Rajasthan are eligible to take the Rajasthan JET test in 2026. 

  • Applicants in the OBC-NCL, SC, ST, and reserved categories will receive 5% of the qualifying score waiver.

  • Applicants who have lived in the state for more than three years are eligible to fill out the Rajasthan JET 2026 admission exam form.

  • Aspirants must be at least 12th-grade graduates of the Rajasthan Board.

  • Candidates in the general category must have at least 50% of their class 12 grades. The minimum cumulative score for candidates of the reserved group is 45%. 

  • Candidates must be under 25 years old as of January 1, 2026, to take the Rajasthan JET examination.

  • Students must have finished courses in biology, agriculture, chemistry, mathematics, and physics in their prior exam to sit for the Rajasthan JET 2026 exam.
