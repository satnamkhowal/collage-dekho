WBJEE Cutoff 2026

West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board releases the WBJEE cutoff 2026 following the counselling process. The board will soon release the WBJEE cut off 2026 dates. The WBJEE round 1 cutoff 2026 is expected to be released in August 2026. The WBJEEB will release the WBJEE opening and closing ranks 2026 for each participating college, stream, and category. The WBJEE counselling 2026 cut off will signify the minimum and maximum ranks required for admission to a particular college. The WBJEE 2026 cutoff is determined based on several factors, including the number of participants, the difficulty level of the WBJEE exam, the number of available seats, and the past year cutoff trends. Once the WBJEE 2026 round 1 cutoff are released we will provide the same on this page. Meanwhile, you can check the past year cutoff eabks to get an idea about the WBJEE 2026 cut off.

The WBJEE 2026 round 1 cutoff will be released separately for each category and each round, expectedly in August 2026. The board will first release WBJEE round 1 cut off 2026, followed by the cutoff ranks for other rounds. Once, released we will be providing the WBJEE cutoff 2026 on this page.

    How WBJEE Cutoff 2026 Scores are Calculated?

    To calculate the WBJEE 2026 cutoff scores the marking scheme needs to be followed. The calculation of score depends on the category as mentioned in the WBJEE exam pattern 2026. 

    Category 1

    The marking scheme of category 1 is mentioned below.

    • Only 1 option is correct
    • Each question comprises of 1 mark
    • ¼ marks will be deducted for every incorrect answer

    Category 2

    The WBJEE marking scheme in category 2 is as follows:

    • 1 option is correct
    • Each question comprises of 2 marks
    • ½ will be deducted for every incorrect response

    Category 3

    Students can take a look at the category 3 marking scheme of WBJEE below.

    • Probability of 1 or more option to be correct
    • Each question carries 2 marks
    • No negative marking in this category

    FAQs about WBJEE Cut Off

    What is WBJEE opening and closing rank 2026?

    The opening and closing WBJEE counselling 2026 cut off are the minimum and maximum ranks required for admission to WBJEE participating colleges.

    When will the WBJEE round 1 cutoff 2026 be released?

    The WBJEE round 1 cut off 2026 is expected to be released in August 2026.

    Can I get Jadavpur with a 5000 rank?

    No, Jadavpur University is one of the top universities in West Bengal, and a 5000 WBJEE rank is less to get admission there. To get BTech admission into the top branches of Jadavpur University, you need a ranking in the top 1000.

    What is a safe score for the WBJEE 2026 exam?

    For the WBJEE 2026 exam, a score of 160 to 180 is typically regarded as a decent score.

    When will the WBJEE cutoff 2026 be released?

    The WBJEE 2026 cutoff will be released during the WBJEE 2026 counselling in the form of opening and closing ranks. 

    What are the factors determining the WBJEE 2026 cutoff?

    The authorities consider the total number of students who appeared for the examination, the difficulty level of the exam, the total number of vacant seats, and the previous year’s cutoff to determine the WBJEE 2026 cutoff.
