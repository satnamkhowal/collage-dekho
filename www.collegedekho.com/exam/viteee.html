VITEEE Exam Pattern 2026

VIT Vellore will release the VITEEE Exam Pattern 2026. The exam pattern includes important exam details, like question type, exam mode, duration, marking scheme, etc. The VITEEE 2026 exam will be conducted online as a computer-based test, lasting 2 hours and 30 minutes. It consists of a total of 125 multiple-choice questions broken down into five sections: Mathematics with 40 questions, Chemistry with 35 questions, Aptitude with 10 questions, Physics with 35 questions, and English with 5 questions. Each correct answer will earn 1 mark, and there is no negative marking for incorrect responses.

VITEEE 2026 Syllabus

VITEEE Syllabus 2026 will be released on the VIT Vellore official website. The VITEEE 2026 Syllabus includes five main subjects, namely Physics, Chemistry, Aptitude, English, and Mathematics. There are various chapters and topics included in these subjects from which questions will be asked in the exam. To prepare for the exam students must refer to the official syllabus released by VIT only.

VITEEE Registration 2026

VITEEE 2026 registration process is ongoing on the official website: vit.ac.in. Candidates are advised to fill out the application form correctly and not provide any misinformation, or else their candidature might be cancelled in the later phases of the exam. Check the VITEEE registration process 2026 below:

Step 1: New User Registration

Visit the VITEEE 2026 official website - viteee.vit.ac.in. Enter the required information in the portal. For verification, an OTP will be given to your registered mobile number. A password will be issued to your registered email address after the OTP has been confirmed. This will be your registered email address and phone number for further communication.

Step 2: Sign-in 

Log in using the registered email address and the VIT password.

Step 3: Filling Out Application Form

Complete the application form with the required details. Upon the completion of the primary information, an Application Number will be created. Use this application number for future interactions.

Step 4: Online Payment

The VITEEE registration fee of INR 1350 must be paid using Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, or Paytm. For the Overseas exam centre, INR 90 is equivalent to USD 90.

Step 5: Document Uploading

Upload your scanned photograph and signature in JPG format with 10 to 200 KB size. The photograph dimension should be 3.5 cm width x 4.5 cm height and the signature dimension should be 3.5 cm width x 1.5 cm height.

Step 6: Downloading Application Form

Download and save the completed application form for future reference and correspondence. 

VITEEE 2026 Admit Card

VITEEE Admit Card 2026 is released in online mode and can only be accessed through candidates' login on the official website: vit.ac.in. Candidates have to generate their admit card by booking a slot for the VITEEE exam. The VITEEE 2025 Admit Card will comprise details like the name of the candidate, application number, address of the test centre, date and time of the VITEEE exam, and other details. Candidates have to carry their VITEEE hall ticket 2026 to the exam centres or else they will not be allowed to appear for the VITEEE 2026 exam.

VITEEE 2026 Cutoff

VIT University will only issue the stream-wise and campus-wise closing ranks. The cutoff ranks will be available after the declaration of the results. Candidates can go through the VITEEE cutoffs of previous years to get a fair idea of what score they would need in order to qualify for the counselling process.

VITEEE 2026 Counselling

VIT University will commence the VITEEE Counselling 2026 via online mode. Only those candidates who will secure a rank in the VITEEE Merit List 2026 will be eligible to participate in the counselling process and fill in their choices for the desired courses and colleges in order of preference. Based on the ranks of the candidates, the choices filled, and the availability of seats, the authorities will release the VITEEE Seat Allotment 2026. To confirm the allocated seat, candidates will have to submit at least 50% of the admission fee. 

VITEEE Results 2026

VIT will release the result of VITEEE 2026 in the online mode on the official website. Candidates who will appear for the VITEEE Exam 2026 wwill be able to check the VITEEE Result 2026 at viteee.vit.ac.in. Candidates are required to use their application number and password to access the results. The scorecard of the candidates will comprise information such as the application number, scores obtained, rank secured, etc.
