What is TANCET?

The Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test (TANCET) is a state-level exam that allows you to pursue postgraduate studies such as MBA and MCA in top colleges across Tamil Nadu. The test is organised every year by Anna University on behalf of the Government of Tamil Nadu. Along with TANCET, the university also conducts CEETA-PG for M.E., M.Tech., M.Arch., and M.Plan programmes. For 2026, nearly 200+ reputed colleges are expected to accept TANCET scores, offering around 15,000 MBA and MCA seats. Over 45,000 candidates appear annually, making it one of the most competitive PG entrance exams in the state. The TANCET exam is held in offline mode, consisting of 100 multiple-choice questions to be completed in two hours. Counselling for MBA and MCA admissions is managed by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Chennai.

TANCET 2026 Eligibility Criteria

TANCET Eligibility Criteria 2026 has been released by Anna University at tancet.annauniv.edu/tancet. To be eligible to apply for TANCET 2026, candidates must meet the following minimum eligibility requirements set by the authorities as part of the TANCET eligibility criteria 2026. To be eligible for various postgraduate programs, candidates must meet specific criteria.

TANCET 2026 MBA Eligibility Criteria

To apply for the MBA program, you must meet the following requirements:

  • You should hold a Bachelor’s degree with at least 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/OBC candidates).

  • Your undergraduate education must follow one of these patterns:

    • 10 + 2 + 3 or 4 or 5 years

    • 10 + 3 years Diploma + 3 years

    • 10 + 2 followed by B.E./B.Tech./B.Arch./B.Pharm.

    • 10 + 2 + AMIE

    • 10 + 3 years Diploma (recognised by the State Board of Technical Education) + AMIE

TANCET 2026 MCA Eligibility Criteria

For the MCA program, eligibility rules are as follows:

  • You must possess a BCA or B.Sc. degree in IT/Computer Science.

  • If your degree is in another field, you should have studied Mathematics at the 10+2 level or during graduation.

  • A minimum of 50% marks (45% for reserved categories) is required in the qualifying exam.

  • Accepted education patterns include:

    • 10 + 2 + 3 or 4 or 5 years

    • 10 + 3 years Diploma + 3 years

    • 10 + 2 + AMIE

    • 10 + 3 years Diploma (recognised by the State Board of Technical Education) + AMIE

TANCET Exam Pattern 2026

The TANCET 2026 exam pattern varies by subject, with the MBA exam consisting of 100 questions divided into five sections: reading comprehension, quantitative aptitude, business analysis, data sufficiency, and English usage, while the MCA exam includes four sections: quantitative aptitude, analytical reasoning, logical reasoning, and computer awareness. Both exams are conducted for 2 hours and contain a total of 100 questions, with 115 for M.E./M.Tech/M.Arch/M.Plan candidates. Each section carries specific weightage: 25 questions for each MCA section and 20 for each MBA section, culminating in a total of 100 marks. As per the TANCET exam pattern 2026, the exam is conducted in English, employs multiple-choice questions with four answer choices, and is conducted in a pen-and-paper format. According to the TANCET marking scheme 2026, candidates will be given one mark for correct answers and deduct one-fourth of a mark for incorrect responses, which is equivalent to 0.25.

  • Hall Ticket Download: Obtain your admit card once released and verify all details, such as your exam centre and registration number.
  • Exam Appearance: Attend the TANCET 2026 exam on May 9, 2026, conducted in offline (pen-and-paper) mode.

FAQs about TANCET

When will the TANCET 2026 results be released?

The TANCET 2026 results are expected to be released in April 2026 on the official website.  

Will the TANCET 2026 exam have descriptive-type questions?

No, the TANCET 2026 exam will not hold any descriptive-type questions. The TANCET exam 2026 will have only MCQs (Multiple Choice Questions). 

Can I edit the TANCET application form after submission?

No, candidates will not be able to edit or modify their TANCET 2026 application form after the submission. Anna University does not open any application correction window to make corrections to the application form. Candidates who are going to fill out the TANCET application form are advised to fill out the online form carefully.

Is there any age limit to appear for the TANCET 2026 exam?

No, there is no age limit specified by Anna University to appear for the TANCET exam. However, the candidates willing to appear for the TANCET 2026 exam must meet all the eligibility criteria to fill out the application form. 

What is the mode of the TANCET exam 2026?

The TANCET 2026 exam will be conducted in offline mode, where the appearing candidate has to answer the MCQs (Multiple Choice Questions) in the OMR (Optical Mark Recognition) sheet. 

In how many cities are TANCET exam centres located?

The TANCET 2026 exam will be conducted across 15 cities in Tamil Nadu that are Chennai, Karaikudi, Tirunelveli, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Chidambaram, Nagercoil, Vellore, Dindigul, Salem, Villupuram, Erode, Thanjavur, and Virudhunagar. Candidates will have to select cities while filling out the application form. The TANCET exam centre will be allocated to the selected cities by the candidates. That’s why it is advised to select the exam centres carefully.

Is the TANCET exam easy to qualify?

The TANCET exam is considered moderate in terms of difficulty. For MBA exams, the difficulty standard is typically compared with the CAT exam. In comparison with the CAT difficulty level, TANCET is considered easy to crack.

Is TANCET only for the Tamil Nadu students?

Candidates belonging to states apart from Tamil Nadu can also appear for the TANCET exam but they need to fulfill the eligibility criteria prescribed by the college/university authorities.

Why is TANCET conducted?

TANCET is a state-level entrance exam that is conducted with the intention to offer candidates admission to various postgraduate programmes in the top government and private colleges across Tamil Nadu.

Is TANCET score valid for admission in all M.Tech colleges across Tamil Nadu?

TANCET score is valid for M.Tech admission in Government Colleges, Private Colleges, Anna University & its constituent colleges and Annamalai University. 

Which courses can I get admission through TANCET?

TANCET is conducted for admission in M.E/ M.Tech, M.Plan, MBA and MCA. 

Are candidates with a valid GATE score exempted from TANCET?

Candidates with a valid GATE score are exempted from TANCET.
