CUSAT CAT 2026 Eligibility

The CUSAT CAT 2026 eligibility criteria will differ for different programmes offered by CUSAT CAT. Candidates must have cleared 12th standard exams from a recognised board with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and English subjects with 60% total marks and at least 50% in Mathematics.

At the time of admission, candidates for LL.B.(Hons) programmes like BBA, LL.B. (Hons), and B.Com must also have completed the 10+2 examination. Applicants need to have scored at least 60% in the Science and Commerce streams and at least 55% in the Arts and Humanities streams. To qualify for admission, candidates for SC / ST or SEBC must have obtained a score of at least 50% in the 12th standard examination.

The applicant must have obtained a bachelor's degree from any university or institute that is recognised for postgraduate programmes, such as MBA (Master of Business Administration), and they must have scored a minimum of 50% in aggregate. SEBC applicants are eligible for a 5% reduction in the required minimum marks.

CUSAT CAT 2026 Exam Pattern

Applicants must check the exam pattern of the CUSAT CAT 2026 before planning to start preparations for the exam. Here are some significant pointers related to the CUSAT CAT 2026 exam pattern - 

  • The exam pattern will vary as per the programmes offered.
  • The test will be organised via the CBT mode and will have MCQ-based questions.
  • The exam duration will be 2.5 hours to 3 hours and may vary per course.
  • There are 225 questions in the entrance test for subjects like the B.Tech examination. 
  • For B.Com / BBA / LLB (Hons.), LLB, and LLM, there will be 225 total questions. 
  • Aspirants will get three marks for each correct answer, and one mark will be deducted for any wrong attempts.

CUSAT CAT 2026 Syllabus

The CUSAT CAT exam syllabus will differ per the offered programmes. Aspirants must follow the prospectus of the CUSAT CAT which will be published by the conducting authority as and when the application form will be released.

For instance, candidates aspiring to join the LLM programme will have to complete the subjects studied during the LLB , BA LLB etc, and as prescribed by the Bar Council of India.

For programmes like B Tech Programmes except B.Tech Marine Engineering / M.Sc (Five years integrated) in Photonics, M.Sc (Five year integrated) in Computer Science, the questions will be based on the 10+2 syllabus. Out of 250 questions, aspirants will have to attempt 125 from Mathematics, 75 and 50 from Physics and Chemistry each.

For 5 - Year Integrated M. Sc (Biological Sciences / Chemistry / Mathematics / Physics / Statistics), aspirants will have to answer questions based on 12th standard syllabus. The major topics will focus on Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.

CUSAT 2026 Preparation Tips

Here are some important tips for CUSAT CAT 2026 preparation -

  • A strategic approach to CUSAT CAT 2026 preparation is required. Candidates must examine the CUSAT CAT syllabus, exam pattern, previous years' question paper, before developing a strategy. These will teach the aspirants about the subjects to study, the exam complexity level, the different kinds of questions, the marking scheme, and other important information that will help them pass the CUSAT CAT 2026 with a high score.
  • The CUSAT CAT preparation strategy is an important step toward success. As a result, when making a decision which subjects to study first, applicants must be extremely careful. 
  • Applicants must examine the CUSAT CAT exam pattern and syllabus carefully and make a schedule and allocate time to subjects based on their abilities and weaknesses.
  • Make sure not to start new chapters when a month or so remains for the exam.
  • The applicants must have a grasp of the concepts covered in the chapters that they have finished.
  • Regularly practise the formulas, reactions, equations, block diagrams, flowcharts, and so on, particularly the difficult ones.
  • Solve as many practise questions as possible, including previous year questions and difficult questions, questions they have got wrong at any point, and other tough questions.
  • Take CUSAT CAT practice tests and previous year exams just as seriously as the actual exam.
  • Recognize the mistakes and flaws after each test and work on them.
  • To improve the time management, make note of how much time they need to spend on each question as they practise and take tests.
  • Candidates must find out how to choose which queries to answer during a test. The idea is to know a good grip forehand, as which section questions are the easiest for the aspirants to attempt comparatively.
