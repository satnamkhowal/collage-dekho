The KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test) is a state-level entrance exam conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) for providing admission to the eligible students across the available courses in Karnataka. It serves as a gateway if you are seeking to pursue undergraduate courses in the subjects of Engineering, Technology, Pharmacy, Nursing, Agriculture, Yoga, and Naturopathy at government, private, and autonomous institutions within the state.

KCET Eligibility Criteria 2026

KCET Eligibility Criteria 2026: To apply for KCET 2026, you need to meet certain criteria set by the KEA exam conducting body. The set of parameters is here for you to check your chances in the KCET 2026 eligibility criteria. For engineering courses, the eligibility criteria require you to complete the 12th grade, 2nd PUC, or its equivalent from a recognized board or authority. Those currently in the 12th grade are also eligible to apply. Additionally, you should have completed the 12th grade with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics or Biology from a recognized board and obtained a minimum of 45% in PCM/PCBsubjects (40% for restricted categories).

For B. Pharma, you need to complete Class 12 with 45% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics or Biology from a recognized board, with a 5% relaxation for the SC/ST category. B.V.Sc., A.H., and B.Sc. Nursing require a minimum 12th-grade average of 50% in PCB and English, with a 5% relaxation for the SC/ST category.

For B.Arch, a minimum 12th-grade average of 50% is required, with Mathematics as a mandatory subject and a valid JEE Main (Paper 2) or NATA scorecard. If you are applying for BDS/MBBS/AYUSHprograms, then you must have a 12th-grade average of 50% in PCB, with Englishas a required subject and a valid NEET scorecard.

KCET Exam Pattern 2026

KCET 2026 exam pattern will be an offline written examination. It will be held in two sessions each day with morning and afternoon shifts. You have to choose your preferred group for the subjects Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects and Chemistry or Biology (any one) as optional. Your time slot will be marked on your admit card.

As per the KCET exam pattern 2026, the medium of language will be in English or Kannada. Each session will last for 1 hour and 20 minutes. The questions will be multiple-choice (MCQs). There will be a total of 180 questions, with 60 questions for each subject. Each correct answer scores 1 mark. There is no negative marking.

KCET Counselling 2026

KEA in the latest notification, has stated that the KCET 2026 counselling will start in June 2026 (estimated timeline) after the result release in the last week of May. The process follows an "Application-cum-Verification" approach where a majority of the applicants get their documents verified through an automated process based on SATS and RD numbers.

Through this process, you will receive an online verification slip along with a unique secret key. You will use the key to participate in the "Option Entry" online process in July 2026 (tentative) and in a mock allotment to assess your options. The actual seat allotment is expected to be scheduled for August 2026. You have to take one of the following four 'Choices' to either freeze your seat, keep it on hold for a better option, or withdraw from the process.

FAQs about KCET

When will KCET 2026 exam be conducted?

KCET 2026 exam will be conducted in two sessions on April 23 and 24, 2026.

Who is eligible for KCET 2026?

Applicants who have passed the 2nd PUC or 12th standard or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics or Biology. The minimum aggregate mark (usually 45% for the general and 40% for the reserved category). You have to fulfil any one of the KEA domicile conditions (such as having studied in Karnataka for 7 academic years), etc.

What is the official website of KCET application 2026?

The official website of KCET application form 2026: https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/

What is the entrance exam for Karnataka 2026?

Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) is the entrance exam in Karnataka conducted by KEA.

How many years of study certificate is required for KCET 2026?

To apply for KCET 2026, you must have completed at least seven years of education in a government or government-recognised school located in the state of Karnataka. This includes the period from 1st standard to 2nd PUC or 12th standard. Additionally, you should have passed either the 10th standard or 12th standard examination from a school in Karnataka. If you have taken more than one year to pass a class, it will only be counted as one year of academic study.

Do KCET conduct only for admission into undergraduate degree programs?

Yes, KCET is conducted for admission into undergraduate degree programmes offered by the government, government-aided and self-financing colleges in Karnataka.

Can a foreign applicant is eligible to apply for admission into a Government KCET participating college?

No, the government KCET participating colleges do not accept the admission request from students who are not permanent citizens of India. 

Is the Kannada Language Test compulsory for all the candidates who appear in the KCET 2026 exam?

No, the Kannada Language Test is only for those who apply for admission into the seats reserved for Gadinadu Kannadiga and Horanadu students in government KCET colleges. It is also important to apply for admission through the quota mentioned above. You should have a certificate and be listed in Kodava, Tulu or Kannada as your mother tongue.

I want to study B.Arch from a KCET participating college. Should I appear in KCET 2026 exam or other national-level architecture entrance exams like NATA or JEE Main?

In order to pursue a B.Arch from a KCET-participating institute in 2026, you have to appear for NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture) or JEE Main Paper 2. However, if you want admission in B.Arch through KCET, then you are not required to appear for the KCET exam. You need to attend the KCET counselling by registering for it and then take admission to any of the Karnataka colleges based on your NATA or JEE score.
