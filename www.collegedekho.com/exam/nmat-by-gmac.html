NMAT 2025 Conducting Body

The Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC) serves as the proprietor and overseer of the NMAT exam. As a global non-profit organization, GMAC plays a pivotal role in guiding graduate business schools worldwide. NMAT stands as a prestigious national-level management entrance exam, facilitating admission to esteemed NMAT-designated management colleges like Alliance University, SRM University, BML Munjal University, Ansal University, VIT University, and ICFAI Business School (IBS).

NMAT by GMAC 2025 Eligibility Criteria

Each candidate needs to be aware of the NMAT 2025 eligibility criteria before paying the fees for the exam. Only those who fulfil the eligibility criteria can appear in the NMAT 2025 Exam. The eligibility criteria for NMAT 2025 have been provided below:

Academic Qualification

  • Students should have completed their graduation under the 10+2+3 or four-year education pattern from any stream from a UGC/AICTE-recognised institute or equivalent.
  • Candidates who are awaiting results or are in their final year of graduation are also eligible to apply for the exam. 
  • Admission is granted on a provisional basis until the graduation mark sheet and certificate of the qualifying examination are submitted.

Minimum Aggregate: Candidates should have scored at least 50% in their graduation to be eligible for the exam

Nationality: There are no nationality criteria.

Age Limit: There is no age limit barrier.

Work Experience: Work Experience is not mandatory.

Maximum Attempts: 3 times, i.e. one examination attempt and two retakes.

NMAT 2025 Registration Process

Below are the steps that candidates have to follow to complete the NMAT registration 2025:

  • Step 1: Begin by visiting the NMAT online registration page (register.nmat.org) and creating an account using a valid email ID and other required personal details.
  • Step 2: Upon account creation, access your email inbox, locate the verification link sent by GMAC, and complete the sign-up process for NMAT 2025.
  • Step 3: Provide essential personal information, academic qualifications, and work experience details, and select your preferred MBA courses from the list of colleges accepting NMAT scores. You can choose up to five courses and B-schools. Additionally, indicate your preferred test cities and enter a valid identification number (such as Aadhaar, PAN, Driving License, etc.).
  • Step 4: Ensure your photograph and digital signature are scanned and uploaded according to the specified resolution (maximum 1 MB) and format (JPG/JPEG, PNG, GIF) outlined by GMAC.
  • Step 5: After verifying and submitting all required information, proceed to payment. The application fee amounts to INR 2800 plus applicable taxes. Upon successful payment, you will receive your unique "NMAT ID." Remember to keep a printout for future reference.
  • Step 6: Subsequently, GMAC will notify you regarding exam scheduling, slot booking, and downloading of your hall ticket. These updates will be reflected on your dashboard.

NMAT 2025 Exam Scheduling

NMAT admissions are facilitated by GMAC upon successful registration completion. Subsequently, candidates can schedule their exams. Utilizing the exam scheduling feature, candidates must specify their preferred date and city to proceed. Scheduling represents the sole method for exam participation, accessible via the NMAT login dashboard.

NMAT 2025 Rescheduling and Retake

You can change your NMAT exam date, time, or test centre through the official rescheduling option available on your NMAT dashboard. This facility requires you to pay a rescheduling fee of INR 1200 plus taxes. Make sure to request the change at least 72 hours before your original exam slot. Once you finalise the reschedule, your new exam details will be updated on your admit card.

Regarding exam retakes, you are allowed up to two retakes in addition to your first attempt, making it a total of three tries within the official testing window (from July 1 to June 30 next year). You must pay the exam fee again for each retake and maintain a minimum gap of 15 days between attempts. Some colleges accept the highest score of your attempts, while others may consider only the first score. You can register for retakes right from your NMAT account dashboard after your first attempt.

NMAT 2025 Marking Scheme

GMAC evaluates the performance of candidates based on the marking scheme mentioned below:

  • 3 marks are awarded for every correct objective-type question.
  • In case of any wrong attempt, no marks will be deducted.
  • There is also no negative marking for unattempted questions.

Note: Once a student has finished a particular section, all answers are considered final. There is no facility to go back and change the answers.

NMAT 2025 Scores

After completing the NMAT by GMAC 2025 exam, candidates can expect to receive their scorecard, detailing scaled scores for individual sections as well as the total score, within 48 hours. Notification of scorecard availability will be sent via email, allowing candidates to promptly download their results.

In the NMAT exam, questions are dynamically selected from a question pool based on each candidate's performance on previously answered questions. This adaptive approach tailors the question set to match the examinee's ability level, ensuring that each candidate faces an appropriately challenging array of questions.

Scoring for the NMAT exam involves deriving the candidate's final ability estimate using a probabilistic model approach from modern exam theory. This estimate is then transformed into a scaled score, ranging from 12 to 120, providing a standardised measure of the candidate's performance.

NMAT Exam Analysis 2025

As per the NMAT 2025 paper analysis, the exam presented a moderate to high level of difficulty. The Quantitative Skills section remained the most challenging, especially with questions on bi-quadratic equations, probability, and permutation-combination. Data Interpretation involved time-consuming calculations, making it one of the lengthier parts of the test. Logical Reasoning focused heavily on critical reasoning, with several questions based on assumptions, inferences, and courses of action. Candidates also saw logical Venn diagrams and two puzzles, including input-output and seating arrangement sets. The Language Skills section featured three short but concept-heavy reading comprehensions, four detailed parajumbles, and GMAT-style phrase-based fill-in-the-blanks.

NMAT 2025 Official Marksheet

The official NMAT 2025 mark sheet will comprise two scores - an individual sectional scaled & overall score, and the percentile of a candidate. Calculation of the scaled score is done before calculating the final percentile. The final percentile score is calculated based on the scaled score, and it is updated in the official scorecard and sent to B-schools.

NMAT 2025 Selection Process

The NMAT 2025 selection process will begin after the declaration of cutoffs. GMAC is not responsible for carrying out a centralised admission process. Every participating institute has its own selection process and admission criteria.

  • The NMAT 2025 selection process will include GD-PI rounds and the newly introduced Competency Test. 

  • Candidates who clear the minimum sectional and overall cutoffs will be shortlisted to appear for the GD-PI rounds.

  • After the conduct of the selection process rounds, the final merit list will be prepared based on various parameters: NMAT 2025 score, GD-PI scores, work experience, past academic scores, competency test, and gender diversity.

  • The decision of final admission is based on the combined performance in the NMAT 2025 and GD-PI round.

NMIMS NMAT MBA Competency Test

Since the competency test introduced by NMIMS is new to the selection process, check the salient features of this test mentioned below:

  • It is a self-timed test, and the total time you are given is 45 minutes after reading the instructions. A server clock does the timekeeping.
  • There are a total of 3 sections, including Verbal Ability, Mental Ability, and Managerial Ability, with 9, 6, and 5 questions, respectively. Each question carries 3 marks.
  • There are 4 alternative answers for each question, of which only one is correct. There is no sectional time limit or restrictions.
  • A negative marking of 1 is there for each incorrect answer and no negative marking for unanswered questions.
  • Answers will be submitted automatically at the end of 45 minutes if the submit button is not pressed.

Candidates can register for NMAT 2025 in the following steps:

  • Registration
  • Application Form Filling
  • Filling School Preference
  • Uploading Photograph
  • Selecting Exam Mode
  • Paying Application Fee
  • Scheduling

In which mode NMAT is conducted?

NMAT is a computer-based test (CBT) that is conducted in the online mode. Candidates have no option to take this exam in the offline format.

How many times can I take the NMAT in a year?

Any candidate can sit for the NMAT exam three times a year. This includes the first attempt at the exam and the two retakes that are allowed by GMAC. To take a retake exam, candidates must do the retake registration, pay the retake fees, and complete rescheduling.

Do candidates get a limited number of attempts for NMAT?

No, there is no limit to the number of attempts for NMAT by GMAC. NMAT can be taken by the candidates any number of times if they fulfil the eligibility criteria.

How many times does GMAC conduct the NMAT exam?

The NMAT exam is conducted by GMAC once every year. NAMT by GMAC 2025 will be conducted over a window of 71 days, from October to December 2025.

Who conducts NMAT?

NMAT is conducted by the Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAC), a non-profit organization that also conducts the GMAT exam. The exam is conducted for MBA admission in NMIMS and other popular B-schools in Indi including VIT Vellore, SPJIMR Mumbai, XIM Bhubaneswar, and many more.
