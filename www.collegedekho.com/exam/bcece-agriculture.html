About BCECE Agriculture 2026 Exam

The BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam, also known as the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination, will be conducted by the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board @bceceboard.bihar.gov.in in June 2026. The official notification for the BCECE Agriculture exam 2026 is expected to be released in the first week of April 2026.

Along with the BCECE Agriculture 2026 notification, the BCECE Agriculture registrations 2026 will also begin. Students will be able to fill out the BCECE Agri application form from April to May 2026.

Registered students will be able to download the BCECE Agriculture Admit Card 2026 in the last week of May 2026.

After the examination has been conducted, the BCECEB will announce the BCECE Agriculture 2026 result in July 2026. Students will be able to download their BCECE Agriculture scorecards 2026 by entering their roll number and date of birth.

The BCECE Agriculture counselling process 2026 will begin after the results have been announced by the examination authorities. Students who meet the BCECE Agriculture Cutoff 2026 will be eligible to sit for the Bihar BSc Agriculture and BSc Horticulture counselling and seat allotment process.

Students who wish to take admission to UG courses in Pharmacy, Engineering, and Agriculture programmes must pass this examination. To enrol in any of the education programs offered by several colleges in Bihar, students must clear the BCECE 2026 exam. There is only one stage to this exam; therefore, those who want to submit applications for the academic programs should study to boost their chances of getting into an institution of their choice.

    BCECE Agriculture 2026 Eligibility Criteria

    Students must take a detailed look at the BCECE Agriculture eligibility criteria 2026 before they fill out the application form. Students must meet the eligibility requirements to be accepted for admission to the BSc Agriculture and BSc Horticulture courses in Bihar agricultural colleges and universities. Before July 1, 2026, each student must be at least 17 years old. No upper age limit has been specified by the examination board. Moreover, there is no restriction on the total number of attempts a student can take. The minimum score required for Pharmacy admissions is 45% marks, and for paramedical courses, students must have achieved an aggregate of at least 50% marks. Pupils from the reserved category are exempted from the minimum score requirement by a 5% relaxation.

    BCECE Agriculture 2026 Exam Preparation Tips

    Students can score well on the Bihar Agriculture entrance examination if they prepare the right study plan. Follow the best and effective BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam preparation tips to enhance your performance in this entrance examination. Here are some of the most important tips and tricks that you can follow to prepare for the BCECE Agriculture exam 2026:

    Understand Each Concept Thoroughly: Learning and mastering each topic/theory/concept is crucial, especially the ones most frequently asked in the BCECE Agriculture's previous year's question papers. This way, you will grasp each concept and be able to answer any question posed in the actual examination.

    Review the Entire Syllabus: Do not waste your time by preparing for topics that are not a part of the officially released syllabus, as no questions will be asked about them. Always refer to the official-recommended topics because all questions in the exam will be asked from the same.

    Effective Time Organisation: You should build a customised timetable that is suitable to your needs and study habits. Within your schedule, you should allocate equal time to study for each subject.

    Keep a Check on Your Health: It is crucial to remember that health is an undeniable priority. Make sure you eat healthy, follow a good sleep schedule, stay hydrated, pursue your hobby, exercise often, etc.

    Attempt Previous Year’s Question Papers: Solving past year’s exam question papers is one of the best resources to prepare for the exam. To acquaint yourself with an exam-like atmosphere, you should solve these paper sets by setting a timer to check your level of preparedness.

    Always Clear Your Doubts: If you get even a silly doubt while studying, always raise it with your teacher. You can also clarify your doubts by participating in group discussions or using internet resources.

    Worry Less, Study More: You must not feel pressured while studying. If you get worried or anxious while studying, incorporate Yoga into your schedule or perform meditation. You should always study tension-free.

    Only Refer to Well-Published Study Materials: You must only gather or refer to books or study materials that are published by renowned authors or publishers. Ensure that your preferred book contains sample papers and explains topics in great detail using different examples so that you can remember the things you learnt.

    The BCECE Agriculture 2026 cutoff scores are the lowest acceptable scores for applicants.     FAQs about BCECE Agriculture

    What is the mode of payment for the BCECE Agriculture 2026 application fee?

    The mode of payment for the BCECE Agriculture 2026 application fee is both offline and online. For online payment, test takers may use net banking, debit card, credit card, and UPI. For offline payment, they can pay using an NEFT challan.

    What is the BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam eligibility criteria?

    The BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam eligibility criteria is as follows: students must have completed their Class 12 examination from a recognised board. In addition, they must have studied Physics and Chemistry as main subjects along with one additional subject in a similar stream such as Biology, Mathematics, or Agriculture.

    When will the BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam be conducted?

    The BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam will be conducted in June 2026, for 90 minutes in both English and Hindi languages. The exam will have a total of 100 questions for 400 marks.

    In which language will the BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam be conducted?

    BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam will mainly be conducted in two languages — English and Hindi. Since there will be different question papers for all of the four or five subjects they are appearing in, students must prepare for each one independently. The exam's syllabus primarily draws on the Higher Education curriculum, which the students have already studied.

    Who is the conducting body of the BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam?

    The Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) is the conducting body of the BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam. Aspirants must access BCECE Bihar's official portal and keep checking it for any updates or news regarding the examination.

    Is it important for students to understand the BCECE 2026 exam pattern?

    Yes, it is important for students to understand the BCECE 2026 exam pattern as it helps them know the types of questions asked, the difficulty level, and important topics. It will also introduce them to the marking scheme and the time allotted to each topic.

    Is the BCECE Agriculture exam difficult for an average student?

    The BCECE Agriculture exam is not difficult as it is based on Class 11 and 12 syllabi. Whether the exam is easy or difficult depends on a student’s level of preparation. If a candidate’s basic topics are cleared, then they can achieve excellent marks on the exam.

    When is the last date to apply for the BCECE Agriculture 2026 exam?

    The BCECE 2026 application form submission's last date will be in May 2026.

    When will the BCECE Agriculture 2026 admit card be released?

    The BCECE Agriculture 2026 admit card will be released in May 2026 on its official website. To download their admit cards for the BCECE 2026 exam, you will be required to enter your login details.
