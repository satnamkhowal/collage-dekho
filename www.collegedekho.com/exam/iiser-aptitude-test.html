IISER Aptitude Test 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Before filling out the IISER 2026 Aptitude Test application form, candidates must know IAT 2026 eligibility. Candidates who will be eligible can appear in IISER exam 2026. Check IISER exam eligibility 2026 mentioned below:

IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) General Eligibility (All channels):

Nationality: Candidates who are appearing for IISER entrance exam 2026 must be Indian nationality students. 

IISER Exam Eligibility Age Limit (New): Candidates must have been born on or after October 1, 2000. However, SC, ST, and PwD candidates are eligible for a five-year age relaxation, meaning they should have been born on or after October 1, 1995. Additionally, there is no restriction on the year of completion of Class XII.

Qualification: All the candidates must have cleared the 12th class with science stream from any board recognized. 

Qualifying marks: Candidates of the General / OBC & OBC-NCL category should have scored a minimum of 60% marks. Students from the SC/ ST / PWD category will require a minimum of 55% marks to fill out the application form. 

Qualifying subjects: Students must have passed the 12th class with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology subjects.

Exam Day Guidelines for IISER Aptitude Test 2026

Candidates must go through the list of common guidelines that must be kept in mind by all the candidates who will be appearing for the IISER 2026 entrance examination.

Guideline

Description / What to Do

Eat and Sleep Well

Get 7–8 hours of proper sleep before the exam day. Eat a balanced meal so you can stay focused and concentrate during the IISER Aptitude Test 2026.

Carry the Admit Card

Keep your IISER 2026 admit card and required stationery ready beforehand. Read the admit card carefully to ensure you are carrying all necessary documents and following all instructions.

Report on Time

Reach the exam venue at least 1 hour early. Arriving early helps you settle down calmly and complete all formalities without stress.

Stop Studying and Stay Confident

Avoid last-minute studying just before the exam as it can cause nervousness. Stay calm and confident, and trust the preparation you have done throughout the year.

Time Management

Manage your time carefully during the exam. Allocate appropriate time for each section of the IISER 2026 question paper to maximize efficiency.

Avoid Difficult Questions Initially

Do not spend too much time on difficult questions. Focus on attempting easier questions first so you do not miss scoring opportunities.

Revision

Reserve 5–10 minutes at the end for revision. This allows you to check answers, correct mistakes, or attempt any questions you might have skipped.

FAQs about IISER Aptitude Test

When does counselling start for IISER 2026?

IISER 2026 counselling will start with the opening of the online registration and choice-filling process in June 2026, and the deadline will be until July 2026.

When is IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) 2026 exam scheduled for?

IAT 2026 will be held in May 2026. This is the official exam date, so make sure you prepare accordingly.

When does IAT 2026 registration start?

The registration for IAT 2026 starts in March 2026 and ends in April 2026. After this, you won’t be able to apply, so make sure you submit your form on time.

Who is eligible for IISER entrance exam?

To apply for IAT 2026, you must have:

  • Passed Class 12 (or equivalent) in 2025 or 2026 with science subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and/or Biology).
  • A minimum percentage of 60% in your board exams (55% for reserved categories).

You can check the detailed criteria on this page!

What is the minimum 12th percentage for IISER?

You must have a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate in the 12th standard or equivalent examination. This applies to those from the general, EWS, and OBC-NCL categories. For candidates belonging to the SC/ST and PwD categories, the minimum percentage required is 55%.

Can I download IAT 2026 syllabus in PDF format?

Yes, you can download the IAT 2026 syllabus PDF from this page & official IISER admission website. The syllabus is based on topics from Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology, mostly from Class 11 and 12.

How do I register for IISER Aptitude Test 2026?

You need to visit the official IISER admission website (iiseradmission.in) and fill out the online application form. You must provide personal details, educational qualifications, upload documents, and pay the application fee. The direct link will be provided on this page!

What are the application fees of IISER?

The application fees for IISER vary based on the category you belong to:

  • General/OBC/EWS: ₹2000
  • SC/ST/PwD/Female: ₹1000

What is the exam pattern for IAT 2026?

IAT 2026 is an online computer-based test. The exam includes MCQs from four subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology. The duration is 3 hours, and the marking scheme includes 4 marks for correct answers and a negative marking of 1 mark for incorrect answers. There will be no marks awarded for unanswered questions.

When will IAT 2026 admit card be available?

The admit card for IAT 2026 will be available in May 2026, on the official IISER website. You need to log in with your registration credentials to download it. Make sure to print a copy and carry it to the exam center.

What courses are offered through IAT 2026?

Through IAT 2026, you can get admission into:

  • 5-Year BS-MS at all IISERs
  • 4-Year BS in Economic Sciences at IISER Bhopal
  • 4-Year BS in Economic and Statistical Sciences at IISER Tirupati (New)
  • 4-Year B.Tech. (in Chemical Engineering, Data Science & Engineering, and Electrical Engineering & Computer Science) at IISER Bhopal

What is the age limit for IISER admission?

As per the latest information for the academic year 2026, applicants must have been born on or after October 1, 2000. However, SC, ST, and PwD candidates are eligible for a five-year age relaxation, meaning they should have been born on or after October 1, 1995. Additionally, there is no restriction on the year of completion of Class XII.

Is IAT 2026 result released?

No, IAT 2026 result will be announced on the official website tentatively in June 2026. You can check it by logging into your IISER admission account using your application credentials. The official date has not been released yet!
