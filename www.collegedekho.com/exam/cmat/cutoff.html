CMAT Cutoff 2026

The CMAT cutoff 2026 will be announced by participating institutes, likely in the last week of February 2026 or the first week of March 2026.It indicates the minimum score or percentile required to secure admission to a particular college. Leading MBA colleges typically consider CMAT scores in the 95 to 99 percentile range during admissions. Scoring somewhere between 280 and 350 marks out of 400 gives the aspirants a strong chance of admission to their preferred B-Schools.

Those who meet the CMAT cutoff are shortlisted for further stages of the admission process, which typically include a Written Ability Test, Group Discussion, and Personal Interview. It is important to remember that CMAT cutoffs vary across colleges and categories. Reviewing the expected CMAT cutoff 2026 can help aspirants understand the score range needed for their preferred institutes.

The CMAT 2026 exam was held on January 25, 2026, and the result is expected to be declared in the second or third week of February 2026.

    Factors Determining CMAT 2026 Cutoff

    The cutoff varies for all CMAT participating colleges every year due to various factors, as mentioned below.

    • The difficulty level of the CMAT exam.

    • The number of candidates who have appeared in the entrance exam.

    • The total number of candidates who have passed the entrance exam.

    • The total number of seats available in various management programs of the participating institutes.

    • Reservation: The prescribed reservations are  15%, 7.5%, 27%, 5% & 50.5% for SC, ST, OBC(NC), PwD & General category respectively.

    CMAT Selection Process 2026

    The CMAT cutoff is the basic requirement for participating in the CMAT selection process for some of the top B-schools in India that accept CMAT scores for MBA admission. The participating colleges declare the cutoff once the CMAT result is announced which commences the CMAT selection process. The CMAT selection process is divided into the following stages:

    • Appearing for CMAT 2026: The CMAT selection process 2026 begins with the CMAT exam for which candidates must appear if they wish to seek admission to some of the top CMAT participating MBA colleges.
    • Declaration of CMAT 2026 Result: The announcement of the CMAT 2026 Result is the second step in the candidate selection process. The CMAT 2026 scorecard containing the candidates' mark distribution is released together with the results. 
    • Shortlisting of Candidates: A shortlist of students is created based on their CMAT 2026 score. The shortlisting of the candidates happens right away following the announcement of the results.
    • CMAT Counselling Process: The final admission procedure involves an elaborate counselling process which is conducted by each MBA college separately. The CMAT 2026 counselling process typically involves the Group Discussion, Personal Interview, and Written Ability Test. For the final selection of candidates, their overall performance is assessed and a composite score is calculated, based on which admission is offered.

    What After CMAT Cutoff 2026?

    After the release of the CMAT cutoff, candidates can expect the commencement of the next phase of the selection process for various management programs. Once the cutoff is released, candidates who meet the required cutoff score are eligible to participate in the subsequent rounds of the admission process, which may include group discussions, personal interviews, and other evaluation criteria set by individual institutions. Candidates need to stay updated with the official websites of the respective institutions to which they have applied, as these sites typically provide detailed information about the post-cutoff selection process.

    Additionally, candidates should be prepared to submit any required documentation and fees within the specified timelines after the release of the cutoff scores. It's also advisable for candidates to track important dates related to the admission process, such as interview schedules and result announcements. Overall, after the release of the CMAT cutoff, candidates should focus on preparing for the next stages of the admission process and ensure that they fulfil all the requirements set by the participating institutions.

    How to Apply to Colleges Accepting CMAT 2026 Score?

    FAQs about CMAT Cut Off

    Are there sectional cutoff requirements for CMAT?

    No, sectional cutoff requirements for CMAT are not required since every CMAT accepting institute has their own admission criteria. Candidates only need to meet the CMAT overall cutoff score that is set by colleges for MBA admission. It is advised that candidates check the official website and eligibility criteria for the colleges they prefer before applying for admission to check the exact MBA admission requirements.

    How much CMAT score is required for Great Lakes Institute of Management?

    The CMAT score required for Great Lakes Institute of Management is 95+ percentile. Since Great Lakes Institute of Management is one of the top colleges for management education in India, the CMAT score requirements for MBA admission at this college are high. Candidates must check the official website for Great Lakes Institute of Management to find the CMAT cutoff.

    What is the CMAT cutoff for JBIMS?

    The CMAT cutoff for JBIMS is 99.9 percentile. JBIMS is one of the most premier and exclusive management institutes in India. This is why the admission criteria for admission at JBIMS is very high to only select the elite candidates for management admission. The CMAT cutoff for JBIMS is expected to be above the 99 percentile this academic year.

    Are CMAT cutoff based on raw score or percentile score?

    The CMAT cutoff is based on the CMAT percentile score since percentile scores are a better indicator of a candidate’s performance. Due to the fact that MBA colleges use the CMAT percentile to choose and narrow down applicants for admission, it is more important than the CMAT score. The top MBA programs consider both sectional and overall percentiles when choosing which applicants to accept based on CMAT performance.

    What is the CMAT cutoff for top MBA colleges in India?

    The CMAT cutoff for top MBA colleges in India like Jamnalal Bajaj Institute of Management Studies (JBIMS), Sydenham Institute of Management Studies And Research And Entrepreneurship Education (SIMSREE), Goa Institute of Management (GIM), and others falls above the 95 percentile. Candidates must score above 350 to secure MBA admission at some of these colleges through CMAT.

    What is a good score in CMAT?

    A good score in CMAT is a percentile score above the 90 percentile since most of the top B-schools accepting CMAT scores set their CMAT cutoff above the 90 percentile to only select the best candidates for MBA courses. Therefore, candidates must aim to score at least 300 or more to be eligible for the MBA selection process for top colleges accepting CMAT.

    Are CMAT cutoffs the only MBA admission criteria?

    No, CMAT cutoffs are not the only MBA admission criteria through CMAT. Candidates must have an excellent academic and professional profile to be accepted for MBA courses at some of the best colleges in India. Therefore, candidates seeking MBA admission through CMAT must also work on other aspects of the exam.

    What is the CMAT cutoff 2026?

    The CMAT cut off 2026 is the minimum score, rank or percentile that a candidate must have secured in the CMAT exam in any given year, as required by the candidate’s desired institution. The CMAT cut-off is released individually by each college accepting the test, which is determined by considering several factors including the number of seats in a college, the number of students passing the test and many more.
