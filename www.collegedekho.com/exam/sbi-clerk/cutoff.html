Factors Determining SBI Clerk Cut Off 2025

Aspiring students who wish to sit for the SBI Clerk exam should keep an eye out for the factors that determine these cutoff scores. Check out the factors that determine the SBI Clerk Cutoff scores as listed below:

  • Total number of applicants appearing for the SBI Clerk 2025 Exam
  • Difficulty level of the examination
  • Total number of seats to be offered
  • Total number of candidates qualifying for the SBI Clerk 2025 Exam
  • Previous years’ cut-off.

Steps to Check SBI Clerk Cut Off 2025

Check out the steps given below if you wish to check the SBI Clerk 2025 Cutoff:

  • Visit the official website of the SBI Clerk Exam 2025.
  • On the homepage of their official website, a link to access the SBI cut-off will be given.
  • Click on that link.
  • Clicking on the link will lead the applicants to a new page.
  • On this page, a dialogue box will appear, asking the applicants to fill in their login credentials to check the cutoff scores.
  • Applicants should enter all the required login credentials.
  • After the applicants have logged in successfully, a new page will open up with the cut-off scores of SBI Clerk 2025.
  • The applicants are advised to go through the cut-off scores in detail for future reference.

FAQs about SBI Clerk Cut Off

When will the SBI Clerk Prelims Results 2025 be released?

The SBI Clerk 2025 Prelims examination is expected to be conducted in September 2025. Shortly after the exam, the SBI Clerk Prelims results will be published on the official website of the State Bank of India.

What is the exam date of the SBI Clerk examination 2025?

The exam dates for SBI Clerk 2025 will be released along with the official notification in August 2025.

When will the SBI Clerk 2025 result be declared?

The SBI Clerk 2025 results will be announced once the Prelims and Mains examinations have been successfully conducted. Stay updated with the official website of the State Bank of India to get insight into the SBI Clerk 2025 exam dates.

What is the fee for the SBI Clerk exam in 2025?

Students belonging to the General and OBC categories are required to pay a sum of INR 750 to register for the SBI Clerk exam. Students from other reserved categories are exempted from the registration fee.

Is the SBI Cutoff released state-wise?

Yes, the State Bank of India releases the SBI Clerk Cutoff scores in a state-wise manner.

When will the SBI Clerk 2025 Cutoff be released?

The SBI Clerk 2025 Cutoff will be released by the State Bank of India after the SBI Clerk results 2025 have been announced. The important dates for SBI Clerk examination 2025 will be released in August 2025.
