NEET PG 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Students must meet the NEET PG 2026 eligibility criteria to be eligible for the NEET PG exam 2026. Below mentioned is the detailed NEET PG 2026 eligibility criteria for students' reference. 

1. Nationality:

  • Candidates who have Indian citizenship can apply for the NEET PG 2026 exam.

  • Applicants who are currently overseas citizens of India and have passed the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination can apply for the NEET PG 2026.

  • Foreign nationality candidates can take a shot at the NEET PG 2026 exam.

2. Academic Qualification:

  • Candidates need to possess an MBBS degree or provisional MBBS certification to become eligible for the NEET PG 2026 exam as stated by the Indian Medical Council Act 195.

  • Aspirants who have permanent or provisional registration certificates of MBBS qualification that are issued by the State Medical Council or the Medical Council of India can attempt the NEET 2026 PG exam.

  • A 1-year, on-site internship is a must to be eligible for the NEET PG 2026 exam. The internship has to be completed before August 11, 2026.

3. Age Limit:

  • Candidates of all ages can appear for the NEET PG 2026 exam as there’s no age restriction.

NEET PG 2026 Preparation Tips

To succeed in the NEET PG 2026 exams, it's vital to craft a well-organised study plan, not only ensuring a high score but also boosting confidence significantly. Students can find how to prepare for the NEET PG exam 2026 given below for their reference.

  1. Know the Syllabus and Exam Pattern: Before starting students' preparation, thoroughly understand the NEET PG 2026 syllabus and exam pattern. This clarity will serve as a roadmap throughout the students' study journey.

  2. Deep Learning is Key: Focus on understanding each topic deeply. Instead of memorising information, build a strong foundation of concepts.

  3. Use Sticky Notes for Revision: Create sticky notes to consolidate information in one place. These notes come in handy for quick revision before bedtime, enhancing retention.

  4. Take Regular Breaks: Schedule timely breaks to maintain a healthy balance. This practice is crucial for optimal performance in the NEET PG 2026 exams.

  5. Review Previous Years' Papers: Analyse past years' question papers to identify frequently asked topics. This insight into the exam pattern will help students focus on high-priority areas.

  6. Practice with Mock Tests: Regularly attempt mock tests and solve previous years' papers. This familiarises students with the exam format, improves time management, and refines problem-solving skills.

  7. Clear Doubts Promptly: Address all doubts promptly. Seek help from teachers, classmates, or online resources to maintain a clear understanding of the topics.

  8. Prioritise Health and Confidence: Maintain a healthy lifestyle by eating well and getting sufficient sleep. A positive mindset significantly impacts performance during the NEET PG 2026 exam.

FAQs about NEET PG

Who conducts the NEET PG 2026 exam?

NBE is the official authority to conduct NEET PG 2026 exam at designated centres across the country. Its official website is https://natboard.edu.in/.

How to apply for NEET PG 2026?

Aspirants need to register to submit their application form for NEET PG 2026 exam. Registration is followed by submitting details in the application form, paying the application fee, uploading relevant documents, and submitting the complete form for approval.

Does NBE send the admit card of NEET PG 2026 to candidates offline?

No. Aspirants have to download the NEET PG 2026 admit card online from the official website.

In total, how many questions are asked in the NEET PG 2026 exam?

NEET PG exam 2026 comprises 200 questions that are divided into 3 parts- paraclinical, preclinical, and clinical. All questions are asked from the subjects falling under these sections.

Which institutes do not grant admissions based on NEET PG 2026 scores?

AIIMS - New Delhi and other branches, JIPMER - Puducherry, PGIMER - Chandigarh, Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology- Trivandrum, and NIMHANS - Bengaluru are the colleges that don’t accept NEET PG scores.

Is there any sectional time limit in the NEET PG exam?

Yes, there's sectional time limit in the NEET PG exam. Students have 42 minutes to attempt all questions per section. 

Do I need to attach a photograph to NEET PG Admit Card 2026?

Yes, aspirants need to attach a passport-size photograph on the downloaded NEET PG admit card 2026.

How much do I have to pay to apply for NEET PG 2026?

The NEET PG 2026 exam fee for general category candidates is expected to be Rs 5,015 (including GST). For SC/ ST and PwD aspirants, the exam fee is likely to be from Rs 2,750 to Rs 3,835.

When will NBE issue admit card for NEET PG 2026?

The NEET PG admit card 2026 is expected to be released a few weeks before the exam day. Aspirants need to carry the hall ticket to the test centre without fail. Students also have to bring a government-approved ID for complete verification.

What time will the NEET PG 2026 exam be conducted?

NEET PG 2026 exam time is not out yet. Aspirants are advised to reach an hour before the reporting time at the designated NEET PG 2026 centre.

Will NEET PG 2026 be MCQ-based?

Yes, each and every question of NEET PG 2026 will be of a multiple-choice type.
