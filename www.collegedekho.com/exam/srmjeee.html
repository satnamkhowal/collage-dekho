The SRMJEEE 2026 exam is a university-level entrance examination that is conducted in order to offer admissions into various engineering courses. Conducted thrice a year, this exam is held in an online mode. Qualifying for this exam enables you to take admission in various courses across Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral courses in Engineering, Management, Medicine, Science, Humanities, and Health Sciences.

SRMJEEE 2026 Exam

SRM University conducts the SRMJEEE 2026 exam for admission to various B.Tech programs at its campuses. The SRMJEEE exam 2026 will be held in three phases in an online, remote proctored mode. The conducting authority has announced the exam dates for the upcoming session 2026. As per the official schedule, SRMJEEE phase 1 exam will be held from April 23-28, followed by phase 2 exam from June 10-15, and finally, phase 3 exam on July 4 & 5, 2026. SRMJEEE 2026 Eligibility Criteria

If you are willing to apply for the SRMJEEE entrance exam 2026, you must check if you meet the SRMJEEE 2026 eligibility criteria set by the conducting authorities. The eligibility criteria are a set of conditions that all applicants must fulfill for the admissions process. These include various parameters, like the applicant's age, nationality, domicile, academic qualification, qualifying subjects, and so on. 

1. Age Limit: You must be at least 16 years and 6 months old.

2. Educational Qualification: You must have passed the 12th grade with a grade point average of 60% from a recognized board or university.

3. Subjects: You must pass the qualifying test in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/ Biology/ Botany, and Zoology/ Biotechnology.

4. Nationality: You must be of Indian origin, a Non-Resident Indian (NRI), a PIO, or an OCI card holders are eligible to take the admission test.

5. Appearing Exam: Applicants who are scheduled to take the 12th grade in 2026 are also eligible to apply.

6. Kashmiri Migrants: Those who are Kashmiri migrants are eligible for the SRMJEEE 2026 exam.

SRMJEEE 2026 Application Form

SRMIST has activated the SRMJEEE 2026 application form link on its official website. During the process of filling out the SRMJEEE 2026 application form, you must provide accurate personal and academic details, upload the necessary documents, and make the required fee payment within the given deadline.

Steps to Fill Out SRMJEEE Application Form 2026

To fill out the online SRMJEEE 2026 application form, you must follow the steps given below. 

Step 1: Registration

You must start by visiting the SRMJEEE 2026 official website and clicking on the SRMJEEE registrations 2026 link. To finish the SRMJEEE 2026 registration process online, you need to provide information such as your name, Email ID, Mobile Number, Password, City, State, and Captcha code. Upon the successful submission of the aforementioned information, an email will be sent to your registered email address. Also, an SRMJEEE login will be established.

Step 2: Email Verification

After successfully registering, you must now validate the email you received from the authorities.

Step 3: SRMJEEE Application Form Fill-Up

You must now log in to the exam site and fill in all the required information on the SRMJEEE 2026 application form.

Step 4: Document Uploading

You must upload all the required documents, like educational qualification, passport-size photo, signature, residence proof, etc in the specimen described by authorities. 

Step 5: Payment of the Application Fee

Click on the "Make payment" button to be brought to the payment page, where you must pay the Rs 1200 application fee in online mode. If you intend to sit the SRMJEEE exam 2026 more than once, you must pay Rs. 600 for each phase (1200+600+600).

Step 6: Submission of Application Form

At the final stage, aspirants must check the declaration box and submit their SRMJEEE application form 2026.

SRMJEEE 2026 Admit Card

The SRMJEEE 2026 admit card will be released for registered candidates a few days before the examination. You will be required to have the SRMJEEE 2026 admit card/hall ticket with you during the examination. The admit card carries some of the most important exam information, such as the roll number, exam center name, exam subject codes, etc. 

Some important points related to the SRMJEEE 2026 admit card can be checked below. 

  • The admit card of SRMJEEE 2026 will be released to those who complete the registration and the slot booking process.

  • You will be able to download the hall ticket from the official website, srmist.edu.in

  • The admit card will be made available through online mode only, and SRMIST will not dispatch physical admit cards.

  • You must download and keep a copy of the SRMJEEE admit card 2026 as the same needs to be uploaded on the day of the examination.

  • You must carefully check all the details mentioned in the SRMJEEE 2026 admit card to avoid discrepancies/errors. In case of such an event, you must inform the exam conducting authorities immediately for rectification. 

Steps to download the SRMJEEE 2026 Admit card

Once the SRMIST releases the SRMJEEE 2026 admit cards, you must follow the steps mentioned below to download your admit card. 

  • Go to the official website srmist.edu.in.

  • Complete the slot booking process.

  • Once slot booking is done, the SRMJEEE 2026 admit card will be generated automatically.

  • You must download the hall ticket and take a printout for future reference.

SRMJEEE Mock Test 2026

You are advised to take the SRMJEEE 2026 mock test series to learn how the entrance exam will be conducted. The duration of the mock test will be 20 minutes, and you will be invigilated/proctored by a remote proctor who will be observing you via webcam during the test.

  • A photograph of you will be taken; You must ensure the photograph meets the requirements.

  • The SRMJEEE Mock test will begin.

Do’s and Don’ts:

Here is a set of SRMJEEE exam do's and don'ts to be followed by the examinees:

  • You must not change or move from your seats during the examination. They shall not be allowed to take breaks during the test. 

  • You must not sit for the exam wearing headphones/goggles

  • You must face the computer or laptop screen throughout the exam

  • Examinees are not allowed to keep digital watches, mobile phones, or any other electronic gadgets during the test

  • You taking the remote exam must ensure that the room you are seated in has sufficient light. You must also ensure that you are the only person sitting in the room. There should be no noise from outside or the surroundings. 

  • You are not allowed to communicate or take guidance from anyone during the examination

  • Examinees are strictly prohibited from browsing any other website or page on their computer/laptop/mobile phone during the test

  • You must be logged in from a single device only

  • You are not allowed to read out the questions or instructions 

  • You must ensure that their face is visible and not covered with a mask/clothing/hair while taking the exam 

  • You are not allowed to eat/drink anything except for plain water during the test

  • You must not keep any notebooks/textbooks/study materials/papers with you while appearing for the exam

SRMJEEE results will be released days after the SRMJEEE 2026 exams. In order to access the SRMJEEE result, you must provide your login credentials, i.e., NetID and password. Only those who pass the exam with the qualifying marks will be shortlisted for the SRMJEEE 2026 counselling process as per the selection criteria specified by the institute.

SRMJEEE Counselling 2026

The students who qualify for the SRMJEEE exam 2026 with a valid rank shall be eligible to participate in SRMJEEE counselling 2026. SRM University conducts individual counselling for each phase of the exam. Seats are assigned to you depending on your performance in the admission exam, choices made, and seat availability at the participating colleges. You must first register on the portal of the institute and pay the counselling fees. You must select your preferred SRMIST campus and courses based on the rankings. The SRMJEEE 2026 counselling and choice filling will be conducted separately for each phase of the exam.

SRMJEEE 2026 Counselling Process

  • You must first log in to the portal, i.e. www.srmist.edu.in login and check the rank list, since only those mentioned in the SRMJEEE 2026 rank list are eligible

  • The next stage is for qualifying students to adjust their course and school preferences

  • Seats will be assigned based on the choices indicated, the availability of seats, and the cutoff.

  • Students who have received a confirmed seat allotment must pay the entrance costs. Document verification is also required.

  • Your admission to the institute will be verified once payment is received

FAQs about SRMJEEE

How can I prepare for SRM entrance exam?

You can prepare for the SRM entrance exam by studying the complete syllabus, solving previous year's papers, mock tests, doing revision, etc.

What is the total mark for SRMJEEE 2026?

The SRMJEEE 2026 exam will be held for a total of 125 marks. 

Is the SRM exam easy?

The SRMJEEE engineering entrance exam is not as tough compared to JEE Main and BITSAT. However,  it is a little tough as it is a national-level engineering exam, so you must do proper exam preparation to crack the test. 

What is the pattern of the SRMJEEE exam 2026?

The SRMJEEE 2026 exam will be held in online mode as a Remote Proctored Online Test. The paper will be held for a total of 130 marks and will include MCQ-type questions. There will be no negative marking in SRMJEEE 2026.

Where can I check the SRMJEEE Syllabus 2026?

The syllabus of SRMJEEE 2026 will be released on the official website - srmist.edu.in.

What is a good score in SRMJEEE 2026 exam?

Generally, 90+ marks are considered a good score in the SRMJEEE exam. 

Is 17000 a good rank in SRMJEEE exam?

17000 should be an average rank in the SRMJEEE exam.

When can I apply for SRMJEEE 2026?

The SRMJEEE 2026 registration and application form filling process for both phases of the exam will start tentatively from November 2025. You can apply for SRMJEEE 2026 exam from the official exam website. 

When will the SRMJEEE 2026 result be declared?

The SRMJEEE Phase 1 result will be declared in May 2026. The Phase 2 and Phase 3 SRMJEEE result will be released once exams for the both phases are completed.

Which branch is best in SRM University?

Due to the contemporary era of computer and technology, computer science and engineering is the best branch to join at SRM University since there are 100% placements, notably in CSE, and CSE offers higher employment chances in the future.

How many times SRM exam is conducted?

For the academic year 2026, SRM University will conduct the SRMJEEE exam 2025 in 3 phases.

Can I join SRM without an entrance exam?
