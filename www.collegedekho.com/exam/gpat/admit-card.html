How to Download GPAT Admit Card 2026?

Candidates must follow the below mentioned steps in order to download the admit card of GPAT 2026

  • First, candidates must visit the official website of the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) 
  • Next, candidates are required to click on the "GPAT Admit Card" link, mentioned on the official website
  • Candidates must enter their login details such as GPAT application number, date of birth and security code to access the admit card. The login credentials are generated at the time of the GPAT registration process

  • Now, the admit card of GPAT 2026 will be displayed on the screen

  • At this point, all candidates must carefully verify all the important information mentioned on the admit card of GPAT 2026

  • Then, click on the "Download Admit Card" option that will appear on the screen

  • Finally, download and take several printout copies of the admit card of GPAT 2026 for future reference

Common Errors on GPAT Admit Card 2026

Before we discuss the steps to rectify errors on GPAT Admit Card, let's first take a look at some common errors that candidates may come across:

  1. Incorrect spelling of name, parent's name, or address
  2. Wrong exam center or exam date
  3. Incorrect photograph or signature
  4. Errors in category or gender
  5. Technical errors such as blank spaces, missing information, or printing errors

Steps to Rectify Errors on Pharmacy Admit Card

For instances wherein a candidate finds any errors on their GPAT Admit Card, they can rectify certain information by following the step-by-step process mentioned below:

Step 1: Identify the error

The first step is to carefully review the Admit Card and identify the error. It is recommended to check the Admit Card as soon as it is released to ensure that any errors are identified early.

Step 2: Contact the Official Authorities

After identifying the problem, candidates must immediately contact the NBEMS-GPAT helpline to enquire about the correction process. They can do this via email, phone, or in person, providing the correct details and explaining the error. For that, the candidate will be required to provide their application number, registration number, and other relevant details to help the authorities rectify the error.

Step 3: Submit Supporting Documents

To verify the candidate's claim, candidates may be asked to submit supporting documents to prove the correct information in some cases. For example, if the name is misspelled, the candidate may be asked to submit a copy of their Aadhaar Card or other government-issued ID.

Step 4: Download the Corrected Pharmacy Admit Card

Once the correction is made, candidates can download the corrected Admit Card for GPAT from the official website using their application number and password. They should verify all details on the corrected Admit Card before taking a printout.

Guidelines For Admit Card of GPAT 2026

Mentioned below are some of the important guidelines for GPAT Admit Card 2026 that every candidate should keep in mind to appear for the upcoming GPAT exam 2026:

  • All candidates are required to report to the exam centre at least 30 minutes before the time of the commencement of the examination as mentioned on the GPAT admit card.
  • All candidates must not forget to attach their recent (coloured) Passport-size Photograph to their GPAT 2026 hall ticket before entering the examination hall. 
  • The GPAT admit card 2026 will act as a hall ticket for candidates appearing for the GPAT examination, and therefore, failing to produce the same may land in rejection of the candidate's entry into the exam hall on the day examination. 
  • All candidates are required to carry a Government issued Identity Proof (Aadhar Card/Voter's Card/Passport) with themselves on the day of examination. 
  • All candidates should carefully verify all the details mentioned on the pharmacy admit card, therefore, to avoid any discrepancy or errors. In case the same occurs, then they must immediately contact the help desk of AICTE.

Forgot GPAT Admit Card 2026 Application Number?

It is possible that the candidates might forget the application number for GPAT 2026 admit card. In such cases, candidates can retrieve it by following the below-mentioned steps. Students must first check the message or email ID registered during the application process. If the application number is still not available follow these steps –

  • Visit the official website, gpat.nta.nic.in

  • Click on the login window and select Forgot Application Number

  • Enter the details asked such as Name, Date of Birth, Father’s Name, Mother’s Name, and Security Pin

  • The application number will now be visible on the screen and sent to the registered contact number/email ID

  • Candidates are advised to save the message/ email

How to Retrieve GPAT Admit Card 2026 Password?

The GPAT Registration process is complicated and candidates need to input a lot of crucial information. They also have to upload several documents. Under this long-drawn process, it is common that students might forget their student account password. But, there is no need to worry in such a scenario. NBEMS has made provisions for the convenience of students. Candidates can follow the following steps to retrieve GPAT admit card password

  • Visit the admit card link on the official website, when available
  • Click enter the application number 
  • Click forgot password
  • An OTP will be sent to the registered mobile number of the candidate
  • Choose from the suitable option from 3 options available to retrieve the password
  • Candidates can then reset their password

Permitted Items Inside GPAT Exam Centre

For taking the entrance examination, candidates must carry the following documents along with them:

  • Any one of the original government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar card, voter ID card, PAN card, etc.)

  • GPAT Admit card

  • A Passport Size Photograph (same as that uploaded in the application form)

  • PwD certificate issued by the Competent Authority (for a candidate claiming relaxation under PwD category)

Prohibited Items Inside GPAT Exam Centre

All those candidates who are going to appear for the entrance exam may note that the following items are prohibited inside the exam center of GPAT: 

  • Electronic Devices such as Mobiles Phones, Tablets, Digital Calculators, Smart Watches, etc.
  • Stationary items such as Notebooks, Pages Containing Texts, Pencil Cases, etc.
  • Edible items 
  • Jewellery items such as Necklaces, Bracelets, Anklets, etc.

Discrepancies in GPAT 2026 Admit Card

Candidates are advised to check all the details mentioned on the admit card for GPAT 2026. In case candidates find any discrepancy on the GPAT hall ticket, they should contact the provided helpline number (0120-6895200) immediately, between the mentioned time – 10 AM to 5 PM. Candidates can carry the same admit card during the examination as admit card is mandatory for the exam. NBEMS will take appropriate actions to make corrections in the discrepancies on the GPAT admit card.

Instructions Printed on the GPAT Admit Card 2026

The GPAT 2026 Admit Card displays a list of instructions specified by the NBEMS, to be followed by all candidates appearing for the GPAT 2026. Refer to the gist of the important instructions mentioned below:

  • Candidates must bring a coloured printout copy of the Admit Card on an A4 size white paper on the day of examination to avoid mishaps of any kind.
  • Candidate's Passport-size Photograph as attached on the admit card must be exactly the same as uploaded in the application form.
  • For candidates under PwD quota, they must bring a PwD certificate issued by the governing authority on the day of examination. 
  • All important information regarding the exam centre, such as exam centre code, address, contact details, etc. are mentioned on the GPAT admit card 2026. 
  • Everything about the exam day guidelines is mentioned on the GPAT admit card 2026 in detail.

FAQs about GPAT Admit Card

Is it necessary to carry the GPAT admit card in black and white copy or coloured copy at the exam center?

As per the directions, candidates will be required to carry a colored copy of GPAT admit card to the exam center.

If there is any discrepancy in my GPAT admit card, how do I get the same addressed?

If a candidate finds any discrepancy in the GPAT admit card, then he/she should contact on helpdesk number- 022-66258304.

Will I receive my GPAT admit card via post or any other offline mode?

No, the GPAT admit card is not sent to the candidates via post. Candidates have to access their GPAT admit card on the official website of NTA-GPAT.

I have forgotten my GPAT candidate credentials. What am I supposed to do to download my GPAT admit card?

The candidates have to follow these simple steps in case they forget their GPAT credentials to download the admit card for GPAT: 1. Click on the "GPAT Admit Card 2023" link that will be made available on this page. 2. Click on the "Forgot Application Number/Password" link. 3. Enter the requisite details in the respective text boxes. 4. Solve the captcha code and click on the "Get Application Number/Sign In" link. 5. Security questions will appear on the computer screen (for password only). 6. Choose the most suitable option and click on "Submit"
