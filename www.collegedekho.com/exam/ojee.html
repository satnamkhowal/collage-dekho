Odisha Joint Entrance Examination (OJEE) is a state-level entrance exam conducted by the OJEE Committee for admission to B.Pharm, MBA/PGDM, MCA, and M.Tech colleges of Odisha. OJEE 2026 Exam

The OJEE, along with the Skill Development and Technical Education Department, Odisha, has released the complete schedule for the OJEE 2026 exam. The online OJEE 2026 registration will commence from January 28, 2026, and the last date to complete the registration process is March 22, 2026. The Odisha Joint Entrance Examination Committee conducts the OJEE 2026 exam for admission to various undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Odisha. The state-level entrance exam is held annually in an online (CBT) mode. The OJEE exam will be held on May 4, 5, 7, 8, and 9, 2026. The process of application is completely online through the official OJEE exam website. The application fee is INR 1,000 for one course. OJEE eligibility criteria are provided by the Odisha Joint Entrance Examination Committee. We have provided course-wise UG, PG and Lateral entry eligibility criteria for the OJEE 2026 exam:

Undergraduate Courses

Here is the table of undergraduate courses you can select according to your eligibility criteria.

UG Course

Eligibility Requirements

B.Tech (4 Years)

- Passed 10+2 with subjects including Physics, Mathematics, and any one from Chemistry, Computer Science, Electronics, IT, Biology, Informatics Practices, Biotechnology, Technical Vocational, Agriculture, Engineering Graphics, Business Studies, or Entrepreneurship (as per AICTE).
- OR Passed a minimum 3-year Diploma with at least 45% marks (40% for the reserved categories), subject to seat availability in the First Year if lateral entry vacancies are exhausted.

- Passed 10+2 with Physics and Chemistry as compulsory subjects, along with Mathematics or Biology.

- Passed a 3-year Diploma or 2-year (Lateral Entry) Diploma with at least 45% marks (40% for the reserved categories) in any Engineering/Technology branch.

- OR Passed D.Voc. in the same/allied sector (B.Sc. students considered after Diploma students).

- Passed a diploma in a related Pharmacy program with at least 45% marks (40% for the reserved categories).

- Passed a 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology as compulsory subjects with at least 45% marks (40% for the reserved categories).

Lateral Entry to B.Sc. (3 Years)

OJEE 2026 Exam Day Guidelines

The OJEE 2026 Admit Cards will feature several guidelines and important OJEE 2026 exam day instructions. You should carefully read all these instructions and ensure that you comply with them on the day of the examination. We have listed down some of the important OJEE 2026 exam day guidelines below:

OJEE 2026 Preparation Tips

OJEE exam 2026 participants should start their preparations at least 4-6 months before the exam date. The initial crucial step involves creating a well-structured study timetable that covers all topics in the OJEE syllabus while allowing enough time to revise and practice OJEE previous year question papers. Start by focusing on significant topics, grasping the fundamentals, and mastering basic concepts. Begin with the more challenging subjects before progressing to the easier ones. Once you have completed the OJEE 2026 syllabus, you should move on to solving OJEE sample papers and OJEE previous year question papers. You should also attempt the OJEE 2026 mock test regularly to enhance your speed and accuracy. This practice will also help you gain a thorough understanding of concepts and easy memorization of formulas. Refer to the study materials and the best books for OJEE 2026 exam preparation.

The OJEE 2026 result is expected on May 31, 2026, in the form of a rank card. OJEE 2026 Counselling Process

If you qualify for the OJEE 2026 exam, you will be eligible to participate in the OJEE 2026 counselling process. The OJEE 2026 Counselling is conducted separately for each course. You must first register for the counselling and then lock in your choices. The counselling cell publishes a list of seats available for institutions based on the merit list and the choices made by you at the time of option entry. OJEE 2026 counselling registration and choice filling generally begin in June 2026. Those who are shortlisted on the merit list will be invited to participate in the counselling process, which will be conducted online.

During the counselling, you will need to select your preferred colleges and courses for seat allocation. You must present the required documents at the allotted institute when you report for admission. Documents to bring for counselling include class 10th & 12th marksheets and passing certificates, registration slip, OJEE exam 2026 Rank card, Passport-sized photograph, Caste, Income and Character certificates. Additionally, you must submit a part-fee payment of INR 500 for the SC/ST/PC categories and INR 1000 for the General category. Along with a letter confirming your seat assignment, you must report to the institution and pay the outstanding balance within the given due dates.

OJEE Cutoff 2026

OJEE cutoff 2026 will be declared after the seat allotments are released. The participating colleges of OJEE 2026 will decide their cutoff scores, which will be published by the Odisha Joint Entrance Examination authority. The OJEE 2026 cutoff refers to the minimum marks required from students for admission in the participating institutes of OJEE 2026. It is necessary for you to successfully get placed on the cutoff list to be eligible for further rounds of the selection process.

OJEE 2026 Admission Process

The OJEE selection process includes the OJEE exam 2026, followed by a Group Discussion and a Personal Interview. After the results are announced, the OJEE Cell will release the cutoff and rank list. The OJEE exam 2026 cutoff will show the opening and closing ranks, and all the colleges will follow this cutoff. During the counselling, you will be given seats based on your rank and the choices you selected in your application. For the admission process, you need to submit several documents, such as the seat allotment letter, OJEE admit card 2026, OJEE rank card 2026, past academic certificates and mark sheets, registration slip, and other required documents.

FAQs about OJEE

What is last date for OJEE 2026 registration?

Last date to submit OJEE 2026 application form is expected in March 2026.

What is B.Tech OJEE lateral entry syllabus?

Syllabus for B.Tech OJEE lateral entry is Engineering Mathematics, Engineering Mechanics, Basic Electrical Engineering, Basic Electronics Engineering, and Computer Awareness.

What is OJEE syllabus for MCA exam?

The MCA syllabus for OJEE 2026 exam features mainly two sections, Mathematics and Computer Concepts. Some of the topics for the Mathematics syllabus are Logic, Sets, Relation and Function, Number system, Determinants and matrices, Trigonometry, Coordinate geometry of two dimensions, Coordinate geometry of three dimensions, etc. Similarly, some of the important topics of Computer Concepts are Number System with general base, Number base conversion, Elementary arithmetic operation, Introduction to the algorithm, and the computer languages, etc.

When can I get my OJEE 2026 admit card?

You will be able to download the OJEE 2026 admit cards from the official website after the completion of the OJEE 2026 registration process. The OJEE 2026 admit card will be available to download from April 2026.

Where will the OJEE 2026 exam be held?

The OJEE 2026 exam will be held in delegated exam center cities across Odisha. There are a total of 30 exam center cities in Odisha. The exam will also be held in Jharkhand, Bihar, and West Bengal. 

Who will conduct the OJEE 2026 exam?

The Odisha Joint Entrance Exam Committee will be conducting the OJEE 2026 in May 2026.

When can I register for the OJEE 2026 exam?

The OJEE 2026 registration is expected to begin in January 2026. The last date to fill out the OJEE 2026 application form is expected in March 2026. 

Have OJEE 2026 dates been announced?

No, the official OJEE 2026 exam authorities have not released the exam dates yet. However, the OJEE 2026 exam is expected to be held in May 2026. 

Will OJEE 2026 exam be conducted in offline mode?

No, the OJEE 2026 exam will be conducted in Computer Based Test (CBT) or online mode. 

How many questions are asked in the OJEE question paper 2026 for MCA and Lateral Entry MCA?

The question paper shall contain 120 questions, which will be divided into two sections. The student are given 2 hours (120 minutes) to solve the OJEE 2026 question paper for MCA and LE MCA.

Is the OJEE 2026 rank card sent via email?

Students must note that the rank card of OJEE 2026 will not be sent via email or post. Applicants are required to download it from the official website by clicking on the OJEE Rank card 2026 download link on the homepage. 

What is the eligibility criteria for MCA course through OJEE 2026?

A student must have cleared bachelor's degree in any stream with a minimum of 3 years of duration with Mathematics as a mandatory subject in 10+2 or graduation level with minimum 50% marks (45% for reserved category students).
