Steps to Download KCET Question Paper

To download the KCET Previous Year Question Papers with Solutions and Answer Keys PDF, candidates are required to follow the steps mentioned below:

  1. Visit the official website of the KCET 2025 exam at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in.
  2. Click on the link provided on the website to access the KCET question paper.
  3. Provide your email address to check the KCET Previous Year Question Papers pdf.
  4. The direct link to download the KCET question paper will be sent to your registered email ID.

KCET Sample Questions 2026

Check the KCET 2026 Sample Questions from the following -

Question 1 - The ratio of the dimensions of the Planck constant and that of the moment of inertia has the dimensions of

a. time

b. frequency

c. angular momentum

d. velocity

Solution - Answer: b

Question 2 - A particle is projected with a velocity v so that its horizontal range is twice the greatest height attained. The horizontal range is - 

a. v2/g

b. 2v2/3g

c. 4v2/5g

d. v2/2g

Solution - Answer: c

Question 3 - A stone of mass 0.05 kg is thrown vertically upwards. What is the direction and magnitude of the net force on the stone during its upward motion?

a. 0.49 N vertically upwards

b. 0.49 N vertically downwards

c. 0.98 N vertically downwards

d. 9.8 N vertically downwards

Solution - Answer: b

Question 4 - The kinetic energy of a body of mass 4 kg and momentum 6 Ns will be

a. 2.5 J

b. 3.5 J

c. 4.5 J

d. 5.5 J

Solution - Answer: c

Question 5 - The ratio of the angular speed of a second hand to the hour-hand of a watch is

a. 720:1

b. 60:1

c. 3600:1

d. 72:1

Solution - Answer a

Question 6 - If the mass of a body is M on the surface of the earth, the mass of the same body on the surface of the moon will be -

a. M/6

b. M

c. 6 M

d. zero

Solution - Answer: b

FAQs about KCET Question Papers

Where can I get KCET Previous Year Question Papers with Solutions and Answer Keys PDF?

KCET Previous Year Question Papers with Solutions and Answer Keys PDF can be obtained from the official website of KEA i.e. kea.kar.nic.in 2025 or our CollegeDekho’s KCET Previous Years Question Papers.

What is the official website of the KCET question paper?

The official website of the KCET question paper cetonline.karnataka.gov.in.

Is KCET very tough?

KCET exam is not tougher than JEE Main, as the state-level exam is based on the state syllabus.

Where can candidates find the KCET question papers PDFs?

The year-wise KCET question papers PDFs have been provided on this page.

Is 120 marks good in the KCET exam?

If candidates score 120/180 in KCET, their rank will be around 351-360, which is a very good rank in KCET and getting admission with this rank will be very easy.

What is the KCET exam question paper?

​​KCET Question Papers include 60 questions from Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology depending upon the course applied. The questions are framed from 1st and 2nd year PUC syllabus and for every correct answer 1 mark is awarded.

Where do most of the KCET questions come from?

The KCET Question Paper includes 60 questions from the subjects of Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology depending upon the course applied. The questions are framed from 1st and 2nd year PUC syllabus and 1 mark is awarded for every correct answer.

How to score 150 in the KCET 2025 exam?

To score more than 150 marks in the KCET 2025 exam, candidates are required to give equal importance to all the subjects namely Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics/ Biology. Also, if candidates are planning to score 150, it is necessary to know the KCET exam pattern, syllabus and the key topics with a heavyweight. KCET question paper PDFs have to be practised regularly as well.

Are the KCET question papers pdfs enough for the KCET preparation?

KCET question paper PDF are a useful resource to prepare for the KCET 2025 exam. Moreover, the KCET previous year question papers can also be used to identify those portions of the KCET syllabus that have been repeated every year.
