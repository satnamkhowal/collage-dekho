Automobile Engineering Course Overview

Automobile Engineering Course is a four-year programme which helps students to learn about designing, manufacturing as well as maintaining automobiles. The study of a BTech in Automobile Engineering introduces students to vehicle engineering which includes trucks, buses, motorcycles, cars, etc. For someone who’s wondering what an automobile engineer does, an automobile engineer is to be held accountable for developing heavy-duty vehicles such as vans, trucks, and pick-up carriages for long-distance conveyance. The Automobile Engineering syllabus includes fluid mechanics, electronics, aerodynamics, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, safety engineering, supply chain management, and other similar topics. 

The field of automotive engineering is concerned with the assessment of repairs and their reconstruction. Courses in Automobile Engineering are offered at both the undergraduate and postgraduate levels across various automobile engineering colleges and universities with popular specialisations such as Aerodynamics, Chassis, Motorsport, Electric Vehicles, etc. As per the Automobile Engineering eligibility criteria, candidates must have passed their Class 10+2 exam with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects, with a minimum of 50% - 60% aggregate marks from a recognised board. Automobile Engineering Admission is done on the basis of merit and popular entrance exams that include JEE Main, JEE Advanced, MHT CET, WBJEE, etc. 

Some of the top colleges offering automobile engineering courses are Jadavpur University, Madras Institute of Technology, Chennai, IIT Roorkee, etc. The average automobile engineering course fee ranges from INR 10,000 to INR 4,00,000 per annum. Some of the top recruiters of automobile engineers are companies like Mahindra & Mahindra, Hero Moto Corp, TATA, Toyota, Honda, Yamaha, Ashok Leyland, etc. Moreover, the average automobile engineering salary ranges from INR 2,00,000 to INR 12,00,000 per annum.

FAQs 
 

Q1. Which are the upcoming trends in automobile engineering?

The human-machine interface, voice recognition interpretation, and artificial intelligence are some of the most important trends in automobile engineering. AI is particularly useful for driverless cars and improving safety measures. Data analytics involves storing and improving the necessary data about the vehicle to ensure that it is safe and healthy. Advanced combustion technology focuses on building facilities and developing combustion nodes to make machines that are low-combustible without sacrificing quality. Some of the newest inventions include Schneider Electric, Awayr, and RS View 32. With these advancements, automobile engineering is becoming safer, more efficient, and more reliable.

Q2. How to prepare for automobile engineering?

If you're interested in becoming an automobile engineer, having a strong foundation in Mathematics and Physics is important. Consider pursuing a bachelor's degree in mechanical or automotive engineering, and stay updated on the latest industry trends. It's also important to gain hands-on experience through internships or practical projects and become proficient in computer-aided design (CAD) software. Learning about engine design, vehicle dynamics, and automotive systems is also crucial. Joining student organisations and attending conferences can help you network with professionals in the field. It's important to stay curious and continuously learn about emerging technologies, and you may even consider obtaining relevant certifications. By balancing theoretical knowledge with practical skills, you'll be well-prepared for a career in automobile engineering.

Required Skill Set For Automobile Engineering Course 

Professionals aiming to establish a successful career in the field of automobile engineering must possess a sound understanding of both technical as well as communication skills which are both soft and polite. The necessary soft communication skills that an automobile engineer must have include effective teamwork, problem-solving abilities, and efficient time management. 

On the other hand, some of the crucial technical skills that an automobile engineer must have a good command in are as follows :

1. Proficient Communication Skills: Automobile engineers need to possess excellent communication skills to collaborate with their team members effectively and efficiently. 

2. Problem-Solving and Analytical Skills: Automobile engineers must be creative and skillful enough to analyse data and come up with innovative solutions to complex problems. Hence, they need to have strong analytical skills and problem-solving abilities. 

3. Teamwork: As automobile engineers work as a team on various projects, teamwork plays a significant role in their profession. 

4. Critical Thinking: Critical thinking is crucial for automobile engineering students as their work demands accuracy, practical thinking, decision-making, and clearness of actions. 

5. Ability to Work Under Pressure: The ability to work efficiently under pressure from the higher authorities is crucial for automobile engineers to prove their mettle in this industry. 

6. Time Management: Automobile engineers must be proficient in managing their time as the automotive industry is highly dynamic and fast-paced. 

The technical skills necessary for automobile engineering are : 

1. Knowledge of Electrical Systems: Understanding electrical systems is the base of the automotive field. 

2. Good Understanding of Mathematics and Physics: Automobile engineers must have a strong foundation in subjects like Mathematics and Physics to excel in this field. 

3. Reasoning Skills: Reasoning skill is a fundamental component of technical knowledge for automobile engineering. 

4. Proficiency in Working with Different Tools: Automobile engineers must have the ability to work with different tools used in vehicles and repair systems. 

5. Expertise in Scientific Method: In automobile engineering, it is imperative to be able to use the necessary scientific method to solve engineering problems.
