Delhi has some of India’s most competitive BTech institutions, with six colleges ranked within the NIRF Engineering top 70 in 2025. These include Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, Jamia Millia Islamia, Delhi Technological University, Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi. The city offers more than 25 engineering colleges across government-funded and private categories, with over 20,000 AICTE-approved BTech seats.

BTech admissions in Delhi are conducted through multiple counselling systems depending on the institution. IIT Delhi admits students through JEE Advanced and JoSAA, while NIT Delhi and Jamia Millia Islamia use JEE Main-based admissions. DTU, NSUT, IIIT Delhi, and IGDTUW participate in JAC Delhi counselling, which offers 6,491 BTech seats and reserves 85% seats for Delhi-region students. GGSIPU-affiliated colleges such as MAIT, MSIT, and BPIT admit through IPU counselling based on JEE Main or IPU CET scores.

The colleges differ significantly in fee structure and academic focus, making B Tech colleges in Delhi fees an important factor for students comparing institutions. Jamia Millia Islamia has one of the lowest BTech fees in Delhi at around INR 76,900 for four years, while Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi charges approximately INR 19.55 lakhs for its BTech programme. IIT Delhi remains the most competitive institute in the region, with the 2025 JEE Advanced closing rank for CSE at 126 (General category). Delhi Technological University and Netaji Subhas University of Technology continue to attract high JEE Main rankers through JAC Delhi, especially for CSE, IT, and AI-related branches.

Placement outcomes across Btech colleges in Delhi colleges remain strong due to the city’s concentration of technology, consulting, and finance companies. IIT Delhi reported a median BTech package of INR 19 LPA with over 1,275 offers in 2025, while DTU recorded an average BTech package of INR 15.75 LPA and a highest domestic offer of INR 85.30 LPA. NSUT’s CSE-related branches reported average packages in the INR 18–20 LPA range, and NIT Delhi recorded a highest package of INR 58.5 LPA. Major recruiters across Delhi campuses include companies such as Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Goldman Sachs, and Deloitte.

Top 10 BTech Colleges in Delhi — NIRF Rankings 2025

Delhi's top 10 BTech colleges are almost entirely government-funded — a unique national characteristic. All six NIRF top-70 Engineering colleges in Delhi are accessible through JEE Main. Delhi is the only Indian city where a single JEE Main exam opens access to six top-70 national BTech institutions.

GGSIPU (Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University) affiliates MAIT, MSIT, BPIT, BVCOEW, GTBIT, JIMS, and 50+ other BTech colleges in Delhi NCR. These private colleges charge IP University-regulated fees of INR 2–3 Lakhs/year and admit through IPU CET or JEE Main. They award GGSIP University degrees — not government degrees — but at near-government fee levels, making Delhi uniquely value-dense for private BTech options.

Top 10 Government BTech Colleges in Delhi

Delhi’s government BTech colleges include institutions such as Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, Delhi Technological University, Netaji Subhas University of Technology, and Jamia Millia Islamia. These colleges admit students mainly through JEE Main, JEE Advanced, JoSAA, and JAC Delhi counselling. Fee structures vary widely, from under INR 1 lakh at JMI to higher-fee institutes like IIIT Delhi. Most colleges also offer Delhi-region reservation through JAC Delhi counselling.

Private BTech Colleges in Delhi Without JEE

Most private BTech colleges in Delhi are GGSIPU-affiliated and accept IPU CET as a JEE Main alternative. IPU CET is Delhi's own state-level BTech entrance exam — covering Class 12 PCM syllabus, significantly easier than JEE Main. All major GGSIPU-affiliated private BTech colleges in Delhi (MAIT, MSIT, BPIT, BVCOEW, GTBIT, JIMS) accept IPU CET without requiring JEE Main.

IPU CET (Indraprastha University Common Entrance Test) is the most important alternative to JEE Main for GGSIPU-affiliated private BTech colleges in Delhi. It covers Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics at Class 12 level (150 MCQs; 150 minutes). Students with 60%+ Class 12 PCM marks but no JEE Main score can access 7 of the above 10 colleges exclusively through IPU CET. Register at ipu.ac.in.

BTech Admission in Delhi 2026

Four distinct BTech admission systems operate in Delhi. The right system depends entirely on the target college. Most Delhi students should attempt JEE Main + IPU CET simultaneously — JEE Main opens JAC Delhi (DTU/NSUT/IIIT Delhi/IGDTUW) and JoSAA (NIT Delhi/JMI); IPU CET opens GGSIPU private colleges as a parallel option.

BTech Placements & ROI at Top Delhi Colleges 2025

IIT Delhi leads Delhi BTech placements comprehensively. DTU delivers the largest placement volume in Delhi state engineering (1,831 placed). NSUT CSE average (INR 18–20 LPA) exceeds DTU's overall average (INR 15.75 LPA), validating NSUT's tighter JAC Delhi cutoff. Delhi's placement ecosystem is uniquely strong — 500+ MNCs with major Delhi-NCR presence guarantee off-campus opportunities in addition to on-campus drives.
