Mechanical Engineering Course Overview

Mechanical Engineering course is one of the oldest and most extensive branches of engineering, centred on the creation, production, and operation of machinery and its parts. India has become an important manufacturing country, and mechanical engineers are very important. It is reported that the demand for competent professionals is high and expected to grow at the rate of 5% only.

Mechanical engineering course is a broad discipline of engineering with significant opportunities for sub-specialisation in robotics, aerospace, automobiles, HVAC, biomechanical engineering, and others. The duration of a Mechanical Engineering course can vary from 3 to 5 years, depending on the level. A Mechanical Engineering student will learn about designing automobiles, electric motors, aircraft, and other heavy vehicles. Candidates who wish to be admitted into mechanical engineering courses can take them after the conclusion of their class 12th from a recognised institution with at least 50% marks in aggregate having Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as the main subjects. Candidates must have a BE/BTech in Mechanical Engineering to be eligible to enroll in MTech Mechanical Engineering courses.

Mechanical Engineering Syllabus includes subjects such as Machine Design, Mechanical Measurements, etc. Mechanical Engineering Admission can be done through merit as well as entrance exams. Some of the top Mechanical Engineering entrance exams are JEE Main, JEE Advanced, MHT CET, TS EAMCET, WBJEE, etc. Online mechanical engineering can be done from reputable institutes such as Coursera, EdX, Swayam, etc. The offline Mechanical Engineering courses can be pursued at IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, IIT Roorkee, Chandigarh University, CT University, UPES Dehradun, Jadavpur University, Manipal Institute of Technology, and more. Depending on the course structure, the offline mechanical engineering fees are approximately INR 1,00,000 to INR 15,00,000. 

After completing the course, students are introduced to Mechanical Engineering jobs that include design engineers, purchase and quality control executives, instructional designers, mechanical engineers, service engineers, etc. The average Mechanical Engineering Salary ranges between INR 1.2 LPA and INR 7.7 LPA.

Skills Required to Pursue Mechanical Engineering

Here is an elaboration of the skills required to pursue mechanical engineering in India successfully.

Technical Skills for mechanical engineering freshers 

Mathematics: Mechanical engineers should possess good mathematical skills, especially with calculus, algebra, and differential equations. Engineers apply mathematics in areas such as fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, mechanics, and other branches as well in engineering. 

Technical knowledge: One must be able to follow up with fracture mechanics, stress analysis, fatigue, and material science, which are needed to come up with mechanical systems that, once in operation, will guarantee safety and durability.

Design: Mechanical engineers must be able to design mechanical systems by drawing on a computer without any help and produce technical drawings.

Fabrication: One must have adequate knowledge of processes that are used to create the form and substance of various products, including welding, machining, and assembling. They are supposed to be capable of creating prototypes and production parts for any integrated circuit solutions.

Communication and Interpersonal Skill Category: Mechanical engineers need to communicate with other employees and clients in that organisation. 

In addition to these foundational competencies, mechanical engineers should cultivate the competencies in the following skill sets: 

People skills: Mechanical engineers have to plan and manage an intricate engineering project. They should be able to schedule, plan, and implement projects.

Problem-solving: Solving complex problems from the stick of mechanical engineers and professionals. Deductive troubleshooting, data gathering, analysis of patterns and trends, and synthesis of patterns and trends into solutions are components of the mix.

Creativity: Issues that mechanical engineers solve require some ingenuity. They should have innovative attitudes and approaches.

Employment opportunities: Mechanical engineers may head a project or a team. They should point the course, encourage people, and prompt them in their performances.

Mechanical Engineering Course Entrance Exams

Admission to the majority of engineering colleges in India is primarily done through the entrance exam. Among all, JEE Main is the popular mechanical engineering entrance exam conducted that offers admission to top colleges like NIT, IIITs, GFTI, etc. There are several other national-level, state-level, and university-level entrance exams for Mechanical Engineering courses also conducted for admission, as listed below: 

JEE Main: JEE Main is the common test for admittance to B.E/B.Tech & Architecture courses and it is conducted by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and right now the National Testing Agency (NTA). Some of the institutes where it is used as the first step to securing a place in the undergraduate engineering programs include the NITs, BITS, COEP, and other Central technical institutes. 

JEE Advanced: This exam is the second part of the JEE Main, and the test is taken for admissions into the IITs—Indian Institutes of Technology. JEE Advanced can be given only by those candidates who have qualified in JEE Main. This examination aims at testing the knowledge and problem-solving ability of engineering students. 

BITSAT: The Birla Institute of Technology and Science Admission Test (BITSAT) is conducted for admission to the first year of its integrated-first degree programs in engineering at the three campuses offered at Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad. 

SRMJEEE: The SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination (SRMJEEE) is conducted for entry into the institute’s BTech courses. SRMJEEE test takers, who have been selected, are admitted to the campus according to the choices made at the time of counselling for a specific course. 

GATE: The GATE acronym means the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering and is conducted all over India for admission to various postgraduate degree courses in engineering, technology, and architecture streams. Though GATE score is used by candidates for employment, especially in government organisations and public sector undertakings. 

MHT CET: The MHT CET is conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra, for admission to undergraduate engineering courses offered by the various universities in colleges across the Maharashtra state. 

WBJEE: West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination (WBJEE) is the state-level exam for admission to undergraduate engineering programs in colleges in West Bengal.

How can I apply for mechanical engineering entrance exams?

Applications for entrance exams like JEE Main, BITSAT, and SRMJEEE can be filled online through their official websites. Registration windows open a few months before the exam date, and candidates must provide personal details and educational qualifications and pay the application fees.

How many times can I appear for BITSAT?

BITSAT allows candidates to appear for two sessions in a single year. However, they must choose one session or both at the time of registration.

How is JEE Main different from JEE Advanced?

JEE Main is primarily for admissions to NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, while JEE Advanced is for entry into the IITs. The difficulty level of JEE Advanced is higher, focusing more on conceptual understanding and problem-solving abilities.
