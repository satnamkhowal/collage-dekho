Hotel Management Course Overview

Hotel Management courses are professional academic programmes offered by different colleges and universities at various academic levels on the basis of the candidate’s requirements. To be eligible for admission to this professional course at the UG level, candidates must have scored at least 50% aggregate marks in Class 12th exams. Some popular entrance exams for courses in hotel management are NCHMCT JEE, AIMA UGAT, AIHMCT WAT, etc. The hotel management syllabus & subjects have been designed in such a manner that students from any stream can easily adjust to the course curriculum. Furthermore, admissions to hotel management courses are accepted based on merit as well as entrance tests. 

A diploma in the hotel management field is another popular industry-based training program for those who need to find work immediately after completing the course. IHM Pusa, IHM Bangalore, and IHM Kolkata are some of the best hotel management colleges in India with a course fee ranging between INR 30,000 and INR 1 LPA. After completing this course, students can pursue various hotel management career opportunities such as those of a Chef, Hotel Manager, Executive Chef or Baker, Hospitality Executive and Catering Officer, or Hospitality Executive or Catering Officer. The typical starting salary of a graduate in this course ranges between INR 2.5 LPA and INR 4.5 LPA.

Types of Hotel Management Courses

One of the most sought after programmes after 12th grade is in the field of hotel management. These courses are provided at different academic levels and are offered to prospective candidates in all three modes of learning - full-time, part-time and online/distance schooling. The various types of hotel management courses are discussed below : 

Full-Time Hotel Management Courses

Full-time on-campus attendance is required of students, as are consistent assignment completions and semester-by-semester offline assessments. As part of the course prerequisites, students would also be expected to engage in giving practicals and deliver projects. Additionally, by actively interacting with their teachers and fellow students, they would get to learn advanced topics and acquire real-life experience in the field. 

Part-Time Hotel Management Courses

A part-time hotel management course is available for candidates who are unable to commit to the regular programme or who are employed full-time in their current positions. The duration of a hotel management course under the part-time programme varies depending upon the type and level of the programme. 

Distance/Online Hotel Management Courses

Similar to the part-time course, distance/online programmes can be taken at any time and from any location (if permitted) depending upon the type and level of the programme. This course is designed for students who are working professionals and who cannot attend full-time or even part-time courses. This course is offered by colleges such as Acharya Nagarjuna University (ANU), Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU), Yashwantrao Chavan Maharashtra Open University (YCMOU), Guru Nanak Institute of Hotel Management (GNIHM), and more.

Required Skill Set for Hotel Management Course

Gaining a variety of job-specific skill sets can help people advance in their careers and is necessary for their constant growth after completing hotel management courses. Below mentioned are some of the qualities that a candidate must possess in the field of hotel management :

Analytical Skills : Most visitors might not even let the hotel know what they need, instead they might have a flexible approach. Therefore, even with the lack of direct communication from the customer’s end, hotel managers must understand what they want and prefer. Thus, there arrives a need for the candidate to be empathetic and analytical enough in order to gauge what a customer may need during their stay. 

Practical Intelligence : Situational awareness and a quick to resolve attitude comes in handy for hotel managers. They must possess the capacity to anticipate problems and know the ways to deal with adverse situations and problems. Candidates enrolled in a hotel management course benefit from internships that give them hands-on experience in the said field that can be later utilised in their actual workspaces. 

Teamwork : Teamwork is one of the most essential parts of running a hotel. Different teams oversee different responsibilities - some take care of the front desk, others take up room servicing and kitchen assistance, and others look out for certain areas of a hotel. Every team ought to be able to cooperate and support one another's activities to run everything smoothly. 

Multitasking : Hotel Managers should be adept at multitasking because if an unexpected situation were to prevail upon them, they should be able to carry out their respective responsibilities and take care of the necessary situation at hand as well. All hotel employees must be prepared to jump in and complete the task at hand. Hotel managers must be multitaskers because problems typically arise in clusters rather than one at a time. 

Cooking Techniques : Students get to learn a variety of cooking techniques and the making of different cuisines in the hotel management course curriculum. They receive explicit instruction in both the theoretical as well as the practical aspects of cooking. 

Excellent Interaction : Being able to interact professionally and with much clarity while conversing with customers, having a confident body language is essential for hotel managers who wish to provide their customers with outstanding service. For this reason, whenever a hotel manager speaks with their guests, they should be friendly and should pay attention to their needs. 

Resilience : Those new to the hospitality industry need to understand that this is not a career made for the timid. Due to rigorous multitasking, a lot of rushing back and forth, and sometimes impatient clients or non-cooperative coworkers, a hotel manager will typically need to manage increased levels of stress to get through a shift. Due to the same reason, a hotel manager must be resilient and should be able to function even in acute moments of stress. 

Flexibility : Working in the hospitality industry often demands a knack for managing unpredictability within the work sphere. Your shifts may vary on a regular note, you may be called upon to complete unforeseen jobs around the office at an odd hour, and you may also encounter consumers who exhibit behaviours or requests that they need help with. So, in order to cope up with all of these tasks, a hotel manager must be flexible and willing to give their best so that the customer’s needs are well taken care of.

Hotel Management Course Admission Process in India

In many colleges, admission to hotel management courses is typically based on merit. For instance, to get admission to a diploma programme in hotel management; previous academic performance is considered when deciding whether to admit a candidate or not. Candidates are shortlisted for admission to hotel management professional programmes on the basis of their performance in the previous year's examination. For admission to these courses, some colleges only offer admission on the basis of entrance exams. The following infographic highlights a brief hotel management admission process in India : 

Certificate : There is no one specific admission process for certificate courses, but students who have completed Class 10+2 will find it easier to get admission to Hotel Management certificate courses. 

Diploma : The admission process is applying to hotel management institutes that provide diploma programmes and registering for admission by giving academic information (passing Class 10th or 12th is mandatory). 

Bachelor’s and Master’s Degree : A candidate with a 10+2 merit of 50% marks in Class 10+2 (for bachelor’s) and merit of 50% in any bachelor's (for master’s) Hotel Management courses is qualified for admission to bachelor's degree programmes in Hotel Management, either directly or through entrance tests. 

Doctoral : A candidate with at least 50% aggregate marks in any master's course in hotel management must pass an entrance exam to be admitted to the PhD programme. All PhD programmes in India, including those provided by institutions of hotel management, are admitted through the CSIR UGC NET entrance exam.

Am I eligible for admission at Dr. Narayana College of Hotel Management, Secunderabad if I have a 5-year education gap?
-lahari -Updated on July-31-20251 Answers
I

Intajur Rahaman, Content Team

-Answered on July-31-2025

Dear Student,

If you have a 5-year education gap after completing 10+2 (or equivalent), you are still eligible to apply to the Bachelor of Hotel Management & Catering Technology (BHM & CT) program at Dr. Narayana College of Hotel Management, Secunderabad. As per the BHM eligibility criteria at Dr. Narayana College of Hotel Management, you must have passed 12th grade from a recognized board (any stream – Science, Commerce, Arts). There is no specified age limit or gap restriction for BHM admission at this institute; only an age range of 18–21 years is sometimes mentioned for BHM & CT, but this appears flexible and not enforced strictly.

I just passed class 12 with 46 percent. Can I get admission in IIHM?
-Md -Updated on Sept-25-20241 Answers
S

Shivangi Ahirwar, Content Team

-Answered on Sept-25-2024

Dear Student,

If you have passed class 12 with 46%, you can apply for admission to IIHM in various like Diploma in Hotel Management, Diploma in Hospitality Management, Diploma in Hospital Management, Diploma in Culinary Management, Bachelor of Tourism Management, BBA Hotel Management, and BBA Hospitality Management. However, for undergraduate courses like Bachelor of Hotel Management, Bachelor of Hospitality Management, Bachelor of Hospital Management, and Bachelor of Culinary Management, you must have at least 50% marks in class 12th if you belong to the general category. There is usually a relaxation of 5% for candidates belonging to the reserved categories. Applicants are required to check the official website of IIHM for detailed information on the admission process, eligibility criteria, and important dates. It's important to note that meeting the minimum eligibility criteria does not guarantee admission, as selection is also based on entrance exams, interviews, and other evaluation processes. In addition, IIHM also offers postgraduate programs such as Master of Hotel Management, Master of Hospitality Management, and Master of Culinary Management for which the eligibility criteria may vary.
