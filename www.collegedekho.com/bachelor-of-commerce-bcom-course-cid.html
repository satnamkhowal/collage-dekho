What is BCom Course?

BCom course full form is Bachelor of Applied Commerce. It’s a three-year undergraduate professional course that covers commerce and other related subjects. BCom is accredited by the University Grants Commission (UGC), giving students many managerial abilities and knowledge about finance, accounting, taxation, and management.

To be eligible for BCom admission, you should have passed the 12th standard in any stream with at least 50 percent marks. Normally, admission is given based on merit, and some colleges offer BCom admission without an entrance exam.

The subjects in the BCom syllabus are accounts, economics, math, company law, marketing, IT Tax & Banking. The program is spread over six semesters, and few institutions offer only specialists. Some of the best colleges for BCom in India include SRCC, Hindu College, Ramjas College and Loyola College. The BCom course fees vary from INR 10,000 to INR 2,00,000 per year.

After finishing the course successfully, students can start BCom jobs such as finance managers, accountants, tax consultants, etc., along with an average BCom salary for freshers ranging from INR 6 LPA to INR 20 LPA.

FAQs

What is a BCom Course?

A BCom is a 3 years undergraduate degree course encompassing important BCom Subjects like Cost and Management Accounting, Economics, Statistics, Organisational Behaviour, Taxation, Marketing, etc. This course is studied by those who want to make a career in Finance, Accounting, Business, or any other Commerce related field.

Why is BCom popular?

The biggest reason behind the popularity of BCom degree is that it clears the basic concepts of accounting, taxation, business administration, and finance very thoroughly if done from a reputed college, thus paving the way for more advanced studies like CA, CFA, CPA, FRM, MBA, MCom, etc., along with a lucrative annual pay package.

Is BCom a 4-year degree?

No, it is not a 4-year degree course; however, if you are pursuing a distance BCom course, you may be allowed to complete it in 4 years, but this also depends on the specific institute and its provisions.

BCom Course Admission: Step-by-Step Process

The admission process for BCom in India includes the following steps.

Selecting a College: Go through the list of colleges and choose a college to complete your BCom degree. Create an examination list of the universities that suit your interests and professional plans.

Check Eligibility Criteria: Consult the college entrance requirements if there are any entrance examinations. Applicants must be Class 12 passed or equivalent from a recognised Central/State Board of Education. 

Merit-Based or Entrance Exam: Most colleges admit students on merits, while others accept students who have passed the entrance tests. 

Eligibility Confirmation: Confirm whether there is any given criterion that you do not meet before applying for admission to any of the institutions. 

Application and Fees: After you have compiled your list of the most desirable colleges, you should always submit your application together with the necessary fee. 

Results and Final Steps: When you finish the exam, you have to see your score. Adhere to the other processes as indicated by the institution to finalise the admission process.

An online BCom is a 3-year undergraduate course in Commerce where all you need is a computer and the internet, and you don’t have to go all the way to your professor to get knowledge.

Teachers, for instance, with the most experience, tend to go for voice-overs to teach concepts in videos. They then allow students to interact through both the pre recorded sessions and live classes.

Most course tutors have recently begun to accept that interactive tools have an impact on student performance since they mimic different learning styles. Tools using pictures, simulations, and case studies have therefore become true assets in the classroom.

A few of the major benefits of online BCom course in India include the following:

  • Study when it suits you best, balancing your learning with your job or private life responsibilities.
  • Overcome geographical limitations and access programs from prestigious universities worldwide.
  • Gaining skills in Internet education platforms and electronic messaging is more and more appreciated at work nowadays.
  • Avoid expenses associated with on-campus living and transportation.
  • Before you decide on this path, think about how you like to learn because studying BCom online can be challenging with needing self-control, dealing with distractions, and keeping your motivation up.

FAQs about BCom

What is the scope after BCom?

After completing this BCom, there’s a lot of excellent scope for graduates in most business fields, be it finance, accounting, marketing, business administration, human resources, etc. Fresh BCom graduates can start working as Accountant, Business Executives, Financial Analysts, Tax Consultants, Marketing Executives, etc. Graduates can also opt for further advanced certifications/ courses like MBA, M Com, CA, CFA, CPA, etc. to gain further in-depth knowledge about a particular field and command even higher starting remuneration along with respect and awesome career growth. 

Which job is best after a BCom degree?

The best jobs after a BCom degree are Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary, Chartered Financial Analyst, Business Manager, Accountant, Accounts Manager, etc. Most of these aforementioned jobs are only available after completion of advanced certificate courses.

Which type of BCom is best?

The regular BCom course doesn’t offer any specialisation types, however, a few institutions across India might offer specialisations in the form of optional subjects like Accounting and Finance, Banking and Insurance, Taxation, E-Commerce, Marketing, Human Resource Management, etc. These specialisations are considered to be some of the best BCom types currently available in India.


 

What is the salary of a BCom fresher in India?

The first gross salary for the BCom graduates provided by companies in India begins at INR 3,00,000 to INR 4,00,000. As made clear, this salary is a relative factor depending on one’s knowledge, skills, intelligence, type of company, and region.

Can I study BCom at home?

Yes, you can opt for either an online or distance BCom course and study at your own pace in the comfort of your home.
