Agriculture Courses in India

Agriculture courses cover a wide range of scientific and technical topics, including Plant Science, Horticulture, Dairy Technology, Food Science, Forestry, and Agricultural Technology. The primary difference is the focus of each of these fields. Agriculture courses are offered at various educational levels at universities and colleges, including bachelor's, certificate, diploma, master's, postgraduate certificate, and PG diploma, in addition to several kinds of PhD degrees. Agriculture course syllabus covers a wide range of technical and scientific topics such as Agricultural Technology, Food Science, Dairy Industry, Food Technology, Plant Science, Horticulture, and Forestry.

Some agricultural courses include Diploma in Agriculture, Horticulture, BSc Fisheries, BSc Agriculture, and Agricultural Engineer. 

  • BSc Agriculture: A four-year course that covers agricultural science, farming techniques, equipment, and other related topics. The course also teaches students to effectively implement modern agricultural techniques and equipment in practical applications.
  • Diploma in Agriculture: A two-year full-time course that teaches students about agriculture, livestock and crop management, agricultural extension, and agricultural chemistry.
  • Diploma in Horticulture: A comprehensive course that equips students with technical and managerial skills.
  • Agricultural Engineering: This program teaches students about the various ways to operate the electrical inputs required in agriculture.
  • Diploma in Food Processing: A two-year course that involves both theoretical and practical training in food production.
  • Diploma in Organic Farming: A course that focuses on the principles and practices of organic farming, which involves the use of natural and organic materials for soil fertility, pest and disease control, and crop management. 

Every year, India administers a number of agriculture entrance exams. Students can apply for exams such as ICAR AIEEA, GBPUAT, and CUET in order to be admitted to undergraduate programmes. Aside from these, websites such as Udemy, Coursera, and edX offer a plethora of online agriculture courses. Students can pursue agriculture courses after their 10th and 12th classes; these are usually offered as certificate and diploma programmes. Depending on the course and the organisation, Agriculture Certificate Programmes have different course duration ranging from a few hours to a few months or years.

Students can find well-paying positions in the public and private sectors with a bachelor's or master's degree in Agriculture. Candidates may be hired as Scientists conducting Agricultural Research, Agricultural Officers, Production Managers, Farm Managers, and so forth. Candidates who are interested in these fields can also choose careers in Teaching, Banking, Insurance, etc. Candidates with a degree in agriculture get a good chance of landing jobs in government agencies like the National Dairy Development Board, Food Corporation of India (FCI), National Bank of Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), and Staff Selection Commission, as well as in the government sector at UPSC (Indian Forest Services), Staff Selection Commission, and IBPS Specialists Officer (AO). In India, average salary for graduates of agriculture courses ranges from INR 2 to 15 LPA.

What are the Types of Agriculture Courses?

As highlighted above, there are several different types of agriculture courses and degree types that students can choose from if they wish to study an agriculture course. Being a broad subject including different study areas and specialisations, aspirants will have to determine the field of study they wish to create a career in, as well as the specialisations they plan to focus on too. However, here we will specifically focus on agricultural courses in India, rather than allied courses. Listed below are a few of the Agriculture courses that students can choose from in India.

  • Certificate/Diploma Agriculture Courses: The first level of education available in the field of agriculture is certificate or diploma courses. Several universities across the nation offer a variety of certificates/diplomas in agriculture courses. It should be noted that the courses will be distinguished by the specialisation of agriculture offered by the institution. Such courses often take anywhere between 6-18 months to complete and earn the certificate/diploma.
  • Bachelor Courses in Agriculture: The more full-fledged agricultural courses start from the bachelor level, primarily with a Bachelor of Science (BSc) degree in a specific specialisation or branch of agriculture. BSc degrees often take up to 3 years to complete and are often the minimum requirement for an entry-level job in established agricultural organisations and companies.
  • Masters Courses in Agriculture: Similar to bachelor's degree courses, universities and colleges across the nation and the globe offer a range of Master's courses in agriculture. Similar to UG courses, aspirants will also need to select a particular specialisation or branch of agriculture to pursue as their master's degree programme.
  • PG Diploma/Certificate Courses in Agriculture: Similar to UG certificate and diploma courses, PG Diploma/Certificate courses also offer an entry point into the agricultural industry, but graduates of the course stand in between Bachelor degree holders and Masters degree holders in terms of qualifications and skills.
  • Doctorate Courses in Agriculture: The final level of education available in Agriculture are doctorate programmes in agriculture wherein candidates actively participate in evaluating current agricultural practices and suggest or innovate new agricultural practices for the benefit of the industry and society at large.

To find out the different types of agricultural courses you can apply to, in India, check out the following information on the different types of agriculture courses available in the country. It should be noted that each degree programme will entail different eligibility criteria and entrance requirements. Therefore, candidates are advised to thoroughly study the admission processes and requirements for their desired courses.

Why Choose Agriculture Courses?

The demand for Agriculture courses has been increasing for many years. Over the years it has increased a lot. It is one of the most popular courses after completing class 12 from science stream. The reason for choosing Agriculture courses is the enhanced growth and career opportunities after doing Agriculture courses. There are various benefits of doing an Agriculture course. The candidates can check the main benefits of choosing the Agriculture course mentioned below.

  • Endless opportunities - The agricultural industry offers a diverse and interesting range of career opportunities with a vast array of quality employers. Although, there’s a misconception that the agricultural sector is just for people who want to run or work on a farm and that there’s little or no money there, the misconception is gradually changing. This means there are a huge number of opportunities further up – and down – the supply chain too.
  • Agriculture is everywhere - Agriculture isn’t only limited to rural areas. Many job seekers assume agriculture careers entail working on farms with crops and animals. However, there are many job opportunities that don’t involve getting your hands dirty. For example, manufacturers, suppliers, and other agriculture companies occupy the cities.
  • Growing industry - The agriculture industry is growing at an incredibly rapid pace and so does the demand for qualified candidates. The job market is booming, which presents a unique opportunity for college graduates to find work quickly, pursue different areas of expertise to discover the niche that best suits them, and ultimately, grow their careers much faster than they might in other industries.
  • Technology - Technology is everywhere, even on farms and fields. Since advancements in technology are continuously evolving to improve productivity, more technology-savvy candidates are needed. If you’re interested in helping to feed the world and have an interest in technology, then agriculture is the perfect space for you to find a meaningful job that will allow you to help shape the future of the industry.
  • Competitive salary - In the current agriculture job market, employers are struggling to find high-quality talent. The shortage in labor has forced employers to keep their salaries competitive in order to attract the highest quality candidates and retain their top employees. This means you’ll likely discover a significant difference in salary for an agriculture job compared to that of the same job in another industry.
  • Job security - Agriculture isn’t going anywhere any time soon. And with the constant demand for qualified candidates across many areas of the industry, you can feel secure in knowing that you’ll always be able to find the next opportunity.

Difference Between Horticulture and Agriculture Course?

What differentiates horticulture courses from agriculture courses, and which should students pursue? Here are some things to consider before selecting a course, along with the reasons why one may be better suited to you than another.

Agriculture CoursesHorticulture Courses
Developed as a Science of growing food crops as well as herding and rearing animals, agriculture courses cover a wide variety of subjects and topicsPrimarily seen as a subject under agriculture, Horticulture courses specifically focuses on the cultivation and maintenance of plants.
Agriculture tackles practices involving the cultivation of food crops and animal farming and elaborates on other elements such as irrigation which facilitates various agricultural practicesHorticulture courses highlight practices such as the cultivation and maintenance of gardens, and cultivating fruits and vegetables domestically.
Agriculture courses highlight practices that are usually conducted on a large scale, wherein, sustainable and effective practices are adopted.Horticulture courses highlight practices conducted on a small scale, wherein the focus lies on the quality of the product while ensuring sustainable and effective practices are involved.

Agriculture Admission Process in India

Agriculture courses in India require a specific set of skills and knowledge, so the application process may differ from other courses. The majority of Agriculture course colleges still select applicants via an entrance exam, which is followed by a group discussion or interview. The following are the admission procedures for Agriculture courses in India.

  • Entrance-Based Admissions
  • Merit-based admissions

Also Read: Maharashtra BSc Agriculture Admission 2024

The general steps to admission for Agriculture courses vary depending on the college, but they generally go like this:

Step1: Submit an online application for the agricultural course you want to take.

Step 2: In the event that admission is determined based solely on merit, pay the registration fee and any other expenses. Entry-level agriculture courses require applicants to take and pass an exam.

Step3: Depending on the process type, those who made the short list will be invited to proceed to the following round of interviews, group discussions, or counselling.

Step4: After finishing the previous steps, candidates must turn in all required documentation and fees to be admitted to the Agriculture course.

In addition, students can apply for admission in agriculture courses through the entrance exam organized at national, state and university level. Candidates can apply for this course after completing their 10+2 education. Some colleges also offer admissions on the basis of the merit list of the qualifying exam. Some agriculture entrance exams are ICAR AIEEA entrance exam, MHT CET, BCECE exam, CUET exam, etc.

To communicate information about newly developed agricultural technologies and the evolving trends in crop variety, knowledgeable and skilled specialists are required in the field. This is crucial since most people in the agriculture industry lack the knowledge or credentials necessary to become knowledgeable about the newest innovations. In addition to the possibility of self-employment, the agricultural course offers career opportunities in a variety of industries. An agriculture degree opens up a person's world to many opportunities.

Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) 

ICAR, which is a division of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (GoI), is a great place for people who wish to work in agriculture to pursue a career. You can pursue a job profile related to research and development while employed at ICAR. Technologists, Senior Research Fellows (Agriculture), Junior Research Fellows, etc. are typical job profiles offered by ICAR. For students pursuing a BSc in Agriculture, it may be a very wise career choice. 

National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) 

Renowned financial organisation NABARD hires a number of agricultural specialists each year to contribute to India's rural and agricultural development. This is one of the best companies for BSc agriculture graduates to work as an assistant officer, office attendant, agricultural officer, etc. if they want to work in banking. 

National Dairy Development Board (NDDB)

With its main office located in Anand, Gujarat, NDDB strives to support farmer cooperatives and turn dairy farming into a lucrative enterprise for rural Indian milk producers. For dairy farmers, the NDDB implements a range of income-generating activities that set the stage for their sustainable way of life. Graduates in agriculture are appointed by the NDDB to a range of positions, including farm coordinators, managers, and deputy managers. On the NDDB website, candidates can view the open positions and submit an online application.

FAQs about Agriculture

What are the top entrance exams for agriculture courses in India?

The top entrance exams for agriculture courses in India include CUET, BCECE, ICAR, MHT CET, KEAM, OUAT, LPUNEST, AAU VET, AGRICET and many more. Apart from these tests, aspirants may have to appear for university-conducted entrance tests that are specifically designed for agriculture course or specific degree courses. Therefore, shortlist the best universities you wish to study at, note down the admission requirements and fulfill each criteria as required.

Do I have to give entrance exams for agriculture courses?

Depending on the institution and the degree you are applying for, it may be necessary to appear for an entrance test to enrol in your desired course and university. In most cases, each university may conduct its own entrance exams, however, some of the top universities accept admissions based on national or state-conducted tests such as JEE, CAT, CUET, KCET and many more.

Who can apply for agriculture courses?

The variety of courses available under the subject of agriculture warrants attention from a varied number of individuals from different academic background. However, many of the agriculture courses are often technical and research-oriented in nature and therefore are perfect for students with a science background. However, the eligibility criteria for each agriculture course or specialisation on offer will dictate who should apply for the same.

Can I get an R&D job with a BSc Agriculture degree?

Yes, the technical and research-oriented nature of the BSc course qualifies graduates to take up R&D jobs in the respective fields. However, with a BSc degree, candidates must also possess certain important skills like researching, critical and analytical thinking skills, out of the box thinking, excellent presentation and communcation skills and more to build a career in the field.

Does BSc Agriculture have a good scope?

Yes, BSc Agriculture is one of popular course options offered by universities in India and abroad that qualifies students for varied career prospects. The technical nature of the degree allows students an in-depth access to crucial information that is key for the development of current and invention of new agricultural practices and methodologies. Therefore, students will gain impeccable prospects, both academically and professionally after graduating from a BSc Agriculture course.

What agriculture course specialisations are available in India?

Universities and colleges across the country offer a range of agriculture courses and specialisations across different levels of education. Among these specialisations include Forestry, Entomology, Food Science and Technology, Agriculture Engineering, Plant Protection, Crop Physiology, Land and Water Management, Soil Science, Environmental Sciences, and many other specialisations. Being a broad subject, the variety of specialisations will vary between universities.

Are agriculture courses hard to study?

Depending on the agriculture course you choose to study, the difficulty level will vary. For instance, the certificate, diploma and PG diploma courses would be comparatively easier than the UG and PG courses. However, being a varied field, agriculture courses sees a number of technical and research-oriented fields, which may be more difficult to complete considering the complexities of the topics, which would only increase with increasing degree level.

Is there an MBA course for agriculture?

Yes, B-schools in India and across the world offer an MBA in Agri-business which has gained traction among MBA aspirants across the globe. The course trains and qualifies students to build a business in crop cultivation, food production and a number of allied fields of agriculture. MBA in Agri-business is one of the top courses with the best career prospects for agriculture aspirants.

Is agriculture a good option after 12th?

Yes, studying agriculture is one of the better option in India and abroad, simply because of the variety of options available in terms of academic courses and career prospects. Studying agriculture opens up a number of opportunities after 12th both academically and professionally, as an undergraduate agriculture course paves the way towards high paying jobs in the industry.

Which agriculture course is the best?

Determine the best field of agriculture course will be subjective to each individual, based on their personal interests and career goals. However, some of the top agriculture course in India include Horticulture, Food Science and Industry, Animal Science, Park Management and Conservation and Environmental Science among many others. Aspirants are advised to determine the agriculture specialisation they wish to pursue in order to select the best university and course.

What is the starting salary of a graduate in Agriculture?

The starting salary of a graduate in Agriculture will depend on a number of factors including the level of qualification they hold, the job role they have been offered, the company offering the role as well as the prior work experience they possess. However, a normal range for a fresh agriculture graduate varies from INR 10,000 - 30,000 per month.

Which are the popular Agriculture branches?

There are numerous popular branches of Agriculture courses to choose from, among which include Crop Production, Agricultural Engineering, Agronomy, Horticulture, Forestry, Dairy Technology and Fisheries Science. Most universities offer such various branches or specialisations of agriculture across different levels of education, therefore, it is important to choose the preferred branch prior to shortlisting universities for admissions.

 

I have secured 56 % marks in my graduation. Am I eligible to take admission in good universities at Masters level?

The eligibility criteria differs from college to college. However, most of the universities require a minimum of 50-55% for taking admission at Master’s level. So, You are eligible but do check the eligibility criteria before applying online/offline.

Can I pursue agriculture related course in distance mode?

 Yes, one can do agriculture related course through distance mode from all the colleges that offers distance education. IGNOU offers distance education in the field of agriculture to the students.

I have secured 56 % marks in my graduation. Am I eligible for agriculture courses at masters level in India?

To be eligible for agriculture at master's level in India, candidates will have to hold a valid bachelor’s degree specifically in agriculture or allied fields. In addition to this, candidates must also have secured at least 50-55% in the final examinations of their UG course along with clearing an entrance test (if applicable), to enrol at some of the best universities in the country.

Can I pursue an agriculture course in distance education mode?

Yes, various universities and colleges across the country offer a variety of agriculture courses in distance education. Institutions like IGNOU, Allahabad Agricultural Institute, Karnataka State University and National Institute of Agriculture all offer agricultural courses in distance education. Students can also take up part-time courses in agriculture if they cannot find a suitable distance education course.

Does it cost too much to pursue an Agriculture course in India?

No, it does not cost too much to pursue an agriculture course in India, however, there are several elements that affect the cost of an agriculture course. For instance, the type of degree programme, selected, the type of higher education institution offering the course, as well as the demand for the programme, along with other parameters determine the tuition fees for agriculture courses in India.

What are top recruitment firms in the Agriculture sector?

There are various firms across the country looking for skilled and qualified candidates in the field of agriculture. Some of these top recruiters for agriculture graduates include DripTech India Pvt Ltd, Heritage Foods, Fumigation Services, Randstad India Pvt Ltd etc. Apart from job options, students can also look out for academic options, suh as a Masters or higher degree, to widen their prospects.

What are the types of Agriculture courses?

The variety in the types of agriculture courses to choose from are immense and depend on the level of education you wish to achieve. For instance, there are B.Tech and BSc courses in Agriculture, MTech or MSc courses in Agriculture as well as certificate, diploma and PG diploma courses in  Agriculture. Therefore, it is crucial to define the type of agriculture course you wish to study.

 

What is the duration of Agriculture courses?

The duration of an agriculture course will depend heavily on the type of degree programme you have chosen. For instance, a US agriculture course can take 3-4 years to complete, meanwhile, a postgraduate agricultural course can take 2 years to complete. Meanwhile, certificate, diploma and PG diploma courses can take anywhere from 6 months to 2 years, while, a PhD can take anywhere from 3 to 5 years.

What is an Agriculture course?

An Agriculture course is field of study wherein all aspects related to the industry of agriculture are taught such as horticulture, agriculture, faming, poultry farming, farm management, dairy farming, animal husbandry, agricultural biotechnology, and similar other subjects. Dealing the large scale production and maintenance of food crops and animal rearing, agriculture courses qualify students for a lucrative career in the industry.
