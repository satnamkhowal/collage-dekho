Marine Engineering Course Overview

Marine Engineering course is a branch of engineering that is directly related to the construction and operation of mechanical equipment for docks, seagoing craft, and harbor installations. The Marine engineer works to build, design, and maintain vehicles used on or around water. This includes aircraft carriers, ships, submarines, tankers, sailboats, etc. Marine engineers are accountable for the ship's internal system which comprises the propulsion, refrigeration, electrical, and steering systems. Marine Engineering syllabus covers the skills and knowledge required to work with ships, boats, vehicles, and other marine structures. It teaches how to design, maintain, and repair critical machinery and systems used in constructing and maintaining these structures.

The field of Marine Engineering contains the design, operation, and maintenance of propulsion systems in maritime vessels, offering a dynamic career path for those sincere about the ocean and have a keen interest in problem-solving at the technical level. A wide range of vessels, including ships, boats, submarines, and offshore oil rigs, are serviced by marine engineers. Marine engineers are in charge of the mechanical, electrical, and piping systems along with the installation, design, operation, and maintenance of the vessel's propulsion system. They are also responsible for the upkeep and repair of the system.

The Marine Engineering admission process is done on a merit basis or via entrance exams like IMU CET, BITSAT, JEE Advanced, etc. To be eligible for the undergraduate marine engineering course a candidate must have completed 10+2 with a minimum of 50% marks along with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Some of India's top marine engineering colleges are GKM Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology, Ganpat University, AMET University, etc. India's average marine engineering fee is around INR 50,000 to INR 3,00,000. After the conclusion of the marine engineering course, candidates are offered jobs as Chief Marine Engineer, Chief Security Officer, Navy Personnel, Maintenance Engineer, Design Engineer, etc. The average marine engineering salary in India is between INR 8,00,000 to 10,00,000 Per Annum.

Why Choose a Marine Engineering Degree?

The shipping industry is one of the most essential industries that propel the progress of any country, particularly India, as it significantly boosts India's and the global economy. This is an extremely fulfilling job with many opportunities in several industries relating to our enormous seas. Marine engineers find employment in shipping corporations, shipyards, the Indian Navy,  engine manufacturing companies, shipbuilding enterprises, ship design firms, maritime universities, and research institutions, among other places. The following are some of the major reasons you should study marine engineering. 

  • The job opportunities are predicted to expand by 9% per year over the next decade, which should be good news for aspiring marine engineers. 
  • The fact that container ships transport over 90% of the world's products emphasizes the vast scale of maritime engineering. 
  • According to studies, the employment of marine engineers and naval architects is predicted to increase by 12% between 2016 and 2026, faster than the average for all occupations. 
  • The Indian government has made substantial investments in the merchant navy industry. 

In Demand Profession

As the trade business is increasing and every year a huge quantity of goods is transferred through the sea, the demand for marine engineers is increasing. Shipping companies like the Indian Navy, ABG Shipyard, and Sea Ports need several excellent marine engineers who will manufacture repair vessels like ships, tankers, submarines, sailboats, etc. 

Rapid Career Advancement

Working in the marine engineering industry entails a great deal of responsibility right from the outset. Whatever your skill set and experience, Marine Engineers and Marine Technicians are directly accountable for creating a safe vessel that can carry hundreds of people every year, and sailors are directly responsible for their colleagues' lives for every second on the water. The precise details of boat construction, high safety regulations, and fast-paced labor provide people working in the Marine sector with a variety of continually expanding talents

Become a Part of Something Big

The marine sector is responsible for conveying more than 90% of the world's trade; without sea transport, the world economy would collapse. Joining the marine sector means becoming a part of something more than just manufacturing boats and fueling the enthusiasm for superyacht racing. It also involves supporting economies, businesses, and individuals all across the world. The marine sector is also pioneering the way for ethical industry practices and commercial and economic environmental responsibility.

Unusual Work Experience

Tired of sitting at an office all day? Jobs in the marine sector allow you to work in an exciting and ever-changing environment. Working in the marine sector means meeting people from all over the world and having unique experiences that no other job can provide.

Long Weekends

Longer vacations and substantial leave allowances are two of the many benefits of working in the marine engineering business. Long durations of absence from home are tempered with flexibility and lengthy periods of vacation time for family and friends.

This profession has a lot of scope in India. With the increase in commercial operations across different nations, ships and aircraft are the most important system of the transportation network, hence marine engineers are needed to manufacture these transports.

Marine Engineering Eligibility Criteria

Candidates who want to apply for the marine engineering courses should fulfill the eligibility criteria factors like educational qualifications, minimum marks, age limit, etc. As per the diploma in marine engineering eligibility criteria, candidates should make sure they have passed class 10 and class 12 from the science stream with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects with a total aggregate of 40% marks from a recognized board. Check the Marine Engineering eligibility criteria for diploma, UG, and PG courses given below.

Marine Engineering Eligibility Criteria for Undergraduate Degree

  • Candidates who want to pursue an undergraduate programme in this field should have passed their senior secondary examination or its equivalent with at least 60% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) and 50% in marks in English either in Class 10 or 12.
  • Candidates who have passed a diploma in marine engineering can also apply for the undergraduate programme.
  • Candidates should be aged between 17 to 25 years. Some colleges don't have age limit criteria.
  • Some institutes may assess a candidate's medical fitness, which may include elements such as constitution, digestive system, genitourinary system, skeletal system, neurological system, vision, lymphatic system, cardiovascular system, ear nose throat, speech, respiratory system, skin, and other tests.

Marine Engineering Eligibility Criteria for Postgraduate Degree

  • Candidates who want to get admission to a master's programme in this field should have a bachelor's degree with a BE/B Tech in Mechanical Engineering/Marine Engineering from a recognized university
  • Score a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in their BE/BTech in Mechanical/Marine Engineering. 
  • Candidate must have completed their 10+2 exams at a recognized school.

Top Entrance Exams for Marine Engineering 2024

To get admission to the Marine Engineering course, candidates need to pass the entrance exams required by the respective desired college. The most popular marine engineering entrance exams are - 

JEE Main

Joint Entrance Examination or JEE Main is an engineering entrance conducted at the national level by NTA. The JEE Main is conducted for engineering degree programmes like B E, and B Tech administered in IIITs, NITs, and CFTIs. JEE Main 2024 is scheduled to be conducted in two sessions. The session 1 JEE Main 2024 exam was conducted from January 24 to February 1, 2024, and Session 2 JEE Main 2024 will be conducted from April 4 to 15, 2024.

GATE

Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) is a national-level exam administered by IIT Kharagpur to choose candidates for master's programmes such as M E and M Tech or analytic courses at India's premier institutions like as IITs,  NITs, and IIITs. The GATE test result is also used to hire candidates by Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs). GATE 2024 was conducted on February 3, 4, and 10, 11 2024.

IMU CET

Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test (IMU CET) is a test administered by Indian Maritime University to select candidates for admission to undergraduate (excluding BBA), postgraduate, and research programmes. Several colleges admit candidates based on their IMU CET results as well. IMU CET 2024 exam date is June 8, 2024.

BITSAT

BITS Admission Test (BITSAT) is an online computer-based entrance examination for admission to BITS Pilani's integrated first-degree programmes. BITSAT has been held since 2005 and is one of India's most competitive engineering admission tests. The BITSAT 2024 exam date for session 1 is May 19 to 24, 2024. While the session 2 BITSAT exam date is June 22 to 26, 2024.

Marine Engineering Admission Process in India

Admission to marine engineering courses can be done through an entrance exam or on a merit basis. It majorly depends upon the college you are applying for admission as each college has its admission gateway. Check the information about the marine engineering admission process given below.

Marine Engineering Admission Through Entrance Exam

The majority of the marine engineering colleges in India offer admission through entrance exams like JEE Main, IMU CET, JEE Advanced, GATE, etc. To gain admission to the marine engineering course via the entrance exam follow the process given below. 

Fill out the Application Form: Keep track of the release of the notification of the respective entrance exam. Once notification is out, the application process will commence as per the due date. Fill out the online/offline application form for the entrance exam and complete the registration process by paying fees, uploading documents, certificates, etc. Once the application process is over, the exam conducting will release the admit card a few days before the exam. Download the admit card and keep it safe.

Entrance Exam: Prepare for the entrance exam according to the prescribed syllabus and pattern. After the exam is conducted the result will be released shortly. Candidates who will qualify for the result will be eligible for counselling. 

Counseling and Seat Allotment: During counseling, candidates can choose their preferred specialization, college, and course based on their rank and availability of seats in the colleges. Seat allotment is done based on the candidate's rank and choices filled during counseling.

Document Verification: Once the seat allotment is done, candidates need to appear for the document verification round. They must submit their original documents for verification, including educational certificates, scorecards, identity proof, and other relevant documents

Admission and Fee Payment: After the document verification round, candidates need to complete the admission formalities, which include paying the admission fee and submitting the required documents. Upon successful completion, the candidate's admission to marine engineering will be confirmed.

Marine Engineering Admission on Merit Basis

Candidates can also take admission to marine engineering courses based on merits. Admission on merits is done through the performance in the previous class. Universities and institutions offering marine engineering admission on a merit basis evaluate applicants' academic performance, taking into consideration their marks or grades in relevant subjects. 

To gain admission to a marine engineering course through merits follow the steps given below.

Shortlist College: Firstly, find and shortlist some marine engineering colleges that offer admission through merits. Keep the fees, course structure, college location, scholarship options, etc in mind while shortlisting some colleges. 

Fill Application Form: The respective colleges will release the application form on their official website which you must fill out carefully. Provide original education certificate, required mark sheets, ID proof, etc during the registration process and pay the application fees.

Release of Merit List: After analyzing your application, the university will prepare and release the merit list on its official website, if your name appears on the merit list you will be shortlisted and granted admission.

Counselling and Seat Allotment: Many colleges offering admission through merits also conduct additional counselling and seat allotment rounds after the release of the merit list.

Note: Some colleges also conduct group discussions and personal interview rounds to assess students' skills and knowledge.

Marine Engineering Syllabus and Subjects

Marine Engineering syllabus covers subjects that relate to the construction and repair of ships as well as other sailing boats. Candidates through the marine engineering syllabus study about, Naval Architecture, Mechanics of Materials, Applied Thermodynamics, Marine Electrical Technology and Electrical Measurements, Control Engineering and Automation, Marine Internal Combustion Engineering, Fluid Machines, among others.

The Marine Engineering course curriculum incorporates several instructional approaches. Practical lessons are included in classroom instruction. Students who are interested in studying engineering and working in the maritime industry might look into mechanical design and development, manufacture, and maintenance of marine equipment. Students are assigned projects to help them comprehend ideas and gain hands-on experience. By the end of the ninth semester, projects and employment training should be completed. 

Candidates in this course will create microscopic micromachines for diverse machine-making activities. The Marine Engineering syllabus is designed to educate students about employment in the local and global shipping industry. The goal of the Marine Engineering curriculum is to ensure that the disciplines covered create all of the related concepts, procedures, research, and practicals required to establish a better scope for the industry.

The Marine Engineering course is a study that teaches students to work in areas such as the Navy, Shipbuilding, Engineering, Ports, Harbors, Transportation, Maritime Universities, Research & Development, Navigation, etc. The subjects related to this course vary according to specializations and institutions. The syllabus includes theory papers and a project paper. The compulsory subjects include Naval Architecture, Mechanics of Machines, Material Science, Computer Science, and Marine Steam Engineering.

Marine Engineer Work Environment

The work environment of a marine engineer is different from a 9 to 5 job. Throughout the course of a project, marine engineers may work in a variety of settings, from an office where they create drawings to the deck of one of the ships they developed. Marine engineers go to shipyards, building sites, and docks to monitor the progress of their projects. Some marine engineers are also employed on offshore oil rigs to ensure safe drilling. They may work for for-profit enterprises, non-profit groups, or government agencies such as the Navy.

FAQs about MN Engineering

What is the job of Chief Engineer?

In Marine engineering, a chief engineer is a high-ranking officer who is in charge of the Marine Engineering department on a ship. Their job is to make sure that all the machines and equipment on the ship are working properly and are well-maintained. They are responsible for keeping the ship running smoothly and safely.
 

Which are the top marine engineering job profiles?

Some of the marine engineering job profiles are Chief Security Officer, Navy Personnel, Design Engineer, Chief Marine Engineer, and Maintenance Engineer.
 

Which are the top recruiting companies in marine engineering?

Aryatech Marine & Offshore Services, Manning Consulting, Indian Navy, Ocean Engineering Services, and Indian Coast Guard are some of the top recruiting companies in marine engineering.
 

What are the constraints of being a Marine Engineer?

A career as a marine engineer can be rewarding but also comes with some significant drawbacks. One of the biggest challenges is the demanding work schedule, with long hours and limited breaks. Spending several months at sea away from family and friends can also lead to feelings of isolation and loneliness. The engine room can be uncomfortable and pose potential health risks. While salaries are good, career advancement opportunities may be slower compared to other engineering fields. The marine industry is cyclical, and economic downturns can impact job security. Working with machinery in challenging environments poses inherent risks of accidents and injuries.
 

Can I pursue Marine Engineering after Class 10?

If you have completed grade 10 and are interested in marine engineering, there are some courses you can choose from. After finishing 10+2 schooling, you can enroll in marine engineering courses. One popular option is a diploma in marine engineering. These programs focus on practical and hands-on learning about marine engineering and typically take three years to complete. Once you have a diploma in marine engineering, you can work as a marine engineer on different types of boats, ships, and offshore oil and gas platforms.
 

Can I pursue a certificate course in Marine Engineering?

If you're interested in learning more about Marine Engineering and want to gain specialized knowledge in this field, you can pursue a certificate course. These programs usually cover topics like ship systems, navigation, and marine technology. To ensure you receive quality education and improve your career prospects in the maritime industry, it's recommended to look for accredited institutions or maritime academies that offer such courses.
 

What does Marine Engineering study?

If you're interested in studying marine engineering and naval architecture, you can expect to take classes in computer-assisted design, fluid mechanics, ship hull strength, and material mechanics. Additionally, many programs offer opportunities for students to gain hands-on experience by spending time at sea.
 

Is Marine Engineering costly?

The marine engineering course fees vary from institute to institute. The average cost of marine engineering is INR 50,000 to INR 10,00,000.

Which course is best for Marine Engineering?

If you're interested in becoming a marine engineer, there are a variety of courses you can take to get there. Some options include a Diploma in Marine Engineering, BTech in Marine Engineering, MTech in Marine Engineering, or GME. Many institutions, such as the International Maritime Institute, Samundra Institute of Maritime Studies, TMU Pune, and IMU Kolkata, offer undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Marine Engineering.
 

Are marine engineers highly paid?

Yes, marine engineers are paid handsome salaries. The highest salary of a marine engineer is INR 2 lakhs per month.
 

Is Marine Engineering a good job?

Yes, marine engineering is one of the most promising careers as it not only provides scope for huge carrer advancement but also teaches a lot.
 

What is the qualification of marine engineer?

To become a successful marine engineer, you have to join marine engineering. For that, you need to have scored at least 60% in your 12th exams. And, if you are from SC/ST or handicapped category, the minimum marks required are relaxed to 55%. Also, you should have completed your previous course study in the science stream with mathematics, physics, and chemistry subjects. Depending on the university guidelines, you may need to pass an entrance test or might be admitted based on merit.
 

What can I do after 12th in marine engineering?

If you're interested in pursuing a career in marine engineering after completing your Class 12, there are several courses you can consider. Some of the popular options include a Diploma in Nautical Science, a Diploma in Marine Engineering, a BTech in Marine Engineering, a BSc in Maritime Science, a BSc in Ship Building and Repair, and a BBA in Shipping Management. These courses can equip you with the necessary knowledge and skills to work in the maritime industry.
 

Which IIT has Marine Engineering?

Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Madras, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Delhi, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Bombay are some of the IITs offering Marine Engineering Courses.
 

Which course is best for marine engineering?

Diploma in Marine Engineering, Diploma in Nautical Science, BTech in Marine Engineering, B.Sc in Ship Building and Repair, B.Sc Maritime Science, and BBA in Shipping Management are some of the best marine engineering courses after the conclusion of Class 12.
 

Can we do marine engineering after 12th?

Yes, we can do marine engineering after 12th.
 

Is marine engineering costly?

Marine engineering course fees may vary depending on the institute, and location. However, the average marine engineering course fee may range from INR 4,00,000 to INR 10,00,000 per annum.
 

What is the qualification for Marine?

To take admission in marine engineering, candidates must pass class 10+2 from a recognized board with at least 60% marks in aggregate with PCM subject combination. 
 

How can I join Marine after 12th?

You can join marine engineering after 12th, candidates have to apply for admission exams such as JEE Main and IMU CET, and upon succeeding those, candidates need to pass an interview round and a medical exam. IMU CET exam is mandatory for candidates who wish to pursue marine or nautical course from DGS approved colleges in India.
 

Is marine engineering a difficult course?

The difficulty level of marine engineering course depends on student to student. It may be hard for the ones who are not willing to spend time and work hard on the course. 
 

Are Marine Engineers highly paid?

Yes, marine engineers are highly paid. A Marine Engineer may make up to INR 24 LPA. An Entry Level Marine Engineer with less than three years of experience receives an annual salary of 4.7 Lakhs.

Is a marine engineer a good career?

Marine engineering provides job chances both in India and abroad. There are positions available in the government sector, as well as commercial organizations that hire maritime engineers.

Is Marine Engineering a permanent job?

A marine engineer's work might be both temporary and permanent. The nature of the employment is determined by dedication and capability.

 

Is marine engineering difficult?

Marine engineering courses may be challenging at times, needing knowledge and dedication to pass the appropriate certifications and further your career. There's also the fact that most marine engineers must be physically and psychologically robust enough to operate at sea.

What is B Tech marine engineering?

BTech Marine Engineering is a four-year standard engineering degree program that trains students to become Marine Engineers through Applied Thermodynamics, Control Engineering and Automation, Marine Electrical Technology, and other courses.
