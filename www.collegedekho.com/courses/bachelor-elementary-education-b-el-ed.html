About Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed)

Bachelor of Elementary Education is a 4-year professional degree course that offers training in elementary education. In the first 3 years of the course, candidates study a combination of theory and practicals. Whereas in the last year of the course they teach directly in school, as a part of their training.The programme is available in both Hindi and English language. The programme is designed to impart subject knowledge, communication skills, child psychology, and pedagogical knowledge.

Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed) Career Options and Job Prospects

B.El.Ed degree opens various employment opportunities in the education sector. It also enables students for appointments in NDMC, MCD, Kendriya Vidyalayas, Navodaya Vidyalayas and other private schools across the country. Other job profiles offered to a B.El.Ed candidate is a pre-primary teacher, junior content writer, career counsellor, proofreader, etc. The average starting salary of B.El.Ed graduate is from Rs. 3 lakh to Rs. 10 lakh. Some of the most sought-after jobs in B.El.Ed:

  • Government/ Private Schools
  • Coaching Centres
  • Education Consultancies
  • Publishing Houses
  • Colleges/ Universities
  • Research & Development Agencies
  • Private Tuition/ Home Tuition

FAQs about B.El.Ed

Can I pursue a BA course along with D.El.Ed?

Yes, candidates can pursue a BA course with D.El.Ed but on a condition that they have to pursue one course via distance learning mode. Candidates can opt to study BA course via distance learning and they can study D.El.Ed course full-time.

What is the D.El.Ed course fee in IGNOU?

The course fee for the IGNOU D.El.Ed two-year programme is INR 12,000 for students who are interested to pursue the D.El.Ed course via distance learning mode.

Can I pursue CTET after completing the D.El.Ed course?

Yes, candidates who have passed the D.El.Ed course or are appearing in the final year of the two-year professional diploma in elementary education are eligible to apply for the CTET.

What is the salary of a candidate with a B.El.Ed degree?

A candidate with a B.El.Ed degree can expect a starting salary of Rs 1.80 to 2.40 Lakhs per Annum.

Is there an entrance exam for admission in B.El.Ed?

Delhi University conducts Entrance Test for admission in B.El.Ed course. Other institutes accept admission on merit basis.

How do I get admission in B.El.Ed?

You can fill in the application form for the B El Ed course at the respective institute. The applications are mostly available online. You will have to appear in the entrance exam (if required) else admission will be considered on merit basis.

What is the difference between B.El.Ed and B Ed?

B El Ed is a 4 years course that can be opted after Class12th. B.Ed is a 3 years course and can be opted after graduation. B.El.Ed is an Elementary Education course which helps candidates pursue a career in primary education whereas B.Ed helps candidates pursue a career in secondary education.

What is the average fee of a B.El.Ed course?

Fee of B.El.Ed course varies institute-wise. The average fee ranges between Rs 20,000 to 1.00 Lakhs.

What is the scope of B.El.Ed course?

A candidate with a B.El.Ed degree can apply for jobs in government/ private schools. They can also get jobs in Education Consultancies, Publishing Houses, Research & Development Agencies etc.

Which colleges in India offer B.El.Ed courses?

Amity University, Lucknow, Sanskriti University, Mathura, Miranda House DU, Subharti University, Meerut, Invertis University, Bareilly, Gargi College, Delhi are some of the popular colleges in India that offer B.El.Ed course.

What is the eligibility criteria for admission in B.El.Ed course?

The candidate must pass Class 12h with an aggregate percentage of 50% to 55%.

What is the duration of the B.El.Ed course?

The duration of the B.El.Ed course is 4 years.

What is the objective of B.El.Ed course?

B.El.Ed a 4-year undergraduate degree course that provides training in elementary education. The course is designed to impart subject knowledge and help them develop communication skills.

What are the top colleges offering Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed) courses ?

I want admission in Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed). Do I have to clear any exams for that ?

Delhi University Science , Delhi University Arts are some of the exams that you can appear for to get admission in Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed) courses.

What is the average fee for the course Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed) ?

Is a graduate with a B.Sc Food Technology eligible for D.El.Ed?
-sarita -Updated on Sept-13-20241 Answers
A

Anmol Arora, Content Team

-Answered on Sept-13-2024

Dear Student,

If you are a graduate with a B.Sc. Food Technology, you are eligible for the D.El.Ed course. However, your marks in the final year of degree examination must not be less than 50% and you must have completed your graduation from a recognised university. Moreover, you must have also completed 10+2 from a recognised board of school education with minimum 50% marks in any stream to apply for the D.El.Ed course. If you are someone who has appeared for the final exam of graduating degree and is awaiting their result, then also you can apply for the D.El.Ed course, however, you will need to provide your original marksheets and certificates later on to finalize your admission.
