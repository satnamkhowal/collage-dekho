CFA Course is split into three levels with respective qualifying exams. The course equips candidates with theoretical and practical knowledge of various investment avenues in stock markets or private markets including fixed-income securities, equities, and derivatives. Candidates who clear the 3 levels of this course are qualified to become analysts/managers in various arenas of investment, wealth, and risk management, or clinch other lucrative opportunities in finance.

CFA Course Overview

CFA Course is offered by the CFA Institute. The course is also known as the Chartered Financial Analyst. This course is ideal for candidates interested in finance or investment management. It assesses their proficiency in investment fundamentals, valuing assets, wealth planning, and portfolio management.

CFA can take a minimum of 2-3 years to complete. There are three levels of the CFA exam; CFA Level 1, CFA Level 2, and CFA Level 3.  However, there is no time limit to complete the CFA exams.  For the CFA admission, candidates must have completed their bachelor's degree from a recognised institute Ideally, UG final-year students can also appear for the Level I exam.

Alternatively, candidates are required to have either a combination of 4,000 hours of work experience and/or higher education that was acquired over a minimum of three sequential years before attempting the exams.

As far as the CFA Syllabus is concerned, it includes the following: CFA Level I syllabus lays the foundation with core subjects like ethical and professional standards, quantitative methods, economics, etc.  The CFA Level II syllabus covers in-depth topics like fixed income, derivatives, alternative investments, etc. At CFA Level III, challenging topics like portfolio management and wealth planning, financial reporting and analysis, etc. are assessed to test competence.

CFA remains a much sought-after professional certification for many investment analysts and portfolio managers globally. After completing the CFA course, candidates receive a postgraduate-level professional certification from the CFA Institute.

Investment banks, wealth management firms, and asset management companies - both domestically and internationally - offer abundant job opportunities for these esteemed professionals along with lucrative salaries. Some of the top CFA jobs include risk manager, investment banker, research analyst, etc.

In India, CFA salary ranges from INR 3 - 44.4 lakhs; globally, this figure skyrockets to a staggering INR 1,12,00,000. Based on the Level of certification and years of experience one can aspire to get a lucrative salary.

What is a CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) Course?

CFA course full form is Chartered Financial Analyst. This course’s details are as given below:

  • CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) is a professional designation course administered by the CFA Institute, USA. It is the world's most accepted and trusted qualification in finance and investment. 
  • It is divided into three levels, each exam is of 6 hours.
  • CFA Course addresses topics like financial statement analysis, mathematics and statistics applied economics, corporate finance, portfolio management, and ethical and professional standards. 
  • Completion of the program happens when one passes three challenging levels that proves their professional abilities in investment analysis and portfolio management. 
  • Holders of charters are highly needed by investment banking, equity research, fund/ asset management, and corporate finance.

Can I study CFA course online?

Yes, various institutes offer the opportunity to pursue the Chartered Financial Analyst or CFA course online or via distance learning modes. The CFA's curriculum texts, which you may self-study with, constitute significant components of the exam; however, many institutes enhance your education by providing video lectures and access to an online question bank. These institutes provide exam preparation, and tips and conduct mock tests to help ace the CFA exams. For the final CFA exams, the physical appearance of the candidates at exam centres is mandatory. The CFA programme has no direct admission process. It involves passing exams conducted by the respective CFA institute. The CFA course incorporates a difficult three-level exam that assesses participants’ knowledge about investment analysis, portfolio management, and ethical behaviour.

CFA Course Exams

CFA course includes three levels of exams--these cover an extensive range of topics in finance, economics, and ethics--the CFA program offers rigorous education and assessment.

Level I:  CFA level exam delves into the fundamentals of investment analysis – asset valuation, financial statements, economics, and quantitative methods, etc.

Level II: This level delves into the intricacies of portfolio management, encompassing crucial aspects such as asset allocation; risk management--a fundamental component in any successful investment strategy—and rigorous performance evaluation.

Level III: This level teaches ethics and professional standards; it also goes deep into advanced topics in portfolio management--specifically, those about wealth planning.

Candidates for the CFA charter must achieve a passing score in all three exam levels.

Notoriously difficult, the CFA exams boast a pass rate of approximately 50%; yet, passing confers significant rewards. High demand for CFA charter holders allows them to earn substantially more than their non-chartered counterparts.

Here are some additional details about the CFA exams:

Four times a year, levels I and II offer computer-based exams; level III offline assessments are conducted twice annually.

Several resources - study materials, practice questions, and online courses - stand readily available; their purpose is to aid your exam preparation.

The CFA exams are done through a computer-based mode and you can take it four times each year for levels I and II, while only twice per year exams are conducted for CFA level III.

CFA Course Admission Process in India

  • Register online for the CFA Programme on the CFA website link (www.cfainstitute.org) to access your account.
  • Enrol in the CFA Programme and pay the enrollment fee and the exam fee.
  • Prepare for Exams by following the CFA Institute study guide, mocks, and supplemental material to pass through the exams.
  • Pass All Three Levels where different aspects of the curriculum are tested.
  • Join CFA Institute as a Regular Member after having successfully passed three levels of the CFA exam.
  • Adherence to the Standards of Professional Conduct and the Code of Ethics established by the CFA Institute.

Once they have fulfilled all the requirements, candidates get the CFA certification, a globally accepted degree.

The CFA course syllabus is a tough, three-level curriculum that covers a broad range of topics in investment analysis, portfolio management, and ethics. Each level of the programme builds on the knowledge and skills learned in the previous level.

Since Portfolio Management and Wealth Planning represent 30%-35% of the exam, more details are provided here:

The title will include the following sub-topic distinctions:

  • PM - Asset Allocation; Trading; Performance Evaluation; and Investment Manager Selection (10%-20%)
Ethics & Professional StandardsCovers the most advanced topics in ethics & professional conduct, including the implications of new technologies for the investment profession.
Elective TopicsChoose two from four subjects: Equity Investment, Fixed Income, Alternative Investments, or Quantitative Methods.

Specialised Pathways

(2025 onwards)

Two new specialisations Private Wealth and Private Markets will be provided along with the traditional Portfolio Management path.

Private Market: Covers the management of equity and other funds from the perspective of a General Partner (GP). It includes all key techniques applied in the workflow, valuation, and other indices that are crucial in the 4 asset class domains - equity, fixed income securities, cash and equivalent marketable securities, and commodities.

Private Wealth: Covers key principles for assisting high net worth individuals (HNIs) manage their assets from a global perspective. It is structured as a process where seeds are sown and later they grow into fruitful trees.

How are Level III Specialised Pathways Structured?

  • Candidates can choose either of the three "specialized pathway" options at registration. Once it's done, the study materials will be provided as per the Pathway chosen. 
  • Pathways can not be changed once registration is completed.
  • 65-70% of the Level III exam topic weight will relate to the "Common Core" while the remaining 30-35% will relate to the selected Pathway. Questions related to the chosen pathway will be a mix of item-set and essays just as for the common core.
  • There will be a mandatory 10-15 hour Practical Skills Module for Level III starting in the same window. It will focus on Portfolio Construction and more details will be duly informed. This will not be graded although completion will need to be documented.
  • There will be no difference in the CFA Charter granted upon passing Level III. 
     

What stays the same?

Common core subjects aim to improve the knowledge, skills and ability about investment management which transcend the three pathways workflows. It will cover:

  • Asset allocation: Capital market expectations, macro forecasting, handling constraints.
  • Portfolio construction: Equity, Fixed Income, and Alternatives portfolio construction, Institutional versus private wealth portfolio construction, trading costs.
  • Performance measurement: Performance attribution, manager selection, Global Investment Performance Standards.
  • Derivatives & risk management: Options strategies, swaps/forwards/futures strategies, currency hedging strategies.
  • Ethics: Code of ethics, standards of professional conduct, Asset Manager Code.

FAQs about CFA

Will the CFA course increase my salary?

Absolutely yes, candidates who pass each level of the CFA exam, will see a substantial increase in their annual salary.

How difficult is CFA for Indian students?

The CFA exam is considered to be one of the most difficult exams in the world. Therefore, it is also very difficult for Indian students; that’s the reason why most Indian students go after an MBA in Finance instead of choosing a CFA course because an MBA is relatively much easier than a CFA.

Is CFA Level 1 easy?

Every candidate encountering CFA Level 1 test will face varying degrees of difficulty. Some will find it difficult because it is mainly comprehensive and requires it to be taken seriously while others having some previous finance knowledge can find it a bit easier. The CFA Level 1 exam covers topics like Fixed Income, Derivatives and Financial Statement Analysis, and each of these is considered to be the most difficult section of the exam.

Are CFAs in demand?

Yes, the CFA course is in huge demand in India due to the growing finance industry and reputed companies needing finance experts to do portfolio management, wealth management, financial data analysis, etc. and keep the company’s financial health in sound condition.

Which degree is best for CFA?

A degree in finance, accounting, commerce, or business administration/ management are good for preparing you with the basic concepts that are applicable in the CFA course. Therefore, you must choose degrees like B Com, M Com, BBA, MBA, B Com Hons, BBM, etc. to be well prepared for the CFA course.

Is 6 months enough for CFA Level 3?

Six months could be enough for CFA Level III if as per the CFA Institute you are willing to devote a total of 344 hours for a total of 6 months which means you must intensely study for at least 13-14 hours per week. 

What is the age limit for CFA?

There’s no maximum age limit to attempt the CFA exams, however, you should be a minimum of 20 years old to be eligible for CFA Level 1. 

Is CFA MCQ based?

Yes, CFA Level 1 and Level 2 questions are MCQ based, however, CFA Level 3 has a few constructed response questions in addition to the MCQs.

Is 1 year enough for a CFA?

No, you can't complete the CFA course in just one year. According to the CFA Institute, a candidate typically takes around 2 to 4 years to complete the CFA course and earn a CFA charter.

Who hires CFA in India?

Many top multinational companies hire CFA in India like KPMG, Deloitte, Amazon, HSBC, SBI, HDFC, Larsen and Toubro, Flipkart, Myntra, etc. CFAs are hired by these firms at a very lucrative paycheck.

Can I do CFA with B Com?

Yes, you are eligible to register and attempt for the CFA Level 1 in B Com 2nd year or in the 2nd year of any graduation. You can also attempt for CFA Level 2 in the final year of your BCom or any graduation. Therefore, you can attempt 2 CFA levels in your graduation and pass them even before completing your graduation course.

Can a 12th pass apply for CFA?

No, you are not eligible to apply for the CFA course after completing your 12th from a recognized school board. You must either complete your graduation (any stream) or be in the last year of your graduation or 4000 hours of professional paid work experience before registering for CFA Level 1. 

Does CFA require Articleship?

No, the CFA course doesn’t require you to undergo any articled training unlike the CA course in India. However, if you have done paid articled training during your CA course then it will definitely count towards your 4000 hours work experience during your eligibility assessment for the CFA programme.

Does CFA require maths?

Yes, CFA is a course which deals with financial analysis and this does require a candidate to be adept in basic maths skills. If you are not good at Maths then you should reconsider opting for this course. Also, you must have a knowledge of how to operate a calculator to quickly solve questions during your CFA exams without failing any levels.

What is the CFA fees in India?

The total CFA coaching fees in India can range anywhere between INR 2,00,000 and INR 4,00,000.

Is the CFA exam hard?

Yes, CFA exams are considered to be one of the toughest exams, even more tougher than top law and medical entrance exams. The average pass percentage for CFA levels hover at around 48% - 50%.

Who gets paid more CA or CFA?

These days CA and CFA get paid almost the same amount. The average annual salary of a CA in India is around INR 7,00,000 to INR 40,00,000 whereas the average annual CFA salary is around INR 3,00,000 to INR 44 00,000. 

Who earns more CFA or MBA?

A Chartered Financial Analyst or CFA generally earns more than an MBA graduate because they are an expert specialist in the field of finance, wealth and portfolio management as compared to an MBA graduate. However, if you do a specialised MBA course like MBA in Finance, MBA in Digital Marketing, MBA in Marketing, MBA in Information Technology, etc. then you can earn a salary that might go even higher than that of CFAs. Generally, CFA earns around INR 6,00,000 to INR 12,00,000 whereas the starting MBA salary is between INR 4,50,000 and INR 6,00,000. 

Is CFA better than CA?

CFA is better than CA if you want to delve into core finance and learn wealth management, portfolio management, investment analysis, etc. whereas CA is a much better option for if you want to dive deeper into accounting, taxation, and auditing along with business laws and basic to intermediate finance.

What exactly does CFA do?

CFA or Chartered Financial Analyst provides crucial financial guidance to other firms/ individuals, helps with financial planning, makes investment decisions, creates solid financial strategies, manages portfolios, and collaborates with other professionals in the field of finance.
