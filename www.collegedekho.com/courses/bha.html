About Bachelor in Hospital Administration (B.H.A.)

Bachelor in Hospital Administration or BHA is a three-year undergraduate degree. The program is opted by candidates who are looking for a career in management, administration and leadership of hospitals, healthcare systems and hospital networks. The main aim of the program is to prepare healthcare professionals who can respond effectively to the diverse and ever-changing healthcare sector.

The course provides students with adequate knowledge and skills to carry out the administrative tasks of the clinical and support services departments. The three-year bachelor level program provides the skills required to carry clinical and support service operations. Bachelor in Hospital Administration is open for candidates who have completed their 12th from any of the relevant streams.

Objective of Bachelor in Hospital Administration (B.H.A.)

Some of the major objectives of the BHA program are provided below.

  • Communicate efficiently, work in communities, set priorities and effectively manage workload
  • Assessing the management, growth and development challenges faced by hospital managers
  • Understand the principles of professional ethics and good governance
  • Review and assess programs using organizational and institutional principles, planning and management strategies for human, financial, material capital etc.

Bachelor in Hospital Administration (B.H.A.) Eligibility Criteria

Bachelor in Hospital Administration (B.H.A.) Eligibility Criteria

Candidates who are applying for admission in the BHA program will have to satisfy each of the condition mentioned in the  BHA eligibility criteria in order to get eligible for admission

  • He/ She must have cleared the 12th(Senior Secondary Examination) with at least 50% aggregate.
  • The minimum percentage required for admission may be different depending on the college you are going for admission.

Bachelor in Hospital Administration (B.H.A.) Admission Process

Admission to the three years Bachelor in Hospital Administration will be done on the basis of scores in 12th examination followed by the personal interview and group discussion round. There are some institutes that conduct their own entrance exam for admission. Candidates are advised to check the admission process of the college in order to check if there is an entrance test or not. The interview round will be conducted at the campus of the college itself. All the information regarding admission can be verified from the information brochure of the college. 

Bachelor in Hospital Administration (B.H.A.) Course Fee

The average tuition fee for the Bachelor in Hospital Administration (B.H.A.) ranges from INR 1 to 3.5 lakh per annum. The annual tuition fee depends upon the college, students are going for admission. Students will have to submit the course fee in order to enrol for the program. Course fee for the BHA program can be checked from the prospectus of the college. In addition, students may also check the scholarship or loan facility offered by the college to know the concession in the total fee.

Career Options and Job Prospects

There are many options available after the completion of Bachelor in Hospital Administration (B.H.A.) program.
