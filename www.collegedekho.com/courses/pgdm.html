The PGDM course, or Post Graduate Diploma in Management, is a highly sought-after program for aspiring managers. It is designed to equip students with essential management skills and prepare them for leadership roles in various industries. Unlike an MBA, the PGDM is more practical and industry-oriented, often offered by autonomous institutes that are not affiliated with any university, which allows them to update the curriculum more frequently to keep pace with changing industry standards and practices.

What is PGDM Course?

PGDM full form is Post Graduate Diploma in Management. It is a two-year Business Management course that can be pursued in full-time, part-time, online, or distance learning mode. It offers knowledge of various aspects of Business Management and Administration and imparts skills to manage various functions within a company. Students who are interested in making a career in Management or wish to start their own business can opt for a PGDM course. PGDM subjects include Financial Accounting, Managerial Economics, Marketing Management, Managerial Accounting, Strategic Management, Business Analytics, Business Ethics, Business Law, and more.

The PGDM admissions process and fees vary in different colleges. Many top colleges need scores of entrance exams like CAT, GMAT, CMAT, ATMA, XAT, MAT, and NMAT for admissions, whereas some offer PGDM admission based on a 10+2 merit-based system. XLRI Jamshedpur, SPJIMR Mumbai, NMIMS Mumbai, MDI Gurgaon, and SIBM Pune are some of the top colleges in India. The PGDM course fee ranges from INR 1 lakh to 20 lakh. PGDM caters to industry requirements and helps management aspirants develop relevant skills to transform into industry-ready professionals. The course adds value beyond the regular MBA curriculum and is industry-oriented, unlike MBA, which is more theoretical.

A PGDM course focuses more on grooming programs, mentorship, and industry exposure. Also, the placement services are more rigorous with this program. Hence, pursuing a PGDM from a recognized institute is usually a great option. The graduates can find various PGDM jobs, including Marketing Manager, Operations Manager, Human Resources Manager, Finance Manager, Business Analyst, and more. Depending on the job profile and area of specialization, one can get an average PGDM salary between INR 4 lakhs to 10 lakhs per year. If you are interested in pursuing a PGDM course, read below for complete details so that you can make an informed decision!

Why Choose a PGDM Course?

Pursuing a PGDM can be a transformative experience. It challenges you intellectually, exposes you to new ideas, and helps you develop as a management professional and as an individual. If you are looking for some strong reasons to go for a PGDM course, then check out the points below.

  • PGDM courses are designed to equip students with a diverse set of skills necessary for effective management. These skills include leadership, communication, problem-solving, decision-making, and analytical skills.
  • Many PGDM courses offer various specializations, allowing students to focus on a specific area of business that aligns with their interests and career goals. Popular specializations include marketing, finance, human resources, operations, and international business.
  • PGDM courses are regularly updated to reflect the latest trends and developments in the business world. This ensures that students learn relevant skills and knowledge that can be applied in real-world situations.
  • Business schools often provide a platform for students to network with industry professionals, alumni, and fellow students. Building a strong professional network can open doors to new opportunities, partnerships, and collaborations.
  • Many PGDM courses incorporate practical components like internships, industry projects, and case studies. This hands-on experience helps bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application.
  • With businesses operating in a globalized world, PGDM courses often offer an international perspective. This may include opportunities for international internships, exchange courses, or modules focused on global business practices.
  • If you're interested in starting your own business, PGDM courses can provide you with the knowledge and skills needed to plan, launch, and manage a successful venture.
  • Pursuing a PGDM can enhance your career prospects and open doors to higher-level positions in organizations. It can also make you more competitive in the job market. Graduates with a PGDM tend to have higher earning potential compared to those with only an undergraduate degree. The specialized knowledge and skills gained in a PGDM course can get you higher salaries.

PGDM Course Eligibility Criteria

Participants for the PGDM course may be from any discipline such as Engineering, Humanities, Commerce, Economics, Medicine, or any other branch of education. Fresh graduates, as well as candidates with relevant work experience, are welcome to apply. The eligibility criteria for PGDM courses slightly vary in different institutes, keeping the main requirements the same:

  • To be eligible for admission to the PGDM course, the participants must possess a Bachelor’s degree or equivalent, with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks or equivalent in any discipline, recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) for admission to postgraduate programs. 
  • At premier universities such as IIM, a minimum of 60% or 6.5 CGPA on a scale of 10 should have been achieved at the graduation level. 
  • There is no set age limit for taking the PGDM course.
  • The bachelor’s degree or equivalent qualification obtained by the candidate must entail a minimum of three years of education after completing higher secondary schooling under the 10+2 system or equivalent. 
  • The applicants to the PGDM course are selected based on past academic performance in 10th, 12th, and graduation; CAT/XAT/GMAT/CMAT score; written test; essay writing; group discussion; extempore, and personal interview. Appropriate weightage is given to the work experience of PGDM course applicants.

Common Admission Test

CAT is a qualifying exam for admission to MBA, PGDM, and other PG-level management courses in India. The eligibility for taking the CAT exam is 50% marks in graduation, for reserved categories the qualifying marks are relaxed up to 5%. The test consists of three sections: Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (VARC), Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning (DILR), and Quantitative Ability (QA). The exam is taken online for three hours, with one hour per section.

NMAT by GMAC

NMAT by GMAC is a national-level entrance test conducted for MBA/PGDM admissions at NMIMS University and other reputed B-schools in India as well as abroad. It is a computer-based exam and has three sections - Language Skills, Quantitative Skills, and Logical Reasoning. NMAT 2022 exam scores will be accepted by some of the top MBA colleges in India for MBA admission 2023-25 batch like NMIMS Mumbai; SPJIMR Mumbai (PGMPW); ISB (Certificate programme); VIT Vellore; K J Somaiya Mumbai; XIM Bhubaneswar and many other MBA colleges in India.

Xavier Aptitude Test

XAT is a national-level aptitude test conducted by XLRI Jamshedpur for admission in MBA/PGDM programs in XLRI and other top MBA colleges like XIMB, IMT Ghaziabad, TAPMI Manipal, etc. The exam duration is 3 hours. The syllabus encompasses Verbal and Logical Ability, General Knowledge, Decision Making, and Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation.

Symbiosis National Aptitude Test

SNAP Test is a common aptitude test conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed) University for admission to the MBA/PGDM programs. The exam is conducted once a year. The duration of the exam is 60 minutes or 1 hour.

PGDM in Information Technology

PGDM Admission Process in India

PGDM admission process typically involves the same stages across most management institutes. Below are the steps that every PGDM candidate must follow to gain admission into a PGDM program:

  • Apply for Entrance Exams: The first step is to apply for the PGDM entrance exams that are accepted by the colleges you are interested in. Ensure that you meet all the requirements of the entrance exam.
  • Qualify for Entrance Exams: If you qualify for the entrance exam and meet the minimum cutoff, you will be called for the selection rounds. You need to register to participate in the counselling and fulfil all the requirements.
  • Participate in Counselling: Based on the entrance exam scores, candidates are shortlisted and invited for Group Discussions, Written Ability Test, or Personal Interview rounds. PGDM colleges that offer direct admissions to PGDM, on a merit basis, also conduct personal interview rounds.
  • Appear in the Merit List: After evaluating candidates based on entrance exam scores, GD/PI performance, academic performance, and work experience (if any), the final list of selected students will be announced.
  • Report to the College: Those who have been allocated a seat in the PGDM college will be required to report (offline or online) and pay the admission fees. Classes will commence thereafter.

Direct PGDM Admission Process Without Entrance Exam

Many universities offer direct PGDM admission to students without requiring the entrance exam scores. To apply for admission, candidates must submit their applications to the colleges or universities based on their preference and interest. This means that the earlier a student applies, the greater their chances of securing a seat in their desired program. 

However, it is important to note that certain eligibility criteria must be met to be considered for admission. For instance, students should have scored at least 50% in their graduation from any discipline. Additionally, candidates who possess some work experience between 1-3 years will be given preference over those who do not have any work experience.

Furthermore, students can also opt for admission through the management quota or Institute level quota seats into private MBA colleges. To be eligible for this, students must have a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in their graduation. Individuals who are interested in pursuing an MBA program should keep these admission requirements in mind and plan accordingly to increase their chances of securing a seat in their desired college or university.

PGDM Abroad Option for Indian Students

Studying PGDM course abroad can be an excellent choice for many individuals. Pursuing an international PGDM program can provide several benefits and open up numerous opportunities for career growth and personal development. The reason why an international PGDM is a sought-after credential is that it offers a comprehensive and globally recognized curriculum that includes diverse subjects such as finance, marketing, operations, and human resources management. Besides, international PGDM programs often offer exposure to a diverse and multicultural environment, which helps students develop cross-cultural competencies and gain a unique perspective on global business practices.

Additionally, students who complete an international PGDM program may have better chances of securing high-paying jobs with reputable multinational companies, both in their home country and abroad. An international PGDM is a sought-after credential by many individuals for the following reasons:

  • The average annual compensation for individuals graduating with PGDM from distinguished foreign institutions is significantly higher than those graduating from Indian universities.

FAQs about PGDM

What is PGDM syllabus?

The syllabus for a PGDM includes subjects like Principles of Management, Organizational Behavior, Managerial Economics, Business Communication, Business Ethics and Corporate Governance, Strategic Management, Operations Management, Financial Management, Marketing Management, Human Resource Management, Supply Chain Management, Quality Management, Digital Marketing, etc.

What is the duration of a PGDM course?

The duration of a PGDM course is 1 to 2 years. Some institutes offer PGDM programs that are completed in one year, often referred to as "fast-track" or "accelerated" programs. Some PGDM programs span over two years and include a more comprehensive curriculum.

What are the top PGDM colleges in India?

The top PGDM colleges in India include IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Bangalore, IIM Kozhikode, IIM Calcutta, IIT Delhi, IIM Lucknow, IIM Indore, IIM Rohtak, IIT Kanpur, IIM Ranchi, XLRI Jamshedpur, MDI Gurgaon, SPJIMR Mumbai, NMIMS Bangalore, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai, etc.

Is PGDM course more expensive than MBA?

Yes, a PGDM course is generally more expensive than an MBA since it is offered by autonomous authorities. A PGDM can cost you around INR 5 to 24 LPA while an MBA course will cost you around INR 2 to 20 LPA.

What is the average salary for PGDM graduates?

The average salary offered to a fresher after pursuing PGDM is Rs 1,60,000 annually. The average PGDM salary in India is INR 7.73 lakh per year. A Finance Manager earns an average salary of INR 9.91 lakh per annum in India. The average pay of a Product Manager in India is INR 17 lakh per annum.

What is the average fees for PGDM course?

The average PGDM course fees range from INR 95,000 to INR 15,00,000 in India. The fees of private colleges are generally higher than those of government colleges. After graduation, candidates can earn up to INR 6-15 lakh per annum, depending on the PGDM job profile.

Which is better - PGDM or MBA?

PGDM and MBA both are good courses and the decision to choose one is purely based on what a candidate needs from the course. A PGDM course is industry-oriented and focuses more on grooming programs, mentorship, and industry exposure. However, an MBA course is more theoretical in nature.

Why should one study a PGDM course abroad?

One should study a PGDM course abroad because the average annual compensation for individuals graduating with PGDM from distinguished foreign institutions is significantly higher than those graduating from Indian universities. Their teaching team and guest lecturers have a wealth of knowledge to keep the students engaged and learning.

Does PGDM course offer a good career?

Yes, a PGDM offers an excellent career because the average compensation for graduates with a PGDM is INR 7.73 Lakhs per annum. PGDM graduates can apply for job roles in finance, IT, Human resources, Business Research, and Business Marketing departments. They also stand a chance in research organisations in job roles like analytical experts, business analysts, etc. 

How to get admission to PGDM course?

Admission to the PGDM program can be done in two ways: direct admission based on merit or through entrance exams like CAT, XAT, MAT, etc. Candidates meeting the cut-off scores of colleges accepting entrance exams become eligible for Group Discussion/Personal Interview/Written Ability Test.

What is the difference between MBA and PGDM?

MBA is a Degree program whereas a PGDM is a diploma program. PGDM courses are industry oriented unlike MBA, which are more theoretical in nature. PGDMs focus more on grooming programs, mentorship and industry exposure. Also the placement services are more rigorous with PGDM programs. 

What are the eligibility criteria for PGDM course?

The eligibility criteria for PGDM course include a Bachelor’s degree or equivalent, with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks or equivalent in any discipline, recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). At premier institutes like IIMs, a minimum of 60% or 6.5 CGPA on a scale of 10 should have been achieved at the graduation level.

Why should one study a PGDM course?

One should study a PGDM course as it can significantly improve a candidate’s employability. Students pursuing PGDM can get very high remuneration. Some PGDM graduates will earn annual packages of more than INR 20 LPA even at the entry level.

When to do a PGDM course?

Deciding when to pursue a PGDM course depends on various factors including your career goals, current educational background, work experience, and personal circumstances. If you have a clear career goal that requires specialized management skills or knowledge in a specific field, you should opt for a PGDM course.

Why is PGDM a diploma and not a degree?

PGDM is offered by autonomous institutions and business schools that are not affiliated to any University. As a result, such institutes are not allowed to provide MBA degrees. PGDM programs are accredited by bodies like AICTE or AMBA (Association of MBAs) for international recognition.

What is a Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM)?

PGDM or Post Graduate Diploma in Management – a two-year business management diploma program offered by autonomous B-Schools recognised by AICTE that caters to industry requirements, and helps management aspirants develop relevant hard skills and soft skills to transform into an industry-ready management professional.
