An MBA in Human Resources (HR) equips students with the skills and knowledge necessary to manage an organization’s workforce effectively. This course covers various aspects such as recruitment, training and development, performance management, compensation and benefits, and employee relations. It aims to develop professionals who can strategically align human resources with the overarching goals of an organization. This specialization prepares individuals for roles such as HR managers, talent acquisition specialists, and employee relations managers, emphasizing the importance of people in achieving business success.

What is MBA in HR Course?

MBA in HR or Human Resource Management is a two-year specialized program focusing on recruitment, management, and providing direction and guidance to an organization's workforce. MBA in HR teaches students the relevance and method of understanding an organization's objective, customer, operating system, and culture. The course is available in full-time, part-time, distance learning, and online modes. To be eligible for MBA in HR admission, a student must have a bachelor's degree from a recognized university and qualify for applicable entrance exams. Many colleges in India also offer direct admission without entrance exams.

Among the top MBA in HR colleges in India are IIMs, Xavier Labour Relations Institute (XLRI), Management Development Institute, Tata Institute Of Social Sciences, and others. MBA in HR typically costs candidates between INR 5 lakhs to INR 20 lakhs. Those who graduated with an MBA in HR course can work in a variety of firms and settings, regardless of industry. The annual basic salary for an MBA in HR graduate is INR 6.42 lakhs on average. Experience with MBA in HR jobs often influences salary. The compensation of an HR Senior Manager ranges from INR 8 lakhs to INR 14 lakhs. Why Choose MBA in HR?

When asked why one should choose to study MBA in HR, the scope of this degree is always considered. The breadth of an MBA in HR is vast, and its program is designed to provide in-depth knowledge and training that will give you a competitive advantage in the market. Consider the following reasons for pursuing an MBA in HR.

Detailed HR Curriculum

The human resource curriculum is an extensive field. It includes labour laws and tactics that an HR professional will need to carry out their job. The extensive curriculum gives in-depth knowledge as well as practical instructions to assist in dealing with real-life circumstances.

Improve Managerial Skills

The MBA in HR program assists in the development of organizational skills required for human resource management. It teaches students how to operate under pressure, solve difficulties, and come up with creative solutions.

Learn Leadership Qualities

Human resource professionals are leaders who understand the requirements of their employees and create a pleasant work environment that increases productivity and efficiency. The MBA in HR program fosters leadership and interpersonal abilities, which assist professionals in carrying out their responsibilities efficiently.

Understand Organizational Psychology

HR professionals have a wide range of responsibilities. It entails comprehending the structure and purpose of the organization. They understand the psychology of the business and the goals of projects and brands, and they help to simplify it with the workforce.

Job Stability

Regardless of the industry, there will always be a demand for an HR professional. Every sector deals with people, and every industry needs HR specialists to manage them.

Opportunities for Professional Development

An MBA in HR will provide you with a plethora of professional options. HR executives, HR managers, and other positions are available. An MBA in human resources will boost your resume. It will assist you to get ahead of other applicants for employment openings.

Higher Salary

When compared to a non-MBA candidate, an applicant with an MBA in HR will be paid more. Furthermore, HR experts with the necessary experience and abilities are typically highly compensated firm employees. These positions are well paid in the sector after gaining appropriate work experience and applying for worldwide HR chances.

What is an Online MBA in HR Degree?

An online MBA in HR program is specifically tailored to equip individuals with the necessary skills and knowledge required to excel in leadership roles within HR departments or other business settings. This online MBA program is an excellent choice for working professionals who are looking to advance their careers in human resources, talent management, or related fields without leaving their jobs. It is particularly valuable for aspiring professionals seeking leadership positions where a strong understanding of both business and human resources is essential.

One of the most significant advantages of this program is that it enables professionals to gain the knowledge and skills necessary to navigate the complexities of managing people within a business context. The curriculum of an online MBA in HR includes a core set of courses covering fundamental business areas such as finance, marketing, operations, strategy, and organizational behaviour, just like traditional MBA programs.

Moreover, an online MBA program in HR also provides students with specialized courses that focus specifically on human resources, such as employee relations, compensation and benefits, HR analytics, talent acquisition, and organizational development. This combination of specialized HR courses and core business courses makes an online MBA in HR a well-rounded and comprehensive program for professionals seeking to advance their careers in the field of human resources.

What is the Difference Between MBA in HR and Master in HR?

If an individual is absolutely certain he/she wants to pursue human resource management as a career, a master’s in human resource management would be ideal for him/her because it prepares students for human resource management careers with an intense HR-focused education.

On the other hand, MBA in HR provides students with a comprehensive business education with a curriculum covering everything from marketing to accounting. An MBA in HR enables students to choose HR electives, but the education is comprehensive, not HR-focused. If one is interested in HR but is also interested in business in general, an MBA with a human resource management specialization may cater to his/her needs. 
The tuition for an MBA in HR programme will be much more expensive. However, MBA in HR graduates will be qualified for senior management positions and can expect to earn a competitive salary - even a six-figure income.

The curriculum is where there are the biggest differences between an MBA program with a specialization in HRM and a master's program in HRM. A master's in HRM program focuses nearly entirely on helping students gain knowledge and abilities in organizational behavior analysis and human resource management. Students enrolled in an MBA in HRM program complete a variety of business courses covering topics important to general business managers, like finance and accounting, as well as many focus courses on topics pertaining to human resource management. MBA in HR Eligibility Criteria

Students must meet the following eligibility requirements to pursue MBA in HR:

  • Students willing to pursue MBA in HR must possess a Bachelor’s degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline, with aggregate marks of 50% or an equivalent CGPA score from any recognized University. Some popular bachelor's degree choices for students aspiring to opt for an MBA in HR would be BBA, B.Tech., and B.Com.
  • Students from the SC/ ST/ PWD background can avail themselves of a relaxation of 5%.
  • Students desiring to pursue MBA in HR must apply and appear in the specific MBA in HR entrance test acceptable by the institute. For example-IIMs accept CAT, XLRI accepts XAT, and SCMHRD accepts SNAP scores.
  • Students aspiring to pursue MBA in HR must qualify for national-level entrance examinations such as CAT, XAT, GMAT, SNAP, NMAT, or CMAT by obtaining the cut-off scores specified by the institute they wish to enroll.

National-Level Entrance Exams for MBA in HR

To pursue MBA in HR, national-level common entrance tests like those mentioned below are considered:

Common Admission Test (CAT)

CAT (Common Admission Test) is a qualifying exam for admission to MBA, PGDM and other PG-level management courses in India. The eligibility for taking the CAT exam is 50% marks in graduation, for reserved categories the qualifying marks are relaxed up to 5%. The test consists of three sections: Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (VARC), Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning (DILR) and Quantitative Ability (QA). The exam is taken online over three hours, with one hour per section. 

Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT)

The Xavier Aptitude Test is a national-level aptitude test conducted by XLRI Jamshedpur for admission in MBA/PGDM programmes in XLRI and other top MBA colleges like XIMB, IMT Ghaziabad, TAPMI Manipal, etc. The exam duration is 3 hours. The syllabus encompasses Verbal and Logical Ability, General Knowledge, Decision Making, and Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation.

Symbiosis National Aptitude Test (SNAP)

Symbiosis National Aptitude Test or SNAP Test is a common aptitude test conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed) University for admission to the MBA/PGDM programmes. The exam is conducted once a year. The duration of the exam is 60 minutes or 1 hour.

Management Aptitude Test (MAT)

The All India Management Association (AIMA) is responsible for conducting the MAT exam to facilitate Business Schools (B-Schools) to screen candidates for admission to MBA and allied programmes. To become eligible for taking the MAT exam, students must hold a Bachelor's Degree with a minimum of 3 years duration or equivalent in any discipline.

MBA in HR Admission Process in India

The MBA admission to the Human Resources specializations is similar to other MBA courses. Mentioned below are the steps students need to go through to get admission to MBA in HR courses.

  • Appear for the Entrance Exam: Admission to MBA in HR is largely determined by cutoff scores in entrance exams such as CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, NMAT, SNAP, and others. Before registering for a particular exam, make sure your desired colleges accept it. 
  • Apply to the Colleges of Your Choice: Fill out the application form for the MBA in HR  colleges you wish to get admission to. If you wish to pursue online or certification courses, fill out their application forms and pay the fees to begin the course.
  • Meet the Required Cutoff: These cutoff scores establish a minimum threshold for eligibility, and only those who meet or exceed them are considered for the next stage of the admission process.
  • Participate in Counselling Rounds: Once an applicant has qualified for the entrance exams, they must participate in a series of additional evaluations conducted by the concerned authorities. These typically include Group Discussions (GD), Personal Interviews (PI), and Written Ability Tests (WAT). These evaluations are designed to assess a candidate's communication skills, reasoning ability, and overall fit for the program. 
  • Get Shortlisted & Report to the Colleges: Based on your entrance exam score, academic profile, and performance in the selection rounds, you will get shortlisted for final admission. After you are allotted a seat in an institute, report to it and complete all the admission formalities to begin classes.

FAQs about MBA HR

Which is better- full-time MBA in HR or Executive MBA in HR?

Among full-time MBA in HR or Executive MBA in HR, both courses are beneficial for someone who wants to work in this sector. They must remember that an executive MBA is preferable if they have two to five years of relevant work experience rather than a full-time MBA, which is best suited for recent graduates.

What is MBA in HR admission process?

The MBA in HR admission process consists of an entrance exam, a group discussion, and a personal interview. To be considered for admission, candidates must pass all of these steps. Colleges offering direct admission don't need entrance exam scores.  Also, GD/PI may not be required for part-time, distance learning, and online MBA in HR programmes.

Which are the top companies hiring MBA in HR graduates?

The top companies hiring MBA in HR graduates include Reliance, Infosys, Tech Mahindra, Pepsi, Wipro, KPMG, Larsen & Turbo, L'Oreal, Unilever, Amazon, Deloitte, Hudson Global Inc, Cognizant, Capgemini, Accenture, etc. MBA in HR graduates can find work as a Labor Relations Manager, Human Resources Manager, Recruitment Coordinator, etc.

Can MBA in HR graduates work in the government sector?

Yes, MBA in HR graduates can work in the government sector. The top government organizations include the Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD), Bharat Electronics Limited, SBI, and the Indian Institute of Corporate Affairs. Job opportunities in the government sector pay well and provide financial security as well.

What is MBA in HR salary in India?

MBA in HR salary in India ranges between INR 2 LPA to INR 5 LPA for freshers. Candidates are offered salaries ranging from 10 to 24 LPA after five years of experience. HR professionals in mid-level roles earn between 10 and 18 lakhs per year, while those in senior positions earn between 25 and 40 lakhs.

What are the employment opportunities for MBA in HR graduates?

The employment opportunities for MBA in HR graduates are diverse. One can easily obtain a managerial position after completing an MBA in HR degree in any desired industry since human resources department is the backbone of every small to large business.

How does MBA in HR help students attain effective leadership?

The MBA in HR degree is about dealing with people, which requires professionals to be competent leaders who motivate employees, gain their trust, and resolve difficulties through effective communication. The MBA programme supports students in building an understanding of and mastering effective management practices.

What are the benefits of pursuing an MBA in HR?

The benefits of pursuing an MBA in HR are immense. This degree will provide you with an advanced grasp of how organisations work, how people are hired, recruited, and trained. You'll have improved interpersonal communication skills and organisational understanding, increased employment chances, perspectives on organisational psychology, etc.

Who is a human resource manager?

Human Resources Managers assist employees through one-on-one interactions. They are in charge of a company's recruitment process and keep track of department data, evaluate employee performance and pay, and oversee workplace health and safety compliance. They also serve as mediators between executives and employees, enforcing disciplinary measures and resolving conflicts.

What are the various job profiles available for MBA in HR graduates?

The various job profiles available for MBA in HR graduates include Human Resource Manager, Human Resource Generalist, Staffing Director, Technical Recruiter, Compensation Manager, Employee Relations Manager, Director of HR Training and Development, Human Resource Consultant, International HR Professionals, etc.

What is a part-time MBA in HR?

A part-time MBA in HR can be completed concurrently with your job or any other normal course by attending classes on weekends or in the evenings. It is a 2-3-year postgraduate programme with 4-6 semesters. Top colleges offering this programme are Delhi School of Management, Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, Mahatma Gandhi University, MIT School of Distance Education, Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute of Management, New Delhi Institute of Management, etc.

Is doing MBA in HR a good option?

MBA in HR is a good option if you want to spend your career getting the most out of people and driving talent acquisition and performance at a business. With so many colleges providing HR specialities, shop around to find the best program for you.

Is MBA in HR easy to pursue?

MBA in HR can be comparatively easier to pursue as compared to other management specialisations for dedicated management students. However, it can be a demanding and stimulating management programme if you have no prior knowledge of the topics involved in it.

What are the skills required for MBA in HR?

The skills required for MBA in HR include Strategic thinking, Planning Skills, Leadership Skills, Communication Skills, Workplace Culture Development Skills, Conflict Management and Problem Solving Skills, Advising and Coaching Skills. During the course, students will get various opportunities to work on these skills.

Why study MBA in HR abroad?

Studying MBA in HR abroad from reputed foreign universities can help individuals earn potentially higher and get recruited for senior management positions in universally acclaimed companies. The average annual compensation for individuals graduating from distinguished foreign institutions is significantly higher than those graduating from Indian universities.

What is a one-year MBA in HR?

A one-year MBA in HR is an excellent option for working professionals who are not willing to quit their jobs to pursue a degree. A work experience of a minimum of 5 years or above is preferred by many colleges.

What is an Executive MBA in HR?

An Executive MBA in HR is specifically designed for working corporate executives and senior managers. To be eligible for this course, candidates must have a minimum of 3-5 years of work experience. An Executive MBA in HR course is designed to strengthen the managerial insight and business expertise of a professional.

What is the difference between Master in HR and MBA in HR?

Master in HR prepares students for human resource management careers with an intense HR-focused education. On the other hand, an MBA in HR provides students with a comprehensive business education with a curriculum covering everything from marketing to accounting.

What is the eligibility criteria for MBA in HR?

The eligibility criteria for MBA in HR include a Bachelor’s degree in any discipline with aggregate marks of 50% or an equivalent CGPA score from any recognized University. Some popular bachelor's degree choices for students aspiring to opt for an MBA HR would be BBA, B.Tech. and B.Com.

What are MBA in HR subjects?

MBA in HR subjects are Principles of Management and Organisational Behaviour, Marketing Management, Research Methods for Management, Managerial Economics, Corporate Communication, Human Resource Management, Operations Management, Accounting and Finance for Managers, Manpower Planning and Resourcing, Human Resource Information Systems, etc.

Who should study an MBA in HR?

Individuals who are interested in managing and using people effectively can pursue an MBA in HR. Individuals aspiring to manage the implementation and integration of technology through improved staffing, training, and communication with employees must pursue an MBA in HR.

Is MBA in HR a good course to pursue?

Yes, MBA in HR is a good course to pursue. Human Resource Management is the backbone of any organization responsible for creating a skilled and knowledgeable workforce. Pursuing an MBA in HR will provide more opportunities and expertise in the various fields of HR such as training, employee relations, and strategy.

What are the top colleges offering MBA in HR course?

The top colleges offering MBA in HR course include Management Development Institute (Gurgaon), S.P. Jain Institute of Management and Research (Mumbai), Indian Institute of Management (Kozhikode), Department of Management Studies - IIT Roorkee, Shailesh J Mehta School of Management, IIT Bombay, and many others.

What is the average fee for MBA in HR ?

The average fee for MBA in HR is INR 5 lakhs to INR 20 lakhs for the entire course. Before enrolling in the course, candidates must visit the official website of their desired colleges to check the fee structure.

What is the duration of MBA in HR ?

The duration of MBA in HR is 2 years for a full-time course. The part-time and online/distance courses may take up to 3 years to complete. The duration of the Executive MBA course is generally 15-18 months.
