MPhil Overview

MPhil, or Master of Philosophy, is a two-year postgraduate academic research programme. An MPhil course can be pursued by candidates from any field, including humanities, commerce, science, law, and education. Aspirants for an MPhil degree must study both theory and practise. Aside from that, candidates must conduct research and present their findings. Candidates who have completed an MPhil course may wish to pursue a PhD programme. Those who want to pursue an MPhil course must first meet the eligibility requirements. The criteria vary by university, but the basic eligibility set is that candidates must have obtained at least 55% in the subject in which they wish to pursue an MPhil degree at the postgraduate level. Candidates for admission to an MPhil course are typically shortlisted based on their performance in an entrance exam, which is followed by a Personal Interview (PI) round. 

MPhil syllabus and subjects cover research-based topics and managerial approaches to discern research-based concepts while also preparing students for a lucrative career. The top MPhil entrance examinations are JNUEE, GATE, MPhil Entrance Test (MET), and UGC NET. MPhil specialisations in Psychology, English, History, Economics, Management, and other fields are among the most popular. The Master of Philosophy degree is offered by Jawaharlal Nehru University, Banaras Hindu University, University of Calcutta, University of Hyderabad, and other prestigious institutions. The average MPhil course fee is between INR 1100 and INR 1 lakh. An MPhil course in a government college, on the other hand, costs less than INR 10,000 per year.

Students who successfully complete the MPhil programme may find employment as a teacher, speaker, social worker, editor, research assistant, consultant, assistant professor, scientist, etc. The average MPhil salary in India is INR 4.92 LPA, according to Payscale.

Why Choose MPhil?

The MPhil degree programme has its own set of advantages and opportunities that set it distinct from other career paths and responsibilities. Some of the most compelling reasons to enroll in MPhil courses are as follows:

  • The MPhil Course has long been regarded as a bridge between the master's degree and the doctoral programme. The course guides candidates through a holistic approach to teaching their minds about the complexities of research, as well as developing research skills and information that will aid them in completing a full-fledged PhD programme
  • Good Pay and Benefits: Candidates should expect a good return on their investment in the MPhil degree programme. Candidates should expect to pay between INR 1100-INR 200,000 for an MPhil course fee (The numbers are considerably higher for the private MPhil colleges). An MPhil degree candidate makes an average MPhil salary of INR 508,600 per year, according to the website PayScale. As a result, the candidates can swiftly recuperate their study costs. Medical insurance, dental examinations, and other benefits are available to MPhil course candidates
  • In a Nutshell Candidates who have finished their MPhil studies are typically hired as teachers or research scientists. One of the most secure MPhil careers in India is that of a teacher. The Honorable Supreme Court of India argued in 2018 for the employment security of all teachers at all levels of education in India
  • Specialty: The MPhil degree programme offers a wide range of specialisation options, allowing students to pick from a variety of MPhil subjects of study based on their interests. In India, there are over 30 specialities to choose from. Due to the surge in mental health problems in India, where more than 150 million people require counselling, the MPhil in Psychology specialised degree is gaining popularity.
  • Entrepreneurship Opportunities: As previously said, most people choose a teaching career after completing their MPhil degree programme. After completing their MPhil, many MPhil course graduates open tutoring centres. According to the Times of India, 7.1 crore students opt for tuition at various levels, resulting in business prospects.

MPhil Course Eligibility Criteria

MPhil Eligibility Criteria for admission vary from university to university, but the basic criteria or requirement remains the same for the majority of universities. Those interested in pursuing an MPhil degree must meet the qualifications listed below in order to be considered for MPhil college admission:

  • MPhil admission requires students to have completed their master's degree from any recognised institution with a minimum mark of 55%. Candidates applying for the MPhil course have no upper age limit. In addition to basic MPhil eligibility criteria, some institutions hold an MPhil entrance exam as part of the selection process. To be eligible for admission, students must perform well in the MPhil entrance exam
     

Apart from these, some of the colleges' standout MPhil eligibility criteria are as follows:

  • Candidates applying to the University of Calcutta must provide a domicile certificate stating that they have lived in the state for more than ten years. Candidates must also bring their original certificates with them when submitting the MPhil online application form.

How to Apply for MPhil?

Admission to MPhil is granted in two ways: merit-based or through an entrance examination. The application process for MPhil varies depending on the admission procedure. However, applicants can apply to their preferred MPhil colleges online. Students must fill out the required information in an online application form on the official website of their respective universities to apply for MPhil courses online.

For Entrance Based MPhil Admission

Candidates can apply for MPhil courses by following the steps outlined below:

Step 1: Check into the application form requirements and deadlines listed by various colleges and universities if you want to an admission to a MPhil programme.

Step 2: Register for the entrance exams required for MPhil admissions at the colleges and universities you want to attend

Step 3: Once confirmed, save the application process for future use. Also, for admission and other important notifications, keep an eye on the exam or the university's official website

Step 4: After appearing and passing the MPhil entrance examination, candidates must fill out an application form for their desired university with personal information such as academic details, entrance examination results, and so on

Step 5: Candidates who successfully meet the university's eligibility requirements are shortlisted for the counselling round (if any) to finalise the admissions process

Step 6: After clearing the counselling session, candidates must pay the university's entrance fee in order to be confirm their admission to the MPhil course

For Merit Based MPhil Admission 

Candidates can apply for MPhil courses by following the steps outlined below:

Step 1: Fill out the online MPhil application form

Step 2: Pay the registration fee and any additional fees if admission is granted based on merit

Step 3: The shortlisted applicants will receive invitations to the subsequent phases of the hiring process, such as individual interviews or counselling

Step 4: Candidates must submit all the required documents and fee in order to confirm their admission to MPhil course

MPhil Admission Process

MPhil admission to the best MPhil colleges and universities is accomplished through various entrance exams administered by national authorities or individual universities. The majority of private universities admit students based on a merit list. The MPhil entrance exams are usually held in the months of May and June each  year. However, candidates must keep the following points in mind when applying for MPhil admission.

  • Candidates must check the MPhil eligibility criteria and entrance exam details on the official websites of the universities where they wish to enroll.
  • Candidates must go to the official website of the relevant university and the entrance examination conducting bodies, fill out the required form, and upload the required documents.
  • Students must pass these exams (which they must be eligible to take) in order to be admitted to the MPhil course.
  • Following the test, a MPhil merit list of finally qualified candidates is prepared, and candidates are invited for further admission consideration by the respective university or college. A personal interview or discussion is usually held.
  • Following the final allocation of seats to candidates, the candidates are asked to pay the MPhil course fee and register for the upcoming academic year.
  • Any scientists or professionals working for R&D institutes of any state or central government are eligible to directly appear for an interview at Delhi University if the institute has a memorandum of understanding with the university.

MPhil Study Abroad

The most important requirement for studying MPhil courses in foreign countries is a stellar academic record. Candidates must have passed their postgraduate level exam with a minimum percentage of 60-80 percent from a reputable university. Candidates must also achieve at least a 70% in their higher secondary level examinations (65 percent in math and English).

Some MPhil abroad study general information is provided below:

  • The overall cost of pursuing an MPhil Abroad is high, with candidates paying MPhil fees ranging from INR 10 Lac to INR 20 Lac. MPhil fees can reach INR in some cases. Furthermore, candidates must consider the respective countries' cost of living.
  • Candidates can also apply for MPhil scholarships from various  organizations as well as universities. Look into the Scholarships for Studying MPhil Abroad programme.
  • To pursue the MPhil course, candidates must take English proficiency tests such as TOEFL and IELTS.
  • In some cases, candidates must provide a letter of recommendation as well as proof of relevant work experience.

An MPhil comes after an MSc or MA in the academic hierarchy. It's the highest level of Masters qualification you can obtain and involves a research project that is even more extensive than that required for a taught Masters degree.

MPhil coursework necessitates a significant amount of research, time, and dedication. Furthermore, if you have taken a course in which you have a genuine interest and want to go the distance, you will find the coursework enjoyable to complete.

Students pursuing an MPhil receive a monthly stipend. Some universities have fellowship programmes for MPhil students.

According to the UGC guidelines, the candidate cannot complete the MPhil via correspondence.

The MPhil is a research degree in and of itself that will equip you with knowledge and abilities akin to those of a PhD. Even though it lacks the novelty and originality that define a PhD, the work is still an accomplishment and an important contribution to knowledge.

The most prestigious master's degree is the MPhil, which offers a PhD-level skill set and a more in-depth research experience than an MSc or MA.

Is the MSc and MPhil the same thing?

The MSc degree is theoretical, whereas the MPhil degree is research-based. While both are postgraduate degrees and are typically pursued after a bachelor's degree, the MPhil and MSc are distinct in other ways.

Is an MPhil equivalent to a doctorate?

What is the scope of an MPhil?
