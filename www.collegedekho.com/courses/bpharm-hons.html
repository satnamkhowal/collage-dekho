About B.Pharm (Hons.)

B.Pharm (Hons.) is a four-year undergraduate course. The course deals with the study to understand the properties and impacts of medicines and developing the skills required to counsel patients about their use. B.Pharm Hons. covers all facets of healthcare including biochemical areas that concern the preparation of medicines and implementing them for the right diagnosis which is essential to build a successful career as a Pharmacist or for other related fields.

What makes B.Pharm (Hons.) course so popular is that apart from traditional pharmacy course is that one tends to learn about Operations, Sales and Marketing in the Pharmacy field which opens up multiple job opportunities.

B.Pharm (Hons.) Eligibility Criteria

Candidates need to fulfil the following eligibility criteria in order to get admission in B.Pharm (Hons.) course.

  • Candidates should have passed Class 12th examination with minimum 60% marks from any recognized board

  • Candidates should have secured three diploma programs in pharmacy and can also apply for B.Pharm (Hons.) courses.

  • Candidates who hold a 3 or 2 years diploma degree after their class 10th examination can also apply for admissions.

  • The candidates should be minimum of 17 years of age at the time of admission

B.Pharm (Hons.) Admission Process 2020

Admission to the B.Pharm (Hons.) courses is done on the basis of candidates performance in the entrance examination. Some of the popular B.Pharm (Hons.) entrance exams include BITSAT, WBJEE. However, certain institutes consider 10+2 marks for admissions.

B.Pharm (Hons.) Career Options and Job Prospects

FAQs about B.Pharm (Hons.)

Is biology necessary for B.Pharm (Hons)?

No, Biology is not necessary for B.Pharm (Honours) course.

What are popular jobs available for B.Pharm (Hons) graduate?

Some of the popular jobs for B.Pharm (Hons.) graduates are Drug Inspector, Quality Control Associate, R&D Scientist, Sales & Marketing Executive, Community Pharmacist, etc.

Does B.Pharm (Hons) admissions require NEET?

It is not compulsory to appear for NEET entrance exam for B.Pharm (Honours) admission.

What is the minimum age to apply for B.Pharm (Hons)?

Candidates need to be at least 17 years of age at the time of admission to be eligible for B.Pharm (Hons) admission.

What is the salary of a student who has just completed B.Pharm (Hons)?

The average salary of B.Pharm (Hons) will be around Rs. 2 LPA to Rs. 3 LPA, initially. The salary depends on several factors such as the experience of the candidate, educational qualification, profile of the company and whether the individual is working in private sector or government sector.

Which is better B Pharm (Hons) or Pharm.D?

In reality, Pharm.D degree is better than B.Pharm (Hons) as Pharm.D stands for doctor of pharmacy and is a six-year educational programme. The course includes more pharmacy clinical aspects than a B.Pharm (Hons) course.

What is the minimum percentage required for B.Pharm (Hons) admission?

Students needs to secure minimum 60% marks in 10+2 examination or its equivalent qualification.

What is the eligibility criteria of B.Pharm (Hons)?

The student should have passed 10+2 or it’s equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics or Biology/Botony with a minimum aggregate score of 60% or more.

What is the average course fee for B.Pharm (Hons)?

The average course fee for B.Pharm (Hons) ranges between Rs 15,000/- to Rs 1,25,000 per year.

What is the duration of the B.Pharm (Honours) course?

The duration of a B.Pharm (Hons) course is 4 years in India.

What are the top colleges offering B.Pharm (Hons.) courses ?

Following are some of the top colleges offering Pharmacy courses : Poona College of Pharmacy (Pune) , The Faculty of Technology and Engineering_ The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda (Vadodara) , Delhi Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Research (Delhi) , Institute of Chemical Technology (Mumbai) , Bombay College of Pharmacy (Kalyan). You can explore these colleges on our website to check if they offer B.Pharm (Hons.) or not.

I want admission in B.Pharm (Hons.). Do I have to clear any exams for that ?

Himachal Pradesh Common Entrance Test , Karnataka Common Entrance Test , Vignan's Scholastic Aptitude Test , Maharashtra Common Entrance Test (Engg.) , Banasthali University Aptitude Test are some of the exams that you can appear for to get admission in B.Pharm (Hons.) courses.

What is the average fee for the course B.Pharm (Hons.) ?
