B Ed Special Education Overview

B Ed Special Education is a two-year professional undergraduate degree course dealing with preparing teachers to educate specially-abled or physically challenged students. It is an education course that focuses on the unique requirements and characteristics of children with special needs. The course is specifically designed to prepare aspiring teachers to work with students with disabilities, learning difficulties, and other special needs, and provides training in specialized teaching techniques and approaches.

This course focuses on educating those children whose educational activities must be meticulously planned and tracked individually. Self-help skills, activities for daily life, sociability, language promotion, motor and cognitive skills are the training areas for B Ed Special Education.

A UG degree with at least a 50% aggregate marks is required for admission to the B Ed Special Education programme. The majority of universities only admit students on the basis of their academic performance, while a small number also do so based on the results of entrance exams like UP B.Ed JEE, IPU CET, TS EDCET, AP EDCET, MAH B.Ed CET.

Among the institutions that offer a B Ed in Special Education are Sanskriti University, BHU, North-Eastern Hill University, etc. The typical course fees for this programme at various institutes ranges from INR 50,000 to INR 1,50,000.

Candidates who hold a B Ed Special Education degree could be qualified for a range of well-liked job roles, such as education consultants, primary teachers, content writers, etc. Graduates of this course frequently receive salaries between INR 3,00,000 and INR 7,00,000 per year.

Why pursue B Ed Special Education?

The B.Ed in Special Education equips future educators with the knowledge and skills necessary to educate and learn about individuals with disabilities. It enhances the applicants' soft skills and helps them communicate more effectively so they can recognise each student's unique needs and provide appropriate guidance.

Let's examine some of the factors that make the B.Ed in Special Education a superior educational choice.

Educational Requirement: Candidates who want to teach at the junior, middle, or senior levels in any government or sponsor school now have to have a B.Ed degree, even if it is for the department of Special Education.

Huge Work Potential: For a sizable portion of the Indian population, special schooling continues to be taboo. Students with special needs who go unreported and are forced to stop their studies in India are in dire need of qualified teachers. There are 26.8 million students in India who require special education, according to reports. Among them, 5 million people between the ages of 10 and 19 and 2 million children between the ages of 0 and 6 who require special schooling. The enormous job potential is thus explained.

Good Salary and Fringe Benefits: Special Education teachers in India make an average compensation of INR 2,00,000 - 6,00,000 per year. A salary of INR 7,20,000 per year is typical for Special Education teachers at government colleges, in addition to benefits like retirement plans, deferred compensation, provident funds, and many more.

Varied Employment Options: It creates a variety of work prospects, particularly in the educational field. After the training is over, candidates can work as educational counselors, content writers, social workers, etc.

Education for a Good Cause and Job Satisfaction: Students are rushing to enroll in such a programme because teaching has traditionally been seen as a prestigious vocation. On the other hand, this course gives flexible scheduling and job satisfaction to educate children with special needs.

Growing Demand: Since there is a need for qualified, skilled professionals to teach kids with special needs, such as children with hearing impairment, mental retardation, or visual impairment, candidates with a B.Ed Special Education degree are in high demand.

B.Ed Special Education Admission Process

Admission requirements for the B.Ed Special Education differs between institutions. While some institutions base admission decisions on merit basis i.e the marks obtained in the most recent qualifying examination whereas other institutions hold their own entrance exams and interview processes. Final admission decisions are then made based on the combined results of the entrance exam and interview results.

Most of the logical questions on aptitude and teaching are found in entrance exams that are administered by various institutions. The popular BEd Special Education entrance exams are UP B.Ed JEE, MAH B.Ed CET, IPU CET, AP EDCET, TS EDCET.

Merit-based Admission Process

The step-by-step merit based admission process for BEd Special Education is mentioned as below:

  • Colleges issue admission notices to their respective official websites.
  • Candidates that meet the eligibility requirements must visit the college's website and complete the application either offline or online.
  • On their notice board or the official website, colleges announce and publish merit lists based on cut-off scores.
  • Successful applicants must visit the relevant college and complete all processes before being officially admitted.

Entrance Exam based Admission Process

The step-by-step entrance exam based admission procedure for the B Ed Special Education degree course is mentioned as below:

  • On their respective official websites, the various exam-conducting bodies post information about the dates of entrance exams, the deadlines for submitting applications, and the availability of admit cards.
  • Candidates who meet the minimal qualifying requirements must complete the online entrance exam application form by providing necessary information, followed by payment of the required application fee.
  • Before the entrance exam, candidates must download their admission cards from the appropriate website.
  • The organization in charge of administering the exam posts a merit list of those who passed along with their corresponding marks online.
  • The successful applicants are contacted for counseling, which is followed by a college selection interview.
  • Candidates must attend the relevant college after counseling to fulfill all necessary processes for admission.
