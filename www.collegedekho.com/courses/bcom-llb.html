About Bachelors of Commerce and Bachelor of Legislative Law (B.Com + LLB)

B.Com + LLB is a five-year undergraduate program that is a combination of law and commerce studies. The course is divided into ten semesters designed to provide students with knowledge of subjects of the commerce and business background along with different laws vital in a professional world. 

B.Com+ LLB is a great career choice for those who have an interest in business and law together as it allows candidates to gain full knowledge and gain two degrees within the span of five years i.e. Bachelors of Commerce and Bachelor of Legislative Law. The medium of instruction for B.Com + LLB is English in most of the universities.

Bachelors of Commerce and Bachelor of Legislative Law (B.Com + LLB) Eligibility Criteria

Mentioned below is the eligibility criteria that candidates must meet to get admission in B.Com + LLB courses. 

  • Minimum Qualification Required: 10+2
  • Minimum Marks Required: 50% to 60%, varies from college to college.
  • Age Limit: The maximum age limit for seeking admission in B.com + LLB course is 20 years for general category & 22 years for reserved category.

B.Com + LLB Admission Process

  • Admission to the B.Com + LLB program is strictly on the basis of national-level common entrance exams
  • Government/ state colleges conducted their common entrance tests.
  • Candidates are then allocated colleges as per their performance in the exam.
  • Top scorers are allocated the top colleges like NLUs and have the chance to choose from plenty of options.

FAQs about B.Com LLB

How much salary can be earned after B.Com LLB?

Depending on the job profile and the organisation the salary might vary from individual to individual. However, immediately after completing B.Com LLB, a candidate can earn from 4,50,000 per annum to 5,40,000 per annum.

Which law schools are good for B.Com LLB?

The prestigious law schools goof for B.Com LLB are Gujarat National Law Universities (GNLU), Institute of Law, Nirma University, School of Law, UPES, School of Law, SRM University, University Institute of Legal Studies, Panjab University, Amity Law School etc.

What subjects are available in B.Com LLB?

The subjects taught in B.Com LLB across the tenure of 10 semesters are Business Statistics, Financial Accounting, Law of Contract, Women, Child and the Law, Consumer Protection Law, Business Finance, Constitutional Law, Law of Crimes, Legal Language, Jurisprudence, Company Law, Civil Procedure Code, Property Law, Public International Law, Taxation, Land Laws including Ceiling, Professional Ethics etc.

How is B.Com LLB different from BA LLB?

The basic difference between B.Com LLB and BA LLB is that the former one deals with commerce subjects while the latter one explores arts subjects along with the law. Both the degrees however same career opportunities to students.

Can students from science stream pursue B.Com LLB?

Yes, students from science stream can go for B.Com LLB.

Is there any upper age limit to pursue B.Com LLB?

As per the guidelines issued by most of the colleges, the upper age limit to pursue B.Com LLB is 20 years for general category students while 22 years for students falling under reserved categories.

What future options can be explored after B.Com LLB?

Career options that can be explored after completing B.Com LLB are Litigation Lawyer, Legal Advisor, Judge, Company Secretary etc. Aspirants can also join corporate sectors, distinguished law firms or can appear for judiciary exams.

What are the advantages of pursuing B.Com LLB?

Students do not have to wait for a graduation degree to pursue B.Com LLB, they can do it right after completing class 12th. The course provides an in-depth understanding of the subjects and grants two degrees to the students (business commerce and law).

How long is the duration of B.Com LLB?

The duration of B.Com LLB is of 5 years.

What is difference regular LLB and B.Com LLB?

B.Com LLB can be pursued after completing class 12th while for pursuing LLB a graduation degree is required from a recognised college or university. B.Com LLB explores law as well as business commerce subjects. However, LLB majorly deals with Law subjects only.

Some of the popular specializations for B.Com. + LL.B. are : Tax Law , Philosophy , Commerce , Law , International Law.

I want admission in Bachelors of Commerce and Bachelor of Legislative Law (B.Com + LLB). Do I have to clear any exams for that ?

Delhi University Science , Delhi University Arts , Panjab University Under Graduate Law Entrance Exam , Andhra Pradesh Law Common Entrance Test , UPES Law Studies Aptitude Test are some of the exams that you can appear for to get admission in Bachelors of Commerce and Bachelor of Legislative Law (B.Com + LLB) courses.

In which year of B.Com LLB course a student can pursue internship?

It is recommended that the student starts his/ her internships, latest by 3rd semster

I am a Commerce Student. Which LL.B Course should I choose?

Law as a course is a very democratic subject tailored for maximum intake of the masses, therefore the eligibility criteria only desires a certain tenure of education and unlike MBBS or Engineering does not require a specific set of subjects to apply. However, it does prescribe a minimum standard of grades before entry to the course. Now, as a student of Commerce, a degree in law can provide you with flourishing opportunities in terms of careers both as a practising lawyer and also as an in-house counsel. We recommend that as a student of Commerce one should follow-up 10+2 with either B.Com LL.B (Hons) or BB.A LL.B. As a student of BB.A LL.B the interpretation of Law shall be done from a corporate law perspective and this will help you develop your outlook towards legal disputes through the perspective of corporate organisations, providing you with a skill set which will help you. B.Com LL.B, on the other hand, shall provide you with the perspective of Taxation and compliance, and if you couple this with CS it will lead to better opportunities in the corporate sector. The LL.B Course curriculum by itself is pretty standard and therefore it is upon the student to choose the specialisation of their respective graduation degrees.

Which is better between B.Com LLB & BBA LL.B

It is a subjective comparison. However, a student can decide which is better for him or her if they relate the degree to the kind of environment of law they want to work in. B.Com LL.B gives the student a focused outlook towards taxation/ compliance and trade / accountancy laws providing them an opportunity to be an expert in commercial laws. BBA LL.B gives a more business oriented outlook towards law, which helps students develop a niche into corporate laws. Therefore, it is safe to presume that both degrees are at par with each other but the outcome of the two solely would depend upon the student and his/her intention to pursue the particular course.

What are the career prospects after studying B.Com LLB?

You can get a license and start practising as a Lawyer/ Legal Advisor/ Legal Journalist/ Consultant

What are the best options after B.Com LLB?

To enhance your skills after B.Com LLB, you can opt for a master's degree like an MBA and pursue a career as a Corporate Lawyer.

What is the course curriculum for B.Com LLB ?

The course curriculum of B.Com LLB includes commerce, business, english, finance, auditing and accountancy subjects. The entire syllabus of B.Com LLB can be checked here - https://www.collegedekho.com/courses/bcom-llb/

What is the eligibility criteria for admission in B.Com LLB?

To be eligible for B.Com LLB course, candidates must have passed class 12 or equivalent examination with a minimum of 55% marks. Admission to the B.Com LLB course will be on the basis of scores in the national level entrance exam.

What is taught in first year of B.Com LLB?

The first year syllabus of B.Com LLB includes - Business Statistics, Economics, Auditing 1, Business Communication, Law of Contract 1, Law of Contract 2, Financial Auditing, English 1, Women, Child & Law.

Can someone practice as a Lawyer after B.Com LLB?

Yes, after completing your B.Com LLB Course + internship, you are eligible to get a license and start your practice as a lawyer.

Does any NLU provide B.Com LLB?

Yes, some of the NLUs do offer B.Com LLB - Gujarat National Law University Gandhinagar, NLU Jodhpur offer B.Com LLB course
