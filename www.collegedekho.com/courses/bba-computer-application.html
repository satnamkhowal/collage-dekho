BBA in Computer Application Overview

The Bachelor of Business Administration in Computer Application, popularly called the BBA in Computer Application, is a 3-year undergraduate course offered by hundreds of universities and colleges across India. Often opted by candidates clearing their Class 12 board exams, the BBA in Computer Application deals with all facets of software development and information technology from the perspective of business administration. In other words, students will be trained specifically to be leaders and managers in the field of IT and Computer Applications.

Universities across the country, especially B-schools, offer BBA in Computer Applications for school graduates every year. Those interested in pursuing the course will have to meet the minimum academic requirements, which will be unique to each institution offering the course, along with completing other admission requirements. These requirements may also include sitting for group discussions and personal interviews as well.

The full-time on-campus undergraduate course, BBA in Computer Applications, has been designed to provide the basic knowledge and skills in leadership and management, specifically in the field of Computer Science and IT. Moreover, the opportunities and job availability for graduates of this course are immense as well, as has been highlighted on this page.
