D El Ed Overview

Diploma in Elementary Education or D El Ed is a 2 years diploma teacher training course that can be pursued after completing 10+2 from a recognised board in any stream. To pursue a Diploma in Elementary Education course, one can either take direct admission after class 12 or one can appear for the Diploma in Elementary Education entrance exams.

 D El Ed course involves both classroom teaching and training to teach up to the primary level in schools. D El Ed offers training to aspiring teachers so they can instruct children in classes I through VIII in primary and upper primary schools, ranging in age from 6 to 15 years old.

Candidates who have passed their 10+2 or equivalent test with a minimum average aggregate of 50% are eligible to apply for admission to the D El Ed colleges, according to the D El Ed eligibility requirements. For untrained teachers working around the nation, D El Ed NIOS is another choice.

Some of the popular entrance exams include AP DEECET, Bihar Diploma in Elementary Education JET, and SCERT Orissa. Diploma in Elementary Education courses are offered both online and offline.

The popular job roles offered to candidates after completing Diploma in Elementary Education include Primary Teacher, Junior Teacher, Tutor, School Administrator, School Assistant, Content Writer, School Head Mistress etc. The Diploma in Elementary Education course fee ranges between INR 5,000 to 50,000 LPA.

Some of the top colleges offering D El Ed course include Jamia Milia University Delhi, Maulana Azad National Urdu University, Hyderabad, Pacific University Udaipur, Amity University Noida, Integral University Lucknow, Bombay Teachers’ Training College Mumbai etc.

D El Ed Eligibility Criteria

Different eligibility conditions apply to universities that offer Diploma in Elementary Education programs. A few colleges accept students based on their 10+2 grades, while others have their own entrance tests. Candidates interested in pursuing a Diploma in Elementary Education degree must meet the following requirements.

  • Candidates must have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized institution.
  • The total marks should be a minimum of 50% or its equivalent at the 10+2 level.
  • The age limit may differ from one state board to the other. However, the usual minimum age requirement is 18 years, with a maximum age limit of 35 years.
  • Candidates should be able to communicate effectively, think creatively, be patient, and have a desire to teach.

Note: Students who have finished their certification program in the science stream must have a cumulative grade point average of 60% or above.

D El Ed Admission Process

Diploma in Elementary Education Admission can be obtained in one of two ways: through an entrance exam or through a merit list. Entrance exams are held in states such as Bihar, Odisha, and Assam. States such as West Bengal and Uttar Pradesh, on the other hand, use a merit list to determine admission. Final admission will be granted on the basis of below five phases:

Stage 1: Candidates must first determine their eligibility. If they are eligible, they must then apply for admission in the state of their choice.

Stage 2: Students receive their admit cards to appear for the entrance exam after applying or filling out the application form.

Stage 3: Take the entrance exam and clear it. Candidates will be chosen based on their scores in the Diploma in Elementary Education Entrance Exam.

Stage 4: Candidates will be contacted for an interview/ counseling round in addition to their admission exam score.

Stage 5: Admission to the Diploma in Elementary Education program is determined by the entrance test score and performance in the personal interview/ group discussion followed by counseling.

Uttarakhand Diploma in Elementary Education exam is held once a year at the state level.

NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education

The NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) program is developed primarily for untrained and in-service teachers who work in elementary schools in various states around the country. On the initiative of the Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD), the NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) program was developed by the Academic Department of NIOS, in accordance with the NCF 2005 (National Curriculum Framework), RTE 2009 (Right to Education), and NCFTE 2010 (National Curriculum Framework for Teacher Education) acts. The NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education is also provided via online distance learning mode.

NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education Eligibility Criteria

The following are the requirements for the National Institute of Open Schooling's (NIOS) Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd):

  • The applicant for NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) 2022 admission must have earned the specified qualification from a well-recognized institute or university and have a satisfactory academic record.
  • The candidate must be an Indian Citizen.
  • Untrained Teachers in Upper Primary classes from 5th to 9th grade must have a BA/ BSc qualification with a required State sponsorship in all cases.
  • Untrained teachers working in primary schools from grades 1 to 4 must have a Secondary Qualification or its equivalent from CBSE or any other recognized Indian board with at least 50% aggregate marks. 
  • SC/ ST/ OBC/ PH applicants are eligible for a 5% discount.

NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education Admission Process

The requirements for the NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) course are comparable to those for the normal Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) course. The main difference is that the degree would only be legitimate for primary school teachers who are currently employed. Admission to the NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) is done through an online application and after passing the entrance exams.

The following is the NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) admission procedure:

  • Visit the official website for more information.
  • Make an online registration by clicking on the apply link.
  • In the application form, the applicant must include all personal information such as his or her name, parent's names, residence, gender, date of birth, and so on.
  • On the online application form, upload the scanned photograph, signature, and other relevant papers.
  • After clicking the register button, the registration number and login information will be emailed to the email address you provided.
  • Pay the application fee of Rs. 250, which can be done online or offline.
  • For future reference, download the NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) application form.

Candidates can get their NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) Hall Ticket/ Admit Card from the official NIOS Portal a week before the exam. By entering their Admit Card Number and Password, students can gain access to the portal and obtain the necessary information about the NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) Results, Merit List, and Cut Off Marks.

NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education Course Fees

The Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD), Government of India, has set the following fees for the two-year NIOS Diploma in Elementary Education (DElEd) course:

D El Ed Course Scope

Candidates can work in private schools, government schools, nurseries, daycare centres, online educators, coaching centres, and private tuition classes after completing the Diploma in Elementary Education. Candidates must take CTET or State level TET tests after completing the DElEd program in order to begin a career as an elementary school teacher. Candidates interested in working in government schools must take the Teacher Recruitment Exam (TRT) administered by their state government. Since the compensation in the government sector is roughly 30% greater than in the private sector, most candidates prefer to apply to government schools.

FAQs about D.El.Ed

Is distance DElEd valid?

Yes, distance DElEd (Diploma in Elementary Education) course is valid in India. However, it is very important to check that the course has recognition from the correct educational bodies and meets all required standards. The National Institute of Open Schooling provides a Diploma in Elementary Education that is acceptable through Open and Distance Learning mode. This diploma is acknowledged for teachers who must get necessary qualifications according to the Right to Education Act.

How many subjects are there in DElEd?

Typically, the D El Ed course comprises around 15 to 20 subjects in total. These often include major topics like Society Today, The Relationship Between Work and Education, How Diversity Influences Teaching, Understanding Childhood Growth in Children, Learning Mathematics Instruction Techniques among other subjects. This curriculum is designed to encompass different facets of primary education including methods of instruction techniques for teaching children effectively historical developments in this field various educational philosophies as well as recent tendencies observed within classroom environments.

What is the age limit for DElEd in Bihar?

The minimum age limit to apply for DElEd Bihar is 17 years and above whereas there’s no maximum age limit as of January 1, 2024. 

Is DElEd equivalent to graduation?

No, it’s not the same as a graduation degree course because DElEd is a 2-year long diploma course which can be pursued after completion of class 12th.

What is the importance of DElEd?

The significance of the DElEd, or Diploma in Elementary Education course is that it provides future teachers with the required understanding and capabilities they need to be excellent in teaching young students. It's a course made for those who will teach at primary school levels. It pays attention to how children grow up, methods of teaching, making lesson plans and how much influence teachers have on their students' development. The D El Ed course focuses on mixing lessons in the classroom, work experience through internships, and hands-on training to make sure that teachers are ready to give good education at primary and upper primary school levels.

Which is better, BTC or DElEd?

D El Ed is far better than BTC. DElEd is a more detailed and diploma level course than BTC which is just a basic training certificate and now it’s being phased out and replaced with D El Ed course.

What is the fees for DElEd in Bihar?

The course fees for DElEd admission in Bihar ranges from INR 10,000 to INR 50,000. You must check with the specific college/ university that you have applied for about the precise course fees.

What is the syllabus for DElEd entrance exam 2024?

The syllabus for DElEd entrance exam 2024 has major topics such as General Hindi/ Urdu, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, General English and Logical & Analytical Reasoning. Different topics under these subjects are included in each part of the syllabus. Usually, there are 120 multiple-choice questions worth 1 point each in the exam format. You have to finish them within 150 minutes. However, this is just a general exam format and it tends to change according to the state and the specific institute to which you’ve applied.

Can I become a government school teacher after D El Ed?

Yes, after completing the D El Ed course you can become a government school teacher. While trying for a job as a government teacher, the aspirants should apply for the Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) and State Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) among many other government recruitment examinations. These exams give an opportunity to embark on a teaching career either in a government or private school. Along with that candidates have the alternative choice to do their higher studies by doing B.Ed or by getting a degree at bachelor level.
