About - LL.M Criminal Law

A postgraduate law program called the Master of Law in Criminal Law or LLM Criminal Law is geared toward students who desire to work as defense attorneys, litigation officers, or detectives. Aspirants are able to comprehend the various facets of court procedures and legal principles thanks to the training. Graduates of this curriculum are also given responsibility for formulating public policy, implementing measures to reduce crime, and protecting the rights of crime victims. 

Candidates can pursue LLM course after completion of their 3 year or 5 year LLB program. A lot of reputed law institutes provide admission to their two-year/three-year LLM program based on national-level, university-level or state-level law entrance examinations like CLAT, AILET, TS LAWCET, etc. Few law institutes provide admission on merit-basis as well. 

LLM course is offered through offline and distance learning mode. The normal duration of an LLM course is two years in India but it can be of three years duration if done through distance education. The average course fee for an LLM postgraduate course is around INR 2 - 3 lakhs. A few of the top colleges providing LLM courses are NLU Kolkata, Siksha O Anusandhan University, Symbiosis Law School, etc.

What is LLM Criminal Law?

A widely popular area of law is criminal law. Criminal law encompasses the laws, legislation, and norms that specify behavior that is against the law when it endangers the welfare and safety of the public. Criminal law also specifies a penalty that may be applied to someone or a group of persons who break any laws.

Thus, crimes against society or the state are the focus of criminal law. The practice of criminal law is one of the oldest legal disciplines. Those who pursue this legal specialty interview clients, question witnesses, conduct trials, correlate evidence and results, prepare a case on behalf of their client, and examine and cross-examine witnesses in court.

Candidates have the option of pursuing both undergraduate and graduate programmes in the area of criminal law. In order to specialize in Criminal Legal in UG level courses, candidates must complete integrated law programmes; however, in PG level law schools, candidates can specialize in Criminal Law right away. LLM in Criminal Law, LLM in Criminal and Security Law, LLM in Criminal Law and Criminal Justice and Administrative Law, LLM in Criminal and Business Law, LLM in Criminal Law and Criminal Justice, etc. are some of the well-known PG level courses provided in the topic of criminal law.

Candidates may also enroll in diploma and certificate programmes in the area of criminal law in addition to these degree programmes. The Post Graduate Diploma in Criminal Law, the Post Graduate Diploma in Criminal Justice, and similar programmes are a few of these well-liked courses.

Why pursue LLM Criminal Law?

Candidates with this specialization are in high demand given the state of the present criminal justice system and the need for improvements. In the past, many renowned attorneys had a solid reputation and commanded substantial fees for taking on criminal cases. The number of criminal offenses has significantly increased throughout the years. As a result, a criminal law course would be quite useful for attorneys. Lawyers can enrol in specialised courses in criminology and forensic science, cybercrime, domestic violence, human rights, and sexual abuse after completing their LLB and LLM degrees.

They can work as assistants in private law offices to gain experience and learn how to argue cases before starting their own firms and representing themselves. Additionally, they can provide legal counsel for businesses that hire them. They can advocate for a particular case on behalf of the general public as a public prosecutor or on behalf of the suspect as a private attorney.

  • Those who want to deepen their awareness of the criminal justice system and particular criminal law concerns can benefit from taking up LLM Criminal Law course.

National-Level Entrance Exams

Candidates must hold a 3-year LLB degree from an accredited university, a 3-year integrated LLB degree, or an equivalent LLB degree.

LLM Criminal Law Scope

Opportunities in the field of criminal law are numerous due to the rise in criminal cases. A criminal attorney's primary responsibility is to represent or defend clients who are charged with crimes. Public defenders are criminal defense attorneys employed by the government. As private criminal attorneys, they are also employable. In public practice, the attorney may work for the Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) or the Public Defender Service (PDS).

Criminal attorneys typically begin their careers by working as experienced and well-known criminal attorney's assistants. You can begin working independently once you've gained experience. A criminal attorney serves as the client's advisor and advocate. It takes a lot of patience to work on a case for long hours, which is part of the job description for criminal attorneys in India.

The major responsibilities of a criminal lawyer include client interaction, gathering facts & evidence through witness interviews, and communicating with the client. Additionally, they engage in police interaction and thorough case studies. Depending on the specifics of the job at hand. It can take hours to prepare the proof. Even a bail application for an offense that is not bailable may need hours of planning and study. Since, every situation is different so there’s no set amount of time to gather evidence, prepare documents, etc. 

Candidates who are sharp-witted, confident, and with a passion for criminal law will achieve huge success in their careers. In India, there’s a dearth of criminal lawyers and there are so many appeals and pending cases related to criminal law and criminal justice in India. So, there’s a booming demand for law professionals who are specialized in criminal law in order to effectively solve criminal cases while providing relief and justice to the innocents. Not only that criminal law is a highly rewarding career emotionally as well as financially.

Graduates of criminal law can also work for a law firm or open their own practice after passing the bar exam. They may also be employed by the high court, the trial court, or the session court.

LLM Criminal Law Jobs

Successful LLM Criminal Law graduates who are interested in furthering their education may pursue a PhD or M.Phil in the field. Such postgraduates can find profitable work prospects in sectors including defense, law companies, and national legal institutions.

Some of the popular job profiles available to LLM Criminal Law graduates are mentioned below:

Criminal Lawyer: Criminal lawyers present evidence in favor of or defend those who have been accused of committing crimes. They handle cases involving offenses involving drugs, violence, theft, and fraud.

Criminologists: In order to understand what drives criminals, criminologists examine the strategies and patterns of criminal conduct. Criminologists must be skilled psychologists because if they understand the motivations behind a criminal's behavior, they can forecast or stop other crimes of the same type.

Public Prosecutor: According to the 1973 Code of Criminal Procedure, the State may designate a Public Prosecutor, an important member of the State Government. She or he is not a member of the investigative team. She or he is a statutory autonomous authority.

Lecturer/ Professor: To supervise or help with supervising undergraduate, taught graduate (Masters), or research graduate (MPhil/PhD) students. To teach at the undergraduate and graduate level in areas designated and periodically reviewed by the Head of Department.

District and Sessions Judge: After years of employment and legal education, veteran advocates are appointed as district and session judges. They are the main component of the court's jurisdiction since they hear the courtroom debate and determine the verdict for the accused.

FAQs about LL.M Criminal Law

What specializations are available in LLM Criminal Law?

LLM Criminal Law is a specialization in itself but there are law institutes that combine the criminal law branch with other branches to increase the chances of employment for all the law aspirants. Such specializations are: LLM in Criminology and Forensic Science, LLM in Security Law and Criminal Law, LLM in Criminal Law and Criminal Justice, etc.
 

Is criminal law a good career option?

Criminal law, arguably among the most well-known legal disciplines, keeps the legal system running smoothly. Criminal law may be the right field for you if you want to work in a contested area of the law while assisting people through challenging and complex life situations.

Is the job of a criminal lawyer stressful?

Criminal case complexity can be challenging and stressful. Criminal defense attorneys experience a great deal of stress both during and before court appearances. Defense lawyers have a bad reputation for being dishonest thanks to some people, and this can hurt them in more ways than they may realize.
 

What are the drawbacks of being a criminal lawyer?

A few of the drawbacks of being a criminal lawyer in India are:

  • Long and hectic working hours
  • Frequently, your existence will only consist of work.
  • Clients can be very picky.
  • Working conditions could be poor.
  • You could be sued.
  • It can be very expensive to attend law school.
  • For lawyers, digitalization poses a threat.
     

Is Criminal Law tough?

Criminal law is challenging, but if you're up for the task, you won't find a more interesting, varied, or stimulating legal career. Qualified candidates who desire to pursue the course would preferably have an imaginative thinking since the line of work requires them to reply to hypothetical legal problems involving criminal crimes. These applicants would be keen to add value to the legal, judicial, and related fields. 

What NLUs offer LLM Criminal Law?

NLUs like MNLU Mumbai, NLU Lucknow, RGNUL Patiala, NLU Hyderabad, and NLU Shimla are a few of the NLUs that offer LLM Criminal Law course in India.

Why do students choose Criminal Law?

Many people decide to major in criminal justice because they want to change the world. They want to put people before all else, safeguard the common good, and assist those in need. You can work with crime victims in the field of criminal justice, help solve crimes, or promote community safety.

What can I pursue after LLM Criminal Law?

Doors to opportunity in the public and private sectors open up for students who complete an LLM in criminal law. Graduates of the LLM in Criminal Law are in great demand across a range of institutions and businesses. These candidates work as attorneys, solicitors, law officers, consultants, or advisors, among other positions.

Is LL.M Criminal Law a good option?

Given the status of the current criminal justice system and the need for reforms, there is a significant demand for candidates with this speciality.
