About B.Tech Agricultural Engineering

B. Tech Agricultural Engineering is an undergraduate program which is of a total of 4 years duration, divided into 8 semesters. The candidates who have passed 12th or any equivalent degree in any Science stream can pursue a course in B. Tech Agricultural Engineering. Through this course, the students are provided with a curriculum of the specialised concept of Agricultural Engineering with in-depth theoretical and practical knowledge of Agricultural concepts. The various areas of concepts which are covered by B. Tech in Agricultural Engineering course include food processing, technology in food production, agricultural process, machinery, etc.

The selection of candidates for admission to B. Tech Agricultural Engineering course is done either through direct admission or through a national level entrance exam such as JEE Mains. The average course fee for B. Tech Agricultural Engineering ranges somewhere in between 50K to 2 lakh depending on the type of Institute.

B.Tech Agricultural Engineering Eligibility Criteria

The candidates who wish to pursue a degree in B. Tech Agricultural Engineering must fulfil certain eligibility criteria in order to get eligible for the course. Though the eligibility criteria for admission to B. Tech Agricultural Engineering differs from college to college, there are certain minimum eligibility criteria which the candidates must fulfil for admission.

The eligibility criteria required for admission to B. Tech Agricultural Engineering are given below:

  • The candidates must have passed 12th or an equivalent degree from a recognised institute/board.

  • The candidate must have opted for Mathematics and Physics as the major subjects in class 12th along with one of the optional subjects as Chemistry/ Biology/ Biotechnology.

  • The candidate must have acquired a minimum aggregate of 60% (55% for the reserved candidates) in the qualifying examination. 

  • The candidates who are appearing for the final exam or waiting for the final result can also apply for admission to B. Tech Agricultural Engineering course.

Admission Process

While some colleges offer admission to B. Tech Agricultural Engineering program on the basis of merit obtained by the student in JEE Main, some of the colleges also conduct their own entrance exam for testing the eligibility of students for admission. Based on the merit obtained by the student in the qualifying exam or an entrance exam, the shortlisted candidates are called for the university level test in which their proficiency in terms of 12th level Science, Mathematics Concept, Aptitude and Reasoning are evaluated. Based on the performance of the student in the entrance exam, the qualified candidates are further called for the personal interview round conducted by the colleges. Based on the overall marks obtained by the student in the entrance exam and the interview round, the eligible candidates are offered admission to the program. 

B. Tech Agricultural Engineering Course Fees

The course fee for B. Tech Agricultural Engineering depends on various factors such as the type of the institute,  the popularity/ ranking of the institute, and the merit/ scholarship earned by the student. For instance, the student having higher marks in the entrance exam will be provided a higher scholarship, accounting for a lower course fee as compared to students with direct admission. On the other hand,  the government based institutes will have much lower fees as compared to private institutes. On average, the course fee for B. Tech Agricultural Engineering program ranges somewhere in between INR 50k to 2 lakh.

B.Tech Agricultural Engineering Career Options and Job Prospects

After completing a course in B. Tech Agricultural Engineering,  the candidates can opt for various career opportunities and job prospects. The candidates who have pursued a degree in B. Tech Agricultural Engineering can go for either a government-based or a private based industry. The various job options which the candidates can take after pursuing a degree in B. Tech Agricultural Engineering includes Agricultural Engineer, Microbiologist, Project Manager, Head Green Keepers, Agricultural Managers, Research Analysts, Agronomists, etc. The starting salary of a candidate for B. FAQs about B.Tech Agriculture Engineering

What is the duration of agricultural engineering courses?

The duration of agricultural engineering courses will vary according to the level of education and type of degree the candidate wishes to study. For instance, a BTech with this specialisation is a 4-year course, while, a Diploma in the course is a 3-year course. Both MTech and PG Diploma in Agricultural Engineering are 2-year courses, while, it may take 3-5 years to complete a doctorate (PhD) programme in the discipline.

What are the top colleges offering B.Tech Agricultural Engineering courses ?

Following are some of the top colleges offering Engineering courses : Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (Delhi) , National Institute of Technology (Tiruchirappalli) , Delhi Technological University (New Delhi) , Indian Institute of Technology (Mumbai) , Indian Institute of Technology (Guwahati). You can explore these colleges on our website to check if they offer B.Tech Agricultural Engineering or not.
