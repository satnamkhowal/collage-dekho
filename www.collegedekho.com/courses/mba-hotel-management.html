An MBA in Hotel Management combines the core principles of business administration with a concentrated focus on hotel and hospitality management. This postgraduate degree program prepares students for high-level management and leadership roles within the hotel industry. After completing the course, students are equipped with the strategic, analytical, and practical skills required to successfully manage and lead in the dynamic and diverse world of hotel management.

What is MBA in Hotel Management?

The MBA in Hotel Management is a two-year postgraduate program designed to equip students with the skills and knowledge needed to effectively oversee the administration and operations of hotels, resorts, and other hospitality establishments. Ideal for both career starters and professionals seeking advancement, this program offers flexible options such as full-time, part-time, and accelerated formats. Admission to MBA in Hotel Management courses in India is based on merit and performance in entrance exams like CAT, XAT, CMAT, or MAT. Before applying to an institute, candidates must check the admission requirements carefully.

Renowned institutions offering the MBA in Hotel Management course include the Institute of Hotel Management, Delhi Institute of Hotel Management, Universal Business School, Amity University, Chandigarh University, and Lovely Professional University, among others. The graduates are presented with diverse career opportunities, ranging from further education in the hospitality field to various roles in the industry. The average salary ranges from INR 3 LPA to INR 10 LPA. For comprehensive information on MBA in Hotel Management subjects, career paths, top colleges, fees, and more, continue reading below.

Why Choose an MBA in Hotel Management Course?

Hotel management combines day-to-day commercial activities with customer service tasks. Fast growth, on-the-job training, and prospects for career advancement are all compelling reasons to consider a career in hotel management. Discover what else drives people to choose an MBA in Hotel Management degree, as well as great tips for breaking into this industry.

  • Early Responsibility: As a hotel manager, you will oversee reception, housekeeping, catering, and maintenance. You'll hire employees, manage budgets, handle PR, and set sales goals. Advancement opportunities are excellent for determined graduates, who typically progress from trainee to department head within two years.
  • Great Salary Potential: Salaries in the hotel, travel, and tourist industries vary by position. Assistant general managers' starting salary ranges from INR 25,000 to INR 50,000 per month, while general managers can earn up to INR 70,000 to INR 90,000 per month.
  • Varied Fields of Study: Hotel management is a challenging field that requires adaptability and flexibility due to irregular and unsocial working hours. An MBA in Hotel Management can open doors to managing different departments or working in various locations.
  • Job Satisfaction: As a hotel manager, your goal is to make every guest's stay comfortable and delightful while providing excellent customer service. Positive feedback and ratings will bring job satisfaction and motivate you to achieve better results.
  • Creative Input: Constant innovation is crucial in the hospitality and tourism industry. As a hotel manager with an MBA in Hotel Management, generating new ideas is important to improve guest experience and enhance the organization's reputation.
  • Learn Vital Skills: Management requires both soft and hard skills. Soft skills like leadership, teamwork, and flexibility are highly valued by recruiters and impact visitor satisfaction. Hard skills like business acumen and attention to detail are critical to an organization's success.
  • Travel Globally: An MBA in Hotel Management can offer global opportunities in hospitality, travel, and tourism. As a manager for a large chain hotel, you can gain valuable cultural perspectives from working with people from different backgrounds.

MBA in Hotel Management Eligibility Criteria

Graduates in any stream of study will be eligible to seek admission into the MBA in Hotel Management program. Preference will be given to candidates with working experience. Admission to MBA in Hotel Management will be based on Professional Experience and Personal Interviews. The eligibility criteria for an MBA in Hotel Management slightly vary in different institutes, keeping the main requirements the same:

  • Candidates must complete the bachelor's in any stream under any recognized institution with a minimum aggregate score of 50%.
  • 5% relaxation is allowed to students of reserved categories like OBC, SC, and ST.
  • Candidates appearing for final year examinations can also apply provided they secure a minimum of 50% marks. 
  • A minimum of 2 years of work experience is preferred.
  • It is a mandate at several educational institutions to conduct admission to Hotel Management based on entrance exams like CAT, GMAT, CMAT, etc. Cut-off scores depend highly on category reservations and college reputation.

Skills Required for MBA in Hotel Management

Apart from Business Knowledge, Strategic thinking, Brand Awareness, etc. MBA in Hotel  Management candidates must be equipped with the following important hard and soft skills:

  • Communication skills: An individual must possess excellent verbal and written communication skills and the ability to convince others. 
  • Detail-Oriented: To ensure each guest has an unforgettable experience, managers must remain extra vigilant about every detail. Delegating tasks among staff members is an important aspect of management. Multi-tasking is crucial when overseeing several aspects of the hotel. 
  • Operational Knowledge: Basic understanding of concepts like accounting, human resource management, or cost control, managers are required to make decisions daily.
  • Teamwork: Building a cooperative and open team environment will help improve both your staff’s productivity and guest experience.
  • Financial Management: Hotel managers must have the ability to read financial reports, understand basic financial concepts and analyze financial data. They may be responsible for bank deposits, cashouts, revenue management, and accounts payable and receivable. 
  • Leadership skills: Candidates must be equipped with strong leadership skills. Individuals with great leadership skills empower teams to achieve their full potential, make decisions, and strategize well to help organisations thrive.

MBA in Hotel Management Admission Process

Admission to top colleges in India for MBA in Hotel Management is typically granted based on scores obtained in entrance exams. The admission process is discussed in detail below.

  • Appear for Entrance Exam: If you are interested in pursuing an MBA program from a reputed college, then you must first clear an entrance test. CAT, MAT, XAT, SNAP, ATMA, etc. These exams are administered by various institutes/authorities at different times of the year. Once you pass the entrance test, you can start applying to the universities of your choice.
  • Participate in Counselling: Students who qualify for the entrance exams are invited for further rounds of selection, which may include a personal interview (PI), group discussion (GD), or both. These rounds aim to assess your communication skills, analytical abilities, problem-solving skills, and other relevant attributes.
  • Appear in the Merit List: The universities will finally release a list of selected candidates based on their performance in the entrance test and the subsequent rounds of selection. If you find your name on the list, then you can accept the offer by paying the fees and securing your seat in the program.

Career Options After MBA in Hotel Management

Hotel management is an umbrella term that encompasses a variety of professional roles in the hotel industry. Candidates can be absorbed in several areas within a hotel, such as the front office, operations, accounting, maintenance, security, sales and marketing, and food, and can specialise in the field of their choice to pursue a successful career path. The scope of hotel management is expanding dramatically.

Let us take a closer look at four common careers that you can get with an MBA in Hotel Management degree.

Job ProfileJob Description
Hotel ManagerThey are responsible for maximizing profits, ensuring guest satisfaction, and maintaining operational effectiveness. By analyzing operating information, they work towards achieving budgeted cost standards. They have a strong working knowledge of all aspects of the hotel and have a proven track record of managing, motivating, and leading a full-service hotel team.
Restaurant Manager They train and supervise staff, ensure all food safety procedures are strictly adhered to according to sanitary regulations, work closely with management to meet revenue objectives, implement appropriate strategies to resolve adverse trends and improve sales, etc.
Food and Beverage Manager They organise, direct and evaluate food and beverage service; recruit and train staff; schedule their shifts; monitor staff performance and provide feedback; purchase and control inventory, etc.
Head ChefThey control and direct the food preparation process; approve and polish dishes before they reach the customer; manage and work closely with other Chefs of all levels; create menu items, and recipes and develop dishes ensuring variety and quality, etc.
Hospitality ExecutiveThey manage and coordinate all the different departments in an establishment in the hospitality or restaurant sector. Their role is to define the commercial and management strategy of the establishment in line with profit targets, the quality charter and hygiene and safety norms.
Duty Manager A duty manager is in charge of the overall upkeep of the restaurant, which includes the safety and hygiene of the establishment. A Duty Manager works with other managers and employees to resolve customer complaints and other issues related to restaurant operations.
Spurs ChefAfter the Head Chef, the Sous Chef or Culinary Chef is in command. They play a vital role in food preparation and presentation.
Banquet ManagerThe Banquet Manager is responsible for coordinating the delivery of all food and beverages for all hotel parties, gatherings, events, and conferences. He must collaborate closely with the food and beverage service manager and the catering department to guarantee that food, beverages, and snacks are served to customers on schedule.
Floor SupervisorsFloor supervisors keep guest spaces and working areas clean and organized, monitor personnel, track daily revenue, produce performance reports, maintain disciplinary standards, train new employees, and distribute day-to-day responsibilities.
