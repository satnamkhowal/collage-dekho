What is an Executive MBA Course?

Executive MBA course or, the EMBA Course, is a specialised management programme which is administered at the postgraduate level. This course is specifically curated for working professionals who seek to build their CV and increase their career prospects. Typically lasting between 1 to 2 years, the EMBA course duration can vary as per the course structure of an institution. Admission to an Executive MBA course is done on the basis of a candidate's merit, entrance exam scores, and 3 to 5 or more years of work experience. However, some colleges offer direct admission, requiring a bachelor's degree and relevant work experience. The Executive MBA syllabus offers an in-depth understanding of various facets of business management, preparing students to navigate complex challenges, seize opportunities, and drive organisational success in their respective fields. 

According to the Executive MBA eligibility criteria, a candidate must have completed their bachelor’s degree with an aggregate percentage of 50% marks. In addition to that, the candidate should also have appeared for the Executive MBA entrance exams like CAT, CMAT, MAT, NMAT etc. Graduates in this course are more likely to choose leadership positions because of their skill acquisition and advanced knowledge arena. Salaries of Executive MBA candidates are much higher than those of candidates who don’t hold the same background. 

Renowned B-schools like IIMs, IITs, FMS Delhi, SPJIMR, and ISB Hyderabad offer courses in EMBA, with fees ranging from INR 4 LPA to INR 14 LPA. Upon graduation, candidates can pursue Executive MBA jobs such as Sales Executive, Business Development Manager, or Senior Finance Manager, with an average Executive MBA salary ranging from INR 12 LPA to INR 40 LPA in India. Choosing an Executive MBA course can be a strategic investment in your career, providing you with the tools and opportunities to achieve your professional goals and aspirations. If you're intrigued by the prospects of the course, continue reading below to discover all the comprehensive details about it.

Why Choose an Executive MBA Course?

An Executive MBA degree course is a multifaceted investment for a working professional to fast-track their career and expand their career opportunities in the field of Executive MBA. With a comprehensive understanding of business, a candidate will be able to qualify for some of the top managerial roles all around the world. If you are someone who’s aspiring to take admission into an EMBA course, then take note of the factors that have been mentioned below to determine if pursuing this course will suit your needs or not : 

Career Growth: Pursuing an Executive MBA course can act as a significant boost for mid-career professionals who are looking to make their way into higher management or executive roles in the corporate sector. The course will equip them with the skill set required by the top managers and bestows them with the leadership skills and a keen vision necessary for top-level positions.

Networking Opportunities: EMBA programs bring together professionals from diverse industries, enhancing one’s network with valuable contacts. These interactions can lead to new business opportunities, partnerships, and insights into different sectors.

Enhanced Knowledge and Skills: The EMBA curriculum is tailored to address the complex challenges of modern business environments. It helps sharpen decision-making abilities, improve analytical skills, and understand global business trends.

Work-Life Balance: Executive MBA programs are designed with the working professional in mind, allowing students to balance their education with their professional and personal responsibilities. Classes are often scheduled on weekends or in intensive modules.

Immediate Application of Learning: Since EMBA students are usually employed full-time, they have the unique advantage of applying new concepts and strategies directly to their current roles, offering immediate benefits to their employers while also solidifying their learnings.

Executive MBA Admission Process in India

The EMBA admission process depends on the scores of common entrance exams. Accepted entrance exams are GMAT, GRE, and EMAT. After taking the entrance exams, the admission process is divided into certain steps by the institutes. Here is the complete Executive MBA admission process mentioned below step-by-step : 

Step 1 : Entrance Exam

GMAT is the most commonly accepted entrance exam to courses in Executive MBA. Most IIMs consider GMAT for a professional degree course in the field of Executive MBA. A 750+ score on GMAT is considered a proficient score to attain admission into the top colleges for Executive MBA. IIMs like IIM Kozhikode, IIM Shillong, and IIM Rohtak accept CAT scores. The CAT score is valid for up to a time span of 5 years. IIM Ranchi, IIM Raipur, and IIM Trichy conduct their own exam.

Step 2 : Language Test

This round is mandatory in IIM Bangalore EPGP and IIM Udaipur's PGPX. After taking and qualifying for the entrance exam, candidates have to appear for the language test round.

Step 3 : Group Discussion

This round evaluates the candidate’s overall personality – knowledge, point of view, communication skills, leadership skills, and behaviour in a group. In order to obtain excellent scores, one must be well-versed in current affairs worldwide. Most of the MBA colleges offering Executive MBA courses conduct the GD round to judge the suitability of an individual for business management education and career. 

Step 4 : Essay Writing

A candidate’s goals and aspirations with regards to the field of business administration are evaluated through the Writing Ability Test (WAT) which is an essential part of the admission process to an Executive MBA Course. The Writing Ability Test carries a huge weightage in the Executive MBA admission process. 

Step 5 : Personal Interview

The personal interview round is the final round in which a candidate’s overall personality is to be evaluated critically. The candidate will be evaluated on the basis of his or her personality traits and attitude. The panel tends to look out for distinct personality traits in a candidate and then accordingly decides whether he/she would be able to add value to the programme or not.

Courses After EMBA Degree

Executives have various options for further education and professional development after completing an Executive MBA (EMBA) program. The courses they choose to pursue depend on their career goals, interests, and desired skills. Here are a few options to go for.

  • Fellow Programme in Management: This four-year residential course is designed to provide in-depth training in various fields of business and management, preparing participants to become experts in their chosen area of research.
  • PhD in Business Administration: It involves in-depth study and research in areas like management, finance, marketing, operations, or organisational behaviour, which is useful for those pursuing careers in academia or advanced research.
  • Specialised Master's Degrees: Executives can pursue specialised master's degrees to deepen their expertise in specific business areas or to pivot into new industries. Examples of specialised master's degrees include MFin, Master of Marketing, Master of Supply Chain Management, or Master of Data Analytics.
  • Executive Education Programs: Business schools and executive education providers offer short-term programs for experienced professionals on leadership, strategy, innovation, digital transformation, and industry trends.
  • Advanced Leadership Programs: Top executives can enhance their leadership skills by enrolling in advanced leadership development programs offered by leading business schools or executive education providers. These programs focus on driving organisational change and navigating complex business challenges.
  • Certificate Courses: Executives can get certified in specific areas to enhance their credentials. Examples include CFA, CPA, PMP, Six Sigma, and CMA.
  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation Programs: One can enrol in programs that focus on startup creation, venture capital, innovation management, or corporate entrepreneurship to learn the knowledge and skills needed to launch new ventures or drive innovation within existing organisations.
  • Industry-Specific Programs: Executives can pursue industry-specific programs or certifications such as healthcare management for healthcare, tech management or cybersecurity for technology, and hotel management or tourism for hospitality.

FAQs about Executive MBA

Can I pursue an Executive MBA online?

Yes, you can pursue an Executive MBA online. Many universities and business schools offer online EMBA programs designed specifically for working professionals who need flexibility in their studies. Online EMBA programs typically cover the same core business concepts as traditional EMBA programs.

Is a part-time Executive MBA a good option?

Yes, a part-time Executive MBA is a good option as it offers classes in the evenings, at weekends, or in a modular format. If you need to continue working while pursuing your Executive MBA, a part-time program may provide the flexibility you need to balance work and personal commitments.

How can an Executive MBA increase my career prospects?

Pursuing an Executive MBA can increase your career prospects in several ways. As it offers advanced education in various business disciplines, you can become a more versatile and effective leader, increasing your value to employers. EMBA from a reputable business school enhances your professional credibility and can open doors to opportunities.

What are the top colleges to study Executive MBA in India?

Some of the top colleges to study Executive MBA in India include IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Bangalore, IIM Kozhikode, IIM Calcutta, IIM Lucknow, IIM Indore, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, XLRI Jamshedpur, MDI Gurgaon, Symbiosis Institute of Business Management, SP Jain School of Global Management, and NMIMS School of Business Management.

Is Executive MBA degree valid?

Yes, Executive MBA degree is valid, be it online or offline, if it has been obtained from a recognised university, college or institute. The degree is equivalent to a postgraduate degree in India.

Is Executive MBA approved by UGC?

Yes, an Executive MBA is approved by UGC and is an accredited formal degree program. However, the same should vary from one B-school to another. One must make sure to check with the concerned institute if the EMBA course they have on offer is approved by UGC.

Is Executive MBA as good as regular MBA?

Yes, Executive MBA is as good as regular MBA. However, EMBA tends to be more intensive because of its cut-down structure and focus more on training individuals for the executive level. That said, both courses cover the same material and offer similar job opportunities.

How can I get admission into Executive MBA offered by IIMs?

To get admission into the Executive MBA offered by IIMs, candidates need to appear for entrance exams like GMAT, CAT, XAT, GATE, PGP-WE Entrance Exam, GRE, etc. It is necessary to have a bachelor's degree with a minimum of 50% marks and work experience of 3-10 years, depending on the colleges.

What is the difference between Executive MBA and MBA?

Both MBA and Executive MBA are professional management degrees that impart knowledge of business management, operations, and administration. MBA is an excellent choice for graduates looking to make a career in the Management field. While Executive MBA is great for working professionals who want to gain managerial skills to grab better career opportunities. Each course serves a different purpose for different individuals based on how best they can juggle classes with the responsibility of a day job.

What is Executive MBA fees?

The course fees for pursuing an Executive MBA course range from INR 8 to 22 lakh. The fees for Executive MBA from IIMs is higher than that of other popular colleges in India. Executive MBA is generally costlier than Regular MBA.

Do IIMs offer Executive MBA courses?

Yes, IIMs offer Executive MBA courses. IIM Ahmedabad offers a 1-year PGPX course through GMAT for candidates with 50% marks in bachelor's degree and 5 years of work experience. IIM Bangalore, IIM Calcutta, IIM Indore, IIM Udaipur, etc. also offer Executive MBA courses.

What jobs can I get after Executive MBA?

The jobs candidates can get after an Executive MBA include Sales Manager, Business Development Manager, Brand Manager, Communications Manager, Quality Control Manager, Senior Finance Manager, Senior Business Analyst, Customer Service Manager, Cash Manager, Chief Financial Officer, Product Manager, Logistics Manager, and Chief Executive Officer.

Is Executive MBA worth investing in?

Yes, Executive MBA is definitely worth investing in if you are looking to accelerate your career after having a work experience of 5-10 years. By studying an Executive MBA course, you can bring about a change in your vision of business and master strategic corporate issues, and develop a capacity for entrepreneurship. Individuals having significant professional experience become eligible for a huge jump in salary, position, as well as a location after pursuing an EMBA degree.

What are the benefits of pursuing EMBA?

There are a lot of benefits to pursuing an EMBA. It allows working professionals to get a global perspective of business, provides an international dimension to one’s career, and opens up new fields of expertise. EMBA helps build a network of influential connections. It provides an in-depth understanding of various management subjects.

What is Executive MBA salary?

The Executive MBA salary ranges between INR 12 LPA to INR 40 LPA in India. The salary varies depending on factors such as industry, location, job role, and experience. It may increase with time as the candidate gains more experience and expertise in their field.

Which entrance exam scores are accepted for admission to EMBA in India?

Scores of entrance exams like CAT, EMAT, NMAT by GMAC, MAT, CMAT, XAT, SNAP, etc. are accepted for admission to EMBA in India. Before applying for a particular exam, candidates must that whether their desired college accepts its score.

What are the top recruiters of Executive MBA?

The top recruiters of Executive MBA are TCS, Wipro, KPMG, Capgemini, Amazon, Flipkart, Google, Axis Bank, MTNL, BSNL, ONGC, Coal India, State Bank of India, Punjab National Bank, Regulatory Authority of India, National Council for Cement and Building Materials, and more.

Who should pursue an Executive MBA?

Working professionals who have already gained significant experience in the field of business management and leadership and are looking to further enhance their knowledge and skills should pursue an Executive MBA. It is suitable for those who are already working in executive or senior management positions and want to advance their career prospects.

What is Executive MBA eligibility criteria?

The Executive MBA eligibility criteria include a 10+2 education and a bachelor's degree in any field from a recognized university with a minimum mark of 55% on average. A work experience of 3-8 years is required, but it varies as per the college.

Is an online Executive MBA course available?

Yes, an online Executive MBA course is available for working professionals. It has a duration of 15-20 months and caters to the needs of experienced professionals who are typically working full-time and seeking to enhance their managerial and leadership skills.

What is the difference between an MBA and Executive MBA?

Executive MBA course is specially designed for working professionals with 5-10 years of experience seeking career advancement while a regular MBA course is designed for students seeking management education and who want to build a career in business administration.
