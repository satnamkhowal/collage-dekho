MTech Course Overview

MTech full form Master of Technology is a two-year postgraduate engineering degree offered in various specializations like civil engineering, mechanical engineering, chemical engineering, electrical engineering, etc. MTech in Data Science and MTech in Computer Science are some of the most popular MTech specializations. Candidates can pursue an M Tech course after completing graduation in BE or B Tech from a recognized institute. MTech admissions are done majorly via national-level entrance exams like GATE. Some colleges also conduct their entrance exams or offer admission based on merits. AP PGECET, TS PGECET, TANCET, etc. are some other popular MTech entrance exams. MTech Syllabus teaches students about creating new pathways for research and development and acquiring existing technologies in science and technology. 

MTech Admission can be done for candidates who want to pursue regular (full-time), sponsored (full-time), part-time M.Tech, online MTech, and project staff (for those who are currently working on funded projects). MTech courses are regulated by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), the University Grants Commission of India (UGC), and the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). The Indian government is implementing various scholarship programs and funding initiatives to promote postgraduate engineering education actively. As a result, MTech has become a more viable option for students who wish to pursue it.

Candidates who want to pursue M.Tech after MCA and some choose an MTech course after doing M.Sc. from some of the top MTech colleges. As per the eligibility criteria for MTech, candidates have to secure at least 60% marks in BTech or related fields. After completing the M Tech course, candidates can take up positions such as Project Manager, Senior Engineer, Research Associate, Development Engineer, Construction Manager, etc in public, and private sectors or MNCs.  Some of the top M Tech colleges in India are IIT Bombay, KJ Somaiya College Of Engineering, Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Technology, VJTI, ICT Mumbai, etc. The average MTech course fees range between INR 50,000 to 2,00,000 annually. After the conclusion of the course, candidates are offered MTech jobs as Senior Engineer, Project Manager, Researcher, and Technical Consultant. The average starting MTech Salary ranges from INR 3,00,000 to 6,00,000 Per Annum.

Why Choose M Tech Degree?

Candidates who have completed their MTech can expand their career opportunities by improving their practical knowledge. They have the potential to attain high-ranking positions in the industrial sector and serve as consultants for research and development institutions. In addition, they can seek employment in Public Sector Undertakings that deal with core industries such as Mining, Chemicals, Construction, and Real Estate, among others. Pursuing M Tech after the B Tech will open several career opportunities for you and at the same time will embed you with more technical knowledge and skills in engineering. 

Why Study MTech Course?

Following are the benefits of pursuing M Tech after B Tech. 

Acquire Technical Knowledge

After you pursue MTech, you acquire in-depth technological knowledge and authority over the topic. You acquire more technical skills and sharpen your knowledge in your discipline of engineering. 

Secure Promotion in Your Workplace

As one's expertise in technology grows, there are possibilities to move into managerial positions. MTech degree may lead to professional prospects in a variety of fields, including Chemical Engineering, Neural Networks, Robotics Engineering, VLSI Design, and many more.

Get a Rise in Salary

Candidates can salary hike up after pursuing MTech and their chances of promotion also rise. Candidates with an M Tech degree in India may expect to earn between INR 5 LPA to INR 7 LPA. Professionals with more than ten years of expertise can expect to earn up to INR 15,00,000 per year or even more. 

Make a Career in Research-Based Jobs

Research is one of the most important and wide-ranging sectors of India. There will never be a shortage of research-based employment around the globe. Those aiming for research-based professions can readily advance with an MTech degree in hand. Because an MTech will give the information, grasp of the subject area, and skills essential for pursuing a research career, it might be useful for people looking for a research-based profession.

Types of M Tech Programs

Candidates who are willing to pursue MTech courses can opt for sponsored (full-time), regular (full-time), part-time M.Tech, online MTech, and project staff (for those who are currently working on financed projects). Below, we have discussed the types of courses one can choose.

M Tech Full Time Couse

One of the most popular degrees among research enthusiasts who wish to engage in the field of technical research is MTech or Master of Technology. After completing a BTech or BE. The M Tech full-time course is a  2-year postgraduate degree with four semesters. In full-time M Tech, program candidates are required to attend the colleges, hold the required attendance, pass the semesters, make projects, etc.  

M Tech Part-Time Course

Working professionals who are unable to attend a full-time Master of Technology program might enroll in a part-time M Tech program. To be admitted to this program, students must have completed a B.Tech degree with a specific cutoff rate. To pursue M Tech part-time course A B.E/B.Tech degree or equivalent in relevant specialties and must have a grade point average of at least 55 percent. Work experience is sometimes necessary. This varies with each college, and sometimes no prior job experience is required.

M Tech Distance Learning Course 

M Tech is a two-year post-graduate program for individuals who have earned a bachelor's degree in technology from a recognized university. In the MTech distance learning course, traditional classroom education is replaced with the MTech online learning software. Students in distance learning education take online classes to learn. They are free to take courses from wherever they wish. The projects and assignments are submitted online in distance learning. Students will get a deeper grasp of the disciplines they learned throughout their bachelor's degree through the distance M Tech program.

IGNOU M Tech

Candidates seeking M Tech admission at IGNOU are chosen based on their eligibility for the course, followed by the requisite entrance score/merit in the test. IGNOU admissions are held twice a year, in January and July. IGNOU admits students based on their performance in the last qualifying examination. MTech Eligibility Criteria

M Tech course eligibility criteria are the protocols that an institute maintains for admitting candidates into several institutions. Candidates who wish to gain expertise in different areas of engineering and build a successful career in industry, research, or development, can pursue MTech Course. Hence, those who wish to pursue a profession as a Principal Engineer, Civil Engineer, Mechanical Engineer, Consultant, or Research Associate should register for this course. For admission to M Tech courses in India, below are the basic eligibility criteria that the institutions follow for taking admission of the candidates. Candidates need to note that the MTech eligibility criteria vary from college to college and some colleges have age-limit criteria also.

Who Should Study M Tech Courses?

  • Candidates must have a valid GATE score and a BE or BTech degree from a recognized college or university.
  • The most important criterion in determining whether or not a candidate is admitted to the Master of Technology program is their performance on the entrance exam (GATE) or another institution's entrance exam.
  • Candidates who have completed a five-year scientific or applied science course are eligible to apply for the M Tech program.
  • Candidates who have achieved an M.Sc degree in an area such as science, math, statistics, computer science, or any other similar topic are eligible to apply.
  • MCA degree graduates can also apply for specializations in fields like software engineering design.

M Tech Eligibility Criteria for Part-Time Course

  • Candidates must have a minimum of 55% in their B.Tech/B.E program.
  • A minimum of two years of work experience in the particular subject from a recognized firm/company/industry or research/governmental organization.
  • Before applying for admission, candidates should review the qualifying requirements of each institute.

What is an Online M Tech Degree?

Online M Tech programs are specially designed for people who are busy with their work or other commitments and cannot attend regular on-campus classes. These programs make use of digital technologies and online platforms to provide lectures, coursework, and assessments. Students can access study materials, participate in discussions, and submit assignments from anywhere.

It's important to know that different institutions may have different types of online M Tech programs. Some offer fully online courses, while others have a mix of online and on-campus classes. If you're considering enrolling in an online M Tech program, it's essential to do some research beforehand. Check if the institution is accredited, learn about the reputation of the program, and make sure you understand the requirements and features of the online learning format.

M Tech Admission Process in India

The M Tech admissions are usually done via the engineering entrance examination. However, some colleges such as Lovely Professional University, and VIT Bhopal University also accept merit-based admissions. The MTech admission process differs from one institute to the next. Check the M Tech admission process via the entrance exam and merit-based given below.

M Tech Admissions via Entrance Exams

Admission to MTech is also done through university-level and state-level entrance exams besides national-level exams. GATE, WBUT, PGET, and other entrance tests are some of the popular M Tech entrance exams. To know more about the M Tech admissions through entrance exams get details below.

Fill out the application form: Candidates must wait for the release of the official notification of the MTech entrance exams. After the notification is out, the exam conducting body opens the registration process. Candidates must fill out the M Tech exams application form by providing all the details, uploading documents, and paying fees. 

Sit for the exam: After completing the registration process, the candidate must download the admit card for the exam which is usually released 7 to 10 days before the exam. Candidates must bring the admit card to the exam center and appear for the exam. Passing the M Tech exam with good grades might be challenging due to tough competition. Therefore, candidates are always advised to prepare properly for the entrance exams.

Wait for the release of the Result: The exam conducting body will release the M Tech exam result soon after the exam is conducted. The result will include names of students who qualified in the exam with their name, roll number, marks secured, cutoff, etc. Candidates must download the result and keep it safe for further admission rounds 

Appearance for Counselling and Seat Allotment: After the announcement of the result, further counselling and seat allotment rounds will be conducted. M Tech counselling process includes registration, choice filling, document verification, seat allotment rounds, etc. The authorities give admission into the M.Tech course by allocating seats based on merit, availability of seats, and preferred selections.

Direct M Tech Admission Process Without Entrance Exam

M.Tech merit-based admission is done through the graduation score. To know more about MTech merit-based admission, check the details given below. 

Fill out the Application Form: To take M Tech admission through merit-based, first shortlist colleges offering admission like that. The respective college will open the application portal. Candidates should fill out the M Tech application form carefully as it will decide whether they will get a seat in the college or not. Students should provide educational details, and upload an educational certificate, and other required documents to complete the registration process. 

Release of the Merit List: After the registration process, the colleges will release the merit list after analyzing the application form. Candidates whose names appear on the merit list will be selected for M Tech direct admission. After the release of the merit list, some colleges offer seats, and some college also conducts additional selection rounds. 

Further Admission Rounds: After the release of the merit list, some colleges offer seats, and some college also conducts additional selection rounds. Candidates may have to appear for the counselling process, group discussion, or personal interview round.

M Tech Syllabus and Subjects

The M Tech course has two types of subjects - core and optional. Core subjects are the ones that all students have to take, regardless of their specialty. Optional subjects are electives that provide flexibility and diversity to the course. In the first year of the course, students learn about general engineering subjects, while in the following three semesters, they learn about specific courses related to their specialty. Additionally, practical workshops help students understand their specialty in depth.

The M Tech course involves both classroom teaching and hands-on workshops. These workshops aim to give students opportunities to apply their knowledge in real-life situations. Classroom teaching allows students to ask questions and get answers to them. Depending on the specialization students opt for, the MTech course contents may change. What is the Difference Between MTech and ME Courses?

Candidates are often confused about pursuing a Masters in Technology (MTech)  or a Master’s in Engineering (ME) after a bachelor's degree in engineering. Both are master’s programs in the engineering domain but there are differences between them. To help you get a clear idea, we have provided a detailed difference table below. 

M Tech ME 
MTech is known as Master of Technology.ME is known as Master of Engineering.
M Tech is technically oriented and focuses on developing the technical skills of students ME is more of a knowledge-oriented degree and focuses on giving candidates theoretical knowledge about engineering. 
MTech is meant to develop Research Engineers who can work in industry and research organizations as "Technologists."M.E. is often developed for students who intend to work after graduation. Also for those who are already employed in the engineering field.
MTech is prepared to prepare candidates as research engineers who can get the position of “Technologist” in the research institutes and industries.ME is developed for candidates who prepare themselves for employment in the field of research and development.

FAQs about M.Tech

Is the MTech distance education course from BITS in Pilani valid?

The MTech course offered by BITS Pilani through distance education is a legitimate and recognized course holding the same value in terms of employment and further education opportunities as traditional MTech courses and has been approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC).

BITS Pilani's Work Integrated Learning Programmes (WILP) are designed for working professionals who are looking for career advancement without having to give up their employment. The WILP M.Tech program offers a wide variety of specializations, including Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Construction Management.
 

What is the GATE cutoff for 2023?

The GATE cutoff 2023 for different MTech courses has been mentioned. The GATE cutoff 2023 for CS is 32.5, Electrical Engineering is 25, Mechanical Engineering is 28.4, ECE    is 29.9, Physics is 31.1, and Instrumentation Engineering is 34.8.
 

What is the difference between GATE Qualifying Cutoff and GATE Admission Cutoff?

The GATE Qualifying Cutoff is used to decide if a candidate can be admitted to any postgraduate program. On the other hand, the GATE Admission Cutoff is utilized to pick candidates for admission to a specific program in a particular institute. Additionally, the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore determines the qualifying cutoff, while the respective institute decides the admission cutoff.
 

Which is better BTech or MTech?

Having a MTech is better than BTech especially when it comes to getting good job opportunities. BTech is an undergraduate degree that can help you land entry-level jobs in the tech industry. However, if you want to have more advanced and specialized job opportunities across the globe, pursuing an MTech degree can be a great option. It can provide you with professional and practical training in a specific area of study that interests you the most. So, if you are someone who is passionate about engineering and wants to achieve your career goals, an MTech degree can be a decent choice for you.
 

Who is eligible for MTech?

Candidates who have completed their BTech with at least 60 to 70% marks in aggregate from a recognized institution are eligible to pursue MTech courses.
 

Which MTech course is best?

MTech in artificial intelligence, information technology, computer science engineering, mechanical engineering, and civil engineering are some of the best MTech courses.
 

Is MTech a 2 year course?

Yes, MTech is a 2 year course.
 

What is MTech degree?

MTech is a course that concentrates on the advanced study of science and technology. The priority of the MTech course is engineering and information technology.
 

What is M Tech full form?

M Tech full form is Master of Technology. After completing two years of study in engineering, candidates receive an MTech degree as a professional postgraduate engineering master's degree. M Tech is a specialised degree that focuses more on practical knowledge and hence the emphasis is done on the “technological” aspect compared to the theoretical one.

What is the M.Tech course duration?

The M.Tech course duration is two years for a full-time on-campus degree programme. However, if a student opts for distance/online or part-time M Tech courses, it may take nearly three years to complete. To be eligible for MTech admission, prospective students must pass the state-level entrance test or the GATE exam.

What is the M Tech fee in India?

The M Tech fee in India ranges from INR 50,000 to INR 4 LPA depending on the type of course specialisation a student has chosen as well as on their preferred university. Four options for obtaining a Master of Technology degree are available: full-time or regular M Tech, sponsored, part-time, and project staff (mostly for individuals involved in funded projects). Hence, your M Tech fee will differ depending upon the type of specialisation.

Who should opt for M Tech courses?

The following are a few pointers that make it clear who should opt for M Tech courses:

  • Candidates interested in a profession in science and technology who are curious about how certain machines, gadgets, or software are produced.

  • Candidates who pursue an MTech after completing a BTech have the chance to work for reputable MNCs and earn a higher salary.

What is an integrated M Tech course?

An integrated M Tech is a combination of a BTech and an MTech degree. Students interested in incorporating their undergraduate and postgraduate studies opt for such integrated BE courses. After passing their 10+2 exams, students can opt for an integrated MTech course that lasts for an overall duration of five years (3 + 2 years).
