All About M.Tech Information Technology Course

M.Tech Information Technology or M.Tech IT is a 2-year postgraduate degree programme that teaches both practical and theoretical knowledge on the analysis of computer-based information systems at a higher level. This postgraduate degree programme in Information technology is recommended especially for those candidates who are specializing in intelligent networks, wireless networking, software engineering, computer programming and other fields related to them. M.Tech information technology students can specialize in a variety of fields like communications, networking, embedded systems, software engineering, computer science and many more.  

Usually, the institutions offering admission into the 2-year M.Tech information technology course accept the admission application of those who have a B.E or B.Tech degree in a relevant discipline from any AMIE recognized institution. Moreover, a minimum of 60% aggregate score is mandatory for the aspiring candidates to be eligible to apply for M.Tech information technology in a reputed institution.

After obtaining the M.Tech information technology degree from a reputed university or institution, a student can get hired as a Network Analyst, IT Expert, Computer Programmer, PHP Developer, Software Engineer and so on. The average salary offered to an M.Tech IT degree holder is in the range of INR 3 LPA and INR 10 LPA.

M.Tech Information Technology Admission Process

There are different ways through which an M.Tech information technology aspirant can take admission into a reputed college -

Entrance based admissions

Aspirants have to appear in a reputed PG entrance exam like GATE, OJEE, AP PGECET, TANCET etc and based on the score obtained can apply for admission in a reputed institute

Merit-based admissions

Aspirants can apply directly in an institute offering M.Tech IT based on the previous qualifying degree exam score

M.Tech Information Technology Career Options and Job Prospects

One can always find a nice paying job in a reputed company like HCL, ASUS, Sasken Communication, Siemens, Cisco Systems, Wipro, BSNL etc with an M.Tech degree in information technology. Such companies hire the highly-skilled students in the following job profiles -

  • IT Specialist

  • Technical Consultant

  • Quality Analyst

  • Software Developer

  • PHP Developer

  • Computer Programmer 

During the initial stages of the career, one can earn in the bracket of INR 1 LPA to INR 3 LPA but after having spent some time with reputed companies such as the ones mentioned above, one can easily land a high paying job. The annual salary package in such cases can go as high as INR 20 LPA.

Which GATE exam have to appear for PG Marine Engineering admission at the Great Eastern Institute of Maritime Studies?
-Esha -Updated on Sept-02-20251 Answers
F

Falak Khan, Content Team

-Answered on Sept-02-2025

To get PG Marine Engineering admission at the Great Eastern Institute of Maritime Studies, you do not need to appear for the GATE exam. The Great Eastern Institute of Maritime Studies offers postgraduate course admission based on their initial assessment exam, which they conduct. You first need to fill out the application form at the institute's official website, after which you will be notified of the assessment test. You need to give and pass the assessment test. The admissions committee will establish the cut-off score for the exam. If you score above the cut-off score will be invited to an interview on the GEIMS campus in Lonavala. After interview selection, you will need to pass a full medical examination in Mumbai, as per the Merchant Shipping (Medical Examination) Rules, 2000. The final merit list is determined by academic scores, online test scores, a personal interview, and a medical exam. Once selected, you have to pay a non-refundable booking fee before the deadline to secure your seat.
