Interested individuals in the hospitality and travel industry can take up this course as it teaches effective leadership skills, customer skills, and the latest industry technology.

Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM) Overview

BHM or Bachelor of Hotel Management is a three-year bachelors course in the hospitality industry. Some colleges also offer four-year courses to students like Welcomegroup Graduate School of Hotel Administration. Nowadays, hospitality management degrees are employed in any industry or field that requires personnel to manage and run a firm, not only the hotel industry. A bachelor of hotel management programme provides a thorough examination of the hospitality business. Obtaining this degree will prepare you to manage the operations of hotels, motels, and resorts at their best. The syllabus of the Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM) provides students with a foundation for building a comprehensive understanding of the hospitality sector. Tourism, Human Resources, Culinary Arts, Event Management, Travel and Tourism, Law, Finance, Business Management, Accounting and Taxation, Marketing and Sales, and other sub-disciplines are included in the Bachelor of Hotel Management curriculum. Students are also taught soft skills such as communication skills. time management and ability to work under pressure. 

The eligibility for a Bachelor of Hotel Management degree is based on their performance in class 12 as well as qualifying and acceptable marks on entrance tests such as the NCHMCT JEE, AIMA UGAT, UPSEE, AIHMCT, GNIHM, and others. Some of the top Bachelor of Hotel Management colleges in India are the Army Institute of Hotel Management and Catering Technology, Department of Hotel Management Christ University, AIMS Institutes, International Institute of Hotel Management, and others. Candidates often spend between INR 3 lakhs and INR 15 lakhs for the course. Top recruiters of BHM are Oberoi Group of Hotels, Taj Group of Hotels, Casino Group of Hotels, Accor Hotels and Holiday Inn Group of Hotels. The average salary after a Bachelor of Hotel Management is between INR 3 LPA and INR 6 LPA depending on the job role and the size of the hotel.

Why Study Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM)?

A bachelor's degree in hotel management will teach students how to handle numerous aspects of a hotel or hospitality service, such as sales and marketing, food and beverage, front desk, accounting, food production, housekeeping, and various kitchen skills. Many government and private colleges in India offer Bachelor of Hotel Management programmes, which have proven to be a profitable and interesting subject for many students. Hotel management is a popular career path for good reason. Let us look at some of the benefits of this course and field:

  • The Hotel Industry is Booming: Hotels are increasing at a rate of roughly 5% per year worldwide. This means that even if you wish to start small, there are lots of prospects in the hotel sector. With this expansion, hotels will require additional employees to meet demand.
  • Opportunities for International Placement: Hotel management employment can be found all around the world, from New York City to Delhi! Working in this sector allows you to explore the world!
  • High Annual Salary: A hotel manager's average pay is quite substantial. Graduates’ compensation will be determined by where they work, the kind of your employment, and how long they have been in the industry (as well as many other factors). Hotel managers can climb through the ranks of their firm, from the front desk clerk to the assistant manager to the general manager.
  • Job Satisfaction: If money is not the sole reason graduates want to work in hotel management, it is probably because they enjoy being around people, assisting them with their requirements, and ensuring that every guest has an exceptional experience.
  • Multiple Job Opportunities: There are numerous career opportunities available in the hotel management industry. Among these include running a hotel, a restaurant, and a spa/resort. It is also feasible to work in fields such as sales or marketing.
  • Financial Security: Hotel management is sometimes seen as one of the more stable industries in terms of pay and job security. Many people pursue this profession because they wish to provide financial security for their families or themselves.
  • Opportunities for Travel: If you enjoy travelling, working as a team member at a hotel may be exactly up your alley! There are hotels all over the world, so there are lots of options for folks who wish to travel without spending a fortune or taking time off from work.
  • Learn Essential Skills: A Bachelor of Hotel Management degree will teach aspirants the abilities they will need to succeed in the sector, such as how to manage people, design and manage budgets, and stay up to date on industry standards.
  • Opportunity to Work in a Dynamic Field: Many people believe that working in hospitality entails only greeting customers and serving meals. Indeed, hotel management is a dynamic and ever-changing profession that encompasses a wide range of disciplines such as hotel management, real estate, tourism, events, restaurant experience, and health and wellness.

Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM) Admission Process

Admission to the BHM program is on the basis of the UGAT - Under Graduate Aptitude Test conducted by AIMA, All India Management Association. Some of the private universities like Christ University & GD Goenka School of Hospitality conduct their own entrance tests. Few colleges also offer merit-based admissions on the basis of class 12th results like Manipal University.

BHM admissions are chiefly done on a merit basis. But some of the institutes conduct their own entrance tests, often called aptitude tests. IIM eCHAT, UPSEE, etc are some of the most common entrance tests that are conducted for the Bachelor of Hotel Management admission. Entrance-based and merit-based admission processes are described below:

BHM Entrance Exam-Based Admission

In the Entrance exam-based admission process, candidates need to appear in the institute-level entrance exam. Instead of conducting their own admission tests, some colleges accept the national-level entrance exam score based on which the colleges call the candidates for counselling sessions. A personal interview test and GD sessions are often conducted as the next rounds of admission. Based on these performances, a candidate is allowed admission.

BHM Merit-Based Admission

In this process of admissions, colleges publish notifications making an application invitation. The willing candidates apply online though few colleges still receive offline applications. The filled-in application form comprises the details of the candidate’s academic and personal information. Reviewing the applications, scores of previous qualifying examinations, courses applied and the availability of seats against that course colleges prepare a merit list. Candidates then report to the college on a scheduled date for document verification. On the successful completion of document verification, final admission is confirmed.

Lateral Entry Admission

BHM Lateral entry is possible when students have completed Diploma in Hotel Management and they get direct admission into the second year of the BHM course. To be eligible for lateral entry, one must have a diploma in the course from a recognised institute. The eligibility criteria may vary from one university to another. 

Documents Required for BHM Admission

The BHM admission documents that students must bring on admission day are:

  • Mark sheets of 10th and 12th
  • Certificates of 10th and 12th
  • Passport size photographs
  • Transfer certificate 
  • Character certificate
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Disability certificate (if applicable)

To be admitted to the BHM or Bachelor of Hotel Management programme, aspirants must meet the following eligibility requirements:

FAQs about BHM

What is the course Bachelor of Hotel Management all about?

BHM, or Bachelor of Hotel Management, is an undergraduate programme that focuses on the fundamentals of hotel management and catering technology. It is a three-year undergraduate programme, which is pursued by students after class 12 and is divided into 6 semesters.

What is Hotel Management? Is it the same as Hospitality Management?

Both fields are highly similar and belong to the same industry. Hotel management is overseeing the day-to-day operations and functioning of a hotel. In contrast, hospitality management is a broader word that refers to any industry that deals with people management. Casinos, nightclubs, conferences, events, sales, company growth, and even hotels are all possibilities.

What are the eligibility criteria for pursuing a Bachelor of Hotel Management?

Students interested in pursuing a Bachelor of Hotel Management degree must have finished their Class 12 from a recognised board with at least a 50% aggregate score. Colleges often favour individuals who have passed an entrance exam to demonstrate their eligibility for this programme. Personal interviews and group discussions are also taken at some universities.

Is a Bachelor of Hotel Management a good course to be pursued?

Yes, the Bachelor of Hotel Management programme is indeed a good course to be pursued by aspirants in today's day and age. The programme provides good opportunities for students who want to work in top hotel management firms and organisations at the management level. Candidates who want to work in the tourist industry in any capacity, such as travel consultant, travel manager, travel marketer, and so on.

What can I pursue after the Bachelor of Hotel Management course?

After completing the Bachelor of Hotel Management programme, students can either seek employment in the hospitality and travel industries or can opt for higher studies. Master of Hotel Management (MHM), MSc in Hotel Management, Masters in Travel and Tourism Management, MBA in Tourism and Hotel Management, Post Graduate Diploma in Hotel Management, Post Graduate Diploma in Travel and Tourism, Post Graduate Diploma in Hospitality Management, Post Graduate Diploma in Accommodation Operation and Management, and other courses are available to candidates after completing the BHM course.

What is the scope of the Bachelor of Hotel Management course?

Due to its wide breadth in India and other nations, anyone can enrol in this course. The hotel chain is expanding rapidly as a result of globalisation. After completing this degree, the student will have numerous job prospects with prominent hotel chains such as the Taj group, ITC, Oberoi hotels, and the Hilton group, among others. The merchant navy and cruise ships both have a high demand for skilled professionals.

What are the prominent job prospects that aspirants can pursue after the Bachelor of Hotel Management programme?

After completing a course in Bachelor of Hotel Management, aspirants can get the job with specific job roles or designations given such as Kitchen Stewarding Supervisor, Assistant Manager, Restaurant Manager, Guest Relation Executive (GRE), Assistant Manager Administration, Assistant Guest Delight Manager, Front Officer Manager, Restaurant Captain, and more.

What kind of degree is a Bachelor of Hotel Management?

A Bachelor of Hotel Management degree blends business administration ideas with hotel disciplines such as operations and food production management. Degree candidates can go into hospitality management, tourism management, restaurant management, and event coordination.

What is the difference between a Bachelor of Hotel Management and BSc in Hotel Management?

A BSc in Hotel Management is an undergraduate degree programme that provides an overview of hotel management without focusing on specific subjects. It is more technical in nature and has an impact on the fundamentals of hotel management. A BHM, on the other hand, is primarily focused on management issues and operations in hotel management.

Who is the Bachelor of Hotel Management course designed for?

Candidates who want to work in the hotel and hospitality industry as a manager, chef, caterer, and so on. Because the training also teaches culinary skills, it is ideal for restaurant owners. Because this is a vocational degree, it is slightly more expensive than the other two, but it also has a wider scope and greater pay possibilities.

Is there any entrance exam for the Bachelor of Hotel Management course?

There is a pool of college-level and national-level entrance exams for admissions in Bachelor of Hotel Management courses that aspirants have to give like UPSEE, ECHAT, NCHMCT JEE, AIMA UGAT, AIHMCT, GNIHM and alike.

What other degrees are related/similar to the Bachelor of Hotel Management course?

Other degrees that are sort of related to the Bachelor of Hotel Management course or belong to the same industry are BSc in Hotel Management, BA in Hotel Management, BBA in Hotel Management, BBA in Hospitality Management, BBA in Aviation Management, BBA in Travel and Tourism, BA in Travel and Tourism, BSc in Hospitality Management, and many more. 

Is Maths subject compulsory for the Bachelor of Hotel Management course?

No, Maths as a subject in Class 12 is not a compulsory eligibility criterion for pursuing a Bachelor of Hotel Management course in India. Having said that, aspirants must have English as a compulsory subject in Class 12 with passing marks as it is a mandatory subject for this course.

Are Hotel jobs good for career development?

Hotel entry-level and associate employment can provide you with valuable skills and experience that you can use to advance in the hotel's organisational structure. Many hotel teams have management jobs available, which can give you leadership chances and higher income. Hotel housekeeping supervisors, for example, frequently begin in entry-level housekeeping positions and move to higher responsibilities by demonstrating initiative and expanding their skills.

Does the hotel industry provide job security to graduates?

Since Bachelor of Hotel Management graduates provides an important service to people all over the world, skilled hospitality professionals are in high demand. Many cities have numerous hotels, ensuring that hotel personnel have career opportunities that meet their requirements. Some hotel personnel develop skills for many similar roles, such as guest services coordinator and receptionist, to boost their job security. As a result, they may work in a number of jobs and fill in for other team members as needed.

Who is a Hotel Manager?

A Hotel Manager is the individual in charge of running a hotel and its workers on a daily basis. Hotel managers are in charge of managing personnel as well as planning, marketing, coordinating, and administering hotel services such as cuisine and lodging.

What is the average salary of a Hotel Manager in India?

According to recent trends, the average starting salary of a Hotel Manager in India is anywhere between INR 4 lakhs to INR 7 lakhs per annum. The salary can obviously vary and touch further skies depending on the place/hotel you work for, work experience, skills, education qualifications, etc.

Does Bachelor of Hotel Management graduates gain diverse interpersonal interactions?

Yes, depending on the location of the hotel and their job duties, working in the hotel business can provide graduates with the opportunity to meet individuals from all over the world. Positions in guest services or administration might allow them to deal with a diverse range of guests, ensuring that they have a pleasant stay. They can improve their language abilities and learn about diverse cultures and countries by interacting with guests and coworkers from various backgrounds.

What is the biggest perk of working in a hotel?

Working at a hotel may entitle you to discounted services at that location or others in the hotel family, which is one of the most significant benefits of working in a hotel. When you travel, you and your family members may be eligible for a discount on rooms or suites. Hotel managers may also provide staff subsidised access to fitness centres, pools, and other amenities, as well as free meals at the hotel restaurant during specific hours.

What is the biggest drawback of working in a hotel?

Many hotels are open on weekends and holidays to serve guests that travel during these times. Working in a hotel allows you to continue working when other businesses are closed, which is a major disadvantage for most aspirants. Holidays can be an excellent opportunity to work extra hours and raise your compensation, but if you're used to regular business hours, it may be difficult to adjust to a new work schedule.

The Bachelor of Hotel management average course fee ranges anywhere between INR 3 lakh - INR 15 lakhs in India. However, students can pursue hotel management certificates and diploma courses for less than INR 3 lakhs.

What are the popular specializations related to Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM) ?

Some of the popular specializations for B.H.M. (Bachelor of Hotel Management) are : Forestry Management , Catering Technology , Hospitality Science , Hospitality Management , Political Science.

What is the duration of Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM) course?

The Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM) programme is a three-year undergraduate degree programme in hotel management.
