Bachelor of Fine Arts Course Overview

Bachelor of Fine Arts or BFA is a three-year full-time undergraduate degree focused on the study of performing or visual arts. Some institutions may also refer to it as a Bachelor of Visual Arts course. It covers a range of topics from literature, and animation to sculpture, painting and much more. To be eligible for this course, students must have completed 10+2 with at least 50% to 60% aggregate marks, while those with an Arts/ Humanities background are preferred at the time of admission. The BFA course syllabus and subjects cover topics such as Advertising Art, Basics of Computer Fundamentals, Communication Design, Communication Illustration, Computer Graphics, Environmental Studies, Health Education, History of Indian Art, and more.

Some top entrance examinations for this course are CUET UG, URATPG, PUBDET, NSD Entrance Exam, and more. Further, admission to this course is granted based on merit and also on the candidates’ performance in the entrance exams. A few popular colleges that provide BFA courses are Chandigarh University, Mangalayatan University, Kurukshetra University, Lovely Professional University (LPU), etc. with an average fee of INR 85,000 to 6,00,000. After completing the course, candidates can work as Painters, Animators, Fine Artists, Actors, Musicians, etc. with an average starting salary of INR 2-58. LPA. Continue reading to get detailed information about the subjects/syllabus, specialisations offered by popular colleges, eligibility criteria, admission process, entrance exams, etc. about the BFA course.

What is an Online Bachelor of Fine Arts Degree?

For an online Bachelor of Fine Arts course aspirants, studying painting, drawing, and other topics are more convenient. Three well-known Indian universities — BRAOU, TNOU, and YCMOU — offer this course in an online mode of learning. The completion of 10+2 in any subject is a prerequisite for enrollment in this programme. Furthermore, there is no obligation to pass an entrance exam.

A wide range of subjects, including cinematography, photography, mixed media, painting, digital illustration, and much more, are covered in the six to eight-semester online Bachelor of Fine Arts course. To comprehend and have some exposure to the subject matter, students are given video content and the opportunity to participate in separate practice sessions on weekends. Furthermore, the total fee for this course varies from INR 20,000 to 50,000, depending on the institute. With this online BFA course, students can pursue employment in publishing houses, museums, art galleries, and more.

How to Apply for a BFA Course?

There are two ways to get into a BFA course: through merit or through an entrance exam. The admissions process for BFA courses varies depending on the admissions process. Applicants can, however, apply online to their top BFA  colleges.
 

For Entrance Based BFA  Admission 

Candidates can apply for BFA courses by following the steps outlined below:

Step 1: Check into the BFA admission form requirements and deadlines listed by various colleges and universities if you want an admission to a BFA programme

Step 2: Register for the entrance exams required for BFA admission 2023 at the colleges and universities you want to attend

Step 3: Once confirmed, save the application process for future use. Also, for admission and other important notifications, keep an eye on the exam or the university's official website

Step 4: After appearing and passing the BFA entrance examination, candidates must fill out an application form for their desired university with personal information such as academic details, entrance examination results, and so on

Step 5: Candidates who successfully meet the university's eligibility requirements are shortlisted for the counselling round (if any) to finalise the admissions process

Step 6: After clearing the counselling session, candidates must pay the university's entrance fee in order to be confirm their admission to the BFA course
 

For Merit Based BFA  Admission

Candidates can apply for BFA courses by following the steps outlined below:

Step 1: Fill out the online BFA application form

Step 2: Pay the registration fee and any additional fees if admission is granted based on merit

Step 3: The shortlisted applicants will receive invitations to the subsequent phases of the hiring process, such as individual interviews or counselling

Step 4: Candidates must submit all the required documents and fee in order to confirm their admission to BFA course

Course Curriculum for Bachelor of Fine Arts

As a three-year programme, the Bachelor of Fine Arts is usually divided into 6 semesters, complete with course work, i.e. both core and elective subjects, as well as projects and research work that may be inclusive. While the degree-conferring institution holds the authority to determine the curriculum for the course, there are certain guidelines that the university will have to abide by, as has been outlined below:

  • 6 semesters of core and elective subjects pertaining to their respective specialisations.
  • Students will be participating in different teaching methodologies leading to the completion of the course.
  • Students may be asked to complete several projects or one final dissertation or research project that will impact the overall score a student earns.

Universities and institutions will also hold the authority to determine the type of teaching methodology they wish to employ, to complete the BFA course. Most institutions may employ a combination of the common methodologies, as highlighted here:

  • Conventional Classroom-based Teaching
  • One-on-One sessions for dissertation
  • Seminars and Workshops

A common practice among higher education institutions is to include a research project as an integral part of the curriculum. Therefore, it is important for students to have a good research topic on hand that they can discuss with their supervisors and start working on when required.

These subjects and topics may vary with universities offering the degree in Bachelor of Fine Arts.

What is a Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA)?

The Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) is a three-year undergraduate programme offered as an arts and design programme across the country. The course has been designed to instil knowledge, skills and qualifications leading to various jobs in the creative industries like films, music, theatre, animation and much more, allowing students to build a lucrative career in their desired creative field.

Is BFA a good career option?

Yes, pursuing a Bachelor of Fine Arts can be a good career choice for those interested in building a career in the creative industry. Much like every industry, with good skills and experience, a BFA graduate will be able to pursue a lucrative career. Students can also pursue further studies or apply for a management course after graduation.

What is the eligibility criteria for obtaining admission in BFA course in any college or university in India?

The eligibility criteria for BFA courses in India require applicants to have cleared the class 12 board exams in any stream.
