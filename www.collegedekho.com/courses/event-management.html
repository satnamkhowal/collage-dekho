Event Management Course Overview

Event Management course focuses on planning and hosting small- or large-scale events. It provides education and training in planning, organising, and executing events of various types, including parties, functions, weddings, seminars, festivals, games, political rallies, and more. This field offers undergraduate and postgraduate courses that can vary from one to three years, depending on the course type such as diploma, certificate, and degree. The Event Management syllabus and subjects include Event Marketing, Research Methodology, Organisational Behavior, Event Planning and Control, etc.

Event management is considered one of the most exciting and challenging careers that demand creative vision, a keen eye for detail, and utmost dedication. Further, the event management course admissions process is granted based on merit as well as entrance exam scores. Some top colleges in India that offer this course include Amity University, PIMR Indore, NIMS University, MIT World Peace University, AAFT Noida, Whistling Woods International, etc. The average fee for this course in India ranges between INR 40,000 to 5,00,000. Graduates can find various event management course jobs like Event Organisers, Event Managers, Wedding Planners, Venue Managers, Site Coordinators, Social Media Managers, etc. with an average event management course salary of between INR 2.5-3.5 LPA. Find more details about the event management course admission process, syllabus, top colleges, fees, career opportunities, etc. on this page.

Why Choose an Event Management Course?

Over the past few years, event management has grown at an accelerating pace, positioning it as one of the most lucrative sectors and a significant contributor to India's GDP. A professional who chooses the Event Management course expands their knowledge of the world and learns something new with every event. If you are a bright, outgoing person searching for something exciting and demanding that will stimulate your imagination and increase your practical knowledge, this course is a wonderful fit for you. The following are some convincing reasons for taking an event management course.

Acquire Transferable Skills

You can learn a range of skill sets to oversee the planning, execution, and collection of data regarding various event kinds by enrolling in an event management course. In addition to these perks, it gives you transferable skills that you can use in your daily life and that are necessary for many other career options, like budgeting, hustling culture, and many more.

Immense Work Satisfaction

To plan, create, and carry out an event, professionals have to dedicate a significant amount of time and effort. Many event planners claim that organising a big, successful event brings them a great lot of satisfaction in their work.

Opportunities to Explore New Places

Participating in occasions like award presentations, destination weddings, and business gatherings will allow you the opportunity to go to breathtaking and unique places. Even while this field of employment requires the utmost commitment, it also offers fulfilling experiences.

Continuous Growth

The event management industry has experienced remarkable growth in the last few years. People are using event organisers and planners to make any occasion special, from big public events to small-scale private parties and get-togethers. An event management course can lead to an array of employment progression opportunities.

Skill-Based Evaluation

With rare exceptions, completing difficult entrance exams is not a prerequisite for admission to an institution offering event management courses. When evaluating an application, factors like their capacity for multitasking, creativity, organisation, and patience are all considered. If you want to be sure that your potential is not constrained by your admission exam results, this is the right path for you.

Event Management Course Eligibility Criteria

The eligibility criteria for this course depend on the level and course type. Find below the eligibility criteria to get admission into different types of event management courses.

UG Course Eligibility Criteria

The following are the undergraduate event management course eligibility criteria:

  • Students must have completed 10+2 education with at least 50% aggregate marks in any field of study.
  • Admissions are done through the merit list prepared based on class 12 board exam results. However, some universities may take a basic aptitude test as part of the selection process.

PG Course Eligibility Criteria

The following are the postgraduate event management course eligibility criteria:

  • Candidates must have completed their graduation with at least 60% aggregate marks in a related discipline.
  • Applicants must sit for entrance examinations such as CAT, MAH-CET, CMAT, SNAP, MAT, etc. or those specified by their preferred institution.

Eligibility Criteria for Certificate, Diploma, and PG Diploma

The following are the eligibility criteria to be considered for admission to even management courses certification, diploma, or PG diploma:

  • To enrol in an event management certificate programme, there are no requirements that must be met. On the other hand, certain universities accept applicants who have passed their board exams for class 12. The event management certificate courses can be done separately or integrated with another degree programme.
  • Applicants must have obtained at least a 55% aggregate mark in their 10+2 examination from an accredited educational board.
  • Candidates must graduate from an accredited university with at least 50% aggregate marks in any field, or an equivalent CGPA, to be eligible to enrol in a PG diploma course in event management.

Skills Required for Event Management Course

The following are some skills required for an event management course:

  • Interpersonal/Social Skills: A successful event manager must have excellent interpersonal skills since they require dealing with various groups of people like stakeholders, clients, and others.
  • Leadership Skills: Good leadership qualities and instincts are prerequisites for any type of management, especially event management. An event manager needs to be able to control and guide the people working under them, to be successful.
  • Communication Skills: Effective communication is essential for event managers since they are responsible for the flow of information among all the internal and external aspects of the organisation. 
  • Financial Aptitude and Economic Knowledge: An event manager also needs to have in-depth knowledge and understanding of the financial factors affecting their business. This will help them make prudent decisions that directly affect the revenue of the organisation.
  • Amicable & Professional Personality: An event manager has to be smart and their behaviour and mannerisms need to be professional and friendly at the same time. This will help them win the confidence of clients and other stakeholders, which is a big advantage in this business.

Event Management Entrance Exams

While some top event management colleges in India offer direct admission, others require students to take entrance exams. However, for postgraduate courses or advanced studies like MBA in Event Management, colleges and universities conduct their admission based on the scores of exams like CAT, MAT, NMAT by GMAC, etc. Given below are some event management entrance exams that prospective students may need to sit for based on their preferred college’s requirements.

CAT: The Common Admission Test or CAT is an entrance examination conducted online to grant admission into IIMs and other prestigious business schools in the country. It is conducted annually by a combined body of the IIMs.

MAT: The Management Aptitude Test (MAT) is a national-level entrance examination conducted four times a year by the All India Management Association (AIMA) for admission to MBA and Postgraduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) in colleges and universities.

NMAT by GMAC: NMAT by GMAC is a national-level entrance examination conducted for admission into MBA courses at NMIMS and other top business schools in the country by the Graduate Management Aptitude Council.

CMAT: The CMAT exam is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) every year for admission to MBA/ PGDM courses in more than 1000 B-schools in India. Approximately 70,000 candidates appear for this exam every year.

NCHMCT JEE: Conducted by NTA at a national level, the National Council for Hotel Management & Catering Technology Joint Entrance Exam or NCHMCT JEE offers admission to courses related to the Hospitality and Hotel management industry.

Event Management Admission Process: Direct Admissions

As mentioned above, some colleges offer admission to event management courses without requiring students to sit for any entrance exam. This admission is granted based on merit i.e., marks obtained by a student in their 12th board examination. The admission process for direct admission varies from one college to another as students will be required to follow the steps formulated by their preferred institution.

To apply for direct admission to event management courses, candidates should first shortlist a list of colleges that offer admission with entrance examinations. FAQs about Event Management

What are the best event management courses for master’s degree students?

The best event management courses for master’s degree students are an MBA in Event Management & Public Relations, an MBA in Event Management, and an MA in PR & Event Management. These courses last for two years and can be pursued after the completion of a bachelor’s degree in a related field from a recognised university.

What are the best event management courses for bachelor’s degree students?

The best event management courses for bachelor’s degree students are BBA in Event Management, BBA in Event Management & PR, and BA in Event Management. These courses last for three years and can be pursued after the completion of a 10+2, certification or diploma programme in a related field.

Can I pursue event management courses after BCom?

Yes, candidates can pursue event management courses after BCom. Following a bachelor of commerce degree, candidates can pursue numerous certificate and master's degrees. Pursuing a programme with a background in commerce will be beneficial and simple.

Is Maths required for event management courses?

No, Maths is not required for event management courses, however, a fundamental grasp of the subject is necessary. Comprehending the fundamentals of mathematics promotes a grasp of the financial facets of organising events, such as accounting and budgeting. The key to event management is balancing inventiveness and people skills.

Is event management a permanent job?

Yes, event management is a permanent job as this field is evergreen and the events keep happening. Nonetheless, employment as an event manager is a profession that provides both temporary and long-term opportunities. Employers choose the terms of employment for their employees; they hire people to meet their needs.

Are there any age criteria for admission to event management courses?

No, there are no age criteria for admission to event management courses. Individuals of any age group can pursue the programme. Applicants in the programme might be of any age, depending on the type of their preferred institution. While there is no age restriction for diploma or master's programmes, candidates may encounter certain age limits when enrolling in bachelor's courses in colleges.

What stream is required for event management courses?

There is no specific stream required for event management courses. These programmes are available to candidates in any stream, including commerce, science, and the arts. It is critical to pursue the course with passion and dedication if you want to be good at organising both aesthetically pleasing and useful events.

Are event management courses considered a good career option in India?

Yes, event management courses are considered a good career option in India as they are among management aspirants' most popular courses. Completing this course is required for non-formal events like proposal ceremonies, birthday celebrations, baby showers, and wedding functions. Even though the work option may seem appealing, long-term success is limited to those who have completed event management programmes and acquired the essential skills required for effective event operation.

Is there scope after event management courses?

Yes, extensive scope after event management courses is available for prospective candidates in numerous fields with lucrative career opportunities. Considering that people with a wide range of educational backgrounds can operate in this profession, there are many employment options in India. Nonetheless, businesses favour qualifications in allied fields including management, public relations, tourism, and hospitality.

What is the course curriculum of Event Management?

The course curriculum of Event Management includes theoretical and practical learning. While the master's programme is divided into four semesters, the bachelor's programme is divided into six. Fieldwork, dissertations, and summer training are all components of the teaching-learning process.

What does the syllabus of Event Management contain?

The syllabus of Event Management contains topics like event planning, marketing and promotion, team management, event marketing, leadership, event safety and security, basic event accounting, leisure and tourism events, event logistics, fundamentals of professional photography and weddings and special events. 

What are the skills required for Event Management students?

The skills required for Event Management students that help them emerge as excellent professionals are organisational skills, problem-solving skills, communication skills, attention to detail, leadership qualities, planning skills, time management skills and the ability to function under pressure. 

Can I pursue event management courses after 12th grade?

Yes, students can pursue event management courses after 12th grade such as certification or diploma programmes to gain the skills required in this field. After completing the 12th grade, both public and private universities in India offer a variety of courses in disciplines such as Advertising and Public Relations in integration with the event management course.

What is the duration of event management courses?

The duration of event management courses depends on the type and the level of the programme a student has opted for. The following enlists the same:

What entrance exams are conducted for admission to event management courses?

These exams are typically administered to evaluate a candidate's potential in areas like creativity, problem-solving, communication, and management.
