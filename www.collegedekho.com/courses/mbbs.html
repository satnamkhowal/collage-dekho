MBBS course is a 5.5-year undergraduate medical course that students can pursue after passing the NEET entrance exam. This course is the stepping stone for aspiring doctors to enter the field of medicine and learn practical skills and knowledge to become a doctors. MBBS full form stands for Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery. The MBBS course in India is quite popular and necessary to work as a medical practitioner in hospitals and healthcare institutions.

MBBS Course Overview

MBBS course or Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery is a five-and-a-half-year undergraduate program for aspiring doctors. Students can pursue an MBBS course after 12th after securing a minimum of 55% marks in the Class 12 medical stream. Students are also requested to take NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) entrance exam to be eligible for admission to the best MBBS colleges. The top MBBS colleges in Indiainclude AIIMS Delhi, PGIMER Chandigarh, CMC Vellore, NIMHANS Bangalore, JIPGMER Bengaluru, and many more.

MBBS course subjects, syllabus, and curriculum contain topics that prepare students to diagnose and treat patients, undertake medical research, and level up with postgraduate studies. After 5 years of studies, students must complete their 6 months of Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship (CRMI). Only then they will be awarded the MBBS degree. The MBBS degree will make the students MBBS doctors or medical practitioners.

MBBS course fees begin from INR 30,000 and go up to INR 1.5 to 2 Crores. MBBS degree holders can work in hospitals- private or government, go as a doctor in the Indian Defence team, or open their own clinic. They can also pursue higher studies and choose specialisations such as MS or MD degrees, MPH, DNB, international education, CMS, etc.

An MBBS graduate during the first few years of their practice has the prospect of earning anywhere between INR 55,000 to INR 20 LPA. With further education and experience, the MBBS course salary of doctors increases manifolds.

Why Choose MBBS Course?

Medical aspirants often wonder about choosing an MBBS course as a viable career option, based on a variety of reasons from personal calling to reaching financial stability. While everyone's understanding of judgment tor choosing an MBBS course may be different, here is a practical MBBS review for a brighter future:

  • Noble Professsion: A "doctor" in general is considered to be one of the noblest professions in the world. Therefore, studying medicine allows a candidate to serve society as well as gain a lot of respect in return. There is also a sense of satisfaction that is derived from being able to serve society in a meaningful way.
  • Financial Gains: Financial stability is another important reason for many people to choose medicine as it is one of those professions that are hard hit in cases of any worldwide financial crisis. Although the gains might not always compare to other professions, the inherent stability makes up for it. 
  • Higher Chances Of Expanding Knowledge: The field of medicine is extremely diverse and vast and hence one can never cease to learn new things. MBBS is just the gateway or the initial stage of gaining knowledge for a bright future in medicine.
  • Several Specialisations: There are immense options to choose from and specialise in after the completion of the MBBS degree. One can move forward with medicine or choose other specialisations like surgery, dermatology, neurology, nephrology, psychiatry, etc. 
  • Vast Career Scope: Numerous job options are available for MBBS graduates across the globe, including positions in private practice, hospitals, clinics, and research organisations. 
  • Further Education Options: Postgraduate programs like a Master of Public Health are also another option to choose from for students who complete their MBBS degree. 
  • Global Approach: With perseverance and dedication to the job, one can also reach the global stage since good doctors are required everywhere.
  • Humanitarian Work: Additionally, doctors have been working along with eminent humanitarian societies such as Red Cross, and Amnesty International to provide access to medical and healthcare facilities to everyone across the globe. 

The field of medicine and MBBS is also for those people who like to be challenged on a daily basis and possess strong decision-making skills. One needs to think very clearly and employ knowledge to save people’s lives. Compassion and having a strong belief system to help bring positive changes in society are also a part of the world of medicine.

MBBS Course Eligibility Criteria

In order to study the MBBS course, students must appear for the NEET or National Eligibility cum Entrance Test conducted by the NTA (National Testing Agency). Based on the rankings received in the entrance examination, candidates will be able to gain admission to various government and private MBBS colleges in India. Here are the eligibility criteria for obtaining MBBS admission:

  • The MBBS course eligibility age is at least 17 years to be eligible for admission to an MBBS course.
  • While the minimum marks criteria for admission may vary, at least 50% (unreserved), 45% (PwD), and 40% (OBC/ ST/ BC) of marks from candidates will be considered to be eligible for MBBS admission.
  • Passing the entrance examination i.e. NEET with the best possible scores increases the chances of getting placed in the top MBBS colleges in the country. 
  • NEET applicants should also have Physics, Chemistry and Biology as their 10+2 exam subjects.

MBBS Course Entrance Exam - NEET

There is only one entrance exam for MBBS which is the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test or NEET conducted by NTA. Here are the important details for the MBBS entrance exam: 

  • This entrance exam is held every year to allow students admission to 542 medical colleges, 313 dental, 47 BVSc and AH colleges, and 914 AYUSH colleges.
  • NEET is conducted in around 13 languages- Assamese, Bengali, English, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu.
  • The exam is conducted in offline mode and there are a total of 158002 seats available. 
  • A candidate should also be at least 17 years of age when registering for the NEET exam. Recently the upper age limit for appearing in the exam has been removed. 
  • Students must also have PCB or PCMB in their class 12 exam in order to appear for NEET.

  1. Know The Exam Pattern: NEET is an MCQ-based exam for all subjects. Knowing the marking scheme and the nature of questions will help in better practice and revision.

How to Get Admission for MBBS Course?

Students wishing to pursue a course in MBBS after completing their 10+2, can do so in various ways. The Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery courses offer admission to eligible candidates through two ways, entrance exams and merit-based admissions. 

  • Entrance exams: Candidates can get admission to MBBS Courses by appearing for NEET conducted by NTA for government and public institutions across India. Upon qualifying the same, aspiring candidates will be eligible to earn a Bachelor of Medicine and a Bachelor of Surgery degree. The cutoff will determine the student’s chances for admission. The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) conducts NEET to fill up 15% of All India Quota (AIQ) seats.
     
  • Merit-based: Candidates can also be granted admission to MBBS at colleges and universities, which offer admission to MBBS Courses based on the marks secured in 10+2 or equivalent examination. Candidates are then selected based on the prescribed cut-off required for the admission process. The counselling process is conducted for the remaining 85% of State Quota (SQ) seats after NEET by State Medical Admission Authorities.

MBBS Course Admission Process 2024

The MBBS admission 2024 in India has a generic process for all government and private institutions explained as follows:

  1. Candidates must apply for the NEET exam and take it.
  2. They must go through exam-specific counselling and the final seat allotment process.
  3. Once the seat allotment is over, the seats can be confirmed by paying the admission fees.

MBBS Course Subjects, Syllabus & Curriculum

The MBBS syllabus & subjects are extremely vast and cover all the core concepts of medicine and surgery. The course has been designed by keeping in mind that students must be equipped with all medical-related topics and develop skills that are relevant to the industry. The MBBS course syllabus and subjects in India are divided into three stages pre-clinical, para-clinical, and clinical stages, here we will discuss in detail the various MBBS subjects that are taught in the various stages of the MBBS syllabus:

Pre-clinical- The first two semesters of an MBBS course are dedicated to pre-clinical studies and include subjects such as anatomy, biochemistry, biophysics, physiology, etc. It is an introductory phase where students get a headstart with intensive learning. They cover all majority concepts in their basic and fundamental form. It is compulsory to qualify in the first phase of the pre-clinical phase of the MBBS degree in order to move on to the next stage.

Para- Clinical- The second phase of the MBBS degree program which runs for three semesters. Some of the subjects that are taught at this level are forensic medicine, pathology, community medicine, pharmacology, microbiology, OPDs, clinical postings, etc.

Clinical- This is the final academic stage in the whole MBBS course and it comprises the clinical phase. It stretches from semester 5 to 9 where the course focus remains on the study of specialisations in medicine and surgery. Subjects taught in the clinical phase are psychiatry, dermatology, obstetrics, gynaecology, and paediatrics. It also includes subjects such as orthopaedics, ophthalmology, ENT, anaesthesiology, surgery, and its allied subjects.

Study MBBS Course Abroad

While deciding to study MBBS abroad, there are a few things that need to be kept in mind

Eligibility and requirements

Although each country has its own criteria and list of rules, here are some guidelines to study MBBS course abroad for Indian students:

Age

The student has to be at least 17 years of age on December 31 of the particular year of application for admission to study MBBS abroad.

Academic requirements

All candidates willing to pursue an MBBS degree abroad have to meet these requirements:

  • Candidate must complete his or her 10+2 from a recognised board. An aggregate percentage of 50% must be achieved in Physics, Chemistry and Biology along with English as a compulsory subject in their 10+2 exam.
  • Candidates are also required to qualify for the NEET exam even if they are planning to study MBBS abroad. The NEET score for each candidate will be valid for a period of three years.
  • Students must also produce an Eligibility Certificate from the Medical Council of India during the time of admission to any foreign medical university.
  • Based on the English requirements of the university or college, students could also be required to appear for English proficiency tests like IELTS, TOEFL, PTE or OET. 
  • Students cannot suffer from any contagious diseases at the time of admission and need to submit a medical certificate for the same.

Skills Required for MBBS Course

The path to becoming a doctor is one of the most daunting and challenging ones without a certain number of skills, students cannot successfully complete their MBBS course and become good doctors. Hence, here is the list of required skillsets for MBBS students:

Academic competence

This skill perhaps goes without saying. Any student without academic competence will not be able to survive in the field of medicine and medical science. Any practising doctor requires to have a strong and sound knowledge base which will be helpful for providing the best care to patients. For this same reason, medical students have to spend a huge chunk of their time studying where they learn about aetiology, pathophysiology, diagnosis and treatment for various diseases in medical colleges. 

Communication skills

Possessing good communication skills is considered to be a huge asset for medical students and doctors as it helps them to identify the problems of patients and provide them with proper remedies. Moreover, medical professionals rarely work in isolation. Hence when working in teams, it is absolutely essential to possess good communication skills. 

Leadership skills

In the field of medicine and medical science, doctors will be required to make important decisions within a short span of time and the outcome will affect someone’s health. Again, effective leadership skills are not limited to patient-related decisions, doctors are also often involved in administrative tasks where they make decisions for positive outcomes. Thus possessing effective leadership skills where one can take the role of a leader and guide a team of doctors and other professionals is crucial. 

Teamwork

Doctors need to work in teams, and when working in a group, it is important to understand that each and every team member has their own unique personality as well as perspectives. But with differences, frictions can arise. Hence, they need to be managed effectively. 

Empathy

Doctors need to be empathetic towards their patients. In their years of clinical practice, instances may occur when they might only see the disease and not the person. Therefore, empathising with patients and their family members is a crucial part of the healthcare industry.

FAQs about MBBS

Can I become a cardiologist without MBBS?

No. Aspirants have to mandatorily complete their MBBS studies to pursue a career as a cardiologist.

Can an MBBS doctor do surgery?

No, you cannot. By becoming an MBBS graduate, you can practice as a physician. But, to conduct surgeries, you need an MD degree.

Is MBBS from China recognised in India?

Yes, the MCI recognises some of the MBBS courses from certain Medical colleges in China.

What are some of the common MS specialisations after completing an MBBS course?

Some of the most common specialisations after completing an MBBS course are: 

ENT, General surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, ophthalmology, orthopaedics, otorhinolaryngology, traumatology and surgery.

 

Is MBBS worth studying?

MBBS is a course for anyone who is willing to dedicate the majority of their lives to learning new things on a daily basis and changing the course of life for other people. There are lots of responsibilities when one chooses to become a doctor, and the decision to study medicine should be well thought out. 

 

What can I do after completing the MBBS course?

Students can choose to go for postgraduate studies like MD or MS courses, begin private practice or opt for other courses like DNB, MPH, CMS, MHA, courses.

 

Do I have to qualify NEET for studying MBBS abroad?

Yes, Indian students have to qualify for NEET even if they choose to study MBBS abroad.

Where can I study MBBS outside India?

Some of the top choices for Indian students to study MBBS outside India are China, Russia, Ukraine, Nepal, the Philippines, the Caribbean, Poland, etc.

How long does it take to complete the MBBS course?

The course duration for MBBS is 5.5 years.

 

Which is the best medical college in India to study MBBS?

AIIMS New Delhi is the top institute in India to study for an MBBS degree and it has gained the 1st position in NIRF ranking ‘24.

Is there any entrance exam for MBBS other than NEET?

There is only one entrance exam in India for MBBS which is NEET.

 

Can I complete MBBS in 4 years?

 No, in India, the course duration for MBBS is 5.5 years which includes an internship.

Name some of the popular colleges which offer MBBS course?

Maharishi Markandeshwar (Ambala), Swami Rama Himalayan University (Dehradun), Parul University (Gujarat), Jaipur National University (Jaipur), Sankalchand Patel University (Visnagar) etc are some of the popular colleges that offer MBBS course.

What is the average salary of MBBS graduates?

The initial salary of an MBBS graduate will be in between Rs 25,000 to Rs 35,000/- per month, initially. Depending upon the experience and knowledge of the person, the salary can be range between Rs 8 LPA to Rs 65 LPA.

What are higher education prospects after MBBS?

Students can choose to pursue either an MD or MS in different specialisation such as Optometry, Ophthalmology, Paediatrics, Anaesthesiology and many more.

What is the minimum age to apply for MBBS?

Candidates need to be a minimum of 17 years of age as on 31st December of the year of admissions, to be eligible for MBBS admission.

What is the difference between an MBBS and MD?

MBBS is an undergraduate program designed to practice surgery and medicine around the world. While MD, on the other hand, is a medical postgraduate course offered to applicants who have completed their MBBS degree in India.

What is the minimum eligibility required for an MBBS course?

The student should have passed 10+2 examinations with minimum 50% marks with physics, biology/zoology, chemistry and English as their compulsory subjects and should have cleared NEET exam of the respective year.

Is NEET required for an MBBS course?

NEET is the most only entrance exam accepted in India to get a confirmed MBBS admission. More than 15 lakhs students appear for NEET exam each year.

What is the average course fee for MBBS?

The average course fee for MBBS ranges between Rs 1,00,000/- to Rs 10,00,000 per year.

What is the full form of MBBS?

The full form of MBBS is Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery. It is an undergraduate program in Medicine and Surgery.

What are the popular specializations related to Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) ?

Some of the popular specializations for M.B.B.S. (Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery) are : Pharmacology , Audiology & Speech Language Pathology , Pathology , Microbiology , Medical Sciences.

I want admission in Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS). Do I have to clear any exams for that ?

Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination , Delhi University Science , National Eligibility and Entrance Test-UG , Delhi University Arts , JIPMER MBBS Entrance Exam are some of the exams that you can appear for to get admission in Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) courses.
