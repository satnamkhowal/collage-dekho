BBM is an undergraduate program designed to equip students with foundational and advanced knowledge of various business principles. Through a combination of theoretical studies and practical exposure, students learn to analyze business environments, strategize, and make informed decisions. The curriculum promotes critical thinking, effective communication, and team collaboration skills, preparing graduates for successful careers in diverse business settings and industries. The BBM degree is a doorway to numerous job opportunities and further educational prospects in business studies.

BBM Course Overview

BBM full form is Bachelor of Business Management. It is a three-year undergraduate course related to the field of Management and Commerce which can be pursued after Class 12th. BBM course focuses on the study of subjects related to the management of businesses and organizations and teaches students the skills necessary to take leadership roles in the business world. BBM course helps candidates learn about entrepreneurship, financial risk analysis, trading, and commerce. It sharpens the analytical and critical thinking skills of a student by training them in the practical as well as theoretical aspects of business and business management.

Some of the central BBM subjects taught during the course include Management Principles and Practices, Introduction to Communication, Macroeconomics, Microeconomics, Accounting for Managers, Business Statistics, Human Resource Management, Cost Accounting, Corporate Accounting, and Business Mathematics. The BBM admission process is based on merit and entrance exams such as SET, IPU CET, NPAT, CUET UG, etc. The average BBM course fee ranges between INR 50,000 and INR 1,00,000 per year.

After acquiring the degree, students become eligible for employment in the most reputable business companies and earn a BBM salary of about INR 3 LPA to INR 7 LPA. They can find job profiles including Operations Manager, Finance Analyst, Business Development Manager, HR Manager, etc. Companies like Google, Goldman Sachs, Infosys, TCS, Accenture, etc. offer exciting BBM jobs to graduates with competitive salaries. Find out more about BBM, its syllabus, subjects, top colleges, fees, job prospects, salary, and more below.

What is BBM Full Form?

BBM full form is Bachelor of Business Management. It is an undergraduate-level degree program that is designed to impart the basic principles and skills of business management to students. The course curriculum of BBM is structured to provide a solid foundation in various aspects of business administration, including accounting, finance, marketing, human resources, operations, and information systems. The program is typically divided into six semesters, with each semester lasting for about six months. During the three-year duration of the course, students are exposed to a wide range of theoretical and practical concepts related to business management, which enables them to develop a comprehensive understanding of the field. Upon completion of the BBM program, graduates can pursue various career paths in the business world, such as management, entrepreneurship, consulting, banking, and more.

NMIMS NPAT

The full form of NPAT is the National Test for Programs After 12th. The NMIMS NPAT exam is conducted at the university level. SVKM’s Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS) is the conducting body for the NPAT exam. NPAT is conducted to get admission in various UG and integrated PG courses in Mumbai by constituent schools in Shirpur and Bengaluru Campuses.

SET

The full form of SET is the Symbiosis Entrance Test. Symbiosis International (Deemed University) is the conducting body for the exam. The SET exam is a gateway for admissions to undergraduate courses such as Management, Law, Engineering, Economics, Mass Communication, Computer Studies, and Liberal Arts. The weightage for the written exam is 50% and for GD/PI/WAT is 50%. 

CUET UG

The Common University Entrance Test, earlier known as the Central Universities Common Entrance Test is an all-India test being organized by the National Testing Agency for admission to various undergraduate programmes in Central Universities of India. The candidates must have cleared Class 10+2 or equivalent or 10+3 diploma approved by the State Board of Technical Education to become eligible for the CUET exam. 

AIMA UGAT

Under Graduate Aptitude Test (UGAT) is a standardized test being administered by AIMA annually to screen candidates for various undergraduate programs such as Integrated MBA (IMBA), BBM, BBA, BCA, BHM, B.Com, etc. To take the AIMA UGAT exam, one must have a 10+2 in any subject from a recognized board. Through this examination, students are judged based on their knowledge of the English language, logical reasoning, analytical skills, and general knowledge.

BBM Admission Process in India

Admission to the top colleges offering BBM is conducted on the basis of a candidate’s performance in the entrance exams and the GD/PI rounds. However, a number of colleges also offer direct admission into BBM courses on the basis of merit. Check the admission process for BBM mentioned below.

Admission Through Entrance Exams

To apply for the BBM program, interested candidates are required to appear for entrance tests which serve as the primary screening process. The results of the test are used to determine the eligibility of the candidates for the next selection rounds. After the results are declared, the institutes offering the program will release their respective cut-off scores, which is the minimum score required to qualify for the next rounds of selection - Group Discussion (GD), Personal Interview (PI), and Written Ability Test (WAT).

Candidates who secure the cut-off scores are then invited to participate in the selection rounds. The GD round assesses candidates on their communication skills, leadership qualities, and their ability to work in a team. The PI round aims to evaluate the candidate's personality, confidence, and overall suitability for the program. The WAT round tests the candidate's written communication skills, including their ability to express their thoughts clearly and concisely. Only candidates who successfully clear all the selection rounds and are deemed suitable for the program are offered admission.

Direct Admission

Apart from the typical admission process mentioned above, some BBM colleges also provide direct admission to aspirants. Direct admissions are provided based on merit, academic prowess, or personal interview performance, depending on the admission requirements of the institute. Direct BBM admission is a good option for candidates who don't want to prepare for entrance exams.

Direct BBM Admission Process Without Entrance Exam

Many BBM colleges in India offer direct admission without requiring candidates to appear for entrance exams. If you want to get direct admission to BBM courses without entrance exams, you will need to follow these steps:

  • Completion of your 10+2 or an equivalent examination from a recognized board is a mandatory requirement. The minimum percentage required in the 10+2 examination may vary from college to college or university to university. However, most institutions have a minimum requirement of 50%. 
  • You must complete the application forms of the colleges or universities you are interested in. These forms can usually be found online on the respective institution's website. 
  • If your application is selected, you may be called for a personal interview. Upon completion of the interview, you will be required to pay the admission fee to secure your seat in the BBM program.

Courses After BBM Degree

A Bachelor of Business Management course is an ideal choice for aspiring managers to begin their careers as it lays the foundation for students to build a career in the field of business management. However, many aspirants tend to continue their academic journey even after completing their BBM course. Some of the best courses after BBM are mentioned below:

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

The most sought-after course to pursue after completion of a BBM course, an MBA course is the ideal postgraduate management course that aspirants can pursue to become excellent hires for top-tier management jobs.

Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM)

A PGDM program is a great choice for BBM graduates because it is somewhat similar to an MBA program with a few differences. Some candidates favour a PGDM program over an MBA because it has an updated course structure and curriculum. A PGDM graduate's career prospects are comparable to those of an MBA graduate, though.

Master of Commerce (M.Com)

A common choice for BBM graduates, an M.Com course is the best pathway for aspirants if they wish to become teachers/professors or even researchers in the field of business and commerce. An M.Com can also benefit students who are planning to pursue a Ph.D. after completing their Master’s degree.

Master of Management Studies (MMS)

The Master of Management Studies (MMS) is a fantastic choice for students looking for courses to take after their BBM. This program is career-oriented and focuses on enhancing management skills. Students are given a lot of focus in this course on developing their business management, human resource management, and marketing management skills.

Professional Courses

BBM graduates can choose to upgrade their skills by enrolling in professional programs like Chartered Accountancy (CA) or Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA), both of which offer highly lucrative career options. Additionally, due to their extensive field of knowledge, management students are always given preference after completing such courses.

Certificate Courses

There are several certification courses available to students in management or other related fields if candidates do not wish to devote a significant portion of their time to another full-time course. These include PG Certificate Programmes in Web Analytics/Search Engine Optimisation, PG Diploma Programmes in Digital Marketing, etc.

The BBM course duration is 3 years for a full-time program. The part-time, online and distance learning courses usually take 3-4 years to complete. It is an undergraduate course related to the field of Management and Commerce which can be pursued after Class 12th.

Is a part-time BBM course available?

A part-time BBM course is available at various colleges in India. It is a three-year undergraduate course that is analogous to a regular BBM course. The students don't need to be physically present at the institution to pursue the course. A part-time BBM course is an excellent option for working professionals who are not willing to quit their jobs.

What is BBM course fees?

The average BBM course fees range from INR 10,000 to INR 2 lakh for the three-year course. The fees depend on various factors such as the institution, the course curriculum, additional facilities offered, and the location of the college.

Can I do BBM after 12th?

Yes, students can do BBM after the 12th. Students seeking BBM admission must have completed Class 12th from a recognized board with at least 50% marks in the qualifying examination. Admission to the BBM courses is merit-based.

Why should one study BBM abroad?

One should study a BBM course abroad because individuals graduating from reputed foreign universities become eligible for being recruited at senior management positions in universally acclaimed companies. Students pursuing BBM courses abroad become eligible for job profiles like Food Service Managers, Construction Managers, Human Resource Managers, Administrative Service Managers, etc.

What is the difference between BBA and BBM?

The difference between BBA and BBM lies in the specializations offered by them. BBA deals with Accounting, Entrepreneurship, Finance, Real Estate, Human Resource Management, International Business, Information technology, etc. While a BBM course deals with Finance, Operations Management, Management Information Systems, etc.

What are the entrance exams for Bachelor of Business Management (BBM)?

The entrance exams for the Bachelor of Business Management (BBM) are CUET PG, NMIMS NPAT, SET, and AIMA UGAT. Candidates must have cleared 10+2 with a minimum aggregate of 55% to appear for the BBM entrance exams.

Is a Bachelor of Business Management (BBM) a good course?

The BBM course is good for the candidates who have an analytical mind, good decision-making skills, practical and innovative approach towards problem-solving and an interest in the practical aspects of business management.

Does BBM offer good career options?
