B Tech IT Overview

B Tech IT is a four-year undergraduate course that deals with computer hardware, support, software applications, design, implementation, development, and management of computer-based information systems. The control, development, communication, storage, and transmission of data are all topics covered in the B Tech Information Technology course. Further, the curriculum is robust and students are exposed to modern technologies so that they can develop the skills they need to succeed in the field. Prospective students can enrol in this course after completing their 12th grade and graduating with a minimum 50% to 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

Admission to B Tech IT courses can be made based on the applicant’s entrance exam scores and merit rank. Entrance exams for these courses are JEE Main, JEE Advanced, UPSEE, WBJEE, VITEEE, and more. Further, students can pursue B Tech Information Technology courses in both offline and online modes of learning. In India, the average fee for this course is approximately INR 25,000 to INR 2 LPA. In India, a few popular B Tech IT colleges are Anna University, IIT Bhubaneswar, Delhi Technological University, MIT Manipal, VIT Vellore, etc. After the completion of the course, students can attain positions such as Software Developer, Support Specialist, Network Engineer, Maintenance Engineer, Web Developer, etc. with an average salary of 6 LPA. Types of B Tech IT Programs

B Tech IT is one of the most favorable engineering programmes students pursue after completing their 12th grade. This course is provided to prospective students in mainly two modes of schooling: full-time and part-time courses. 

However, studying B Tech Information Technology in distance/online mode is not possible. This is because the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Indian government do not allow any type of B Tech courses in online/distance forms of education. Although a few academic institutes and online educational platforms provide this course online, this type of degree is not considered valid in India by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). 

Given that B Tech IT needs practical training and hands-on expertise, online courses or theoretical concepts cannot suffice the need for this technically inclined course. The following are the types of B Tech Information Technology courses offered:

Full-Time B Tech IT Courses

Such types of courses require students to attend full-time in-person classes, complete regular assignments, and take semester-wise offline exams on campus. They must also complete projects and perform practicals as part of the course curriculum. With this, students gain practical experience in the subject as well as in-depth learning through actively engaging with their teaching staff.

Part-Time B Tech IT Courses

For students who cannot engage in the full-time programme or who are employees working for a certain company, there is a part-time course available at institutes like Dr. MGR Educational and Research Institute (Chennai). Further, the part-time B Tech IT programme lasts the same duration as the full-time course.

B Tech IT Eligibility Criteria

B Tech IT eligibility criteria vary from one university to another. This is because some institutes provide merit-based admissions based on results obtained in 10+2, while other institutes follow entrance exam screening criteria to evaluate a student’s candidature. Given below are the commonly followed eligibility criteria for the B Tech Information Technology course:

  • Candidates must have passed class 12th with a minimum of 50% to 60% aggregate. Along with this, the candidates must have taken Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as mandatory subjects.
  • Candidates who have passed class 12th with Computer Science as one of the subjects are also eligible for admission.
  • Candidates must clear the entrance exam to be eligible for the admission process.
  • Students who pass the entrance exam must take part in counselling to be considered for admission to various B Tech IT colleges across the nation.

B Tech IT Entrance Exams

Candidates aspiring for B Tech IT admission must clear an entrance exam, i.e., national-level/ state-level/ university-level entrance exam. For example, JEE Main is the national-level entrance exam for admission to B Tech courses at NITs, GFTIs and other private engineering colleges in India. States like Rajasthan, Odisha, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and Maharashtra also accept JEE Main scores for B Tech Information Technology admission. Therefore, the candidates need to clear the entrance exam.

On the other hand, various states conduct their engineering entrance exams for B Tech IT admissions. For example, AP EAMCET in Andhra Pradesh, WBJEE in West Bengal, MHT CET in Maharashtra (JEE Main score is also accepted), TS EAMCET in Telangana, KCET in Karnataka etc. Candidates need to clear the respective entrance exam to be eligible for the admission process.

Various deemed universities in India also conduct an entrance exam for B.Tech IT admission. Some of the top deemed university entrance exams are SRMJEEE, VITEEE, BITSAT, Vignan SAT, SUAT, KLEEE, etc. After conducting the entrance exams, various exam bodies conduct a counselling process and each student who cleared the entrance test must take part in it to get admission.

Direct B Tech IT Admission Process Without Entrance Exam

Direct admission to a B.Tech IT program without an entrance exam typically involves a straightforward process. First, check with the selected educational institution to confirm if they offer direct admission. Once confirmed, the next step is to meet the eligibility criteria, which usually includes having a minimum required percentage in your high school (10+2) examinations in relevant subjects.

Submit the necessary documents, which may include your academic transcripts, identification proof, and passport-sized photographs. Some institutions may also ask for a statement of purpose or recommendation letters. After submitting the required documents, you may need to fill out an application form either online or in person. Pay attention to any application fees that might be applicable. Once the application is processed and accepted, you will receive an admission offer. Review the terms and conditions, and if you agree, proceed to secure your seat by paying the admission fees within the specified duration.

FAQs about B.Tech IT

What is the BTech IT course?

BTech IT is a four-year bachelor's degree course in Information Technology that teaches about computer-based systems and covers both hardware and software components of a computer system.
 

What is B Tech IT?

B Tech IT is a four-year undergraduate course in the field of Information Technology that can be taken after the completion of 10+2 studies. An extensive curriculum and practical experience with current technologies are part of the B Tech Information Technology programme, which helps students obtain the skills required for a career in this field. After graduation, students can work as Java Developers, Software Engineers, Application Software developers, etc.

What is B Tech IT salary per month in India?

The average starting B Tech IT salary per month in India (estimated take home) ranges between INR 5,859 and INR 17,501.
