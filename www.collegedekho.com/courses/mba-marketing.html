An MBA in Marketing is an advanced degree program designed to equip students with a wide range of skills and knowledge in the field of marketing. It aims to develop strategic thinkers who can create and implement effective marketing strategies to drive business growth. Graduates learn to analyze market trends, understand consumer needs, and make data-driven decisions. This specialization prepares individuals for high-level marketing roles in diverse industries, enhancing their ability to lead marketing campaigns and teams successfully.

What is MBA in Marketing?

MBA in Marketing is a two-year postgraduate degree that focuses on marketing, sales, consumer trends, strategies, and research in various fields. In this course, candidates spend significantly more time studying Marketing in depth. Students can pursue an MBA in Marketing course in a variety of modes, including full-time MBA, part-time MBA, distance learning MBA, online MBA, executive MBA, and 1-year MBA. Admission into the MBA in Marketing course is usually based on the merit and results of entrance exams such as the CAT, XAT, MAT, NMAT, SNAP, etc. A candidate must hold a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized board. Some colleges also offer direct admission to candidates in MBA in Marketing courses based on their academic performance and work experience (if any).

Marketing Management, Marketing Strategy, and Customer Behaviour are some of the core MBA in Marketing subjects that students will study during four semesters. Among the top MBA in Marketing colleges in India are IIMs, MICA, SIIBM, NMIMS, SPJIMR, and others. MBA Marketing fees range between INR 3 lakhs to INR 25 lakhs. MBA Marketing graduates are qualified to work as Marketing Managers, Chief Marketing Officers, Brand Managers, Sales Managers, Market Research Analysts, Customer Relationship Managers, etc. One can expect a starting salary of INR 7 LPA to INR12 LPA, which can increase as per expertise and experience. Why Choose MBA in Marketing?

Marketing is one sector that allows for a great deal of creativity and invention. It is not constrained by any formula. To be effective, a marketer must think "outside the box." An MBA in marketing is ideal for people with a competitive, fierce, and active mentality. The programme provides numerous possibilities for students to think outside the box, which will benefit them later in their careers as members of the marketing team.

Having said that, the benefits of pursuing an MBA Marketing specialization might be used to justify the decision. The scope of an MBA in marketing is vast, as practically every business and organisation requires marketing to compete. While discussing the MBA in marketing scope, the following benefits are the rationale behind the scope.

Career Advancement

An MBA in Marketing can assist candidates in obtaining a career promotion and qualifying for management and executive roles in their existing firm. Because postgraduate business programmes stress the development of leadership and communication abilities, graduates can easily advance to marketing roles. These talents are essential for any senior-level function, whether they are engaged in sales, business development, media and entertainment, advertisement, brand management, or even general management.

Higher Earning Potential

A master's degree can significantly increase your remuneration package. According to Payscale, annual salaries for MBA Marketing graduates can range from INR 3 lakhs to INR 20 lakhs, depending on seniority, organisation, and sector. Furthermore, reputable and regional organisations hire MBA in Marketing talent for a variety of marketing positions. As a result, the job duties that come with lucrative pay scales can be extensive.

Knowledge Amplification

Most MBA Marketing programs are designed to provide graduates with a foundation in 'core' business education and to enrich that knowledge with marketing-specific electives. Finance, human resources, accounting, corporate management, organisational behaviour, and other basic disciplines are covered. The elective courses, on the other hand, focus on issues such as market research, consumer behaviour, product management, brand management, strategic selling, and promotional campaign management.

Most Desired Profession

Marketing jobs or sales management jobs are widely sought after by many professionals looking to break into the business. Because marketing and sales are highly rewarding careers, a large number of people apply for open positions.

Various Career Possibilities

One of the primary reasons for the preceding remark is that businesses themselves have a plethora of marketing alternatives. Every year, FMCG, retail, fashion, and healthcare industries release new items that must be advertised. It is not achievable without a strong marketing staff. Furthermore, there is a continuing need to expand into more unique markets and areas, which necessitates the use of marketing professionals.

Growth and Advancement

As competition increases, so do the opportunities for advancement in a marketing career. A marketing professional with an MBA has many more prospects for advancement than those without one. The reason for this is that excellent management is just as important as having a good team. It is perhaps even more vital since an organisation requires strategic direction to perform well, which efficient management may supply.

Who Should Pursue MBA in Marketing Course?

Pursuing an MBA in Marketing is a wise choice for individuals with a passion for marketing strategies, consumer behaviour, brand management, and market research. Various types of people can benefit from an MBA in Marketing, including:

  • Business Professionals: Those who are already working in marketing or related fields and want to advance their careers while deepening their understanding of marketing principles.
  • Entrepreneurs: Those who are starting their businesses or ventures and want to develop the skills to market their products or services effectively to target audiences.
  • Career Changers: People from diverse backgrounds who want to transition into marketing roles or industries and require a strong foundation in marketing theory and practice.
  • Digital Marketers: Those working in digital marketing roles who want to enhance their knowledge of digital marketing channels, analytics, and online consumer behaviour.
  • Sales Professionals: Salespeople who want to expand their skill set beyond sales tactics and learn how to develop comprehensive marketing strategies to drive business growth.
  • Marketing Researchers: Individuals interested in market research and data analysis who want to learn advanced techniques for gathering and interpreting market data to inform marketing decisions.
  • Global Marketing Professionals: Those working in international business or interested in expanding their careers globally, who want to understand the nuances of marketing in different cultural contexts.
  • Brand Managers: Individuals responsible for managing and promoting a company's brand image and identity, who want to gain strategic insights into brand building and positioning.
  • Product Managers: Professionals involved in product development and management who want to understand how marketing concepts can inform product strategy and launch plans.
  • Consultants: Experts who specialize in marketing or business strategy and want to deepen their expertise to better serve their clients' needs.

MBA in Marketing Eligibility Criteria

Students must meet the following eligibility requirements to pursue MBA in Marketing:

  • Bachelor’s degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline from a recognized university with minimum aggregate marks of 50%. 
  • IIMs require a minimum aggregate score of 60%.
  • Reserved category candidates (SC/STs) can avail themselves of a relaxation of 5%, which makes the passing percentage 45%. 
  • Candidates should also qualify for any nationalized entrance examination such as CAT/MAT/GMAT/XAT/CMAT, etc. 
  • Candidates qualifying for the national-level entrance exams will be called for a written test and personal interview.
  • For Executive MBA Programs work experience is required.
  • For studying an MBA in Marketing abroad, a minimum of 3 years of full-time experience is required.
  • Additionally, candidates would also have to furnish IELTS/TOEFL/PTE scores to apply for an MBA abroad.

Skills Required for MBA in Marketing

  • Communication Skills: A Marketing Manager must possess excellent verbal and written communication skills and the ability to convince others. He should be able to convey his ideas clearly to his team as well as the target audience of his company's products or services.
  • Analytical Skills: For possessing a managerial role in marketing, candidates need to develop the skills of analyzing certain situations and enhancing their problem-solving skills. It also includes the ability to apply proper logical thinking that can break any complex problem into simpler parts and then provide solutions for them.
  • Teamwork: An MBA in Marketing candidate must have teamwork skills to excel as a manager because while working for greater interests, it is important that all have to cast their differences aside and work together to achieve good results. This quality is developed a bit from college days when a group of individuals is assigned a particular project.
  • Leadership Skills: Candidates must be equipped with strong leadership skills. Individuals with great leadership skills empower teams to achieve their full potential, make decisions, and strategize well to help organizations thrive. Leadership skills for marketing managers involve setting strategic goals, motivating employees to perform well, mentoring less experienced team members, and delegating tasks to other employees.

Common Admission Test (CAT)

Common Admission Test (CAT) is a qualifying exam for admission to MBA, PGDM, and other PG-level management courses in India. The eligibility for taking the CAT exam is 50% marks in graduation, for reserved categories the qualifying marks are relaxed up to 5%. The test consists of three sections: Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (VARC), Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning (DILR), and Quantitative Ability (QA). The exam is taken online over three hours, with one hour per section.

Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT)

Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT) is a national-level aptitude test conducted by XLRI Jamshedpur for admission in MBA/PGDM programs in XLRI and other top MBA colleges like XIMB, IMT Ghaziabad, TAPMI Manipal, etc. The exam duration is 3 hours. The syllabus encompasses Verbal and Logical Ability, General Knowledge, Decision Making, and Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation.

Symbiosis National Aptitude Test (SNAP)

Symbiosis National Aptitude Test or SNAP Test is a common aptitude test conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed) University for admission to the MBA/PGDM programs. The exam is conducted once a year. The duration of the exam is 60 minutes or 1 hour. 

Common Management Admission Test (CMAT)

CMAT is a popular national-level exam conducted by the National Testing Agency once a year. The exam assesses a candidate's aptitude across various areas such as Quantitative Techniques, Logical Reasoning, Language Comprehension, General Awareness, and Innovation and Entrepreneurship.

ICFAI Business Studies Aptitude Test (IBSAT)

ICFAI Business Studies Aptitude Test (IBSAT) is an online aptitude test conducted by ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education (A Deemed to be University under Section 3 Of UGC Act 1956), for students seeking admission to the MBA/Ph.D. Programs Of IBS Hyderabad and MBA program at IBS Hyderabad (off-campus) at Bangalore.

List of Popular MBA in Marketing Specializations

The most common specializations found within MBA in Marketing include:

Marketing Management

Marketing management is a process of controlling the marketing aspects, setting the goals of a company, organizing the plans step by step, taking decisions for the firm, and executing them to get the maximum turnover by meeting the consumers' demands. The average course fee ranges from INR 2-8 lakhs.

Operations Management

Operations management (OM) is the administration of business practices to create the highest level of efficiency possible within an organization. It is concerned with converting materials and labour into goods and services as efficiently as possible to maximize the profit of an organization.

Salesforce Management

It is the development of a sales force that includes coordination of sales operations, as well as the training and application of sales methods that result in achieving sales goals and objectives. For a business to make revenue, a sales force management strategy is critical.

Consumer & Brand Insight Strategy

A customer insight strategy is the game plan that you (and your team) develop for gathering and analyzing customer data, and then turning that data into valuable insights and key actions that your company will implement.

International and Multinational Marketing

International Marketing is a multinational process of planning and executing the conception, pricing, promotion, and distribution of ideas, goods, and services to create an exchange that satisfies individual and organizational objectives. Multinational marketing is the process of advertising and selling products and services to customers around the world.

MBA in Marketing Admission Process in India

If you're planning to pursue an MBA in Marketing degree, there are some steps you need to follow to get admitted into a management institute. Here is the basic admission process.

Step 1: Apply for Entrance Exams - The first step is to apply for the MBA in Marketing entrance exams that the colleges you're interested in accept. Make sure you read and understand all the requirements of the entrance exam.

Step 2: Qualify for Entrance Exams - If you score well in the entrance exam and meet the minimum cutoff, you'll be called for selection rounds. You'll have to register to take part in the counselling and meet all the requirements.

Step 3: Participate in Counselling - Based on your entrance exam scores, you'll be shortlisted and called for Group Discussions, Written Ability Test, or Personal Interview rounds. Some MBA colleges offer direct admissions to MBA in Marketing based on merit and conduct personal interview rounds.

Step 4: Appear in the Merit List - After evaluating candidates based on entrance exam scores, GD/PI performance, academic performance, and work experience, the final list of selected students will be declared.

Step 5: Report to the College - If you're allocated a seat in the MBA college, you'll need to report (offline or online) and pay the admission fees. The classes will commence after that.

FAQs about MBA Marketing

Which are the best foreign universities to study MBA in Marketing abroad?

The best foreign universities to study MBA in Marketing abroad are Yale University (USA), Northwestern University (USA), London Business School (UK), University of Montreal (Canada), Australian National University (Australia), National University of Singapore (Singapore), Seoul National University (South Korea), Insead (France), LMU Munich (Germany), Copenhagen Business School (Denmark), and University of Auckland (New Zealand).

What is the difference between an MBA in Marketing and a Master’s in Marketing programme?

A Master's degree in marketing generally lasts one year. This marketing course is designed for students who have recently completed a bachelor's degree or have limited work experience. An MBA with a specialty in marketing, on the other hand, can last up to two years and permits students to pursue more advanced marketing courses. As a result, it attracts people with prior work experience who want to change industries or roles.

Who should pursue an MBA in Marketing course?

MBA Marketing programmes are designed for fresh graduates and people with 1-5 years of work experience who wish to advance or start a career in marketing. Some MBA in Marketing candidates may already work in marketing, but this advanced degree is also appropriate for individuals who want to transition from another role.

What are some of the MBA in Marketing electives that students study?

In the MBA in Marketing programmes, students may also come across advanced or technical electives. Some of these MBA in Marketing electives include Models for Marketing Strategy, E-commerce, Strategic Brand Management, Advertising Management, Product Management, Entrepreneurial Marketing, Social Impact Marketing, Content Marketing, SEO, etc. 

What is a part-time MBA in Marketing course?

A part-time MBA in Marketing course is designed to accommodate working professionals who want to pursue further education while continuing to work. It takes longer than 2 years to complete the degree because it will be taught using a combination of online and in-person tactics.

What is an online MBA in Marketing course?

An online MBA in Marketing programme, contrary to a part-time MBA in Marketing programme, is taught virtually and entirely online, even if it is a full-time degree. The online MBA in Marketing may also be referred to as distance education programmes.

What is the difference between MBA in Marketing and MBA in Digital Marketing?

MBA in Marketing and MBA in Digital Marketing are two alternatives for students interested in a career in marketing. An MBA in Digital Marketing is a post-graduate programme provided by numerous institutes that focuses on teaching students about digital marketing tactics as well as the many digital platforms utilised for marketing. MBA in Marketing is a two-year degree that includes a curriculum that teaches classic marketing strategies.

What is the starting salary of an MBA in Marketing graduate?

The starting salary of an MBA in Marketing graduate ranges between INR 40,000 and INR 50,000 per month on average, whereas those with an MBA in Marketing from a reputed B-school earn at least INR 60,000 per month. The salary increases with experience and expertise.

Who is a Market Research Analyst?

A Market Research Analyst is a professional who devises data collection methods, analyses data to draw insights, and then communicates the findings to business development teams. Market Analysts provide critical support in the creation of marketing strategies.

Can an MBA in Marketing graduate become a Digital Marketing Manager?

Yes, an MBA in Marketing graduate can work as a Digital Marketing Manager. They maintain the company's digital presence and market its products through web-based platforms and online channels. This covers website content, Social Media Marketing, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Pay Per Click (PPC), content marketing, and other channels.

What are the benefits of an MBA in Marketing?

An MBA in Marketing has numerous benefits. One of the most significant benefits of a marketing position is that you are more likely to earn a higher income. This is because a marketing function is closely related to business growth. Another advantage you will have is that after you complete your MBA, you will be able to enter a variety of sectors.

What skills are learnt in an MBA in Marketing?

An MBA in marketing sharpens many skills. Communication is an important life skill. Another essential skill is time management. Leadership skills are highly valued in MBA Marketing graduates. Analytical skills will aid you in identifying difficulties, patterns, and so on. Strategic thinking will assist you in understanding and controlling situations.

What is the course fee for an MBA in Marketing in India?

MBA Marketing course fees generally range between INR 7 and INR 30 lakhs per year, depending on the institute. The average expenses for an MBA course with any specialisation, including an MBA in Marketing, in India's top-ranking MBA colleges like the IIMs, can range between INR 23 LPA to INR 30 LPA.

What is the best course to study after MBA in Marketing?

The best courses to study after MBA in Marketing include Digital Marketing Certifications, Six Sigma Certification License, Risk Management Courses, PhD in Marketing, PhD in Consumer Behaviour, PhD in Business Administration, Master's in Data Science, etc.

Can I pursue MBA in Marketing after a B.Com degree?

Yes, students can pursue MBA in Marketing after a B.Com degree. Candidates should keep in mind that getting an MBA at practically any reputable institution or college in India also requires passing competitive tests such as the CAT, MAT, XAT, NMAT, SNAP, and others.

What are the career options after an MBA in Marketing?

The career options available after an MBA in Marketing are Marketing Manager, Project Manager, Market Research Analyst, Social Media Manager, Digital Marketing Manager, etc. Since Marketing is one of the essential areas of a business, professionals can find work opportunities in private as well as government sectors.

What are the subjects taught in the MBA in Marketing course?

The subjects taught in the MBA in Marketing are Management Concepts, Business Environment, Strategic Management, Marketing Management, Human Resource Management, Management Information Systems, Accounting and Managerial Decisions, International Marketing, Sales and Distribution Management, Advertising and Sales Promotion, Entrepreneurship and Managerial Economics, etc.

What is the duration of the MBA in Marketing course?

The duration of a full-time MBA in Marketing Management is two years. The programme is divided into 4 semesters of 6 months each. It is one of the most popular course specialisations in the field of marketing. Students learn advanced marketing concepts such as digital marketing, customer behaviour, advertising, and other aspects of the industry.

Is CAT compulsory for MBA in Marketing?

CAT is compulsory for MBA in Marketing courses offered by IIMs. However, students seeking admission into MBA in Marketing from other colleges can also appear for other entrance exams like MAT, CMAT, XAT, ATMA, SNAP, etc. Candidates must check their eligibility for MBA in Marketing before applying to their desired college.

Is an MBA in Marketing a good option?

An MBA in Marketing is an excellent option because it offers better earning opportunities. If your goal is to advance your career in marketing or related fields such as brand management, advertising, or market research, an MBA with a specialization in marketing can be beneficial.

When to pursue an MBA in Marketing?

Individuals may pursue an MBA in Marketing right after graduation if they are seeking higher education. Working professionals who have an experience of 3-4 years can also opt for MBA in marketing to enhance their skills & knowledge and seek better career opportunities.

What is the difference between MBA in Marketing and MBA in Finance?

MBA in Finance course includes the study of the organisation, maintenance and efficient distribution of finances in differing business environments and settings. The entrance exams for MBA in Marketing are CAT, XAT, CMAT, SNAP, NMAT by GMAC, CMAT, etc. Students have to qualify for these entrance exams if they want to get admission to colleges where these scores are mandatory. However, many colleges offer direct admission without entrance exams too.
