MSc Data Science Overview

M Sc Data Science is a two-year interdisciplinary program that focuses on disciplines like Calculus, C- Programming, and Descriptive Statistics. M Sc Data Science course puts light on storing, analyzing, and recording data for extracting future predictions as well as insights extracted from structured to unstructured data. It aids in the discovery of patterns in raw data that can be used to formulate important business decisions.

Candidates who have completed their bachelor's in subjects such as mathematics, computer science, or engineering and are willing to study further can take up research-based Data Science courses. As per the current trends, Data Science is considered to be a popular course to pursue. The course can be pursued in both online and offline modes. The average course fee including both government and private institutions is somewhere between Rs 1.5 LPA to 2 LPA.

Skills That Make You The Best M Sc Data Science Professional

Following are the skills such as proficiency in calculus and mathematics along with programming, statistics, and interpersonal communication skills that are required for becoming the best M Sc in Data Professional.

Calculus and mathematics

The most prioritized skill that is needed to be succeeded in M Sc in data science is proficiency in calculus, graphs, and linear algebraic equations since it forms the base of the subject. Candidates need to be Proficient in mathematics as it is essential since the basic skill in data science is problem solving skills. Therefore, candidates must be very calculative to function efficiently as data scientists. Good knowledge of calculus and mathematics is essential because recruiters might ask to solve critical situations on the spot which will be needed to use the required skills.

Programming 

The second most important skill needed for candidates in M Sc in data science is proficiency in Programming . Candidates will have to be very skillful in Programming languages such as JAVA, Python, machine learning, etc. Since professionals in data science have to be efficient with problem-solving skills, these Programming skills count as a positive side for the candidates during the whole duration of their professional careers.

Statistics

Candidates should be intensely skilled in statistics as it is one of the required skills needed for proficiency in data science. Students have to play with numbers as they have to deal with various problems like statistical distributions, graphs, charts, etc. Therefore, these tools should be understood and implemented by professionals. 

Interpersonal and communication skills

Despite all the above skills being learned, the last but not least important skill that has to be understood and implemented is good communication and interpersonal skills. Candidates should be proficient in communicating as all the above skills lie dependent on effective communication within the organization. If there is no effective communication, candidates will not be able to express and transfer their views and thoughts. Therefore, candidates should master the art of communicating keeping in mind the art of mastering mathematics, Programming, and statistics.

Why do students choose MSc data science?

Students choose MSc data science because gaining a master's degree in data science opens up an intriguing professional path with quick growth potential. Which is better MTech or MSc data science?

Whether MTech or MSc data science is better depends on a student’s personal interests and if they prefer to learn by theoretical or practical means. In particular engineering or technology domains, the Master of Technology (M Tech) programme emphasises technical proficiency and applied expertise. In the Master of Science (MSc) programme, research and theoretical knowledge are prioritised across several fields, encompassing the social sciences, humanities, and sciences.

Is MSc data science difficult?

Yes, MSc data science is difficult because it requires a strong math, statistics, and computer programming foundation. With enough effort and dedication, anyone may, nevertheless, gain the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in this field.

What is the scope of MSc data science?

The scope of MSc data science is vast, offering numerous professional opportunities to prospective students. After completing the course, students can work as data architects, data scientists, statistical programmers, operations analysts, data analysts, business analysts, research data scientists, operations managers, and more.

Who is eligible for MSc data science?

Students eligible for MSc data science must satisfy the following requirements:

  • They must have a three-year bachelor's degree in disciplines such as mathematics, computer science,  statistics, or a closely related discipline is required.
  • They must have completed each of the 10+2+3/4 levels with at least 50% aggregate marks or an equivalent grade.

What is MSc Data Science?

MSc Data Science is an interdisciplinary programme that lasts two years and mainly focuses on calculus, C- programming, and descriptive statistics. It focuses on storing, analysing, and recording data for extracting future predictions as well as insights extracted from structured to unstructured data. If a student wants to pursue a career in Data Science, then they must have completed their bachelor’s degree with a minimum aggregate of 50% from a recognised university.

Is an MSc in Data Science a good career?

Yes, an MSc in Data Science is a good career. Graduates with a master's degree in data science typically earn high salaries. Whether students enter a master's degree programme in data science with a bachelor's degree in a related field or are looking to completely change careers, obtaining an advanced degree usually results in a significant pay raise.

What is the average MSc Data Science salary in India?

The average MSc Data Science salary in India ranges between INR 5 LPA and INR 15 LPA. An Entry Level Data Scientist with under three years of experience can expect to earn an average salary of INR  9 LPA. Those in the middle of their career with 4-9 years of experience make an average wage of INR 14.4 LPA, while a data scientist with 10-20 years of experience gets a typical salary of INR 21.2 LPA.

Can I do MSc in Data Science after class 12?

No, you cannot do MSc in Data Science after class 12 since it is a postgraduate degree. To pursue this course, candidates must have completed their undergraduate degree with at least 50% aggregate from a relevant discipline with subjects such as mathematics, statistics, computer science, etc. For college admission, students are additionally required to pass national-level or state-level entrance exams or other exams that may be conducted by a particular college.

Is coding required for MSc in Data Science?

Yes, coding is required for MSc in Data Science. All jobs related to the field of data Science require some level of coding as well as experience with technical tools and technologies. In short, an individual in this field needs moderate Python knowledge, SQL grasp, and knowledge of a Cloud Platform are optional but preferred to understand the subject in a better way.

Is the NIMSEE entrance exam compulsory for admission into MSc in Data Science course?

No, the NIMSEE entrance exam is not compulsory for admission into MSc in Data Science course. Several national and state-level entrance exams can be taken by prospective candidates such as CUCET, IIM JAM, JNUEE, CUET, and more for admission purposes. In addition to these exams, students may also be required to sit for the university-level entrance exams (if any) and participate in an interview conducted by their respective colleges.

What are the eligibility criteria required for admission to MSc Data Science?

The eligibility criteria required for admission to MSc Data Science have been discussed below briefly:

  • Students who have successfully qualified in Statistics or Mathematics for at least two years are eligible for the course.

  • The marks required for screening the candidates is at least 50% marks from a well-recognised university.

Is MSc Data Science hard?

Yes, MSc Data Science is hard to learn given the field’s level of difficulty and incorporation of mandatory subjects such as Statistics, Mathematics, and Computer Science. Several reasons contribute to this, but the most important one is that it requires a wide range of skills and knowledge. Data Science jobs can be more difficult to learn than other fields of technology due to the often technical requirements.

What is the duration of the MSc Data Science course?

The duration of the MSc Data Science course is two years for a full-time on-campus programme. If you are willing to pursue this course online, the duration will vary that is estimated to be between six months and one year, depending on the terms and conditions of the conducting body. Furthermore, if you want to pursue this as a part-time course, then the duration may also vary depending upon your preferred institute.

The MSc Data Science selection process is based on the student's entrance exam scores. The eligible candidates are offered admission to the course based on a compilation of the student's grades.
