About M.Sc - Information Technology (IT)

M.Sc. Information Technology (IT) focuses on making a student technically competent in the theory of Information Technology, its application and research related aspects. The programme aims to make students eligible for IT Industry as well as research programmes.

M.Sc. IT programmes are designed to impart theoretical as well as application knowledge to the students and prepare them for research in the field. Although most of the concepts and subjects are similar to most computer courses, however, some subjects are different. Students are also expected to submit a thesis and project at the end of the programme.

M.Sc Information Technology aspirants should adapt some skills in them to ensure that they can perform in the industry. They should have project management skills, leadership skills, numerical skills, communication skills, teamwork skills etc.

Why Choose M.Sc Information Technology?

The important reasons why one should opt for M.Sc IT Information Technology are as follows:

Demanding Course- M.Sc IT Information Technology course is in huge demand of the candidates who want to pursue a higher study after their bachelor’s degree. With the expansion of the IT field and the increasing number of job scopes here, B.Sc IT qualified students show their interests to pursue M.Sc IT programmes.

Job scopes in Other field- The students after pursuing M.Sc Information Technology (IT) course can be recruited in different fields other than the IT industries. They can begin their career in finance, teaching, banking etc.

Different Job Roles- M.Sc IT graduates can be recruited in in different job roles like Application Programmer, Software Developer, Maintenance Engineer etc. India is now known as the world’s largest IT sourcing destination all across the world. E-Commerce, online retailing all have contributed a lot for the rapid growth of the IT industry. Being the fastest growing industries it gives immense job scopes to the M.Sc IT professionals.

Offers Decent Salary- M.Sc Information Technology Course is the best option for the IT students because this course makes them able to work with industry required expertise and in return, they also get a decent salary.

M.Sc - Information Technology (IT) Eligibility Criteria

Candidates who have completed their graduation from a recognised university or institution in a related subject with a minimum overall aggregate of 50% or more are eligible to apply for the M.Sc information technology admission at various reputed universities/institutions.

Both the marks requirement and age requirement may vary depending on the institutions. Students need to be sure about the M.Sc Information Technology eligibility criteria by checking the websites.

M.Sc Information Technology Admission Process

Those institutes which accept entrance exams scores like the scores of TANCET, DUET, TISSNET etc accept the admission application of only those candidates who have secured a valid rank in either of the M.Sc IT entrance exams. Based on the entrance exam scores, a merit list is prepared and the counselling process is conducted before the final allocation of seats. 

Institutes offering merit-based admissions into their B.Sc IT programme consider the scores of the last qualifying examination in which the candidates had appeared. Again a merit list on the basis of the qualifying exam scores is prepared and seats are allocated accordingly.

For both the admission processes of M.Sc Information Technology (IT) candidates need to visit the official website of the university. They are advised to read about the eligibility criteria and other sections very carefully. As eligibility criteria may vary depending on the university/institute they wish to apply for, they should focus on that. While filling up the form students should enter the details with attention. Because, some universities may not have application form correction facility. After the form fill up, while they upload the photos, signature and other documents, they must ensure that everything is as per size and format specifications. The MSc IT application fees can be paid online and it can not be refunded.

M.Sc - Information Technology (IT) Career Options and Job Prospects

Be it research, teaching or IT sector, all the options are open on the completion of M.Sc. IT programme. Candidates can pursue UGC NET and start teaching Information Technology and coding to college students. Another lucrative option is to foray into the private sector and apply for programming and developing profiles. Here are some profiles that are open after this course:

  • IT Consultant
  • Network Administrator
  • Web Developer
  • Systems Support Administrator
  • Security Agencies
  • Security Expert
  • Tracker
  • Software Engineer
  • Computer Programmer
  • Interface Engineer

Salaries after pursuing M.Sc. in Information Technology can vary from Rs. 3 to Rs. 5.75 lakh per annum. Some of the private sector jobs pay better salaries in development profiles. Experienced IT experts and consultants get salaries up to Rs. 8 lakh per annum or more depending on their skills.

M.Sc Information Technology Future Scopes

M.Sc Information Technology Course provides enormous scopes to the students. By pursuing the M.Sc IT course, one can be recruited by the top organizations like IBM, Accenture, Infosys, HCL Technologies etc.

With experience and expertise, the M.Sc IT graduates can earn upto 1500,000 per annum.

As the industry has grown on a large scale and is expected to have this growth in the future, the M.Sc IT students can  expect a bright future for them.

A career of research professional is also there. Those who want to do research work in this field can enroll themselves in the PhD courses.
