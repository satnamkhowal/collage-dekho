About B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management

BSc (Hons) Agri-Business Management is an undergraduate degree programme that is offered to create professionals with agricultural and management skills.

The demand for business and management professionals in the agricultural field has increased in the past few years and B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management is an apt course for those who have an interest in the same field.  The four years B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management course syllabus is designed to meet the needs of the agriculture industry.

A candidate who is enrolled in B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management course studies various processes involved with agriculture like breeding, crop rotation, machinery etc along with the marketing and sales strategy of food and agriculture products. An individual who is involved in agriculture production, distribution, marketing and sales is associated with agribusiness.

Skills Required to Pursue B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management:

Following skills are required to pursue B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management course:

  • The candidate must have an interest in agriculture, business and management.
  • He/ She must have some technical knowledge
  • He/ She must have practical learning skills

B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management Eligibility Criteria

Following are the eligibility criteria to pursue BSc (Hons) Agri-Business Management course: -

  • The candidate must have passed Class 12th in any stream.
  • He/ She must have scored at least 50% marks in the qualifying examination.

B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management Admission Process

Admission in B.Sc (Hons) Agri-Business Management course is based on the national level or state level entrance examination. ICAR AIEEA, MHT CET, TS EAMCET, AP EAMCET, MP PAT, BCECE are some of the common entrance exams for admission in  BSc (Hons) Agri-Business Management.
