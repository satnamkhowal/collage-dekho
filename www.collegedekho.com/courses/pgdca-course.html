PGDCA Course, or Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Applicationis a masters level programme that lasts only a year. It aims to provide an advanced understanding of both theoretical and practical knowledge of computer applications. To be eligible for the course students must have passed their 10+2 level along with the graduation degree from a recognised university. It is important to score a minimum of 50% marks in graduation. In this course, students learn about operating systems, website development, software engineering, and much more.

PGDCA Course Overview

PGDCA course, or Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Applications is a 1-year diploma programme. This course is specially curated for students who are eager to learn about IT trends. It is an ideal choice for students who enjoy studying subjects like programming languages, and operating systems. To pursue this master's degree, the candidates must have graduated in any stream from a well-known university with a minimum 50% aggregate. It may be an added benefit if students have studied computer science subjects at their graduation level.

 PGDCA course admissions are done based on merit or entrance exams. Some of the entrance exams for PGDCA courses that universities conduct are JMI EEE ( Jamia Millia Islamia Engineering Entrance Exam), DUET (Delhi University Entrance Test), etc. The PGDCA course can be pursued both full-time and part-time. Some universities that offer full-time classes for PGDCA courses are Jamia Millia Islamia, Abhyuday University, Delhi University, Amity University, etc. However, part-time PGDCA courses can be pursued from IGNOU, Delhi. 

PGDCA course syllabus consists of subjects like programming, database management systems, software engineering, web technology and lab work. PGDCA course fees can range between INR 10K to INR 1.5 LPA. After completing the course, students can expect jobs in sectors like the IT Industry, networking, cyber security and governmental organisations. They can work as software engineers, website developers and programmers, system network administrators, ethical hackers, etc. The average salary after PGDCA course may range between INR 2 LPA to INR 8 LPA.

Types of PGDCA Courses

PGDCA courses can be pursued in full-time, part-time and online mode. Even though there are different options to complete the PGDCA course, it is advantageous to pursue the programme in full-time mode as the course requires practical knowledge and understanding. All three types of modes are explained in detail below.

Full-Time PGDCA Course: The PGDCA regular course is the most popular among all three modes. Students are expected to attend regular classes as well as lab sessions for both theoretical and practical knowledge. The duration of the regular PGDCA course is one year, and the fees of the course range from INR 7K to INR 1 LPA.

Part-Time PGDCA Course: The part-time PGDCA course is available for students who cannot attend regular classes and seek to complete their programme through the distance mode of learning. The duration of the course may vary in part-time mode but ideally, it is of one year. Institutes such as IGNOU, Lovely Professional University, etc. offer the course in distance mode.

PGDCA Online Course: The PGDCA course can also be done in online mode. The duration of the course depends on different platforms offering the programme virtually. Some of them can be completed in 6 months, while others can last a year.

PGDCA Course Eligibility Criteria

The PGDCA course eligibility criteria depend on the marks scored at the graduation level. The students are expected to score a minimum of 50% marks in their graduation with any stream. However, if you have studied computer science subjects in graduation, you might get some preference. To get detailed information about the eligibility process, refer to the given points. 

Qualifying exam: The candidate applying for PGDCA must be a graduate in any discipline such as BA, BCom, BSc, BBA, BCA, BTech, etc.

Minimum marks: Candidates are required to have a minimum of 50% aggregate in UG courses. Also, graduation from recognised universities would be considered relevant.

Age limit: Age is not a limiting factor for PGDCA courses. However, some universities do abide by certain age criteria. For instance, Vallabh Government College does not allow applicants older than 26 years of age (28 for females) to be considered eligible for admission into the PGDCA courses.

PGDCA Course Admission Process in India

Admission to the PGDCA course is done based on merit scored in graduation or entrance exams. The PGDCA course admission process also varies based on the type of Institute you are applying to. A detailed step-by-step guide is provided to explain the admission process for the PGDCA course.

Step 1: Application form. Start by filling out the application form for the college you want to apply to. This can be assessed through the official website of the university or college.

Step 2: Application form fees. Moving on, after filling in the particulars submit the application form fee. It is important to note that the application fee is non-refundable.

Step 3: Entrance exam. Appear for the entrance exam conducted by the university and wait for the results to be released.

Step 4: Counselling round. After the entrance exam results, the selected candidates will be called for the next round of counselling or personal interviews.

Step 5: Document submission. Finally, submit all the necessary documents and pay the course fee to enrol in the programme.

DCA is the study of basic computer knowledge and PGDCA is a more technical and deep study of computer applications.  The subjects taught in DCA courses are Introduction to Computer and Internet, Computer Organisations and Operating Systems, MS Word, and MS Excel whereas on the other hand, the subjects taught in the PGDCA course are PPM AND OB, Web Programming, Soft Skills, and Java.

What are the subjects taught in the PGDCA course in the first year?

Some of the subjects that are taught in the PGDCA first year are Fundamentals of Information Technology, Programming, Soft Skills, Software Engineering, Business Process, Oracle, Web Programming, and Practical Work.

Can I do PGDCA via distance mode?

Yes, you can pursue the PGDCA course via distance mode. Many colleges and universities are offering PGDCA to candidates who cannot attend the classes in offline mode. The PGDCA distance or online courses mostly opt for candidates who are mostly working professionals or are not able to visit the campus physically.

Who can do PGDCA course?

Candidates who have completed a minimum of 10+2+3 years of formal education with Mathematics as one of the main subjects are eligible to enrol for PGDCA course.
