BBA in Hotel Management Overview

BBA in Hotel Management is a 3-year undergraduate course designed for those wishing to join the hospitality industry. From working within a resort to different hotels and clubs or any other hospitality industry areas, BBA in Hotel Management course will ingrain the necessary skills and knowledge for a range of hospitality roles. BBA in Hotel Management covers everything related to the Hotel & Hospitality industry throughout the six semesters over a period of three years.

The core BBA in Hotel Management subjects includes Hospitality Marketing, Hospitality Accounting, Business Communication, Financial Management, Organisation Behaviour, Legal Studies, Event Management, Information Technology, Transport Management, and more. Students can opt for BBA in Hotel Management courses in various modes, such as full-time, part-time, online, distance learning, and more as per their convenience.

To be eligible to get admission into BBA in Hotel Management course, candidates must have passed Class 10+2 from a recognized board with 55% aggregate marks. Some colleges and universities offering the course may also ask the students to sit for an entrance exam like AIMA UGAT, NCHM JEE, or CUET along with a group discussion and personal interview as part of the selection process. The top BBA in Hotel Management colleges includes the Indian Institute of Hotel Management Kolkata, Indraprastha Institute of Technology and Management Delhi, Institute of Hotel Management Catering and Nutrition Delhi, IHM Bhubaneswar, and more. The average annual fees may range from INR 45,000 to INR 3 Lakhs.

Pursuing a BBA in Hotel Management course will open up a number of lucrative opportunities for graduates in roles such as Front Office Manager, Hotel Manager, Supervisor or Manager within different sections of the hotel, and much more. Freshers can expect a salary between INR 2 Lakhs to 4 Lakhs per year which can increase as per the experience. Get complete details about the BBA in Hotel Management course on this page.

Why Study BBA in Hotel Management?

One of the commonly chosen courses at the postgraduate level is often an MBA, as the opportunities that open up for a candidate are immensely lucrative. However, choosing a BBA in Hotel Management will often open several avenues to a lucrative career right after school. Highlighted below are a few reasons why studying BBA in Hotel Management may be the right choice for you:

  • Lucrative Career Paths: While graduating with a BBA in Hotel Management may not lead to high-paying jobs right away, the course will set you on the path for such a career. Gaining managerial or leadership experience in the first few years may help you easily apply for an MBA, which will significantly improve your career prospects further.
  • Increasing Options: After a BBA in Hotel Management, candidates will be equipped with the basic knowledge and understanding of hospitality management. And with a number of hotel chains opening up in India and abroad, graduates will find a number of options available for them to earn valuable experience.
  • Get Training for a Range of Roles: Similar to the above-mentioned points, a BBA in Hotel Management course is designed to train candidates for a variety of roles across the hospitality industry. Thus, this will allow you to keep your options open in the initial years, allowing you to determine and finalize the role you wish to achieve.
  • Cheaper Option than MBA: As stated above, candidates often choose to pursue an MBA to enhance their career prospects in a specific field. However, an MBA often comes with a pocket-burning price tag. Therefore, a cheaper option for those wishing to pursue a managerial or leadership role in the hospitality industry can choose to pursue a BBA and not only reduce the costs but also the time invested to earn the necessary skills.

BBA in Hotel Management Eligibility Criteria

Admissions to a BBA in Hotel Management will be conducted and the prospective students selected by the university or college offering the course. Most colleges offering the course will determine the minimum admission requirements and eligibility criteria for BBA in Hotel Management courses individually. However, listed below are some of the common requirements that applicants must present during the admission process.

  • All applicants must have cleared their class 12 board examinations from either a national education board such as CBSE, or ICSE, etc or a state education board like UP board, Rajasthan board, etc.
  • Candidates from all academic backgrounds can apply for the course, provided they present a minimum score of 50-55% in their board examinations.
  • Candidates may be asked to appear for a university-level entrance exam along with a group discussion and personal interview as part of the selection process for the course.
  • Some highly selective universities may also ask the applicants to submit a Statement of Letter and/or a Letter of Recommendation along with their application form.

Note: The admission to the courses will be unique and individual to each university. Therefore, read the eligibility criteria for BBA in Hotel Management offered by the desired university before submitting any application and documentation.

How to get Admission to BBA in Hotel Management?

Admissions to the popular hospitality course offered by the top B-schools in India will be similar to every other BBA course offered. It should be noted that the admission process for the BBA in Hotel Management will differ from institute to institute, therefore, check the individual admission processes before applying to any college. Outlined here are some of the general steps on How to get admission to BBA in Hotel Management.

  • Ensure that all the BBA in Hotel Management course requirements have been met such the minimum Class 12 board exam scores and entrance test requirements.
  • Most universities and colleges offering the course will conduct their own entrance exams to enrol the prospective students. However, some universities accept applications based on State or National entrance exams as well.
  • Compile all the documents, including the entrance test score reports and all other documents for submission.
  • Fill out all the details required in the application form correctly and completely.

  • Wait for the university to provide their response with regard to the candidate’s admission to the course.

Once candidates have been selected for admission, they will be asked to follow the next steps in confirming their admission. This would include a payment of a security deposit which would confirm their seat at the particular university. However, as stated above, the application and selection processes will vary between different institutions.

FAQs about BBA HM

Can I get a senior management position with a BBA in Hotel Management?

In some cases, it is possible to achieve a senior management position with a BBA in Hotel Management, however, the more established organisations would require its prospective senior management to possess a minimum of a master's degree, preferably an MBA or similar degree. One method you can adopt is to apply for an Executive MBA in sponsorship of the company where you wish to become senior management.

Will I find work abroad with a BBA in Hotel Management?

Yes, it is possible to work with a BBA in Hotel Management, however, the number of opportunities available will be limited. If you wish to pursue a lucrative career in the hospitality industry abroad, it is recommended to pursue a relevant MBA or a PGDM course as well, which is recognised by organisations abroad as well.

Can I do law after my BBA in Hotel Management?

Yes, you can enrol for an LLB course after completing your BBA in Hotel Management. If you are interested in pursuing a career as a lawyer specialising in Hotel Management, then that is possible as well. The minimum requirement for an LLB is to possess a recognised bachelor's degree in any discipline.

Can I study BBA in Hotel Management after class 12?

Yes, the minimum general requirement for a BBA in Hotel Management course is for the applicant to have cleared the class 12 board exams in any stream, i.e. Arts and Humanities, Commerce with or without maths or Science. Candidates must ensure that they score at least 50-55% in their board exams if they wish to study the course anywhere in India.

What is the scope of a BBA in Hotel Management?

Among some of the benefits of pursuing a BBA in Hotel Management are the future prospects after graduation. Upon graduation, students can either choose the academic or the professional route. Both routes often lead to lucrative career options, however, it might take longer for a student to gain good salary packages with a BBA, while, someone with a BBA and master's degree may earn a higher salary right after graduation.

Is BBA in Hotel Management expensive?

Depending on the institution you choose to study at, a BBA in Hotel Management course can range anywhere from INR 2.5-20 LPA. Inevitably, private institutions will be more expensive than government institutions. Moreover, the tuition fees for BBA in Hotel Management will also be determined by the demand for the course at a particular university or college in the country.

Is an entrance exam necessary for BBA in Hotel Management?

Most universities and colleges offering the course may ask applicants to appear for some sort of an entrance exam or a written test that assesses the aptitude of a prospective student. However, some universities may just use merit to shortlist and select students for the BBA in Hotel Management courses. For this purpose, it is necessary to go through the admission policies and requirements of your desired university and college.

Where can I work after my BBA in Hotel Management?

BBA in Hotel Management graduates can work across a multitude of hospitality-related organisations. From cruise lines and global hotel chains to even higher education and hospital administration, graduates of the course will find relevant job roles across different sectors in the economy. The wide array of topics covered under the programme qualifies students to work across a wide range of job roles in the hospitality industry.

What subjects are covered in BBA Hotel Management?

Varying from university to university, the course curriculum and subjects covered in BBA Hotel Management often include topics like Marketing management, Operations management, Personnel management, Financial Accounting, Industrial Relations, Public Administration, Computer Awareness, Statistics for Managers and much more. It should be noted that the subjects will cover topics of relevance to the hospitality industry as well as general managerial and leadership skills and knowledge.

Is BBA in Hotel Management better than BHM?

There are pros and cons of pursuing both a BBA in Hotel Management and BHM, with different career prospects for both after graduation. While pursuing a BBA in Hotel Management will open up a slew of leadership and management opportunities for graduates, BHM opens a variety of job roles in both the kitchen and the front office, including managerial roles.

Can I become a Hotel Manager after BBA in Hotel Management?

Yes, those graduating with a BBA in Hotel Management are capable of becoming hotel managers. However, to achieve the role in the field candidates may have to work a few years as either a receptionist or a departmental manager before being able to become a hotel manager. However, it is possible to become a Hotel Manager with just a BBA degree.

Is BBA Hotel Management courses a good choice?

Yes, BBA Hotel Management courses are perfect for candidates looking to create a lucrative career in the hospitality industry.
