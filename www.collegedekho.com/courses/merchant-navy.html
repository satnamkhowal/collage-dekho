Merchant Navy course offers a dynamic career in global shipping, focusing on navigation, marine engineering, and maritime management. Unlike the defense navy, it is a commercial field dealing with the transportation of goods, fuel, and essentials across countries. Students can pursue diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate programs, with entry via exams like IMU-CET, JEE, and TMI SAT. Top specialisations include Nautical Science, Marine Engineering, ETO, Maritime Law, and Naval Architecture. Leading institutes like IMU, TMI, AMET, SIMS, and AEMA provide training, placement, and sponsorship opportunities. Course fees range from INR 2,00,000 to 6,00,000 per year, with scholarships and loan options available. Career roles include Deck Cadet, Marine Engineer, and Captain, offering lucrative salaries from INR 30,000 to 10 Lakhs per month.

Merchant Navy

Merchant Navy course equips you with the skills needed for a career at sea. This course is one of the most captivating courses offered as part of maritime studies. The Merchant Navy plays a vital role in international commerce, dealing with the shipment of products, fuel, and other necessities on a global scale. The Merchant Navy differs from the defence navy, which deals with commercial shipping. It also provides the chance to visit new destinations and earn lucrative wages.

Selecting this course gives you access to exciting yet well-structured careers in navigation, marine engineering, and maritime management. The future of the Merchant Navy becomes even more promising as globalisation, automation, and digital innovations envelop the shipping industry. You will undergo training in this course to gain experience in technical know-how. You will also get global exposure, tax advantages, and a guaranteed career path.

Top Merchant Navy Specialisations

Different career paths in the Merchant Navy can accommodate a person’s specific interests and capabilities. Each field will help you learn the skills needed for an important position at sea. 

List of Popular Specialisations

Specialisations within the Merchant Navy are structured to prepare you for specific departments of a ship. Each area provides you with the specialised knowledge and leadership skills needed to meet certain challenges of operating at sea.

  1. Nautical Science (Deck Department): Focuses on navigation, ship handling, and cargo operations. Graduates progress from Deck Cadet to Captain.
  2. Marine Engineering (Engine Department): Deals with maintenance and operation of ship engines and machinery, leading up to the rank of Chief Engineer.
  3. Electro-Technical Officer (ETO): Specialises in electrical systems, automation, and power distribution onboard vessels.
  4. Maritime Law & Logistics: An emerging field covering shipping laws, port management, and global logistics operations.
  5. Naval Architecture & Shipbuilding: Focuses on ship design, construction, and offshore engineering solutions.

The stream you choose can have a considerable effect on the trajectory of your career, your duties, and your salary. So make sure to decide your specialisation carefully. If you have any specific queries, you can contact our counsellors for assistance. 

How to Select the Right Specialisation for Your Goals

The selection of a specialisation within the Merchant Navy is influenced by your competencies and aspirations, both short- and long-term. Proper alignment of your objectives will give the right course a greater impact on your future opportunities and income.

  • Career Interest: If you enjoy navigation and leadership, Nautical Science is ideal. If you are mechanically inclined, Marine Engineering is the better choice.
  • Future Scope: Engineering and ETO roles are in high demand with the rise of automation.
  • Long-Term Growth: Deck officers have command opportunities, while engineers and ETOs specialise in technical leadership.
  • Lifestyle Preference: Deck officers manage overall operations, while engineers work hands-on with machinery.

Merchant Navy Eligibility & Admission Process

Merchant Navy qualifications include specific academic and professional criteria to ensure readiness for maritime studies.

Merchant Navy Qualifications

  • 10th / 12th pass with PCM for officer-level courses.
  • Graduation in Mechanical, Electrical, or Naval Architecture for advanced courses like GME or ETO.
  • Minimum 50-60% marks in English and core subjects as required by DG Shipping-approved programs.

Merchant Navy Exams Requirements (or Direct Admission)

  • IMU-CET is mandatory for most pre-sea officer courses after 12th.
  • AIMNET (All India Merchant Navy Entrance Test) is another recognised exam for entry into maritime programs.
  • JEE Advanced scores are accepted by certain institutes offering marine engineering programs.
  • Some online programs allow direct admission if you meet their eligibility criteria and have sponsorship from shipping companies.

Professional Experience (When Needed)

  • Required for advanced courses like Electro-Technical Officer (ETO) or Graduate Marine Engineering if you are joining post-graduation.
  • Typically, 0-2 years of relevant industrial experience is acceptable for online program enrollment.

We advise you to focus on building strong fundamentals in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, and prepare seriously for IMU-CET. If you are aiming for advanced roles, gain technical experience early to enhance admission and placement chances.

How to Apply for Merchant Navy Course

Merchant Navy application involves simple steps and proper documentation to secure admission.

Step-by-step Application Process

  1. Start by checking the top Merchant Navy programs.
  2. Check the eligibility criteria for the selected course.
  3. Fill out the official online application form of the institute.
  4. Submit academic documents and sponsorship details (if any).
  5. Pay application or registration fees.
  6. Await confirmation and enrollment instructions from the institute.

Required Documents

  • 10th & 12th mark sheets / Graduation certificates
  • Passport-sized photographs and identity proof
  • Medical fitness certificate (if pre-sea practical is included)
  • Sponsorship letter (if applicable)

Application Deadline (if any)

  • Deadlines vary by institute. 
  • Make sure to fill out the merchant navy exam forms on time.  
  • Many online programs offer rolling admissions throughout the year.
  • Some institutes may have fixed sessions (e.g., July & January intakes).

We would advise you to stay proactive by preparing documents in advance and securing a medical fitness certificate early. Aim for sponsored programs whenever possible, as they provide both training and job assurance.

Merchant Navy Course Duration, Curriculum & Structure

Merchant Navy programs are designed to balance flexibility with hands-on maritime education.

Typical Program Duration

Merchant Navy course duration may vary. 

  • Ranges from 6 months to 4 years, depending on the course.
  • Flexible schedules for working professionals.

Modular vs Semester Scheme

The Merchant Navy course structure is both modular and semester-wise. 

  • Modular: Focused short-term skill-based modules.
  • Semester: Traditional semester-wise structure covering all core subjects and electives.

Live vs Recorded Lectures, Assignments, Exams, and Group Projects

The Merchant Navy curriculum will include the following.

  • Live Lectures: Real-time interaction with instructors.
  • Recorded Lectures: Flexible access for revision.
  • Assignments & Exams: Regular assessments to monitor progress.
  • Group Projects: Collaboration on case studies and maritime simulations.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Leadership and team management on ships
  • Analytical skills for navigation, cargo operations, and engineering
  • Ethical and safety standards in maritime operations
  • Problem-solving and decision-making under sea conditions

Electives, Capstone Projects, Internships

  • Electives: Specialised subjects like Marine Logistics, Safety Management, Ship Design
  • Capstone Projects: Real-world problem-solving exercises related to ship operations
  • Internships: Pre-sea or on-board training (DG Shipping-approved institutes)

Merchant Navy Course Fees

Merchant Navy course fees vary depending on the program type, duration, and institute's reputation. You can also benefit from scholarships, sponsorships, and education loan options.

Typical Fee Range

Here’s a breakdown of Merchant Navy course fees in India.

  • Fees generally range between INR 2,50,000 - 8,00,000 for officer-level courses.
  • Short-term GP Rating courses may cost around INR 1,50,000 - 2,50,000.
  • Marine Engineering and B.Sc. Nautical Science (full-time) may go up to INR 10-12 Lakhs at reputed institutions.

Scholarships and Loan Options

To ease financial burden, you can access Merchant Navy scholarships, sponsorships, and education loans offered by institutes, companies, and banks.

  • Government Schemes: Scholarships for SC/ST/OBC and economically weaker sections.
  • Shipping Company Sponsorships: Many shipping firms sponsor students, covering fees and offering job placement.
  • Bank Loans: Education loans are available from public/private banks with moratorium benefits.
  • Merit-Based Scholarships: Offered by top institutes based on entrance exams or academic excellence.

FAQs about Merchant Navy

What are the qualifications required to join the Merchant Navy?

For officer-level classes, physics, chemistry, and mathematics must be passed along with 10+2. For GP rating, passing the 10th standard is sufficient. Medical fitness and eyesight standards must be adhered to. Further, IMU-CET, AIMNET, JEE Advanced, and other similar entrance exams may be required for certain courses. 

Is the Merchant Navy a government or private career?

The Merchant Navy is a commercial field that includes private international shipping companies as well as government companies such as the Shipping Corporation of India. Both sectors offer a lot of opportunities, which are dependent on the partnerships of the institute as well as your performance in exams and training. 

What are the job roles after a Merchant Navy course?

Graduates can work as Deck Cadets, Marine Engineers, Electro-Technical Officers (ETO), Ratings (GP), and eventually rise to senior roles like Chief Engineer or Captain. Career growth depends on sea-time experience, exams, and performance on board.

Can I study Merchant Navy courses online?

Yes, both theory and simulations of modules may be studied online. However, training before joining the sea and the time spent at sea need to be completed in person. These programs will give you the opportunity to study and lay the basics from anywhere. You will also require training in DG Shipping-approved institutes after the theory studies. 

What is the average salary in the Merchant Navy?

Salaries commence at INR 30,000 to 50,000 per month for a fresher Deck Cadet. Salaries improve substantially with promotions, and senior officers, including Captains or Chief Engineers, earn around INR 6 to 10 Lakhs per month, depending on the company, ship, and experience.

Dear student,

~You should have passed your 12th standard with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) as compulsory subjects from a recognised board. ~You should be between 17 to 25 years at the time of joining the course ~You need to be physically fit as per the standards set by the Directorate General of Shipping (DGS) for sight, hearing with good stamina & endurance ~You have to be an Indian citizen

If you meet the above criteria, you need to choose a DGS certified course like:

~Diploma in Nautical Science (DNS): A 1-year pre-sea training course that leads to a B.Sc. (Nautical Science) degree after further sea service and examinations. This is a common route to become a Deck Cadet and eventually a Deck Officer.

~B.Sc. Nautical Science: A 3-year degree program that covers navigation, seamanship, and maritime safety. Graduates can join as Trainee Navigating Officers.

~B.Tech. Marine Engineering: A 4-year engineering degree focused on the maintenance and operation of ship machinery. Graduates can join as Junior Engineers in the engine department.

~Electro-Technical Officer (ETO) Course: A 4-month pre-sea training course (after a degree or diploma in Electrical/Electronics Engineering) to become an Electrical Officer on board.

~GP (General Purpose) Rating Course: A 6-month pre-sea training course that allows candidates with a 10th or 12th pass (with minimum 40% in English) to work in both the deck and engine departments at the rating level.

You will also need to appear for entrance exams like:

~IMU CET (Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test)

~JEE exam (Joint Entrance exam)

~Sponsorship exams

Our advice would be to research and choose DGS-approved maritime training institutes based on the course you want to pursue and your entrance exam results/sponsorship.
