BBA+MBA Course Overview

Bachelor of Business Administration and Master of Business Administration or BBA+MBA is a four- or five-year-integrated programme opted by students after clearing 12th standard. The duration of the programme may vary from college to college as some of the colleges might reward the degree after the completion of 4 years of education. Thus, it helps to save an academic session in the process. This integrated course nurtures and nourishes both practical managerial skills and communication skills. Also, the BBA+MBA programme will allow candidates to gain management skills at a beginner as well as a master's level. 

The integrated MBA programme is beneficial for those students who are sure to pursue a master's education after a bachelor’s degree. There are only some institutes available in the country that offer this dual degree programme as it is a new approach. IIM Rohtak, IIM Indore, Xavier University Bhubaneswar, Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies and Nirma University Institute of Management. The admission procedure is quite simple and depends on the university. Some universities are open to direct admissions while others require students to qualify for entrance examinations such as JIPMAT, IPM Aptitude Test, NPAT and UGAT. 

The dual degree BBA+MBA programme includes internships as well as live projects which enable students to adopt managerial skills practically. The course fee may range roughly from INR 5,00,000 to INR 8,00,000 per annum while the average salary package received by students on completion is INR 6,50,000 to INR 12,00,000. Moreover, the career opportunities after BBA+MBA are immense — students get to work in various job roles such as marketing manager, sales manager, brand manager, project manager and equity research analyst.

Why Study BBA+MBA?

BBA+MBA is a career choice for students who are interested to dedicate four to five years to studying business management. Though after Class 12th it is too early to make such a choice, it is beneficial for those who have long-term plans of entering the management side of the business. Here are the top reasons why BBA+MBA can be opted for:

  • Students get to study a unique syllabus that is not repeated during their course of study.
  • They do not need to take a break from education and continue their management studies without worrying about entrance exams for MBA.
  • The course will provide a comprehensive view of industries. Students will learn to function in different industries and the course will also prove beneficial if they want to change industries or start their own businesses.
  • Real-life case studies are taught to students during the course. They will get to know about the operations of different companies, the rights and wrongs they do, and how their decisions impact their businesses and other related businesses. Also, one will get to know about businesses across nations. 
  • Candidates will get opportunities to do internships in various corporate offices and gain exposure to industry practices. Also, candidates are highly sought after by businesses that have received education from top business schools.
  • The course helps students gain skills that are in high demand by employers. Critical thinking, problem-solving and other important skills will be taught along with soft skills such as leadership skills, communication skills and team-building skills.
  • Students get great opportunities to network through seminars, workshops, meetings and conferences where they can meet veterans of the industry.

BBA MBA Entrance Exams

Admissions to BBA MBA are mostly direct while some institutes prefer taking entrance tests for admission purposes. These integrated programmes are a new vertical besides engineering and medical that students can opt for. Therefore the entrance exams are held to test the eligibility of candidates for the integrated MBA programme who would later take up roles in business and management fields. Here are the top BBA MBA entrance exams after 12th:

  • IPMAT Indore: This BBA MBA entrance exam is conducted by IIM Indore and is a national-level entrance test. Students must score at least 60% in higher secondary and must be a maximum of 20 years old. IPMAT is conducted in a computer-based mode where there are three sections namely Quantitative Ability (MCQs), Quantitative Aptitude (Short Questions) and Verbal Ability. Students get 2 hours to complete 90 questions. The exam is of moderate difficulty. The colleges that accept IPMAT Indore scores are the Indian Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT), NIRMA University, NALSAR University, and Jagdish Sheth School of Management.
  • JIPMAT: National Testing Agency (NTA) is in charge of conducting JIPMAT or Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test for five years integrated management course at IIM Bodh Gaya and IIM Jammu. The test contains three parts: Quantitative Aptitude (33 questions)Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning (33 questions) and Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (34 questions). The exam duration is 2.5 hours. 4 marks are awarded for each correct answer while 1 mark is deducted for each wrong answer.
  • IPMAT Rohtak: This integrated MBA entrance exam is taken for admission to IIM Rohtak. The exam pattern consists of three sections including verbal ability, quantitative ability and logical reasoning. Each section consists of 40 questions and is in multiple-choice question format. 
  • LPUNEST: LPUNEST is conducted by Lovely Professional University which is a scholarship cum entrance test for BBA MBA admissions. It is an online test where a total of 150 questions are asked and the total marks are 500. 
  • MUCMET: Mumbai University Common Management Entrance Test or MUCMET is conducted every year to admit students to integrated MBA at Mumbai University. The test is in pen and paper mode and is for 2 hours. The syllabus includes General Awareness, Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension, Quantitative Aptitude and Logical and Abstract Reasoning.

BBA+MBA Eligibility Criteria

The general eligibility criteria for the integrated BBA+MBA program are the same as that of the BBA program: 

  • Students applying for admission must have passed the 12th examination from any stream. 
  • Besides this, students may check the information brochure or eligibility criteria for the college to check more about the eligibility criteria. 
  • Students who have appeared for the 12th examination may also apply for admission. Such candidates will have to submit the 12th mark sheet before the last date allotted by the college.
  • Aspirants must be more than 17 years of age. There is no upper limit to studying the course still students must check with their respective universities.
  • The marks achieved in class 10th are also considered while evaluating candidates for admission.
  • Students must secure at least 60% in their higher secondary. Some institutes accept scores as low as 45%. There is a relaxation of 5% for students from reserved categories.
  • There is no specific subject requirement to study BBA+MBA. Students from any stream can apply for the course.

BBA+MBA Admission Process

The admission process of the BBA+MBA process will differ from college to college. Students who are going for admission may check the detailed selection process for the college from its official website. In general, candidates will have to appear for the entrance examination of that college or get direct admission to the universities based on their marks in higher secondary. 

BBA+MBA Direct Admission 

Students have to fill up the application form by logging in to the website of the respective university. Thereafter, the university will publish a merit list of students who have been selected. for the course. A counseling session will be held after which students finally can get admitted. 

BBA+MBA Entrance-Based Admission 

The step-by-step entrance-based admission process is given below:

  • Students will have to appear for entrance examinations such as IPMAT, JIPMAT, NPAT, UGAT, and IPM Aptitude Test scores.
  • Once the results are out, they will be able to participate in counseling sessions.
  • The college will further conduct the personal interview round after the successful completion of the entrance examination. 
  • Candidates who clear all the rounds successfully will be called for document verification. 
  •  Colleges will further consider candidates' performance in the co-curricular activities at the time of selection. Once the selection process is over, candidates will be invited to submit the annual fee in order to reserve their seats.

Note: There are some colleges that will grant admission directly on the basis of the entrance exam scores.

BBA MBA Course Curriculum

The course curriculum of BBA MBA focuses on building foundation in first three years and developing management skills in the next two years. A combination of analytical rigour and wide exposure equips students for leadership roles and positions of responsibility in different sectors.

  • The course is divided into ten terms of six months or fifteen terms of four months.
  • Students complete a social internship at the end of 3rd year.
  • A business internship needs to be completed between the 4th and 5th year.
  • The courses in Mathematics, Statistics and Economics help to build analytical rigour.
  • The courses in Psychology, Sociology, Political Science and Economics provide an understanding of the core disciplines on which management study is based.
  • Courses in Science, Engineering, Humanities, Literature and Fine Arts provide a broader perspective.

FAQs about BBA+MBA

What is BBA+MBA?

BBA+MBA is a uniquely designed course that can be pursued after 12th by students who have a firm aim to pursue management for five straight years. It aims to balance thoughts, actions and experimentation with logical reasoning, analysis and fundamentals of business. The course nurtures business and leadership skills.

Do I need to appear for the entrance exam for MBA during BBA+MBA?

No, you do not need to appear for the entrance exam for MBA during BBA+MBA. That said, you need to appear for the test only before you are selected for the course. In this way, you can save a year which you might have had to dedicate to MBA entrance preparation. 

Do BBA+MBA have foreign exchange opportunities?

Yes, there are foreign exchange opportunities for BBA+MBA. However, you must remember that not all institutions offer this scope. Students can be a part of these programmes and gain enriching experiences by visiting and learning in foreign countries. 

What are the eligibility criteria for BBA+MBA?

The eligibility criteria for BBA+MBA is at least 50% to 60% in the higher secondary examination. Besides, they have to clear entrance tests or get admission based on merit. The merit list is prepared generally on the basis of test scores and the academic profile of students.

What are the popular job roles for BBA+MBA?

The popular job roles for BBA+MBA are management consultant, business head, HR manager, sales manager, retail manager, real estate manager, insurance, investment banker, event manager and digital marketing manager. One can also start an enterprise of their own with their understanding of business and management. 

What does the course curriculum of BBA+MBA include?

BBA+MBA course includes live projects, internships, industrial visits, interactions with industry experts and case studies. This enables students to get exposure and explore diverse areas related to business administration. The course curriculum helps them receive holistic development that enhances their skills. 

What are the advantages of BBA+MBA course?

In BBA+MBA course, students can move on to advanced levels of study after the second year of the study itself. They are groomed to explore new opportunities in the management profession. Also, they are preferred over simple graduate students. 

What are the companies that hire BBA+MBA graduates?

The companies that hire BBA+MBA graduates are Tata Consultancy Services, Cognizant, Wipro, Tesla, Nike, LinkedIn, HCL Technologies, Hewlett Packard, Genpact, Deloitte etc. Students get a good head start at the very beginning of their careers as they get to work in top positions. 

Is work experience required to pursue BBA+MBA?

No work experience is required to pursue BBA+MBA. It is an integrated undergraduate-level and postgraduate course that is covered over four to five years.  Contrary to traditional MBA, where work experience may be required, it is not mandatory for an integrated MBA programme. 

Is a personal interview an important part of admission to BBA+MBA?

Most business schools conduct personal interviews to admit students to BBA+MBA. It is an effective way to know more about the goals and ambitions of candidates. They also test the knowledge of students that are required to pursue the course. 

What is the duration of the course BBA+MBA?

BBA+MBA is a 5-year integrated course. Some colleges also offer 4-year integrated MBA courses, thereby saving a year. Also, if you do BBA and MBA separately, you may need to take a year drop to prepare for the entrance examinations. 

What are the popular specialisations related to BBA+MBA?

Some of the popular specialisations for BBA+MBA are Human Resources Management, Agribusiness / Rural, Operations Research, International Business, Finance and etc. Students can decide their specialisation in the advanced years of study. In the initial years of BBA, the course content will be the same for all specialisations. 

What is the average fee for the course BBA+MBA?

The average fee for the course BBA+MBA is INR 5,00,000 to INR 8,00,000 per year. Most universities charge separately for BBA and MBA. A particular amount has to be paid for the first three years while MBA fees are charged for the last two years. 

What are the top colleges offering BBA+MBA courses?

The top colleges that offer BBA+MBA courses or integrated MBA courses are Mumbai University, IIM Rohtak, IIM Indore, IIM Jammu, Kurukshetra University, Lucknow University, Indian Institute of Foreign Trade, SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies and others. 

I want admission in BBA+MBA. Do I have to clear any exams for that?

Yes, some institutes require you to clear entrance examinations to get admission to BBA+MBA. The most popular ones are JIPMAT, IPMAT, UGAT and NPAT. The exams are held between June and August of a particular academic year.

Skills Required for BBA MBA

A combination of technical skills and interpersonal skills makes an irresistible combination for BBA MBA graduates. The application of skills is an important part of having them. Employers would seek candidates who have mastered core skills in accounting, finance, marketing, and operations and apply them to real-world settings. Here are some of the skills that contribute to the success of BBA MBA students:

  • Adaptability: A business environment is ever changing and being able to adapt to it is extremely important. They must know how to adapt to sudden changes in work structure or any situation that may arise. 
  • Communication: Communication is extremely important for businesses. Pitching for deals to giving the right messages with great efficiency requires great communication skills. 
  • Leadership: The business world is all about the right kind of leadership to flourish. It is highly required for efficient teamwork and introspection on the work that is being done. Leadership skills bring in a sense of responsibility that brings value to an organisation.
  • Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving: Critical thinking is needed to observe and analyse the business ecosystem. Problem-solving skills are required to solve any immediate snag that may arise. 
  • Initiative and Drive: These are two significant factors that run a business setup. A highly driven person can turn around everything that comes their way into an opportunity. An MBA graduate needs to take initiative and a clear-cut strategy to drive the business.
