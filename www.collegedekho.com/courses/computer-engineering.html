Computer Engineering Course Overview

Computer engineering blends the fields of electrical engineering and computer science. A computer engineer is involved in both the design of computer architecture and the design of hardware and software. Candidates can seek Computer Engineering after completing their Class 10. Candidates can pursue Computer Engineering at diploma, graduation, and postgraduate levels. Computer Engineering Syllabus qualifies specialists to be employed in several areas that include scrutinizing and developing from uncomplicated microprocessors to software designs, highly featured circuits, and operating system development.

Candidates are admitted into Computer Engineering courses on a merit basis and entrance exams like JEE Main, JEE Advanced, GATE, MU OET, VITEEE, TS POLYCET, AP EAMCET, COMEDK UGET, MHT CET, WBJEE, etc. Candidates willing to pursue the Computer Engineering course should score an aggregate of 50% in the last qualifying exam with a subject combination of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM subjects). Besides, candidates also have to appear and crack the entrance exams accepted in the chosen institute. 

Computer Engineering is available to be pursued online, offline, as well as through distance education. Some of the top institutes that offer Computer Engineering courses are IIIT Bangalore, VIT, BITS Pilani, SRM Institute of Science and Technology, IIT Madras, IIIT Jaipur, NIT Trichy, etc. The average Computer Engineering course fee ranges from INR 9,300 to INR 20,00,000 Per Annum. After the conclusion of the computer engineering course, candidates are offered jobs as Project Engineer, Computer Programmers, Software Developers, and Web Developers. The average computer engineering salary in India is INR 3,00,000 to INR 10,00,000.

Types of Computer Engineering Programs

Computer Engineering courses can be pursued under Diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D. courses in India. The domains include Diploma in Computer Engineering, B.E or BTech in Computer Engineering, ME/M.Tech/MSc in Computer Engineering,  or M. Phil/Ph.D. in Computer Engineering. Let us discuss the curses in detail.

Diploma Course in Computer Engineering

  • Candidates can seek Diploma Course in Computer Engineering after passing their Class 10
  • To be eligible for a diploma course, it is mandatory to have a PCM background in class 10+2
  • Candidates can get admission to good colleges if they have scored at least 40%
  • The average duration is 3 years
  • A diploma in Computer Engineering has good demand, but not as much as a graduate in the field.

Undergraduate Course (Bachelor of Technology in Computer Engineering)

  • A Bachelor of Technology in Computer Engineering course highlights conceptual learning, theory, and applications
  • Candidates are required to score at least 50% marks aggregate in class 10+2 to be eligible 
  • To get admission to a top Indian Computer Engineering college, it is mandatory to crack JEE Mains and Advanced
  • To attempt JEE, it is necessary to have 75% with the PCM background in 10+2
  • A good score on the JEE exam will help in getting admission to tier-1 College.

Postgraduate Course (Masters of Technology in Computer Engineering)

  • A postgraduate course would help the candidates to specialize in the field of their interest within electronics engineering and progress industry knowledge
  • It is a two-year course that requires candidates to score at least 60% and the GATE exam
  • GATE score can be waived off if the candidate belongs to an IIT, and scores at least 6.5 overall and 8.0 CPI within 2 years of his/her graduation.

Doctoral Course (Ph.D. in Computer Engineering)

  • To pursue a doctorate in Computer Engineering, it is compulsory to have a postgraduate degree in the same
  • The duration of doctorate courses can vary from 6 months to 5 years
  • To become eligible for a doctorate-level course, candidates need to have a minimum GATE or NET score 
  • Candidates are required to score at least 50% marks in the last qualifying examination

Computer Engineering Entrance Exams

 The entrance exams required for admission to computer engineering courses have been mentioned in the subheads below. 

JEE Main

The Joint Entrance Examination Main commonly known as the JEE Main is a national-level entrance examination that is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for candidates all over India and abroad for admission into the top engineering institutes in India. 

JEE Advanced

The Joint Entrance Examination Advanced more commonly known as JEE Advanced is a national-level entrance exam conducted by the top IITs. Every year more than 2.5 Lakh candidates register for the entrance exam.

GATE

The Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering or GATE is a post-graduation national-level entrance exam that is conducted for the candidates who are willing to be admitted into the MTech course across the institutes of India.

BITSAT

BITSAT is an entrance exam exclusively for admissions into the Birla Institute of Technology and Science. The exam is conducted on an annual basis.

COMEDK UGET

Consortium of Medical, Engineering, and Dental Colleges of Karnataka or COMEDK UGET is an entrance exam for undergraduate engineering courses conducted by the Consortium of Medical, Engineering, and Dental Colleges of Karnataka.

Computer Engineering Admission Process in India

  • The admission procedure to computer engineering courses is done through merit and entrance exams.
  • There are national-level, state-level, and university-level exams. some of the popular national-level exams are JEE Main, and JEE Advanced while state-level exams include WBJEE, AP EAMCET, TS EAMCET, and KEAM. University-level exams include BITSAT, SRMJEEE, VITEEE, etc. These entrance exams are either online or offline.
  • Candidates are required to score 50% marks in their last qualified exams with physics, chemistry, and mathematics as compulsory subjects along with English.
  • After the candidates appear for the entrance exams, they should aim at clearing the cutoff of the respective college to which they want to be admitted. After the results are released, they should wait for counseling and seat allotment procedures.
  • Pay the admission fees, complete the admission formalities, and visit the college when asked by the authorities.

Direct Computer Engineering Admission Process Without Entrance Exam

  • The direct admission procedures are available in private computer engineering colleges through management quota. The fees of this procedure are generally higher than the normal admission procedure.
  • Candidates who have not appeared in any entrance exams need not worry. Direct admission to computer engineering doesn't demand any entrance exam marks.
  • Some institute offers admission to candidates through a direct admission procedure if the candidate belongs to the NRI quota.
  • Another route for direct admission is through lateral entry admissions which are applicable for candidates who are willing to pursue a BTech in computer engineering. They are admitted in the second year of the course through their diploma in computer engineering degree.

Computer Engineering Scope & Salary in India

The scope of Computer Engineering courses in India is very high and rewarding. With the increase in the demand for technology and innovation, computer engineers are enjoying success all around the world. Bachelor of Computer Engineering jobs is available for graduates in both the public and private sectors. The graduates are qualified with the knowledge and skills they need to construct a successful career in the field. Computer Engineering Course provides that the candidates obtain an in-depth comprehension of the subject. Graduates can operate in a wide variety of sectors and job roles owing to the assortment of their education.

Computer Engineering Course Industries

Some of the core Computer Engineering Industries have been listed in the following pointers.

  1. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning - Businesses are integrating Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning into their operations in increasing numbers. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are one of the most popular domains. These professions have a fantastically promising future and are growing quickly. Professionals trained in this field can be hired as Software Engineer, AI Engineer, ML Engineer, AI Specialist, Data Scientists, Data Analyst, and Technical Architect, etc.
  2. Information Security - Information Security deals with setting up a network of safe procedures devoid of failures to safeguard sensitive data from unauthorized credentials. Presently, almost all types of businesses across industries operate through information security and cloud technology to store data and defend essential details. Professionals holding a Computer Science Engineering degree in Information Security can be employed as Information Security Analysts, Information Security Engineers, etc.
  3. Robotics Process Automation- Robotics Process Automation or RPA is a promising technology that forges robots that imitate human behavior by interacting with relevant systems and software. The software and technologies required for robotic process automation must be acquainted with professionals in this sector. Professionals in this field can be recruited as Process Automation Developers, RPA Project Managers, RPA Operator, RPA Business Analysts, RPA Engineer, Associate Program Managers, RPA Developers, etc.
  4. Cyber Security & Forensics- Often organizations across industries face issues with Cyber Experts in Cyber Security & Forensics discipline are in control of sidestepping cybercrime. If it ever accidentally happens, gathering and analyzing computer-based proof to identify the offender becomes a challenge. Therefore the emergence of Internet transactions has made it fundamental to protect sensitive personal data. Professionals holding a Computer Engineering degree and specializing in Cyber Security can be hired as Incident Response Officers, Cyber Security Engineers, Cyber Security Forensic Officers, Forensics Consultants, Cyber Security Investigators, Digital Forensic Analysts, etc.
  5. Cloud Technology- The term "cloud technology" defines a web of servers with an online entrance that accommodates data. These servers are employed by certain software and databases that mandate routine updates. Graduates with Computer Engineering degrees can be hired as cloud analysts, cloud engineers, cloud infrastructure managers, cloud computing engineers, etc.

FAQs about CE

What is computer engineering?

Computer engineering incorporates the fields of computer science and electrical engineering. A computer engineer is concerned with both the design of hardware and software and the design of computer architecture.
 

What is the future of computer engineering?

The computer engineering industry is quickly growing and is expected to increase by 22% from 2020 to 2030, with corresponding high salaries. Those interested in entering the computer engineering field should consider enrolling in a computer engineering, computer science, or software engineering degree program.
 

Is computer engineering 4 years?

The duration of B.E/BTech computer engineering course is 4 years.
 

Which is the best computer engineering course?

Introduction to Software Engineering by IBM, Computer Architecture by Princeton University, Master of Engineering in Computer Engineering by Dartmouth College are some of the best computer engineering courses.

What is the course of computer engineering?

Computer Engineering is a four-year course that deals with the study of computer systems. The Computer Engineering curriculum covers both software and hardware designs and develops the ability of the candidate to examine computer systems and the construction of electronic equipment.
 

What is the top salary for computer engineering?

The highest salary that a Computer Engineer can earn is INR 20,00,000 p.a. which is equal to INR 1,70,000 per month.
 

Which engineering is best for computer?

Some of the best computer engineering courses are data science, network engineering, information security engineering, embedded program engineering, and hardware engineering.

Which is better CS or CE?

The key difference between the two is that Computer Science is more theoretical and a better fit for people who enjoy doing research, analyzing, and strategizing, while Computer Engineering is more practical. It depends on the interest of the candidate and what he or she wishes to pursue.
 

Is Computer Engineering and B Tech same?

Computer engineering courses and BTech both are engineering courses but the major difference is computer engineering is a theoretical course while BTech is a technical course.
 

What is a computer engineering course?

Computer Engineering course helps candidates learn how to develop, prototype, and test microchips, circuits, processors, conductors and any other component used in computer devices or systems 
 

What is the best computer engineer salary?

The computer engineer average salary is INR 2,00,000 per annum to INR 15,00,000 per annum.
 

Is computer engineering a good course?

For those passionate candidates about computers and digital technology, a career as a computer engineer can be an outstanding option. 
 

What do computer engineers do?

Computer engineers make devices, develop computer networks and design new technologies. Computer engineers are concentrated on computer hardware and comprehending how to optimize hardware for other applications via an interpretation of how software interacts with hardware.
 

Do Google and Microsoft hire computer engineers?

 Yes. Google and Microsoft are some of the top recruiters of computer engineering graduates.
 

Which cities offer the highest salary for computer engineers in India?

Bangalore, Gurgaon, and Pune are amongst the top cities with the highest paying jobs in the field of computer engineering.
 

Is computer engineering good for government jobs?

Government organizations like DRDO, ISRO, ONGC, BARC, NIC, ECIL, etc. routinely hire computer engineers.

Who are the Computer Engineering top recruiters of IIT Bombay?

Microsoft, Google, Accenture, Amazon, and TechFest are some of the top recruiters who hire Computer Engineers from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay.
