BA English Course Overview

Bachelor of Arts in English is the full form of BA in English. A Bachelor of Arts (BA) in English is an undergraduate degree program that typically lasts three years. It helps students develop skills like communication, writing, and creative thinking. BA English is an undergraduate programme that focuses on English Literature, Cultural Studies, the History of English Literature, Modern Literature, and Rhetoric. BA English includes dramas, novels, and poems from various historical periods to debate and discuss the times in which they were written, as well as the author's motivation, cultural significance, literary acumen, and character depth. BA English syllabus include subjects such as Professional Communication, Communicative English, English poetry from Elizabethan to Augustan, English poetry from Pre-Romantic to Victorian, English poetry from Elizabethan to Victorian, English prose from Elizabethan to Victorian, English prose from Late Victorian to Modern, and so on.

BA English admission 2024 will be based on both merit and entrance exams. To be eligible for BA English admission, candidates must have passed the class 12th board exams with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks or equivalent from a recognised board. Also, the Delhi University will conduct BA English Admission 2024 based on the marks obtained by candidates in the CUET entrance exams and in 10+2. Top Colleges for BA English include Kirori Mal College, Delhi, Lady Shriram College for Women, New Delhi, Jadavpur University, St. Xavier's College, JNU Delhi, BHU Varanasi and Fergusson College, St. Stephen's College, Miranda House, and others.

Following their BA in English, students can pursue postgraduate studies, such as an MA in English. MBA courses are among the preferred options available to students. MBA graduates with a BA in English will be more qualified for managerial positions. Graduates from BA English programmes can work as content writers, proofreaders, journalists, professors, or English teachers. BA English salaries range from INR 3 to 10 LPA. Some of the top recruiters for BA English graduates are HT Media, NDTV, Indian Express Group, Hindu Group, India Today Group, and NDTV.

All About BA English

BA English is an undergraduate degree programme that includes core subjects like Indian Classical Literature, Popular Literature, and American Literature, as well as electives like Text and Performance, Language Literature and Culture, Media and Communication Skills, and so on. BA English is not limited to regular courses; students can also study it remotely.

What is BA English? 

BA English is an advanced degree programme offered by a number of universities and colleges that focuses on English literature, cultural studies, rhetoric, and literary history. It is a three-year undergraduate degree programme offered by several universities and colleges throughout the country. After completing their BA English courses, students can pursue further studies such as an MA or PhD in English.

BA English Admission Process in India

The majority of BA English colleges still select applicants via an entrance exam, which is then followed by a group discussion or interview. The admission procedures for BA English programmes in India are as follows.

  • Entrance-Based BA English Admissions
  • Merit-Based BA English Admissions

Although admissions procedures differ from college to college, the general steps to admission are outlined below. 

For Entrance Based BA English Admission

Candidates can apply for BA English courses by following the steps outlined below:

Step 1: Check into the application form requirements and deadlines listed by various colleges and universities if you want to an admission to a BA English programme.

Step 2: Register for the entrance exams required for BA English admissions at the colleges and universities you want to attend

Step 3: Once confirmed, save the application process for future use. Also, for admission and other important notifications, keep an eye on the exam or the university's official website

Step 4: After appearing and passing the BA English entrance examination, candidates must fill out an application form for their desired university with personal information such as academic details, entrance examination results, and so on

Step 5: Candidates who successfully meet the university's eligibility requirements are shortlisted for the counselling round (if any) to finalise the admissions process

Step 6: After clearing the counselling session, candidates must pay the university's entrance fee in order to be confirm their admission to the BA English course

For Merit Based BA English Admission 

Candidates can apply for BA English courses by following the steps outlined below:

Step 1: Fill out the online BA English application form

Step 2: Pay the registration fee and any additional fees if admission is granted based on merit

Step 3: The shortlisted applicants will receive invitations to the subsequent phases of the hiring process, such as individual interviews or counselling

Step 4: Candidates must submit all the required documents and fee in order to confirm their admission to BA English course

Courses After BA English Degree

Students who successfully complete their BA in English have the option to continue their education with an MA or PhD in English. The requirements for admission to the English MA and PhD programmes differ. Details about them are listed below.

MA in English 

MA in English is a two-year postgraduate degree course that students can pursue after completing BA English. Students must complete their graduation in a relevant field with at least 50% aggregate marks or an equivalent CGPA from a recognised university. The average fee for an MA in English ranges between INR 40k and 2L. Some of the top universities that offer MA in English courses include the University of Calcutta, Delhi University, JNU, and Jamia Millia Islamia. MA in English can also be pursued through distance education at colleges such as IGNOU, Osmania University, and Rabindrabharati University, among others.

PhD in English

The most popular option for students to pursue after earning an MA in English is a PhD in English. In India, a number of colleges and universities, including JNU, Calcutta University, Mumbai University, and others, offer PhD programmes in English. The following qualifications are needed to pursue a PhD in English:

  • Students should earn a master's or postgraduate degree in a relevant field from an accredited university with a minimum cumulative GPA of 50%.
  • A 5% relaxation of the total aggregate marks will be granted to students belonging to the SC/ST category.

MA in Journalism

Candidates who have finished a bachelor's degree programme in journalism or BA English can apply for a master's degree programme. Coursework and real-world journalism experiences put MA in Journalism candidates to the test. Students are taught how to build strategic communication skills, industrial competence, and enhanced writing and editing abilities throughout the curriculum.

MBA

MBA is a two-year postgraduate management program that focuses on human resources, finance, marketing, sales, and customer service administration. The purpose of this course is to generate qualified professionals who will be able to manage effectively in today's entrepreneurial environment. MBA graduates can work in a variety of industries, including fashion, marketing, education, and information technology. BA English graduates can opt to do their MBA from some of the top universities in India.

Admissions to the BA in English course will be based on the eligibility criteria as determined by the university or institution offering the course. However, general eligibility criteria for BA English require a minimum of 50% in the Class 12 board exams with English as a subject. Admissions to top universities would require applicants to present even significantly higher scores, especially in the English subject.

What are the jobs after BA English?

After completing a BA in English course, graduates can find employment in a number of sectors and industries. Some of the industries include publishing houses, journalism, media and advertising, web content writing and even teaching. Moreover, candidates can also take up short-term courses to enhance their skills and knowledge to widen their career prospects after BA English.

How much salary does a candidate with a BA English degree earns during the starting days?

Depending on the type of job role the graduate has selected or the company they wish to work at, a BA English graduate will be able to earn anywhere from INR 15,000 to 30,000 per month, initially. Candidates will be able to hike up their salaries afterwards with valuable experience and additional skills, which they can also achieve by pursuing online courses as well.

Are the teachings of great philosophers also taught in BA English?

Yes, students will be taught the teachings of some of the greatest philosophers in history, however, the philosopher in question and the type of teachings included would be determined by the university offering the course. How can I score good marks in BA English semester exams?

While there are different methods to score high in BA English semester exams, thorough study and research is the only method to ensure a good score. However, some tips to score high are: attempt the long answer questions first as they carry the most marks per question, attempt the easiest sections first and keep the most difficult for last, thus, allowing you to manage your time.

Is only English Literature taught during the entire duration of the BA English course?

No, English literature is not the only subject taught during the entire duration of BA English course. Universities also include subjects language and linguistic subjects focusing on the grammar and structure of writing and speaking English. In addition to this, there are a variety of English literature subjects along with elective subjects that can vary from psychology to physical education to complete as well.

What is the duration of the BA English programme?

Depending on the type of degree programme chosen, the duration of a BA in English course will vary from university to university. Can I become an IAS officer with a B.A English degree?

Yes, if you have done your B.A in English from any recognised university or institute, you are eligible to apply for the UPSC entrance exam and after qualifying the same, you can become an IAS officer.

Is BA in English an easy course?

Yes, BA in English course can be considered an easy course to complete since all it requires is dedicated studying of different books, novels, poems, prose and other types of English literature. Can I do Law after BA in English?
