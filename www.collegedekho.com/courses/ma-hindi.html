MA in Hindi Course

MA in Hindi is a two-year postgraduate degree programme in Hindi. It is a degree programme that brings together fundamental disciplines such as Hindi Poetry, Fiction, Drama, Literary Criticism, and Literary History. MA in Hindi students learn about Hindi literature, linguistics, and philosophy. The MA Hindi syllabus is divided into two years, with four semesters. MA Hindi subjects include Bhakti (devotional - Kabir, Raskhan), Shringar (beauty - Keshav, Bihari), Veer-Gatha (heroic warriors), Adhunik (modern), and others.

MA in Hindi stands for Master of Arts in Hindi, which is a humanities programme that studies Hindi language and literature. The MA in Hindi syllabus includes prose, poetry, history, and other relevant subjects with the objective of strengthening students' understanding of Hindi language and literature. For aspiring writers and linguists, an MA in Hindi is an interesting option. MA Hindi admission is based on merit as well as entrance exams such as the CUET PG, CUSAT CAT, and others. Candidates for MA Hindi admissions must have a bachelor's degree in any discipline from an accredited university with a minimum average score of 50%. MA in Hindi courses are offered full-time, part-time, and via distance learning. 

Many popular colleges and universities, including SNDT College, St. Stephen's College, Hindu College, Miranda House, Christ University, Loyola College, and others, offer MA courses in Hindi. The average MA in Hindi course fee is between INR 10k - INR 60k. Following the degree, graduates can pursue MA Hindi jobs such as content writing, editing, news reading, translating, professorship, and others. The MA in Hindi salary ranges between INR 2 and 5 LPA. Furthermore, candidates for a PhD in Hindi must also have an MA in the subject.

MA Hindi Admission Process in India

Candidates who meet the eligibility requirements can apply for MA Hindi admission 2024 at various colleges and universities. MA Hindi admission procedures differ between institutes. Some institutes offer admission to MA Hindi on a merit basis, while others hold an entrance examination to fill MA Hindi seats. The MA Hindi Admission Process has been thoroughly discussed in the section below.

  • Merit List: The applicants’ MA Hindi admission will be on the ground of marks secured in the previous examination or entrance test held by the university. Applicants must fall under the merit list for the selection process of MA Hindi Admission
  • Entrance Exam: All reputed colleges and universities conduct various entrance examinations for the candidates for MA Hindi admission process. Some of the popular entrance exams conducted for MA Hindi admission are CUET PG, CUSAT CAT, and others. Candidates must pass the respective entrance exam and proceed through the final round of counseling for seat allocation in order to get admission 

Candidates can also apply for MA Hindi admission in other private colleges. MA Hindi admissions are not as difficult in private institutes as they are in government institutes. Candidates can also pursue an MA in Hindi through distance education. MA Hindi distance education admission is not difficult. A variety of institutions, including Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU), offer MA Hindi degrees.

Most importantly, candidates seeking MA in Hindi admission 2024 must meet the eligibility criteria or be rejected.

MA Hindi Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet the mentioned eligibility criteria to be considered for MA Hindi admission:

  • The applicant must have secured a minimum aggregate score of 50% in the 12th class from any recognized board/institution
  • The applicant must be a graduate with a bachelor’s degree or equivalent in any stream
  •  It is mandatory to own a graduation degree from a college or university which is affiliated
  • Some colleges and universities hold entrance exams for MA Hindi course. Candidates must pass the entrance exam to be considered for admission.

Courses After MA Hindi

The following course can be pursued by candidates post MA Hindi. 

PhD in Hindi

Candidates with an MA in Hindi are also eligible to apply for direct admission to the PhD programme in Hindi. Candidates for the PhD in Hindi programme must have completed their MA in Hindi with an overall grade point average of at least 50% - 55%. Students must complete their PhDs in Hindi within three years. Students are given an additional two years if they are unable to complete their PhD thesis by the deadline. The duration of the PhD in Hindi course varies by college. Various entrance exams, including the UGC NET JRF, are held to grant admission to PhD programmes in Hindi. PhD in Hindi fees typically ranges between INR 20 K and INR 1.50 LPA. Candidates may choose to work as professors or in other professions like writing, editing, journalism, and so forth. Depending on the field and level of experience, a PhD in Hindi can earn you anywhere from INR 2 to INR 10 LPA on average.

FAQs about MA Hindi

Is it possible to become a teacher after completing an MA in Hindi?

Yes, after earning a Master of Arts (MA) in Hindi, it is possible to work as a teacher. If you had an MA in Hindi, you would be well-prepared to teach at all levels of education. Here are some possibilities:

High School Teacher: As a high school teacher, you will be able to teach secondary students in Hindi. Many schools require postgraduate degrees for teaching positions.

Professor in a College or University: An MA in Hindi will qualify you to work as a professor in a college or university. Typically, this includes conducting research, writing for academic journals, and teaching advanced courses.

Language Instructor: As a lecturer or language instructor, you could work in educational institutions or language schools. 

What are the benefits of pursuing Bachelor of Arts (BA) or Master of Arts (MA) in Hindi Honours?

A Bachelor of Arts (BA) or Master of Arts (MA) with honours programme offers a thorough immersion in language and culture, fostering advanced proficiency and specialised comprehension. In addition to enhancing research and cultural awareness, it provides BA students with a foundation in Hindi language, literature, and critical analysis. On the other hand, an MA enables even greater specialisation, advanced study, and employment opportunities in education, research, cultural preservation, and global engagement. Alumni of this programme are therefore greatly appreciated for what they have done to further and maintain the Hindi language and culture.

Which college offers the best MA in Hindi?

The following are a few well-liked universities for MA Hindi:

  • University of Delhi (DU):The Department of Hindi at University of Delhi (DU) offers Master of Arts degrees in Hindi with different areas of specialisation. DU is well-known for its humanities programmes.
  • Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU): The School of Language, Literature, and Culture Studies at JNU is well-known for its outstanding academic standing, and it offers an MA in Hindi. 
  • Banaras Hindu University (BHU): The Faculty of Arts at BHU, which is situated in Varanasi, offers an MA in Hindi. 
  • University of Mumbai: Through its Hindi Department, the University of Mumbai offers an MA in Hindi. 
  • Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI): JMI's Department of Hindi at Delhi University offers an MA in Hindi.
