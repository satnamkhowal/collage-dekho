BSc Home Science Course Overview

BSc in Home Science is a three year undergraduate programme that teaches students about the economics and management of running a home or building. The BSc Home Science is interdisciplinary, covering the fundamentals of math, physics, chemistry, and biology. Students study topics including nutrition, human development, home economics, textile and fashion design, extension education, health, environment, home management, and traditional culture in BSc in Home Science syllabus.  

BSc Home Science Admission is available through both an entrance exam and direct admission. The top entrance exam for BSc Home Science is CUET. Students who have completed Class 12 in any subject are eligible to apply for this course. Many colleges prefer a student who has studied science subjects in class 12 and scored more than 50%.

The BSc Home Science syllabus includes subjects such as Human Development, Communication Skills, Textile, Ethics, Extension Education, Family Dynamics, Child Behaviour, Food Science, and Food and Nutrition, among others. This course prepares students to become child care providers or health care workers. 

The best BSc Home Science colleges and universities include Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Lady Irwin College, the Institute of Home Economics, and others. BSc Home Science course fee usually varies between INR 10K and INR 1L. However, the BSc Home Science distance course fee varies between INR 15K and INR 55K. Candidates can pursue entry-level BSc in Home Science jobs after earning their degree, including hotel management, culinary arts, hospitality management, and other related fields. A Bachelor of Science in Home Science can earn between INR 2 - INR 6 LPA, depending on the candidate's qualifications.

BSc Home Science Eligibility Criteria

All applicants must fulfil requirements in order to be considered for a Bachelor of Science in Home Science programme. To pursue a BSc in Home Science degree, you must fulfil the criteria for admission listed below:

Admission to the BSc Home Science programme is determined by both merit based and an entrance exam. However, some Indian colleges do offer direct admission, or admission without taking an entrance exam, for BSc Home Science.
