B Pharma Course Overview

B Pharma Course prepares students for professional training in the field of Pharmacy. The course duration is 4 years. B Pharma stands for Bachelor of Pharmacy and eqips students with knowledge about the pharmaceutical world on an undergraduate level. Candidates who pursue B Pharma become eligible for B Pharma jobs like pharmacists, drug inspectors, research and development officers, and other important roles in the pharma sector. These graduates are well-prepared to take the healthcare sector to heights. 

B Pharma Admission is conducted mainly through common entrance and entrance examinations conducted by different colleges and universities. These include NEET, WBJEE, BITSAT, UPSEE, MHT CET, etc. However, some specific institutes also conduct merit-based admissions to B Pharmacy courses. TheB Pharma course eligibilityrequires candidates to complete their 10+2 with 50% marks in aggregate in the Science stream with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English as core subjects. 

The B Pharma Syllabus highlights some of the important aspects of the course curriculum, such as Drugs and Medicines, Medicinal Chemistry, Pharmaceutical Engineering, etc. Some of the best B Pharma colleges across India are Jamia Hamdard University, Jadavpur University, Sister Nivedita University, Oxbridge Group of Institutions, Himalayan Pharmacy Institute, etc. 

The minimum B Pharma course fees normally ranges between INR 40,000 to INR 1 LPA. However, the course fee for B Pharmacy may rise to INR 4 LPA depending on the type of institute and its location. While in India, this course offer various job roles such as Drug Inspector, Wholesaler, Pharmacist, etc., the job opportunities abroad also offer lucrative job prospects in B Pharmacy. The average B Pharma salary ranges between INR 5 LPA to INR 12 LPA.

Types of B Pharmacy Courses

For bachelor’s, there are mainly four types of B Pharma courses available to students, namely; Bachelor of Pharmacy, Bachelor of Pharmacy (lateral entry), Pharmacy (Hons.), and Bachelor of Pharmacy (Ayurveda).

  • Bachelor of Pharmacy ( B Pharma) - Students who want to learn about how medicinal medications affect our bodies and pursue a profession in pharmacy. Students need to complete their 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/ Biology as core topics in order to pursue a Bachelor of Pharmacy. The degree lasts four years and students study a variety of trade skills.
  • Bachelor of Pharmacy ( B Pharma Lateral Entry) - Students who have completed their Diploma in Pharmacy or D. Pharma and wish to pursue further studies in pharmacy are eligible for lateral entry to B. Pharmacy. The lateral entrance strategy permits a candidate to begin their B. Pharma degree in the second year. As a result, the course will take three years to complete.
  • Bachelor of Pharmacy ( B Pharma Hons.) - A Bachelor of Pharmacy (Hons.) is an undergraduate degree offered to people who have finished eight semesters of pharmacy school. B. Pharma (Hons.) provides students with experience in the field of pharmacy. Students are taught a variety of elements, technicalities, and norms related to pharmacy.
  • Bachelor of Pharmacy (B Pharma Ayurveda) - This is an eight-semester four-year undergraduate programme. Students in the Bachelor of Pharmacy (Ayurveda) programme learn about many areas of Ayurveda. Students study about healthy living, wellness, and well-being in addition to healthcare. Ayurveda is often known as "life science." The B Pharma course teaches students how to use lifestyle modifications and natural therapies to prevent and treat illnesses.

B Pharma Course Specialisations 

There are several B Pharma specialisations that a student can opt for in the field of Pharmacy. Therefore, check out the B Pharma course specialisation after 12th list mentioned below for reference:

  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry: In pharmaceutical chemistry, students are taught about the different aspects of drug design, testing, and development. This course specialisation also trains in the process of treatment and/or drug safety. Students also learn about biology, engineering, and technology principles that can be applied to the development of new procedures and processes.
  • Pharmaceutics: It studies areas related to pharmacies, such as biomedical analyses, pharmacokinetics, pharmacology, and pharmacodynamics, and is referred to as pharmaceutics. The course also deals with the development, design, distribution, and use of medications for disease prevention and relief.
  • Pharmacology: It studies pharmacological reactions based on biological principles. It supplements the investigation of medications' biological effects, chemical properties, and therapeutic applications.
  • Ayurveda: Students in this B Pharma specialisation learn about the pharmaceuticals and therapies used in the Ayurvedic system.

B Pharma Course Admission Process

How to get admission to a B Pharma course? B Pharma admissions are made based on both an entrance exam and a merit list. Students interested in pursuing a B Pharma degree must get the required percentage in 10+2 and the qualifying exams, which is 50%. Students can take either the national-level B Pharma entrance exam or state-level admission exams like the TS EAMCET, BITSAT, and WBJEE, among others.

B Pharma Admission by Merit List: The colleges/universities use the applicants' qualifying exam scores as the major criterion for admission.

B Pharma Admission through Entrance Exam: For admission to the B Pharma course, several colleges/universities administer an entrance exam. Admissions are granted based on the results of the candidates' B Pharma entrance exams.

State-Wise B Pharma Admissions

The state-wise B Pharma Course Admission process in India involves giving respective state entrance exams. The eligibility criteria for the above-mentioned state-wise B Pharma entrance exams are more or less similar to one another. Every state releases a dedicated B Pharma merit list comprising the names list of the eligible candidates. The counselling process for B Pharma Admissions is further conducted based on the respective merit lists and the entrance exam results. Thus, the qualifying cutoff score for the entrance test is the primary criterion determining the eligibility of candidates for state-wise B Pharma Admissions. B Pharma Course Study Abroad

Several countries offer B Pharma courses or Bachelor of Pharmacy programs. Some of the top places to study pharmacy are the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. The UK offers a four-year master's degree in B Pharma course, whereas the US offers a two-year B Pharma course/program. Some B Pharma scholarships are available to help with the B Pharma course fees.

Students having a B Pharma degree from another country can get high-paying jobs. The B Pharma course study abroad requirements involve students with a cumulative grade point average of 65 to 70 per cent. They must take B Pharma entrance exams such as the TOEFL, IELTS, and PT. 

After completing a B Pharma course, you can work as a military pharmacist, a veterinary pharmacist, or a hospital pharmacist, among other things.

B Pharma Course Abroad Eligibility Criteria

The requirements for B Pharma degrees vary by institution and country, but a strong background in chemistry is required, particularly in combination with biology, physics, and mathematics. 

You will be expected to have good grades in all of these areas, as well as a high level of proficiency in the language in which your programme will be taught because B Pharma degrees are often quite competitive. Basic B Pharma eligibility criteria for studying B Pharma at a foreign university vary from nation to nation. 

The following are the essential B Pharma eligibility criteria required:

  • In the class 12 exam, a minimum of 65 percent from a recognised board is required.
  • A total of 65-70 percent in five academic courses.
  • Language Proficiency Exams such as the TOEFL, IELTS, or PTE must be passed by the student.

B Pharma eligibility criteria for Language Proficiency Score Eligibility:

  • TOEFL: 80
  •  PTE: 58
  •  IELTS: 6.5

These are some of the essential requirements; however, the college may have additional B Pharma eligibility criteria as per their requirements.

FAQs about B Pharma

Is B Pharm course easy to study?

B Pharm is considered of moderate to high difficulty level. Subjects such as remedial mathematics and biostatistics sometimes prove to be difficult for certain students. However, if you're interested in pursuing the programme and possess the dedication for the same, you can surely complete the B pharm course with high scores.

What are the govt exams after B Pharma?

After completing the B Pharma course, candidates can appear for government pharmacist exams such as DHS/ DMER/ RRB /ESIC/ RBSK/ZP, etc. 

Which is the best field in pharmacy?

Aspirants can explore these fields after completion of B pharmacy course:

  • Hospital pharmacist

  • Community pharmacist

  • Primary care pharmacist

  • Researcher / academic

  • Government and NGO roles

Which B pharma company offers the highest salary?

Companies such as Dr. Reddy's Laboratories, Aurobindo, sun pharmaceuticals,  NATCO Pharma, Gland Pharma, Bharat Biotech, Cadila, and Aventis, are a few of the highest-paying pharma companies present in India.

What is B Pharma course Fees?

The B Pharma course fees vary depending on the college or school. The B Pharma course fee normally ranges between INR 40,000 to INR 4,00,000. (annually).

Are B Pharma graduates called doctors?

No, B Pharma is a completely different degree course. Individuals are not called doctors after completing their B Pharma studies.

Is B Pharm better than BSC?

B pharm is a great option if you want to pursue a career in the industry with R & D departments etc. On the other hand, BSC primarily focuses on pure science. Depending on aspirants’ interests, some find B Pharm better, others prefer BSC.

Does B Pharmacy have maths?

Most of the institutes include maths in their curriculum. However, it is not a compulsory subject. It can differ from 1 college to another.

Is Biology compulsory for B Pharm?

No, Biology is not mandatory. Candidates need to score 50% in class 12th with compulsory subjects like Chemistry, Physics, Biology/Mathematics.

What can I opt for after B Pharma?

When it comes to job opportunities, the candidates can opt for Drug Inspector, Pharmacist, Pharmaceutical marketing, Pathological lab scientist, Health inspector, Chemical/drug technician, medical devices and quality control manufacturing, Medical Underwriter, etc. For Higher Education, candidates can pursue Diploma in Pharmacy (D Pharm), Bachelor of Pharmacy (B Pharm), Master of Pharmacy (M Pharm), Doctorate in Pharmacy (Pharm D), etc. 
 

Is the Entrance examination compulsory for B Pharma?

Yes, some universities require an entrance exam for admittance to the B Pharma programme. Students must have taken science disciplines in high schools, such as physics, maths, chemistry, or biology, in order to study B Pharma. Students are accepted immediately into some colleges, like Annamalai University, depending on their Class 12 Boards test results. There are no entry examinations required at these schools.
 

Is B Pharma a good career?

B Pharmacy admittance allows for a rewarding profession for pupils with such high numbers. It is a four-year degree programme with eight semesters. You will be able to work in a variety of situations once you have earned your B Pharma degree. Patients, on the other hand, will be the ones who will be closest to you.
 

 

Can I do B Pharma after the 12th?

Yes, B Pharma courses are accessible after 12th grade. It is one of the most popular disciplines of study. A bachelor's degree in pharmacy is a four-year curriculum that prepares students for careers in science and medicine.
 

What is B Pharma?

The Bachelor of Pharmacy (B. Pharma) is a four-year undergraduate curriculum in which students learn about the methods and processes of medication preparation as well as how medicine and drugs are dispensed.
 

What is the scope of B.Pharm?

A student can join the pharmaceutical industry, cosmetics industry, herbal industry or a clinical research organization (CRO) in various departments such as production, quality control, R&D and marketing etc, after B.Pharm. Apart from this students can also join as a Hospital Pharmacist, Drug Inspector or Government Analyst and work as a part of the Healthcare team in various public or private sectors. Students can even start their own business after B.Pharm. Candidates can also pursue higher studies after B.Pharm like M.Pharm or MBA.

Is NEET score required for B.Pharm admissions?

It is not compulsory to appear in NEET for B.Pharm course. Most colleges/ universities/ institution provides admission to B.Pharm courses on the basis of the scores obtained in State-Level B.Pharm entrance tests or University-Level B.Pharm entrance tests.

What are the popular entrance exams for B Pharm?

PU CET, BITSAT, etc. are some of the popular entrance exams for B.Pharm course.

What are the eligibility criteria required to pursue a B.Pharm course?

In order to pursue the B.Pharm course, candidates need to pass Class 12th with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics/Biology as their main subject with minimum 50% aggregate marks.

Which is better B Sc Chemistry or B.Pharm?

B.Sc in Chemistry deals with studying Chemistry, while B Pharm is a graduate degree course related to Pharmaceuticals. B.Sc. Chemistry is better if one likes research, enjoys working in a lab, or wants to pursue higher studies in chemistry.

B.Pharm is better if you’re more interested in healthcare, pharmaceuticals, and direct involvement in patient care, drug development, or the pharmaceutical industry.

 

What is the salary that the student receives after the completion of B.Pharm?

The annual salary after B.Pharm that you receive, depends on the institute you have graduated from and increases with experience. After 2 to 3 years of experience, B.Pharm graduates can earn a package of Rs. 9 to 12 Lakh per annum.

What is the difference between D.Pharm and B Pharm?

D.Pharm is a diploma course of 2 years which the student can choose after their class 12th examination. After the completion of the degree, one can own a medical store or get a job as a pharmaceutical salesperson. B.Pharm is a better pharmacy education where you will be awarded a bachelor's degree and have more employment prospects in both government and private sectors, comparatively.

Is B.Pharm a good course to study?

Yes, after the 12th board exams, B.Pharm is one of the most popular career-oriented courses among science students. Throughout the course, students study about drugs, medicines, pharmaceutical, medical chemistry, etc. The course offers a wide range of jobs opportunities in both the public and private sectors.

I want admission in B.Pharma. Do I have to clear any exams for that ?

Karnataka Common Entrance Test , Common Entrance Examination AMPAI WB , Delhi University Arts , Uttar Pradesh State Entrance Exam , Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination are some of the exams that you can appear for to get admission in B.Pharma courses.
