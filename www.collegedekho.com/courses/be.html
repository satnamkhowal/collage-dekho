BE course, also known as Bachelor of Engineering, is a four-year undergraduate degree. It teaches students the engineering side of science- concepts, theories, principles, and applications. Students can choose any specialisation in BE, like Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, etc., and study important BE course subjects like Physics, Mathematics, Structural Analysis, and others depending on the chosen specialisation. BE course eligibility involves getting a minimum of 50%- 70% in Class 12 from a recognised board with Physics, Chemistry, and Maths as mandatory subjects.

BE Course Overview

BE course or Bachelor of Engineering is a four-year undergraduate degree course that concentrates on the study of engineering principles, concepts, and practices. The entire course is divided into eight semesters. It prepares students for a career in various engineering fields by providing them with a solid foundation of mathematical, scientific, and technical knowledge. A BE degree course is an academic degree achieved for undergraduate studies in one of the Engineering Sciences. The Bachelor of Engineering degree is a program that offers several areas of specialisation such as Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Science, Robotics, and Chemical.

Bachelor of Engineering Admission requires candidates to have completed their class 10+2 or equivalent from a recognised institute. According to BE course eligibility, Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics subjects are important in Class 12. Admission to BE courses can be done through merit as well as entrance exams. Some of the well-known entrance exams include JEE Main, JEE Advanced, KEAM, KLEEE, WBJEE, AP EAMCET, TS EAMCET, etc. Bachelor of Engineering syllabus covers core subjects, elective subjects, and lab subjects covering the entire duration. Though the subjects vary from university to university, the core BE subjects include Physics, Linear Algebra, Engineering Chemistry, Basic Electrical Engineering, etc.

In India, Bachelor of Engineering courses are delivered both offline as well as online/distance modes of schooling. Some of the top colleges offering these courses are the Indian Institute of Technology, Madras; Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay; Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur; Jadavpur University, and more. On average, the BE course fees for top colleges range from roughly INR 2,50,000 - INR 5,00,000. Some of the Bachelor of Engineering Jobs include Software Developers, IT System Managers, Software Engineers, etc. Graduates in this field earn an initial Bachelor of Engineering Salary of INR 3-12 LPA on average.

Why Choose a BE Course?

Bachelor of Engineering course scope is broad, with employment opportunities ranging from computer technology to teaching to the automobile sector. The BE course seeks to provide students with all of the necessary information to ensure their success. Following are the points which show why one should choose a Bachelor of Engineering course:

Perfect balance between work and personal life

A Bachelor of Engineering is one of the professions where one has the perfect blend and balance between professional as well as personal life.

Engineering requires the candidate to be creative

As engineering requires the creation and imagination in the work-life, inventing and discovering things, When a candidate opts for engineering, one has to be very creative and imaginative.

Chance to work with passionate people

Since engineering requires work with a lot of people i.e. teamwork, one has to collaborate with various heads inside and outside the field. 

Creating impression in the work field

Engineers are known for the best problem-solving attitude. Hence, when there is a situation of problem-solving, engineers are the ones to come forward with their mind-blasting ideas, creating an impression in the organisation. 

No boredom in the work field

Candidates who work as engineers in a particular organisation are often always busy and in a constant way discovering and stimulating their unique ideas. Therefore they are never bored with whatever they are doing.

Big salary earning

The engineers never earn a salary of fewer than five digits. The BE scope and salary of the candidate, in the beginning, are always impressive.

Job flexibility

There is job flexibility enjoyed by the individuals working as engineers in the work-life. For every BE specialization, there are various job options that an engineer can choose. Therefore there is always job flexibility chosen by the engineers.

Extensive opportunities

There are many opportunities faced by engineers all across the world. An efficient engineer can work as a designer of skyscrapers in China, on the other and as a designer of a water purifier in India.

BE Course Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must make sure they check the eligibility criteria of the Bachelor of Engineering course before applying for the course. The BE course eligibility in India is as follows -

Educational Qualification: Interested candidates should pass class 10+2 or equivalent from a recognised board.

Minimum Aggregate Marks: Interested candidates should secure at least 50% marks in aggregate with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. The minimum marks secured by the candidate can be as high as 70% depending on college to college.

Age Limit: Few colleges and entrance exams can have a restriction on age for all the aspirants who aspire to take admission in BE courses. Candidates should be at least 18 years of age. The maximum age of the candidate counted from the date of the examination should not be more than 24 years of age.

Note: Original copies of all necessary and important documents along with the mark sheets of the candidates have to be carried out at the time of admission at the chosen college or university that the candidate is interested in.

Bachelor of Engineering BE Course Entrance Exams

There are various entrance exams in India, subdivided into national, state level, and university levels for the benefit of the candidates. Some of the national-level Bachelor of Engineering entrance exams are JEE Main, and JEE Advanced while the state-level Bachelor of Engineering exams include WBJEE, MHT CET, AP EAMCET, and university-level exams include BITSAT, VITEEE, and SRMJEEE. Mentioned below are some exams that are related to admission for the Bachelor of Engineering course:

  • Joint Entrance Exam Mains [JEE Main] - The Joint Entrance Examination is conducted by NTA or the National Testing Agency of India. It is conducted for admissions to the National Institute of Technology, IIITs, Government Funded Technical Institutes, and other top institutions all over India.
  • Joint Entrance Exam Advance [JEE Advanced] - The JEE Advanced is conducted for admissions to IITs or the Indian Institute of Technology. Candidates who come under the ranks of 2.5 Lakhs are eligible to apply and sit for JEE Advanced. 

University Level Bachelor of Engineering Entrance Exam

The entrance exams which are related to the Independent University exams:

  • BITSAT - BITSAT is conducted by the Birla Institute of Technology and Science -Pilani for students who are interested in taking admissions in the Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad campuses. 
  • Vellore Institute of Technology Engineering Entrance Exam [VITEEE] - VIT is conducted by the Vellore Institute of Technology for candidates who are interested in taking admissions for the Vellore and the Chennai campus.
  • SRM Joint Entrance Exam [SRMJEE] - The SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination or SRMJEEE is conducted by the SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) for admission to Engineering programs offered by the institute. SRMJEEE is a national exam that acts as a common entrance examination for all SRM Group institutes and universities located at various places across India. You can also check What is a Good Score & Rank in SRMJEEE 2024.
  • Manipal Entrance Test [MET] - The Manipal Entrance Test (MET) is conducted by the Manipal Academy of Higher Education (formerly Manipal University) for admission to various programs at the Manipal Academy of Higher Education.
  • Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology Entrance Examination [KIITEE] - KIITEE is conducted by the Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) that provides admission at KIIT for various engineering and other courses

State-Level Bachelor of Engineering Entrance Exam

Some of the entrance exams conducted by the State:

  • Karnataka Common Entrance Test [KCET] - Karnataka Common Entrance Test or KGUCET is the entrance exam conducted by the Karnataka government for admissions into undergraduate courses
  • West Bengal Joint Entrance Exam [WBJEE] - WBJEE is conducted by the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination Board. It is conducted for admissions in undergraduate Bachelor of Engineering courses.  
  • Agriculture and Medical Common Entrance Test (AP EAPCET) - Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU), Kakinada on behalf of the Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE) conducts the AP EAPCET exam for admission to various professional courses offered in universities and private colleges throughout the state of Andhra Pradesh.
  • Maharashtra Common Entrance Test (MHT CET) - The MHT CET is conducted by the state common entrance test cell of Maharashtra. The exam is conducted based on class 11 and class 12 syllabi.

Bachelor of Engineering BE Course Admission Process in India

Bachelor of Engineering admissions is done via an entrance exam and on a merit basis. However, most of the engineering colleges of India offer admission via an entrance exam in which you have to sit and score a rank to qualify. Before applying for admission, make sure that you check the eligibility criteria. To know more about how to get admission into BE course, read the important details below. 

Step 1: Select the College

To get admission into BE course, first, you must shortlist some colleges to which you want to take admission. Visit their official website and check what is their admission process. Do they accept admission through an entrance exam or direct admission? If the college you prefer to study accepts students through direct admission fill out the application form and wait for the release of the merit list. 

Step 2: Check For the Entrance Exam

If the college you wish to pursue a Bachelor of Engineering course accepts admission via entrance exam, then check which entrance exam scores are they accepting. There are various national levels entrance exams such as JEE Main, which is accepted by NITs, and GFTIs. If your goal is IITs, then you have to qualify JEE Main + JEE Advanced exam. Many colleges offer admission based on state-level exams such as WBJEE, AP EAMCET, TS EAMCET, KEAM, and MHT CET. Other university-based entrance exams such as VITEEE, SRMJEEE, BITSAT, UPESEAT, etc

Step 3: Sit for the Entrance Exam

Once you are aware of how your desired Bachelor of Engineering college is accepting admission prepare for it. Qualifying for engineering entrance exams requires dedication and thorough exam preparation. Therefore, you should begin your preparation early, make a proper study plan, and start studying for the exam. Read the important exam day guidelines prescribed by the exam conducting authority and follow them. 

Step 4: Attend Counselling and Seat Allotment 

Once you have appeared in the entrance exam, wait for the release of the merit list. If your name appears on the merit list it indicates that now you are eligible for counselling and seat allotment. Sometimes these counselling processes are conducted by the exam conducting authority and sometimes by the respective college. Based on counselling and seat allotment, you will be allotted a seat in the BE engineering college. 

Step 4: Visit College Campus

After the college is chosen, you will have to visit the campus of the respective college to conduct the admission formalities

Bachelor of Engineering Merit-Based Admission

There are several engineering colleges in India like Dr. M.G.R. Educational and Research Institute, Chennai, PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, BMS College of Engineering, Bangalore, etc that offer admission to students based on their Class 12th marks. NRI and management quota seats are also available at such colleges. Yet, only 25-30% of seats are designated for such admissions. 

For engineering admission 2024-25 via merits, candidates must wait for the release of the application form. Once the application window for BE admissions is open candidates must complete the registration process, provide details about their Class 12th marks, and upload supporting documents. The college will contact you to schedule a counselling session. During the counselling, the institution will check the documents and certificates you provided with your application. After receiving the letter of confirmation, you can pay the course fees to finish the admissions process. Also, some universities offering merit-based BE admission ask for a donation fee at the time of application. 

BE Course Lateral Entry Admissions 

If you want to have a Diploma in Engineering you can apply for BE Lateral Entry. Through this, you will be admitted directly into the third semester of the BE programme, i.e. the second year. BE lateral entry admissions are mostly based on an entrance exam. State education authorities administer these admissions examinations, known as LEETs, on an annual basis.

Candidates for BE lateral entrance must have finished their bachelor's degree or diploma in engineering with a minimum aggregate of 45%. Candidates who fall into the reserved category will be spared from paying the application fee by 5%. Those with a DVoc (Diploma in Vocation) in relevant subjects are also encouraged to apply. There is no upper age limit for taking the LEET tests.

Direct BE Course Admission Process Without Entrance Exam

A few colleges of engineering offer direct admission into their programs for a Bachelor of Engineering without the need for an entrance examination. The rest of the process is left up to each college. However, the general steps for admission into such programs after you have chosen a specialisation may be as follows: 

  1. Look up colleges offering direct admissions to their programs for a Bachelor of Engineering. Look for eligibility criteria, fees, and more details about each college.
  2. Check out the dates of application: After finalising the list of colleges, check their dates and ensure that you submit the application within the given timeframe.
  3. Fill up the Bachelor of Engineering Admission Application Form: Fill in the application form at the college office on or before the deadline for submission. You need to provide all the documents needed like school results, identity proof, etc as required by the college.
  4. Wait for the decision of admission: you need to wait until your selection comes after you have provided your application and requirements for getting admitted to this college.

Where can BE course graduates find employment?

Government Organisations, Multinational companies, Construction, ISRO, Universities, and Laboratories are some of the potential employment areas for bachelor of engineering candidates.

What is B.E. Cutoff?

The cutoff of the Bachelor of Engineering is calculated due to the merit or entrance exam scores of a particular student. A cutoff list is prepared by the university after considering factors like the number of students who appeared for the exam, maximum marks gained, seats available and so on.

What is Bachelor of Engineering Application Process?

When the colleges open the application process, they will notify on their website. Students should start applying to their preferred universities. Based on the marks scored, other admission procedures will follow.
 

Is Bachelor of Engineering possible in distance education?

Yes, it is possible to pursue a Bachelor of Engineering through online sources but it will be considered invalid. As per the Supreme Court and the AICTE in India, candidates must pursue engineering through offline mode to get recognised in the industry.
 

Can I do Bachelor of Engineering online?

Currently, only a handful of engineering colleges in India offer full online degree courses in engineering. Several, though, offer certificate courses in online engineering technology - electrical, civil, mechanical, chemical, and electronics. Many major mainstay degree programs, though still require a class presence.

Does BE and BTech have the same value?

Yes, BE and BTech have the same value. Universities that provided engineering degrees alongside other degrees referred to them as BEs (Bachelor of Engineering), while institutions intended only for engineering studies referred to their degrees as B Techs (Bachelor of Technology). No degree, not even a BE or B Tech, has more value than the other.

Is BE and BTech same in India?

No, BE and BTech is not the same in India. An engineering degree is called a BE, whereas a technology degree is called a BTech. In terms of knowledge, BE is more oriented towards knowledge, while BTech is more oriented toward skills. BTech curriculums are consequently updated as skills change in response to industrial needs.

What is BE full form?

BE full form is Bachelor of Engineering, which is an undergraduate degree programme with an overall duration of four years that is further divided into eight semesters. As part of an engineering degree programme, students gain practical and theoretical knowledge of engineering components.
