MBA in Event Management Overview

MBA in Event Management is a 2-year postgraduate specialized degree course. It deals with different aspects of Event Management, such as planning, organizing, managing, budgeting, customer service, catering, logistics, etc. Students who pursue an MBA in Event Management course learn about the essentials of managing various events like parties, functions, weddings, seminars, festivals, games, political rallies, and more.

Those who enjoy the adrenaline rush that comes with the challenges and excitement while managing an event and love being in a creative process can go for an MBA in Event Management degree. Graduates in any discipline from recognized boards can apply for MBA in Event Management and learn everything right from conceptualizing to executing an event.

To get admission into top MBA in Event Management colleges, students need to qualify for entrance exams like CAT, MAT, GMAT, XAT, MAT, SNAP, etc. However, some colleges also offer direct admission on the basis of academic performance and other skills. Some of the top colleges offering MBA in Event Management courses include the Institute of Management & Entrepreneurship Development, PIMR, Sage University, Amity Global Business School, NMIMS, and Lovely Professional University.

The average MBA in Event Management fees ranges between INR 2,00,000 - INR 15,00,000. After graduation, one can easily find various job roles like Event Manager, Event Planner, Wedding Planner, Event Account Manager, Client Service Provider, etc. The starting salary for MBA in Event Management graduates is around INR 2.6 Lakhs per annum while at mid-level positions, one can expect a salary of about INR 3.6 Lakhs. Event Managers with an experience of 5 years or more can receive a salary of around INR 5.9 Lakhs per year. Get complete information about the MBA in Event Management course on this page.

Why Choose MBA in Event Management?

Students can choose to complete MBA in Event Management on the basis of the following arguments:

  • Exciting and Interesting Work: Event Management is definitely a stressful job area, however, it also provides the perfect adrenaline rush and excitement which is the selling point of the corporate culture. Additionally, there are often various creative and innovative angles involved to make any event successful. Hence, as graduates, students can rest assured to properly enjoy their job, which is often a point of concern for many professionals.
  • Scope of Exposure: In order to conduct an event, the number of areas that have to be contacted for outsourcing, supplying, and other organization areas of the event, allow the professionals to get exposure to varying areas of the industry. It allows them a chance to re-evaluate their decisions, future plans, and areas of professional advancement.
  • Interpersonal Skill Development: As a professional in Event Management, graduates need to interact with varying stakeholders, and in varying manners as well. The dynamic conditions help them face many challenges and at the same time develop important interpersonal skills, such as communication, delegation, proper directing, negotiation, contract, and terms conditioning, understanding market forces, etc.
  • Networking Scope: As the job responsibilities put graduates to connect and contact with varying people and stakeholders, and on different levels of hierarchy, they manage to create a wide network of personal and professional connections. Such networking is not only useful for career development but also in handling varying personal life responsibilities as well.
  • Growth Scope: The scope of growth is simply unlimited in Event Management. The industry is quite organized but dynamic at the same time. Graduates will find opportunities and risk-induced growth factors at almost every stage of their careers. They can use the same to boom their professional career or even set up their own business.

Types of MBA in Event Management Courses

There are various types of courses available for an MBA in Event Management. These can be bifurcated and understood on basis of program delivery and course duration:

Full-time MBA in Event Management Course

These courses shall last for up to two years and are conducted on campus. The course curriculum is extensive and has a proper balance of both theoretical and practical concepts.

Part-time MBA in Event Management Course

These courses conduct on-campus classes but in half duration. That is the studies do not last the whole day or for eight to ten hours, instead only three to four hours. The course duration may get extended to over four years, depending on students' pace of completing their credits.

Online and Distance MBA in Event Management Course

Online and distance education courses are specially designed to aid students in completing their education while continuing with their general lifestyle. They can attend the classes anytime during the course as they are conducted digitally via video conferencing and recordings.

One-year MBA in Event Management Course

There are various b-schools that offer one-year courses in MBA in Event Management rather than two years courses. The subjects and topics are covered similarly, however, the theoretical portion dominates the course study. In fact, 12 months of the 15 months duration are dedicated to theoretical studies and the remaining for practical project work or internships, as the case may be.

Institutes/ colleges providing admission into the MBA in Event Management will sort candidates on the basis of the scores in the entrance exam. There are many entrance tests conducted for management admission in the country.

How to Get Admission for MBA in Event Management?

In order to get admission to MBA courses, including various specializations, students need to clear a three-step admission process. Similarly, to get admission to MBA in Event Management programs, the same process needs to be completed to get enrollment in the desired b-school.

The overall process of admission can be followed by the students as described below:

Step 1: Shortlist the b-schools to apply for and check out the entrance exam scores accepted by each of them.

Step 2: Select the most common entrance exam, whose scores are accepted by the majority of shortlisted institutions. Apply for all the relevant entrance exams and prepare for them.

Step 3: Attempt the exams and use the scores to clear the cut-offs mentioned by the b-schools shortlisted.

Step 4: Apply to all the shortlisted b-schools whose cut-offs are satisfied along with the previous academic records, personal statement, student CV, and any additional documents required by the respective school.

Step 5: Await the invitation to attend the Group Discussion (GD) rounds of the applied b-schools.

Step 6: Attend GD rounds and as per the instructions of the respective b-school appear for the personal interview session.

Step 7: Await the confirmation letter or college acceptance letter, and accordingly enroll in the respective b-school.

FAQs about MBA Event Management

Are there internship opportunities in integrated MBA?

Yes, there are internship opportunities between the fourth and fifth years of an integrated MBA. Students get to work with industry players and learn valuable lessons about the real-life functioning of the business world. IIMs make students involve in social internships in the second year of the course. 

Is it possible to leave the BBA MBA course after three years?

Yes some BBA MBA programmes come with an exit option. Students can leave the integrated MBA programme at the end of the third year and will be awarded only a BBA degree. ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education (Deemed-to-be University), Hyderabad is one such institution that allows students to exit. You must check in with the institutions about exit after 3 years before enrolling. 

What are the employment areas for BBA MBA graduates?

The employment areas for BBA MBA graduates are banking and finance, management consulting, hospitality sector, healthcare sector, marketing firms and other top multinational companies. Several other industries require BBA MBA graduates for smooth functioning.

Is mathematics important for the BBA MBA course?

No, there is a hard and fast rule that mathematics is important for the BBA MBA course. However, the entrance exam as well as the integrated programme has accounting and other finance-related chapters that require knowledge of mathematics. Therefore proficiency in mathematics will be fruitful. 

Can students of the science stream apply for MBA-integrated courses?

Yes, students of the science stream can apply for MBA-integrated courses. Students from all the streams are eligible to take up this course provided they have 60% in their higher secondary. Also qualifying for the entrance exams is important. 

Is BBA+MBA costly?

Yes, BBA+MBA is an extremely costly course as the course expands for five years and every year they need to pay a huge amount of money. The course fees range from INR 20 to 30 lakhs to support the course.  

Are the subjects repeated in BBA+MBA?

No, the subjects and curriculum are not repeated in BBA+MBA. Every year, students get to study new modules. The first three years focus on foundations while the next two build business skills.  In the traditional separate BBA and MBA courses, students may need to repeat some subjects. 

 

Which are some of the recommended books for BBA+MBA entrance exams?

Some of the recommended books for BBA+MBA entrance exams are  Modern Microeconomics by Koutsoyiannis Business Mathematics by Sharma,  Fundamentals of Management, Essentials Concepts and Applications by Robbins, I.M. Fundamentals of Database Systems by Elmasri and Navathe and J.K. Management Accounting by Pandey.

What is the admission procedure for BBA+MBA?

The admission procedure for BBA+MBA includes submitting online applications to colleges with the necessary documents. Then they have to appear for an entrance exam where their proficiency in English,  Quant and Reasoning will be analysed. Additionally, students will have to appear for a personal interview.

What should the students be careful of before studying BBA+MBA?

The students should be careful of the following few pointers before studying BBA+MBA:

  • Applicants must be sure of their long-term goals as they will not have a chance to change their career paths.
  • They must select universities that are renowned and have a good scope of employment.
  • One should also understand the curriculum. 

What are the skills required for MBA Event Management?

The skills required for MBA Event Management are excellent client relationship management, decision-making and problem-solving, creativity and innovation, management of budgets, time management, practical understanding and behaviour, understanding of business and a cool temper to handle pressure and unwanted situations. 

What are the areas of recruitment for MBA Event Management graduates?

The areas of recruitment for MBA Event Management graduates are hotels, the hospitality sector, family events, the healthcare department, the manufacturing industry, product-based companies, facility management services, event management companies, IT companies, government sectors, etc. 

What are the job profiles after MBA Event Management?

The job profiles after MBA Event Management are Event Production Manager, Event Promotion Manager, Media Planning Manager, Marketing Manager, Promotion and Advertisement Officer, Project Management Director, Technical Events Manager, Senior Client Service Manager and Area Events Manager. There are several other job roles related to event supervision, management and finances available. 

Do we need to submit project reports for MBA Event Management?

Yes, it is necessary to submit project reports for MBA Event Management after the internship is complete. Students have to write down their experiences regarding the internship opportunity and the valuable lessons learnt during that time.

What are the elective subjects in MBA Event Management?

The elective subjects in MBA Event Management are HR Planning and Development Management of Industrial Relations Compensation, Industrial Marketing Consumer Behaviour, Retail Marketing Management, International Marketing Advertising and Sales Management, E-Commerce and Enterprise Resource Planning Strategic Information Systems, and Design Communication Networks For Business. 

Which teaching methodologies are used in MBA Event Management?

The teaching methodologies that are used in MBA Event Management are problem-based learning, co-curricular activities, field trips, lectures, projects, thesis writing, workshops, e-learning, discussions and internships. It can be said that MBA Event Management merges both classroom and real-world teachings. 

Is internship compulsory in MBA Event Management?

Yes, an internship is compulsory in MBA Event Management. It is generally done in the second and fourth semesters for a few weeks which gives them the right kind of exposure and helps them build networks. Students get to learn negotiating, organising, planning, managing and other related skills.

What are the top entrance exams for MBA Event Management?

The top entrance exams for MBA Event Management are CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT, ATMA and SNAP. There are other state-level entrance exams like MAH MBA CET and institute-specific tests which students can opt for if they are targeting a particular institution. 
 

Can I get direct admission for MBA Event Management?

Yes, you can get direct admission for MBA Event Management. Though most colleges in India prefer an entrance-based admission route, some colleges accept students based on their academic profile in 10th, 12th and graduation. Work experience is an additional bonus in case of direct admission. 

Is work experience necessary to study MBA Event Management?

No, it is not necessary to have work experience to study MBA Event Management. It can be easily studied by freshers who do not have exposure to a professional environment. Some business colleges might give some extra points to those having work experience but most of them would not consider it as discredit. 

Is mathematics mandatory for MBA Event Management?

No, mathematics is not mandatory for MBA Event Management. However, the knowledge of mathematics cannot be undermined as event management includes budgeting and finance handling related to events. It is easier to handle those aspects if individuals have prior knowledge of the subject. 

What are the basic eligibility criteria for MBA Event Management?

The basic eligibility criteria for MBA Event Management is graduation in any stream with at least 50% marks. Reserved candidates can avail of 5% relaxation on marks. Candidates who are in the final year of their graduation are also eligible to apply. 

How do MBA in Event Management professionals earn money?

MBA in Event Management professionals earns money by charging 20-25% of the overall event cost. The amount depends on the scale, size and budget of the event. They can set contracts with huge companies that will give regular contracts for events and earn a sizeable amount of money. 

Is MBA in Event Management high in demand?

Yes, MBA in Event Management is high in demand with an increase in corporate events, festivals, live functions and exhibitions increasing. This leads to a demand for services provided by event managers and the future years will see an upward trend in this industry. 

Can I graduate with an MBA in Event Management from IGNOU?

Yes, you can graduate with an MBA in Event Management from IGNOU. Indira Gandhi National Open University offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programmes on a distance learning basis. You can apply for MBA in Event Management course offered by the university and can use the same to apply for jobs as reputed and successful as with on-campus programmes.

Skills That Make You The Best Event Manager

The interpersonal skills of a successful event manager is governed by an attractive demeanour, the convincing and persuasion skills, and the art of negotiating with diplomacy to procure things within the client’s budget. MBA in Event Management also requires you to attract clients through a presentable conversation that can quickly attract their confidence in the event management services of the company. 

A skilled event management professional must have the qualities to uniquely design and structure events for various clients. Here are some of the skills that individuals should inculcate to emerge as successful event managers and entrepreneurs.

  • Financial Knowledge: It is necessary to learn how to optimise the financial resources so that the events can be planned according to budget and the financial risks can be warded off.
  • Public Relations: It is indispensable for pulling off any event. It is necessary to maintain a balanced relationship with the client and the audience for the event to create a harmonious atmosphere.
  • Business Acumen: It is extremely necessary to be part of big organisations or to start an entrepreneurial journey of their own. They must know to channelise the available financial and material resources in the most profit-earning manner. 
  • Digital Literacy: Digital knowledge helps to make intelligent moves in the operations from the ideation of plans to their implementation.
  • Marketing Expertise: The events aim to popularise them among people. This requires careful branding and presentation through market research and assessment of market trends.
