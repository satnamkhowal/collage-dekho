Master of Science (MSc) Course Overview

The MSc full form is Master of Science. The Master of Science (MSc) is a two-year postgraduate programme that provides in-depth knowledge and specialisation in a specific field of study. To be eligible for an MSc admission, candidates must have a Bachelor of Science (BSc) with an aggregate of 50% to 60% from an accredited college or university. The course fee of an MSc course usually ranges between INR 10k and INR 1.50LPA. The CPGET, ICAR AIEEA, CUET PG, MCAER CET, AP PGCET, IIT JAM, URATPG, MAH CET, and other entrance exams are often held for MSc courses. Banaras Hindu University, Bharathiar University, Calcutta University, Delhi University, and other well-known universities are among those that provide MSc degrees in India.

Universities and colleges offering science-related MSc specialisations include those in Physics, Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, Botany, Biotechnology, Microbiology, Environmental Sciences, Food Sciences, Life Sciences, and so forth. Business, Social Sciences, Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, and Science are all covered in the MSc syllabus. In India, there is also an MSc in Distance Education. Colleges offering MSc Distance Education accept applicants directly. The fee of an MSc distance education course ranges from INR 10K to 40K. 

Following the course, candidates may select MSc jobs such as Project Assistant, Lab Manager, Pharmaceutical Associate, Regulatory Affairs Specialist, etc. Top recruiters of MSc degree holders include Ranbaxy, GAIL, Bhabha Atomic Research Centre, and BARC. After completing a Master of Science programme, the typical MSc salary ranges from INR 5 LPA to 15 LPA.

Why Choose an MSc Course?

MSc courses may prove advantageous in the long run, despite being difficult, costly, and psychologically taxing. A Master of Science degree opens up many career options in both public and private sectors, such as research, academia, medical labs, biotechnology companies, food institutes, and more.

Here are some of the reasons why you should pursue an MSc:

  • MSc programmes usually provide comprehensive and specialised knowledge in a specific field. 
  • Professional Growth: Getting an MSc degree can open up new job opportunities and paths. 
  • Research and Innovation: If you want to learn more about your field and conduct research, an MSc can allow you to conduct original research, publish papers, and contribute to creative solutions.
  • Greater Earning Potential: Having an MSc degree can translate into a higher earning potential in many industries than having only a bachelor's. 

Candidates with an MSc degree have a great opportunity to apply for research and technical positions at many top institutes and companies known for their R&D and innovations. This is due to numerous advances in the science and engineering industries. A Master of Science degree can help you advance in your career. Learning in one field can lead to a career path of one's choice in another. People can pursue a Master of Science in health facilities after working in the field for a few years or right out of a Bachelor of Science programme.

Lastly, MSc postgraduates have a lot of opportunities, and salaries vary depending on the domain and sector. There are numerous opportunities available for students in both the private and public sectors. MSc graduates can expect to earn a handsome salary of INR 5 - INR 10 LPA.

Master of Science Entrance Exams in India

There are many entrance examinations conducted at national level and university level for taking MSc admission. The students must know about the different types of entrance examination conducted for Master of Science in a variety of streams. Some of the popular entrance examinations required for taking Master of Science admission are listed below. 

CUET- The Central University Entrance Exam (CUET), a national entrance exam for admission to undergraduate programmes offered by all participating universities, is administered by the NTA.

IIT JAM- The Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs), which are a part of the Ministry of Human Resources Development (MHRD), alternately administer the Joint Admission Test for MSc (JAM). The Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, as well as other postgraduate programmes at IITs, provide integrated PhD programmes. IIT JAM is a competition held to admit students to these programmes.

JNUEE- Jawaharlal Nehru University Entrance Examination is conducted annually and it is a university level examination. This entrance examination is helpful for the students who are willing to pursue any course in Master of Science from prestigious JNU. However, for JNU 2024 admission, CUET PG will be considered now. 

BHU PET- Banaras Hindu University Postgraduate Entrance Test entrance examination is conducted by Banaras Hindi University for postgraduate programs in multiple courses including Master of Science. Note: for BHU admission 2024, CUET PG will be considered. 

BITSAT- It is a National level entrance examination conducted by Birla Institute of Technology and Science Admission Test for admission to various courses including Master of Science.  

TISS NET- Tata Institute of Social Sciences National Entrance Test is conducted annually by the institute for taking admission in postgraduate courses. Master of Science is one of the prominent courses being offered by TISS. The test is conducted online for 100 minutes where students need to attempt 100 questions.  

CUSAT CAT- CUSAT CAT is the abbreviation for the Cochin University of Science and Technology Common Admission Test. It is held for programmes in Engineering, Environmental Studies, Humanities, Law, Marine Sciences, Medical Sciences & Technology, Science, Social Sciences, and Technology.

NEST- The National Entrance Screening Test (NEST) is jointly administered by the National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER), Bhubaneswar, and Mumbai University's Department of Atomic Energy Centre for Excellence in Basic Sciences (UM-DAE CEBS), Mumbai.

Master of Science Admission Process in India

The MSc course admission is done in two ways. The MSc admission process is detailed below for candidates.

  • Merit-Based Admission: Admission to the course is based solely on the candidate's performance in the qualifying examination. Some colleges also create a cutoff list based on the qualifying examination for admission.
  • Entrance Based: Admission to the course is based on the candidate's performance in a national, state, or college level entrance exam. Admission is done through the counselling process.

Steps to apply for MSc Course are listed below:

Step 1: Application Form - Candidates must complete the required application form for the MSc course.

Step 2: Take the Entrance Exam - Following the application process, candidates must sit for the relevant entrance exam. In the case of programs that offer direct admission, this step can be skipped.

Step 3: Making a merit list - Based on the results of the entrance exam or other qualifying exam, merit lists will be made by admissions officials.

Step 4: Seat Allocation - The candidate's merit rank will be taken into consideration when allocating seats. It should be noted that if there are multiple participating institutes, the candidate may be asked to choose their preferred academic institution during counseling and seat allocation.

Direct MSc Admission Process Without Entrance Exam

At most colleges and universities, admission to MSc programmes is based on merit. Candidates for the MSc course programme will be accepted based on their performance in a written 10+2 exam, a series of in-person interviews, and/or a relevant qualifying exam. In such cases, candidates are typically shortlisted based on their academic record as well as their performance in a previous relevant exam.

MSc Syllabus will vary on the basis of specialization opted by the student. As there are many specializations under Master of Science, the MSc syllabus for the same will be dependent on the selection of the MSc subjects under a particular specialization. The syllabus of MSc normally includes core MSc subject list and elective subjects related to the course opted. The core MSc subject list are compulsory subjects and add value to the specialization while elective MSc subjects list include the important aspects of any specialization. All the compulsory core MSc subjects have both theoretical and practical aspects.

FAQs about MSc

What are the advantages of an MSc?

A Master's degree is an excellent way to gain expert knowledge in this field. This also opens up a plethora of job opportunities that will help you secure your future and earn a higher salary. An MSc in Computer Science also qualifies you for teaching positions in colleges and other higher education institutions.
 

What specifically are MSc subjects?

A Master of Science (MSc) is a two-year postgraduate degree in specialised science fields such as Chemistry, Mathematics, Physics, Biology, Botany, Life Sciences, Bio-Technology, and Microbiology.
 

Is there a job after an MSc?

One of the best career paths for science students is to become a professor or academician. It is an excellent field for training the next generation and imparting the necessary skills to become tomorrow's leaders. Furthermore, becoming a lecturer is one of the most popular jobs available after completing the MSc.
 

Is an MSc a qualification?

Yes, an MSc is a prestigious qualification that is recognised globally and is a'second cycle' degree.
 

Is NEET required for MSc?

Many high-paying medical specialisations, particularly BSc/MSc and BTech courses, can be pursued without NEET scores. Paramedical courses are also ideal for those who want to study medicine without taking the NEET or the MBBS.
 

Is an MSc the same as an MBA?

The MBA is aimed at those who have dabbled in the professional world, whereas a specialised master's degree - such as an MSc or a MIM (Masters in Management) - is aimed at pre-experience candidates who may have only recently completed an undergraduate degree.
 

Can we do MSc after the 12th?

No, an MSc (Master of Science) cannot be pursued after Class 12. To be eligible for an MSc programme, one must have a BSc degree. However, after Class 12, integrated MSc courses (BSc + MSc) are available.

What exactly is an MSc degree?

An MSc, or Master of Science, is the European equivalent of the American MS degree, also known as Master of Science. Both are STEM degrees, with a focus on science, engineering, technology, mathematics, or medicine. In addition, some MSc programmes concentrate on social sciences.
 

What is MS's salary?

Master Student salaries in India range from INR 2 Lakhs to INR 13.6 Lakhs, with an average annual salary of INR 3.3 Lakhs based on 260 recent salaries.
 

Is MS easier than MBA?

There is no comparison between the difficulty of an MBA and a master's degree because both degrees are suitable for students based on their aptitude. If you have some professional work experience, an MBA degree will be easier to understand than a master's degree.
 

Can I pursue an MSc after completing my 12th?

No, after Class 12, you cannot pursue an MSc (Master of Science). To be eligible for an MSc course, one must have a BSc degree.
 

What exactly is a Masters of Science?

Masters of Science degrees concentrate on subjects such as Sciences, Engineering, Technology, Mathematics, Medicine, and some Social Sciences such as Finance, Psychology, Sociology, and others. A scientific thesis is usually required to earn an MSc degree, but this depends on the type of study programme.
 

 

Which is better, MS or MSc?

These are various abbreviations for a Master of Science degree. MSc is more commonly used in the United Kingdom and Europe. In the United States, MS is the preferred form. The abbreviation SM stands for Scientiae Magister, which translates to Master of Science in English.

Is there jobs options in abroad after pursuing MSc degree?

Yes, there are many job options in abroad for MSc degree candidates. Some of the job options are as follows - auditor, administrator, research scientist, marketing communication coordinator, etc.

 

What are some of the job roles available for students with an MSc degree?

The job role depends on the MSc specification that a candidate holds. Some of the general job roles are Science teacher, Lab Researcher, Clinical Specialist, Taxonomist, Chemist etc.

 

What is the salary of a candidate with an MSc degree?

The entry-level salary of a fresher with an MSc degree is between Rs. 3 lakh to Rs. 4 lakh per annum.

 

What are the popular MSc specialisations?

MSc Physics, MSc Chemistry, MSc Biology, MSc Botany, MSc Mathematics, MSc Zoology, MSc Nursing, MSc Computer Science, MSc Data Science are some of the popular MSc specialisations.

 

What are the advanced courses available after pursuing MSc course?

The advance course available after doing MSc course are as follows- Double Masters- In order to enhance better job opportunities and salary packages, many students opt for double masters and they mostly go for either a management program or opt for Masters in Technology as there are more job opportunities after doing MTech, Master of Philosophy (MPhil)- The students who are keen on doing research and get an additional degree post-graduation often look out for Master in Philosophy after doing Master of Science. MPhil enables better career opportunities in academics and research. Earlier, students used to complete their MPhil before pursuing Ph.D., Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)-After completing Master of Science, many students want to pursue PhD as it enables them to make their career as professor and they can teach in leading colleges and universities of India.

 

What are the top MSc colleges in India?

There are many MSc colleges  in India. Some of the top universities are as follows - JNU, BHU, University of Hyderabad, St. Thomas, Chandigarh University, Jain University, etc.

 

What is the average MSc fee for the MSc course?

The average MSc fee is 40K per annum.

What is the duration of the MSc course ?

The duration of the Master of Science course is 2 years.

What is the full form of MSc?

The full form of MSc is Master of Science.

Master of Science is a two year postgraduate program which offers advanced knowledge in a number of specialized science fields such as physics, chemistry, biology, botany, mathematics, zoology, nursing, pharmacy.
