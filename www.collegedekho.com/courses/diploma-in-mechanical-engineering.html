Diploma in Mechanical Engineering Overview

Diploma in mechanical engineering is a three-year course programme that deals with applications of Physics, manufacturing, analysing, designing, dealing, and applying all the basic principles of machines. The mechanical diploma course provides students with in-depth knowledge and necessary skills that allow them to choose various career options after the completion of the course. This course can be pursued by the candidates after class 10. Further, students who want to pursue a diploma mechanical will have to secure at least 50% marks in class 10. However, a relaxation of 5% is given to the candidates belonging to the reserved categories. 

Admissions to diploma in mechanical engineering courses are done through various entrance examinations conducted by the state examination board. After the completion of the course, candidates can opt for BTech in mechanical engineering in the second year according to the lateral entry procedure. In this course, students will undertake diploma mechanical engineering subjects such as Engineering Mechanics, Engineering Mathematics 1, Engineering Drawing 1, and so on. The top colleges for diplomas in mechanical engineering are Lovely Professional University, Amity University, Chandigarh University, BFIT Group of Institutions, etc. After the completion of the course, students can earn a starting salary of between INR 6 LPA and INR 10 LPA. To get more diploma in mechanical engineering course details, continue reading below.

Diploma in Mechanical Engineering Entrance Exams

The diploma in mechanical engineering entrance exams includes Delhi CET, TS POLYCET, AP POLYCET, JEECUP, DCECE, DEECET, Jharkhand PECE, Punjab PET, MP PPT, HSTES DET, Odisha DET, etc. 

  • JEXPO - The West Bengal State Council Of Technological and Vocational Education and Skill Development conducts a state-level examination named JEXPO or Joint Entrance for Polytechnics West Bengal. This entrance exam is conducted in an offline pen-paper mode and is objective in nature.
  • AP POLYCET - The AP POLYCET is an objective-based entrance examination that is conducted by The State Board Of Technical Education & Training for admission to various diploma engineering courses. The students aspiring to get admission to top colleges in Andhra Pradesh must take this exam.
  • JEECUP - JEECUP or the  Joint Entrance Examination Council Of Uttar Pradesh is a state-level examination which gets conducted by The Uttar Pradesh Educational Council. This examination is conducted in an online mode where students get 2 hours and 30 minutes to solve 100 objective-type questions.

Distance Diploma in Mechanical Engineering Admission

For the Distance Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, entrance exams are not required by most institutes. Admission is based on the marks obtained in the 10th-level board exam. Details of the various diplomas in Mechanical Engineering programs can be found in the following section.

IGNOU Diploma in Mechanical Engineering 

  • The Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) offers a Distance Diploma in Mechanical Engineering Course for people who have completed their 10th grade with 55% marks or have passed 10th grade with ITI. 
  • You can also apply for this course if you have passed 12th grade in physics, chemistry, and maths or if you have relevant experience in the manufacturing industry. 
  • The duration of this course ranges from a minimum of 3 years to a maximum of 6 years, and the course fee is INR 12,500. 
  • Anyone can apply for this course, as there is no age limit.

IIME Diploma in Mechanical Engineering 

  • The IIME Distance Diploma in Mechanical Engineering is a three-year program that you can enroll in after completing either your 10th or 12th exams. 
  • If you have passed your 10th, you need to have three years of work experience, and if you have passed your 12th, you need one year of work experience. 
  • The course fee for this program is INR 60,600.

Neptune Institute of Management and Technology Diploma in Mechanical Engineering 

  • This particular course lasts for three years. 
  • To be eligible for the course, candidates must have passed their 10th grade or an equivalent examination. 
  • Additionally, the candidates must have a minimum work experience of two years. 
  • The fee for the course is INR 8600.

Career Options and Job Prospects after Diploma in Mechanical Engineering

There are several career options after one pursues a mechanical engineering diploma. Such profiles which are in demand are  Mechanical engineers, locomotive engineers, and automobile engineers. The following table highlights a few career options and job prospects after completing mechanical diploma course:

Job ProfileJob Description
Mechanical EngineerMechanical engineers are capable of designing different types of manufacturing parts, used in vehicle machinery such as trucks, cars, etc. Supervising a team of engineers to make sure the final product reaches the specifications and requirements.
Mechanical ConsultantA mechanical engineering consultant is an engineer who advises customers on mechanical engineering projects. Mechanical engineers develop and maintain power-generating machinery such as generators, turbines, air conditioners, heaters, and internal combustion engines.
Locomotive Engineer A locomotive engineer operated large trains on specific routes that involve transporting of passengers and different industrial goods to various places. Their expertise is involved in supervising sensors and qualifiers, used for evaluating different factors such as voltage, temperature, and regulation. 
Automobile EngineerAn automobile engineer designs and prepares drawings and products and makes modifications to them. They also conduct research and develop designing components regarding vehicles and the procedures used in their testing. Automobile Engineers are responsible for various works of manufacturing, testing, and product development in the automobile industry.
Industrial EngineerIndustrial engineers create employment evaluation programs. Industrial engineers look for solutions to reduce waste in manufacturing operations. They create efficient systems that combine employees, machines, materials, information, and energy in order to manufacture a product or offer a service.

Upcoming Trends in Mechanical Engineering

The field of mechanical engineering is constantly variable and experiencing a lot of ups and downs. Constant development is how mechanical engineering operates. As time passes, many upcoming trends are yet to be noticed in mechanical engineering by mechanical engineers. the latest upcoming trends in the line of mechanical engineering have been discussed below:

  • 3D printing- The feature of 3D Printing requires the use of CAD software, also referred to as additive manufacturing. The 3D printing process involves slicing the object into ultra-thin slices, and then transferring them into a printer for printing them into fine plastic layers, each layer bonding with the preceding layers. The introduction of 3D printing has brought about some incredibly new inventions through the technological moves taken by the innovators.
  • Internet of Things or IoT- The Internet of things is a system that deals with interrelated computing machines with unique identifiers. These machines gather the ability to transfer all the data over networks, negating the need for any human-to-computer or computer-to-computer reactions. Any system that can be a part of the Internet of Things if they are logged in with the IP address. 
  • Electric vehicles- The invention of electric vehicles is an important invention that will be a trend in the future. To ensure the conservation of the scarce resources of nature such as petroleum, coal, and diesel, mechanical engineers have invented the idea of electric vehicles. The invention of electric vehicles will thereby ensure sustainable development which the world aims for today. Many years have been spent perfecting the performance of the machine, safety, as well as the quality of electric vehicles.

Courses After Diploma in Mechanical Engineering

If you are interested in mechanical engineering after the conclusion of the diploma course, there are three educational paths you can consider - BTech, MTech, and PhD. 

  • BTech in Mechanical Engineering is a four-year undergraduate course that you can take after completing a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering. With lateral entry, you can skip the first year and join directly in the second year of the course. 
  • MTech in Mechanical Engineering is a two-year postgraduate course that covers the study of mechanics and kinematics. This course will teach you how to build and install machines and tools. 
  • Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering is a research-oriented course that lasts from 2 to 5 years. In this course, you will learn about thermodynamics, mechanics, and scientific computing.
