About PG Diploma in Hospital Management

PG Diploma in Hospital Management is a two-year master level program available for the students who are looking to master their skills in hospital education. Postgraduate Diploma Course in Health Management will build the practical leadership and management skills required to tackle the challenges of these changing health environments and to maintain partnerships with other care providers, customers and policymakers. It will update the candidates who are interested in hospital management with necessary and required skills for managerial profiles and planning positions in health industries as well as hospitals. 

Candidates who have completed their bachelor education or any other relavant education in hospital management may opt for this program. The PGD in the Hospital and Health Management program incorporates the study of management methods in the field of healthcare or hospital administration. This focuses on providing students with the information and skills needed by health care management professionals. The program consists of four semesters where the syllabus for first two semesters will be the same for all the students while the last two will cover topics related to Health care services depending on the specialisations. Besides this, it will cover including theoretical sessions and laboratory visits.

PG Diploma in Hospital Management Eligibility Criteria

The eligibility criteria for PG diploma in Hospital Management depends on the college the candidate is going for admission. Candidates who have cleared BDS/ BAMS/ MBBS or any equivalent degree recognised by MCI/ AICTE/ UGC are eligible to apply for PG Diploma in Hospital Management. Candidates who have cleared any other degree while having biotechnology as the main subject are also eligible for admission. It must be noted that the applying candidate must have scored 50% marks at both post-graduation and graduation level. Besides this, no candidate will be allowed for admission if he or she has not satisfied any of the criteria.

PG Diploma in Hospital Management Selection Process

The selection process for the postgraduate program will be decided by the college. Admission to the course is based on the student's educational credentials and allied experience, as well as success in the qualifying entrance examination and a personal interview round. Applicants also need to complete a compulsory1-year rotating internship at the end of the MBBS before applying. Besides this, there are some additional requirements that will be decided by the selection committee. Candidates may check the instructions available on the official website of the college to know the detailed selection and admission process.

PG Diploma in Hospital Management Career Options and Job Prospects

There are several opportunities available after completing the PG diploma in Hospital Management. Students can either opt for a doctoral program or a short term Diploma / Certificate Program. In addition to this, candidates who are willing to work can find a suitable profile to start their career. Some of the areas of employment available after PG Diploma in Hospital Management are listed below.

  • Mental Health Organizations
  • Pharmaceuticals
  • Public Health Departments
  • Laboratories and Clinics
  • Hospital Consulting Firms
  • Hospitals
  • Academic Institutions
  • Health Insurance Companies
  • Health Agencies
  • Nursing Homes

Besides this, students may opt for profiles listed below

  • Nursing Directors
  • Heads of Departments
  • Administrators
  • Healthcare Finance Managers
  • Deputy Superintendents
  • Medical Directors
  • Deans & Directors of Medical Colleges
