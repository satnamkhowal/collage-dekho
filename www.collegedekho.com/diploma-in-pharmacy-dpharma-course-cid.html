D Pharma Course Overview

D Pharma Course is a 2-year professional degree course that provides aspiring students with the training and knowledge they would need in order to enter the pharmaceutical industry. The full form of the D Pharma Course is Diploma of Pharmacy course. As for the course details of the D Pharma degree course, it has four semesters in total and some of the most important D Pharma subjects are Pharmacology, Toxicology, Pharmacognosy, Antibiotics, Hypnotics, and more. The scope of a D Pharma course extends far and wide as it could help you to land up in jobs like pharmacy assistant, technicians, or go on to pursue higher studies in the same subject matter. 

To be eligible to apply for a D Pharma course after 12th, a candidate must have passed Class 10+2 with an aggregate of at least 50% marks in the Science stream with compulsory subjects as Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Mathematics. D Pharma eligibility criteria also specifies the minimum age limit for a candidate i.e. 17 years at the time of their admission. The age limit implies that a candidate must have compulsorily completed their 10+2 to stand eligible for a course in Diploma in Pharmacy. Admissions to D Pharma courses are based on both merit as well as entrance exams. 

Some of the top colleges that offer the D Pharma degree course are the Institute of Chemical Technology, Jamia Hamdard University, JIS University, and NMIMS, with an average D Pharma course fee that ranges between INR 10,000 and INR 1 LPA. There are a number of D Pharma job opportunities available across the pharmaceutical industry with lucrative D Pharma salary packages after a candidate has completed this course. For reference, the average D Pharma salary offered to candidates working across hospitals, community pharmacies, and other pharmaceutical-related industries usually ranges between INR 2 LPA to INR 10 LPA.

Why Choose a D Pharma Course?

Diploma in Pharmacy (D Pharma) course is a demanding course in India, which aims to train qualified candidates to fill orders under a pharmacist's supervision, enter patient’s information, identify prescriptions, and process paperwork for orders, understand and apply drug and medical terminology, as well as basic pharmacology. Many graduates pursue careers in a hospital pharmacy or community pharmacy where they are directly involved in the healthcare sector working in partnership with physicians. The curriculum of the diploma course is designed to teach students to understand drug dosage calculations, regularly used pharmacy computer systems, compounding, pharmacy practices and procedures.  

Courses in D Pharma offer their own set of benefits and career opportunities that distinguish the course from career roles and responsibilities in other industries. Mentioned below are some of the most major reasons as to why students choose to study a D Pharma degree course : 

  • Pharmacists have a significant social responsibility since they define the health-care industry. They contribute to the health and well-being of a society's citizens.
  • Pharmacists can work in a variety of settings, including hospitals, nursing homes, colleges, and the medical business as a whole.
  • Healthcare is a vocation that is constantly changing and evolving. Candidates in the healthcare field have a lot of room for advancement and development.
  • Courses in D Pharma provide flexibility, as it is a 24-hour job, it is possible to work at any time, a candidate can choose to work as per the day shift or the night shift according to their own preference.

Difference Between D Pharma Course and Pharm D

Diploma in Pharmacy and Doctor of Pharmacy are two very distinct programmes in the field of pharmacy with differences in their duration, curriculum, and the level of learning experience they offer to students. While Diploma in Pharmacy or D Pharma is comparatively a shorter diploma course that lasts for 2 years, the course duration of Doctor of Pharmacy or Pharm D is of 6-years. The former focuses on training students to work as pharmacy technicians or assistants under the supervision of licensed pharmacists. However, the latter is an advanced professional doctoral degree that prepares individuals for direct patient care roles and comprehensive pharmacy practice.

D Pharma Course Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for D Pharma Admissions, all candidates are required to fulfil a set of eligibility requirements specified under the D Pharma eligibility criteria. Thus, those who are interested in pursuing a degree in D Pharmacy must meet its eligibility requirements as given below : 

  • All candidates are required to complete their 10+2 qualification or its equivalent from a recognised board to pursue a D Pharma course.
  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology, or Mathematics must be the core subjects of study in the 10+2 or its equivalent. 
  • It is mandatory for interested candidates to secure a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate in their 10+2, to be deemed eligible for D Pharma admission.
  • Candidates from the SC/ST/OBC (non-creamy layer), differently-abled, and other categories will receive a 10%-mark reduction with regards to the eligibility criteria. 

Note: It is important for candidates to meet the above-mentioned D Pharma eligibility criteria to be accepted for admission across the top universities in India.

Required Skill Set for D Pharma 

All candidates who are interested in enrolling into a D Pharmacy degree course must possess a variety of skill sets in order to understand the concepts taught under a diploma in pharmacy and get the most out of their learning experience. Participants can work upon their skills and increase their knowledge base during the time they are enrolled into this programme. The most essential D Pharmacy required skill set is mentioned below : 

Communication Skills 

Candidates must have the ability to communicate with healthcare facility members and patients effectively. Empathy is the key to having a good conversation with anyone so the candidates must be able to understand a patient’s needs and demands and talk professionally with other professionals in the pharmaceutical industry. 

Interpersonal Skills 

Overall, candidates studying D Pharma must work upon developing their interpersonal skills. By interpersonal skills, we mean to imply that the candidates must be adept in interacting and communicating with others. They should have an elucidate tone and should be able to talk smoothly without making the patient uncomfortable. 

Marketing and Organisational Skills 

Candidates aspiring to build a career in the Pharmaceutical Sector must be well-organised and should be aware of time management tactics to save the day. D Pharma graduates may have to juggle between multiple tasks at hand and thus, multitasking skills come in handy at the workspace. Marketing skills are not a very essential aspect of the skill set required for a D Pharma course, however, they can be quite useful in this industry when communicating with other professionals. 

Medical and Scientific Research Skills 

Under the D Pharma course, candidates get to learn the basic medical and scientific research skills that are required of them at later stages of their career. However, since the candidate must have a background in Science due to their Class 10+2, they must have some basic knowledge about the basics of chemistry medications. 

Medical Writing and Counselling Skills 

Candidates must be eager to learn skills like those of counselling and of writing prescriptions. Counselling patients with regards to how they're supposed to take their medications and what all precautions they should take during their respective treatments is of prime importance. The ability to communicate and counsel patients with ease should be one of the attributes in an aspiring D Pharma candidate. 

Technical Skills 

Candidates must pay attention to details because errors in the medication sector could lead to serious damages to a patient’s health and body. Moreover, they should be adept in customer service because as pharmacists they would have to deal with ailing customers on a regular basis and having a calm and polite demeanour will help them to attract more people towards their pharmacy.

The University's D. Pharma programme offers a superb combination of academic and practical education that qualifies students for direct employment upon completion of the programme. The University has a talented teaching staff that goes above and beyond to ensure that students receive a high-quality education.

Is the Entrance examination compulsory for D Pharma?

Yes, some universities demand students to take an entrance exam before being accepted into the D Pharma programme. To study D Pharma, students must have studied science courses in high school, such as physics, arithmetic, chemistry, or biology. Some universities accept students instantly based on their Class 12 Boards test results. These schools do not require entrance examinations.

What can I opt for after D Pharma?

When it comes to job opportunities, the candidates can opt for Junior Clinical Research Associate (CRA), Data Analyst, Pharmacist, Pharmacist in Charge, Clinical Pharmacist, Hospital Pharmacy Director, Hospital Staff Pharmacist, Assistant Professor, etc. In terms of Higher Education, candidates can pursue Bachelor of Pharmacy (B Pharm), Master of Pharmacy (M Pharm), Doctorate in Pharmacy (Pharm D), etc.
