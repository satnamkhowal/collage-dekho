Bangalore is home to some of India's most prestigious engineering institutions, including IISc Bangalore, IIIT Bangalore, RV College of Engineering (RVCE), BMS College of Engineering (BMSCE), and PES University. Among these, IISc Bangalore stands out with the highest overall performance score of 1,321/2,000 and a placement score of 903.23/1,000. IIIT Bangalore follows closely with an overall score of 1,215 and a placement score of 820.67.

The cost of pursuing a BTech degree in Bangalore varies widely across institutions. Government colleges generally charge between INR 2.94 lakh and INR 8.38 lakh for the entire program, while fees at private colleges can range from approximately INR 12 lakh to nearly INR 26 lakh. Students can also expect strong placement outcomes, with average salaries spanning from around INR 12 LPA to over INR 36 LPA. IIIT Bangalore has reported the highest placement package among the colleges listed, reaching INR 1.45 crore per annum.

When evaluating value for money, IISc Bangalore emerges as one of the strongest options, offering an average package of INR 28 LPA and an ROI exceeding 330%. Admission to most BTech colleges in Bangalore is primarily based on entrance examinations such as KCET, COMEDK UGET, and JEE Main, making them the key gateways for aspiring engineering students.

For budget-conscious candidates, VIAT Muddenahalli is among the most affordable government engineering colleges in the region, with fees of just INR 12,900. In the private category, colleges affiliated with Bangalore University offer BTech programs starting at approximately INR 1.2 lakh. Based on placement performance, IISc Bangalore, UVCE Bangalore, and AIT Bangalore are among the top government engineering colleges, while IIIT Bangalore, RVCE Bangalore, and BMSCE Bangalore rank among the leading private institutions in the city.

A glimpse into some of Bangalore's top BTech colleges, featuring iconic academic blocks and campus landmarks from IISc Bangalore, IIIT Bangalore, RVCE, and BMSCE.

Top 10 BTech Colleges in Bangalore 2025 — NIRF Rankings

Bangalore's BTech hierarchy is India's most distinctive: a world-class research institution (IISc; NIRF #1 Overall) sits alongside a PPP IIIT with some of the country's best BTech placement outcomes (IIIT Bangalore; avg INR 36.75 LPA), a cluster of old and highly respected autonomous private colleges (RVCE, BMSCE, MSRIT; 1940s–1960s heritage), and 189 VTU-affiliated private colleges. Karnataka's unique KCET system gives home-state students access to premium colleges at subsidised fees.

Government BTech Colleges in Bangalore

Bangalore has approximately 23 government engineering institutions. The most important for BTech aspirants are IISc (BS; JEE Advanced), UVCE (BTech; KCET; cheapest fees), and AIT Bangalore (Air Force Institute of Technology; very competitive). All government colleges in Bangalore use KCET for Karnataka domicile admission.

UVCE Bangalore (est. 1917) is remarkable: one of Asia's oldest engineering colleges, with fee INR 1.92 L total and highest package INR 28.5 LPA. Notable UVCE alumni include Infosys co-founder N.R. Narayana Murthy, Wipro founder Azim Premji (attended but did not complete), and multiple ISRO scientists and senior IAS officers. Despite NAAC B+ (lower than RVCE's A+), UVCE's heritage, central Bangalore location, and very low fee make it the best government BTech option for Karnataka students below IISc/IIIT-B cutoffs.

Top 10 Government BTech Colleges in Bangalore

Note on IIIT Bangalore: IIIT Bangalore is technically a PPP (Public-Private Partnership) institution jointly sponsored by the Karnataka state government and the IT industry — not a purely government institution. However, it is listed here as a quasi-government reference since it is state-sponsored. Its admission is through JEE Main + IIIT Bangalore own counselling (iiitb.ac.in) — NOT KCET or COMEDK. Students targeting IIIT Bangalore and UVCE need different exam routes.

Top 10 Private BTech Colleges in Bangalore

Bangalore's private BTech landscape is dominated by three types: (1) Old-heritage autonomous colleges (RVCE 1963; BMSCE 1946; MSRIT 1962) with strong NIRF ranks and VTU affiliation; (2) PES University (own university degree; autonomous); and (3) a large cluster of VTU-affiliated private colleges (DSCE, BIT, NMIT, BMSIT, CMRIT, NHCE, Jain). All admit through KCET + COMEDK.

BTech Colleges in Bangalore with Low Fees

UVCE Bangalore (University Visvesvaraya College of Engineering; est. 1917; KR Circle; state govt) at INR 1.92 L total for 4 years is the cheapest BTech in Bangalore (zollege confirmed). Among private colleges, some VTU-affiliated institutions charge as low as INR 65,000 per year (INR 2.6 L total).

  • INR 2–4 L total — AIT / Govt-aided Colleges (KCET + COMEDK): Govt aided; 100% placement assistance; low fees.

  • INR 25.97–26.36 L total — IIIT Bangalore: Avg INR 36.75 LPA; highest INR 1.45 Cr; JEE Main CSE 5,516; best BTech placement in Bangalore by avg.

RVCE and BMSCE's ROI within private colleges is exceptional: INR 5–8 L total at RVCE against avg INR 24.50 LPA = degree cost recovered in approximately 2.5–4 months of average salary. RVCE CSE (KCET cutoff ~234; most competitive Karnataka private BTech) at avg INR 24.50 LPA is comparable to several IIT-level outcomes at non-IIT private fees — making it arguably India's best value non-NIT/IIT private BTech when KCET rank allows.

Best BTech College in Bangalore for CSE

IIIT Bangalore leads on average package (INR 36.75 LPA) and JEE Main competition (CSE closing 5,516 — top ~0.5% JEE Main). RVCE leads on accessible autonomy: KCET rank ~234 (Karnataka domicile) for avg INR 24.50 LPA overall at under INR 8 L total fee. PESU and BMSCE follow for accessible KCET/COMEDK CSE with strong IT-sector outcomes.

Entrance Exams Accepted for BTech Colleges in Bangalore

Karnataka students should register for BOTH KCET (for Karnataka domicile seats; home-state advantage at RVCE, BMSCE, MSRIT, UVCE) AND COMEDK UGET (for All India quota seats at the same colleges — potentially accessible even with lower KCET rank). RVCE's KCET CSE cutoff (~234 GM R1) is more competitive than COMEDK CSE (~169 R1) — which seems counterintuitive but reflects smaller seat availability through COMEDK vs KCET. Check actual available seats per quota at kea.kar.nic.in and comedk.org.

Frequently Asked Questions: BTech Colleges in Bangalore

Which is the best BTech college in Bangalore?

IISc Bangalore (Indian Institute of Science; NIRF #1 Overall; BS programme; JEE Advanced + JoSAA; highest INR 86 LPA) is the best research and technical institution in Bangalore and India's #1 ranked institution nationally. For conventional BTech: IIIT Bangalore (NIRF #74–75 Engineering; avg INR 36.75 LPA; JEE Main closing CSE 5,516; Electronics City) leads on placement. RVCE Bangalore (NIRF 101–150 Engineering; avg INR 24.50 LPA; highest INR 67 LPA; KCET ~234 CSE; fee INR 5–8 L) is India's best-value private autonomous engineering college by ROI. BMSCE (NIRF 101–150; NAAC A+; est. 1946; median INR 10 LPA; KCET + COMEDK) and PESU (NIRF 101–150; avg INR 13.21 LPA) follow in Bangalore's private tier.

Which entrance exam is needed for RVCE, BMSCE, and MSRIT admission?

RVCE (RV College of Engineering), BMSCE (BMS College of Engineering), and MSRIT (MS Ramaiah Institute of Technology) all admit through KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test; kea.kar.nic.in) AND COMEDK UGET (comedk.org). Karnataka domicile students use KCET for the Karnataka quota seats (85%+ seats). All-India students use COMEDK UGET for the All India open quota seats. Both systems are separate and students should register for both. KCET 2026 result has been declared. COMEDK UGET 2026 counselling registration is open until June 10, 2026. Neither RVCE, BMSCE, nor MSRIT uses JoSAA or JEE Main as primary admission routes.
