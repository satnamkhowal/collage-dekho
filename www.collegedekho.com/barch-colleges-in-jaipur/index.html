Architecture guide

B.Arch and architecture study in Jaipur

Architecture is a professional, studio-intensive programme. Compare recognition, admission rules and learning facilities carefully.

Admission and recognition

Architecture programmes can have specific Class 12 subject requirements and aptitude or entrance conditions. Verify the current academic-year rules with the institution and the applicable professional or counselling authority.

Inspect the learning environment

Ask to see design studios, model-making facilities, computer labs, recent student work and the jury or review process. A good-looking campus is not a substitute for a strong architecture department.

Budget beyond tuition

Architecture students may have recurring expenses for printing, model materials, software, site visits and a suitable computer. Include these costs when comparing options.

Programme checklist

  • Current programme recognition/status.
  • Studio hours and student-to-faculty interaction.
  • Model-making and workshop facilities.
  • CAD/BIM and visualisation software access.
  • Site visits, internships and practical exposure.
  • Total programme and material costs.
