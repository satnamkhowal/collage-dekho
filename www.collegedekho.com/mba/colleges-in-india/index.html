Top MBA Colleges in India 2026 Admissions

Most of the MBA colleges in India take admission on the basis of an entrance exam followed by a personal interview and group discussion round. The detailed eligibility criteria might vary from college to college but the basic eligibility for admission has been listed below:

  • Candidates must have passed BBA or equivalent in a relevant stream from a recognised college/ university.
  • Most of the colleges require candidates to have an aggregate score of 55% and above. However, some reserved candidates might get a relaxation of 5% on this criteria, depending on the college.
  • Candidates who are appearing for their final year of graduation can also apply for admission.

Selection Process for MBA Colleges in India

Admission to top MBA colleges in India is done either through an entrance exam or merit in the entrance exam. Most of the top management colleges in India offer admissions based on entrance exams. However, some private colleges in India offer direct admissions based on merit or management quota. Usually, the selection process for the best MBA colleges in India goes through the 3 stages as listed below:

Through Entrance Exam:

  • Written Admission Test (WAT): The initial step in the selection process for top MBA colleges in India is to qualify for the written test. Entrance exams like CAT, CMAT, MAT, MAH CET, XAT, and ATMA are accepted for admission to management colleges in India.
  • Group Discussion (GD): Candidates who qualify for the written test will have to appear for the group discussion round. Here a topic will be given to a group of students to speak upon.
  • Personal Interview (PI): The final round of selection for the best MBA colleges in India is the personal interview round. Candidates who qualify for this round will be selected for final admission.

Direct Admission:

  • Step 1: Check eligibility criteria, fee structure, and placement records for the college you wish to apply at.
  • Step 2: Fill out the admission form in online or offline mode.
  • Step 3: Submit required documents like graduation, 10th, and 12th mark sheets, transfer certificate, etc.
  • Step 4: If your application is accepted, you will be notified of the admission.
  • Step 5: Report to the allotted college for document verification, pay the required fees, and complete the admission formalities.

*Note: These are the general steps in the admission process and the actual selection process might vary from college to college.

Entrance Exams Accepted Top MBA Colleges in India 2026

Most of the top MBA colleges in India require candidates to qualify for an entrance exam. Now, students must be wondering which entrance exams they need to appear for to get admission to MBA colleges in India. There are various exams accepted for admission such as CAT, CMAT, MAT, MAH CET, XAT, and ATMA.

  • CAT (Common Admission Test) - CAT is a national level entrance exam for admission to top MBA colleges in India. Graduates in any discipline can apply for the CAT exam. The selection is based on CAT scores and subsequent GD-PI rounds.
  • MAT (Management Aptitude Test) - MAT is also a national level entrance exam for admission to the best MBA colleges in India. For this exam, candidates need to graduate with BBA or equivalent. The selection is based on MAT scores and subsequent GD-PI rounds.
  • CMAT (Common Management Admission Test) - CMAT is a national level entrance exam for admission to MBA programs in India. Graduates in any discipline can apply for the CMAT exam. The selection is based on CMAT scores and subsequent GD-PI rounds.
  • XAT (Xavier Aptitude Test) - XAT stands for Xavier Aptitude Test. It's a national level management entrance exam conducted by XLRI Jamshedpur for admission to MBA and PGDM programmes at top MBA colleges in India.
