Shortlisting checklist

  • Exact course and specialisation offered at the campus you will attend.
  • Current recognition, approval or affiliation where applicable.
  • Entrance exam, counselling or merit route for the current cycle.
  • Complete annual cost including non-tuition charges.
  • Department facilities, labs, library and practical exposure.
  • Programme-specific internship and placement evidence.
  • Commute, hostel or PG options and daily student costs.
  • Refund and cancellation policy before any payment.
Important: Do not treat an old fee, ranking, placement package or seat count as current. Verify mutable facts from the institution and the relevant regulator or counselling authority.
