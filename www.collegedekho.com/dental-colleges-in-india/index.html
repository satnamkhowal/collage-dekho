                        According to the NIRF ranking 2024, Maulana Azad Institute of Dental Sciences in Delhi and King George's Medical University in Lucknow are among the top government dental colleges in India.

                        Which city is best for BDS in India?

                        Chennai, Manipal, and Delhi are cities where some of the top dental colleges like Saveetha Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences, Manipal College of Dental Sciences, and Maulana Azad Institute of Dental Sciences are located.

                        Which is the best BDS college in India through NEET?

                        The best BDS colleges in India based on the NIRF ranking 2024 include Saveetha Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences in Chennai, Manipal College of Dental Sciences in Manipal, and Maulana Azad Institute of Dental Sciences in Delhi. Admission to BDS courses in these colleges requires appearing in the NEET exam.
