DPG Degree College Job Ready Degree

Indian students highly value securing a job early in their careers. To support this goal, DPG Degree College offers four job-ready courses designed to prepare students for the job market. DPG Degree College courses which are Job ready are B.Tech CS in Full Stack Development (IXP), BBA in Digital Marketing (IXP), BCA in Full Stack Development (IXP), and MBA in Digital Marketing (IXP). DPG Degree College fees range for these assured courses is Rs 85,000 to Rs 1,85,000 per year. These job-ready courses offered by DGP Degree College are taught using the unique three-teacher model where faculty, corporate coaches and mentors from the industry teach the students. To get admission to DPG Degree College job ready degree, students need to score a minimum of 50% marks in the qualifying exam.
