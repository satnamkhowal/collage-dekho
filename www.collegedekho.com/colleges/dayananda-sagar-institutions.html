About Dayananda Sagar Institutions

Dayananda Sagar Institutions is also known as DSI. It is a private Institutions located in Shavige Malleshwara Hills, Kumaraswamy Layout, Bangalore, Karnataka. DSI was established in 1979. Dayananda Sagar Institutions Admissions 2026

Dayananda Sagar Institutions Admission 2026 is open for UG and PG programmes in Engineering, Management, Commerce, Science, Architecture, and Dental Sciences. Popular courses include BE, BArch, MTech, MCA, and MBA. BE admission is based on KCET, COMEDK UGET, or JEE Main scores. MBA admission requires CAT, MAT, KMAT, or Karnataka PGCET scores. Eligible candidates can apply online through the official website at dayanandasagar.edu. Below is the Dayananda Sagar Institutions admission process:

Step 1 – Register for Entrance Exam: Appear for the relevant entrance exam (KCET / COMEDK UGET / JEE Main for B.E.; CAT / MAT / PGCET for MBA/MCA; GATE for M.Tech).

Step 2 – Register on Dayananda Sagar Institutions Admission Portal: Visit apply.dayanandasagar.edu and create an account.

Step 3 – Fill Application Form: Select your desired programme and specialisation. Enter personal, academic, and entrance exam score details accurately.

Step 4 – Upload Documents: Submit scanned copies of required documents, including mark sheets, ID proof, and category certificate (if applicable).

Step 5 – Pay Application Fee: Pay the non-refundable application processing fee online.

Step 6 – Counselling / Shortlisting: For KCET/COMEDK, participate in state-level counselling conducted by KEA/COMEDK for seat allotment. For Management Quota and PG courses, attend GD/PI rounds at the DSI campus.

Step 7 – Seat Allotment: Receive seat allotment letter based on rank, choice, and availability.

Step 8 – Document Verification: Visit the allotted college for original document verification.

Step 9 – Fee Payment: Pay the prescribed course fee (tuition + other charges) to confirm admission.

Step 10 – Reporting to College: Report to the college on the designated date and complete the enrolment formalities.

Dayananda Sagar Institutions FAQs

How can I apply online to Dayananda Sagar Institutions

To apply online to Dayananda Sagar Institutions, visit apply.dayanandasagar.edu and create an account. Take relevant entrance exams like KCET/COMEDK/JEE for UG or CAT/MAT/PGCET for PG. Fill the form with personal, academic details, and scores. Upload documents like marksheets and ID. Pay the application fee online. Submit, then attend counselling or GD/PI. Verify documents, pay fees, and report to college. Admissions open for 2026.

How are placements at Dayananda Sagar Institutions?

In 2025, Dayananda Sagar Institutions had good placements. Over 1400 students registered, and 1181 got jobs from 350+ companies. The highest salary was Rs 56 LPA, average was Rs 8 LPA.

Is BTech Course Offered By Dayananda Sagar Institutions?

Dayananda Sagar Institutions does not offers BTech course; it offers the BE course, which is equivalent to BTech. Although the names are different, both programs cover similar engineering disciplines and provide students with the technical skills needed for careers in various engineering fields.

How to get admission in Dayananda Sagar Institutions?

To join Dayananda Sagar Institutions, first take entrance exams like KCET, COMEDK UGET, or JEE Main for BE. For MBA, it's CAT, MAT, KMAT, or PGCET. Then register on their website at apply.dayanandasagar.edu. Fill the form with your details and scores. Upload documents like marksheets and ID. Pay the fee online. Go for counselling or GD/PI. Get seat allotment, verify documents, pay fees, and report to college. Dayananda Sagar Institutions admission is open for 2026.

Is Dayananda Sagar University, Bangalore recognized by the UGC?

Yes, Dayananda Sagar University, Bangalore has been recognised by University Grants Commission (UGC).

Dayananda Sagar Institutions Campus Facilities

Dayananda Sagar Institutions campuses are designed to support both academic and personal development. Below is an overview of key campus facilities available to students.

  • Hostels: Separate boys' and girls' hostels are available on-campus with 24x7 security, Wi-Fi, hot water (solar and electric), TV rooms, indoor games, and a multi-cuisine mess. 
  • Libraries: The DSI Central Library spans 4,500 sq. mt. with RFID access and over 1 lakh physical and digital books.
  • Sports Complex: Campuses offer facilities for basketball, volleyball, football, cricket (practice nets), tennis, kabaddi, kho-kho, table tennis, chess, carrom, and squash. A fully equipped multi-gymnasium and aerobics hall are available.
  • Laboratories: 130+ high-tech labs across disciplines at DSI, including power electronics, digital electronics, HDL/DSP, computer science, and biotechnology labs. DSI has state-of-the-art engineering and research labs aligned with VTU and autonomous curriculum requirements.
  • Medical Facility: On-campus health centre with basic medical assistance and first aid. Regular health check-ups are organised for students.
  • Transport: College bus services from key locations in Bangalore are available for daily commuters.
  • Cafeteria: Multiple canteens on campus offering vegetarian and non-vegetarian food at affordable prices. Maintains hygiene standards and provides nutrition for students.
  • Student Clubs and Associations: DSI campuses have active clubs including the Centre for Performing Arts (CPA), dancing, singing, social and photography clubs, IEDC (Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Cell), and departmental technical clubs.
  • IT Infrastructure: Wi-Fi-enabled campus, computer labs with licensed software, ERP system (ums.mydsi.org), and audio-visual auditoriums.

  • AIC-DSU Foundation-scholarship
  • Post Matric Scholarships Scheme for Minorities
  • Merit cum Means Scholarship for Technical courses
  • Post-matric scholarship for students with disabilities
  • Scholarships for top class education for students with Disabilities
  • Central sector scheme of scholarships for college and university students
