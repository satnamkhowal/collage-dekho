Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University Job Ready Degree

Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University offers a total of five job-ready courses in multiple specialisations. These courses are different from the regular courses offered at the university. Candidates can take up these courses to master specific skills that will not only upgrade their knowledge but also make them job-ready right after the completion of the course. At the undergraduate level, the courses available are B.Tech in RPA (IXP), B.Tech in Blockchain, B.Des in Game Design & Animation (IXP), B.Des in Digital Product Design (IXP), BBA in Digital Marketing (IXP), BBA in Fintech(IXP), B.Tech in Data Science (IXP), BCA in Full Stack Development (IXP) and BCA in Data Science (IXP). The fee structure is in the range of Rs 55,000 - Rs 95,000 per semester. At the postgraduate level, the job-ready course available is MBA in Digital Marketing and the fee is Rs 80,000 per semester. Students who meet the eligibility criteria can avail scholarships at Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University.

Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

Admission in job ready “Assured” courses offered by Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University is done based on the candidate’s merit in the qualifying examination. There is no entrance examination for admission in these courses.

Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Scholarships

Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University scholarships are both merit-based and need-based. As admissions in the job-ready courses are direct, scholarships are applicable through the DBUU admission cell. Five board categories of scholarships are available - domicile, meritorious, category scholarships, financial assistantships, and over-and-above scholarships. The university is offering a total scholarship of three crores for 2024.
