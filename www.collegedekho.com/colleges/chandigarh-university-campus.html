Chandigarh University Campus

The campus of Chandigarh University (CU) is a fully residential campus that accommodates all the students. The classrooms and lecture halls are spacious and can seat people in great numbers. Chandigarh University campus has hostels for boys and girls that have all amenities. The hostels are equipped with canteens, balconies, common rooms, playgrounds and sports grounds, and gardens. The hostels are located near the main building of the college so that students can reach their classes easily. The laboratories of Chandigarh University are equipped with the latest technical equipment and apparatus. The main building of Chandigarh University has a huge auditorium where all programmes and events of the university are conducted. Chandigarh University also has different clubs for the students to participate in.

Chandigarh University Facilities

Chandigarh University Campus has separate hostels for boys and girls with all modern facilities and attached mess. The hostel mess serves fresh and hygienic food. You will be provided with facilities like internet, music room, common room with TV & cable facility, room for indoor games and a reading room with a collection of national/international newspapers, magazines, etc. The hostel's houses have a fully equipped gym and separate courts for Volley Ball, Basket Ball, and Badminton. The concerned staff looks after the university assets and ensures that the hostel remains responsive all the time.

Sanctity of the Hostels

  • The hostels being a part of the University premises deserve a sacred status.
  • Use of bad and abusive language should be avoided within the hostel / University premises.
  • Students are not permitted to keep-in-their possession, room crackers, dangerous weapons, firearms, contraband items or instruments that can cause serious injury to others. Students should not indulge in any illegal and/or unethical activity on the hostel premises.
  • Smoking, consumption of alcoholic drinks and use of narcotic drugs in the hostel premises is strictly prohibited. Neither the students nor their visitors are allowed to bring or consume liquor, drugs, or any other intoxicants in the hostel premises. Severe action will be taken against those in possession of drugs or found under their influence.

Chandigarh University Hostel Fee Structure 2026

Chandigarh University provides a safe and comfortable hostel environment where students can focus on their studies without difficulty. The campus has separate hostels for boys and girls with modern facilities and an attached mess that serves fresh and hygienic food. Students get access to internet, common rooms with TV, music rooms, indoor games, and reading rooms with newspapers and magazines. The hostels also include a gym and sports courts for volleyball, basketball, and badminton. Trained staff are available to manage facilities and ensure smooth day-to-day operations.

Chandigarh University Campus FAQs

What are the various facilities available on campus at CU , Chandigarh ?

CU , Chandigarh offers various facilities to its students including Academic Zone, Auditorium, Boys Hostel, Canteen, Computer Lab and more.

What kind of medical facilities are available at Chandigarh University?

A registered medical doctor is always available during work hours on campus. In case of emergency three ambulances are always present at the campus of Chandigarh University

Is Chandigarh University a safe campus?

As a part of the safety precautions taken up by Chandigarh University, the campus is guarded by security guards 24x7 and is completely under CCTV surveillance.

Can a student cook food inside his hostel room in Chandigarh University?

Yes, the student can cook food inside his hostel room using Induction Heaters.

Does Chandigarh University provide hostel facilities?

Yes, Chandigarh University provides hostel facilities for both boys and girls.
