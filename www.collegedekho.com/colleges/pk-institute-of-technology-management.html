About PKITM Mathura

P.K. Institute of Technology & Management is situated in Mathura in Uttar Pradesh state of India. Established in 2009, PKITM is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. P.K. Institute of Technology & Management offers 5 courses across 2 streams namely Engineering, Education..Popular degrees offered at P.K. Institute of Technology & Management include B.Tech, Certificate..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, P.K. Institute of Technology & Management is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at P.K. Institute of Technology & Management, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at P.K. Institute of Technology & Management is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
