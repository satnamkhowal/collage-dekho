Jigyasa University Job Ready Degree

To ensure students are equipped with the necessary knowledge and skills for the professional world, Jigyasa University has introduced three job-oriented courses. These courses are BA in Digital Marketing, BA in Restaurant Management and BSc in Animation. The duration of courses is three years. The annual fees for BA Digital Marketing and BSc Animation is Rs 80,000 and Rs 85,000 respectively. If you want to amplify your professional skills and kickstart your professional career in the best possible way then a Job Ready Degree at Jigyasa University is the right choice for you.

Jigyasa University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions

Admission in Job Ready “Assured” courses at Jigyasa University is offered on merit basis. There is no entrance exam. Candidates are required to fulfill the eligibility criteria and pay admission fee to secure admission.
