About SMS Patiala

School of Management Studies is situated in Patiala in Punjab state of India. Established in 1969, SMS is a Private college. School of Management Studies offers 5 courses across 1 streams namely Management..Popular degrees offered at School of Management Studies include MBA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, School of Management Studies is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at School of Management Studies, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations.
