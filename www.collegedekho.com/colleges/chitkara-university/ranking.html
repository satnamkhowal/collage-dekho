Chitkara University Ranking 2025

The Ministry of Education (MoE) has released the NIRF ranking 2025. As per the rankings, Chitkara University has achieved an NIRF rank of 78 in the university category with a score of 49.32. In terms of category-wise rankings, the institute has achieved NIRF 89th rank in the engineering category with a score of 47.34, NIRF 78th rank in the management category with a score of 48.3, and NIRF 38th rank in the architecture and planning category with a score of 48.16.
