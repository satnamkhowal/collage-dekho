About Budge Budge Institute of Technology B.Tech

Budge Budge Institute of Technology B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree program at Budge Budge Institute of Technology Kolkata, candidates must have completed their higher secondary education (10+2) with a minimum aggregate of 45% in the science stream. They should have studied Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, candidates must have a valid rank in the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination (WBJEE) or the Joint Entrance Examination Main (JEE Main). Admission is also open to those who have qualified in the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination for Lateral Entry (JELET) for direct admission to the second year of the B.Tech. program. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 60,000 - 1,31,350.
