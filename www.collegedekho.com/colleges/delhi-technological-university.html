About DTU

Delhi Technological University, also known as DTU, is a Government University, established in 1941. Located in New Delhi, Delhi, the DTU campus spans over 164 acres. The university is affiliated with itself as a state university and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and accredited with an ‘A’ grade by NAAC. DTU is ranked 27th in the Engineering category and 66th in the overall category by NIRF in 2024.

DTU offers a wide range of Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Doctoral programs in the streams of Engineering, Management, Design, Economics, and Sciences. BTech, MBA, MTech, and BA (Hons) Economics are some of the popular courses. Admissions at DTU are based on Entrance Examinations. The university accepts JEE Main for BTech, CAT for MBA, GATE for MTech, and CUET for BA (Hons) Economics.

As per the rankings, Delhi Technological University (DTU) has achieved an NIRF rank of 30 in the Engineering category with a score of 60.85.

DTU Admission 2025

DTU admissions are based on National Level Entrance Examinations. Students seeking admission at DTU must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the university and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for DTU admissions:

  • BTech: Candidates seeking admission to BTech must appear for the JEE Main and participate in the JAC Delhi Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the CAT and participate in the interview process conducted by DTU.
  • MTech: Candidates seeking admission to MTech must appear for the GATE or DTU’s own entrance exam for non-GATE candidates and participate in the CCMT or DTU counselling process.
  • BA (Hons) Economics: Admissions to BA (Hons) Economics are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination (best of four subjects in 10+2).

DTU Delhi FAQs

What is the DTU Delhi fees?

The fees for various DTU Delhi courses vary as per the specialisation. The fee for B.Tech is Rs 8.44 lakh, for BBA, the fee is Rs 2.82 lakh, for MBA, the course fee is Rs 3.96 lakh, the M.Tech fee is Rs 3.23 lakh, and, the fee for PhD is Rs 45,000.

Is Delhi Technological University private or government?

DTU Delhi is a government university located in Bawana. It is affiliated with the University of Delhi and is accredited by the NAAC. The university was established in 1941 and the official website of the university is dtu.ac.in.

What are the Delhi Technological University courses?

DTU Delhi offers UG, PG, and PhD courses. The courses are offered in streams like engineering, technology, management, and, science. The courses offered at the university are B.Tech, M.Tech, BBA, B.Des, M.Sc, MBA, PhD, and, M.Des. These courses are offered in various specialisations.

What is the Delhi Technological University address?

The address of DTU Delhi is Delhi Technological University, Shahbad Daulatpur, Main Bawana Road, Delhi-110042, India. The contact number is 011-27852203. The university has a campus size of 163 acres. Apart from the main university campus, the university also has 2 other campuses named East Delhi Campus and Delhi School of Management (DSM) Campus.

What are the Delhi Technological University notable alumni?

Some of the notable alumni of DTU Delhi include Om Prakash Sharma, Sushant Singh Rajput, Arpita Singh, Durjoy Datta, Sachin Garg, Sanjay Gupta, Uday Reddy, Divya Garg, and, Ravish Sharma.

What is the highest package for DTU placements?

The highest domestic CTC offered at Delhi Technological University is Rs 64.23 lakh per annum. The highest international CTC is Rs 1.8 crore per annum. Further, the average CTC is Rs 15.16 lakh per annum.

What are the Delhi Technological University rankings?

Delhi Technological University NIRF ranking 2023 is 61 for the Overall category, 29 for the Engineering category, and, 40 for the University category. The Delhi Technological University World University Ranking 2023 falls between the range of 601-800.

Is the DTU cutoff 2023 released for B.Tech?

Yes, the DTU B.Tech cutoff 2023 has been released for 3 rounds. The cutoff is released by the Joint Admission Committee (JAC) for the university. The cutoff is released in the form of ranks that vary as per the categories and specialisations.

Which exam will be accepted for Delhi Technological University course admissions?

The DTU admission is based on the scores of various national-level entrance examinations like JEE Main, GATE, CUET UG, CUET PG, CAT, and, CEED. For the B.Tech admissions, the JEE Main scores are valid, for MBA, the CAT scores, M.Des admission is based on the CEED scores, and for various other courses, the CUET UG and CUET PG exams are valid.

What is the DTU official website?

The official website of DTU Delhi is dtu.ac.in.

Is there any DTU entrance exam?

Yes, DTU accepts a variety of national exams like JEE Main, GATE, CAT, UCEED, and, CEED. Apart from this, the university also conducts its own entrance exams for certain courses. Based on these exams, the university releases its cutoff list.

Does DTU offer MBA?

Yes, the DTU MBA is one of the popular courses which is offered by the Delhi School of Management (DSM) department of the university. MBA is offered in many specializations and is UGC-approved.

What are the DTU courses?

Is the DTU campus good?

DTU has 3 sub-campuses which offer courses in different disciplines. These include the DTU main campus, the Delhi School of Management (DSM), and the University School of Management and Entrepreneurship (USME) which is also known as the DTU East Delhi Campus.
