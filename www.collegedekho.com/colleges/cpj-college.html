About Chanderprabhu Jain College of Higher Studies & School of Law

Chanderprabhu Jain College of Higher Studies and School of Law was run by the Rishi Aurobindo Educational Society. The college aims to provide attentive professional programmes in emerging areas of higher education in disciplines that have high job potential. It was established in 2007 and affiliated with Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University and approved by Bar Council of India (BCI). The institute is also recognised by government of NCT of Delhi and is recognised by UGC.

CPJ College courses is characterised by the quality of education across a broad range of disciplines from undergraduate courses to postgraduate programs covering BBA (CAM), BBA (General), BCA, B.Com (H), BA LLB (Honours), BBA LLB (Honours) and LLM. For CPJ College admission, students can enroll in any course either online or offline by visiting the campus. The institute is well equipped with all the facilities that help in enhancing the learning environment. CPJ Delhi campus offers access to the air-conditioned and well spacious classroom along with a library stocked with hundreds and thousands of books, a conference hall, cafeteria, health clinic and more. The college also provides hostel facilities for both boys and girls. Chanderprabhu Jain College of Higher Studies & School of Law fees differ on the basis of course type and duration.

The institute welcomes leading companies from different industries and sectors during CPJ College placement. Upon the completion of the degree, most of the students are able to secure a place in a company. The University Grant Commission (UGC) introduced the 1-Year LL.M. Degree Program with the goal of enabling the current legal education system to compete with those of industrialised nations like the USA and the UK. The goal of the LL.M. programme is to provide students the tools they need to do worldwide research on legal challenges, which will help them think creatively, invent new solutions, and apply their knowledge to pressing global concerns. According to UGC guidelines and in accordance with its affiliation with GGSIPU, Delhi, the CPJ School of Law provides a 1-year LL.M. postgraduate law programme. The mission of CPJ School of Law is to provide superior legal education while rising to the status of a renowned hub for higher education and scholarly research.

Chanderprabhu Jain College of Higher Studies & School of Law Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The CPJ College Campus mixes cutting-edge classrooms and study areas with a gorgeous natural setting. All lecture halls, computer labs, libraries, auditoriums, conference rooms, cafeterias, and other spaces are completely air-conditioned, well illuminated, roomy, and well-equipped to employ the most recent software and hardware. In order to give the student community a cosy, secure, and enriching atmosphere, the entire campus is completely air-conditioned, Wi-Fi connected, and outfitted with CCTV Cameras. The academic assistance, housing, sports, cultural, and recreational resources offered by CPJ Campus enable our students to actively engage in global learning. For a little cost, the libraries provide a Book Bank Facility whereby students can borrow textbooks for the duration of the semester. Additionally, the college participates in Manu Patra and the DELNET Consortium. The availability of a cafeteria and a Coffee Day Xpress store on campus is also advantageous to the students. For a low Transport Fare, CPJ College operates its own fleet of buses for both students and employees that travel the entirety of Delhi.

Dear student,

Chanderprabhu Jain College of Higher Studies & School of Law offers two integrated law courses at the undergraduate level: BA LLB and BBA LLB. In order to apply to any of these courses, you have to fulfil the eligibility criteria that have been set by CPG College for BA LLB and BBA LLB. The admission process of BA LLB and BBA LLB at Chanderprabhu Jain College of Higher Studies & School of Law is done through the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT). You will have to appear for CLAT in the year in which you seek admission. A merit list will be prepared by the college on the basis of the marks scored by applicants in the law entrance exam of CLAT.
