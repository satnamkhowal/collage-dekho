About Jamia Millia Islamia

Jamia Millia Islamia, also known as JMI, is a Central university established in 1920 in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, and relocated to Delhi in 1935. The university is recognised by UGC and accredited by NAAC with Grade A++. JMI Delhi has been ranked 13th in the Overall category and 4th among top universities in India in the NIRF 2025 rankings.

At Jamia Millia Islamia University, B.Tech Computer Engineering is among the most sought-after programs, with a total academic fees of INR 78,100. Admission to the B.Tech course is based on JEE Main scores, with the general category cutoff ranging between 17,752 and 42,668 ranks. For admission to various courses, the university conducted JMI entrance test. It also accepts national-level exam scores such as CUET, NEET, NATA, CUET PG, and NCET.

Jamia Millia Islamia Admission Process 2026

Jamia Millia Islamia admission process starts in March–April, while entrance examinations are conducted from late April onwards. Based on the program, admissions are granted either through the JMI Entrance Test or through national-level exams like JEE Main, NEET, or NATA. The step-by-step JMI admission process is given below in brief:

  • Register on the official admission portal (admission.jmi.ac.in) and fill out the application form
  • Appear for the JMI Entrance Test or relevant national-level exam (JEE Main, NEET, NATA, etc.)
  • Shortlisted candidates may be called for GD/PI (for select courses)
  • Check the merit list released by the university.
  • Participate in the counselling and seat allotment process.
  • Complete document verification and pay the admission fee to confirm the seat

For course-wise admission process and latest updates, candidates must check Jamia Millia Islamia Admission 2026.

Jamia Millia Islamia Campus

Jamia Millia Islamia University is located on a 239-acre campus in New Delhi. For commuting, the nearest bus stops to the campus are Nai Basti and Batla House. The JMI library is fully air-conditioned and houses more than 3.7 lakh books. It also has a reading hall that can accommodate up to 1,275 students. The list of facilities available at the Jamia Millia Islamia campus is given as follows:

  • Hostels: The university hostels are well-furnished and can accommodate around 1,200 students. They are equipped with facilities such as dining halls, a gymnasium, a mess, and Wi-Fi, as JMI operates as a partly residential campus. 
  • Zakir Hussain Central Library: A well-equipped library with over 3 lakh books, along with journals and digital resources for students and researchers.
  • Smart Classrooms: Modern lecture halls with advanced audio-visual systems to enhance interactive and technology-enabled learning.
  • Health Centre: Provides basic medical care and emergency healthcare services for students and staff.
  • Wi-Fi Enabled Campus: The entire campus is connected with high-speed Wi-Fi for easy access to academic and online resources.
  • Cafeteria and Dining Facilities: On-campus cafeteria and mess facilities are available for students.
  • Seminar Halls and Auditoriums: Fully equipped spaces for cultural programs, guest lectures, conferences, and seminars.
  • Sustainable Campus Initiatives: The university promotes eco-friendly practices such as rainwater harvesting, solar street lighting, and bio-waste management systems.
