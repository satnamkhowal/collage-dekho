Institute of Chemical Technology (ICT), Mumbai Placement 2025

ICT Mumbai placements 2025 achieved 95% participation among eligible students, resulting in 760 placements from 850 offers extended by companies in core chemical sectors. The highest package reached Rs. 60 LPA, secured by a B.Chem.Eng graduate in a process engineering role at ExxonMobil, while the average package stood at Rs. 10 LPA for UG programs and Rs. 12 LPA for PG programs.

Chemical branches recorded a higher average of Rs. 11.5 LPA due to strong industry demand, with top recruiters including BPCL, Reliance, and Honeywell. This performance reflects ICT Mumbai's focus on specialized chemical technology education, as detailed in the ICT Mumbai overview. Students targeting 2026 admissions can use these stats to gauge career prospects in chemical engineering and allied fields.

For more information, check ICT Mumbai.

Is admission for plain MSc chemistry in ICT Mumbai possible with just the entrance exam? Because I didnt have maths in 12th or maths in any Bsc level.

V
Vandana Thakur •  Apr-01-2026

Dear Student, 

Admission to the M.Sc. The Chemistry program at the Institute of Chemical Technology, Mumbai, is strictly based on the performance in their entrance examination. However, candidates must satisfy the specific eligibility criteria regarding undergraduate coursework. According to official institutional guidelines, you are generally required to have passed Mathematics at the 10+2 level to be eligible for postgraduate science streams. Since you did not have Mathematics in Class 12th grade or during your Bachelor of Science course, you may not meet the mandatory prerequisite for enrollment. It is highly recommended to verify the current year's handbook or contact the Institute of Chemical Technology admission office. These mathematical requirements are foundational to their rigorous chemical sciences curriculum.
