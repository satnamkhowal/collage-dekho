Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology Application Process 2025

The AIMT Lucknow application process is carried out in online mode. Aspiring candidates must follow the steps to fill out the application form for admission to the Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology.

Step 1: Visit the official website of the institute, which is aimt.edu.in. 

Step 2: Go to the “Admission” tab on the home page of the official website.

Step 3: Access the “Online Registration Form” from this section.

Step 4: A registration form will open with the required information, like the name, mobile number, email, city, as well as the entrance exams the candidate has appeared for. 

Step 5: Fill in all the required information correctly, since this is a crucial step in the Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology admission process.

Step 6: Once the registration form is filled out, a confirmation email will be sent to the registered email id of the candidate. Confirm the email to proceed further.

Step 7: After the confirmation of the email id, the applicant will be able to access the application form. Fill out the AIMT Lucknow admission form with all details.

Step 8: Attach all the documents in a scanned format. The size and format of each document must be as mentioned in the application form. 

Step 9: After uploading the documents, pay the application fee and click on “Submit”. Take a printout for future reference.

Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology FAQs

What is the address of AIMT Lucknow?

The address of AIMT Lucknow is Maurawa Road, Mohanlalganj, Uttaar Gaon, Uttar Pradesh - 226301. The institute is located approximately 24 kilometres from Lucknow Railway Station, in Uttar Pradesh.

Does AIMT Lucknow offer any scholarships to its students?

Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology offers two main types of scholarships to support its students. The first is the Samaj Kalyan Vibhag Scholarship, an initiative by the Uttar Pradesh State Government aimed at assisting financially unstable students. The second is the fellowship/scholarship specifically for students enrolled in the M.Tech programme. This scholarship is awarded as per merit.

Are there any relaxation criteria for reserved categories at AIMT Lucknow?

At Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology, SC/ST candidates are given a 5% relaxation in the eligibility criteria. The standard requirement for courses is a minimum of 50% for general candidates and SC/ST candidates can qualify with 45% marks.

Does AIMT Lucknow offer distance education or online courses?

Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology does not provide distance education or online courses. They focus solely on full-time programmes. AIMT offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma courses across various fields such as engineering, IT, accounting, business, and management.

Does Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology provide hostel facilities for students?

Yes, AIMT Lucknow offers hostel facilities for its students. The campus includes separate hostels for boys and girls. The boys' hostel can accommodate around 300 students, while the girls' hostel has space for approximately 100 students. These hostels are located on the campus and provide essential amenities, including medical facilities, Wi-Fi, a mess, banking services, and a telephone facility.

How many departments are there at AIMT Lucknow?

Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology has several academic departments that cater to various fields of study. These include the Department of Applied Science and Humanities, Department of Business Administration, Department of Civil Engineering, Department of Computer Science and Information Technology, Department of Electronics and Communication, and the Department of Mechanical Engineering.

Which entrance exams are accepted for admission to AIMT Lucknow?

AIMT Lucknow accepts a variety of entrance exams, depending on the course. For B.Tech admissions, the institute considers JEE Main scores. Undergraduate courses such as BCA, B.Sc, B.Com, and BBA require CUET UG scores for admission. For the M.Tech programme, admission is based on GATE scores. For the MBA programme, AIMT Lucknow accepts scores from exams like CAT, MAT, XAT, and GMAT.

Is AIMT Lucknow a public or private college?

AIMT Lucknow, officially known as Ambalika Institute of Management and Technology, is a private educational institution. It was established in 2008 and is located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. The institute is affiliated with Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) and recognised by the Board of Technical Education Uttar Pradesh (BTE). Additionally, AIMT is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and is the only college in Lucknow accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).
