About IIT Bombay Placements 2025

The complete official IIT Bombay placement report 2025 has not been published by the college. According to the official 2025 partial placement report, 75% placement rate was achieved by placing 2420 students in the IITB placement. The highest package of IIT Bombay offered during the 2025 placements is INR 2.2 crore PA offered to a BTech student. Moreover, the IIT Bombay average package in 2025 was INR 23.5 LPA, and the median package was INR 17.92 LPA. The average package of IIT Bombay CSE is INR 28.00 LPA. More than 100 companies visited the IIT Bombay campus for placements. Some of the top recruiters for IIT Bombay Placements are Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, Tata Steel, etc.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Do students get international offers during IIT Bombay placements?
Yes, IIT Bombay consistently receives numerous international placement offers from top global firms. Students are directly recruited for high-paying roles in countries like the US, Japan, Taiwan, and Singapore, primarily in software engineering, trading, and advanced core technical domains.
Can non-CS branch students grab IT jobs at IIT Bombay?
Absolutely. Many IT and software companies open their profiles to all branches. Students from mechanical, civil, or chemical engineering with strong coding skills, algorithmic knowledge, and technical problem-solving abilities frequently secure highly lucrative software development roles during campus placements.

IIT Bombay Placements FAQs

How strict is the CGPA cutoff for IITB placements?

CGPA cutoffs vary heavily depending on the hiring company. While top-tier finance and consulting firms often demand a strict minimum CGPA of 8.0 or higher, many software and core engineering companies prioritize exceptional coding and practical technical skills instead.

Can a student accept multiple job offers at IITB?

The placement cell strictly enforces a one-student-one-job policy. Once a student officially accepts a final job offer, they are completely deregistered from the remaining placement process, ensuring fair employment opportunities for all other unplaced graduating batchmates.

What percentage of IIT Bombay offers are international packages?

At Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, around 2–5% offers are international. These offers usually come with very high packages and are given to top-performing students in branches like CSE and Electrical Engineering.

What is the median salary for UG programs per NIRF?

According to the official 2025 NIRF report for IIT Bombay, the median CTC for 4-year undergraduate programs was INR 19.61 LPA, with 731 students placed. Meanwhile, the 5-year UG programs recorded a median CTC of INR 19.25 LPA.

What is the lowest salary in IIT Bombay?

The official 2025 partial placement report does not disclose the exact lowest salary offered. However, the overall median package stands strong at INR 17.92 LPA, ensuring that the vast majority of placed students secure highly competitive compensation packages.

Is it 100% placement in IIT Bombay?

No, the overall campus placement rate for 2025 is 75%, as many students voluntarily opt out for higher studies or entrepreneurship. However, specific premium programs like the SJMSOM MBA achieved a flawless 100% placement success rate this season.
