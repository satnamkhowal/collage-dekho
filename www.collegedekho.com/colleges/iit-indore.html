About IIT Indore

Indian Institute of Technology Indore, popularly known as IIT Indore is located in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. At the UG level, IIT Indore offers popular courses like B.Tech in Computer Science, B.Tech in Electrical Engineering, and B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering. At the PG level, courses like M.Tech in VLSI Design, M.Sc in Physics, and MBA are offered.  It takes admission through entrance exams like JEE Advanced, UCEED, GATE, IIT JAM, CEED, etc. Admission for MTech for this year has closed, GATE applications for 2026 will start from August 2025 and the exam will be conducted in February 2026. For BTech admissions, JoSAA counseling is almost at its conclusion, the registered candidates can check their cutoff from the official site.

In 2024, IIT Indore achieved 16th rank in the 'Engineering' category by the NIRF ranking while it achieved 516th rank in the ranking by QS World Ranking 2025.

The placement statistics of IIT Indore reflects great performance. According to the latest placement statistics, students received the highest package of 68 LPA with an average of 25.45 LPA. The median salary was 19.48 LPA, a total of 234 UG and 63 PG students were placed. The top recruiters include Amazon, Zomato, Accenture, Microsoft, Deloitte, and Flipkart.

At the end of the last semester of the B.Tech programme, IIT Indore gives an option to convert B.Tech into an integrated B.Tech+M.Tech programme of five years.

IIT Indore Eligibility Criteria 2025

  • For B.Tech: Students must pass Class 12 from a recognized board with Mathematics, chemistry, and physics as compulsory subjects and any optional subject from, biology, biotechnology, or computer. In addition, JEE Main and Advanced scores are required along with higher secondary certification. 
  • For M.Tech and M.Sc : PG courses require a bachelor’s degree in a relevant stream minimum aggregate of 60% (55% in the SC/ST/PWD category). In addition, GATE and IIT JAM scores are required along with graduation certificates for M.Tech and M.Sc. admission, respectively.
  • For PhD: For PhD admission at IIT Indore, students should have a qualifying score in GATE, NET, CSIR or JRF test along with a master’s degree in sciences or M.Tech in the relevant stream from a recognized university with a minimum aggregate of 60% (55% in case of SC/ST/PWD category)

For IIT Indore B.Tech admissions

  • Selection criteria: JEE Advanced score/rank are required for admission. Qualified candidates of JEE Advanced will be called for the online counselling session..
  • Eligibility criteria: Students must have a senior secondary pass certificate with at least 60% marks (55% for SC/ST) from a recognised university with physics and mathematics as compulsory subjects.

For IIT Indore M.Tech admissions

  • Selection criteria: Valid score in GATE entrance exam followed by COAP Counselling.
  • Eligibility criteria: Must have completed a bachelor’s degree in a relevant engineering field with at least 8.5/ 6.25 for General and SC/ST, respectively (For IITs Graduates) and a minimum 60% aggregate if candidates are graduated from non-IITs.

For IIT Indore M.Sc admissions

  • Selection Criteria: The IIT JAM entrance examination scores are used to select candidates for the M.Sc programme. Previous academic records are taken into account while determining the final merit list.
  • Eligibility Criteria: Candidates must have a bachelor’s degree in a relevant discipline from a recognised university with at least 60% marks. (55% for SC/ST) to be eligible for admission to the IIT Indore M.Sc. programme.

For IIT Indore PhD admissions

  • Selection criteria: Candidates who have been shortlisted are then needed to face the IIT Indore PhD interview. Based on their performance in the last qualifying exams and entrance exam, candidates will be invited to a personal interview round for further consideration. The entrance exams for PhD admission at IIT Indore are UGC/ CSIR NET (JRF) examination, SET, GATE (with a valid score), or Teacher Research Fellowship. The final merit list is compiled using a combination of entrance tests, interviews, and academic record scores. Statement of Purpose (SOP) and Letter of Recommendation (LOR) must be submitted by the applicant before the interview
  • Eligibility criteria: Candidates must have received at least 7 CGPA in the required disciplines (without rounding off) in their master’s degree from universities/ institutions accredited by the UGC / AICTE / NCTE to be considered for this programme.

IIT Indore Application Process 2025

Every year, the admissions in IIT Indore UG and PG courses begin in May, while the common application process starts in April. For PhD, the admission takes place twice a year, January and July. Students who are interested in a particular course can apply online. 

The IIT Indore online application process has been started for M.Tech/MSc/MSR Programmes. Candidates interested in applying for admission should go to the IIT Indore official website to fill out the application form and pay the application fee. For all courses, the IIT Indore application fee is Rs 800. The following is the IIT Indore application process:

IIT Indore UG admission process 

The admission to UG programmes at IIT Indore is made based on Joint Entrance Examination Advanced (JEE Advanced) rank, which then is administered by a centralised counselling process that JoSAA conducts at the all-India level in which candidates are allowed to choose the institution and course of their choice. Then, admission is granted based on their rank in JEE advanced and seat availability in the institution. For further updates, students need to check the college website like physical reporting, fee payment, class commencement etc.

IIT Indore PG admission process

The admissions to PG programmes at IIT Indore are based on GATE and IIT JAM rank for M.Tech and M.Sc courses, respectively. When the entrance exam results are out, candidates can apply for the same on the IIT Indore admission portal after checking their eligibility. Besides filling out the IIT Indore admission application form, students are required to register at COAP. All participating National Importance institutes publish seat allotment results on this portal only. Once they are granted admission to the institution of their choice, candidates need to follow the college website for further updates regarding physical reporting and fee payment.

IIT Indore PhD admission process

Admission in IIT Indore PhD programmes is based on UGC/ CSIR NET (JRF) examination, SET, GATE (with a valid score), or Teacher Research Fellowship scores. As the application for admission is out on the IIT Indore official website, candidates must follow the following process:

  • Visit the official website of IIT Indore.
  • Click on PhD admission.
  • Register yourself with a valid phone number and email id
  • Login to the portal with credentials sent to your mail id
  • Fill out the IIT Indore application form accordingly as asked.
  • Upload the required documents
  • Pay the application fee of Rs 800 through net banking, debit card, or credit card.
  • Check the website for further updates.
  • If shortlisted, attend the IIT Indore selection process, which can be a written test, presentation or a personal or professional interview.
  • Upon selection, collect your offer letter and pay the course fee after document verification at the time of physical reporting.

IIT Indore Application Process 

The official website of the Indian Institute of Technology Indore informs candidates about the admission process. Candidates must keep themselves updated  with the status of the application forms by logging in to the admission portal of IIT Indore. Once accepted, applicants are called to participate in the selection process which may include an entrance exam, group discussion, or personal interview. If the candidate is shortlisted, they are sent an admission letter that must be submitted to the college.

IIT Indore Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The 501-acre IIT Indore campus has everything a student will need for their development. The campus has been upgraded with a SMART card facility. The card serves as an identity proof for students that enable them to access books through the library, and to labs. It can also be used as a debit card at the campus restaurant,  printing, scanning and photocopying services, parking area, buy tickets on the market bus, use it in the dispensary, pay an overdue fee in the library, pay tuition fees and miscellaneous fees, manage attendance etc.

  • IIT Indore campus has a well-equipped health centre with modern instruments and qualified medical staff to provide dedicated health services to the students and the staff.
  • The college has a counselling centre involved in student life at the institute campus at different levels of interactive processes. The counsellor also helps freshers with their academic and non-academic concerns.

IIT Indore Scholarships

All the students belonging to SC, ST and PwD categories are exempted from paying the course tuition fee irrespective of their family income. IIT Indore scholarships and financial assistance are for all of its students who are studying various UG and PG courses in a regular mode. However, the student is required to meet the minimum eligibility and merit to avail of the IIT Indore scholarships facilities. Some of the significant IIT Indore scholarship schemes are described below:

Merit Cum Means Scholarship: 

  • This scholarship is for B.Tech and M.Sc students who belong to General and OBC Categories 
  • The family annual income of General and OBC students should be less than 5 lakh and 8 lakh respectively to avail the same.
  • Top 25% of students are chosen for this scholarship on the basis of their merit in the entrance exam
  • Samsung Star Scholarship:
  • Samsung India Electronics Private Limited awards this scholarship
  • 100% Scholarship is awarded to students are graduated from Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya.
  • This scholarship is available for B.Tech and dual degree courses only
  • Students are selected based on their score in JEE advanced.

Central Sector Scholarship:

  • This scholarship is offered explicitly to the SC/ST category students who belong to economically weak families.
  • The scholarship is restricted only to the 1st year students enrolled on B.Tech programmes.
  • To avail of the scholarship, the annual family income should be less than Rs 4,50,000 from all sources.

Foundation for Excellence:

  • This scholarship is offered to the students who excel in academics and has an excellent academic record in past
  • The scholarship is restricted only for the firstyear students enrolled for B.Tech and integrated M.Tech programmes.
  • To avail the scholarship the annual family income should be less than INR 1.8 lakhs from all sources.
  • The basic requirements for this scholarship are 70% or above in the 12th grade along with score of more than 152 in JEE Advanced.

Apart from the above-mentioned scholarships offered by the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, the Government of India or some private sources also offer financial aid in the form of scholarships to the students. The main motive of the scholarships offered is to uplift socially backward students. Following are the other scholarships offered by ministries of Indian Government:

  • DAAD Scholarship 
  • Commonwealth Scholarship 
  • NHFDC Scholarship

IIT Indore Curriculum

IIT Indore Curriculum is based on teaching students application-based.  The course curriculum is designed by industry demand so that students are industry-ready from the start of their course. On joining the institute, each student is assigned to a faculty advisor who provides guidance and suggestions related to the academic, professional, and personal growth to the assigned student. This guidance enables the students to complete their course of study smoothly and satisfactorily. 

Each department/ discipline and IIT Indore research programme has a Discipline Committee (DC) to deal with all the academic matters of its students. The committee members and conveners of DC are appointed by the concerned head of the department. The students' faculty advisors report all the academic matters related to the students to the concerned DC.
