About Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech

Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. degree at Rattan Institute of Technology and Management Faridabad, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 examination from a recognized board with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they must have studied Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Computer Science as an optional subject. It is important to secure a minimum aggregate score of 45% (40% for reserved category candidates) in the qualifying examination. Furthermore, candidates must qualify for any national/state-level engineering entrance examination such as JEE Main/Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) or any other relevant examination accepted by the institute. Meeting these criteria is essential for admission to the B.Tech. program at Rattan Institute of Technology and Management Faridabad. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 43,000 - 1,72,000. Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Rattan Institute of Technology and Management is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech Application Process

Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Rattan Institute of Technology and Management have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Rattan Institute of Technology and Management and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Rattan Institute of Technology and Management. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Rattan Institute of Technology and Management B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
