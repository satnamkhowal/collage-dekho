About GITAM Vizag

Gitam University is a private university located in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. The institute was established in 1980. Gitam University has been recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an A grade. As per NIRF Ranking 2023, Gitam University has been ranked 51st under the Engineering category. Other notable rankings include its position among the top universities in the South India region. Library, Computer Labs, Auditorium, Hostel, and Sports Complex are some of Gitam University’s facilities. Gitam University courses are offered at UG (Undergraduate), PG (Postgraduate), and Ph.D. levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the university include B.Tech, MBA, M.Sc., BBA, and Ph.D. programs. Gitam University admission is based on merit for most undergraduate courses and entrance exams for certain programs. The university accepts the GAT (Gitam Admission Test) for B.Tech and MBA admissions, along with other national-level exams like JEE Main for engineering courses. Regarding Gitam University placements, the highest package offered in 2023 was ₹30 LPA and the average package offered was ₹6 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Deloitte, and Cognizant.

Why Join GITAM University Vizag?

Here are some reasons why Gitam University stands out as a top choice for students:

  1. Diverse Academic Programs: Offers over 120 programs across undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma levels in Engineering, Management, Science, Commerce, Humanities, Law, and Social Sciences.
  2. Experienced Faculty: Faculty members have an average of 15-20 years of teaching and industry experience.
  3. State-of-the-Art Infrastructure: A 100-acre campus with facilities like Tech-Enabled Classrooms, Modern Laboratories, Hostels, a Library, an Auditorium, a Sports Complex, and a Research Center.
  4. Strong Placement Record: Placement rate of 90%, with the highest package of ₹30 LPA per annum.
  5. Global Exposure: Collaborations with institutions like the University of California, Berkeley, University of Melbourne, and the University of Manchester for research and internships.
  6. Holistic Development: Focus on extracurricular activities through Student Clubs, Societies, Annual Cultural Events, Sports Activities, and Workshops.

GITAM University Vizag Admissions 2025

GITAM University Vizag admission to its courses is done based on an entrance exam. The university accepts GITAM Admission Test (GAT) scores for admission to various programs, including B.Tech, MBA, and M.Tech courses. Candidates must also ensure that they meet the eligibility criteria for the respective courses before applying for GITAM University Vizag admission. 

GITAM University Vizag Admission Process 2025

  1. Eligibility Check: Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria for their chosen program. For example, for B.Tech, candidates must have passed 10+2 with at least 60% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. For MBA, a graduate degree with a minimum of 50% marks is required.
  2. Application Form: Fill out the application form available on the official GITAM University website.
  3. Entrance Exam: Appear for the GITAM Admission Test (GAT) on the announced date, if applicable, or other entrance exams based on the course.
  4. Document Submission: Submit required documents like class 10 and 12 mark sheets, identity proof, passport-sized photographs, and category certificates (if applicable).
  5. Counseling Process: Participate in counseling sessions as scheduled by the university, where seat allocation and document verification will take place.
  6. Fee Payment: Complete the admission process by paying the necessary fees as per the university guidelines.

FAQs about GITAM University Vizag

What is the GITAM University address?

GITAM Visakhapatnam campus is located in Gandhi Nagar, Rushikonda, Visakhapatnam in the state of Andhra Pradesh. If you do not stay close to college campus you can make a call at 0891-2840501 or email your queries at registrar@gitam.edu.

What are the qualifying marks in GITAM (GAT) University?

There is no any fixed GAT qualifying marks to take admission in the GITAM University Vizag courses. Though a merit list is prepared based on GAT cut off combined with other parameters. While attempting the GITAM Admission Test, candidates must try to secure 50% or above to increase their chances of admission.

What are the GITAM Vizag facilities offered to the students?

GITAM University Vizag campus has all the basic amenities that are necessary for the holistic development of its students. The infrastructure at GITAM University Visakhapatnam is technologically advanced and regularly updated in par with the given educational standards. Even the GITAM University Vizag facilities provided to the students are regularly upgraded as and when needed. Some of the top GITAM University Visakhapatnam facilities include a library, cafeteria, hostel, ATM, study center, medical clinic, student advisory cell, gym, computer labs and auditorium, laboratories and wifi connectivity, just to name a few.

Does GITAM Vizag accept JEE?

Yes, for GITAM Vizag admissions to B.Tech courses, candidates with a valid JEE score are eligible to apply and need not appear for GITAM Admission Test (GAT). Shortlisted candidates will be called for a personal interview, and if selected, candidates have to pay the fees and get their documents verified to confirm their GITAM Vizag admissions.

Are the degrees offered by GITAM University valid across countries like the USA and Canada?

Yes, all the degrees offered by GITAM University are UGC-recognised and thus, valid across all countries. Not only is the university UGC recognised but most of the courses are approved by AICTE. Moreover, the NIRF has ranked GITAM University in four categories namely university, engineering, pharmacy, and dental. Also, it has received many awards and recognitions. It was ranked the best private engineering institute in Andhra Pradesh.
