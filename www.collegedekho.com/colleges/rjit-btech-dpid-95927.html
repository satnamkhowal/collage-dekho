About Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech

Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. degree in Rustamji Institute of Technology (RJIT) Gwalior, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. They must have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 45% (40% for reserved categories) in the qualifying examination. Additionally, candidates have to qualify for any national or state-level engineering entrance examination, such as JEE Main or MP PET. Shortlisted candidates based on their entrance exam scores will be called for counseling rounds. Meeting these requirements ensures eligibility for the B.Tech. program at RJIT Gwalior, a renowned institution for technical education in India. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 3,15,000. Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Rustamji Institute of Technology is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech Application Process

Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Rustamji Institute of Technology have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Rustamji Institute of Technology and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Rustamji Institute of Technology. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Rustamji Institute of Technology B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
