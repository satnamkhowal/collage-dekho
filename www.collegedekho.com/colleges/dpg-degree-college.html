About DPG Degree College

DPG Degree College, established in 2015, is a private un-aided co-educational institution located in Gurugram, Haryana. The college operates a Wi-Fi-enabled campus that spans across a 10-to-23-acre area in Sector-34, situated approximately 5–6 km from HUDA City Centre. It is recognized under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a Grade A. The institution is affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU), Rohtak.

The institution provides undergraduate, postgraduate, integrated, and diploma programs across streams including engineering, pharmacy, law, management, commerce, data science, and arts. Annual tuition fees range between INR 31,000 and INR 1,50,000, with course durations varying from 1 to 5 years.

Key programs include:

B.Tech: INR 89,000 – INR 1,50,000 (4 Years)

MBA: INR 1,50,000 (2 Years)

BCA / BBA: INR 59,000 – INR 95,000 (3 Years)

B.Sc / BA / B.Com: INR 40,000 – INR 52,000 (3 Years)

Integrated Law (BA/BBA/B.Com LLB): INR 77,000 (5 Years)

Admission is primarily based on academic merit from the last qualifying examination (10+2 for undergraduate and graduation for postgraduate courses). General eligibility requires a minimum aggregate of 45% to 50% marks, with a relaxation down to 42.75% or 47.5% for SC/ST candidates. Technical courses like B.Tech require a background in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics along with valid JEE Main scores, while MBA applicants are evaluated on graduation scores and national entrance exams like CAT, MAT, XAT, or CMAT. The application process requires online or offline registration accompanied by a fee of INR 5,500.

The campus features air-conditioned classrooms, department-wise library sections, medical facilities, on-campus AC/non-AC hostels, and departmental laboratories (Physics, Chemistry, Computer, Zoology, Botany, Geology, and Geography). Sports infrastructure includes cricket, football, basketball, badminton, and volleyball facilities.

In the 2024 placement cycle, the institution recorded a highest package of Rs 9 LPA and an average package of Rs 5 LPA. Primary recruiters include TCS, Tech Mahindra, and Capgemini. Additionally, the college offers institutional merit-based scholarships ranging from INR 5,000 to INR 30,000, determined by predefined percentage slabs in the qualifying examinations.

DPG Degree College Admission Process 2026-27

The DPG Degree College welcomes applications from enthusiastic and dedicated students. With merit-based admissions, the college ensures that deserving candidates have the opportunity to pursue their academic aspirations. DPG Degree College admission process includes registration on the official website, filing the application form, meeting the eligibility, paying the application fee as per the course, etc.

Step 1: Visit the official website of DPG Degree College. 

Step 2: On the website's homepage, the 'Admissions' Tab is present. Click on that and start the application process. 

Step 3: Basic information about the applicant, including personal and educational details, is required. 

Step 4: Attach transcripts, mark sheets, and other academic documents after this. 

Step 5: The identity proof must be scanned and uploaded. 

Step 6: After everything is filled, you will be directed to the payment gateway. 

Step 7: Make the payment of Rs 5,500 and keep a receipt of the transaction in case of any difficulties later on. 

Step 8: The application has been submitted, and you will have to wait for the admissions office to get back to you. 

Step 9: To avoid rushed applications, keep an eye on the DPG Degree College official website for application deadlines.

Step 10: If any documents have to be prepared, do so well before the deadline. Late applications will not be entertained. 

Step 11: Keep physical copies of all documents handy, as you will be required to present them at the time of DPG Degree College admissions. Mere soft copies will not suffice.
