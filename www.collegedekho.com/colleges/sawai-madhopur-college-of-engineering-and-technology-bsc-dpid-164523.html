About SMCET Jaipur BSc

SMCET Jaipur BSc is a 3 - 3.5 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the BSc degree program at Sawai Madhopur College of Engineering & Technology in Jaipur, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education with a minimum aggregate of 50% in the science stream. Additionally, candidates must have studied Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects in their qualifying examination. Admissions are granted based on merit and performance in the entrance exam conducted by the college. It is also essential for candidates to meet any other specific requirements set by the college or university. The annual fee for BSc is INR 27,000 - 37,500. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for SMCET Jaipur BSc admission are given below:

SMCET Jaipur BSc Fees

SMCET Jaipur BSc fees is 27,000 INR (Yearly) and goes up to 37,500 INR (Yearly) depending on the specialization. The average fees for the BSc course is 33,933 INR (Yearly). SMCET Jaipur BSc admission is offered on the basis of the merit of the qualifying exam.

SMCET Jaipur BSc Admission 2026

Admission to the BSc at SMCET Jaipur is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the SMCET Jaipur BSc admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

SMCET Jaipur BSc Application Process

SMCET Jaipur BSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BSc course at SMCET Jaipur have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill SMCET Jaipur BSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of SMCET Jaipur and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of SMCET Jaipur. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the SMCET Jaipur BSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the SMCET Jaipur BSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of SMCET Jaipur BSc Application Form.

Documents Required for SMCET Jaipur BSc Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
