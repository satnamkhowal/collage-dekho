About Indian School of Business Management & Administration (ISBM), Gwalior

Indian School of Business Management & Administration, Gwalior is situated in Gwalior in Madhya Pradesh state of India. Established in 2002, is a Private college. The college is accredited by Govt of Madhya Pradesh, AICTE. Indian School of Business Management & Administration, Gwalior offers 28 courses across 5 streams namely Management, Hotel Management, Media and Mass Communication, Commerce and Banking, IT..Popular degrees offered at Indian School of Business Management & Administration, Gwalior include BMS, BSc, PGD, MMS, Executive MBA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Indian School of Business Management & Administration, Gwalior is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Indian School of Business Management & Administration, Gwalior, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Indian School of Business Management & Administration, Gwalior is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
