About IIT Gandhinagar

Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Gandhinagar was established in 2008. It is one of the new gen IITs. It is located in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. IIT Gandhinagar is the only institute from Gujarat that has been ranked among the top 1000 universities in the world. It has been identified as an institute of National importance by the Government of India. IIT Gandhinagar offers a variety of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in fields such as engineering, science, and humanities. UG courses like BTech, PG courses in MTech, MSc and MA. In the NIRF rankings 2024, IIT Gandhinagar achieved the 29 position in the 'Overall' category. In the 'Overall' category, IIT Gandhinagar ranked in the top 50 in the country for the 3rd consecutive year in 2024.

Admission for MTech for this year has closed, GATE applications for 2026 will start from August 2025 and the exam will be conducted in February 2026. For BTech admissions, JoSAA counseling is almost at its conclusion, the registered candidates can check their cutoff from the official site. IIT Gandhinagar achieved a 76.22% placement rate in 2024. The overall average package in 2023 was higher at 19.60 LPA. In 2024, IIT Gandhinagar top recruiters are from various sectors, including technology, consulting, finance, and banking.

IIT Gandhinagar is spread over 400 acres of campus to its name, IIT Gandhinagar is an award-winning campus equipped with all modern facilities. IIT Gandhinagar is the first IIT that provides cognitive science programs in India. It also houses more than 10+ hi-tech labs that help in fostering and nurturing the students. It also hosts an extensive library that consists of the latest information in technology, science, engineering, social science, and humanities. One can also explore the 7000+ collection of premium digital newspapers and magazines that will help in providing the daily dose of current affairs and news. Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar faculty consists of 190+ distinguished members from the best educational institutions across the world that share their knowledge and expertise among the students. With Career Development Services provided by IIT, Gandhinagar, the college has excellent placement records.

Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar Eligibility Criteria for 2025

  • For applicants wishing for admission in the B. Tech course, the selection criteria are based upon the scores of JEE Advanced, and the applicant needs to pass Class 12 with PCM stream
  • The selection criteria for M. Tech applicants include scores of GATE and the need to pass B. Tech/ BE degree with a minimum of 60% aggregate (55% aggregate for ST/ SC/ PWD students)
  • The selection criteria for MSc applicants include IIT JAM and desirable marks in B. Sc or an equivalent bachelor’s degree
  • The selection criteria for M. A. applicants include a written test and personal interview and graduation with a 55% aggregate
  • The selection criteria for PGDIIT include graduation with 55% aggregate and interview of the eligible candidates
  • To apply for PhD at the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar, the applicants need to complete a master’s in engineering/ technology with an aggregate of 60% and the eligible candidates also have to pass a written test and personal interview and give the entrance exam of UGC-NET / CSIR-NET.

IIT Gandhinagar Placements

The Career Development Service (CDS) office at the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar handles all the placements for the students. The office is committed to empowering its students and helping them realize their true potential and also aiding them to pursue their careers in any given vertical including higher study, competitive examinations, and entrepreneurship.

The resources of CDS can be availed by the students and they can explore the different career options effectively. CDS conducts sessions on resume preparation, mock screening tests, group discussions, personality development, and interview tips among others. 

The office provides a platform for recruiters and students to explore different avenues of their potential synergistic alliance. The UG programs have collectively created a placement record of 80%. For the batch of 2022, there has been a 60% increase in PPO and 30% increase in acceptance rate, and a 55% increase in the number of students placed.

IIT Gandhinagar Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar (IITgn) Campus

The 400 acres campus of the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar is a state-of-art, award-winning vibrant 21st-century campus. 

The campus houses 

  • 6 hostel blocks for accommodating UG and PG students
  • Common hostel library and reading space
  • Central dining facility for all students
  • Two independent mess facilities
  • Well equipped kitchen
  • Other dining facilities include large central cafeteria, several small food and beverage joints
  • Recreation spaces and different sports fields

One of the most distinctive architectural features of the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar is the 100 feet tall landmark tower. 6 architectural firms from different parts of the country have collaborated on this venture and a wide range of innovative technologies have been implemented to make it an intelligent campus. 

Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar (IITgn) Infrastructure

  • The library at the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar houses a rich collection of books on different subjects including study materials for engineering, science, arts, and social science subjects.
  • A sports complex is available on the campus which includes a basketball court, cricket ground, football ground, and volleyball courts among others.
  • Midas touch salon which is a unisex salon is present on the premises of the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar which takes care after the basic grooming needs of the students. 
  • Lab facilities are available and the campus also includes a gym, medical facilities, auditorium, and wi-fi enabled campus.

IIT Gandhinagar Scholarships

The students of the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar have access to different scholarships and financial aid. The schemes are offered by governments, donors, alumni, or by the institution itself. 

Some scholarships by IIT Gandhinagar are

  • Wavering tuition fees for undergraduate students 
    • Whose family income is Rs 100,000 get full remission of tuition fees
    • Whose family income is between Rs 1 Lakh to Rs 5 Lakh get half remission of the tuition fees
    • Students whose family income is less than INR 9 Lakh can have access to interest-free loans to cover the entire tuition fees under the Vidyalakshmi scheme
  • Free basic messing and pocket allowance are provided to SC/ ST students applying for B. Tech and M. Sc programs whose annual income does not exceed Rs 4.5 Lakh
  • Students with the highest CPI in their respective disciplines are awarded Rs 2000 per month for 10 consecutive months. But if the student has won a scholarship of equal or of higher value, then they can only receive INR 5000 as a one-time receipt.
  • Each year, six scholarships are provided to students to encourage excellence in sports and games. Students can receive Rs 2000 per month for 10 consecutive months. 

There are also several financial aids and scholarship programs that a student can take advantage of, visit the official website of the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar for more information.

Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar Facilities

  • Within the premises of the Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar, there are eight centrally air-conditioned hostels. Hostels are managed by hostel management committees. These hostels include a mini library with a reading area, convenience shops and eateries, a lounge area, music and art rooms. A resident caretaker looks after the routine maintenance checkups of the hostels. Basic facilities are provided to each student at the beginning of the academic year. Pantries are also available on each floor of the hostels.
  • The Institute serves nutritious food to its students throughout the year. There are several eateries and convenience stores available on the premises of the campus. Indian Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar was awarded with the “Eat Right Campus” certificate with a 5-star rating from the Minister of Health and Family Welfare.
  • Students have access to medical care 24*7. Qualified medical practitioners along with a physiotherapist, nurses, an ambulance and a fully equipped dispensary are available on the campus.  Hospitalization of all students is covered under the institute's medical insurance policy.
