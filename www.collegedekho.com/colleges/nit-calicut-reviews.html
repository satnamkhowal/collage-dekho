National Institute of Technology Calicut

Overall The campus has such a great vibe and the extracurricular activities are great has a ton of options to choose from. Life here is heavily centred on Tathva and Ragam - probably two of the best few campus fests in South India!

Infrastructure NIT has top-notch infrastructure. The labs are well-equipped, the classroom modern, and the Wi-Fi is reliable. The library is resourceful.There are common rooms, Wi-Fi, study rooms, playing courts for basketball, badminton, cricket, volleyball, open gym, etc

My journey at Nit Calicut

Overall It was very tough decision for me to move to such a long-distance college. But it was a great experience here. This college taught me to face real-life problems and if you are an extrovert kind of person you will surely enjoy it here in Kerala there are different spots where you can explore and go on adventure trips with friends. That thing is an important thing you should do. but if you are a purely vegetarian kind of person then you may be struggling because here veg food even in restaurants not as good as north Indian food. Here restriction is only till the first year after that you can go anywhere at any time.

Placement placements are quite good that is why this college comes under top 3 nit colleges in terms of placements and here you can sit in any company's placements based on the cgpa criteria's set by the companies. and here companies start coming after your 4th semester of graduation to offer internships where some students even get PPO (pre placements offers) and many get placed till starting of their 4th year. and their is a platform named as carrier development point which prepares you for placement interviews and all.

Infrastructure here infrastructure is good but it is divided into different parts due to the main road passing through. so you have to go a slightly long to go to classes so you should go early otherwise some professors may not allow you to enter even if you are 5 minutes late. but now an underground subway construction is going on in college to connect campus. although buses go from mega hostels to campus but usually they are filled with massive crowds. so keep in mind if you try to go by bus either you go early or have the power to suffer in that huge crowd. there is a huge library where you can study at any time any where and issue books from crores of books between 6 am to 12 am. and there are magazines and newspapers also provided every day.

Faculty faculties are very humble if you need any kind of help but strict in terms of rules and regulations. every faculty is qualified with a PhD and classified into different types based on their experience and projects done by them. such as assistant professor(2-3 years) and associate professor (5-7 years) and professor (8-10 years). there is no restriction for you to do research under any professor but you need to clear the min cgpa criteria which is around 7 to 7.5 or it depending on your work and professor. you can approach them to their office between 9 to 5 or you can directly mail them regarding any issue.

Hostel There are a total of 11 hostels and 3 Mega hostels counting all Btech, Mtech, PhD, MBA, BSC, Barch, etc types of students. In your first, you may struggle because, in a room of a capacity of 3 people, there are 4 students in each room. where 2 students will be from Kerala and the other two will be from other states than Kerala. there are playing courts like a badminton court, open gym, basketball court, volleyball court, cricket net, etc. outside the different hostels and inside each hostel there is a canteen to buy necessary items and one common room where there is a big TV and playing equipment like a chessboard, carrom board, tennis table, etc. and after second year you will be shifted to mega hostel where two person will be living in one room and there are study rooms and one mega xerox and canteen for necessary items and other things same as other hostels.

College Best Things

Overall For my JEE Main rank, I got this chemical engineering so I opted for it. Many intentions and teachers are good and facilities are good. They introduce the correct and useful curriculum. Here, fests are not as good as expected.

Placement Students in their seventh semester are eligible to participate in the placement process at our college. Renowned companies such as Oracle, Bajaj, and DE Shaw have visited our campus, offering attractive opportunities. The highest package offered was approximately 60 Lacs, while the average package stands at around 12 Lacs.

Infrastructure There will be AC, labs, a library, and study rooms. All classrooms will be well furnished so you will feel very comfortable listening to classes. There will be availability of Wi-Fi across campus and hostels. In case of a medical emergency, there will be a health care centre that is totally free.

Faculty The teachers exhibit a commendable level of assistance and engagement, although a few may display strictness and arrogance towards students. However, considering their age, it is understandable for occasional outbursts to occur. The curriculum comprises a mid-semester examination and an end-semester examination. It proves highly beneficial for students inclined towards the core subjects, although the majority of students express a keen interest in the IT field.

Hostel The washrooms are maintained in a clean and tidy manner. The quality of the mess food is not terrible, but dishes prepared with coconut oil may not be enjoyable for students from outside the state. The promotion of sports and games is highly encouraged, and there is a health center available for medical services.

The Reality of placements in NITs

Placement Almost 90% students get placed in core branches such as civil, mechanical. In CS and ECE department placements are almost 100%. Over 131 companies visited the campus last year giving about 1000 job opportunities to the students. Average package goes around 15 lakhs per annum.

Infrastructure The campus is built beautifully and is spacious too. There are separate academic building and department buildings. There are separate labs for each department with all necessary equipment such as Fluid Machinery Lab, Hydraulic Lab, Offshore Lab, Electrical Lab, CAD Lab and many more.

Faculty The quality of faculties and their teaching style is very effective and as per the needs of today's industry. Almost all of them are graduated from NITs and IITs with PHDs from foreign universities. They are well experienced and provide excellent knowledge of the respective subjects.

Hostel There are in total 11 hostels. First year students are given separate hostels. From second year onwards, students are given rooms in Mega hostels. Girls have their own hostels situated in greenery. Overall, the hostels are nice and decent. One issue with hostels is that there are common washrooms. Otherwise its fine.
