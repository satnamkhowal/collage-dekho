IFIM Bangalore Job Ready Degree

If you are looking for a course which will help you get placed right after you graduate, then you should consider IFIM Bangalore job ready courses. The assured courses at IFIM Bangalore are BBA in Digital Marketing (IXP), BCA in Full Stack Development, and B.Com in Fintech (IXP). These courses help students get industry-relevant knowledge and acquire job-specific skills. The fees for IFIM Bangalore job ready courses range from Rs 1,10,000 to Rs 1,85,000 per year. Students with merit who live in Karnataka can get scholarships at IFIM Bangalore. Interested students can check the table below for details on IFIM Bangalore job ready courses.

IFIM Bangalore Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

IFIM Bangalore job ready courses admission process requires candidates to first meet the required eligibility criteria. Then they will be called for a personal interview. After passing the interview round they will receive an admission letter from the institute.
