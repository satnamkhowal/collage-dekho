About Pt. L.R. College of Technology- Technical Campus

Pt. L.R. College of Technology- Technical Campus is situated in Faridabad in Haryana state of India. Established in 2010, PLRCT is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak and accredited by AICTE. Pt. L.R. College of Technology- Technical Campus offers 16 courses across 4 streams namely Engineering, Pharmacy, Management, IT..Popular degrees offered at Pt. L.R. College of Technology- Technical Campus include B.Tech, B.Pharm, BBA, BCA, M.Tech..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Pt. L.R. College of Technology- Technical Campus is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Pt. L.R. College of Technology- Technical Campus, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Pt. L.R. College of Technology- Technical Campus is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
