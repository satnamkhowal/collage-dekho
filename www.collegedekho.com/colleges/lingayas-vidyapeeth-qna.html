How can I get admission in lingyas University

S
Saniya Pahwa •  Sep-14-2023

Dear Rashi

You can apply for admission to Lingaya's Vidyapeeth if you meet the basic eligibility criteria for your desired course. The application process can be carried out in online as well as offline modes. In order to apply online, all you have to do is visit the official website of the university and fill out the application form available in the admission section. After uploading the needed documents and paying the fee, your form will be submitted. Further, for Lingaya's Vidyapeeth admission in the offline mode, you can visit the campus and fill out the form in person and submit it. Once you have successfully applied, you must appear for the Lingaya's Vidyapeeth Entrance Test (LET). If shortlisted, you will be called for further counselling and admission rounds. 

Hope this helps!

what is fashion design : I study fashion design

r
rubina •  Mar-24-2023
Fashion designing is among the highest paying and popularly growing field pursued by many creative minds. LPU, or Lovely Professional University, offers a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Fashion Design program at its School of Fashion Design. The program is designed to provide students with a comprehensive education in the field of fashion design, including design principles, textile science, garment construction, and business aspects of the industry. The program is delivered through a combination of classroom instruction, studio work, and practical experience in the form of internships and industry projects. Students will also have the opportunity to participate in fashion shows, design competitions, and other events to showcase their work and gain exposure to the fashion industry.The curriculum of the program is regularly updated to keep pace with the changing trends and technologies in the fashion industry, and the faculty members are experienced professionals who bring industry knowledge and expertise to the classroom. Overall, pursuing fashion design at LPU can be a great choice for those who are passionate about the field and want to acquire the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in the industry. Fashion designing aspirants can opt for a variety of professions and the most popular among them are as follows: • Fashion Designer • Fashion Illustrator • Fashion Stylist • Fashion Coordinator • Fashion Consultant • Fashion Merchandiser
