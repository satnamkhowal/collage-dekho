About BSACET Mathura B.Tech

BSACET Mathura B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the B.Tech degree program at B.S.A. College of Engineering & Technology, Mathura, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. They must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in the qualifying examination. Additionally, candidates must have a valid score in JEE Main or UPSEE entrance exams. Reservation criteria as per government regulations will also be applicable. Candidates are required to fulfill all eligibility criteria set by the college to be considered for admission to the B.Tech program. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 75,626. BSACET Mathura B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at BSACET Mathura is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the BSACET Mathura B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

BSACET Mathura B.Tech Application Process

BSACET Mathura B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at BSACET Mathura have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill BSACET Mathura B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of BSACET Mathura and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of BSACET Mathura. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the BSACET Mathura B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the BSACET Mathura B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of BSACET Mathura B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for BSACET Mathura B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
