About Satyam Shivam Sundaram Group of Institutions

Satyam Shivam Sundaram Group of Institutions (SSSGOI) Patna is a private educational group established in 2000 in Patna, Bihar. The group operates more than 13 constituent colleges and seven CBSE-affiliated schools across Patna, Jehanabad, Nalanda, Barauni, and Greater Noida, serving over 15,000 students with the support of more than 1,000 faculty and staff members.

SSSGOI offers more than 22 programmes across Engineering, Pharmacy, Nursing, Paramedical, Management, Education, Information Technology, and Vocational Studies at undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and ITI levels. For the 2026-27 academic session, annual course fees range from INR 12,500 for the ITI Fitter programme to INR 2.00 Lakhs for the B.Sc. Nursing programme. The programmes are approved by the respective regulatory bodies, including AICTE, PCI, NCTE, and NCVT, and are affiliated with Aryabhatta Knowledge University and Patliputra University, as applicable.

The SSSGOI campus spans more than 50 acres and provides modern academic and student support facilities, including separate hostels for boys and girls, a library, transport services, sports facilities, and a Wi-Fi-enabled campus. Admission to most programmes is based on merit in the qualifying examination, while programmes regulated by the respective statutory bodies follow the prescribed admission process.

Satyam Shivam Sundaram Group of Institutions Admission 2026

Admissions to Satyam Shivam Sundaram Group of Institutions Patna (SSSGOI) are open for the 2026-27 academic session. Admission to most programmes is based on merit in the qualifying examination. Admission to B.Sc. Nursing may require a valid score in JENPAS UG (Bihar) or an equivalent state-level nursing entrance examination. B.Ed and D.El.Ed admissions may also require a valid score in the prescribed state-level entrance examination, along with the qualifying degree. Admission to Pharmacy programmes is based on merit in the 10+2 examination with Science subjects.

How to Apply for SSG Admission 2026

Step 1: Visit the official SSG website and access the online admission application form.

Step 2: Complete the application form with the required personal, academic, and contact details.

Step 3: Upload the prescribed documents, including academic certificates, photograph, identity proof, and category certificate (if applicable).

Step 4: Pay the prescribed application or admission fee through the available online payment mode.

Step 5: Submit the application form and download or save the confirmation page for future reference.

Step 6: Report to the allotted institute or campus with the original documents for verification and complete the admission formalities.

Note: Candidates may contact the SSG admissions office for assistance regarding the application process, eligibility, or document requirements.

SSSGOI Patna Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

Satyam Shivam Sundaram Group of Institutions (SSSGOI) Patna is located on a 50+ acre campus in Patna, Bihar. The campus provides academic infrastructure and student support facilities for programmes across Engineering, Pharmacy, Nursing, Management, Education, Paramedical, and Vocational Studies. The campus offers the following facilities:

  • Hostel: Separate hostel accommodation for boys and girls with dedicated wardens, furnished rooms, a mess facility, water purifiers, water coolers, television, and indoor games.
  • Library: Central library with reference books, journals, magazines, and other learning resources covering various disciplines. The collection is updated regularly to support academic and research activities.
  • Transport: College bus services connecting the campus with different parts of Patna and nearby areas.
  • Digital Classrooms and Laboratories: Modern classrooms and computer laboratories to support teaching and practical learning across various programmes.
  • Wi-Fi Campus: Campus-wide Wi-Fi connectivity for students and faculty.
  • Sports Facilities: Indoor games such as Carrom, Chess, and Table Tennis, along with outdoor sports facilities on campus.
  • Cafeteria: Cafeteria facility for students and staff.
