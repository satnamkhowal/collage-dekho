About Sharda University

Sharda University is a private university in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, established in 2009. The university is recognised by UGC and accredited by NAAC with an A+ grade. Sharda University Noida campus spans 63 acres and is home to 19,200+ students from 95+ countries and 29 Indian states. The university has 17 schools offering 135+ programmes at diploma, UG, PG, integrated, and doctoral levels in Engineering, Medicine, Dentistry, Law, Business, Pharmacy, Nursing, Design, Allied Health Sciences, and more.

In NIRF 2025, Sharda University ranked 87th in the university category and 101-150 in the engineering category. The university conducts admissions through the Sharda University Admission Test (SUAT) for most of its programs. However, it also accepts national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, NEET, CLAT, CAT, MAT, XAT, NATA, and GATE for admissions. Sharda University cutoff 2026 for MBA is 65 percentile in CAT/XAT or 75 percentile in MAT. 

As per Sharda University placements 2025, the highest package stood at INR 1.7 Cr. More than 600 recruiters participated in the placement drive, including 150+ MNCs and 55+ Fortune 500 companies. Top recruiters included Cognizant, Amazon, Johnson Controls, Mahindra Rise, Videocon, MTS, and Tech Mahindra. The university also has 330+ academic MoUs with universities across the USA, UK, Italy, Canada, Russia, and more.

Sharda University Admission 2026

Sharda University admissions for 2026-27 are open. Candidates can apply for the desired program at www.sharda.ac.in/apply. For admission to most of its programs, Sharda University conducts SUAT in online mode across multiple exam centres. Students who have valid scores in national-level exams like JEE Main, NEET, GATE, CAT, MAT, XAT, CLAT, NATA, or CUET are exempted from SUAT for the respective courses.

Minimum eligibility for UG programmes at Sharda University is 10+2 with 50–60% marks in relevant subjects from a recognised board. PG programmes require a bachelor's degree with 50–55% marks in the relevant discipline. SC/ST candidates get a 2.5% marks relaxation; Differently Abled (PH) candidates get 5%. Candidates must check sharda.ac.in/admissions for updates.

Sharda University Admission Process 2026 (Step-by-Step)

  • Step 1: Visit the official admission portal at www.sharda.ac.in/apply and create your account.
  • Step 2: Register for SUAT at suat.sharda.ac.in OR apply with valid national exam scores (JEE Main, NEET, CAT, etc.) — such candidates are exempt from SUAT. SUAT Application fee is INR 1,500 for male and INR 1,000 for female students.
  • Step 3: Book your SUAT exam slot and appear for the online test. MBA applicants also need to appear for a Group Discussion and Personal Interview round.
  • Step 4: Check SUAT results on your registered email ID. Accept your programme offer and confirm the admission.
  • Step 5: Submit documents for verification and pay the admission fee (INR 30,000 one-time, non-refundable) and first semester programme fee to secure your seat.

Applicants must carry academic mark sheets, valid ID proof, payment receipt, and passport photos at the time of admission. Entrance exam scorecards are required only when specified for the chosen programme. For more details, refer to Sharda University Admission 2026.

Sharda University Campus

Sharda University campus is spread across 63 acres in Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida. The university campus includes smart classrooms, advanced research centres, hostels, sports facilities, medical infrastructure, transport services, and various student support amenities. 

  • Academic Infrastructure: IBM Labs, VLSI Design Center of Competency, and 12 advanced research centres for interdisciplinary research across engineering, health sciences, STEM, and social sciences. The campus also has Wi-Fi-enabled smart classrooms and modern academic blocks.
  • Library: Central library with digital resources, e-journals, online databases, and dedicated study zones for students and researchers.
  • Medical Facilities: Sharda Hospital, a 1,600+ bed super-speciality teaching and research hospital located within the campus, offering healthcare services, clinical training, and emergency support.
  • Hostels: Separate hostels for boys and girls with accommodation capacity for 3,800+ students. Hostel facilities include Wi-Fi, mess, dining halls, laundry services, common areas, and AC/non-AC room options. Hostel fees at Sharda University range from INR 1,38,000 to INR INR 2,35,000 per year.
  • Sports Facilities: Sports complex with multiple courts, tracks, grounds, and facilities for indoor and outdoor sports activities. The university also promotes participation through technical, cultural, literary, and sports clubs.
  • Auditoriums & Events: Five fully air-conditioned auditoriums equipped with modern audio systems and projectors for seminars, conferences, cultural programmes, and university events.
  • Transport Facilities: University-operated bus fleet connecting Delhi, Noida, and Greater NCR for students and staff commuting to campus.
  • Campus Facilities: Banking services, postal facilities, grocery stores, laundry services, dining facilities, and 24x7 Wi-Fi connectivity are available inside the campus premises.
  • Student Life & Culture: Sharda University has students from 95+ countries. The university hosts annual cultural festivals and technical fests with participation from national and international students. The campus also operates “Suno Sharda 90.8 FM”, an in-house community radio station.
  • Diversity: As per official university data, female students constitute around 45% of the student population, which is among the highest representations in Delhi-NCR universities.

Sharda University FAQs

Does Sharda University have a good placement?

Yes, Sharda University has a good placement across different available programs. The university has a 100% placement rate in the year 2022 for the MBA and this year the highest package bagged was Rs 40 LPA. Students can check the website for more details about placements.

Is there any entrance exam for Sharda University?

Yes, Sharda University conducts its university-level admission entrance exam referred to as Sharda University Admission Test (SUAT). It is held for admission to various UG and PG-level courses. However, if students have valid scores in different national-level entrance exams as suggested by the university, they will be exempted from appearing in SUAT.

How is the Sharda University placement?

The placements at Sharda University Greater Noida have always been good. It has maintained a record of 65% to 100% placements since its inception. There is a dedicated placement department that conducts the various placement activities and invites numerous reputed companies like TCS, Wipro, HSBC, etc. to the campus. In the Sharda University campus placements of the year 2022, the highest placement package offered to the students was Rs 45 LPA. More than 550 companies took part in the Sharda University placements. The Sharda University average package offered to the students during the campus placements for that year was Rs 5.65 LPA. Overall, the placements are great, as the placement department ensures that every student secures proper job opportunities.

Is the Sharda University Admission Test (SUAT) necessary for admission?

The Sharda University Admission Test or SUAT is a single platform Admission Test conducted by the university itself to grant admission to the various UG and PG courses offered here. The SUAT is accepted for courses like B.Tech, BCA, BBA, B.Com, BA (Hons),  B.Sc, MCA, MBA, M.Sc. (Nursing) and integrated courses. As the university does not have the provision for direct admission, this admission test acts as the basic requirement for Sharda University admissions. Candidates who have taken the National or State Level Tests such as JEE Main, CAT, MAT, CLAT, LSAT, NATA, etc. are exempted from taking the SUAT, as they will be considered for admission based on those exam scores.

What are the Sharda University B Pharma fees?

The four-year undergraduate Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) degree at Sharda University introduces students to the characteristics and effects of pharmaceutical medications. The syllabus follows guidelines set forth by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). Pharmaceutics, pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacognosy, and pharmacology are among the fundamental pharmacy courses. The Sharda University B Pharma fees are Rs 2,00,000 (1st year), Rs 2,06,000 (2nd year), Rs 2,12,180 (3rd year) and Rs 2,18,545 (4th year). Students after graduating with a Sharda University BPharma course degree, will be able to secure job roles like Drug Inspector, Hospital Drug Coordinator, Analytical Chemist, Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Executive, Medical Sales Representative, etc.

What is Sharda University NIRF Ranking 2023?

The official website (nirfindia.org) has published the NIRF Ranking for Sharda University for 2023. With a score of 44.11 in the University category, the acquired rank is 87. It received a score of 45.60 and a rank of 62 in the Pharmacy category. Dr. Rajkumar Ranjan Singh, Minister of State for Education and External Affairs, announced the NIRF 2023 University rankings. Some of the factors used by the NIRF 2023 to rank the universities include teaching, resources, graduation rates, inclusion research, professional practice, learning, promotion, and reputation. The NIRF Ranking list included Sharda Noida in the University and Pharmacy sections.

What are the requirements for Sharda University course admissions?

Sharda University admissions are determined on the basis of the academic excellence of the applicant from their most recent qualifying exams, valid entrance exam scores and GD-PI, which are conducted by the university itself. For Sharda University course admissions, it accepts scores from the national level entrance exams such as JEE Main, GATE, NEET, CLAT, CAT, MAT, NET, SUAT, and other exams. The university also conducted its own entrance test called the Sharda University Admission Test (SUAT). Students who already have the national-level entrance test scores will be exempted from SUAT All applicants must satisfy Sharda University admission requirements to proceed further with the selection process.

What is the Sharda University student login?

A login website where students can enter a username and password to access Sharda University’s programmes and educational resources is known as a student portal. It is distinctive and a student's login credentials are required to enter. Candidates have the option for Login and New Registrations. From the student dashboard, candidates will be able to check admission, results, exams and related updates. In the student login, they will also be able to check their daily attendance records. The Sharda University student login (student.esharda.net) is used to refer to the login page where students can enter their username and password to access the programmes and other learning-related materials.

What are the Sharda University BCA fees?

The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) is an undergraduate programme of three years duration offered by the Sharda School of Engineering and Technology. It offers two specialisations namely Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning and Cloud Computing and IoT. The programme is suitable for candidates who seek to make a career in the IT field. Candidates are able to work as testers, developers, web designers, etc. For admission to this programme, one must have passed 10+2 with at least 50% with English and Mathematics as main subjects. Candidates have to take the SUAT exam, to secure admission. The Sharda University BCA fees are Rs 1,54,500 per year.

What is the Sharda University B.Sc Agriculture fees?

The Sharda School of Agricultural Sciences (SAS) offers a four-year professional bachelor's degree programme in the field of Agriculture. The B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture programme is spread over eight semesters.  The Sharda University BSc Agriculture curriculum involves visits and hands-on experience in laboratories, experimental plots, poly houses and fields in peri-urban, rural and industrial settings. The course offered both theory and practicals. Candidates who have passed 10+2 with at least 55% marks in PCM/ PCB and English are eligible. The Sharda University BSc Agriculture fees is Rs 1,50,000 (first year), Rs 1,54,500 (second year), Rs 1,59,135 (third year) and Rs 1,63,909 (fourth year).

What is Sharda University admission process?

The application process for admission is done online on the university’s official website. Candidates can follow the steps listed below to apply for any undergraduate or postgraduate course at Sharda University Greater Noida and complete the admission process: Visit the official website at sharda.ac.in. 'Apply Now' tab should be chosen. Fill out the required fields on the Sharda University application form 2023. Create your System ID, confirm the programme or specialism, and then continue with the fee payment. Sharda University application fees are Rs. 1,500 for men and Rs. 1,000 for women. The application number will be confirmed to your registered email address and mobile number.

What is the Sharda University MBA eligibility criteria?

At Sharda University, the two-year MBA programme is offered which is broken up into four semesters. Admission is determined by a student's graduation grade, which must be at least 50%. English proficiency is a must for all applicants for the Sharda University MBA admission process. Admission is granted on the basis of merit, entrance exam scores and Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) sessions. It is also necessary to pass tests like the SUAT, MAT (with a 75% percentile or above), or CAT/ XAT (with a 65% percentile or above), as well as GD and PI sessions. Scholarships are also offered to students securing higher percentages in CAT/ MAT, etc. entrance exams.

What is Sharda University address?

Founded in 2009, Sharda University (SU) has been well-known in the sectors of education and research. With students from more than 85 nations and academic partnerships with more than 250 universities across the world, including those in the US, UK, Russia, Slovenia, Italy, and Canada, Sharda Noida has emerged as India's single global university. It is accredited by NAAC with an A+ Grade. It offers over 200 UG, PG, Diploma, Doctoral and Certificate programmes. The Sharda University address is plot no 32, 34, Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201310.

Is direct admission available at Sharda University?

No, at Sharda University Greater Noida, admission is conducted on the basis of both the candidate's academic merit from their previous qualifying exam and valid entrance examination scores. For admission to same courses such as B.Tech, MBA, MBBS, etc. national level entrance exam scores are accepted such as JEE Main, CAT, NEET UG, etc. However, for other courses, admission is granted on the basis of the Sharda University Admission Test (SUAT). Candidates have to meet the basic eligibility requirements to be able to apply for their chosen course. For all undergraduate programmes completion of Class 12 from one of India's recognised boards, is required. Graduation from any Indian university that is regarded as a prerequisite for all postgraduate programmes. Before making a final admission decision, Sharda University also has the option to assess a candidate's aptitude using GD/PI.
