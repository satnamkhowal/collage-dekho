About IIT Guwahati

IIT Guwahati, established in 1994, is a premier government institute in India known for its scenic 700-acre Brahmaputra riverside campus. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, dual degree, and doctoral programs in engineering, design, sciences, management, and humanities. Ranked 8th in engineering by NIRF 2025. Admissions are based on national-level entrance exams like JEE Advanced, UCEED, IIT JAM, GATE, and CEED. The campus is equipped with modern hostels, advanced labs, libraries, sports complexes, medical facilities, and 24/7 high-speed internet. IITG emphasizes research and innovation, with 10 major research centers, over 150 patents filed, and global collaborations with MIT, Duke, and NUS. Students actively participate in cultural, technical, entrepreneurial, and sports events like Alcheringa, Techniche, Inter-IIT sports, and UDGAM. Scholarships, merit aids, and stipends support deserving students.

 IIT Guwahati’s placements are strong, with top recruiters like Google, Microsoft, Bajaj, and HPCL offering packages up to ₹2.05 Cr. The institute combines academic excellence with holistic student growth.

IIT Guwahati – Additional FAQs for Students

What kind of student activities and festivals are held at IIT Guwahati?
IIT Guwahati hosts many popular festivals and events, including Alcheringa (cultural fest), Techniche (tech fest), and UDGAM (entrepreneurship summit). With over 300 clubs, students participate in sports, arts, coding, robotics, debate, music, and more, creating a vibrant campus atmosphere.
Does IIT Guwahati offer scholarships for students?
Yes, IIT Guwahati provides several scholarships such as IMS, Merit-cum-Means, SC/ST fee waivers, and GATE stipends for postgraduate students. These financial aids help both meritorious and economically weaker students manage their academic expenses.
What makes IIT Guwahati a preferred choice for students?
Students choose IIT Guwahati for its scenic 700-acre Brahmaputra riverside campus, strong academic programs, modern labs, active student clubs, and high placement packages. Its national ranking, research output, and supportive campus life make it one of India’s top technical institutes.

For IIT Guwahati B.Tech Admissions

Candidates must obtain a minimum of 75% in 10+2 or equivalent examinations with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as primary courses to be eligible for admission to B.Tech at IIT Guwahati. Admission to the B.Tech programme would be based on a valid JEE Main score followed by JEE Advanced.

For IIT Guwahati B.Des Admissions

A 10+2 in any stream from a recognised board is required for admission to the B.Des. programme. Students are admitted to the B.Des. programme through the UCEED test, which is administered by IIT Bombay. For more information on UCEED, go to the UCEED website.

For IIT Guwahati M.Tech Admissions

Candidates must possess a qualifying UG degree in their relevant specialisation. They must have a minimum CPI of 6.0 on a 10-point scale or a minimum of 60% of total/aggregate marks in UG Degree. Selection for M.Tech at IIT Guwahati is based on GATE marks. Candidates possessing a B.Tech degree from an IIT are exempted from the eligibility for a valid GATE score.

For IIT Guwahati M.Des Admissions

With CEED

  • Candidates in this category must have passed the All India Common Entrance Examination for Design with a valid score (CEED). A four-year bachelor's degree or equivalent in design, engineering, architecture, interior design, or a related profession with a minimum CPI of 6.0 out of 10 or 60 marks is required.
  • NID's 4-year Professional Diploma in Design or CEPT's 5-year Professional Diploma in Design, with a minimum CPI of 6.0 out of 10 or 60% of marks.
  • The final admission of candidates in this category is determined by their performance in the written test and/or interview performed by the relevant academic department/centre, as well as their academic credentials.

Without CEED

  • Candidates in this category must hold a B.Tech or BDes degree from an IIT and have a minimum CPI / CGPA of 8.0 on a 10-point scale. They are exempt from having to have a valid CEED score. A four-year bachelor's degree or equivalent in design, engineering, architecture, interior design, or a related profession with a minimum CPI of 6.0 out of 10 or 60 marks is required.
  • NID's 4-year Professional Diploma in Design or CEPT's 5-year Professional Diploma in Design, with a minimum CPI of 6.0 out of 10 or 60% of marks.
  • The final admission of candidates in this category is determined by their performance in the written test and/or interview performed by the relevant academic department/centre, as well as their academic credentials.

Students applying for Sponsored and Part-Time admission must have at least two years of relevant work experience.

For IIT Guwahati M.Sc Admissions

Candidates must possess a B.Sc degree with a valid IIT-JAM score.

For IIT Guwahati M.Sc (R) Admissions 

Candidates must have a B.E/B.Tech/M.Sc. with a minimum of 60% marks in the Qualifying Degree in a relevant field

For IIT Guwahati MBA Admissions

  • Candidates must possess a Bachelor's or Master's degree with a minimum grade point average of 60% or a CPI or CGPA of 6.0 out of 10.
  • A relaxation of 5% in marks (i.e., minimum 55 per cent) or equivalent in CGPA (i.e., minimum 5.50 on a 10-point scale) is allowed for SC/ST/PwD applicants.
  • Applicants who are in their final year of studies are also eligible to apply. If they are chosen, their admission will be conditional on them meeting the required eligibility criteria by October of the following academic year.
  • A qualifying Common Admissions Test (CAT) score (CAT).

For IIT Guwahati PhD Admissions

  • Candidates must have the minimum qualifying marks in their relevant master’s degree.
  • The final selection is based on a seminar/presentation on a topic of the candidate's choice pertaining to their discipline that will be part of the admissions process.
  • Candidates must create portfolios, projects, and job experiences that are related to their research interests.
  • A formal interview/test will follow the lecture, covering fundamental and advanced themes in the connected issues.

IIT Guwahati Admission FAQs

What are the eligibility criteria for IIT Guwahati programs?
Eligibility varies by program: B.Tech needs 75% in 10+2 with PCM, B.Des needs 10+2, M.Tech requires a relevant UG degree, M.Des requires CEED or IIT degree, M.Sc requires B.Sc and IIT JAM, MBA needs a bachelor’s with 60% or CAT score, and PhD requires a relevant master’s with seminars or interviews for final selection.
How can students apply for IIT Guwahati admissions?
Students must apply through the respective entrance exam portals. For B.Tech, JEE Advanced scores are required; for B.Des, UCEED; for M.Tech, GATE; for M.Sc, IIT JAM; and for M.Des, CEED. Sponsored or part-time applicants need work experience, and final admission may include interviews, written tests, or portfolio reviews depending on the program.
What courses are offered at IIT Guwahati?
IIT Guwahati offers B.Tech, B.Des, M.Tech, M.Des, M.Sc, MBA, PhD, and dual degree programs across engineering, design, science, and management. Each program has specific eligibility criteria, and admissions are based on national exams like JEE Advanced, UCEED, GATE, IIT JAM, and CEED, ensuring students are selected for their merit and field of interest.

IIT Guwahati Campus and Facilities 2026

The Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, founded in 1994, is nestled in the lap of the seven sisters, amidst breathtaking mountains and the mighty Brahmaputra's shore. The organisation was founded with the primary objective of serving the country's brightest brains and of pushing them to their utmost in scientific, mental, physical, and social realms.

The campus of the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati is located on a 285-hectare parcel of land on the north bank of the Brahmaputra River, some 20 kilometres from the city centre. The campus provides a great academic environment, with the gorgeous Brahmaputra on one side and hills and lakes on the other.

IIT Guwahati is considered one of India's most attractive and scenic campuses. Furthermore, the institute's perfect balance of natural beauty and magnificent architecture adds to its allure.

Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati Infrastructure

Academic departments, centres, labs, classrooms, and other facilities are housed in the 74,000-square metre Academic Complex. The Institute's offices are housed in a large Administrative Building. A contemporary Computer Centre and Central Library are located in the Computer and Communication Centre. 

A lecture hall complex with four 250-seat lecture rooms, a 1500-seat auditorium with a state-of-the-art sound system, a smaller mini-auditorium, and a conference complex with four conference halls provide space for teaching and organising various events/programs.

The Lakshminath Bezbaroa Central Library, is a portal to the latest information in the sciences, engineering, technology, humanities, and social sciences. IIT Guwahati Scholarships FAQs

What financial support do postgraduate students receive at IIT Guwahati?
Postgraduate students at IIT Guwahati who qualify through GATE receive a monthly stipend of ₹12,400. SC/ST students and Merit-cum-Means scholars also receive fee waivers or yearly grants based on eligibility.
Who is eligible for the IMS scholarship at IIT Guwahati?
The IMS scholarship at IIT Guwahati is awarded to the top 25% of B.Tech and M.Sc students whose family income is below ₹5 lakh. Eligible students receive ₹25,000 per year to support their academic expenses, encouraging strong academic performance throughout the program.
What types of scholarships does IIT Guwahati offer to support students?
IIT Guwahati offers several scholarships such as IMS, Merit-cum-Means, SC/ST tuition waivers, and GATE stipends. These scholarships help students by covering tuition fees, providing yearly financial support, or offering monthly stipends. They are designed to assist meritorious students and those from financially weaker backgrounds.
