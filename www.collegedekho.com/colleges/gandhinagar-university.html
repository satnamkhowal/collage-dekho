About Gandhinagar University

Gandhinagar University Admissions 2023: The university accepts CAT scores for the MBA exam. CAT registration 2024 is ongoing. The last date to register for CAT is September 13, 2023. The admit card will be published on October 25, 2023, and CAT 2023 exam is scheduled for November 26, 2023. The university also accepts MAT exam. The last date for MAT registration 2024 (CBT mode) is September 12, 2023. For the PBT mode, the last date is August 29, 2023, and for the IBT mode, the last date of MAT registration is September 13, 2023. 

Gandhinagar University is a private university established in the year 2006. The university is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). The university has a total of seven constituent colleges such as Gandhinagar Institute of Technology, Gandhinagar Institute of Management etc. The university faculty members hold handsome experience in teaching. Gandhinagar University has secured three-star ratings in Gujarat State Institute Ranking Framework (GSIRF) 2022. The university provides admission in UG and PG programmes.

Admission to Gandhinagar University courses is done on a merit basis and candidates score in the entrance examination (depending on the course). The university provides placement and summer internship opportunities to students. The university has signed a MoU with foreign universities to provide overseas exposure to students. Gandhinagar University campus has modern facilities such as internet connectivity, boys' and girls' hotels, a well-stocked library etc. for students. The university also has numerous student clubs that organise academic and extracurricular activities at regular intervals.

Candidates who want to apply for Gandhinagar University admission must first meet the eligibility criteria determined by the university. The university provides admission in UG and PG courses. Gandhinagar University admission in BBA, B.Com and B.Sc courses is done based on candidates performance in the last qualifying examination (Class 12). Candidates need to qualify GUJCET for admission in B.Tech courses. For Gandhinagar University admission in the MBA course, the university takes into consideration CMAT / XAT / MAT / CAT scores. The university provides admission in M.Tech courses based on the GATE / Gujarat PGCET scores of candidates. Gandhinagar University Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The university has a well-equipped academic infrastructure with classrooms, laboratories, a library, and a computer centre. The Gandhinagar University campus is located in a lush green area and is about 13 km from Gota Crossroad on Sarkhej-Gandhinagar Highway. The university has a resource centre with a large collection of books on technical, science, management, and general topics. 

For student accommodation, Gandhinagar University has built full-fledged hostel buildings, with separate complexes for male and female students. The university has a fleet of buses that have stops in all the areas of Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar for students and faculty members. In addition to the above, the institute also has a number of other facilities, such as a canteen, a medical centre, and a security force. These Gandhinagar University campus facilities are provided to ensure that students have a comfortable and safe learning environment.

Gandhinagar University Events and Clubs

Gandhinagar University offers a variety of extracurricular activities for its students, including its annual clubs and events. These activities provide students with opportunities to develop their skills, learn new things, and have fun. They also help students to become more well-rounded individuals and to develop a sense of community.

The clubs at Gandhinagar University offer a variety of activities, such as book meets, movie meets, jamming sessions, and poetry meets. These clubs provide students with the opportunity to explore their interests and to meet other students who share their passions. The events at Gandhinagar University are a great way for students to get involved in campus activities. These events include annual fests, national days and festivals, and prom nights. They provide students with the opportunity to celebrate their culture, learn about new cultures, and make memories that will last a lifetime.
