About Veterinary College and Research Institute (VCRI), Chennai

Veterinary College and Research Institute, Chennai is situated in Chennai in Tamil Nadu state of India. Established in 1903, is a Private college. Veterinary College and Research Institute, Chennai offers 18 courses across 5 streams namely Engineering, Science, Management, Medical, Vocational..Popular degrees offered at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Chennai include B.Tech, BVSc, PGD, MVSc, MSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Veterinary College and Research Institute, Chennai is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Chennai, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Chennai is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.

Is Direct admission possible for veterinary in VCRI chennaiIf it's possible what's the procedure

S
Sanjukta Deka •  Jul-03-2023

Dear student, 

Direct admission is not possible for Veterinary at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Chennai. The college only admits students through the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) or the State Level Entrance Exam (SLET). NEET is a national-level exam that is held every year for admission to undergraduate medical and dental courses in India. The SLET is a state-level exam that is held every year for admission to veterinary courses in Tamil Nadu. If you are interested in studying veterinary at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Chennai, you will need to take the NEET or the SLET. The minimum qualifying marks for the NEET are 50% for general category students and 40% for reserved category students. The minimum qualifying marks for the SLET are 50% for all categories. The application process is online and can be found on the college's website. The college will conduct a counseling process for all the applicants who have qualified for admission. The counseling process will be based on the merit of the applicants and the availability of seats.
