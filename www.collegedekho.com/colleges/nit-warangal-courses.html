NIT Warangal Courses and Fees 2026

NIT Warangal provides a wide range of educational opportunities with over 80 programs available at undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral levels. These programs span disciplines such as Engineering, Sciences, Management, Architecture, and Humanities. Renowned courses include B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, M.Sc, B.Arch, and Integrated M.Sc, offered across 13 academic departments. The tuition fees at NIT Warangal vary depending on the course and category, with additional fee remissions for SC/ST students and economically backward students (family income <INR 1 lakh). Some Frequently Asked Questions about NIT Warangal:

1. What courses does NIT Warangal offer?

NIT Warangal offers Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degrees in various specializations like Computer Science and Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Biotechnology, and Mechanical Engineering. They also provide postgraduate programs, including a Master of Business Administration (MBA) and postgraduate degrees in artificial intelligence and machine learning

2. What is the selection process for NIT Warangal?

Admissions to NIT Warangal are based on national level entrance exams like JEE, GATE, CAT, etc.

3. What is the total fees of NIT Warangal for 4 years?

The total fees of NIT Rourkela for BTech ranges between INR 5,00,000 – INR 7,00,000.

4. Can I get NIT Warangal without JEE?

No, you generally cannot join NIT Warangal for the B.Tech program without JEE. JEE Main scores are mandatory for B.Tech admission, followed by JOSAA or CSAB counseling. However, if you are an NRI, PIO, or a child of an OCI, you may be eligible to apply for a limited number of seats through the Direct Admission of Students Abroad (DASA) scheme, which does not require JEE Main. 

5. Which are the top 5 NITs in India?

The top 5 NITs (National Institutes of Technology) in India are NIT Trichy, NIT Surathkal, NIT Rourkela, NIT Warangal, and NIT Calicut.

Can I get scholarship at NIT Warangal?

D
Diksha Nautiyal •  Jun-25-2020

Dear student, 

This depends on whether or not you can meet the eligibility criteria for scholarships provided at NIT Warangal. To avail merit-based scholarships, you must have scored over 9.1 CGPA in the first semester of your programme. You JEE Main rank also determines your eligibility. The college offers scholarships to students with JEE Main rank equal to or less than 2,000. You can also get a scholarship based on your total annual family income. It must also be notified that the institute does not award more than one scholarship to one student even if the candidate is eligible for more than one scholarships.

NIT Warangal Courses FAQs

What types of courses does NIT Warangal offer?

At the UG level, NIT Warangal offers B.Tech, BSc+MSc , etc, while at the PG level, NIT Warangal offers M.Tech, MSc, MCA, MBA, M.E., etc.

Which courses are popular at NIT Warangal?

The most popular NIT Warangal courses include the B.Tech, BSc+MSc, M.Tech, MSc, MCA and MBA programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, BSc+MSc, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MSc, MCA, MBA, M.E. , etc. NIT Warangal also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does NIT Warangal accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at NIT Warangal. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, BSc+MSc and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is NIT Warangal easy to get into?

Admission in NIT Warangal courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the NIT Warangal Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include DASA UG, CAT, MAT, NIMCET, CCMN, CBSE 12th, CCMT, TSBIE, TS EAMCET, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, UGC NET, CSIR NET, GATE, IIT JAM etc.

What is the top package of NIT Warangal?

Companies like GAIL, HPCL, ISRO, etc. visited during placements.

Is NIT Warangal very expensive?

The annual cost of undergraduate programmes is around Rs 63000 for B.Tech. The annual NIT Warangal fee structure for PG programmes is between Rs 30000 for MSc to Rs 140000 for MBA.

What is the annual fee of NIT Warangal?

The annual NIT Warangal fees fall between Rs 10100 to Rs 500000. Depending on the degree level, duration, and area of specialisation, the fees differ for various programmes. NIT Warangal courses at the Ph.D, PG and UG levels are available.Around 48 courses are available under different degree levels; some of the well known ones are B.Tech, BSc+MSc, M.Tech, MSc, MCA, MBA, and so on.

What is the most popular course at NIT WARANGAL ?

Engineering courses are the most popular courses opted by students at NIT WARANGAL . Of these, B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) courses are the most sought after amongst both students and parents.
