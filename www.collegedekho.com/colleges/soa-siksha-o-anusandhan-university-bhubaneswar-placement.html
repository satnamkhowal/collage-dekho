Siksha 'O' Anusandhan University Placement Overview 2025

The placements for Siksha 'O' Anusandhan University are managed by its placement cell every year. The cell is responsible for preparing students for placements and bringing recruiters to the campus. As per Siksha 'O' Anusandhan University placement report 2025, a total of 256 companies visited the campus for placements, and a total of 1,836 offers were made. 1,836 students were placed with an average package of Rs 5.75 LPA. Moreover, the highest package offered during Siksha 'O' Anusandhan University placements was Rs 46 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visited Siksha 'O' Anusandhan University for placements included Accenture, Adobe, Amazon, Cognizant, Deloitte, etc.
