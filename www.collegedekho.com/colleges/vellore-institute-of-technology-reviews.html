VIT Vellore Expert's Review and Verdict

Campus at a Glimpse

VIT Vellore was established on July 20, 1984 with the primary objective of delivering high-quality higher education that matches global standards. It is a well-known private university located in Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India. Over the years, VIT Vellore has grown to become one of India's prominent institutions for higher education and research. The institution consistently explores and implements innovative approaches to enhance the quality of higher education. 

VIT Vellore campus boasts a diverse and international environment, welcoming students from across the world. VIT strongly promotes experienced and knowledgeable educators who play a crucial role in nurturing the students. VIT has set global benchmarks in teaching and research, motivating students to tirelessly pursue excellence, which has become a way of life for them. The partnerships facilitate student and faculty exchanges and encourage collaborative research projects, benefiting all participating institutions. Many of VIT Vellore students, who engage in research projects abroad, bring back high-quality work and hold India in high regard, ultimately bringing honor to the institution. 

Not many know, because of its popularity, VIT Vellore campus is said to be extremely overcrowded, thus restricting the students to peacefully relax inside the VIT campus.

VIT Vellore Academia & Batch Size

VIT Vellore provides a diverse range of academic offerings, including 66 undergraduate programs, 58 postgraduate programs, 15 integrated programs, 2 research programs, and 2 MTech industrial programs. Additionally, there are opportunities for pursuing full-time PhD degrees in Engineering and Management disciplines, PhD programs in Science and Languages, and integrated PhD programs in Engineering disciplines. Within the university, research centres affiliated with specific schools actively promote collaborative research projects involving students from different departments. 

The university's Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS) accommodates learners with varying paces, allowing them to adjust their credit loads accordingly. Out of the courses offered at VIT Vellore, more than 40% of courses incorporate ICT-enabled classrooms and an in-house learning management system, empowering faculty members to employ effective ICT-enabled teaching methods. However, students say that students with a CGPA between 8 to 9 are given first preference in the selection. We believe that all students must have equal opportunity.

Our Take:

The academia at VIT Vellore campus is considered one of the best as per industry standards. All disciplines are updated with the latest syllabus with an option of choosing elective subjects from a variety of streams. However, the large batch size is a huge concern for VIT Vellore as the classroom becomes overcrowded, that becomes unmanageable both at the student's and faculty's end.

VIT Vellore Infrastructure 

The VIT Vellore campus covers an extensive expanse of 372 acres. What sets VIT Vellore apart is its modern infrastructure designed to cater to the needs of both students and faculty. Every building within the university is equipped with fully furnished and well-maintained laboratories. Additionally, the campus is equipped with various amenities to accommodate a wide range of events, including auditoriums, spacious outdoor areas, open stages, and more.

Classrooms and Lecture Halls: 

The intelligently designed classrooms or smart classrooms are equipped to create a comfortable learning environment, featuring ample space and essential tools. These spacious classrooms are furnished with a whiteboard and a projector used for instructional purposes. Consequently, instructors have the flexibility to employ various teaching methods as needed, ensuring that the teaching process remains dynamic and that lectures are effectively delivered. However, students claim that the smart classrooms are reserved for guest lectures, seminars, workshops etc. On a day to day basis, students are required to use regular classrooms with just a fan.

 

Library:

The central library occupies a space of 7770 square meters, spanning over seven floors. It houses specialized collections of materials in various fields, including Mathematics and Sciences, Engineering and Technology, Biotechnology, Humanities, Social Sciences, and Management. These collections encompass a wide range of resources, such as printed books, e-books, back volumes, CDs, and DVDs. The library also holds subscriptions to both national and international journals in both print and electronic formats.

Internet Connection: 

IT infrastructure at VIT Vellore campus is extensively deployed, linking 56 buildings with a highly efficient and high-speed network backbone. The computer network is constructed on the CISCO switching platform, with the current backbone operating at 10 Gig speed. More than 12,000 IP-enabled devices are connected to this swift network. Recognizing the needs of faculty and students who utilize digital media for research, teaching, and learning, they have allocated 10Gbps of internet bandwidth. 

Hostels: 

VIT Vellore provides hostel facilities with all the amenities for the students. The hostel is inside the campus and it is one of the largest hostels in Asia. While all infrastructural facilities at VIT Vellore hostel are great, often students complain about the freedom and flexibility at the hostel. There are lot of restrictions for hostellers, especially for girls. The basic details about the hostel are as follows:

Auditorium and Conference Halls:

VIT Vellore University is committed to providing its students with opportunities to interact with national and international experts in various fields. To facilitate this, they regularly host national and international conferences, symposia, and workshops on campus. With this objective in mind, VIT management has carefully designed and integrated a range of modern conference facilities into the VIT campus.

One of the notable examples is the 'Anna Audi,' frequently used by students, which is nothing short of a marvel. With the capacity to accommodate 1800 individuals and spanning an area of 15,436 square feet, it has been equipped with centralized air conditioning, Wi-Fi service, a splendid stage, green rooms, and optimal lighting. This auditorium is utilized for a variety of events and conferences and is conveniently situated adjacent to the volleyball and basketball courts, serving as a prominent landmark within VIT.

Sports Centre:

In pursuit of a healthier lifestyle while managing the demands of academics, it is essential to prioritize emotional and physical well-being. With this principle in mind, VIT Vellore places significant emphasis on sports and views them as an essential component of the educational curriculum. VIT features multiple centers dedicated to physical education and sports, fostering student fitness and cultivating a spirit of competition. The university offers an extensive array of outdoor playgrounds and indoor courts, covering a sprawling 20-acre expanse.

Hang-Out Areas/Cafes:

VIT has been amply blessed with three fully equipped multi-cuisine canteens present at three strategic locations in college. Two at the far ends of college, one each in G D Naidu Block and the Silver Jubilee Tower and one more at the centre of the college near the Technology Tower. All the three canteens are on contract basis with external caterers and provide quality food at student friendly prices. Hosting a wide array of cuisines and dishes, they cater to the needs of the students from every corner of the country providing them home style food with no compromise on the quality.

Our Take:

VIT Vellore has unparalleled infrastructure when it comes to academic delivery. The entire campus is wifi-enabled and has smart classrooms. The internet speed is superb and students rarely complain about the same. Be it library or sports, the campus has it all and in abundance. They have state-of-the-art and marvellous structures in the form of auditoriums and conference halls. The hostels and washrooms are well maintained and students rarely complain about infrastructures at VIT Vellore.

But yes, there are a lot of restrictions at the hostel especially for girls. The students have to report to the hostel before 9 PM and no celebrations are allowed inside the hostel premises. First-year students are not allowed to step out of the Hostel. Classrooms become really hot during summer and students are not able to use the facilities available. The hostelers also complain that the students who have CGPA 8 and 9 are given preference in the second and third years during the hostel allocations. 

VIT Vellore Internships and Placements 

Internship

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) assures all its students get internships so that they get a diverse array of job opportunities upon the completion of their courses. Some of the well-known companies where the students get an opportunity to do internships are Intel, eBay, Amazon, Dell, GE, Texas Instruments, Schneider Electric, DE Shaw & Co, Hitachi, HP, PayPal, Tata Motors, TCS, Cognizant, Accenture, Wipro, Infosys, and many others which actively hire the interested and hard-working students to work.

Placement

The CDC (Career Development Centre) is a pivotal factor in establishing VIT's placement track record. It is responsible for the seamless operation of placement activities on campus. Moreover, the centre actively supports students in their training endeavours and ensures their placement in top-notch national and international firms. The placement process at Vellore Institute of Technology commences in January and extends for a year until students secure placements in leading MNCs with attractive compensation packages exceeding three lakhs. Students eagerly anticipate the placement process, often referring to it as a "Mega Event." The Placement Cell ensures that all students are proficient in coding and aptitude skills to excel in placement interviews. In July, recruiting companies visit the campus, offering a wide range of job opportunities to fulfil students' career aspirations.

Many claim that VIT Vellore has a student force of more than 30,000 students with roughly 400 to 500 teachers/ faculty members to handle them, i.e 1:60 ratio, being far from ideal. The management at VIT Vellore is good in many aspects but sometimes they are rude and non-cooperative while solving the problem of the students. VIT Vellore Location 

VIT Vellore is situated in the city of Vellore in the southern part of Tamil Nadu and usually has a tropical climate which makes it hot almost throughout the year. It is a well-known private university and is easily accessible from various parts of Tamil Nadu and neighbouring states. 

VIT Vellore is approximately 14-16 kilometres (about 9-10 miles) away from Vellore Katpadi Railway Station. This railway station serves as the primary railhead for the city of Vellore and is well-connected to the different cities and towns across India. Chennai International Airport, which is one of the nearest major airports to VIT Vellore, is approximately 135-150 kilometres (about 84-93 miles) away from the campus.
