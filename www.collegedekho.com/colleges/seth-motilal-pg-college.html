About Seth Moti Lal P.G. College

Seth Moti Lal P.G. College is situated in Jaipur in Rajasthan state of India. Established in 1952, is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya Shekhawati University and accredited by NAAC, AICTE, National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). Seth Moti Lal P.G. College offers 10 courses across 5 streams namely Science, Commerce and Banking, IT, Management, Arts..Popular degrees offered at Seth Moti Lal P.G. College include BSc, B.Com, BCA, BBA, MA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Seth Moti Lal P.G. College is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Seth Moti Lal P.G. College, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Seth Moti Lal P.G. College is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
