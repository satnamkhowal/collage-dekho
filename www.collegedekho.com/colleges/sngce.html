About SNGCE Ernakulam

Sree Narayana Gurukulam College of Engineering is situated in Ernakulam in Kerala state of India. Established in 2012, SNGCE is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University and accredited by AICTE, NBA, IAO, NAAC. Sree Narayana Gurukulam College of Engineering offers 18 courses across 3 streams namely Engineering, IT, Management..Popular degrees offered at Sree Narayana Gurukulam College of Engineering include B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, MBA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Sree Narayana Gurukulam College of Engineering is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Sree Narayana Gurukulam College of Engineering, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Sree Narayana Gurukulam College of Engineering is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
