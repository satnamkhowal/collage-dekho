Getting a seat at a National Institute of Technology sounds great on paper, but not all NITs are equal. If your JEE Main rank is somewhere between 60,000 and 2,00,000, you may be looking at NITs in Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram, Nagaland, Manipur, Sikkim, Meghalaya, Uttarakhand, Goa, or Puducherry. These are real NITs with government backing and UGC-recognised degrees. Still, they differ significantly from top-tier NITs like NIT Trichy or NIT Warangal in terms of placements, facilities, and industry connections.

Lovely Professional University (LPU) in Punjab often comes up as a serious alternative for students in this rank range. It is a private deemed university with NAAC A++ accreditation, NIRF Engineering rank 48 (2025), and reported placements including a highest package of INR 53.24 LPA. The question is: does that make LPU better than a low-ranked NIT?

The honest answer is, it depends on the branch, the scholarship amount, and what the student values most. A low-ranked NIT still gives a government-backed degree, a strong peer group, and very low fees. LPU offers a larger campus, more industry-linked programmes, and stronger placements in certain branches, but at a much higher cost.

What is a "Low-Ranked NIT" and Which NITs Fall in This Category?

All 31 NITs across India are government-funded central institutes. They all admit students through JoSAA counselling using JEE Main rank. But there is a wide gap between top-tier NITs and newer or remotely located NITs.

Low-ranked NITs in 2025-26 (NIRF Engineering rank 75+): NIT Arunachal Pradesh, NIT Manipur, NIT Mizoram, NIT Nagaland, NIT Sikkim, NIT Meghalaya, NIT Uttarakhand, NIT Puducherry, NIT Goa, NIT Andhra Pradesh, NIT Agartala, and NIT Srinagar are generally considered lower-tier NITs. These are also the ones accessible for JEE Main ranks above 60,000–70,000 in many popular branches.

Fee Comparison: LPU vs Low-Ranked NIT for BTech

NIT fees are significantly lower than LPU fees, and this is the single biggest advantage of any NIT, even a low-ranked one. Government-funded NITs charge a flat tuition fee structure that is far below what private universities charge. LPU fees depend on the specialisation chosen and can vary widely.

LPU scholarship impact: LPUNEST score-based scholarships can reduce LPU tuition fees by 20% to 60%, depending on the category achieved. For example, a student scoring well in LPUNEST might bring a INR 2,80,000/year fee down to INR 1,12,000–INR 1,68,000 per year. With a scholarship, the cost gap between LPU and a low-ranked NIT becomes smaller — but a low-ranked NIT still remains cheaper.

Key takeaway on fees: Even with the highest scholarship at LPU, NIT fees are lower. However, the total cost difference shrinks considerably for students who earn a good LPUNEST scholarship. Students should always calculate the net fee after scholarship before comparing.

What students often miss: NIT students have a stronger advantage for government jobs (GATE-based PSU recruitment) and M.Tech admissions at IITs. NIT degrees are generally treated with more weight by PSU recruiters. LPU students, on the other hand, have access to a wider variety of industry-linked electives and programmes in AI, Data Science, and emerging tech that many smaller NITs simply do not offer.

Branch Matters a Lot: LPU CSE has better placements than Civil, Mechanical, and Chemical Engineering. If you are choosing between CSE at LPU and Civil Engineering at a low-ranked NIT, choose CSE at LPU. It offers better placement opportunities. If you are choosing between CSE at a low-ranked NIT and Civil Engineering at LPU, the decision is not as simple. Both offer good placement.

The NIT brand: The three letters "NIT" carry significant weight in the Indian job market and for higher education. Campus Life, Location, and Infrastructure

Many low-ranked NITs are located in states like Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram, Nagaland, and Sikkim. While this is not a disadvantage in terms of academics, it does create practical challenges — expensive travel home, limited access to internship opportunities nearby, and fewer industry interactions. LPU's Punjab location gives students easy access to Delhi NCR companies and good travel connectivity across India.

LPU scholarship categories are assigned at the time of admission based on LPUNEST score. Students who apply with JEE Main scores can also qualify directly for category-based scholarships. Scholarship applies to tuition fee only, not to hostel or exam fees.

Example: If a student gets Category B scholarship and joins B.Tech CSE (standard) at LPU, the fee comes down from INR 2,80,000/year to INR 1,68,000/year. Over 4 years, the total tuition cost (after scholarship) would be approximately INR 6.72 Lakhs — which is much closer to the NIT fee range of INR 5–8 Lakhs total.

Who Should Choose LPU and Who Should Choose a Low-Ranked NIT?

This is the real question. Here is an honest, student-first answer:

Choose LPU if:

  • The branch available at a low-ranked NIT is not preferred — for example, if a student can only get Civil or Chemical at a low-ranked NIT but can get CSE at LPU.
  • The student qualifies for a Category A or Category B LPUNEST scholarship, which brings the fee close to the NIT-level range.
  • The student wants CSE with an emerging specialisation (AI, Data Science, Cyber Security) that is not well-developed at the NIT being considered.
  • Industry exposure, internship access, and proximity to job hubs matter more — LPU's Punjab location provides far better connectivity than remote NITs.
  • Campus infrastructure, facilities, and student life are important — LPU's 600-acre campus with hospitals, malls, and sports infrastructure is far ahead of most low-ranked NITs.
  • The student is not targeting government jobs or PSU recruitment, where the NIT brand has a clear advantage.
  • Higher studies abroad are not a priority — LPU's research output is lower than NITs on metrics used by foreign universities.

Choose a low-ranked NIT if:

  • Getting a government-backed degree is a priority — the NIT tag matters significantly for PSU recruitment, government job applications, and for parents who value the government institution label.
  • Fee is a major constraint and no scholarship is available at LPU. NIT fees are genuinely much lower.
  • The student plans to prepare for GATE after B.Tech — NIT degrees are treated more favourably in M.Tech admissions at IITs and other top institutes.
  • The branch available at the NIT is the same branch the student wants. NIT Civil is a better career path than LPU Civil for most students.
  • The student has a disciplined, self-driven approach to academics. Remote NIT campuses can actually help in focused preparation without city distractions.
  • Long-term academic or research goals are in focus — NIT has more credibility in academic circles than LPU for research, publications, and PhD applications.

The one-line rule:

CSE or IT at LPU with scholarship > NIT in a non-preferred core branch. For the same branch, the NIT degree wins on brand. But for a better branch with a scholarship, LPU can be the smarter practical choice.
