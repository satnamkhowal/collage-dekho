LPU Ranking 2025

The Ministry of Education (MoE) has released the NIRF ranking 2025. As per the rankings, the university has been ranked 49th overall among all government and private universities in the country, reflecting its consistent academic excellence and research growth. LPU has also secured impressive discipline-wise positions;13th in Pharmacy, 17th in Agriculture, 24th in Architecture and Planning, 26th in Law, 44th in Management, 48th in Engineering, and 40th among Research Institutions across India.
