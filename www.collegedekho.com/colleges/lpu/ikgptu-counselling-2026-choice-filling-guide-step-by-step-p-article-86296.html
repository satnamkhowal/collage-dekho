If you've already registered for IKGPTU (I.K. Gujral Punjab Technical University) Centralized Online Counselling 2026, choice filling is the single most consequential step left in your admission journey. Your registration and fee payment get you into the system — but it's the order in which you arrange your college and branch preferences, and how carefully you lock them, that actually determines where you end up. Get this step wrong, and you could lose out on a better seat even with a strong rank. This guide walks through exactly how IKGPTU's choice filling and seat locking process works, based on the university's own counselling FAQs, so you can move quickly and correctly.

Where Choice Filling Fits in the IKGPTU Counselling Process

Per IKGPTU's official process flow, Centralized Online Counselling runs through these stages in order:

  1. Centralized Online Counselling Registration
  2. Online Counselling Fee Payment — INR 2,000, one-time, non-refundable per course (covers all rounds; no need to pay again in Round 2 if already paid)
  3. Preparation of Rank — provisional merit list, objection window, then final merit list
  4. Choice Filling — you arrange colleges/branches in order of preference
  5. Allotment of Seat
  6. Online Reporting at Allotted Institute — uploading documents and depositing semester fee
  7. Provisional Confirmation by the allotted institute after document/fee verification

If you're already registered, you're sitting right before or at Step 4 — which is exactly why getting choice filling right, right now, matters so much.

Step-by-Step: How to Fill Your Choices

  1. Log in to the official counselling portal using the credentials created at registration — never use a third-party site or agent link.
  2. Open the choice-filling module once it's active for your round (this opens after the final merit list is declared).
  3. Add colleges and branches one at a time, referring to the official Information Brochure available on the IKGPTU website for the full list of institutes and courses.
  4. Arrange your list in strict order of preference — the system will try to allot your highest-listed choice first, and only moves down the list if that seat isn't available to your rank/category.
  5. Add as many choices as you're genuinely willing to accept. IKGPTU places no limit on the number of choices you can fill.
  6. Edit, delete, or re-arrange freely — as many times as you want — right up until you lock your list or the choice-filling deadline passes, whichever comes first.
  7. Review your entire list one final time before locking — check branch names, campus locations, and category-specific seat types carefully.
  8. Lock your choices. Once locked (or once the window closes, whichever happens first), your list is final and cannot be edited for that round.

Tips for Choice Filling — What Actually Improves Your Outcome

  • Fill the maximum number of choices you'd realistically accept. It's not mandatory, but IKGPTU's own guidance confirms that more choices meaningfully improve your chance of getting seated in Round 1 rather than waiting on Round 2 or spot counselling.
  • Order matters more than volume. Put your genuinely most-wanted college/branch combination at the very top — the system allots your highest available choice on your list, not the "best fit," so a poorly ordered list can cost you a preferred seat even if it was technically available.
  • Don't list options you wouldn't actually accept, just to pad the list — if one of those gets allotted and you don't report, you lose your seat entirely with no fallback.
  • Confirm your quota before filling choices. You can participate under only one quota — either the 85% Punjab Resident Quota or the 15% Outside-Punjab Quota — based on your actual state of eligibility. Filing under the wrong quota risks having your candidature rejected at the reporting stage, even after a successful allotment.
  • Double-check your eligibility status if you have a compartment/reappear in your qualifying exam — such candidates are not eligible to participate in Centralized Online Counselling at all, and choices filled under incorrect eligibility claims will not be honoured at reporting.
  • Cross-check category-specific seats carefully — general, SC/ST, BC, PwD, defence, and sports categories each have their own seat pools within each college/branch combination; make sure you're selecting the correct category-tagged option where relevant.

What "Seat Locking" Actually Means (and What Happens After)

Seat locking happens automatically once the choice-filling window for your round closes — your entire preference list freezes and becomes the basis for that round's allotment. You can no longer add, remove, or reorder anything after this point for that round.

Here's what to expect once your locked choices go into allotment:

  • If you get a seat: You must complete online reporting — upload the requisite documents and deposit the semester fee through the portal within the reporting window stated in your provisional allotment letter.
  • If you don't report within the prescribed time: Your allotted seat is automatically cancelled and released into the next round. You lose your right over that seat with no exception.
  • If you're not satisfied with your Round 1 seat: You can participate in Round 2. Your Round 1 seat stays reserved for you (once reported and fee-deposited) while Round 2 is processed — so reporting and reserving your Round 1 seat first is generally the safer move before chasing an upgrade.
  • If you get a better seat in Round 2: Your previous Round 1 seat is automatically cancelled, and you become eligible for a fee refund from that earlier institute as per norms — but you must formally submit a request letter regarding the upgraded seat at both the new and the previous institute.
  • If you don't get a seat in Round 2 upgradation: Your original Round 1 seat is retained, provided you had reported and reserved it. If you hadn't reserved it, you cannot claim it back.
  • Fee note: You do not pay the INR 2,000 counselling fee again for Round 2 if you already paid it in Round 1 — this is a one-time fee covering all rounds for that course.

Documents Required at the Time of Reporting

Once you're allotted a seat, keep these ready in original for upload/verification:

  • 10th DMC
  • 12th DMC (for UG courses)
  • Diploma DMCs (for Lateral Entry courses)
  • DMCs/Degree of graduation (for PG courses)
  • Residential certificate (domicile or other valid address proof)
  • Reserved category certificate, if applicable
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Migration certificate of the qualifying exam
  • Aadhaar card (mandatory for scholarship/scheme beneficiaries)
  • Income certificate (if applying under Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme or Post-Matric Scholarship)
  • Sikh Religion exam scorecard (for B.Tech 1st Year, if applicable)
  • Entrance exam scorecard, where applicable
  • NATA scorecard (for B.Architecture)
  • Centralized Online Counselling registration form and provisional allotment letter

Final Takeaway

If you've already registered for IKGPTU Counselling 2026, the choice filling stage is where careful, deliberate action pays off directly — fill as many genuinely acceptable choices as you can, order them by true preference (not just "safe" options), confirm your quota and category selections are accurate, and lock your list only after a final careful review. Once locked, there's no going back for that round, and missing your reporting window afterward means losing the seat outright. Move through this step methodically, and keep the official IKGPTU counselling portal open as your only source of truth for round-wise deadlines.
