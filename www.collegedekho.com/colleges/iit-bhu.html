About IIT BHU

Indian Institute of Technology (BHU) Varanasi, established in 1919 as Banaras Engineering College and designated as an IIT in 2012 through an Act of Parliament, is a premier public technical institute of national importance, approved by UGC and AICTE. Renowned for blending a century-old legacy of academic excellence with cutting-edge innovation, IIT (BHU) emphasizes interdisciplinary research, industry collaboration, and holistic development, fostering engineers and leaders who drive technological and societal progress. Popularly called “The Cradle of Engineering Excellence,” the institute prioritizes value-based education, entrepreneurial mindset, and practical problem-solving through its rigorous techno-academic framework.

The institute’s flagship 4-year B.Tech program, offered across 11 core engineering disciplines including Computer Science, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, and Mining Engineering, along with integrated dual-degree (IDD) options, integrates advanced specializations in emerging areas like AI, Data Science, VLSI Design, and Renewable Energy. The curriculum, continually updated for autonomy, incorporates industry-relevant modules in partnership with global firms and features hands-on learning via project-based courses, simulations, and capstone projects. Delivery methods include interactive lectures, collaborative labs, flipped classrooms, and experiential assignments, supplemented by weekly skill-building sessions on technical communication, innovation workshops, entrepreneurship bootcamps, coding challenges, cultural immersion, sports, and outreach initiatives.

IIT (BHU) boasts a sprawling 1,300-acre fully residential campus within the Banaras Hindu University ecosystem, featuring smart classrooms with AV aids, world-class research labs (including high-tech tools like Transmission Electron Microscopes), central and departmental libraries with over 1,38,000 volumes and digital resources, computer centers, auditoriums, amphitheaters, multi-sport complexes (tennis, squash, basketball, volleyball), multi-gyms, wellness centers with yoga and meditation halls, on-campus hostels with Wi-Fi and 24/7 security, medical facilities, and study abroad exchanges. The institute achieved a 92.33% placement rate in the 2024-25 phase (as of November 2025), with the highest package at ₹2.2 CPA and over 350 recruiters participating, including 297 PPOs. Leading recruiters encompass Google, Microsoft, Uber, Goldman Sachs, Amazon, Qualcomm, Flipkart, Tata Steel, ITC, Samsung, and Accenture, bolstered by a global alumni network of over 7,000 strong and vibrant corporate ties via the Student Alumni Interaction Cell (SAIC).

IIT BHU FAQs

When is the IIT JAM exam for IIT BHU MSc admission?

Admission to the IIT BHU M.Sc courses requires passing the IIT JAM test. The application form was made available on the official website (jam.iitm.ac.in) by the organising body, IIT Madras. Candidates may sit for the exam if they have a bachelor's degree with a minimum of 55% aggregate marks. The test will be held on February 11, 2024. There are two M.Sc specialisations available: physics and chemistry.

What is the IIT JAM 2024 application process for IIT Varanasi admission?

Online applications are accepted for the IIT JAM test. The JOAPS portal requires candidates to register by providing their name, mobile number, working email address, and password. Below is a list of the steps necessary for IIT BHU MSc admission: Go to jam.iitm.ac.in. Then select "Candidate Portal (JOAPS)" from the webpage.  Complete the required fields to complete IIT JAM registration. Use your special log in information to log in and complete the application. Submit the necessary documents. Make the registration fee payment. After that, submit the application.

What is the IIT Varanasi fees structure?

The courses at the Indian Institute of Technology BHU are offered at UG, PG and Doctoral levels, in the fields of engineering, technology and science. Other than this, an integrated programme is also offered here. The degrees are B.Tech, B.Arch, B.Tech + M.Tech, M.Sc, M.Tech and PhD. The fees is different for all the programmes. The fees also vary on the basis of the student category (GEN/SC/ST). The IIT Varanasi fees range from Rs 10,000 to Rs 1,00,000 per semester, for tuition only.

How to get the JEE Advanced 2023 Result, which is necessary for IIT BHU admissions?

The JEE Advanced Result 2023 was made public by IIT Guwahati on June 18, 2023. The conducting body posted the IIT JEE result 2023 on the official website (jee adv.ac.in 2023). The following steps describe how to check the JEE Advanced Result 2023:  Visit the website jeeadv.ac.in. Click on the link for the JEE Advanced score report. Now sign in with the necessary information. To view the JEE Advanced results, candidates must log in using their registration ID (JEE Advanced roll number) and password. Check the JEE Advanced result, then download it after verification.

Is IIT BHU Varanasi recognised by the UGC?

Yes, the Indian Institute of Technology Varanasi or IIT BHU is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). It was ranked 15 for B.Tech by NIRF 2023 and 9 by The Week 2023. It was ranked 31, overall by the NIRF Ranking 2023.

When was IIT BHU established?

The Indian Institute of Technology Varanasi, also known as IIT BHU, was founded in 1919.n The institute conducts research independently despite sharing a location with Banaras Hindu University. The Banaras Engineering College, College of Technology, and College of Mining and Metallurgy combined to form IIT BHU. The Varanasi campus spans around 400 acres. Both the UGC and the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) have given the institute their approval. It offers undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral-level courses.

IIT BHU Infrastructure

Indian Institute of Technology (BHU) Varanasi is a fully residential institute set on a sprawling 1,300-acre green campus along the banks of the Ganga. It houses over 8,000 students in 19 well-equipped hostels (AC/non-AC rooms with Wi-Fi, mess, and recreation facilities), features more than 50 advanced labs including VLSI Fab, Robotics (ABB arms), and TEM, and offers a 24×7 central library with 1.4 lakh+ books and 50,000+ e-journals. The campus provides a 24-bed hospital with telemedicine, a 400-acre sports complex (cricket, football, tennis, squash, swimming; hosts Khelo India), smart classrooms, auditoriums, amphitheatre, free shuttles, 100% Wi-Fi coverage, and solar-powered sustainable operations. Blending heritage architecture with modern infrastructure, IIT BHU delivers a vibrant, eco-friendly environment for academics, research, and holistic growth.

Here’s the information in simple bullet points:

  • Hostels: 19 total (15 boys, 4 girls); new builds with AC triples (~₹15K/term), Wi-Fi, messes, recreation rooms; guaranteed for all UG students.
  • Labs: 50+ specialized, e.g., VLSI Fab, Robotics (ABB arms), Heat Transfer, Geotechnical; plus 200+ departmental setups for UG/PG research.
  • Library: 24/7 access, 1.4L+ books, 50K journals, digital labs; open 8 AM–11 PM extended hours.
  • Medical: 24-bed hospital with telemedicine, 24/7 OPD, pharmacy, ambulance.
  • Sports: 400-acre complex hosting Khelo India; indoor/outdoor arenas, multi-gym, pools.
  • Transport: Free shuttle to Varanasi station; bike/scooter parking post-year 1.
  • Sustainability: Solar-powered grids since 2024; lush 1,300-acre green cover with airstrip and eco-trails.
