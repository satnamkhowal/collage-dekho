Jaipur National University Job Ready Degree

Are you looking for a job ready course that provides you with the requisite skills in the present technology-driven digital world? Jaipur National University is providing four such “Assured” courses at the UG level under multiple specialisations. Jaipur National University courses that you can take admission in other than the traditional courses are BCA in Full Stack Development (IXP), BBA in Fintech(IXP), BBA in Digital Marketing (IXP), B.Tech in RPA (IXP) and B.Des in Game Design & Animation (IXP). Admission at Jaipur National University in these courses is done through a personal interview round and the fees fall within the range of Rs 47,500 - Rs 70,000 per semester.

Jaipur National University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

Jaipur National University admission in the job ready “Assured” courses offered is based on the candidate’s performance in the personal interview. There is no entrance exam or any other selection criteria for admission in these courses.
