Jaipur National University Courses 2026 Overview

Jaipur National University courses are offered at the undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and doctoral levels. At the undergraduate level, BTech programmes are available in Computer Science and Engineering, Cyber Security, AI&ML, and in several other disciplines. The JNU Jaipur offers around 30+ courses across UG, PG, diploma and PhD levels. The institute also provides MBA, MPharm, MSc and several other programs at PG level and DPharm programs at diploma level. The Jaipur National University fee structure costs between Rs 17,500 to Rs 9,86,040 as total fees depend on the program selected by the student.

Jaipur National University Courses and Programs FAQs

Does the School of Media Studies offer practical training in radio broadcasting?
Yes, students in the Journalism and Mass Communication programmes at Jaipur National University receive practical training in radio broadcasting. They get hands-on experience at Radio JNU 89.6 FM, the university’s community radio station. Here, students learn programme hosting, scriptwriting, voice recording, and sound editing, helping them understand how radio broadcasting works in a real studio environment.
Can I pursue an MBA if I am interested in the healthcare sector?
Yes, Jaipur National University (JNU Jaipur) offers a specialised MBA in Hospital and Healthcare Management for students interested in the healthcare sector. The programme focuses on areas such as hospital operations, healthcare management, quality control, and health insurance. Students also receive practical exposure through the university’s on-campus JNU Hospital, which helps them understand the functioning of healthcare institutions.
What integrated education programmes are offered at JNU Jaipur?
Jaipur National University (JNU Jaipur) offers four-year integrated education programmes for students who want to build a career in teaching. These programmes combine a subject-specific degree with a teaching qualification in a single course. Some of the integrated programmes include B.A.–B.Ed. and B.Sc.–B.Ed., which allow students to complete both degrees together instead of studying them separately.

Jaipur National University Courses FAQs

Which PG specializations are available in Agriculture?

Students pursuing M.Sc. Agriculture at Jaipur National University (JNU Jaipur) can choose from specialisations such as Agronomy, Horticulture, and Genetics & Plant Breeding. These specialisations allow students to focus on specific areas of agricultural science during their postgraduate studies.

Does JNU Jaipur offer any courses specifically focused on Indian Heritage?

Yes, Jaipur National University offers an elective course called “Understanding India” under the NEP 2020 curriculum. The course covers topics related to Indian heritage, history, nationalism, and traditional knowledge systems. It helps students understand the cultural and intellectual traditions of India as part of their academic studies.

Can students pursue advanced pharmacy studies at JNU Jaipur?

Yes, students who have completed a B.Pharm degree can pursue advanced pharmacy studies at Jaipur National University through the M.Pharm programme. This postgraduate course focuses on pharmaceutical research and advanced pharmaceutical studies. Students learn about drug development, pharmaceutical technology, and research methods used in the pharmaceutical and healthcare sectors.

Which courses are suitable for students interested in technology fields?

Students interested in technology and computing can pursue programmes such as B.Tech, BCA, MCA, and M.Tech at Jaipur National University. These courses focus on computer science, programming, software development, and engineering concepts. Through these programmes, students gain the knowledge and skills needed for careers in the IT industry, technology companies, and software development fields.

Which courses combine business education with legal studies?

Jaipur National University offers the BBA LL.B. integrated programme for students who want to study business and law together. The course includes subjects related to business management and legal studies. Students learn about corporate law, business regulations, and management concepts, which helps them prepare for careers in corporate law, legal consulting, and business-related legal fields.
