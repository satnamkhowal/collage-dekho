About Presidency University Bangalore

Presidency University Bangalore, also known as Presidency University, is a private university established in 2013. Located in Bengaluru, Karnataka, the Presidency University campus spans over 67 acres. The university is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and Bar Council of India (BCI), and accredited by NAAC with an A+ grade. Presidency University is ranked 101-150 by NIRF under the Universities category in 2024.

Presidency University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs in streams such as Engineering, Management, Law, Commerce, Design, and Media Studies. BTech, MBA, BCA, and BBA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Presidency University are based on both merit in the qualifying examination and entrance examinations. The university accepts JEE Main, KCET, and COMEDK UGET for BTech; CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, ATMA, KMAT, and Karnataka PGCET for MBA; and CLAT, LSAT, and PULAT for law courses.

For placements, Presidency University’s placement rate is 99.04% as per the NIRF 2024 report. During the placement drive of 2023, the highest salary package offered was INR 13.58 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 5.96 LPA.

Presidency University Bangalore Admission 2025

Presidency University Bangalore admissions are based on national-level, state-level, and university-based entrance examinations, as well as merit in the qualifying examination. Students seeking admission at Presidency University Bangalore must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the university and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for Presidency University Bangalore admissions:

  • BTech: Candidates seeking admission to BTech must appear for JEE Main, KCET, or COMEDK UGET and participate in the respective counseling process for seat allotment.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, ATMA, KMAT, or Karnataka PGCET and participate in the counseling process, followed by extempore and personal interview.
  • BCA/BBA: Admissions to BCA and BBA are based on KCET or UGAT scores, followed by an interview.
  • BA Journalism & Mass Communication/BCom: Admissions are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination, followed by an interview.

FAQs about Presidency University Bangalore

When do I need to pay the Presidency College Bangalore fees?

The first-year fees are paid after the counselling round is conducted as per the schedule shared in the provisional admission letter for the confirmation of the seats. At first, the registration amount has to be made as per the date given, followed by the next installment amounts.

Can I get admission to Presidency University Bangalore if I don’t have any entrance test?

No, you cannot get admission to Presidency University Bangalore if I don’t have any entrance tests. Admission to the university is both merit and entrance-based. Candidates must qualify for JEE Main, COMED-K, KCET, CAT, XAT, CLAT, LSAT, TANCET, etc. to seek admission to the university.

Can I avail of any scholarships at Presidency University Bangalore?

Yes, you can avail of Merit-based Scholarships for Design and Engineering students under the Management category, LLB, BA, BSc, BBA, BCA and BCom students, and postgraduate students in Engineering, Management and Law, Scholarships based on CAT/ MAT/ XAT/ CMAT, AICTE PG Scholarship for MTech and MBA students, Defense Scholarship for Management category students.

Will I get good placements after pursuing a course from Presidency University Bangalore?

Yes, you will get good placements after pursuing a course from Presidency University Bangalore. According to the placement report for 2023, a total of 2200 students have been placed for whom the highest package stood at Rs 13.58 LPA (for management degree) followed by Rs 11.8 LPA (for Engineering students). The average package was recorded at Rs 5.21 LPA.

What are the entrance exams accepted at Presidency University Bangalore?

The entrance exams accepted at Presidency University Bangalore are JEE, State Entrance Exam (CET), COMEDK, EAMCET for B.Tech., CLAT, LSAT for Law, CAT, MAT, XAT, ATMA, K-MAT, TANCET for BBA, UGAT for BBA, and UCEED, IIAD, AIEED, CEED, NIT, NIFT for B.Des.

What are the merit-based scholarships at Presidency University Bangalore?

The merit-based scholarships at Presidency University Bangalore are designed for Design and Engineering students under the Management category; LLB, BA, BSc, BBA, BCA and BCom students; and candidates pursuing postgraduate programmes in Engineering, Management and Law. The scholarship amount for these categories stands at Rs 20,000.

What is the cutoff for B.Tech in Presidency University Bangalore?

The cutoff for B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Presidency University Bangalore is expected to range from 34369 to 34374 ranks. The KCET cutoff 2024 for CSE is expected to vary from 34098 - 34108 for the General (Gender-Neutral) category. Who is eligible for MBA admission to Presidency University, Bangalore?

Candidates obtaining a Bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% marks (45% for SC/ST) and securing valid scores in CAT/XAT/MAT/CMAT/ATMA/KMAT Karnataka are eligible for MBA admission in Presidency University, Bangalore.

The highest package for MBA courses during Presidency University Bangalore placements last year was Rs 13.58 LPA. The average package was Rs Rs 5.21 LPA LPA.
