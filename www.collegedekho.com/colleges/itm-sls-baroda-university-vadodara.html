About ITM (SLS) Baroda University

ITM (SLS) Baroda University, established in 2011, is a private university located in Vadodara, Gujarat. The university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), Council of Architecture (COA), and the Gujarat Nursing Council. It holds accreditation from relevant accrediting bodies, ensuring academic quality and institutional credibility.

The university offers programs at undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma levels. Some of the most sought-after courses include B.Tech, B.Sc, MBA, B.Des, and BBA (Hons). Admissions to these programs are based on a combination of merit and performance in national-level entrance examinations. Accepted entrance exams include GUJCET, JEE Main, NATA, CMAT, and CAT.

ITM SLS Baroda University Application Process 2026

The ITM SLS Baroda University application process begins with completing an online entry form. Students are advised to evaluate their eligibility and selection criteria before completing the forms.

The ITMBU online application process has begun with UG and PG courses. Students wishing to apply for admission must go to the official website of the University to complete the application form and pay the application fee. The basic requirement for applying for any student degree course is that students must have 10 + 2 degrees with a mark of at least 50% in the relevant subjects.

Candidates must also appear on the JEE Main/ GUJCET Exam to apply for B.Tech. Welcome to B.Pharm. administered by the Joint Admission Committee for professional studies. Operating NATA schools are required to apply for B.Arch approval. In addition, candidates must receive 35% marks at the 10th level on any recognized board to apply for a diploma in Architecture.

To apply for any postgraduate course, candidates must have at least 50% official qualifications in the same subject they wish to apply for. Candidates must also have valid qualifications and positions at CMAT to apply for an MBA at the institution. Candidates will be selected on the basis of eligibility in the relevant graduation examination and post-graduation consultation on the advice of ACPC.

Step 1: Visit the Apply Online link in the acceptance menu on our website.

Step 2: Register yourself. Enter your details and select the application you want to access. You will receive a username and password on your mobile phone and email.

Step 3: Log in to the same portal now using your username and password.
