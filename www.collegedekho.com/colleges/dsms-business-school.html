About Durgapur Society Of Management Science, Durgapur (DSMS)

DSMS Durgapur is located in Durgapur, West Bengal. It was established in 1990. The college is affiliated with the Kazi Nazrul University (KNU) and the West Bengal University of Health Sciences (WBUHS). DSMS Durgapur is approved by INC and WBNC.

DSMS Durgapur has three colleges under it: DSMS College Of Tourism and Management, DSMS College Of Healthcare Management, and DSMS Institute Of Nursing. 

DSMS Institute Of Nursing offers GNM and B.Sc Nursing programmes. GNM course has an intake of 44 and B.Sc Nursing has an intake of 60. GNM course is three years and B.Sc nursing is four years in duration. The DSMS Institute of Nursing was established in 2021. The nursing courses are affiliated to West Bengal University of Health Sciences (WBUHS), Indian Nursing Council (INC), West Bengal Nursing Council (WBNC).

DSMS Durgapur courses are offered in the fields of business administration, hospital management, finance management, and computer application. DSMS Durgapur admissions are based on the merit of the students. The DSMS placements are also offered to eligible students.

Documents Required:

Durgapur Society Of Management Science, Durgapur (DSMS) Placements

DSMS Durgapur has an active placement cell that has special advisory programs for students to inform them about various available opportunities in their chosen field of discipline. Liaison work is executed between the potential employers and students to maintain all the influential contacts. DSMS Durgapur placements are conducted every year. Some of the top recruiters of this DSMS Durgapur are TCS, Bajaj Allianz, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank, Capgemini, and Accenture.
