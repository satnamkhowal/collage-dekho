hOWTOGET ADMISSION IN BSc (H) Geology in Department of Geology in Lucknow University

H
Himani Daryani •  Jul-31-2025

To get admission in BSc (Hons) Geology at the Department of Geology, Lucknow University, you need to apply through the university’s official admission portal. The admission is based on merit, which means your marks in Class 12 (especially in Science subjects) will be considered. You must have passed Class 12 with the Science stream (Physics, Chemistry, and either Mathematics or Biology) from a recognized board. Just make sure you fill out the application form on time when Lucknow University opens its UG admission process. Currently, LU UGET 2025 counselling is ongoing. Keep checking the university’s official website for updates on the admission dates, eligibility, and merit list announcements.
