Sanskriti University Admission 2026

Sanskriti University admissions are ongoing. To get admission, candidates must have to meet the minimum eligibility criteria set by the university for various offered courses. The university accepts various entrance exams for admission such as CUET UG, JEE Main, CUET PG, Sanskriti University National Admission Test (SUNAT), MAT, CAT, XAT, NMAT, etc. For admission to the UG courses, candidates must have passed class 12 from a recognised board with a minimum of 45% marks in the relevant stream. Entrance exams like CUET, UG, JEE Main, NEET UG are accepted for admission to the UG courses. To become eligible for the PG courses, candidates must have a graduate degree in the relevant stream from a recognised university. Entrance exams like CUET PG, SUNAT, MAT/ CAT, NMAT, XAT, and NAT are accepted by the university for PG admission at Sanskriti University.

Various scholarships are also offered to the candidates based on their merit, economic background and more. There are a total of 26 courses offered by the university in different specialisations. To apply for admission to the offered courses, candidates must apply online through the official website or you can also visit the admission office of the university to submit the duly filled application form in offline mode.

Sanskriti University Application Process 2026

Sanskriti University application process for the academic year 2025 is carried out in both online and offline modes. To be considered for admission, all applicants must complete the application. The following outlines the step-by-step procedure for completing the Sanskriti University application form.

How to Fill out the Sanskriti University Application Form?

Online method:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Sanskriti University. 
  • Step 2: Click the "Apply Now" button on the page in the upper right corner. A new page will appear on your screen. 
  • Step 3: Applicants must provide the required information on the application form, including their name, email address, state, discipline, programme, city, and mobile number. 
  • Step 4: An OTP will be sent to the candidates. After entering the OTP, select "Apply Now." 
  • Step 5: Verify your specialisation or programme. A system ID will be created for you. 
  • Step 6: Pay the Sanskriti University application fee using one of the offered payment options.
  • Step 7: The candidates must scan and upload a copy of their signature and photo to the online application. Following completion of the required fields, a payment option will be available for the non-refundable registration costs and form submission. 
  • Step 8: A registration number will be generated for each student for future use following the successful processing of the form submission.

Offline method:

  • Step 1: The application form can be picked up in person from the Sanskriti University office for Rs 1200 or sent via postal mail with a demand draft for Rs 1250. 
  • Step 2: The candidate must deliver the completed application form they downloaded, together with a Demand Draft for Rs 1250 made payable to Sanskriti University in Mathura, if they wish to submit it offline. The Bank Draft and the properly completed application form will be sent to the Registrar. 
  • Step 3: Students will get their registration numbers via email following the submission of their completed application form and bank draft.

Sanskriti University Admission FAQs

What percentage is required to get admission to Sanskriti University?

The percentage required for admission to Sanskriti University varies according to the degree you want to pursue. To be eligible for undergraduate programmes, students must have passed their 10+2 exam from a recognised board with at least 50% marks. A minimum cumulative GPA of 50% must be scored in an undergraduate degree from a recognised university for candidates seeking to the postgraduate programmes. For diploma programmes, candidates must have passed class 10 with a minimum of 50% marks overall.

Can I get scholarship in Sanskriti University at the time of admission?

Even though Sanskriti University provides a variety of scholarships, being awarded one at the time of enrollment is dependent on a number of variables. Your academic standing on the qualifying exam determines whether you are awarded a merit scholarship (typically class 12th grades for undergraduate programmes). Certain scholarships may be contingent upon achieving high marks on admission tests, such as the Sanskriti University National Admission Test (SUNAT) or national exams, such as the CAT, MAT, XAT, or NMAT, for a given programme. Programmes such as the Chancellor's Scholarship Scheme require the inclusion of extra elements, like family income.

Is it compulsory to live in hostel to pursue education at Sanskriti University?

No, staying in the hostel is not a requirement for admission to Sanskriti University courses. For those who would rather live on campus, the university provides hostel facilities; however, living on the hostel campus is not required for enrollment or attendance.

What is the application fee for the Sanskriti University admission?

The application fee for the Sanskriti University admissions is Rs 1,200 for each course, which is non-refundable. This is the fee if the candidates are submitting the application form directly at the university campus. If the candidates are unable to visit the university campus in person, they can also submit the application form by post with an application fee of Rs 1,250.

Does Sanskriti University have a separate entrance exam for admission?

Yes, Sanskriti University National Admission Test (SUNAT) is administered by the university itself to provide admission to the courses. Various other entrance exams are also accepted by the university such as JEE Main (B.Tech), NEET UG (BAMS), CUET UG (all UG courses), CUET PG (all PG courses), MAT, XAT, CAT, NMAT (for MBA courses) and more.

Is it good to take admission to Sanskriti University?

Yes, it is always a good decision to take admission to Sanskriti University, situated in Mathura. The university offers a wide range of courses across various fields, including engineering, science, management, law, arts, and more. Sanskriti University is recognised by UGC and provides a variety of undergraduate and graduate programmes. With a stated 91% placement rate for the 2022 year, they have a stellar placement history. Additionally, they collaborate internationally on student exchange programmes.

Does Sanskriti University offer direct admission to offered courses?

For admission to the offered courses, Sanskriti University can not get a seat directly. The university accepts various entrance exams such as CUET UG, CUET PG, SUNAT, MAT, CAT,XAT, NMAT or more followed by a PI and GD rounds for selection to the different courses.

Does Sanskriti University accept CAT for admission?

Yes, Sanskriti University accepts CAT for admission to the MBA course. Candidates who wish to get admission to MBA programmes at university must have a valid score in CAT. MAT, XAT, NMAT, SUNAT, etc. are also accepted for admission to MBA.

What is the mode of application for Sanskriti University admission?

The mode of application for Sanskriti University admission to all the offered courses is both online and offline.

What is the criteria for Sanskriti University admission?

To get admission to Sanskriti University, interested candidates have to follow the admissions process. The application form can be completed in person at the admission office or online at the official website. Candidates must first fulfil the minimal requirements for admission to the course of their choice. Subsequently, applicants need to complete the Sanskriti University application form and submit the required application fee for each subject they are applying for. The selection of successful candidates is determined by their combined performance in the entrance exam and the most recent qualifying exam. After that, you have to show up for the document verification and counselling. Finally, in order to guarantee a spot at the university, pay the admission fees.
