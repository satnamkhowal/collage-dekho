Impact College of Engineering and Applied Sciences Job Ready Degree

Imagine getting an offer letter right after you graduate. That would be a dream come true for many students. Such a thing is possible with Impact College of Engineering and Applied Sciences job ready courses. These courses makes you ready for the job market. Impact College of Engineering and Applied Sciences offers a BTech in Full Stack Development (IXP) course which is designed to equip you with skills which are in high demand now and will be in the future. Impact College of Engineering and Applied Sciences fees for Job ready degree is Rs 2,45,000 per year. BTech in Full Stack Development (IXP) is a four-year course. Students must have passed class 12 with 50% marks and with PCM course combination to apply for this job ready course. Interested students can check the table below for more information on Impact College of Engineering and Applied Sciences job ready courses.

Impact College of Engineering and Applied Sciences Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

Impact College of Engineering and Applied Sciences admission to job ready courses is based on a personal interview round which is conducted by the institute. Students who meet the eligibility criteria for BTech in Full Stack Development can fill out the application form and pay the admission fee. The institute will contact the applicant for the interview round.
