About CGC Landran

With a distinguished legacy of 25 years, CGC Landran, under the aegis of the Sri Guru Ramdass Educational Society, has been committed to providing quality higher education. Established in 2001, CGC Landran, affiliated with IKGPTU, has grown into a sprawling 45-acre campus that accommodates approximately 13,000 students from across the country. The institution offers 50+ undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Engineering, Management, Biotechnology, Pharmacy, Computer Applications, and Hotel Management.

The institution has received recognition from prestigious accreditation and ranking bodies, including NAAC (A+), NBA, QS I-GAUGE, NIRF, IPR, IIC, and ARIIA, and has secured ₹25 crores in grants for funded projects. The campus features state-of-the-art facilities, including an Artificial Intelligence (AI) Skills Lab, a Drone Technology Lab, an Advanced Manufacturing Lab, an AICTE Idea Lab, an Intellectual Property Rights Cell, and a Centre of Excellence for Cyber Security, all of which create opportunities for learning and skill development aligned with current industry requirements.

CGC Landran has established global academic partnerships with over 65+ reputable institutions across the USA, UK, Australia, and beyond. Its 48,000-strong alumni network further enhances its legacy and industry presence. 

CGC Landran boasts an impressive placement record, with over 1,300 multinational companies visiting the campus and more than 10,000 job offers made, including the highest package that exceeds INR 1.14 Cr PA

Additionally, CGC Landran plays a significant role in Punjab’s startup ecosystem through the Department of RISE (Research, Innovation, Special Projects & Entrepreneurship). The centre has mentored nearly 125+ student startups and supports rural entrepreneurs. 

A vibrant campus life, dedicated faculty, and exceptional students make CGC Landran a beacon of academic excellence and a launchpad for outstanding careers.

CGC Landran Admission 2026

The admission process is based on entrance exams and/or merit, as applicable for the chosen programme, and eligibility is determined by Class 12 or graduation criteria defined for the specific programme. The CGC Landran Admission process is conducted either online or offline and includes form filling, document scrutiny, fee submission, and counselling, as applicable.

  • Admission to B.Tech is entrance exam-based, with maths and physics as compulsory subjects in 12th.
  • For B.Tech lateral entry, one must have scored a minimum of 45 % in the diploma of engineering.
  • For admission to B.Sc, BBA, BCA, B.Pharma, and B.Ed, the candidate needs to have scored 45 - 50 % in their 12th board exams.
  • Admission to M.Tech is based on GATE, followed by PTU counseling with B Tech in relevant streams.
  • Admissions to M.Sc, and MCA at Chandigarh Group of Colleges are merit-based with graduation in a relevant discipline.
  • For getting admission into B. Pharma, a 45 % score in the 12th board exam is a must, along with physics and chemistry as compulsory subjects.
  • Admission to the MBA is merit-based with graduation in relevant fields.

Chandigarh Group of Colleges Application Process 

Interested candidates can access the website, log in to follow the application procedure, and stay informed about the status of a particular student's application. The official website of the Chandigarh Group of Colleges provides information about the application process. Once the college accepts the application, the students must attend counseling and complete the admission procedure.

Chandigarh Group of Colleges Application Fees 

The application form for admissions can be filled out online from the official website of Chandigarh Group of Colleges, Landran, or it can be done offline with a payment of INR 1000 as the application fee.

How to Fill Chandigarh Group of Colleges Application Form?

Students who wish to get admission can easily apply online or simply walk in and get it done at the Chandigarh Group of Colleges, Landran campus.

Online Application:

  • The candidates have to visit the Chandigarh Group of Colleges' official website.
  • After selecting the apply online option from the admission icon, the candidate needs to create a login ID and then fill up the application form.
  • The Landran application process requires submitting requisite documents, photographs, and signatures that a candidate has to upload in the given area.
  • The final step would be to transfer 'Prospectus' & 'College Fee' to the Institute account through an online payment mode.
  • Candidates should take a printout of the filled application form of the Chandigarh Group of Colleges, Landran, and keep it safe for other procedures.

Walk-in Admissions:

  • The candidate needs to collect the application form from the Chandigarh Group of Colleges, and fill-up the form.
  • The application form should be submitted along with the student's original documents, application form fee, and five passport size photographs.
  • After an eligibility check and fee submission, the admission will be confirmed.

CGC Landran FAQs

Is CGC Landran fees allowed to be paid in installments?

Academic fees must be paid full in advance in the start of every Semester. The CGC Landran hostel fee ranges from INR 1,40,000 to INR 1,60,000 per year which can be paid in two installments. Transport fee must be paid full in advance in the start of every year.

Who are the leading employers who have visited the campus for CGC Landran placements?

At CGC, Landran, the Corporate Resource Centre continuously strives to close the knowledge gap between industry and academics, provide students with training, and keep them up to date on industry standards. To mention a few, recruiters like Amazon, Dell, Wipro, Cipla, Capgemini, CSC, Axis Bank, Google, Microsoft, HCL, Infotech, Oracle, and Cognizant have hired from this campus.

CGC Landran is affiliated to which university?

CGC Landran is associated with IKGPTU Jalandhar, NCHMCT, and Punjabi University (Patiala). The courses at CGC Landran are affiliated to these universities only.

What is the Chandigarh Group of Colleges Landran fees for M.Sc?

CGC Landran Mohali, M.Sc. Biotechnology fees is INR 1,84,000. The eligibility for the course is BSc. Biotechnology or BSc (Medical/ Zoology/ Botany/ Microbiology/ Genetics/ Biochemistry/ Pharmacy/ Agriculture/ Veterinary/ Engineering/ Nursing/ Medical Lab Technology) or BTech (Biotech) in the related discipline.

How is the CGC Landran Mohali infrastructure?

CGC Landran Mohali boasts a modern and well-equipped infrastructure to cater to the academic and holistic development of its students. It offers state-of-the-art classrooms equipped with smart boards, projectors, and audio-visual facilities for interactive learning.
