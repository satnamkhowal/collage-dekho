Parul University Average Package 2026

Parul University, located in Gujarat, India, is renowned for its diverse range of academic programs and robust placement opportunities. The average package offered to graduates plays a pivotal role in evaluating the institution's effectiveness in preparing students for the professional world. In 2024, Parul University reported an average package of approximately INR 4.50 LPA across most courses, with certain specialized programs like BTech experiencing higher averages ranging between INR 7 LPA to INR 8 LPA. This comprehensive overview delves into the placement statistics, average packages across various courses, factors influencing these packages, and the fee structure at Parul University.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about Parul University

1. What is the average package for MBA at Parul University?

  • The Parul University average package for MBA was approximately INR 7 LPA in 2022 and INR 3.6 LPA in 2021. While 2024 data is not fully available, the university’s strong industry ties suggest competitive salaries for MBA graduates.

2. What is the average package for CSE at Parul University?

  • The Parul University average package for CSE (BTech in Computer Science and Engineering) ranges between INR 4-5 LPA based on recent placement drives. Top offers for CSE students have reached up to INR 18 LPA from companies like Orad Consultancy Pvt Ltd.

3. What is the highest package offered at Parul University?

  • The Parul University highest package recorded in 2024 was INR 46 LPA, offered to a BTech student. In 2023, the highest package was INR 30 LPA, indicating a significant increase in top offers.

4. What is the placement rate at Parul University?

  • Parul University boasts a 100% placement rate for the 2021-2022 academic year. In 2024, over 2,200 students were placed through 1,300+ recruiters, showcasing a strong Parul University placement rate.

5. What are the MBA fees at Parul University?

  • The Parul University MBA fees range from INR 1.5 lakh to INR 8.8 lakh for the two-year program, depending on the specialization and mode (on-campus or online). The online MBA costs approximately INR 90,000.

6. What is the average package for BTech at Parul University?

  • The Parul University average package for BTech students in 2024 was around INR 4.5 LPA. This includes various specializations, with Computer Science Engineering often securing slightly higher averages.

7. Which companies visit Parul University for placements?

  • Parul University placement companies include top-tier firms like Amazon, Google, TCS, Infosys, IBM, Adobe, Tesla, Amul, and Godrej. Over 2,200 recruiters participate annually, offering diverse roles across sectors.

8. What is the MBA placement percentage at Parul University?

  • While specific Parul University MBA placement percentage data for 2024 is unavailable, the university achieved a 100% placement rate across programs in 2022. MBA students typically secure roles in reputed companies like TCS and BYJU’S.

9. What are the general fees at Parul University?

  • Parul University fees vary by program. For example, MBA fees range from INR 1.5 lakh to INR 8.8 lakh, while BTech fees are around INR 5.96 lakh for four years. Additional costs like hostel fees (INR 64,000–INR 1.08 lakh annually) may apply.

10. What are the fees for BTech at Parul University?

  • The Parul University fees for BTech are approximately INR 5.96 lakh for the four-year program, with annual fees ranging from INR 1.2 lakh to INR 1.5 lakh, depending on the specialization.

11. What is the admission process at Parul University?

The Parul University admission process involves:

  • BTech: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology, plus a valid JEE Main or GUJCET score. Selection is based on merit and counseling.
  • MBA: A bachelor’s degree with 50% aggregate (45% for reserved categories). Admission is merit-based, with no mandatory entrance exams like CAT/MAT.
    Applications can be submitted online or offline, followed by document verification and fee payment.

12. What is Parul University’s NIRF ranking?

  • In the Parul University NIRF ranking for 2024, the university is placed in the 101-150 band for the overall category and ranks 11th in Innovation. It is also among the top 75 business administration institutes and top 100 engineering institutes in India, per other rankings.

For further details, visit Parul University’s official website or contact the admission office at 1800-123-1104.
