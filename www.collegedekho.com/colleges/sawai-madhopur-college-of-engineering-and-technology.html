About SMCET Jaipur

Sawai Madhopur College of Engineering & Technology is situated in Jaipur in Rajasthan state of India. Established in 2013, SMCET is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. Sawai Madhopur College of Engineering & Technology offers 240 courses across 17 streams namely Commerce and Banking, Design, Engineering, Management, IT..Popular degrees offered at Sawai Madhopur College of Engineering & Technology include B.Tech, BSc, BA, BSc+MSc, BA+MA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Sawai Madhopur College of Engineering & Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Sawai Madhopur College of Engineering & Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Sawai Madhopur College of Engineering & Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
