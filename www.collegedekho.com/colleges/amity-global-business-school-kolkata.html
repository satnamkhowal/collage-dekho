About Amity Global Business School (AGBS), Kolkata

Amity Global Business School (AGBS), Kolkata is situated in Kolkata in West Bengal state of India. Established in 2008, is a Private college. Amity Global Business School (AGBS), Kolkata offers 60 courses across 12 streams namely Management, IT, Engineering, Law, Arts..Popular degrees offered at Amity Global Business School (AGBS), Kolkata include PGD, B.Tech, BSc, BA, B.Des..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Amity Global Business School (AGBS), Kolkata is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Amity Global Business School (AGBS), Kolkata, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Amity Global Business School (AGBS), Kolkata is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
