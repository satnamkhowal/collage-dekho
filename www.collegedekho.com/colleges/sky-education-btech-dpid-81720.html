About Sky Education B.Tech

Sky Education B.Tech is a 4 Years year program. To be eligible for the B.Tech degree program at Sky Education, New Delhi, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education with a minimum aggregate of 50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Additionally, they must have a valid score in a recognized entrance exam such as JEE Main or any other equivalent state-level engineering entrance exam. Candidates from the reserved category will be given relaxation in the minimum aggregate percentage as per government norms. It is also mandatory for candidates to fulfill any other specific eligibility criteria set by the university or regulatory bodies. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 3,00,000. Sky Education B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Sky Education is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Sky Education B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Sky Education B.Tech Application Process

Sky Education B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Sky Education have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Sky Education B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Sky Education and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Sky Education. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Sky Education B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Sky Education B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Sky Education B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Sky Education B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
