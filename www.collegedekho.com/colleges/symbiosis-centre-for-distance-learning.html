About Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning Pune

Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning (SCDL), Pune is one of India's prominent autonomous distance learning institutes. SCDL is located in Pune, Maharashtra and has come a long way in offering quality education to the students since its establishment in 2001. Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning is approved by the All India Council of Technical Education (AICTE). 

SCDL Pune offers a range of PG Diploma, PG Certificate, and  Diploma and Certificate programs in fields such as Business Management, International Business, Information Technology, Banking and Finance, Supply Chain Management, Customer Relationship Management, Insurance Management, Education Management, Business and Corporate Law and Entrepreneurship Development and many more. Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning has been ranked 1st among the Top Distance Learning Institutes by Careers 360 Rating 2012 and ranked 2nd among the leading institutes by DNA Indus Learning Survey 2012. SCDL has received several other awards as well. 

SCDL has partnered with several national and international companies to offer corporate programs.

SCDL Pune is an ISO 9001:2015 certified Institution. In 2018, Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning was awarded ISO 9001:2015 certification. The certificate was awarded to SCDL for the lucrative establishment of "Quality Management System" by NVT Quality Certification Pvt. Ltd. NVT Quality Certification Pvt. Ltd. is accredited by ANAB. To provide quality education to the students, SCDL Pune collects the study material from its sister management institutes and the corporate industry. 

The curriculum offered by SCDL is similar to that of any reputed management college. The curriculum is designed and updated by the top experts available in the industry holding several years of educational experience. Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning, Pune, offers to learn through online training, printed study content, virtual faculty interaction, and e-mentoring. SCDL offers 24 Post Graduate Programs, 10 Post Graduate diplomas, and 10 Diploma and Certificate Programs. 

The students seeking admission to SCDL Pune must apply on the website and follow the process. The student should hold a bachelor's degree from a recognised institute. SCDL also offers placement assistance to the students by connecting them with several recruiters.

How to Fill SCDL Pune Application Form?

Step 1: Visit the Sign-Up and register on the SCDL online application portal. Login to the portal to purchase the application form & fill the application form online with the login credentials received at your registered email address. Download the e-prospectus for future reference. 

Application forms are available online only. The SCDL Pune application fee is Rs 1,200 and it is non-refundable. Purchase request for an application form by post is not accepted by Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning Pune.

Step 2: Upload your passport size, recent colour photograph, Photo Identity Proof, educational certificates, signature & work experience certificate (if any) in the prescribed format. 

Step 3: Submit your complete program fee as per the stipulated timelines at Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning. You can pay the fees online through the payment gateway. Another method of payment is through a demand draft for the enrolled program.
