About Chitkara University BA

Chitkara University BA is a 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the B.A. degree at Chitkara University, Patiala, applicants must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board with a minimum of 45% marks in aggregate. The annual fee for BA is INR 1,14,000 - 6,00,000. Chitkara University BA Admission 2026

Admission to the BA at Chitkara University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Chitkara University BA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Chitkara University BA Application Process

Chitkara University BA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BA course at Chitkara University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Chitkara University BA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Chitkara University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Chitkara University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Chitkara University BA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Chitkara University BA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Chitkara University BA Application Form.

Documents Required for Chitkara University BA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
