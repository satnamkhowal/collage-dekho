JECRC University Campus

JECRC University has one of the best campuses in India. The 32-acre campus of JECRC University is equipped with all modern facilities and amenities. Most of the campus is covered with greenery that offers a pleasant and vibrant learning environment. Rain harvesting at JECRC University makes sure that each drop of rain is conserved.

The central library at JECRC University is one of the major highlights of the campus. The library is open for twelve hours, and students can access all education-related content at the library. In order to provide first-hand knowledge to students, the varsity is slowly transforming its library into a digital library.

The computer labs at JECRC University have the latest technology and equipment. The varsity has 15 computer labs comprising of 500 computers with 25 MBPS internet connectivity.

JECRC University has the best residential facilities for students. Single rooms are offered to students at the hostels. The common living room at JECRC University is equipped with television, internet access, and pantry.

The sports complex at JECRC University provides professional guidance and coaching for interested students. The hostel mess provides delicious, hygienic and nutritious food to students. The campus has a first-aid centre for emergency medical care.

JECRC University Hostel

On-campus residential facility for students has been very thoughtfully designed and planned. Each unit in the JU Hostel Complex allows privacy and personal space to a student while giving a chance to dwell together and foster comradeship. An apartment style -three private, single occupancy rooms open into a common living room equipped with a television set, internet access and a pantry making it a complete independent unit. The hostels are air-conditioned/ Non air-conditioned, comfortable and conducive for the students to have a productive campus life, providing a perfect setting for academic pursuits.

JECRC University Campus FAQs

What are the various facilities available on campus at JECRC , Jaipur ?

JECRC , Jaipur offers various facilities to its students including Library, Computer Lab, Sports, Hostel, Cafeteria and more.

Is the JECRC Jaipur campus WiFi enabled?

Yes, the JECRC University Jaipur campus is WiFi enabled which helps the students get access to any information from anywhere in the campus.

How are the mess and cafeteria facilities which are offered at the JECRC Jaipur campus?

The mess and cafeteria facilities which are offered at JECRC Jaipur campus are excellent that ensures the students do not miss the vibes of being at home.

Are there any clubs at the JECRC University Jaipur?

Yes, there are several clubs for students who want to develop or showcase their extracurricular skills through these clubs by being a part of them.

Does JECRC University Jaipur provides accommodation facilities to its students?

Yes, JECRC Jaipur offers accommodation facilities (hostel) to both its boy and girl students. It offers separate accommodation facilities for outstation and local students as well.

What sports facilities are available for the students at the JECRC University Jaipur campus?

The students are offered various sports facilities within the JECRC Jaipur campus like badminton, football, volleyball, basketball, in addition to a well-equipped gymnasium to ensure the students can maintain their strength and endurance.

What is the total campus area of JECRC University Jaipur?

The JECRC University Jaipur encompasses 32 acres of land which it calls its campus.

Do the students have to maintain a minimum attendance percentage to be eligible for the JECRC Jaipur scholarship programme?

Yes, the students have to maintain a minimum attendance percentage of 75% and secure the minimum aggregate to be eligible for the JECRC University Jaipur scholarship programme.
