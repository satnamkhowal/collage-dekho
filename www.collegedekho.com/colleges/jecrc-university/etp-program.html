JECRC University Job Ready Degree

In the rapidly evolving industry, JCERC University is offering six new age job ready degree programmes. These programmes are meticulously designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements. BBA in New Age Sales and Marketing, is a three year course that equips students with modern marketing strategies. It has an annual fee of Rs 1,45,000 and requires a minimum of 50% in class 12 from any recognised board. The MBA programme at JCERC University offers three specialisations - New Age Sales and Marketing, Applied HR, and Global Financial Operations. These programmes have a fee of Rs 1,25,000 annually and require a graduation degree with a minimum of 50% marks. JCERC University job ready degrees are not just about earning a qualification but about preparing you for a successful career in the future.

JECRC University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

JCERC University offers a hassle-free admission process for its job-ready degree programmes. Candidates are selected purely on the basis of merit in their qualifying examination. There are no entrance exams to worry about. All that's required is to fill out the JCERC University application form, pay the admission fees and and attending a personal interview.
