About Parul University Fees 2026

Parul University fees range from INR 32,000 to INR 33.75 Lakhs across undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, integrated, medical, law, pharmacy, design, and certificate programs. Parul University B.Tech fees is INR 7 Lakhs for the 4-year duration, while Industry Embedded B.Tech programs range up to INR 10.6 Lakhs. The total fees for the MBA program ranges from INR 4.5 Lakhs to INR 6 Lakhs, while MCA and M.Sc programs cost around INR 3.2 Lakhs. 

BBA at Parul University costs around 4.65 Lakhs to INR 8.75 Lakhs, BCA costs INR 4.35 Lakhs to INR 11.25 Lakhs, and B.Sc has a total fees of INR 2.40 Lakhs to INR 9 Lakhs. Medical programs such as BAMS have a total fee of INR 22.30 Lakhs to INR 33.75 Lakhs, BHMS costs INR 11.15 Lakhs to INR 15.75 Lakhs, and MBBS costs INR 99 Lakhs to INR 1.10 Crore. Diploma programs cost INR 2.34 Lakhs to INR 6.25 Lakhs for the full duration.

  • Overall Fees: Parul University fee structure ranges from INR 32,000 to INR 33.75 Lakhs for the entire duration.
  • UG Fees: Undergraduate fees range from INR 32,000 to INR 14.16 Lakhs depending on the course and specialization.
  • PG Fees: Postgraduate courses cost between INR 1.26 Lakhs to INR 6 Lakhs for the full duration.
  • Diploma Fees: Diploma programs range from INR 2.34 Lakhs to INR 6.25 Lakhs.
  • B.Tech Fees: Parul University B.Tech fees is INR 7 Lakhs for 4 years, which includes tuition and institutional charges. Industry-embedded B.Tech programs can go up to INR 10.6 Lakhs.

How to Pay Parul University Fees?

Students at Parul University can pay their fees through both online and offline modes. The online payment facility is available on the official portal and supports multiple options such as net banking, debit/credit cards, and UPI. For offline payments, students can visit the campus and pay via demand draft (DD). It is important to keep the payment receipt safely for future reference.

Steps to pay Parul University fees:

  1. Visit the official fee payment portal
  2. Select the relevant fee category (tuition, hostel, registration, etc.)
  3. Enter details like enrollment number, name, and course
  4. Choose a payment method (net banking/card/UPI)
  5. Verify the amount and complete the transaction
  6. Download and save the payment receipt
  7. For offline payment, submit a DD in favour of “Parul University” at the campus cashier

At the time of admission, students are required to pay a non-refundable application fee (INR 300–500) along with any applicable security or hostel deposits. Certain programs, such as online MBA, may offer installment-based payment options through the portal.
