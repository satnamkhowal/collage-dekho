VIT Vellore Admission 2026

VIT Vellore Admission 2026 follows a clear and fully merit-based system for all undergraduate and postgraduate programs across engineering, management, IT, and sciences. B.Tech admissions are based only on VITEEE, with applicants expected to score at least 60% in Class 12. Non-engineering UG courses such as BBA, BCA, and B.Sc are offered through direct Class 12 merit. For PG admissions, VITMEE is used for M.Tech and MCA, while MBA accepts CAT, XAT, MAT, or VITBEE scores, giving added advantage to strong GATE and CAT performers.

The entire admission journey—from registration and application submission to online exams, ranking, counseling choice-filling, and document verification—is handled smoothly through VIT’s official online portals, ensuring transparency and eliminating offline steps. Scholarships based on entrance exam ranks and scores help top candidates reduce their tuition significantly. With strong placements, advanced labs, and flexible program options, VIT Vellore continues to be one of India’s most trusted choices for higher education.

VIT Vellore Admission Process 2026

VIT Vellore’s admission process for 2026 is fully merit-based, transparent, and conducted online, covering registration to final enrollment. Key steps include:

  • Registration: OTP-verified on the official portal; an application number is generated.
  • Application Submission: Upload academic documents and select the relevant stream (PCM/BPCB).
  • Fee Payment: Complete the non-refundable VIT Vellore fee to validate the submission.
  • Entrance Exam: Computer-based test with 80 questions in 90 minutes (VITEEE/VITMEE depending on program).
  • Counseling & Seat Allotment: Login-based choice filling; partial advance fee (₹10,000) secures the seat.
  • Document Verification & Reporting: Submit original documents at the campus; provisional admission possible if Class 12 marksheet is pending.

VIT Vellore Admission FAQs

What documents are needed for the VIT Vellore application process?

As per the VIT Vellore admission process, you need a scanned photo and signature, age proof, Class 12 Admit Card, entrance exam hall ticket and scorecard, community certificate (if applicable), and mark sheets for the application form.

Can I get direct admission in VIT Vellore?

Vellore Institute of Technology does not offer direct admission. It is necessary that you go through the entrance exams and selection rounds. VIT Vellore conducts VITEEE for B.Tech, VITMEE for M.Tech, and VITREE for PhD admissions.

Is it very tough to get admission to VIT Vellore?

It is not that hard to get admission to VIT Vellore Institute; all eligible candidates must be well prepared to sit for the entrance exam conducted by the authority and follow all the steps to get admission in VIT Vellore.

Does VIT accept the JEE Main score?

No, it does not accept the JEE Main score for admission. VIT Vellore conducts VITEEE for admission to UG courses like B.Tech.

What is the selection process for M.Tech admission at VIT Vellore?

Candidates who wish to pursue M.tech courses have to appear for the VITMEE, which is conducted by the institute, and get valid marks. A list will be prepared on the basis of the marks obtained in VITMEE. Selected candidates will be called for online counselling.
