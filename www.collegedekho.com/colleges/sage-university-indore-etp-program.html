SAGE University Indore Job Ready Degree

With the rising need for skilled courses, SAGE University Indore has come up with a few job ready “Assured” programmes both at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels in design, engineering and management streams. These courses are skill-based and will provide students with the latest knowledge in their respective fields. The courses are different from the traditional courses available at SAGE University Indore. B.Des in UX (IXP), BBA in Insurance and Investment Management (IXP), MBA in Insurance and Investment Management (IXP), B.Des in Animation and Visual Effects (IXP), and B.Tech+MBA in Big Data Analytics (IXP) are the SAGE University Indore Job Ready Degree programmes. The fee ranges from Rs 75,000 to Rs 2,00,000 per year. If you are interested, go through the information provided below to know more about these “Assured” programmes in detail:
