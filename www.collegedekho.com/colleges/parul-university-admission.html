Parul University Admission Process 2026

Both direct admission and entrance-based admission takes place at Parul University. For B.Tech, B.Arch, BAMS, BHMS, entrance exam scores such as JEE Main, GUJCET, NATA, or NEET are accepted.

For most UG and PG programs including BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA, MBA, MCA, M.Sc, and MA, admissions are granted based on merit in the qualifying examination. Certain programs like B.Des, BPES, and some Allied Health or postgraduate medical programs may require university-level entrance tests, portfolio review, or interviews.

Below is the step-by-step process for Parul University admission for merit based programs like BBA, BCA, B.Com, B.Sc, MBA, MCA, etc.:

  1. Visit the Parul University admission portal at admissions.paruluniversity.ac.in
  2. Register with your name, email ID, and mobile number. A password is sent to your phone.
  3. Log in and select your desired program from the dropdown. Confirm your specialisation if applicable.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and contact details accurately in the online Parul University admission form.
  5. Upload scanned copies of required documents (Class 10 and 12 marksheets, transfer certificate, passport photo, Aadhaar card).
  6. Pay the Parul University registration fee of INR 500 online via debit card, credit card, or net banking.
  7. Submit the form. Note your application number for future reference.
  8. An admission counsellor from PU will call you to guide the next steps, including seat confirmation.
  9. If shortlisted, candidates can complete document verification at the campus or via courier.
  10. Pay the first-semester/semester fees to confirm and lock your seat.

Parul University BTech Admission Process 2026 

  1. Register and appear for JEE Main (conducted by NTA) or GUJCET (conducted by GSEB).
  2. Register for ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses) Gujarat counselling at acpc.gujarat.gov.in
  3. Enter your JEE Main/GUJCET rank and choice-fill your preferred BTech specialisations at Parul University in order of preference.
  4. ACPC allots seats based on merit rank and availability. Check allotment results on the ACPC portal.
  5. If the seat is allotted, report to Parul University campus for document verification. Carry original documents.
  6. Pay the first-semester BTech fees at the fee counter or via online transfer to confirm your seat.

Students who miss the ACPC counselling window or want a management quota seat can apply directly through Parul University's portal. Direct admissions include merit-based evaluation (50% weightage to entrance score + 50% to Class 12 marks for BTech).

Parul University MBBS Admission Process 2026

  1. Register and appear for NEET UG (conducted by NTA). 
  2. After NEET results, register for MCC (Medical Counselling Committee) AIQ counselling at mcc.nic.in which covers All India Quota seats.
  3. Gujarat state quota MBBS seats are filled through ACPDC Gujarat counselling. Register at acpdc.dte.gujarat.gov.in after NEET results.
  4. Once the seat is allotted, report to Parul Institute of Medical Science and Research for document verification and fee payment.

The minimum NEET score for Parul University MBBS admission through the state quota is the 50th percentile for General category and 40th percentile for reserved category, as mandated by the National Medical Commission (NMC).

How to Apply for Parul University Admission 2026

The Parul University application process is entirely online. There is no offline form for the main campus programs. Follow these steps to fill the Parul University admission form for the 2026-27 session.

  1. Open the Parul University admission portal: admissions.paruluniversity.ac.in
  2. Click 'Register' if you are a new user. Provide your full name, mobile number, email ID, city, and date of birth. A one-time password (OTP) will be sent to your phone.
  3. Log in using your registered credentials. This is the Parul University admission login page.
  4. From the dropdown, select the faculty (e.g., Engineering & Technology, Management Studies) and the specific program you want.
  5. Fill in your personal details: name, father's name, date of birth, gender, address, and mobile number.
  6. Enter your academic details: Class 10 and 12 board, year, marks, and stream. For PG programs, add your graduation details. For BTech, enter your JEE Main or GUJCET score if applicable.
  7. Upload scanned documents and pay the Parul University registration fee of INR 500 using debit card, credit card, net banking, or UPI. This fee is non-refundable.
  8. Submit the form and save or print your application confirmation. Your application number is your reference for all queries.
  9. An admission counsellor from PU calls you within 2-3 working days. They guide you through seat confirmation, document verification, and fee payment.

After applying, you can log in to the Parul University admission portal at any time to check your application status, upload additional documents, or complete fee payment for seat confirmation.

Documents Required for Parul University Admission 2026

Keep the following documents ready in scanned PDF or JPG format for Parul University course admissions. Original documents are required at the time of physical document verification on campus.

  • Class 10 marksheet and passing certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet and passing certificate (or provisional marksheet if result is awaited)
  • Graduation degree marksheets (all semesters/years) - for PG admissions only
  • Entrance exam scorecard - JEE Main / GUJCET (BTech), NEET (MBBS/BAMS/BHMS), NATA (BArch), CAT/MAT/CMAT (MBA), PU-DAT (Design), GATE (MTech)
  • Transfer certificate (TC) from last institution attended
  • Character/school leaving certificate
  • Migration certificate (for students from boards outside Gujarat)
  • Domicile or nativity certificate (required for Gujarat state quota seats)
  • Category certificate - SC/ST/OBC/EWS issued by competent authority (if applicable)
  • Defence or ex-serviceman certificate (if applying under defence quota)
  • Passport-size photographs - 4 to 6 copies
  • Aadhaar card (candidate and both parents)
  • Passport (for international students)
  • Anti-ragging affidavit - available on the official portal, to be signed and uploaded
  • Medical fitness certificate (mandatory for MBBS, BAMS, Nursing, and allied health programs)
  • Gap certificate (self-declaration) - only if there is a gap year between studies

Students applying for NRI or management quota must additionally provide a parent's income proof and, where applicable, NRI proof of residence. For diploma-to-BTech lateral entry, attach your diploma marksheets and completion certificate.

Parul University Admission FAQs

Does Parul University conduct any entrance exams?

Parul University conducts its entrance exam known as the Parul University Design Admission Test (PU-DAT) for admission to B.Des programmes. Moreover, it accepts various entrance exams such as GUJCET/JEE Main for admission to B.Tech and CAT/MAT/CMAT for admission to MBA.

How to apply for Parul University?

Follow the steps to apply for Parul University admission. Visit the official website of Parul University> Click on the admission link> Complete the registration> Get a call from the university> Complete the application and enrollment process> Pay the Parul University admission fees> Get the confirmation.

What is the admission criteria for MBA at Parul University?

Admission to the MBA at Parul University is based on the students performance in CAT/MAT or CMAT examinations. Aspiring students must have a bachelors degree with at least 50% marks to apply for admission to the MBA programme.

Can I get direct admission in Parul University?

Yes, you can get direct admission to various courses at Parul University. Such courses are BA, BBA, BCA, B.Com, B.Sc, B.Pharm, LLB, MA, M.Com, MCA, M.Sc, etc. For Parul University admission to these courses, register through the official website and confirm your admission after completing the enrollment process.

How can I get admission to Parul University?

To get admission to Parul University, go through the eligibility criteria of the desired course. Once the Parul University eligibility criteria are met, visit the official website. Now complete the registration process at the official admission portal. After submitting the Parul Univesity application, wait for a call from the academic counsellor of the university for the next steps.

What is the Parul University B.Tech counselling process?

ACPC conducts the Parul University B.Tech counselling process to provide admission to its various B.Tech programmes. The counselling process is based on the GUJCET scores obtained by the applicants. ACPC also releases the Parul University B.Tech cut off in a round-wise format after the completion of each admission round. After the counselling is done, selected candidates can pay the B.Tech fees which is Rs 90,000 for HS and Rs 1,00,000 for OS candidates and secure their admission.

What documents are required during the Parul University admission process?

Candidates applying for admission at Parul University must have their certificates and mark sheets for Class 10 and Class 12, a character/ school leaving/ transfer document, their entrance test rank/ score card (if applicable), three passport-size photographs of the student, proof of residence, Aadhaar card of the student and their parents, a self-attested anti-ragging affidavit signed by both the student and their parents and an income affidavit.

What is the eligibility criteria for Parul University bachelors in design programme?

Candidates applying for the B.Des programme at parul University may be from any stream and should have completed their Class 12 from a recognised national or international board of education The PID DAT exam is also required for B.Des admissions held by Parul University in-house. Based on a candidate's performance in the exam, the university publishes the Parul University B.Des cut off list and offers admission to successful individuals.

What is the B.Pharma eligibility criteria at Parul University?

Students must pass their Class 12 exams in Science from an accredited board to be eligible for admission to the B.Pharma programme at Parul University. For them to qualify for the admissions procedure, they must additionally sit the JEE Main, GUJCET, or NEET exam. The centralised Parul University B.Pharm counselling process is administered by the ACPC. In addition, Parul University takes into account students' scores on the GUJCET, JEE Main, or NEET while considering direct admission to the B.Pharm programme.

Is there an entrance exam for Parul University admission?

Admission to undergraduate, graduate, and doctorate programmes at Parul University is based on both merit and entrance examination results. Many national admission exams are accepted by Parul University, including the GUJCET, CAT, MAT, CMAT, GATE, JEE MAIN, NEET, NATA, PID DAT, NEET, UGC NET, and CSIR NET. Parul University also organises the Parul Institute of Design Design Aptitude Test or PID DAT exam for admission to its B.Des programme.

How to get admission to Parul University M.Tech programme?

Applicants can apply to Parul University's M.Tech programmes if they hold a bachelor's degree in a relevant field with at least 50% of the required grades from an accredited institution or university. Admission to the M.Tech programme at Parul University is determined by the candidates' GATE scores. Following the conclusion of the Parul University M.Tech counselling process, ACPC announces the cutoff scores. Direct admission is offered by the institution to fill the open seats after the centralised counselling procedure is complete.

What is the Parul University B.Arch eligibility criteria?

According to Parul University's B.Arch eligibility criteria, applicants must have completed Class 12 with PCM and at least 50% of the required marks from an accredited board. For the five-year Parul University B.Arch programme, the candidate’s NATA and JEE Main paper 2 marks are taken into consideration. Based on NATA guidelines, ACPC manages the B.Arch counselling process at Parul University. The Parul University B.Arch cut off is made public to grant admission to qualified applicants.

How are students selected for Parul University MCA admissions?

Parul University MCA admissions are based on the candidate's score in the bachelor’s degree or BCA (50% and above) and their CMAT entrance exam score. During the Parul University MCA counselling process, ACPC offers admission to candidates based on their CMAT scores. The institution uses a direct admission procedure to fill management quota seats as well. The Parul University MCA fees is Rs 1,23,800 per year for a duration of two years.

How can I get admission to Parul University for B.Tech?

Parul University B.Tech admissions are offered based on the candidate’s JEE Main or GUJCET. Students with at least 45% overall in physics, maths, chemistry, computer science, electronics, information technology, biology, informatics, biotechnology, technical vocational subjects, agriculture, engineering graphics, business studies, or entrepreneurship (any of the three) in their Class 12 diploma are eligible to apply. Moreover, candidates must have a qualifying score on the JEE Main or GUJCET entrance exams.

What is the Parul University B.Tech counsellling process?

ACPC conducts the Parul University B.Tech counselling process to provide admission to its various B.Tech programmes. The counselling process is based on the GUJCET scores obtained by the applicants. ACPC also releases the Parul University B.Tech cut off in a round-wise format after the completion of each admission round.

What is the eligibility criteria for Parul University MBA admissions?

Parul Univesity offers a two-year full-time MBA programme in various specialisations. As per the Parul Univesity MBA eligibility criteria, candidates need to have a bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% marks from any recognised institute/ university. The ACPC holds the MBA counselling process every year based on CMAT for Parul University MBA admissions. They also need to have a valid score in CAT, MAT, or CMAT exam. The Parul University MBA fees is Rs 1,66,000 per year for a duration of two years.

What is ACPC?

Admission Committee for Professional Courses (ACPC) is a body under the Government of Gujarat that oversees the admission process for professional courses in the colleges, universities, and institutes in Gujarat. ACPC conducts Parul University counselling process for various courses such as B.Tech, MBA, M.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Arch, MCA, and BHMCT for admission to the surrendered seats. Parul University cut off is released every year to offer admissions to these courses after the completion of the ACPC counselling.

Does Parul University offer direct admission?

Yes, Parul University offers direct admission to its programmes. A number of seats at Parul University are available for direct admission under the management quota. However, candidates need to meet the prescribed Parul University eligibility criteria to be eligible for direct admission. Eligible candidates can apply for direct admission from the official website. Students applying for the B.Tech, MBA, M.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Arch, MCA, and BHMCT programmes may have to undergo ACPC counselling for admission.

How do I apply to Parul University?

Interested students can fill out the Parul University application form online. Candidates need to qualify for the national-level entrance exams such as GUJCET, JEE Main, NEET, CAT, MAT, CMAT, GATE, etc. to be eligible for applying. However, some courses also admit candidates based on their merit in the last qualifying examination. Admissions to B.Tech, MBA, M.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Arch, MCA, and BHMCT programmes are offered based on their entrance exam scores and ACPC counselling.
