About Jaipur National University BPT

Jaipur National University BPT is a 4.5 Years year program. Jaipur National University requires applicants for the Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.P.T.) degree to have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as compulsory subjects with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate. The annual fee for BPT is INR 1,02,100. Jaipur National University BPT Admission 2026

Admission to the BPT at Jaipur National University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Jaipur National University BPT admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Jaipur National University BPT Application Process

Jaipur National University BPT application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BPT course at Jaipur National University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Jaipur National University BPT application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Jaipur National University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Jaipur National University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Jaipur National University BPT application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Jaipur National University BPT application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Jaipur National University BPT Application Form.

Documents Required for Jaipur National University BPT Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
