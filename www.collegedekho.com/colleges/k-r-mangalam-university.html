About KR Mangalam University

KR Mangalam University (KRMU) is a private university established in 2013 under the Haryana Private University Act, located in Gurugram, Haryana. It is UGC-recognized and holds NAAC ‘A’ accreditation, along with approvals from bodies such as BCI,PCI, COA,NCTE, and other professional councils. It has signed a number of MoUs with foreign universities, including the University of Plymouth, Jinggangshan University (JGSU), the University of Houston, USA, the Confucius Institutes, and theDubna State University, Moscow, among others.

The university offers a diverse range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes across Engineering & Technology, Management & Commerce, Law, Journalism & Mass Communication, Design, Architecture, Education, Humanities, Medical Sciences, Computer Applications, Liberals Arts and Basic Applied Sciences. With a strong focus on hands-on training, experiential learning, live projects, internships, and research, students gain real-world exposure through state-of-the-art labs, studios, moot courts, media labs, and innovation centers from the very start of their journey.

KRMU follows an industry-aligned curriculum supported by corporate partnerships. The university conducts guest lectures, expert sessions, workshops, certification programmes, and placement-focused training to support student skill development.

KRMU has an established research ecosystem with 4,000+ research publications and 100+ teaching and research laboratories. The university is involved in patent filings, consultancy projects, and funded research activities.

Admissions are based on national entrance exams such as JEE,CLAT,NID,NATA CAT, MAT, CMAT, and CUET-UG/PG, depending on the programme. The university also conducts its own entrance examination, KREE.

 The KR Mangalam University placements assistant have a rate of 100% with the highest packages of Rs 56.6 LPA.

KR Mangalam University Admission Process 2026

  • KR Mangalam University admission will be done on the basis of various entrance exams like JEE Main, CLAT, NATA, UPSEE, UGAT, CAT, MAT, etc.
  •  Apart from this, the university also conducts its own admission test for which students need to fill out the KR Mangalam University application form through the site admissions.krmangalam.edu.in. 
  • From the 2026 session, KR Mangalam is also accepting the Central Universities Entrance Test (CUET) for admission to its UG and PG courses. 
  • CUET is a common entrance examination that enables admission to many central, private, and deemed universities. 
  • Students can check the results on cuet.samarth.ac.in.
  • Now that the CUET result has been announced, KR Mangalam University will release its own merit list and counselling schedule based on the CUET Samarth scores of students. 
  • The admission portal for KR Mangalam University will also be activated soon for the CUET-based courses. 
  • Students must remain in constant touch with the university for the same. 
  • There will not be any centralised counselling conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) as it happens for other exams like the JEE and NEET.
  • The university will also accept the CUET PG result 2026 for its postgraduate admissions. 
  • Students can check their CUET PG result on cuet.nta.nic.in. They can also check the results on the website ntaresults.nic.in. 
  • For PG admissions, KR Mangalam University will notify students about the further admission process on its official website krmangalam.edu.in. 
  • For the postgraduate admissions as well, there will not be any centralised counselling by the NTA. 

KR Mangalam University Eligibility Criteria for 2026

  • For B.Tech courses, the candidate should pass the 10+2 examination from CBSE or any other recognised board in the Science stream with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with at least 50% aggregate. 
  • Candidates with a Diploma in Engineering may be considered for lateral entry into the second-year
  • For B.Sc (H), the candidate should pass the 10+2 examination from CBSE or any other recognized board with Mathematics as one of the subjects and at least 50% aggregate. 
  • For BCA, the candidate should pass the 10+2 examination from CBSE or any other recognized board with Mathematics/Computer Science/Information Practice as one of the subjects with at least 50% aggregate.
  •  For M.Tech, the candidate should hold a bachelor’s degree from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate
  • For MCA, the candidate should pass BCA/B.SC with Mathematics as a major subject or B.E./B.Tech in CS/IT from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate.
  • Postgraduate degree in the equivalent subject from a recognized Indian University approved by UGC/AICTE and aggregate not less than 50%.

KR Mangalam University Placements 2025

KR Mangalam University, with its mission of making the students industry ready, tends to provide a futuristic curriculum and cutting-edge technology. The university’s “campus to corporate” initiative helps build a mutually beneficial relationship for esteemed corporates and institutions. Through this connection, the students can grab wonderful and dream job opportunities at leading companies across various industries. 

KRMU has a “CORPORATE RESOURCE CENTRE” which generates ample placement opportunities for the students. The centre has various counselling and training programs to help the students. These programs equip them with the necessary skills to attract lucrative offers from prominent companies. 

The KR Mangalam University Placement Policy highlights are mentioned below:

  • Corporate Resource Centre will be providing placement opportunities only for KRMU Students
  • Interested students should register with the Centre as soon as they reach the final year.
  • The KR Mangalam University placements start in September each year.
  • Orientation and Policy talk will be organized for students at the beginning of their academic session to provide insight into the policies of CRC.

KR Mangalam University Placement Cell

Every year many recruiters from various sectors visit the KRMU campus. Along with placements, they organize various events like The Corporate Talk Series, Guest Lectures, Internships, Live Projects, and Case-Study Competitions to enable the students to engage with the corporate world.

They arrange HR conclaves and symposiums for the students to interact with industry experts. This helps them gain insight and make important decisions about their professional carrier.

KR Mangalam University placements and training team guide the students to feel more confident during the interview process and help them in training, skill development, personality development and communication skills. It also helps in resume writing, workplace etiquette, and stress management skills.

KR Mangalam University alumni state that the training and placement cell helped them in getting good job opportunities in several reputed companies and organizations. The dedication of the faculty members is also commendable.

KR Mangalam University Placement Process

One of the main motives behind starting the placement and training cell at KR Mangalam University Gurugram is to make the students ready for the industry and professional world. They conduct mock interviews to train students to help them understand what to expect during the recruitment drive and identify their improvement areas.

The KRMU placement process includes:

  • The placement team holds personal interactions with the candidates
  • Sharing standard resume formats with the students.
  • Training them to face the recruitment process 
  • Organizing group discussions and mock interview sessions.
  • Enabling the MNCs and HR agencies to visit the campus to provide placement opportunities.
