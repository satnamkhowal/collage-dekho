Introduction to BM Group of Institutions

BM Group of Institutions, also known as BM is a Private, established in 2007. Located in Gurgaon, BM Group of Institutions campus spans over 13 acres. The college is affiliated to Maharishi Dayanand University, Rohtak and recognized by the AICTE, PCI, NCTE. BM Group of Institutions is ranked Not specified by NIRF under Engineering, Management, Applied Sciences in Not specified. NIRF ranking: Not specified

BM Group of Institutions offers a wide range of various programs in the streams of Engineering, Management, Applied Sciences. B.Tech, MBA, BBA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at BM Group of Institutions are based on Both merit and entrance exams. The college accepts JEE Main for B.Tech, CAT/MAT/XAT for MBA, GATE for M.Tech. For placements, BM Group of Institutions placement rate is 85%%. During the placement drive of 2024, the highest salary package offered was INR 30 LPA LPA and the average package stood at INR 6.5 LPA LPA.

BM Group of Institutions FAQs

What is BM Group of Institutions annual fees for BPharm course?

The annual fees for Bachelor of Pharmacy course at BM Group of Institutions is Rs 80,000. BPharm is a four year undergraduate programme offered by the institute in full-time mode. The course is divided into 8 semesters and the duration of each semester is six months. The eligibility criteria for BPharm is candidates must have passed Class 12 with PCM/PCB from a recognised board.

Which entrance exams scores are accepted for BM Group of Institutions course admissions?

For admission to UG and PG courses, BM Group of Institutions accepts numerous national level entrance examination scores. JEE Main scores are taken into consideration by the institute for BTech admission. At the postgraduate level, BM Group of Institutions Gurgaon accepts GATE and CAT scores for admission in MTech and MBA courses, respectively. Admission in the majority of the BM Group of Institutions courses is done on the basis of marks obtained by candidates in the last qualifying examination.

What kind of scholarships are available at BM Group of Institutions?

The institute has both merit based and need based scholarships for students. BM Group of Institutions merit based scholarships are offered to students based on academic performance. On the other hand, need based scholarships are provided to students who belong to economically weaker sections of the society and are in need of financial assistance. The institute also has special scholarships for girls students.

Is BM Group of Institutions a private college?

Yes, BM Group of Institutions is a private college. The institute was established in 2007 and it is affiliated to Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU), Rohtak. Further, it is approved by All India Council for Technical Education and Haryana State Board of Technical Education. The institute offers 17 courses such as Bachelor of Technology, Bachelor of Business Administration, Master of Business Administration, Master of Technology etc.

What are the facilities available at BM Group of Institutions?

The campus of BM Group of Institutions Gurgaon has adequate facilities for academics and extracurricular activities. The institute has separate boys and girls hostels, a well stocked library, department based laboratories, spacious classrooms, modern auditorium, medical centre etc. BM Group of Institutions facilities allows students to get theoretical and practical knowledge. The college also has sports facilities for students.

What is the BM Group of Institutions hostel fees structure?

The university has individual hostels for boys and girls. BM Group of Institutions hostel fees vary as per the accommodation and room type. The annual fees for a 3 seater non-AC room (boys hostels) is Rs 60,000. On the other hand, the annual fees for a 2 seater non-AC room (girls hostel) is Rs 70,000. The fees for 3 seater non-AC and 2 seater non-AC rooms is Rs 80,000 and Rs 90,000 respectively.

Can I get direct admission in BM Group of Institutions?

The college provides merit based admission in various courses such as DEd, DPharm, BCA, BBA etc. For admission in these courses, students are not required to appear for any entrance examination. They are offered admission in BM Group of Institutions on the basis of marks obtained by them in the last qualifying examination i.e. Class 12 scores for UG courses and graduation score for PG courses.

Does the BM Group of Institutions provide entrance based admission?

Yes, the college provides entrance based admission in Bachelor of Technology (BTech) and Master of Technology (MTech) courses. For BM Group of Institutions admission in BTech courses, the college accepts JEE Main scores. On the other hand, GATE scores are taken into consideration for MTech admission. The college considers MAT scores for admission in the Master of Business Administration (MBA) course.

BM Group of Institutions affiliated to which university?

BM Group of Institutions is affiliated to Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU), Rohtak. The college is a private institute established in the year 2007. BM Group of Institutions Gurgaon offers diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate courses in full-time mode. The institute is also approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).
