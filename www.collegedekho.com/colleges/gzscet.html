About Giani Zail Singh Campus College Of Engineering & Technology

Giani Zail Singh Campus College Of Engineering & Technology is situated in Bhatinda in Punjab state of India. Established in 1989, GZSCET is a Public college. The college is accredited by AICTE. Giani Zail Singh Campus College Of Engineering & Technology offers 26 courses across 4 streams namely Engineering, IT, Management, Science..Popular degrees offered at Giani Zail Singh Campus College Of Engineering & Technology include B.Tech, B.Arch, BBA+MBA, M.Tech, MSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Giani Zail Singh Campus College Of Engineering & Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Giani Zail Singh Campus College Of Engineering & Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Giani Zail Singh Campus College Of Engineering & Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
