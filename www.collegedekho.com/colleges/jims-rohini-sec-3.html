About JCC

Jagannath Community College, Sector-3, Rohini, also known as JCC, is a private college established in 1993. Located in New Delhi, the JCC campus is centrally situated in Rohini. The college is affiliated with Jagannath University, NCR Bahadurgarh, and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). JCC is an approved Skill Knowledge Provider (SKP) under NSQF by AICTE. JCC is ranked A++ on an all-India basis by Business & Management Chronicle and 6th for placements. JCC offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and certificate programs in streams such as Management, Information Technology, Design, Commerce, Arts, and Hospitality. BBA, BCA, BDes, and MBA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at JCC are based on both merit in the qualifying examination and entrance examinations. The college accepts CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, and IPU CET for various courses.

For placements, JCC placement rate is approximately 80%. During the placement drive of 2025, the highest salary package offered was INR 12 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 4.5 LPA.

Jagannath Community College, Sector-3, Rohini Admission 2026

JCC admissions are based on merit in the qualifying examination and entrance examinations. Students seeking admission at JCC must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the college and appear for the relevant entrance examination or written test conducted by JCC. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for JCC admissions:

  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for CAT, MAT, XAT, or CMAT and participate in JCC’s group discussion and personal interview for seat allotment.
  • BBA, BCA, BDes, BCom, BA, B.Voc, MCA, Diploma, Certificate: Admissions to these programs are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination and a written test conducted by JCC, followed by group discussion and personal interview.
  • BA (Economics): Candidates seeking admission to BA (Economics) must appear for IPU CET and participate in the counseling process for seat allotment.

How are the hostels of JIMS Rohini sector 3?

Hostels rooms at JIMS Rohini Sector 3 are spacious with windows opening into balcony, common room and dining room facility, Wi-Fi connection, RO water dispensers, and hot & cold water supply round the year are some of the offerings in the hostel.

Does JCC offer hostel accommodation?

Yes, JCC does offers hostel accommodations to both male and female students.

What kind of student clubs are there at JCC?

Student clubs at JCC provide opportunities for activities like Extempore, Debate, Quiz, Role Play, Spell-Bee, Poster Making, Article Writing, Poetry, Elocution, Team Building Games, and New Product Development.

How is the life of an student at JCC?

The life of a typical student at JCC is not only constricted to the classroom walls. It extends to different stages and platforms to showcase or explore new talents. It involved getting in contact with leading industry experts to prepare them to excel in future.

Is JIMS Rohini Sector 3 a good college for Management courses?

JIMS Rohini sector 3 is an approved Skill Knowledge Provider (SKP) by AICTE that facilitates employment and entrepreneurship programmes. It is also a Study Centre of IGNOU to run MBA. So, yes, JIMS Rohini Sector 3 or JCC is one of the ideal colleges for a student aspiring to pursue Management programmes.
