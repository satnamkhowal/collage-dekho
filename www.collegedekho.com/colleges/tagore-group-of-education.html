About Tagore PG Girls College

Tagore PG Girl College or TPGGC, is a private institution situated in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Established in the year 2004, Tagore PG Girl College is one of the colleges in Rajasthan that provides UG and PG courses in Arts, Science and Commerce streams. Tagore PG Girls College is affiliated with the University of Rajasthan, Jaipur, and is approved by the Directorate of College Education, Govt. of Rajasthan, Jaipur. Tagore PG Girls College campus size is 11333 Sq ft, and it contains all the required buildings like administrative blocks, classrooms, labs, a library, and more. 

Tagore PG Girls College courses are B.Sc, B.Com, BA, MA, and M.Sc. The TPGGC Jaipur won the Asia Education Summit And Award in 2017.

Tagore PG Girl College facilities are Auditorium, Library, Laboratories, Canteen, Anti-Ragging Cell, Games & Sports, Career Guidance & Counselling Cell, Scholarship (Government) facilities, Transportation, Hostel, Swimming pool, Competitive Classes, Special Fee Concession to Tagorians & Meritorious, Red Ribbon Club, National Service Scheme, and Garima Prakoshth.
