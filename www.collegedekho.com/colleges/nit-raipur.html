About NIT Raipur

National Institute of Technology Raipur, is a centrally funded technical institute located in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. NIT Raipur was established in 1956 and is recognised as an Institute of National Importance under the National Institutes of Technology Act. The institute is approved by AICTE and functions under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. As per NIT Raipur NIRF ranking 2025, it has been ranked 86th in Engineering, 35th in Architecture, and within the 151-200 band for the Overall category.

NIT Raipur courses are offered across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels in engineering, architecture, sciences, and management. The institute offers more than 40 academic programmes, including B.Tech, B.Arch, M.Tech, M.Sc, MBA, MCA, and PhD courses. Some of the most popular NIT Raipur courses include B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Information Technology. NIT Raipur admission is entirely entrance-based and is conducted through national-level examinations such as JEE Main, GATE, JAM, CAT, and NIMCET, followed by centralised counselling.

NIT Raipur placements are managed by a dedicated Training and Placement Cell that facilitates internships, pre-placement offers, and full-time recruitment. The institute provides strong academic infrastructure, modern laboratories, central library facilities, on-campus hostels, sports complexes, and research centres. Its location in a developing industrial region also supports industry interaction and practical exposure for students.

NIT Raipur Courses 2026

NIT Raipur courses are offered at undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels across multiple streams, including Engineering, Architecture, Sciences, and Management. At the undergraduate level, NIT Raipur offers B.Tech and B.Arch programmes across core and emerging engineering disciplines. Postgraduate offerings include M.Tech specialisations, M.Sc programmes in sciences courses. NIT Raipur also offers PhD programmes in engineering, science, and humanities departments.

The NIT Raipur fees vary depending on the course level and category of the student. For 2026, the overall NIT Raipur fee range is approximately Rs 15,000 per year for PhD programmes to around Rs 70,000 per year for professional postgraduate courses. Hostel and mess charges are separate from the academic fees.

NIT Raipur Admissions 2026

NIT Raipur admission for 2026 is conducted strictly through entrance-based selection for all major programmes. Undergraduate admissions are based on JEE Main ranks followed by JoSAA and CSAB counselling. Postgraduate admissions are carried out through CCMT for M.Tech, CCMN for M.Sc. PhD admissions at NIT Raipur involve an institute-level written test and personal interview.

The NIT Raipur admission process includes entrance exam qualification, centralised counselling, seat allotment, document verification, and physical reporting at the institute. Some postgraduate and doctoral programmes may include additional evaluation stages such as written tests or interviews.

NIT Raipur Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

Library: The NIT Raipur library has over 1,00,000 print volumes, over 1,07,000 ebooks (text and reference books), PhD theses, online journal archive collections, and online protocols. It also includes subscriptions to Indian print magazines and internet databases.

Guest house: Guests, staff members, parents, and visitors are accommodated in the NIT Raipur. The guest house has three living rooms, one dining hall, and one hall. All rooms have modern conveniences such as air conditioning, television, and refrigerators.

Hostel: The NIT Raipur hostel offers separate sleeping quarters for females and males. There are five male hostels and three female hostels on campus. Each hostel is self-contained, with features including a recreation room, a badminton court, and a garden. Every room at the hostel is well-equipped. Each NIT Raipur hostel has a caretaker in charge of the day-to-day operations.

Medical Services: The NIT Raipur features a dispensary located next to the NCC office and has three rooms, two for doctors and one for the dispensary personnel. The dispensary provides free OPD consultations, medication delivery, and hospitalisation ambulance service.
