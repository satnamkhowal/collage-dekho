About ISB Hyderabad PGD

ISB Hyderabad PGD is a 15 Months - 1 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the Post Graduate Diploma program at the Indian School of Business (ISB) Hyderabad, candidates must possess a bachelor's degree or an equivalent qualification in any discipline. Additionally, applicants should have a minimum of two years of full-time work experience after completing their undergraduate degree. The selection process for the program is highly competitive and takes into consideration various factors such as academic performance, leadership potential, and personal attributes. Candidates are required to submit their GMAT/GRE scores, academic transcripts, essays, and letters of recommendation. Shortlisted candidates are then invited for an interview as part of the final selection process. The annual fee for PGD is INR 29,88,000 - 42,13,000. ISB Hyderabad PGD Admission 2026

Admission to the PGD at ISB Hyderabad is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the ISB Hyderabad PGD admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

ISB Hyderabad PGD Application Process

ISB Hyderabad PGD application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the PGD course at ISB Hyderabad have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill ISB Hyderabad PGD application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of ISB Hyderabad and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of ISB Hyderabad. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the ISB Hyderabad PGD application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the ISB Hyderabad PGD application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of ISB Hyderabad PGD Application Form.

Documents Required for ISB Hyderabad PGD Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
