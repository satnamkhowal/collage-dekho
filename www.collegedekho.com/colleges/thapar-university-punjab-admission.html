Thapar University Application Process 2026

The online application for TIET Patiala admission 2026 is managed through the official portal, admission.thapar.edu, where candidates submit details, upload documents, and pay ₹1,500 via net banking, debit, or credit card. The process supports BTech (JEE Main or Class 12 merit), MBA (CAT/XAT), and MTech (GATE) programmes.

  • Step 1: Register
    Visit admission.thapar.edu, click “Apply Now”, and create an account with your email and mobile number. Verify via OTP.
  • Step 2: Fill in Application Form
    Enter personal details, academic records (Class 10/12 marks), and the JEE Main 2026 roll number or CAT/GATE scores. Select up to two BTech branch groups (e.g., CSE + ECE).
  • Step 3: Upload Documents
    Upload scanned copies of Class 10/12 marksheets, JEE Main/CAT/GATE scorecard, passport-size photo, signature, and category certificate (SC/ST/OBC, if applicable). Make sure that the files are under 2MB, in PDF/JPEG format.
  • Step 4: Pay Application Fee
    Pay ₹1,500 (non-refundable) online. Additional ₹1,500 applies for multiple branch groups in BTech lateral entry.
  • Step 5: Submit and Track
    Submit the form and track the status via webkiosk.thapar.edu using your application ID.

Documents Required for TIET Patiala Admission 2026 Verification

During the TIET Patiala admission 2026 counselling sessions, scheduled from June, 2026, at the Patiala campus, candidates must present both original documents and self-attested photocopies for verification. This step confirms eligibility for programmes like BTech (requiring 60% in Class 12 PCM and JEE Main scores), MBA (CAT 85+ percentile), and MTech (GATE 500+ score). Failure to provide complete documents can lead to seat cancellation, so prepare scans for the online application (formats: PDF or JPEG, max 2MB per file) and physical copies for in-person checks. Preferred photo size is 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm (passport-style, white background, <50KB for uploads), with signatures in black ink on white paper (max 100KB JPEG). All academic documents must be from recognised boards (CBSE/ICSE/State) and attested by gazetted officers if needed for categories like SC/ST.

Core Academic Documents

These establish your educational background and meet the 60% Class 12 threshold for BTech or equivalent for PG programmes.

  • Class X Marksheet and Certificate: Original and one self-attested copy; scan as PDF (<2MB) showing date of birth and subjects. Preferred resolution: 300 DPI for clarity.
  • Class XII Marksheet and Certificate: Original and self-attested copy; must reflect 60% aggregate in PCM (55% for SC/ST) from 2023-2026 exams. Upload a detailed PDF with subjects and percentages; no provisional certificates are accepted post-results.
  • Diploma Certificate (for Lateral Entry BTech): Original for three-year diploma holders with 60% marks; include semester-wise marksheets in PDF format (<2MB total).

Entrance Exam Documents

Valid scores are mandatory for merit calculation—JEE Main for 50% BTech seats, CAT for MBA, and GATE for MTech.

  • JEE Main 2026 Admit Card and Rank Card: Original admit card and downloaded rank card; scan both as separate PDFs (<1MB each). Include percentile details for branches like CSE (96.21 cutoff reference).
  • CAT 2026 Scorecard (for MBA): Original; upload PDF with sectional scores; valid for applications till December 5, 2026.
  • GATE 2026 Scorecard (for MTech): Original; PDF scan (<1MB) showing 500-700 score range for specialisations like VLSI.

Category and Reservation Documents

Applicable for SC/ST/OBC/EWS candidates claiming relaxation (5% in eligibility) or reserved seats (15% SC, 7.5% ST).

Identity and Migration Documents

These verify personal details and enable seamless transfer from previous institutions.

  • Aadhaar Card or PAN Card: Original for Indian nationals; photocopy attested. For NRI/IEP, use passport (valid till 2027+) and visa copies; scan the passport photo page as JPEG (<100KB).
  • Migration/Transfer Certificate: Original from the last attended school/college; issued within 6 months of application. PDF upload (<2MB); not required for CBSE students in the first year.
  • Character Certificate: From principal/head of institution; original, dated post-March 2026.

Additional Documents for Scholarships and Special Cases

Submit during counselling for merit-based aid (50% tuition waiver for JEE AIR <500) or need-based support (₹1 Lakh/year for income <₹8 Lakhs).

  • Income Certificate: Original from Tehsildar; for family income proof under ₹8 Lakhs. PDF (<500KB); valid for FY 2024-25.
  • Gap Year Affidavit (if applicable): Notarised affidavit explaining gap post-Class 12; for candidates from 2023/2024 exams. Typed on stamp paper, scanned as PDF (<1MB).
  • Two Passport-Size Photographs: Recent colour photos (3.5 x 4.5 cm, white background); one affixed on the application form, one spare for records. Digital version: <50KB JPEG, 150x150 pixels minimum.

Submission Guidelines for Scans and Physical Copies

  • Online Uploads (via admission.thapar.edu): All files in PDF (preferred for multi-page docs) or JPEG; total size <5MB per application. Use a scanner at 200-300 DPI; avoid mobile photos for marksheets to prevent rejection.
  • Counselling Verification: Carry originals in a folder; photocopies self-attested on each page. Digital copies are emailed to admissions@thapar.edu if requested for spot rounds.
  • NRI/IEP Specifics: Passport-sized photo (4x6 cm for visa pages); IELTS/TOEFL scores as PDF (<1MB) for English proficiency.

Thapar University FAQs

What are the exact upload specifications for documents during the online application at admission.thapar.edu for Thapar University 2026?
All documents must be uploaded in PDF (preferred for multi-page files) or JPEG format, with each file under 2MB (total application size <5MB). Recommended resolution is 200–300 DPI for clarity. Passport-size photo should be <50KB (3.5 × 4.5 cm, white background), signature <100KB (black ink on white paper), and other documents like marksheets and scorecards should be clear scans (avoid mobile photos).
What additional documents are needed for scholarships or gap-year cases during Thapar University 2026 admission?
For merit-based scholarships (e.g., 50% tuition waiver for JEE AIR <500) or need-based aid (₹1 lakh/year for income <₹8 lakhs), submit an original income certificate from Tehsildar (FY 2024-25). For gap years, provide a notarised affidavit explaining the gap after Class 12 (scanned as PDF <1MB). Two recent passport-size photographs (3.5 × 4.5 cm, white background) are also required—one affixed and one spare.
Which identity and migration documents are required for Thapar University admission 2026 counselling?
All candidates need an original Aadhaar or PAN card (for Indian nationals) or passport (for NRI/IEP candidates). A migration/transfer certificate from the last attended institution (issued within 6 months) is required, except for first-year CBSE students. A character certificate from the principal/head of the last institution (dated after March 2026) is also mandatory.
What category certificates are required for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD candidates applying to Thapar University 2026?
SC/ST candidates need an original caste certificate issued by Tehsildar/SDM (post-2020) along with a self-attested copy. OBC-NCL applicants require a non-creamy layer certificate (family income < ₹8 lakhs) as per GoI format. EWS candidates need a 2026-27 EWS certificate with income proof. PwD candidates must submit a disability certificate from the Chief Medical Officer for 40%+ benchmark disability. All must be uploaded in PDF/JPEG format (<500KB–1MB).
What entrance exam documents are mandatory for different PG programmes at Thapar University Patiala?
For MBA, submit the original CAT 2026 scorecard (valid till December 5, 2026) with sectional scores. For MTech, provide the original GATE 2026 scorecard showing a score in the 500–700 range (depending on specialisation like VLSI). For BTech, include the JEE Main 2026 admit card and rank card (percentile details are crucial for high-demand branches like CSE).
