About IEM Kolkata MBA

IEM Kolkata MBA is a 2 Years program offered in multiple specializations. The eligibility criteria for M.B.A. at the Institute of Engineering and Management is a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks. The annual fee for MBA is INR 3,00,000 - 3,50,000. IEM Kolkata MBA Admission 2026

Admission to the MBA at IEM Kolkata is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the IEM Kolkata MBA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

IEM Kolkata MBA Application Process

IEM Kolkata MBA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MBA course at IEM Kolkata have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill IEM Kolkata MBA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of IEM Kolkata and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of IEM Kolkata. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the IEM Kolkata MBA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the IEM Kolkata MBA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of IEM Kolkata MBA Application Form.

Documents Required for IEM Kolkata MBA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
