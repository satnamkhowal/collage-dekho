About Federal Institute of Science and Technology

Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT)is a private self-financing engineering college located in Mookkannoor, near Angamaly in Ernakulam District, Kerala. The institute was established in 2002. Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT)has been recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) and the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an 'A+' grade. Hostels, a library, research centers, and sports amenities are some of the Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT)facilities. Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT)courses are offered at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering, and MBA. Admission is based on entrance exams. The college accepts KEAM for B.Tech admissions CAT/CMAT/KMAT for MBA, and GATE for M.Tech for admission. Regarding Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT)placements, the highest package offered in 2023 was RS 17.04 LPA and the average package offered was RS 5.18 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include TCS, Infosys, and Wipro.
