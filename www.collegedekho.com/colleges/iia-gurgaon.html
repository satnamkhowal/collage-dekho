About Indraprasth Institute of Aeronautics

Indraprasth Institute of Aeronautics is a venture of Indraprasth Group of Institutions. IIA Gurgaon is a private institution established in the year 2001. Ever since the institute was established it has been focused on transforming the Indian aviation industry through its well-structured courses which impart education through theory, simulation, and practical methods.

Indraprasth Institute of Aeronautics is approved by DGCA and Jet Serve Aviation Private Limited. This eminent institute of aeronautics has become one of the top and most well-desired colleges in India in a short span of time. IIA Gurgaon has established partnerships and MOUs with Air India, Jet Serve Aviation Pvt. Ltd., Indamer Aviation Pvt. Ltd., Shaurya Aeronautics Pvt. Ltd., and Dhillon Aviation. With well-coordinated and industry-leading partners Indraprasth Institute of Aeronautics is offering world-class training and education to its students.
