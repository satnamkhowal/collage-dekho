SOA Bhubaneswar Admission 2026: Courses, Eligibility, Application Form, Last Date

Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) Deemed to be University, established in 2007 in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, is a private deemed university accredited with an A++ grade by NAAC and approved by UGC, AICTE, and other regulatory bodies like ICAR, MCI, PCI, and BCI. Ranked 24th in the University category and 26th in Engineering by NIRF 2024, and 451–500 in QS Asia 2023, SOA operates through 12 faculties, including the Institute of Technical Education and Research (ITER) for Engineering. It offers over 170 Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Doctoral programs in Engineering, Medicine, Management, Pharmacy, Law, Nursing, Agriculture, and more. Admissions are primarily entrance-based, through the Siksha 'O' Anusandhan Admission Test (SAAT) or national-level exams like JEE Main, NEET, CAT, MAT, CLAT, and GATE. Applications are managed online via admission.soa.ac.in. Below are the details for SOA Bhubaneswar Admission 2025, including eligibility, selection criteria, application process, and key dates.
