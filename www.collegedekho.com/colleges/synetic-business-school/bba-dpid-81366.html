About SBS Ludhiana BBA

SBS Ludhiana BBA is a 3 Years year program. To be eligible for the BBA degree program at Synetic Business School in Ludhiana, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Additionally, applicants must have a strong command of the English language and possess good communication skills. It is also preferred that candidates have a keen interest in business and management studies. Admission to the program may also be subject to performance in entrance exams or personal interviews conducted by the college. Overall, students who are motivated, dedicated, and eager to learn about the world of business are encouraged to apply for the BBA degree at Synetic Business School. The annual fee for BBA is INR 50,000. SBS Ludhiana BBA Admission 2026

Admission to the BBA at SBS Ludhiana is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the SBS Ludhiana BBA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

SBS Ludhiana BBA Application Process

SBS Ludhiana BBA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BBA course at SBS Ludhiana have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill SBS Ludhiana BBA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of SBS Ludhiana and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of SBS Ludhiana. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the SBS Ludhiana BBA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the SBS Ludhiana BBA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of SBS Ludhiana BBA Application Form.

Documents Required for SBS Ludhiana BBA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
