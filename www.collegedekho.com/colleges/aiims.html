All India Institute of Medical Sciences Eligibility Criteria for 2025

For being eligible, AIIMS students must fulfill the following requirements.

  • Candidate must be a citizen of India.
  • He/she should be 17 years of age or should attain it as of 31st December following the year of admission. 
  • The candidate applying for AIIMS Delhi admission must be born on or after 2nd January 2007 are not eligible to apply.
  • He/she Should pass 12th class under the 10+2 scheme, Senior School Certificate Examination or Intermediate Science or any equivalent examination from a recognized board or university win Indian State with English, Chemistry, Physics, and Biology as subjects.

For Bachelor of Optometry, B.Sc. (Hons.) Medical Technology in Radiography Courses, B. Sc. In Dental Operating Room Assistant, B.Sc. in dental Hygiene, B.Sc. in Operation Theatre Technology:

  • A student should get a 50% minimum aggregate for Gen /OBC (NCL) (45% in case of SCs / STs Categories) in English, Physics, Chemistry, and either Biology or Mathematics.

For B. Sc Nursing (Post Basic)

  • Should pass 12th class under the 10+2 system or equivalent examination under any recognized board or university.
  • Candidates who have passed 10+1 on or before 1986 are also eligible.
  • Should have a Diploma in General Nursing and Midwifery from any institution that the Indian Nursing Council recognizes.
  • Should have registration as RM, RN (Registered nurse, Registered Midwife) with any of the State Nursing Council.

For MBBS –

  • The minimum aggregate marks obtained in English, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology should be as follows –
  • For General and OBC candidates: 60%, For SC/ST candidates: 50% & For PWBD candidates: 45%
  • Students with CGPA grades only will have to apply for the conversion factor.

For P. HD courses –

The candidate should have one of the following qualifications mentioned below.

Medical qualifications:

  • Should qualify MBBS/BDS with a minimum aggregate of 55% marks or MD/MS/MDS/DM/M. Ch in the respective subject, or should have a National Board of Examination diploma.
  • Candidates who have qualified any of the above from a college that the Medical Council of India does not recognize are not eligible.

Nursing Qualifications:

M.sc degree with a minimum aggregate of 55% in a nursing speciality

Non-medical:

  • A Master's degree (2 Years Course) should be from an Indian University or equivalent.
  • Candidates holding M. Sac (other than Nursing), M. Tech, and BAMS degrees will be eligible for the Non–Medical category; they should have at least 60% marks in the last eligibility examination that they have qualified.

For DM courses 

  • The minimum requirement is that a candidate should have MD. Other eligibility requirements differ with the specializations.

For M. Ch courses 

  • The minimum requirement is that a candidate should have an MS degree. Other conditions depend on specialization.

AIIMS Delhi (AIIMS), Delhi Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

As AIIMS Delhi is one of the top colleges, the campus and infrastructure facilities are commendable.

AIIMS Delhi campus is on several acres of land and consists of a medical hospital and research centre. It provides world-class facilities to its students and also concentrates on extra-curricular activities. 

They provide the students with labs, sports facilities, hostels, etc., to make them an all-rounder. 

All India Institute of Medical Sciences Facilities 

Hostels: AIIMS hostel facilities include clean and spacious rooms for boys and girls. Most of the hostels have gyms and sports areas for basketball, tennis, and volleyball. It also has a separate room for watching television. 

Library: The institution has a library named BB Dikshit library which is open for 24 hours and contains a lot of books. 

Computer facility: Twenty-four hours of free internet access is provided in the computer facility area to provide maximum student convenience. 

Swimming pool: An eight-line swimming pool is available for the students.

Gymnasium: The gym is located next to the Men’s hostel and helps the students stay fit. 

Café/ Canteen: The café or canteen facilities provide healthy and tasty food to the students. There is also a faculty mess where all the faculty members can sit and eat together. 

Some of the extra facilities provided to the hostel students include computer typing shops, book shops, courier services, juice shops, general stores, etc.

AIIMS Delhi (AIIMS), Delhi Scholarships

  • According to various sources, AIIMS provides scholarships to students in the form of merit depending on their financial needs. 
  • UG, MBBS, and nursing students get a scholarship. 

AIIMS Raipur scholarship facilities:

  • For ST students, the institution provides two schemes under the central sector to attain higher education Rajiv Gandhi National Fellowship and Top-Class Education. 
  • The Rajiv Gandhi Fellowships are provided to students to pursue higher education qualifications such as M. Phil, P. HD courses, and UGC fellowships. 
  • The Top-Class Education for ST students provides scholarships at the graduate and postgraduate levels in various professional fields such as Engineering, Information Technology, Law, etc. 
  • These two schemes are called the “National Fellowship and Scholarship for Higher Education of ST Students” to deliver better student services. 

  Under this scholarship, ST students will get the following benefits – 

  • Total tuition fees and other non-refundable government dues ( 2 lakhs per annum will be the maximum limit)
  • Living expenses to the extent of INR  2200 per month for each student. 
  • Books and stationery of INR  3000 per annum for each student. 
  • Latest computers and their accessories at the maximum cost of INR  45000 as one-time assistance for students.
