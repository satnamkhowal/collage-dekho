About Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech

Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Quest Group of Institutions Mohali, aspiring candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria. Firstly, candidates must have completed their 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they should have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 45% (40% for reserved categories) in the qualifying examination. Furthermore, candidates must have a valid score in any national/state-level engineering entrance examination, such as JEE Main/Punjab CET. Admissions are based on merit, and shortlisted candidates are invited for counseling and seat allocation based on their rank and availability of seats. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 87,000. Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Quest Group of Institutions is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech Application Process

Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Quest Group of Institutions have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Quest Group of Institutions and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Quest Group of Institutions. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Quest Group of Institutions B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
