Kalinga University Raipur Job Ready Degree

If you are concerned about employment after completing your education, then the Job Ready “Assured” course by Kalinga University is the right choice for you. The course will allow you to get hands-on experience and enable you to cope with a fast-changing environment. The “Assured” course offered by Kalinga University is BDes in UX (IXP). Its annual fee is INR 123,000, and the duration is 4 years. Candidates who have passed Class 12 in any stream with 50% marks are eligible to apply for admission.

Kalinga University Raipur Job Ready Courses 2026 - Admissions

There is no entrance exam for admission to the Job Ready course at Kalinga University. Candidates are offered admission directly on a merit basis. They have to meet the eligibility criteria and deposit the course fee to confirm admission.
