Manipal University Job Ready Degree

Beyond regular programmes, Manipal University also offers job ready “assured” courses that are designed to get students job-ready. These programmes are created with industry requirements in mind, so that candidates graduate with the skills employers are looking for.  The university offers 1 job ready programme in B.Des in UX (IXP) which is of 3 years duration. The annual fee for this programme is Rs 1,74,000. Candidates who have passed 12th in any stream with a minimum aggregate of 50% are eligible to apply for the course. Manipal University selects candidates for admission to these job ready degree on the basis of home based test followed by a personal interview. Manipal University also offers Dr. TMA Pai Merit Scholarship for this programme through which candidates with more than 80% aggregate marks in 12th can get up to 100% tuition fee waiver. Hence, if you are looking for a degree in BDes which gives you career assurance, B.Des in UX (IXP) by Manipal University is for you!

Manipal University Job Ready Degree 2024 - Admissions

Admission to job ready degree by Manipal University is based on home based test followed by a personal interview. Candidates who are interested in admission will have to apply for admission through online or offline application mode. It is also important to check the eligibility criteria before applying for admission. Shortlisted candidates will have to sit for the home based test and obtain the minimum passing marks. Qualified candidates will be further called for the personal interview round. The final selection will be done on the basis of both rounds (test + PI) scores.
