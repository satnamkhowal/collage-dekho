About Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana B.Tech

Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana, interested candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 examination or equivalent from a recognized board, with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, candidates must have studied Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Technical Vocational subject as an elective subject. A minimum aggregate score of 45% in the qualifying examination is required for general category candidates, while reserved category candidates must obtain a minimum of 40%. Admissions are based on merit, and candidates may also need to appear for entrance exams, as per the institute's guidelines. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 80,000 - 1,14,000. Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana B.Tech Application Process

Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana B.Tech Application Form.
