About REVA University

REVA University is a NAAC A+ accredited private university in Bengaluru, established in 2012, offering 100+ programmes across Engineering, Management, Law, Architecture, Commerce, Science, Liberal Arts, and Performing Arts through its 15+ schools. The university is recognised by the UGC, holds a QS I-GAUGE Diamond Rating, and has NBA-accredited programmes in selected disciplines.

REVA University courses and fees 2026 range from INR 45,000 per year for diploma programmes to INR 6,75,000 per year for B.Tech specialisations. The total B.Tech fee ranges from INR 8.00 Lakhs to INR 27.00 Lakhs, while the MBA fee is INR 12.00 Lakhs for the 2-year programme. Admission is based on REVA CET, merit, and accepted national/state-level entrance exams, depending on the course.

In NIRF 2025, REVA University was ranked in the 151–200 band among universities and 201–300 in Engineering. The university's 45-acre Bengaluru campus features smart classrooms, advanced research labs, startup incubation facilities, separate hostels, sports infrastructure, healthcare facilities, and a vibrant student community with 45+ clubs and societies.

REVA University Admission Process 2026

REVA University admission process 2026 starts with online registration and ends with seat confirmation. Depending on the programme, students can apply through REVA CET, merit, or valid national/state-level entrance exam scores. BTech admissions accept KCET, JEE Main, COMEDK, CUET, or REVA CET scores, while MBA admissions also include GD and PI rounds. Law admissions are offered through CLAT or REVA CET, BArch admissions require NATA or JEE Paper 2, and PhD admissions are conducted through REVA PET followed by an interview. The complete admission process at REVA University is given below:

Step 1: Register Online
Visit the REVA University admission portal and complete the online application form.

Step 2: Choose the Admission Route
Apply through REVA CET or submit a valid national/state-level entrance exam score, depending on the programme.

Step 3: Attend the Required Selection Process
Appear for REVA CET, GD/PI, interview, audition, or other programme-specific selection rounds, if applicable.

Step 4: Check the Merit List
REVA University releases the merit list based on entrance exam scores, qualifying marks, and selection round performance.

Step 5: Participate in Counselling
Shortlisted candidates must attend counselling or seat allotment and choose their preferred programme or specialisation.

Step 6: Complete Document Verification
Submit original academic and personal documents for verification.

Step 7: Pay the Admission Fee
Pay the required tuition or admission fee to confirm the allotted seat.

Step 8: Complete Final Enrolment
Download the admission letter and complete the final enrolment process at the university.

REVA University Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

REVA University offers a modern and student-friendly campus designed for academic learning, comfortable living, and daily student needs. The campus provides everything needed for a comfortable and productive student life:

REVA University Campus and Facilities

  • REVA University has a 45-acre green campus located at Rukmini Knowledge Park, Kattigenahalli, Yelahanka, Bengaluru.
  • The campus features 9 academic blocks named after renowned personalities such as Sir C.V. Raman, Sir M. Visvesvaraya, and Swami Vivekananda.
  • Students from over 27 countries are part of the REVA University campus community.

Academic Infrastructure

  • Smart classrooms with ICT-enabled teaching facilities are available across all schools and departments.
  • The university has 20+ advanced laboratories and research facilities, including NBA-accredited labs for CSE, ECE, and Mechanical Engineering.
  • REVA University houses AI Verse, a dedicated space for Artificial Intelligence and immersive technologies.
  • AR/VR Labs, Fab Labs, and Idea Labs support innovation, prototyping, and experiential learning.
  • The REVA Library and Information Centre provides access to digital databases, journals, and academic resources.
  • The REVA Research Centre promotes innovation and interdisciplinary research, with 1,000+ patents filed.
  • REVA NEST serves as the university’s startup incubation centre and has supported over 90 startups.
  • Game Box offers indoor recreational and gaming facilities for students.

Hostel and Accommodation

  • Separate hostel facilities for boys and girls are available across 9 residential blocks.
  • The hostels accommodate more than 3,000 students.
  • Students can choose from single, twin-sharing, triple-sharing, four-sharing, and premium AC rooms.
  • Hostel fees range from approximately INR 1,40,000 to INR 2,85,000 per year, depending on the room category.
  • The hostel mess serves four meals daily with both vegetarian and non-vegetarian food options.
  • A refundable hostel caution deposit of INR 5,000 is charged at the time of admission.

Sports and Student Life

  • REVA University provides national-level sports infrastructure, and students regularly participate in AIU tournaments and Khelo India events.
  • The university has 45+ student clubs and societies covering academics, culture, technology, and entrepreneurship.
  • NCC units for Army, Navy, and Air Wing, along with NSS activities, are available for student participation.
  • Food courts and multiple canteens are available within the campus.
  • Transport services connect the campus to different parts of Bengaluru.
  • The campus also includes the REVA Health Centre and REVA Diagnostic Centre.
  • Guest house accommodation is available for visiting parents and faculty members.
  • Eventus, the annual cultural and technical fest, is one of the major student events at REVA University.

REVA University Scholarships 2026
