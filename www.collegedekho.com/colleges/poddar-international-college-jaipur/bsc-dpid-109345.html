About PIC Jaipur BSc

PIC Jaipur BSc is a 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the BSc degree program at Poddar International College, Jaipur, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Additionally, they must have studied Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects in their qualifying examination. Candidates from the Science stream with a strong academic background and a passion for pursuing a career in the field of science are encouraged to apply. Admission to the BSc program at Poddar International College is competitive, and candidates will be selected based on their academic performance and performance in the entrance examination conducted by the college. The annual fee for BSc is INR 30,000. PIC Jaipur BSc Admission 2026

Admission to the BSc at PIC Jaipur is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the PIC Jaipur BSc admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

PIC Jaipur BSc Application Process

PIC Jaipur BSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BSc course at PIC Jaipur have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill PIC Jaipur BSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of PIC Jaipur and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of PIC Jaipur. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the PIC Jaipur BSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the PIC Jaipur BSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of PIC Jaipur BSc Application Form.

Documents Required for PIC Jaipur BSc Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
