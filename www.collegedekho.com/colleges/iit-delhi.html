About IIT Delhi

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, commonly known as IIT Delhi or IITD is a public Institute of National Importance established in 1961. Located in Hauz Khas, South Delhi, the IIT Delhi campus is spread across 320 acres. Other than the main campus, IIT Delhi has a satellite campus in Sonipat, Haryana, and an international campus in Abu Dhabi, UAE. IITD ranked 123rd in QS World University rankings 2026.

IIT Delhi has 16 departments, 6 schools, and 19 Centres of Excellence, offering 150+ courses at UG, PG, doctoral, and executive levels. B.Tech is the most popular course offered by the institute with admission based on JEE Main & JoSAA counselling. The institute accepts GATE for M.Tech UCEED for BDes, IIT JAM for MSc, CEED for MDes, and CAT for MBA admissions. 

BTech fees at IIT Delhi range from INR 62,550 for SC/ST/PwD to INR 8.63 Lakhs for General category students with family income above INR 5 Lakhs. In 2024-25 placements, the BTech median package at IITD was INR 19 LPA, with 1,200+ job offers from 100+ companies including Google, Microsoft, Goldman Sachs, and BCG.

IIT Delhi B.Tech Admission Process 2026

The step-by-step process for B.Tech admissions at IIT Delhi is given as follows:

  • Register and appear for JEE Main 2026 (NTA) to qualify for JEE Advanced.
  • Register for JEE Advanced 2026 at jeeadv.ac.in and appear for the exam.
  • Check JEE Advanced results and qualify for minimum cutoff marks.
  • Register on the JoSAA 2026 portal (josaa.nic.in) using your JEE Advanced rank.
  • Fill choice preferences: select IIT Delhi and desired branches in order of preference.
  • Participate in JoSAA counselling rounds (up to 6 rounds). Accept or upgrade seat as allotted.
  • Pay the JoSAA seat acceptance fee (online, via UPI/debit/credit card/net banking).
  • Complete online document verification on the JoSAA portal.
  • Report to IIT Delhi for physical document verification and fee payment before the deadline.

For more admission related details and latest updates, visit IIT Delhi Admission 2026.

IIT Delhi Campus

IIT Delhi campus area is 320 acres and located about 4 km from Hauz Khas Metro Station (Yellow Line), offering strong connectivity. The campus is divided into four zones: academic, student residential, faculty housing, and cultural & recreational areas. IIT Delhi has over 12,500 students, with hostel accommodation available for around 50% of students. The following facilities are available at the IIT Delhi campus:

  • Hostels: IIT Delhi has 13 hostels (10 for boys and 3 for girls). Hostels include both non-AC and AC blocks, with one mess per hostel. AC hostels (Dronagiri, Satpura, Sahyadri) are mainly for PG students, with fees of INR 36,000–51,000 per semester.
  • Library: The Central Library is open 24x7 and has over 3.5 lakh volumes. It provides access to online journals like IEEE, Elsevier, and other databases for academic and research use.
  • Sports & Fitness: The campus includes a football stadium, cricket ground, hockey field, and 2 swimming pools. Facilities also include basketball courts, tennis courts, badminton courts, and a gym
  • Medical Facility: The IIT Delhi Hospital offers 24×7 emergency care, outpatient services, and counselling support (BSW) for students and staff.
  • Food & Dining: IIT Delhi has multiple canteens, hostel messes, Cafe Latitude, Amul outlets, and juice shops. Food options are available across the campus.
  • Clubs & Societies: Student life is supported by groups like BRCA (recreation), BSA (sports), EDC (entrepreneurship), Robotics, Music, NSS, and NCC. These clubs promote extracurricular and leadership activities.
  • Connectivity: The campus has 24×7 high-speed Wi-Fi with optical fibre backbone and VPN access for students.
  • Cultural & Technical Events: IIT Delhi hosts events like Rendezvous and Tryst. The campus also organises workshops, hackathons, and competitions throughout the year.
  • Satellite Campuses: IIT Delhi has two main satellite campuses located in Haryana: the Sonipat Campus and the Jhajjar Campus. Additionally it also has an international campus in Abu Dhabi (UAE).

IIT Delhi FAQs

What is the fee payment process for IIT Delhi JoSAA counseling?

After seat allocation, candidates need to pay the seat acceptance fee online to confirm their admission. The fee payment process and deadline details are provided on the JoSAA website. The JoSAA counselling fees are Rs. 35,000 (general) and 15,000 (SC, ST, GEN-PwD, GEN-EWS-PwD, OBC-NCL-PwD, SC-PwD, or ST-PwD).

Can I withdraw from IIT Delhi JoSAA counseling after accepting a seat?

Yes, candidates have the option to withdraw from IIT Delhi JoSAA counseling after accepting a seat. However, there might be certain penalties or restrictions associated with withdrawal, and it's advisable to carefully consider the decision before accepting or withdrawing. Also, if you withdraw your seat, you’re seat will be offered to the student next to you on the list.

What happens if I am allocated a seat in IIT Delhi JoSAA counseling?

If a candidate is allocated a seat for IIT Delhi, they have the option to either accept the seat or choose the "float" or "slide" option. Floating allows candidates to participate in subsequent rounds for better options while sliding allows them to move to a different course/institute within the same institute type.

What documents are required for JoSAA counseling?

Candidates need to carry various documents for verification during the reporting process. Commonly required documents include JEE Main/JEE Advanced admit card, rank card, class 10 and 12 mark sheets, category certificate (if applicable), and other relevant certificates.

When was the result jee advanced 2023 announced?

IIT Guwahati has declared the JEE Advanced 2023 result on June 18 at 10:00 AM. The authority has released the direct link to check the IIT Delhi JEE Advanced result 2023 on the website, jee adv.ac.in 2023.

Where can I check the JEE advanced results?

Students can check the JEE advanced results on https //jeeadv.ac.in result.

Is the IIT Delhi PhD admission 2023 open?

The PhD admissions at IIT Delhi ended on April 6, 2023.

Does IIT Delhi provide any internships?

Yes, IIT Delhi provides summer internships to its students. For the IIT Delhi summer internship 2023, the institute is conducting a Summer Research Fellowship programme.

What is the IIT Delhi fees for B.Tech?

The IIT Delhi fee structure varies from specialisation to specialisation. The fee ranges from Rs 8 lakh to Rs 9.42 lakh.

Does IIT Delhi offer M.Tech?

Yes, the IIT Delhi M.Tech is one of the most popular courses offered by the institute. Admission to M.Tech is based on the GATE scores.

What is the IIT Delhi cutoff for B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering?

The IIT Delhi cut off for B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering is based on the JEE scores. The cutoff for 2023 is yet to be released. However, the cutoff for 2022 was 102 rank in the JEE Advanced exam.

What is the CAT result date 2022?

The CAT 2022 result which was earlier expected to release in the second week of January 2023 has been released early on December 21, 2022.

Will IIT Delhi accept the CAT 2022 result?

Yes, IIT Delhi will accept the CAT 2022 scores for admission to its MBA programmes. The CAT 2022 exam date was November 27, 2022, and the result has been released on December 21, 2022.

Which university is IITD , Delhi affiliated to ?

IITD , Delhi is affiliated to the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi.

IITD , Delhi was established in the year 1961.
