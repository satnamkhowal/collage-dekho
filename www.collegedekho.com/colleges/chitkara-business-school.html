About Chitkara Business School, Rajpura

Chitkara Business School, Rajpura is situated in Rajpura in Punjab state of India. Established in 2008, is a Private college. The college is accredited by UGC. Chitkara Business School, Rajpura offers 18 courses across 3 streams namely Management, Commerce and Banking, Arts..Popular degrees offered at Chitkara Business School, Rajpura include BBA, B.Com, MBA, MSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Chitkara Business School, Rajpura is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Chitkara Business School, Rajpura, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Chitkara Business School, Rajpura is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
