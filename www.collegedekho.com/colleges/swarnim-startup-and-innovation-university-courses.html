Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University Courses and Fees 2026

Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University, Gandhinagar offers a diverse range of academic programs across diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and certificate levels. Diploma courses range from INR 31,500 to INR 1,25,000 with a duration of 3 years. Undergraduate programs such as B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Arch, B.Des, BBA, BCA, and others span 3 to 5 years, with fees ranging from INR 35,000 to INR 2,50,000. Postgraduate courses including M.Tech, M.Sc, MBA, M.Des, MCA, and more are generally 2 years long, with fees from INR 35,000 to INR 3,50,000. The university also offers specialized programs like MPH, MPM, and master’s degrees in Environment & Sustainable Development and Geoinformation. Law, commerce, journalism, and healthcare programs are available with durations between 2 to 5 years and moderate fee structures. Professional healthcare and allied science courses like B.Sc Nursing, MPT, and pharmacy-related programs range from INR 29,000 to INR 1,47,735. Additionally, certificate and short-term diploma courses are offered with 1-year durations and fees starting at INR 30,000.

Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University Courses FAQs

What types of courses does Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University offer?

At the UG level, Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University offers B.Tech, BSc, BCA, B.Des, BBA, BA, B.Com, B.Arch, BJMC , etc, while at the PG level, Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University offers MSc, M.Tech, MBA, MA, MCA, M.Plan, M.Com, M.Des, MPM, PGDCA, etc.

What are the Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University courses after 10th?

Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University offers Diploma in Fire Technology And Safety and certification Certificate in Design Engineering after class 10.Diploma is a 1.0 Years programme, admission to which is granted based on merit of the last qualifying exam.

Which courses are popular at Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University?

The most popular Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University courses include the B.Tech, BSc, BCA, B.Des, BBA and BA programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, BSc, BCA, B.Des, BBA, BA, B.Com, B.Arch, BJMC, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include MSc, M.Tech, MBA, MA, MCA, M.Plan, M.Com, M.Des, MPM, PGDCA , etc. Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, BSc, BCA, B.Des, BBA, BA, B.Com, B.Arch, BJMC and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University easy to get into?

Admission in Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Certificate, UG, Ph.D, Diploma and PG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include CBSE 10th, CBSE 12th, GSEB 12th, ACPC Admission, JEE Main, NATA, CMAT, GUJCET, GSEB 10th, CAT, GATE etc.
