What is the CSE (AI & ML) management quota fee for RVR & JC College of Engineering Guntur?

R
Rupsa •  May-06-2025

The RVR and JC College of Engineering fee for Computer Science and Engineering (AI & ML) is Rs 1,05,000/- for one year for the Covener quota.. Whereas the management quota fees for the college for the CSE (AI & ML) have not been mentioned specifically on the rvrjcce.ac.in/fee website. The fees amount also includes other elements like tuition fees, one-time admission fees, exam fees, registration fees, etc. The students might have to pay some extra charges depending on the course they opt for. Apart from AI & ML, if you plan to secure admissions to some other CSE programs offered by the college, like CSE (data science), Computer Science & Business Systems, Information Technology, CSE (Internet of Things), the fee structure will remain the same for the covener quota.
