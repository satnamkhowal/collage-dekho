Can a student pursue MSc Chemistry degree at IITs without having Maths in class 12?

n
namrata das •  Aug-20-2025

No, Mathematics is a compulsory subject in Class 12 for a student aspiring to pursue MSc Chemistry at IITs. Mathematics is a compulsory prerequisite during the undergraduate program as well. Specifically, IIT JAM (the Joint Admission Test for Masters) eligibility for MSc Chemistry requires that candidates have completed a bachelor's degree in Chemistry or a related subject with at least 50-55% marks.

Is there good placements for pharmacy students also.?

A
Akshay Singh •  Nov-22-2023
LPU offers the best undergraduate four-year course for Pharmacy Studies, which is approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). This course is a prerequisite for becoming a registered pharmacist and provides the necessary skills to work in various sectors such as industry, clinical setups, drug control organizations, education, and research in the pharmaceutical field. To be eligible for this course, students must pass their 10+2 exams with a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in subjects like English, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology or Maths. They also need to qualify the LPUNEST exam. Apart from theoretical knowledge, LPU also focuses on the practical aspect of the course. The university is ranked 17th among all government and private universities in India according to the NIRF ranking. The LPU School of Pharmacy is recognized as one of the top 25 pharmacy schools in India. Our students have the opportunity to participate in global research and conferences related to medicine, drugs, and pharmaceutical sciences. Placements are a significant aspect of our program, and we have renowned companies from various industries visiting our campus for recruitment. Pharmacy firms like Lupin, Cipla, Ayurvett, and others actively recruit talented students from Lovely Professional University. Here are some key features of our Pharmacy program: 1. Approval by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). 2. Ranked 17th among the top 30 Pharmacy colleges in India by NIRF 2023. 3. Diverse student population from all states of India and around the world. 4. Live Projects & Consultancy: Collaborations with pharmaceutical industries provide practical experience in solving real-world problems. 5. Innovative Pedagogy. 6. Strong connections with the corporate world. 7. Significant number of Patents & Publications every year. 8. Animal House: The department has an approved animal house for experiments on animals. 9. Well-equipped library with online subscriptions. At LPU, we strive to provide the best education and opportunities for our Pharmacy students. For further information you may visit LPU official website. Thank you.
