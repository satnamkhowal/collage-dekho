About BOSE Cuttack

Bhubanananda Orissa School of Engineering is situated in Cuttack in the Odisha state of India. Established in 1923, BOSE CUTTACK is a private college.  Before 1923, there was no scope for technical education in Odisha. Students mostly used to go to Bihar School of Engineering for diploma courses. BOSE Cuttack has contributed largely to the advancement of technical education in Odisha. 

 The college is accredited by AICTE.  Bhubanananda Orissa School of Engineering offers 11 courses across 1 stream namely Engineering. BOSE Cuttack courses are highly popular among the students of Odisha. The BOSE Cuttack admission fees are also affordable. The B.Tech programme is offered under various specialisations. 

BOSE Cuttack campus is spread over 44 acres. The classrooms, labs, and academic and administrative buildings are spacious and well built. Students can engage in extracurricular activities within the campus. 

The BOSE Cuttack placement is varied and fulfills the needs of its students. Many companies visit the campus during the placement drives to recruit eligible students.

BOSE Cuttack Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The BOSE Cuttack campus is spread over 48 acres. Almost 1898 students are currently studying at this campus. Let’s have a look at the different aspects of the campus and how they are useful for the students. 

Library

The college library contains more than 45,000 books. There are a total of 30 journals and periodicals. The reading section can accommodate 80 students at a time. Besides the hard copies, there are e- resources also kept in the library. 

Classrooms

There are 60 classrooms in the college covering 19200 sq ft. Each academic department has its own classrooms. 

Auditorium

The college auditorium holds all seminars, workshops and cultural programmes.  

 Laboratory facilities

The labs are meant for practical teaching and practice. Each department has its own set of labs. The labs are equipped with all the necessary devices that are used for advanced practical teaching. 

Alumni Association

BOSE Cuttack has an association of alumni. It has good networking among the alumni of BOSE Cuttack. 
Cafeteria

The college has a cafeteria where students can have snacks and other items at affordable prices. 

Hostels

There are separate hostels for men and women. The security in the hostels is strictly enforced. The students have all the basic amenities in the hostel rooms. 

IT Facilities

The college provides students with computer facilities. Students can use computers with i3/i5/i7  Processor, and LAN with high-speed internet connection.
