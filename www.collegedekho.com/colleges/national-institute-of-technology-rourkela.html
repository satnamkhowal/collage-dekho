About NIT Rourkela

NIT Rourkela, established in 1961 as Regional Engineering College and upgraded to a National Institute of Technology in 2002, is a public technical university located in Rourkela, Odisha. It is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and approved by AICTE, holding an “A” grade accreditation from NAAC. NIT Rourkela is ranked 19th in the Engineering category and 31st in the Overall category by NIRF 2024. The institute’s 1,200-acre campus hosts over 8,000 students, including international students from 10+ countries, and features modern facilities like smart classrooms, a central library with over 1.2 lakh books, a health center, hostels for boys and girls, sports facilities, a gym, and a cafeteria, fostering holistic student development. 

The institute offers over 100 programs across undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral levels in streams such as Engineering, Sciences, Management, Architecture, and Humanities. Popular UG courses include BTech, BArch, and Integrated MSc, while PG courses include MTech, MBA, MSc, and MA, offered in various specializations. Admissions are based on entrance exam scores, with JEE Main for BTech/BArch, GATE for MTech, IIT JAM for MSc, and CAT/MAT/CMAT for MBA, managed through centralized counseling processes like JoSAA, CCMT, and CCMN. NIT Rourkela’s fee structure varies by program, with BTech annual fees ranging from INR 91,000 to INR 2,25,000 for general category students. 

The institute’s Training and Placement Cell facilitate robust placement drives, with the 2024-25 placement report indicating a highest package of INR 52.98 LPA and an average package of INR 12.59 LPA for BTech students, with over 1,300 recruiters, including top companies like Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, and Qualcomm, placing 1,374 students across sectors like IT, core engineering, finance, and consulting.

How to Apply for NIT Rourkela 2025?

The NIT Rourkela application process is completely online. Candidates need to follow the steps mentioned below to complete the NIT Rourkela application process. 

  • Step 1: Register on the official admission portal of NIT Rourkela with correct personal details, including name, email ID, mobile no, state, gender, and password
  • Step 2: Fill in the NIT Rourkela application form.
  • Step 3: Apply for the required entrance exam.
  • Step 4: Candidates are required to book a slot with their preferred date and time to appear for the relevant entrance exam.

NIT Rourkeral Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

NIT Rourkela’s 1,200-acre campus is designed to provide a conducive environment for academic, extracurricular, and personal growth, featuring modern facilities and robust infrastructure. The list of all the facilities is given below:

  • Central Library - Biju Patnaik Central Library (BPCL) was named after the founding Chairman of REC Rourkela, operational since 1965, BPCL is the institute’s primary learning resource center. It houses over 1.2 lakh books, 84,000+ print volumes, 1,400+ journals, 60,000 e-books, 700 CDs, and access to databases like IEEE, Springer, and Elsevier. The air-conditioned library offers digital resources, a reading room, and 24/7 access during exams. Recent upgrades include expanded e-resource subscriptions and a renovated study area, supporting teaching and research for 8,000+ students and faculty.
  • Hostel - NIT Rourkela maintains 11 hostels, including 7 for boys, 2 for girls, and 2 for married research scholars, accommodating over 3,000 students. Hostels feature single, double, or triple occupancy rooms, common rooms, indoor game areas, lounges, and dining halls with mess facilities. Each hostel is managed by a Chief Warden, Wardens, and Assistant Wardens, with student-led Hall Working Committees overseeing maintenance, mess, cultural, and sports activities.
  • Indoor and Outdoor Sports Facilities - NIT Rourkela provides world-class sports facilities, including the Dilip Tirkey Stadium (football and hockey), Sachin Tendulkar Cricket Ground (floodlit), tennis courts, basketball courts, volleyball courts, badminton courts, kabaddi grounds, and an athletic track. Indoor facilities include table tennis, chess, carrom, and a gymnasium with modern equipment and two instructors.
  • Canteen - The campus features three canteens (Calorie Canteen, SLR Canteen, Hexagon Canteen) and a Campus Café, open on working days. The Calorie and SLR Canteens, located between the Audio-Visual Hall and CSE department, serve a variety of Indian and continental cuisines at subsidized rates. Hexagon Canteen, near hostels, is popular among hostel residents. The Campus Café operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM (except Sundays), offering quick bites and beverages.
  • Shopping Centre - Two shopping centers on campus cater to students, faculty, and locals, providing provisional items, stationery, groceries, and daily essentials. Located near hostels and academic areas, they ensure convenience for residents.
  • Guest Houses - NIT Rourkela has two guest houses: North Block (air-conditioned rooms) and South Block (AC suites), with 33 rooms equipped with telephones and TVs. They accommodate institute visitors, newly appointed faculty, staff, delegates, and participants in conferences, seminars, and workshops.
  • Health Centre - The on-campus health center, led by Dr. Champak Bhattacharyya with three permanent doctors, operates 24/7 with an attached Apollo Pharmacy. It provides medical consultations, diagnostics, and emergency services via an ambulance. The center caters to students, faculty, and staff, with additional support from a Community Welfare Service Hospital.
  • IT Infrastructure - NIT Rourkela’s IT infrastructure supports a campus-wide network with over 17,000 nodes, connecting academic blocks, classrooms, laboratories, administrative offices, the library, hostels, and residential areas. The network, managed by the Computer Centre, provides 5 Gbps bandwidth from multiple ISPs, ensuring 24/7 internet connectivity. Smart classrooms feature advanced audio-visual aids, and labs include specialized facilities for civil, mechanical, electrical, and computer science engineering.
  • Free Wi-Fi - The campus offers free Wi-Fi across academic areas, hostels, and select residential zones, accessible via a captive portal login. Wi-Fi supports academic tasks, research, and extracurricular activities, with high-speed LAN (10 Mbps) available in hostels and labs.
  • Auditorium - The Bhubaneswar Behera Audio-Visual Auditorium, with a seating capacity of over 1,000, is air-conditioned and hosts seminars, conferences, cultural events, and student symposiums. A 400-seat open-air theatre in the Student Activity Centre complements it for larger events.

Events and Clubs at National Institute of Technology, Rourkela

National Institute of Technology, Rourkela Events 

Student Activity Centre (SAC):

The Student Activity Centre (SAC) is the central hub for student activities, student organizations, programming, events, sports, recreation, and fun at the NIT, Rourkela campus. The Student Activity Centre is organized into four primary societies and several clubs. Besides, every society has satellite clubs that have their management under the overall supervision of SAC. The facility is devoted to recreation and socialization to serve students, faculty, staff, alumni, and guests.

Technical Society:

Technical Society is one of the most enthusiastic societies in the Student Activity Centre, NIT Rourkela, with 18 Satellite Clubs under its roof. The objective of this society is to promote and encourage technical innovation and entrepreneurial qualities among students by organizing various modeling workshops, Technical Seminars, Training Courses, and many more. These are the subject areas where the satellite clubs of technological society are currently involved: Astrophysics, Automobile, Aerospace, Robotics, Fluid Dynamics, Software, Infrastructure, Mathematics, Business Management, and Entrepreneurship. The technological society also hosts a National Level Techno-Management festival annually that attracts participation from various organizations and institutions of our country.

Games and Sports Society:

The Institute has many excellent fitness facilities and playgrounds with an attractive stadium for Athletics, Basketball, Badminton, Cricket, Cross Country, Handball, Hockey, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, Football, Netball, Softball, Swimming, Tennis, Volleyball, and a fully-equipped fitness center.
