LPU Placements FAQs

Who are the top recruiters at LPU?

Some of the top recruiters that participate in Lovely Professional University placements are Amazon, Google, Intel, Microsoft, Accenture, Deloitte, Hindustan Unilever, ICICI Bank, Hilton Hotel etc.

How many companies visit LPU?

Every year thousands of companies visit LPU and hire students. During the LPU placements 2023, as many as 1107 companies visited the campus for recruitment. Some of the companies visiting LPU are Cognizant, BOSCH, Amazon, Cloudera, VMWare, HackerOne, Phonepe, Flipkart, Informatica, Voltas, Sony, and P&O Cruises. These companies offered a variety of roles across different sectors, providing students with a wide range of career opportunities.

Is LPU good for placements?

Yes, Lovely Professional University has an excellent placement record. The university has been conferred with the National Employability Award 2024. Every year, the university achieves over 90% placements. Companies from diverse industries hire students and offer handsome salaries.

Which branch of LPU has the best placement record?

As per the overall placement record of LPU, BTech Computer Science and Engineering has the best placement record. In 2023, Yasir, a student of BTech CSE course bagged a package of Rs 3 crore per annum. Furthermore, CSE students are hired by top-notch companies like Google, Amazon, Microsoft etc.
