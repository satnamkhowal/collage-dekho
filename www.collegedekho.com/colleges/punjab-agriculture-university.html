About PAU Ludhiana

Punjab Agricultural University, established in 1962 in Ludhiana, Punjab, ranks 3 in NIRF 2025 for agriculture and allied sectors with a score of 75.83. As pau university, it leads as the best agriculture university in Punjab, securing first among state agricultural universities in IIRF 2025 and 93rd globally in EduRank 2025 for agricultural institutions. Punjab agriculture university Ludhiana enrolls over 3000 students across 82 programs in six constituent colleges, focusing on research that drove India's Green Revolution. For 2026 admissions, applications open April 2026 on pau-apms.in, with entrance tests like CET in mid-June and counseling in July, targeting Punjab residents for 85% seats.

Punjab Agricultural University operates under UGC and ICAR accreditations, with NAHEP funding for advanced labs. Students benefit from 407 PhD scholars pursuing research, with 198 UG graduates opting higher studies per NIRF 2025.

Punjab Agricultural University Fees Structure 2026

Punjab Agricultural University in Ludhiana offers affordable fees for its agriculture-focused programs, based on the 2025-26 prospectus. Undergraduate courses like B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture start at Rs. 86,720 for the first year, including one-time charges of Rs. 23,570 for admission and Rs. 10,600 for refundable securities, with subsequent years at Rs. 52,550. Self-supporting UG programs such as B.Tech Biotechnology cost Rs. 2,17,740 initially due to higher tuition. Postgraduate fees for M.Sc are Rs. 94,500 first year, while PhD stands at Rs. 1,02,590. Payments are semester-wise, and scholarships like ICAR (up to Rs. 12,640 monthly) can offset costs. Hostel adds Rs. 24,480-36,980 annually, separate from academics.

PAU Ludhiana Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

PAU Ludhiana has a huge campus that spreads across 494 hectares of land. It also has an off-campus area of 1,793 hectares. For its significant contributions to raising food production, livestock and poultry production in the state and ushering in an era of Green Revolution in India, PAU Ludhiana was named the Best Agricultural University in India in 1995. It has four constituent colleges as well namely, the College of Agriculture, College of Home Science, College of Agricultural Engineering and Technology, and College of Basic Science and Humanities. 

The Punjab Agricultural University campus is well-equipped and has all the required amenities for the proper academic and holistic development of the students. Facilities like computer labs, training labs, research halls, counselling cells, lecture rooms, hostels, etc. are provided.

Punjab Agricultural University Facilities

Some of the facilities offered at the PAU Ludhiana campus are the following:

  • Computer Labs: The computer lab is well furnished and equipped with desktops updated with the latest software for students.
  • Training Lab: A well-equipped training lab is provided on Combine Harvester under Institute Industry Collaboration with CLAAS. Along with this Tractors under Institute Industry Collaboration with New Holland are also provided.
  • Counselling Cell: The counselling cell at the PAU Ludhiana campus has smart boards and a video conferencing facility.
  • Examination Halls: All the exam halls are air-conditioned with a seating capacity of 200 students.
  • Conference Halls: It is fully air-conditioned with a seating capacity of 200 students.
  • Lecture Rooms: The lecture rooms at the Punjab Agricultural University campus are well-equipped with multimedia projectors.
  • Sports Facilities: The Punjab Agricultural University facility for sports offer badminton courts, basketball courts, volleyball courts, tennis courts, cricket ground, football ground, hockey ground, astroturf, running track, swimming pool, table tennis and gym.
  • Hostel: PAU Ludhiana hostels have facilities such as a well-furnished room with attached bathrooms.
  • Other Facilities: The university also has a research hall, research farms and a farm machinery testing centre.
