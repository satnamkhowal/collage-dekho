About Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya

Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya or SBPGM is a private institution established in 2007 by Sri Balaji Shiksha Samiti. It is affiliated with the Rajasthan University. SBPGM is approved by NCTE. The Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya is located in the 'Pink City, Jaipur. The college is located near the Bus stand and Railway station, making it easy for students to travel from and to SBPGM. The college ensures maximum security and protection of the students.

SBPGM courses are B.Sc, B.Com, BCA, B.Ed, M.Ed, etc. For admission, the college offers direct admission to all courses except B.Ed and M.Ed. For these two courses, students need to qualify for the required entrance exam.

The college campus is 6.5 acres of land within a pollution-free environment. SBPGM aims to provide a purposeful education and ample scope for progress and academic excellence to every student. Students can access all the amenities including a unique Digital Library of Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya, which is connected to 1962 national libraries through DELNET and has more than 50,000 books. The Campus includes a gym, sports complex, convenience store, a swimming pool with FAX and STD facilities, and furnished hostel accommodations. The hostel includes mess facilities, provides nutritious food at affordable prices, and has transport services for students at Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya.

Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya Courses and Admission 2026

Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya offers BSc, B.Com, BCA, B.Sc+B.Ed, B.Ed, and M.Ed courses. Annual fees range from INR 16,000 to INR 42,000. Course duration varies from 2 to 4 years. Eligibility requires 10+2 or graduation as per course and NCTE norms. SBPGM fees can be paid in instalments. The college offers UG and PG courses for its students. Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya Admission 2026

SBPGM admission to all courses except education stream courses is provided based on qualifying exam marks. For B.Ed and M.Ed students need to qualify for entrance exams like PTET and PMET, respectively. The process for SBPGM admission is provided below. 

SBPGM Application Process 2026

The application forms for admission into a course in SBPGM are available offline and online. The details to be kept in mind for the online admission process are:

  • Download the application form from the official Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya website
  • Mention all the details in the application form and provide all necessary details
  • Attach all copies of the asked documents.

Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya Admission Required Documents 2026

  • Certificate or Mark sheet of secondary school (Original and two copies)
  • Senior secondary school mark sheet (Original and two copies)
  • The Transfer certificate of senior secondary school ( Original and two copies)
  • Certificate of Migration (Original and two copies)
  • If applicable, proof of reserved category (Original and two copies)
  • Domicile certificate (2 attested copies)
  • Anti-ragging affidavit by students and parents
  • 4 colour passport size photographs with the name of the student written on the back of the photographs.

Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The Campus and infrastructure of Sri Balaji PG Mahavidyalaya are huge. There are many facilities for the students to give them opportunities to showcase their talents and gain expertise in relevant fields.

The facilities offered in SBPGM are:

Library

The Library of SBPGM has a collection of about 50,000 books, 179 journals, and magazines connected to 5096 national libraries through DELNET.

Hostel

SBPGM has well-furnished hostel rooms with wifi connection, air conditioning and medical facilities.

Cafeteria

The cafeteria has healthy, nutritious food choices for students and a variety of options to choose from.

Labs

Various lab facilities for different fields like computer, science, languages and engineering.

Gym and Sports complex

The SBPGM gym is equipped with advanced machines for physical training and various sports courts like tennis, badminton, basketball, table tennis etc.

Shuttle service

The shuttle service of SBPGM is to facilitate students to travel to and from the college since the college is located near the bus and railway stations.

Auditorium

The SBPGM auditorium can accommodate many students for holding conferences, programs, seminars etc.

Convenience Store

A store containing all the daily use items of perishable and non-perishable products for the convenience of students.
