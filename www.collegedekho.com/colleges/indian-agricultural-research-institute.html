About Indian Agricultural Research Institute

The Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI) Delhi, commonly known as Pusa Institute, is India's national institute for agriculture research, education and extension. The institute was established on April 1, 1905. It has a campus area of 500 hectares, and it is located approximately eight km west of the new Delhi railway station. It is financed and administered by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR).

The ICAR-IARI Delhi campus provides high-quality facilities and has experienced faculties. ASRB examination centre, Phytotron facility, Pesticide Referral Laboratory, SEM facility, Library services, and Farm Machinery Testing facility are among the top-notch facilities available here.

The Indian Agricultural Research Institute courses are offered at postgraduate degree levels, these include M.Sc, M.Tech and PhD. Admission to these courses is based on an entrance examination undertaken by the Education Division of ICAR, past academic merit is also considered and on the basis of these, the ICAR finalizes the admission.

Admission to the ICAR-IARI Delhi PhD courses is made on the basis of the performance of candidates in the written entrance examination (80% weightage)+academic score (10% weightage)+interview (10% weightage).

The Indian Agricultural Research Institute fees for the courses offered are moderate ranging from Rs 27,000 to Rs 43,000 (annually) approximately. IARI even offers scholarships or fellowships as financial assistance to postgraduate students.

The mission of IARI Delhi is to deliver quality education, encourage high standards, and align educational programs with future requirements and possibilities. They aim to provide postdoctoral research, faculty development, and human resource development opportunities in creative and cutting-edge technical domains, primarily through international collaboration. 

It has international collaborations with the Potash and Phosphate Institute of Canada (PPIC), United State Agency for International Development (USAID), International Development Research Centre (IDRC), Consultative Group on International Agricultural Research (CGIAR), International Maize and Wheat Improvement Centre (CIMMYT), United Nations Environment Program Regional Research Centre for Asia and the Pacific (UNEP RRC.AP).

Indian Agricultural Research Institute Placements

The institute has a dedicated placement cell that looks over all the placement-related activities. It also helps with the preparation of resumes and practices for interviews. The IARI Delhi placement cell conducts pre-placement activities, mock interviews, aptitude tests, industry visits, seminars, etc. The cell acts as a bridge between academics and the industry. It makes sure every student from the institute gets proper job offers and equal opportunities. The ICAR-IARI placement cell also gives opportunities to research scholars, such as R&D in Government and private enterprises.

The ICAR-Indian Agricultural Research Institute placement cell guides students, approaches companies, organizes training sessions, teaches soft skills, personality development, interpersonal and communication skills and provides career counselling. The institute provides placement to its final-year students. All the activities associated with the Indian Agricultural Research Institute Delhi placements are taken care of by the placement cell.

Indian Agricultural Research Institute Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The present campus of the institute is a self-contained sylvan complex spread over an area of 500 hectares. It is located approximately 8 km west of New Delhi Railway station and about 16km east of the Indra Gandhi International airport Palam. 

The institute has 20 divisions and five multi-disciplinary centres in Delhi, eight regional stations, two off-season nurseries, three all-India coordinated research projects with headquarters at IARI, and 10 national sectors functioning under the all-India coordinated research project. The IARI Delhi campus has a sectioned staff strength of 3540, comprising scientific, technical, administrative, and support personnel. 

Indian Agricultural Research Institute Delhi Infrastructure 

The IARI Delhi is very good for studying agriculture. It provides you with better infrastructure to make your college journey beautiful. It has Wi-Fi facilities on the entire campus, and it gives you high-quality classrooms, science labs, and a very high-tech library. There is a health centre offering medical facilities to students and a good quality canteen that gives you very tasty food. The playground is very big for playing sports like cricket, football, tennis, etc. 

Indian Agricultural Research Institute Delhi Facilities 

Good facilities attract students, and IARI provides you with high-quality facilities like classrooms, hostel facilities, sports, auditoriums, etc. 

The IARI Delhi campus has eight hostels, out of which five are for male students, two for female students, and one is for married couples. Students of the institute are engaged in various sports activities. The auditorium is entirely air conditioning and has a capacity of 350 people.

Indian Agricultural Research Institute Scholarships

The institute offers scholarships or fellowships to the students as financial assistance to support their education. The IARI Delhi scholarships are provided based on the candidate's performance under the general open competition scheme. The dean's decision on the candidate's prior academic record and subsequent performance in postgraduation are taken into consideration.

Here are some of the Indian Agricultural Research Institute Delhi scholarships:

IARI Scholarships

It offers Junior and Senior scholarships and is awarded to M.Sc and PhD students admitted under General Open Competition Scheme. The junior scholarships for the M.Sc course are for two years and the senior scholarships for the PhD course are for three years. For SC/ ST/ PH students, the scholarships are extendable up to a period of six months to one year, respectively.

The amount of the M.Sc and PhD scholarships are Rs 7,560 and Rs 13,125 per month, respectively. Along with this a contingent grant of Rs 6,000 per year for M.Sc students, and Rs 10,000 per year for PhD students will be paid for experimental material, books, thesis, study tours, etc.

Scholarship for In-service Candidates/ Departmental Students

For the in-service candidates joining M.Sc/ PhD courses at IARI Delhi, the scholarship amount will be of Rs 3,000 per month in addition to a full leave salary. This is in addition to the payment of the contingent grant. In-service candidates who are not in receipt of JRF/ SRF and receiving a partial salary (half or leave without pay) are also eligible.

ICAR Fellowship

It has a scheme to award Senior Research Fellowship of the value of Rs 15,000 per month for the first two years and Rs 17,500 for the third year onwards for PhD students with a contingent grant of Rs 10,000 per annum. 

For Junior Research Fellowship of the value of Rs 8,640 per month for M.Sc students with a contingent grant of Rs 6,000 per annum.
