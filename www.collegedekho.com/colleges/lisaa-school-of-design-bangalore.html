About LISAA School of Design (LISAA), Bangalore

LISAA School of Design is part of the world-famous LISAA France. This is a private institute and has been co-founded by Avi Keswani.  Avi Keswani is the director of the institute as well and has more than 2 decades of teaching experience along with industry experience.  

LISAA School of Design in Bangalore is managed by CREO Valley. CREO Valley is affiliated with Bengaluru Central University to offer Degree Courses in Fashion Design, Interior Design, Graphics & Communications Design, Animation & Multimedia Design.

The institute spans over a 3-acre land consisting of all the major and state of the art facility for the students. LISAA School of Design supports foreign student exchange programs which can be really helpful for students in their careers. It also offers Student Exchange program with more than 7 other locations in France.

As the institute has branches all over India and overseas as well, admission to the LISAA School of Design is done through a special entrance exam known as LISAA CAT at the institute itself. LISAA School of Design has various courses at UG and PG levels along with many certifications. 

LISAA is a reputed Design school located in France. LISAA has campuses in various locations across the globe such as Bangalore, Paris, Bordeaux, Toulouse, Rennes and Nantes. It is one of the popular Design schools in India, which is recognised by the Ministry of Culture and Communication - France.

The Bangalore campus of LISAA has a multi-cultural environment, which suits the students hailing from various parts of the country. The institute is well known for its state-of-the-art infrastructure and facilities.

LISAA Bangalore has tie-ups with popular institutes across the globe, and students enrolled in design courses of the institute will get a chance to spend some of their time in these institutes. The student-exchange programmes of LISAA and its partner institutes are a great help to students in gaining practical exposure and enhancing career opportunities.

The industry-crafted curriculum of LISAA Bangalore helps students gain in-depth knowledge of the topic. Project work is included in the curriculum of LISAA Bangalore, and each student must take part in the project work to obtain the degree. The project work process of LISAA Bangalore is rigorous and each student will have an opportunity to explore more about the subject. At the same time, the project work will enhance the career opportunities of students.

Some of the major advantages of LISAA Bangalore are its industry connect, project-based learning and international student exchange programs. These programmes enable students to use their skills and capabilities completely besides enhancing the same.

Students of LISAA Bangalore participate in International Events pertaining to Design and Film Making. Through these competitions, students compete at the global level where they get a chance to meet the experts and professors from some of the top Design universities across the globe.

LISAA School of Design Application Process 2026

Every year, the admissions for UG and PG courses at LISAA School of Design begin in March. The admission process is carried out by the admission cell which overseas everything ranging from documentation to onboarding of students for all the courses. 

The application form can be availed online from the official website or one can visit the institute and get the application form. Candidates also need to appear for the LISAA CAT entrance exam for getting accepted at this institute.

Application Process for LISAA School of Design

Online Admission Process

Candidates who want to secure admission at the LISAA School of Design can apply for the same through their official website. Candidates would be required to create an account first and then fill in all the mandatory information. Once done the candidate would have access to the application form which they need to fill out and attach all the mandatory documents. While submitting the form the candidate needs to pay a nominal fee of INR 1000 which can be paid via credit/debit cards or through UPI as well. Once submitted the candidate would receive an acknowledgement mail as well.

Offline Admission Process

Candidates who are seeking admission at LISAA School of Design can also take admission via offline mode by visiting the admission cell directly. The candidate would be given an admission form which they need to fill out with all the required details. Candidates can clear any questions or doubts they have about a specific course at the admission cell itself. While submitting the form the candidate needs to pay a nominal fee of INR 1000 which can be paid via credit/debit cards, net banking or through UPI as well. Once submitted the admission cell would give a receipt of acknowledgement to the candidate.

LISAA School of Design Application Fees

The general application form fee for the LISAA School of Design is Rs 1000.

How to Fill LISAA School of Design Application Form?

  • Step 1- Submit the inquiry form and register for LISAA’s Creative Aptitude Test (CAT). Or send an email and one of the Admission Team members will get in touch soon.
  • Step 2 - The admission team will send the entrance test and other details over emails.
  • Step 3 - The student has about a total of 6 hrs to complete the exam and submit the scanned answer sheet via email.
  • Step 4 - Results are declared usually within 24 hrs (please reach out to your Admission Team member if you do not hear within this timeframe).
  • Step 5 - Personal interview through Skype, Phone, or in-person & Portfolio Assessment (if required).
  • Step 6 - Fee Payment within 10 days of receiving confirmation to reserve the seat.
