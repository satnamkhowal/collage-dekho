IIT Guwahati Courses 2026

IIT Guwahati offers a wide range of programs across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels in engineering, science, design, and humanities. Its 4-year BTech programs span 11 specializations, including core branches like CSE, ECE, Mechanical, and Civil, as well as emerging fields such as Data Science, AI, and Biotechnology. Admission requires at least 75% in Class 12 PCM (65% for SC/ST) along with a valid JEE Advanced rank, with fees ranging from INR 6.78 lakh for GEN/EWS/OBC students to INR 2.78 lakh for SC/ST/PwD. The institute also offers BDes and BS programs, requiring UCEED or JEE Advanced scores respectively.

 IIT Guwahati Postgraduate options include MTech, MS(R), MSc, MDes, MA, and MBA programs, typically requiring a relevant undergraduate degree, a minimum 6.0 CPI, and competitive scores in exams like GATE, JAM, CEED, or CAT. PhD and dual-degree programs demand a master’s or BTech with strong CPI and GATE/NET scores. Admissions across programs involve national exams, interviews, and counseling, ensuring merit-based selection.

The results of any free IIT JAM mock test you attempted will usually be accessible on the same website or platform where you took the test. You can check your results by doing the following:

  • Check the platform or website:

Return to the website where the mock exam was held if it was one of such sites (such as the platform of a coaching centre or an educational website). Use the login information you used to register to access your account, if you have one. Look for a section that says "My Results," "Test History," or "Dashboard," since these might include your mock test results and feedback.

  • Instant Results:

Results from a lot of free practice exams are available right away. If so, see if your score appeared on the platform immediately after you finished the test.

  • Notification via Email:

After you finish the test, some platforms send you an email with your findings. Look for any correspondence on the outcomes of your practice exam in your inbox (and spam folder).

  • Speak with Support:

You can get in touch with the helpdesk or support staff of the website or platform where you took the test if you are unable to locate your results. They ought to be able to help you find your score.

IIT Guwahati Courses FAQs

What are the seat intakes for BTech emerging fields like Data Science and AI?

BTech in Data Science & AI at IIT Guwahati has 70 seats. Admission requires 75% in PCM (65% for SC/ST) and a strong JEE Advanced rank, usually under 3,500. These programs are designed to meet industry demand in AI and data-driven technologies. Students receive training in machine learning, data analysis, and AI applications over 4 years, along with lab access and project opportunities to prepare them for advanced research or career roles in the technology sector.

Which entrance exams are needed for undergraduate programs?

Undergraduate admissions at IIT Guwahati mostly require JEE Advanced for BTech and BS programs. For BDes, students need UCEED scores. Admission also considers Class 12 marks, with a minimum of 75% in PCM (65% for SC/ST). After qualifying the exams, students go through JoSAA counseling for seat allocation. These exams ensure that admission is merit-based, selecting students who are well-prepared for rigorous academic programs in engineering, science, and design.

What undergraduate programs are offered at IIT Guwahati?

IIT Guwahati offers a variety of undergraduate programs including BTech, BDes, and BS across engineering, design, and science. BTech has 11 specializations such as CSE, ECE, Mechanical, Civil, Data Science, AI, and Biotechnology. Admission requires 75% in Class 12 PCM (65% for SC/ST) and a valid JEE Advanced rank for BTech. BDes and BS admissions require UCEED or JEE Advanced scores. These programs provide high-quality education, labs, and research opportunities over 4 years.

How many seats are there for BTech CSE at IIT Guwahati?

BTech in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) has 120 seats at IIT Guwahati. Students need 75% in Class 12 PCM (65% for SC/ST) and a strong JEE Advanced rank for admission. Other BTech specializations have slightly fewer seats depending on demand. The intake ensures personalized attention in labs, projects, and classroom learning. Students also get access to research and innovation opportunities throughout the 4-year program.

What postgraduate programs are available at IIT Guwahati?

IIT Guwahati offers MTech, MS(R), MSc, MDes, MA, and MBA programs. These programs usually require a relevant undergraduate degree and a minimum CPI of 6.0. Admission depends on competitive national exams like GATE, IIT JAM, CEED, or CAT, sometimes combined with interviews. Postgraduate programs focus on advanced technical knowledge, research, or professional skills

What are the eligibility criteria for MTech programs?

For MTech at IIT Guwahati, students must have a BTech degree in a relevant field with at least 6.0 CPI. Admission also requires a good GATE score in the relevant paper. For example, CSE students need GATE CS >600, ECE students need GATE EC >550, and Mechanical students need GATE ME >500. Some specializations also consider interviews for final selection. This ensures candidates have both strong academic background and aptitude for research or technical learning.

How long are PhD and Dual-Degree programs?

PhD programs at IIT Guwahati are generally 5 years long, while dual-degree (MTech + PhD or MS + PhD) programs last 6 years. These programs require a relevant master’s degree (or strong BTech for dual-degree) and a valid GATE/NET score. Students engage in research, coursework, and advanced study in engineering, science, design, or humanities. Dual-degree options allow BTech students to continue directly into a research path without completing a separate master’s degree.

Which entrance exams are required for PhD programs?

PhD and dual-degree admissions require GATE, UGC-NET, or CSIR-NET scores, followed by a written test and interview. Students applying for dual-degree programs may use a strong BTech record with GATE scores. These exams ensure that candidates have sufficient knowledge and research potential. Selected students gain access to research labs, guidance from faculty, and fellowships, enabling them to complete advanced research projects over 5–6 years.

What are the popular BTech specializations?

The popular BTech specializations at IIT Guwahati include Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication, Mechanical, Civil, Data Science, AI, and Biotechnology. There are also programs in Engineering Physics, Chemical Engineering, Chemical Science & Technology, Mathematics & Computing, and Energy Engineering. Students choose based on interest and career goals. Admission requires high marks in Class 12 PCM and JEE Advanced ranks, ensuring students are prepared for a strong technical curriculum over 4 years.

How is admission to MDes programs done?

Admission to MDes programs at IIT Guwahati requires a BDes or BTech degree, a minimum 6.0 CPI, and a CEED score above the 50th percentile. Some MDes programs, such as Electronic Product Design, prefer candidates with an electronics or engineering background. Selected students may also attend interviews. The 2-year MDes programs focus on design, product development, and research, combining theory, workshops, and hands-on projects to develop professional skills.
