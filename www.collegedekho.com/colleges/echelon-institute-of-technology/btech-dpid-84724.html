About Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech

Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Echelon Institute of Technology Faridabad, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board, with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they must have studied Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Computer Science as an elective subject. The minimum aggregate marks required in the qualifying examination is 45% for General category candidates and 40% for SC/ST category candidates. Admissions are also subject to the candidate's performance in the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE Main) or the Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) entrance examination. Meeting these criteria will enable aspiring students to embark on a rewarding B.Tech. journey at Echelon Institute of Technology Faridabad. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 88,000 - 1,10,180. Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Echelon Institute of Technology is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech Application Process

Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Echelon Institute of Technology have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Echelon Institute of Technology and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Echelon Institute of Technology. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Echelon Institute of Technology B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
