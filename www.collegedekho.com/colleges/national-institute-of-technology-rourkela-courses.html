NIT Rourkela Courses and Fees 2026

NIT Rourkela provides a wide range of educational opportunities with over 100 programs available at undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels. These programs cover fields such as engineering, sciences, management, architecture, and humanities. Some well-known courses include BTech, MTech, MBA, MSc, BArch, and Integrated MSc. The tuition fees at NIT Rourkela vary depending on the course and category.

NIT Rourkela Courses FAQs

What types of courses does NIT Rourkela offer?

At the UG level, NIT Rourkela offers B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc+MSc, B.Arch , etc, while at the PG level, NIT Rourkela offers M.Tech, MBA, MSc, MA, etc.

Which courses are popular at NIT Rourkela?

The most popular NIT Rourkela courses include the B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc+MSc, B.Arch, M.Tech and MBA programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc+MSc, B.Arch, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MBA, MSc, MA , etc. NIT Rourkela also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does NIT Rourkela accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at NIT Rourkela. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc+MSc, B.Arch and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is NIT Rourkela easy to get into?

Admission in NIT Rourkela courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the NIT Rourkela Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include DASA UG, CCMT, MAT, CCMN, XAT, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, UGC NET, GATE, IIT JAM etc.

What is the top package of NIT Rourkela?

NIT Rourkela highest package recorded in 2023 year is Rs 52.9 Lacs.

Is NIT Rourkela very expensive?

The annual cost of undergraduate programmes is around Rs 11000 for B.Tech. The annual NIT Rourkela fee structure for PG programmes is between Rs 11000 for M.Tech to Rs 600000 for MBA.

What is the annual fee of NIT Rourkela?

The annual NIT Rourkela fees fall between Rs 11000 to Rs 625000. Depending on the degree level, duration, and area of specialisation, the fees differ for various programmes. NIT Rourkela courses at the Ph.D, PG and UG levels are available.Around 87 courses are available under different degree levels; some of the well known ones are B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc+MSc, B.Arch, M.Tech, MBA, and so on.

Does NIT Rourkela offer hostel accommodation?

Yes. Students at NIT Rourkela can apply for hostel accommodation.

What are the popular B.Tech specialisations available at NIT Rourkela?

Chemical Engineering,Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering,Metallurgical and Materials Engineering are the the most popular B.Tech specialisations.

What is NIT Rourkela fees for M.Sc?

NIT Rourkela M.Sc fees is Rs 48,600 per annum.

Does NIT Rourkela offer any diploma course?

NIT Rourkela offers PG Diploma courses at an affordable cost.

What is the eligibility criteria for NIT Rourkela M.Tech course?

To apply for M.Tech, candidates must have scored at least 60-65% in graduation.

What is the most popular course at NITR , Rourkela ?

Engineering courses are the most popular courses opted by students at NITR , Rourkela . Of these, B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) courses are the most sought after amongst both students and parents.
