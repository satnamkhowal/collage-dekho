LPU Scholarship Overview 2026

LPU offers scholarships based on a student’s performance in the LPUNEST exam. There are four scholarship brackets—Category A, B, C and D—based on score cut-offs. Higher scores place students in higher categories with larger fee reductions. All programmes follow the same structure, where each group receives a fixed percentage of scholarship depending on the LPUNEST category achieved. Better scores lead to higher scholarship benefits across all programme groups.

LPU Scholarship

Scholarships at LPU are awarded based on a candidate’s performance in the LPUNEST exam. There are four scholarship brackets, determined by the candidate’s LPUNEST score relative to the cut-offs for Category A, B, C, and D. A higher LPUNEST score places the student in a higher scholarship category. The scholarship percentage for each programme is then applied according to these categories.

LPU RISE Scholarship 2026

Students who rank high in national entrance examinations, have secured admission in the best colleges, or have top ranks in school board examinations, will be awarded 100% programme fee waiver, residential and/or transportation fee support, as per the eligibility criteria and if they maintain a minimum CGPA of 8.0 every year.

LPU RISE Scholarship Application Process 2026

• Application can be submitted at the time of taking admission or after taking admission
• Applications accepted until the last date of admission.
• Include all mandatory or required documents such as
o Application Form
o Valid AIR Rank Card
o Admission Offer Letter and/or Admission Allotment Letter
o Proof of Category (If required)
o Academic Transcripts and ID Proof (if applicable)
o Admission Fee Receipt and/or Enrolment Letter
o Merit List from Education Board
o Undertaking for Continuation of Benefits etc.

Programmes Considered

The applications are accepted in all the programmes except programmes with Tie-up or Work integrated
programmes or any other programme defined by the university.


Selection & Disbursement

• Evaluated by the Admission Verification Team.
• Results communicated within 10-15 working days.
• Scholarship credited to student’s fee account within 10 working days of confirmation by Admission
Verification Team.


Continuation of Benefits Applicable to all categories
• Minimum CGPA 8.0 every year
• Failing to meet conditions will downgrade to general scholarship bracket (as per other best criteria).

Scholarship Guidelines for School Education Board Toppers

  • Applicants must have secured a Top 20 rank in a recognised Indian school board merit list. Eligibility is for students who have passed in the year 2026 and that board exam had a minimum of 1000 candidates.
  • Applicants need to attain a minimum CGPA of 8.0 or 80% marks in marks based programmes every year to continue receiving the scholarship. Scholarship may be withdrawn if the required academic performance is not met. It will be reinstated when the required CGPA or marks is met.

Scholarship Brackets

For each category, there are three brackets of Scholarship (Bracket 1, 2 and 3) that are defined. Each bracket has its own respective criteria that need to be met for availing the applicable scholarship. Therefore, the students meeting the requirement of Bracket- 1 gets the highest Scholarship and those in Bracket - 2 or 3 get a relatively lesser scholarship amount.

Scholarship Phases

Admissions in LPU are done in different phases, which are bifurcations of the admission time period. The amount of scholarship also depends on the phase in which the candidate takes admission to the university. Phase I starts as early as January and continues till the end of April. The subsequent phases, Phase II, III, IV, and V, are conducted only if the seats in the concerned programme remain vacant. The amount of scholarship reduces with each passing phase and no scholarship is offered in Phase V (if conducted).

LPU Scholarship FAQs

Is there any Lovely Professional University scholarship for international students?

Yes, LPU offers scholarships to international students as well. For international applicants, concessions are available up to 50% of the total tuition fee for the entire duration of the course. However, the scholarship is granted only after the verification of the original documents and mark sheets of the candidates.

Is there any Lovely Professional University scholarship test?

Yes, LPU conducts the LPUNEST exam which stands for the Lovely Professional University National Entrance and Scholarship Test each year to allot scholarships to deserving students. Besides scholarships, this test is also used to allot admissions.

How can I get the Lovely Professional University scholarship?

In order to avail the scholarships provided by LPU, candidates are required to clear the Lovely Professional University National Entrance and Scholarship Test (LPUNEST). This exam is used by the university to allocate seats as well as scholarships to students.

How can I avail a scholarship at LPU?

Candidates can apply for scholarships at LPU provided they meet the eligibility criteria for it. If a student falls in any of the categories mentioned for the scholarship scheme at LPU, he/ she can fill the application for the same.

Which national level entrance exams are considered by LPU for giving scholarships?

LPU considers these national and university level competitive entrance exams for providing scholarships: JEE Main (Paper 1 and 2 both), LPUNEST, CEED, NATA, NEET, CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, NMAT, UCEED, CEED, LSAT, CLAT, GATE, GPAT.

For what courses does LPU offer scholarships?

Scholarships at LPU are provided to the students who have enrolled in any of the following courses: B.Tech, B.Arch, MBA, M.Tech, M.Pharm, Ph.D, Design Courses, Law Courses, Medical Courses.

What is the scholarship scheme for B.Tech at LPU?

Students who have more than 90% marks in class 12th/ over 90 percentile in JEE Main/ more than or equal to Category 1 cut off of LPUNEST are given a fee waiver of 50%. Those who score between 80% and 89.9% in class 12th/ have 85 - 90 percentile in JEE Main/ have an LPUNEST score of more than or equal to Category 2 cut off can avail a fee waiver of 40%. A discount of 30% in total fee is given to candidates who have 70% - 79.9% marks in class 12th/ JEE Main score between 80 and 84.99 percentile/ LPUNEST score of more than or equal to Category 3 cut off.

How much fee waiver is offered to students at LPU on the basis of their merit?

LPU gives 50% fee waiver to students who score above 90% marks, 40% fee waiver to those who have aggregate marks between 80% and 89.9%, and 30% fee waiver to candidates whose marks fall in the range of 70% and 79.9%.

Does LPU provide financial assistance to its students?

Yes, LPU has several scholarship schemes that are aimed at supporting the students financially. These scholarships are given on the basis of merit and need.
