About Patel Institute of Management and Technology

Patel Institute of Management & Technology (PIMT), Rajpura is a prominent self-financed institution established in 2004. Located in Rajpura, Punjab, the institute is dedicated to providing quality education in the fields of management, technology, and related areas. Commonly known as PIMT Rajpura, the institute operates under the academic affiliation of Punjabi University, Patiala, ensuring its courses meet university and industry standards.

PIMT is also recognized and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, which affirms its compliance with national norms for technical education. Since its inception, the institute has focused on academic excellence, skill development, and fostering innovation among students. With its commitment to producing competent professionals and a curriculum aligned with current industry needs, PIMT has emerged as a preferred choice for students seeking a career in business and technology.
