About RIG Institute of Hospitality & Management, Rohini Campus

RIG Institute of Hospitality & Management, Rohini Campus is situated in New Delhi in Delhi state of India. Established in 2014, RIG Rohini is a Private college. RIG Institute of Hospitality & Management, Rohini Campus offers 6 courses across 2 streams namely Hotel Management, Science..Popular degrees offered at RIG Institute of Hospitality & Management, Rohini Campus include BA, BSc, Diploma, Certificate..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, RIG Institute of Hospitality & Management, Rohini Campus is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at RIG Institute of Hospitality & Management, Rohini Campus, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at RIG Institute of Hospitality & Management, Rohini Campus is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
