About Amritsar Group of Colleges (ACET), Amritsar

Amritsar Group of Colleges (AGC) is a private institution located in Amritsar, Punjab. Established in 2002, AGC has been recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) and the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an 'A' grade. As per the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) 2025, AGC has been ranked at 201-250 under the engineering category. The campus boasts facilities such as tech-enabled classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, hostels, a gymnasium, and a cafeteria. AGC offers courses at undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, B.Sc. in Agriculture, and MBA. Admissions are based on entrance exams such as JEE Main for B.Tech. and CMAT for MBA. Regarding AGC placements, the highest package offered in 2024 was 42 lakh per annum, and the average package offered was Rs. 5 lakh per annum. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include Wipro, TCS, Infosys, and Byju's.

Why Join Amritsar Group of Colleges?

Here are some reasons why AGC stands out as a top choice for students:

  • Diverse Academic Programs: Offers 73 programs across undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma levels in streams such as engineering, management, agriculture, and computer applications.
  • Experienced Faculty: Faculty members have an average of 10 years of teaching and industry experience.
  • State-of-the-Art Infrastructure: A 12-acre campus with facilities like tech-enabled classrooms, well-equipped labs, hostels, gymnasium, and cafeteria.
  • Strong Placement Record: placement rate of 40%, with the highest package of Rs. 5 lakh per annum.
  • Global Exposure: Collaborations with institutions for research and internships.
  • Holistic Development: Focus on extracurricular activities through various clubs, societies, and events.
