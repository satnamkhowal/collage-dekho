About Christian Medical College (CMC), Vellore

Christian Medical College (CMC) Vellore is a private Christian minority deemed-to-be university for medical education, healthcare, and research founded in 1900 by Dr. Ida Scudder. The institution is located in Vellore, Tamil Nadu, and operates a 3675-bed multi-specialty hospital spread across its Vellore, Ranipet, and Chittoor campuses, providing world-class healthcare to millions annually. 

CMC College is affiliated with The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University. In the NIRF 2025 rankings, CMC Vellore achieved 3rd position in the Medical Category. It is accredited by NAAC with an A+ grade and approved by the National Medical Commission (NMC).

Vellore CMC offers 190+ undergraduate, postgraduate, and super-specialty programs, including MBBS, B.Sc Nursing, BPT, MD, MS, MPH, MPT, MOT, DM/MCh, Fellowship, Diploma, PGD, and many more such programs, all integrating academic rigor with hands-on hospital training. MBBS admission is through NEET UG 2026; Nursing and AHS through CMC's own Computer-Based Test (CBT); Medical PG through NEET PG 2026.

In 2025, placement record at CMC Vellore is outstanding, with 100% placements for the MBBS batch and a median salary of INR 47.69 LPA. Graduates are placed in leading hospitals such as CMC Hospitals, AIIMS, and private healthcare centers across India. The institution also fosters global opportunities for students through collaborations with medical universities abroad.

CMC Vellore Admission 2026

CMC Vellore admission 2026 is open for MBBS, BSc Nursing, 20 AHS Degree programmes, Medical PG (MD/MS/DM/MCh), Nursing PG, Master's, and PhD across all campuses. Applications for Group A and PG Nursing courses are submitted at admissions.cmcvellore.ac.in.

MBBS admission is through NEET UG 2026, followed by TN DME counselling and CMC management selection. BSc Nursing and all AHS courses are admitted through CMC's own Computer-Based Test and personal interviews. Medical PG (MD/MS) admission is through NEET PG 2026, followed by MCC counselling. Higher Specialty (DM/MCh) requires NEET SS. PhD programmes are admitted through CMC's own entrance test and interview.

CMC Vellore Admission  Process 2026

CMC Vellore admission 2026 application process is entirely online at admissions.cmcvellore.ac.in. One application form covers all courses within Group A (MBBS, BSc Nursing, AHS Degree) or Group B (Diploma, PG Diploma, Masters). Follow the steps below to apply.

  • Visit the Official Portal: Go to admissions.cmcvellore.ac.in and click on 'New Registration'.
  • Create an Account: Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to generate login credentials.
  • Fill the Application Form: Log in and complete the application form with personal, academic, and category details. One form covers all Group A courses (MBBS, BSc Nursing, AHS Degree) or all Group B courses (Diploma, PG Diploma, Master's).
  • Upload Documents: Upload scanned copies of Class 10 and 12 marksheets, passport-size photograph, and signature as per specifications.
  • Pay the Application Fee: Pay online via debit card, credit card, or net banking. Fee ranges from INR 100 (AHS Diploma / Fellowship) to INR 1,700 (MBBS / BSc Nursing / AHS Degree).
  • Submit & Save Confirmation: Submit the form and download the confirmation slip. Note the application number for future reference.
  • Send Supporting Documents: Dispatch attested photocopies of all supporting documents to CMC Vellore Admissions Office, Carman Block, Bagayam, Vellore – 632002 by April 4, 2026.
  • Appear for Entrance Exam: Appear for NEET UG 2026 (MBBS), CMC Step 1 CBT (Nursing/AHS), or NEET PG 2026 (Medical PG) as applicable.

CMC Vellore FAQs

What are the popular courses offered at CMC Vellore?

CMC Vellore offers a diverse range of courses across diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels in science, medicine, and surgery. Some of the popular courses include MBBS, BPT, BSc, MD, MS, and MCh. Admissions to these courses are based on entrance exams such as NEET UG, NEET PG, and NEET SS.

Is admission possible at CMC Vellore without NEET?

Admission to the MBBS program at Christian Medical College (CMC) Vellore is contingent upon performance in the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET). CMC Vellore exclusively considers NEET scores for admissions and does not conduct any separate entrance examination.

What is the selection process for B.Sc., BOT, BPT, BPO, and PG diploma programs at CMC Vellore?

Selection for B.Sc., BOT, BPT, BPO, and PG diploma programs at CMC Vellore involves a Computer-Based Test (CBT) comprising 25 questions from physics, chemistry, or mathematics, and 15 general knowledge questions. Shortlisted candidates are then called for a personal interview.

What are the entrance exams required for PG courses like MD and MCh at CMC Vellore?

For PG courses such as MD and MCh at CMC Vellore, candidates need to qualify for NEET PG and NEET SS, respectively. Additionally, applicants must meet specific eligibility criteria related to their bachelor's degree in medicine or surgery from a recognized university.

How can I apply for MBBS admission at CMC Vellore in 2024?

To apply for MBBS admission at CMC Vellore in 2024, candidates must have passed class 10+2 with a minimum of 50% marks and qualified in NEET UG. The application process involves submitting an online application by the deadline, through the official website along with the required documents and payment.

When will the NEET UG 2024 cutoff for MBBS at CMC Vellore be announced?

The NEET UG 2024 cutoff for MBBS at CMC Vellore after the release of NEET UG 2024 results. The NEET UG retest was held on June 23, 2024 while the results were officially declared by NTA on June 30, 2024. The NEET UG 2024 cutoff will be declared soon.

How does CMC Vellore rank among medical colleges in India?

CMC Vellore consistently ranks highly among medical colleges in India. In recent rankings by NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework), CMC Vellore secured the 3rd position in 2021, 2022, and 2023, affirming its excellence in medical education and research.

What facilities does CMC Vellore offer on its campus?

CMC Vellore's 19-acre campus features state-of-the-art labs, a modern gym, Wi-Fi throughout, serene gardens, and multiple canteens. It includes dedicated hostels for different courses and 24/7 medical services.

Are scholarships available for students facing financial challenges at CMC Vellore?

Yes, CMC Vellore offers scholarships across undergraduate programs like Medicine, Nursing, and Allied Health Sciences, based on financial need. These scholarships can cover full or partial tuition fees and living expenses. Additionally, students can apply for scholarships based on academic merit through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). The institution also provides financial aid to economically disadvantaged students, including the Prime Minister's Scholarship.

How can I apply for scholarships at CMC Vellore?

Students can apply for scholarships through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). Detailed instructions on how to apply can be found on the NSP website.

What types of scholarships does CMC Vellore offer?

CMC Vellore offers various scholarships primarily based on academic performance and financial need. Some notable scholarships include the Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme for RPF/RPSF, the PG Indira Gandhi Scholarship for Single Girl Child, and the Pragati Scholarship Scheme for Girl Students (Technical Degree).

Are NEET scores required for all medical courses at CMC Vellore?

Yes, valid NEET UG, PG, and NEET SS scores are mandatory for enrolling in undergraduate and postgraduate medical courses at CMC Vellore.

What is the selection process of CMC Vellore for the MPH program?

Candidates will be selected on the basis of their scores in the entrance examination followed by a personal interview.

What is the application fee of CMC Vellore?

The application fee of CMC Vellore for MBBS, MPH and MSc, BSc (Nursing) & AHS Degree Courses is INR 500, for Diploma in Nursing and other AHS diplomas / Fellowships is INR 100 and for Post Basic Diploma in Nursing is INR 400.
