SRM University Chennai Admission 2026: Courses, Eligibility, Application Form, Last Date

SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST), formerly SRM University, established in 1985 in Kattankulathur, Chennai, is a private deemed university accredited with an A++ grade by NAAC and approved by UGC, AICTE, and other bodies like NMC, DCI, and COA. Ranked 12th in the University category and 24th in Engineering by NIRF 2024, and 1001–1200 by QS World University Rankings 2025, SRMIST spans six campuses (Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Tiruchirappalli, Delhi-NCR, Sonepat) and serves 50,000+ students. It offers over 80 Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), Doctoral, Diploma, and Distance Education programs across Engineering, Management, Medicine, Science, Humanities, Law, and Agriculture through eight faculties. Admissions are primarily entrance-based via SRM-conducted exams (SRMJEEE, SRMJEEM, SRMJEEH) or national exams (JEE Main, NEET, CAT, NATA, CLAT). Applications are online at applications.srmist.edu.in. Below are the details for SRMIST Chennai Admission 2025, including eligibility, selection criteria, application process, and key dates.

SRM University Chennai Admission FAQs

What is the SRM Chennai application process 2024?

The SRM University application process 2024 includes the brief steps mentioned below. Step 1: Visit the SRM University's official portal. Step 2: On the homepage, click the "Admissions" tab. Step 3: Scroll down and hit the “Apply Now” tab based on preference. Step 4: Register yourself and note down the login credentials. Step 5: Fill out the SRM IST application form by logging in

Is an entrance exam required for SRM Chennai admission for PG courses?

Yes, an entrance exam is required for SRM University admission for PG courses, however, there may be a few exceptions like LLM Criminal Law and Criminal Justice which require no particular eligibility. Following this, candidates may be required to provide experience certificates for courses like an MBA or participate in GDPI rounds.

Is an entrance exam required for SRM Chennai admission for UG courses?

Yes, an entrance exam is required for SRM University admission for UG courses in most cases, however, certain programmes have direct entry requirements based on the Class 12 results. Following this, students may be required to participate in personal interviews.

What is the step-wise process for SRM University admission 2024?

The step-wise process for SRM Chennai admission 2024 includes the following: Step 1: Visit the official website of the university and read the SRM Univeristy Chennai admissions guidelines cautiously. Step 2: Register by filling out personal information and a valid email address. Step 3: Apply for admission to your preferred course by paying the registration fees. Step 4: Submit your application and take a printout for future reference.
