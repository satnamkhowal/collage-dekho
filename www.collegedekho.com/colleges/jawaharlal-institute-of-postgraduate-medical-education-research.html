About JIPMER Puducherry

Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education & Research, also known as JIPMER, is a government college established in the year 1956. Located in Puducherry, the college is an Institute of National Importance. The institute is recognized by NMC (National Medical Commission). JIPMER is one of the top medical colleges in India.

JIPMER offers a total of 50+ courses at the UG, PG, and Doctoral levels. The institute offers MBBS, BSc, and other allied health sciences courses at the undergraduate level and MD, MS, DM, MCh, and MSc at the PG level. The college provides admission on the basis of entrance exam scores. The entrance exams required for admission to JIPMER include NEET UG for MBBS and NEET PG for MD/MS/DM/MCh.

JIPMER is a leading medical institution in India, known for its quality education, research, and patient care. Its campus has modern facilities and infrastructure including well-equipped labs, a library, hostels, advanced research facilities, and hospitals etc. for the holistic development of the students and patient care.
