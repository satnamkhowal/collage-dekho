About SRM University

SRM University Andhra Pradesh is a top private university in Neerukonda, Guntur, offering a highly research-focused and industry-aligned learning environment. Established in 2017, SRM University Andhra Pradesh hosts 10,950 students across 50+ UG and PG programs, supported by 490 experienced faculty members and a student–faculty ratio of 22:1. The university is known for its interdisciplinary approach, combining engineering, sciences, and liberal arts, backed by strong research output with 3,500+ publications, 70 Nature Index entries, 60 granted patents, and ₹37.47 crore in funded projects. Its 190-acre smart campus includes advanced AI and VLSI labs, sustainable-tech centres, high-end research facilities, and hostels for 5,000+ students, along with 530+ research scholars driving innovation. SRM AP holds NAAC A++ accreditation, NBA approval for key BTech branches, AICTE approval, and ARIIA recognition for innovation and entrepreneurship. With 35% female enrollment and international collaborations with 20+ global universities, such as the University of Wisconsin, the university offers strong diversity and global exposure. Its solar-powered systems, zero-waste operations, and UN-SDG-aligned sustainability practices further enhance its appeal, making SRM University Andhra Pradesh a preferred choice for students seeking modern, research-intensive education within Andhra Pradesh.

SRM University Andhra Pradesh Admission Process 2026

SRM University Andhra Pradesh opens BTech admissions through the SRMJEEE entrance exam and also accepts AP EAPCET scores. Candidates must score at least 60% in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics to be eligible. The university conducts its admission rounds in multiple phases, followed by merit lists, counselling, and final seat allotment. Academic sessions begin soon after the completion of the admission process.

  • Apply Online: Submit at applications.srmap.edu.in; upload documents.
  • Entrance Exam: SRMJEEE for all-India quota (30% seats); AP EAPCET for state (70%).
  • Counselling: Choice filling post-results; document verification at Guntur campus.

Is there scholarship on merit basis in SRM University, Ghaziabad?

P
Prateek Lakhera •  Jul-21-2025

Dear student,

Yes, SRM University, Ghaziabad, offers academic scholarships to meritorious students who perform exceptionally well in SRMJEEE, JEE Main, or the Class 12th board examinations. To be eligible for a scholarship, students must meet specific criteria such as securing a rank within the top 100 in the SRMJEEE 2025 exam, achieving a rank within the top 20,000 in the JEE Main 2025, or being the state topper in the HSC examination conducted by their respective state board or CBSE within India. Additionally, SRM University provides performance based scholarships for students in the SRMJEEE 2025 exam. Generally, students are eligible for a 100% scholarship if they secure a rank between 24 to 68 in Phase 1, 15 to 44 in Phase 2, and 09 to 23 in Phase 3 of the SRMJEEE 2025. The university also offers various other SRM University Ghaziabad Scholarships 2025 based on different criteria, such as the Founder’s Scholarship, Socio-Economic Scholarship, Unnat Bharat Abhiyan Scholarship, Differently-Abled Scholarship, Employee Ward Scholarship (EWS), and the Alumni Scholarship etc.
