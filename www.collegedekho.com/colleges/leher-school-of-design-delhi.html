About Leher School of Design Delhi (LSOD)

Leher School of Design (LSOD), formerly known as LISAA School of Design, is a private design institute established in 2011. It is located at Okhla Phase II, South Delhi, New Delhi. The institute operates under PJVS Edu Ventures Private Limited and holds ISO certification. It has an industrial collaboration with Swami Vivekanand Subharti University (SVSU), Meerut, through which UG degrees are awarded.

LSOD offers 11 design programmes across Diploma, Advanced Diploma, and undergraduate levels in Fashion Design, Textile Design, Interior Design, Graphics & Web Design, and Animation & Multimedia Design. Diploma programmes are 1 year, Advanced Diploma programmes are 2 years, B.Sc programmes are 3 years, and BFA/B.Des is 4 years. Annual fees are INR 1,30,000 for Diploma programmes and INR 2,00,000 for degree programmes.

The campus spans 8,000 square feet and has 15 faculty members across Fashion, Interior, and Graphic Design departments. Campus facilities include two Interior Design classrooms, two Fashion Design classrooms, a Graphics & Web computer lab, a CAD room, a stitching room, a foundation studio, an outdoor class space, and organised marketing visits to fabric markets and industry setups.

Between 2013 and 2023, LSOD placed 5,400 out of 6,000 registered students, a 90% placement rate. A total of 5,800 offers were made. Over 650 companies participated in campus recruitment. Monthly salaries range from INR 35,000 to INR 2,50,000, with an average of INR 40,000.

LSOD has received two awards: Best Design School in Delhi by Times Group in 2013, and Best Graphic & Web Design School by Education Times of India in 2015. Merit-based scholarships are available for UG applicants based on entrance test performance, personal interview, and family income below INR 4,00,000 per year.

LISAA School of Design Admission 2026

LSOD Delhi accepts applications for 2026 admissions both online via its official website and offline at the admission cell. The application fee is Rs 1,000. Required documents include marksheets, photo ID, photograph, character certificate, and transfer certificate where applicable.

Leher School of Design Delhi Application Process 2026

Below are provided with the details of the online and offline application process for Leher School of Design admissions:

Online Admission Process

  • Candidates who want to secure admission at the LSOD can apply for the same through their official website. 
  • Candidates would be required to create an account first and then fill in all the mandatory information. 
  • Once done the candidate would have access to the application form which they need to fill out and attach all the mandatory documents. 
  • While submitting the form the candidate needs to pay a nominal fee of Rs 1000 which can be paid via credit/debit cards or through UPI as well. 
  • Once submitted the candidate would receive an acknowledgement mail as well.

Offline Admission Process

  • Candidates who are seeking admission at LSOD can also take admission via offline mode by visiting the admission cell directly. 
  • The candidate would be given an admission form which they need to fill out with all the required details. 
  • Candidates can clear any questions or doubts they have about a specific course at the admission cell itself. 
  • While submitting the form the candidate needs to pay the LSOD application fee of Rs 1000 which can be paid via credit/debit cards, net banking or through UPI as well. 
  • Once submitted the admission cell would give a receipt of acknowledgement to the candidate.

LSOD Delhi Admission Required Documents 2026

Keep the following documents ready before filling the application form:

  • Class 10 Marksheet (for Diploma/Advanced Diploma applicants)
  • Class 10 and Class 12 Marksheets (for UG applicants)
  • Photo ID — Aadhaar Card or Passport (self-attested copy)
  • Passport-size photograph (recent, coloured, white background)
  • Character Certificate from the last institution attended
  • Portfolio of creative work (recommended, not mandatory for fresh applicants; mandatory for lateral entry)
  • Migration/Transfer Certificate (for students from other boards)

Leher School of Design Delhi Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

LSOD provides dedicated classrooms for Interior and Fashion Design, a Graphics & Web computer lab, CAD room, stitching room, foundation studio, outdoor learning space, and organised marketing visits to fabric markets and industry setups for hands-on exposure.

Interior Design Classroom (2 Rooms)

  • Two dedicated classrooms equipped with drawing boards, material samples, and display walls for interior design students to develop spatial planning and design concepts.
  • Each room supports hands-on learning in space planning, colour theory, and furniture layout, giving students a studio environment to work on real-scale design projects.
  • Students use these rooms to prepare design presentations, mood boards, and model mock-ups as part of their coursework and portfolio development.
  • The classrooms also serve as a space for design critiques, group discussions, and faculty-led reviews to sharpen students' design thinking and communication skills.

Fashion Design Classroom (2 Rooms)

  • Two well-structured fashion design classrooms used for theory sessions, fashion illustration, garment sketching, and trend analysis by fashion design students.
  • The rooms are set up to support lessons in fashion history, textile study, design principles, and collection development as part of the curriculum.
  • Students use these classrooms to work on their fashion illustration portfolios, croqui drawings, and design boards under the guidance of faculty.
  • Regular fashion critique sessions and presentations are held in these rooms, helping students build confidence in communicating their design concepts professionally.

Graphics & Web Computer Room

  • A computer lab equipped with systems loaded with graphic design software including Adobe Photoshop, CorelDRAW, and Illustrator for digital design work.
  • Students use this room for building digital imaging skills, creating layouts, developing textile prints, and learning web-based design tools.
  • The lab supports coursework in fashion communication, digital branding, social media content creation, and visual merchandising.
  • Supervised lab sessions ensure students gain practical software skills that are directly applicable to the fashion and design industry.

CAD Room

  • A Computer-Aided Design (CAD) room where students learn to create precise technical drawings, pattern layouts, and garment specifications digitally.
  • The facility supports training in computer-aided pattern making, which is a standard skill required in the apparel and textile manufacturing industry.
  • Students in UG Diploma programmes use the CAD room as part of their curriculum to develop technical design skills alongside hand-drawing techniques.
  • The CAD room bridges traditional design methods with modern industry tools, preparing students for roles in apparel production and fashion technology.

Stitching Room

  • A practical stitching room fitted with sewing machines where students learn garment construction, basic stitching techniques, and pattern assembly.
  • Students work on making different garments as part of their curriculum, including basic bodice, skirts, men's wear, and kids' wear constructions.
  • The room allows students to move from flat pattern making to actual garment assembly, building technical skills in fabric handling and finishing.
  • Faculty provide hands-on supervision during stitching sessions, ensuring students meet garment quality standards required by the industry.

Foundation Room

  • A foundation studio used for introductory design sessions covering the basics of design, elements and principles, composition, and colour theory.
  • New students begin their design journey in this room, developing fundamental drawing, sketching, and conceptual design skills before moving to specialised areas.
  • The room supports foundation-level exercises in pattern observation, free-hand drawing, and design development to build a strong creative base.
  • Both fashion and interior design students use this room during the early stages of their programme to establish core design competencies.

Outdoor Class

  • An outdoor learning space used for open-air design sessions, observation exercises, and creative activities that benefit from natural light and surroundings.
  • Students engage in sketch walks, material observation, and design inspiration sessions in the outdoor class, encouraging creativity beyond the four walls of a studio.
  • The space is also used for informal group discussions, fashion shoots, and portfolio photography sessions in a natural setting.
  • Outdoor classes give students exposure to real-world environments, which helps in developing contextual and environment-responsive design thinking.

Marketing Visit for Fashion & Interior Design Students

  • Organised industry and market visits are conducted for fashion and interior design students to expose them to live trade environments, fabric markets, and showrooms.
  • Students visit key markets and industry setups to study current trends, sourcing practices, retail display techniques, and product merchandising in person.
  • These visits are a structured part of the curriculum and help students connect classroom learning with real-world business and design practices.
  • Marketing visits build industry awareness and professional networks, giving students a firsthand understanding of how design, trade, and retail operate together.

Why Join LSOD – Leher School of Design?

LSOD (Leher School of Design), formerly known as Lisaa School of Design, is a Delhi-based premier design institute established in 2011 under PJVS Edu Ventures Private Limited. Over 14 years of design education, LSOD has built a curriculum that blends India's rich cultural heritage with contemporary design thinking — preparing students to become industry-ready professionals across multiple design disciplines.

LSOD believes in empowering students not just for creative innovation, but also for environmental sustainability and responsible design. By integrating tradition with modern pedagogy, LSOD provides students a strong foundation that respects the wisdom of the past while shaping the future of design.

Here are some key reasons students choose LSOD for their design education:

  • Industry-Relevant Curriculum — Courses designed in line with current industry demands and emerging design trends
  • Job-Oriented Courses — Focused on building skills that directly translate to professional roles
  • Personalised Curriculum — Learning pathways tailored to individual student strengths and goals
  • 100% Placement Assistance — Dedicated support to help every student secure a placement opportunity
  • Adaptive Creation Labs — Hands-on studio and lab environment built for experimental and project-based work
  • One-on-One Mentoring — Direct guidance from experienced faculty for personalised academic growth
  • Student-Centric Approach — Teaching methodology built around student needs and creative development
  • Well-Connected Location — Conveniently located in Delhi with easy access via public transport
  • Inclusive Education — A welcoming environment that supports diverse learners
  • Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) — Curriculum actively aligned with global sustainability and responsible design practices
  • Project-Based Learning — Real-world projects integrated throughout the programme for practical exposure
