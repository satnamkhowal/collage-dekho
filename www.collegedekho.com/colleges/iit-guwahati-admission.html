IIT Guwahati Admission 2026: Courses, Eligibility, Fees & Selection Process

IIT Guwahati offers UG, PG, and PhD programs through national exams like JEE Advanced, UCEED, GATE, CEED, JAM, and CAT. Students must meet Class 12 or graduation marks criteria and then participate in JoSAA, COAP, or institute-level counseling. Most courses require tests plus interviews for final selection. Programs range from B.Tech and B.Des to M.Tech, MBA, MS(R), and PhD across engineering, science, design, and management streams.

IIT Guwahati B.Tech Admission 2026

IIT Guwahati offers a full-time B.Tech for 4 years in 13 specializations, including Computer Science, Electronics & Communication, Mechanical, Civil, and Chemical Engineering. Admission is based on JEE Advanced scores. Eligibility requires 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, securing 75% marks (65% for SC/ST/PwD) or top 20 percentile in board exams.

Frequently Asked Questions – IIT Guwahati Admission 2026

What is the fee range for IIT Guwahati courses?
Annual fees vary by program: around INR 1.69 lakh for B.Tech/B.Des/BS, INR 84k for M.Tech/M.Des/MS(R), INR 74k for M.Sc/MA, INR 2.94 lakh for MBA, and INR 94k for Ph.D., with lower fees for SC/ST/PwD students.

IIT Guwahati accepts national-level exams like JEE Advanced for B.Tech and BS, UCEED for B.Des, GATE for M.Tech and MS(R), CEED for M.Des, JAM for M.Sc, and CAT for MBA. Some courses also include interviews or institute-level tests.

How is the selection process completed at IIT Guwahati?
IIT Guwahati’s final selection depends on exam scores plus interviews or counseling rounds. UG programs use JoSAA counseling after JEE Advanced/UCEED, MTech uses COAP, and MBA uses CAT followed by GD/PI. Research programs have written tests and interviews for final selection.
Does IIT Guwahati conduct its own tests for some courses?
Yes, IIT Guwahati conducts institute-level written tests and interviews for programs like MA, MDes, MS(R), and PhD. These are held after the initial shortlisting based on national exam scores wherever applicable.
What entrance exams are needed for different courses at IIT Guwahati?
IIT Guwahati uses national exams like JEE Advanced for BTech and BS, UCEED for BDes, GATE for MTech and MS(R), CEED for MDes, IIT JAM for MSc, and CAT for MBA. PhD and Dual Degree programs require GATE/NET scores along with a written test and interview.

IIT Guwahati 2026 Document Verification FAQs

When and how does IIT Guwahati verify the submitted documents?
IIT Guwahati first checks documents during online JoSAA or COAP registration, but final verification happens on campus after admission. Students must carry original certificates for confirmation. If any document is missing, unclear, or mismatched with the online uploads, the institute can cancel the seat. So applicants should ensure all documents are correct and updated.
Are category and EWS certificates mandatory for IIT Guwahati admission?
Yes, IIT Guwahati requires valid category certificates for SC, ST, and OBC-NCL students, following the JoSAA format. EWS applicants must upload an income certificate issued for the current financial year. PwD candidates must submit the official PwD certificate. Incorrect or outdated certificates may lead to rejection during the verification stage.
What documents are required for IIT Guwahati admissions 2026?
IIT Guwahati requires students to upload key documents like JEE Advanced admit card, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, category certificates, EWS certificate, PwD certificate, photo, and signature during JoSAA or COAP registration. Additional documents such as domicile or gap certificates may be asked if needed. All these documents help verify a student’s eligibility for admission.
