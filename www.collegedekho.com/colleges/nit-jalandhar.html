About NIT Jalandhar

Dr B R Ambedkar National Institute of Technology was established in the year 1987 as Regional Engineering College and was given the status of National Institute of Technology (Deemed University) by the Government of India on October 17, 2002, under the aegis of Ministry of Education, New Delhi. The Ministry of Education, Government of India declared the Institute as “Institute of National Importance” under the Parliament Act 2007. A large number of reputed Industrial companies in the country visit the Institution and select the final year students as Engineers/ Management Trainees.

National Institute of Technology (NIT) Jalandhar has achieved an NIRF rank of 55 in the engineering category with a score of 53.38.The institute offers 50+ courses in 12 streams at UG, PG and doctoral levels. Admission to NIT Jalandhar courses are done based on entrance exam scores. JEE Main, GATE, JAM etc. scores are considered for admission in courses offered by NIT Jalandhar. The institution has a placement cell that controls the placement process. During NIT Jalandhar placement for the year 2024, the highest salary package offered was Rs 64 LPA.

NIT Jalandhar Courses and Admission 2026

NIT Jalandhar is offering courses like Under Graduation, Post Graduation, MBA, Ph.D., M.Sc., and many more. The fee for each course is different from one another. In the discipline of Humanities and Management, NIT Jalandhar courses offers a two-year full-time MBA degree. Admission to NIT Jalandhar's MBA program is determined by CATMAT/GMAT/CMAT scores, followed by a Group Discussion and Personal Interview. If seats remain unfilled, the institute holds its own entrance exam, which is followed by a GD and PI round. A one-year PG Diploma in Management is offered at NIT Jalandhar. The institute also provides a Postgraduate Diploma in Engineering.

NIT Jalandhar admission in BTech courses is based on the JEE Main score, followed by JoSAA Counseling. The JoSAA counselling for 2025 has already started with the first round of allocation being already done. The GATE score is accepted for admission to NIT Jalandhar's MTech program, which is followed by CCMT Counseling. GATE exam registration for 2026 is all set to begin in August,2025. The IIT JAM score is used to determine NIT Jalandhar MSc admissions in 2026, followed by CCMN Counseling. It is a full-time two-year curriculum that comprises core courses, electives, and project work. The GATE/UGC NET score is used to determine NIT Jalandhar Ph.D. admissions, followed by a "presentation and interaction" process.

Candidates having a BTech/BS/BE degree from a centrally supported technical institute with a minimum of 75 percent aggregate or a CGPA of 8.0 or better on a 10-point scale and a valid GATE score are also eligible for direct admission to the Ph.D. program. As it is a government university, NIT Jalandhar's fees are lower than those at other universities, allowing students from low-income families to attend. Course prices at the National Institute of Technology Jalandhar vary depending on the course. The table below contains all NITJ courses, along with their qualifying requirements and fees.

NIT Jalandhar Placements

NIT Jalandhar students were offered a total of 974 offers in the placement session of 2024. A total of 814 students were placed by these recruiters across various branches. The highest package offered in the 2024 cycle was 54 Lacs for BTech students and 32 Lacs for MTech students while the average package stood at 9.78 Lacs. The overall placement rate stood at 86.31%. Three companies extended internship offers to students during the summer internship placement drive at the NIT Jalandhar campus. Internships were offered to more than 29 students. The highest monthly stipend was INR 80,000. Microsoft made it available. The lowest monthly stipend was INR 40,000. Recruiting firms such as Microsoft, American Express, Oracle, Sabre Corporation, and others took part in the 2024 placement drive.

Students' applications are submitted to firms in order to be chosen for the interview process, and companies give different amounts or recruitment packages depending on the nature of the job. Students are given job offers along with information about their start dates. In addition, the placement service of the National Institute of Technology Jalandhar assists students in obtaining implant training and internship opportunities in various organizations.

The best offer made to NIT Jalandhar students during the 2024 placement drive was INR 54 LPA, which was the biggest offer ever. This year’s placement reports are yet to be published by the institute. NIT Jalandhar FAQs

How do I apply for NIT Jalandhar?

A candidate seeking admission to NIT Jalandhar can apply for various programmes by visiting the institute's official website and registering on the application portal.

Which B.Tech branch had the most students placed at NIT Jalandhar?

The CSE branch had the most students (around 79), followed by ECE (74) and ME (72). (70).

What services are available to students at NIT Jalandhar?

NIT Jalandhar provides its students with the following services: bus transportation, dispensaries, hostels, post offices, banks, canteens, a central seminar hall, a gymnasium hall, an open-air theatre, and a shopping centre that sells basic necessities.

How many students were placed at NIT Jalandhar last year, and who were the top recruiters?

In the previous year's NIT Jalandhar placement drive, 472 students were placed. Adobe, Microsoft, Vedanta Group, Capgemini Solutions, Trident Group, and others were among the major recruiters.

What is the procedure for taking entrance exams at NIT Jalandhar?

Entrance exams are followed by counselling sessions. The counselling sessions assist candidates in understanding the benefits of learning and preparing at NIT Jalandhar.

What are the admission requirements for the B Tech programme at NIT Jalandhar?

Candidates for the NIT Jalandhar B.Tech programme must have passed 10+2 with at least 60% marks and must have studied Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry in 10+2.

What is the B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering programme like at NIT Jalandhar?

NIT Jalandhar had 100% placements in its B.Tech Mechanical Engineering branch in 2019. The program's average package was INR 6 LPA, with the highest package ranging from INR 10 to 11.4 LPA.

Which branch at NIT Jalandhar B.Tech is the best?

NIT Jalandhar B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering is regarded as the best in terms of infrastructure, placement statistics, and faculty.

Is there a public transportation system at NIT Jalandhar?

Yes. The institute provides bus transportation for students to and from campus. There are two buses that serve both students and faculty members, and they run twice a day.

Is there a hostel available at NIT Jalandhar?

Yes, NIT Jalandhar provides accommodation for its students, with 10 hostels for boys and 3 hostels for girls, all of which have a mess facility and different occupancy rooms.
