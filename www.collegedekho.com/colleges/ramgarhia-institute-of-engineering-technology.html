About RAMGARHIA INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY

The Ramgarhia Group of Institutes (Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology, Ramgarhia Institute of Management & Advanced Studies, Ramgarhia Institute of Health Science & Research, and Ramgarhia Institute of Nanny Care) has the vision to build truly world-class institutes that will serve the needs of Indian and international industry, research, and educational institutions. Since its establishment in 2003, the Ramgarhia Group of Institutes has developed enormously and is well renowned in the region for providing high-quality education.

RMIT Phagwara offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in Engineering, Management, Computer Applications, Fashion Technology, Hotel Management, Beauty Therapy & Aesthetics, and Nursing available at these institutions. These institutes are connected with IKG-Punjab Technical University Jalandhar, Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi, and Punjab Nurses Registration Council, Mohali, and are approved by the All-India Council for Technical Education / UGC / Punjab Government.

Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology admission is offered to a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses. Admission to the programme is based on both merit and entrance examinations. Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology accepts applications online. Admission to B. Tech programmes is based on JEE Mains scores, GMAT scores for M. tech programmes, and CMAT scores for MBA programmes, while Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology admission to other programmes is based on merit. Eligibility criteria and fees for different courses differ. 

Steps to apply 

Before applying to Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology, make sure you meet the eligibility requirements. For admission to RIET, candidates should follow the steps outlined below:

  • Step 1 - Visit the Institute's official website.
  • Step 2 - On the webpage, click the RIET Admission Form.
  • Step 3 - Fill in all of the required fields in the form.
  • Step 4 - Pay the application fee and click the submit button.
  • Step 4 - Keep a printout of the form with you for future reference.

Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology Placements

There are some top companies that visit Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology placements every year. As per RIET placement statistics, the highest package offered to students is Rs 10 LPA. Meanwhile, the average and lowest package for the college is Rs 5.5 LPA and Rs 3.4 LPA respectively.

The Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology placement system is of excellent quality, and all of the students were placed with well-known organisations such as Airtel, Justdial, HDFC, JARO Education, and many others. The placement is managed by RIET placement cell which manages the recruitment and preparation of students through placement prep classes, resume building training, soft skill training, etc.

RAMGARHIA INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

On the major railway line, Phagwara is situated in the centre of Punjab's Doaba region. Both the Railway Station and the Bus Stop are around 1 km apart from Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology. It is set on 30 acres of beautiful green terrain with verdant fields all around, giving a pollution-free learning environment. Ramgarhia Institute of Engineering and Technology campus is located on a major highway with transportation available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. It's easy to get to from the airport (Amritsar-116 Km, Adampur-32 Km and Ludhiana-51 Km) 

On the college site, there are separate dorms for boys and females with amenities such as indoor games, reading rooms, and a gym. Pure vegetarian food is supplied to cater to the tastes of students from all across India.
