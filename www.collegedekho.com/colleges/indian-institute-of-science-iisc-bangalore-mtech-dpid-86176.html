About IISC Bangalore M.Tech

IISC Bangalore M.Tech is a 2 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a Master of Technology (M.Tech.) degree at the prestigious Indian Institute of Science (IISC) in Bengaluru, candidates must meet specific eligibility criteria. Applicants should hold a Bachelor's degree in Engineering/Technology or equivalent from a recognized university or institution. Additionally, they should have a valid GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) score. The GATE score requirement may vary based on the specialization chosen. Candidates from reserved categories may also be required to meet specific criteria as per government regulations. Meeting these eligibility requirements is crucial for aspiring students to secure admission to the M.Tech. program at IISC Bangalore. The annual fee for M.Tech is INR 9,000 - 8,81,000. IISC Bangalore M.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the M.Tech at IISC Bangalore is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the IISC Bangalore M.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

IISC Bangalore M.Tech Application Process

IISC Bangalore M.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the M.Tech course at IISC Bangalore have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill IISC Bangalore M.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of IISC Bangalore and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of IISC Bangalore. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the IISC Bangalore M.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the IISC Bangalore M.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of IISC Bangalore M.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for IISC Bangalore M.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

IISc : the premier research institute of India"

Overall Admission to the B. Sc. program at IISc Bangalore takes place via 4 modes: JEE Mains, JEE Advanced, IAT and NEET. Till 2021 there was a KVPY mode of entry but it was replaced by IAT (IISER Aptitude test) since last year. Cutoffs for different categories via different exams canbe checked at official website of the UG department of IISC: bs-ug.iisc.ac.in. The only eligibility criterias are to successfully pass class 12 examinations from a recognised board and to clear the cutoff of any of the 4 exams mentioned above in your category. Applications for the program are to filled at the official webiste in april-may every year. If you clear the cutoff then the institute will reach you on your official email-ID with a provisional offer letter and you will be invited for an in-person counselling session to the Bangalore campus in June. There are 3 rounds of counselling, and at the in person counselling you are given all the information about the program, fees and future prospects.

Infrastructure The college has a very vibrant social life. There are 2 fests held across the year. The first fest is RHAPSODY, the annual fest of IISc held inseptember of every year. It includes several concert Apart from that, several smaller libraries like Ranade library are found in departments. There are several music, dance and educational clubs on campus which regularly host events and competitions. There are regular club meetings and academic excursions which make laerning fun.

Faculty The internship opportunities are open second year onwards and are very easy to obtain. Two types of internships are available: industrial and academic. Academic internships involve working under a professor with his team on their project over the course of a summer break. For this the student can approach any professors on campus, or even at other universities in India or abroad. However, these internships generally do not involve any stipends. Industrial internships are provided by the campus placement cell and generally involve a stipend depending on the job you get. Several companies come on campus to take interns for industrial research training.

Hostel The hostel facilities provided by the campus are top notch. The hostels have been divided into blocks, with UG students being assigned E block, U block and S block. The rooms are well equipped with beds, fans, cupboards, and lights. The rooms in E block are twin sharing, while single occupancy rooms are available in U & S block. Room allotment is random. The hostels are assigned on the first day of coming to campus and are very cheap at 5000 per month. The hostels are located at a central location of the campus so that classrooms are nearby. The food provided at the campus is ofexcellent quality. There are 5 messes A and B be North Indian veg and North Indian non veg, C and D being South Indian veg and South Indian nonveg,and E mess providing a composite diet. The menus are decided by a mess committee elected from the students by the students. 4 meals are provided everyday, breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner. Special items are made on festivals, dessert and beverages are there.
