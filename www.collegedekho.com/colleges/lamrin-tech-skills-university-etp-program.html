Lamrin Tech Skills University Job Ready Degree

Lamrin Tech Skills University offers numerous new age Job Ready courses. These courses are much more focused on building a career for the students, rather than adding one more degree to your educational qualifications. There are a total of 2 assured courses offered by the college. Both the courses are available at the undergraduate & postgraduate levels. These courses are designed to help you learn the required skills and acquire knowledge which will make you job-ready when you graduate. The Job Ready “Assured” courses at Lamrin Tech Skills University such as BCA in Cloud Computing/Cyber Security (IXP) and MCA in Cloud Computing/Cyber Security (IXP) are offered at an annual fee which ranges from Rs 50,000 to Rs 75,000. Admission to BCA is offered based on the merit of candidates in the last qualifying exam i.e. class 12. And for admission to MCA course, candidates must have a BCA, B.Sc CS or B.Sc IT degree from a recognised university with 50% marks (at least).

Lamrin Tech Skills University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

To become eligible for the Job Ready “Assured” courses offered by Lamrin Tech Skills University, interested candidates must have to meet the merit requirements as the admission process is direct. Interested students must fill out the application form and pay the application fee with all the required documents to get admission to their chosen course.
