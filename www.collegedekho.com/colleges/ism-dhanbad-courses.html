IIT Dhanbad Courses and Fees 2026

Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Dhanbad, formerly known as ISM Dhanbad, offers a diverse range of programs at Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Doctoral levels. The institute is renowned for its focus on engineering and technology education. At the UG level, IIT Dhanbad offers popular courses like B.Tech and Integrated B.Tech + M.Tech. At the PG level, it offers courses such as M.Tech, MBA, and M.Sc..

The annual fees at IIT Dhanbad range from approximately ₹60,000 to ₹8.3 lakh, depending on the type and level of the course.

The results of any free IIT JAM mock test you attempted will usually be accessible on the same website or platform where you took the test. You can check your results by doing the following:

  • Check the platform or website:

Return to the website where the mock exam was held if it was one of such sites (such as the platform of a coaching centre or an educational website). Use the login information you used to register to access your account, if you have one. Look for a section that says "My Results," "Test History," or "Dashboard," since these might include your mock test results and feedback.

  • Instant Results:

Results from a lot of free practice exams are available right away. If so, see if your score appeared on the platform immediately after you finished the test.

  • Notification via Email:

After you finish the test, some platforms send you an email with your findings. Look for any correspondence on the outcomes of your practice exam in your inbox (and spam folder).

  • Speak with Support:

You can get in touch with the helpdesk or support staff of the website or platform where you took the test if you are unable to locate your results. They ought to be able to help you find your score.

IIT Dhanbad Courses FAQs

What types of courses does IIT Dhanbad offer?

At the UG level, IIT Dhanbad offers B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech , etc, while at the PG level, IIT Dhanbad offers M.Tech, MSc, MBA, etc.

Which courses are popular at IIT Dhanbad?

The most popular IIT Dhanbad courses include the B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, M.Tech, MSc, MBA and PhD programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MSc, MBA , etc. IIT Dhanbad also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does IIT Dhanbad accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at IIT Dhanbad. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is IIT Dhanbad easy to get into?

Admission in IIT Dhanbad courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the IIT Dhanbad Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include GMAT, CAT, MAT, GRE, XAT, COAP, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, UGC NET, CSIR NET, GATE, IIT JAM etc.
