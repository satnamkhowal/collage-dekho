About RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech

RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at RVR and JC College of Engineering Guntur, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects from a recognized board or university. They should have secured a minimum aggregate score of 45% (40% for reserved categories) in the qualifying examination. Additionally, candidates must qualify in the Engineering, Agriculture, and Medical Common Entrance Test (EAMCET) conducted by the Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE) or any other equivalent entrance examination. Meeting these criteria is essential for aspiring students to be considered for admission into the B.Tech. program at RVR and JC College of Engineering Guntur. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 69,500 - 1,08,000. RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at RVR and JC College of Engineering is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech Application Process

RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at RVR and JC College of Engineering have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of RVR and JC College of Engineering and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of RVR and JC College of Engineering. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for RVR and JC College of Engineering B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
