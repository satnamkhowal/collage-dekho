About SBS Ahmedabad

Shanti Business School (SBS), established in 2010 as a corporate citizenship initiative by the Chiripal Group in Ahmedabad, stands as a beacon of quality management education in Gujarat. This institution offers AICTE-approved Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) programs, meticulously designed to integrate rigorous academic curricula with practical corporate exposure, thereby molding students into adept, industry-ready managers.

Leveraging the Chiripal Group's over 50 years of expertise in textiles and education, SBS provides a unique learning environment that emphasizes holistic development. The 2025 batch saw an intake of 120 students, reflecting the school's selective yet inclusive approach. The curriculum boasts diverse electives in key areas such as finance, marketing, operations, human resources, and more, allowing students to tailor their education to their career aspirations. Beyond classrooms, SBS facilitates real-world learning through internships, live projects, and industry interactions, ensuring graduates are well-versed in contemporary business challenges.

In 2025, SBS achieved an impressive 100% placement rate for its 2024 graduating batch, with the highest package reaching INR 16.12 LPA and an average of INR 8.65 LPA. Prominent recruiters include ICICI Bank, Nestle, Deloitte, HDFC Bank, Hindustan Unilever, and many others, underscoring the school's robust industry network and recruiter trust.

For aspiring candidates targeting the 2026 intake, eligibility criteria include a bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories) and valid scores from entrance exams like CAT, MAT, XAT, ATMA, or CMAT. The admission process involves group discussions and personal interviews to assess potential.

SBS's commitment to value-driven education, combined with its state-of-the-art campus and award-winning placement records, makes it an ideal choice for professionals seeking a transformative management journey in Gujarat. With recognitions like Best Management College for Industry Interface, SBS continues to foster innovation and inclusivity in business education.

Shanti Business School Cutoff List

For 2026 admissions, SBS sets cutoffs based on 2025 entrance exam percentiles, prioritizing holistic profiles over rigid scores. The 2025 cycle saw shortlists at these levels, subject to annual adjustments.

  • CAT: 60 percentile overall.
  • CMAT: 70 percentile.
  • MAT: 75 percentile.
  • XAT/ATMA: 65 percentile.

Reserved category applicants receive a 5% relaxation. Final selection weights exams at 50%, GD/PI at 30%, and academics/work experience at 20%. View trends on Shanti Business School Cutoff.

Shanti Business School Campus and Infrastructure

Located on a 12-acre green campus in Shela, Ahmedabad, SBS features modern amenities supporting 500+ students and faculty. The Wi-Fi-enabled setup includes air-conditioned classrooms with projectors and a 200-seat auditorium for events.

  • Library: 10,000+ books, 50 journals, and digital access to JSTOR/EBSCO.
  • Hostels: Separate blocks for 200 boys and 150 girls, with 24/7 security and gym.
  • Sports: Cricket ground, basketball courts, and indoor games like table tennis.
  • Labs: Computer center with 100 terminals and Bloomberg terminals for finance simulations.
  • Other: Cafeteria serving 300, medical room, and transport from city center.
