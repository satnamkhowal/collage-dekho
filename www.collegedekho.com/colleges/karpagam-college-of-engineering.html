About KCE Coimbatore

The Karpagam College of Engineering located at Coimbatore in Tamil Nadu was established in 2000 as an autonomous Institution that is approved by AICTE, New Delhi and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. The college aims to impart quality education to all the students by incorporating creative and innovative teaching and learning methodologies. The college also aims to motivate its students to pursue higher education and research in order to serve society. The innovative teaching-learning process, experienced & qualified facility, independent learning environment and industry-based curriculum are few of the highlights of the college.

The Karpagam College of Engineering offers various Under Graduate (UG), Post Graduate (PG) and Research (Ph.D.) engineering programmes to its students. The courses and the research opportunities offered by the college helps the students to enhance their skills in the field of engineering. The Karpagam College of Engineering, Tamil Nadu also provides placement and training facilities to the students to increase their employment opportunities.

The infrastructure of the Karpagam College of Engineering also has well-equipped libraries and laboratories, hostels, gym, indoor stadium, well-maintained playgrounds, student clubs such as Photography Club, Nature Club, Food and Nutrition Club, Music and Dance Club etc. to engage and explore the talents of the students. By being a part of these clubs, students can develop their leadership skills. Various cultural events, technical events are also organised by the college to increase the participation of the students. By providing excellent infrastructure and well-qualified faculty, the Karpagam College of Engineering create engineers of high quality on par with the international standards who have technical expertise in research and development.

For 95 percentile in JEE main Board CBSE MP, from Indore Suitable college for CS , AI data science, E & T

D
Dewesh Nandan Prasad •  Jun-05-2025

Dear Student, 

With a 95 percentile in JEE Main 2024 and a CBSE (MP Board) background from Indore, you have strong opportunities for Computer Science (CS), Artificial Intelligence (AI), Data Science, and Electronics & Telecommunication (E&T) across NITs, IIITs, and top private institutions. For NITs, your percentile makes you competitive for E&T at NIT Surathkal, Trichy, or Warangal, though CS may require a higher percentile (98+). IIITs like IIIT Bangalore, Allahabad, or Pune offer excellent programs in AI/Data Science, with some flexibility in cutoffs for allied branches. If you prefer private colleges, VIT Vellore, SRM Chennai, or Manipal MIT provide direct admission for CS/AI with your score, while BITS Pilani (via BITSAT) could be an option for ECE. For local MP options, IIITM Gwalior (state quota) or DAVV Indore are worth considering.
