SMS College of Arts & Science Job Ready Degree

If you are worried about your future and want to secure it by opting for a course that will help you land a job after graduation. There are three job-ready courses available at SMS College of Arts & Science. The SMS College of Arts & Science job ready “Assured” courses are BBA, BSc, and BCom. BBA is offered in two specialisations which are Digital Marketing (IXP) and Fintech (IXP). Similarly, BSc is also offered in two specialisations which are Animation VFX (IXP) and Graphic Design (IXP). SMS College of Arts & Science offers BCom in Investment Banking (IXP). Job ready courses train you for the competitive job market by providing you the knowledge required for the current and the future market trends. These assured courses at SMS College of Arts & Science are offered at an annual fee which ranges from Rs 60,000 to Rs 80,000. Students need to score 50% or more marks in class 12 exam for admission to the job ready UG courses offered by this institute. Interested students can check the table below for details on the SMS College of Arts & Science job ready courses.

SMS College of Arts & Science Job Ready “Assured” Courses 2024 - Admissions 

Getting admission to these assured courses offered by SMS College of Arts & Science is easy as there is no need for an entrance exam. Students who meet the eligibility criteria for these job ready courses will get direct admission. Interested students only need to fill out the SMS College of Arts & Science application form and pay the application fee to get admission to their desired course.
