Introduction to Dr. B. Lal Institute of Biotechnology

Dr. B. Lal Institute of Biotechnology is a private institute in Jaipur, Rajasthan. It was founded in 2008 and is approved by the University of Rajasthan. The institute provides undergraduate and postgraduate courses, with the top courses being B.Sc Biotechnology and M.Sc Biotechnology. Admission is merit-based and involves a Personal Interview (PI) round.
Regarding placements, the average package provided in 2023 varied from ₹3 to 6 LPA. The most prominent recruiters who come to the campus include ICICI Bank, Dr. B. Lal Pharmacy, AIIMS, Allen, Albany Medical Centre, Axis Bank, and Life Cell. The institute continues to offer quality education and career prospects in the biotechnology sector.

Why Join Dr. B. Lal Institute of Biotechnology?

Here are some reasons why Dr. B. Lal Institute of Biotechnology stands out as a top choice for students

  • Diverse Academic Programs: Offers a total of 2 programs across undergraduate and postgraduate levels in Biotechnology and Microbiology.
  • Experienced Faculty: Faculty members have an average of over 10 years of teaching and industry experience.
  • State-of-the-Art Infrastructure: A 0.5955-acre campus with facilities like tech-enabled classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, and hostel accommodations.
  • Strong Placement Record: Placement rate with the Median package of Rs 3 to 6 LPA.
  • Global Exposure: Collaborations with institutions like Dr. B. Lal Clinical Laboratory for research and internships.
  • Holistic Development: Focus on extracurricular activities through various clubs, societies, and events.
