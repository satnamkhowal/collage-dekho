About Athena School of Management

Athena School of Management is a private business school located in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Established in 2012. Athena School of Management has been recognised by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). library, modern classrooms, and collaborative spaces are some of Athena School of Management facilities.Athena School of Management courses are offered PG levels. Courses offered by the college is PGPM. Athena School of Management admission is based on the entrance exam. The college accepts NMAT & GMAT for admission. Regarding Athena School of Management placements, the highest package offered  was RS 16 LPA and the average package offered was Rs 10 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include Federal Bank, JP Morgan Chase, ICICI Securities, HDFC Bank, IndusInd Bank, Wipro, Aditya Birla Group, and DCB Bank

Athena School of Management Eligibility Criteria for 2025

Athena School of Management admission depends on whether the students meet the Athena School of Management admission criteria or not. Below are the criteria on which a student can become eligible for admission in Athena School of Management. 

  • The candidate must have completed a bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognized university to be eligible for the programme.
  • The results of the CAT, GMAT, GRE, and NMAT are used as parameters for shortlisting candidates.
  • Athena School of Management administers the ASAT qualifying examination as a substitute for CAT, GMAT, and GRE NMAT.
  • In the qualifying examination, a minimum of 50% of the marks must be obtained.
  • Candidates who are taking the finals of their UG degree can also apply.

Athena School of Management Application Process 2025

Register as a new student on Athena School of Management official website. The Athena School of Management admissions department will get in touch with you and guide you through the whole Athena School of Management admissions process. You must download the application form from the website. This is to be submitted after the written test, group discussion (GD), and personal interview (PI).

  • Students are required to select the Athena School of Management as an option when registering for NMAT. 
  • Download and fill out the application form from the official website of the Athena School of Management.
  • After declaring the NMAT results, share them with the Athena School of Management admissions desk. 
  • You will be invited for a personal interview after you qualify for the Athena written test and group discussion. 
  • You will be offered admission based on the results of the test, GD, and PI. 
  • Scholarships will be offered if applicable.  

How to Fill up Athena School of Management Application Form?

  • Visit the Athena School of Management official website and click on apply online
  • You have to fill out the essential details such as name, address, contact details, etc. 
  • Fill in your academic qualifications and work experience (if any)

  • Complete the payment.

Athena School of Management Campus Facilities

Athena School of Management Campus

Athena School of Management campus is located in the Hiranandani Gardens area of Powai. It is a well-planned area with tree-lined boulevards, high-end boutiques and restaurants, spacious sidewalks, and world-class architecture. Athena School of Management campus is well-equipped and well-organized, with all necessary amenities and infrastructure. Athena School of Management, Mumbai, is surrounded by more than 200 corporate houses. This is an advantage to the students that other B-schools do not have. The companies located at the Hiranandani park include Deloitte, Fullerton India, Colgate Palmolive, Tata AIA, JP Morgan, Larsen & Toubro, Sony Pictures, Axis Bank, etc.

 Athena School of Management Infrastructure

With an imposing lobby and a roof garden, the exterior of the institute building is influenced by Greco-Roman architecture. It's a wi-fi-enabled campus with a business atmosphere thanks to the liberal use of glass, oak, and carpeting. There are state-of-the-art classrooms with the most up-to-date digital and electronic resources to enhance the learning experience.

Athena School of Management Facilities
