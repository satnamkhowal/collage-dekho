About SAGE University Bhopal

SAGE University is a leading university in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. It is ranked as one of the best universities in central India. It was established in 2020 by the SAGE Group, a parent firm of various companies, including five colleges and two schools. SAGE University is awarded the best-emerging university in central India. It functions under the patronage of Shri Agrawal Education Public Trust and is one of the top private universities in M.P. It has another campus in Indore. The university is stretched across 75 acres of lush green land having 15 schools and tie-ups with CollegeDekho, Masai, SUNSTONE, etc. The SAGE Bhopal is well known across the state and the country. It has been offered awards such as the ‘Best Education Sector Award,’ ‘Exemplary Contribution in Education Recognition Award’ by Zee News, ‘Leading Private University in Central India,’ ‘The Best Upcoming University in India,’ and many more. 

SAGE University Bhopal admission to all courses is done through an online exam conducted by the SAGE University called SEE (SAGE Entrance Exam). All the courses are approved by AICTE. The university is recognised by UGC and the Bar Council Of India (BCI). SAGE University Bhopal is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). The university accepts national and international applications. The selection process is based on the marks obtained in this entrance test. The SAGE University provides scholarships to its students based on Class 12 results or SEE results for UG and PG courses. The teaching faculty of SAGE University Bhopal are all industrial experts or PhD holders. Students get regular get lectures, workshops, seminars, etc to get familiar with their industry. All students are assigned a mentor for them to discuss problems. 

SAGE University Bhopal has tie-ups with Apollo Hospital, Agrawal Industries Construction, and Bada Business. Students get a session with Vivek Bindra, founder and CEO of Bada Business about entrepreneurship and business management. SAGE Bhopal provides skill-based knowledge and to foster entrepreneurship, the university has a ‘Entrepreneurship Development Cell’. In addition, the university has ‘I Nurture Projects’ which promotes the overall growth of the student. Focus is given to problem-based learning and students receive projects each semester. As for SAGE University Bhopal facilities, a Yoga coach and a football field is available for students.
