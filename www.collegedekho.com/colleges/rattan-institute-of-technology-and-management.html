About Rattan Institute of Technology and Management

Rattan Institute of Technology and Management is situated in Faridabad in Haryana state of India. Established in 2008, RITM is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak and accredited by AICTE. Rattan Institute of Technology and Management offers 17 courses across 3 streams namely Engineering, Management, IT..Popular degrees offered at Rattan Institute of Technology and Management include B.Tech, BBA, M.Tech, MBA, Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Rattan Institute of Technology and Management is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Rattan Institute of Technology and Management, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Rattan Institute of Technology and Management is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
