About College of Veterinary & Animal Sciences

College of Veterinary & Animal Sciences is situated in Latur in Maharashtra state of India. Established in 1987, CVAS is a Government college. The college is accredited by NAAC. College of Veterinary & Animal Sciences offers 2 courses across 2 streams namely Engineering, Medical..Popular degrees offered at College of Veterinary & Animal Sciences include BVSc, PhD..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, College of Veterinary & Animal Sciences is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at College of Veterinary & Animal Sciences, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at College of Veterinary & Animal Sciences is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
