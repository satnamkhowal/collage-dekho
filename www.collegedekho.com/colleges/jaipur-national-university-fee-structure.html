Jaipur National University Fees 2026 Overview

Jaipur National University fees for the year 2026 vary based on the programme, specialisation, and duration. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and doctorate programmes in engineering, management, law, pharmacy, health sciences, computer applications, sciences, commerce, arts, and hotel management.

For undergraduate programmes, the total fees range from Rs 1,51,600 to Rs 9,86,040. The flagship BTech programmes total fees cost between Rs 5,18,400 to Rs 7,01,360, while other professional programmes such as BA LLB, BBA LLB, BSc Nursing and PharmD fees range from Rs 7,18,080 to Rs 9,86,040.

The Jaipur National University fee structure for postgraduate programmes range from Rs 82,400 to Rs 4,38,000. The MBA programmes total fees cost between Rs 2,78,200 to Rs 4,38,000, while other master’s programmes such as MTech, MCA and MPharm range from Rs 1,84,000 to Rs 2,27,000.

Jaipur National University Fees FAQs

Does the university charge a penalty for late fee payments?
Yes, Jaipur National University may charge a late fee if the semester fee is not paid by the due date. The late fee usually starts with a small amount and may increase if the payment is further delayed. In some cases, access to services such as the student portal or exam form submission may also be restricted. Students are advised to contact the accounts office in advance if they expect any delay in payment.
What are the transport fees for students living off-campus?
Jaipur National University offers a bus transport facility for day scholars living off-campus. The transport fee is charged separately from the course fee and depends on the distance between the student’s pickup location and the campus. For the 2026 academic session, the transport fee generally ranges from Rs 25,000 to Rs 35,000 per year. The transport service is optional and the fee is paid at the beginning of the academic session if the student chooses to use it.
Do international students have a different fee structure here?
Yes, Jaipur National University has a separate fee structure for international and NRI students. The fees are usually charged in US Dollars (USD) and are generally higher than the fees for Indian students. This fee structure may include additional administrative and support services for international admissions. However, international students may also be eligible for merit-based scholarships. For exact details, applicants should contact the JNU Jaipur International Admissions Cell.

Jaipur National University BTech FAQs

Does JNU Jaipur charge separate lab and practical fees for engineering students?
Yes, Jaipur National University may charge separate lab and practical fees for some engineering programmes. These fees are usually charged once per year and generally range between Rs 5,000 and Rs 8,000, depending on the engineering branch. The charges cover the cost of laboratory facilities, equipment, and materials used in practical training and workshops. Students should check the official JNU Jaipur engineering fee structure for their specific branch during admission.
How much are the semester examination fees for engineering students?
At Jaipur National University, B.Tech students must pay a separate semester examination fee in addition to their tuition fee. The exam fee is usually around Rs 4,000 per semester, which covers the cost of conducting exams and processing results. Students may also be required to pay a one-time ERP fee of about Rs 500, which provides access to the student portal to check grades, attendance, and fee details.

Jaipur National University MBA FAQs

Are industrial tour costs included in the MBA fees at JNU Jaipur?
No, industrial tour costs are usually not included in the MBA fee at Jaipur National University. The university may organise industrial visits and study tours to provide practical exposure, but the travel, accommodation, and participation costs are charged separately. The fee may vary depending on the destination. These tours help students to gain industry exposure and understand the real business operations.
What is the hostel and mess fee for MBA students at JNU Jaipur?
At Jaipur National University, MBA students who choose campus hostel accommodation must pay separate hostel and mess fees. The total cost usually ranges from Rs 95,000 to Rs 1,35,000 per year, depending on the type of room and facilities. Triple-sharing non-AC rooms are usually cheaper, while double-sharing AC rooms cost more. The fee generally includes mess meals, hostel facilities, and basic services. Students should check the latest JNU Jaipur hostel fee structure during admission.
