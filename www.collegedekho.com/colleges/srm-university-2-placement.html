SRM Chennai Placements 2025

During the SRM Chennai placements in 2024, the highest package offered is Rs 52 LPA, while the average package is Rs 7.50 LPA. Over 853 companies participated in the recruitment process, with more than 5,176 offers to the students. The top recruiters included industry giants such as Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and IBM. In the SRM University Chennai placements in 2023 the highest package was Rs 57 LPA. A total of 1,313 companies visited the campus and made 9,011 offers to students, including over 3,327 high-paying offers. Companies like Microsoft, Google, and TCS were among the top recruiters at SRM Chennai that offered opportunities to students across different engineering and technology branches.

SRM University Chennai Placements FAQs

How many companies visit SRM Chennai?

Each year, SRM Chennai invites a significant number of companies for its placement drives. In recent years, the number of visiting companies has been over 800, with some years reaching over 1,300, as the campus has a huge network of industry partners.

What is the minimum package in SRM Chennai?

The minimum package offered to graduates during SRM Chennai placements is around Rs 3.5 LPA. This package can vary based on the student's branch, role, and the hiring company.

Does SRM Chennai have 100% placement?

While SRM Chennai has a strong placement record, it does not guarantee 100% placement for all students. The placement percentage is high, often reaching over 90%, but it can vary based on the overall performance of the batch and recruiter requirements.

Who are the top recruiters of SRM Chennai?

Top recruiters at SRM Chennai include major companies such as Microsoft, Google, Amazon, IBM, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and Accenture. These companies frequently visit the campus for recruitment, offer high packages to SRMIST students.

What is the highest package for BTech in SRM Chennai?

The highest package for BTech graduates in SRM Chennai placements in 2024 has been Rs 52 LPA. In 2023 the highest package offered to the students was 57 LPA.

Which branch of SRM Chennai has the best placements record?

The Computer Science and Engineering branch at SRM Chennai consistently has the best placement record. In the SRM Chennai placements 2024, CSE students received some of the highest packages and had the highest placement rates compared to other branches. Students from CSE received higher offers from leading tech companies like Microsoft, Google, and Amazon.

Is SRM Chennai good for placements?

Yes, SRM Institute of Science and Technology Chennai is known for its strong placement performance. In the 2024, over 853 companies visited the campus, offering more than 5,176 job opportunities to students. The highest package offered was Rs 52 LPA, while the average package was Rs 7.50 LPA.
