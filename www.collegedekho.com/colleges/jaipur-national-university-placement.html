Jaipur National University Placement Overview 2025

The placements for Jaipur National University are managed by its placement cell every year. The cell is responsible for preparing students for placements and bringing recruiters to the campus. As per Jaipur National University's placement report for 2025, a total of over 200 companies visited the campus for placements and a total of 1,500 offers were made. 1,200 students were placed with an average package of Rs 6 LPA. Moreover, the highest package offered during placements was Rs 15 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visited Jaipur National University include TCS and Infosys.

Jaipur National University Placements Cell

The Jaipur National University placement cell is known as the Corporate Resource Cell. The Corporate Resource Cell of JNU Jaipur trains the students and makes them employable. JJaipur National University placement cell offers facilities like - soft skill training, job trend tracking, writing and oral skill development, personality development, and campus placement. 

The Jaipur National University placement cell also organises group discussions and mock interviews. Dr. Purnima Nag is the director of the JNU Jaipur placement cell. The Corporate Resource Cell of this university follows the following placement process to help students get their desired jobs. 

First, they create student profiles on the university website. Then, they organise workshops and industry visits to familiarise students with the work environment, then they invite companies for recruitment, and then the placement cell trains students according to their job goals. They also help students understand the job roles. Then, they encourage them to complete industry-related projects and finally help students prepare before the interview round.

Jaipur National University Placements FAQs

Does JNU Jaipur offer internships for its students?

Yes, the JNU Jaipur does offer internships for its students. Students, with the help of the placement cell, can get internship opportunities during summer vacations. Internships at this university are offered to students who volunteer and to those who have compulsory internships in their respective courses. For example, B.Ed students will have to go through a compulsory six months internship in their final year. Students can get valuable industry experience and a stipend by doing internships during summer vacations.

How is the placement for pharmacy courses at Jaipur National University?

The placement data for pharmacy courses at Jaipur National University is available in the NIRF report 2023. According to the NIRF report 2023, the median package for four-year UG pharmacy courses in 2022 was Rs 4,00,000 per annum. Similarly, the median package for two-year PG pharmacy courses in 2022 was Rs 3,50,000 per annum. During 2022 placements for four-year UG courses in pharmacy, 100% of students were placed. As for the two-year PG pharmacy courses in 2022, 16% of students were placed.

What is the Jaipur National University placement percentage?

The Jaipur National University placement percentage can be calculated by using the data provided in the NIRF 2023 report of this university. According to the said report, the placement percentage of three-year UG courses in 2022 was 31%. Similarly, the placement percentage of four-year UG courses in 2022 at Jaipur National University was 32.50%. Moreover, the placement percentage of two-year PG courses in 2022 was 70%.

What is the JNU Jaipur placement trend?

The JNU Jaipur placement trend can be judged by checking the NIRF report 2023 of this university. According to the NIRF report 2023, the Jaipur National University median package for the three-year UG programme in 2022 was Rs 4,00,000 per annum. Whereas it was Rs 3,50,000 per annum in 2021. Similarly, the median package for the three-year UG programme in 2020 was Rs 3,00,000 per annum. Moreover, according to the data we received from various sources, the total number of offers made in 2022 at this university was 700, while it was 650 in 2021 and 600 in 2022. This clearly shows that the Jaipur National University placement trend is going upwards.

Where are JNU Jaipur alumni placed?

The JNU Jaipur alumni are placed in various sectors in India and abroad. According to the data we collected from various sources, 17% of the Jaipur National University alumni are placed in the information technology sector, 24% of the alumni are placed in the sales sector, 22% of them are working in the operations sector, another 20% of the university alumni are working in the business development sector, and the remaining 17% of the alumni are working in the engineering sector.

What is the placement rate for Jaipur National University?

The Jaipur National University placement data for 2023 will be released soon. However, if we refer to the available data for 2022, 2021, and 2020, we will be able to see that the number of total placements has increased every year. In 2020, the total number of placed students was 600, which increased gradually to 700 in 2022.

What are the placements for CSE Jaipur National University?

The Jaipur National University has not released the department-wise placement data for this year or the previous few years. However, according to our research, the Jaipur National University CSE highest placement package would be around Rs 25 lakhs per annum in the year 2022. Once the 2023 placements are released, you can find the JNU Jaipur B.Tech in CSE placement information here.

How are students trained for campus placements at Jaipur National University?

The Placement Cell of Jaipur National University organises personality development programmes, written and oral skill enhancement workshops, student-industry interactions, special training with industry experts, presentation and communication workshops, and all-round development and grooming workshops to prepare students for placements. Before the campus placements start the Corporate Resource Cell or placement cell informs students about the job roles and also offers mock interviews.

Who are the top recruiters at Jaipur National University?

The Jaipur National University recruiters list includes both national and international companies. Some of the top recruiters at this university are Byju’s, IBM, Oberoi Group, Amazon, EY, Bosch, Reliance, Wipro, Intel, Decathlon, HP, and Deloitte. These companies visit the university campus almost every year to recruit final year students of various courses. The placement cell maintains a good relationship with these top recruiters, which helps them invite these companies every year.

How are the placements at Jaipur National University?

Jaipur National University provides placement opportunities for all students who opt for professional courses. As per the JNU Jaipur placement 2022, the highest salary package offered was Rs 27 lakhs per annum. The average package was Rs 5.50 lakhs per annum. According to the NIRF report the median package for UG courses in 2022 was Rs 4.50 lakhs per annum.
