About Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech

Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech is a 2 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. degree at Government Engineering College, Gandhinagar, Gandhinagar, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have successfully completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. They must have secured a minimum aggregate score of 45% (40% for reserved categories) in their qualifying exams. Additionally, candidates must have a valid score in the Gujarat Common Entrance Test (GUJCET) or the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main. Meeting these criteria will enable aspiring students to apply for the B.Tech. program at Government Engineering College, Gandhinagar, and embark on their journey towards a successful career in technology. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 6,000 - 18,200. Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech Application Process

Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

Gandhi Engineering College (GEC)

Overall GEC is renowned for providing high-quality engineering education with a heavy emphasis on hands-on training. The campus has modern infrastructure and well-maintained labs. The teachers give each pupil individualized attention and are supportive. Numerous students from the college are hired by leading IT and core organizations, demonstrating the college's strong placement record. However, some students may find it inconvenient because of the location's relative remoteness.

Placement Gandhi Engineering College has a strong track record of placement, especially in the field of engineering. Students are typically offered packages that range from INR 3 to 4 LPA. Employers such as TCS, Wipro, and Infosys regularly come to the university to fill positions. Employability is greatly increased by the college's emphasis on skill development through workshops and internships.

Infrastructure Gandhi Engineering College (GEC), has a modern campus with well-organized facilities. Modern technology is installed in the labs and classrooms to improve the educational experience. The vast library offers a variety of scholarly resources. The well-kept sports complex accommodates a range of extracurricular activities. Students would also benefit from increased transportation options and improved dorm amenities.

Faculty The faculty at Gandhi Engineering College (GEC), is dedicated to offering top-notch technical education. Here, instructors emphasize both academic and practical elements to make sure students are prepared for the workforce. They offer guidance for both academic and personal growth and are friendly. GEC's emphasis on entrepreneurship and innovation is noteworthy, however several labs may benefit from modern equipment to improve the educational experience.

Hostel Clean, basic hostel facilities with minimum amenities are offered by Gandhi Engineering College. Even though it can seem monotonous, the mess meal is good. Good security measures and a helpful environment characterize the hostel. Both personal and academic use are supported by the generally dependable Wi-Fi connectivity. The beautiful, verdant campus provides a calm residential area. For engineering students, this is an economical and cozy option overall.
