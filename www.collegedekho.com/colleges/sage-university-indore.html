About SAGE University Indore

SAGE University Indore, commonly known as SAGE Indore, was established in 2017 in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. SAGE University Indore is one of the private universities led by the Sagar Group of Educational Society. SAGE University Indore is approved and recognised by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), Bar Council of India (BCI), Council of Architecture (COA), and University Grants Commission (UGC). It has been recognised as the youngest university in the country to get an “A+” grade in the NAAC accreditation. The university campus is spread across an area of 75 acres. Classrooms, laboratories, libraries, hostels, sports, etc are some of the SAGE University Indore facilities.

SAGE University Indore courses are offered at UG, PG, certificate, and doctoral levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the university include BTech, BCom, BCA, B.Pharm, MBA, etc. SAGE University Indore admission is based on either merit or entrance exam scores. The entrance exams accepted for admission are JEE Main for BTech, CAT/ MAT/ XAT/ CMAT for MBA, GATE for MTech, etc. Regarding SAGE University Indore placements, the highest and average package offered during 2023 placements is Rs 43 LPA and Rs 5.10 LPA for the BTech branch. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include Genpact, Accenture, Google, Samsung, KPMG, EY, Deloitte, Amazon, etc.

SAGE University Indore Application Process 2026

Candidates who are interested in SAGE University Indore admission will have to apply for admission. Candidates can apply for admission in online mode by visiting the official website, i.e. sageuniversity.in, with an application fee of Rs 1600. Alternatively, candidates can also apply for SAGE University Indore admission in offline mode with an application fee of Rs 1600. 

The following are the detailed steps for the SAGE University Indore application process:                          

  • Visit the official SAGE University Indore portal; register with the candidate's phone number, e-mail and other details asked.
  • Once the candidate’s email is verified, the candidates are then required to fill out the SAGE University Indore online application form. 
  • After filling out the application form, candidates are required to pay Rs 1,600 as SAGE University Indore application fees for the application form submission.
  • Candidates will later receive a call/ mail from the university admission team about career counselling and the SAGE Entrance Exam (depending on the course applied).
  • The admission reporting process happens once the candidate meets the particular eligibility criteria. In this final process, students are required to report to the university premises for document verification and semester fees payment.

Does SAGE University Indore provide scholarship opportunities?

How are the facilities at SAGE University Indore?

Ans SAGE University Indore appears to have remarkable amenities in overall that offer students a cosy and well-equipped atmosphere. The university has a spacious, 75-acre campus that is lush and green and has contemporary facilities. Most of the buildings' infrastructure including the hostels and classrooms, have air conditioning and Wi-Fi. There are numerous facilities on campus to meet your requirements for sports, education, and entertainment. In addition, there are state-of-the-art labs for hands-on learning, large libraries with an abundance of resources, well-furnished classrooms, and computer labs with fast internet access.

Does UGC recognise SAGE University Indore?

Ans Indeed, the University Grants Commission (UGC) has recognised SAGE University Indore. Under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, it received recognition from the UGC on August 24, 2017. The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) has also given it approval for its technical programmes. It has been accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an A+ grade, the highest grade the organisation can give. As such, you can rest certain that SAGE University Indore is a recognised and reputable university.
