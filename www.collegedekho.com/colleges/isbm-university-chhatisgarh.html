About ISBM University

ISBM University, also known as ISBM Chhattisgarh, is a private university located in Gariyaband, Chhattisgarh. It was established in 2016 and is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). The university is also approved by AICTE, the Bar Council of India (BCI), and the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). ISBM University has a modern campus with academic buildings, laboratories, a library, hostels, and sports facilities.

ISBM University offers 200+ courses at the undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, B.Voc, and doctoral levels. Popular courses include B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Sc, B.Com, BA, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Sc, M.Com, B.Pharm, and LLB programmes. Admission to most undergraduate and postgraduate courses is based on merit in the qualifying examination, while PhD admission is based on the university entrance test or valid national-level entrance exam scores, as per the university guidelines.

ISBM University Admission 2026

ISBM University admission 2026 is open for undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, B.Voc, and PhD programmes. Admission to most courses is based on merit in the qualifying examination. Candidates applying for UG courses are selected based on Class 12 marks, while PG admission is based on graduation marks. PhD admission follows the university's selection process. Candidates can apply online through the official ISBM University admission portal or offline by submitting the application form at the university. The application fee is INR 900.

ISBM University Admission Process 2026

The admission process at ISBM University is similar for most courses. Candidates can apply online through the official admission portal or in person at the university admission office. Admission is based on the eligibility criteria and selection process prescribed for each programme. The steps for completing the ISBM University admission process are outlined below. Follow these steps to complete the ISBM University admission process:

  • Visit the official ISBM University website and click on "Apply Online", or collect the application form from the university admission office.
  • Fill in the application form with your personal, academic, and course details.
  • Upload or submit the required documents along with the application form.
  • Pay the application fee through the available online or offline payment methods.
  • Submit the completed application form.
  • The university will verify your application and documents. Selection for most UG, PG, Diploma, and Certificate courses is based on merit in the qualifying examination.
  • Shortlisted candidates must visit the campus for document verification.
  • Pay the admission fee to confirm your seat.

For PhD admission, candidates must meet the eligibility criteria and appear for the university entrance test if required. Candidates with valid UGC NET, CSIR NET, or GATE scores may be exempted from the entrance test as per the university rules. Shortlisted candidates must complete the interview, document verification, and fee payment to finalise admission.

ISBM University Chhattisgarh Placements

ISBM University through its strong network and alliances is committed to providing 100% placement services to its students pursuing a degree program. It aims to create resources and understanding of career opportunities while enhancing students' transition from university to career.
