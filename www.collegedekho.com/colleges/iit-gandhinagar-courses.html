IIT Gandhinagar Courses Eligibility 2026

Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar eligibility for different courses varies by the programme itself. For BTech and BTech+MTech, the candidates need at least 75% in 10+2 with physics, chemistry and mathematics as the compulsory subjects. MTech requires a BTech or equivalent degree in a relevant discipline with at least 55% marks or a 5.5 CGPA. The MSc and MA require a relevant bachelor's degree, while PhD eligibility is a master's degree or a BTech from one of the centrally funded technical institutions (CFTI)

  • SC/ST/PwD candidates are eligible for a 5% relaxation in the minimum marks requirement for all programmes.
  • The executive master’s programmes also need a minimum of 2 years of work experience to be eligible in addition to the academic qualifications.
  • NIT BTech graduates with more than 8.0 CGPA are eligible for the Early Admit MTech.
  • Only the students of IITs and IISC are eligible for the Start Early PhD, while other candidates are eligible for all other PhDs available at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar.
  • Only the senior members of the industry, academia, or administration with a minimum of 20 years of work experience are eligible for the Professional Leader's PhD at IIT Gandhinagar.

IIT Gandhinagar BTech 2026

Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar BTech is an 8-semester programme offered across 8 specialisations. The BTech curriculum includes mandatory courses in life sciences, design thinking, and humanities. The 4-week foundation programme under the 1st semester covers creativity, ethics, teamwork, and communication. The 7th semester can be used for an external internship in companies, research and development laboratories, or universities abroad. Students can earn up to 16 academic credits, which count toward the total credits required to graduate.

  • Students willing to change their branch can proceed after the 1st semester and must secure a CPI ≥ 6.50 in the 1st semester.
  • Every BTech branch allows students to optionally pursue honours (additional core coursework), a minor from 26 options, a dual major, or a dual degree.
  • IIT Gandhinagar cutoff in 2026 for BTech material science can be in the band of 7500-11500, making it the most accessible branch for admission.
  • The BTech seat intake at IIT Gandhinagar is 360, comprising all the branches.

IIT Gandhinagar BTech 2026 FAQs

What undergraduate research opportunities are available to BTech students at IIT Gandhinagar?
IIT Gandhinagar places strong emphasis on undergraduate research. Nearly 40% of its BTech graduates have received international research opportunities at top global institutions and organisations, among the highest rates in the country. The institute also runs a competitive Summer Research Internship Programme (SRIP) for students who wish to pursue research on campus. Beyond this, the institute maintains a liberal travel support policy to encourage undergraduate students to attend scientific conferences abroad and present their research work. All BTech students are also required to complete a mandatory 4-credit open project course in a discipline of their choice after their first year.
What is the BTech curriculum structure at IIT Gandhinagar?
The BTech at IIT Gandhinagar is an 8-semester, 4-year programme with a distinctly interdisciplinary structure. It begins with a 4-week foundation programme covering creativity, ethics, teamwork, and communication. The 1st year is largely common across all branches, incorporating life sciences, design thinking, humanities, and mathematics alongside branch-specific engineering subjects. From the 2nd year onwards, students move into core branch coursework while retaining the flexibility to add minors, honours, or a dual major. The 7th semester can be utilised for an external internship at companies, R&D labs, or universities abroad, earning up to 16 academic credits counted toward graduation. Students can also opt into the 5-year dual degree track, where the 9th and 10th semesters are dedicated to MTech-level coursework and thesis.
What is the Integrated Circuit Design & Technology (ICDT) BTech branch at IIT Gandhinagar?
Integrated Circuit Design & Technology (ICDT) is one of the 8 BTech branches currently offered at IIT Gandhinagar, with a seat intake of 30, making it the smallest among all BTech branches. The core focus areas of the programme include chip design, semiconductor fabrication, and VLSI. Like all BTech branches at IITGN, students in ICDT can pursue minors, honours, a dual major, or a dual degree and are eligible for branch change after the 1st semester. ICDT is also available as a minor for students from other BTech branches and as a PhD specialisation.
What to choose between the CSE 4-year and 5-year dual degree at IIT Gandhinagar?
The 4-year BTech in CSE is suited for students targeting industry roles or who want the flexibility to explore minors, a dual major, or honours within a standard degree timeframe. The 5-year BTech + MTech dual degree in CSE is better suited for students inclined toward research or advanced specialisation, as the final two semesters (9th and 10th) are fully dedicated to MTech-level coursework and a thesis. Both programmes share the same first 8 semesters of BTech curriculum, and the 7th semester in either track can be used for an external internship earning up to 16 academic credits. Importantly, students do not need to decide at admission. At IIT Gandhinagar, the branch change to or from the dual degree programme is permitted after the 1st semester based on CPI performance.

IIT Gandhinagar BTech + MTech 2026

Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar BTech + MTech allows students to earn two separate degrees without taking admission to the MTech programme. The dual degree programme spans 10 semesters. The first 8 semesters cover the BTech curriculum, and the final 2 semesters are dedicated to the MTech-level coursework and thesis. The 5th year is largely research-focused. 

  • Only the students already enrolled in a BTech programme at this institution are eligible for the dual degree programme. 
  • 7th Semester can be used for an external industrial or academic internship, earning up to 16 academic credits counted toward graduation
  • Students can change their branch to or from the dual-degree programme after the 1st semester, based on their CPI.
  • All BTech fee waivers under the IIT Gandhinagar scholarships apply equally to this programme.

IIT Gandhinagar MTech 2026 FAQs

Can MTech students at IIT Gandhinagar do their thesis in industry or outside their discipline?
Yes, and this is one of the more distinctive features of the MTech programme at IITGN. Students have a lot of flexibility in designing their academic path and can carry out their research even outside their immediate discipline or even in industry, all while developing an insight and perspective that is geared towards pushing the boundaries of knowledge. The programme aims at graduating well-rounded engineers ready to pursue leadership careers in R&D, teaching or industry. This means an MTech student in Mechanical Engineering could, for instance, work on a thesis problem in collaboration with an industry partner at the IITGN Research Park, or pursue a cross-disciplinary problem that sits at the intersection of their branch and another department. The thesis advisor and the student jointly decide the research direction, giving students considerable ownership over their academic path.
What is the MTech Coursework Track at IIT Gandhinagar, and is it different from the regular MTech?
Yes, it is a separate and distinct track. The MTech Coursework Track is designed for students who want to pursue non-research careers and seek stronger skills and knowledge. The curriculum is built around interdisciplinary skills, allowing students to learn from courses offered by their parent department as well as from several courses across other departments. Unlike the regular MTech, which requires 32 to 48 credits of thesis work, the Coursework Track carries a reduced thesis component and a heavier course load. Notably, a GATE score is not required to apply for the MTech Coursework Track. Students on this track are also eligible for international double degree programmes, where the student spends part of the time, typically one year, at IIT Gandhinagar and the remaining time at the partner institution abroad, after which degrees from each institution are awarded.
What is the credit and thesis requirement for the regular MTech at IIT Gandhinagar?
The MTech programme at IIT Gandhinagar consists of both coursework and a project or thesis. Students are required to complete 32 to 48 credits of coursework, 32 to 48 credits of thesis, and 2 credits of the mandatory postgraduate orientation programme called 'Aarohan'. The thesis component is designed to train students in independent thinking and the integration of knowledge toward solving R&D problems. Students have considerable flexibility in designing their academic path and can carry out their research even outside their immediate discipline or in industry. Additionally, all MTech students must complete two physical education courses, one each in their first two semesters, graded on a pass/no-pass basis.

IIT Gandhinagar MSc 2026 FAQs

How is the MSc programme at IIT Gandhinagar different from a conventional MSc degree?
The MSc programme at IITGN is designed to carve out a different niche from the large number of MSc offerings in India. It enables students to acquire both breadth and depth in their areas of study, orients them toward scientific research, exposes them to areas beyond their core subjects, and includes mandatory curriculum components from humanities and social sciences. This means even an MSc student in mathematics or physics will have structured exposure to the humanities, consistent with the institute's interdisciplinary philosophy. The MSc programme places a strong emphasis on both coursework and a compulsory research project. It is expected that the thesis project will result in high-quality scholarly publications and presentations at national and international conferences. The institute also provides travel scholarships of up to INR 60,000 for MSc students to present their work at conferences.
Is there an Early Admit MSc track at IIT Gandhinagar, and who qualifies?
Yes. IIT Gandhinagar offers an Early Admit MSc in three specialisations, with admission based on a written test or interview and the student's past academic scores. BSc and BTech students from select colleges and NITs who will graduate in the current academic year and are among the top five rank holders in their departments at the end of their pre-final or final year are eligible to apply. This track, similar in spirit to the Early Admit MTech, is designed to give high-performing students from top institutions a direct pathway into the MSc programme without waiting for the standard JAM-based admission cycle. Students admitted through this route join the same programme and curriculum as regularly admitted MSc students.
What is the MSc Cognitive Science programme at IIT Gandhinagar, and who is it open to?
The MSc in Cognitive Science is a two-year, four-semester full-time programme. Its objective is to offer students from different backgrounds a breadth of knowledge and research techniques across a wide range of areas in cognitive science. The total credits required for graduation are 80, and the curriculum includes core courses, electives, and a compulsory research project. Core courses include Cognitive Psychology, Computation and Cognition, Philosophy of Mind, Fundamental Neuroscience, Research Methods, and Experimental Techniques in Cognitive Science. Notably, unlike the other three MSc programmes at IITGN which require IIT JAM, admission to the Cognitive Science programme is through the institute's own written test and interview process. This makes it one of the few MSc programmes in India open to graduates from any bachelor's degree background, including BA, BSc, BTech, BCom, and MBBS.

IIT Gandhinagar Executive Master's 2026

Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar executive master's programme is designed for working professionals who want to build expertise in a focused knowledge domain while continuing in their existing professions. The programme spans 4 semesters, and the students must undertake 52–54 credits in this timeframe. The IITGN executive master's does not include thesis submission in the curriculum.

  • IIT Gandhinagar Executive Master's is delivered primarily online with a minimum residential requirement of 30 days on campus over 2 years.
  • The programme is divided into 6 modules, each lasting 12–14 weeks, covering foundation and core courses, leadership & writing courses, and 2 capstone projects.
  • Sessions are held during evenings and weekends with live interactive online sessions plus self-paced learning modules.

IIT Gandhinagar Courses FAQs

Can PhD students at IIT Gandhinagar go abroad for research during their degree?

Yes, IIT Gandhinagar runs an Overseas Research Exposure Scheme that supports full-time PhD students with up to USD 12,500 to spend 4 to 6 months at an overseas university or research lab. Students have been hosted at institutions including MIT, Caltech, Georgia Tech, Duke University, and Carnegie Mellon University. This is in addition to the institute's travel support for presenting research at international conferences, making IITGN one of the more globally connected IITs for doctoral researchers.

Does IIT Gandhinagar offer international double degree programmes at the MTech level?

Yes, IIT Gandhinagar and the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Thailand, offer a double-degree master's programme that allows students to earn two separate master's degrees, one from IITGN and another from AIT, by integrating the strengths of both institutions. The programme is available in 7 specialisations, including medical engineering, data science and artificial intelligence. Environmental Engineering and Management, Geotechnical Engineering, Remote Sensing and GIS, Sustainable Energy and Water Engineering and Management. On the structure, students starting at IITGN spend their first two semesters at IITGN before moving to AIT for the remaining two semesters, and each student is assigned a faculty advisor at both institutions. Both institutes provide on-campus residence and assist with visa processing.

How does the grading system work at IIT Gandhinagar?

IIT Gandhinagar uses a 10-point CPI (Cumulative Performance Index) scale. Letter grades are awarded for each course, with 'A' equivalent to 10 points, 'A minus' to 9, 'B' to 8, 'B minus' to 7, and so on. Not all courses follow letter grading. The General Education Basket courses (up to 4 credits, covering areas like arts, theatre, and sports) are graded on a Pass/Fail basis, as is the mandatory A comprehensive viva voce is conducted every semester. For students on the MTech and PhD thesis track, thesis units are graded as 'S' (Satisfactory) or 'U' (Unsatisfactory) rather than through the standard letter grade system. To qualify for honours, grades in all credit courses must be a B minus or higher, and the student must not be on academic probation.

What is the credit structure for the BTech programme at IIT Gandhinagar?

BTech students must complete a minimum of 169–175 credits to graduate, depending on their discipline. Of these, discipline-specific courses account for 62–64 credits. IIT Gandhinagar The remaining credits are spread across mandatory institute-level courses, humanities and social sciences, science baskets, open electives, and project courses. Students also get 16 credits of open electives and must complete an Open Project Course worth 4 credits, which can be pursued in any discipline of their choice after the first year, including at the IITGN Research Park in collaboration with industry partners. Up to 16 credits of additional project courses can also count toward graduation.

What are the mandatory humanities and social sciences courses in the BTech curriculum?

All BTech students must complete 28 credits under the Humanities and Social Sciences basket. This includes two mandatory writing courses, Introduction to Writing I and Introduction to Writing II (2 credits each), and three additional mandatory courses: Economics (4 credits), Introduction to Philosophy (4 credits), and World Civilisations and Cultures (4 credits). The remaining 12 credits are completed through HSS or management electives, along with 4 credits from a general education basket covering areas like cultural expression, performing arts, theatre, fine arts, and sports. IIT Gandhinagar Notably, courses under the general education basket are graded on a pass/fail basis, not through the standard letter grade system.

How does the honours programme work at IIT Gandhinagar BTech?

The BTech honours designation is available as an add-on to the base BTech degree. To earn it, students must complete 20 additional credits of courses that deepen their core competence in their discipline, along with 8 credits of BTech projects related to that discipline. IIT Gandhinagar students do not need to declare their intent to pursue honours at the time of admission or even during the course. Students keep accumulating credits throughout their four years and, at the time of graduation, claim their degree as BTech, BTech with Honours, BTech with Minor, or a combination, based on the credits collected.

What is IIT Gandhinagar's Summer Research Internship Programme (SRIP), and who can apply?

The Summer Research Internship Programme (SRIP) is IIT Gandhinagar's flagship initiative that brings motivated students from prominent institutions across the country to its campus. It allows undergraduate and master's students to participate in cutting-edge research projects under the mentorship of IITGN faculty, with access to state-of-the-art laboratories and instrumentation facilities. SRIP is conducted in offline (in-person) mode on campus. Interns are required to work with an IITGN faculty member, make a presentation, and submit a report at the end of their tenure. A weekly stipend of INR 2,000 is paid upon completion, though the amount may vary for faculty-funded projects.

Does IIT Gandhinagar offer MSc in Computer Science?

No. IIT Gandhinagar does not offer an MSc in Computer Science. The MSc programmes available at IITGN are chemistry, cognitive science, mathematics, and physics. Admission to all MSc programmes is through IIT JAM, except Cognitive Science, which requires an additional interview. Students who want to study computer science at the postgraduate level at IITGN can apply for the MTech in Computer Science & Engineering (through GATE) or the Dual Degree (BTech + MTech) track at the undergraduate stage.

Are classrooms branch-specific at IIT Gandhinagar?

No, classrooms at IIT Gandhinagar are not branch-specific. The institute employs a shared academic complex design across its six academic buildings, where classrooms, lecture halls, labs, and faculty offices are allocated by course schedule rather than branch affiliation. This encourages interdisciplinary collaboration. The Building 7 at the IITGN campus includes 16 classrooms and 4 halls used by CSE, EE, and HSS alike. Modern AV-equipped rooms support flexible timetabling.

What minors can I pursue in BTech at IIT Gandhinagar?

IIT Gandhinagar currently offers 26 minor programmes that any BTech student can opt into by completing additional relevant courses. These include engineering-domain minors such as artificial intelligence, robotics, quantum technologies, biomedical engineering, and integrated circuit design & technology, as well as interdisciplinary minors like data analytics, management, design, earth sciences, archaeological sciences, cognitive science, and sustainable development. A student in mechanical engineering, for instance, can simultaneously complete a minor in computer science & engineering and a minor in management. Minors are completed within the 4-year BTech without needing to extend the programme in most cases.
