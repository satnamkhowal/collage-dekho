Hostel

There are different hostels in Sanskriti University for boys and girls. The hostels are equipped with some of the most modern facilities for students. The university hostels have air-conditioned rooms and metal detector machines at the entrance which assures top-class security for the students who come to study at Sanskriti University from different parts of India. Adding to the security features, the hostels are under the surveillance of CCTV cameras. The Sanskriti University hostels are well-maintained, clean and hygienic. It has dedicated study rooms and recreational spaces inside the hotels such as indoor games to give the residents a homely feeling and a suitable environment to study.

Campus Safety

In order to ensure the safety of its students, faculty and staff, Sanskriti University has taken the following strict measures:

  • Sanskriti campus including hostels is secured by high boundary wall with barbed wire to prevent unauthorized entries.
  • The campus has only one entry gate manned 24x7 by armed security guards.
  • Bags and belongings of the students and the visitors are checked manually by the guards.
  • Metal detector is also provided at the entrance.
  • Electronic register is maintained for all visitors and unknown entrants at the entry point. Details of visitors along with their identity proof and contact numbers are examined and recorded in the electronic register at the entrance.
  • CCTV cameras are provided at strategic points throughout the campus.
  • Canteen, mess, etc. are closely monitored for cleanliness, hygienic, etc. and necessary action is taken whenever required.
  • Students are provided ID cards and displaying of such cards in the campus is compulsory.
  • Special security arrangements are made at the girls’ hostel. No male is allowed to enter the girls’ hostel. Girl students are not allowed to leave/enter the hostel after specified hours.
