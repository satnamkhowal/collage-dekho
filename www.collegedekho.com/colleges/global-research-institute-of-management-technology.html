About GRIMT Yamunanagar

Global Research Institute of Management and Technology, Yamunanagar, formerly known as GRIMT, was established in Yamunanagar, India, in 2008 as a management and technology institute. GRIMT is approved by AICTE, Affiliated by Kurukshetra University, Kurukshetra and Haryana State Board of Technical Education (HSBTE), Panchkula (Haryana). GRIMT has received approval from AICTE, PCI, and DTE.  The Institute has inked memorandums of understanding (MOUs) with ISDR (India) and other Indian and international institutions.

Global Research Institute of Management & Technology in Yamunanagar courses offered are B.Tech, M.Tech, B.Pharma, BBA, BCA, and Diploma programmes. Admissions at GRIMT are merit-based. JEE Main/HSTES are accepted for Global Research Institute of Management & Technology Yamunanagar admission.  

GRIMT placement has recorded 200 students getting placed in 2021, with the top package being Rs 8 lakhs per annum and the average package being Rs 2 lakhs per annum. Several notable organizations, including IBM, Maruti Suzuki, P & R Group, DLF, TATA, UKB, ICICI, Kotak, and Deloitte, visit the school to recruit students. GRIMT has its own sports grounds which includes Football, Basketball, Badminton and Volleyball ground. It has a parking area, canteen facility and Gymnasium in a well spread lush green campus.

Global Research Institute of Management and Technology Courses and Admissions 2026

Global Research Institute of Management and Technology offers several UG, PG and diploma courses. The popular GRIMT courses include BTech, BBA, BCA, MTech, MBA, DMLT, etc. Students can specialize in CSE, CE, ME, etc. under diploma, BTech and MTech programs. Global Research Institute of Management and Technology admissions are based on the merit of the qualifying examination. For UG and diploma programs candidates must be class 12 pass with a minimum of 45% score. Students holding a bachelor’s degree are eligible to apply for PG admissions. GRIMT Yamunanagar Scholarships

The institute provides multiple scholarships to the students.

At the Global Research Institute of Management and Technology Yamunanagar scholarship is provided based on the merit scores of students:

Mata Savitri Devi Beti Bacho Beti Padhao Merit Scholarship

This scholarship will be granted to the females applying to the Institute. The amount of the scholarship will be Rs 5100/- (L/S One time) and will be distributed along with the merit certificate during the college's annual day.

Academic Scholarship

Merit-based scholarships, which range from 25% fee waivers to 100% fee waivers, are available for undergraduate and postgraduate programs based on past academic performance.

Financial Aid

Scholarships are awarded solely on the basis of the financial standing of a student's family and past academic performance. Applicants must have a family income of at least 2.5 lac per year. Family income is between 2.5 to 5 lac per year.

The Lala Premchand Special Meritorious/Competitions

The scholarship is offered to highly qualified candidates and students completing competitive examinations like GATE, IES/HES/IAS and passing the competitive exams at least with a valid score. The scholarship is valued at INR 11000/- (L/S One Thousand).
