FAQs about Mangalmay Group of Institutions

Is the Mangalmay Group of Institutions good?

The Mangalmay Group of Institutions Greater Noida is a NAAC-accredited engineering and management institute. It provides quality education in streams like engineering, commerce, management, etc. The placement rate at the institute is 95% and the highest package offered goes up to Rs 37 lakh per annum. Reputed firms like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Genpact, and the like visit the campus to hire students during the placement drive. The facilities available at the institute include hostels, transportation, wi-fi, labs and libraries, etc. Further, it has been ranked in the AAA category by the Business and Management Chronicle.

Does Mangalmay Group of Institutions have any international collaborations?

Yes, the Mangalmay Group of Institutions has achieved more than 40 national and international collaborations in the last 5 years. The most popular international collaborations that the institute has are with the International Business School, in Hungary and the Australia National Institute of Management and Technology which is affiliated with the University of Southern Queensland. Apart from this, some of the national collaborations of the institute include The Times of India, Edu4Sure, IBM SkillsBuild, SkillOxide, and, EduNet.

Is Mangalmay Group of Institutions private or government?

Mangalmay Group of Institutions Greater Noida is a premier private engineering and management institute. It was established in 2002 under the Mangalmay Group and is accredited by the NAAC as well. Which exams are accepted at Mangalmay Group of Institutions Greater Noida?

The institute accepts scores of national-level entrance exams for admission to its BTech and MBA courses only. For BTech admission, scores of the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) and Uttar Pradesh State Entrance Exam (UPSEE) are accepted. For MBA, scores of exams like the Common Admission Test (CAT), Management Aptitude Test (MAT), and, Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT) are accepted. Based on the combined evaluation of the candidate’s previous academic merit and performance in these entrance exams, seats are allotted.

Is Mangalmay Group of Institutions AICTE-approved?

Yes, Mangalmay Group of Institutions Greater Noida is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) as well as the Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD) of the Government of India. Further, the institute is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) and affiliated with universities like Dr APJ Kalam Technical University, Lucknow (AKTU) and Chaudhary Charan Singh University, Meerut (CCSU).
