About SUS Institute of Computer

SUS Institute of Computer is situated in Mohali in Punjab state of India. Established in 2001, SUSIOC is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. SUS Institute of Computer offers 7 courses across 3 streams namely Engineering, Management, IT..Popular degrees offered at SUS Institute of Computer include B.Tech, BCA, MCA, MBA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, SUS Institute of Computer is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at SUS Institute of Computer, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at SUS Institute of Computer is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
