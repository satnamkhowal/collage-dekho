About Jagran Lakecity University

Jagran Lakecity University (JLU) is a private university located in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. Established in 2013, JLU has been recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a 'B+' grade. As per the NIRF Ranking 2024, JLU has been ranked 151-200 in India and 2nd in Chhattisgarh. The university offers a range of facilities, including state-of-the-art classrooms, laboratories, hostels, and sports facilities.

JLU offers courses at undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and diploma levels. Some of the popular courses include Engineering, Law, Pharmacy, Arts & Humanities, Science, Commerce & Management, Biotechnology, Information Technology, Journalism & Mass Communication, and Design. Admissions are based on merit, with the university accepting various entrance exams for different courses. Regarding placements, the highest package offered in 2023 was ₹10 LPA, and the average package was ₹4.5 LPA. Top recruiters include HDFC Bank, Mahindra, ICICI Bank, and Vedanta.

These attributes make Jagran Lakecity University a compelling choice for students seeking quality education and holistic development.

FAQs about Jagran Lakecity University (JLU)

What are the entrance exams accepted by Jagran Lakecity University?

Jagran Lakecity University accepts a variety of entrance exams for admission to its different programes. The specific entrance exams accepted for each program vary. For B.Tech, the university accepts JEE, for law programme CLAT, for MBA MAT/GMAT/XAT scores are accepted. JLU Bhopal also conducts its own entrance exam known as JLU Entrance Test (JLUET). JLU Bhopal also considers the candidate's academic performance in class 10th and 12th for admission to some of its programmes.

Is there any student club at Jagran Lakecity University?

Yes, there are a number of student clubs at Jagran Lakecity University (JLU Bhopal). These clubs are run by students and are a great way for students to get involved in extracurricular activities, meet new people, and learn new skills. Some of the student clubs at JLU Bhopal are sports and adventure club, photography club, music club, dance club, literary club, dramatic club, and community service club. Student clubs at JLU Bhopal organise a number of events and activities throughout the year. These events and activities are an excellent way for students to get involved in the university community.

Does JLU Bhopal offer International student exchange opportunities?

Yes, Jagran Lakecity University, Bhopal (JLU Bhopal) offers international student exchange opportunities. The university has exchange agreements with a number of universities around the world, including Vancouver Film School, Hochschule Konstanz University, University of Arts London, University of Cambridge, University of Lincoln, Birmingham City University, etc.  Through these collaborations, the students of JLU Bhopal get global exposure under International degree programmes that include credited electives, international teaching months, exchange programs for faculty and students, semester exchanges, and summer schools.

What is the JLU Bhopal admission process?

Visit the JLU Bhopal website and create an account. Once you have created an account, you can complete the online application form and pay the application fee. Once you have submitted your application form and required documents, the JLU Bhopal admissions team will shortlist candidates based on their academic qualifications and entrance test scores (if applicable). Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview. Once the interview process is complete, the JLU Bhopal admissions team will offer admission to selected candidates.

Does JLU Bhopal provide a hostel facility for international students?

Yes, Jagran Lakecity University, Bhopal (JLU Bhopal) provides its students private rental and shared accommodation facilities. The hostels are well-equipped with all modern amenities, including air conditioning, laundry facilities, and wi-fi. The hostel fee starts from $2775 to $1350. In addition to the hostel rent, students may need to provide additional expenses for gas, electricity, telephone connection (optional), and internet connection (optional).

What extracurricular activities are conducted at JLU?

Students at JLU are exposed to many extracurricular activities including cultural events, sports activities etc. The university also has many Student Clubs, which are perfect to cultivate hobbies among like-minded people and enhance skills. The university also has its own campus radio and blog, both of which have active participation of the students.

How is it to study at JLU?

JLU is one of the leading private universities in Central India where academic excellence is given the topmost priority. Plus, at JLU, students live and study in a global environment with access to some of the best facilities and technology to aid their studies and research.

What is the JLU Bhopal fees for MBA programme?

JLU Bhopal offers a postgraduate programme of 2 years with five specialisations, i.e. marketing, finance, HR, business analytics, and entrepreneurship. JLU Bhopal MBA fees stand at Rs 1,80,000 per year for Indian students. Whereas for International students, the fee stands at $4,670. For admission to the MBA programme, the candidate must have a minimum mark of 60% in the qualifying exam, followed by a personal interview. Exams accepted by JLU are JLUET/ CAT/MAT and other management-approved entrance examinations.

What are the scholarships offered by Jagran Lakecity University?

JLU Bhopal offers scholarships in three broad categories, i.e. based on academic records, sports scholarship, and chancellor scholarship. Academic scholarships are provided on the basis of marks scored by candidates in entrance exams such as CAT/XAT/MAT/CLAT/CUET/LSAT/AILET/NID & NIFT AIR, etc.  Whereas sports scholarship is award to students who is recognized by Indian Olympic Association or BCCI. In addition to JLU scholarships, the university also offeres education loan and fee concession schemes.

Jagran Lakecity University (JLU) 2026 Admission Dates:

Jagran Lakecity University admission Selection Process

Candidates should meet the eligibility for the respective course applied at Jagran Lakecity University. Scores of the relevant entrance exams are considered followed by an interview depending on the programme applied. There is no requirement for an entrance exam for most courses at the JLU Bhopal

JLU Bhopal Application Process

Applicants can fill out the online application form available on the JLU official website at home or from the admission desk at the Jagran Lakecity University Bhopal. Application forms can be filled by both Indian as well as International students

JLU Application Fee and Payment Method

The JLU Bhopal application fee is Rs.1100 which can be paid through credit card, debit card or bank payment gateways. For lateral entry admission a demand draft payable to ‘Jagran Lakecity University, Bhopal’ is to be made

Important Dates

The application is available on the JLU website. The last date to register is 20th January 2023. The JLU entrance exam conducted by the University, JLUET is to be held on 22nd January 2023

Counselling Centres

The JLU admission process is handled by the University and counselling is held on campus

Lateral Entry or Migration

Students transferring from other colleges can enter Jagran Lakecity University Bhopal, the 3rd or 5th semester based on the program. For more information, you can check the ‘Study at JLU’ tab on the official website

Documents Required

Mark sheets of 10th and 12th for UG courses. Marksheets of 10th, 12th and graduation for PG courses. Recent passport photographs and a copy of Aadhaar. Copies of passport, visa and financial certification are needed in case of International students at JLU Bhopal 

How to Apply to Jagran Lakecity University

Visit the Jagran Lakecity University official website and register by filling in the details. Proceed with email verification and then fill out the JLU Bhopal application form. Then proceed with the payment and final submission of the form. 

Exams Accepted

CLAT, LSAT, CLAT PG, AILET PG, JEE, JLU Bhopal entrance test JLUET based on the course applied
