About Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU), Coimbatore

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU), Coimbatore, established in 1971, stands as one of India’s top agricultural universities with a legacy tracing back to the Madras Agricultural College founded in 1906. Spanning over 1,350 acres, TNAU operates 15 constituent colleges and 40 research stations across Tamil Nadu, offering education, research, and extension services in agriculture and allied sciences. The university is affiliated with the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) and consistently ranks among India’s top institutions for agricultural studies.

In NIRF Ranking 2025, TNAU secured the 6th position in the Agriculture category with NIRF Score 67.06 , further strengthening its academic reputation. It also ranks within the 251–300 range globally in QS Agriculture & Forestry rankings. These accolades reflect its excellence in agricultural innovation, sustainability, and technological research.

TNAU offers a diverse range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs, including B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture, B.Tech in Agricultural Engineering, M.Sc in Agronomy, and Ph.D. in Biotechnology. The curriculum integrates classroom learning with field-based training, ensuring students gain practical exposure to real-world agricultural challenges.

The TNAU Placement Cell actively connects students with leading recruiters such as ITC Limited, Nestlé, PepsiCo, and Syngenta, offering placement packages up to INR 10 LPA for top-performing students. Additionally, the university supports rural development and entrepreneurship through its Directorate of Extension Education and farmer outreach programs.

TNAU also offers various scholarships including ICAR and State Merit schemes, promoting equitable access to quality agricultural education. With state-of-the-art laboratories, research farms, and international collaborations, TNAU continues to lead India’s agricultural transformation, making it a top choice for students seeking a career in agriculture, horticulture, food technology, or agri-engineering.

TNAU Cutoff 2026

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU) offers admission to its UG programs primarily through merit-based aggregate scores from Class 12, without an entrance exam for state quota candidates. Horticulture, Forestry, and Agricultural Engineering follow similar competitive patterns. These aggregates are calculated as (Biology or Botany + Zoology)/2 + (Physics + Chemistry)/4 out of 200, ensuring fair assessment of science proficiency. Historical trends show stable aggregate cutoffs over the last four years, highlighting consistent demand for top courses. How to get admission at TNAU?

You must take the following steps in order to be admitted to M.Sc. courses at Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU): You must have completed a Bachelor of Science degree in agriculture or a closely related discipline with a minimum of 55% overall marks in order to be eligible for admission to M.Sc. programmes at TNAU. Online applications are accepted by TNAU for admission to M.Sc. courses. You must first register for an account on the university's website before completing the online application. The application form must be submitted with all necessary supporting documentation, including scanned copies of your transcripts from the 10th and 12th grades, your undergraduate degree transcript, and your transfer certificate (if applicable). To admit students to its M.Sc. programmes, TNAU holds its own entrance examination. The curriculum for the undergraduate degree programme in agriculture or a closely related discipline serves as the basis for the admission exam. Interview invitations will be extended to the shortlisted candidates. Based on their performance in the admission exam and the interview, students will be chosen at the end.

Is there a placement cell at TNAU?

Yes, the Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU) has a placement cell. The placement cell is in charge of planning student campus placements and offering career advice and direction. Additionally, the cell collaborates with businesses to create job profiles and choose qualified applicants for placement. A placement officer oversees the TNAU placement cell, which is supported by a group of placement coordinators. For students in all of the university's undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, the placement cell arranges campus placements. Additionally, the placement cell has a database of businesses that are interested in hiring TNAU students. Students have access to this database, which is updated frequently, to look for employment prospects. The TNAU placement unit is crucial in assisting students in getting jobs at reputable businesses. The cell offers students a variety of tools and services to aid in their job search and boost their chances of landing a job.

How many research stations are there in Tamilnadu Agricultural University?

There are 45 research stations spread out across Tamil Nadu that are part of the Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU). These research facilities are engaged in creating and providing farmers with agricultural technologies. The following categories make up the research stations: Agricultural Research Stations (ARS): There are 14 Agricultural Research Stations (ARS) at TNAU. Research on a range of agricultural products, including rice, maize, sorghum, millets, cotton, sugarcane, oilseeds, pulses, and vegetables, is being conducted at these stations. Horticultural Research Stations (HRS): There are 11 horticultural research stations (HRS) at TNAU. Research on a range of horticulture crops, including fruits, vegetables, flowers, and spices, is being conducted at these stations. Sugarcane Research Stations (SRS): There are two sugarcane research stations (SRS) in TNAU. These facilities are engaged in studies on the production and processing of sugarcane. Regional Research Stations (RRS): There are 16 regional research stations (RRS) in TNAU. These stations carry out research on the particular requirements of the farmers in those agro-climatic zones of Tamil Nadu.

When was Tamil Nadu Agricultural University established?

In 1971, the Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU) was founded. Its origins can be traced back to the 1868 founding of an agricultural school at Saidapet, Madras, Tamil Nadu, India, which was later moved to Coimbatore in 1906. It was connected to Madras University in 1920. By providing research outputs, TNAU assisted the State Agricultural Department and took on full responsibility for Agricultural Education and Research. One of India's top agricultural universities, TNAU is renowned for the quality of its teaching, research, and extension efforts. Numerous undergraduate, graduate, and doctorate programs in agriculture and related subjects are available at the institution.
