About Max Institute of Allied & Paramedical Education (MIAPE), Uttar Pradesh

Max Institute of Allied & Paramedical Education (MIAPE) is located in Ghaziabad in the Uttar Pradesh state of India. It was established in the year 2016 as a private college. The institute is affiliated with the Datta Meghe Institute of Medical Sciences. Max Institute of Allied & Paramedical Education (MIAPE) offers multiple courses in paramedical & medical fields. The college offers more than 20 courses across various streams. The Max Institute of Allied & Paramedical Education (MIAPE) Uttar Pradesh courses are affiliated with Indian Medical Association. At Max Institute of Allied & Paramedical Education (MIAPE), Uttar Pradesh, extra co-curricular activities are also kept a major focus. Apart from this, BSc, Diploma, and certification courses are also offered at this college.

Max Institute of Allied & Paramedical Education (MIAPE) Uttar Pradesh has a robust infrastructure and is believed to be a leader in research and innovation.  MHE is backed by 17 Multi-Speciality Max Hospitals, technology-enabled classrooms with 2D and 3D projections, House Skill Labs, Live interactive sessions with Doctors, Internships at leading hospitals, and 100% placement assistance. 

Max Institute of Allied & Paramedical Education (MIAPE) is one of India’s leading providers of integrated world-class healthcare services. The institute is a part of Max Healthcare Institute Limited (Max Healthcare) and has a network of 17 state-of-the-art, patient-centric healthcare facilities (3400+ beds) spread across the National Capital Region (NCR) and states of Punjab, Uttarakhand, and Maharashtra.

Apart from hospitals, Max Healthcare also operates Max (Home) dedicated to delivering the best health and wellness services at the doorstep and Max Lab provides pathology services. Committed to the highest standards of medical and service excellence, patient care and scientific and medical education, Max Healthcare leads with our purpose ‘To Serve and To Excel’ in everything we do. 

To develop a world-class pool of talented & qualified professionals, they have launched three-year Bachelor in Science courses and two-year diploma courses. Their Allied Healthcare courses provide the students with the skills, information, and competencies they need to conduct a variety of medical procedures and grab employment opportunities in the healthcare sector.
