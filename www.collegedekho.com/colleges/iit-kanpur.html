IIT Kanpur, established 1959 as India's fourth IIT, spans 1,055 acres with 18 departments serving 10,000+ students, ranking 4th in NIRF Engineering 2025 and 5th overall among 2,000+ institutions evaluated on teaching, research, and outreach. For 2026 admissions, 1,188 UG seats fill via JoSAA post-JEE Advanced May 18, 2026, while 800 PG spots via GATE 2026 COAP May 2026; total 2,500 seats including 500+ PhD.

IIT Kanpur FAQs

IIT Kanpur vs IIT Bombay: Which is better for research?

IIT Kanpur slightly outperforms IIT Bombay in research output and NIRF research scores, recording 65 compared to 62 for Bombay. Kanpur produces around 1,200 research papers annually, reflecting its strong focus on innovation and academic publishing, while IIT Bombay averages about 1,000 papers per year. However, IIT Bombay maintains an edge in placements, with an impressive Rs 32 lakh average package compared to Rs 26 lakh at IIT Kanpur. Overall, Kanpur excels in R&D and academic rigor, whereas Bombay remains ahead in corporate outreach and job opportunities, balancing research and career outcomes effectively.

How to reach IIT Kanpur from Delhi?

Reaching IIT Kanpur is convenient by multiple routes. Travelers can take the Shatabdi Express, which takes around 6 hours to reach Kanpur Central (CNB) from Delhi. From the station, a taxi or cab costs about Rs 250 and takes approximately 30 minutes to reach the campus. Alternatively, by road, one can drive via NH27, covering roughly 8 hours of travel. Regular UPSRTC buses also operate on this route, dropping passengers at the Kalyanpur stop, around Rs 500 per ticket. The campus is about 16 km from CNB, well-connected and easily accessible throughout the day.

What are the top online courses at IIT Kanpur 2026?

IIT Kanpur offers two key online programs through its E&ICT Academy for 2026 admissions — the MTech in Data Science & Analytics, priced at Rs 6 lakh for 3 years with 150 seats, starting November 15, 2025, and the PG Diploma in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, costing Rs 2.5 lakh for 1 year with 200 seats. Both programs are designed for working professionals and are delivered online through industry-integrated modules. Admission is merit-based, with institute-level entrance assessments replacing GATE. Flexible scheduling and Coursera collaboration ensure quality learning aligned with current AI and data industry demands.

What scholarships are available at IIT Kanpur 2025 for UG students?

IIT Kanpur offers 10 special merit awards for the top 100 JEE Advanced rankers, providing a full fee waiver worth Rs 9.19 lakh along with a monthly scholarship of Rs 5,000 throughout the BTech program. Additionally, under the INSPIRE scheme, around 45 students ranked in the top 1% of their Class 12 board exams receive Rs 80,000 per year as academic support. These scholarships aim to encourage excellence and reduce the financial burden on talented students. Awards are renewed annually based on maintaining strong academic performance and satisfactory conduct at the institute.

What is IIT Kanpur cutoff 2025 for BTech CSE (General)?

In JoSAA Round 6, IIT Kanpur’s closing rank was 271 for top BTech programs, marking its strong academic demand among the 54,378 JEE Advanced 2026 qualifiers. The institute also reserves a 10–15% supernumerary quota for female candidates to promote gender diversity in engineering. Admission through JoSAA counseling is entirely merit-based, considering category-wise cutoffs and preferences filled during registration. The consistently low closing ranks reflect IIT Kanpur’s national standing, advanced research infrastructure, and placement performance. Competition remains intense, especially for BTech Computer Science and Electrical Engineering, which close within the top 300 All India Ranks each year.

What was the highest placement package at IIT Kanpur 2025?

IIT Kanpur recorded outstanding placement results with a 93% placement rate, generating over 1,109 offers from more than 250 top recruiters, including Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and Qualcomm. The average package stood at Rs 26.27 lakh per annum, while international offers reached Rs 2.25 crore, highlighting the institute’s global reputation. Many students secured roles in software development, data science, research, and consulting across India and abroad. IIT Kanpur’s strong alumni network, dedicated career cell, and robust industry partnerships continue to attract leading companies, ensuring high employability and excellent career opportunities for graduating students every year.

When does IIT Kanpur PG admission 2026 application open?

Applications for IIT Kanpur’s January 2027 intake will be open from October 21 to November 10, 2026, through the official portal pgadmission.iitk.ac.in. Candidates applying for postgraduate programs need to pay an application fee of Rs 500 for General/OBC/EWS and Rs 250 for SC/ST/PwD categories. The admission process covers around 800 MTech seats and 220 MSc seats across various departments. Selection will be based on valid GATE or JAM 2026 scores, followed by departmental shortlisting and interviews wherever applicable. Applicants are advised to complete registration well before the closing date to avoid last-minute issues.

What is the total fee for BTech at IIT Kanpur 2026?

The total fee for the BTech program at IIT Kanpur is Rs 9.19 lakh for four years, which includes Rs 8 lakh tuition spread over eight semesters and Rs 1.19 lakh covering other academic and facility charges. Students from SC, ST, and PwD categories are eligible for a 100% tuition fee waiver, making the program highly affordable for them. The fees also include access to laboratories, library facilities, and student welfare amenities. Payment is structured semester-wise, and additional expenses such as hostel and mess charges are applied separately as per institute regulations and accommodation type.

How many UG seats are available at IIT Kanpur for 2026?

IIT Kanpur offers a total of 1,188 undergraduate seats across 18 programs, including 940 BTech and 248 BS seats. Admissions are conducted through the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) based on JEE Advanced 2026 results, which will be held on May 17, 2026. The institute does not have any affiliated colleges, and all programs are offered directly on its main campus. Seat allocation follows national reservation policies, with categories for General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, and PwD candidates. IIT Kanpur ensures a transparent admission process emphasizing merit, diversity, and academic excellence across science and engineering disciplines.

What is IIT Kanpur's NIRF ranking in Engineering 2025?

Which university is IIT KANPUR affiliated to ?

IIT Kanpur is affiliated to the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, an autonomous institution of national importance established by the Government of India. As part of the prestigious IIT system, it operates under the Ministry of Education and follows academic and administrative autonomy. The institute awards its own degrees and conducts various undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, science, management, and humanities. Being an autonomous public technical university, IIT Kanpur designs its own curriculum, conducts independent research, and maintains collaborations with top global universities to foster innovation and technological advancement in India and beyond.

When was the college , IIT KANPUR established ?

IIT Kanpur was established in the year 1959 by the Government of India as one of the first Indian Institutes of Technology. Founded with the vision to promote excellence in science, engineering, and research, it received early academic assistance from a consortium of nine US universities under the Kanpur Indo-American Programme (KIAP). Over the years, IIT Kanpur has become one of India’s top technical institutions, known for its innovation, research output, and global collaborations. The institute’s modern campus, advanced laboratories, and multidisciplinary programs make it a leader in engineering and technology education in India.

Is IIT KANPUR a government college?

IIT Kanpur is a public institute of national importance, established by the Government of India in 1959 under the Institutes of Technology Act. It is funded by the Ministry of Education and operates as an autonomous public technical university. Known for its excellence in science, engineering, management, and research, IIT Kanpur offers various UG, PG, and PhD programs across multiple disciplines. Being a public institution, it emphasizes affordable education, innovation, and national development, providing students with world-class infrastructure, government scholarships, and global exposure through strong academic collaborations and research-driven initiatives.
