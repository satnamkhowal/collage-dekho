About CIAE Bhopal

Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering is situated in Bhopal in Madhya Pradesh state of India. Established in 1976, CIAE is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering offers 58 courses across 11 streams namely Engineering, Arts, Science, Commerce and Banking, Education..Popular degrees offered at Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering include B.Tech, BSc, B.Com, M.Phil, MSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.

Can I take admission in Bsc agriculture?

P
Priya Haldar •  Aug-01-2023

Dear Chandani,

You can be admitted to the B.Sc Agriculture programme at the Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering if you fulfil some eligibility criteria. These are:

  • You must have passed the Class 12th examination with compulsory physics, chemistry, mathematics, and biology subjects.
  • You must have obtained a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate in the 10+2 examination.
  • You must have appeared for and qualified for the ICAR AIEEA (UG) entrance examination.

The ICAR AIEEA (UG) entrance examination is a national-level examination conducted by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) for admission to B.Sc. Agriculture courses in various agricultural universities and institutes across India.
