About IIT Bombay

Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay, also known as IIT Bombay, is a public institute established in the year 1958. Located in Mumbai, Maharashtra, the institute is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is also approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). As per NIRF 2025, IIT Bombay has been ranked 3rd under the Overall category.

IIT Bombay offers 80+ courses at the UG and PG levels. At the UG level, the institute offers courses like BTech, BDes, BSc, etc. Meanwhile, at the PG level, courses like MBA, MSc, MTech, etc. are offered. These courses are offered in various specialisations. The IITB fees for the UG and PG courses range from INR 1.13 Lakhs to INR 15.15 Lakhs. The IIT Bombay fees for 4 years BTech is INR 8.59 Lakhs. Admission to Bombay IIT is based on entrance exam scores, followed by the counselling process. The entrance exams required for admission to the IIT Bombay include JEE Advanced for BTech, UCEED for BDes, IIT JAM for MSc, GATE for MTech, etc. 

The institute has a placement cell for the training and placement of students. As per the 2025 IIT Bombay placement report, the highest package offered was INR 2.2 Crore per annum, and the average package offered was INR 23.5 LPA. Some of the top recruiters are Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, Tata Steel, etc.

Most Commonly Asked Question

In which year was IIT Bombay officially established?
IIT Bombay was established in 1958. It was the second Indian Institute of Technology to be founded, initially set up with international assistance from UNESCO and funding from the Soviet Union.
What is the male to female ratio at IIT Bombay?
Thanks to the introduction of female supernumerary seats by the government, the gender ratio has significantly improved. Currently, female students make up roughly 20% to 22% of the undergraduate batches, fostering a much more diverse and inclusive campus environment.
How difficult is academic pressure at IIT Bombay courses?
Academics at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay can be challenging due to competition and advanced syllabus. Students need time management and regular study habits to handle pressure and maintain good performance throughout the course.

IIT Bombay Admission 2026

IIT Bombay admission 2026 for all the UG, PG, and PhD has started. IIT Bombay courses admission 2026 is based on the entrance exam scores like JEE Advanced for BTech, UCEED for BDes, IIT JAM for MSc, GATE for MTech, etc, followed by the counselling process.

For a PhD at IIT Bombay, students must have valid scores in exams like GATE, CEED, or JRF (CSIR, UGC, DBT), followed by a written test and interview. BTech student passed from any IIT with an 8.0 CPI are exempt from GATE and eligible for direct IIT Bombay admission.

IIT Bombay Admission Process 2025

To secure a seat at IIT Bombay, students must follow a clear and structured process based on their course level as there is no single IIT Bombay application form for all programs, so students need to choose the correct admission path. First, they appear for the required entrance exam. Next, take part in centralized counselling. Finally, complete the institute enrollment process.

Overall, the admission process includes 3 major phases: Examination, Centralized Counselling, and Institute Enrollment. To get hassle-free admission, they should focus on key steps in the IIT Bombay admission process, prepare well for IIT entrance exams, understand JoSAA counselling, and complete their IIT Bombay application correctly. The step-by-step IIT Bombay admission process 2026 is given below for all programs:

BTech, BS, BDes, Dual Degree (BTech + MTech)MTech, MSc, MS by ResearchMBA (SJMSOM), MDes (IDC)PhD
  • Entrance: Clear JEE Main to qualify for JEE Advanced (for Engineering) or sit for UCEED (for Design).
  • Counselling: Register on the JoSAA portal for B.Tech/B.S. or the UCEED portal for B.Des.
  • Seat Allocation: Allocation occurs over 5–6 rounds based on your All India Rank (AIR) and choice preferences.
  • Confirmation: Pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) online to "Freeze" your seat.
  • Entrance: Secure a valid score in GATE (M.Tech/M.S.) or IIT JAM (M.Sc.).
  • Portal Registration: Apply directly via the IIT Bombay PG Portal and simultaneously register on COAP (for M.Tech) or JOAPS (for M.Sc.).
  • Direct vs. Interview: M.Sc. seats are allotted by rank. M.Tech seats may require an additional departmental written test or interview.
  • Acceptance: Accept the offer on the COAP/JOAPS portal to secure the seat.
  • Shortlisting: Based on CAT (for MBA) or CEED (for M.Des) scores.
  • Evaluation: Shortlisted candidates are called for a Personal Interview (PI). M.Des candidates must also present a Design Portfolio.
  • Final Merit: The list is generated using a weighted average of exam scores, interview performance, and academic/work profile.
  • Application: Submit a Research Proposal and SOP through the IITB Academic portal.
  • Screening: Departments' shortlist based on GATE/JRF scores and research alignment.
  • Selection: Mandatory Written Test followed by a faculty Interview to defend your research interests.

Most Commonly Question Asked

What happens if I fall sick during my semester exams?
You must immediately notify the academic office and submit a valid medical certificate from the IIT Bombay Hospital. The institute allows students with genuine medical emergencies to write re-examinations later without facing severe academic penalties.
Do I need a local guardian in Mumbai for admission?
No, having a local guardian in Mumbai is not mandatory. The institute handles all student welfare through dedicated hostel wardens, faculty advisors, and the on-campus hospital, ensuring complete support for outstation students during their degree.
Can I revert to my previous seat after choosing float?
No. If you select "Float" and are upgraded to a higher preference in the next round, your previous seat is automatically cancelled and offered to someone else. You cannot reclaim an old seat after an upgrade.

IIT Bombay FAQs

What is IIT Bombay eligibility criteria for B.Tech admission?

Candidates who wish to apply for Indian Institute of Technology Bombay B.Tech admission need to pass Class 12 from a recognised board. Further, they need to qualify JEE Main first and then JEE Advanced for IIT Bombay B.Tech admission.

What are the different facilities available at IIT Bombay campus?

IIT Mumbai campus has all the facilities to provide a learning and comfortable environment to students. The institute has individual hostels for boys and girls students, well stocked library, smart classrooms, fully air conditioned auditorium, indoor and outdoor sports facilities, medical centre etc.

Is IIT Bombay an Institute of National Importance?

Yes, the Indian parliament in 1961 declared IIT Bombay as ‘Institute of National Importance.’ Being an Institute of National Importance, IIT Bombay receives higher funding from the government and has better research facilities for students.

What is the campus size of IIT Bombay?

IIT Bombay campus covers an area of 550+ acres. The college campus is lush green, is well equipped and has modern infrastructure. Some of the basic facilities available at IIT Bombay campus are well stocked library, auditorium, smart classrooms etc.

Can I get admission in IIT Bombay with JEE Advanced?

Yes, the institute accepts JEE Advanced scores for admission in B.Tech and B.Tech + M.Tech integrated courses only. IIT Bombay admission to other courses such as MBA, M.Sc, M.Des etc. is done based on entrance exams scores like CAT, IIT JAM, CEED etc.

Is IIT Bombay a government college or private?

Indian Institute of Technology Bombay is a government college. The institute was established in the year 1958 and it is the first college built with foreign assistance in India. IIT Bombay offers UG, PG, integrated and doctoral courses.

When was the college , IITB , Mumbai established ?

IITB , Mumbai was established in the year 1958.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Can outsiders or tourists visit the IIT Bombay Powai campus?
Outsiders cannot freely roam the academic areas or hostels without prior permission or a valid visitor pass. However, tourists can visit the campus during major public events, like the Techfest or Mood Indigo festivals, which are open to everyone.
Do IIT Bombay hostels provide vegetarian and non-vegetarian food?
Yes, the hostel messes at IIT Bombay cater to diverse dietary preferences. They serve both vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals. Additionally, students can access a dedicated Jain food counter and several night canteens for late-night study snacks across the campus.
Is there a night curfew in IIT Bombay hostels?
No, there are no strict night curfews inside the campus. Students can freely move around the premises, access 24/7 reading rooms, and visit night canteens. However, security protocols are strict at the main exit gates late at night.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Does IIT Bombay offer the National Talent Search Scholarship?
Yes, students who are officially recognized as NTSE qualifiers receive the National Talent Search Scholarship, which provides a direct financial stipend of INR 2,000 per month during their studies.
Are there tuition waivers for reserved category students?
Yes, SC, ST, and PwD category students are eligible for a 100% tuition fee waiver. They can also avail of free messing facilities based on specific financial criteria.
