About Amritsar Group of Colleges (ACET), Amritsar B.Tech

Amritsar Group of Colleges (ACET), Amritsar B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the B.Tech degree program at Amritsar Group of Colleges, Amritsar, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. They must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in the qualifying examination. Additionally, candidates must have a valid score in JEE Main or any other relevant entrance examination approved by the college. Meeting these eligibility criteria will ensure that students have the necessary academic background and skills to excel in the rigorous B.Tech program offered at Amritsar Group of Colleges. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 65,000 - 1,08,750.
