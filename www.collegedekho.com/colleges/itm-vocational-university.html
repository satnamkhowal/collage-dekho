ITM Vocational University Admissions 2025

ITM Vocational University admission to its courses is done on the basis of entrance exam/ merit in the qualifying exam. The college accepts JEE for B.Tech, GUJCET for B.Tech, and ITM University Entrance Exam for BBA scores for admission. Candidates must also ensure that they meet the eligibility criteria for the respective courses before applying for ITM Vocational University admission.

ITM Vocational University Admission Process 2025

  • Eligibility Check: Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria for their chosen program (12th with PCM for B.Tech, Bachelor's degree for MBA).
  • Application Form: Fill out the application form available on the official website.
  • Entrance Exam: Appear for ITM University Entrance Exam on 10th July 2025, if applicable.
  • Document Submission: Submit required documents like Marksheet, Entrance Exam Scores, ID Proof.
  • Counseling Process: Participate in counseling sessions as scheduled.
  • Fee Payment: Complete the admission by paying the necessary fees.

ITM Vocational University Scholarships

The scholarships are provided by ITM Group of Institutions. The scholarships are offered to undergraduate and postgraduate students based on their academic records and their families' financial status. Candidates who aspire to avail of a scholarship may go through the eligibility criteria of the ITM Vocational University scholarship mentioned below: 

  • Candidate must be enrolled in a full-time program.
  • Candidate must have a minimum grade point average of 2.5.
  • Candidate demonstrating financial need or other achievements.

Types of ITM University Vadodara Scholarship:

Need-Based Scholarships: These scholarships are for students with very low family income. To avail of this scholarship, students must complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) and submit a scholarship application.

Academic Merit Scholarships: These scholarships are awarded to students with exceptional academic records. To avail of this scholarship, students must have a high GPA and submit a scholarship application. 

Scholarships for International Students:  This scholarship is only for international students. To avail of this scholarship, students must show proof of international student status.

Scholarships for Women: ITM Group also offers scholarships only to women. To avail of this scholarship, students must submit a scholarship application along with their academic records and achievements.
