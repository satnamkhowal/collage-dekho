About NIT Silchar

NIT Silchar was founded in 1967 and became a National Institute of Technology in 2002. In the NIRF 2025 Engineering rankings, it is placed 40th. The institute admits about 903 B.Tech students through JEE Main 2026 and JoSAA counselling. The 625-acre campus offers 11 B. Tech branches, 25 M.Tech specialisations, as well as MBA, MSc, and PhD programs. The total B. Tech tuition fee is around ₹5 lakh for four years, and hostel fees range from ₹30,000 to ₹45,000 per year. In 2024, 520 B.Tech students were placed with an average package of ₹13.54 LPA and the highest package of ₹52.89 LPA from Amazon. About 200 companies offered 768 job roles. Admissions are merit-based: 75% marks in Class 12 (PCM) for B.Tech, GATE for M.Tech, and CAT for MBA. There is also a 40% home-state quota for Assam students.

NIT Silchar Fees Structure

NIT Silchar’s B.Tech program has a total tuition fee of about ₹5,00,000 over 8 semesters, charged at ₹62,500 per semester. NIT Silchar FAQs

What courses are offered at NIT Silchar?

NIT Silchar offers a wide range of academic programs across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels. The institute provides 11 B.Tech branches with 903 seats (including female supernumerary), 25 M.Tech specialisations with 500+ seats via CCMT, an MBA program with 60 seats, MSc programs via CCMN, and PhD courses across 10 departments. Popular B.Tech branches include Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering. The curriculum emphasises research, innovation, and industry collaboration. Admission is based on national-level exams like JEE Main, GATE, and CAT, ensuring merit-based entry. The institute’s multidisciplinary offerings make it a leading technical education hub in India.

Does NIT Silchar have a gym?

Yes, NIT Silchar has a fully equipped gymnasium for students, faculty, and staff. The gym features modern workout equipment for cardio and strength training. In addition, the institute promotes a healthy lifestyle through sports facilities like cricket grounds, football fields, basketball courts, and indoor games. Students can also join fitness clubs and participate in inter-NIT sports events. The gym is open during convenient hours and is maintained by the Physical Education Department. With an emphasis on both academics and well-being, NIT Silchar ensures students have access to all amenities necessary for a balanced and healthy campus life.

Which NIT has the best campus life?

NIT Silchar is often listed among the NITs with the best campus life. Spread across 625 acres, the lush green campus features modern hostels, sports facilities, a central library, lakes, and cultural hubs. Students enjoy a vibrant mix of academics and extracurricular activities, with popular fests like Incandescence and Tecnoesis attracting nationwide participation. The peaceful setting in Assam adds to its charm, promoting a relaxed yet productive environment. Clubs in robotics, dance, drama, music, and entrepreneurship ensure active student engagement. The campus fosters a close-knit, diverse community, offering one of the most enjoyable student experiences among NITs in India.

Does Microsoft visit NIT Silchar?

Yes, Microsoft has visited NIT Silchar for campus placements in previous years, offering high-paying roles in software development and related domains. Along with Microsoft, top global recruiters such as Amazon, Oracle, and Goldman Sachs also participate in placement drives. In 2024, the highest package at NIT Silchar was ₹52.89 LPA from Amazon, reflecting the quality of students and technical training. The institute’s strong coding culture, active technical societies, and internship support help students prepare for top-tier companies. While Microsoft’s selection process is highly competitive, NIT Silchar students consistently perform well in such global placement opportunities.

Is DTU better than NIT Silchar?

Both DTU (Delhi Technological University) and NIT Silchar are reputed engineering institutes, but DTU generally ranks slightly higher in terms of placement opportunities and location advantage. DTU benefits from its proximity to major industries in Delhi NCR, while NIT Silchar offers a peaceful, research-oriented campus environment in Assam. In NIRF 2025, DTU ranked higher than NIT Silchar, but the latter offers strong academics, experienced faculty, and excellent return on investment. Students seeking metropolitan exposure may prefer DTU, while those looking for a serene campus life with solid technical learning and core engineering focus often choose NIT Silchar.

Is NIT Silchar good or bad?

NIT Silchar is considered one of the best NITs in India, offering quality education, experienced faculty, and excellent infrastructure. It consistently ranks within the top 40 engineering institutes in NIRF rankings. The average placement package in 2024 was ₹13.54 LPA, with top companies like Amazon and Deloitte recruiting students. The campus provides a balanced mix of academics and extracurriculars, with modern labs, hostels, and clubs. Students often highlight the peaceful environment, cultural diversity, and good peer network. Overall, NIT Silchar is a very good option for engineering and management aspirants seeking academic growth and strong placement support.

What is NIT Silchar famous for?

NIT Silchar is known for its strong academic excellence, research-driven environment, and high placement records. Established in 1967 and upgraded to an NIT in 2002, it ranks among the top 40 engineering institutes in India (NIRF 2025). The 625-acre campus offers 11 B.Tech branches, 25 M.Tech specialisations, MBA, MSc, and PhD programs. Its B.Tech graduates are highly sought after, with recruiters like Amazon, Microsoft, and Infosys. The institute is also renowned for its vibrant campus life, innovative culture, and scenic environment in Assam, making it a preferred choice for engineering aspirants across India.
