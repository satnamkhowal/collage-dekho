About Techno India University

Techno India University is also known as TIU. It is a private university located in Salt Lake City, Kolkata, West Bengal. The university was established in 2012. It is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and approved by AICTE for technical programmes. Techno India University is accredited by NAAC and offers industry-integrated courses in collaboration with leading companies.

Techno India University offers 20+ courses at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Popular courses include B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Sc, B.Des, MBA, MCA, M.Sc, and allied health science programmes. Techno India University fees vary depending on the course and specialisation. Admission to the university is based on WBJEE, JEE Main, CAT, MAT, CMAT, and the university's own admission process, depending on the programme.

The Techno India University campus extends up to 300 acres. The facilities offered at TIU are lecture theatres, smart classrooms, WiFi, a library, laboratories, on-campus hostels, a multi-cuisine cafeteria, canteens, a market, a swimming pool, a bank, an ATM, a post office, and more. Techno India University placement facility is offered to all final-year students.

Techno India University Admission 2026

Techno India University admission 2026 is open for undergraduate and postgraduate courses. Techno India University FAQs

What is the fee structure of Techno India University for B.Tech course?

Techno India University B.Tech courses comprise 4 years with 8 semesters. The B.Tech courses include Computer Science and Engineering, Civil Engineering, Biotechnology, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering. The Techno India University fee structure is the same in almost all specializations of B.Tech. The fee structure is around Rs. 45,000 per semester, which includes Rs. 43,800 (semester fees) and Rs. 1,200 (exam fees).

Is there any scholarship at Techno India University?

Yes, there are many. Some of them are as follows: Swami Vivekananda Merit cum Means Scholarship: Formally known as Swami Vivekananda Merit cum Means scholarship (SVMCM Scholarship) is provided by West Bengal State Govt to the students who passed their last examination with at least 75% marks. CM Relief Fund Scholarship/ Nabanna Scholarship: This scholarship is provided to economically weaker students who have scored more than 60% marks in the last examination. OASIS Scholarship for SC, ST, and OBC Students: The scholarship is provided to the SC, ST and OBC candidates by the Backward Classes Welfare Department of West Bengal. Students must score at least 50% to be eligible for the scholarship.

Does Techno India University have hostel facilities?

Techno India University in West Bengal offers a handful of facilities including hostels, Wifi, a Lab, a Library, Sports, and so on. The university provides a special concern for the well-being of students, where the hostels have state-of-the-art infrastructure focusing on features of safety and comfort. The university has separate hostels for boys and girls with 24*7 camera surveillance.

What are the eligibility criteria for Techno India University for the UG courses?

One who is seeking admission to the UG degrees like B.Tech and B.Arch program must have passed the 10+2 exam with a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate. He/she should have either studied English along with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as compulsory subjects, or for Diploma in Engineering and Integrated B.Tech course, he/she must have passed 10th from a recognized Board with Science and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Other than academics, for Techno India University B.Tech admissions, candidates must have a valid WBJEE/JEE Mains/TNJEE score and a score in the NATA/JEE Mains exam is required for B.Arch admissions.

Is Techno India University good for the B.Tech program?

Yes, Techno India University is good for the students who are opting for the B.Tech program. Techno India University is UGC-accredited and approved by AICTE which offers the highest placements in the private engineering institutions in West Bengal. Moreover, Techno India University has recorded an 80% placement rate.

Is a Techno India University degree valid?

Yes, a Techno India University degree is valid. This is because the university is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), the Bar Council of India (BCI), and the Council of Architecture (COA). Moreover, TIU is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is also accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).

What are the entrance exams accepted at Techno India University?

Entrance exams accepted at Techno India University for the four-year B.Tech programme are WBJEE/ JEE Main/ TNJEE. Similarly, the entrance exams for the two-year M.Tech programme at TIU are GATE/ TGATE. Students who wish to get admission to MBA must pass the CAT/ MAT/ TMAT exam. For admission to Law courses like LLB or integrated BA+LLB, students will have to qualify for CLAT/ TNJEE.
