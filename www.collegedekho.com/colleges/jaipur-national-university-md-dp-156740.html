About Jaipur National University MD

Jaipur National University MD is a 3 - 6 Years program offered in multiple specializations. Jaipur National University requires applicants for the M.D. (Doctor of Medicine) degree to have a Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) degree from a recognized university. Applicants must also have a valid registration with the Medical Council of India or any other State Medical Council. The annual fee for MD is INR 1,49,400. Jaipur National University MD Admission 2026

Admission to the MD at Jaipur National University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Jaipur National University MD admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Jaipur National University MD Application Process

Jaipur National University MD application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MD course at Jaipur National University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Jaipur National University MD application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Jaipur National University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Jaipur National University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Jaipur National University MD application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Jaipur National University MD application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Jaipur National University MD Application Form.

Documents Required for Jaipur National University MD Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
