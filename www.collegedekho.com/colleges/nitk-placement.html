NIT Surathkal Placements 2025

NIT Surathkal placement reports of 2023 has been concluded. In 2023, NIT Surathkal achieved an overall placement rate of 87.22%, with 1,509 out of 1,730 eligible students securing job offers. The highest package was Rs 54.75 LPA, received by a BTech student. In NIT Surathkal placements of 2023, the average package for BTech students was Rs 18.26 LPA, while MTech and MCA students received average packages of Rs 12.64 LPA and Rs 16.76 LPA, respectively. A total of 355 students received offers above Rs 20 LPA.

During the NIT Surathkal placements, among the various courses, the BTech program had the highest placement rate of 93%, with 796 out of 856 eligible students placed. The placement rate for MCA students was 94.44%. A total of 355 students of NIT Surathkal received offers above Rs 20 LPA. The median package for BTech students was Rs 15 LPA, while for MCA and MBA students, it was Rs 13.59 LPA and Rs 8.10 LPA, respectively.

NIT Surathkal Placements FAQs

How many companies visit NIT Surathkal?

NIT Surathkal invites over 100 companies during its placement drives each year. Some of the leading companies that regularly visit the campus for placements include Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Intel, Oracle, Goldman Sachs, IBM, Qualcomm, Cisco, and Nokia Solutions and Networks.

What is the placement percentage at NIT Surathkal?

In 2023, NIT Surathkal achieved an overall placement percentage of 87.22%, with 1,509 out of 1,730 eligible students securing job offers. The BTech programme had the highest placement rate, which was 93%, as 796 out of 856 eligible students were placed. The MCA programme had a 94.44% placement rate, where 51 out of 54 students secured placements. The MTech programme had an 83.05% placement rate, with 603 out of 726 students placed. For the MBA programme, the placement rate was 72.09%, with 31 out of 43 students placed.

What is the highest package in NIT Surathkal for MBA?

The highest package offered to MBA students during NIT Surathkal placements of 2023 was Rs 9.83 LPA. In 2022 the highest package for MBA was INR 14 LPA.

Does NIT Surathkal have 100% placement?

In NIT Surathkal placements of 2023, the overall placement rate was 87.22%. While the BTech programme had a high placement rate of 93%, other programs such as MBA and MSc had placement rates of 72.09% and 54.90% respectively.

Who are the top recruiters of NIT Surathkal?

NIT Surathkal invites several leading national and international recruiters. Top companies that frequently visit the campus include Nokia Solutions and Networks, BEL (Bharat Electronics Limited), Google, Microsoft, Goldman Sachs, IBM, Intel, Oracle, Adobe, Cisco, Qualcomm, and Amazon.

What is the highest package for BTech in NIT Surathkal?

In NIT Surathkal placements of 2023, the highest package offered to a BTech student was Rs 54.75 LPA. In 2022, the highest package for a BTech student was Rs 45.03 LPA.

Which branch of NIT Surathkal has the best placement record?

The BTech course at NIT Surathkal has the best placement record among all branches. In 2023, BTech students achieved a 93% placement rate, the highest across all programmes. Additionally, BTech students received the highest average package of Rs 18.26 LPA and the highest package of Rs 54.75 LPA.

Is NIT Surathkal good for placements?

NIT Surathkal has a good placement performance. In 2023, the institute had an overall placement rate of 87.22%, with 1,509 out of 1,730 graduating students securing job offers. The BTech program, in particular, had a 93% placement rate and competitive packages offered by leading companies.
