VIT Vellore Placements 2025

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) recorded an outstanding placement year in 2025, with over 14,000 offers from 868 top companies across IT, consulting, manufacturing, and product sectors. The highest package reached ₹1 crore, while the average CTC was ₹9.9 LPA, and the median for B.Tech students stood at ₹8.99 LPA. CSE led with near 100% placements, followed by ECE, Mechanical, MBA, and MCA, reflecting strong recruiter demand in tech, analytics, and core branches. 

Vellore Institute of Technology recruiters included Microsoft, Amazon, TCS, Infosys, Deloitte, Qualcomm, and Reliance, offering roles in software, cloud, analytics, finance, and engineering. VIT’s Career Development Centre supports students from Semester 3 with workshops, mock interviews, soft skills labs, internship portals, and alumni mentorship, ensuring high placement readiness. Year-wise trends show consistent growth, highlighting VIT’s stable and attractive placement environment.

VIT Vellore Placements FAQs

What was the median package for B.Tech students in 2025?

VIT Vellore reported a median salary of ₹8.99 LPA for UG B.Tech students in 2025, showing that over half of the graduates received packages at or above this level. This indicates that even average-performing students secured competitive salaries, reflecting the university’s strong placement record, high recruiter participation, and steady demand across all engineering branches.

How does VIT help students prepare for placements?

VIT Vellore’s Career Development Centre (CDC) helps students from Semester 3 onwards with workshops, mock interviews, and soft skills labs. It also provides access to internship portals and alumni mentorship. These support programs help students improve technical, analytical, and communication skills, preparing them well for placements and increasing their chances of getting good job offers.

How many companies visited VIT Vellore for placements in 2025?

VIT Vellore hosted 868 companies for its campus placement drive in 2025, giving students excellent job opportunities. Graduates from all branches were recruited in IT, consulting, manufacturing, and product-based companies. Major hiring areas included software development, cloud computing, data analytics, finance, and core engineering. Top recruiters such as Microsoft, Amazon, TCS, Intel, Deloitte, and Qualcomm offered high-value roles. The placement drive showcased strong industry participation and ensured that students secured rewarding career options across multiple sectors.

What was the highest package offered at VIT Vellore in 2025?

VIT Vellore recorded a highest package of ₹1 crore in 2025, primarily awarded to top-performing students in software and IT roles. Most of these offers went to graduates from CSE and related branches, reflecting the strong demand for tech talent. This demonstrates the university’s ability to provide excellent career opportunities, rewarding high achievers with competitive packages and access to top recruiters across IT, analytics, and engineering sectors.

What was the average salary (CTC) for students in 2025?

VIT Vellore recorded an average salary package of ₹9.9 LPA across all branches. Students from B.Tech, MBA, and MCA programs received competitive offers from leading companies. Engineering graduates, especially from CSE and IT-related branches, secured high-paying roles in software, data analytics, cloud computing, and core engineering. MBA and MCA students also landed strong positions in consulting, finance, and IT services

How many total offers were made in 2025?

VIT Vellore made 14,044 placement offers across all programs, including B.Tech, MBA, and MCA. Students received Dream, Super Dream, and Marquee offers from top companies. This large number of offers shows that many top companies participate, and VIT’s placement system works well, giving graduates good job opportunities in different fields and courses.

Which branches performed best in 2025 placements?

VIT Vellore's CSE students performed the best, with nearly all getting placed and the highest salaries reaching 100 LPA. Other branches like ECE, Mechanical, MBA, and MCA also saw good placement results. Companies showed strong interest in hiring graduates for technical, management, and analytics roles, providing excellent job opportunities across various fields.

Who were some of the top recruiters at VIT Vellore?

VIT Vellore had top companies recruiting, including Microsoft, Amazon, TCS, Infosys, Deloitte, Qualcomm, Intel, HSBC, Bank of America, Reliance, and Wipro. They offered jobs in software development, cloud computing, data analytics, finance, embedded systems, and core engineering, giving students from all branches diverse career options and opportunities.

How has placement performance changed over the past few years?

VIT Vellore has seen steady placement growth in the last three years. The highest salaries reached about 100 LPA, and the average pay was around 9–10 LPA. Every year, more than 12,500 job offers were made, showing strong company interest and good opportunities for students from all branches.
