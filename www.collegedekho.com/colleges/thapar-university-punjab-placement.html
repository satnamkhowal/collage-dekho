Thapar University Placements FAQs

Is Thapar University good for placements?

Yes, Thapar University is a decent choice for students who are looking for a university that offers adequate job opportunities for fresh graduates. This is because, in 2023 Thapar University welcomed 334 recruiters and they offered 1884 job offers. Also, a total of 1774 students were placed in 2023 and the placement rate was 83% for UG courses.

Who are the top recruiters of Thapar University?

A few of Thapar University top recruiters are Airbnb, Deloitte, Samsung, Infosys, Maruti Suzuki, Microsoft, Royal Bank of Scotland, Axis Bank, and Flipkart. Keep in mind that companies from various industries visit the university for offering job opportunities to students.

Does Thapar University have 100% placement?

No, Thapar University does not offer 100% placements. However, the placement percentage of this university comes very close to 100%. For example, in 2023 the placement percentage was 83% while in 2022 it was 96%.

What is the placement percentage at Thapar University?

Thapar University placement percentage in 2023 was recorded at 83% for the under graduate courses. Similarly, the placement percentage of Thapar University in the year 2022 was 96% for the UG course students.

How many companies visit Thapar University?

Over 300 companies visit Thapar University every year for campus placements. In 2023, the total number of recruiters was 334. In 2022, the number of Thapar University recruiters was 395.

What is the highest package at Thapar University for MBA?

According to Thapar University placement report 2023, MBA course students received the highest package of Rs 21.35 LPA. In addition, the average package received by MBA students in 2023 was Rs 7 LPA.

                  Some FAQs about TIET Patiala Placements

                  What is the Thapar University highest package in 2024-25?
                  With the 2025 placement being in the process, the highest package at TIET Patiala has not yet been out. However, last year in the academic season of 2023-24, the highest package was INR 55.75 LPA, offered to a BTech CSE student by a leading tech firm. This figure, reported in the official placement brochure, reflects the institute's strong industry ties, particularly in software and IT sectors, making it a top draw for high-paying roles.

                  What is the Thapar University average package for CSE?
                  The average package for BTech CSE at Thapar University has steadily risen over the past five years, from ₹9.14 LPA in 2021 to ₹11.38 LPA in 2025 (ongoing). In 2023, it peaked at ₹11.73 LPA, driven by strong product-sector placements. While 2024 saw a slight dip to ₹11.25 LPA, the overall trend remains upward. Placement rates have also improved, crossing 80% in recent years. CSE continues to be the most lucrative stream at TIET Patiala. 

                  Is TIET Patiala placement good?
                  Yes, placements at TIET Patiala are consistently strong and improving. From 2018 to 2025, the average package rose from ₹8.60 LPA to ₹11.38 LPA (ongoing), with placement rates climbing from 67.5% to ~83%. In 2023, over 1,660 students were placed with an average of ₹11.73 LPA and a highest offer of ₹55.75 LPA. The institute attracts top recruiters across product, consulting, and tech sectors, making it a reliable choice for career outcomes, especially in CSE and MCA.

                  What is the Thapar University placement rate?
                  Thapar University’s placement rate has steadily improved over the years, reflecting its growing industry reputation. In 2023, the placement rate reached 79.20%, up from 72.10% in 2021. By 2024, it climbed to 81.85%, and in 2025, it stands at ~83% (ongoing). This upward trend aligns with rising average packages and expanding recruiter diversity, especially in tech and product sectors. The consistent growth signals strong placement support and competitive outcomes across engineering streams.

                  How many companies visit TIET Patiala for placements?
                  In 2024-25, 711 companies visited TIET Patiala for campus placements, as reported in the official placement brochure. These included top firms like Google, Deloitte, and TCS, offering 1,495 total offers, with a focus on BTech and MCA programmes, ensuring diverse opportunities for students.

                  Does TIET Patiala have international placements?
                  Yes, TIET Patiala secured 75 international placements in 2024-25, as per the official placement report. These offers, primarily in software and analytics roles, were extended by global firms to BTech CSE and MTech students, highlighting the institute's growing international appeal for 2025-26 aspirants.
