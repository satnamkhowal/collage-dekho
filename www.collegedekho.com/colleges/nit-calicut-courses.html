NIT Calicut Courses 2026

NIT Calicut B.Tech courses for 2026-27 maintain Rs 62,500 tuition per semester for general category students with family income above Rs 5 lakh, alongside Rs 61,000 one-time fees for caution deposit and admission. Targeting JEE Main 2026 rank holders, the institute provides 1,042 seats in 10 B.Tech programs, 800+ in 26 M.Tech specializations, 38 in M.Plan, 75 in three M.Sc, 50 in MCA, 75 in MBA, and PhD slots across 15 departments, with UG totals at Rs 5.98 lakh sans hostel.

NIT Calicut Courses FAQs

How does NIT Calicut ITEP fee compare to B.Tech in 2026?

At NIT Calicut, the ITEP B.Sc-B.Ed program has a tuition fee of Rs 7,500 per semester, totaling approximately Rs 1.48 lakh for the full duration, with 30 seats available. In comparison, the B.Tech program has a tuition fee of Rs 62,500 per semester, amounting to around Rs 6.58 lakh for the entire course, offering 1,042 seats across 10 specializations. The ITEP program focuses on integrated teacher education, blending science and pedagogy, while the B.Tech program emphasizes technical and engineering education, reflecting diverse academic pathways available at the institute for students with different career goals.

What one-time fees apply to all NIT Calicut courses in 2026?

NIT Calicut charges approximately Rs 61,000 as a one-time payment covering the caution deposit, admission fee, and development charges applicable to all UG, PG, and PhD programs. This amount is paid at the time of admission, in addition to the regular tuition and hostel fees. The caution deposit is refundable upon completion of the program, subject to clearance of dues. These charges help maintain campus infrastructure, laboratories, and student facilities, ensuring a high-quality academic and residential environment. NIT Calicut’s transparent fee structure supports accessibility while maintaining world-class educational and research standards across all levels of study.

What is the total fee for NIT Calicut B.Tech CSE 2026?

The total fee for the B.Tech program at NIT Calicut for General category students is approximately Rs 6.58 lakh for the entire four-year duration. This amount includes around Rs 2.50 lakh towards tuition fees, along with additional charges for hostel, examination, and other facilities. The program offers 132 seats, providing opportunities for students to pursue high-quality technical education in a premier national institute. NIT Calicut’s strong academic curriculum, modern infrastructure, and excellent placement record make it one of the top choices for engineering aspirants across India seeking well-rounded professional development.

Are there fee waivers for SC/ST students at NIT Calicut 2026?

Yes, NIT Calicut provides a full tuition fee exemption for eligible students across all undergraduate (UG) and postgraduate (PG) programs, along with a reduction in other institute fees. This financial support can help students save up to Rs 2.50 lakh during their B.Tech course. The exemption is mainly applicable to students from economically weaker sections, reserved categories, and those meeting specific government criteria. By offering this benefit, NIT Calicut ensures that financial constraints do not hinder meritorious students from accessing quality education, promoting inclusivity and equal academic opportunities across diverse social and economic backgrounds.

What is the NIT Calicut M.Tech fee for CCMT admits in 2026?

The tuition fee for M.Tech programs at NIT Calicut is Rs 35,000 per semester, amounting to approximately Rs 2.32 lakh for the entire two-year duration, excluding hostel and other charges. The institute offers over 800 seats across 26 specializations in various engineering and technology disciplines. Each program is designed to provide advanced technical knowledge, research exposure, and hands-on learning opportunities. With expert faculty, state-of-the-art laboratories, and strong industry collaborations, NIT Calicut’s M.Tech programs prepare students for successful careers in research, academia, and core industries, maintaining its reputation as one of India’s leading technical institutions.

How many B.Tech courses and total seats does NIT Calicut offer in 2026?

NIT Calicut offers 10 B.Tech specializations through JoSAA counseling, with a total intake of 1,042 seats. Popular branches include Biotechnology with 40 seats and Production Engineering with 66 seats, along with other core and emerging disciplines such as Computer Science, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering. Each program is designed to provide a strong academic foundation combined with practical learning and research exposure. With modern laboratories, expert faculty, and excellent placement records, NIT Calicut ensures holistic development and industry readiness for students aspiring to build successful careers in engineering and technology.

What scholarships cover NIT Calicut fees for EWS students in 2026?

NIT Calicut offers a tuition fee waiver scheme to support students from economically weaker backgrounds. Students with an annual family income below Rs 1 lakh receive full tuition fee remission across all undergraduate programs. Those with a family income between Rs 1 lakh and Rs 5 lakh are eligible for a two-thirds tuition fee reduction, paying only Rs 41,667 per semester. This financial assistance program ensures that deserving students can pursue quality technical education without financial barriers, promoting inclusivity and equal opportunities for learners from diverse economic backgrounds at one of India’s premier engineering institutions.

Is hostel fee included in NIT Calicut course fees for 2026?

No, the hostel fee at NIT Calicut is charged separately from the academic fees. Students are required to pay around Rs 12,000 per semester for hostel accommodation, in addition to the academic charges. For general category B.Tech students, the tuition fee is approximately Rs 62,500 per semester, excluding other expenses such as examination, library, and mess charges. The institute provides well-maintained hostels with essential facilities to ensure a comfortable stay. By keeping hostel costs reasonable, NIT Calicut enables students from across the country to access quality education and campus life without excessive financial burden.

What is the NIT Calicut MBA fee structure for 2026?

The tuition fee for the MBA program at NIT Calicut is Rs 75,000 per semester for regular admissions, amounting to approximately Rs 3.22 lakh over the two-year duration. The program offers around 75 seats with specializations in Finance and Marketing, providing students with strong managerial, analytical, and leadership skills. Designed to meet modern industry demands, the curriculum combines classroom learning with case studies, projects, and internships. NIT Calicut’s School of Management Studies ensures excellent placement support, with graduates securing roles in leading companies across finance, consulting, and marketing sectors, making it a preferred choice for management aspirants.

Can foreign students afford NIT Calicut M.Sc fees in 2026?

Yes, for international students applying through the Study in India (SII) or Indian Council for Cultural Relations (ICCR) programs, NIT Calicut charges a tuition fee ranging from $525 to $1,050 per semester, depending on whether the student is from a SAARC or Non-SAARC country. The institute offers a total of 75 seats across M.Sc. programs in Chemistry, Mathematics, and Physics. These programs provide advanced theoretical and research-based learning supported by modern laboratories and expert faculty, enabling global students to gain high-quality education and exposure at one of India’s leading technical institutions.
