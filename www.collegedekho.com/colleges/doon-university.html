About Doon University

Doon University (DU) was established in 2005 and is located in the lush green Shivalik range of Uttarakhand. The university has a campus area of 22.26 acres and is a State Government University. It is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is a public state university. The Government of Uttarakhand established the university under the Doon University Act (Uttaranchal Adhiniyam Sankhya 18). NAAC has given the university a B++ grade. The Doon University ranking as per the EduRank was 6 out of 16 in Dehradun, 14 out of 33 in Uttarakhand, and 518 out of 876 in India.

The university offers around 56 courses in various fields. The Doon University courses include UG, PG, and, Doctoral programmes in streams of management, sciences, design, media, language, etc. The popular degrees one can opt for are B.Des, BA, B.Sc, M.Sc, MA, MBA, M.Tech, etc. The Doon University fees for each course are affordable ranging from Rs 34,000 to Rs 74,000 per annum approximately.

The Doon University course admissions are based on both the candidate's past academic merit and entrance exam scores. The exams accepted include the university-conducted entrance test and also other national-level exams such as GATE. For M.Tech admissions, the GATE scores are accepted.

The Doon University fee structure is designed for the benefit of students. The Doon University faculty is well-qualified.

The Doon University placements have a great record of students being hired by reputed firms like Amazon, Infosys, Reliance and Policy Bazaar. In the recent placements, 144 students bagged placement offers through the campus placements.

Doon University Courses and Admission 2026

Doon University FAQs

What languages are offered by the Doon University School of Languages?

The university offers Chinese, English, French, German, Spanish, and, Japanese.

What is the Doon University official website?

The official website of Doon University Dehradun is doonuniversity.ac.in.

What is the Doon University email id?

The email id of the university is registrardoon@gmail.com.

What are the recruiters for Doon University MBA placement?

The Doon University MBA placement is provided by the School of Management in firms like the Royal Bank of Scotland, Amazon, Infosys, American Express, HDFC Life, etc.

What is the Doon University address?

The address of the university is Mothorowala Rd, Doon University Campus, Kedarpur, Uttarakhand 248001.

What are the Doon University courses?

The university offers UG, PG, as well as Doctoral courses. These programmes are offered in streams of social sciences, management, environmental studies, media and communication studies, languages, physical sciences, and, design.

What is the Doon University ranking?

The Doon University has been ranked 6 out of 16 in Dehradun, 14 out of 33 in Uttarakhand, and 518 out of 876 in India, as per the EduRank 2021.

What is the Doon University MBA fees?

The fees for MBA is Rs 74,000 for the first year.

What is the Doon University fees for application?

The application fee for general category students is Rs 800 and for the reserved category students it is Rs 400.

Doon University is private or government?

The university is a State Government University which was established under the Doon University Act 2005.

Does the School of Languages offer part-time courses as well?

Yes, a one-year certificate course in Chinese, French, German, Japanese, and Spanish is offered by the School of Languages.

What happens if the exam date clashes?

If a student finds that his or her exam dates have clashed and they have two or more exams at the same time (e.g. NET or elective), he or she must contact the school administration no later than two (2) business days after the official examination timetable is released.

What is the rule of grade improvement examination?

The student must register at the beginning of the semester in which the course is given with the consent of the Course Teacher/Instructor for grade enhancement in the course(s). Students who wish to improve their grades in courses may enroll in a maximum of two courses from each semester (Odd/Even) of the previous year with grades of D or E. A student may not seek to enhance their grades in a single subject more than once. All examinations (Mid or Make-up, End, Practical, Viva-voce where applicable) must be taken to pass a topic. For assigning a grade in a subject, the instructor shall offer fresh evaluation marks (max 20 as relevant). The passing year for the award of the degree will not change in case of improvement of grades.

Can a candidate get admission to Doon University programs without an entrance exam prescribed by the University?

The university generally conducts an entrance exam for availing of all its courses. But for certain courses some different All India basis examinations are held which is enough for admission at Doon University. Otherwise in UG programs, passing 10+2 with an adequate amount of marks is the minimum requirement.

What is the process of procuring a seat in the MBA at Doon University?

A student must pass the GD and PI rounds after being selected through the CAT examination. The GD topic is current events, and students have 20 minutes to complete it. The PI round lasts roughly 15 minutes and includes questions regarding academics, experience, and other topics. A student might also do practice interviews to prepare for these rounds.

What is the process for hostel registration at Doon University?

A student must select Hostel during the admissions process for the University hostel registration process. He or she will be required to complete a hostel application and pay a fee before being assigned a room in the first semester. He or she will share a room at first, but after that, the student will have their own room.

What are the hostel facilities provided at Doon University?

University has a boy and a girl’s hostel. Both have very spacious rooms. The hostel facilities are fantastic. Hostels feature comfortable rooms and free Wifi is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. The hostel is cleaned regularly to ensure a sanitary atmosphere. The food is also of good quality and clean. Students organize a Mess Committee to oversee the quality of the foods and ingredients served. The hostel cuisine changes depending on the day of the week, and dessert is also served after dinner. There is no chicken in the non-vegetarian curry.

How is the campus life at Doon University?

Every year in April or March, the university hosts an annual festival. Other events include cultural festivals, technology festivals, sports festivals, and many others. The library is always well stocked with books, not only from the curriculum but also from other genres such as novels, general knowledge books, and preparation books. The library not only provides journals offline but also has a subscription to all reputable periodicals, allowing everyone to stay current. A basketball court, a shared cricket and football area, and an indoor badminton court are all available at the school. University's theatrical club is particularly good because it competes in numerous events across the country and provides ample growth opportunities.

What are the hostel fees for both men and women at Doon University?

Hostel fees for males and females include: 1. Lodging (per semester): Rs. 3000.00 2. Electricity, water, utility charges (per semester): Rs 1500.00 3. Mess Caution Money (One time- refundable): Rs. 2000.00 Mess charges: As fixed through the catering tender process, Approx. Rs.3000.00 per month

What is the rule of Back paper examination?

If a student receives an F or G in a course, the course(s) must be repeated in the next Semester or whenever the course(s) is offered by taking all examinations (Mid or Make-up, End, Practical, Viva-voce as appropriate). A student must register at the beginning of the semester in which the course is given to clear the back paper in that course. Students are permitted to attend classes and must appear for all examinations. All examinations (Mid or Make-up, End, Practical, Viva-voce where applicable) must be taken to pass a topic. For assigning a grade in a subject, the instructor shall offer fresh evaluation marks (max 20 as relevant).

What should a student do if he/she misses any mid-semester examination?

Students who miss the Mid-Term Examination of any course due to serious illness (as confirmed by an authorized Medical Practitioner), the death of a parent, one's marriage, or any other justifiable reason determined by the competent authority may be granted a Make-Up Exam. It will be taken one month following the mid-term test. This exam will cover the entire course of the subject(s) up to the Mid-Term Examination.

Does SoPS organize some extra-curricular activities?

The Doon Science Club organizes a variety of activities at the school. On National Science Day, the club hosts a variety of scientific and technological events. Aside from that, speeches by prominent professors and scientists are scheduled to inspire the pupils. Students are also invited to tour several research labs to gain knowledge of current research projects in other areas.

What are the career aspects in the School of Physical Sciences?

Students can improve their critical thinking, mathematical problem-solving skills, computing and programming skills, and understanding of scientific data and analysis by earning a master's degree in Physical Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Computer Science). Aspirants with a Master's degree have a promising career path ahead of them. Scientist positions are available at the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre in Mumbai, the Defense Research and Development Organization (DRDO), the Indian Space Research Organization, the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology, the Meteorological Department of India, ONGC, GSI, and other Indian institutions. Computer science students can work in software firms. Those who want to teach can work as lecturers in high schools and intermediate schools/colleges.

Is it easy to get a seat at Doon University in German studies?

It is not difficult to obtain a German studies seat at Doon University. Previous year papers are freely accessible and available on the university's web portal. A pupil should go over them once. Sample question papers are also made available by the university to give students an idea of the question paper format. Some of the questions may be asked again on exam day. Having a good command of grammar and a basic understanding of the German language will help you pass the admission exam.

Doon University provides placement in government banks?

No, Doon University does not offer job opportunities in government banks. In truth, no university or institute offers direct employment in a government bank or organization. To get into a bank, one must pass admission exams and interviews conducted by government agencies.
