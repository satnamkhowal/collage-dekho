Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology & Research is a deemed-to-be university and one of the leading UGC-approved higher education institutions located in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. All the Vignan's Foundation for Science Technology and Research courses are affordable. Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology and Research (Deemed to be University) Courses and Admission 2026

Vignan University admission process for B.Tech is based on the score obtained in the JEE Main. Result for JEE Main 2024 session 1 is released by NTA. Students can register for the session 2 of the JEE Main 2024 exam by March 02, 2024 to become eligible for admission to this university. For Vignan University admission to M.Tech, GATE score card is required. GATE 2024 exam has been concluded and the result is scheduled to be announced on March 16, 2024.

Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology and Research admission to B.Tech/B. Pharmacy aspirants shall be offered on the basis of their performance in Vignan's Scholastic Aptitude Test (V-SAT), an online test conducted by the university. Candidates who have cleared other entrance exams like AP or TS EAMCET/JEE (Mains)/JEE (Advanced) with at least a CGPA of 7.0 in Andhra Pradesh or above 60% marks in aggregate in Telangana and the other South Indian States shall be offered admission on the basis of the same and exempted from taking the V-SAT test.
