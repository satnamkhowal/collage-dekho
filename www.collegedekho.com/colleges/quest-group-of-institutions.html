About Quest Group of Institutions

With the aim of providing professional education for international standards, the Quest Infosys Foundation established the Quest Group of Institutions in 2009 in Mohali (Chandigarh Tri-city). Located in the city of Mohali, Punjab, Quest Group of Institutions, also known as QGI, Punjab, is a private institution founded in 2009. The institute is accredited by the All-India Council of Technical Education (AICTE), a permanent body of Technical Education under the Department of Labor, Government. The Quest Group of Institutions fees structure is very nominal and very affordable for the people of the state.

QGI, Punjab is affiliated with IKG Punjab Technical University, Government. The university is ranked among the top universities in India by the Department of Human Resource Development (MHRD), Government of India. The mind is not a filling vessel, but an unquenchable fire ”- the guiding principle of the Quest Group of Institutions.

Quest Infosys Group is one of the leading, most advanced groups in the field of Technical and Professional Education in Punjab. Since its inception in 1995, Quest Infosys Group has awarded various certificates, diplomas & degrees in the field of technology and crafts in all possible forms of education namely Full-Time, Part-Time and Internet in various parts of the country. 

They have trained and empowered more than 30,000 students and have become one of the leading groups in the field of professional education in the region. Quest Group of Institutions admissions begin each year following the publication of board examination results. Before enrolling for any course offered by the Ludhiana Group of Colleges, candidates should familiarize themselves with the entire admission process.

Students at Quest Group of Institutions are trained to hone their thinking skills and to be given the necessary learning culture to achieve their aspirations. Projects made by students here under the appropriate guidance of the intellectuals who provide intimate knowledge as part of their UG and PG curriculum.

In addition to academics, the institute also provides relevant sports, cultural, after-school, and social activities to boost their self-esteem, build team spirit, and instill strong sportsmanship in the youth. Emphasizing the need for Soft Skills such as flexibility, communication skills, decision-making, team performance, stress management, problem-solving ability, etc. The Quest Group of Institutions placement has the necessary resources to ensure that its students have a good chance of finding employment. It has developed significant ties with industry and corporations that perform annual campus placements and a network of contacts.

How to Fill Quest Group of Institutions Application Form?

Students can fill the application form in hard copy after taking it from the Institute campus or fill it out online on the institute's official website. Students must keep their documents ready while filling the form to avoid any manual errors as this might lead to application rejection while verification of the documents. In the case of the online application, if students have any doubt in a section then they can contact the Institute admission cell via call or email and they will respond soon.

Interested candidates have to apply online for admission to the various programs through their official website. The applicants must select the concerned course while filling out the Quest Group of Institutions application form and payment of the application fee can be made in both online and offline modes.

The steps to apply are given below: -

  • Visit the official website of the institute
  • Under the Admissions option click on “Online Admission Registration”.
  • Fill up the application form with authentic academic and personal details.
  • Select the course you are interested in.
  • Pay the application fee through Online Banking, Credit/Debit Card, Demand Draft/Cheque
  • Upload the bank receipt and Photograph/Signature. 
  • Submit the final application form.

Quest Group of Institutions Application Process 2024

Walk-in Inquiries

Students or candidates who want to see admission in any department can walk into the admission cell situated on the campus and take the Quest Group of Institutions application form. The form can be submitted to the admission office and a nominal application fee of INR 1000 is to be paid while submitting the form which can be paid by credit/debit card, net banking or through UPI as well and if the candidate is shortlisted, they will be reached out via phone or email by the admission team. The candidate will be given an acknowledgment receipt upon successful acceptance of the application form.

Online Application

Since the onset of the corona pandemic, most of the Quest Group of Institutions admission process is now online, and candidate can submit their application via the official link and pay the application fee of INR 1000 via net banking, through credit/debit cards or even UPI system as well. The list of needed documents should also be uploaded on the same site by the candidate. Once the application is sent the candidate would receive an acknowledgment through email and via message as well.

Quest Group of Institutions Placements

The QGI Mohali Placement Cell places great emphasis on strengthening Industry-Institute collaboration. Their goal is to participate in the development of student work by building strong skills and competencies and maintaining relationships with key employees. The Placement Cell oversees the following important student activities:

  • Placement of jobs through campus interviews
  • Work assistance
  • Industrial contacts and photographic activities on behalf of the Agency
  • Industrial Tourism and Education Tourism
  • Student Personal Development

The QCI Placement Cell adheres to the transparency policy and extends the invitation to companies interested in hiring their students. Companies then submit their letter of recommendation/answer sheet and the date of the campus visit is set taking into account the interests of all parties involved.

The Placement cell of the institute takes charge of the student's future and makes sure that every student can procure a job in the relevant field and also receives good remuneration. The Placement cell organizes orientations, training sessions and workshops for the students to train them in the interview process and help them achieve their career goals.
