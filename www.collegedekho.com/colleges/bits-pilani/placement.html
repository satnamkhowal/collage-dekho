About BITS Pilani Placements 2025

BITS Pilani placement 2025 report has been released. As per media reports, the institute recorded an overall placement rate of 54.35% during Semester 1 of the 2025 placement season across its three Indian campuses: Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad. Out of 1,700 registered students, 924 secured job offers from reputed organizations. The overall average package across the campuses stood at INR 28.64 LPA, while the average package for the 2025 batch was INR 31.63 LPA. Key recruiters included Accenture, EY, IBM, General Electric, and Volvo Eicher.

For MBA (Business Analytics), placements for the Class of 2025 are ongoing. As per the latest available official statistics, the highest package offered is INR 21.6 LPA, while the average and median packages are INR 14.36 LPA and INR 14.64 LPA, respectively. Earlier, BITS Pilani recorded a placement rate of 82.75% for First Degree (FD) courses and 73.61% for Higher Degree (HD) courses during the 2023–2024 placement cycle.

BITS Pilani NIRF Placement Report 2025

Engineering UG programs recorded a median package of INR 18 LPA, while for PG Engineering programs, it stood at INR 15.73 LPA. Pharmacy programs recorded INR 13.7 LPA (UG) and INR 5.57 LPA (PG) median packages.

BITS Pilani Placements FAQs

How many companies visit BITS Pilani?

The number of companies visiting BITS Pilani in 2023 stood at 109. Some noteworthy companies in 2023 were EY, KPMG, VI, IBM, etc. The university reported that 279 recruiters visited the campus to offer job opportunities in 2022. Some of these recruiters were Google, Walmart, Microsoft, Amazon, etc.

What is the placement percentage at BITS Pilani?

BITS Pilani placement percentage in 2023 for MBA students was 95%. In 2022, the overall placement percentage of this university was recorded at 71.83%. The placement percentage increases when the number of recruiters visiting the campus increases.

What is the highest package in BITS Pilani for MBA?

For MBA in Business Analytics, the highest package at BITS Pilani was recorded at Rs 27 LPA in 2024. For the same course, the highest package in 2023 was Rs 22 LPA and the placement percentage was 95%. BITS Pilani highest package for MBA in Business Analytics was Rs 31.86 LPA in 2021.

Does BITS Pilani have 100% placement?

No, BITS Pilani placement percentage is not 100% in 2023. It was recorded at 95% for the MBA course. As for the overall placement percentage for 2023, it is yet to be released officially. For 2022, the overall placement percentage of this university was 71.83%.

Who are the top recruiters of BITS Pilani?

The top recruiters of BITS Pilani include top companies like EY, Google, Amazon, Accenture, Microsoft, Walmart, etc. The university invites companies from different sectors like Consulting, BFSI, FMCG, education, etc to recruit candidates. Students can also get placed in top Indian companies like Aditya Birla Capital, Flipkart, BigBasket, etc with the help of BITS Pilani placement cell.

What is the highest package for BE in BITS Pilani?

BITS Pilani highest package for BE is not released separately. The highest package (overall) for BITS Pilani was Rs 60.75 LPA in 2023 and Rs 1.33 Crore per annum in 2022. As for the overall average package of BITS Pilani it was at Rs 30.37 LPA in 2023 and Rs 28.99 LPA in 2022.

Is BITS Pilani good for placements?

Yes, BITS Pilani is good for placements. This is because the placement percentage for MBA in 2023 stood at 95%. Also, the highest package of BITS Pilani in 2023 was at Rs 60.75 LPA. For MBA in Business Analytics, the highest package in 2023 was at Rs 22 LPA. Moreover, top companies like KPMG, Google, EY, VI, Big Basket, etc recruit students from BITS Pilani.

Are there any sample portfolios available for BITSDES?

A
Aarushi Jain •  Apr-01-2025

Dear Student, 

When you are making your portfolio for BITS Design School (BITSDES), keep in mind that although the school does not offer sample portfolios, it does give a lot of direction to enable you to make your own. The most important thing is to concentrate on demonstrating a combination of project diversity, depth, and solid technical skills. To assist you in this regard, BITSDES has posted an effective YouTube video on "How to Make a Portfolio for BITS Design School" from which you'll get tips and suggestions for communicating your skills and creativity successfully.

Apart from the official advice, you may also learn valuable insights from online forums where previous applicants post their experiences. For instance, one applicant submitted product designs, pencil drawings, and computer artwork as part of their portfolio. Although these instances may provide you with valuable insights, keep in mind that they are individual experiences and not official advice from the school.

To get a wider perspective, you can also look at general resources on building design portfolios. There are many websites that provide articles and advice on making instructional design portfolios, which, although not BITSDES-specific, can still give useful tips and ideas for your portfolio creation.
