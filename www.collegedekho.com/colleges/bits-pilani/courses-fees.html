About BITS Pilani Courses 2026

BITS Pilani courses list includes 60+ programs, including Integrated First Degree (UG), Higher Degree (PG), PhD, and Work Integrated Learning Programmes (WILP). Courses like B.E., B.Pharm., and M.Sc. (dual degree) are offered at the UG level, while M.E., M.Pharm., MBA, and M.Sc. are offered at the PG level. BITS Pilani fees range from INR 2–3 Lakhs for WILP programmes to INR 25–31 Lakhs for Integrated First Degree programmes.

BITS Pilani offers full-time academic programs across Engineering, Sciences, Pharmacy, Management, and Humanities, along with research-driven PhD programmes across 14+ departments. The institute accepts BITSAT for B.E., B.Pharm., and M.Sc. (dual degree), GATE or BITS HD Test for M.E. and M.Pharm., BAAT/CAT/XAT/GMAT for MBA, and GPAT/NET/BITS PhD Test for doctoral programs, along with interviews for select courses.

BITS Pilani Courses FAQs

Is BITS Pilani easy to get into?

Admission in BITS Pilani courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the BITS Pilani Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include GMAT, NMAT, CSIR, XAT, BITS HD, SNAP, GPAT, JEE Main, NEET, BITSAT, CAT, JEST, GATE, UGC etc.

Does BITS Pilani offer online or short-term courses?

BITS Pilani does offer an online course for its students. The online course is BSc in Computer Science. It is a three-year bachelor's degree which will cost students Rs 3,10,000 for the complete course. This course is taught in the English language. Class 12 qualified candidates can apply for this course. BITS Pilani does not offer any short-term courses for its students.

What types of courses does BITS Pilani offer?

BITS Pilani offers courses at UG, PG and doctoral levels. These courses are offered in the stream of management, engineering, pharmacy, science, and more. Some popular courses offered at BITS Pilani are BE, ME, MBA, MPhil, MSc, MPharm, etc. Students can also opt for integrated courses like BSc MSc which is a four-year course and students need to qualify BITSAT exam to get admission.

Can I pay my BITS Pilani fee online?

Yes, it is possible to pay the fees for BITS Pilani in online mode. Online fee payments can be made by using Net Banking, Credit or Debit Cards. Students can pay the fees by either logging in on www.onlinesbi.com or making payments through - www.Paytm.com/education fee payment tab.

What are the BITS Pilani courses after 10th?

There are no courses at BITS Pilani that students can opt for after completing Class 10. The minimum requirement for admission to these courses offered by BITS Pilani is to qualify for the Class 12 exam from a recognised board. By completing Class 12, students can opt for UG courses. For PG courses, students must complete graduation in a relevant field to get admission.

Which courses are popular at BITS Pilani?

BITS Pilani popular course list includes BE, MBA, ME, MPhil, MSc, MPharm, PhD, etc. The fees for BE is Rs 5,50,000 per year. Similarly, the fees for MBA and ME is also Rs 5,50,000 per year. Students interested in PhD courses must pay a fee of Rs 87,800 per year. BITS Pilani also offers MEd programme which is an educational stream course. The fee for MEd is Rs 5,50,000 per year.

What is the difficulty level of the BITS Pilani BITSAT?

BITS Pilani BITSAT exam is an entrance test which is compulsory for admission to UG courses. The difficulty level of BITSAT is not too high as the exam contains questions based on the NCERT syllabus for Class 11 and 12. Therefore, students who have recently completed Class 12 exams will find the test not too hard. The syllabus for the BITSAT exam is released every year by BITS Pilani on their official website.

How to get a free seat in BITS Pilani?

There is no free seat available at BITS Pilani. For admission to the courses offered by this university, students need to qualify for the required entrance exams. For example, students need to qualify for the BITSAT exam for admission to UG courses like BE. For PG admission, students need to qualify for the BITS HD exam. However, students who are toppers of their respective boards can get direct admission to UG courses offered by BITS Pilani.

Does BITS Pilani accept 12th marks?

Yes, Class 12 is the qualifying exam for UG courses at BITS Pilani. Therefore, students who wish to get admission to courses like BE and BSc MSc courses must qualify Class 12 exam from a recognised board to get admission. After qualifying for Class 12, students need to apply for the BITSAT exam and get a valid scorecard.

Is BITS Pilani easy to get?

Yes, it is easy to get admission to BITS Pilani as eligible candidates need to qualify for only one entrance exam to get admission. The entrance exam syllabus is based on the qualifying exam syllabus. For example, BITSAT is based on the Class 11 and Class 12 syllabus while BITS HD is based on the graduation level syllabus.

What is the top package of BITS Pilani?

According to BITS Pilani placements 2023, the overall highest package was recorded at Rs 60.75 Lakhs per annum. The overall average package for BITS Pilani courses was Rs 30.37 Lakhs per annum. For MBA in Business Analytics, the highest package was Rs 22 Lakhs per annum in 2023. For the same course, the average package was Rs 15.9 Lakhs per annum.

Is BITS Pilani very expensive?

BITS Pilani is a private university and the course fee charged by the university is not high compared to other private universities in India. Compared to government colleges, the fees are indeed higher. The semester fees for all courses at BITS Pilani except PhD is Rs 2,45,000. Students can pay the fees on a semester basis. PhD fees at this university is Rs 87,800 per year.

What is the annual fee of BITS Pilani?

BITS Pilani annual fees is Rs 5,50,000 for all UG and PG courses. The fee structure of this university is as follows: admission fee is 53,900 (one-time fee), semester one and two fees is Rs 4,90,000 (combined) and the summer term fee is Rs 85,700 (not compulsory).

A
Aarushi Jain •  Apr-01-2025

Dear Student, 

When you are making your portfolio for BITS Design School (BITSDES), keep in mind that although the school does not offer sample portfolios, it does give a lot of direction to enable you to make your own. The most important thing is to concentrate on demonstrating a combination of project diversity, depth, and solid technical skills. To assist you in this regard, BITSDES has posted an effective YouTube video on "How to Make a Portfolio for BITS Design School" from which you'll get tips and suggestions for communicating your skills and creativity successfully.

Apart from the official advice, you may also learn valuable insights from online forums where previous applicants post their experiences. For instance, one applicant submitted product designs, pencil drawings, and computer artwork as part of their portfolio. Although these instances may provide you with valuable insights, keep in mind that they are individual experiences and not official advice from the school.

To get a wider perspective, you can also look at general resources on building design portfolios. There are many websites that provide articles and advice on making instructional design portfolios, which, although not BITSDES-specific, can still give useful tips and ideas for your portfolio creation.
