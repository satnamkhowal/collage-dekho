About BITS Pilani Admission 2026

BITS Pilani admission 2026 is open for BE/B.Pharm/M.Sc through BITSAT 2026. The last date to apply for BITSAT 2026 session 2 is May 02, 2026. For admission to ME/M.Pharm programs, the application deadline for BITS HD entrance test has been extended to April 25, 2025, and the exam is scheduled for May 25 & 26, 2026. The institute does not accept admissions based on JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or management quota. However, Board toppers (state and central board rank-1 holders) are eligible for direct admission without a BITSAT score. 

  • The institute conducts BITSAT (BITS Admission Test) for admission to First-Degree programmes, including B.E., B.Pharm., and M.Sc.
  • For Higher Degree (HD) programs such as M.E. and M.Pharm., BITS Pilani conducts the BITS HD Online Test and also accepts national-level exam scores like GATE for M.E. and GPAT for M.Pharm.
  • Admission to MBA in Business Analytics is based on the Business Analytics Admission Test (BAAT), personal interview, CAT/XAT scores (optional), work experience (optional), and academic background.
  • For PhD Admissions, the institute conducts its own PhD entrance test. However, candidates with GATE / UGC NET/CSIR NET scores are exempted from appearing for the entrance exam.

BITS Pilani Eligibility Criteria for Admission 2026

For First degree programs (BE, B.Pharm, Integrated M.Sc) at BITS Pilani, Candidates must have completed class 12th with 75% in PCM/PCB. For Higher Degree programs (ME/M.Pharm), the eligibility is B.E./B.Tech/B.Pharm with at least 60% marks. Detailed eligibility criteria for BITS Pilani admission 2026 are given below:

BITS Pilani UG Eligibility Criteria 2026

Eligibility for B.E. and B.Pharm at BITS Pilani requires candidates to complete Class 12 with PCM (for B.E.) or PCB (for B.Pharm.). The minimum aggregate in PCM/PCB is 75%, with 60% in each subject individually. There is no age limit for BITSAT 2026. A minimum of five subjects in Class 12 is required, including Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, one language, and any one additional subject.

BITS Pilani revised eligibility criteria in 2026: Students who reappear for all Class 12 subjects in a year other than 2025 or 2026 are no longer eligible to apply for BITSAT. However, CAIE (Cambridge) and IB board students are eligible with A-level or higher-level subjects, respectively.

For BITS Pilani admission 2026 to Higher Degree (M.E., M.Pharm.) programmes, candidates must have a B.E./B.Tech/B.Pharm degree with a minimum 60% aggregate. MBA eligibility requires a bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years) with 60% aggregate and Mathematics/Statistics in Class 12 or higher.

BITS Pilani Admission Process 2026

BITS Pilani admission process 2026 involves online registration, application form submission, entrance exam (BITSAT/BITS HD/BAAT), result declaration, counselling or interview, seat allotment, fee payment, and final document verification. The step-by-step admission process for all programmes, including B.E., M.E., MBA, and Ph.D, is explained below with course-wise requirements at each stage.

STEP 1: Online Registration on the Admission Portal

  • Go to the official BITS Pilani admission at admissions.bits-pilani.ac.in. Given below are the direct links to application portals for the respective programs:
ProgramApplication Portal
B.E., B.Pharm, M.Sc admissions.bits-pilani.ac.in/FD/FD.html
M.E., M.Pharm pdc.edvertek.com
MBA admissions.bits-pilani.ac.in/MBA/mba.html
PhDadmissions.bits-pilani.ac.in/PhD/phd.html
Direct Admission (Board Toppers – B.E.)admissions.bits-pilani.ac.in/BT/bt.html
  • Register using a valid email ID and mobile number that will remain active throughout the admission cycle.
  • After successful registration, the system generates a unique Application Number and Password, which must be saved securely as it will be used for all future logins, admit cards, and results.
  • BITSAT applicants who applied for Session 1 can log in using the same credentials to apply for Session 2. Creating a new registration is not allowed and may lead to rejection.

STEP 2: Fill Application Form and Pay Application Fee

After registration, the next step is to complete the detailed application form.

  • Candidates must fill in personal details, academic records, category details, entrance exam information, and programme/campus preferences.
  • Scanned copies of photograph and signature, & other documents must be uploaded as per the specified format, including correct file size, dimensions, and resolution.
  • BITSAT applicants can select up to three preferred test cities. If applying for both sessions, this must be indicated during form filling. City allocation works on a first-come-first-served basis.
  • Once the form is completed, candidates must pay the application fee online.
  • Payment is accepted only through online modes such as net banking, debit card, or credit card. Offline payments are not allowed.
  • After submission, candidates should download and print the completed application form as proof.

Step 3: Admit Card Download and Slot Booking

Once applications are accepted, candidates proceed to exam logistics.

  • BITSAT & BITS HD applicants must book their exam slot (date, time, centre) through the admission portal. 
  • Admit cards will be available for download after slot booking. Slots are allotted on a first-come-first-served basis.
  • Ph.D applicants receive admit cards only if they have to appear for a written test. Candidates with valid fellowships (NET-JRF, etc.) can directly proceed to interviews.
  • A printed admit card is mandatory at the exam centre and digital copies are not accepted.

STEP 4: Appear for Entrance Examination

Candidates must appear for the respective BITS Pilani entrance exam. The course-wise list of entrance exams accepted are as follows:

ProgramEntrance Exam Accepted

B.PharmBITSAT

Candidates can also practice a sample BITSAT test available on the official website before appearing for the entrance test.

STEP 5: Check Results and Merit Lists

  • BITSAT results include score and merit rank, with the higher score (if two attempts) considered automatically.
  • BITS HD results are released separately for BITS HD candidates and GATE/GPAT candidates, each having independent merit lists.
  • MBA candidates who qualify for the entrance test are shortlisted directly for interviews, so no merit list is released.
  • Ph.D candidates receive written test results and interview calls, while exempted candidates directly receive interview invitations.

STEP 6: Appear for Personal Interview (If Applicable)

  • MBA candidates shortlisted via BAAT must attend a Personal Interview at the Pilani campus. Evaluation includes analytical skills, communication, academic background, and work experience.
  • Ph.D candidates must attend an in-person interview at the Pilani campus, regardless of whether they appeared for the written test or were exempt.
  • No interviews are conducted for B.E., M.E., B.Pharm, M.Pharm, or M.Sc programs.

STEP 7: Preference Filling and Counselling (If Applicable)

  • After results, BITS Pilani opens an online preference-filling window.
  • BITSAT and BITS HD candidates must login and submit programme-campus preferences (e.g., B.E. CSE at Pilani, B.E. CSE at Hyderabad).
  • Multiple preferences can be listed, and allotment depends on merit rank and preference order. 

STEP 8: Seat Allotment and Offer Letter

  • Seat allotment for BE, B.Pharm, M.Sc, ME, and M.Pharm is conducted through multiple counselling iterations. Seats are allotted based on merit rank, preferences, and seat availability.
  • Candidates receive a provisional allotment letter on allotment after each round.
  • For MBA and Ph.D, A formal offer letter is issued after the final merit list is released. The offer letter specifies the reporting date, fee payment deadline, and documents required.

STEP 9: Admission Acceptance Fee Payment & Document Verification

  • To confirm admission, candidates must pay the acceptance fee within the deadline.
  • Failure to pay results in automatic cancellation of the allotted seat.
  • The fee is adjusted against the first semester fee.
  • After fee payment, candidates must complete document submission (mark sheets, scorecard, ID proof, category certificates).
  • Original documents must be presented for verification.

STEP 10: Report to the Campus

  • Candidates must report to the BITS Pilani campus on the specified date with all original documents and photocopies.
  • BITS Pilani is fully residential, so hostel allocation is done after arrival.
  • Hostel and mess fees are paid on campus. Check BITS Pilani Fees 2026 for the complete fee structure.
  • Orientation and academic registration take place in the first few days.
  • Course registration is completed through the ERP system.

BITS Pilani Admission FAQs

What is the fee for BITS Pilani application form 2024?

If a candidate is appearing for just one session of the BITSAT then the application fee is Rs 2900 (for females) and Rs 3400 (for males). If appearing for both sessions of the BITSAT exam, then the fee is Rs 5400 (for males) and Rs 4400 (for females).

Does BITS Pilani offer admission to PG courses without the BITS HD exam?

Yes, BITS Pilani does offer admission to PG courses without the BITS HD exam; in this case, candidates must qualify either GPAT/ GATE exam to get admission for a limited number of seats.

What UG courses are offered by BITS Pilani for admission?

BITS Pilani offers integrated first degree courses (also known as UG courses) like B.Pharm, M.Sc, and B.E. For admission to the aforesaid courses, candidates must qualify the BITSAT entrance test conducted by BITS Pilani across all its campuses.

Is it compulsory to live in hostel at BITS Pilani or book hostel at the time of admission?

No, it is not compulsory for applicants to live or book a hostel prior to admission at BITS Pilani. Students can choose to opt for hostel accommodation if they need one after getting admission. Students of regular courses at BITS Pilani can get admission to hostels by paying the hostel fee.

What percentage is required to get admission in BITS Pilani?

For BITS Pilani admission to different UG and PG courses students need to score a minimum of 60% marks in the qualifying exam. For example, students need to score 60% marks in Class 12 to become eligible for admission to BE. For PhD admission, students need to score 55% marks in MA for admission to PhD in Humanities and Social Sciences. For other PhD specialisations, students need to score 60% in the PG exam.

Which Branch is best to get admission at BITS Pilani?

The best MBA branch at BITS Pilani is MBA in Business Analytics as the 2024 placement data show that the highest package offered to this course’s students was Rs 27 LPA. For BE the most popular branches at BITS Pilani are Computer Science, Civil, Manufacturing Engineering, etc. Fees for MBA and BE is Rs 5,50,000 per annum for both courses.

What is the application fee for the BITS Pilani admission?

BITS Pilani application fee depends on which course the student is applying for. For example, BITSAT application fees is Rs 3,400 for males and Rs 2,900 for female candidates for UG courses. For international students, the application fee is Rs 6,750 for UG courses. For PhD admission, the application fee is Rs 2,600.

Can I join BITS Pilani after 10th?

No, students can get BITS Pilani admission after completing Class 10 because the minimum requirement for admission is Class 12 for UG courses. After completing Class 12 students need to qualify for the required entrance exam to get admission at BITS Pilani.

Can I get scholarship in BITS Pilani at the time of admission?

Yes, students can get scholarships up to 100% at the time of BITS Pilani admission process based on need and merit. This scholarship is called the ‘Merit-Cum-Means (MCN) Scholarship’. It offers top students a tuition fee waiver of between 15% to 100%.

Does BITS Pilani accept CAT for admission?

Yes, for BITS Pilani MBA admission students with valid CAT score cards can apply for admission. If a student does not have a CAT scorecard then they can qualify for the Business Analytics Aptitude Test (BAAT) conducted by the university. The BAAT exam is conducted in the first quarter of every year.

Does BITS Pilani accept JEE Main for admission?

No, JEE Main score is not accepted for BITS Pilani admission to BE courses. The university does not accept any state or all India-level entrance exams for BE admission. It conducts its own entrance exam for admission to UG engineering courses which is BITSAT. Only with a valid BITSAT score a student will be able to apply for BE admission.

Does BITS Pilani have the entrance exam for admission?

Yes, BITS Pilani has its own entrance exams for admission. For UG courses the university conducts the Birla Institute of Technology and Science Admission Test (BITSAT). Similarly, for admission to PG courses, the university conducts the Birla Institute of Technical Science Higher Degree (BITS HD). For MBA admission the university conducts the Business Analytics Aptitude Test (BAAT).

What is the last to apply for admission at BITS Pilani?

BITS Pilani admission 2024 is ongoing and students who wish to apply for the admission entrance test BITSAT (session one) need to apply by April 11, 2024. The session two application dates of this exam are May 22 - June 10, 2024. BITSAT HD 2024 application is open between March 06 - April 19, 2024.

Is it good to take admission in BITS Pilani?

Yes, it is good to take admission in BITS Pilani because the university charges nominal fees for its courses. It also offers multiple specialisations for UG, PG and doctoral courses. For example, BE is available in specialisations such as Civil, Chemical, Computer Science, Electrical and Electronics, Electronics and Instrumentation, Manufacturing, and Mechanical. BITS Pilani also offers placement opportunities for its students. In 2023 the highest package was Rs 60.75 LPA.

Does BITS Pilani offer direct admission?

Yes, BITS Pilani does offer direct admission to its UG courses if the student is a board topper of any state or central government board. Such students do not need to qualify for the BITSAT exam and can get admission based on their merit. There is no other way to get direct admission at this university. Other students need to qualify the required entrance exam and meet the eligibility criteria to get admission.
