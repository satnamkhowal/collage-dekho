LPU Admission 2026 Overview

Lovely Professional University (LPU) admissions for 2026 are now open for undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral programs. LPU is accepting applications through the LPUNEST 2026 (Lovely Professional University National Entrance and Scholarship Test). Candidates must register online via the official LPU admission portal to participate in the ongoing Phase 3 admission cycle.

LPU University offers over 150 programs across engineering, management, law, agriculture, and design. LPU admission is conducted primarily through the LPUNEST exam, which serves as both an entrance test and a scholarship evaluation. The university also accepts scores of national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, CAT, MAT, and CUET for selected courses. To apply for admission at LPU, students must visit the LPU admission website, complete the application form, pay the registration fee, book a slot for the LPUNEST exam, and participate in counselling for seat allotment.

LPU Admission Process 2026 (Step-by-Step)

The LPU admission process 2026 is completely online and includes six steps from registration to seat confirmation. Admission to most programmes at LPU is based LPUNEST for both eligibility and scholarship. Candidates who appeared for respective national-level entrance examinations for admission to few courses must appear for LPUNEST to get the scholarship. Some postgraduate programs, like the MBA, involve an additional personal interview round. Step-by-step process for how to get admission in LPU is as follows:

  • Online Registration: Create an account on the LPUADMIT portal at admission.lpu.in . Enter personal details, contact information, and select the desired program.
  • Application Form Submission: Fill out the detailed LPU application form. Provide academic history and upload a recent photograph and signature.
  • Application Fee Payment: Pay the LPUNEST application of INR 1,000 for the soft-copy LPUNEST Kit or INR 1,500 for the printed kit, fee using a credit card, debit card, net banking, or UPI.
  • Slot Booking: Log in to the portal and book a preferred date and time for the LPUNEST examination. Students can choose a remote proctored exam or a test center-based exam.
  • Appear for LPUNEST: Take the LPUNEST exam on the scheduled date.
  • Result Declaration: Check the exam result and scholarship status on the designated date.
  • Online Counselling and Preference Filling: Qualified candidates must participate in online counselling. Fill in the preferences for the desired specialization or campus.
  • Seat Allotment: LPU allocates seats based on the LPUNEST rank, candidate preferences, and seat availability.
  • Admission Confirmation and Fee Payment: Secure the allotted seat by paying the initial admission fee of INR 10,000. Upload all mandatory academic documents for verification.

Important points for LPU Admission process:

  • For LPU MBA admission 2026, candidates are also required to submit a video essay and attend a personal interview after the entrance score is uploaded.
  • For LPU BTech CSE admission 2026 under the Type-A route, candidates are also required to take a Knowledge-Quotient (KQ) Test plus an interview. 
  •  Candidates who already hold a valid score in JEE Main, CUET, CAT, MAT, NATA, or other accepted exams can skip LPUNEST for admission, but should still consider attempting it for the merit-scholarship benefit.

LPU Admission FAQs

Can I get a refund if I cancel my LPU admission after paying fees?

LPU follows a sliding-scale refund policy aligned with UGC guidelines. The provisional admission fee of INR 10,000 is partially refundable depending on when withdrawal happens — fully refundable if cancelled at least 15 days before the formal start of the academic session (less a small processing charge), and progressively reduced after the session begins. Tuition fee refunds follow the same date-based scale, with no refund granted after the first month of classes. Specific non-refundable components are: the INR 1,000 / INR 1,500 LPUNEST application fee, the prospectus fee, late fees, examination fee, and any benefit availed during the period before withdrawal. Students should download the official refund policy from lpu.in before cancelling and submit the request through the RefundTrackingSystem portal at LPU.

How is the LPU admission process for international students?

International students apply at intadmit.lpu.in, separately from the main LPUADMIT portal used by Indian applicants. The application requires a Bachelor's-level qualification (or 10+2 for UG courses) with a minimum 55% aggregate, an English Language Requirement (typically IELTS 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0, or TOEFL 79+, or PTE 58+), a valid passport, financial support documentation, and visa-eligible health certification. International applicants do not appear for LPUNEST in most cases; admission is granted directly based on academic records. International scholarships go up to 50% of the total tuition fee for the duration of the course. Programmes are offered in 150+ disciplines and roughly 1,800+ international students from 50+ countries study at LPU, supported by a dedicated International Relations Cell and student-services team.

Does LPU have a management quota for admission?

LPU does not run a traditional management quota system the way some other private universities do. All LPU admissions are conducted through a published, scholarship-linked merit channel — either LPUNEST or accepted national exams — and fee structures are fixed and openly published on lpu.in/nest. The closest equivalent to a management quota is the BTech CSE Type-A route, which adds a Knowledge-Quotient (KQ) Test and personal interview alongside the entrance score, and the LPU PET / TABS routes for sports and cultural performers. Anyone offering 'management seat' or 'capitation seat' admission at LPU outside the official channels should be reported to info@lpu.co.in. The university has explicitly clarified that no representative will request fund transfers to personal accounts.

Can I take admission in LPU through JEE Main score?

Yes, JEE Main score is a fully accepted route for BTech admission at LPU. Candidates with valid JEE Main scores can apply directly through the LPUADMIT portal and skip LPUNEST for admission. The JEE Main score also determines a separate scholarship slab: 90+ percentile typically qualifies for 50% tuition waiver, 85–89.99 percentile for 40%, and 80–84.99 percentile for 30% (subject to the latest scholarship cycle). Importantly, candidates with JEE Main can still appear for LPUNEST to potentially qualify for a higher scholarship category — the better of the two scores is used. JEE Main is not accepted for non-engineering programmes; for MBA, the equivalent exams are CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, and NMAT.

What is the LPU admission fee or seat-confirmation amount?

The provisional admission fee at LPU is INR10,000 across all programmes — paid online after seat allotment to lock the seat. This amount is fully adjusted against the first semester's programme fee. Two exceptions exist: M.Sc. Agriculture and MA Hindi/Punjabi do not follow the ₹10,000 provisional fee structure (specific terms apply per the prospectus). The remaining balance fee — semester tuition plus university charges and any optional hostel/mess/transport fees — must be deposited within the deadlines set in the offer letter, typically before the academic session starts in July or August. The LPU refund policy is non-refundable for the prospectus, application, late, and entrance fee components; the main programme fee follows a sliding refund scale based on the date of withdrawal.

Can I take admission to LPU without LPUNEST or JEE Main?

Yes, for selected programmes. LPU offers direct admission based on Class 12 marks for several diploma, B.Sc., BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA, and B.Des programmes provided the candidate meets the qualifying-exam aggregate. However, even in these cases, taking LPUNEST is strongly recommended because the same exam decides the scholarship category, and bypassing it usually means paying the full programme fee. For flagship courses — BTech, MBA, B.Pharm, B.Arch, and Law — admission is strictly entrance-based, and candidates must qualify either LPUNEST or an accepted national exam. Distance and online programmes, run through lpude.in and lpuonline.com, accept direct admission for almost all courses without any entrance test.

How can I get 100% scholarship at LPU?

100% scholarship at LPU is rare and is awarded under specific schemes. The most common routes are: scoring in the top LPUNEST bracket (Category A) in Phase I, ranking in the top 20 of any recognised State or Central school board, qualifying as an orphan with valid documentation, or winning a national / international medal in sports recognised under LPU TABS. Innovation, startup, or hackathon-based scholarships can also reach 100% if proven achievements meet the Bracket-1 criteria. For most students, a realistic LPUNEST scholarship works out to 30%–60% tuition waiver — to push it higher, the score should cross 300+ marks (out of 500) in Phase I and be supported by strong qualifying-exam marks. Continuation of any scholarship after Year 1 is conditional on maintaining the minimum CGPA and attendance set by LPU.

What is the minimum percentage in Class 12 to get into LPU?

The minimum Class 12 percentage at LPU varies by programme. BTech, B.Pharm, and B.Sc. (Hons.) require 60% aggregate with the relevant science subjects (PCM or PCB). BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA, B.Des, B.Arch, BBA LLB, and BA LLB need 50% aggregate. Diploma courses accept students with 50% in Class 10. The university gives a 5% relaxation in the qualifying percentage to candidates from North-East states and Sikkim, dependants of Defence personnel, and wards of Kashmiri migrants. Students with marks just below the cut-off can still be considered for some specialisations through the LPUNEST route, where a strong test score can compensate for low qualifying-exam marks — but only for the eligibility threshold, not for scholarship.

Is LPUNEST mandatory for LPU admission?

LPUNEST is mandatory for admission to most LPU programmes, including BTech, BTech (Hons.), MBA, B.Pharm, B.Des, BA LLB, B.Arch, M.Tech, MCA, and Agriculture courses — where it serves as both the eligibility test and the scholarship-determining exam. For some programmes such as Distance Education, Online Education, and selected diploma courses, LPUNEST is not required for admission but may still be taken to claim a scholarship. Candidates with valid scores in JEE Main, CUET, CAT, MAT, NMAT, NATA, GATE, GPAT, or CLAT can opt out of LPUNEST for admission and use those scores instead. However, the university recommends attempting LPUNEST anyway because the same exam decides the merit-scholarship slab — and most students find LPUNEST less competitive than national-level alternatives.

What are the LPU admission criteria for the BTech course?

To apply for most BTech programmes at LPU, you need at least 60% in Class 12 with Physics, Math, and English. Admission requires you to take LPUNEST, or JEE Main (Phase II onwards). For BTech in Biotechnology, Food Technology, and Biomedical Engineering, you will need Chemistry or Biology instead of English. Specific specialisations within the BTech programme may have different requirements.

Does LPU accept CUET for admission?

LPU accepts CUET UG and CUET PG scores for admission to its undergraduate programmes. You have the option to apply with the scores of CUET or their own entrance exam, LPUNEST. Additionally, scholarships are available for students applying through LPUNEST.

How can I apply for admission to LPU?

To enrol, visit the LPU admissions page at https://www.lpu.in/admission/admissions.php. The application process includes filling out a form, uploading necessary documents, and, for certain programmes, completing the LPUNEST entrance exam. You can secure your place by paying a provisional admission fee. Make sure to review the website for application deadlines and specific programme prerequisites.

Can I be admitted to LPU based on my Class 12th score?

As per the LPU admission process, you need to take LPUNEST, which is conducted by the university itself, along with your 12th-grade marks. Mostly, a minimum of 60% in Class 12th is required (including Physics, Maths, and English for certain programmes). Your 12th-grade performance is important, but LPUNEST is compulsory for final selection.
