Lingaya's Vidyapeeth Campus

The University campus is located in the city of Faridabad at Nachauli on Old Faridabad – Jasana Road. Lingaya has a sprawling campus situated in green pastures and surrounded by pollution free. The total build up area of the university is more than 97500 sqm including 14309 sqm. for other stages of construction. The campus is provided with various facilities including  Academic blocks, Air-conditioned Library, Workshops, Laboratories, Auditorium, Student Activity Centre, Play Grounds, Gymnasium, Hostels, etc. 

The campus of Lingaya's Vidyapeeth is centrally equipped with air-conditioned computing facility that serves a work-oriented open environment generally available in the industry. A 24*7 working Wi-Fi facility is available having a fibre optic-based network backbone. The availability of air-conditioned /non-airconditioned hostel facility at the college separately for both boys and girls provides a comfortable boarding and lodging service for the students. All the hostel buildings are equipped with high-class facilities including Plasma TV (with Dish Connection), guest rooms, common rooms, sickbay and washrooms with running hot water, 24 hrs power backup, gymnasium, library along with wifi. A swimming pool is also provided at the boy's hostel. Students may enjoy the hygienic and nourishing food served at the air-cooled cafeteria. The cafeteria serves vegetarian as well as non-vegetarian food to the students. An Air-conditioned auditorium with a large seating capacity is also available at the campus of Lingaya's Vidyapeeth along with seminar halls equipped with latest LCD projection and sound

systems, for conducting guest lectures, paper presentation, seminars etc. Students, faculty and staff may opt for the air-conditioned transport facility provided by the university from all the major locations in NCR including Faridabad. Medical facility with availability of highly qualified medical officer provides a 24*7 assistance to the students. An ambulance is always available at the campus in the case of an emergency. 

The central library is available at the college campus of Lingaya's Vidyapeeth catering to the needs of the staff and students in full measure. The college library is equipped with a wide range of books collected from various disciplines.  The Library subscribes to around 150 journals and periodicals (National and International). The Library also subscribes to online journals database including Science Direct, IEEE, DELNET etc, providing access to over 17 lac documents of all schools of Engineering. Many other facilities including the computer lab and modern classrooms are also available at the college campus.

Lingaya's Vidyapeeth Hostel & Fees Structure

The University offers separate hostels for boys and girls supervised 24X7 by wardens and vigilant security personnel. Apart from the infrastructural soundness, both the hostels provide sufficient scope for interaction and relationship building. It provides a congenial atmosphere that gives new students optimal scope to know their seniors, learn from them, leverage their knowledge and build lasting relations with them. The hostels also provide neat and green environment with sports facilities for the students to relax and enjoy their stay.The hostels have mess facility where students can enjoy nutritious and hygienic food.

How can I get admission in lingyas University

S
Saniya Pahwa •  Sep-14-2023

Dear Rashi

You can apply for admission to Lingaya's Vidyapeeth if you meet the basic eligibility criteria for your desired course. The application process can be carried out in online as well as offline modes. In order to apply online, all you have to do is visit the official website of the university and fill out the application form available in the admission section. After uploading the needed documents and paying the fee, your form will be submitted. Further, for Lingaya's Vidyapeeth admission in the offline mode, you can visit the campus and fill out the form in person and submit it. Once you have successfully applied, you must appear for the Lingaya's Vidyapeeth Entrance Test (LET). If shortlisted, you will be called for further counselling and admission rounds. 

Hope this helps!
