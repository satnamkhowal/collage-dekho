About OM Sterling Global University

OM Sterling Global University is a private multidisciplinary university offering 100+ undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programmes across engineering, management, pharmacy, health sciences, law, agriculture, architecture, computer applications, hospitality, and humanities. The university provides career-focused education through multiple schools and campuses, with programmes designed to combine academic learning, practical training, and industry exposure.

Popular courses include B.Tech, BCA, BBA, B.Com, B.Pharmacy, B.Sc, BA, LL.B, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Sc, M.Pharma, LL.M, M.A, and M.Com, along with diploma programmes in engineering, pharmacy, hospitality, and teacher education. The university also offers Honours and Honours with Research options in selected undergraduate programmes.

Admission is based on merit in the qualifying examination or entrance test scores, depending on the programme. Course fees generally range from INR 40,000 to INR 95,000 per year for most programmes, making the university an affordable option for students seeking professional and academic education. The university supports students through modern laboratories, libraries, hostels, sports facilities, industry training, placement assistance, and scholarship opportunities.

OM Sterling Global University Admission 2026

OM Sterling Global University admission 2026 is open for undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programmes across engineering, management, pharmacy, health sciences, law, agriculture, computer applications, architecture, and humanities. The university offers admission through merit-based selection and entrance-based processes, depending on the programme and level of study.

Candidates can apply online by completing the registration process, filling out the application form, uploading the required documents, and paying the prescribed application fee. Admission to most undergraduate programmes is based on performance in the qualifying examination, while selected postgraduate and professional courses may consider relevant entrance examination scores as part of the selection process.

OM Sterling Global University Admission Process

OM Sterling Global University follows a straightforward admission process for undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programmes. Admission is based on merit in the qualifying examination or entrance test scores, depending on the course. Candidates must complete the application process, submit the required documents, and pay the admission fee to secure their seat. Follow the steps below to apply for OM Sterling Global University admission 2026:

Step 1: Register Online

  • Visit the university admission portal and create an account using a valid email ID and mobile number.

Step 2: Fill the Application Form

  • Complete the application form with personal, academic, and course-related details. Select the preferred programme and specialisation.

Step 3: Upload Required Documents

  • Upload scanned copies of marksheets, certificates, photographs, identity proof, and entrance exam scorecards (if applicable).

Step 4: Pay the Application Fee

  • Submit the prescribed application fee through the available online payment modes and save the payment receipt for future reference.

Step 5: Appear for Entrance Examination (If Applicable)

  • Candidates applying for courses that require entrance test scores must appear for the relevant examination and submit their results during admission.

Step 6: Merit List and Shortlisting

  • The university prepares merit lists based on qualifying examination marks and entrance test performance, wherever applicable.

Step 7: Document Verification

  • Shortlisted candidates must attend the document verification process with original academic and identity documents.

Step 8: Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation

  • After successful verification, candidates must pay the prescribed admission or first-year fee to confirm their seat.

Step 9: Enrolment and Commencement of Classes

  • Students receive admission confirmation and complete the enrolment process before the start of the academic session.
