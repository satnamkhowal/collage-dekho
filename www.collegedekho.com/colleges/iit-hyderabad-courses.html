IIT Hyderabad PG Courses 2026

IIT Hyderabad offers three types of postgraduate programs for 2026. For MTech, there are 350 seats available through GATE exam with a semester fee of Rs 30,000, and students receive fellowship support. The MBA program has 60 seats through CAT exam, costing Rs 1 lakh per semester. PhD programs offer 150 seats for students who clear NET or GATE exams, providing a monthly stipend of Rs 31,000. These courses last between two to five years depending on the program.

IIT Hyderabad UG Courses 2026

IIT Hyderabad offers four-year BTech undergraduate programs for 2026 admissions with a total of 595 seats. To apply, students need 75% marks in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, plus they must qualify JEE Advanced. Seats are allotted through the JoSAA counseling process. Students can change their branch after the second semester if they score above 8.0 CPI (grade point). The program requires completing 160 credits to graduate and features special aerospace labs funded by ISRO for hands-on learning.

  • Check the platform or website:

Return to the website where the mock exam was held if it was one of such sites (such as the platform of a coaching centre or an educational website). Use the login information you used to register to access your account, if you have one. Look for a section that says "My Results," "Test History," or "Dashboard," since these might include your mock test results and feedback.

After you finish the test, some platforms send you an email with your findings. Look for any correspondence on the outcomes of your practice exam in your inbox (and spam folder).

You can get in touch with the helpdesk or support staff of the website or platform where you took the test if you are unable to locate your results. They ought to be able to help you find your score.

IIT Hyderabad Courses FAQs

What types of courses does IIT Hyderabad offer?

At the UG level, IIT Hyderabad offers B.Tech, B.Des , etc, while at the PG level, IIT Hyderabad offers M.Tech, M.Phil, MSc, M.Des, etc.

Which courses are popular at IIT Hyderabad?

The most popular IIT Hyderabad courses include the B.Tech, B.Des, M.Tech, M.Phil, MSc and M.Des programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, B.Des, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, M.Phil, MSc, M.Des , etc. IIT Hyderabad also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does IIT Hyderabad accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at IIT Hyderabad. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, B.Des and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is IIT Hyderabad easy to get into?

Admission in IIT Hyderabad courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the IIT Hyderabad Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include UCEED, CEED, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, GATE, IIT JAM etc.

What is the top package of IIT Hyderabad?

Is IIT Hyderabad very expensive?

The annual cost of undergraduate programmes is around Rs 200000 for B.Tech. The annual IIT Hyderabad fee structure for PG programmes is between Rs 10000 for M.Phil to Rs 50000 for M.Tech.

What is the annual fee of IIT Hyderabad?

The annual IIT Hyderabad fees fall between Rs 10000 to Rs 800000. Depending on the degree level, duration, and area of specialisation, the fees differ for various programmes. What is the most popular course at IIT HYDERABAD ?

Engineering courses are the most popular courses opted by students at IIT HYDERABAD . Of these, B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) courses are the most sought after amongst both students and parents.
