About NIMT Jaipur BSc

NIMT Jaipur BSc is a 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree at NIMT Global Institute of Management & Technology Jaipur, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Students must have completed their higher secondary education (10+2) or an equivalent examination from a recognized board or university. They should have studied Science subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics in their 10+2 curriculum. Additionally, candidates must have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 50% in their qualifying examination. However, specific eligibility requirements may vary depending on the specialization chosen within the B.Sc. program. Prospective students are advised to check the institute's official website or contact the admissions department for detailed and up-to-date information. The annual fee for BSc is INR 90,000. NIMT Jaipur BSc Admission 2026

Admission to the BSc at NIMT Jaipur is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the NIMT Jaipur BSc admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

NIMT Jaipur BSc Application Process

NIMT Jaipur BSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BSc course at NIMT Jaipur have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill NIMT Jaipur BSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of NIMT Jaipur and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of NIMT Jaipur. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the NIMT Jaipur BSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the NIMT Jaipur BSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of NIMT Jaipur BSc Application Form.

Documents Required for NIMT Jaipur BSc Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
