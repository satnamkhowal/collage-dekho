Is 10th pass student eligible for Diploma course in Mechanical Engineering at Chitkara University, Patiala?

P
Prateek Lakhera •  Jun-12-2025

Dear student,

Unfortunately, Chitkara University, Patiala, doesn’t offer a diploma course in Mechanical Engineering. The university, however, does offer undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Mechanical Engineering. Additionally, a different campus, Chitkara Polytechnic, Jhansla, offers diploma in Mechanical Engineering course for a duration of 3 years to 10+2 passed students.

We hope this information was helpful to you. If you have further queries regarding the diploma in Mechanical Engineering course admission, you can write to hello@collegedekho.com or call our toll free number 18005729877, or simply fill out our Common Application Form on the website.
