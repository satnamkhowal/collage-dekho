About Panjab University

Panjab University, also known as PU or PUCHD, is a public state university established in 1882. Located in Chandigarh, India, the PU campus spans over 550 acres. The university is affiliated with itself and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Association of Indian Universities (AIU), and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). Panjab University is accredited with an A++ grade by NAAC with a CGPA of 3.68 in 2023 and is ranked 38th in the University category and 60th in the Overall category by NIRF in 2024.

Panjab University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and doctorate programs in streams such as Engineering, Management, Pharmaceutical, Arts, Commerce, Science, Law, and Medical Sciences. BTech, MBA, BPharm, BA LLB, and BSc are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Panjab University are based on merit in the qualifying examination or entrance examinations like JEE Main for BTech, CAT for MBA, PU-CET (UG/PG) for various UG and PG courses, and PU-LLB for LLB.shiksha.comgetmyuni.com

For placements, Panjab University’s placement rate is approximately 91% for MBA (UBS Chandigarh) in 2023, with 105 out of 115 students placed. During the placement drive of 2023, the highest salary package offered was INR 58.48 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 13.7 LPA for MBA and INR 8.89 LPA for BTech (UIET). According to the NIRF 2025 report, 1,124 UG and 1,355 PG students were placed.

Panjab University Admission 2025

Panjab University admissions are based on national-level entrance examinations (JEE Main, CAT, GATE, GPAT), state-level counseling (JAC Chandigarh), or university-based entrance exams (PU-CET, PU-LLB). Students seeking admission at Panjab University must fulfill the eligibility criteria and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for Panjab University admissions:

  • BTech: Candidates must appear for JEE Main and participate in JAC Chandigarh Counselling for seat allotment.
  • MBA: Candidates must appear for CAT and participate in the university’s counseling process.
  • MPharma: Candidates must appear for GPAT and participate in the university’s counseling process.
  • MTech: Candidates must appear for GATE and participate in GATE CCMT Counselling.
  • BPharm, BSc, BA LLB: Admissions are based on PU-CET (UG) or PU-LLB scores, followed by counseling.
  • MA, MSc, BCom: Admissions are based on merit in the qualifying examination or PU-CET (PG)/CUET PG scores.
