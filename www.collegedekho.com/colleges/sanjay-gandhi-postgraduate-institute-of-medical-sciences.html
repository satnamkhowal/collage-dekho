About SGPIMS Lucknow

On December 14, 1980, the then-President of India, Shri Neelam Sanjiva Reddy, laid the foundation stone for the Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences on the current King George's Medical University site.  The Medical Council of India (MCI) has authorised the institute, and the University Grants Commission (UGC) has recognised it. NIRF 2025 has placed SGPGIMS Fifth in the top medical institute category. 

The Institute provides students with the option to choose over 22 Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences courses spread across a myriad of disciplines. Right from the undergraduate to the Ph.D level, candidates have a range of choices. Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences, Lucknow requires prospective applicants to provide the scores received in the NEET PG, and NEET SS exams, and appear in the written test as well as interviews conducted by the university.

Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences primarily provides courses in medicine and related specialities. In response to patient demand, the departments of Critical Care Medicine, Hematology, Pediatric Gastroenterology, and Pediatric Surgical Specialties were established throughout time. The departments of Plastic Surgery, Maternal & Reproductive Health, Pulmonary Medicine, Molecular Medicine & Biotechnology, Opthalmology, and Neonatology were established under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Sewa Yojna initiative.

This SGPIMS campus spans over 550 acres and has high-tech amenities such as contemporary classrooms and digital laboratories. There are also sports facilities, a well-equipped gymnasium, research institutes, computer laboratories, large classrooms, convocation halls, housing facilities, and other amenities on campus.

Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences believes in providing the students here with an extremely well-rounded and enriching experience, as a result of which, it provides them with numerous extracurricular activities. It has also been upgraded with all the latest technological updates, as a result of which it has a centralized wi-fi campus and digital library facilities. The Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences in Lucknow also has affiliations with various universities in the United States, Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, Singapore, and New Zealand, among others.

Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences, Lucknow, was established in 1983. The Institute is approved by UGC, MCI, and the respective state regulatory authorities. SGPIMS, Lucknow is recognized by various regulatory bodies and has even been accredited by eminent frameworks. The institute provides M.Ch., DM, Ph.D., MD, PDCC, or Post-Doctoral Fellowships and Senior Residency programmes in a variety of disciplines. One of the most popular courses at SGPGIMS is MD. The NEET PG entrance test is used to determine admission to the programme. SGPIMS Lucknow address is New PMSSY Rd, Raibareli Road, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226014. 

SGPIMS Placements are important because they allow students the opportunity to work at leading firms. Major corporations such as HSBC, Standard Chartered, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, Tata Capital, Yes Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Indiabulls Ventures, and others are among the top recruiters at the Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences.

SGPGIMS Lucknow Application Process 2026

The admissions for the academic year of 2023 has commenced at the Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences, Lucknow. Candidates who are interested in a particular course can apply online.

  • For the undergraduate programs, students are required to submit grade 10+2 board exam marks as well as aptitude test scores.
  • For admissions into the postgraduate courses, students will have to appear in the entrance exams accepted by this university itself, NEET-PG, NEET, and a personal interview.
  • For the PG courses, scores received in the previous qualifying examination of the bachelor’s degree are considered an eligibility criterion.
  • Candidates must submit a copy of the registration cards for the entrance exams, and once the results have been released, the scorecard must also be provided.
  • Selection is merit-based, and the counselling process determines the course and its relevant specialization that a student can opt for.
