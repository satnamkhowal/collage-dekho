Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University Placements 2025

Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University (SSIU) Gandhinagar offers its students a unique space for innovation and entrepreneurship. They are given the space and guidance necessary to be motivated to think outside the box and create their own opportunities. With a multidisciplinary course choice, the students are provided with placement opportunities no matter which domain they opt for, which is made possible through the various activities organised by the SSIU training and placement cell. 

Over the past five years, SSIU has maintained an impressive 100% placement rate, demonstrating its strong industry connect and student readiness for global careers. In 2023 alone, more than 400 reputed companies visited the campus, offering over 2,600 placement opportunities to deserving candidates.

The university’s placement performance is marked by remarkable figures — the highest national package reached an exceptional ₹84 LPA, while the highest international offer stood at ₹48 LPA. The average package across disciplines was an impressive ₹5.18 LPA, reflecting consistent placement quality.

Prominent recruiters such as Google, Microsoft, TCS, Infosys, and KPMG have been instrumental in shaping student careers, while SSIU’s dedicated training, mentoring, and incubation programs empower aspiring entrepreneurs to transform innovative ideas into successful ventures.

Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University Placement Cell

The Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University training and placement cell consists of numerous subject matter experts who believe in leaving no stone unturned when it comes to nurturing the students here and facilitating a seamless placement process. This cell not only organises various job fairs but also trains the students and maintains placement records for every batch. Under the helm of this training and placement cell, numerous trends have developed over the years, all of which are moving upward. 

Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University Placement Process 

The  Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University placement process comprises numerous steps, all of which begin during the final and pre-final semesters of the courses here. The placement process starts with the training of the students, through which they are given provisions to upskill and receive exposure. It ends with the onboarding of students into their respective companies after the selection procedures during the campus placement programs conducted here. 

The SSIU placement process commences much prior to the actual placement fairs conducted during the year. This involves the conduction of various activities such as workshops, live projects, seminars and more, through which the students are given opportunities to upskill and receive ample exposure. As a result of this, the students here can bag numerous venerable placement prospects. 

Once the students have been trained, the training and placement cell at this institute organises various job fairs, through which recruiters scout talent from among the existing pool of candidates. 

Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University Placement Policy

The policy followed by the training and placement cell here at Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University is to reduce the skill gap as much as possible by providing the students with ample resources and opportunities and thereby improve their placement prospects.

Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University Placements FAQs

When do the placements at Swarnim Startup and Innovation University begin?

According to the placement schedule of Swarnim Startup and Innovation University, the placements for B.Tech students begin in the 7th semester, which is the pre-final semester. The placement process is conducted by the Training and Placement Cell (TPC) of the university. The TPC organises campus interviews for students of all years. The top companies that recruit from SSIU include Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Amazon, and Microsoft.

Does Swarnim Startup and Innovation University provide 100% placement?

No university can guarantee 100% placement since there are a number of factors that can affect a student's chances of getting placed, such as their academic performance, their skills and experience, and the overall job market. That said, SSIU has a good placement record. In 2023, the university placed 98% of its students with an average salary package of Rs 4.5 lakhs per annum.

According to the placement report of Swarnim Startup and Innovation University (SSIU), Gandhinagar, over 400 companies visited the university for its campus placement drive in 2023. These companies comprise almost all the sectors and areas of the industry.
