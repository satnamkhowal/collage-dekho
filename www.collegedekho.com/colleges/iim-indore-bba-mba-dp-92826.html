About IIM Indore BBA+MBA

IIM Indore BBA+MBA is a 5 Years year program. To be eligible for the B.B.A + M.B.A. degree program at the prestigious Indian Institute of Management, Indore, candidates must meet certain criteria. Applicants must have completed their higher secondary education (10+2) or equivalent from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate score of 60%. Additionally, they must have a valid score in the Common Admission Test (CAT) conducted by the IIMs. Shortlisted candidates are then invited for a Written Ability Test (WAT) and Personal Interview (PI) round. The final selection is based on the candidate's overall performance in CAT, WAT, PI, academic record, and work experience. The annual fee for BBA+MBA is INR 5,00,000. IIM Indore BBA+MBA Admission 2026

Admission to the BBA+MBA at IIM Indore is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the IIM Indore BBA+MBA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

IIM Indore BBA+MBA Application Process

IIM Indore BBA+MBA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BBA+MBA course at IIM Indore have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill IIM Indore BBA+MBA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of IIM Indore and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of IIM Indore. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the IIM Indore BBA+MBA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the IIM Indore BBA+MBA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of IIM Indore BBA+MBA Application Form.

Documents Required for IIM Indore BBA+MBA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

Lively life at IIM Indore

Overall The integrated program in management is a residential program. It means you have to stay on campus for the span of 5 years. It helps you connect with your peers and professors. It's a very rigorous course which covers from philosophy to psychology and calculus to algebra etc. it prepares the students in an all rounder way and this course is contextually relevant to this generation

Placement The highest package till date is 1Cr per annum few year ago. The average package is 25 LPA and the median package is around 18 LPA. The institute has recorded 100 percent placement record till date and even releases a report after every placement to maintain transparency. In addition to final placements there are others like summer placements and social placements that contain credit weights and are mandatory. There are committees that oversees these placements and ensure everybody lands one.

Infrastructure If you are talking about the buildings then the college looks bit old and outdated but if you are talking about the amenities, then they are top notch. There is an Olympic sized swimming pool, 2 gyms, squash courts, sauna bath area, badminton court, tennis court, basketball courts, football turf, cricket stadium, volleyball court, 2 auditoriums, library and and they even provide subscription to sites like perlego and some jounals. Overall the facilities are the best

Faculty The faculty here are top class. Most of them Ph.Ds from various international universities and are down to earth. They are friendly with the students and encourage them to ask questions and solve doubts. Since most professors stay on campus too, they even organise stuff like walk and talk where you can discuss about anything freely. These discussions don't limit to academics but also humanities and politics.

Hostel The program is residential which means the students are required to stay on campus even when the person belongs to the same city. It provides connections and increases bonds and blossoms friendship. The hostel rooms and bathrooms are big enough. There's hot water 24/7. The rooms are 4 sharing or 3 sharing for the first 3 years but in the 4th and 5th year, you can opt for double sharing or single rooms.
