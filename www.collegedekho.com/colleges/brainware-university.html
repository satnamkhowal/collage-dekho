About Brainware University Kolkata

Brainware University Kolkata is a private state university established on February 25, 2016. It is located at 398, Ramkrishnapur Road, Barasat, North 24 Parganas, Kolkata – 700125, West Bengal. The university operates across a 9-acre campus at Barasat Kazipara, approximately 15 km from central Kolkata. The university is recognised by UGC, AICTE, PCI, BCI, INC, WBNC, AIU, and the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

The university runs 9 schools and 26 departments, offering 70+ UG, PG, PhD, and Diploma programmes across Engineering, Pharmacy, Law, Management, Nursing, Allied Health Sciences, Agriculture, Media Science, Humanities, and more. Flagship courses include BTech in CSE, AI & ML, and Data Science, MBA, BPharm, and B.Sc Nursing. Brainware University admissions 2026 are through the Brainware Eligibility Test (BET) for most UG and PG programmes. WBJEE, WBJEE JENPAS (UG), CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT, and WBJEMAT scores are also accepted for specific programmes. 

Brainware University records a steady 98% placement rate in 2025. As per the 2024 placement report, more than 750 students were placed across multiple sectors. Brainware University highest package was INR 27 LPA, while the average was around INR 3.78 LPA. Leading recruiters such as Amazon, TCS, Adobe, and Fortis Healthcare.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Does Brainware University offer any online or distance courses?
No, Brainware University currently does not offer online or distance education programs. All 70+ undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma courses are strictly full-time, regular classroom programs conducted physically at the Barasat campus.
What are the daily class timings at Brainware University?
Academic classes generally run from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. However, specific daily schedules, practical lab timings, and occasional Saturday classes vary depending on your chosen department and the current semester timetable.

Brainware University Kolkata Admission 2026

Brainware University admission 2026-27 is open for UG, PG, Diploma, and PhD programmes. Most admissions are through the Brainware Eligibility Test (BET) — a 1-hour, 100-mark MCQ-based online test. For nursing, WBJEE JENPAS (UG) scores are used. MBA admissions accept CAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT, and WBJEMAT scores. The complete Brainware University admission process is online. The university also accepts offline applications at its Admission Department on any working day from 10 AM to 6 PM.

Brainware University Admission Process 2026 

Brainware University starts with registering on the official university website, followed by appearing in the BET entrance exam for most of the UG and PG course, the detailed step is given below:

  • Visit brainwareuniversity.ac.in and click 'Apply Online'. Register with your name, mobile number, email ID, and course preference. Pay a seat-booking fee of INR 5,000 (adjusted against programme fees).
  • Appear for the Brainware Eligibility Test (BET) online. Students with 80%+ in qualifying exams may get direct admission without BET in certain programmes.
  • Send scanned documents (marksheets, Aadhaar, and photograph) to admission@brainwareuniversity.ac.in. The Admission Cell will guide you.
  • Make payment via NEFT, IMPS, or UPI and send payment details to accounts@brainwareuniversity.ac.in.
  • Complete the Anti-Ragging form. The university will issue your Admission Confirmation Letter.

(Source: Brainware University – Admission Procedure Page, 2025-26)

Most Commonly Question Asked

Can I join the Brainware MBA without CAT scores?
Yes, if you have not appeared for national entrance exams like CAT, MAT, or XAT, you can still apply for the MBA program by clearing the university's own Brainware Eligibility Test (BET) and an interview.
Does Brainware University accept gap year students for admission?
Yes, Brainware University accepts applications from students who have taken a gap year. However, you must meet the standard academic eligibility criteria and submit a legally notarized gap certificate during your final physical document verification.
Can I skip the BET exam if I scored 80%?
Yes, candidates scoring 80% and above in their Class 12 board exams are often eligible for direct admission into several undergraduate programs without taking the Brainware Eligibility Test (BET), subject to immediate document verification.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Can I sit for Brainware placements with active backlogs?
No, most top-tier IT and multinational companies strictly require you to clear all active backlogs before appearing for their recruitment drives. However, a few bulk recruiters might allow you to sit if you clear them before your final joining date.
Can I accept multiple job offers during Brainware placements?
Brainware University generally follows a strict "One Student, One Job" policy to ensure fair opportunities for everyone. Once you secure and accept a formal offer from a company, you are usually restricted from participating in further campus drives.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Does the JENPAS UG cutoff drop in the mop-up round?
Generally, yes. Cutoff ranks often relax in the final rounds as vacant seats are adjusted. For instance, the B.Sc Nursing General cutoff opened at 21,907 in Round 1 and relaxed up to 28,107 by Round 2.
Is there a minimum passing cutoff for the BET exam?
Brainware University does not publish a rigid numerical cutoff score for the BET. Selection is dynamic, based on the overall applicant pool's performance that year, the number of vacant seats, and your past academic board percentages.
How much rank relaxation do reserved categories get in cutoffs?
SC, ST, and OBC candidates receive significant rank relaxations during state counselling. For example, while the General GNM cutoff closed strictly at 49,880, the SC category cutoff extended much further, closing at 59,012.

What is the average fees for the undergraduate programs offered by Brainware University?

The average fees for the undergraduate programs offered by Brainware University ranges between Rs 44,400- Rs 2,74,500 per annum. The fees is quite affordable when compare to other private universities.

What is the duration of PG courses offered by Brainware University?

The duration of PG courses offered by Brainware University is two years divided into four semesters. The programs offered can only be pursued after completion of graduation from a recognised university in a relevant stream with minimum marks.

What is the duration of UG courses offered by Brainware University?

The duration of UG courses offered by Brainware University varies depending upon the course. Some courses have a duration of three years like BA, B.Com, whereas others have a duration of four years like B.Tech. Some popular courses like BBA LLB have a duration of five years.

Brainware University Kolkata Campus

Brainware University is built on a 9-acre campus at Barasat Kazipara, Kolkata. The campus houses over 10,000 students. It is fully Wi-Fi enabled and has separate buildings for academic, hostel, and administrative functions. 

It is part of the Brainware Group of Institutions, which has a 36-year legacy in education in West Bengal. Most Commonly Question Asked

What sports facilities are available inside the Brainware campus?
The 9-acre Barasat campus features excellent recreational facilities, including a dedicated cricket ground, multi-sport outdoor arenas for football and volleyball, and well-equipped indoor sports rooms for table tennis, carrom, and chess to encourage physical fitness.
Is Brainware University considered a Tier 2 college?
Nationally, Brainware University is generally categorized as a Tier 3 institution. However, it is one of the top-performing private universities within West Bengal, recognized for its rapidly improving infrastructure, modern curriculum, and strong 98% placement record.
Is Brainware University a government or private university?
Brainware University is a private state university. It was established in 2016 under the West Bengal Act XXXI of 2015. Despite being private, it is fully recognized by the UGC, AIU, and the Ministry of Education.
