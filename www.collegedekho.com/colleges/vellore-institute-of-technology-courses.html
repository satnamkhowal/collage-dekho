VIT Vellore Courses FAQs

Is VIT Vellore easy to get into?

Admission in VIT Vellore courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the VIT Vellore Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include GMAT, MAT, NIMCET, Tamilnadu 12th, CBSE 12th, VITMEE, NATA, UCEED, XAT, AIEED, NID, VITEEE, CMAT, CEED, CAT, NMAT by GMAC, NIFT etc.

What is the top package of VIT Vellore?

The highest package offered to a VIT Vellore student during the 2023 placement season was Rs 1.2 Crore per Annum. Some of the top recruiters at VIT Vellore include Udaan, Snapdeal, Aspire, Amazon, VISA, KPIT, PayPal, Myntra, MRF etc.

Does VIT Vellore offer online or short-term courses?

Yes, VIT Vellore offers online and short-term courses through its online learning platform called VIT Online Learning. These programmes are designed for working professionals who want to upskill or pursue higher education without compromising on their work commitments.

What types of courses does VIT Vellore offer?

VIT Vellore offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses across various disciplines including Engineering and Technology, Science, Management and Business Administration, and so on.

Can I pay my VIT Vellore fee online?

Yes, you can pay your VIT Vellore fees online. VIT offers an online fee payment facility for students. The exact procedure might differ slightly depending on the programme and year.

Which courses are popular at VIT Vellore?

Some of the most popular courses offered by VIT Vellore includes B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering, B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering, B.Tech in Information Technology, and so on.

What is the difficulty level of the VITEEE exam?

The difficulty level of VITEEE is generally considered to be moderate, closer to, but slightly easier than JEE Mains. Compared to exams like JEE Advanced, VITEEE focuses more on fundamental concepts and application rather than in-depth theoretical knowledge.

How to get a free seat in VIT Vellore?

Unfortunately, VIT Vellore doesn't offer "free seats" in the conventional sense. Admission to VIT is primarily based on merit and performance in entrance exams, not financial aid solely.

Does VIT Vellore accept 12th marks?

VIT Vellore considers 12th marks for eligibility purposes, but not directly for admission through entrance exams. Minimum marks in 12th board exams (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics for B.Tech) are mandatory to qualify for appearing in the entrance exam (VITEEE) for engineering programmes.

Is VIT Vellore easy to get?

VIT Vellore admission primarily relies on entrance exams like VITEEE (for engineering) and scores in national level exams like CAT/XAT/MAT etc. (for other programmes). Scoring well in these exams requires thorough preparation and facing stiff competition from other aspiring students.

Is VIT Vellore very expensive?

Compared to other government colleges in India, VIT Vellore can be considered expensive. The annual tuition fees range from ₹8,000 to ₹1,98,000, placing it considerably higher than most government colleges offering similar programmes.

What is the annual fee of VIT Vellore?

The annual fee of VIT Vellore ranges from Rs 8,000 to Rs 1,98,000. It's important to note that the fees vary depending on the specific course chosen. For BTech the fee ranges from Rs 8,000 - Rs 1,98,000.

What is the most popular course at VIT , Vellore ?

Engineering courses are the most popular courses opted by students at VIT , Vellore . Of these, B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) courses are the most sought after amongst both students and parents.
