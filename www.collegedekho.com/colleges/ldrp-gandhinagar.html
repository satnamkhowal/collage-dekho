About LDRP Institute of Technology and Research

LDRP Institute of Technology and Research, also known as LDRP is a Engineering and Technology, established in 2005-2006. Located in Gandhinagar, LDRP Institute of Technology and Research campus spans over Not specified. The college is affiliated to Kadi Sarva Vishwavidyalaya (KSV) and recognized by the Constituent institute of Kadi Sarva Vishwavidyalaya (KSV). LDRP Institute of Technology and Research is ranked global: 15888, national: 1398 by NIRF under Technical Education. NIRF ranking: global: 15888, national: 1398

LDRP Institute of Technology and Research offers a wide range of various programs in the streams of Technical Education. B.E. in Computer Engineering, MBA, MCA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at LDRP Institute of Technology and Research are based on ACPC rank, GUJCET. The college accepts GUJCET for B.E., GATE for M.E./M.Tech, CMAT for MBA.
