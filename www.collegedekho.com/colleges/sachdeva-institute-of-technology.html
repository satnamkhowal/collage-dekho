About Sachdeva Institute of Technology

Sachdeva Institute of Technology is situated in Mathura in Uttar Pradesh state of India. Established in 2001, SIT is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. Sachdeva Institute of Technology offers 13 courses across 3 streams namely Engineering, Management, IT..Popular degrees offered at Sachdeva Institute of Technology include B.Tech, MBA, M.Tech..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Sachdeva Institute of Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Sachdeva Institute of Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Sachdeva Institute of Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
