About MMMUT

Established in 2013 as a non-affiliating technical university under the Uttar Pradesh Government, MMMUT is located in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. It is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited by the NBA, with approvals from AICTE. MMMUT is ranked 60 in the Engineering category and 68 in the University category by NIRF 2025. The university offers over 30 programs at undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral levels in streams such as Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, and Sciences. Popular UG courses include BTech, BBA, and BPharm, while PG courses include MBA, MTech, MCA, and MSc. Admissions are based on national or state-level entrance exams like JEE Main (BTech), CUET UG (BBA, BPharm), CUET PG/CMAT/CAT (MBA), GATE (MTech), and MET for non-qualified candidates. The university conducts centralized counseling through its admission portal.

MMMUT’s Training and Placement Cell ensures robust placement opportunities, with comprehensive training in soft skills, technical aptitude, and interview preparation. As per the 2025 placement report, the highest package offered was INR 48 LPA, with an average package of INR 7 LPA. Over 108 recruiters, including top companies like Amazon, Microsoft, TCS, Wipro, BEL, and MRF Tyres, participated, placing 973 students across sectors like IT, core engineering, and consulting. The 125-acre campus hosts over 5,000 students, including international students, and features modern facilities such as smart classrooms, a central library with over 1,00,000 books, a 550-bedded hospital, separate hostels for boys and girls, sports facilities, a gym, and a cafeteria, fostering holistic student development.

MMMUT Cutoff

MMMUT cutoff is based on the scores of various entrance exams including the JEE Main, GATE, CUET UG, CUET PG, and, MMMUT EE. Candidates aspiring for admission to the university must meet the minimum cutoff for the respective course. MMMUT cutoff JEE Main is valid for the B.Tech admission. Keep in mind that the MMMUT cut off is determined by a number of factors, including the number of applicants, the number of seats, and the history of the admissions from the previous year.

MMMUT FAQs

Where can I check the MMMUT Gorakhpur result?

The Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology result is declared on the official website of the university which is mmmut.ac.in. Apart from this, the results for various entrance exams are released on their respective official websites.

Is the MMMUT Gorakhpur cutoff JEE Mains released for 2024?

Yes,  Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology cut off for 2024 has been released for BTech. The university has released the cutoff for the All-India quota and Home State quota in the form of Opening Ranks and Closing Ranks.

What is the cost of the Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology BTech programme?

The Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology's Btech programme fees per year range from Rs 55,000 - Rs 1,61,000.

For admission to the BTech programme at Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology, which examinations are accepted?

JEE Main is accepted by Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology for admission to the BTech programme.

Does the NBA accredit Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology?

Yes, the NBA has approved Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology.

Does MMMUT accept CUET?

Yes, MMMUT accepts CUET UG for taking admissions in UG courses and CUET PG for PG courses.

How many courses are in MMMUT?

MMMUT offers a total of 6 courses at UG and PG levels.

Can I get direct admission in MMMUT?

No students can take direct admission in MMMUT. Every year entrance exam is conducted by the university and those who secure cut off marks should appear in counselling to take admission in MMMUT.

What are the cut off for MMMUT?

The MMMUT decides different ranks as cut off for different courses. Candidates who will secure a minimum passing MMMUT cutoff marks will be eligible for admission.

Can I get admission in MMMUT at 85 percentile?

Applicants who want to take admission in MMMUT must note that candidates should have passed 10+2 examination with at least 55% marks (while 50% in case of candidates belonging to the SC/ST category) and with at least 60% average marks (55% in case of candidate belonging to SC/ST category). It is most likely that 85 % marks will get you admission.

Is MMMUT private or government?

MMMEC was established in the year 1962. It is a semi government university. In 2013 MMMEC has been given the status of Deemed university & it became MMMUT.

Is MMMUT recognized by the UGC?

Yes, MMMUT is recognized by the UGC.

When did MMMUT establish?

MMMUT was established in the year 2013.

MMMUT Campus Life & Facilities

Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology (MMMUT), Gorakhpur, provides a comprehensive range of facilities to support academic excellence, extracurricular engagement, and student well-being. The 125-acre campus is equipped with modern infrastructure to foster a conducive environment for learning, research, and holistic development. 

  • Library: The Central Library at MMMUT is a key academic resource with a vast collection of over 1,16,516 books, including bound journals and ISI codes. It offers e-resources such as Wiley E-Books (138 titles), Pearson E-Books (559 titles), Springer E-Books (885 titles), and AICTE E-Resources for scientific, technical, and allied subjects.
  • Sports Complex: The Amar Shahid Bandhu Singh Stadium and other sports facilities promote physical fitness and extracurricular engagement.
  • Hostels: MMMUT is a residential institution with seven boys’ hostels (Subhash Bhawan, Raman Bhawan, Vishveswaraya Bhawan, Tagore Bhawan, Ambedkar Bhawan, etc.) and four girls’ hostels (Saraswati Bhawan, Sarojani Bhawan, Kalpana Chawla Bhawan, Subhadra Sadan), accommodating over 2,110 students (1,420 boys, 690 girls).
  • Medical Facilities: An on-campus Health Centre managed by a Medical Committee provides healthcare services to students, faculty, and staff.
  • Innovation Hubs: MMMUT fosters innovation through advanced research labs and a startup ecosystem.
