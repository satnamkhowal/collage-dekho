About NIT Jamshedpur

National Institute of Technology (NIT) Jamshedpur was established as a joint venture of the Government of India and the Government of Bihar in 1960; NIT Jamshedpur is the foremost institute in India and a highly regarded center for teaching and research. The institute has obtained the status of an Institution of National Importance under the Ministry of Education (MoE). NIT Jamshedpur is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). NIT Jamshedpur NIRF Ranking 2022 is 90th under the engineering category. The institute offers excellence in its academic courses with 150+ NIT Jamshedpur faculty and 4000+ students. NIT Jamshedpur has more than 12 departments that offer seven UG, 23 PG, and several PhD courses. NIT Jamshedpur courses include UG and PG-level programmes in engineering, science, and humanities. The NIT Jamshedpur campus spreads over 341.3 acres. National Institute of Technology Jamshedpur got the 101-150th rank in the engineering category in the NIRF ranking for the year 2024 whereas it was ranked 86th in the engineering category by the NIRF in 2021 and 79th in the engineering category in 2020. It has collaborated with several other universities worldwide for research projects, conferences, and publications. It has many active Memorandum of Understanding (MOU's) with national and international universities. 

NIT Jamshedpur admission 2025 is ongoing for various courses. For BTech admission, JEE Main 2025 has already been conducted and JoSAA counselling is currently ongoing, round 1 and 2 have already been conducted and round 3 results are to be declared on July 4, 2025. JEE Mains 2026 applications will start from November 2025 and the 1st session will be conducted in January, 2026. For MTech admissions, GATE registration will commence in August,2025 and exam will be held on February 7, 8, 14, and 15, 2025. Admission to PhD is based on the UGC NET score, which will be conducted in June 2026. NIT Jamshedpur admission to MCA is based' on NIMCET. NIT Jamshedpur tuition fee for typically ranges from1,40,000 - 5,00,000, depending on the programme and level of study.

NIT Jamshedpur Admission 2025

NIT Jamshedpur admissions are based on national-level entrance examinations. Candidates must meet the eligibility criteria and appear for the relevant entrance exam. The admission process includes:

  • BTech: Candidates must appear for JEE Main and participate in JoSAA or CSAB counseling for seat allotment.
  • MTech: Candidates must have a valid GATE score and participate in CCMT counseling.
  • MCA: Admissions are based on NIMCET scores followed by counseling.
  • MSc: Admissions are based on IIT JAM scores and CCMN counseling.

NIT Jamshedpur FAQs

Who releases the NIT Jamshedpur B.Tech cutoff?

NIT Jamshedpur offers B.Tech admissions based on the JEE Main scores. JoSAA conducts the centralised counselling process and releases the NIT Jamshedpur B.Tech cut off list.

What are the maximum and minimum scores for M.Tech CSE at NIT Jamshedpur?

NIT Jamshedpur GATE cutoff 2022 for M.Tech CSE was 495 (minimum score) and 527 (maximum marks) for the open category.

What is the NIT Jamshedpur M.Sc cutoff for physics?

NIT Jamshedpur provides admission to its M.Sc courses through JAM. In 2022, NIT Jamshedpur M.Sc cutoff 2022 was 40 (closing marks) for the open category.

What is NIT Jamshedpur GATE cutoff?

NIT Jamshedpur offers admission to M.Tech courses based on the GATE scores. CCMT is the conducting authority of the NIT Jamshedpur GATE counselling process. It also releases the NIT Jamshedpur GATE cutoff list.

What is the opening and closing cut off for B.Tech in CSE at NIT Jamshedpur?

NIT Jamshedpur B.Tech CSE cutoff 2022 is yet to be out. In 2021, the opening and closing ranks for B.Tech CSE at NIT Jamshedpur was 8664 and 8670 for the open gender-neutral category.

What are the facilities available at NIT Jamshedpur?

NIT Jamshedpur is situated on the outskirt of Jamshedpur on 341.3 acres of sprawling land. The NIT Jamshedpur campus provides facilities like a central library, a computer centre, a guest house, medical facilities, transport facilities, thirteen hostels, a post office, two ATMs, a branch of SBI, a gymnasium etc.

Is NIT Jamshedpur good for placement?

NIT Jamshedpur offers all necessary placement support to its graduating students. It prepares the students for the placement process and invites companies to recruit its students. As per the NIT Jamshedpur placement report 2022, the placement percentage was 97.2% and the highest package offered was Rs 72.5 lakh per annum.

Is NIT Jamshedpur good?

NIT Jamshedpur is among the first eight Regional Engineering Colleges (RECs) established with the aim of uplifting the technical education landscape in the country’s rural areas. The list of NIT Jamshedpur courses has some of the advanced B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc, and PhD courses.

Is NIT Jamshedpur a government institution?

NIT Jamshedpur is a public government institution established by the Government of India and the Government of Bihar in 1960. In 2022, it was formally given the status of a Deemed University. The institution is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).

What is the NIT Jamshedpur NIRF Ranking?

NIT Jamshedpur has been ranked by NIRF among the top engineering colleges in India several times. According to the NIRF Ranking 2022, NIT Jamshedpur is ranked 90 in the engineering category. In 2021, the NIT Jamshedpur NIRF Ranking was 86 in the engineering category.

When was NIT Jamshedpur established?

NIT Jamshedpur was established in 1960 as the result of a joint venture between the Government of India and the Government of Bihar. The institute is a part of the chain of Regional Engineering Colleges (RECs) in India and is among the first eight Regional Engineering Colleges (RECs).
