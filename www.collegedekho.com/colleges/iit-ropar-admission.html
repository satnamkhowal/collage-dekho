IIT Ropar Admission for its undergraduate engineering courses is competitive and is based on the marks obtained in Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main. The aspirants have to qualify JEE Main, for which the registration is usually in November, in order to be considered for the next stage. Seat allotment is done through the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) counseling, which is centralized and conducted by JoSAA, usually from June after the results are out.

IIT Ropar Admission 2026: Courses, Eligibility, Application Form, Last Date

Indian Institute of Technology Ropar (IIT Ropar), established in 2008 in Rupnagar, Punjab, is a premier public technical university recognized as an Institute of National Importance. Accredited with an A grade by NAAC, it ranks 22nd in Engineering and 48th Overall by NIRF 2024, and 148th in Southern Asia by QS Asia Rankings 2025. Spread across a 525-acre campus along the Satluj River, IIT Ropar offers Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Doctoral programs in Engineering, Sciences, Humanities, and Data Science through 12 departments. Admissions are entrance-based, requiring JEE Advanced for B.Tech, GATE for M.Tech, IIT JAM for M.Sc, and GATE/CSIR NET/UGC NET for Ph.D. Applications are managed online via exam portals or iitrpr.ac.in. Below are the details for IIT Ropar Admission 2025, including eligibility, selection criteria, application process, and key dates.

When will the JEE Advanced 2023 Result for IIT Ropar admissions be made public?

A
Ankita Sarkar •  Jun-14-2023

Hello Arya,

The JEE Main 2023 exam results are taken into consideration for admission to the B.Tech programmes at the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar. The JEE Advanced Result 2023 release date is June 18, 2023. It will be released by IIT Guwahati on the official website (jeeadv.ac.in). To view their results, candidates must log in using their login credentials (registration ID and password).  For the JEE Advanced 2023 exam, 80+ points are regarded as good, whereas the minimum passing criteria is 70 marks. The JEE Advanced exam will only be available to the top 2,50,000 JEE Main exam takers. To check the IIT JEE result 2023 candidates can follow the procedure below:

  • Go to jeeadv.ac.in, the official website.
  • Select the 'IIT JEE Advanced Result Link' link.
  • Your Roll number, birthdate, and phone number should be entered in the corresponding fields.
  • The "I'm not a robot" checkbox should be selected.
  • When you click the "Submit" button, the JEE Advanced Result 2023 will be shown on the screen.
  • Take a printout of the result by downloading it for future use.
