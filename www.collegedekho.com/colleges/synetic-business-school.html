About SBS Ludhiana

Synetic Business School was established in 2004 under the aegis of a non-profit organisation named Zayn Educational Trust. It aims to develop the best business school in Punjab, with excellence in matching an international standard that alone maintains the standard in business education. The institute is aligned with IK Gujral Punjab Technical University. It has a highly qualified and experienced teaching staff that ensures the overall development of the students. The school is approved by UGC. The institute is located on the Chandigarh Highway, Ramgarh Sahibana Road, Ludhiana, Punjab and offers access to different courses.

SBS Ludhiana courses are offered to the students at the diploma, UG as well as PG levels. Students who are looking to enrol in any course at this institution can apply through the online mode. Some of the popular courses offered by the business school are BBA, B.Com, BCA, B.Sc-IT, B.Sc-Textile, B.Sc-MLS, M.Com, MBA, M.Sc-IT and Diploma. The institute is well equipped with all the facilities that help enhance the learning environment. The Institution has a highly qualified and experienced teaching staff that ensures the overall development of the students. The admission is offered on basis of merit in the last qualifying exam.

SBS Ludhiana campus offers access to air-conditioned and spacious classrooms to the students. Additionally, a well-stocked library with hundreds and thousands of books, a conference hall, a cafeteria, a health clinic, and more are also available at the institution. The college also provides hostel facilities for both boys and girls. SBS Ludhiana also organises fests, annual sports events, and other cultural events and activities. The placement rate of the business school is 90%.
