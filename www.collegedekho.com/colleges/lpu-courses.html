LPU Courses FAQs

Are LPU courses globally recognised?

LPU courses are globally recognised through multiple international accreditations and partnerships. The university is accredited by NAAC with A++ grade (3.68/4 score), recognised by UGC, AICTE, NBA, COA, BCI, NCTE, PCI, and ICAR within India. Internationally, LPU has WES (World Education Services) recognition for credential evaluation in US/Canada, ACBSP accreditation for business programmes, and student exchange tie-ups with 300+ foreign universities across USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Singapore, and South Korea. LPU is ranked 5th in India in THE World University Rankings 2026, has secured QS subject rankings in multiple disciplines, and ranked 197th in THE Asia University Rankings 2026. The 2+2 international credit transfer programmes in B.Tech, BBA, B.Com, B.Sc IT, BHMCT, and B.Des Fashion allow students to spend 2 years at LPU and 2 years at a partner foreign university, earning a recognised dual qualification.

Does LPU offer integrated courses?

Yes, LPU offers several integrated courses that combine two degrees in a single extended programme. The most popular is Integrated B.Tech CSE - MBA (5 years), which combines a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering with a Master of Business Administration. Other integrated programmes include Integrated M.Sc. (Economics) - B.Tech (CSE) (5 years), Integrated BBA-MBA (5 years, fee INR 1.0-2.0 Lakhs per year), Integrated BA + B.Ed (4-Year ITEP under National Education Policy), Integrated B.Sc. + B.Ed (4 Years), Integrated B.Tech - M.Tech in Urban and Regional Planning (5 years), and the 5-year integrated law programmes BA LLB (Hons.) and BBA LLB (Hons.). Integrated programmes save one full academic year compared to taking the two degrees separately, and admission is typically through LPUNEST or CUET. They're useful for students who already know they want both degrees at the start of their journey.

Which LPU course has the highest placement package?

B.Tech CSE has the highest placement packages at LPU consistently. In the 2024-25 placement season, the highest international package was INR 3.25 Crore per annum (Germany) for a B.Tech CSE graduate, and the highest domestic package was INR 62.72 LPA, also for a CSE graduate at Microsoft. Specialised CSE branches like B.Tech CSE - Data Science with ML, B.Tech CSE - AI and Data Engineering, and B.Tech (Hons.) CSE consistently see the top recruiters — Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Adobe, Bosch, and IBM. MBA Business Analytics and MBA Fintech specialisations also see high packages, with the highest MBA placement reaching INR 32 LPA. Other strong-placement courses include MCA Hons. (Data Science with TransOrg tie-up), MBA Financial Markets (with NSE Academy), and B.Pharm (where pharma giants like Sun Pharma, Cipla, and Dr. Reddy's recruit). However, note that average packages are more practical to plan for — average B.Tech CSE package at LPU is around INR 6-9 LPA, and top 25% see INR 10.23 LPA average.

Can I do LPU course without entrance exam?

Yes, several LPU courses allow direct admission without any entrance exam, based purely on Class 12 or graduation merit. These include B.A. (general), BBA (general), BCA (general), B.Com (general), B.Sc (Physics/Chemistry/Maths/Zoology/Botany), Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA), BPA (Music/Theatre), B.Sc (Hotel and Hospitality Management), and most diploma courses. For these merit-based admissions, the cutoff is typically 45%-50% aggregate in Class 12. However, even though entrance exam is not mandatory, taking LPUNEST is strongly recommended because it directly determines your scholarship slab — without LPUNEST, you cannot qualify for the 20%-60% tuition fee waivers under Category A/B/C. For flagship programmes like B.Tech CSE, MBA, B.Arch, BA LLB, and B.Pharm, an entrance exam (LPUNEST or equivalent national exam) is mandatory and cannot be skipped.

Are LPU online courses UGC approved?

Yes, LPU online courses are fully UGC-DEB (Distance Education Bureau) approved and equivalent to regular degrees for higher education and government job purposes. LPU Online MBA and MCA additionally carry AICTE approval — important for management and computer applications graduates applying to companies that specifically check AICTE recognition. The university also has WES (World Education Services) recognition, which means LPU online degrees are accepted by many universities and employers in the US, Canada, UK, and Australia for further studies and work permits. Online courses are delivered through LPU's digital platform with live sessions, recorded lectures, virtual labs, online assignments, and remote-proctored exams. Students can apply year-round at lpuonline.com without needing LPUNEST.

What are LPU courses after 12th for commerce students?

LPU courses after 12th for commerce students include B.Com (3 Years), B.Com (Hons.) / Hons. with Research (4 Years), BBA in 14+ specialisations, Integrated BBA-MBA (5 Years), B.Sc. (Economics), BBA LLB (Hons.) for those interested in corporate law, and BCA for those interested in IT. Specialised commerce-side BBAs include BBA Financial Markets, BBA Business Analytics, BBA Fintech and AI, BBA International Finance, and BBA Cargo, Logistics, and Supply Chain Management. Eligibility is typically 45%-50% in Class 12 with English. Admission is through LPUNEST or CUET. Top recruiters for commerce graduates include TCS, Amazon, Myntra, Dell, IBM, Airtel, KPMG, Deloitte, EY, and HDFC. The Integrated BBA-MBA programme is popular as it saves one year compared to doing BBA + MBA separately.

What are LPU courses after 12th for science students?

LPU courses after 12th for science students cover engineering, computer applications, sciences, pharmacy, agriculture, architecture, and design. The most popular options are B.Tech (15+ specialisations including CSE, AI/ML, Data Science, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical), B.Pharm (4 years), B.Arch (5 years, requires NATA), B.Sc Hons. (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biotechnology, Microbiology, Forensic Science, Agriculture), Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT), Bachelor of Optometry, BCA (with English), and B.Des. PCM students typically apply for B.Tech, B.Arch, B.Sc Maths/Physics, while PCB students go for B.Pharm, B.Sc Biotech, B.Sc Microbiology, Pharm.D, and BPT. Eligibility ranges from 50% to 60% aggregate in Class 12, and most courses use LPUNEST or JEE Main / NEET / NATA for admission.

Is LPUNEST mandatory for all LPU courses?

LPUNEST is mandatory for most LPU courses but not all. It is required for B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Com, B.Pharm, B.Des, BA LLB, MBA, M.Tech, MCA, M.Pharm, M.Des, and most other UG/PG admissions. However, candidates with a valid score in JEE Main (for B.Tech), CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT/NMAT (for MBA), GATE (for M.Tech), GPAT (for M.Pharm), NEET rank under 3 Lakh (for B.Pharm), NATA (mandatory for B.Arch), CLAT/LSAT-India (for Law), and CUET UG/PG (for select programmes) can be exempted from LPUNEST for admission. Note that even if you skip LPUNEST for admission, taking it can still get you a scholarship — LPU offers 20%-60% tuition waivers based on LPUNEST category. For courses like Diploma after 10th, LLB, and select humanities programmes, admission is based purely on merit without any entrance exam.

Which is the most popular LPU course?

B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering is the most popular LPU course, with 6,000-8,000+ students enrolled across the 4-year programme. The course leads LPU placements year after year — in 2025, LPU CSE graduates secured the top campus packages including INR 52.21 LPA at Microsoft and an international offer of INR 3.25 Crore in Germany. Other popular LPU courses include MBA (with 12+ specialisations and INR 64 LPA highest package), BCA (especially the IBM tie-up Data Science variant), BBA, B.Pharm, and BA LLB (Hons.). Specialised B.Tech tracks in AI/ML, Data Science, and Cyber Security are seeing fast-growing demand and now have separate cutoffs and intake numbers.
