About Takshashila University Tindivanam

Takshashila University, also known as TU, is a state-private university established in 2022. Located in Tindivanam, Tamil Nadu, Takshashila University campus spans over 150 acres. The university is affiliated to itself (as a state-private university) and recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and the Government of Tamil Nadu. Takshashila University is ranked among the top 3% universities globally by QS World University Rankings in 2024.

Takshashila University offers a wide range of Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Diploma, Certificate, and Doctorate programs in the streams of Engineering, Management, Pharmaceutical, Arts, Commerce, Science, and Social Work. B.Tech, MBA, B.Sc, and BCA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Takshashila University are based on both merit in the qualifying examination and entrance examinations. The university accepts JEE Main and TNEA for B.Tech, CAT, MAT, XAT, and CMAT for MBA, GATE for M.Tech, and UGC NET or CSIR NET for Ph.D.

Takshashila University Admission 2026

Takshashila University admissions are based on both merit in the qualifying examination and entrance examinations (national, state, or university-based). Students seeking admission at Takshashila University must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the university and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for Takshashila University admissions:

If Admissions Are Based on Entrance Examination:

  • B.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech must appear for the JEE Main or TNEA and participate in the TNEA counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the CAT, MAT, XAT, or CMAT and participate in the university’s counselling process for seat allotment.
  • M.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to M.Tech must appear for the GATE and participate in the university’s counselling process for seat allotment.
  • Ph.D: Candidates seeking admission to Ph.D must appear for the UGC NET or CSIR NET and participate in the university’s interview process.

If Admissions Are Based on Merit in Qualifying Examination:

  • B.Sc.: Admissions to B.Sc. are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • B.A: Admissions to B.A are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • BBA: Admissions to BBA are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • BCA: Admissions to BCA are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • B.Com: Admissions to B.Com are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • B.Pharm: Admissions to B.Pharm are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • M.Sc.: Admissions to M.Sc. are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.

The fee structure of BSc Cardiology at Takshashila University, Tindivanaam is INR 75,000 per year. The duration of the BSc Cardiology at Takshashila University is 3 years. The minimum eligibility criteria to apply for the BSc Cardiology at Takshashila University, Tindivanaam is to pass higher secondary in the science stream with at least 55% to 60% marks in aggregate. For admission to Takshashila University, students need to fill up the application form in online or offline modes. Candidates are offered admission to Takshashila University purely on a merit basis. For more information related to the fee structure of BSc Cardiology at Takshashila University, students can contact the official authority of the college by email address or phone number.
