About GL Bajaj Mathura

G.L. Bajaj Group of Institutions, Mathura, also known as GLBGI Mathura, is a private college established in 2009. Located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, the GLBGI campus spans over 28 acres. The college is affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and Council of Architecture (CoA). GL Bajaj Mathura is ranked first in UPTU results 2019 in the Mathura-Agra-Aligarh region and is among the top 50 AKTU-affiliated colleges in 2024.

GL Bajaj Mathura offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in the streams of Engineering, Management, and Architecture. BTech, MBA, and BArch are some of the popular courses. Admissions at GL Bajaj Mathura are based on entrance examinations and direct admission through management quota. The college accepts JEE Main for BTech, NATA for BArch, and CAT/CMAT for MBA, followed by UPTAC counseling.

For placements, GL Bajaj Mathura's placement rate is approximately 78% as per the 2018 placement report. During the placement drive of 2024, the highest salary package offered was INR 45 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 6 LPA.

GL Bajaj Mathura Admission 2025

GL Bajaj Mathura admissions are based on national and state-level entrance examinations or direct admission through management quota. Students seeking admission at GL Bajaj Mathura must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the college and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for GL Bajaj Mathura admissions:

  • BTech: Candidates seeking admission to BTech must appear for JEE Main and participate in UPTAC counseling for the seat allotment process. Direct admission is available through management quota based on JEE Main rank or 10+2 PCM percentage.
  • BArch: Candidates seeking admission to BArch must appear for NATA and participate in UPTAC counseling for the seat allotment process.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for CAT/CMAT and participate in UPTAC counseling for the seat allotment process.
