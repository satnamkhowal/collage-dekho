About S.S. Jain Subodh Girls P.G. College Sanganer, Jaipur

S. S. Jain Subodh P. G. College is one of the oldest colleges in Jaipur, Rajasthan, founded in 1954 and managed by Shri Shwetamber Sthanakwasi Jain Society. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional programmes. In 1954, the S.S. Jain Subodh PG (Autonomous) College in Jaipur was founded under the auspices of the S.S. Jain Subodh Shiksha Samiti with the goal of providing value-based higher education and contributing to the holistic development of society. In 1918, the Shiksha Samiti was established.
