About Jamia Millia Islamia Placements

Jamia Millia Islamia placement 2025 data has been released for MCA and MSc Bioinformatics. The highest package offered at JMI was INR 18.5 LPA for MCA and INR 6 LPA for MSc Bioinformatics. Around 46% of MCA students got placed, with an average salary of INR 7.85 LPA. Top recruiters included TCS, IBM, and Newgen.

As per the JMI annual report 2024, the highest package offered was INR 59.91 LPA to B.Tech Computer Engineering students. For MBA, the highest package was INR 24 LPA during the same year. A total of 330 students were placed in 2024. In B.Tech Electrical Engineering, 54 students were placed, with salaries ranging from INR 2.2 LPA to INR 22 LPA.

Jamia Millia Islamia Placement Cell & Training Support

The University Placement Cell at Jamia Millia Islamia handles all placement activities. To prepare students for competitive recruitment processes, the university offers training and career development support, including:

  • Resume building and profile development workshops to help students create impactful CVs

  • Group discussion (GD) sessions to improve communication and analytical skills
  • Aptitude and technical training sessions for written tests and assessments

Jamia Millia Islamia Placements FAQs

How many students are placed during Jamia Millia Islamia placements?

According to the JMI NIRF Report 2025, a total of 2,082 students were placed across programs in 2023–24 out of 3114 eligible students across various programs.

Are the PG placements good at Jamia Millia?

Yes, PG placements at Jamia Millia Islamia are quite good across multiple streams. In 2023–24, PG 2-year programs had a median package of INR 13 LPA with a 67% placement rate. For Engineering and law PG programs, the median packages reached up to INR 15 LPA and INR 14.4 LPA, respectively.

What is the average package offered during Jamia Millia Islamia placements?

The overall average package for JMI 2025 has not been officially released. However, median package data based on NIRF Report 2025 is out. BTech students secured a median package of INR 13.78 LPA in 2024. While the median packages for MBA and PG 2-year programs stood at INR 12.6 and 13 LPA, respectively.

How are UG placements at Jamia Millia Islamia?

UG placements at Jamia Millia Islamia are good, particularly for 4-year and 5-year programs. In 2023–24, UG 4-year programs recorded an 84% placement rate with a median package of INR 13.78 LPA. Jamila Millia Islamia placement package for UG 5-year programs was INR 14.4 LPA with around 81% of the students placed.

What is the Jamia Millia Islamia highest package offered for MBA?

The highest package offered for MBA at Jamia Millia Islamia in 2024 was INR 24 LPA. While 2025 data is not yet released, NIRF 2025 shows the median MBA package at INR 12.6 LPA. Top recruiters include reputed companies like Accenture, KPMG, and other leading corporate firms.

What is the Jamia Millia Islamia placement rate?

The placement rate at Jamia Millia Islamia varies by program and stream. As per the NIRF Report 2025, PG 2-year programs recorded around 67% placements in 2023–24, while UG 4-year programs achieved a higher rate of 84%. For MBA, the JMI placement rate was 94% in 2024.

What is the scenario of JMI B.Tech placements (CSE)?

As per the JMI annual report, the highest package reached INR 59.91 LPA, offered to B.Tech Computer Engineering students. Median packages for UG 4-year programs stood at INR 13.78 LPA, with placement rates around 84–85%. So B.Tech CSE placements are decent at JMI.
