About International Automobile Centre of Excellence

International Automobile Centre of Excellence (iACE) Gandhinagar is a joint venture of the Government of Gujarat and Maruti Suzuki India Limited. iACE is India's first automobile centre of excellence set up for engineering and diploma students. The institute has an International alliance with Bendigo Kangan, Australia, and various other International collaborations. iACE Gandhinar courses include a post-graduate programme and an advanced certificate programme. The teaching methodology includes 80% practical and 20% theoretical knowledge.

iACE Gandhinagar admission to the courses is based on the practical assessment test and merit, and the selection process is made directly. iACE Gandhinagar application process starts five months before the course commencement date. Application for admission may be downloaded from the website and submitted with the necessary application fee. International Automobile Centre of Excellence course fees ranges between Rs 57,000- Rs 1,45,000. The Centre of Excellence provides opportunities for students to get industry exposure through internships, industrial visits, and guest lectures.

International Automobile Centre of Excellence Placement Cell collaborates with renowned organisations such as Suzuki Motors, MG Hector, Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, Yangfeng, etc. iACE Gandhinagar placement cell assist students during campus placement drive. The International Automobile Centre of Excellence highest salary in 2023 offered was Rs 12 lakh per annum. iACE Gandhinagar campus has numerous facilities such as a hostel, 24-hour hot and cold-water availability, laundry facility available, wifi, etc.

International Automobile Centre of Excellence Placements

The International Automobile Centre of Excellence provides multiple opportunities for student placement in reputable businesses that are prepared to offer high, medium, and ordinary pay packages. More than 800 companies visited in iACE Gandhinagar campus placement drive. Each year, iACE Gandhinagar placement cell places students in prestigious companies. The iACE Gandhinagar top recruiters include Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, Yangfeng, UNOMinda, TDSG, Hella, Cars 24, Spinny, and Asian Paints. Top organisations offer attractive pay packages. iACE Gandhinagar's average salary stood at Rs 12 LPA in the academic year 2023.
