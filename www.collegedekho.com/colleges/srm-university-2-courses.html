SRM University Chennai Courses and Fees 2026

SRM University Chennai fees range from Rs 10,000 to Rs 50,00,000 per year, depending on the degree level, duration and specialisation of the course. The college offers 41 courses at Certificate, UG, Ph.D, Diploma and PG programmes. SRM University Chennai courses list includes 14 UG courses and 21 PG courses. BTech is offered in a total of 18 specialisations whereas BA is offered in 2 specialisations. At the PG level, the students can choose a wide range of specialisations - 34 courses in M.Tech, 26 course in MSc and many more. SRM University conducts it’s own entrance exam - SRMJEEE for admission to its various courses.  In addition to this, UGC NET for PhD, TANCET for MBA, JEE Main for BTech, NEET for medical courses, CEED for MDes,  CAT for MBA, GATE for MTech are accepted for SRM University Chennai admissions.

At the UG level, SRM University Chennai offers BSc, B.Tech, B.Ed, B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Com, BS, BBA, BCA, BA, Pharm.D, LLB, BA+LLB, B.Com+LLB, B.Des, B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Arch, BDS, B.Pharm , etc, while at the PG level, SRM University Chennai offers M.Tech, MSc, MD, MDS, MPT, M.Pharm, PGD, M.Optom, MBA, MS (Surgery), MCh, M.Phil, MA, MPH, M.Com, MHA, MCA, LLM, M.Des, M.Arch, etc.

The most popular SRM University Chennai courses include the BSc, B.Tech, B.Ed, B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Com and BS programmes. UG courses also include BSc, B.Tech, B.Ed, B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Com, BS, BBA, BCA, BA, Pharm.D, LLB, BA+LLB, B.Com+LLB, B.Des, B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Arch, BDS, B.Pharm, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MSc, MD, MDS, MPT, M.Pharm, PGD, M.Optom, MBA, MS (Surgery), MCh, M.Phil, MA, MPH, M.Com, MHA, MCA, LLM, M.Des, M.Arch , etc. SRM University Chennai also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does SRM University Chennai accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at SRM University Chennai. Admission to programmes such as BSc, B.Tech, B.Ed, B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Com, BS, BBA, BCA, BA, Pharm.D, LLB, BA+LLB, B.Com+LLB, B.Des, B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Arch, BDS, B.Pharm and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is SRM University Chennai easy to get into?

Admission in SRM University Chennai courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Certificate, UG, Ph.D, Diploma and PG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the SRM University Chennai Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include CBSE 10th, CBSE 12th, JEECUP, UGC NET, TANCET MBA, CSIR NET, SRMJEEE, JEE Main, NEET MDS, NEET, SRMJEEE (PG), Tamilnadu 12th, Tamilnadu 10th, TANCET, CEED, CAT, GATE, NEET SS etc.
