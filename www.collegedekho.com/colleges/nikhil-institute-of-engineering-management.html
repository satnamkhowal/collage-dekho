About Nikhil Institute of Engineering & Management

Nikhil Institute of Engineering & Management is situated in Mathura in Uttar Pradesh state of India. Established in 2008, NIEM is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. Nikhil Institute of Engineering & Management offers 8 courses across 1 streams namely Engineering..Popular degrees offered at Nikhil Institute of Engineering & Management include B.Tech, Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Nikhil Institute of Engineering & Management is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Nikhil Institute of Engineering & Management, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Nikhil Institute of Engineering & Management is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
