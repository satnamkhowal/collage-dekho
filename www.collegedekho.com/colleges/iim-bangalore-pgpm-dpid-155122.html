About IIM Bangalore PGPM

IIM Bangalore PGPM (Post Graduate Programme in Management) course is offered at the postgraduate level. The PGPM programme at IIM Bangalore is of 2 years duration, offered through 6 terms. The course is offered as a 2-year full-time residential programme, designed to develop analytical, leadership, and managerial skills among students. The PGPM programme is offered in a total of 3 specialisations, including Management, Enterprise Management and Public Policy & Management.

As per the IIM Bangalore PGPM eligibility criteria, candidates need to pass a relevant UG programme with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved candidates). The selection of candidates for IIM Bangalore PGPM admission is entrance based. Candidates need to have a valid score in the CAT exam for selection. Along with the entrance exam, candidates also need to qualify for a Written Ability Test (WAT) and a Personal Interview (PI) for final selection. The annual fee for IIM Bangalore PGPM course is Rs 9,75,000 to Rs 16,50,000 per year, depending on the type of specialisation.

IIM Bangalore PGPM Eligibility Criteria 2026

It is mandatory for candidates to fulfill the minimum IIM Bangalore PGPM eligibility for admission. The detailed IIM Bangalore PGPM eligibility criteria is given below:

  • Candidates must hold a Bachelor’s degree or equivalent from a recognised university.
  • Candidates must obtain a minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD categories)
  • Candidates must have a valid CAT score
  • Final-year graduation students are also eligible to apply for the IIM Bangalore PGPM course, given they fulfill the minimum aggregate requirement.

IIM Bangalore PGPM Admission Process 2026

Admission to the IIM Bangalore PGPM programme is entirely entrance based. Candidates need to have a valid score in the CAT exam for admission. The step-by-step IIM Bangalore PGPM admission process is given below:

Step 1 - Appear for CAT: Candidates must appear for the CAT examination conducted by the IIMs and obtain a valid score in the exam.

Step 2 - Shortlisting for WAT + PI: Candidates are shortlisted for written test and personal interview based on CAT percentile, academic profile, and category-wise cutoffs. It is also mandatory for candidates to pass these rounds,

Step 3 - Written Ability Test (WAT): Shortlisted candidates must appear for the Written Ability Test, which assesses written communication and analytical skills.

Step 4 - Personal Interview (PI): Through personal interview round, candidates are evaluated on their communication skills, academic knowledge, work experience (if any), leadership potential, and overall personality

Step 5 - Final Selection: Final selection for IIM Bangalore PGPM admission is based on a composite score, including:

  • CAT score
  • WAT score
  • PI performance
  • Academic consistency
  • Work experience and diversity factors

Step 6 - Offer Letter & Admission Confirmation: Selected candidates for IIM Bangalore PGPM admission receive a provisional admission offer and must pay the acceptance fee to confirm their seat.
