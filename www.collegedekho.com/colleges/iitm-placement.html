IIT Madras Placement Overview 2025

The placements for IIT Madras are managed by its dedicated placement cell every year. The cell is responsible for preparing students for placements and bringing recruiters to the campus. As per the IIT Madras placement report 2024, a total of 256 companies visited the campus for placements, and a total of 1,612 offers were made. 1,091 students were placed with an average package of INR 22 LPA (B.Tech). Moreover, the highest package offered during IIT Madras placements was more than INR 4 Crore per annum. Some of the top recruiters who visited IIT Madras for placements included Apple, Google, Texas Instruments, Qualcomm, Bajaj Auto Ltd., Microsoft, and JP Morgan Chase & Co..

FAQs About IIT Madras

Is IIT Madras good for placements?

Indian Institute of Technology Madras is one of the best colleges for placements in India. Every year more than 90% students from engineering courses and 100% students from management courses get placed in top-notch companies with handsome packages during IIT Madras placements.

Which branch of IIT Madras has the best placements record?

As per the IIT Madras placement report of the last few years, management courses have the best placement record at the institute. In the year 2023 and 2024, IIT Madras achieved 100% placement for the Master of Business Administration (MBA) course.

What is the highest package for BTech in IIT Madras?

The institute is yet to release the 2024 highest package for BTech. However, in 2023, IIT Madras highest package for BTech was Rs 1,31,63,530 per annum. It was offered to a BTech Computer Science and Engineering student.

Who are the top recruiters of IIT Madras?

Apple, Microsoft, Google, Intel, Cognizant etc. are some of the top recruiters of IIT Madras for the engineering domain. For management domain, the top recruiters are Accenture, Big Basket, KPMG, Deloitte, HDFC Bank etc.

Does IIT Madras have 100% placement?

Yes, IIT Madras has 100% placements for Master of Business Administration (MBA) course. The college achieved a 100% placement for MBA in the year 2024 and 2023. For engineering courses, the placements are around 90%.

What is the minimum package in IIT Madras?

The institute has not released the minimum package data for the year 2024. In the year 2023, the minimum package offered during IIT Madras placement was Rs 5,40,000 per annum.

What is the highest package in IIT Madras for MBA?

As per the IIT Madras placement report 2024 for MBA, the highest package stood at Rs 29,65,000 per annum. In 2023, the highest package for MBA was Rs 30,06,000 per annum. A 100% placement has been achieved by IIT Madras for the MBA course in 2024.

What is the highest package in IIT Madras for BTech CSE?

The 2024 placement data is yet to be released by the institute. According to the IIT Madras placement report 2023, the highest package for BTech CSE was Rs 1,31,63,530 per annum. It was also the overall highest package of the institute in 2023.

What is the placement percentage at IIT Madras?

IIT Madras placement percentage is around 90% for engineering courses and 100% for management courses. The institute has a Placement & Internship Cell that looks after placement and summer internships at the campus.

How many companies visit IIT Madras placements?

During phase 1 and phase 2 placements of 2024 (till May), a total of 256 companies participated in IIT Madras placements. In the year 2023, more than 480+ companies hired IIT Madras students. Some of the regular recruiters are Google, Microsoft, Dell, KPMG, ICICI Bank etc.

Placements at IIT Madras: Paving the Path to Professional Success

Placement Placements at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras) are a testament to the institute's commitment to excellence and its reputation as a premier engineering institution in India. The institute's dedicated placement cell works tirelessly to facilitate interactions between students and prospective employers, organize recruitment drives, and provide career guidance and support to students throughout the placement process. Diverse Recruitment Pool: IIT Madras attracts a diverse pool of recruiters from various sectors including technology, finance, consulting, manufacturing, and research and development. Leading multinational corporations, startups, government organizations, and research institutions vie for the opportunity to recruit top talent from the institute, offering a wide array of job profiles and career paths to students. Industry Connections: The institute's strong industry connections and alumni network play a pivotal role in facilitating placements. Recruiters value the rigorous academic training, practical exposure, and problem-solving skills that students acquire during their tenure at IIT Madras, making them highly sought after in the job market. Internship Opportunities: In addition to placements, IIT Madras also provides students with ample internship opportunities, allowing them to gain valuable industry exposure, hands-on experience, and professional skills. Many internship experiences often translate into pre-placement offers (PPOs) or full-time job offers, providing students with a head start in their careers. High Placement Statistics: The placement statistics at IIT Madras are consistently impressive, with a high percentage of students securing job offers from reputed companies even before the completion of their degree programs. The average salary packages offered to students are competitive, reflecting the quality of education, talent, and skills that they possess. Career Guidance and Support: The placement cell at IIT Madras offers comprehensive career guidance and support services to students, including resume building workshops, mock interviews, personality development sessions, and industry-specific training programs. These initiatives help students prepare effectively for the recruitment process and enhance their employability quotient. In conclusion, placements at IIT Madras serve as a gateway to promising career prospects and professional success for students. A
Apoorva Bali •  Jan-30-2025

The results of any free IIT JAM mock test you attempted will usually be accessible on the same website or platform where you took the test. You can check your results by doing the following:

  • Check the platform or website:

Return to the website where the mock exam was held if it was one of such sites (such as the platform of a coaching centre or an educational website). Use the login information you used to register to access your account, if you have one. Look for a section that says "My Results," "Test History," or "Dashboard," since these might include your mock test results and feedback.

  • Instant Results:

Results from a lot of free practice exams are available right away. If so, see if your score appeared on the platform immediately after you finished the test.

  • Notification via Email:

After you finish the test, some platforms send you an email with your findings. Look for any correspondence on the outcomes of your practice exam in your inbox (and spam folder).

  • Speak with Support:

You can get in touch with the helpdesk or support staff of the website or platform where you took the test if you are unable to locate your results. They ought to be able to help you find your score.
