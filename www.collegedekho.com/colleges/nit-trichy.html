About NIT Trichy

National Institute of Technology Tiruchirappalli, also known as NIT Trichy, is a public institute established in the year 1964. Located in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, the institute is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is also approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). NIT Trichy is also accredited by the NBA. As per NIRF 2025 ranking, NIT Trichy has been ranked 30th in Overall Category with NIRF Score 60.05, 9th in the Engineering Category with NIRF Score 68.14 , 9th in the Architecture and Planning Category with NIRF Score 64.30, 33rd in Research Institutions Category with NIRF Score 51.40, and 57th in the Management Category with NIRF Score 52.47.

NIT Trichy offers courses at the UG, PG, and PhD levels. At the UG level, institute offers courses like BTech, BArch, etc. Meanwhile, at the PG level, courses like MBA, MSc, MTech, MCA, etc. are offered. These courses are offered in various specialisations. The institute provides admission on the basis of merit and entrance exam scores. The entrance exams required for admission to the NIT Trichy include JEE Main for BTech and BArch, IIT JAM for MSc, GATE for MTech, CAT/ MAT/ CMAT for MBA, etc.

The institute has a placement cell for the training and placement of students. As per NIT Trichy placement statistics, the highest package offered was Rs 64 LPA, and the average package offered was Rs 17.50 LPA. Its campus has modern facilities and infrastructure, including separate hostels for boys and girls, an auditorium, smart classrooms, library, laboratories, gym, etc. for the holistic development of the students.

NIT Trichy Admission Process 2026

National Institute of Technology Tiruchirappalli admission process 2026 is conducted through national-level entrance exams and centralised counselling. Candidates must qualify the required entrance exam based on the chosen programme and register for the respective counselling process. Seats are allotted according to entrance exam rank, category, and preferences. Selected candidates must accept the seat, complete document verification, pay the admission fee, and finish institute registration to confirm admission:

Step 1: Select the Programme

  • Candidates must first choose the programme they wish to apply for, such as BTech, BArch, MTech, MArch, MSc, MBA, MCA, MA, PhD, or MS by Research, based on their academic background and career goals.

Step 2: Qualify the Required Entrance Examination

Applicants must appear for and qualify the relevant national-level entrance exam:

  • BTech / BArch: JEE Main
  • MTech / MArch: GATE
  • MSc: IIT JAM
  • MBA: CAT
  • MCA: NIMCET
  • MA (English): CUET-PG
  • PhD / MS (Research): National fellowships or institute selection process

Step 3: Register for Centralised Counselling

After qualifying the entrance exam, candidates must register on the appropriate counselling platform:

  • JoSAA / CSAB for BTech and BArch
  • CCMT for MTech, MArch, and MPlan
  • CCMN for MSc and MSc (Tech)
  • DASA, ICCR, or Study in India for international admissions

Step 4: Fill Choices and Participate in Seat Allotment

  • Candidates must fill their preferred programmes and institutes during counselling.
  • Seat allotment is done based on entrance exam rank, category, preferences, and seat availability.

Step 5: Accept the Allotted Seat

  • Once a seat is allotted, candidates must accept the seat within the given deadline and pay the seat acceptance fee through the counselling portal.

Step 6: Document Verification and Reporting

  • Candidates must upload and verify academic and personal documents online. Physical reporting at NIT Trichy may be required as per counselling guidelines.

Step 7: Complete Institute Registration

  • After successful verification, candidates must pay the remaining admission fee and complete institute-level registration to confirm admission.

NIT Trichy Application Process 2026

Candidates can fill out the NIT Trichy application form in online or offline modes. The admission form can be downloaded from the college website. The step-by-step process to fill out NIT Trichy application form is explained below:

  • Visit the official website at nitt.edu. Go to the “Admissions” section and click on the “Application Form” link.
  • Download the application form. Save the PDF form on your device and take a printout.

    • Class 10 & 12 mark sheets
    • Admit card/ rank card of the respective exam
    • Caste certificate (if applicable)
    • Domicile certificate
    • Passport-sized photographs
  • Submit the application.
  • Keep a copy for future reference.
