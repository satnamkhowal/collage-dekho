About Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur BSc

Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur BSc is a 3 Years year program. To be eligible for a BSc degree at Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Additionally, they must have studied Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects in their qualifying examination. Candidates may also be required to clear an entrance exam conducted by the college or meet any other specific requirements set by the institution. Meeting these criteria will ensure that students have the necessary foundation to excel in their BSc program at Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur. The annual fee for BSc is INR 20,000. Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur BSc Application Process

Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur BSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BSc course at Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur BSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur BSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur BSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur BSc Application Form.
