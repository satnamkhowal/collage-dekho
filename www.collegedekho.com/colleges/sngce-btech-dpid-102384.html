About SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech

SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Sree Narayana Gurukulam College of Engineering Ernakulam, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their higher secondary education (10+2) or its equivalent with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Additionally, they should have a valid score in entrance examinations such as KEAM (Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical) or any other national-level engineering entrance exams. Meeting these requirements ensures that students possess a strong foundation in the core subjects and are well-prepared to excel in the rigorous academic curriculum offered by the college. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 50,000. SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at SNGCE Ernakulam is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech Application Process

SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at SNGCE Ernakulam have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of SNGCE Ernakulam and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of SNGCE Ernakulam. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for SNGCE Ernakulam B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

Extracurricular activities like sports and cultural events are available, but they might not be as extensive as in other colleges. Social life on campus is average, with students forming friendships and participating in various clubs and societies. Overall, SNGCE College provides a satisfactory educational experience, but it may not stand out compared to other institutions in terms of academic rigor or extracurricular opportunities.

Infrastructure SNGCE College's infrastructure is decent, providing students with basic facilities. The campus includes well-maintained classrooms equipped with necessary amenities like projectors and whiteboards. The library offers a good collection of books and study materials, although it could benefit from more digital resources. Laboratories are functional, providing students with hands-on experience in their respective fields of study. Faculty SNGCE faculty is knowledgeable and diverse, bringing expertise to various disciplines. They support students through teaching and mentorship, fostering academic growth.
