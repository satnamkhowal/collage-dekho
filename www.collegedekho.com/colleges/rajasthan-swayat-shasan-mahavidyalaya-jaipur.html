About Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, Jaipur

Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya is a prestigious college in Jaipur city which is relentlessly pursuing the path of excellence in academic pursuits. Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya was established in the year 2004 by Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Sansthan with the objective to reduce the growing gap between the demand and supply of trained professionals. It is affiliated to the University of Rajasthan, Jaipur. 

Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya aims at preparing the students for sequential knowledge learning and train them to become perfect professionals and leaders in society. To inculcate professionalism and management skills in the students, strategic teaching is imparted with a focus on conceptual as well as empirical learning. Along with theoretical teaching, the practical application of knowledge is the core concern of the education of the college. 

Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya courses are offerd in the fields of Science, Arts and Commerce. Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya, with its dynamic team of well-qualified and experienced faculty, focuses on fostering talent in the most conducive environment on the campus. Rajasthan Swayat Shasan Mahavidyalaya placement is remarkably good. The purpose of all the courses in the college is to expose the students to concepts, techniques & decision working tools through an interactive learning process. Rajasthan Swayat Sashan Mahavidyalaya placement is also varied.
