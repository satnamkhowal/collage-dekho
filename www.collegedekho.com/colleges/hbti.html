Harcourt Butler Technical University Application Process

Candidates can follow the steps listed below if they want to apply for admission to the university. The Harcourt Butler Technical University admission procedure is conducted in both online and offline modes. 

  • Candidates can visit the HBTU Kanpur official website (hbtu.ac.in) for the application form.
  • The application form is released on the official website.
  • Candidates need to register themselves with a valid email id on the official website’s HBTU Kanpur admission portal.
  • Once the candidates are logged in, the application form will appear.
  • Fill out the form with the required documents and submit it via online mode.
  • The Harcourt Butler Technical University application fee for the form must be submitted only in online mode.
  • Take a printout of the filled application form and send it to the university with all the attached documents.
  • Candidates will be selected based on their qualifying marks.
  • The list of selected candidates will be released on the official website.
  • The selected candidates then have to visit the university for further admission procedures.

HBTU Kanpur Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The 77-acre east campus and the 271-acre west campus make up the two campuses of HBTU Kanpur, which provides a friendly atmosphere for students' holistic development and overall growth. The distance between the two campuses is around three kilometres. The university offers the necessary resources to both the staff members and students.

The HBTU Kanpur campus has received aid from the World Bank (phases TEQIP-I, TEQIP-II) for various developmental projects. This aid is to be spent on the infrastructural development, new and updated laboratory equipment, maintenance and repair of old laboratory equipment, funding for research and lectures attended by faculty in international universities and aim towards community development and overall growth.

Harcourt Butler Technical University Facilities

Given below are some of the HBTU Kanpur facilities offered at the campus:

Central Library: The university features a well-kept Tagore Central Library with more than 80,000 books, 25,000 printed journals and 400 or more electronic publications. Students can access the internet on computers in the library.

Central Workshop: Undergraduate, postgraduate, and research scholars/students can all receive instruction at the university's Central Workshop, which also helps them with the creation of their projects and experimental settings.

Laboratories: The HBTU Kanpur lab facility is top-notch. Each department has a designated laboratory such as civil engineering lab, chemistry lab, computer lab, electrical lab, electronics lab, mechanical lab, physics lab, etc.

Hostels: The university offers hostel facilities for both boys and girls. It has enough dorm rooms to accommodate many students. The HBTU hostels have their own mess, a canteen, a library, and a computer centre. The students are assigned based on merit and in accordance with university regulations. There is an option for single-occupancy rooms or shared rooms. 

Computer Centre: The students, staff, and teachers have access to the well-maintained computer labs for research and instruction. The computer centre at HBTU has good hardware and functional internet access.

Canteen: The East Campus of HBTU features a canteen that offers vegetarian snacks, fast food, tea, coffee, soft drinks, and mixed meals.

Auditorium: East Campus of the university has a fully air-conditioned auditorium equipped with good light and sound system. 

Bank: A fully operational branch of the Central Bank of India and an ATM are both located on the Harcourt Butler Technical University East Campus.

Sports Complex: There are a variety of facilities available, including basketball courts, handball courts, cricket grounds, football fields, tennis courts, table tennis courts, and hockey rinks.

Other Facilities: The other facilities offered at the Harcourt Butler Technical University campus are a virtual studio, guest house, post office, gymnasium, health centre, convenience store, bank, incubation centre, music room, dance room, etc.

HBTU Kanpur Scholarships

The university offers scholarships to its students in the form of fee waivers, under which they are only required to pay a third of their tuition. There are just a few seats available in this category for students from the reserved category who have achieved high qualifying marks and are eligible for the HBTU Kanpur scholarships. Additionally, the government of Uttar Pradesh offers scholarships to students who meet the requirements and are members of reserved categories and have low incomes.
