About Gandhinagar Institute of Technology

Gandhinagar Institute of Technology is a private engineering college located in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. The institute was established in 2006. Gandhinagar Institute of Technology has been recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). As per NIRF Ranking 2025, Gandhinagar Institute of Technology has been ranked at 251-300 under the Engineering category. Some of the facilities at Gandhinagar Institute of Technology include modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, hostels, and a sports complex. Gandhinagar Institute of Technology offers courses at undergraduate (UG) levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include B.Tech in Computer Science, Electronics, and Civil Engineering. Admission to Gandhinagar Institute of Technology is based on merit as well as entrance exams. The college accepts JEE Main and GUJCET scores for admission. Regarding placements, the highest package offered in 2024 was Rs. 18 LPA, and the average package offered was Rs. 4.5 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include TCS, Wipro, and Infosys.

Why Join Gandhinagar Institute of Technology?

Here are some reasons why Gandhinagar Institute of Technology stands out as a top choice for students:

  • Diverse Academic Programs: Offers 9 programs across undergraduate levels in Engineering, Computer Science, and Technology.
  • Experienced Faculty: Faculty members have an average of 12 years of teaching and industry experience.
  • State-of-the-Art Infrastructure: A 20-acre campus with facilities like tech-enabled classrooms, specialized labs, hostels, and sports grounds.
  • Strong Placement Record: Placement rate of 85%, with the highest package of Rs. 18 LPA per annum.
  • Global Exposure: Collaborations with institutions like the University of New York for research and internships.
  • Holistic Development: Focus on extracurricular activities through various clubs, societies, and events.

Gandhinagar Institute of Technology Admissions 2025

Gandhinagar Institute of Technology admission to its courses is done based on entrance exams. The college accepts JEE Main for B.Tech courses, GUJCET for regional admission, and GATE for M.Tech courses. Candidates must also ensure that they meet the eligibility criteria for the respective courses before applying for Gandhinagar Institute of Technology admission.

Gandhinagar Institute of Technology Admission Process 2025

  • Eligibility Check: Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria for their chosen program (10+2 for UG courses, and bachelor’s degree for PG courses).
  • Application Form: Fill out the application form available on the official website.
  • Entrance Exam: Appear for JEE Main, GUJCET, or GATE, depending on the program.
  • Document Submission: Submit required documents like mark sheets, photographs, and identity proof.
  • Counseling Process: Participate in counseling sessions as scheduled.
  • Fee Payment: Complete the admission by paying the necessary fees.
