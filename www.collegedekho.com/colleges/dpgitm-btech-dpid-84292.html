About DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech

DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. degree at DPG Institute of Technology & Management Gurgaon, aspiring candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria. Firstly, they should have completed their 10+2 examination or its equivalent from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, candidates must have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 45% (40% for reserved categories) in the qualifying examination. Furthermore, they must have a valid score/rank in JEE Main or Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) counseling. Meeting these criteria will enable candidates to apply for the B.Tech. program at DPG Institute of Technology & Management Gurgaon. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 89,000 - 2,68,000. DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at DPG Institute of Technology & Management is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech Application Process

DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at DPG Institute of Technology & Management have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of DPG Institute of Technology & Management and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of DPG Institute of Technology & Management. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for DPG Institute of Technology & Management B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
