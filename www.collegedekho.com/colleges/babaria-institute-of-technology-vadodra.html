About BABARIA INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY

Babaria Institute of Technology is a private engineering college located in Vadodara, Gujarat. The institute was established in 2004. Babaria Institute of Technology has been recognized by AICTE and is accredited by NAAC. The institute offers facilities such as a well-equipped library, advanced laboratories, hostels, and sports complexes.

Babaria Institute of Technology courses are offered at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include B.Tech, M.Tech, and MBA. Babaria Institute of Technology admission is based on entrance exams. The college accepts JEE Main and GUJCET for B.Tech admissions and GATE for M.Tech admissions. Regarding Babaria Institute of Technology placements, the highest package offered in 2024 was Rs. 9.98 LPA, and the average package offered was Rs. 4.5 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and Accenture.

Why Join Babaria Institute of Technology?

Here are some reasons why Babaria Institute of Technology stands out as a top choice for students:

  • Diverse Academic Programs: Offers 15+ programs across undergraduate and postgraduate levels in engineering and management.
  • Experienced Faculty: Faculty members have an average of 10+ years of teaching and industry experience.
  • State-of-the-Art Infrastructure: A 130-acre campus with facilities like tech-enabled classrooms, modern laboratories, and hostels.
  • Strong Placement Record: placement rate of 85%, with the highest package of Rs. 9.98 LPA per annum.
  • Global Exposure: Collaborations with institutions like IITs and foreign universities for research and internships.
  • Holistic Development: Focus on extracurricular activities through various clubs, societies, and annual events.
