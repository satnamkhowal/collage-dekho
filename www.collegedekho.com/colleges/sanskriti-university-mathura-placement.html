Sanskriti University Placement Overview 2025

The placements for Sanskriti University are managed by its placement cell every year. The cell is responsible for preparing students for placements and bringing recruiters to the campus. As per Sanskriti University placement report 2025, a total of 200+ companies visited the campus for placements, and a total of 273 offers were made. 273 students were placed with an average package of ₹6.40 LPA. Moreover, the highest package offered during Sanskriti University placements was ₹54 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visited Sanskriti University for placements included Genpact, Larsen & Toubro, Hilfiger, Coca-Cola, Wipro, and Tech Mahindra.

Sanskriti University Placements FAQs

What is the placement percentage of Sanskriti University?

The overall Sanskriti University placement percentage is 91%. The university has provided the course-wise placement percentage that highlights 100 percent placement for the MBA programme with the highest package of 45 LPA. Further, the university has maintained 100 percent placement for BBA and diploma programmes as well. The Sanskriti University B.Tech placement percentage is 93 percent with the highest offered package of Rs 54 LPA.  Moreover, the university has achieved 100 percent placement for the M.Tech programme with the highest package of Rs 6.5 LPA.

How effective is Sanskriti University placement cell?

The Sanskriti University placement cell ensures that the maximum number of students are placed on campus and that eligible applicants are given a good start in their careers. The T&P cell also makes certain that the placements are of high quality. Every student is provided with placement opportunities by the T&P cell of Sanskriti University. The T&P Cell is operational all year. On a regular basis, an increasing number of reputable companies are contacted so that students have more opportunities to appear for interviews in a wider number of organisations and choose the best options for their placement in specific organisations.

Who are the top recruiters of Sanskriti University?

Sanskriti University holds good industrial relations with several organisations in India and abroad. Every year, top recruiters from many industries attend the Sanskriti University placement sessions. The top recruiters for engineering discplines are Amazon, IBM, HCL, Tata Steel, Oracle and more. On the other hand, Sanskriti University MBA recruiters include TSC, HDFC Bank, Nestle, BYJU'S, Godrej, NIIT, Kotak Mahindra Bank and more. Corporate meetings are held at least twice a year. Such gatherings allow corporate leaders to interact with students and learn about their aptitude, attitude, expertise, personality, and so on.

How is Sanskriti University B.Tech placement?

Sanskriti University engineering placement is among the highest package-catching placements of the university. The Sanskriti University placement report as per NIRF 2023 has been made available by the university. As per the NIRF report 2023, the placement data for B.Tech and M.Tech courses is made available. The total seat intake for B.Tech and M.Tech programmes was 499 and 5, respectively in the academic year 2022. Moreover, 112 B.Tech students were placed in 2022 with a median salary of Rs 2.8 LPA. On the other hand, only 1 M.Tech student was placed with a salary package of Rs 5.1 LPA.

How is MBA placement at Sanskriti University?

The Sanskriti University MBA placement as per NIRF report 2023 is now available. Every year, the university releases the Sanskriti University management placement report. The NIRF report 2023 contains the placement data for the academic year 2022. As per the data, the SU MBA batch strength for the academic year 2022 was 126 students. Out of these students, 57 students graduated in the stipulated time. The total MBA students placed in 2022 were 52 and the Sanskriti University MBA median package offered was Rs 4.25 LPA. No student in the academic year 2022 was selected for higher studies.

Is Sankriti University placement good for pharmacy students?

For the pharmacy stream, Sanskriti University offers 3 programmes namely D.Pharm, B.Pharm and M.Pharm. The art and science of making and dispensing pharmaceuticals and medicines is known as pharmacy. The Sanskriti University B.Pharm course is a 4 years regular course which is intended to deliver valuable health care and biochemical scientific information. As per the NIRF report 2023, the total number of B.Pharm students placed at Sanskriti University was 50 and the median salary offered to the students was Rs 3.84 LPA. Moreover, 4 students were selected for higher studies.

Is Sanskriti University placement good for agriculture courses?

For the agriculture stream, the Sanskriti University B.Sc Agriculture (Hons.) course is offered by the university. It is a four-year bachelor's degree programme. Agriculture science, the application of contemporary scientific equipment and techniques in agriculture, livestock and poultry management are among the topics covered in the programme. The Sanskriti University B.Sc Agriculture placement stats as per the NIRF report 2023 are now available. As per the report, the total B.Sc Agriculture students placed in 2022 were 89. The median salary offered to the students was Rs 2.8 LPA and 10 students were selected for higher studies.

What is the highest placement offered at Sanskriti University?

The Sanskriti University highest package varies for each course. As per the official website, some of the students were offered multiple job offers during the campus placement drive. However, collectively, the highest package offered to one of the B.Tech students was Rs 54 LPA. The Sanskriti University MBA highest package was Rs 45 LPA followed by the M.Tech highest package of Rs 6.5 LPA. Not only that, the university has released the Sanskriti University diploma placement results as well. The highest diploma package offered to one of the students was Rs 2.2 LPA.
