About ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech

ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at ITM (SLS) Baroda University Vadodara, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects. Additionally, they must secure a minimum aggregate score of 50% in their qualifying examination. The university also considers scores from national level entrance exams such as JEE Main or GUJCET. It is important to note that the eligibility criteria may vary based on government regulations and the university's discretion. Prospective students are advised to visit the official university website for the most accurate and up-to-date information. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 85,000 - 3,92,000. ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at ITM (SLS) Baroda University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech Application Process

ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at ITM (SLS) Baroda University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of ITM (SLS) Baroda University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of ITM (SLS) Baroda University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for ITM (SLS) Baroda University B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
