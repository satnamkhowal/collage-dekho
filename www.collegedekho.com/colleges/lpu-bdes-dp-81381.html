About LPU B.Des

LPU B.Des is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. Lovely Professional University requires applicants for the B.Des. (Bachelor of Design) degree to have a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board. Applicants must also have passed English as a subject in 10+2 or equivalent examination. The annual fee for B.Des is INR 1,60,000 - 2,80,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for LPU B.Des admission are given below:

LPU B.Des Fees

The annual fee for the LPU B.Des program ranges from INR 1,60,000 to INR 2,80,000, depending on the chosen specialization. Admission for 2025 is based on LPUNEST, UCEED, UG NID, or UG NIFT, with applicable relaxations and exemptions as per university norms.

LPU B.Des Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility for B.Design at LPU requires either passing 10+2 with at least 50% marks (English compulsory, waived for some) or holding a relevant diploma for lateral entry. Admission is through LPUNEST, UCEED, UG NID, or UG NIFT, with relaxations and exemptions for eligible candidates

Eligibility ComponentRequirement
Educational QualificationPass in 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board
Minimum Marks Required50% aggregate marks
Mandatory SubjectEnglish (waived if studied in English medium or equivalent subject)
Entrance Exams AcceptedLPUNEST, UCEED, UG NID, UG NIFT
LPUNEST ExemptionNot required if candidate scores 90% or more in the qualifying exam
Relaxation Criteria5% relaxation for North-East states & Sikkim candidates, Defence personnel & dependents, and Wards of Kashmiri Migrants

A Launchpad for Fashion Careers: Overall Review

Overall My journey through the B.Des Fashion Design program at LPU has been incredibly enriching and creatively stimulating. The university provides an excellent platform for aspiring designers, focusing on both artistic development and industry readiness. The admission process was clear, with eligibility typically requiring a creative aptitude test (like LPUNEST for design) and a good academic record. LPU also offers various scholarships, including those based on design entrance exam scores and merit, making quality design education more accessible. They also facilitate education loan options through banking partners. LPU truly serves as a launchpad for a successful career in the dynamic fashion world.

Placement LPU's placement cell for Fashion Design students works diligently to open doors to various segments of the fashion industry. They facilitate internships and placement opportunities with design houses, retail brands, and export houses. The university organizes portfolio review sessions, mock interviews, and workshops on market trends, which are crucial for a successful entry into the industry. Eligibility for placements often involves a strong portfolio and academic performance. Many of our seniors have secured roles as designers, merchandisers, and stylists, demonstrating the effectiveness of the placement support.

Infrastructure LPU's infrastructure for the B.Des Fashion Design program is truly inspiring and perfectly tailored for creative pursuits. The design studios are spacious, well-lit, and equipped with industry-standard machinery and tools, allowing for practical hands-on experience. We have access to dedicated labs for textile printing, pattern making, and garment construction, fostering skill development. The campus also features exhibition spaces to showcase student work, which is fantastic for gaining exposure. The overall aesthetic of the campus encourages innovation and provides a stimulating environment for aspiring designers.

Faculty The faculty in the Fashion Design program at LPU are exceptional mentors who truly understand the nuances of the fashion industry. They are a blend of experienced academics and professionals who bring real-world design challenges and trends into the classroom. Their teaching methodology encourages experimentation, critical thinking, and individual artistic expression. They are always available to provide constructive feedback, guide us through complex design projects, and connect us with industry opportunities. Their dedication to nurturing individual design talent is truly remarkable.

Hostel The hostel facilities at LPU offer a comfortable and secure living space, allowing fashion design students to focus on their creative endeavors. The rooms are adequately furnished, providing a quiet environment for sketching and ideation. The common areas are well-maintained, fostering a sense of community among students. The mess offers a variety of food options, ensuring students receive nutritious meals. With comprehensive security measures and supportive hostel staff, it truly provides a worry-free environment, which is essential when deadlines for design projects are looming.

FAQs about LPU B.Des

Who are the top recruiters for the LPU BDes placements?

Some of the top recruiting companies that hire B.Des graduates from LPU are Bullspree, Navigus, Syska, Amour Design, Pepperfry, Khadi India, Hind Rubbers, Reliance Industries Ltd, Under Armour, Kabir Leathers, etc.

What are the entrance exams accepted for LPU BDes course admissions?

The entrance test accepted for admission to the B.Des courses at LPU is the LPUNEST online test. It is conducted by the university itself. Other than this, it also accepts CUET exam scores for admission.

What is the Lovely Professional University BDes average package?

The average package offered to the students of the LPU B.Des courses is Rs 5.4 LPA.

What are some of the industry tie-ups of Lovely Professional University?

Some of the industry tie-ups for the B.Des programme of LPU include the Quality Circle Forum of India, Punjab Energy Development Agency, CBL, AIESEC, Confederation of Indian Industry and GEM ASSOCHAM.

What is the Lovely Professional University BDes highest package offered?

The highest package offered to the students of Lovely Professional University B.Des course is Rs 12 LPA.

How many specialisations are under the Lovely Professional University BDes courses?

There are a total of 5 specialisations under the B.Des course offered at LPU namely Product & Industrial, Multimedia, Fashion, Interior & Furniture and User Experience (UX/ UI).

What is the exam pattern for LPUNEST for the BDes course?

LPUNEST for B.Des consists of 100 MCQs with total marks of 100. The sections are Design Thinking (50 marks), General Awareness (25 marks) and Communication Ability (25 marks). The exam duration is 2 hours 30 minutes.

What is the highest stipend offered to LPU BDes students?

The highest stipend offered to the students of the LPU B.Des courses is Rs 25,000 per month.

What are the LPU BDes eligibility criteria?

For admission to the B.Des courses at LPU, candidates must have passed 10+2 with 50% marks in aggregate (with English) or an equivalent exam from a recognised Board/ University/ Council, subject to qualifying LPUNEST or UCEED or CUET or UG NID DAT (Prelims) or UG NIFT or NIFT B.FTech.

What are the Lovely Professional University BDes fees?

The Lovely Professional University BDes fees structure ranges from Rs 1.20 lakh per semester.
