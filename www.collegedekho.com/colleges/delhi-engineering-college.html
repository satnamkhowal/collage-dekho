About DEC Faridabad

Delhi Engineering College is situated in Faridabad in Haryana state of India. Established in 2009, DEC is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak and accredited by ISO, DTE, AICTE. Delhi Engineering College offers 14 courses across 1 streams namely Engineering..Popular degrees offered at Delhi Engineering College include B.Tech, Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Delhi Engineering College is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Delhi Engineering College, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Delhi Engineering College is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
