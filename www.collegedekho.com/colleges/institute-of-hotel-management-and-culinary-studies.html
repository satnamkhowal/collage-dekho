About Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Studies, Jaipur

Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Studies offers admission to various UG, diploma and certificate courses in the field of hotel and hospitality management. IHMCS courses list includes a total of seven job-oriented programmes. These include short-term courses from a month to three months and long-term courses from a year to three years of duration. Some of the  Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Studies courses are B.Voc in Hotel Management, Diploma in Culinary Arts and Certificate in Bakery and confectionery. 

Situated in Jaipur, the Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Studies is one of the most popular institutes for students wishing to pursue a career in the hospitality industry. Affiliated with the Rajasthan ILD Skills University, IHMCS Jaipur is quite sought-after for its excellent placements and global exposure. Some of the top recruiters at IHMCS placements are the Taj Group, ITC group of hotels, Radisson, Hyatt Regency, Oberoi Group, Courtyard Marriott, Indigo, and many more. 

IHMCS Jaipur facilities include spacious classrooms, spaces designated for culinary students, a stellar cafeteria, recreational services as well as workshop facilities for students. Moreover, the Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Sciences also has various foreign collaborations, such as the IHMCS collaboration with College De Paris, allowing the students to receive ample industrial exposure.

Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Studies, Jaipur Courses and Admission 2026

IHMCS Jaipur is a popular hospitality management institute in Jaipur, Rajasthan. IHMCS Jaipur course list includes certificate, diploma and undergraduate courses. IHMCS B.Voc in Hotel Management is offered as a UG programme. Class 12 pass-outs are eligible to apply for this course. IHMCS diploma programme is offered with various specialisations such as Diploma In Hotel & Event Management, Food & Beverage Production, Culinary Arts, Food & Beverage Service, Housekeeping and Front Office. The institute offers a Certificate in Bakery and Confectionery programme. IHMCS fees for these course ranges from Rs 50,000 to 1,05,000. 

Students can check out the IHMCS Jaipur courses along with fees and eligibility criteria below.

Admissions at IHMCS Jaipur for 2024 are open. Interested students can apply for admission to various courses such as B.Voc, Diploma and certificate programmes. The IHMCS admission process 2024 is divided into three steps, the first step is to appear for an online test, then for the personal interview and finally register and apply for the course if selected. The Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Studies application form can be filled out offline and online. Check out the complete application process for IHMCS admission below. 

IHMCS Jaipur Application Process 2024

After qualifying for the entrance exam and PI, the candidate can register and apply for admission as given below.

Step 1: Download the application form from the website or apply online. 

Step 2: Fill out the IHMCS application form carefully with all the required details.

Step 3: Upload the required documents online or attach them with the physical form. 

Step 4: Pay the IHMCS Jaipur application fees which is Rs 500 online or offline. 

Step 5: Submit the application. 

The students will receive an admission confirmation letter through e-mail. For counselling the student must bring the documents given below. 

Documents Required for IHMCS Admission 2024

  • Matriculation/ High School Certificate 
  • Migration Certificate 
  • Transfer Certificate 
  • Aadhar Card
  • Four passport-size photographs

Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Studies, Jaipur Placements

The training and placement cell here at the Institute of Hotel Management and Culinary Studies is responsible for overseeing all the tasks related to placement opportunities and guiding the students through the entire process. The students at this institute are provided with placement offers from some of the best hospitality management companies in and outside the country. Some of the top recruiters at IHMCS Jaipur placements are Radisson, Hyatt Regency, Oberoi Group, Courtyard Marriott, Indigo, Taj Group, ITC group of hotels and many more. The institute organises interaction opportunities for students to meet celebrity chefs. Students also get to visit top hotels, attend mentorship programmes and skill-based workshops.

With hotel management and culinary sciences being the prime focus of this institute, the skills offered here are in high demand in the current job market. The students here bag offers from some of the most prestigious tourism chains in the country. 

The IHMCS placement cell also provides ample training and exposure to the students with the help of internships, workshops, seminars, projects, clubs and more. This allows them to be well-equipped in dealing with the assessment procedures set in place by prospective recruiters during the   IHMCS Jaipur Placements.
