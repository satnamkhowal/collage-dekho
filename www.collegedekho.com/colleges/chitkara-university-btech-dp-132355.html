About Chitkara University B.Tech

Chitkara University B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. The eligibility criteria for B.Tech. at Chitkara University, Patiala is a minimum of 50% marks in 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Candidates must also have a valid score in JEE Main or Chitkara University Engineering Entrance Test (CUEE). The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 2,00,000 - 8,00,000. Chitkara University B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Chitkara University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Chitkara University B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Chitkara University B.Tech Application Process

Chitkara University B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Chitkara University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Chitkara University B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Chitkara University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Chitkara University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Chitkara University B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Chitkara University B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Chitkara University B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Chitkara University B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

Overall As an ex- student and alumni of Chitkara University, I'm thrilled with the diverse range of courses offered, catering to various interests and career paths. The admission process is straightforward, with helpful guidance every step of the way. What's even better is the availability of scholarships, easing the financial burden and making quality education accessible to all. Chitkara University's scholarship options truly reflect their commitment to supporting students in their academic journey. With a supportive environment, top-notch faculty, and ample opportunities for personal and professional growth, Chitkara University is the perfect place to pursue higher education.

Placement At Chitkara University, I've witnessed an exceptional placement track record firsthand, with more than 90% of graduating students securing job offers before completing their studies. Renowned companies such as TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Google, Airtel, HCL, and numerous others actively engage in the placement process. Notably, the highest salary offered reaches INR 40 LPA, with an impressive average of INR 8.5 LPA. I received two offers, one from TCS and the other from HCL. The CTC offered was Rs 7.5 LPA and Rs 5.2 LPA, respectively.

Infrastructure The university campus is excellent, kept in top-notch condition. Wi-Fi coverage extends throughout the entire campus. The hostel amenities are of high quality, and the food served in the mess is satisfactory. Additionally, the university provides accessible medical facilities for students' needs.

Faculty Indeed, the teachers are supportive and adept at aiding your understanding of subjects. The teaching standard is commendable and clear. If you study diligently, you'll encounter minimal difficulty, and the semester exams prove manageable, especially if you prepare during the sessional tests, as they offer valuable insights for scoring well.

Hostel At Chitkara University, the hostel is like a comfy, safe, and convenient home. They keep it tidy and provide everything we need, like big rooms, good food, laundry, and fun areas. The university makes sure we're always safe with security all the time. Plus, they have caring wardens and helpers creating a friendly vibe where we all feel like we belong, especially for the first timers.
