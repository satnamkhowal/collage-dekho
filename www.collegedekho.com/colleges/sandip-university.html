About Sandip University

Sandip University is a private university located in Nashik, Maharashtra. The university is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited by the National Assessment Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an ‘A’ grade. Sandip University is also a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), the Education Promotion Society of India (EPSI), the All India Management Association (AIMA), etc. It is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), the Bar Council of India (BCA), etc. Last year, Sandip University was ranked 67th in India for MBA courses by TOI. Times B-School Ranking Survey ranked Sandip University as the 4th top-ranking university under the ‘Top Emerging B-School’ category. 

Sandip University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs through its 8 schools of study. Students can choose courses across different disciplines, including engineering, technology, management, law, and science. The university takes pride in having experienced faculty members from IITs and NITs and a strong alumni base of 10,000+ students. Sandip University guarantees 100% placement assistance to all its students. It also has an excellent track record of placing its students in leading companies and organizations. 

Sandip University has a sprawling campus of 250+ acres equipped with modern facilities. Along with this, the university provides various facilities such as scholarships, internships, industrial training, value-added courses and certifications, international exposure through collaborations with global universities, and much more.

Sandip University Admission 2026

Sandip University provides admission to undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and PhD courses. Interested students can fill out the Sandip University admission form available on the official website. Selection of candidates is based on their performance in the last qualifying examination or entrance exam. For example, Sandip University admission to UG courses such as B.Tech is based on SU JEE/MH-CET/JEE Main scores whereas admission to BBA and B.Com is based on Class 12th marks. Similarly, Sandip University PG admission to the MBA programme is based on CAT/MAT/XAT/State-CET or SU-MAT scores and admission to M.Sc and MCA is merit-based. The admission process for all courses generally starts with registration followed by an entrance exam/personal interview or merit-based selection. More details about Sandip University admission 2025 are mentioned below. 

Sandip University Application Process 2025 

The application process for Sandip University begins with registration for the desired course. The entire application process is online and hassle-free. Students just need to visit the official website and apply. Below are the important steps for the Sandip University application process to be followed by students. 

Step 1: Visit the official website of Sandip University and click on the ‘Admission’ button.

Step 2: Click on ‘Apply Now’ and fill out the Sandip University registration form following the instructions on the application portal.

Step 3: Upload the required documents such as Class 10/12/Graduation mark sheets. 

Step 4: Complete the Sandip University application fee payment online. 

Step 5: Receive provisional registration details on the registered email and phone number.

Sandip University Nashik Cutoff

Sandip University FAQs

Is Sandip University good for BTech?

Yes, Sandip University is considered a good choice for B.Tech by its alumni. The college provides good placement and exposure opportunities. Companies like Gabriel India Ltd. Nashik, Polygenta Technologies Ltd., Bosch Limited, Gebbs Healthcare Solutions Pvt. Ltd., MSS India, QSpider and Excel R recruited B.Tech students at Sandip University in 2023.

Does Sandip University provide scholarships?

Yes, Sandip University Nashik provides three kinds of scholarships based on merit and eligibility. Firstly, a merit-based scholarship is offered by Sandip University to students obtaining 75% to 90% marks in qualifying examinations. 50% to 100% of tuition fees is waived under this scheme. Similarly, sports scholarships and other scholarships are offered to eligible students.

What is the rank of Sandip University in India?

Sandip University was ranked in 4th position under the Top Emerging B-School by the Times B-School Ranking Survey 2023. Along with this, the university has been ranked as 3rd Top BBA Private University and 5th Top B-School Private University by Times Top Institutes for West India Survey 2020.

Is Sandip University's degree valid?

Sandip University is a self-financed private university recognised by UGC and accredited by NAAC with an A grade . The courses offered by Sandip University are approved by various councils such as BCA, PCI, AICTE, etc. Hence, the degrees awarded by the university are valid.

Is Sandip University good?

Yes, Sandip University is considered one of the leading private universities in India. It holds the prestigious NAAC A accreditation indicating a high standard of education and facilities. It offers a plethora of courses in various disciplines and industry-oriented education. Additionally, Sandip University provides experienced faculty, vibrant student life, modern infrastructure and 100% placement assistance to its students,

Are Sandip University placements better than Poornima University?

Yes, Sandip University Nashik provides better placement opportunities than Poornima University. A quick comparison reveals that the highest package offered at Sandip University was Rs 1.2 Crore (international) and Rs 28 LPA (domestic). On the other hand, the highest package offered at Poornima University stood at Rs 44 LPA. Along with this, more than 250 companies visited during Sandip University's placement in 2023.

Is Wi-Fi facility available at the Sandip University campus?

Yes, the campus of Sandip University is fully Wi-Fi enabled. Students can access online study material from anywhere across the campus. The campus has advanced labs with high-speed internet connectivity. Along with this, ICT-enabled facilities such as are available in classrooms at Sandip University.

Is attendance necessary at Sandip University Nashik?

Yes, a minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory for all the courses and subjects. Unless urgent, students have to attend minimum lectures, practical classes, and presentations to appear in final examinations. In the case of Training and Placement activities, 100% of attendance is mandatory. Also, attendance for group activities, case studies, and seminars conducted in every semester is compulsory.

What is the Sandip University hostel fees and facilities?

The annual fee for Sandip University hostel is 95,000 for most rooms and Rs 1,35,000 for double occupancy rooms. In addition to that, Rs 5,000 is to be paid as a security deposit. Sandip University offers hostel facilities to men and women with modern facilities. The rooms of the hostel are spacious, well-lit and well-furnished.

Does Sandip University offer an educational loan facility?

Yes, Sandip University provides educational loan facilities to students. The campus has branches of Bank of India and HDFC Bank to provide loan assistance to its students. Along with this, there are two facilities available for easy payment of tuition fees. Eduvanz and Financepeer are the two options providing easy EMI facilities to students at Sandip University.

Can I apply for more than one course at Sandip University?

Yes, you can apply for more than one programme at Sandip University. You can fill out one application form for each programme. However, you can’t join more than one course at a time. You will be allotted one course as per the admission criteria of Sandip University.

How to check Sandip University results?

Sandip University releases its results on a separate portal, www.sandipuniversity.edu.in/result/results.php. Visit the Sandip University result portal and click on the desired link. Now fill out the required fields such as ‘PRN’, and ‘Date of Birth’ and click submit. In this way, you can check Sandip University results for any programme.
