About Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech

Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Bon Maharaj Engineering College Mathura, interested candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Firstly, applicants should have successfully completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they must have secured a minimum aggregate score of 50% in these subjects. Furthermore, aspiring candidates must qualify in relevant entrance examinations such as JEE Main or any other state-level engineering entrance test. Meeting these requirements is essential to be considered for admission to the esteemed B.Tech. program at Bon Maharaj Engineering College Mathura. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 2,20,000. Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Bon Maharaj Engineering College is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech Application Process

Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Bon Maharaj Engineering College have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Bon Maharaj Engineering College and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Bon Maharaj Engineering College. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Bon Maharaj Engineering College B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
