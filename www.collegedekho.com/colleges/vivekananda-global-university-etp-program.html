Vivekananda Global University Job Ready Degree

Vivekananda Global University offers 5 UG and PG programmes designed to get candidates job-ready in the fields of banking, marketing, blockchain, UX, and other fields. These programmes focus on the skills and knowledge needed in the industry and prepare students accordingly. These courses are offered in BBA, BTech, and BDes programmes at the UG level. Meanwhile, at the PG level, MBA and MDes courses are offered. For these courses, Vivekananda Global University also offers upto 10% merit based scholarships based on qualifying exam scores. The fee for these programmes ranges around Rs 1,00,000 to Rs 3,00,000 per year. If you're passionate about these fields and want a course that prepares you for your future, then these job ready degrees are for you!

Vivekananda Global University Job Ready Degree 2024 - Admissions

The programmes offered by Vivekananda Global University require an entrance exam to qualify for admission. For BTech admission, candidates need to qualify for the JEE Main exam. For BDes admission, candidates need to qualify for the Design Aptitude Test (DECODE) exam. Apart from this, the university also conducts Vivekananda Global University Common Entrance Test (VGUCET) for admission to various UG and PG programmes.
