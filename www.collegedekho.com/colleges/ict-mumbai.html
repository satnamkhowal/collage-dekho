About Institute of Chemical Technology (ICT) Mumbai

Institute of Chemical Technology (ICT) Mumbai stands as India's leading deemed university focused on chemical engineering and technology education since 1933, achieving NIRF 2025 Engineering rank 41 with a score of 57.96. With campuses in Mumbai, Jalna, and Bhubaneswar, ICT processed over 1,200 admissions in 2025 via JEE Main and MHT CET, offering specialized B.Tech programs where 95% of eligible students secured placements averaging ₹10 LPA. This guide delivers exact 2025 data for your 2026 entry, covering cutoffs starting at 500 rank for Chemical Engineering, fees at ₹89,100 annually for UG, and highest package of ₹60 LPA from top recruiters like Reliance and BPCL.
