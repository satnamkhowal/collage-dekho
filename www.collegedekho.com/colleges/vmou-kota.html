About Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU)

Vardhman Mahaveer Open University, also known as VMOU, is a public open university established in the year 1987. Located in Kota, Rajasthan, the university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Distance Education Bureau (DEB), National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), and Association of Indian Universities (AIU). It is also accredited with an NAAC A grade. VMOU Kota has established 7 Regional Centres and 100+ Study Centres as an institutional device.

VMOU offers a total of 70+ courses at the diploma, UG, and PG levels. At the UG level, the university offers courses like BSc, BBA, BEd, BCA, etc. Meanwhile, at the PG level, courses like MBA, MSc, MA, MCom, MCA, etc. are offered. These courses are offered in various specialisations. For VMOU Kota admission 2026, an entrance exam is required for MBA, MCA, BEd, and PhD admission, which is conducted by VMOU on its own. While other courses have a direct merit-based admission process.

As the VMOU placement report 2025 has not been published by the University. But according to the NIRF report 2025 submitted by them, it shows that in the 2024 placement drive, a total of 1,709 students had secured jobs with median salaries reaching up to INR 9.0 LPA for 2-year UG programs and INR 5.0 LPA for 2-year PG programs.

Most Commonly Question Asked

How does VMOU distance learning system work for students?
VMOU follows a distance learning system where students study at home using books and online material. VMOU also provides weekend classes and support through study centres, helping students manage studies along with jobs or other responsibilities easily.
Is a VMOU distance degree valid for studying abroad?

VMOU Admission 2026

VMOU admission 2026 for the January session has been closed, but the July session admission will begin soon for all the UG and PG courses. Its admission process is completely online through its official website (online.vmou.ac.in). The university has two main admission sessions every year. 

  • January session: usually starts in early January and continues till March. 
  • July session: generally runs from early July to September.

Students can take VMOU admissions in two ways, depending on the course they choose. Most regular undergraduate and postgraduate courses like BA, BCom, BSc, MA, MCom, and certificate programs offer direct admission without any entrance exam. For UG courses, students must have passed 10+2 from a recognised board. For PG courses, a relevant bachelor’s degree is required.

However, some professional courses require an entrance exam score taken by the university. These include MBA, MCA, PhD, and B.Ed programs. The distance B.Ed course has stricter rules. Students must have a graduation or post-graduation with at least 50% marks. They also need teaching experience or training approved by NCTE.

VMOU also provides some fee benefits to make education more affordable. Female students from Rajasthan can get full tuition fee support under the Balika Doorasth Shiksha Yojana. Also, if students choose digital study material (PDF) instead of printed books, they get a 15% discount on course fees during admission.

VMOU Admission Process 2026

Vardhman Mahaveer Open University application process 2026 is conducted entirely online. The university does not accept physical or offline application forms at its regional centres or headquarters.

Here is the step-by-step official VMOU application process 2026 for fresh admission in the 2026 academic session:

Step 1: Visit the Official Admission Portal: Go to the official VMOU admission website: online.vmou.ac.in. Click on the "Fresh Admission" link (usually highlighted under the 'Apply Now' section for the January or July session).

Step 2: Fill out the Application Form: You will be directed to an online form. You need to fill in the following details carefully:

  • Program Details: Select your Regional Centre, Study Centre, and the exact Degree/Diploma you wish to pursue.
  • Applicant Details: Enter your name, date of birth, father's/mother's name, and contact information (ensure your mobile number and email are active, as all OTPs and updates will be sent here).
  • Study Material Preference: Choose whether you want printed hard copies of the books or digital soft copies (PDFs). Selecting the soft copy option instantly triggers a 15% discount on your tuition fee.

Step 3: Enter Academic Qualifications: Add your past academic records, starting from 10th grade up to your highest qualification (12th grade or Graduation, depending on the course you are applying for).

Step 4: Upload Required Documents: You must upload clear, scanned copies of your original documents. Do not upload photocopies unless they are strictly self-attested.

  • Recent Passport-Size Photograph
  • Scanned Signature
  • Age Proof (Usually your 10th-grade mark sheet)
  • Educational Mark Sheets (10th, 12th, and/or Graduation)
  • Valid ID Proof (Aadhar Card, Voter ID, etc.)
  • Category Certificate (If applying under SC/ST/OBC/EWS)

Rajasthan Domicile Certificate (Crucial for female candidates applying for the 100% fee reimbursement under the Balika Durstha Shiksha Yojana).

Step 5: Pay the Admission Fee: 

  • Once the documents are uploaded, the portal will calculate your total payable fee (including registration, tuition, and postal charges).
  • Pay online using a Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, or UPI.

Important: Do not refresh the page while the payment is processing.

Step 6: Submit and Print: After a successful payment, the screen will display a confirmation receipt containing a unique Control Number (or Reference Number). Download and print the payment receipt and the submitted application form for your personal records.

What Happens After You Apply?

  • Document Verification: The VMOU admission department will digitally verify your uploaded documents. This process usually takes anywhere from 15 to 30 days.
  • Generation of Scholar Number: Once your admission is officially confirmed, VMOU will send you a 6-digit Scholar Number via SMS and email. This is your permanent roll number for the duration of your degree.
  • VMOU Student One View: You will use this Scholar Number to log into the "VMOU Student One View" dashboard on the main website to track your book dispatch, download assignments, and view exam dates.

VMOU Examination System 2026

VMOU conducts its academic assessments primarily through Term End Examinations (TEE). Because the university has two admission sessions (January and July), VMOU exams are also held twice a year. Here is a breakdown of how the examination system works:

  • Schedule: Term-end exams are typically held every year in June and December.
  • Shifts: Exams are usually conducted in two shifts: Morning (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM) and Evening (2:00 PM – 5:00 PM).

Core Components of Evaluation

Depending on your specific course, your final grade is determined by a combination of the following:

  • Theory Papers: Standard written exams taken at designated test centers across Rajasthan and other cities.
  • VMOU Internal Assignments: Mandatory assignments that must be submitted (either online or to your Regional Centre) before the theory exams begin.
  • Practical Camps & Exams: For lab-heavy or technical subjects like BSc, BCA, or Geography, VMOU holds mandatory practical counseling camps at Regional Centres. You must attend these camps to be eligible to sit for the practical exam.

Passing Criteria

  • For the majority of undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs, you must score a minimum of 36% marks independently in both your theory papers and internal assignments to pass.

Admit Cards (Permission Letters)

  • Admit cards are usually released on the official website a week or two before the exams start.
  • You can download your "Permission Letter" directly from the Student One View portal or the main website using your Scholar Number.
  • You must carry a printed copy of the admit card and a valid Government ID proof to the examination hall.

VMOU Defaulter Exams (For Missed or Failed Papers)

  • If you fail a paper, are absent on exam day, or forget to submit an internal assignment, you are categorized as a "Defaulter" for that specific paper.
  • VMOU provides a safety net: by filling out an online Defaulter Form and paying a nominal fee, you can sit for that pending paper in the very next exam cycle (e.g., clearing a missed June paper in the December cycle).

Re-evaluation and Re-totalling

  • VMOU Re-evaluation: If you are unsatisfied with your theory marks, you can apply for re-evaluation within 15 days of your result being declared.
  • Re-totalling: Allowed within 30 days of your result declaration.

Note: You can only request re-evaluation or re-totalling for theory papers. It is strictly not permitted for practicals, internal assignments, project reports, or viva-voce.

VMOU Result 2025

VMOU result status for the 2025 academic year depends on your exam session. VMOU TEE results were announced step by step between October and December 2025. If you appeared in the June 2025 session, the university has already released all Term End Examination (TEE) results for undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and certificate courses. Students can now download their final VMOU marksheets.

On the other hand, the results for the December 2025 session are still not out yet. Since these VMOU exams were held in January and February 2026, the checking process is still going on. The university is likely to start releasing these VMOU Kota results from April and May 2026, usually starting with smaller courses and then moving to larger programs like BA and BSc.

How to Check Your VMOU 2025 Result?

Once your result is officially released, VMOU does not send marksheets directly to your email. You must download the provisional marksheet yourself using these steps:

  • Visit the Portal: Go directly to the official results page: online.vmou.ac.in/results.aspx
  • Find Your Course: Look for your specific program code (e.g., BA-I, MSCMT-P) and the "Date of Declaration" in the live table.
  • Click for Details: Click the "Click Here for Detailed Results" link at the top or bottom of the page.
  • Enter Credentials: Enter your Scholar Number. If you do not remember your scholar number, there is an option to search your result using your Name and Date of Birth.
  • Download: Submit the details to view your subject-wise marks, grades, and overall Pass/Fail status. Save the PDF for your records.

Important Post-Result Rules

  • Passing Marks: You must secure a minimum of 36% in both your theory exams and practicals/assignments to pass a subject at VMOU.
  • Re-evaluation: If you are unsatisfied with your score, you can apply for a copy re-evaluation online. The window strictly closes 15 days after the date your specific result was declared.
  • Defaulter Exam (Backlog): If you receive an "NC" (Not Cleared) status on your result, you will need to fill out a "Defaulter Form" and pay a nominal fee (approx. INR 200-300 per paper) to reappear for that specific exam in the next cycle.

VMOU Promotion Rules: The "Promotee" System

VMOU follows a highly flexible promotion policy standard among open and distance learning institutions. At VMOU, moving to the next academic year is commonly referred to as Promotee Admission. Here is how the promotion rules work:

Automatic Promotion (No "Pass" Required)

Unlike traditional universities, you do not need to pass all (or even any) of your current year/semester exams to be promoted to the next year. You are eligible to take admission in the 2nd or 3rd year even if your previous year's results are still pending, if you missed the exams, or if you failed certain papers.

Filing the Promotee Form

Promotion is not automatic in the sense that you don't have to do anything; you still must officially enroll for the next year.

  • To move to the next academic year, you must fill out the VMOU Promotee Form (often listed as the Second/Third Year Admission Form on the portal).
  • You must pay the full tuition fee for the upcoming year during the official admission cycles (either the January or July session).
  • Important Note: You must fill out this form before the university's deadline for that session, regardless of whether your previous exams have taken place or your results have been declared. Do not wait for your previous results to apply for the next year.

Clearing Backlogs (The Defaulter System)

Because you are promoted without passing, any papers you failed, missed, or didn't submit assignments for are carried forward as backlogs.

  • You can clear these pending papers in any subsequent Term-End Examination (TEE) cycle (held in June and December).
  • To take the exam for a backlog paper, you simply fill out a Defaulter Form through your Student One View dashboard and pay a nominal per-paper fee before the next exam cycle begins.

The Maximum Duration Constraint

While you can freely move to the next year and carry backlogs, there is a hard deadline for graduating. You must clear all your papers across all years within the Maximum Course Duration set for your specific program.

  • For example, a standard 3-year Bachelor's degree (like BA, BCom, or BSc) typically has a maximum completion window of 72 months (6 years).
  • If you do not clear all your first, second, and third-year papers within this total maximum timeframe, your enrollment will lapse, and you will not receive your degree.

VMOU FAQs

Can I study MBA at VMOU Kota?

Yes, students can study MBA at VMOU Kota in distance learning mode. The university offers a distance learning MBA programme under its School of Commerce and Management. To be eligible for this course, you must have a bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% marks. You must also take the CAT, MAT, CMAT, or VMOU MET exam for MBA admission at VMOU.

How can I apply for VMOU scholarships?

Students can apply for state-level scholarships by visiting the dedicated website at sje.rajasthan.gov.in. For national-level scholarships, apply through the National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in/. Special scholarships for female candidates can be applied through the online scholarship portal of the Government of Rajasthan at hte.rajasthan.gov.in/online-scholarship.

Which entrance exams do I need to take for VMOU admission?

For admission to VMOU, candidates need to take entrance exams like CAT, MAT, CMAT, VMOU MET (for MBA), VMOU B.Ed Entrance Test, VMOU M.Sc Entrance Test, VMOU MCA Entrance Test, and VMOU PhD Entrance Test.

Can I get scholarships at VMOU?

Yes, students can get scholarships at VMOU. These include Post-Graduate Merit Scholarship for University Rank Holders, Post-Graduate Indira Gandhi Scholarship for Single Girl Child, Post Graduate Scholarships for Professional Courses for SC/ST Candidates, and Ishan Uday for North Eastern Region (NER).

What facilities does VMOU provide?

VMOU provides a number of facilities, including a central library, science and technology labs, computer labs, guest houses, a dedicated helpdesk for learners, an auditorium, seminar halls, and more. The campus is spread across 75 acres and has a total built-up area of 53,033 square meters.

How can I apply for VMOU admission?

To apply for VMOU admission, visit vmou.ac.in, register with your details, choose a course, upload the required documents, and pay the application fee. If necessary, register for and pass entrance exams and possibly attend an interview. Successful candidates will get an admission letter, after which the admission fee must be paid to start the course.

What is the VMOU course fee?

The VMOU fee ranges from Rs 2,000 to Rs 53,760 annually for different courses. The annual fee for MBA is Rs 29,200, for M.Sc is Rs 6,700 - 12,000, for B.Ed is Rs 53,760, and for B.Sc is Rs 11,200 - 14,200.

What are the eligibility criteria for VMOU admission?

The eligibility criteria for VMOU admission to UG courses include 50% marks in Class 12th (45% in case of reserved category students). For PG courses, students must have a bachelor’s degree with 50% marks and a valid scorecard of applicable entrance exams. PhD candidates need to have 55% marks in post-graduation and valid scores in UGC NET (JRF)/ SET/ GATE/ Teacher Research Fellowship.

What are the VMOU placements 2023 statistics?

As per the VMOU placement 2023 statistics, the highest and average packages offered during VMOU placements 2023 were Rs 7.2 LPA and Rs 6 LPA, respectively. Around 80% of students were placed in companies like ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, and Dell.

What are the courses offered by VMOU Kota?

VMOU offers a total of 23 courses at undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and PhD levels in the fields of Arts, Science, Education, Management, etc. Some of the most sought-after courses include B.Ed, M.Sc, MCA, MBA, MA, BA, B.Sc, BCA, BJMC, MSW, and M.Lib.I.Sc. These courses are offered under its 22 departments, 7 regional centres, and 5 schools.

VMOU Student One View (E-Corner)

VMOU Student E-Corner, officially known as Student One View, is a centralized online portal where enrolled students can track their academic journey from admission to graduation. This online VMOU portal serves as a single dashboard for managing your distance education, allowing you to:

  • Track VMOU Admission Status: Confirm whether your application and fee payment for the current academic session have been successfully processed.
  • Download VMOU Study Materials: Access e-books, Self-Learning Materials (SLM), and direct links to official video lectures.
  • Check VMOU Assignment Status: Verify if the university has received and graded your submitted internal assignments.
  • Download VMOU Admit Card: Print your permission letters (hall tickets) for upcoming Term-End Examinations (TEE) or practical exams.
  • View VMOU One View Results: Access your mark sheets and exam results directly.
  • Manage Defaulter/Re-evaluation Forms: Apply for paper re-evaluations or register for defaulter exams if you missed a paper or did not clear it.

How to Access Your Profile?

To log in to the VMOU Student One View dashboard, follow these steps:

  • Visit the official VMOU online portal at online.vmou.ac.in (or navigate there via the "Student One View" button on the main vmou.ac.in homepage).
  • Enter your Scholar Number, remeber If you have forgotten your Scholar Number, you can click the "Search Scholar No. By Name" link on the login page to retrieve it using your full registered name.
  • Enter your Date of Birth.
  • Select your preferred language (Hindi or English).
  • Click Submit to open your personalized dashboard.

VMOU Campus and Facilities

VMOU main campus is on Rawatbhata Road in Kota, Rajasthan. It covers a large area of about 75.41 acres filled with greenery. Unlike regular universities, where many students attend daily classes, this campus mainly has administrative offices, big material distribution centers, research areas, and spaces for students who come for required practical sessions. Some highlights of the VMOU campus are listed below:

  • Central Library: The library is in a unique round-shaped building spread over 2,036 square meters and is the academic center of the campus.
    • It has a large collection of books, journals, research papers, and audio-visual materials.
    • Students can also use premium digital resources like Springer, Wiley-Blackwell, Project MUSE, and J-Gate.
    • The library uses SOUL 2.0 software and also has a special collection related to Mahatma Gandhi.
  • Educational Media Production Centre (EMPC): Since this is a distance-learning university, it has a modern multimedia studio on campus. This center uses advanced audio and video technology to record, edit, and share lectures and study materials with students across the state.
  • Science & Computer Laboratories: The campus has well-equipped labs for Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology, and IT. These labs are mainly used by distance learners who come for 10–20 days of compulsory practical training.
  • IT Infrastructure: There is a computer center with strong internet and power backup. It helps students and researchers with online work, applications, and accessing the “Student One View” portal.
  • Guest House & Accommodation: The campus provides guest houses and temporary hostel facilities. These are useful for visiting teachers, researchers, and students coming from far places for practicals or exams.
  • Sports Facilities: There are large open grounds and facilities for indoor and outdoor games like cricket, football, volleyball, and basketball. These help students stay active during events and sports activities.
  • On-Campus Services: The campus also has basic facilities like a bank, post office, cafeteria, health center, and proper parking areas.

What do you do at a Regional Centre?

These VMOU regional centres are helpful in the following ways:

  • Grievance Resolution: If you have major issues with your admission, missing study materials, or result discrepancies that cannot be solved online, the RC is your point of physical contact.
  • Offline Applications: While admissions are online, processes like applying for a physical migration certificate, submitting a physical defaulter form (in rare cases), or getting duplicate marksheets are processed through the RC.
  • Practical Camp Organisation: The RC schedules and manages the mandatory 10-to-20-day practical training camps for science, geography, and computer students in that specific zone.
  • Degree Distribution: Physical degrees and diplomas are often distributed through convocation ceremonies or mailers organised by the Regional Centres.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Does VMOU Kota have a regional centre outside Rajasthan?
While VMOU's primary academic jurisdiction and exam centers are strictly within Rajasthan, the university does maintain a special administrative centre located in New Delhi to handle specific university-level liaisons and coordinate with national educational bodies.
How many regional centres does VMOU have in Rajasthan?
VMOU operates seven regional centres strategically located across Rajasthan to assist distance learners. These are situated in Ajmer, Bikaner, Bharatpur, Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, and Udaipur, acting as administrative hubs for offline grievance resolution and camp organization.

VMOU Study Centers

Underneath each of the 7 Regional Centers is a massive network of Study Centers. These are not independent VMOU buildings; rather, they are established, reputed local government or private colleges that VMOU has partnered with. When you fill out your online admission form, you will select the Study Center closest to your home.

What do you do at a Study Center?

These VMOU study centers are helpful in the following ways:

  • Weekend Counseling (Contact Classes): VMOU does not have regular daily classes. Instead, they organize "contact camps" at your study center (usually on weekends or holidays). These are optional sessions where you can meet professors to clear doubts about your self-study materials.
  • Practical Lab Work: If you are a BSc, BCA, or MSc student, your mandatory practical lab sessions (handling chemicals, using microscopes, or coding) will take place at the labs of these local affiliated colleges.
  • Examination Centers: Your local study center (or a college very close to it) will typically serve as your designated center for your final Term-End Examinations (TEE).
  • Library Access: As a VMOU student, you are generally allowed to use the library facilities at your designated study center during specific hours.

How the Flow Works for a Student?

To better understand the role of the main campus, the regional center, and the study centers, look at the workflow bel

  • You apply online to VMOU Headquarters.
  • VMOU Headquarters sends your books to your home via post.
  • You go to your local Study Center for weekend doubt-clearing and practical labs.
  • If a major administrative issue arises that the Study Center cannot fix, you contact your Regional Center.
