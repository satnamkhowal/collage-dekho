Chandigarh University Courses FAQs

What is the fee for BE at Chandigarh University?

The BE program at Chandigarh University is offered across 20 specialisations with a duration of 3 to 4 years (8 semesters). The annual tuition fee ranges from Rs 1,99,000 to Rs 3,71,000, depending on the chosen specialisation. Candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) with at least 60% aggregate, along with a valid score in CUCET or JEE Main. The program is conducted in full-time regular mode.

Does CU offer online or short-term courses?

Yes, CU offers a comprehensive range of online degree programs through CU-IDOL (Chandigarh University Institute of Distance & Online Learning). You can pursue BA JMC, BBA, BCA, MCA, MA English, M.Com, MBA, and M.Sc Data Science entirely online.

Can I pay my CU fee online?

Yes candidates can pay CU fee online using the following steps: Go to the Chandigarh University student portal Log in using your credentials. Click on the "Fees" tab. Select the semester and fee type you want to pay. Click on "Pay Online." Choose your preferred payment method (credit card, debit card, net banking, etc.). Enter your payment details and complete the transaction.

What are the CU courses after 10th?

Chandigarh University offers several options after 10th. These include Engineering Diplomas: Mechanical, Civil, Computer Science, Electronics & Communication, Automobile, etc., Pharmacy Diploma: Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm), Hotel Management Diploma: Diploma in Hotel Management (DHM), Business Administration Diploma: Diploma in Business Administration (DBA), and IT Diplomas: Information Technology (IT), Computer Applications (CA), etc.

Is CU very expensive?

CU fees vary significantly depending on your chosen program, level of study (undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral), and scholarship eligibility. For example, BE programme can range from Rs 1,99,000 - Rs 3,71,000 annually, while MBA programmes can range from Rs 2,56,000 - Rs 3,97,000 annually. CU offers several scholarships based on merit, entrance exam scores, financial aid schemes, and specific categories like sports or cultural achievements. These scholarships can significantly reduce your tuition fees.

What is Chandigarh University fee structure?

Chandigarh University fees range from Rs 80,000 to Rs 3,97,000 for the first year (depending on the type of course). The Annual fee for BE at CU is Rs 1,99,000 - Rs 3,71,000 depending on the specialisation. Meanwhile, Chandigarh University fees for BArch is Rs 11,10,000 (total fees).

Can you name some of the BE specialisations at Chandigarh University?

The Bachelor of Engineering (BE) at Chandigarh University is offered in 20 specialisations. Artificial intelligence and data science, CSE graphics and gaming, CSE devOps, robotics and automation, civil engineering, automobile engineering, information technology etc. are some of the renowned Chandigarh University BE specialisations. The duration of BE courses is four years and they are divided into eight semesters.

Which courses are most famous at Chandigarh University?

Bachelor of Engineering (BE) and Master of Business Administration (MBA) are the two most famous courses at Chandigarh University. The university offers BE degree in 20 specialisations and MBA degree in 12 specialisations respectively. Chandigarh University fees for BE is Rs Rs 1,99,000 - Rs 3,71,000 per annum. On the other hand, the annual fees for MBA at Chandigarh University is Rs 2,56,000 - Rs 3,97,000

What are the courses in Chandigarh University after 12th?

After completion of class 12, candidates are eligible for most of the Undergraduate courses offered at the university. At the UG level, Chandigarh University offers a variety of courses in streams like engineering, management, pharmacy, arts, commerce, science, etc. Some of the most popular UG courses are BE, B.Arch, BCA, BBA, B.Sc, B.Pharm, BPT, BA, B.Com, BA LLB, and, BFA.
