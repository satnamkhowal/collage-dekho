About Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc

Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc is a 3 Years year program. To be eligible for the BSc degree program at Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology, Jaipur, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in the science stream from a recognized board. Additionally, candidates must have a strong foundation in subjects like Biology, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Entrance exams may be conducted by the institute for admission, and candidates must meet the required cutoff marks to secure a seat in the program. Prior experience or knowledge in the field of biotechnology will be an added advantage for applicants. The annual fee for BSc is INR 20,000. Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc Admission 2026

Admission to the BSc at Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc Application Process

Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BSc course at Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc Application Form.

Documents Required for Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology BSc Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
