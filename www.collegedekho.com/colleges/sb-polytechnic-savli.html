About S.B. Polytechnic College

S.B. Polytechnic College is situated in Vadodara in Gujarat state of India. Established in 2009, SBPC is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Gujarat Technological University and accredited by AICTE. S.B. Polytechnic College offers 4 courses across 1 streams namely Engineering..Popular degrees offered at S.B. Polytechnic College include Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, S.B. Polytechnic College is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at S.B. Polytechnic College is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
