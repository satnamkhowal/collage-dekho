About Parul University MD

Parul University MD is a 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. Candidates must appear for the AIAPGET and meet the eligibility criteria for the desired specialization to be eligible for admission to the MD program at Parul University. The application fee is INR 300 and the total course fee is INR 6.13 Lakh. Selection is based on the merit scored in the AIAPGET exam and the announcement of selected candidates is on July 24, 2023. Documents required for admission include certificates and mark sheets of Class 10 and 12, character/school leaving/transfer document, three passport-size photographs, proof of residence, photocopy of Aadhaar Card of the candidate and their parents, a self-attested anti-ragging affidavit signed by both the student and their parents, and an income affidavit. The annual fee for MD is INR 1,35,000 - 4,75,000. Parul University MD Fees

Parul University MD fees is 1,35,000 INR (Yearly) and goes up to 4,75,000 INR (Yearly) depending on the specialization. The average fees for the MD course is 2,31,666 INR (Yearly). Parul University MD admission 2025 will be based on GSEB 10th, CBSE 10th, NEET PG.

Parul University MD Admission 2026

Admission to the MD at Parul University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Parul University MD admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Parul University MD Application Process

Parul University MD application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MD course at Parul University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Parul University MD application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Parul University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Parul University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Parul University MD application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Parul University MD application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Parul University MD Application Form.

Documents Required for Parul University MD Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
