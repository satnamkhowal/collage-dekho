IIT Guwahati Placements Overview

IIT Guwahati’s 2025 placements were strong, with more than 1,000 students getting job offers from 260 companies. Although the overall placement rate dropped to 62% due to market slowdown, the salary packages remained high, especially for BTech, BDes, and design-related programs. The highest package reached INR 1.25 crore, while the average BTech salary stayed above INR 25 LPA. CSE, Mathematics & Computing, and Data Science branches performed the best, each recording around 90% placement with excellent salaries. Core branches like Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, and Electrical also saw steady outcomes.

Top recruiters included Google, Microsoft, Qualcomm, Texas Instruments, Bajaj, HPCL, Navi, and Piramal, offering roles in software, core engineering, electronics, fintech, and research. The Career Development Centre (CCD) supported students through resume training, coding bootcamps, mock interviews, soft-skill sessions, and an internship portal that helped secure many PPOs.

FAQs: IIT Guwahati Year-wise Placement Trends (2023–2025)

How consistent has CSE branch placement been at IIT Guwahati?
The Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) branch at IIT Guwahati maintained over 90% placement consistency from 2023 to 2025. Even when overall placement percentages dipped, CSE students consistently received high packages, demonstrating strong demand for tech graduates and the branch’s enduring industry relevance.
What has been the highest package offered at IIT Guwahati in recent years?
The highest packages at IIT Guwahati were INR 2.40 Cr in 2023, INR 2.05 Cr in 2024, and INR 1.25 Cr in 2025. While 2025 saw a dip due to market conditions, top-performing students continued to secure lucrative offers, particularly from sectors like software, IT, and core engineering.
How have IIT Guwahati B.Tech placements trended over the past three years?
IIT Guwahati placements from 2023 to 2025 show a slight decline in placement percentage, with 78% in 2023, 71% in 2024, and 62% in 2025. Despite the drop in 2025 due to market slowdown, average salary packages remained strong, reflecting consistent value for graduates across different years.

IIT Guwahati Placement Cell & Training Support

IIT Guwahati’s Career Development Centre offers structured placement support through training sessions, coding bootcamps, soft-skill guidance, and an internship portal to prepare students for successful recruitment.

  • IIT Guwahati’s Career Development Centre (CCD) manages the entire placement process, beginning every year on December 1.
  • The cell ensures smooth and fair hiring by following structured placement policies.
  • Students receive regular training through resume-building sessions held twice each semester.
  • CSE and Mathematics & Computing students get focused preparation through coding bootcamps.
  • Soft-skill training is provided through guest talks and guidance from alumni working at top companies like Google.
  • CCD also runs an internship portal that helps students track opportunities and contributed to a high number of PPOs in recent years.

IIT Guwahati Placements FAQs

How many companies visit IIT Guwahati?

IIT Guwahati recruiters have increased steadily over the past three years. For example, in 2021 the total number of recruiters who visited IIT Guwahati for campus recruitment was 160. This number increased by 20 in 2023 and was recorded at 180. During the IIT Guwahati placements 2023 the total number of companies who visited the campus for offering jobs were 246.

What is the highest package in IIT Guwahati for MTech?

As per the IIT Guwahati placements course-wise, MTech highest package in 2023 was Rs 57.70 LPA for international job offers. For domestic job offers the highest package for MTech in 2023 reached Rs 51.32 LPA. In addition,  IIT Guwahati average package for MTech in 2023 was recorded at Rs 16.51 LPA.

What is the minimum package in IIT Guwahati?

IIT Guwahati minimum package in 2023 according to the placement report was Rs 5.32 lakh per annum for UG courses. As for PG courses, the minimum package in 2023 was recorded at Rs 4.20 lakh per annum. In 2022, the IIT Guwahati minimum package for UG and PG courses was Rs 6.50 LPA and Rs 4.50 LPA, respectively.

Which branch of IIT Guwahati has the best placement record?

According to the IIT Guwahati placement record for 2023, the Engineering and design branches received the best placements. In 2023, the highest package was offered to BTech and BDes students. In addition, the best average package among all other courses was offered to BTech and BDes students in 2023, which was Rs 25.75 LPA.

What is the highest package for BTech at IIT Guwahati?

IIT Guwahati highest package for BTech students in 2023 was Rs 2.40 crore per annum for international placements. For domestic placements, the highest package received by BTech students in 2023 was Rs 1.20 crore per annum. In 2022, the highest package offered to BTech students was Rs 1.20 crore per annum (internationally) and Rs 72 LPA (domestically).

Is IIT Guwahati good for placements?

The placements at IIT Guwahati are good. This is because 78.04% of students were placed in 2023. Compared to 2023, the 2022 overall placement percentage was at 74.09%. Moreover, the highest package recorded in 2023 was at Rs 2.40 crore per annum. Whereas the highest package in 2022 was at Rs 2.05 crore per annum.

What is the placement percentage at IIT Guwahati?

The overall IIT Guwahati placement percentage in 2023 was recorded at 78.04%. The 2023 placement report shows that the placement percentage of B.Tech was 83.03%. Similarly, the placement percentage of B.Des, M.Tech, M.Des, M.Sc, MA, and MS (R) in 2023 was 74%, 72.89%, 89.66%, 65.09, 85.37, and 50% respectively.

Who are the top recruiters of IIT Guwahati?

The companies that visit the IT Guwahati for placements are domestic, international, startups, PSUs, etc. In 2023, the top IIT Guwahati recruiters were GAIL, Navi, Google, TVS R&D, Qualcomm, etc.
