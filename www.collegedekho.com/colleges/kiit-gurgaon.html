About KIIT College of Engineering Gurgaon

KIIT College of Engineering, Gurgaon, established in 1969, is a private AICTE-approved institution affiliated with MDU Rohtak. The campus spans 15 acres and is located 11 km from Rajiv Chowk on the NH-8 Jaipur Highway.

The college offers B.Tech, BBA, and BCA undergraduate programmes along with M.Tech and MBA at the postgraduate level. B.Tech is available in Computer Science Engineering, Information Technology, and Electronics and Communication Engineering, with annual fees ranging from ₹75,000 to ₹75,500. BBA and BCA annual fees are ₹59,500 and ₹61,500 respectively.

B.Tech admission is through HSTES 2026 counselling based on JEE Main rank. M.Tech requires a valid GATE score, MBA requires CAT, MAT, or XAT scores, while BBA and BCA admissions are merit-based with no entrance exam.

The campus includes computer labs, library with over 20,000 books, separate hostels for boys and girls with 150-seat capacity each, auditorium, sports facilities, canteen, gym, and medical facilities. Free Wi-Fi is available across the campus.

The Training and Placement Cell maintains active industry connections. The college holds international collaborations with NICT Japan, LDC Pennsylvania USA, LFMG Brazil, and Nokia China.

KIIT Gurgaon Admission 2026

KIIT College of Engineering Gurgaon Admission 2026 is open for B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, BBA, and BCA. B.Tech admission is through HSTES 2026 counselling based on JEE Main rank. HSTES registration runs till June 19, 2026 at techadmissionshry.gov.in. M.Tech requires a GATE score; MBA requires CAT/MAT/XAT. The college is AICTE-approved and affiliated with MDU Rohtak.

KIIT Gurgaon Admission Process 2026

Getting admission to KIIT Gurgaon depends on which course you are applying for. B.Tech admission is entirely counselling-based through HSTES 2026 — there is no separate KIIT entrance exam for B.Tech. For MBA, the college holds a personal interview (PI) after shortlisting based on CAT/MAT/XAT scores. M.Tech admissions follow GATE score-based shortlisting and PI.

KIIT Gurgaon B.Tech Admission Process 2026

B.Tech admission at KIIT COE Gurgaon is done through the Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) counselling, based on JEE Main 2026 All India Rank. Here is the step-by-step process:

  • Appear in JEE Main 2026 (Session 1 or Session 2) and get your All India Rank (AIR).
  • Register on the HSTES portal (techadmissionshry.gov.in) between May 6 and June 19, 2026. Pay the HSTES counselling registration fee: INR 1,000 for General and INR 700 for Reserved categories. Haryana residents must link their PPP ID during registration.
  • Complete online verification of your marks and documents on the HSTES portal within the specified window.
  • Fill your institute and branch choices on the HSTES portal during the choice-filling window (from June 10, 2026). Select KIIT College of Engineering as your preferred institution and rank your branch preferences.
  • Lock your choices before the deadline. You can modify choices until the locking date.
  • HSTES releases the Round 1 seat allotment result on June 19, 2026. Check your allotment using your counselling number on the portal.
  • If allotted a seat at KIIT COE, report to the college physically between June 22 and June 25, 2026. Carry all original documents listed in the documents section below.
  • Pay the semester fee at the institute to confirm your seat.
  • If not allotted in Round 1, participate in Round 2 counselling (July 2026). The HSTES portal will display vacant seats after Round 1 for Round 2 choice filling.
  • KIIT also accepts direct institute-level applications for B.Tech in cases of management quota or vacant seats. Apply through www.kiit.in in that case.

KIIT Gurgaon M.Tech Admission Process 2026

  • M.Tech admission at KIIT COE Gurgaon is based on GATE 2026 score. The GATE 2026 examination has already been conducted. Here is the process for M.Tech:
  • Get your GATE 2026 scorecard. KIIT accepts GATE scores in CSE and ECE for the M.Tech programme.
  • Apply directly through the college's official website (www.kiit.in) or visit the admissions office at the Gurugram campus.
  • Submit your GATE scorecard, graduation marksheets, and other required documents for shortlisting.
  • Shortlisted candidates are called for a Personal Interview (PI) at the KIIT campus.
  • Final selection is based on GATE score and PI performance. A merit list is prepared and admission offers are sent.
  • Pay the admission fee to confirm your seat. 

KIIT Gurgaon MBA Admission Process 2026

MBA admission at KIIT COE Gurgaon is based on valid scores in CAT, MAT, or XAT. The institute offers a 2-year full-time MBA with 60 seats. The process is as follows:

  • Appear in CAT 2025 / MAT / XAT 2026 and get your valid scorecard.
  • Apply through www.kiit.in between June 12 and July 12, 2026. Fill the online application form with accurate details.
  • Upload your graduation marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, and other required documents.
  • KIIT COE shortlists candidates based on entrance exam scores.
  • Attend the Personal Interview (PI) on the KIIT campus in Gurugram.
  • Final merit list is based on entrance exam score + PI performance. Admission offer letter is sent to selected candidates.
  • Pay the MBA fee to confirm your seat. 50% of total seats are filled based on qualifying exam merit; the remaining 50% are filled based on entrance exam rank.

KIIT Gurgaon BBA / BCA Admission Process 2026

BBA and BCA admissions at KIIT COE are merit-based on Class 12 marks. No entrance exam is required. Apply through www.kiit.in between June 5 and July 10, 2026, submit your Class 12 marksheets, and attend a merit-based counselling session at the institute.

KIIT Gurgaon Placements

The Training and Placement Cell at KIIT keeps close liaisons with Industries, businesses and recruiting agencies. It also facilitates the training of students in different organizations. KIIT Group of Colleges is committed to providing all possible assistance to its graduates and post-graduates in their effort to find employment. This commitment is demonstrated by the existence of a full-time Training and Placement officer (TPO) at KIIT. The Training and Placement service operates year-round to facilitate contact between companies and graduates. KIIT Placement has already developed a strong database with respect to placement associates. A well-knit and strong interface with the industry enables the institute to continuously send students for summer training and internship programmes to reputed companies.

Staff in the Placement Cell is available to respond to students’ questions and concerns of all kinds. The aim is to ensure that students have the information and skills necessary for an effective job search. Training and Placement Cell is actively fostering industry meets and alliances. Guest lectures, workshops, and Round Table conferences are organized at KIIT Campus. Eminent professors, scientists and industry experts drawn from India and abroad take part in these activities from time to time. KIIT College of Engineering has the distinction of housing the Computer Society of India (CSI) -Gurgaon Chapter Secretariat located within its Campus. Various professional activities have also been organized in collaboration with the Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers (IETE).
