About L M Thapar School of Management MBA

L M Thapar School of Management MBA is a 2 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue an M.B.A. degree at L M Thapar School of Management Mohali, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum aggregate score of 50%. Additionally, they must have a valid CAT/MAT/CMAT/GMAT score. Candidates appearing for their final year exams are also eligible to apply. The selection process includes a group discussion and personal interview. Aspiring students should possess strong analytical and problem-solving skills, along with excellent communication and leadership abilities. The annual fee for MBA is INR 1,89,480 - 8,99,000. L M Thapar School of Management MBA Admission 2026

Admission to the MBA at L M Thapar School of Management is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the L M Thapar School of Management MBA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

L M Thapar School of Management MBA Application Process

L M Thapar School of Management MBA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MBA course at L M Thapar School of Management have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill L M Thapar School of Management MBA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of L M Thapar School of Management and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of L M Thapar School of Management. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the L M Thapar School of Management MBA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the L M Thapar School of Management MBA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of L M Thapar School of Management MBA Application Form.

Documents Required for L M Thapar School of Management MBA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
