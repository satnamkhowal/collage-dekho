About Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur is a private state university established in 2017 under the Sanjay Ghodawat University Act passed by the Government of Maharashtra. The university is located at Atigre on the Kolhapur–Sangli Highway in Maharashtra. The university campus spans 165 acres. The University is NAAC accredited with an A Grade and has NBA accreditation for eligible engineering programmes. The university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and approved by AICTE, PCI, BCI, and other relevant regulatory bodies.

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur has 8 schools offering programmes in Engineering and Technology, Computer Applications, Commerce and Management, Design, Science, Life Sciences, Pharmacy, and Legal Studies. SGU Kolhapur offers 30+ programmes across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels. Popular Sanjay Ghodawat University courses include B.Tech, MBA, B.Design, B.Pharm, MCA, BBA, and BCA. The university offers seven B.Tech specialisations and five B.Design specialisations.

Admissions at Sanjay Ghodawat University are based on national-level and state-level entrance examinations such as JEE Main, MHT-CET, CAT, CMAT, MAH MBA CET, and other accepted entrance tests. Candidates can also apply through the SGU Entrance Test (SGUET) for selected programmes.

According to the latest available placement report, the highest package offered was INR 36 LPA, while the average package stood at INR 3.20 LPA. Top recruiters include TCS, Infosys, Amazon, Mphasis, L&T, and HDFC Bank.

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur Admission 2026

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur, admissions are currently open for undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across Engineering and Technology, Computer Applications, Commerce and Management, Science, Life Sciences, Pharmacy, Design and more. SGU Kolhapur admissions are conducted through national and state-level entrance exams as well as the university's own entrance test, SGU Entrance Test (SGUET).

Sanjay Ghodawat University B.Tech admissions accept JEE Main and MHT-CET scores through the Maharashtra DTE CAP process. MBA admissions consider CAT, CMAT, and MAH MBA CET scores. B.Design admissions are based on UCEED, NID DAT, and NIFT scores, while B.Pharm admissions accept NEET scores. Candidates who do not have valid national or state-level entrance exam scores can apply through SGUET or PERA-CET. Applications can be submitted online through the university admission portal or by contacting the SGU admission office.

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur Admission Process 2026

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur admission process is based on merit and entrance exam scores, depending on the programme. The university accepts scores from national-level, state-level, and approved entrance examinations such as JEE Main, MHT-CET, CAT, CMAT, NEET, UCEED, NID DAT, NIFT, PERA CET, and SGU Entrance Test (SGUET). Candidates can apply through the university admission portal and complete the admission process by following the steps below:

  • Online Registration: Visit the official SGU website and fill out the admission enquiry or application form with personal and academic details. 
  • Entrance Exam Verification: Submit your valid entrance exam scorecard (MHT-CET, JEE Main, PERA CET, CAT, CMAT, etc.), depending on the chosen program.
  • Counselling & Merit List: Shortlisted candidates are invited for a counselling process or a personal interview (where applicable). SGU releases merit lists based on entrance scores and qualifying exam marks. 
  • Document Verification & Fee Payment: Once a seat is allocated, candidates must verify their original documents and pay the requisite institutional fees to secure their admission. 
  • Seat Allocation Policy: SGU reserves 70% of its total intake seats for candidates holding a Maharashtra Domicile, while the remaining 30% of seats are open to All India students.

FAQs about Sanjay Ghodawat University

What are the eligibility criteria for admission to Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur?

The eligibility criteria for admission to Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur vary depending on the course and the level of study. For undergraduate courses, students must have passed the class 12 board exam with a minimum percentage of marks along with a valid JEE Main, PERA CET, MHT CET or SGU CET score. For postgraduate courses, students must have passed the relevant undergraduate degree with a minimum percentage of marks along with valid GATE, MAH MBA/MMS CET, CAT, CMAT, GMAT, ATMA, MAT marks varying from course to course.

What accreditations and affiliations does Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur have?

After obtaining the necessary permits from the various Indian government educational agencies, Sanjay Ghodawat University was officially recognised in 2017 as opposed to its original founding year of 2009. Sanjay Ghodawat University was dual certified in June 2019 by ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 21001:2018 and has all the approvals from organisations including AICTE, UGC, NAAC, COA, and PCI.

Where is Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur located?

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur is located at Wadi, Kolhapur, Maharashtra, India. The exact address is near Kolhapur - Sangli Highway, A/p Atigre - 416 118 Tal. - Hatkanangale Dist. - Kolhapur Maharashtra, India. You can contact SGU at +91-9011039800, +91-90110 22567, +91-8830868610 or email them at rajesh.vashikar@sanjayghodawatuniversity.ac.in.

How is the Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur campus?

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur has a well-developed campus spread over 165 acres. It has all the necessary facilities for students, including digital classrooms, hostels, laboratories, a bank/ATM, staff quarters, a supermarket, a gymnasium, and a cricket ground. The hostels are equipped with modern amenities, including Wi-Fi access, stationery shops, copier facilities, a mini supermarket, and a self-laundry facility. The hostel blocks are under surveillance of CCTV to ensure the safety of the students. The university also has state-of-art sporting facilities, such as football and hockey pitches, tennis courts, a basketball court, and a table tennis hall.

What is the application process for admission to Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur?

To apply for admission to Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur, you must first check your eligibility for the desired course. Once you are eligible, you can fill out an enquiry form on the university website. Someone from the admission committee will then contact you to provide further instructions. You will need to submit all of the required documents, including your previous academic transcripts, entrance exam scores, and a copy of your passport-size photograph. You may also need to appear for the university entrance exam, depending on the course you are applying for. The application fee for admission to Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur is Rs 1,000.

Does Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur offer any scholarships?

The Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur cut off varies depending on the course and the level of study. Application period opens, and in-person interviews start based on the Sanjay Ghodawat University merit list made public on the official website.

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur Campus

Sanjay Ghodawat University Kolhapur is spread across a 165-acre residential campus located at Atigre on the Kolhapur–Sangli Highway. The campus includes academic buildings, hostels, sports facilities, dining areas, and other student support services within a single campus area.

Sanjay Ghodawat University is located approximately 21.5 km from Kolhapur Airport and 4.5 km from Hatkanangale Railway Station, providing convenient connectivity for students travelling from different parts of Maharashtra and India.

The university's residential campus offers a dedicated learning environment with academic, accommodation, sports, and recreational facilities available within the campus premises.

Sanjay Ghodawat University Scholarships

Scholarships in Sanjay Ghodawat University is offered to students based on various criteria. The scholarships offered include merit, minority, EWS and loyalty scholarships. The details regarding Sanjay Ghodawat University scholarship is given here.

  • SGU offers merit-based scholarships, and around 15% of the fee is waived off.
  • Special waiver for bachelor of architecture course depending on previous grades and entrance test results. 100% of the fee is waived off for a certain percentage of rank holders.
  • Shri. Sanjay Ghodawat Loyalty Scholarship is another scholarship option offered by the University, which provides a 10% fee waiver. Those who are merit list can receive the 15% from the merit-based scheme, adding to a total of 25% waiver.
