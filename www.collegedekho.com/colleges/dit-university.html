About DIT University 2026

Dehradun Institute of Technology, or DIT University, is a State Private University established in 1998. The university's 21-acre campus is located at Mussoorie Diversion Road, Makkawala, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248009. DIT University holds a NAAC A grade accreditation and is approved by AICTE, UGC, PCI, COA, INC, AIU, and IAU. It is QS I-GAUGE Diamond-rated and ISO certified. The university's Pharmacy department is ranked 83rd by NIRF 2025.

DIT University in Dehradun offers 70+ programmes at UG, PG and doctoral levels, including BTech, BSc, BArch, MPharm, MBA, MTech, & PhD, etc. The flagship programme is BTech CSE with 7 specialisations. DIT Dehradun admissions are offered on a merit and entrance basis. For BTech JEE Main scores are needed; B.Arch uses NATA; MBA uses CAT, XAT, MAT, or CMAT; and M.Tech uses GATE score or rank. The university also conducts DUET (DIT University Entrance Test) for students without national exam scores, and reserves 10% of BTech seats for Class 12 board toppers (≥95%) without any entrance exam requirement.

DIT University 2025 placement report is pending. In 2024, the highest package was INR 44 LPA (CSE), average INR 5.76 LPA, median INR 5.16 LPA, with 69.28% placements and 400+ recruiters like Amazon and IBM.

DIT University Admission 2026

DIT University admission 2026 is open at applications.dituniversity.edu.in. Applications are accepted year-round with no fixed closing date for most UG programmes. DIT University courses admission is given on a merit basis and on an entrance basis: national/state entrance exam or DUET (DIT's own test), and direct merit-based admission. For B.Tech, Class 12 board toppers with ≥95% PCM qualify for direct admission without any exam (10% seats reserved).

For admission in BTech courses, JEE Main scores are taken into consideration. JEE Main Paper 2 scores are accepted for DIT University admission in BArch. At the postgraduate level, the university accepts GATE and CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT/UKSEE for admission in MTech and MBA, respectively. 

DIT University Entrance Test (DUET) is also conducted by the university for admission in MPharm, MTech, and PhD courses.

DIT University Application Process 2026

Candidates who are interested in admission to DIT University courses can fill out the application form online or offline. To make the application smooth, the stepwise procedure to fill DIT University Dehradun application form in online mode. 

  • Register at applications.dituniversity.edu.in, create an account with name, email, and mobile (OTP verification).
  • Fill the DITU application form, select the programme, specialisation, and up to 3 preferences. Enter academic details and entrance exam score (leave blank if exam not yet taken).
  • Pay INR 1,250 application fee (non-refundable, all categories, online only).
  • Appear in the entrance exam (JEE Main / NATA / GATE / CAT / UKSEE) or appear for DUET on campus. B.Des, BCA, B.Sc, B.A, M.Sc, M.A: no exam required.
  • Check merit list / counselling notification at dituniversity.edu.in/admissions/counseling-notifications. For MBA: attend compulsory GD and PI. For M.Tech/PhD: attend PI on campus.
  • Report to campus (Gate No. 2) on the assigned date with original documents and DD of INR 15,000 in favour of 'DIT University, Dehradun', payable at Dehradun.
  • Pay the semester fee online at diterp.dituniversity.edu.in. and One-time fees.

DIT University FAQs

What is the NIRF ranking of DIT University?

DIT University was ranked 83rd in the Pharmacy category in NIRF 2025. The university has also received recognition in global rankings and quality certifications for academics, research, innovation, and student learning experience.

Are there scholarships for JEE Main qualified candidates at DIT University?

Yes, DIT University offers merit scholarships for students with good JEE Main ranks. Depending on rank and Class 12 marks, eligible students can receive tuition fee waivers ranging from 20% to 100% under different scholarship categories.

How many schools are there in DIT University?

DIT University Dehradun has a total of 9 schools. School of Computing, School of Engineering and Technology, School of Pharmaceutical and Populations Health Informatics, School of Physical Sciences, School of Liberal Arts and Management, School of Architecture, Planning and Design, College of Nursing and DIT College of Healthcare Professions are DIT University schools and departments that offers UG, PG and doctoral courses.

What is the campus size of DIT University?

DIT University Dehradun campus is spread over an area of 21 acres. The university campus has advanced facilities for students. There is a well stocked library, boys and girls hostels, cafeteria, auditoriums, playground of multiple sports etc. For practical knowledge of students, DIT University has department based laboratories, computer labs.

Is DIT University a private university?

Yes, DIT University Dehradun is a private university established in 1998 as Dehradun Institute of Technology. Later in 2013, it was conferred with university status by UGC. The university is recognised by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and Council of Architecture (COA). More than 100 courses are offered by DIT University.

Does DIT University have foreign collaborations?

Yes, DIT University Dehradun has signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with numerous overseas universities to provide foreign exposure and maximum academic opportunities to students. Some of the overseas partners of DIT University are National Dong Hwa University (Taiwan), Tribhuvan University (Nepal) University of Nebraska (USA), The Milwaukee School of Engineering (Wisconsin, USA) etc. The university has a student exchange programme under which students are sent to partner universities for a semester.

What is NAAC accreditation of DIT University?

With a CGPA of 31.9 out of 4 point scale, DIT University has been given ‘A’ grade by National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) in 2023. The NAAC accreditation will remain valid for next five years i.e. till July, 2028. The university vision is to instil leadership quality in each student and help them in becoming a good and wise citizen.

Is DIT University Dehradun recognised by UGC?

Yes, in the year 2013, Dehradun Institute of Technology was granted university status by the University Grants Commission (UCG) and it became DIT University. The university is NAAC-accredited and recognised by AICTE. The university is also approved by PCI, COA, INC, AIU, and several other regulatory bodies.

How To Reach DIT University Dehradun?

DIT University is situated on the Mussoorie Diversion Road in the Makkawala area of Dehradun, Uttarakhand. Its location at the foothills of the Himalayas makes it easily accessible via air, rail, and road. Below is the detailed guide on how to reach the campus:

1. By Air (Jolly Grant Airport)

The Jolly Grant Airport (DED) is the nearest airport, located approximately 32–35 km from the university.

  • Connectivity: Regular flights operate from major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Hyderabad.
  • Onward Journey: From the airport, you can hire a pre-paid taxi directly to DIT University. The drive typically takes 60 to 90 minutes, depending on traffic in the city center.

2. By Rail (Dehradun Railway Station)

The Dehradun Railway Station (DDN) is located approximately 10–12 km from the campus.

  • Major Trains: Dehradun is well-connected by high-speed trains such as the Vande Bharat Express, Shatabdi Express, and Jan Shatabdi from New Delhi, as well as trains from Kolkata, Mumbai, and Lucknow.

Onward Journey:

  • Taxis/Private Cabs: Available 24/7 outside the station.
  • Auto-rickshaws: A common mode for reaching the Mussoorie Diversion area.
  • City Buses: Look for buses heading towards Rajpur Road or Mussoorie Diversion.

3. By Road (ISBT Dehradun)

Dehradun is well-linked to North India via a robust network of National Highways (NH 72 and NH 58).

  • From Delhi: The distance is roughly 250 km. You can take a luxury (Volvo) or state-run bus from ISBT Kashmiri Gate, which takes about 5–6 hours.
  • From ISBT Dehradun: The Inter-State Bus Terminus (ISBT) is about 15–18 km from DIT University.
  • Local Connectivity: You can board a city bus or hire a taxi/auto-rickshaw from ISBT to reach the campus.

4. Local Transport Within Dehradun

Once you are within the city, reaching the university is straightforward:

  • Vikrams (Blue 7-Seaters): These are the most common public transport in Dehradun. You can take a Vikram (Route No. 1 or 5) towards Rajpur Road and then change for the Mussoorie Diversion.
  • E-Rickshaws: Widely available for short distances near the campus area.
  • University Transport: For enrolled students and staff, DIT University operates its own fleet of buses covering major parts of the city.

Pro Tip: If you are visiting for admissions or official work, use Gate No. 2 for entry. Most navigation apps (Google Maps) will lead you directly to the main reception via the Mussoorie Diversion Road.

DIT University Scholarships 2026

DIT University offers 12 types of scholarships, from merit-based to special category. All scholarships reduce your tuition fee only (not hostel or mess). You can combine certain scholarship categories for a bigger discount. The more you score in Class 12 or JEE, the higher your savings. The total of all combined scholarships cannot exceed full tuition.

A: Merit & Score-Based — For new students

B: Uttarakhand / 13 Himalayan States Scholarship

Special Category — Stackable on top of A, B, or L

C: Girl Students Scholarship

D: Defence Personnel Children

E: Sports Scholarship

F: Sibling Scholarship

G: DIT Alumni Scholarship

H/I/J: Need-Based Scholarships (Single mother's child / Differently abled / Orphan student)

K: DIT Employees' Children

L:  CUET Score-Based Scholarship

M: Lateral Entry Scholarship

Best case example: A girl student from Uttarakhand scores 92% in Class 12 PCM and joins B.Tech CSE. She gets: 50% merit (A) + 25% UK state (B) + 10% girl scholarship (C) = 85% off her tuition fee in Year 1. As long as she stays in the top 10% of her batch, she keeps getting a merit scholarship in Years 2–4 as well.

Key Rules for the Maximum Scholarship:

Stacking rules: You can only pick one from A, B, or L as your main scholarship — whichever is highest. Then you can add C (girls), D (defence), E (sports), F (sibling), G (alumni), H/I/J (need-based) on top of that. The total can never cross 100% of your tuition fee. If you are from Uttarakhand and also scored high in boards, pick the higher of the two — you still keep the UK state discount for the rest of your programme.
