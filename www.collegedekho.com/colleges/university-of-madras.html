About University of Madras

The University of Madras, also known as Madras University, is a public state university established in 1857, one of India’s oldest universities. Located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, it operates across multiple campuses, including Chepauk, Guindy, and Marina, covering approximately 70 acres. The university is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), accredited by NAAC with an A++ grade (CGPA 3.59), and affiliated with the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). As per NIRF Rankings 2025, University of Madras ranks 38 under the University category and 541-550 globally by QS World University Rankings 2025. It affiliates over 200 colleges and institutions in Tamil Nadu.

Madras University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and doctoral programs in streams such as Arts, Science, Commerce, Management, Law, and Social Sciences. Popular courses include BSc, BA, BCom, MSc, MA, MBA, and MCA. Admissions are primarily merit-based for UG courses and entrance-based for PG and professional courses, accepting exams like TANCET for MBA/MCA, university-conducted entrance tests for MSc/MA, and Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test for other programs.

For placements, Madras University has a placement rate of approximately 65% (based on NIRF 2024 data). In 2023, the highest package offered was INR 12 LPA, with a median package of INR 4.2 LPA for UG 3-year programs and INR 6.5 LPA for PG 2-year programs.

Madras University Admission 2025

Madras University admissions are merit-based for most UG programs and entrance-based for PG and professional programs. Candidates must meet the eligibility criteria and apply through the university’s online portal. The admission process includes:

  • BSc/BA/BCom: Admissions are based on merit in the qualifying examination (10+2).
  • MBA/MCA: Candidates must appear for TANCET or CAT (for MBA) and participate in counseling.
  • MSc/MA/MCom/LLB: Admissions are based on university-conducted entrance test scores.
  • PhD: Candidates must clear the university’s PhD Entrance Test and interview.

Madras University FAQs

Does Madras University offer hostel facilities?

Yes, Madras University offers hostel facilities to both men and women PG students. The hostel is located on the Chepauk campus of the University of Madras. Essential facilities like beds, study areas, and storage space, in addition to facilities such as a recreation room, mess services, and laundry are offered at the Madras University hostels.

Does University of Madras offer scholarships to students?

Yes, numerous scholarships are offered to students at Madras University all of which come under Endowment Scholarship. Candidates can check the official website of Madras University to find the application form for the various Endowment Scholarships available. Candidates will also find the eligibility criteria and terms & conditions for each scholarship on the official website.

What is the placement rate at Madras University?

The placement rate at Madras University is decent and a sizable chunk of the students who participate in the placement drive get lucrative job opportunities. For the placement drive conducted by the University of Madras in 2023, out of a total 1,293 students, 468 were placed.

Does Madras University release its own cutoff?

No, Madras University does not release its own cutoff since admission to a number of courses offered by the university is done on the basis of merit. However, for courses like MBA & MCA, candidates are required to appear for TANCET for which cutoffs are released by Directorate of Technical Education, Chennai.

What is the NIRF 2023 ranking for Madras University?

The NIRF 2023 ranking for Madras University is 50th in the University category. The NIRF ranking for Madras University has fallen since it was ranked 39th in the University category last year.

Is the admission process for all courses the same at Madras University?

No, the admission process for all courses is not the same at Madras University. Admission to a number of courses offered by the University of Madras is done on the basis of merit. However, admission to courses like MBA, MCA, etc. is done through entrance exams like TANCET. Candidates who wish to seek MBA/MCA admission at Madras University must do so through TANCET counselling.

Is University Of Madras, Chennai recognized by the UGC?

Yes, University Of Madras, Chennai is recognized by the UGC.

Which university is University Of Madras , Chennai affiliated to ?

University Of Madras , Chennai is affiliated to the University Of Madras.
