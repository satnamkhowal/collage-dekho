WBJEE B.Tech CSE Cutoff 2026: Check Closing Ranks Here

WBJEE B.Tech CSE Cutoff 2026 will be released during the counselling process after each round of seat allotment. You should score the cutoff marks to get admission to participating colleges. Computer Science Engineering is one of the most opted engineering specialisations, so the cutoff is quite competitive at the participating colleges. The expected WBJEE B.Tech CSE cutoff 2026 for Jadavpur University (OBC - B category) is 150-200; for UNIVERSITY OF KALYANI, KALYANI (for OBC - B category) is 1600-2100; for Netaji Subhas Engineering College, Garia, Kolkata (EWS category) is 10761-20761 and so on. The cutoff is more competitive for reserved category students at top colleges like Jadavpur University.

A third-year B.Tech ECE student of IEM Kolkata, Mainak Mondal has developed a product named 'Anti Raping Gloves' that primarily focuses on acting as a shield as far as women's safety is concerned. 

These gloves can produce high voltage electric shock, send messages to another mobile number (guardian or police station) that is pre-stored and can also determine the exact location of the person. The device contains a rechargeable battery, is lightweight and has a non-conductive material to protect it from direct contact with the skin. It is made with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi-and-Bluetooth combo dual-core 32-bit LX6 ultra-low-power (ULP) co-processor and has an electronic circuit in between the knuckle and the wrist. 

The patent of this device was filed in 2018. It is also one of the selected projects of the victorious group of MAKAUT (Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology, West Bengal) Conclave 2019.

When will the MAKAUT CET counselling dates be published?

A
Aarushi Jain •  Jul-08-2025

Dear Student, 

The MAKAUT CET 2025 exam was conducted on June 8, 2025, and the result was announced on June 28, 2025. You can expect the MAKAUT CET 2025 counselling schedule and choice filling to commence in July or August 2025. Seat allotment is then likely to be done in August 2025. The West Bengal University of Technology, also referred to as Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), typically announces the counselling schedule a few weeks after announcing the CET results. Usually, the counselling schedule is released on the official MAKAUT website (makautwb.ac.in) and the special counselling portal.

After publishing the notice, it will contain step-by-step instructions for registration, choice filling, seat allotment, and document verification. It's always best to check the official website frequently or subscribe to their updates so you don't miss any important announcements.
