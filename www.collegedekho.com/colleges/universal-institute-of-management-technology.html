About Universal Institute Of Management And Technology

Universal Institute Of Management And Technology is situated in Mohali in Punjab state of India. Established in 2009, UIMT is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Punjab Technical University and accredited by AICTE. Universal Institute Of Management And Technology offers 54 courses across 11 streams namely Management, Engineering, IT, Science, Commerce and Banking..Popular degrees offered at Universal Institute Of Management And Technology include BSc, B.Tech, PGD, M.Tech, MBA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Universal Institute Of Management And Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Universal Institute Of Management And Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Universal Institute Of Management And Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
