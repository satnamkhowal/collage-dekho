About Amity University, Lucknow

Amity University Lucknow is a private university established in 2004. Located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, the Amity University Lucknow campus spans over 1200 acres. The university is a constituent unit of Amity University, Uttar Pradesh, and is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Bar Council of India (BCI), Council of Architecture (COA), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), and National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE). It is accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC. Amity University Lucknow is ranked 24th by India Today 2024 in the Architecture category and 11th by The Week 2025 in the Design category. The Amity Institute of Pharmacy is ranked 11th by NIRF 2023.

Amity University Lucknow offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs in streams such as Engineering, Management, Law, Pharmacy, Biotechnology, Commerce, Arts, Design, and Computer Applications. BTech, MBA, BBA, BCA, and BA LLB are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Amity University Lucknow are based on merit in the qualifying examination and entrance examinations. The university accepts JEE Main and AMCAT for B.Tech., CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT for MBA, NATA for B.Arch., and CLAT for law programs.

For placements, Amity University Lucknow’s placement rate is approximately 90%. During the placement drive of 2023, the highest salary package offered was INR 45 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 8 LPA.

Amity University Lucknow Admission 2025

Amity University Lucknow admissions are based on merit in the qualifying examination and national/university-based entrance examinations. Students seeking admission at Amity University Lucknow must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the university and appear for the entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for Amity University Lucknow admissions:

  • B.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech must appear for the JEE Main or AMCAT and participate in the Amity University Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT and participate in the Amity University Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • B.Arch: Candidates seeking admission to B.Arch must appear for the NATA and participate in the Amity University Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • B.A LLB: Candidates seeking admission to B.A LLB must appear for the CLAT and participate in the Amity University Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • BBA: Admissions to BBA are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • BCA: Admissions to BCA are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • B.Com: Admissions to B.Com are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
  • LL.B: Admissions to LL.B are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination.
