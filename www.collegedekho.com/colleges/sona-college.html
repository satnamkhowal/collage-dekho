About Sona College of Technology

Sona College of Technology, also known as SCT, is a private college established in 1997. Located in Salem, Tamil Nadu, Sona College of Technology campus spans over 33.2 acres. The college is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai and recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), University Grants Commission (UGC), and National Board of Accreditation (NBA). Sona College of Technology is ranked 151-200 by NIRF under Engineering in 2024.

Sona College of Technology offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in the streams of Engineering, Management, and Information Technology. B.E. Computer Science and Engineering, B.Tech Information Technology, M.E. Computer Science and Engineering, and MBA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Sona College of Technology are based on entrance examinations. The college accepts TNEA for B.E./B.Tech, TANCET for MBA, and TANCET for M.E./M.Tech.

For placements, Sona College of Technology placement rate is 91% for the academic year 2023-2024. During the placement drive of 2023, the highest salary package offered was INR 22 LPA and the average package stood at INR 5 LPA.

Sona College of Technology Admission 2025

Sona College of Technology admissions are based on state-level entrance examinations. Students seeking admission at Sona College of Technology must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the college and appear for the entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for Sona College of Technology admissions:

B.E./B.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to B.E./B.Tech must appear for the TNEA and participate in the Tamil Nadu Engineering Admission (TNEA) counselling for the seat allotment process.

MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the TANCET and participate in the TANCA counselling for the seat allotment process.

M.E./M.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to M.E./M.Tech must appear for the TANCET and participate in the TANCA counselling for the seat allotment process.
