Jagannath University NCR Haryana Job Ready Degree

Apart from the conventional courses, if employability is what concerns you the most, and you aspire to enroll in a job-oriented course, then we bring to you 4 market-integrated programmes at the UG level and 2 at the PG level by Jagannath University NCR Haryana. These courses help students learn the skills required by the industry in the changing landscape of technology and innovation. So if your budget allows you to shell out Rs 1,40,000 to Rs 1,60,000 a year for a UG programme and Rs 1,50,000 to Rs 2,20,000 annually for a postgraduate degree, then BBA in Digital Marketing(IXP), B.Tech in AIML (IXP), BCA in Full Stack Development (IXP) at the UG level and MBA in Fintech (IXP) and MCA in Full Stack Development (IXP) at PG level are the courses for you.

Jagannath University NCR Haryana Job Ready “Assured” Courses 2025 - Admissions 

For these job-ready courses by Jagannath University NCR Haryana, there is no entrance examination. One can directly fill the application form, pay the fee and enroll in the respective course if the eligibility criteria is met.
