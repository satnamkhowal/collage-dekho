About Pandit Deendayal Energy University - PDPU

Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), established in 2007, is a private university located in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. Spanning 100 acres, PDPU is affiliated with itself as a private university and recognized by UGC, AICTE, and SIRO (Scientific & Industrial Research Organization). It is accredited by NAAC with an A++ grade and holds NBA accreditation for its B.Tech program. PDPU is ranked 101-150 in the Engineering category by NIRF 2024, 89 in Management, and 151-200 in the University category. The university offers 77 courses across 9 streams, including Engineering, Management, Liberal Studies, and Sciences. Popular courses include B.Tech, MBA, B.Sc (Hons), and M.Tech. Admissions are merit-based or entrance-based, accepting exams like JEE Main, GUJCET, GATE, CAT, XAT, NMAT, and CMAT.

In 2025, PDEU achieved a 95% placement rate, with the highest package of INR 60 LPA and an average package of INR 8 LPA for B.Tech.

PDPU Admission 2025

PDPU admissions are open for 2025 and are conducted online via the university’s website. Admissions are merit-based or entrance-based, followed by counseling or interviews for certain programs. Key admission processes include:

  • B.Tech: Requires 10+2 with 45% in PCM and a valid JEE Main/GUJCET score, followed by ACPC counseling (50% seats) or PDPU counseling (50% All-India seats).
  • MBA: Requires a bachelor’s degree with 50% and a valid CAT/XAT/NMAT/CMAT score, followed by a Personal Interview.
  • B.Sc (Hons)/BA (Hons)/BBA (Hons): Merit-based admission with 10+2 marks; BBA (Hons) requires SAT or institute’s aptitude test and interview.
  • M.Tech: Requires a B.E./B.Tech degree with 60% and a valid GATE score.
  • Ph.D.: Requires a master’s degree with 55%, followed by a written test and interview.

How to get admission in CS through Management quota in Pandit Deendayal Energy University, Gandhinagar?

S
Shivani •  May-28-2025

To secure admissions to the Pandit Deendayal Energy University, you cannot apply with the management quota. There is no management quota offered by the university; instead, they have NRI seats for non-indian residents. PDEU has a management quota for programs like MBA, but it is not the primary source of admission. For B.Tech programs, PDEU has a different admission process for All India Quota and government seats. Along with accepting exams like JEE & GUJCET, the PDEU also evaluates the student's academic performance for admissions to the B.Tech programs.
