About Punjabi University Patiala

Punjabi University, Patiala, is a Public State University established in 1962 in Patiala, Punjab. It is the second university in the world named after a language, following Hebrew University of Israel. Spanning 600 acres, the university is accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC and approved by UGC, AICTE, PCI, and BCI. It is ranked 59 in the Pharmacy category by NIRF 2025. Punjabi University offers over 1,760 courses across 17 streams, including Engineering, Arts, Commerce, Science, Management, and Law, at Diploma, UG, PG, and Doctoral levels. Admissions are based on merit or entrance exams like JEE Main, GATE, CMAT, CAT, AILET, and NEET. The university has a strong placement record, with a 100% placement rate for Pharmacy (PG) in 2023 and an average package ranging from INR 2.60 LPA to INR 6.75 LPA.

Punjabi University Patiala Admission 2025

Admissions at Punjabi University are based on merit or entrance exam scores, followed by counseling or interviews for certain courses. Candidates must apply through the official website (pupadmissions.ac.in). Key admission processes include:

  • B.Tech: Requires 10+2 with 50% in PCM and a valid JEE Main score, followed by counseling.
  • MBA: Requires a bachelor’s degree with 50% and a valid CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT score.
  • B.Pharm: Requires 10+2 with 50% in PCB/PCM and a valid NEET score.
  • LLB: Requires a bachelor’s degree with 45% (40% for SC/ST) and a valid AILET or state-level entrance score.
  • MCA/M.Sc/Ph.D.: Merit-based or entrance-based (Punjab MET for MCA, NEET for M.Sc Botany, UGC-NET/CSIR-NET for Ph.D.), followed by interviews for Ph.D.

Punjabi University FAQs

What number of colleges is Punjabi University associated with?

The university includes 274 associated colleges, 27 regional center/neighbourhood campuses, and more than 70 teaching and research departments and chairs on its main campus.

Are there any remote learning Punjabi University courses available?

Indeed, a candidate can choose from about six of these programmes through correspondence or distance learning, including the BLibISc, BA, Diploma in Gurmat Sangeet, BCom, BBA, and BCA.

What position does this university hold?

In the NIRF-2019 pharmacy rating, Punjabi University is rated between 150 and 200 and 16 overall.

The Punjabi University offers how many different courses?

The institution now provides more than 300 undergraduate courses, around 240 graduate courses, and more than 5 doctoral courses.

How many departments are there at Punjabi University?

Presently, the institution enrols about 14,000 students in a variety of courses and has more than 70 teaching and research departments.

Is Punjabi University financially worthwhile?

Since 1962, Punjabi University has been among the top universities, getting an "A" grade from the NAAC. With reasonable prices, the institution places a strong emphasis on innovative, developing approaches in academia. In addition to this, it collaborates with 14 international universities and institutions. Shiksha evaluations, however, state that the placements fall short of expectations.

Is Punjabi University a public or private institution?

How is the library at Punjabi University?

Punjabi University has a world-class library with a seating facility for 700 readers. The library is well stocked with books, bound periodicals, current journals and newspapers. The library at Punjabi University is open for 360 days of the year and for 20 hours in a day.

Yes, Punjabi University takes the placement of students very seriously. It has its own placement cell that not only grooms students but also offers an interface between the students and prospective employers.

Yes, Punjabi University has separate hostels for boys and girls. A total of six residential hostels at Punjabi University are for male students while seven residential hostels are for female students.

Punjabi University offers multiple courses across various streams. However, some of the flagship degrees offered at Punjabi University are B.Tech, B.Sc, M.Tech, MBA, M.Com, LLB etc.

Punjabi University Patiala Scholarships

Scholarships at the Punjabi University, Patiala are provided to the students based on merit. Scholarships are provided so that in any circumstances, the education of students does not come to a halt. The scholarship program was introduced to provide financial assistance to financially weak students. The Punjabi University, Patiala scholarships are also divided into some categories, they are as follows:

Departmental Scholarships-

The departmental scholarship program of the Punjabi University, Patiala ensures Rs 500 each, every month. This money is available for the students who are in various postgraduate courses, and courses such as B.Pharm, LLB, B.Tech, etc. This amount is available in the teaching departments of the University concerning the course. The details of this scholarship are:

  • One merit-based scholarship is awarded to the students in each course. 
  • The departmental scholarship is awarded to the students in the order of merit. This Punjabi University scholarship is provided based on the result secured by the students in their annual examination. 
  • Another important factor to keep in mind is that the course duration must be more than a year. 
  • This Punjabi University scholarship is given to the students every quarter. The head of the department is responsible for generating the bill(s). 

UGC National Scholarships-

UGC National scholarships of the Punjabi University are provided to students who are from MA, Ba, B.Com, and B.Com (Hons) courses in Economics. An amount of Rs Rs. 300 is provided to the students who have managed to secure at least 60% marks in the above-mentioned courses. After awarding the scholarship to the students, it continues for the entire course duration. The students must keep in mind that they have to maintain an aggregate score of 55% throughout their course duration.

Dr S.S. Joshi Memorial Scholarship-

Dr S.S. Joshi Memorial Scholarship is awarded to the students who belong to the MA course in Linguistic and Lexicography. The duration of this scholarship is for 10 months. The students are awarded this Punjabi University scholarship based on their need cum merit which is checked during the time of admission. The students have to keep in mind that they keep securing at least 55% marks in their course duration. 

Under this scholarship, the students of the Punjabi University, Patiala are awarded an amount of Rs Rs. 1000 monthly. 

Dr Jeet Singh Sheetal Memorial Scholarship-

Dr Jeet Singh Sheetal Memorial Scholarship is awarded to the students who belong to the MA in Punjabi course. In this scholarship program, the students are awarded an amount of Rs 6,000 annually. 

Apart from these scholarships, some other scholarships are awarded to the students of the Punjabi University, Patiala. The scholarships and their details are mentioned below:
