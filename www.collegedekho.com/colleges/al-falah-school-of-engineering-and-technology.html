About Al-Falah School of Engineering & Technology (AFSET), Faridabad

Al-Falah School of Engineering & Technology is situated in Faridabad in Haryana state of India. Established in 1997, is a Private college. Al-Falah School of Engineering & Technology offers 15 courses across 2 streams namely Engineering, Management..Popular degrees offered at Al-Falah School of Engineering & Technology include B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Al-Falah School of Engineering & Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Al-Falah School of Engineering & Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Al-Falah School of Engineering & Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
