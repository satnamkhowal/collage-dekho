IIT Hyderabad Placements Overview 2025

The placements for IIT Hyderabad are managed by its Centre for Career Development (CCD) every year. The CCD is responsible for preparing students for placements and bringing recruiters to the campus. As per IIT Hyderabad's placement report 2024, the highest package offered during placements was INR 90 LPA, while the average package stood at INR 22.5 LPA. A total of 320 companies visited the campus, and 521 offers were made, including 43 international offers. The placement rate for UG and PG students was 70.99% and 69.74%, respectively. Some of the top recruiters who visited IIT Hyderabad for placements included Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, Flipkart, TSMC, Adobe.

IIT Hyderabad Placements 2025 Highlights

IIT Hyderabad's 2025 placement season showed strong results, with 96% of students getting jobs. The technology sector was the biggest recruiter. Students received 53 international job offers, which is 20% more than last year. Most companies hired for software development and data analytics roles. These numbers come from the institute's first phase placement report and show that IIT Hyderabad students are in high demand, especially in tech fields.

IIT Hyderabad Average and Highest Package 2025

IIT Hyderabad placements 2025, the highest package of Rs 90 LPA went to BTech CSE graduates in software roles at global tech firms, while the overall average package settled at Rs 33.96 LPA across all programs, up from Rs 32 LPA in 2024. BTech students saw an average of Rs 22.5 LPA, with medians at Rs 21 LPA for UG as per NIRF 2025 data.

IIT Hyderabad Branch-Wise Placements 2025

IIT Hyderabad's 2025 placements, Computer Science students did the best with 98% getting jobs out of 120 students, earning an average salary of Rs 30 lakh per year. Electrical Engineering also performed well with 93% placements and Rs 24 lakh average salary. Traditional engineering branches like Mechanical had 82% placements, with most students joining manufacturing companies. This information comes from the institute's official placement reports and shows that computer-related fields continue to have the highest job opportunities and salaries.

Top Recruiters at IIT Hyderabad Placements 2025

Top recruiters at IIT Hyderabad placements 2025 included 353 firms, with tech giants like Google and Microsoft hiring 45 students for software roles, while consulting firms such as Goldman Sachs offered 28 positions in finance analytics. International recruiters contributed 17 visits, focusing on AI expertise.

i just attempted a free mock test where will i get the results

A
Apoorva Bali •  Jan-30-2025

The results of any free IIT JAM mock test you attempted will usually be accessible on the same website or platform where you took the test. You can check your results by doing the following:

  • Check the platform or website:

Return to the website where the mock exam was held if it was one of such sites (such as the platform of a coaching centre or an educational website). Use the login information you used to register to access your account, if you have one. Look for a section that says "My Results," "Test History," or "Dashboard," since these might include your mock test results and feedback.

  • Instant Results:

Results from a lot of free practice exams are available right away. If so, see if your score appeared on the platform immediately after you finished the test.

  • Notification via Email:

After you finish the test, some platforms send you an email with your findings. Look for any correspondence on the outcomes of your practice exam in your inbox (and spam folder).

  • Speak with Support:

You can get in touch with the helpdesk or support staff of the website or platform where you took the test if you are unable to locate your results. They ought to be able to help you find your score.

Please let me know if you need help with anything else regarding to the IIT JAM mock test!
