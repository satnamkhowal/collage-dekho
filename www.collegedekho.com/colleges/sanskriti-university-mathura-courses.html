Sanskriti University Mathura Courses and Fees 2026

Sanskriti University Mathura offers a total of more than 60 courses at UG, PG, Diploma, Integrated, Certificate, and Doctoral levels. These courses are available in various disciplines such as engineering, management, agriculture, hospitality, fashion design, and more. At the UG level, Sanskriti University offers popular courses like B.Tech, BBA, BCA, and B.Sc. While at the PG level, courses like MBA, M.Tech, MCA, and M.Sc. are offered. The college also offers other courses at diploma, integrated, certificate, and Ph.D. levels. The college also offers various specialisations like Computer Science Engineering, Data Science & Business Analytics, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning under B.Tech; Human Resources, Finance, Marketing Management under MBA; and more. The annual Sanskriti University fees range from ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000 depending on the type and level of courses.
