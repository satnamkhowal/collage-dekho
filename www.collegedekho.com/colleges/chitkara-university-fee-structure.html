Chitkara University Fees 2026 Overview

Chitkara University fees for the year 2026 vary based on the programme, specialisation, and duration. The university offers undergraduate postgraduate and doctoral programmes in engineering, management, pharmacy, design, law, and health sciences.

For undergraduate programmes, the total tuition fees range from Rs 1,80,000 to Rs 18,50,000. BE programmes cost between Rs 6,80,000 and Rs 18,50,000, while business programmes such as BBA and BCom range from Rs 3,50,000 to Rs 14,60,000.

The Chitkara University fee structure for postgraduate programmes cost between Rs 1,20,000 to Rs 8,00,000 for two-year. MBA specialisations are cost between Rs 2,80,000 and Rs 8,00,000, while other master’s programmes such as MPharma and MSc range from Rs 2,00,000 to Rs 3,60,000.
