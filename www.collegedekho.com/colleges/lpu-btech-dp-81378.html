About LPU B.Tech

LPU B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. LPU B.Tech admission 2026 is mainly through LPUNEST, with JEE Main scores also accepted. Eligibility requires 60% in 10+2 (PCM/PCB) or Diploma/B.Sc for lateral entry. Fees range from Rs 2,80,000 to Rs 9,00,000 per year depending on the branch. The process includes online registration, entrance test, counselling, document verification, and seat allotment. Scholarships may be offered based on performance.

LPU BTech Admission Process 2026

Admission to BTech at LPU is mainly through LPUNEST, while JEE Main scores are also accepted. Students apply online, appear for the test, and then join counselling based on their rank and choices.

  • Apply Online: Register on the LPU website, pay the form fee, and upload 10+2 details.
  • LPUNEST Exam: Book a slot, take the 2.5-hour test based on Physics, Chemistry, Math, and Aptitude.
  • Counselling: Check your rank, fill your preferred branches, and pay the fee to lock the seat.
  • Verification: Submit original documents during reporting before the final admission deadline.
  • JEE Route: Students with a good JEE Main rank (within the required range) can get direct consideration.

LPU B.Tech Admission Eligibility

Students need 60% in 12th with the required science subjects (PCM/PCB) depending on the B.Tech branch. Lateral entry requires 60% in Diploma or B.Sc/BCA, and scholarships may be given to eligible PCM students after a test and interview.

CategoryEligibility 
B.Tech (Regular – CSE/IT/Engg.)60% in 10+2 with Physics + Math + English
B.Tech (Biotechnology / Biosciences)60% in 10+2 with Physics + Chemistry + English + (Math/Bio/Biotech)
B.Tech Lateral Entry (After Diploma/B.Sc.)60% in Diploma (Engg.) OR 60% in B.Sc (Non-Medical/CS) / BCA
B.Tech – Industry Integrated (Futurense, Quantiphi etc.)60% in 10+2 PCM & English
Special Eligibility Cases10th + 10+2 PCM; 60–89% may get scholarship after test + interview
B.Tech with Chemistry Requirement (Chemical, Food Tech, Architecture pathways)60% in 10+2 Physics + Chemistry + Math + English

Documents Required for LPU B.Tech Admission 2026

For B.Tech admission at LPU in 2026, students must upload required academic and identity documents during the application/LPUNEST process and later submit photocopies. These documents ensure eligibility (60% PCM), identity proof, and category benefits.

Class 10 marksheet & certificate

Entrance exam details: LPUNEST Admit Card / Scorecard or JEE Main Score (if applicable)

Character / School Leaving / Transfer Certificate

Three passport-size photographs

Proof of residence (any valid government document)

Aadhaar Card of the student and the parents

Self-attested anti-ragging affidavit (student + parent/guardian)

Income affidavit (if applying for a fee concession or scholarship)

Balanced Campus Life with Good Exposure

Overall My overall experience at Lovely Professional University has been mostly positive. The campus is large, well planned, and very active throughout the year. You get exposure to academics, events, sports, and different cultures because students come from many states and countries. Facilities are good, but managing time is important because the campus is big and schedules can be tight. Self-discipline matters a lot here. Overall, it is a decent option for students who want opportunities along with structured academics.

Placement Placement opportunities are decent, especially for students who maintain good academic records and skills. Many companies visit the campus for internships and final placements, offering roles in IT, management, operations, and other sectors. Training sessions, mock interviews, and aptitude classes are provided. However, not everyone gets high packages, and students need to work on skills, internships, and projects on their own to get better outcomes.

Infrastructure The infrastructure is one of the strongest points of the university. Classrooms are spacious and mostly equipped with projectors and smart boards. Labs are well maintained for practical learning. There is a central library with good seating capacity and digital resources, though it can get crowded during exams. Sports facilities, grounds, gyms, and common areas are easily available. The only drawback is long walking distances between blocks, which can be tiring for new students.

Faculty Faculty members are generally qualified and supportive, especially when students approach them for help. Many teachers explain concepts clearly and encourage practical learning through assignments and projects. Some faculty members are very approachable, while others are more formal in nature. Teaching quality can vary from subject to subject, so self-study is important along with classes. Overall, guidance is available if students show interest.

Hostel Hostel facilities are decent and suitable for student life. Rooms are furnished with beds, study tables, cupboards, and Wi-Fi access. Cleaning is done regularly, and security is strict, which helps maintain safety. Mess food quality is average some days good, some days repetitive. Hostel rules are slightly strict regarding entry timings, which may feel restrictive, but they help maintain order and safety inside the campus.
