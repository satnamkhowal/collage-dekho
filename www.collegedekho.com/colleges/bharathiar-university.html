About Bharathiar University

Bharathiar University, Coimbatore, established in February 1982 under the Bharathiar University Act, 1981, is a prestigious state university named after the renowned Tamil poet Subramania Bharati. The university spans 780 acres at the foothills of Maruthamalai, Tamil Nadu, and has been recognized by the UGC since 1985. It holds an NAAC A++ grade, reflecting academic excellence, research strength, and outreach impact.

In NIRF 2025, Bharathiar University ranked 76th in Overall Category & 46th in University Category, 24th in India (Times Ranking), and 801–1000 globally in THE World Rankings. The university serves around 3,500 on-campus students and 200,000 learners through 140 affiliated colleges, emphasizing inclusive and multidisciplinary education.

Offering 45+ programs across Arts, Science, Commerce, and Management, Bharathiar University has emerged as a hub for research and innovation. In 2025 placements, the median package was INR 9.50 LPA for PG 2-year programs, with top recruiters including Federal Bank, ITC Limited, Nestlé, and ICICI Bank.

With an expanding digital learning ecosystem, Bharathiar University’s School of Distance Education caters to learners nationwide. Its eco-friendly campus, industry-oriented syllabi, and affordable fee structure attract aspirants for 2026 admissions in programs like M.Sc. Biotechnology, M.A. Economics, and M.Com.

Which entrance exam scores are accepted by Bharathiar University, Coimbatore for the M.B.A programme admission?

Bharathiar  University Coimbatore accepts TANCET scores for admission to the M.B.A programme.

Is Bharathiar University a good university?

Bharathiar University Coimbatore was founded in 1982 and is accredited with an A grade by NAAC and approved by the UGC, so its degree is recognised. UGC - CPEPA, DST-PURSE, DST-FIST, UGC-SAP, MoE - RUSA, DRDO, and others provide grants to the university.

How is the Bharathiar University Infrastructure?

Bharathiar University, located in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, has a good infrastructure spread across 778.89 acres of land. It also has a hospital and a hostel on campus, providing students with both medical and lodging options. It has also established a Centre for Life Sciences in collaboration with the DRDO and the Government of Tamil Nadu to conduct research in advanced Life Sciences and other multidisciplinary areas related to defence.

Is Bharathiar University a good place to get an MBA?

Yes, Bharathiar University is a good place to get an MBA. Students can pursue a two- to five-year MSc in one of 26 specialisations, as well as a two-year MCA, MCom, M.Ed, M.P.Ed, MA, MSW, and MBA. The university is ranked 14 in the University category by NIRF 2021, 19 in the Indian University category by Times Higher Education 2021, and is ranked by several other ranking bodies.
