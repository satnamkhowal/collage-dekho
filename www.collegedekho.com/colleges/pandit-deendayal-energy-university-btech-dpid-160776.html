About Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech

Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Pandit Deendayal Energy University in Gandhinagar, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Firstly, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination or an equivalent board examination with a minimum aggregate score of 45% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Additionally, they should have qualified in the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main, which is a national-level entrance exam conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). The JEE Main score is a crucial factor in the selection process. Candidates are advised to check the university's official website for any specific criteria or requirements that may vary from year to year. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 2,81,000. Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Pandit Deendayal Energy University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech Application Process

Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Pandit Deendayal Energy University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Pandit Deendayal Energy University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Pandit Deendayal Energy University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Pandit Deendayal Energy University B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
