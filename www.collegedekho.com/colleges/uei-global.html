About UEI Global (UEIG), Ludhiana

UEI Global, Ludhiana is situated in Ludhiana in Punjab state of India. Established in 2007, is a Private college. The college is accredited by UGC, AIU, NAAC. UEI Global, Ludhiana offers 28 courses across 4 streams namely Management, Hotel Management, Science, Media and Mass Communication..Popular degrees offered at UEI Global, Ludhiana include BBA, BHM, BHM, B.M.M., BSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, UEI Global, Ludhiana is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at UEI Global, Ludhiana, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at UEI Global, Ludhiana is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.

If I float my NCHMCT JEE 2025 first round seat, when is the deadline to upload documents and pay fees?

T
Tiyasa Khanra •  Jun-11-2025

Dear Student,

If you had floated your NCHMCT JEE 2025 first round seat, the deadline to upload documents and pay fees was June 5, 2025. Document uploading, choice of willingness (Floating/ Freezing), payment of seat acceptance fees of INR 20,000, and verification by the document verification centre started on June 3, 2025. “Float” option implies that if you are allotted a new seat in the 2nd round, the seat offered to you earlier would automatically be cancelled. After this, the cancelled seat would be allotted to the candidate next in merit who might have wanted to opt for that seat through his/her earlier submitted choices. The documents required for NCHMCT JEE 2025 Counselling include document supporting reservation claim (Category certificate/ PwD certificate/ KM certificate), Class 10/ date of birth certificate, NCHMCT JEE 2025 admit card, NCHMCT JEE 2025 result/ scorecard, NCHMCT JEE 2025 application form, and Medical Certificate. While appearing for the counselling round, you must pay a registration fee of INR 2,000, that is non-refundable. Additionally, you will also have to pay a participation fee of INR 40,000.
