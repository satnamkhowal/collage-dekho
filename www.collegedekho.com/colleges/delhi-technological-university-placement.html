DTU Placements 2025

DTU provides on-campus, off-campus, and internship opportunities to all its students through its placement cell. The university has a strong placement record with top recruiters from various industries. As per the DTU Placement Report 2024, a total of 1,900 students were placed in top-notch companies. DTU Placements FAQs

How many companies visit DTU?

Over 200 companies including top MNCs, banks, and other corporates visit Delhi Technological University Placement every year. Some of the most visited Delhi Technological University Placement companies are Zomato, Samsung, Microsoft, Deloitte, Boston Consulting Group, Flipkart, McKinsey & Company, etc.

What is the placement percentage at DTU?

The placement percentage at DTU is above 70-80% for most courses. The MBA and BTech programmes at the university have above 90% placements.

What is the highest package in DTU for MBA?

As per DTU placement report 2023, the highest package offered for the MBA programme was Rs 23.78 LPA. The second highest package offered for the MBA programme was Rs 22 LPA. More than 160 recruiters visited the campus for MBA placements.

What is the minimum package in DTU?

The minimum package offered in DTU is Rs 4 LPA. The average package offered is Rs 25 LPA. Moreover, the highest package offered by the university is Rs 82.05 LPA.

Who are the top recruiters of DTU?

Delhi Technological University Placement companies include top MNCs, banks, and other corporates. Some of the most visited Delhi Technological University Placement companies are Zomato, Samsung, Microsoft, Deloitte, Boston Consulting Group, Flipkart, McKinsey & Company, etc.

What is the highest package for Btech in Delhi Technological University?

The highest package offered for the BTech programme was Rs 82.05 LPA. This package was offered to a BTech CSE and IT student. For MBA, the highest package offered was Rs 23.78 LPA in 2023 and for MTech, the highest package offered was Rs 51.03 LPA.

Which branch of Delhi Technological University has best placements record?

All BTech, MTech, and MBA branches of Delhi Technological University have excellent placement records. For MBA, the highest package offered was Rs 23.78 LPA in 2023, MTech was Rs 51.03 LPA and for BTech, the highest package offered was Rs 82.05 LPA. So all of these branches are good for placements.

Is Delhi Technological University good for placements?

Yes, DTU is good for placements. According to DTU placements report 2023, more than 2684 offers were made from top companies like Zomato, Samsung, Microsoft, Deloitte, Boston Consulting Group, Flipkart, McKinsey & Company, etc. The highest package offered was Rs 82.05 LPA. This shows that Delhi Technological University has very good placements.
