  • Fill in the required details under the ‘Admission Open 2024’ section
  • Click on the ‘Register’ tab to move ahead with the application

Swami Vivekanand Institute of Engineering and Technology Rajpura Eligibility Criteria for 2024

  • Students need to have 50% aggregate marks in UG courses in 10+2 or equivalent exams. Candidates who want to enroll in B.Tech programs must have passed the 10+2 from an accredited school with mathematics and physics as compulsory subjects, along with chemistry, biology, biotechnology, or computer science as an optional subject. For the LEET program, the candidate must have a diploma-level degree in technology or science with at least a 50% aggregate (45% for reserved candidates).
  • For the B.Voc course, the candidate must have passed 10+2 from a recognised school with the relevant streams in all subjects. The candidate must obtain a minimum of 55% in 10+2 (50%) with all subjects taken together.
  • For the B.Pharma course, applicants must have passed the 10+2 exam with english, mathematics, and physics as the compulsory subjects, along with chemistry, biology, or biotechnology as an optional subject. They must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 50% in the 10+2 exam.
  • For B.Com, B.CA, B.Ed, and BBA, candidates must secure at least 50% in their senior secondary class 10+2.
  • PG including M.Tech, M.Sc, M.Pharma, and M.Com courses require a bachelor's degree in a relevant stream.
  • For the PGD course, the Candidate must possess a certificate of passing the 10+2 examination from a recognised school in any relevant stream. The candidate must attain a minimum aggregate of 50% in the 10+2 exam (45% for reserved candidates). The candidate must possess a degree from a recognised university within two years
  • For the Diploma course, a candidate must have a minimum of two years of ITI diploma from a recognised institute or a 10+2 degree with vocational or non-medical subjects.

Swami Vivekanand Institute of Engineering and Technology Rajpura Application Process 2024

Every year, the admissions for UG and PG courses at SVIET Rajpura begins in March. Candidates who are interested in a particular course can apply online.

  • Admission to the MBA and M.Tech program is based on undergraduate-level scores.
  • Aside from BA, BBA, and B.com, admission to UG courses is based on Class 12 results.
  • Class 12 results determine admission to integrated courses.
  • Candidates must submit an online application on the official website.
  • Lateral entry admissions are also available for the B.Tech program. The eligibility is diploma after grade 10, i.e., 10+3.

Swami Vivekanand Institute of Engineering & Technology, Chandigarh FAQs

Which specializations of BTech are offered by SVIET?

Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering and Computer Science Engineering are the specializations of BTech available at SVIET.

Are there any distance learning courses available at SVIET?

No. SVIET does not offer any distance learning courses. The courses available at SVIET are offered in the regular, full-time mode.

Which grading system is followed by SVIET?

SVIET follows the SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) and CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) system.

Is Swami Vivekanand Institute of Engineering and Technology a good college?

SVIET Chandigarh is known as one of the finest institutions of higher learning located in the region. The college focuses on good quality teaching backed by modern infrastructure and solid placement support is provided to the students.

Is SVIET approved by AICTE? Which other organizations have approved it?

Yes. SVIET is an AICTE-approved institute. The group of institutes have been approved by the relevant governing bodies such as the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE).

Which institutes are a part of the Swami Vivekanand Group of Institutes?

The institutes that are part of SVGOI are Swami Vivekanand Institute of Engineering & Technology (SVIET), Swami Vivekanand College of Education(SVCE), Swami Vivekanand Faculty of Information Technology & Business Management (SVFIT&BM), Swami Vivekanand Polytechnic college (SVPC), Swami Vivekanand Industrial Training Centre (SVITC), Swami Vivekananda College of Pharmacy (SVCP), and Swami Vivekanand Faculty of Technology & Management (SVFTM).

SVIET Banur is affiliated to which university?

Swami Vivekananda Institute of Engineering and Technology is an affiliate-institute of Punjab Technical University (PTU) Jalandhar, Maharaja Ranjit Singh Punjab Technical University (MRSPTU) Bhatinda, Punjab State Board of Technical Education and Information Technology (Chandigarh) and Punjabi University Patiala. The paramedical courses offered by SVIET are affiliated from Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS).

Is SVIET Banur a private institute or a government institute?

Swami Vivekananda Institute of Engineering and Technology is a private higher education institution located in Banur, near Chandigarh city in Punjab.
