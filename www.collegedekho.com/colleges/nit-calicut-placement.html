NIT Calicut Placements 2025

NIT Calicut Placements 2025 saw over 1,000 students placed through 188 recruiting companies. The highest package reached Rs 47 LPA, while the average package stood at Rs 14.29 LPA. This is the second year in a row that placements crossed 1,000, showing strong demand for NIT Calicut students in both tech and core sectors. Students from BTech, MTech, MBA, and MCA programs actively took part, with some getting multiple offers. Most placements were from Indian companies, but a few students also received international offers from top firms like Google.

NIT Calicut Placements FAQs

Are there international placements in NIT Calicut 2025?

International placements at NIT Calicut in 2025 were relatively limited, as most offers came from leading domestic companies across technology, consulting, and core sectors. However, a few exceptional students received prestigious global roles from top multinational firms such as Google and other tech giants. These international offers reflected the global competence and strong technical foundation of NIT Calicut graduates. While the majority of students opted for high-paying roles in India, the institute continues to strengthen its global recruitment network to expand international placement opportunities in future placement seasons.

What training support does the Placement Cell at NIT Calicut offer?

The Centre for Career Development (CCD) at NIT Calicut plays a key role in preparing students for placements from their second year onward. It offers structured training programs including resume-building workshops, mock interviews, aptitude and coding practice sessions, and soft skills development. The CCD also coordinates internships, securing over 187 internship opportunities for students in 2025 across top companies. In addition, it provides career counseling and guidance to help students identify their strengths, set goals, and make informed career choices, ensuring they are industry-ready and confident during campus recruitment drives.

What is the average package for ECE branch in NIT Calicut 2025?

In NIT Calicut Placements 2025, the BTech Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) branch achieved an impressive average package of Rs 19.29 LPA. Over 140 students were successfully placed, recording a placement rate above 95%. Top recruiters such as Qualcomm, Texas Instruments, Cisco, Oracle, and Samsung offered roles in core electronics, VLSI design, embedded systems, and communication technologies. The strong placement record highlights the high demand for NIT Calicut’s ECE graduates and the program’s focus on industry-oriented learning, research, and practical skills, enabling students to secure top positions in leading global and Indian technology companies.

How do NIT Calicut Placements 2025 compare to 2024?

NIT Calicut Placements 2025 recorded over 1,000 students placed with an average package of Rs 14.29 LPA, showing clear improvement from 2024, when 1,080 students were placed with an average of Rs 12.11 LPA and the highest package of Rs 51 LPA. Despite a slight drop in the number of recruiting companies from 208 to 188, the rise in the average package reflects stronger salary offers and higher placement quality. The results highlight the growing recognition of NIT Calicut graduates and the institute’s continued success in attracting top recruiters across technology and core engineering sectors.

What is the median package for UG programs at NIT Calicut 2025?

The median package for undergraduate BTech programs at NIT Calicut Placements 2025 stood at Rs 12.50 LPA, according to NIRF-aligned data. This reflects steady placement outcomes and strong employability among engineering graduates across various branches. The institute’s focus on practical learning, technical innovation, and industry collaboration has helped maintain competitive salary trends. Top recruiters such as D.E. Shaw, Google, Salesforce, and Texas Instruments offered high-value roles in software, analytics, and core domains. The consistent rise in placement performance highlights NIT Calicut’s growing industry reputation and commitment to producing skilled, job-ready engineering professionals.

Who are the top recruiters for NIT Calicut Placements 2025?

Top recruiters for NIT Calicut Placements 2025 included leading global firms such as D.E. Shaw, Google, Goldman Sachs, Salesforce, Qualcomm, Texas Instruments, Oracle, Cisco, Atlassian, and SAP Labs. These companies offered high-paying roles across technology, finance, and analytics domains. In addition, several reputed core and engineering firms like L&T, IBM, TCS, Bosch, Micron, and Maruti Suzuki also participated in the drive, providing diverse opportunities to students from various branches. Their participation highlights NIT Calicut’s strong academic reputation, industry-oriented curriculum, and consistent performance in attracting top recruiters for both tech and core engineering roles.

What is the placement percentage for CSE in NIT Calicut 2025?

The placement rate for BTech Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at NIT Calicut in 2025 reached an impressive 96.77%, showcasing strong industry demand for its graduates. Over 150 students from the CSE department were successfully placed with top recruiters offering attractive roles in software development, data science, and related domains. The average package for CSE students stood at Rs 22.03 LPA, one of the highest across all branches. Major recruiters included D.E. Shaw, Google, Salesforce, Oracle, and Qualcomm, reflecting NIT Calicut’s growing reputation for producing highly skilled and job-ready engineering professionals.

How many students were placed in NIT Calicut 2025?

Over 1,000 students were successfully placed during NIT Calicut Placements 2025 across BTech, MTech, MBA, and MCA programs. A total of 188 top companies visited the campus, offering diverse roles in technology, finance, management, and core engineering sectors. The placement drive witnessed strong participation from leading recruiters such as D.E. Shaw, Google, Salesforce, Goldman Sachs, and Texas Instruments. Many students received multiple offers, reflecting the high industry demand for NIT Calicut graduates. The institute’s consistent placement success highlights its academic excellence, skilled student pool, and strong ties with national and international companies.

What is the average package for NIT Calicut Placements 2025?

For undergraduate BTech students, the average package was Rs 13.39 LPA, while postgraduate students from MTech, MBA, and MCA programs recorded an average of Rs 11.51 LPA. This shows consistent performance and growing industry trust in NIT Calicut graduates. Top recruiters such as D.E. Shaw, Google, Salesforce, Oracle, and Texas Instruments offered lucrative roles in technology, analytics, and core engineering, reinforcing the institute’s excellent placement standards and academic reputation nationwide.

What is the highest package offered in NIT Calicut Placements 2025?

This achievement highlights the strong academic and technical excellence of NIT Calicut students. The institute continues to attract top recruiters offering competitive packages in both tech and core sectors. Along with D.E.
