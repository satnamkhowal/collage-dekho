About SURTECH B.Tech

SURTECH B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Dr. Sudhir Chandra Sur Institute of Technology and Sports Complex Kolkata, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 examination or its equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. They should have secured a minimum aggregate score of 45% (40% for reserved categories) in the qualifying examination. Additionally, candidates must qualify in any national or state-level engineering entrance examination, such as JEE Main or WBJEE. Meeting these criteria will enable students to embark on a comprehensive and industry-oriented B.Tech. program at Dr. Sudhir Chandra Sur Institute of Technology and Sports Complex Kolkata. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 84,100 - 4,01,000. SURTECH B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at SURTECH is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the SURTECH B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

SURTECH B.Tech Application Process

SURTECH B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at SURTECH have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill SURTECH B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of SURTECH and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of SURTECH. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the SURTECH B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the SURTECH B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of SURTECH B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for SURTECH B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
