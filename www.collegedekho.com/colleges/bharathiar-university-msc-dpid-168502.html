About Bharathiar University MSc

Bharathiar University MSc is a 2 - 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. The eligibility criteria for pursuing an M.Sc. (Master of Science) degree at Bharathiar University in India require candidates to have a bachelor's degree in the relevant field of study from a recognized university. The minimum qualifying marks may vary depending on the specific discipline. Additionally, candidates must have successfully completed their undergraduate degree with a minimum aggregate score as specified by the university. Admission to the M.Sc. program may also involve an entrance examination or interview conducted by the university. It is important for prospective students to check the university's official website or admission brochure for the most accurate and up-to-date eligibility requirements. The annual fee for MSc is INR 4,950 - 75,160. Bharathiar University MSc Admission 2026

Admission to the MSc at Bharathiar University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Bharathiar University MSc admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Bharathiar University MSc Application Process

Bharathiar University MSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MSc course at Bharathiar University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Bharathiar University MSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Bharathiar University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Bharathiar University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Bharathiar University MSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Bharathiar University MSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Bharathiar University MSc Application Form.

Documents Required for Bharathiar University MSc Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
