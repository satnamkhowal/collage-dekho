Lingaya's Vidyapeeth Courses FAQs

What types of courses does Lingaya's Vidyapeeth offer?

At the UG level, Lingaya's Vidyapeeth offers B.Tech, BSc, BCA, BA, B.Pharm, B.Arch, B.Com, BBA, LLB, B.Ed, BJMC, BBA+LLB, BBA+MBA, BA+LLB, B.Com+LLB, B.Des , etc, while at the PG level, Lingaya's Vidyapeeth offers MBA, M.Tech, MSc, LLM, MA, MS, MCA, M.Com, M.Ed, MJMC, M.Plan, PGD, M.Pharm, etc.

What are the Lingaya's Vidyapeeth courses after 10th?

Lingaya's Vidyapeeth offers Diploma in Architecture and certification Certificate in Child Guidance and Counseling after class 10.Diploma course is 3.0 Years and admission is granted based on CBSE 12th, HBSE 12th and Interview.Diploma is a 3.0 Years programme, admission to which is granted based on merit of the last qualifying exam.

Which courses are popular at Lingaya's Vidyapeeth?

The most popular Lingaya's Vidyapeeth courses include the B.Tech, BSc, BCA, BA, B.Pharm and B.Arch programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, BSc, BCA, BA, B.Pharm, B.Arch, B.Com, BBA, LLB, B.Ed, BJMC, BBA+LLB, BBA+MBA, BA+LLB, B.Com+LLB, B.Des, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include MBA, M.Tech, MSc, LLM, MA, MS, MCA, M.Com, M.Ed, MJMC, M.Plan, PGD, M.Pharm , etc. Lingaya's Vidyapeeth also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does Lingaya's Vidyapeeth accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at Lingaya's Vidyapeeth. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, BSc, BCA, BA, B.Pharm, B.Arch, B.Com, BBA, LLB, B.Ed, BJMC, BBA+LLB, BBA+MBA, BA+LLB, B.Com+LLB, B.Des and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is Lingaya's Vidyapeeth easy to get into?

Admission in Lingaya's Vidyapeeth courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in PG, UG, Ph.D, Diploma and Certificate courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the Lingaya's Vidyapeeth Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include CLAT, GATE, NIPER, HBSE 12th, NIMCET, NATA, CBSE 12th, GPAT, NEET, JEE Main, LSAT, AILET, HSTES, B.Ed. etc.
