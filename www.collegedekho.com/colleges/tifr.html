About Tata Institute of Fundamental Research

Tata Institute of Fundamental Research is a deemed university awarding masters and doctoral degrees. The institute is a National Centre of the Government of India under the Department of Atomic Energy. TIFR Mumbai was established with the support of the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust under Dr. Homi Bhabha’s vision.  

TIFR has been marked as one of the best institutions and accredited ‘A+’ grade by NAAC. Located at Navy Nagar, Colaba, Mumbai, the institute has an affiliated campus in Serilingampally near Hyderabad. Research at TIRF is distributed across three schools:

  • School of Mathematics
  • School of Natural Science
  • School of Technology and Computer Science 

Tata Institute Fundamental Research has 3 institutes outside its main campus located in Colaba and Mumbai, namely Homi Bhabha Centre for Theoretical Sciences at Deonar, Mumbai, and International Centre for Theoretical Sciences in Bangalore.

Tata Institute Fundamental Research Mumbai Eligibility Criteria for 2024

  • Students need to have completed their graduation with required subjects to get admission in postgraduate courses. For getting admission in Ph. D, students must have completed their postgraduation in a relevant stream from a recognized university.

Tata Institute Fundamental Research Mumbai 2024-24 Admission Dates

TIFR Mumbai results of the Nationwide Entrance Examination were declared on 2nd February 2023, and some students were short-listed. Interviews of these short-listed students are to be held from Mar-May 2023. For more details, students can visit the official website.

Tata Institute Fundamental Research Mumbai Entrance Exams

For final TIFR Mumbai admission, the institute conducts an online entrance test (GS Entrance Exam) followed by an interview of the short-listed candidates. However, if the candidate has a valid GATE score, they can be exempted from the TIFR entrance exam. In case, a student appears for the TIFR Mumbai entrance exam and also submits the GATE score, better performance will be considered for admission.

Tata Institute Fundamental Research Mumbai Application Process 2024

The TIFR Mumbai application process is easy and can be done both online as well as manually.

For online applications, students can find the link on the official website. Candidates applying for the courses are required to carefully read all the instructions before filling out the form. They cannot register multiple times for the same course while filling out the form. TIFR Mumbai application fee can be paid online via internet banking/ Debit Card/ Credit Card or Demand Draft. If a student is paying via Demand Draft, then he/she will have to mention the name, reference code, and telephone number behind the DD. 

For offline applications, students are required to send a request for an application form. The request for the application form must be made along with a self-addressed stamped envelope. 

  • For Biology- National Centre for Biological Sciences UAS- GKVK Campus, Bellary Road, Bangalore 560065.
  • For other subjects except for Science Education- University Cell, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Homi Bhabha Road, Colaba, Mumbai 400005.

Students applying for courses in the above-mentioned subjects must send the complete application form along with DD and two passport size photographs to the address mentioned above. 

  • For Science Education- Students applying for the courses in this particular subject will have to visit the HBCSE website.

Tata Institute Fundamental Research Mumbai Application Fees

The Tata Institute Fundamental Research Mumbai application fee is non-refundable.

  • For online applications, male candidates will have to pay Rs 600, and female candidates will have to pay Rs 100.
  • For manual applications, male candidates will have to pay Rs 650, and female candidates will have to pay Rs 100.

Tata Institute of Fundamental Research Scholarships

The main aim of the TIFR Scholarship Program is to encourage the students. Many bodies are helping the institution to deliver the scholarships, such as Doncaster Foundation, the Webb Family, and Eventi Capital Partners. 

Tata Institute of Fundamental Research scholarship facilities for PG and doctoral programs help the candidates with financial assistance and reward academic performances. The scholarships are given below:

  • Ph. D scholarship: 

IFR Mumbai Ph. D students receive a monthly fellowship of Rs 25,000, which is increased to Rs 28,000 after the registration.

Integrated Ph. D students receive a monthly fellowship of Rs 16,000 for the first year. When the performance of the candidate is satisfactory, the fellowship is enhanced to Rs 25,000 per month. After the registration for Ph. D, the fellowship is increased to Rs 28,000 per month. 

  • PG (M. Sc) Scholarship

Tata Institute of Fundamental Research Mumbai M. Sc students receive a monthly fellowship of Rs 12,000.
