About PCMT B.Tech

PCMT B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Pailan College of Management and Technology Kolkata, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 examination or its equivalent from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they should have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 45% in these subjects. For candidates belonging to reserved categories, a relaxation of 5% is provided. Furthermore, candidates must qualify in any national or state-level engineering entrance examination, such as JEE Main or WBJEE, to be considered for admission. Meeting these eligibility requirements ensures a strong foundation for students to embark on their journey towards a successful career in the field of technology. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 93,750. PCMT B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at PCMT is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the PCMT B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

PCMT B.Tech Application Process

PCMT B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at PCMT have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill PCMT B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of PCMT and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of PCMT. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the PCMT B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the PCMT B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of PCMT B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for PCMT B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
