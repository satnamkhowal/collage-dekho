About IIT Delhi Courses 2026

IIT Delhi courses list includes 100+ programs offered through 16 departments, 11 centres and 6 schools. Students can pursue UG, PG, PhD and executive programs. Courses like BTech, BDes, BS are offered at the UG level, while MTech, MBA, MSc, MDes, MPP, MA and Executive MBA are offered at the PG level. IIT Delhi course fees range from about INR 62,550 for PhD to around INR 18 Lakhs for Executive MBA.

IIT Delhi offers full-time academic programs across Engineering, Sciences, Management, Design, and Humanities, along with research-intensive PhD programmes across 30+ areas. The institute accepts JEE Advanced for BTech/BS, GATE for MTech and PhD, CAT for MBA, IIT JAM for MSc, CEED for MDes, and UCEED for BDes, along with written tests and interviews for select programmes like MPP, MA, and Cognitive Science.

IIT Delhi Courses FAQs

What types of courses does IIT Delhi offer?

At the UG level, IIT Delhi offers B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech , etc, while at the PG level, IIT Delhi offers M.Tech, MS, MSc, MBA, PGD, M.Des, Masters, M.E., etc.

What are the IIT Delhi courses after 10th?

IIT Delhi offers Certificate in Data Science after class 10.

Which courses are popular at IIT Delhi?

The most popular IIT Delhi courses include the B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, M.Tech, MS, MSc and MBA programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MS, MSc, MBA, PGD, M.Des, Masters, M.E. , etc. IIT Delhi also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does IIT Delhi accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at IIT Delhi. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is IIT Delhi easy to get into?

Admission in IIT Delhi courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, Certificate, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the IIT Delhi Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include CEED, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, GATE, IIT JAM etc.
