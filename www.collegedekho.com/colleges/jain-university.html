Jain University Admission Process 2026

Check out the admission process at Jain University below: 

  • Check out the eligibility criteria: Before applying for Jain University admission, candidates must check out the official eligibility criteria. 
  • Apply online: Then, apply for admission on the official website of the university.
  • Appear for the entrance exam: Only after qualifying for the entrance exam or having a valid score in the prescribed entrance exams of the university students will be able to proceed with the admission process. 
  • PI: Shortlisted candidates will be called for initial screening and PI.
  • GD & PI rounds: The next round includes GD and PI after which the final selection is done. 
  • Fees: The final selected students have to pay the admission fees to secure Jain University admission.

Jain University FAQs

Does Jain University have a scholarship?

Yes, Jain University has scholarships in 6 different categories which include Medha, Natyaswara, Ekalavaya, Dyumma, Atharva and Samasti. To be eligible for these scholarships, students must have passed their class 12th securing minimum aggregate marks and must be Indian citizens.

What is the fee structure of Jain University?

The fee structure of Jain University is different for various course levels and courses, the fee ranges between Rs 1,00,000 to Rs 5,75,000 for UG, PG and PhD levels. The university offers a total of 24 courses in full-time mode.

What is the NAAC rating for Jain University?

The NAAC grade for Jain University is A++ grade with a CGPA of 3.71 on a four-point scale. Students planning to apply to Jain University must check out the NAAC and other rankings before finalising their admission.

Does Jain University have good placements?

As per previous year's trends, Jain University has good placement records, approximately over 30,000 students are placed every academic year. Students are also able to bag internship opportunities through college placements.

Can I get direct admission to Jain University?

No, students have to appear for the JET entrance exam conducted by Jain University to get admission to Jain University. However, valid entrance exam scores such as CAT, ATMA, MAT, CMAT and more are also acceptable by the university.

What are the courses available at Jain University?

There are different courses available at Jain University in multiple disciplines engineering & architecture, design, medicine, sciences and more. Some of the popular courses are BDes, MDes, BTech, MTech and others.

Does Jain University offer any distance education programme?

Yes, Jain University offers various distance and online programmes under the Centre for Distance and Online Education. All these Jain University distance education and online programmes are approved by the Distance Education Bureau, a bureau of the University Grants Commission (UGC). Some of the open and distance learning programmes offered by Jain University are BBA, B.Com, BA, BCA, B.Sc, BMS, and many others.

Is Jain University a good college?

Jain Deemed-to-be University is a reputed private university in Bangalore, India. The university obtained the status of a Deemed-to- be university in 2004 owing to its contribution to the country’s higher education and research. The university also has various national and international collaborations, including the Hiroshima University of Economics, Kokoro College, Kyushu Medical Sports Vocational School, IIT Madras, CISCO, and many others.

Does Jain University conduct its own entrance examination?

Yes, the Jain Entrance Test, commonly known as JET, is conducted by Jain University for shortlisting applicants for admission to its various programmes.

Does Jain University have a management quota?

Yes, Jain University offers admission through the management quota to eligible candidates.

When was Jain University established?

The Jain University was established in the year 2011.

Does Jain University have any rankings?

Apart from the NIRF rankings, Jain University has also been given high positions by ither ranking agencies over the years. In 2019, India Today placed Jain University in the 16th position among the top 50 BBA colleges in India. In the same year, Jain University ranked 11th among the top 66 BBA private institutions in India which was conducted by the Times of India. It has also featured in the QS World University Rankings in the bracket of 91 - 95 among the top Indian universities.

What is the NIRF ranking of Jain University?

The Jain University was given the 85th position in the rankings of NIRF 2020 (National Institutional Ranking Framework) overall. Apart from that, its Faculty of Engineering and Technology secured the 117th rank all over India.

Why is Jain University not AICTE approved?

The jain University functions as a university and hence, it does not require the approval of the AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education). It has been recognised by the UGC which suffices for its authenticity and approval of the government to teach engineering programmes.

Is Jain University accredited by NAAC?

Yes, Jain University has received accreditation by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).

Are scholarships provided at Jain University?

Yes, Jain University has many scholarship schemes which are based on different criteria. The students of Jain University can avail the scholarships under a particular category if they qualify all the conditions listed for it.

Is Jain University funded by the government?

No, Jain University is a private university, therefore the government does not provide for its funds.

What are the documents required for admissions at Jain University?

The documents required for final admission at Jain University are the JET scorecard and hall ticket, Class 10 mark sheet, Class 12 mark sheet. transfer certificate from the last institution studied, migration certificate, copy of Aadhaar Card, copy of passport and visa (for international applicants), and 5 recent passport and 5 stamp-size photographs in formal dress.

Does Jain University have any notable alumni?

Some of the notable alumni of Jain University are KL Rahul (Indian Cricketer), Pankaj Advani (World Billiards Champion), Ambi Subramaniam ( Renowned Violinist) among others.

Does Jain University offer scholarships?

Jain University offers scholarships under two categories - academics and sports. Both of these Jain University scholarships are aimed at encouraging its students to excel in all sectors of life. The JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) Scholarship Committee (JUSC) reviews all the scholarship programmes available at the university. It also reserves the right to introduce any new Jain University scholarships or cease award of scholarships at any time. Students can get the Jaon University scholarship application from the university’s admission office.

Is Jain University Bangalore approved by UGC?

Yes, Jain University is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC). Moreover, UGC granted the university the status of a Deemed-to-be-university in 2004. The university has also been accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an A grade and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), the Association of Commonwealth Universities, the Association of Universities of Asia and the Pacific (AUAP), and the network of International Business and Economics Schools (NIBES).

Jain University Campus Infrastructure and Facilities

All the facilities available at the campus are modern and upgraded. Furthermore, the university campus is well maintained and clean. Jain University Scholarships

Jain University Bangalore scholarships are offered under several categories. The university offers merit based, need based, sports etc. scholarships. In order to avail the scholarships, students must meet the eligibility criteria determined by the university. The scholarships are offered to a limited number of students ever year. The detailed information about Jain University scholarships is given below. 

  • Medha: The Jain University Bangalore Medha Scholarship is provided under the Academics category. It is awarded for the first year of university and may be offered for the next year as well if the student meets the requirements for continuation. Students eligible for this scholarship must be Indian citizens.
  • Ekalavaya: The subsequent scholarship offered, is the Jain University Bangalore Ekalavaya scholarship given to students between the ages of 17 and 21. It is offered under the sports category to students who have passed class marks in the qualifying examination and are Indian citizens. 
  • Natyaswara: The Jain University Bangalore Natyaswara scholarship is provided under the music and dance category. Candidates eligible should be Indian citizens and must have passed class marks in the qualifying examination. This scholarship is also awarded for the first university year.
  • Atharva: This Jain University Bangalore scholarship is provided in the literary category to students who are Indian citizens. All candidates must have passed class marks in the qualifying examination.
  • Dyumna: The Jain University Bangalore Dyumna scholarship is given to students under the specially challenged category. All the eligible candidates must have passing marks in the qualifying exam and must be Indian citizens. All candidates must present medical proof in support of their disability.
  • Samasti: The Jain University Bangalore Samasti scholarship is offered under the community service category. It is given to students who have first-class marks in the qualifying examination and those who are Indian citizens.
