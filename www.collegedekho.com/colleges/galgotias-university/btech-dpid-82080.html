About Galgotias University BTech

Coming with a duration of 3-4 years, the Galgotias University BTech is designed to give students a strong base in engineering fundamentals, with solid practical knowledge. Over 32 specialisations are available under the BTech programme, while the fees range from INR 3.33L to INR 6.99 lakhs for the whole duration. The curriculum blends theory, hands-on labs, internships and projects so students gain real problem-solving skills. A minimum of 50% is required in 10+2 PCM, along with a valid scorecard of CUET UG/JEE Main/SAT for Galgotias University BTech admission.

Galgotias University BTech Course and Fees

Galgotias University BTech programme spans four years, integrating NBA-accredited core modules with 26 specialised tracks like AI/ML and robotics to fuel careers in Noida's booming tech landscape. The Galgotias University BTech fees are INR 6.99 lakhs for the whole duration, covering tuition and labs. Admission to the BTech requires a minimum of 50% in Class 12 PCM, along with the scorecards of JEE Main/CUET UG/SAT. 

  • Cost Efficiency: The annual tuition fee is INR 1.54 lakhs, coupled with INR 20,000 as the exam fee per year.
  • Uniform Pricing: No different fees are there for high-demand tracks like cybersecurity, machine learning, etc.
  • Scholarship Integration: Scholarships are automatically calculated after admission, giving 25% to 100% fee waivers to students who score 90% or more in Class 12 or secure high ranks in entrance exams. Around 30% of students in the 2025 batch are expected to benefit from this.
  • BTech lateral entry fees are INR 3.33 lakhs for whole duration.

Galgotias University BTech Fee Structure 2026

The Galgotias University BTech fee structure includes everything related to the tuition, such as the tuition fee, registration fee and exam fee. However, the BTech fees vary depending on the programme type. For the regular BTech, which runs for 4 years, the annual tuition fee stands at INR 1.54 lakhs, while for the working professionals, the fees are INR 90K per year. For the meritorious students, Galgotias University scholarships are available. 

  • The fee structure includes INR 20K exam fees annually.
  • A registration fee of INR 3K is included and charged one time only.
  • 25-100% fee waivers are available for students with over 90% in 10+2 and all the toppers of all boards, and entrance exams

Galgotias University BTech Eligibility Criteria 2026

The Galgotias University BTech eligibility requires at least 50% in Class 12 with PCM and English (50% for SC/ST). There is no age limit for the candidates. Along with the required criteria, the candidates also must have a valid scorecard of CUET UG/JEE Main/SAT. The working professionals can take admission via lateral entry in the 2nd year and require a minimum of 50% in diploma/BSc/BVoc/DVoc, coupled with 10+2 with PCM.

  • Core Subjects: PCM in 10+2 is mandatory; optional subjects can include Chemistry/CS/IT/Biology.
  • Lateral Entry: Minimum 50% in BSc/3-year diploma, along with minimum 1 year of full-time/regular work experience.

Galgotias University BTech Admission Process 2026

Galgotias University follows a simple and clear admission pathway for its BTech programmes, where students must first ensure they meet the required Class 12 eligibility with PCM subjects. The university then prepares its merit list by giving preference to applicants who have appeared for CUET-UG, JEE Main or SAT. 

Once applications are reviewed and candidates are shortlisted, they receive further instructions for verification and final fee submission to secure their admission. Learning the correct process of Galgotias University admission will help the students to correctly fill in the details without making mistakes, which may lead to cancellation of candidatures.

  • Admission is based on merit, with preference for CUET UG/JEE Main/SAT scores.
  • The online application form must be submitted through the official website.
  • Shortlisted candidates are informed via registered email/SMS.
  • Final admission is confirmed only after document verification and fee payment.
