About Rai University

Rai University is a private university located in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, established in 2012 by the Government of Gujarat. It is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). Rai University also holds membership with the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) and offers programs approved by statutory councils such as the Bar Council of India (BCI) for law and the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) for pharmacy programs.

Rai University offers 25+ courses in UG, PG, Diploma and PhD levels in Engineering, Management, Science, Law, Pharmacy, Design, and allied disciplines. Some popular courses are B.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Com, MBA, MCA and other programs. Rai University admission is based on qualifying marks in state-level and national-level entrance exams. GUJCET and JEE Main, which are accepted for B.Tech, CAT, MAT and CMAT are accepted for MBA and CLAT are accepted for Law. 

For the 2025 placement season, 50+ companies visited the campus. The highest package offered was Rs. 9.20 lakh per annum, while the average package was Rs. 4.20 lakh per annum. Some of the top recruiters included Indian Oil Corporation Ltd, Bharti Airtel, Tata Motors, and HDFC Bank.

Rai University, Ahmedabad Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

  • Library – The library of Rai University Ahmedabad is quite extensive, with more than 30,000 books and international journals. The university also has a wide reading area that can accommodate more than 200 students at a time. The library is also digitized, making it easy to find any books. Students can also take e-books which they can read on their own devices. Most of the books in the library are kept in good condition, and students can borrow a book for 3 days at a time. The library also gets many newspapers which the students and teachers can refer to. There is a separate section in the library that is fully air-conditioned, and this is only available during a specific time of the day.
  • Research Center – Rai University campus also has a  research and development centre with modern equipment which is quite helpful for PhD candidates. This research centre is always supervised by a lab assistant who helps the students get all the required instruments at any time. The students need to pre-book the research center for their work as not more than 10 people can work simultaneously in the research center. 
  • Laboratories – Rai University has Laboratories for many faculties. The Law department has a Moot court, which gives a real-life court vibe, which helps the students. The School of Pharmacy, recognised by PCI (Pharmacy Council of India), has a developed and modern Pharmaceutics lab and one Machine room. There are many types of equipment like vacuum pumps, capsule counters, tincture presses, colony counters, Ph meters, autoclaves, and many others. A developed computer lab helps students to code and learn networking. The mechanical engineering students have a workshop with steam boilers, power transmission, and conversion systems.

Events and Clubs at Rai University

Rai University offers many student clubs and opportunities for its students to maintain their curriculum activities with extracurricular ones.
