About Delhi Metropolitan Education

Delhi Metropolitan Education is a premium educational institute located in Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Its affiliation with the Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University and approval from the Bar Council of India is a testament to its excellence in the field of higher education. DME is equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure and strong academic facilities that help in rendering a dynamic ground for success. 

The founders of Delhi Metropolitan Education have been involved in the field of education for the last 17 years. DME aims to create an inspirational setting where all the global standards of learning are met and ethical and professional practices adhere. The integrated programs at DME aim at creating professionals for the industry and using innovative teaching methods to enhance the skills of all the students. 

DME, Noida has 3 Schools and 4 Academic Programmes as given below:

  • DME Law School: BA.LLB and BBA.LLB (Integrated Programs)
  • DME Media School: BA (JMC) (Bachelors in Journalism and Mass Communication)
  • DME Management School: BBA (Bachelors in Business Administration)

Delhi Metropolitan Education Courses and Admission 2026

Delhi Metropolitan Education courses include four programmes across three specialisations at the undergraduate level. DME Noida courses are offered under the following degrees: BJMC, BBA, and LLB. The institute has some of the best faculty members and syllabi offering a comprehensive approach to the curriculum. Delhi Metropolitan Education courses integrate theory and practical knowledge in the most seamless manner to provide students with the best quality of education. Delhi Metropolitan Education fees range from Rs 1,38,000 to Rs 1,50,570.

Delhi Metropolitan Education admission is done based on merit from the last qualifying exam and valid entrance test scores. CET, CUET and CLAT are the exams accepted for admission to the various UG programmes offered here. Candidates must meet the eligibility criteria for their desired programmes in order to secure admission. The application process for Delhi Metropolitan Education admissions is online and done via the institute's official website.

Delhi Metropolitan Education Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

Delhi Metropolitan Education campus provides students with best-in-class infrastructure and facilities that would help in nurturing their growth. DME focuses on going beyond the bookish knowledge and providing holistic development and education to all students. To meet that belief, the institute hosts cultural and technical activities and encourages students to participate in them.

The Delhi Metropolitan Education campus is equipped with a library that houses over 20,000 books across a variety of objects, an auditorium that provides a platform for the students to showcase their talents, aesthetically designed lecture rooms with air conditioners and overhead projectors. Apart from that, the campus also houses a computer center, seminar hall, amphitheater, moot court, a studio, as well as, a cafeteria for all of the students.
