Parul University Scholarship 2026

Parul University scholarships are available to deserving students at Parul University. Scholarships are awarded to help students with their financial needs. Scholarships in the arts, athletics, and defence are all available to students at the institute. To get financial aid, prospective students must first satisfy the Parul University scholarship requirements.

Scholarship to SC candidates

Income Limit:

  • For boys candidate income limit is Rs 250000/- per year
  • For girls candidate there is no income limit

Amount of Scholarship:

  • Tuition Fee
  • girls candidate having income more than Rs 250000/- is not eligible for tuition Fee. They are eligible for maintenance allowance only.
  • Maintenance allowance in degree course Rs 5500/- per year to boys & girls candidate
  • Maintenance allowance in diploma course Rs 2300/- per year to boys & girls candidate
  • Maintenance allowance in degree course Rs 12000/- per year to boys & girls candidate staying in hostel of institute
  • Maintenance allowance in diploma course Rs 6000/- per year to boys & girls candidate staying in hostel of institute

Scholarship to ST Candidates

Income Limit:

  • For boys candidate whose family income limit is Rs 250000/- per year
  • For girls candidate there is no income limit

Amount of Scholarship:

  • Tuition fee
  • Maintenance allowance in degree course Rs 5500/- per year to boys & girls candidate
  • Maintenance allowance in diploma course Rs 2300/- per year to boys & girls candidate
  • Maintenance allowance in degree course Rs 12000/- per year to boys & girls candidate staying in hostel of institute
  • Maintenance allowance in diploma course Rs 6000/- per year to boys & girls candidate staying in hostel of institute
  • Food bill of Rs 10000/- per year to boys & girls candidate staying in hostel of institute
  • girls candidate having income more than Rs 250000/- per year are not eligible for food bill

Scholarship to NTDNT candidates

Post-metric Parul University scholarships as per the rates prescribed by the government are being awarded to ST (NTDNT) students for prosecuting their studies at post metric, post secondary and post-graduate stages to enable them to complete their studies in recognized educational institutes within the state. Income limit for male female student is Rs 200000/- per year. Parul University scholarship in the form of tuition fee including maintenance allowance & exam fees upto Rs 50000/- is awarded. 

Scheme of Mukhya Mantri Swavlamban Yojna

Parul University scholarship is awarded to the meritorious & needy students who have passed examination of Class 12th in science /general stream or have passed Class 10th examination from the schools of approved board located in Gujarat. Online application is to be submitted on http://mysy.guj.nic.in. Income limit for male & female student is Rs 450000/- per year. Scholarship in the form of tuition fees upto 50% is awarded. Financial assistance for boarding & lodging and equipments & books is granted as prescribed by the Govt.

Special Scholarship Scheme for J&K Students

Special scholarship to the students belonging to Jammu & Kashmir after passing Class 11th or equivalent examination is awarded by the Dept. of Higher Education, Ministry of Human Resource Development, Govt. of India. Income limit for male & female student is Rs 600000/- per year. From the academic year 2015-16 onwards, all the eligible students need to apply online on AICTE’s web portal.
