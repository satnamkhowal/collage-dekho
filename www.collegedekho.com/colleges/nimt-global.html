About NIMT Jaipur

NIMT Global Institute of Management & Technology is situated in Jaipur in Rajasthan state of India. The college is accredited by BCI, AICTE. NIMT Global Institute of Management & Technology offers 25 courses across 7 streams namely Science, Engineering, IT, Management, Arts..Popular degrees offered at NIMT Global Institute of Management & Technology include BSc, B.Tech, BA, B.Com, BCA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, NIMT Global Institute of Management & Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at NIMT Global Institute of Management & Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at NIMT Global Institute of Management & Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
