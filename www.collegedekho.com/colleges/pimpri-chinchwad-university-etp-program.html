Pimpri Chinchwad University Job Ready Degree

Apart from traditional courses, Pimpri Chinchwad University also offers Job ready "Assured" courses at UG and PG level. The courses are BBA in Digital Marketing (ImaginXP) and MBA in Digital Marketing (ImaginXP). The duration of BBA course is two years and MBA is a three years course. The annual fees of the "Assured" courses at Pimpri Chinchwad University is Rs 62,500-1,25,000. Candidates are offered admission on merit basis and the average salary package for Job ready "Assured" courses is Rs 12-15 LPA.

Pimpri Chinchwad University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

Admission in Job ready "Assured" courses at Pimpri Chinchwad University is offered on merit basis. There is no entrance exam for admission. Candidates need to meet the eligibility criteria and pay course fee to secure admission.
