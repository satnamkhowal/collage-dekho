IIT Indore Courses and Fees 2026

IIT Indore offers a total of 15 courses at Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Doctoral levels. These courses span across disciplines such as Engineering, Sciences, and Management. At the UG level, IIT Indore offers popular courses like B.Tech in Computer Science, B.Tech in Electrical Engineering, and B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering. At the PG level, courses like M.Tech in VLSI Design, M.Sc in Physics, and MBA are offered. The college also provides Ph.D. programs in various specializations.

IIT Indore offers specializations such as Defence Technology, Applied Optics, and Electric Vehicle Technology under its engineering programs. The annual IIT Indore fees range from Rs 1.28 lakh to Rs 8 lakh, depending on the type and level of the course.

IIT Indore Courses FAQs

What types of courses does IIT Indore offer?

At the UG level, IIT Indore offers B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech , etc, while at the PG level, IIT Indore offers M.Tech, M.Tech+PhD, MSc+PhD, MSc, MS, MS (Surgery), etc.

Which courses are popular at IIT Indore?

The most popular IIT Indore courses include the B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, M.Tech, M.Tech+PhD, MSc+PhD and MSc programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, M.Tech+PhD, MSc+PhD, MSc, MS, MS (Surgery) , etc. IIT Indore also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does IIT Indore accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at IIT Indore. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is IIT Indore easy to get into?

Admission in IIT Indore courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the IIT Indore Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include GATE, CSIR, UGC, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, JEST, IIT JAM etc.
