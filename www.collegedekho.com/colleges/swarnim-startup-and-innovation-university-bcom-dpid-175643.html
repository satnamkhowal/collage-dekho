Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University B.Com Application Process

Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University B.Com application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Com course at Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University B.Com application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University B.Com application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University B.Com application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University B.Com Application Form.
