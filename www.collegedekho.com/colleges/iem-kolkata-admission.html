Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata Admission 2026: Courses, Eligibility, Application Form, Last Date

Institute of Engineering and Management (IEM) Kolkata admission 2025 is primarily entrance-based for its undergraduate and postgraduate programs. Affiliated with Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), IEM is a private institute accredited with an A grade by NAAC and ranked 151–200 in the NIRF 2023 Engineering category. Candidates can apply for courses like BTech, BBA, BCA, MTech, MBA, MCA, and MSc through online registration and entrance exams such as IEMJEE, WBJEE, JEE Main, CAT, MAT, JEMAT, and WB JECA. The admission process involves appearing for the respective entrance exam, participating in counselling or selection rounds, and document verification. Below are the details for eligibility, selection criteria, entrance exams, application process, and key dates for IEM Kolkata admission 2025.

IEM Kolkata BTech Admission 2025

BTech offers a full-time Bachelor of Technology for 4 years. BTech is offered in specializations including Computer Science and Engineering, CSE (AI and Machine Learning), CSE (IoT and Cyber Security including Blockchain), Electronics and Communication Engineering, Information Technology, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering. IEM BTech admission is based on IEMJEE, WBJEE, or JEE Main scores followed by WBJEEB counselling. The eligibility criteria for BTech admission at IEM is passing 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with a minimum of 70% aggregate marks. IEM Kolkata Admission Process 2025

Step-by-step admission process for IEM Kolkata admission 2025 is as follows:

Step 1: Online Registration

  • Visit www.iemjee.uem.edu.in for IEMJEE or www.iem.edu.in for other courses.
  • Register with a valid email ID and mobile number.

Step 2: Fill out the Application Form

  • Provide personal details, academic qualifications, and entrance exam preferences.
  • Upload scanned copies of the photograph, signature, and required documents.

Step 3: Pay the Application Fee

  • The application fee is INR 2,000 for all courses.
  • Payment modes: Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI.

Step 4: Appear for the Entrance Exam

IEM accepts IEMJEE, WBJEE, JEE Main for BTech; MAKAUT CET, IEMJEE for BBA/BCA; GATE, PGET for MTech; CAT, MAT, JEMAT, IEMJEE for MBA; WB JECA, IEMJEE for MCA; and JAM, PGET, IEMJEE for MSc. Candidates must register and appear for the respective exams.

Step 5: Participate in Counselling or Selection Rounds

  • For BTech: Register for WBJEEB counselling at www.wbjeeb.nic.in.
  • For BBA/BCA: Participate in MAKAUT CET counselling.
  • For MTech: Participate in PGET counselling.
  • For MBA: Attend group discussion and personal interview rounds.
  • For MCA: Participate in WB JECA counselling.
  • For MSc: Attend institute-based counselling.

Step 6: Final Selection

  • For BTech: WBJEEB releases cutoffs (e.g., 2023 WBJEE: 4873–9373 for General; JEE Main: 6198–13293). Seats are allotted based on ranks.
  • For BBA/BCA: Selection based on MAKAUT CET or IEMJEE scores.
  • For MTech: Selection based on GATE/PGET scores.
  • For MBA: Final selection based on entrance score (70%), GD (20%), and PI (10%).
  • For MCA: Selection based on WB JECA or IEMJEE scores.
  • For MSc: Selection based on JAM/PGET/IEMJEE scores.
  • Selected candidates receive confirmation for document verification.

Step 7: Admission Confirmation & Fee Payment

Selected candidates must pay the course fee within the deadline, submit original documents for verification at IEM Kolkata campus (Ashram Building, Salt Lake), and download the admission letter.

IEM Kolkata Admission FAQs

What is the application fee for admission into the Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata?

The applicants have to pay INR 2000/- as application fee for admission into any programme offered at IEM Kolkata.

On what basis is the final admission at IEM Kolkata M.Sc programme offered?

Final admissions at the Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata M.Sc programme is offered on the basis of candidates’ performance in the common entrance test conducted by IEM Kolkata.

Which entrance exam scores are accepted for B.Tech admissions at Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata?

Scores of entrance exams like IEMJEE, JEE Main and WBJEE are accepted for B.Tech admissions at Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata.

Which documents are required while applying for admission into an IEM Kolkata programme?

While applying for admission into any IEM Kolkata programme, the candidates have to produce their class Xth certificate, XIIth certificate, copy of entrance exam scorecard, migration certificate, receipt of application fee payment, recent passport-sized photographs etc.

Is the application process for admission into IEM Kolkata common for all programmes?

Yes, the application process for admission at IEM Kolkata common for all the programmes.

Does the Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata conduct any entrance exam for admission into its programmes?

Yes, the Institute of Engineering and management Kolkata conducts IEMJEE entrance exam for the purpose of admission into its various programmes.
