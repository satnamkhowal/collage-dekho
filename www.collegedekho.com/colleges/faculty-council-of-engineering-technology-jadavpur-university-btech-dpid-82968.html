About Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech

Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech is a 4 Months - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at the Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University Kolkata, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their higher secondary education (10+2) or its equivalent from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate score of 60% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Additionally, they must have qualified in a national-level entrance examination, such as JEE Main or WBJEE, and secure a valid rank. Meeting these requirements is essential for aspiring students to be considered for admission into the B.Tech. program at Jadavpur University Kolkata. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 3,908 - 30,250. Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech Application Process

Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

Jadavpur University Best Review

Overall Jadavpur University is one of the best universities in India in all respects such as study, placements, faculties, infrastructure, etc. In order to take admitted to Jadavpur University in Engineering branches, every student has to give WBJEE entrance exam and they have to score a very good rank to get admission in the top branches there. In order to take admission in the CSE dept. of Jadavpur University one has to score rank under 150 which is very competitive every year. This is one of the best colleges providing very good placements to students every year.

Placement The placement at Jadavpur University is quite extraordinary in terms of both average packages as well as the return on investment. This is one of the top colleges in India providing an average package of 12 lakhs per annum. In the electrical department of Jadavpur University average package goes up to 15 lacks with the highest package of 50 lacks per annum, In the CSE department, the average package is 22 LPA and the highest foreign package goes up to more than one crore. The whole fee structure of college for four years is approx 10000 rupees. This is one of the best colleges in the world providing a very high return on investments in terms of everything.

Infrastructure Jadavpur University is one of the best Universities since the British period. The infrastructure of Jadavpur University is quite good. There are separate buildings for each department and also there is the availability of a library on campus. The campus is evergreen and everyone maintains cleanliness there. There is a big playground on the campus where anyone can come and play their desired games like cricket, football, etc. In that playground, every year a sports competition is also held where students from different places of the country come to play the sports. There are also many canteens on the campus that provide very good quality food at very affordable prices. More ever, there is a lake on the campus which increases the business of the campus. Campus remains open for 24 hours, which makes it beneficial for students.

Faculty Professors of Jadavpur University are quite experienced in their field of domain. Many professors have done their higher studies and Ph.D. from top foreign Universities and from top IITs. In the electrical department of Jadavpur University, there are almost 30-40 professors. Professors teach their subjects to students not only through routine course study but also give industrial training in practical experience in the research domain. They are very friendly with the students and help students with every difficulty in their studies. At Jadavpur University, the faculty-to-student ratio is quite balanced in every department.

Hostel Hostels at Jadavpur University are quite disciplined and maintained. There are two hostels at Jadavpur University, one for boys and another for girls students. The fee structure of the hostel is near about 1000 rupees per month. The food provided in the hostels is quite decent in terms of both the quality and taste of the food. Strict rules are followed in the hostel of Jadavpur University. Students have to fill out the application form to take admitted to the hostels which open every year near January in the Jadavpur University portal.
