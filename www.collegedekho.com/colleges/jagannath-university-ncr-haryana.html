About Jagannath University Bahadurgarh

Jagannath University Bahadurgarh is a private unaided university that was established in May 2013 under under Haryana State Legislature Act No 8 of 2013. The university is situated in Bahadurgarh (Haryana). It is recognised by UGC and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). The university is also approved by several bodies including the Bar Council of India, Council of Architecture, NCTE, and Pharmacy Council of India.

Jagannath University Bahadurgarh is spread across an area of over 26 acres at Bahadurgarh Jhajjar Road, NCR, Haryana. The indoor and outdoor facilities of the university include lecture halls, tutorial rooms, computer center, labs, library, seminar rooms, conference halls, canteen, transport, etc. The university offers separate hostel facilities for both boys and girls. The Jagannath University NCR Haryana hostel fee is Rs 85,000 per semester with a refundable deposit of Rs 5,000.

Jagannath University Haryana courses list includes diploma, UG, PG, and PhD programmes in engineering, education, science, pharmacy, and other streams. The university offers a total of 28 programmes out of which some of the popular courses include BTech, BSc, BPharm, MSc, MTech, MBA, etc. Under BTech programmes, specialisations like mechanical engineering, chemical engineering, computer science engineering, etc. are offered. The university also offers a unique array of market-integrated courses, called Job Ready “Assured” programmes. Jagannath University Bahadurgarh Job Ready Degrees include BCA, BBA, BDes, BTech, MCA and MBA.

Jagannath University Bahadurgarh course admissions are done either on the basis of merit or entrance exams. Entrance exams accepted for admission include JEE Main for BTech, CAT/ MAT for MBA, GATE for MTech, etc. The university also conducts its own entrance test, the Jagannath University Entrance Test (JUEE) for admission. Selection to other courses is based on merit in the qualifying exams. Candidates can apply for JU admission in online or offline modes.

Jagannath University Bahadurgarh Courses and Admission 2026

Jagannath University courses are offered at diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and PhD levels. The courses are available in various streams including engineering & technology, law, management and commerce, education, sciences, medical & allied sciences, etc. Some of the popular courses offered by Jagannath University include BTech, BCA, BBA, BArch, BA LLB, Bed, MEd, MTech, MBA, MSc, BPharm, DPharm, etc. Jagannath University Bhadurgarh fee for BTech programme is Rs 1,40,000 annually. Meanwhile, Jagannath University fee for MTech programmes is Rs 1,10,000 per year. All of these courses are available in regular mode only.

Jagannath University Bhadurgarh admission to its courses is done either on the basis of qualifying exam merit or through an entrance exam. For BTech admission at Jagannath University Bhadurgarh, candidates need to qualify for the JEE Main exam followed by a personal interview. MTech admission is done through GATE exam score and personal interview. Some other entrance exams accepted for Jagannath University admission include NET/ SLUET/ JUEE for MTech, XAT/ CMAT/ CAT/ JUEE for MBA, and LSAT/ CLAT/ AI LET/ JUEE for BA LLB. The university also conducts its own entrance test, the Jagannath University Entrance Test(JUEE) for admission. Selection to other courses is based on merit in the qualifying exams.

Jagannath University NCR Haryana offers B.tech Lateral Entry (LEET) courses to students who want to pursue B.tech after their diploma. B.tech Lateral Entry (LEET) is different from regular B.tech in terms of duration. B.tech Lateral Entry course is basically a 3 years professional UG course for students who have done a 3-year Diploma in any technical field right after class 10th. 

Jagannath University NCR Haryana LEET course or b.tech lateral entry for diploma holders helps diploma students shape their careers in the best possible way by training them to meet industry standards. As there is no academic difference between regular B.tech and B.tech Lateral Entry (LEET) courses, lateral entry students get the same exposure as regular B.tech students to the technologies and their applications.  

Eligibility Criteria For B.Tech LEET

  • Students seeking admission in the B-Tech lateral entry - LEET program must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 45% in a Diploma in Engineering.
  • Students are required to qualify for the entrance test conducted by SAITM - SAJEE to get admission in B-Tech LEET at Jagannath University NCR Haryana.
