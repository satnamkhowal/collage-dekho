About Mohan Babu University

Mohan Babu University, established by the Andhra Pradesh State Legislative Assembly as a state-private university, originated from the prestigious Sree Vidyanikethan Engineering College. It has its roots in the esteemed history of the Sree Vidyanikethan Educational Trust (SVET), guided by the distinguished Dr. M Mohan Babu. The university has been ranked in the 201-300 band in the NIRF Rankings and achieved a noteworthy position in the 51-100 band for NIRF Innovation Ranking. Its academic prowess is further validated by the coveted Diamond band in the QS-IGauge. The university holds prestigious accreditations, including NAAC A+ and NBA Accredited Programs, with a platinum category distinction in the AICTE-CII survey.

In the esteemed SII Green Rankings of 2023, Mohan Babu University secured its position among the top 20 universities in India. The university's strong ties with over 110 MNC recruiters facilitated an impressive 1800+ placement offers for students during the academic year 2023-24.

During Mohan Babu University placements, two students secured lucrative INR 60 LPA offers from Google, one student received an INR 44 LPA offer from Amazon, and another landed a prestigious offer from Yugabyte. Notably, four students were offered INR 29 LPA by Amazon, underscoring the exceptional qualifications of Mohan Babu University graduates.

The University has collaborations with top 100 universities worldwide, including the Penn State University(USA), University of Wisconsin (USA) and RWTH Aachen (Germany). These international partnerships facilitate student exchange, study abroad and joint degree programs, enhancing the overall educational experience.

Facilities at Mohan Babu University include a central library with an extensive collection of 142,512 volumes, 21,504 titles, and 8,125 journals. The campus, spread across 100 acres and secured with CCTV surveillance, features future-ready labs and a 5-star rated hostel, creating an ideal environment for learning.

Embracing a research-driven teaching pedagogy, Mohan Babu University is exemplified through initiatives like the V-hub Innovation Centre, AICTE-IDEA Lab and 30+ research labs. The university has a publication of 175+ patents and over 1500+ papers by faculties indexed in Scopus/WoS. Moreover, the vibrant campus life at Mohan Babu University is further enriched by 65+ hobby clubs. Below are the Mohan Babu University highlights:

Mohan Babu University Admission Process 2025

MBU admission process begins with applying to the course via the MBU official website followed by appearing for the selection process and then receiving the final admission offer. The stepwise detailed MBU admission process is given below:

Step 1: MBU Application Process

Candidates seeking admission at Mohan Babu University need to apply for their desired course through the MBU official website before the application deadline. The candidates need to pay the MBU application fee amounting to INR 500, which is non-refundable.

Step 2: MBU Selection Rounds

Aspirants need to appear for the entrance exam MBU accepts for admission to a particular course. Mohan Babu University conducts MBUCET for admission to BTech courses. Candidates appearing in the exam must note that MBUCET syllabus is based on Class 11 and Class 12 topics.

Admission to Mohan Babu University non-entrance-based courses will begin by registering and filling up the application form. Candidates will be then shortlisted after evaluating the application form and documents submitted

Step 3: MBU Final Admission

Selected candidates need to pay the admission confirmation fee to secure their seats

Documents Required for Admission at Mohan Babu University

  • 10th marksheet and certificates.
  • 12th marksheet and certificates.
  • Graduation marksheet, certificates, and admit card.
  • Entrance exams scorecard
  • SC/ST/ OBC certificate (if applicable).

Q Does Mohan Babu University provide scholarships?

Yes, Mohan Babu University offers scholarships to its students. MBU scholarships are merit-based. Candidates who have qualified for MBUCET Phase 1 will get a scholarship of Rs 50,000 which will be a fee waiver when paying the first-year fees. Similarly, for those candidates who clear MBUCET Phase 2, the scholarship amount is Rs 25,000.

Q How is the infrastructure of Mohan Babu University?

Mohan Babu University's infrastructure comprises smart classrooms with smart boards and LCD projectors, a digital library, a career development cell, a cafeteria, sports facilities, and transport facilities. It has a total of 64 fully modernised laboratories.

Q Is Mohan Nabu University a private college?

No, Mohan Babu University is not a private institution. The institute received a State-Private University status. Mohan Babu University became a State-Private University institution in the academic year 2010-11. It has received an A+ grade from NAAC with a CGPA of 3.47.
