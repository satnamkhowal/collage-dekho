About MEC Bikaner

The Marauder Engineering College was set up by Marudhar Education Society in 2013. Marudhar Education Society is the first self-funded technical institute in the Bikaner district. The campus of the Marauder Engineering College is located on the NH 11 Jaipur Route, Raisar, Bikaner, Rajasthan. Marauder Engineering College is one of the top engineering colleges in Rajasthan, which offers quality education to its students in the field of engineering. Marudhar Engineering College motto is to produce qualified candidates in the field of engineering & technology.

Marudhar Engineering College offers several courses in B.Tech and M.Tech program and each of them provides theoretical and practical knowledge to its students. The college also has highly experienced faculty members who play an important role in the development of highly skilled and trained engineers. In addition to academic skills, the college also instils social responsibility to its students.

Marudhar Engineering College has an excellent campus, spread over 135 acres of land with all the latest infrastructure facilities and facilities. The Marudhar Engineering College Library is fully air-conditioned with a wide range of texts and reference books written by famous Indians and foreign experts. The Institute holds two centralized air-cooled seminars for various activities with the latest audio-visual electronic devices. Besides this, the Institute also provides several other facilities such as Wifi, Cafeteria, Sports, Health & Care Service, Transport, Hostel for Boys and Girls, etc.

The college also conducts various activities to bring out the hidden talents of students. Some of the activities include workshops, conferences, cultural events, technical fest, national and international seminars etc. Students are encouraged to participate in various competitions off-campus so that they get the necessary exposure. 

As far as the placements concerned, Marudhar Engineering College was remarkable. Marudhar Engineering College Placement Cell is managed by highly qualified experts and professionals who support students in all areas. Under the placement cell, students are trained in a variety of skills so that they can be hired by different companies with lucrative salary packages.
