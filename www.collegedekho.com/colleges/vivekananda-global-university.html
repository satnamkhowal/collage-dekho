About Vivekananda Global University

Vivekananda Global University (VGU), Jaipur, established in 2012 under Rajasthan Government Act No. 11/2012, is one of India’s fastest-growing private universities with NAAC A+ accreditation earned in its very first cycle — making it the youngest university in India to achieve this distinction. Recognized by UGC, AICTE, BCI, and ICAR, VGU hosts over 7,500 students from 28 states and 16 countries across its diverse academic programs. The university offers industry-integrated education through 30+ advanced labs supported by Microsoft, IBM, and Bosch, and operates a vibrant startup ecosystem with 200+ ventures incubated on campus.

For 2026 admissions, VGU provides over 80 undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across disciplines such as engineering, design, management, agriculture, law, architecture, and humanities. Annual fees range from ₹90,000 for BBA and BA to ₹2.2 lakh for B.Tech and B.Des, following the 2025–26 official fee structure. Admissions are based on merit or national-level exams such as JEE, NATA, CUET, CAT, and VGU CET, depending on the program.

The university recorded 700+ global placements in 2025 with top recruiters like Deloitte, TCS, Accenture, and Infosys, and offers a 100% placement assistance guarantee with refund policy if no placement is secured. Over 7,000 internships provide hands-on exposure, with stipends reaching up to ₹50,000 per month. Scholarships worth ₹5 crore are awarded annually based on merit, sports, and financial need. With a green campus, modern hostels, and international tie-ups, VGU blends affordability, innovation, and employability to empower students for global success.

Vivekananda Global University Admission Process for 2026

Admissions for 2026 open in November 2025 via VGUCET (university entrance test) or national exams like JEE Main for B.Tech, CAT for MBA, NATA for B.Arch, and CUET for UG/PG. Application on vgu.ac.in/admission involves INR 2,000 fee, document upload (10+2 marksheet, ID proof), and counseling based on merit. Important dates: VGUCET Phase 1 in January 2026, results by February, classes start July 2026. No specific cutoffs released; selection on 45-50% qualifying marks plus entrance score. Over 5,000 seats available with 29% reserved for Rajasthan domicile.

Detailed steps:

  • Register online at vgu.ac.in.
  • Appear for VGUCET (free for merit scholarships).
  • Attend counseling with originals.
  • Pay fees to confirm seat.

Vivekananda Global University FAQs

Is hostel accommodation available at VGU Jaipur?

Yes. On-campus hostels are available for both boys and girls at VGU.

Which popular courses are offered by VGU Jaipur?

VGU Jaipur offers popular courses like MBA, B.Tech, B.Sc Agriculture, M.Tech, BBA, MCA, BCA, and more.

Are scholarships provided to the students at VGU Jaipur?

Yes. VGU offers a number of merit-based and alumni-based scholarship to its students. Under these scholarships, students are provided a tuition fee waiver provided that they obtain and maintain a certain aggregate score or CGPA in their respective eligible courses.

Is there any entrance exam for admission to VGU Jaipur?

VGU Jaipur conducts VGUCET for admission to various courses offered by the university. Besides, the university also accepts national-level entrance exams like JEE Mains, MAT, XAT, CAT, CUET, and UCEED.
