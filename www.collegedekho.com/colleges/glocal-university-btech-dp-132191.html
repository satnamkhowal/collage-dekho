About Glocal University B.Tech

Glocal University B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Glocal University Saharanpur, aspiring students must meet certain eligibility criteria. Candidates must have successfully completed their 10+2 examination from a recognized board with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they must have studied Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Technical Vocational as an optional subject. A minimum aggregate score of 50% in the qualifying examination is required for general category students, while a relaxation of 5% is provided for SC/ST candidates. Admissions are based on merit, and candidates may also need to appear for entrance exams like JEE Main or Glocal University Entrance Test. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 55,000 - 1,30,000. Glocal University B.Tech Fees

Glocal University B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Glocal University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Glocal University B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Glocal University B.Tech Application Process

Glocal University B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Glocal University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Glocal University B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Glocal University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Glocal University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Glocal University B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Glocal University B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Glocal University B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Glocal University B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
