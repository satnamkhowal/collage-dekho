About Chandigarh University Admission 2026

Chandigarh University admission 2026 is open through CUCET for undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. The last date to apply for CUCET 2026 Phase 1 was June 30, 2026, with the exam held till July 01, 2026. CUCET 2026 Phase 2 registration opens on July 01, 2026, with the last date to apply being August 20, 2026, giving candidates who missed Phase 1 another opportunity to apply. Chandigarh University offers admission to 200+ programmes, including BE, BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA, B.Pharmacy, B.Arch, BBA+LLB, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Pharmacy and PhD. CU admissions are primarily based on the CUCET (Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test), which is held in phases. 

CUCET Phase 1 admissions are held between October and May, while Phase 2 admissions are held between July and August. CUCET is mandatory for admission to BE, B.Pharmacy, Integrated Law, MBA, MCA, LLM, PharmD, M.Pharmacy, MPT, M.Optom and Master of Nutrition & Dietetics. Students scoring 90.01–100 marks in CUCET Phase 1 are eligible for a 100% tuition fee waiver. Chandigarh University also accepts national-level tests such as JEE Main, GATE, CAT, MAT, NATA, and other relevant examinations. For Chandigarh University BE admissions 2026, candidates with 90% marks in Class 12 or a JEE Main rank up to 50,000 are exempted from CUCET.

Chandigarh University Admission Process 2026

Chandigarh University admission process 2026 is entirely entrance-based. Candidates must first clear CUCET (or submit a valid JEE Main/CAT/NATA/NCHMCT JEE score for applicable courses), then complete document verification and fee payment at the campus. The six-step process below applies to all major Chandigarh University courses including BE, MBA, MCA, B.Pharmacy, Integrated Law, BBA, BCA and other CUCET-mandatory programmes. Step-by-step process for how to get admission in Chandigarh University is as follows:

  • Step 1: Register on the CUCET portal at cucet.cuchd.in with name, email, mobile number, state, gender and password. An OTP confirms the profile.
  • Step 2: Pay the application fee of INR 1,000 (INR 500 for the second attempt) via net banking, UPI, credit/debit card or demand draft payable to 'Chandigarh University' at Chandigarh.
  • Step 3: Fill the application form with personal, academic (Class 10, Class 12, graduation, where applicable) and category details. Upload a photograph, signature and Class 12 marksheet.
  • Step 4: Select the CUCET exam date and appear for the 2-hour online remote-proctored test comprising 100 MCQs (1 mark each, no negative marking).
  • Step 5: CUCET results are declared on the same day as the exam in most cases. Minimum 30% is required to qualify. The best score across Phase 1 and Phase 2 is considered.
  • Step 6: Pay 30% of the admission fee within 15 days of the result to confirm the seat and lock in the scholarship slab. Complete document verification at the Mohali or Unnao campus, then pay the balance fee before the academic session begins in August 2026.

Key Points to Remember While Getting Admission at Chandigarh University

  • For BE admissions, candidates with 90%+ in Class 12 or a JEE Main rank within 50,000 are exempt from CUCET and can apply directly through the merit route. 
  • MBA candidates can submit valid CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT scores alongside a CUCET attempt for additional scholarship eligibility. B.Arch applicants must submit a NATA or JEE Main Paper 2 score along with the Chandigarh University application form. 
  • Hotel Management aspirants are selected through NCHMCT JEE merit.

How to Apply for Chandigarh University Admission 2026?

To apply for Chandigarh University admission 2026, candidates must register on the CUCET portal (cucet.cuchd.in) in online mode or purchase an offline application form from the admission office in Sector 36-D, Chandigarh. Online is the preferred mode since it syncs directly with the CUCET exam slot booking. The application fee of INR 1,000 covers registration plus a digital copy of the prospectus.

  • Visit the official CUCET portal of Chandigarh University at cucet.cuchd.in and click on 'Apply Now' or 'Register'.
  • Create a candidate profile by entering name, father's name, email, mobile number, state, gender and password. Verify with the OTP received on mobile/email.
  • Log in with the generated User ID and complete the application form. Select discipline, programme, campus (Punjab or Uttar Pradesh) and CUCET exam date.
  • Upload scanned documents such as passport-size photograph, signature, Class 10 marksheet, Class 12 marksheet (or admit card if appearing) and category certificate if applicable.
  • Pay the application fee of INR 1,000 via net banking, UPI, credit/debit card, HDFC Gateway or PAYU Gateway. Offline candidates can pay via demand draft drawn in favour of 'Chandigarh University', payable at Chandigarh.
  • Submit the form and download the confirmation page. Take a printout for CUCET exam-day verification.

Note: Offline applicants can collect the CUCET Brochure-cum-Form from University Admission Offices or authorised centres by paying INR 1,000. The scratch card code on the offline form must be entered on the CUCET website to activate the profile. The completed offline form, along with the DD, must be sent to: Admission Office, Chandigarh University, SCO 223, First Floor, Sector 36-D, Chandigarh, 160036.

Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test (CUCET) 2026

Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test (CUCET) is an online entrance examination. It is mandatory for admission to undergraduate and postgraduate programs at Chandigarh University, including Engineering (B.E.), Pharmacy, MBA, and Integrated Law. The test is divided into two phases: CUCET-I and CUCET-II. The exam serves two purposes: evaluating admission eligibility and calculating scholarship amounts.

Students who score above 90% in their 10+2 exams or secure a rank within the top 50,000 in JEE are exempt from taking CUCET.

CUCET Examination Pattern 2026

The university conducts CUCET as a remote-proctored test. Students take the exam from home using a computer, webcam, and a stable internet connection.

  • Duration: 120 minutes (2 hours).
  • Question Type: 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Scoring: 1 mark is awarded for every correct answer.
  • Negative Marking: There is no negative marking. Incorrect answers receive 0 marks.

How the CUCET Exam Pattern Works?

The test modules change depending on the student's 10+2 stream. All students must complete an English section, followed by domain-specific subjects related to their background. This ensures students are tested on the exact subjects they studied in high school.

Chandigarh University Admission FAQs

Are hostel and transport fees included in Chandigarh University admission fee?

No, hostel, mess and transport fees at Chandigarh University are separate from the tuition fee quoted in the admission prospectus. The INR 1.68–3.18 lakh annual fee for BE, for example, covers only academic charges (tuition, lab, library, exam, development). Hostel accommodation at the Mohali campus starts at INR 1.00 lakh per year for a 5-seater AC room and goes up to INR 1.80 lakh for premium twin-sharing options, with mess charges of around INR 42,000 per year on top. Transport fees for day scholars range from INR 32,000 to INR 52,000 per year depending on distance. At the UP (Unnao) campus, hostel fees are roughly INR 15,000–20,000 lower across all categories. The university also charges a one-time INR 2,000 security deposit (refundable) and INR 2,000 examination fee at the start of each academic year. All optional add-ons, laptop, uniform, sports kit are charged separately and are not bundled into the admission fee.

When is the last date for Chandigarh University admission 2026?

Registrations for Chandigarh University admission 2026 Phase 1 will close on close on Apri 30, 2026. CUCET Phase 2 registrations will open in May 2026 and continie until the end of admissions (typically August 2026). Candidates who miss Phase 1 can still apply through Phase 2, though the scholarship slabs are lower in Phase 2 (e.g., 80–90 marks gets 40% waiver in Phase 2 vs 50% in Phase 1).

Is CUCET mandatory for Chandigarh University admission?

CUCET is mandatory for 18 programmes at Chandigarh University such as BE, B.Pharmacy, Integrated Law (BA+LLB, BBA+LLB, B.Com+LLB), MBA (all specialisations), MCA, PharmD, M.Pharmacy (Pharmaceutics, Pharmacology, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Industrial Pharmacy), LLM, MPT (Musculoskeletal, Sports), M.Optom, M.Nutrition & Dietetics, MMLT (Clinical Biochemistry, Clinical Microbiology) and BA (Hons.) Psychology. For all other programmes, CUCET is optional but strongly recommended because it unlocks scholarship eligibility. Candidates scoring 90%+ in Class 12 or holding a JEE Main rank up to 50,000 are exempt from CUCET for BE admission and can enter through the merit pathway directly.

How many attempts are allowed in CUCET 2026?

CUCET 2026 allows a maximum of two attempts per phase. Candidates can take the exam up to four times in total (two in Phase 1 and two in Phase 2). The first attempt costs INR 1,000 and the second attempt in the same phase costs INR 500. If a candidate appears in both Phase 1 and Phase 2, the best score across all attempts is considered for admission and scholarship determination. This multi-attempt structure is particularly useful for students who want to improve their scholarship slab, for example, someone who scored 78 in Phase 1 (40% scholarship) can retry in Phase 2 to cross the 80-mark threshold for a higher slab. Phase 1 scholarship slabs are more rewarding, so early and repeated attempts in Phase 1 are strategically better.

What is the Chandigarh University admission process for JEE Main candidates?

JEE Main candidates applying for Chandigarh University BE can skip CUCET if their All-India Rank is within 50,000. The process involves registering at cucet.cuchd.in, selecting the 'JEE Main-based admission' option during application, uploading the JEE Main scorecard and choosing specialisation preferences. The JEE Main scholarship slab depends on the rank: up to 1,000 rank gets 100% fee waiver, 1,001–10,000 gets around 50%, 10,001–25,000 gets 30–40%, and 25,001–50,000 gets 20–25%. Candidates ranked beyond 50,000 must appear for CUCET. The JEE Main pathway also applies to B.Arch (via Paper 2) and does not require the CUCET exam fee, though the standard INR 1,000 registration fee still applies to the Chandigarh University application.

Can I get a 100% scholarship at Chandigarh University?

Yes, a 100% tuition fee waiver is available through CUCET if a candidate scores 90.01 marks or above (out of 100) in either Phase 1 or Phase 2. The waiver covers tuition for the entire programme duration (4 years for BE, 2 for MBA/MCA, etc.) but does not include hostel, mess, transport or one-time charges. In 2024-25, 1,700+ students availed the 100% scholarship at CU. The key conditions for renewal are maintaining 7.5 CGPA each academic year without any E, F or I grades and without a semester drop. JEE Main rank within 1,000 also unlocks 100% fee waiver for BE. MBA aspirants with 95%+ percentile in CAT or equivalent can also receive full-fee waivers. The scholarship is discontinued if the candidate fails to meet the renewal conditions, so consistency matters as much as the entrance score.

What happens after clearing CUCET Chandigarh University?

After clearing CUCET, meaning scoring at least 30% on the 100-mark test, candidates receive an email with their scorecard and merit list status. The next step is seat confirmation, which requires a 30% admission fee deposit within 15 days of the result. The exact amount depends on the course and the scholarship slab earned (fees are calculated net of the scholarship waiver). Candidates must then complete document verification at the Mohali or Unnao campus, where originals of Class 10/12 marksheets, CUCET scorecard, Aadhaar and category certificate are inspected. Once verification is cleared, hostel allocation and orientation follow. The balance fee is payable in instalments before the academic session begins in August 2026. Students who do not pay the 30% within 15 days forfeit their seat to the next candidate in the merit list.

Can NRI and international students apply for Chandigarh University?

Yes, Chandigarh University welcomes NRI and international students and hosts learners from 50+ countries at both campuses. The application process for international applicants runs through cuchd.in/international, not the standard CUCET portal. NRI applicants are exempt from CUCET and are admitted based on Class 12 / Bachelor's marks and equivalency certificates issued by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) for foreign qualifications. The NRI seat quota is separate, typically 15% of the intake in each programme — and the fee structure is higher than the Indian quota, ranging from USD 4,500 to USD 8,000 per year depending on the programme. Required documents include passport, visa, TOEFL/IELTS score (for non-English-medium candidates), equivalency certificate, and financial sponsorship proof. The international admission office can be reached at international@cumail.in.

What is the refund policy if I cancel Chandigarh University admission?

Chandigarh University follows the UGC's refund policy for cancellation of admission. If the cancellation request is submitted 15 days or more before the start of the academic session, the university refunds 100% of the fee paid minus a processing charge of INR 1,000. If cancelled less than 15 days before the session start but before 15 days after the session start, 80% of the fee is refunded. If cancelled 15–30 days after the session start, 50% is refunded. Beyond 30 days after the session start, no refund is processed. The security deposit of INR 2,000 is refundable at the end of the course subject to no dues. The scholarship waiver portion is never refunded since it's a concession, not cash paid. Cancellation requests must be submitted via the Student Facilitation Centre and processed within 30 working days.

Which JEE Main rank is needed for Chandigarh University BTech CSE?

Candidates who are interested in Chandigarh University admission can apply online by visiting the admission portal. The university conducts Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test (CUCET) for admission in UG and PG courses. Along with this JEE Main, NATA, CAT, MAT etc. scores are also accepted. Final selection is based on performance in the entrance examination.Chandigarh University BTech CSE admission through the JEE Main route works on two tiers. For direct admission without CUCET, a rank within 50,000 is the published cutoff. The 2024 closing rank for BTech CSE through JEE Main was 58,140 for General category candidates, which eased from 63,416 in 2023, reflecting the 15% seat hike in recent years. For scholarships, JEE Main rank within 1,000 fetches a 100% tuition waiver, 1,001–5,000 gets around 60%, 5,001–10,000 gets 40–50%, and 10,001–25,000 gets 20–30%. Candidates with ranks between 50,000 and 100,000 should appear for CUCET to qualify for admission and scholarship. Note that JEE Main rank does not apply to other branches with the same generosity, CSE is the most competitive; for Civil, Mechanical, Electrical and biotech branches, the JEE cutoff is more relaxed and CUCET remains the cleaner path.
