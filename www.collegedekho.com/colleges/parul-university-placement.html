Parul University Placement Process 2025

The Parul University placement process is managed by the university's Training & Placement (T&P) Cell. Students willing to participate in campus placements must follow the process outlined below:

  • Registration: Students are required to register with the T&P Cell to become eligible for campus placements.
  • IMPACT Training: A dedicated 40-day IMPACT training program is conducted between Semester 6 and Semester 7 (for B.Tech students). It includes aptitude preparation, coding practice, soft skills development, resume building, mock interviews, and group discussions.
  • Pre-Placement Talks (PPTs): Recruiters conduct PPTs to introduce their company, followed by assessments such as written tests, technical rounds, and HR interviews.
  • Selection Process: The recruitment stages typically include aptitude tests (online/offline), technical interviews, coding rounds (for IT roles), group discussions, and HR interviews.
  • One Student–One Job Policy: The university follows this policy, where students who accept an offer can only apply for a “Dream Company” if the offered package meets the defined eligibility criteria.
  • Offer Letter: Final offer letters are issued to selected candidates through the T&P Cell.

Source: Parul University Placement Policy (Official Website)

Parul University Placements FAQs

How many companies visit Parul University?

Parul University total recruiters in 2024 were 1300. Out of the 1300 recruiters many have signed MoUs with the university to keep visiting every year for offering jobs to students. The recruiters who visit the Parul University campus are from a variety of sectors like IT, management, ONGC, finance, etc.

Does Parul University have 100% placement?

No, Parul University does not have 100% placements. The training and placement cell gives its best to achieve that percentage but in recent years, Parul University has not been able to achieve 100% placements.

Is Parul University good for placements?

Yes, Parul University is good for placement opportunities as in 2024 1300 recruiters visited the campus to offer jobs. Parul University average package in 2024 stood at Rs 4.5 LPA.

What is the Parul University placement package for CSE?

Parul University is yet to release the placement packages for B.Tech CSE in 2024. However, In 2022, the highest package for B.Tech CSE at Parul University stood at Rs 18 LPA.

Who are the top recruiters of Parul University?

Parul University top recruiters are Indian Oil, Synoptek, Informatica, Sophos, Deloitte, Juspay, Metadata, E6data, Nestle, Piramal, Finance, SocialPilot, Reliance and others. Some of these companies come back every year to get fresh graduates from the university campus placements.

What is the placement percentage at Parul University?

According to the Parul University NIRF report 2024, the placement data show that for three-year UG courses, the placement percentage in 2023 was 24.97%. For four-year UG courses the placement percentage was 59.22% while for two-year PG courses, it was 79.76%.

How are the placements at Parul University in 2024?

The Parul University placements 2024 season is good as the highest package increased by 100% compared to 2023. The highest package in 2024 was Rs 45.98 LPA, and in 2023, it was 23 LPA.

What is the average package offered at Parul University?

Parul University average package in 2024 is Rs 4.5 LPA, while in 2023, it ranged from Rs 2.5 LPA to Rs 5 LPA. In 2022, the average package offered at Parul University was Rs 4 LPA.

Is Parul University placements 2024 report out?

Parul University placements 2024 report is now out. According to the report, 2,212 students were placed in 2024 and the total recruiters were 1300.

What is the highest package for Btech in Parul University?

Parul University BTech placement data 2024 is yet to be released. In 2023, the highest package, which was Rs 23 LPA, was achieved by Praful Jogachand, a BTech in Petroleum Engineering student.

What is the highest package in Parul University for MBA?

Parul University MBA highest package has not been released by the university for 2024 or 2023. In 2022, the average package for MBA was Rs 7 LPA while in 2021 the average package for MBA was Rs 3.6 LPA.

What are the Parul University BTech company-wise statistics?

Parul University B.Tech CSE students received packages from Orad Consultancy Pvt Ltd for Rs 18 LPA, E6 Data for Rs 15 LPA, Think 360.ai for Rs 9.58 LPA and Raipay Fintech for Rs 9 LPA. The highest package offered was by Orad Consultancy Pvt Ltd and the lowest package was offered by Raipay Fintech.
