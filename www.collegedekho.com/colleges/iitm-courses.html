IIT Madras Courses FAQs

What types of courses does IIT Madras offer?

At the UG level, IIT Madras offers B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Tech, BS+MS , etc, while at the PG level, IIT Madras offers M.Tech, MS, MBA, MSc, MA, etc.

Which courses are popular at IIT Madras?

The most popular IIT Madras courses include the B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Tech, BS+MS, M.Tech, MS and MBA programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Tech, BS+MS, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MS, MBA, MSc, MA , etc. IIT Madras also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does IIT Madras accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at IIT Madras. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Tech, BS+MS and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is IIT Madras easy to get into?

Admission in IIT Madras courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the IIT Madras Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include JEE Main, JEE Advanced, CAT, IIT JAM etc.
