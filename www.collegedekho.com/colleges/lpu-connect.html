LPU to Host “Experience LPU 2026” in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana

Lovely Professional University (LPU) is set to organize an engaging student outreach event, Experience LPU 2025, in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana this July. Aimed at providing students with an inspiring glimpse into LPU’s vibrant academic culture, the event invites all LPUNEST-qualified candidates, regardless of admission status. LPU Launches Future Ready Programmes in AI, Robotics, and Fintech

LPU has opened admissions for its latest industry-aligned programmes in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Robotics, Machine Learning (ML), and Fintech, addressing the soaring global demand for skilled tech professionals. At the undergraduate level, LPU now offers B.Tech degrees in Computer Science with specialisations in Robotics and AI, Applied AI, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics the latter in collaboration with global AI leader Quantiphi. For postgraduate aspirants, the university has launched MBAs in Data Science and AI, and Fintech and AI, blending management education with cutting-edge technology. These programmes are part of LPU’s “EduRevolution” initiative, a transformative approach to learning that prioritizes skill development, gamification, and student autonomy over traditional methods.

According to Dr. Ashok Kumar Mittal, Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha) and Founder Chancellor of LPU. LPU's mission is to stay ahead of the curve and produce graduates who lead the future. He added that AI, data analytics, and robotics are now core to the global job market, not optional. LPU backs these programmes with high-tech labs and a curriculum that evolves with industry trends. This is also visible in LPU's placements as students Sri Vishnu and Betireddy Naga Vamsi Reddy from the B.Tech Robotics and Automation program recently received record-breaking offers of Rs 2.5 crore and Rs 1.03 crore from a top AI robotics firm.

With the fintech sector projected to reach USD 1.5 trillion by 2030, LPU’s new MBA offerings aim to produce leaders at the intersection of business and technology. Drawing students from across India and over 50 countries, LPU continues to be a global hub for innovation and academic excellence.

LPU Direct Admission 2026: Eligibility, Fees, and How to Apply

Lovely Professional University (LPU) in Punjab offers direct admission, which means a student joins a programme through the university's own admission process rather than through a centralised counselling body. LPU conducts its own entrance-cum-scholarship test, the LPU National Entrance and Scholarship Test, called LPUNEST, and also accepts JEE Main and CUET scores for many programmes. The minimum academic requirement across these routes is 60% aggregate marks in Class 12, or the equivalent for the chosen programme.

Does LPU Accept CAT, MAT, CMAT or XAT for MBA Admission?

Yes Lovely Professional University (LPU) officially accepts CAT, MAT, CMAT, and XAT scores for MBA admission, alongside its own entrance test, LPUNEST. As per LPU's official MBA admission page (Mittal School of Business), a candidate can qualify for MBA admission through "LPUNEST or CAT or XAT or MAT or CMAT", followed by a Video Essay and Interview. This means you do not need to write LPUNEST separately if you already hold a valid score from any of these four national-level management entrance exams.

Should You Write LPUNEST If You Already Have a Good JEE Main Score?

Short answer: It's optional, but usually worth it. LPU allows B.Tech candidates with a valid JEE Main score to skip LPUNEST entirely for admission and scholarship purposes. However, since LPUNEST can independently unlock a higher scholarship bracket than your JEE Main percentile might, and the application fee is only INR 1,000 (INR 500 for female candidates), many students choose to write it anyway as a low-cost way to potentially increase their fee waiver.

LPU Admission Through JEE Main vs CUET: Which Route Should You Choose?

Lovely Professional University (LPU) accepts multiple entrance exams for admission LPUNEST (its own test), JEE Main, and CUET UG, among others. If you've already appeared for JEE Main or CUET, you don't necessarily need to take LPUNEST separately; either score can be used directly for admission and scholarship evaluation. But JEE Main and CUET serve different purposes at LPU, and picking the right one depends on the programme you want.
