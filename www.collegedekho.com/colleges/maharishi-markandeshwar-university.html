About MMU Mullana

Maharishi Markandeshwar University Mullana, was established in 1993 and is a private deemed university located in Mullana, Haryana. The university is recognised by the UGC and accredited with NAAC A++ Grade. MMU Mullana offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programmes in engineering, management, pharmacy, nursing, law, sciences, agriculture, and allied health sciences.

MMU Mullana offers 200+ courses across different fields. Some of the most popular programmes include B.Tech, B.Pharmacy, Pharma-D, B.Sc Nursing, MBA, MCA, and M.Tech. Admission is based on merit or entrance exam scores, depending on the course. The university accepts national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, GATE, CAT, MAT, CMAT, GPAT, and CUET for selected programmes.

MMU Mullana is known for its academic facilities, industry-oriented education, research opportunities, and placement support. The university also provides hostel accommodation, sports facilities, libraries, laboratories, and other campus facilities for students.

MMU Mullana Admission Process 2026

The MMU Mullana admission process is conducted online through the university admission portal. Students must complete registration, fill out the application form, upload the required documents, and participate in the selection process based on merit or entrance exam scores. After selection, candidates need to complete counselling, document verification, and fee payment to secure admission. Follow the steps below to apply for MMU Mullana admission 2026:

  • Visit the official MMU Mullana admission portal and register using your email ID and mobile number.
  • Fill in the application form with your personal, academic, and course details.
  • Upload the required documents, including mark sheets, certificates, photographs, and entrance exam scorecards if applicable.
  • Pay the application fee through the online payment gateway.
  • Appear for the required entrance exam if your chosen course requires one. Merit-based courses do not require an entrance test.
  • Check the merit list or selection list published by the university.
  • Participate in the counselling process and select your preferred course or specialisation.
  • Complete document verification by submitting the original documents for verification.
  • Pay the admission fee or the first instalment of the course fee.
  • Download the admission confirmation letter and complete the admission formalities.

After successful fee payment and document verification, the admission process is completed and students can join the programme as per the university schedule.
