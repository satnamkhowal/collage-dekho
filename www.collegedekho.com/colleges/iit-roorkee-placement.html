IIT Roorkee Placements 2025

According to the IIT Roorkee placement report 2025, 61% placement rate was achieved. A total of 1150 students got placed and a total of 1200 offers were made. The highest package offered during 2024 placements is Rs 40.5 LPA offered to a BTech student. Moreover, the average package offered in 2024 for IIT Roorkee was Rs 23.4 LPA and median package as Rs 19.08 LPA. More than 100 companies visited the campus for placements. Some of the top recruiters for IIT Roorkee Placements are Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, Tata Steel, etc.

IIT Roorkee Placements FAQs

Which Companies Visit IIT Roorkee for Placements?

IIT Roorkee top recruiters include Accenture Japan, American Express, Axis Bank, and Barclays. Further, companies visiting IIT Roorkee including Adobe, EXL, EY, ICICI Bank and Accenture participated in the campus placement drive and hired talented students to be a part of their esteemed organisation.

What is the Placement Percentage at IIT Roorkee?

IIT Roorkee placement percentage has been 100% for the MBA programme for the past three consecutive years. The institute has maintained 100% placement records for the MBA programme. As per the reports, the IIT Roorkee MBA placements for the 2023 session were concluded with 100% placements with the highest package and average package of Rs 27.94 LPA and Rs 18.34 LPA respectively.

How are the placements at IIT Roorkee in 2024?

IIT Roorkee placements 2024 began on December 1, 2023. Phase 1 placements were held for four days and phase 2 is likely to begin in January. As per the phase 1 placement reports, the highest international package was offered by Databricks, a US-based software company for Rs 2.05 crore for an international role. 16 students got international placement offers during IIT Roorkee placements phase 1. The Phase 1 placements were conducted for 4 days and 593 job offers (including PPOs) were offered to the students.

What is the average package offered at IIT Roorkee?

IIT Roorkee average package for the MBA programme in academic sessions 2023, 2022, 2021 and 2020 is made available by the institute. As per the report, the average package offered at IIT Roorkee in 2023 was Rs 18.3 LPA for the MBA programme. Further, the Rs 16.8 LPA was recorded as the average package in 2022 followed by Rs 10.5 LPA (2021) and Rs 10.1 LPA (2020).

What is the highest package offered at IIT Roorkee?

IIT Roorkee highest package 2024 till the phase 1 placement session is Rs 2.05 crore per annum offered by Databricks, a US-based company. The highest package this year is more than 2023's highest package. Further, for the MBA programme, the highest package offered at IIT Roorkee in 2023 was Rs 27.94 LPA and the placement percentage was 100 percent. Moreover, the institute recorded an 8% increase in MBA's highest package in comparison with 2022 placements.

i just attempted a free mock test where will i get the results

A
Apoorva Bali •  Jan-30-2025

The results of any free IIT JAM mock test you attempted will usually be accessible on the same website or platform where you took the test. You can check your results by doing the following:

  • Check the platform or website:

Return to the website where the mock exam was held if it was one of such sites (such as the platform of a coaching centre or an educational website). Use the login information you used to register to access your account, if you have one. Look for a section that says "My Results," "Test History," or "Dashboard," since these might include your mock test results and feedback.

  • Instant Results:

Results from a lot of free practice exams are available right away. If so, see if your score appeared on the platform immediately after you finished the test.

  • Notification via Email:

After you finish the test, some platforms send you an email with your findings. Look for any correspondence on the outcomes of your practice exam in your inbox (and spam folder).

  • Speak with Support:

You can get in touch with the helpdesk or support staff of the website or platform where you took the test if you are unable to locate your results. They ought to be able to help you find your score.
