LPU Job Ready Degree

LPU offers one Job Ready course that trains and readies the students to acquire jobs after completion of their degree. The B.Sc in Multimedia & Animation (IXP) course is available for the duration of 3 years. The job-assured course at LPU i.e. B.Tech in UX (IXP) is available to the candidates at an annual fee of Rs 3,00,000. Admission to these courses is offered on the basis of entrance test such as LPUNEST or CUET UG as well as personal interview. The average salary package offered by LPU for the Job Ready course ranges between Rs 12 Lakhs per annum and Rs 15 Lakhs per annum. There are scholarships available at the university for the assured course students who meet the eligibility criteria as well.

LPU Job Ready Courses 2025 - Admissions 

To become eligible for the only Job Ready “Assured” course at LPU i.e. B.Tech in UX (IXP), interested candidates must qualify in LPUNEST or CUET UG. They additionally have to appear for a personal interview round at the university to become eligible for the course. If the candidate is shortlisted, then the candidates can pay their course fee and start their education.
