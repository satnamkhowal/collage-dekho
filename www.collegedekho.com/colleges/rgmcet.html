About RGMCET

Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering and Technology also known as RGMCET Kurnool, was established in the year 1995. It is a private, autonomous college situated in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh. The college is recognised by UGC and has been approved by AICTE. RGMCET Kurnool has been accredited by NAAC with an “A+” grade as well as NBA and has been affiliated with JNTUA Anantapuram. The RGM College of Engineering and Technology campus is spread over an area of 32.04 acres. Till now, the college has been bestowed with more than 160 awards. There are more than 200 faculty members at the college.

The RGMCET Kurnool courses are offered at undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college are B.Tech in Civil Engineering, B.Tech in CSE, M.Tech in Embedded Systems, M.Tech in Software Engineering, MBA and MCA. All the courses at RGMCET Kurnool are offered in full-time mode to interested candidates. The RGM College of Engineering and Technology admission to the B.Tech courses is offered based on the performance of candidates in entrance exams like AP EAMCET, AP PGECET or GATE (for M.Tech course) and AP ICET (for MBA & MCA courses) and marks scored in the last qualifying exam. 

On the campus of RGMCET Kurnool, the students have access to a wide range of amenities, including a gym, boys' and girls' hostels, laboratories, a sports complex, transportation options, banking options, a full-fledged library, a canteen, and others. A specialised placement cell organises the RGMCET Kurnool placements each year to allow graduating students to land the job of their choosing before even finishing their studies. The highest salary package offered to the students was Rs 17 Lakhs per annum and the lowest salary package offered to the students was Rs 1.44 Lakhs per annum in the RGMCET Kurnool placement drive 2023.

RGMCET Courses and Admission 2026

The RGMCET Kurnool courses are available to interested individuals at both the undergraduate and graduate levels. There are a total of 27 Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering and Technology courses available to interested candidates in the engineering, management, and computer science fields. The duration of all the RGMCET Kurnool UG courses is four years, and the duration of the RGMCET Kurnool PG courses is two years. B.Tech. in Civil Engineering, M.Tech in Computer Science, M.Tech. in Machine Design, MBA, and MCA are some of the popular courses offered by RGMCET Kurnool. The institution accepts a variety of entrance tests to grant admission to various courses at RGMCET Kurnool such as AP EAMCET, GATE, AP PGECET, AP ICET. The RGM College of Engineering and Technology fees for various courses varies according to demand and the number of available seats.

The Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering and Technology Kurnool admission is offered for undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral courses. Candidates who have cleared the AP PGECET or GATE for M.Tech, AP ICET for M.B.A. and M.C.A., and AP EAMCET for B.Tech are eligible to participate in the seat allocation counselling. Seats are assigned to candidates during the AP EAMCET counselling process for the B.Tech course, AP PGECET counselling for the M.Tech course, and AP ICET counselling for MBA and MCA courses, in accordance with the RGMCET Kurnool admission criteria. After the seats have been assigned, candidates must submit the required documents and pay the fees to secure the seats of their choice of course for RGMCET Kurnool admission.

RGMCET Placements

A placement officer, one faculty member, and one student from each department comprise a training & placement cell at RGMCET Kurnool. Their responsibility is to oversee efficient functions that provide positive results. The committee plans training programmes and activities for its students at the start of each academic year in order to accommodate placements. For the purpose of training opportunities for RGMCET Kurnool placements, they organise numerous seminars, and sessions led by college graduates, or by other industry professionals. For the final-year students, the RGMCET Kurnool placement process is carried out in a variety of pre-placement internships, awareness programmes, training sessions, and campus interviews to enhance the student's confidence so they may perform well in interviews. More than 70 of the best recruitment firms from various industrial sectors visit the campus each year for job placement. TCS, WIPRO, Infosys, WINT, CMS, ACT, Just Dial, Invensis, Raam Group, Hexaware, and other companies are among the top recruiters participating in the RGMCET Kurnool placement drive.

RGMCET Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The facilities at Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering and Technology campus include boys' and girls' hostels, a library, a sports court, a fitness centre, a cafeteria, an auditorium, a hospital, wi-fi, IT support, transportation, laboratories, a bank, etc. The hostels have nice furniture and good ventilation. The college offers a wide range of indoor and outdoor activities, including chess, carrom, badminton, table tennis, tennis, football, basketball, hockey, cricket, and volleyball. The field that is kept exclusively for outdoor activities is away from the educational building. Facilities at the campus of RGMCET Kurnool also include a gym, a bank, and other services. Moreover, there is a 24-hour ATM facility.

  • Library: The RGMCET Kurnool campus has a complete collection of reference books and textbooks in its own library. The pupils can benefit much from their digital library resources.
  • Hospital: The college has a hospital for any medical emergencies that might arise among the personnel, faculty, and students. You can obtain regular checkups or any other medical requirements there.
  • Canteen: A sizable canteen that operates on the RGM College of Engineering and Technology campus offers sanitary food to the faculty and staff at a much less price.
  • Sports complex: Sports facilities are available at the college to support the both physical and emotional health of the students. They have separate badminton courts, cricket fields, football fields, and table tennis courts to encourage the students to participate in various indoor and outdoor sports.
  • Hostel: Boys' and girls' hostels are available at the campus of Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering and Technology.

RGMCET Scholarships

The RGMCET Kurnool scholarships include the scholarship for the 4-year B.Tech course and the scholarship for candidates who have qualified for the GATE or AP PGECET entrance exam. Candidates must have to fulfil the minimum eligibility requirements in order to qualify for the scholarships. Tuition fees and cash prizes are granted in accordance with each Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering and Technology Kurnool scholarship offered by the college.

  • According to the eligibility requirements, applicants must have received a score in the top 10,000 in the AP EAMCET for the 4-year B.Tech scholarships. This programme waives the entire tuition fee for 4 years.
  • The RGMCET Kurnool scholarships are available for those who have qualified in the GATE or the AP PGECET entrance exam. GATE candidates are offered a sum of Rs 5,000 per month or Rs 60,000 annually, and so on.
