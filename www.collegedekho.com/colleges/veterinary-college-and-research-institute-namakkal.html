About Veterinary College and Research Institute (VCRI), Namakkal

Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal is situated in Namakkal in Tamil Nadu state of India. Established in 1985, is a University college. The college is accredited by ICAR. Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal offers 13 courses across 4 streams namely Science, Engineering, Medical, Management..Popular degrees offered at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal include B.Tech, BVSc, PGD, MVSc, M.Tech..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
