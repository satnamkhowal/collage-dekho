About Heritage Institute of Technology

Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata, also known as HIT Kolkata, is a private autonomous college established in 2001. Located in Kolkata, West Bengal, the HIT Kolkata campus spans over 8 acres. The college is affiliated with Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT) and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and accredited with an ‘A’ grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a 3.13 CGPA. Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata is ranked 231 by Collegedunia and 108 by IIRF in the engineering category in 2025. It was also ranked in the 201–250 band by NIRF in 2022 for engineering.

Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in the streams of engineering, technology, and computer applications. B.Tech, M.Tech, and MCA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata are primarily based on entrance examinations. The college accepts WBJEE and JEE Main for B.Tech, JELET for B.Tech (Lateral Entry), GATE and WB PGET for M.Tech, and WB JECA for MCA.

For placements, Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata’s placement rate is approximately 80–90% for B.Tech CSE students, based on 2022 data. During the placement drive of 2024, the highest salary package offered was INR 30 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 4.78–5.22 LPA, as per NIRF 2025 data.

Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata Admission 2025

Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata admissions are primarily based on national and state-level entrance examinations. Students seeking admission at Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the college and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for Heritage Institute of Technology Kolkata admissions:

  • B.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech must appear for the WBJEE or JEE Main and participate in the WBJEEB Counselling for the seat allotment process. 80% of seats are filled through WBJEE, 10% through JEE Main, and 10% through Management Quota.
  • B.Tech (Lateral Entry): Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech (Lateral Entry) must appear for the JELET and participate in the WBJEEB Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • M.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to M.Tech must appear for the GATE or WB PGET and participate in the MAKAUT Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • MCA: Candidates seeking admission to MCA must appear for the WB JECA and participate in the WBJEEB Counselling for the seat allotment process.
