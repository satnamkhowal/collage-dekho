About Amity University Mumbai

Amity University Mumbai is recognised by the UGC, BCI, and AIU and it also holds an 'A' grade from NAAC. Furthermore, it's a member of the Association of Commonwealth Universities and holds international accreditation from the Accreditation Service for International Colleges (ASIC). Moreover, Amity School of Engineering and Technology is accredited with the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET) , UK, while the Amity Business School have accreditation from the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP) in the USA. Additionally, the Audiology & Speech Language Pathology programmes are recognised by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI), and the College of Architecture (COA) holds its accreditation. 

Amity University Mumbai has 400+ highly qualified and well-trained faculty teaching in undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral courses. This university is India's only & Asia's 2nd University to be awarded LEED (Leadership in Energy & Environmental Design) (USA) Platinum certification. 

Spread over a 30-acre state-of-the-art campus, the university offers 129 courses. Amity Mumbai provides admissions to students based on merit, written test, and personal interview. The eligibility criteria vary from course to course, and the tuition fees are also set accordingly. Amity University Mumbai has built strategic partnerships with the RICS School of Built Environment, University of London, Tata Technologies, CII School of Logistics, and many others. The Corporate Resource Center (CRC) at Amity University Mumbai assures 100% campus placements.

Amity University Mumbai Popular Courses

B.Tech

There are 13 specialisations offered under this course by Amity University Mumbai, including civil, computer science, electronics and communications, mechanical, etc. The entrance exams accepted are JEE-Main and AMCAT. Each stream has its cut-off rank that needs to be secured to get a seat in a particular department. Amity Mumbai is highly recognised, and top companies across the world offer internships and campus placements for bright students with excellent academic performance.

B.Arch

B.Arch is one of the popular Amity University Mumbai courses. The entrance exams accepted are NATA and JEE Main Paper 2. The students who have passed 10+2 with PCM or Diploma with Math as a subject and scored a minimum of 50% can apply for B.Arch at Amity University Mumbai along with entrance exams.

MBA

At Amity University Mumbai, there are 13 specialisations offered under MBA. The specialisations offered are finance, international business, marketing & sales, HR, capital markets, family business management, etc. With globalisation at its peak, countless companies are emerging daily. Therefore, after graduation, an MBA looks like one of the best options for graduates to boost their career prospects.

BCA

After 10+2, if you are interested in entering the field of computer applications, BCA might be the best choice at Amity Mumbai. To apply for this Amity University Mumbai course, you must have completed 12th with subjects like math, computer science, data management, etc.

BBA 

Bachelor of Business Administration at Amity University Mumbai is an excellent course for 10 + 2 passed out students. There are about five different types of BBA offered at Amity Mumbai, such as real estate and infrastructure, banking & finance. 

BFA 

It is one of the most preferred Amity Mumbai courses for students who wish to pursue a career in fine arts at Amity University Mumbai. It is a 4-year course that can be taken after passing the 12th exam in any stream. 

BA (Hons)

Amity University Mumbai offers BA (Hons) courses with specialisations in applied psychology, English, and French. The students who have managed to clear 10+2 exams can opt for this course and become scholars of the future. 

B.Sc 

If a student is interested in applied sciences, Amity Mumbai is the best place to study as there are a plethora of specialisations like Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry, Statistics, VFX & Animation, etc., available to choose and shine.

Candidates can apply online to get admission for any course on the university's official website. Before proceeding with Amity Mumbai admissions, candidates must ensure they satisfy the eligibility criteria for the course they wish to enrol in.
