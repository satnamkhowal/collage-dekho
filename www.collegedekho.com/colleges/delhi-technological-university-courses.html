DTU Courses 2026

Delhi Technological University (DTU) offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctorate programs in the streams of Engineering, Management, Design, Economics, and Applied Sciences. BTech, MBA, BBA, and BDes are some of the popular courses. DTU BTech Fees for 4 years is INR 6,52,300.

Courses Offered

DTU offers a wide range of programs across 17 departments, with a focus on innovation, research, and industry alignment.

Dtu is just wonderful

Overall DTU is one of the top engineering colleges in India, offering a solid academic foundation combined with a dynamic campus life. The admission process is merit-based, using JEE Mains scores, and the competition is fierce, particularly for non-Delhi candidates due to the 85% Delhi quota. The course curriculum is good for foundational knowledge, especially for technical subjects like OOPs, DBMS, and OS. However, some advanced topics only touch the intermediate level. The schedule can be hectic, with exams, projects, and tests tightly packed into a two-month timeframe. Despite the challenges, DTU's strong placement record, internship opportunities, and vibrant extracurricular activities make it a preferred choice for aspiring engineers.

Faculty The faculty at DTU is a major highlight, known for being knowledgeable, approachable, and experienced. Many professors, such as Trasha Gupta and LN Das, are well-versed in their subjects and adopt a balanced teaching approach that helps students understand both theoretical and practical aspects. Some faculty members, like those in applied physics, were particularly renowned for their teaching quality and overall guidance, although faculty turnover occasionally leads to the loss of such gems. While most professors are helpful, a few are stricter with marking and can be inconsistent with conducting classes. Exams vary in difficulty, with some subjects being more challenging than others, depending on the professor. The curriculum offers a solid theoretical base but might lack the depth needed for advanced industry-level work, requiring students to self-learn emerging technologies. Despite minor drawbacks, DTU’s faculty ensures a robust academic foundation while fostering independent learning.

DTU Courses FAQs

What is the DTU fees structure?

The fee structure for all courses varies according to the specialization chosen. The fee for B.Tech is Rs 1,38,000. It is Rs 8.44 lakh for B.Des, Rs 2.82 lakh for BBA, Rs 65,000 for M.Sc, Rs 3.23 lakh for M.Tech, and the DTU fees for MBA is Rs 3.96 lakh.

What are the DTU courses for B.Tech?

B.Tech is the flagship programme of DTU. It is offered in 15 specializations which include computer engineering, mechanical engineering, civil engineering, software engineering, computer engineering, etc.

Is the DTU course registration open for 2023?

The DTU course admissions for 2023 are expected to begin soon. Meanwhile, the various entrance exams accepted for the courses are being conducted. Candidates must make a note of the important dates for such exams like JEE, GATE, CAT, UCEED, and, CEED.

What are the top DTU courses?

Delhi Technological University offers UG, PG, and, PhD courses in the streams of technology, engineering, management, science, and, design. The popular courses opted for by students include B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, B.Des, and, M.Des. Among these courses, B.Tech is the flagship course of the university which is offered in multiple specialisations.

What is the DTU fees for B.Tech?

The fee for B.Tech at the university is Rs 1,38,000. The various miscellaneous fee charges like security deposit, application fee, etc. are not included in this fee amount.

What is the most popular course at DTU , Delhi ?

Engineering courses are the most popular courses opted by students at DTU , Delhi .
