About IIT Dhanbad

Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Dhanbad, also known as Indian School of Mines (ISM) Dhanbad was established in 1926 as ISM by the British India Government. It is one of the prestigious IITs with a ranking of 15 in the engineering category according to NIRF report 2024. The institute holds a global rank of 20th and a national rank of 1st in Mineral and Mining Engineering according to QS World University rankings 2025. The institute holds a global rank of 20 th and a national rank of 1 st in Mineral and Mining Engineering (QS-2025). 

IIT Dhanbad offers courses in several fields, including engineering, computer application and IT, management and business administration, sciences, arts, humanities and social sciences, and medicine and allied sciences. The courses offered include undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes. The undergraduate courses offered are BTech, BTech and M.B.A Mining Engineering, and BTech MTech (dual degree). The postgraduate courses offered include MTech Applied Geology Integrated, MTech Applied Geophysics Integrated, M.B.A, MTech, and M.Sc. The doctoral programmes offered include PhD. IIT Dhanbad Fees for the year 2025-2026 is 67,100 - 14.94 Lakhs across all offered courses. The UG Fees for IIT Dhanbad ranges between 12.01 Lakhs - 14.94 Lakhs. IIT Dhanbad PG Fees is 99,100 - 6 Lakhs. IIT Dhanbad MBA Fees is 3.68 Lakhs for the entire 2 years duration. Admission for MTech for this year have closed, GATE applications for 2026 will start from August 2025 and the exam will be conducted in February 2026. For BTech admissions, JoSAA counseling is almost at its conclusion, the registered candidates can check their cutoff from the official site. 

The Training and Placement Cell at IIT Dhanbad continues to offer robust placement support, ensuring excellent opportunities for students in top-tier companies. With a mission to enhance student employability and professional readiness, the cell organizes regular interactions with leading corporate firms, training sessions, and industry engagement programs. These initiatives aim to not only provide job placements but also shape students’ understanding of the professional landscape. The highest package at ISM Dhanbad for the placement cycle 2024-25 was 84 LPA. The overall placement rate was approximately 70%.

IIT Dhanbad FAQs

Will IIT ISM Dhanbad accept IIT Advanced 2023?

Yes, IIT ISM Dhanbad will accept the JEE Advanced 2023 scores for admission to its various B.Tech programmes in the current academic year. Interested students must apply through JoSAA counselling 2023 for IIT Dhanbad B.Tech admissions 2023. Final admissions will be based on the course-specific IIT ISM Dhanbad B.Tech cut off 2023 based on JEE Advanced 2023.

Can I know the IIT JAM cut off 2023 mathematics & computing at IIT ISM Dhanbad?

IIT ISM Dhanbad cut off 2023 for M.Sc Mathematics & Computing has been released for round 1 and round 2. The cutoffs are based on IIT JAM 2023. IIT ISM Dhanbad JAM cut off 2023 for mathematics & computing for the general category is 380 (round 1) and 399 (round 2).

Is IIT ISM Dhanbad JAM cut off 2023 out?

Yes, the IIT ISM Dhanbad JAM cut off 2023 round 1 for M.Sc admissions 2023 has been released by IIT Guwahati. As per the cut off requirements, the cut off for M.Sc Physics for the General category students is 502. In 2022, the IIT ISM Dhanbad JAM cut off marks for M.Sc Physics was 531 for the General category students.

i just attempted a free mock test where will i get the results

A
Apoorva Bali •  Jan-30-2025

The results of any free IIT JAM mock test you attempted will usually be accessible on the same website or platform where you took the test. You can check your results by doing the following:

  • Check the platform or website:

Return to the website where the mock exam was held if it was one of such sites (such as the platform of a coaching centre or an educational website). Use the login information you used to register to access your account, if you have one. Look for a section that says "My Results," "Test History," or "Dashboard," since these might include your mock test results and feedback.

  • Instant Results:

Results from a lot of free practice exams are available right away. If so, see if your score appeared on the platform immediately after you finished the test.

  • Notification via Email:

After you finish the test, some platforms send you an email with your findings. Look for any correspondence on the outcomes of your practice exam in your inbox (and spam folder).

  • Speak with Support:

You can get in touch with the helpdesk or support staff of the website or platform where you took the test if you are unable to locate your results. They ought to be able to help you find your score.

Please let me know if you need help with anything else regarding to the IIT JAM mock test!
