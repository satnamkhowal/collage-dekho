About Aravali College of Engineering and Management (ACEM), Faridabad

Aravali College of Engineering & Management is a private college, situated in Faridabad district of Haryana. ACEM provides world-class technical and managerial education to the students of both national and foreign origins. Aravali College was founded in the year 2008 by Mr. Dhan Singh Bhadana, an optimistic educationist with the main aim to provide a superior and holistic education that enables the students to succeed and stand out in their chosen fields of career.

ACEM is affiliated to the YMCA University of Science of Technology and approved by the Government of Haryana and all courses are approved by AICTE as well.

ACEM Faridabad UG and PG courses are broadly classified into Engineering and Management stream. In addition to these courses, the college also offers courses in Information Technology. In addition to academic sessions, the institute organizes various guest lectures and discussion lead by eminent speakers from the top industries for providing the students with their rich ideas and experiences.

Admission in UG courses at Aravali College of Engineering and Management based12th class scores and a valid rank secured in respective entrance exams, accepted by the college. PG courses admission at ACEM Faridabad is decided by the college itself on the basis of the overall academic record of the students.

ACEM is committed to imparting quality education to all the students, irrespective of their background so that students can be transformed into responsible, confident, and self-reliant citizens of the society in the future.

ACEM Training & Placement Cell assists students in their life-long career development and placements. The department helps in organizing summer training and industrial visits which are mandatory for both UG and PG final year students. Moreover, it undertakes a PDP program for the overall development of students.

Aravali College of Engineering & Management infrastructure is impressive with a very spacious campus. The campus is equipped with all the basic and advanced facilities that create an ambiance which is conducive for learning. The whole campus is Wi-Fi enabled. ACEM hostels facility is available for both male and female students with proper sanitation and cleanliness. The hostel has 24 hours backup with the commendable security, which is an added advantage. In addition, the college campus has numerous facilities such as sports, Health, ATM’s, central computer lab, counseling service, gym, etc.

Aravali College of Engineering and Management Admission 2026-27

ACEM Faridabad admission process for B.Tech is carried out through HSTES counselling with a valid JEE Main score. For BBA, BCA, and MBA, candidates apply online or directly at the college campus. Required documents include Class 10 and 12 marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, transfer certificate, character certificate, category certificate if applicable, and Aadhaar card.

Aravali College of Engineering and Management Faridabad Admission Process 2026

ACEM, Faridabad follows a separate admission process for engineering and management courses. The steps below are based on available information:

For B.Tech:

  • Apply through Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) with a valid JEE Main score.
  • Participate in HSTES counselling and select ACEM as a preferred college.
  • Report to the college with original documents for verification and seat confirmation.
  • Pay the course fee to confirm admission.

For BBA, BCA, and MBA:

  • Visit the college campus or apply online at www.acem.edu.in.
  • Fill and submit the application form along with required documents.
  • Pay the registration fee at the time of form submission.
  • Attend the personal interview or counselling session if applicable.
  • Pay the course fee after selection to confirm the seat.

Aravali College of Engineering and Management Admission Required Documents 2026

Students must carry the following documents during the document verification process at Aravali College of Engineering and Management. Please keep the original documents and two sets of photocopies ready for admission formalities.

  • Class 10 marksheet and certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet and certificate
  • Graduation marksheets (for MBA applicants)
  • JEE Main / CAT / MAT / CMAT scorecard (as applicable)
  • Character certificate from last attended institution
  • Transfer certificate
  • Migration certificate (if applicable)
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC), if applicable
  • Passport-size photographs
