About Parul University Diploma

Parul University Diploma is a 1 - 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. Parul University offers diploma courses in engineering, medical, hotel management, and design, with a course duration of three years. Admissions are merit-based and require candidates to have a Class 10 qualification with English as a mandatory subject, and a Class 12 degree in science stream with 45% of the aggregate marks. No entrance exam is required. The application process is online or offline, with a fee payable via debit card, credit card, or net banking. Selection is based on merit and is followed by counselling. Documents required include certificates and mark sheets of Class 10 and 12, character/school leaving/transfer document, three passport-size photographs, proof of residence, photocopy of Aadhaar Card of the candidate and their parents, and a self The annual fee for Diploma is INR 25,000 - 1,75,000. Parul University Diploma Fees

Parul University Diploma fees is 25,000 INR (Yearly) and goes up to 1,75,000 INR (Yearly) depending on the specialization. The average fees for the Diploma course is 91,076 INR (Yearly). Parul University Diploma admission is offered on the basis of the merit of the qualifying exam.
