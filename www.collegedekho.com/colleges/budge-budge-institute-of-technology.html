Budge Budge Institute of Technology FAQs

Does Budge Budge Institute of Technology have any collaborations?

Yes, the Budge Budge Institute of Technology has collaborations with various companies to offer students industrial visits and campus placements. Some of the popular companies the college has signed MoUs with are Tata Power Company Limited, Krypton Industries Limited, BMW Industries Pvt. Ltd., Znome Consultancy Services Pvt. Ltd., Tata Consultancy Services Limited, and others. All students can participate in industry visits to these companies throughout their academic years at this college.

What are the facilities available at the BBIT campus?

The facilities available at the BBIT campus are enough for students to enjoy a comfortable campus life. Students can use the BBIT facilities like separate hostels for men and women, free wifi campus, 24/7 ATM facility, free medical checkup, a cafeteria, 24/7 power backup, smart classrooms, and more. Students who are in their final year can also avail of the campus placement facilities provided by the college. According to the 2022 placements, the highest package offered was Rs 22,00,000 per year.

Is Budge Budge Institute of Technology ragging-free?

Yes, the Budge Budge Institute of Technology campus is completely ragging-free. Students who enrol at this college must comply with the anti-ragging policy. Students will be punished according to the policy if found involved in ragging attempts. Moreover, the BBIT hostel buildings are also ragging-free. The wardens in the hostel are on 24/7 duty to stop any ragging attempts on students.

What is the Budge Budge Institute of Technology fees for different courses?

Naturally, Budge Budge Institute of Technology fees for different courses vary from each other. The BBIT fees range is Rs 95,050 to Rs 3,90,950 for the complete course. Students who wish to get admission to B.Tech should know that the four-year B.Tech course fees at BBIT is Rs 3,84,350 in total. As for Budge Budge Institute of Technology MBA fees, it is Rs 3,90,950 for the complete course. The lowest fees in the course list of BBIT is for the two-year Diploma in Engineering, which is  Rs 95,050 in total.

Is BBIT a private or government college?

BBIT or Budge Budge Institute of Technology is a self-financed private college. The college provides admission through entrance tests only. Some of the prominent entrance tests accepted at BBIT Kolkata are JEE Mains, WBJEE, MAT, JEXPO, and more. This college is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a B+ grade and the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). BBIT is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

What are the specialisations of B.Tech offered at BBIT Kolkata?

The B.Tech programme offered at BBIT Kolkata has the following specialisations Civil Engineering (CE), Mechanical Engineering (ME), Electrical Engineering (EE), Electronics and communication Engineering (ECE), Computer Science and engineering (CSE), Information Technology (IT), CSE in Artificial Intelligence, and CSE in Cyber Security.

Is BBIT Kolkata a valid college?

Yes, BBIT Kolkata is a valid college. Budge Budge Institute of Technology Kolkata is affiliated with the Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT) and the West Bengal State Council of Technical and Vocational Education and Skill Development (WBSCTVESD). Therefore, all the degrees granted to graduates are completely valid. The college is also recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). Students can apply for any course they want at BBIT without any worries about it being a fake college.

What are the Budge Budge Institute of Technology scholarships available for students?

Budge Budge Institute of Technology offers various scholarships for students with merit and need. These scholarships are SC / ST / OBC Scholarship, Minority Scholarship, Kanyashree Scholarship, SVMCM Scholarship, NHFDC Scholarship, WBFS Scheme, Pragati & Saksham Scheme, Ishan Uday Scheme, Bihar Credit Card Scheme, and the West Bengal Student Credit Card Scheme.

Can I get admission to the BBIT MBA programme without any work experience?

Aspiring students should know that the BBIT eligibility criteria for MBA admission only require students to pass graduation from any recognised university. Moreover, they will also need to pass MAT/CMAT/JEMAT. There is no mandatory need for work experience for admission to the BBIT Kolkata MBA programme.
