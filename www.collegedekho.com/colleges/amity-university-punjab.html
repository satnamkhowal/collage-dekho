About Amity University Punjab

Amity University Punjab was established in 2021 by the not-for-profit Amity Foundation. It is the eleventh university established by the Amity Group. With a vast hi-tech campus of 40 acres, the university is located 10 minutes away from the Chandigarh International Airport. The university is recognised by UGC (University Grants Commission). It is Asia's only not-for-profit university to get the highest accreditations by WASC (Western Association of Schools and Colleges), the USA, and the QAA (Quality Assurance Agency for Higher Education), UK. The university is also accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities. 

Amity University Punjab is a multidisciplinary university offering more than 115+ undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes. The university has tied up with over 100 international universities, including the University of Oxford, Stanford University, Columbia University, MIT, Harvard University, and the Queen Mary University of London.

The university is ranked amongst the top 3% of universities globally by the QS University Rankings. It is also ranked 1st not-for-profit private university as per India Today's University Rankings and listed as one of India's top universities with innovation achievements by ARIIA. The university has the mission to “change the world, together” and constantly strives to achieve it with quality education.

Amity University Mohali Admission 2026

The admission process at Amity University Mohali is multi-step and differs for each programme. At its core, admission is based on academic merit and/or performance in national entrance exams, followed by a personal interview (PI). Students who score 80% or above in Class 12 are exempted from the PI for most UG programmes.

Amity University Mohali Admission Process 2026

Below is the step-by-step admission process for all programmes.

B.Tech Admission Process

  • Check eligibility: Class 12 PCM with minimum 60% aggregate.
  • Register and fill the application form at amity.edu/mohali (application fee: Rs 1,500).
  • Submit valid JEE Main scorecard or appear in Amity Joint Entrance Exam (AJEE).
  • Shortlisted candidates receive interview call on registered email/mobile.
  • Attend Personal Interview (PI) at the Mohali campus or via online mode.
  • Final selection based on entrance exam score + PI + Class 12 marks.
  • Seat allotment communicated via email/SMS. Pay admission fee to confirm seat.
  • Submit original documents for verification at the time of reporting

MBA Admission Process

  • Check eligibility: Graduation with minimum 50% aggregate.
  • Appear in CAT / MAT / XAT / CMAT / GMAT / NMAT (any one valid score).
  • Fill online application form and pay Rs 1,500 application fee.
  • Eligible candidates are called for the English Language Test (ELT).
  • Attend Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) at Mohali campus.
  • Also appear for the Management Aptitude Test (MAT) on the day of interview.
  • Final selection: entrance exam score + ELT + GD + PI + MAT performance.
  • Pay admission fee and submit original documents to confirm seat.

BArch Admission Process

  • Minimum eligibility: 50% in Class 12 with Maths (PCM) and 50% in NATA.
  • Register on the NATA portal and appear for NATA 2026 exam.
  • Fill Amity Mohali application form and pay Rs 1,500.
  • Shortlisted candidates attend English Language Test (ELT) and PI.
  • Selection based on Class 12 marks + NATA score + ELT + PI performance.
  • Pay admission fee and submit documents for final seat confirmation.

Law Programmes (BA LLB / BBA LLB / LLB / LLM) Admission Process

  • Appear in CLAT UG (for 5-year integrated law) or CLAT PG (for LLM).
  • Candidates with CLAT UG score of 95 and above get direct admission to 5-year law programmes.
  • Fill Amity Mohali application form online and pay Rs 1,500 fee.
  • Attend Personal Interview at the campus.
  • Final selection based on CLAT score + Class 12/graduation marks + PI.
  • Confirm seat by paying admission fee and document submission.

BSc / BCA / BBA / BCom / BA Admission Process

  • Check eligibility as per chosen programme (50-60% in Class 12).
  • Fill online application form at amity.edu/mohali and pay Rs 1,500.
  • Submit CUET UG scorecard (if applying via CUET) or apply on merit.
  • Students scoring 80% or above in Class 12 are exempt from PI.
  • Others attend Personal Interview at the campus.
  • Selection based on Class 12 merit / CUET score + PI (if applicable).
  • Pay admission fee and report for document verification.

MCA / MSc / MA / LLM Admission Process

  • Check eligibility: relevant graduation with 50% aggregate.
  • Apply online and pay Rs 1,500 application fee.
  • Submit relevant entrance exam scores (NIMCET for MCA, CUET PG for MSc/MA, CLAT PG for LLM).
  • Attend Personal Interview at the campus.
  • Final selection: graduation marks + entrance score + PI.
  • Confirm seat via fee payment and original document submission.

Ph.D Admission Process

  • Check eligibility: PG in the respective field with minimum 55% aggregate; UGC/CSIR/DBT-NET qualified candidates get PET exemption.
  • Register and fill the online application form at amity.edu/phd (application fee: Rs 1,500, non-refundable, payable via credit/debit card).
  • Upload required documents: passport-size photograph, Class X mark sheet, Post-Graduation mark sheet, preliminary research idea (within 1,000 words), and employment verification certificate (for part-time applicants).
  • Appear for the Ph.D. Entrance Test (PET) conducted as per UGC Ph.D. guidelines; NET-qualified candidates are exempted from PET.
  • Shortlisted candidates are called for a Personal Interview (PI) at the Mohali campus.
  • Final selection based on PET score + PI performance + past academic record.
  • Admission letter uploaded on the Admission Microsite at amity.edu/mohali within two days of selection.
  • Pay the admission fee as per the schedule in the admission letter to confirm the seat.
  • Submit original documents for verification at the time of reporting for the January or July session.

How to Apply for Amity University Mohali Admission 2026 ?

The application process for Amity University Mohali admission 2026 is entirely online. Here is the step-by-step process to fill the Amity Mohali application form:

  • Go to the official website: amity.edu/mohali or the Admission Microsite.
  • Click on 'Apply Now 2026' on the menu bar.
  • Click on 'Apply Now' to access the Amity University Mohali admission form.
  • Register by filling in basic details: mobile number and email ID.
  • Verify your email ID via the OTP sent to your registered email.
  • Log in again and select your desired programme.
  • Fill in complete personal, academic, and extracurricular details.
  • Upload required documents (mark sheets, photo ID, passport-size photo, etc.).
  • Pay the application fee of Rs 1,500 (non-refundable) via online mode (debit card, credit card, or net banking).
  • Submit the application form. Download and take a printout for future reference.

Note:

Documents to keep handy while filling the application form: Class 10 mark sheet, Class 12 mark sheet (or graduation mark sheet for PG), entrance exam scorecard (JEE/CUET/CAT/CLAT etc.), passport-size photograph, valid government photo ID (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Driver's License), and any sports/achievement certificates if applying under sports quota.

    Amity University Punjab Scholarships

    All qualified students (for programmes following 10+2) are admitted to Amity University Punjab on the Fast Track. That is, they are not required to participate in the admission selection process, and their admittance is determined solely based on their application form. For post-graduate programmes, direct admission is not available. However, a scholarship will be given following enrollment in the programme and verification of authentic papers. Ph.D programmes are not eligible for the Amity University Punjab scholarships. 

    UG Scholarships:

    • Candidates are eligible for a 100% scholarship if they have scored a minimum of 95% aggregate and above in Class 12 (CBSE or ICSE Board). 
    • Candidates are eligible for a 75% scholarship if they have scored between 93%-94.99% aggregate and above in Class 12 (CBSE or ICSE Board). 
    • Candidates are eligible for a 50% scholarship if they have scored between 90%-92.99% aggregate and above in Class 12 (CBSE or ICSE Board). 
    • Candidates are eligible for a 20% scholarship if they have scored a minimum of 90% aggregate and above in Class 12 (CBSE or ICSE Board). 

    PG Scholarship

    • Candidates are eligible for a 100% scholarship if they have scored a minimum of 93% aggregate and above in Class 12 (CBSE or ICSE Board) and 80% in graduation.
    • Candidates are eligible for a 50% scholarship if they have scored a minimum of 90% aggregate and above in Class 12 (CBSE or ICSE Board) and 75% in graduation.
    • Candidates are eligible for a 20% scholarship if they have scored a minimum of 90% aggregate and above in Class 12 (State Board) and 75% in graduation.

    Sports Scholarship

    • Candidates are eligible for a 100% scholarship if they are international players.
    • Candidates are eligible for a 50% scholarship if they are National Level Medal winners (Gold, Silver and Bronze medal only)
    • Candidates are eligible for a 25% scholarship if they have a National Participation certificate.
