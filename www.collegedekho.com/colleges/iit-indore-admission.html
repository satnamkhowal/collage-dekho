IIT Indore BTech Admission 2025

IIT Indore offers a full-time BTech for 4 years. BTech is offered in multiple specializations including Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Metallurgical Engineering and Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Mathematics & Computing, Engineering Physics, and Space Sciences & Engineering. IIT Indore BTech admission is based on JEE Advanced. The eligibility criteria for BTech admission at IIT Indore is 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, securing at least 75% marks (65% for SC/ST/PwD) or being within the category-wise top 20 percentile in their respective Class XII board examination. IIT Indore BTech fees is approximately INR 8,00,000 for the entire duration. More details about IIT Indore BTech are tabulated below:

IIT Indore MTech Admission 2025

IIT Indore offers a full-time MTech for 2 years. MTech is offered in multiple specializations including VLSI Design & Nanoelectronics, Computer Science and Engineering, Defence Technology, Biomedical Engineering, Structural Engineering, and more. IIT Indore MTech admission is based on GATE. The eligibility criteria for MTech admission at IIT Indore is a bachelor’s degree in a relevant engineering field with at least 60% marks (55% for SC/ST) or a CGPA of 6.0/10 (5.5 for SC/ST). IIT graduates with a CGPA of 8.0 or above are exempted from GATE. IIT Indore MTech fees is approximately INR 1,00,000 – 1,50,000 for the entire duration. No, the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Indore does not offer the Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm) programme. The Centre for Biosciences and Biomedical Engineering (BSBE) was initiated at IIT Indore in the year 2012 to promote research and development in areas of Biosciences, Biomedical Engineering and Bioengineering but to get admission opportunity here, you must already hold an M.Pharm degree or M.Tech or MSc in Biological Sciences or any equivalent degree from a recognised university.
