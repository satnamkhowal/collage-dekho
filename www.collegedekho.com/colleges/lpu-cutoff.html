LPU Cutoff 2026

LPU does not publish official cutoffs for admissions. The university conducts LPUNEST for admission to most of its programs. Based on LPUNEST scores, LPU offers scholarships under 4 brackets: Category A, B, C, and D cutoffs. Also, candidates with high percentiles in JEE Main, CAT, XAT, NEET, & GATE are eligible for up to 100% scholarships. LPU cutoff 2026 for B.Tech through JEE Main is 97 percentile for a 60% scholarship on the programme fee, while candidates with 95 percentile are eligible for a 50% scholarship. 

For LPU MBA admissions, candidates with 90 percentile or above in CAT/XAT are eligible for a 50% scholarship on the programme fee. Similarly, NEET scores are accepted for B.Pharm scholarships, where candidates securing a top 20,000 AIR can avail a 100% scholarship along with examination fee and residential fee/transport benefits. For M.Tech at LPU, candidates with 95 percentile or above in GATE are eligible for a 60% scholarship on the programme fee. 

Given below are the LPU cutoff 2026 criteria required to avail scholarships across programmes such as B.Tech, MBA, B.Pharm, M.Tech, B.Arch, B.Sc, and other UG and PG courses.

LPU JEE Main Cutoff 2026

LPU JEE Main cutoff 2026 for B.Tech is 97 percentile for a 60% scholarship on program fee and 95 percentile for 50% scholarship. To get a 100% scholarship on the course fee and examination fee, candidates must secure a top 35,000 AIR in JEE Main. Candidates with 90–95 percentile are eligible for a 40% scholarship, while those with 80–90 percentile can avail a 30% scholarship on the program fee.

LPU CMAT Cutoff 2026

LPU CMAT cutoff 2026 for MBA is an All India Rank between 1–5000 for a 60% scholarship on the program fee. Candidates securing an AIR between 5001–10000 are eligible for a 50% scholarship. LPU NEET Cutoff 2026

LPU NEET cutoff 2026 for B.Pharm is a top 20,000 AIR or AIIMS allotment letter for a 100% scholarship on the programme fee, examination fee, and residential fee/transport. With a NEET rank between 50,001–1,00,000, candidates can avail a 50% scholarship, while those securing ranks between 1,00,001–2,00,000 are eligible for a 40% scholarship on the programme fee.
