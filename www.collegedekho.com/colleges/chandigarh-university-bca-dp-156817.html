About Chandigarh University BCA

Chandigarh University BCA is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. Chandigarh University requires applicants for the B.C.A. (Bachelor of Computer Applications) degree to have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination with a minimum of 45% marks in aggregate from a recognized board. The annual fee for BCA is INR 1,56,000 - 1,76,000. Chandigarh University BCA Admission 2026

Admission to the BCA at Chandigarh University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Chandigarh University BCA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Chandigarh University BCA Application Process

Chandigarh University BCA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BCA course at Chandigarh University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Chandigarh University BCA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Chandigarh University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Chandigarh University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Chandigarh University BCA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Chandigarh University BCA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Chandigarh University BCA Application Form.

Documents Required for Chandigarh University BCA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

FAQs about Chandigarh University BCA

What is the Chandigarh University BCA Ranking?

The BCA ranking of Chandigarh University is 38th in the Top BCA Colleges of India category by Outlook I-Care Rankings 2022, 15th in the Top BCA Colleges in North India category by India Today 2022, and 10th in the Computer Science and Information Systems category by the QS World University Ranking 2023.

What is the Chandigarh University BCA Entrance Exam syllabus?

The entrance exam for BCA at CU is the Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test (CUCET). The syllabus for Computers in the CUCET includes topics like Computer Fundamentals, MS Office (Word Processing and Spreadsheets), Network Computing, Introduction to Programming in C++, and Java Fundamentals.

What is the Chandigarh University BCA review?

CU has been ranked 10th by the QS World University Ranking in the Computer Science and Information Systems category, 15th by India Today for the Top BCA Colleges in North India category, as well as 38th by Outlook I-Care Rankings for the Top BCA Colleges of India category. These rankings have been attained due to the quality of education provided by the university for BCA.

How can I get admission to BCA at Chandigarh University?

Candidates must register for the CUCET test and obtain satisfactory results in order to be eligible for the BCA admission process. Apart from this basic eligibility criteria include passing class 12 with at least 55% marks.

Can I pursue Chandigarh University BCA online?

Yes, Chandigarh University offers the Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) course in the online mode as well as the full-time mode. Candidates can opt for their preferred mode of study for the course. The course fee ranged from 3,93,000 to Rs 4,11,000 depending on the specialisation and mode opted for.

What is the Chandigarh University BCA admission process?

The first step for admission to BCA at Chandigarh University is to apply for CUCET on the website cucet.cuchd.in. Next, appear for the exam on the selected date and time in an online mode. Soon after, the results will be available for CUCET and candidate can submit their application for the further application process.

Is there any Chandigarh University BCA entrance exam?

Yes, the entrance exam accepted for BCA admission at the university is the Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test (CUCET). It is conducted by the university each year in multiple phases for scholarship and admission purposes.

Is Chandigarh University good for BCA?

CU has been ranked 15th by India Today in the Top BCA Colleges in the North India category and 38th by Outlook I-Care Rankings in the Top BCA Colleges of India category in 2022 due to the quality of education for the BCA course.

What is the Chandigarh University BCA syllabus?

The BCA syllabus at CU comprises of components like web designing, operating systems, computer networks, java programming, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and, machine learning.

What is the Chandigarh University BCA fees?

The BCA fees at Chandigarh University range between Rs 3,93,000 to Rs 4,11,000 for the entire course, depending on the specialisation chosen. Apart from this Rs 2,000 for the exam fee and Rs 2,000 for security also need to be paid.
