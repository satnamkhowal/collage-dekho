NIT Trichy Admission 2026

NIT Trichy Admission FAQs

What rank is needed for NIT Trichy?
For the CSE branch, an All India Rank (AIR) below 5,000 is generally required. Other branches, such as Mechanical or Civil, may be available to candidates with ranks up to 20,000.
What percentile is required for NIT Trichy CSE?
Admission to the CSE branch at NIT Trichy typically requires a percentile between 99.5 and 100 in JEE Main. Candidates should refer to the JoSAA cutoff for detailed information.
Can a candidate with 98 percentile get admission to NIT Trichy?
Yes, with 98 percentile a candidate may secure admission to certain branches at NIT Trichy. However, highly competitive branches like CSE usually require a higher percentile.

NIT Trichy Admission Process for BTech 2026

NIT Trichy admission process for B.Tech in 2026 starts with appearing in the JEE Main exam conducted by NTA. Eligible candidates must register for JoSAA counselling, select and lock their preferred branches, and wait for seat allotment. Once a seat is allotted, candidates need to accept it, complete document verification, and report to NIT Trichy to complete the admission process. Any remaining seats are filled through CSAB counselling:

Step 1: Appear for JEE Main

  • Candidates must appear for the JEE Main examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA).
  • JEE Main exam is mandatory for admission to the B.Tech programme at NIT Trichy.

Step 2: Check Eligibility

  • Candidates should have passed Class 12th or an equivalent examination with the required subjects and marks.
  • Admission is based on both JEE Main score and Class 12th performance.

Step 3: Register for JoSAA Counselling

  • Candidates who qualify JEE Main must register on the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) website.
  • JoSAA manages B.Tech admissions for all NITs, including NIT Trichy.

Step 4: Fill and Lock Choices

  • During counselling, candidates must choose NIT Trichy and preferred B.Tech branches.
  • Choices should be carefully reviewed and locked before the deadline.

Step 5: Seat Allotment

  • JoSAA conducts multiple rounds of seat allotment.
  • Seats are given based on JEE Main rank, category, choices filled, and seat availability.

Step 6: Accept the Allotted Seat

  • Candidates who receive a seat must accept it online and pay the seat acceptance fee within the given time.

Step 7: Document Verification

  • Candidates must upload and verify required documents such as mark sheets, JEE Main scorecard, and category certificates.

Step 8: CSAB Counselling (If Required)

  • If seats remain vacant, candidates can apply through CSAB special rounds for further admission chances.

Step 9: Reporting to NIT Trichy

  • After final allotment, candidates must report to NIT Trichy, complete institute-level formalities, and pay the remaining admission fee to confirm the admission.
