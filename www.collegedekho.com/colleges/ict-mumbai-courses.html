ICT Mumbai Courses 2026

ICT Mumbai courses for 2026 emphasize chemical technology and engineering, offering 8 undergraduate B.Tech/B.Chem.Eng programs at the Mumbai campus: B.Chem.Eng (Chemical Engineering, 4 years, 75 seats, process design focus); B.Tech Dyestuff Technology (20 seats, colorants); Fibres and Textiles Processing (34 seats, sustainable textiles); Food Engineering (16 seats, preservation techniques); Oils, Oleochemicals and Surfactants (16 seats, lipid products); Pharmaceutical Chemistry (30 seats, drug formulation); Polymer Engineering (16 seats, composites); Surface Coating (16 seats, paints). B.Pharm (4 years, 30 seats) covers pharmacology.

Postgraduate options include 12 M.Tech/M.Chem.Eng/M.Pharm: M.Chem.Eng (2 years, advanced engineering); M.Tech Bioprocess (biotech); Dyestuff; Fibres; Food; Green Technology; Oils; Perfumery; Pharmaceutical; Polymer; Surface Coating; M.Pharm (pharmaceutics). M.Sc in Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Textile Chemistry.

Doctoral PhD (3-5 years) spans Chemical Engineering, Bioprocess, Pharmacy, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics. Off-campuses (Jalna, Bhubaneswar) offer Integrated M.Tech (5 years) in Chemical Engineering with minors. Eligibility: UG via JEE Main/MHT CET (50% HSC PCM); PG via GATE (60% B.Tech); PhD via entrance/interview (55% Master's). Total programs exceed 40, aligned with industry needs.

For fees related more information, visit ICT Mumbai Fee Structure.

Is admission for plain MSc chemistry in ICT Mumbai possible with just the entrance exam? Because I didnt have maths in 12th or maths in any Bsc level.

V
Vandana Thakur •  Apr-01-2026

Dear Student, 

Admission to the M.Sc. The Chemistry program at the Institute of Chemical Technology, Mumbai, is strictly based on the performance in their entrance examination. However, candidates must satisfy the specific eligibility criteria regarding undergraduate coursework. According to official institutional guidelines, you are generally required to have passed Mathematics at the 10+2 level to be eligible for postgraduate science streams. Since you did not have Mathematics in Class 12th grade or during your Bachelor of Science course, you may not meet the mandatory prerequisite for enrollment. It is highly recommended to verify the current year's handbook or contact the Institute of Chemical Technology admission office. These mathematical requirements are foundational to their rigorous chemical sciences curriculum.
