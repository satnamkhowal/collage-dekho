About NIT Surathkal B.Tech

NIT Surathkal B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) in Mangalore, candidates must fulfill certain eligibility criteria. Aspiring students should have completed their 10+2 education with science subjects (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics) from a recognized board or university. Additionally, they must have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 75% (or equivalent) in their qualifying examination. Furthermore, candidates are required to appear for the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main and secure a valid score. The selection process for the B.Tech. program at NIT Mangalore is highly competitive, and admission is based on the JEE Main scores and subsequent counseling rounds. Meeting these eligibility criteria is essential for students aspiring to pursue a B.Tech. degree at NIT Mangalore. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 1,31,000. NIT Surathkal B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at NIT Surathkal is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the NIT Surathkal B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

NIT Surathkal B.Tech Application Process

NIT Surathkal B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at NIT Surathkal have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill NIT Surathkal B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of NIT Surathkal and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of NIT Surathkal. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the NIT Surathkal B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the NIT Surathkal B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of NIT Surathkal B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for NIT Surathkal B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
