About Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech

Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the B.Tech degree program at Adesh Institute Of Technology, Mohali, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry as compulsory subjects. They must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in their qualifying examination. Additionally, candidates must have a valid score in JEE Main or any other relevant entrance examination approved by the institute. Meeting these eligibility criteria will ensure that students have the necessary academic background and skills to succeed in the rigorous B.Tech program at Adesh Institute Of Technology, Mohali. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 70,000. Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech Application Process

Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
