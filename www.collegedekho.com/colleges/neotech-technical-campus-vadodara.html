About NeoTech Technical & Management College


NeoTech Technical & Management Institute is one of the well-known institutes in Vadodara, Gujarat. The institute was established in the year 2013 to provide quality education and a learning environment for students. NeoTech Technical & Management Institute is a co-ed private school that aims to bring interactive & practical based learning along with proficient facilities and top-notch infrastructure facilities. 

The institute is affiliated with Gujarat Technical University (GTU) and Shri Govind Guru University and is accredited by University Grants Commission (UGC) and All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). NeoTech Technical & Management Institute Vadodara offers more than 15 courses in different streams, such as engineering, paramedical, science, and nursing. Some of the popular courses offered at NTC Vadodara are BE, B.Sc, BPT, and PGD. 

Students can easily enrol for the course by fulfilling the eligibility criteria.  Admission into any course is based on the rank and percentage scored in the state and national level exams. To apply for any of the courses, candidates need to fill out the application and submit it on or before the deadline. The university accepts exams like JEE main and GUJCET. 

NTC Vadodara also conducts placement drive and offers students with good placement opportunities. The institute invites various companies from different sectors. More than 300+ companies visit the campus for recruitment purposes. The institute offers various scholarships for all meritorious and needy students. The institute provides all the facilities to enhance the learning experience of students.
