About Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech

Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech is a 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the B.Tech degree program at Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology, Ahmednagar, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Additionally, candidates must have a valid score in national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main or MHT CET. Meeting these criteria is essential for admission to the prestigious B.Tech program at Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology, Ahmednagar. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 52,500. Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech Application Process

Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
