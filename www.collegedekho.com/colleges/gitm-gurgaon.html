About GITM Gurgaon

Gurgaon Institute of Technology & Management (GITM) is one of the well-known institutes in Gurgaon. The institute was founded in the name of Lord Krishna Charitable Trust by a philanthropic society to provide higher education in line with global standards to meet the needs of the industries and technocrats of today. GITM is certified by AICTE New Delhi, Haryana Govt and is affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University. Within a short time, GITM has evolved into a desirable institution and is increasingly gaining in popularity. The college's goal is to offer higher education on a par with global standards.

GITM Campus is located at Bilaspur, Gurgaon. GITM offers a vast array of creative engineering and management programmes. In order to secure admission, the candidates need to fulfil the eligibility criteria. And have appeared in the mentioned qualifying entrance exam. GITM has a healthy combination of experienced professors who design the course for the best result. Interested candidates can apply for the program by visiting the campus or online registration.

GITM has a world-class infrastructure that includes well-stocked libraries, ultra-modern laboratories, acoustically built lecture halls with the latest audiovisual electronic aids, spacious and well-designed classrooms and tutorial rooms, workshops etc. The GITM campus is completely wi-fi enabled. The canteen of GITM makes sure that the students are provided with healthy and hygienic food. In addition, the college also offers several other facilities such as the Career Development Center, Separate Boys and Girls Hostels, Vast playgrounds, Transport facilities, Medical facilities etc. GITM students always have a handful with various events and extra-curricular activities such as cultural activities, Intra-college matches etc.

The placement cell of GITM trains its students in all aspects. The Order to train the student's institute continuously organizes various seminars, workshops and guest lectures from industry professionals. The placement cell of GITM  also provides various coaching to its students in the field of personality development, personal interview (PI), aptitude, soft skills etc.

GITM Gurgaon specialties

  • Region : Strategic Industrial Hub Proximity - Located on Bilaspur-Tauru Road, providing direct access to the IMT Manesar industrial belt. - Proximity to Gurgaon's Cyber City and Fortune 500 corporate offices facilitates frequent industry interaction.
  • Fees : Mid-Range Professional Education - Rs 3.6 - 4.2 L (Total for 4-year B.Tech program) - Structured fee installments to support students from diverse economic backgrounds.
  • ROI : Good Value for Investment - Competitive fee structure paired with consistent placement opportunities in the NCR region. - High return on investment for B.Tech and MBA graduates entering the IT and manufacturing sectors.
  • Flagship Courses : B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering - Flagship program with curriculum aligned to MDU Rohtak standards. - Emphasis on emerging technologies including Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
  • Placements : Corporate Readiness Program - Dedicated Training and Placement Cell focusing on 'Campus to Corporate' transitions. - Strong focus on IT Services, Automotive, and Manufacturing sectors.

Is there any scholarship in GITM based on merit?

P
Prateek Lakhera •  Aug-19-2025

Dear student,

Yes, the GITM Gurgaon offers scholarships to meritorious students based on their JEE Main ranks and 10+2 marks. As per the available information, the students getting a rank in the JEE Main under 50000 are eligible for 100% scholarships, followed by a 75% Tuition fee scholarship for students getting ranks up to 75000. Similarly, students scoring 90%+ marks in 10+2 level are eligible for a 50% scholarship, and students with 85% to 90% marks in the 10+2 level are eligible for a 50% tuition fee scholarship at GITM Gurgaon.
