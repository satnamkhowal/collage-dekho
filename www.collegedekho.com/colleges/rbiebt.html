About Rayat & Bahra Institute of Engineering & Bio-Technology

Rayat & Bahra Institute of Engineering & Bio-Technology is situated in Mohali in Punjab state of India. Established in 2008, RBIEBT is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Punjab Technical University and accredited by AICTE. Rayat & Bahra Institute of Engineering & Bio-Technology offers 9 courses across 2 streams namely Engineering, Management..Popular degrees offered at Rayat & Bahra Institute of Engineering & Bio-Technology include B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Rayat & Bahra Institute of Engineering & Bio-Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Rayat & Bahra Institute of Engineering & Bio-Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Rayat & Bahra Institute of Engineering & Bio-Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
