About KMC Mangalore

Kasturba Medical College (KMC), Mangalore, established in 1955, is a private medical college under Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), located in Mangaluru, Karnataka. Spanning 31 acres, it features a 1,260-bed teaching hospital (KMC Hospital Attavar) and is recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC), with global accreditations from the General Medical Council of Great Britain and Malaysian Medical Council. KMC Mangalore enrolls approximately 1,500 students with 422 faculty members, offering a strong academic environment. It ranks #35 in Medical by NIRF 2025.

KMC Mangalore offers Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Super-Specialty (SS) programs, including MBBS, MD/MS, DM/M.Ch., B.Sc., and M.Sc.. Admissions are entrance-based via NEET-UG (MBBS, B.Sc.), NEET-PG (MD/MS), NEET-SS (DM/M.Ch.), and MET (M.Sc.). The placement cell ensures robust career outcomes, with 89% MBBS students and 86% MD/MS students placed in 2024, with a median package of INR 8.4 LPA (UG) and INR 15.3 LPA (PG), per NIRF reports.

Admission 2025

Admissions to KMC Mangalore are entrance-based via NEET-UG (MBBS, B.Sc.), NEET-PG (MD/MS), NEET-SS (DM/M.Ch.), and MET (M.Sc.). The process is managed through MCC Counseling for Deemed Universities or MAHE for M.Sc.:

Application Process:

  1. Register on https://mcc.nic.in/ (MBBS, MD/MS, DM/M.Ch.) or https://www.manipal.edu/ (B.Sc., M.Sc.).
  2. Fill out the application form with academic and personal details.
  3. Submit documents: NEET admit card/scorecard, 10+2 marksheet (MBBS, B.Sc.), MBBS marksheet (MD/MS), MD/MS degree (DM/M.Ch.), B.Sc. marksheet (M.Sc.), Aadhar card, category certificate, photographs.
  4. Pay application fees: INR 1,000-2,000 (NEET-based), INR 600 (MET).
  5. Appear for NEET-UG (May 4, 2025, tentative), NEET-PG (March 2, 2025, tentative), NEET-SS (January 2025, tentative), or MET (May 2025, tentative).
  6. Participate in MCC counseling (July-August 2025) for seat allotment or MAHE counseling for M.Sc.
  7. Complete document verification at KMC Mangalore and pay fees to confirm admission.
