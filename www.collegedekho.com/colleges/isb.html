About ISB Hyderabad

Indian School of Business, also known as ISB Hyderabad, is a private institute, established in 2001. Located in Hyderabad, Telangana, ISB Hyderabad campus spans over 260 acres. The institute is an autonomous body and recognized by the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB) and EFMD Quality Improvement System (EQUIS). ISB Hyderabad is ranked 5th in Asia and 27th globally by Financial Times Global MBA Ranking 2025.

ISB Hyderabad offers a wide range of postgraduate and certificate programs in the stream of Management. Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP), PGPpro, and PGPMAX are some of the popular courses. ISB Hyderabad Admissions are based on entrance examinations and merit in qualifying examinations with work experience. The institute accepts GMAT/GRE for PGP, and merit-based selection with work experience for PGPpro and PGPMAX.

For placements, ISB Hyderabad placement rate is nearly 98%. During the placement drive of 2025, the highest salary package offered was INR 72 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 34 LPA.

ISB Hyderabad Admission 2025

ISB Hyderabad admissions are based on national-level entrance examinations and merit in qualifying examinations with significant work experience. Students seeking admission at ISB Hyderabad must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the institute and appear for the required entrance examination or submit relevant professional credentials. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for ISB Hyderabad admissions:

PGP: Candidates seeking admission to PGP must appear for the GMAT/GRE and participate in the ISB Hyderabad selection process, including essays and personal interviews.

PGPpro: Admissions to PGPpro are based on merit in the qualifying examination and a minimum of 5 years of work experience, followed by a personal interview.

PGPMAX: Admissions to PGPMAX are based on merit in the qualifying examination and a minimum of 10 years of work experience, followed by a personal interview.

AMP: Admissions to AMP are merit-based with a minimum of 8 years of work experience, followed by a personal interview.

Certificate in Business Analytics: Admissions are merit-based with preference for work experience, followed by a personal interview.

ISB Hyderabad FAQs

May I know the details about the industry-wise offers made during the ISB Hyderabad placements?

As per the ISB Hyderabad placement report for 2022 and 2021, it was observed that the highest number of job offers was made in the consulting industry with 37% in the year 2022 and 33% in 2021. The job offers in industries such as banking and e-commerce increased in 2022 as compared to the year 2021. The job offer made in the banking industry and e-commerce was 12% and 9% respectively while in the year 2021, it was 10% and 3%. There was a dip in the percentage of job offers made in the tech industry as the percentage in 2021 was 11 and it decreased to 4% in the year 2022.

Should someone still aim for ISB even if the current package is 22+ LPA?

It all depends on the mindset of the aspirants. The management courses offered at ISB Hyderabad are considered to be one of the best in India. The course curriculum is perfectly suited for working professionals. Coming to the ISB Hyderabad placements 2022, the average salary recorded was Rs 34.8 LPA. Thus, one can guarantee a higher salary package after taking admission to any one of the ISB Hyderabad courses.

Can I get into ISB without CAT?

Yes, you can take ISB Hyderabad admission without CAT scores. However, you need to have a valid GMAT or GRE score. Along with meeting the other criteria like eligibility, academic performance, and working experience, candidates need to score a minimum of 710+ marks in GMAT or 320+ in GRE.

Why is ISB Hyderabad so costly?

ISB Hyderabad is one of the top b-school with a good placement record. The courses offered are equivalent to global-level MBA. ISB Hyderabad is perfect for working professionals and has received accreditation from Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB), EFMD Quality Improvement System (EQUIS), and Association of MBAs (AMBA). Thus, it makes ISB Hyderabad one of the top b-schools in India to opt for higher studies and although the fee is high but the excellent placement opportunities at ISB Hyderabad compensate for it as the ROI is higher.

Is ISB Hyderabad better than IIM?

ISB Hyderabad has been ranked No. 1 in India in the Financial Times Executive MBA Ranking 2022. The programmes offered are PGP, PGPpro, PGPMAX and PGP MFAB, Certificate in Business Analytics and Certificate in Leadership Programme in Infrastructure Management. The courses are accredited by AMBA, AACSB and EQUIS. The 80% mean CTC recorded was Rs 32,76,366 and 80% median CTC was Rs 31,00,000 in the year 2022. On the other hand, talking about the IIMs although they are highly demanded by MBA aspirants because of its extremely high cutoffs only a handful gets a chance to pursue their education from these colleges. ISB Hyderabad is a credible institute with great rankings and it is much easier to get into ISB Hyderabad as compared to other IIMs.

Is there any scholarship available at ISB Hyderabad?

Yes, ISB Hyderabad offers various scholarships on the basis of merit and also to those in need of financial assistance. Some of the ISB Hyderabad scholarships are Dean Don Jacob's Memorial Scholarship, ISB Merit Waiver, Lakshya Scholarship for women, Aditya Shembekar Scholarship, ISB Merit by Diversity Waiver, International Diversity Waiver, ISB Need-cum-Merit Waiver, etc.

Is it difficult to get admission in ISB Hyderabad?

No, but candidates must first ensure that they meet the required eligibility criteria for ISB Hyderabad admissions. It must be noted that work experience is mandatory for all the courses except for PGP in Management for Family Business. Eligible candidates have to appear for any of the entrance exams, GMAT, GRE, NMAT, or CAT depending on the course they wish to pursue. Next candidates will be shortlisted for the second stage of ISB Hyderabad admissions process which is an interview based on the entrance exam scores, work experience and past academic records. After qualifying the interview selected candidates will have to get their documents verified and pay the fees.

Is ISB Hyderabad a good college for MBA?

ISB Hyderabad has been accredited by the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB) and EFMD Quality Improvement System (EQUIS). In order to provide global exposure to its students, it has collaborated with management institutions across the globe such as Kellogg, Wharton and London Business School. Talking about ISB Hyderabad ranking, it has been ranked 43 globally in the Economist EMBA Ranking 2020 and 6th in Asia-Pacific as per the Bloomberg Businessweek Best B-Schools 2023-24 Rankings. ISB Hyderabad placement season has witnessed tremendous growth in terms of the placement percentage of its students. As per the iSB Hyderabad placement report 2022, the average salary was Rs. 34.8 LPA and the total number of job offers made were 2072. So these insights will help you decide about the reputation of the college and its credibility.

What is the course duration of ISB Hyderabad PGPpro?

The course duration of this programme is 18 months. PGPpro is a weekend executive MBA (EMBA) equivalent course for mid-career working professionals. PGP in Management for Working Professional fee is Rs 26,86,000.

What is the minimum GMAT score needed for ISB Hyderabad admission?

Although the minimum GMAT score changes every year however candidates must have a 710+ score in GMAT for ISB Hyderabad admission 2023. Furthermore, candidates must note that there are multiple factors like difficulty level of the examination, no of test-takers and so on that influence the ISB Hyderabad GMAT cut off.

Will I get a refund of ISB Hyderabad fees if I withdraw my admission?

Yes, you will get a refund on ISB Hyderabad fees if you withdraw your admission. Candidates who have withdrawn their admission at ISB Hyderabad will be provided with a refund on a pro-rata basis.

Is there a hostel facility at ISB Hyderabad?

Yes students of ISB Hyderabad are offered hostel facilities. They are known as student villages. Each student village accommodate 130 to 210 students in a mix of four-bedroom and studio-serviced apartments. The apartments are air-conditioned, fully furnished and equipped with a cable connection, voice and data ports for telephone, campus LAN and the Internet.

Can I know the ISB Hyderabad fees for the PGP programme?

ISB Hyderabad fees for the PGP programme is Rs 29,50,000. Candidates with an undergraduate or postgraduate degree in any discipline with minimum 2 years of work experience and a valid score in GMAT/GRE can take admission in ISB Hyderabad PGP course. The fee is to be paid annually.

How are the plcaements of ISB Hyderabad?

The placements at ISB Hyderabad are very good.  As per the official ISB Hyderabad placement report 2022, the total number of offers received was 2072, out of which 32 are international offers.  The total number of recruiters was 393 and the average salary offered to the students was Rs 34.8 LPA.

ISB Hyderabad has secured rank 4 in Asia and rank 32 globally in FT Global MBA Rankings 2022. In the Bloomberg Businessweek Best B-Schools 2022-23 Rankings the institute has secured a rank 6.
