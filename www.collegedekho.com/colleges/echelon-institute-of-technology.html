About Echelon Institute of Technology

Echelon Institute of Technology (EIT), Faridabad is a private engineering institute established in 2007, affiliated to Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), New Delhi. The institute is approved by AICTE and holds NBA accreditation. It is located at Kheri-Manjhawali Road, Naharpar, Faridabad, Haryana – 121101, spread across a 17.5-acre campus.

EIT Faridabad offers six programmes — B.Tech, BBA, BCA, MBA, MCA, and M.Tech. Total first-year fees range from INR 1,46,600 for BCA to INR 2,20,700 for MBA and MCA. Admissions are conducted through GGSIPU centralised counselling via the IPU Admission Portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in. Accepted entrance exams include JEE Main, IPU CET, CAT, CMAT, CUET, NIMCET, and GATE.

The institute has recorded 10,000+ placement offers with a highest package of INR 52 LPA and an average package of INR 5.2 LPA. Over 250 companies recruit from campus, including TCS, Infosys, HCL, Google, Accenture, and Flipkart.

Campus facilities include AC classrooms, a library with 50,000+ books, sports complex, gymnasium, hostel for boys and girls, ERP system, and Google Classroom. MOUs are signed with Honeywell, CISCO, IBM, NASSCOM, and AWS. Scholarships on tuition fees range from 10% to 50% based on merit in 12th boards, JEE, CUET, and CAT/CMAT scores.

Echelon Institute of Technology Admission 2026

Echelon Institute of Technology, Faridabad admissions for 2026 are conducted through GGSIPU centralised counselling via the IPU Admission Portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in, with no separate college-level application. Accepted entrance exams include JEE Main, IPU CET, CAT, CMAT, CUET, NIMCET, and GATE depending on the programme applied for.

Echelon Institute of Technology Faridabad Admission Process 2026

Echelon Institute of Technology Admissions for all programmes are conducted exclusively through GGSIPU centralised counselling. Students must apply via the IPU Admission Portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in — no separate college-level application is accepted.

  • Step 1 — Check Eligibility Review the eligibility criteria for your chosen programme before applying.
  • Step 2 — Apply Online Apply through the IPU Admission Portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
  • Step 3 — Appear for Entrance Exam Appear for JEE Main, IPU CET, CAT, or CUET as applicable to your course.
  • Step 4 — Receive Rank / Result Check your exam rank or result from the respective exam authority.
  • Step 5 — Register for Counselling Register for IPU Centralised Counselling as per the published schedule.
  • Step 6 — Fill College & Course Choices Select EIT Faridabad and your preferred programme during choice filling.
  • Step 7 — Seat Allotment Seat allotment is done based on merit rank and preferences submitted.
  • Step 8 — Document Verification Carry original documents to EIT Faridabad for verification.
  • Step 9 — Pay Admission Fees Pay the applicable fee to confirm your admission after verification.
  • Step 10 — Report to College Report to the institute as per the date mentioned in the allotment letter.

Echelon Institute of Technology Campus

Echelon Institute of Technology, Faridabad provides facilities including AC campus, smart classrooms, 50,000+ book library, sports complex, hostel, gymnasium, ERP system, Google Classroom, MOUs with Honeywell, CISCO, IBM, NASSCOM, and AWS, along with placement support, faculty mentoring, and 20+ student clubs.

Fully Centralized Air-Conditioned Campus The entire Echelon campus is centrally air-conditioned, ensuring a comfortable learning environment regardless of the season. Students can focus fully on academics without any weather-related disruptions throughout the year.

State-of-the-Art Infrastructure with Theatre-Mode Classrooms Echelon's theatre-mode classrooms are tiered, spacious, and designed so every student gets an unobstructed view of the teaching board. The modern infrastructure replicates a professional seminar setting, making lectures more engaging and immersive.

Library with 50,000+ Books, Digital Resources & Book Bank Facility The campus library gives students access to over 50,000 books along with digital resources through the National Digital Library (NDL), E-Kumbh, and Delnet. Additional features include a Previous Year Question Paper Bank, daily newspapers, and a Book Bank Facility that provides textbooks for the entire semester.

Interactive Panel Boards in Classrooms All classrooms at Echelon are equipped with interactive smart panels that replace traditional blackboards, enabling multimedia-rich, visually engaging lectures. Faculty can annotate, display videos, and demonstrate concepts in real time, making complex topics far easier to grasp.

Modern Management Laboratories and Centre of Excellence Echelon's management labs and Centre of Excellence are purpose-built spaces where students apply theoretical concepts to simulated real-world business scenarios. These facilities support hands-on learning in areas like data analysis, marketing strategy, and business decision-making.

Robust ERP-Based Student Support System for Real-Time Academic Monitoring The college's ERP system gives students, parents, and faculty real-time access to attendance records, academic performance, and assignment data — all on a single digital platform. This transparent system helps identify academic gaps early and keeps students consistently accountable.

On-Campus Medical Facilities Echelon provides on-campus medical care with trained healthcare staff to handle routine health concerns and first-aid needs without students having to leave campus. Referral arrangements to nearby hospitals ensure that more serious medical situations are addressed promptly and efficiently.

Well-Equipped Sports Complex with Indoor and Outdoor Games The sports complex supports a wide range of indoor games including Carrom, Chess, Table Tennis, and Badminton, alongside outdoor sports such as Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Basketball, Kabaddi, and Javelin Throw. Regular inter-college and intra-college competitions are held, giving students a structured platform to showcase their athletic talent.

Numerous Clubs for Management, Culture, Digital Marketing, NSS, Innovation & Leadership Echelon runs a diverse range of clubs covering areas like management, digital marketing, social media, NSS, innovation, and leadership, ensuring every student finds a community that matches their interest. Active club participation helps students build soft skills, explore passions, and develop a well-rounded personality alongside academics.

Large Fleet of Buses for Safe Transportation Echelon operates a large, well-maintained fleet of buses covering multiple routes across the region, making daily commuting safe and stress-free for students. The supervised transportation service reduces travel burden significantly, allowing students to arrive on campus refreshed and ready to learn.

Separate & Safe Hostel Facilities for Boys and Girls Echelon provides separate, secure hostel accommodations for male and female students, equipped with essential amenities including furnished rooms, dining, Wi-Fi, and 24/7 security. Dedicated wardens and support staff ensure a safe, comfortable, and structured residential environment for outstation students.

Gymnasium to Stay Healthy The on-campus gymnasium is equipped with modern fitness equipment, offering students a convenient space for strength training, cardio, and general wellness within the campus itself. Regular physical activity is encouraged as an integral part of student life, helping reduce stress and improve focus.

Personalized & Continuous Faculty Mentoring System Every student at Echelon is assigned a dedicated faculty mentor who provides ongoing support in academics, career planning, and personal development throughout the college journey. Regular one-on-one sessions ensure early identification of challenges, consistent progress tracking, and sustained motivation from the first year to final placement.

One-to-One Faculty Mentoring Each Semester Structured mentoring sessions are conducted every semester, creating a formal and continuous channel for students to discuss academic performance, career goals, and personal growth with their assigned faculty mentor. This semester-wise approach ensures that guidance evolves with the student's changing needs and aspirations.

Industry-Academia Bridge to Match Corporate Expectations Echelon works closely with leading industry bodies and corporate professionals to ensure its curriculum and teaching approach align directly with current workplace expectations. This active collaboration integrates real industry feedback into academic processes, producing graduates who are job-ready from day one.

MOUs with Honeywell, CISCO, IBM, NASSCOM, AWS & More Echelon has signed formal MOUs with globally recognized organizations including Honeywell, CISCO, IBM Technology, NASSCOM, AWS Cloud, GTT Barclays, NIIT Academy, KVCH, Code Squad, and several others. Live Projects, Internships, Certifications & Hands-On Training via Industry Partners Through its industry partnerships, Echelon facilitates live projects, internships, certifications, and practical training programs that give students real-world exposure well before they graduate. This ensures students are not encountering workplace challenges for the first time in their first job — they've already solved them.

Regular Guest Lectures, Industrial Visits, Certifications & Workshops Echelon regularly organizes guest lectures by industry experts, alumni, and practitioners, complemented by industrial visits, certifications, and hands-on workshops throughout the academic year. Placements with Leading Companies Echelon's placement cell has built strong ties with recruiters including Google, Amazon, Flipkart, PayTM, Maruti Suzuki, Tech Mahindra, Samsung, WIPRO, British Telecom, and many more. Experiential Learning through Live Projects, Case Studies & Simulations Learning at Echelon extends well beyond classrooms, with students regularly engaging in live projects, case studies, business simulations, internships, and industry visits. Seminars, Workshops, Mock Interviews & Interview Readiness Programs Throughout the academic year, Echelon organizes seminars, skill workshops, mock interviews conducted by industry professionals, and comprehensive interview readiness programs. Transparent & Student-Friendly Grievance Handling Mechanism Echelon maintains a clear, accessible grievance redressal system where students can raise concerns related to academics, administration, or campus life and expect timely, fair resolution. A dedicated grievance committee ensures that all complaints are handled with accountability, transparency, and respect for student rights.

Highly Qualified Faculty with 70+ Ph.D Holders Echelon's faculty team includes over 70 Ph.D holders who bring deep domain expertise, industry experience, and active research contributions to the classroom. This academically strong faculty ensures that students receive rigorous, research-informed instruction that prepares them for both competitive exams and professional roles.

Vibrant Campus Life with 360° Education Echelon's campus life goes well beyond academics, with students actively participating in cultural fests, management events, sports meets, clubs, and leadership activities throughout the year. This balanced environment builds confidence, creativity, teamwork, and life skills that round out every student's college experience.

Echelon Premier League – Inter-College Cricket Tournament The Echelon Premier League is a flagship inter-college cricket tournament that brings together student teams in a high-energy, competitive sporting event on campus. The tournament promotes sportsmanship, teamwork, and campus camaraderie while giving cricket enthusiasts a professional-style platform to compete.

Freshers' Party & Star Night with Renowned Celebrities New students are welcomed through a lively Freshers' Party that serves as a warm icebreaker, helping them connect with seniors, peers, and faculty in a festive atmosphere. The Star Night takes celebrations further with performances by well-known celebrities, creating an unforgettable experience for the entire student community.

Convocation Echelon's annual Convocation ceremony is a formal and emotionally significant event that celebrates the academic achievements of graduating students in the presence of their families and distinguished guests. The occasion features inspiring addresses from eminent speakers and serves as a proud milestone for students, faculty, and the institution alike.

Mega Job Fair Organized Every Year Echelon organizes a large-scale Mega Job Fair annually, bringing together numerous leading companies and recruiters to offer students direct employment opportunities across diverse sectors and roles. Students are coached and prepared in advance, making the fair an effective and high-conversion platform for campus placements.
