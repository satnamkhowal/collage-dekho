About PCMT Kolkata

Pailan College of Management and Technology is situated in Kolkata in West Bengal state of India. Established in 2002, PCMT is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. Pailan College of Management and Technology offers 13 courses across 6 streams namely Management, IT, Engineering, Hotel Management, Media and Mass Communication..Popular degrees offered at Pailan College of Management and Technology include B.Tech, BBA, BCA, BTTM, BSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Pailan College of Management and Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Pailan College of Management and Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Pailan College of Management and Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.

Can bihar diploma students apply for wbjee?

R
Rupsa •  Nov-15-2024

Dear Student,

Unfortunately, diploma students from Bihar or any other state with 10th marks are not eligible for WBJEE exam. This is because the minimum WBJEE 2025 eligibility criteria requires that all students (from West Bengal or other states) must have completed their 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as main subjects to get admission to B.Tech degree.
