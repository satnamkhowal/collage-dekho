About Sanjay Rungta Group of Institutions

Sanjay Rungta Group Of Institutions was established in 1999 as a private institute in Bhilai, Chhatisgarh. The institute is approved by the All India Council of Technical Education. The Group consists of two campuses and four different institutes: Rungta Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Rungta College of Dental Science and Research, Rungta College of Science and Technology, and Rungta College of Engineering and Technology, Bhilai. These private institutions are affiliated with the Chhattisgarh Swami Vivekanand Technical University.

The college provides quality education in diversified fields. Sanjay Rungta Group Of Institutions course list include undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and PhD programmes in management, engineering, computer applications, pharmacy, education, ITI, and medical. Admission to these courses required an online application procedure and meeting the eligibility criteria. SRGI accept entrance exams like CAT, MAT, XAT, GMAT, JEE, CG PPT, etc. SRGI Bhilai offers over 83 programmes across all campuses. Applications for admission may be downloaded from the website and submitted along with the necessary application fee. Sanjay Rungta Group of Institutions include two state-of-the-art campuses, and over 150 acres of lush green campus.

The institute has signed MoUs with more than 60 reputed organisations. Sanjay Rungta Group Of Institutions campus has state-of-the-art infrastructure, including separate AC hostels for boys and girls, a gymnasium, well-maintained sports infrastructure and a highly conducive environment for teaching and learning. The institute also has a 100% placement assistance policy and a paid internship.

Sanjay Rungta Group of Institutions FAQs

What are the specialisations offered in M.Sc?

The university offers a postgraduate programme of two years under Rungta College of Science & Technology (RCST), Durg. SRGI offers M.Sc in four specialisations, including microbiology, physics, computer science, and biotechnology. SRGI Bhilai M.Sc fees is Rs 27,000 per annum. For M.Sc admission, candidates must have graduated from a recognised university in their relevant discipline.

How many colleges are there under the Sanjay Rungta Group of Institutions?

There are eight colleges under the Sanjay Rungta Group of Institutions, namely, Rungta College of Science & Technology (RCST), Durg, affiliated to Hemchand Yadav University, Durg, and accredited by NAAC, Rungta College of Dental Sciences & Research (RCDSR), Bhilai, affiliated to Chhattisgarh Ayush & Health Sciences University, Raipur, and recognised by Dental Council of India, RSR Rungta College of Engineering & Technology (RSRRCET), Bhilai, affiliated to CSVTU, Bhilai, and approved by AICTE, Rungta Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, affiliated to CSVTU, Bhilai, and approved by PCI, Rungta Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences & Research, affiliated to CSVTU, Bhilai, and approved by PCI, Rungta Institute of Science & Management, affiliated to Hemchand Yadav University, Durg, and Rungta Private ITI affiliated to National Council for Vocational Training.

How many pharmacy courses are offered by Sanjay Rungta Group of Institutions?

SRGI Bhiali offers B.Pharm and D.Pharm programmes. These courses are offered by the Rungta Institute of Pharmaceuticals Science. The intake of B.Pharm and D.Pharm is 100 and 60, respectively. The institute was established in 2019, approved by the Pharmacy Council of India, recognised by the Government of Chhatisgarh, and affiliated with Chhattisgarh Swami Vivekanand Technology University, Bhilai. SRGI Bhiali B.Pharm and D.Pharm fee is Rs Rs 74,975, and Rs 58,670, respectively.
