About Dr. Sudhir Chandra Sur Institute of Technology and Sports Complex

Dr Sudhir Chandra Sur Institute Of Technology And Sports Complex was established in 2009 and is a private college in Kolkata, West Bengal. The institute aims to create leaders of tomorrow in the field of innovation and technology. SurTech is affiliated with The Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology MAKAUT, WB (UG & PG), West Bengal State Council of Technical & Vocational Education and Skill Development WBSCTVESD (Diploma) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). 

The SurTech courses are offered in Engineering and Technology. The courses are at undergraduate, postgraduate and diploma levels. The admissions at SurTech are subjected to candidates qualifying the admission eligibility criteria. There are various exams that are accepted for admission at SurTech Kolkata.

SurTech placement provides full support to its students to help them get the best out of their placements. The SurTech placement and training cell conducts various workshops, training, career development sessions, seminars and guest lectures for the professional development of the students. The placement cell is also responsible for inviting various companies to the institute for the recruitment process.

Dr. Sudhir Chandra Sur Institute of Technology and Sports Complex facilities include a well-stocked library, language labs and other specialised labs, a green campus, a sports complex, smart classrooms, engineering labs and workshops, an auditorium, canteen and cafeteria, wifi enabled campus, photocopy facilities and hostel accommodation.

Rupsa •  Nov-15-2024

Dear Student,

Unfortunately, diploma students from Bihar or any other state with 10th marks are not eligible for WBJEE exam. This is because the minimum WBJEE 2025 eligibility criteria requires that all students (from West Bengal or other states) must have completed their 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as main subjects to get admission to B.Tech degree.
