What is the fee for IIT Indore MSc Biotechnology through GAT B and what is the hostel fees structure?

S
Shikha Kumari •  Jul-18-2024

Hi,

The tuition fee for IIT Indore MSc Biotechnology through GAT B is Rs 5,000 per semester. Along with the tuition fee, you need to pay per semester registration fee of Rs 6,300 and one time admission fee of Rs 8,100 (for first year students). It also includes a refundable security deposit of Rs 10,000. The hostel fee for IIT Indore MSc students is Rs 16,000 per semester.

What rank is required to clear IIT JAM mathematics for admission at IIT Indore?

A
Aditya •  Jun-15-2023

Dear student, the IIT Indore M.Sc Mathematics cutoff 2023 (Round 3) is 232 (opening rank) and 407 (closing rank). The round 2 closing rank was 308 for the general category and 521 for OBC category students. Candidates with ranks below the IIT Indore JAM 2023 closing ranks are eligible for admission to the M.Sc in Mathematics programme.

Does IIT, indore offers distance m pharma?

D
Diksha Nautiyal •  May-27-2020

Dear Student, 

No, the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Indore does not offer the Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm) programme. The Centre for Biosciences and Biomedical Engineering (BSBE) was initiated at IIT Indore in the year 2012 to promote research and development in areas of Biosciences, Biomedical Engineering and Bioengineering but to get admission opportunity here, you must already hold an M.Pharm degree or M.Tech or MSc in Biological Sciences or any equivalent degree from a recognised university.
