About Lingaya's Vidyapeeth

Lingaya's Vidyapeeth, also known as Lingaya's is a Deemed University, established in 1998. Located in Faridabad, Lingaya's Vidyapeeth campus spans over 40 acres. The college is affiliated to Not applicable (Deemed University) and recognized by the UGC, AICTE. Lingaya's Vidyapeeth is ranked Not specified by NIRF under Private in Not specified. NIRF ranking: Not specified

Lingaya's Vidyapeeth offers a wide range of various programs in the streams of Private. B.Tech, MBA, B.Pharma are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Lingaya's Vidyapeeth are based on Merit-based and entrance exams. The college accepts JEE Main, HSTES for B.Tech, CUET, HSTES for B.Pharma, CUET for B.Sc.

For placements, Lingaya's Vidyapeeth placement rate is 85%%. During the placement drive of 2024, the highest salary package offered was INR Rs 32 LPA LPA and the average package stood at INR Rs 8 LPA LPA.

        About Lingaya's Vidyapeeth

        Lingaya'S Vidyapeeth, formerly known as Lingaya's Institute of Management & Technology, was founded in 1998 as an affiliated college to Maharishi Dayanand University, Rohtak. For years, the institute has been praised/recognized/awarded by various government bodies for its academic excellence & innovative industry-academic curriculum. The Institute was also upgraded to Deemed to be a University Status in 2009 by the Ministry of Human Resources Development and University Grant Commission in recognition of its contributions to technical and quality education.

        Dr Picheswar Gadde is the Chancellor at Lingaya’s Vidyapeeth, whose sole objective is to make education accessible to all, irrespective of their economic or social status. He is a seasoned academician & mentor and has served at various levels both in industry & academia. He has been working to elevate the teaching & learning standards at Lingaya’s Vidyapeeth by focusing more on problem-solving and practical skills based on hands-on experience.

        The university offers more than 130 courses from all the schools with an average fee of Rs 1 lakh per annum. Lingaya’s objective is to facilitate and promote excellence in Education and Research with a focus on Professional Higher Education, and the Vidyapeeth has been consistently working in order to cater for the diversifying needs of contemporary and emerging Global and Industrial needs. 

        The Vidyapeeth is located within the National Capital Region (NCR), Delhi, which has a sprawling campus situated in the serene green pastures and pollution-free sylvan surroundings on a total built-up area of over 1.2 million square feet. The Vidyapeeth campus comprises Academic blocks, Air-Conditioned libraries, Workshops, Laboratories, Auditorium, Student Activity Centre, Play Grounds, Gymnasium, Hostels and several other facilities.
