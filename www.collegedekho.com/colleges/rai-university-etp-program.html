Rai University Ahmedabad Job Ready Degree

In this rapidly changing and technologically advanced world getting a job is a common concern of every student. To tackle this, Rai University Ahmedabad has introduced Job Ready “Assured” courses. The programmes are offered at UG level. B.Des in UX (IXP), B.Tech IT in AIML (IXP), B.Tech CS in Full Stack Development  (IXP), BBA in Applied HR (IXP), BBA in Fintech (IXP) etc. are some of the Job Ready courses offered by Rai University Ahmedabad. It will cost you Rs 67,000-70,000 annually for BTech courses. For BBA courses annual fee is Rs, 70,000 respectively.

Rai University Ahmedabad Job Ready Courses 2026 - Admissions 

Admission to all Job Ready “Assured” courses at Rai University Ahmedabad is done on merit basis. The admission procedure is simple and direct, it does not involve any entrance exam. Admission seekers can get admission by paying “Assured” courses fee and meeting the eligibility.
