About Glocal University

Glocal University, also known as GU, is a private university established in 2012. Located in Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh, the Glocal University campus spans over 350 acres. The university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), AICTE, AIU, NCTE, BCI, and PCI. Glocal University is ranked 165th by NIRF under the University category and 110th in the Pharmacy category in 2024.

Glocal University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and doctorate programs in the streams of Engineering, Management, Pharmaceutical, Arts, Commerce, Science, Law, and more. B.Tech, MBA, BCA, and B.Sc are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Glocal University are based on merit in the qualifying examination or entrance examinations. The university accepts JEE Main for B.Tech, CAT/MAT/XAT for MBA, NEET for B.A.M.S, and CLAT for law courses.

For placements, Glocal University has a strong record, with the highest salary package offered during the 2024 placement drive being INR 18 LPA and the average package at INR 4 LPA.

Glocal University Admission 2025

Glocal University admissions are based on national-level entrance examinations or merit in the qualifying examination. Students seeking admission at Glocal University must fulfil the eligibility criteria set by the university and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the criteria for Glocal University admissions:

  • B.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech must appear for the JEE Main and participate in the JoSAA counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to the MBA must appear for the CAT/MAT/XAT and participate in the university’s counselling process.
  • B.A.M.S: Candidates seeking admission to B.A.M.S must appear for the NEET and participate in the AYUSH counselling process.
  • BA LLB: Candidates seeking admission to BA LLB must appear for the CLAT and participate in the CLAT counselling process.
