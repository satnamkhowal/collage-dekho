About University of Mysore

University of Mysore, India's sixth-oldest public university established on July 27, 1916, by Maharaja Krishnaraja Wadiyar IV, operates across three campuses Manasagangotri (PG hub), Crawford Hall (administration), and satellite sites in Hassan and Mandya serving over 1,20,000 students through 85 postgraduate departments and 226 affiliated colleges. Reaffirmed NAAC 'A' grade (CGPA 3.47, valid till September 2026) and ranked 71st in NIRF Universities 2025 (down from 54th in 2024 due to research metrics), it implements NEP 2020 with 290+ programs. UG admissions 2025-26 extended via UUCMS till November 2025 (50% Class 12 merit, ₹300 fee, 1,20,000 total seats including affiliates like Maharaja's with 200 BA spots); PG via PGEE/PGCET (50% graduation, 15,500 seats, 2nd round open December 2025). Fees remain affordable BA ₹5,870 (60 departmental seats), MSc ₹20,073 with full waivers for SC/ST incomes below ₹2.5 lakhs via Post Matric portal. CPDPS drives 70% placement rate, MBA average ₹4.5 lakhs (highest ₹46.5 lakhs reported 2025). Campus boasts 4,000 acre Wi-Fi-enabled Manasagangotri, 800 hostel beds (₹15,000/year), and 800,000-volume library. This guide answers University of Mysore 2025 ranking drops, 2026 admission extensions (UG applications reopened November 7), seat intake, 85% BA cutoffs, and edges over JSS AHER (NIRF 21st 2025, NAAC A++, BE ₹2.77 lakhs first-year, PGCET till December). University of Mysore FAQs

Is University of Mysore has received recognition?

The University of Mysore, India's sixth oldest university founded in 1916, enjoys full UGC recognition under Sections 2(f) and 12(B), validating its degrees for national and international equivalence. AICTE approval extends to engineering and management programs like B.Tech, M.Tech, and MBA, upholding curriculum and infrastructure standards for 2025-26 intakes. This dual endorsement bolsters research funding, student exchanges, and placement rates (average 70%+). Distance education modes hold UGC-DEB approval too. Confirm status on ugc.gov.in and aicte-india.org for assured credibility and career prospects.

What is the address of University of Mysore Mysuru?

Nestled in the heart of Mysuru, Karnataka, the University of Mysore's main campus sprawls across Crawford Hall at Vishwavidyanilaya Karya Soudha, K.G. Koppal, Saraswathipuram, Mysuru-570005. This iconic Indo-Saracenic edifice, built in 1948, houses administrative offices amid lush 80-acre grounds blending heritage with modern facilities like libraries and labs. Easily accessible via Mysuru Airport (10 km) or railway station (2 km), it fosters a vibrant academic hub. For 2025-26 visitors, parking and Wi-Fi are available; explore via uni-mysore.ac.in maps.

What is the NIRF Ranking 2025 of University of Mysore?

In the NIRF Rankings 2025, the University of Mysore secured the 71st position in the University category with an overall score of 49.98, reflecting its consistent academic excellence and research output. Additionally, it ranked 20th among State Public Universities with a score of 58.00, highlighting its strong performance in teaching, learning resources, and outreach activities. These rankings reaffirm the university’s reputation as one of India’s leading higher education institutions, recognized for its quality education, faculty expertise, and contribution to innovation and research across various disciplines.

What all courses are offered by the University of Mysore?

The University of Mysore offers a wide range of undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and research programs, along with several diploma and certificate courses across diverse disciplines. These include humanities, arts, science, law, commerce, information technology, business management, and education. The curriculum is designed to balance theoretical knowledge with practical learning, fostering intellectual growth and skill development. Each program is regularly updated to align with current academic and industry trends. The university’s multidisciplinary approach ensures that students gain a comprehensive education, preparing them for professional excellence and higher research opportunities.

Does the University of Mysore provides global exposure to students?

Yes, the University of Mysore provides excellent global exposure to its students through academic collaborations and exchange programs with several international universities. These partnerships aim to promote research, innovation, and cultural exchange. Notable collaborations include tie-ups with Berlin Technical University, Germany, and Huanghuai University, China, among others. Through these alliances, students and faculty benefit from joint research projects, international conferences, and study abroad opportunities. Such global connections enhance the learning experience, broaden perspectives, and prepare students to compete and collaborate in an increasingly globalized academic and professional environment.

How many affiliated colleges are there under the University of Mysore?

The University of Mysore offers a diverse range of academic programs, including undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), diploma, postgraduate diploma, and certificate courses across various disciplines such as Science, Arts, Journalism, Engineering, and more. These programs are delivered through the university’s extensive network of around 228 affiliated colleges and 85 postgraduate departments, ensuring wide academic reach and accessibility. The curriculum is designed to meet modern educational and industry standards, fostering research, innovation, and skill development. This comprehensive academic structure enables students to pursue higher education in both traditional and emerging fields.

Is there any placement cell at University of Mysore campus?

Yes, the University of Mysore has established a Centre for Proficiency Development and Placement Services (CPDPS) to support students in enhancing their skills and employability. The center focuses on developing students’ technical knowledge, communication abilities, and overall personality through training programs, workshops, and career counseling. In addition to skill enhancement, it actively collaborates with industries and organizations to facilitate campus placements and internships. By bridging the gap between academics and industry requirements, CPDPS helps students build confidence, improve professional competency, and secure rewarding career opportunities after graduation.

Are there any scholarships awarded by the University of Mysore?

The University of Mysore offers a wide range of scholarships to deserving students, supported by both the state and central governments. These include subject-based, endowment, sports, minority, and single girl child scholarships. The university also provides specialized financial aid for students with physical disabilities, children of military personnel, and political sufferers. Additionally, candidates from socioeconomically disadvantaged backgrounds are eligible for full fee concessions. These scholarship programs aim to promote equal educational opportunities, encourage academic excellence, and support financially challenged students in pursuing higher education without economic barriers.

What are the facilities offered at the University of Mysore campus?

The University of Mysore boasts a well-equipped central library with a collection of over 6 lakh books and 75,000 journal volumes, offering extensive academic and research resources. In addition, the library provides access to 18,000 e-books, 7,500 e-journals, and an online public access catalogue (OPAC) for easy digital reference. The main campus also features excellent infrastructure, including separate hostels for male and female students, an amphitheatre, an auditorium, a cricket stadium, tennis courts, and a swimming pool, ensuring a vibrant academic and extracurricular environment for holistic student development.

How are the hostel facilities at University of Mysore?

The University of Mysore provides easily accessible hostel facilities; however, accommodation is primarily reserved for students coming from distant locations. Priority is given to those enrolled in full-time undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programs. Students pursuing two-year PG Diploma courses may be allotted hostel accommodation based on availability. However, those enrolled in short-term Diploma or Certificate courses are not eligible for hostel facilities. The university ensures a safe and comfortable living environment with essential amenities, fostering academic focus and personal growth for students residing on campus.
