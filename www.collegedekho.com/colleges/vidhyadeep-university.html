About Vidhyadeep University

Vidhyadeep University was founded in 2011 by Trustee Shree Jayantibhai S Patel, Vidhyadeep Charitable Trust in Gujarat. The university is approved by UGC, AICTE, INC, NCTE, PCI, BCI, NCH, GNC, AIU, and GSCPT. 

Vidhyadeep University offers 80+ courses at Diploma, UG, PG, and PhD levels across 15 institutes covering Engineering & Technology, Management, Science, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Nursing, Law, Education, and Homoeopathy.

Admission to B.Tech is through ACPC Gujarat counselling using GUJCET 2026 or JEE Main 2026 scores. BHMS and B.Sc Nursing require NEET UG 2026. MBA admission is through CAT/MAT/CMAT, followed by a Personal Interview.

Vidhyadeep University Admission 2026

Vidhyadeep University admission 2026 is open for UG, PG, Diploma, and PhD courses in Engineering, Management, Science, Pharmacy, Nursing, Law, Education, and Homoeopathy. The university offers admission through two methods. B.Tech and Pharmacy admissions are done through ACPC counselling, while most other courses are offered through direct university-level admission.

For B.Tech, the university accepts GUJCET 2026 and JEE Main 2026 scores. BHMS and B.Sc Nursing admissions require NEET UG 2026 scores, and MBA admissions accept CAT, MAT, or CMAT scores. Students can apply in both online and offline modes. Reserved category students get a 5% relaxation in the minimum eligibility marks for all programmes.

How to Apply for Vidhyadeep University Admission 2026

Vidhyadeep University accepts applications in both online and offline modes. The main online admission portal is available on the university website. For B.Tech and Diploma courses, students must also apply on the ACPC portal because applying only on the university website is not enough for ACPC-based admissions. Below are mentioned step by step guidline:

  • Open vidhyadeepuni.ac.in and click 'Apply Now' or 'Admission 2026-27' on the homepage.
  • Register with your mobile number and email ID. An OTP is sent for verification. Set a password for your account.
  • Log in and select the Faculty and Programme you wish to apply for.
  • Fill Section 1: Personal Details — full name (as per 10th certificate), date of birth, gender, category, nationality, domicile state, and contact details.
  • Fill Section 2: Academic Details — qualifying exam board, year of passing, percentage, and subject-wise marks for relevant subjects.
  • Fill Section 3: Entrance Exam Details — GUJCET rank/percentile (B.Tech/B.Pharm), NEET score (BHMS/Nursing), CAT/MAT/CMAT score (MBA), or GATE score (M.Tech). If no entrance exam is required, skip this section.
  • Upload documents: passport-size photo (JPG, max 100 KB), signature (JPG, max 50 KB), 10th marksheet (PDF, max 500 KB), 12th marksheet (PDF, max 500 KB), qualifying degree certificate for PG (PDF, max 1 MB), Aadhaar card copy (PDF, max 500 KB), and category certificate if applicable (PDF, max 500 KB).
  • Pay the application fee (see table below) via Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, or UPI. Save the payment confirmation.
  • Review and submit the form. Download the application reference number and acknowledgement receipt. Keep it for all future correspondence.

Vidhyadeep University FAQs

What is the eligibility criteria for the Master of Nursing at Vidhyadeep University?

M.Sc Nursing is a 2-year full-time postgraduate course recognised by the Indian Council of Nursing with an annual intake of 40 students. For Vidhyadeep University M.Sc Nursing, the candidate should be a registered nurse, registered midwife, or equivalent with any State Nursing Registration Council. The candidate must have a bachelor's degree in B.Sc Nursing / B.Sc Hons. Nursing / Post-Basic B.Sc Nursing with a minimum of 55% aggregate marks. The candidate must also have a minimum one year of work experience after Basic B.Sc Nursing/Post Basic B.Sc Nursing. Vidhyadeep University M.Sc fees is Rs 60,000 - Rs 65,000 per annum.

Be sure to check the program-specific requirements as well.

How is the Vidhyadeep University campus and infrastructure?

Vidhyadeep University campus has a number of academic and administrative buildings, hostels, a mess hall, a library, a sports complex, and other facilities. The classrooms are spacious and well-ventilated, and they are equipped with modern teaching aids. The laboratories are well-equipped with state-of-the-art equipment, and they allow students to gain hands-on experience in their field of study. The hostels of the university are well-maintained and provide students with comfortable accommodation. The mess hall provides students with nutritious and hygienic food. The library has a wide collection of books, journals, and other resources.

Does Vidhyaddeep University offer scholarships to its students?

Yes, Vidhyaddeep University offers a number of scholarships to its students. These scholarships are based on academic merit, financial need, and other factors. Some of the Vidhyadeep University scholarships are means-cum-merit scholarships, academic merit scholarships, sports scholarships, cultural scholarships, minority scholarships, etc. Students can apply for scholarships at the time of admission or during their studies at the university. Students must submit an application form and the required supporting documents to apply for a scholarship.

What are the courses offered at Vidhyadeep Medical College and Research Centre?

Vidhyadeep Medical College and Research Centre offers a BHMS course of 5.5 years (4.5 years of study + 1-year internship) with an annual intake of 100 students. The subjects under the BHM course include human anatomy, homoeopathic pharmacy, physiology and toxicology, gynaecology & obstetric, community medicine, case taking & repertory, and many more.

How many institutes are there under Vidhyadeep University?

There are 13 institutes run under Vidhyadeep University. Some of them are Vidhyadeep Institute of Engineering and Technology (Diploma), Vidhyadeep Institute of Pharmacy (VIP), Vidhyadeep Institute of Business Administration (VIBA), Vidhyadeep Institute of Science (VIS), Vidhyadeep Institute of Law (VIL), Vidhyadeep Institute of Computer Science and Information Technology (VICIT), Shri SR Patel B.Ed College, Vidhyadeep Institute of Social Work, Vidhyadeep Institute of Physiotherapy, etc.

What is Vidhyadeep University B.Tech fees?

Vidhyadeep University fees for B.Tech is Rs 72,000 per annum. The course is offered by Vidhyadeep Institute of Engineering & Technology established in 2011. The duration of the course is 4 years, offered in regular mode. B.Tech is offered in four specialisations: computer engineering, mechanical engineering, civil engineering, and electrical engineering. The fees can be paid yearly/semester-wise or lump sum.

Go to the desired programme you want to apply for and carefully go through the eligibility criteria to ensure you meet the eligibility requirements of the particular programme. Fill out the information in the application form and submit it after paying the application fee, as mentioned. Once you have submitted your application form, you will receive an email confirmation. The university will then review your application and contact you if you are selected for an interview.

Vidhyadeep University application process is online and can be found on the university's official website. Candidates must submit the application form with the required documents, such as their academic mark sheet, identity proofs, caste certificate (for SC/ST), transfer certificate, and passport-size photographs.
