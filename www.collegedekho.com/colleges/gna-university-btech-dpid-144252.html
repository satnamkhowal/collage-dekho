About GNA University B.Tech

GNA University B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at GNA University Phagwara, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have successfully completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board or its equivalent with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they must have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 50% in the qualifying examination. Furthermore, candidates must qualify in any relevant state/national level entrance examination, such as JEE Main or Punjab Technical University Entrance Test (PTU CET). Meeting these criteria ensures admission to the B.Tech. program at GNA University Phagwara, providing students with a comprehensive education in the field of technology. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 2,00,000 - 2,52,000. GNA University B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at GNA University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the GNA University B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

GNA University B.Tech Application Process

GNA University B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at GNA University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill GNA University B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of GNA University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of GNA University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the GNA University B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the GNA University B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of GNA University B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for GNA University B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
