About Parul University

Parul University, established in 2015 under the Gujarat Private Universities (Second Amendment) Act, is a private university located in Vadodara, Gujarat. It is among the top 1.8% universities in India to hold a NAAC A++ accreditation. Parul University Vadodara has also been rated with a diamond rating in the 2025 QS i-gauge ratings. With 70,500+ students and 6000+ international students from 75+ countries, the university offers 200+ programs in leading faculties.

Parul University offers undergraduate, postgraduate, PhD, diploma, & certificate programs in engineering, science, pharmacy, management, design, and health sciences. Direct admissions are available at Parul University for various UG & PG courses, including BBA, BCA, B.Com, MBA, & M.Sc. The university also accepts entrance exam scores like GUJCET/NEET/NATA/CAT/MAT/CMAT.

In 2025, Parul University Vadodara recorded participation from over 2,200 companies, with 2,500+ offers made across programmes. The highest package reached INR 60 LPA. Major recruiters included Accenture, TCS, IBM, Wipro, Capgemini, and other leading companies across sectors.

Parul University Admission 2026

Parul University course admissions for 2026 are open till May 22, 2026, for UG, PG, Diploma, and PhD programs. Candidates can apply for their preferred program through the Parul University admission portal (admissions.paruluniversity.ac.in) by paying an application fee of INR 500. Admission to government quota seats is conducted via Gujarat ACPC counselling at gujacpc.admissions.nic.in. 

Entrance exams such as JEE Main or GUJCET are accepted for BTech, NEET for MBBS/BAMS/BHMS, NATA for B.Arch, and CAT/MAT/CMAT for MBA, while for several programs like BBA, BCA, B.Com, and BSc, direct admissions are offered based on merit.

Parul University Campus

Parul University has its 250-acre main campus located in Vadodara, Gujarat, along with 2 other campuses in Ahmedabad and Rajkot. The university has all the required facilities, including a library, advanced computing labs, cafeteria, sports facilities, hostels, and more.

  • Library: The university library houses an extensive collection of academic resources, including over 24,000 books, 15,825 online journals, and 1,315 print journals, along with various digital resources for research and learning.
  • Advanced Computing Labs: Parul University features 8 specialised labs designed for advanced learning and innovation, such as the Apple Lab, AI/ML Lab, IoT Lab, Nanotechnology Lab, Extended Reality & Game Lab, Network Architecture Lab, Cloud Computing Lab, and Cyber Security Lab.
  • Practical Learning Facilities: Students gain hands-on experience through 6 dedicated practical learning centres, including the Pragya Simulation Centre, Mock Aircraft Lab, Moot Court, Fabrication Lab, Film & Journalism Studio, and Forensic Science Lab.
  • Cafeteria: The campus includes multiple food courts and dining outlets offering a wide variety of cuisines. Popular chains like Subway and Nescafe are also available within the campus premises.
  • Sports Facilities: The university promotes physical fitness and sports participation by providing facilities for both indoor and outdoor games, including a gymnasium, basketball courts, and swimming facilities.
  • Campus Life & Events: Parul University regularly organises cultural festivals, technical events, concerts, and student activities. It also conducts seminars and leadership sessions with eminent speakers like Kiran Bedi, Gaur Gopal Das, and Ashneer Grover.
  • Speakers & Guest Engagement: The university has hosted 150+ speakers through PU Talks, along with multiple leadership conclaves, academic visits, and graduation events featuring renowned personalities from diverse fields.
  • In-Campus Residency: Accommodation is available for over 27,000 students, with well-equipped hostels offering modern amenities, safety, and a comfortable residential environment.
  • Campus Hospital: A fully functional 24×7 hospital within the campus ensures immediate medical care, emergency services, and healthcare support for students and staff.

Parul University FAQs

Why is Parul University popular?

Parul University is a popular choice amongst students for numerous reasons. Their website highlights their NAAC A++ accreditation and ranking among India's top 50 private universities. Offering a plethora of diverse programs, the university also boasts connections with more than 500 companies for job placements. Practical learning is a key focus, with opportunities in entrepreneurship, research, and global exposure through international students and programs. The campus features an experienced faculty and comprehensive facilities, including healthcare services.

Is Parul University good for placements?

According to the latest placement reports for 2024 from Parul University, the outlook is very encouraging. The highest salary package reported is Rs 46 LPA, while the average stands at Rs 4.50 LPA. More than 1300 companies participated, successfully placing over 2200 students. In 2023, the university placed over 2100 students. The average salary package ranged from Rs 2.5 to Rs 5 LPA, with the highest package being Rs 23 LPA.

What facilities are available at Parul University?

Parul University stands on a 150+ acre campus area comprising large academic buildings, a residential complex, an open-air theatre, banking services, sports facilities, cafeterias, etc. The classrooms, laboratories, and library are well-maintained with modern facilities. Students are provided with exposure and hands-on experience through curricular and extra-curricular activities.

Does Parul University provide scholarships?

Yes, Parul University provides different types of scholarships such as Cultural Scholarships, Sports Scholarships and Defence Scholarships. The students who fall under these categories or fulfil the eligibility criteria receive the scholarship. The scholarship amount ranges from 25% of the tuition fee to 100% of the tuition fee depending on the criteria.

Is Parul University good for study?

Yes, Parul University is a leading private university located in Gujrat. It is the youngest private university in India to receive the NAAC A++ grade. Parul University courses are recognised by various agencies such as UGC, BCI, AICTE, PCI, NMC, Nursing Council and the Ministry of Ayush.

How can I check the merit list in GUJCET in order to check where I stand for Parul University admission ?

To check GUJCET merit for Parul University admission, aspirants are advised to follow the steps given below : 1)Go to the GUJCET result website at result.gseb.org. 2) Select the "Result" link. 3)Enter the seat number from your GUJCET hall ticket. 4)Press the "Go" button. 5)The GUJCET result will be displayed on the screen.

Candidates who are looking forward to take admission may follow the steps given below : 1.Go to the ACPC official website. 2.Click on the Merit Rank Search box.2. Enter your Application Number and the Captcha Code.3.Click on Search.

More than 450 courses are available at Parul University to aid students in advancing their careers and honing their talents. The institution provides a wide range of courses, including undergraduate, graduate, diploma, certificate, and PhD programmes in fields including engineering, medicine, humanities, pharmacy, design, and business. Parul University fee structure was developed with consideration for the fact that it is a self-funded university.

Does Parul University Vadodara offer any scholarships to its students?

What is the Parul University MBA counselling process?

The Parul University MBA counselling process is organised by ACPC every year to offer admission to the MBA programmes at Parul University. ACPC conducts the counselling process only against the surrendered seats based on the CMAT percentile obtained by candidates. It also releases the Parul University MBA cut off in a round-wise format. To be eligible for the Parul University MBA counselling process Students must have passed with a bachelor's degree in any field from an accredited university with a minimum of 50% overall marks. Candidates' scores in national-level management admission tests, such as the CAT, MAT, or CMAT, is also required.
