The admission process for NIT Surathkal’s under graduate B.Tech programs starts with the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main. Candidates aspiring to join in B.Tech for 2026 session should register for JEE Main. The application for JEE Main often opens in February. After the results are declared, students with a valid JEE Main rank participate in the centralised counselling of Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA). The centralised counselling of JoSAA that happens in June is an important stage in the admission process. During JoSAA counselling, the students with a high All India Rank rank (AIR) can choose their preferred choice of branches and institutes. Securing a seat through JoSAA is an indication that the student has been admitted to the college.

NITK Surathkal B.Tech Admission 2025

NITK Surathkal offers a full-time B.Tech for 4 years in 13 specializations, including Computer Science, Electronics and Communication, Mechanical, Civil, and Artificial Intelligence. Admission is based on the JEE Main score followed by JoSAA/CSAB counselling. The eligibility criteria is 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, securing at least 75% aggregate marks (65% for SC/ST/PwD). The total B.Tech fees is approximately INR 5,00,000.
