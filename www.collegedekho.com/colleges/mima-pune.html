About MIMA Institute of Management

MIMA Institute of Management, also known as MIMA, is a Private College established in 2005. Located in Pune, Maharashtra, the MIMA Institute of Management campus spans over 5 acres. The college is affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University and recognised by the AICTE. MIMA Institute of Management is ranked 100 for MBA by Outlook 2025 and 129 out of 235 by The Week.

MIMA Institute of Management offers a wide range of Postgraduate programs in the stream of Management. Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) in Business Administration, Agri-Business Management, and Pharmaceutical Management are some of the popular courses. Admissions at MIMA Institute of Management are based on Entrance Examinations. The college accepts CAT, CMAT, XAT, MAT, and ATMA for PGDM programs.

For placements, the MIMA Institute of Management placement rate is 100% for the PGDM programs. During the placement drive of 2024, the highest salary package offered was INR 18 LPA (International) and INR 11 LPA (Domestic), and the average package stood at INR 8.5-9 LPA.

MIMA Institute of Management Admission 2025

MIMA Institute of Management admissions are based on National Level Entrance Examinations. Students seeking admission to MIMA Institute of Management must fulfil the eligibility criteria set by the college and appear for the entrance examination. Interested students should follow the criteria for MIMA Institute of Management admissions:

  • PGDM Business Administration: Candidates seeking admission to PGDM Business Administration must appear for the CAT/CMAT/XAT/MAT/ATMA/MAH-CET and participate in the institute’s group discussion and personal interview process for seat allotment.
  • PGDM Agri-Business Management: Candidates seeking admission to PGDM Agri-Business Management must appear for the CAT/CMAT/XAT/MAT/ATMA/MAH-CET and participate in the institute’s group discussion and personal interview process for seat allotment.
  • PGDM Pharmaceutical Management: Candidates seeking admission to PGDM Pharmaceutical Management must appear for the CAT/CMAT/XAT/MAT/ATMA/MAH-CET and participate in the institute’s group discussion and personal interview process for seat allotment.

How can I apply for MAT 2025 IBT test?

I
Intajur Rahaman •  Mar-05-2025

Dear Student,

If you wish to apply for the MAT 2025 IBT test, you will be required to visit the official website of MAT 2025 @mat.aima.in. On the official website you will find the ‘Register’ button on the top left side of the page. You must fill in all the required information on the initial dialogue box and click on Register to proceed with the registration process. Once you have generated the MAT login credentials, you can access the registration form. You will also be required to select the test mode of your choice, which is the Internet-based Test (IBT), the test date, the test city, and test details. Subsequently, you must upload a recent passport-size photo and signature. Then, you will be asked to select five MBA colleges of your preference. Finally, to complete the application process you must pay the MAT 2025 application fee and enter all the necessary academic and personal data required. The MAT 2025 application process is the same for all test modes.
