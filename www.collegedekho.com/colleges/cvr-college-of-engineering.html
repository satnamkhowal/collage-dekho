About CVR Hyderabad

CVR College of Engineering, also known as CVR Hyderabad, is a private college established in 2001. Located in Hyderabad, Telangana, the CVR Hyderabad campus spans over 40 acres. The college is affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Hyderabad (JNTUH) and recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), University Grants Commission (UGC), and accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). CVR Hyderabad is ranked 101-150 in the MHRD/NIRF Engineering category in 2024. CVR Hyderabad offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in the streams of Engineering and Technology. B.Tech, M.Tech, and Ph.D. programs are some of the popular courses. Admissions at CVR Hyderabad are based on entrance examinations. The college accepts TS EAMCET and JEE Main for B.Tech, GATE and TS PGECET for M.Tech.

For placements, CVR Hyderabad placement rate is approximately 90%. During the placement drive of 2025, the highest salary package offered was INR 72 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 8.5 LPA.

CVR Hyderabad Admission 2025

CVR Hyderabad admissions are based on state and national-level entrance examinations. Students seeking admission at CVR Hyderabad must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the college and appear for the entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for CVR Hyderabad admissions:

B.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech must appear for the TS EAMCET or JEE Main and participate in the Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TSCHE) for the seat allotment process.

M.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to M.Tech must appear for the GATE or TS PGECET and participate in the Andhra University Counselling for the seat allotment process.
