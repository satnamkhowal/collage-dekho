About BITS Pilani

Birla Institute of Technology and Science Pilani, popularly known as BITS Pilani, is a private deemed university established in 1964. Located in Pilani, Rajasthan, the BITS Pilani campus is spread over 328 acres. Other than the Pilani campus, BITS has three more campuses located in Goa, Dubai and Hyderabad. BITS Pilani Rajasthan is among the first six institutions in India to receive the “Institution of Eminence” status from the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The institute has also been accredited with an A++ Grade by NAAC and was ranked 11th in NIRF Engineering rankings in 2025.

BITS Pilani offers 60+ courses, including first degree, higher degree, & PhD programs across engineering, science, pharmacy, and management fields. B.E. is the flagship program offered by the institute for which admission is done through BITSAT. The institute accepts GATE or the BITS HD Test for M.E., BAAT/CAT/XAT/GMAT for MBA, GPAT for M.Pharm., and conducts its own Ph.D. admission test and interview for doctoral programs.

The BITS Pilani placement report for 2025 has been released. According to media sources, the institute achieved an overall placement rate of 54.35% during Semester 1 across its Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad campuses. Out of 1,700 registered students, 924 received job offers from leading companies. Major recruiters included Accenture, EY, IBM, General Electric, and Volvo Eicher.

BITS Pilani Admission 2026

BITS Pilani admission 2026 is ongoing across UG, PG, and doctoral programs. The B.E. program is the most sought-after course, with admissions conducted through BITSAT 2026 — the institute’s own entrance exam. Unlike IITs/NITs, BITS Pilani does not accept JEE Main or JEE Advanced scores, and there is no management quota or reservation policy. All seats are allotted purely on merit through the BITSAT-based iteration process.

For postgraduate programs, the institute accepts GATE or conducts the BITS HD Test for M.E., BAAT/CAT/XAT/GMAT for MBA, GPAT for M.Pharm., and its own entrance test followed by an interview for PhD admissions.

BITS Pilani BE Admission Process 2026

The step-by-step process for BE admissions at BITS Pilani Rajasthan is given as follows:

  • Register online at the official BITS admission portal (admissions.bits-pilani.ac.in)
  • Fill the BITSAT application form and pay the application fee
  • Book exam slot (city, date, and session)
  • Download the BITSAT admit card
  • Appear for BITSAT 2026 (Session 1 &/or Session 2)
  • Check results and submit campus and program preferences
  • Participate in the BITS iteration rounds (online seat allotment process)
  • Accept allotted seat and pay admission fee
  • Report to the allotted campus and complete document verification

Note: Candidates appearing in both BITSAT sessions will have their best score considered for admission. Refer to BITS Pilani Admission 2026 for course-wise admission process.

BITS Pilani Campus

BITS Pilani has fully residential campuses in Pilani (Rajasthan), Goa, and Hyderabad, along with an international campus in Dubai (UAE). The BITS Pilani campus spans around 328 acres. The following facilities are available across BITS Pilani campuses:

  • Hostels: BITS Pilani campuses are fully residential, with separate hostels for boys and girls. The Pilani campus has 11 hostels, offering single and double occupancy rooms with 24×7 internet connectivity. Hostel fees is INR 32,700 per semester, with additional mess and electricity charges.
  • Library: Each campus has a well-equipped central library with access to global digital resources such as IEEE, Springer, Elsevier, and OpenAthens. Libraries support both academic and research needs.
  • Sports & Fitness: BITS campuses provide facilities for cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, tennis, badminton, squash, and athletics. Major events like BOSM (sports fest) are conducted.
  • Medical Facility: On-campus medical centres are available at all campuses, providing basic healthcare services. The Pilani campus also has tie-ups with hospitals like Medanta for advanced care.
  • Food & Dining: Multiple messes, canteens, food courts, and cafes operate within campuses, offering diverse food options for students throughout the day.
  • Clubs & Societies: There are 100+ student clubs covering technical, cultural, literary, and social interests. Key activities include coding clubs, robotics, music, dance, entrepreneurship, and social service initiatives.
  • Connectivity: All campuses are equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, LAN connectivity, and digital infrastructure to support academics and research.
  • Cultural & Technical Events: BITS Pilani hosts major national-level fests such as OASIS (cultural), APOGEE (technical), and BOSM (sports), along with regular workshops, hackathons, and competitions.
  • International Campus: BITS Pilani also operates an international campus in Dubai, offering the same degree structure and global exposure for students.

BITS Pilani FAQs

What makes BITS Pilani different from IITs?

Private, merit-only via BITSAT, flexible curriculum, zero attendance, mandatory internships. Higher fees (25 lakhs) but comparable placements (26 LPA).

Does BITS Pilani offer scholarships?

Yes, up to 80% tuition for BITSAT toppers, 40% for low-income (under 5 LPA), full waivers for board toppers.

What is the expected BITSAT cutoff for Computer Science Engineering (CSE)?

Around 327-337 for Pilani, 291-311 for Goa, 288-308 for Hyderabad (2025 estimates).

What is the total fee for a B.E. degree at BITS Pilani?

Around 25,76,000 INR over 4 years: 60,500 admission, 2,75,000/semester tuition, 18,800/semester hostel (Pilani).

What is the NIRF ranking of BITS Pilani?

NIRF 2025: 7th in universities, 11th in engineering, 2nd in pharmacy.

What are the placement statistics for BITS Pilani in 2025?

2025 placements: 2,379 offers, 90% placed, average 26 LPA, highest >1 crore. Top recruiters: Google, Micron.

How can I get admission into the B.E. program at BITS Pilani?

Clear BITSAT 2025 with 75% in PCM (12th grade, 60% each subject). Apply January 2025; board toppers get direct entry.

Does BITS Pilani have AC classrooms?

Yes, BITS Pilani has air conditioning facilities for all classrooms, administrative offices, laboratories and lecture theatres. These centrally air-conditioned facilities are the part of the main building of the BITS Pilani Hyderabad campus. Likewise, at the BITS Pilani Goa campus too there are air-conditioned classrooms and other academic infrastructure.

Can I get a scholarship in BITS Pilani?

Yes, BITS Pilani provides several scholarships with up to 100% tuition fee waiver for meritorious student category, academic performance, BITSAT score, JEE scores, and for any other financial needs. Approximately thirty percent of students are offered tuition fee reductions of 15% to 100%. There are several scholarship opportunities for first-degree and higher-degree students.

How is the placement of CSE in BITS Pilani?

A high number of CSE students from BITS Pilani have been offered lucrative job offers in recent years with the highest domestic CTC touching a peak of Rs 60.8 LPA. The placement process is quite smooth, and there are many companies which approach the institute for placements.

How to get admission in BITS Pilani after 12th?

To get admission in BITS Pilani after 12th, you must appear for BITSAT exam and qualify the same along with having at least 75% or above marks in 12th with 60% and above in PCM or PCB. If you are an All India Topper or State Topper then you need not to appear for the BITSAT, you can get directly admitted to the courses of your choice (except PCB toppers who can only opt for B.Pharm).

Is BITS Pilani better than IIT?

BITS Pilani is one of the well-known private colleges, however, IITs are considered superior as a whole. IITs have a more rigid admission criterion and are government-aided institutions, charging lesser fees. On the other hand, BITS Pilani has a flexible system of academics, dual degree and the ‘zero attendance’ policy which some students might prefer. It all comes down to personal preference based on one’s academic preference and values.

When was the college , BITS Pilani established ?

BITS Pilani was established in the year 1964.

Is BITS Pilani a government college?

No, it’s a private deemed university under Birla Education Trust, recognized as an Institution of Eminence.

Are there any sample portfolios available for BITSDES?

A
Aarushi Jain •  Apr-01-2025

Dear Student, 

When you are making your portfolio for BITS Design School (BITSDES), keep in mind that although the school does not offer sample portfolios, it does give a lot of direction to enable you to make your own. The most important thing is to concentrate on demonstrating a combination of project diversity, depth, and solid technical skills. To assist you in this regard, BITSDES has posted an effective YouTube video on "How to Make a Portfolio for BITS Design School" from which you'll get tips and suggestions for communicating your skills and creativity successfully.

Apart from the official advice, you may also learn valuable insights from online forums where previous applicants post their experiences. For instance, one applicant submitted product designs, pencil drawings, and computer artwork as part of their portfolio. Although these instances may provide you with valuable insights, keep in mind that they are individual experiences and not official advice from the school.

To get a wider perspective, you can also look at general resources on building design portfolios. There are many websites that provide articles and advice on making instructional design portfolios, which, although not BITSDES-specific, can still give useful tips and ideas for your portfolio creation.
