Most Commonly Asked Questions on JMI UG Admission 2026

How many B.Tech seats are available at JMI 2026?
As per Jamia Millia Islamia admissions 2026, regular B.Tech programmes such as Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics & Communication, and Computer Engineering offer around 60–75 seats each. Additionally, self-financed programmes like VLSI, Data Sciences, Robotics & AI, and Civil Construction Technology also offer 60–75 seats per specialisation.
Can I get admission in Jamia without a CUET score?
Yes, Jamia Millia Islamia course admissions do not strictly require CUET for all programmes. While BA (Hons) and BSc (Hons) may accept CUET scores, many courses like BBA, BCom (Hons), B.Ed, B.Des, and BA LLB are based on the JMI Entrance Test. Does Jamia accept JEE Main for B.Tech admission?
Yes, for Jamia Millia Islamia admission 2026, B.Tech admissions are based on JEE Main scores instead of the JMI Entrance Test. Candidates must apply on the JMI portal and submit their JEE Main application number. Final selection is done through JMI-prepared merit lists based on JEE ranks and eligibility criteria.

Jamia Millia Islamia Admission Process 2026

Jamia Millia Islamia admission process usually begins in March–April, with entrance exams starting from late April. Depending on the course, candidates are admitted either through the JMI Entrance Test or through national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, NEET, or NATA. Below is the complete step-by-step admission process explained in detail:

Step 1: Online Registration and Application Submission

  • Candidates must visit the official JMI admission portal (admission.jmi.ac.in) and complete the online registration process using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  • After registration, applicants are required to fill out the application form carefully with personal details, academic qualifications, and program preferences.
  • Scanned copies of essential documents such as passport-size photograph, signature, and academic certificates must be uploaded in the prescribed format.
  • The application process is completed only after paying the application fee (INR 700 – INR 1,000), which varies depending on the course.
  • Payment can be made through multiple online modes including debit card, credit card, net banking, or UPI.

Step 2: Appear for the Entrance Examination 

  • For most programmes, candidates are required to appear for the JMI Entrance Test.
  • The exam consists of objective-type questions, and a negative marking of 0.25 marks is applicable for incorrect answers in most courses (except PhD programmes).
  • For certain professional courses, admission is based on national-level entrance exams instead of the JMI test. These include:
    • B.Tech through JEE Main
    • B.Arch through NATA
    • BDS and BPT through NEET
    • ITEP (B.Sc.B.Ed / B.A.B.Ed) through NCET
  • Candidates applying through these exams must still complete the JMI application form (except BDS via NEET counselling) and enter their exam registration details.

Step 3: Interview and/or Group Discussion (if applicable)

  • Certain programmes include an additional selection stage such as Group Discussion (GD) and/or Personal Interview (PI) to assess communication skills, subject knowledge, and overall suitability.
  • This stage is commonly applicable to courses like MBA, Mass Communication and Journalism programmes, B.Ed, M.Ed, PhD programmes, and select PG Diplomas.
  • Candidates who meet the minimum qualifying criteria (generally 30% marks in the entrance test) are shortlisted for this stage.
  • Shortlisted candidates must appear physically at the JMI campus and carry self-attested copies of all required documents, including academic certificates and identity proof.
  • The final merit in such programmes is prepared by considering both entrance test scores and GD/Interview performance.

Step 4: Merit List Release

  • After completion of all evaluation stages, the university releases course-wise merit lists on its official admission portal.
  • The merit list is prepared based on factors such as entrance exam scores or national-level exam ranks, GD/Interview performance (if applicable), and reservation criteria.
  • Candidates must regularly check the official website for updates regarding merit lists and further admission instructions.

Step 5: Counselling and Seat Allotment

  • Candidates whose names appear in the merit list are required to participate in the counselling process, wherever applicable.
  • During counselling, seats are allotted based on merit rank, category, and availability of seats in the chosen programme.
  • For courses like BDS, counselling is conducted through centralized authorities such as the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), where candidates must select JMI during the choice-filling process.

Step 6: Document Verification

  • After seat allotment, candidates must complete the document verification process at the university campus within the specified timeline.
  • The documents required for Jamia Millia Islamia admission 2026 include the following. Candidates must carry original documents along with self-attested copies.
    • Class 10 and 12 mark sheets
    • Graduation certificates (for PG courses)
    • Entrance exam scorecard and admit card
    • Valid ID proof
    • Category certificate (if applicable)

Step 8: Fee Payment and Final Admission Confirmation

  • The final step involves payment of the admission fee within the stipulated deadline to confirm the allotted seat.
  • JMI provides flexibility in fee payment through online modes as well as designated bank branches (Indian Bank / Bank of India on campus).
  • Admission is confirmed only after successful document verification and fee payment, after which the candidate is officially enrolled in the program.

Before starting the admission process, applicants can also go through Jamia Millia placements report to get an idea of the salary packages offered.

Jamia Millia Islamia Application Process 2026

The Jamia Millia Islamia admission form 2026 is available only online at the JMI admission portal: admission.jmi.ac.in. There is no physical/printed application form. The application process has two phases: Registration and Entrance Application Submission.

  1. Visit the JMI admission portal at admission.jmi.ac.in.
  2. Register by entering your basic profile. Keep ready: a valid email ID, a passport-size photograph in JPG format (max 100 KB, white background), and a scanned signature in JPG format (max 100 KB).
  3. After registration, an email with a unique code and login credentials will be sent to your registered email. Verify the email through the link provided.
  4. Log in and apply for the program of your choice. A candidate can apply for multiple programmes by submitting separate forms and paying separate fees.
  5. Pay the application fee online. Payment accepted via Credit Card, Debit Card, or Net Banking only. Application fee is non-refundable.
  6. Submit the form. Download and keep a printed copy for records.
  7. For programmes based on JEE Main / NATA / NEET / NCET, enter the respective application/registration number in the JMI form before submitting.
  8. During the form editing window (April 1–6, 2026), corrections (except Name and Date of Birth) can be made through the portal.

Note: Only one application per program is allowed. Multiple submissions for the same program may lead to rejection of candidature.

Jamia Millia Islamia Reservation Policy 2026

Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) is a minority institution, so its reservation policy is different compared to other central universities. This changes the cutoff for each category. The category-wise JMI reservation criteria is given below:

CategoryReservation Percentage
Muslim Category30%
Muslim Women (within Muslim quota)10%
Muslim OBC / ST10%
Persons with Disabilities (PwD)5%
Internal (Jamia) Students5%

Key Reservation Policy Points:

  • Kashmiri Migrants: Up to 5% seats are reserved over and above total intake
  • EWS Reservation: JMI is a minority institution, so it is not required to implement 10% EWS quota, unlike many central universities.
  • Seat Transfer Rule: If seats in Muslim Women or Muslim OBC/ST categories remain vacant, they are shifted to Muslim General category. Similarly, vacant PwD or internal seats can be transferred to the General category.
  • Extra Seats (Supernumerary): Categories like sports quota or special cases may get seats over and above total intake, but this is not applicable to all courses like Engineering.

Jamia Millia Islamia Admission FAQs

How can I get admission in JMI after 12th?

To get admission in Jamia Millia Islamia after 12th, candidates must have 50–55% in Class 12. Admission is through JMI Entrance Exam or national level exams like CUET, JEE Main, NEET, NATA, CUET PG, or GATE. Final selection involves interview/counselling, document verification, and fee payment.

How can I get admission in Jamia Millia Islamia?

To get Jamia Millia Islamia admission 2026, candidates must apply online through the official admission portal and appear for the required entrance exam. Most courses require the JMI Entrance Test for admissions, while others accept JEE Main, NEET, CUET, NATA, or GATE. Final selection is based on entrance scores, merit lists, and interviews for selected programmes.

Is there any negative marking in the JMI Entrance Test?

Yes, the Jamia Millia Islamia entrance test follows a negative marking scheme. For most objective-type questions, 0.25 marks are deducted for each incorrect answer. However, this rule does not apply to PhD entrance exams. Candidates must also score at least 30% in the entrance test to qualify for further rounds like interviews.

Does Jamia Millia Islamia accept CUET for all courses?

No, Jamia Millia Islamia admission 2026 does not use CUET for all programmes. CUET scores are accepted only for selected undergraduate courses such as BA (Hons) in languages and social sciences, B.Sc. (Biotechnology/Physics), and some B.Voc programmes. Admission to various other courses is based on the JMI Entrance Test or national exams like JEE Main or NEET.

What is the last date to apply for JMI?

For Jamia Millia Islamia admissions 2026, the last date to submit the application form for UG, PG, diploma, and certificate programmes is April 10, 2026. Candidates must complete registration, upload documents, and pay the application fee before the deadline. A correction window is available from April 11 to April 13, 2026.

What is the JMI Entrance Exam date 2026?

As per the official schedule, the Jamia Millia Islamia entrance exam 2026 will commence from April 26, 2026 onwards. The admit cards are expected to be released on April 17, 2026. The exam is conducted in multiple phases depending on the course, so candidates should regularly check the JMI admission portal for updates.

Can I get admission in JMI without an entrance exam?

Jamia Millia Islamia does not offer direct admissions to its programs. Candidates are required to appear for either the JMI entrance test or national level entrance exams like CUET, NEET, GATE, NATA, CUET PG, etc., based on the program they are applying for.

What is the application fee for courses at Jamia Millia Islamia?

For Jamia Millia Islamia admission 2026, the application fees ranges between INR 700 and INR 1,000, depending on the course and faculty. For professional programmes like Engineering, Management, Law, and Mass Communication, the application fee is INR 1,000, while for other UG and PG courses it is INR 700. The fee must be paid online during form submission.

Which branch is best to get admission at Jamia Millia Islamia?

The "best" branch to get admission depends on individual interests, career goals, and aptitude. Jamia Millia Islamia offers a diverse range of courses across various disciplines, including engineering, management, arts, commerce, law, etc. It's essential to choose a branch that aligns with your interests and career aspirations.

What is the application fee for Jamia Millia Islamia admission?

The application fee for Jamia Millia Islamia admission varies depending on the course and category of the candidate. Specific details about the application fee can be found on the university's official website or admission portal.
