About NIT Calicut

Established in 1961 as Regional Engineering College (REC) Kozhikode and upgraded to NIT status in 2002 under the NIT Act, National Institute of Technology Calicut (NITC) is a premier Institute of National Importance. Spanning a 120-hectare (296-acre) lush campus 22 km from Kozhikode along the Calicut-Mukkam route, it hosts ~7,300 students across 15 departments in engineering, sciences, architecture, and management. Fully residential with 12 hostels accommodating 4,000, NITC ranks 21st in NIRF 2025 Engineering and excels in placements—CSE boasts 96.77% rate at ₹19.37 LPA average (2024 data).

BTech admissions via JoSAA 2026 post-JEE Main allocate ~1,152 seats across 10 branches, with ~50% (576) Kerala home state quota, including 183 for CSE. Eligibility: 75% in Class 12 (65% SC/ST). MTech via CCMT 2026 offers ~530 seats in 27 specializations, requiring GATE 2026 and 60% BTech (55% reserved).

Compared to CUSAT, NITC offers superior NIRF rankings and placements but higher fees (₹1.25L/year vs. CUSAT's ₹20K-50K); CUSAT provides broader seats (~3,000 UG) at lower cost.

NIT Calicut FAQs

How does NIT Calicut compare to CUSAT for 2026 admissions?

NIT Calicut is ranked 21st in NIRF Engineering 2025, offering 1,042 B.Tech seats across 11 programs with a tuition fee of Rs 62,500 per semester for general category students. The institute holds NAAC A and NBA accreditations, reflecting strong academic quality, infrastructure, and faculty. In comparison, CUSAT Kochi remains unranked in NIRF Engineering, though it provides a larger intake of 3,000 B.Tech seats at a lower fee of Rs 10,000 per semester and holds NAAC A+ accreditation. NIT Calicut stands out for its higher NIRF ranking, advanced facilities, and strong placement records, making it a preferred choice for engineering aspirants.

What is the campus size and student strength at NIT Calicut?

NIT Calicut’s sprawling 120-hectare campus accommodates 6,500 students, with 23% women, providing a vibrant academic and residential environment. The institute has 12 hostels housing around 4,000 students, offering modern facilities and Wi-Fi connectivity. Academic infrastructure includes 50+ advanced laboratories covering disciplines like VLSI, Robotics, and CAD/CAM, supporting hands-on learning and research. The central library operates 24/7, housing over 2 lakh physical volumes and 10,000+ digital resources, ensuring comprehensive access to academic materials. Combined with sports complexes, medical facilities, and student activity spaces, the campus fosters a holistic learning experience and supports both technical and personal development.

What scholarships are available at NIT Calicut for 2026?

NIT Calicut offers multiple scholarships to support economically and socially disadvantaged students. SC/ST students receive a full tuition waiver of Rs 62,500 per semester, ensuring accessible education in B.Tech and other programs. Additionally, the E-Grantz scheme provides up to Rs 50,000 per year for students from EWS families with an annual income below Rs 1 lakh. Eligible candidates must apply through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) after admission, with the application deadline set for September 2026. These initiatives promote inclusivity and financial support, allowing meritorious students to focus on academics and campus opportunities without financial constraints.

What is the NIT Calicut CSE cutoff for JEE Main 2025 Round 6?

For B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at NIT Calicut in 2025, the closing All India rank (general category) was 5,222 in JoSAA Round 6, with 8,428 home state candidates participating. This rank reflects the high demand for the 183 available CSE seats, including 89 for Kerala residents and 94 for other state candidates. The competitive cutoff demonstrates the branch’s popularity due to strong placements, excellent faculty, and advanced infrastructure. Applicants needed at least 75% in Class 12 (65% for reserved categories) along with JEE Main Paper 1 qualification, highlighting the rigorous selection criteria for one of India’s top engineering programs.

What is the highest package in NIT Calicut placements 2025?

NIT Calicut placements in 2025 were highly successful, with over 1,200 offers from 200+ companies and an overall placement rate of 90.9%. The highest package reached Rs 51 LPA, reflecting strong industry demand for its graduates. The Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) branch led the pack, with an average package of Rs 22.03 LPA and a placement rate of 96.77%. Other undergraduate and postgraduate programs also witnessed significant recruitment, with average packages of Rs 14.29 LPA for UG and Rs 11.51 LPA for PG. Top recruiters included Google, D.E. Shaw, Salesforce, and core engineering firms, demonstrating both IT and core sector opportunities.

What is the tuition fee for NIT Calicut BTech 2026 general category?

For B.Tech programs at NIT Calicut, the tuition fee for general category students is Rs 62,500 per semester, with an additional one-time fee of Rs 61,000 covering admission, caution deposit, and development charges. Students from SC/ST categories receive a full tuition waiver, making education more accessible. Hostel accommodation is optional and charged separately at Rs 12,000 per semester, providing fully residential facilities with modern amenities. This structured fee system ensures transparency and supports students from diverse backgrounds, while the combination of tuition, one-time, and hostel fees reflects the overall cost of pursuing undergraduate engineering education at a premier government-funded institute.

How many BTech seats does NIT Calicut offer for 2026?

NIT Calicut offers a total of 1,042 B.Tech seats across 11 programs in 2026, distributed equally between home state and other state candidates, with 521 seats reserved for Kerala residents. Among these, Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) is the most popular branch, offering 183 seats, of which 89 are for home state and 94 for other state students. This seat allocation ensures balanced access for local and national applicants while maintaining diversity. Other programs include Mechanical, Civil, ECE, EEE, Chemical, Production, Materials Science, Engineering Physics, and Biotechnology, accommodating a wide range of engineering specializations.

What is the NIT Calicut NIRF Engineering rank for 2025?

NIT Calicut achieved a 21st rank in NIRF Engineering 2025 with a score of 66.66, improving from its 23rd position in 2024, reflecting consistent growth in academic quality, research output, and placements. Additionally, the institute secured 2nd rank in NIRF Architecture 2025 with a score of 72.50, highlighting its strong programs beyond engineering. These rankings demonstrate NIT Calicut’s focus on excellence in technical education, innovation, and infrastructure development. With a dedicated faculty, robust student performance, and industry collaboration, the institute continues to strengthen its national reputation and attract high-performing students across multiple disciplines, including engineering, architecture, and science.

Which university is NIT CALICUT affiliated to ?

NIT Calicut is not affiliated to any university, as it is an independent National Institute of Technology (NIT) and an Institute of National Importance under the Government of India. Unlike conventional colleges that operate under state or central universities, NIT Calicut has full autonomy in designing its curriculum, conducting examinations, and awarding degrees. The institute follows guidelines set by the Ministry of Education and the NIT Council. Established in 1961 as REC Calicut and upgraded to NIT in 2002, it grants its own B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc, MBA, MCA, M.Plan, and PhD degrees, recognized nationally and internationally without any university affiliation.

When was the college , NIT CALICUT established ?

NIT Calicut was established in 1961 as the Regional Engineering College (REC) Calicut, under the joint initiative of the Government of India and the Government of Kerala. It was set up to provide high-quality technical education and promote research in engineering and technology. In 2002, the institute was upgraded to National Institute of Technology (NIT) Calicut and declared an Institute of National Importance under the NIT Act, 2007. Over the years, it has expanded its academic offerings to include B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc, MBA, MCA, M.Plan, and PhD programs, attracting students from across India and abroad.

Is NIT CALICUT a government college?

No, NIT Calicut is not a private college; it is a fully government-funded National Institute of Technology (NIT) and an Institute of National Importance under the NIT Act, 2007. Established in 1961 as the Regional Engineering College (REC) and upgraded to NIT status in 2002, it operates under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The institute offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, science, management, and planning, with strong emphasis on research, placements, and industry collaboration. Its governance, funding, and policy decisions are controlled by the central government, distinguishing it clearly from private engineering or management colleges.
