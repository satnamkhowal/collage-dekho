About Banaras Hindu University - Faculty of Science

Banaras Hindu University - Faculty of Science is situated in Varanasi in Uttar Pradesh state of India. Established in 1916, BHUFAS is a Government college. The college is accredited by NAAC. Banaras Hindu University - Faculty of Science offers 103 courses across 12 streams namely Science, Management, Medical, Arts, IT..Popular degrees offered at Banaras Hindu University - Faculty of Science include BSc, B.Voc, BFA, BPA, B.Ed..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Banaras Hindu University - Faculty of Science is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Banaras Hindu University - Faculty of Science, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Banaras Hindu University - Faculty of Science is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.

University and Faculty Overview

Overall Banaras Hindu University(BHU) resides at the heart of Varanasi, it's reputation is not shy to none of us. it stands as a beacon of acedemic excellence, nestled in serene green and huge campus which stands in solitude with rest of city but as said in the city's heart, so as soon as you enter or exit the campus you'll get an entire different vibe. University has all the course that you can name from bachelor's to phd, from engineering(iit) to medical(ims) with massive grounds for every faculty and not to forget one of the biggest library you'll find in india. The campus exudes vibrancy and diversity, fostering an inclusive community where students from all backgroung thrive. One thing that you'll find in BHU is the environment of excellence and ambition in terms of their core subject. So, if you really want to study and gain the entire knowledge of your subject matter then BHU is for you. Spending 3 years in BHU, I can tell you that it is the best you can get in terms of educational quality and opportunities mostly academic based, not telling in terms of extra curricular activities which is there but not like DU. Coming to Faculty of Science, you can ask anybody and i mean ANYBODY, BHU's faculty of Science is the best in BHU and among rest of indian universities. This faculty has been designated as Institute of Science. It has all the different department with their own lecture hall, labs, libraries, garden etc. Although if you are not a final year student then you'll take your lecture in Lecture theatre complex (LTC) which is for all students of any Hons. subject and labs in departments. The Courses which you'll get in ISc BHU are gonna be a combination of three subjects out of which one will be your Hons. assigned at the start of the course but you can change it before your final year where you'll only study this subject. The syllabus are carefully designed for each course and if you really study with discipline then you can crack various competitive exams just by studying your course material. In Nutshell, you can come here without any hesitation whatsoever if you are inclined towards acedemia and for science student, i can't emphasize it more, it is one of the best that you can get in India among central universities.
