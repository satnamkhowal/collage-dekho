About National Dairy Research Institute

National Dairy Research Institute is a Public college located in Bangalore, Karnataka.. The institute was established in 1923. National Dairy Research Institute has been recognised by ICAR . As per NIRF Ranking 2025, National Dairy Research Institute has been ranked at 2 under Agriculture and Allied Sectors category. Library,Hostel,Labs,Cafeteria,Sports Complex,Hospital / Medical Facilities,Wi-Fi Campus,Auditorium are some of National Dairy Research Institute facilities.

National Dairy Research Institute courses are offered at such as UG, PG levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include B-Tech,M-Tech,M.sc. National Dairy Research Institute admission is based on merit/ entrance exam. The college accepts ICAR AIEEA, CBSE 12th, and HBSE 12th etc. for admission. Regarding National Dairy Research Institute placements, the highest package offered in 2024 was Rs 8 LPA and the average package offered was Rs 5 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include Nestle, Amul, Cadbury etc.

National Dairy Research Institute Admissions 2025

National Dairy Research Institute Admission Process 2025

  1. Eligibility Check: Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria for their chosen program 10+2,Graduation 50%, ICAR AIEEA

  13. Income Certificate
