IEM Kolkata Campus

Institute of Engineering and Management (IEM) Kolkata has a beautiful campus which is surrounded by lush green lawns and serene environment. IEM Kolkata campus has all the modern facilities for the faculty and the students.

IEM Kolkata has a beautiful library, updated to the modern level of education. Books and References of all the departments are available at IEM, Kolkata Library. Over 2,50,000  journals, books, magazines & e-journals are kept inside the library, providing a vast resource of adapting knowledge to the students.

The campus of Institute of Engineering and Management, Kolkata provides residential facilities to the students at the campus itself. The hostel of IEM, Kolkata serves good quality food to both vegetarians and nonvegetarians. Dishes from South Indian, North Indian, Western and  Eastern cuisines are available in the delicious menu of IEM’s Mess. Separate Hostels are available for both Boys and Girls ensuring total security to the girl student.

Other Facilities include Medical Centre Sports, Seminar Hall, Laboratories, Computer Lab and Auditorium, ATM etc.

IEM Kolkata Campus FAQs

Where is the IEM Kolkata campus located?

The Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata campus is located in Salt Lake City which is also known as the IT Hub of Eastern India.

Are there any student clubs at the IEM Kolkata campus?

Yes, the IEM Kolkata campus is home to several student clubs like the Entrepreneurship Development Cell, IEM Renewable Energy club etc.

Which facilities are offered at the Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata campus?

The campus of the Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata provides all kind of facilities to its students like an auditorium, computer lab, seminar hall, laboratories, medical facilities etc.

Does the Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata offer any sports facilities?

No, the Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata does not offer any sports facilities to its students.

What kind of food is served at the hostels of IEM Kolkata?

The IEM Kolkata hostels serve all kinds of vegetarian and non-vegetarian food to the hostelers.

Are there any hostel facilities offered within the campus of Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata?

Yes, the Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata offers on-campus accommodation facilities to its students.

What are the various facilities available on campus at IEM , Kolkata ?

IEM , Kolkata offers various facilities to its students including Academic Zone, Canteen, Computer Lab, Library, Medical Facilities and more.
