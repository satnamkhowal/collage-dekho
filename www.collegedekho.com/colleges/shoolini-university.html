About Shoolini University

Shoolini University is a private university located in Solan, Himachal Pradesh, India. It was established in 2009 and is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an 'A' grade. In the QS World Rankings 2025 and THE Shoolini University is ranked as the top private university in India. In the THE World Rankings 2024 and THE Asia University Rankings 2024, Shoolini University bagged first place among the private universities in India. Shoolini University boasts and impeccable record 5000+ successful placements, achieving 100% placement rates in MBA, B.Tech and Hotel Management. With 250+ global alliances with leading universities and 1500+ patents filed, the university stands among the top innovators in India. Shoolini University campus is spread across 20 acres of land. Classrooms, laboratories, libraries, hostels, sports etc are some of the Shoolini University facilities.

Shoolini University courses are offered in a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in engineering, technology, management, pharmacy, biotechnology, and liberal arts. Some of the popular courses offered by Shoolini University include B.Tech, B.Pharm, BA, MBA, M.Tech, etc. Shoolini University admission is based on entrance exams like CLAT, NEET, JEE, CUET, SAT- India etc depending on the course. The university also conducts its own test for admission in few courses like BBA, MPhil and PhD. Regarding placements, the university has placed over 5000 students in 2023. The highest package is recorded at Rs 42 LPA. TCS, Abbot, ICICI Bank, Cipla, Accenture, Nestle are some of the top recruiters that visit the campus during the Shoolini University placements drive.

Shoolini University Solan FAQs

How is Shoolini University for CSE?

The Shoolini University BTech course is four years duration and is divided into eight semesters. The programme is delivered in regular mode and is full time. The Computer Science and Engineering specialisation is considered to be one of the top specialisations. A total of nine specialities are offered under the B.Tech CSE degree. These include Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Data Science, Graphics and Animation, Blockchain & IoT, DevOps, Game Design & AR/VR, UI/UX and Cyber Security. The Shoolini University BTech fees is Rs 1,57,459 - 3,20,000 per year. Students who graduate with a CSE degree secure placements in reputed MNCs and IT companies. Amazon, EY, Deloitte, Wipro, etc. are some of the top recruiters. The average salary package received by a BTech graduate from the university is Rs 15 LPA.

What is the Shoolini University fees for MBA?

The MBA course at Shoolini University Solan is two years long and is offered in a total of 23 specialisations. It is a full-time programme that is designed to prepare students for a successful career in business. Shoolini University MBA course is offered by the School of Business Management or the university. The specialisations offered are Biotechnology, Digital Marketing, Human Resources, Logistics and Supply Chain Management, Financial Markets, Pharma & Healthcare, Business Analytics, Agri Business, Marketing, Communications and Finance. The course is divided into four semesters. The Shoolini University MBA fees range is Rs 2,97,500 - 4,70,000.

What are the Shoolini University scholarships and financial aid options?

Shoolini University offers a variety of scholarships and financial aid options to students, such as merit scholarships, sports scholarships, cultural scholarships, need-based scholarships, and sibling scholarships.
