About Pacific School of Engineering

Pacific School of Engineering is located in Surat, Gujarat, India. PSE Surat is a private college that was founded in 2012 by the Pacific Education Group. The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) has approved the college. Pacific School of Engineering provides 14 courses in one stream - engineering. B.Tech and Diploma are two of the most popular Pacific School of Engineering degrees. The institution is affiliated with the Gujarat Technical University (GTU).  Pacific School of Engineering is a leader in research and innovation and has a strong teaching pedagogy. The Pacific School of Engineering placement options are diverse, with opportunities in both the corporate and public sectors.

Pacific School of Engineering (PSE) is located at the heart of Surat, Navsari, and Bardoli towns and has been offering engineering degree and diploma programmes since 2012. The campus is well connected to the rest of the city by a bus fleet. The classrooms are theatre-style and equipped with audio-visual equipment. Industrial tours, lectures by eminent industrial experts, tech fests, and cultural and sports activities supplement classroom instruction. PSE Surat thus promotes the overall development of engineering students.

PSE' Surat is on amission to provide a higher level of quality education and expose potential young engineers to the world of engineering through various seminars, workshops, and expert lectures. To create professional technocrats, they have taken a unique initiative for industry-institute interaction and organised programmes such as 'Symposium,' 'Industry Meet,' 'HR Talk,' and 'Start-Up India.' Pacific School of Engineering places a premium on frequent industrial visits, vocational training, and Industrial Defined Projects (IDP) to bridge the gap between industry expectations and academic offerings. PSE Surat also organises cultural events, sporting events, and tech fests and commemorates various days such as Engineer's Day, Earth Day, Independence and Republic Day, and Navratri Mahotsav.

Pacific School of Engineering Courses and Admission 2026

Pacific School of Engineering has undergraduate and diploma engineering programmes in various engineering specialisations. PSE Surat courses are approved by the AICTE and as per the industry standards. The available specialisations for the PSE Surat BE courses are chemical engineering, civil engineering, computer engineering, electrical engineering, and mechanical engineering. It also allows lateral entry for both the UG and diploma courses.

PSE Surat provides regular engineering and diploma programmes in a variety of disciplines. Mechanical, civil, electrical, computer, and chemical engineering are the engineering disciplines. PSE Surat admission to engineering disciplines is based on a candidate's JEE Main/GUJCET scores, followed by counselling. To be admitted to PSE Surat BE programmes, candidates must first pass the JEE Main/GUJCET exam, followed by a counselling process. Final admission is granted following document verification and payment of the Pacific School of Engineering admission fee. 

Pacific School of Engineering Surat Eligibility Criteria 2024 

PSE Surat BE Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicants must have passed a Class 12 or equivalent examination from a recognised board, with physics, chemistry, and mathematics as compulsory subjects.
  • Applicants must have a minimum of 45% (general category) in their most recent qualifying examination, with a minimum of 40% for SC/ST candidates.
  • Applicants must have passed the JEE Main/GUJCET with a valid score.

PSE Surat Diploma Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates must have completed Class 10 with a minimum of 50% marks from a recognised board to be admitted to the PSE Surat diploma programmes.

Pacific School of Engineering Surat Application Process 2024

Before taking part in the Pacific School of Engineering allocation process, candidates must first satisfy their eligibility.

  • Eligible candidates should go to http://www.pacific-soe.ac.in/, click on ‘APPLY NOW’, and create an account with a valid email address.
  • The authorities will notify the selected candidates of the time and location of the test and interview.
  • Qualified candidates must report to the college on the date specified by the college authorities, accompanied by the original documents.

Pacific School of Engineering Surat Application Fees 2024

To apply at PSE Surat, Candidates must pay a Pacific School of Engineering Surat application fee. Candidates may find information about the application fee on the PSE Surat website.

Pacific School of Engineering Placements

Pacific School of Engineering Surat career development cell organises campus placements for students. The PSE Surat placement cell assists final-year students with placement. The placement cell works to improve students' key competencies to prepare them to be industry-ready professionals throughout the year. The career development cell serves as a point of contact between employers and students. The PSE Surat recruitment drive is overseen by the placement cell. The placement cell at the institute organises training programmes to improve students' employability skills such as soft skills grooming, interview skills, CV creation, etc. Pacific School of Engineering placement process includes a pre-placement talk, resume distribution to companies, a preliminary shortlist, a campus recruitment process, and selected students joining the workplace.

Internships are also available at Pacific School of Engineering Surat. More than half of the students enrolled in the internship programme receive a paid internship. A pre-placement offer is made to 40% of these interns by the company where they are interning.

Pacific School of Engineering Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

Pacific School of Engineering Surat Campus

At the Pacific School of Engineering campus, students have access to various convenient amenities that make their life 'go easy' during their college days while also making learning more enjoyable. Some amenities include well-ventilated classrooms, recreational areas, a hostel for boys and girls, indoor and outdoor games, a well-stocked library, continuous internet/wi-fi connectivity, medical facilities, etc.

PSE Surat central library is well-stocked with books from all disciplines and current international and national journals. The college library operates on an open access system, with over 2,000 titles and 15,000 books. The library subscribes to national and international journals and magazines. PSE Surat campus library has an excellent selection of books with added value. In college, a library committee has been formed, with one faculty member representing each department to enrich, update, and modernise the library's resources.

Pacific School of Engineering Surat has modern theatre-style classrooms that are well-equipped with technology. Any visitor who comes to see the institute's infrastructure is enchanted and cannot help but admire the classrooms.

PSE Surat Infrastructure

The PSE Surat campus has a large modern library with an extensive collection of text and reference books, magazines, periodicals, journals, CDs, and videos. The classrooms are light and airy, net and clean, well ventilated, and, most importantly, the students enjoy the outside greenery. Expert artisans maintain the fittings and fixtures, and if necessary, maintenance work is performed regularly.

The computer centre has a sufficient number of network-ready branded desktop computers. The entire system runs on a Microsoft Windows platform. The network connects all machines to various peripherals such as printers, scanners, etc. The centre is also open outside of working hours. Most of the arrangements provided to students have real-world applications to help them understand the importance of engineering in all aspects of life.

The dining area at the institute is clean and sanitary. It caters to both students and faculty. The facility is well-equipped with all necessary kitchen equipment and serves fresh snacks, meals, chilled beverages, and ice cream. Breakfast and lunch are served at reasonable prices in the cafeteria.
