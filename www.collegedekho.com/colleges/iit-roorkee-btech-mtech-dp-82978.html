About IIT Roorkee BTech+MTech

IIT Roorkee BTech+MTech is a 5-year programme offered in 2 specialisations. To be eligible for the BTech+MTech dual degree programme at IIT Roorkee, applicants must have a valid score in JEE Advanced and must have passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination with a minimum aggregate of 75% marks in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. The annual fee for BTech+MTech is INR 294,600, comprising tuition, hostel, admission, and other charges. IIT Roorkee BTech+MTech Fee Structure 2026

The IIT Roorkee BTech + MTech fees are INR 294,600 annually. For the whole duration, the total fees are INR 1,473,000. comprising everything, from tuition to hostel and mess. IIT Roorkee also charges library and caution deposits worth INR 3000, which are refunded after completing the course. Alongside, the students also must pay INR 6000 during admission. However, the students should note that this one-time fee is not refundable.

  • The autumn semester fees are INR 155,550, while the spring semester fees are INR 139,050.
  • Annual hostel and mess fees are INR 64,500.
  • For students belonging to either SC,ST and PWD categories, and/or families earning below INR 100,000 annually, the total fees are waived off..

IIT Roorkee BTech+MTech Admission Process 2026

The IIT Roorkee BTech + MTech admission process follows the same national-level structure used for all IIT undergraduate entries. Candidates must clear JEE Main and JEE Advanced, after which seats are allotted through JoSAA counselling based on rank, category, and programme preference. The process is fully online and ensures that every candidate moves step-by-step from exam qualification to seat confirmation in a clear and transparent manner.

1. Qualifying JEE Main
A candidate must appear for JEE Main and score above the qualifying cutoff to become eligible for JEE Advanced.

2. Clearing JEE Advanced
The candidate then appears for JEE Advanced and secures a rank that meets the cutoff for IIT Roorkee’s Integrated BTech + MTech programmes.

3. Registering for JoSAA Counselling
The candidate registers on the JoSAA portal, selects IIT Roorkee’s integrated programmes, and locks the preferred choices before the deadline.

4. Checking Seat Allotment & Accepting the Seat
JoSAA announces the seat allotment results in multiple rounds; once allotted a seat, the candidate accepts it by paying the seat-acceptance fee and uploading the required documents. For the general category, the fees are INR 35,000, while for the SC/ST/PWD candidates, the fees are INR 15,000. These fees are later adjusted to the total fees of IIT Roorkee Integrated MTech.

5. Completing Verification & Final Reporting
The candidate completes the online document verification and then reports to IIT Roorkee as instructed to finish the admission formalities.

6. Paying the Remaining Institute Fees
After final confirmation, the candidate pays the remaining fees for the course, standing at INR 259,000, which completes the process of the IIT Roorkee admission and officially secures the seat.

IIT Roorkee BTech+MTech Scholarships and Financial Aid

IIT Roorkee BTech+MTech scholarships allocate INR 1,000 monthly to the top 25% of low-income students via merit-cum-means (income < INR 5 lakhs). Along with that, full tuition waivers are available for SC/ST/PWD students and for those as well who belong to families with an annual income of less than INR 100,000.

  • Only the students with an annual family income of below INR 500,000 are eligible for the merit-cum-means scholarship.

IIT Roorkee B.Tech+M.Tech Application Process

IIT Roorkee B.Tech+M.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech+M.Tech course at IIT Roorkee have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill IIT Roorkee B.Tech+M.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of IIT Roorkee and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of IIT Roorkee. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the IIT Roorkee B.Tech+M.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the IIT Roorkee B.Tech+M.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of IIT Roorkee B.Tech+M.Tech Application Form.
