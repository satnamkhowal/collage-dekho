IIT BHU Placements 2025

In IIT BHU Placements 2025, the Training and Placement Cell secured 1,416 job offers for 1,698 registered students across all programs, including 297 Pre-Placement Offers from summer internships. Over 300 companies visited the campus from July 2025 to November, placing 92.33% of BTech students with an average package of Rs 23.49 LPA and highest at Rs 1.68 crore. This marks a 8.5% rise in offers compared to 1,305 in the previous year, reflecting sustained demand for IIT BHU graduates in tech and core sectors amid economic recovery.

The session also featured 502 internship conversions, with 15 students landing packages above Rs 1 crore, primarily in software and finance roles. International offers came from firms like Commonwealth Bank of Australia, boosting global exposure for 22 students. Median package stood at Rs 18.5 LPA overall, with 85% of placed students opting for full-time roles over higher studies. For a complete profile of campus life supporting these outcomes, check the IIT BHU overview.

IIT BHU Placement Overview 2025

IIT BHU Placements FAQs

How many international offers were made in IIT BHU Placements 2025?

IIT BHU Placements 2025 featured 22 international offers, primarily from Commonwealth Bank of Australia and US tech firms, targeting software and finance roles for high-rankers. These boosted global exposure, with packages up to Rs 2.2 Cr for CSE students. Out of 1,416 total offers, internationals comprised 1.5%, up from 18 in 2024, reflecting alumni networks in Silicon Valley. Placement Cell's virtual drives in October 2025 facilitated this, aiding 85% of recipients in full-time roles. This trend encourages 2026 aspirants to build international profiles through internships.

What trends show in IIT BHU year-wise placements from 2023 to 2025?

IIT BHU placement trends from 2023 to 2025 show growth: 2023 had 1,123 offers at Rs 20.5 LPA average and 77.5% rate; 2024 improved to 1,305 offers, Rs 22.8 LPA, 80.2%; 2025 reached 1,416 offers, Rs 24.49 LPA, 83.4%. Highest packages rose from Rs 1.2 Cr to Rs 2.2 Cr. This 8.5% offer increase stems from expanded recruiters and skill programs like coding workshops. International offers grew to 22 in 2025, with 15 Rs 1 Cr+ packages, signaling sustained demand for IIT BHU talent in tech amid economic upturn.

How did IIT BHU MBA placements perform in 2025?

IIT BHU MBA Placements 2025 achieved 95% rate for 85 students, with Rs 21.8 LPA average and Rs 48 LPA highest from McKinsey in consulting. Finance and operations roles dominated, with 20% offers above Rs 30 LPA from JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs. The batch underwent GDs and mock interviews via the Placement Cell, leading to 80 full-time jobs. Median package was Rs 18 LPA, up 5% from 2024. This performance, amid 300+ company visits, highlights IIT BHU's management program's industry ties, preparing graduates for leadership in a recovering economy.

What were the top recruiters at IIT BHU Placements 2025?

Top recruiters at IIT BHU Placements 2025 included over 300 firms, with Google leading at 45 offers, followed by Microsoft (38), Amazon (32), Goldman Sachs (25), and De Shaw (22) in software and finance. NVIDIA (20 offers), JPMorgan (18), Uber (15), Reliance (12), and McKinsey (10) targeted data science and consulting roles. These companies conducted Pre-Placement Talks in August 2025, converting 502 internships to PPOs. Tech giants accounted for 45% of offers, boosting average CTC to Rs 24.49 LPA. This diversity supports global exposure for 22 international placements.

Which branch had the best placements at IIT BHU in 2025?

Computer Science and Engineering topped IIT BHU Placements 2025 with 100% placement rate and Rs 35.2 LPA average for its batch, driven by 45 offers from Google, Microsoft, and Amazon in AI and cloud roles. Highest package reached Rs 120 LPA internationally. This outperformed Electronics at 84% (Rs 29.6 LPA) and Electrical at 95% (Rs 24.8 LPA). The branch's curriculum, including quantum computing labs, aligned with market needs, securing 297 PPOs overall. With 155 seats, CSE's success reflects IIT BHU's tech focus, aiding 2026 aspirants in rank planning for JoSAA counseling.

Can a student pursue MSc Chemistry degree at IITs without having Maths in class 12?

n
namrata das •  Aug-20-2025

No, Mathematics is a compulsory subject in Class 12 for a student aspiring to pursue MSc Chemistry at IITs. Mathematics is a compulsory prerequisite during the undergraduate program as well. Specifically, IIT JAM (the Joint Admission Test for Masters) eligibility for MSc Chemistry requires that candidates have completed a bachelor's degree in Chemistry or a related subject with at least 50-55% marks.
