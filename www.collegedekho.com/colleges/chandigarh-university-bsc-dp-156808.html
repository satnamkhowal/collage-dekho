About Chandigarh University BSc

Chandigarh University BSc is a 2 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. Chandigarh University requires applicants to have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate from a recognized board. The annual fee for BSc is INR 84,000 - 2,36,000. Chandigarh University BSc Admission 2026

Admission to the BSc at Chandigarh University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Chandigarh University BSc admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Chandigarh University BSc Application Process

Chandigarh University BSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BSc course at Chandigarh University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Chandigarh University BSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Chandigarh University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Chandigarh University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Chandigarh University BSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Chandigarh University BSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Chandigarh University BSc Application Form.

Documents Required for Chandigarh University BSc Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

What is the B.Sc Fashion Design semester-wise and annual fees structure at Chandigarh University, Mohali?

I
Intajur Rahaman •  Jul-31-2025

Dear Student,

Chandigarh University, Mohali, does not offer a B.Sc. Fashion Design course. However, you can pursue a Bachelor of Design course with specialization in Fashion Design. The B.Des (Fashion) course fee per semester is INR 103000, and the annual program fee is INR 206000 with 2 semesters per academic year. In addition to the aforementioned fees structure, you will also be required to pay Exam Fees and Security fees of INR 2500 and INR 2000, respectively.
