LPU PhD Fees 2026

LPU PhD fees 2026 are INR 50,000 per semester (base programme fee) for both full-time and part-time scholars. The total LPU PhD fee comes to INR 3 Lakhs for the 3-year (6 semester) duration before scholarship. After applying LPUNEST-based scholarships, the per-semester fee paid drops sharply; full-time scholars in the top LPUNEST Category A pay only INR 20,000 per semester, while part-time scholars in the same category pay INR 30,000 per semester. The complete fee structure for the LPU PhD programme with LPUNEST-based scholarship slabs is given below.

LPU Hostel Fees 2026

LPU hostel fees 2026-27 range from INR 70,000 per year for a 5/6/7/8-seater air-cooled standard room (with Early Decision Benefit) to INR 2,00,000 per year for a single-seater AC studio apartment (without Early Decision Benefit). The hostel fee covers fully furnished rooms (bed with mattress, study table, chair, almirah), attached washroom with geyser, 24×7 security with CCTV surveillance, fire safety systems, electricity backup, Wi-Fi, and access to common amenities like study room, TV room, lifts, purified water, and playground. Free electricity units (250 to 1,000 per session, depending on room type) are included; usage beyond limits incurs extra charges. Mess fees are charged separately and range from INR 40,000 to INR 56,000 per year depending on the meal plan. The hostel fee, mess, laundry, swimming pool, gym and indoor facilities are all managed by Pro Bricks (the University's residential services partner).

Lovely Professional University offers a 10% lower hostel fee under the Early Decision Benefit (EDB) for students who book before 15th May 2026. Without EDB (after this date), each room category is INR 10,000 higher per year. Boys and girls have separate hostel buildings on LPU campus, and LPU's residential capacity covers over 25,000 students.

How to Pay LPU Fees?

LPU fees can be paid online through the official student portal at lpu.in or in person at the LPU campus accounts office. The fee payment portal is active before the start of every semester. Here is the step-by-step process:

  • Visit the official LPU fee payment portal at www.lpu.in/frmLoginAccounts.aspx.
  • Log in using your Candidate ID/Verification ID and date of birth.
  • Select the current semester and the fee category (programme fee, examination fee, hostel fee).
  • Choose your payment method: Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI (UPI/RuPay payments have 0% convenience charge).
  • Increase your transaction limit before payment if needed — high-value semester payments often hit default daily limits.
  • Complete the transaction and download the payment receipt.

Students can also pay via bank deposit (Cash) at designated University accounts, NEFT/RTGS/IMPS for large amounts, or Demand Draft drawn in favour of "Lovely Professional University" payable at Jalandhar. Cash payments must always be deposited only into University bank accounts, never handed over to anyone in person — LPU does not assume liability for cash paid outside official channels. The university also has tie-ups with major banks for education loans covering up to 100% of the programme fee, with assistance available through the LPU Education Loan Cell.

LPU Fees Refund Policy

LPU fees once paid are fully refundable (except for the application fee, processing fee, and entrance fee) if the student withdraws before the start of the academic session, with a deduction of INR 500 as processing charges. After classes commence, the refund follows UGC fee refund norms, with the refundable percentage decreasing as time passes from session start.

The LPUNEST application fee, programme transfer fee, late fee, processing fee, and entrance fee are non-refundable under all circumstances. Refund requests must be submitted in writing through the LPU fee refund processing unit or uploaded via the official student portal.
