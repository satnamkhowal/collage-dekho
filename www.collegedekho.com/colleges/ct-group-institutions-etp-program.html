CT University Ludhiana Job Ready Degree

CT University Ludhiana offers one Job Ready course to train and prepare the students to acquire jobs after completion of their degree. The B.Sc in Multimedia & Animation (IXP) course is available for the duration of 3 years. The job-assured course at CT University Ludhiana are offered at an annual fee of Rs 1,00,000. Admission to these courses is offered on the basis of merit in the last qualifying exam i.e. class 12. The average salary package offered by CT University Ludhiana for the Job Ready course is Rs 12 Lakhs per annum. There is no scholarship available at the university for the assured course.

CT University Ludhiana Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

The admission to the only Job Ready “Assured” course at CT University Ludhiana is offered on the basis of merit in the last qualifying exam. We can say that the admission process is direct. Interested candidates can directly fill the application form, pay the fee and enrol in the respective course if the eligibility criteria is met by the candidates.
