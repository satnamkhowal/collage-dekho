The institute has witnessed Students have received 11000 job offers. Over 1200 students from the institute have been seen participating in education workshops at AIT and Missouri University. UCER offers more than 5320 scholarships that range between Rs 5000 to Rs 50,000. The placements have recorded 1025 students selected by Infosys. UCER, Greater Noida Courses and Admission 2026

  • For all bachelor degree courses atUnited Group of Institutions candidate needs to have 50% aggregate in their 12th standard board exams from a recognized institute.
  • For a master's course at United Group of Institutions candidates should have a bachelor’s degree with 60% aggregate marks.
  • For certain courses candidates also need to attempt an entrance exam.

United Group of Institutions Application Process 2025

Every year, the admissions for UG and PG courses at United Group of Institutions begins in March. Candidates who are interested in a particular course can apply online. 

  • Admission for courses BBA and B.Sc is based on the marks of 12th standard boards.
  • Admission for MBA, M.Tech, B.Tech, B.Pharm program is based upon the marks obtained in the accepted entrance exams.

United Group of Institutions Application Process 

Walk-in Inquiries

Students or candidates who want to see admission in any department can walk into the admission cell situated on the campus and take the application form. The form can be submitted to the admission office and a nominal application fee ofRs 1000 is to be paid while submitting the form which can be paid by credit / debit card, net banking or through UPI as well and if the candidate is shortlisted, they will be reached out via phone or email by the admission team. The candidate will be given an acknowledgement receipt upon successful acceptance of the application form.

Online Application

Since the onset of the corona pandemic, most of the admission process is now online, and candidate can submit their application via the official link and pay the application fee of Rs 1000 via net banking, through credit/debit cards or even UPI system as well. The list of needed documents should also be uploaded on the same site by the candidate. Once the application is sent the candidate would receive an acknowledgement through email and via sms as well.

United Group of Institutions Application Fees

The application fee at AKTU affiliated United Group of Institutions is Rs 1000 and this is non-refundable even if the candidate does not take admission at the institute. 

How to Fill United Group of Institutions Application Form?

Candidates have to log onto the United Group of Institutions

  • You have to fill out the basic details like name, address, phone number, email etc
  • Upload a soft copy of the 12th certificate. 
  • For admission to PG courses, enter all marks up to UG level. 
  • Submit once all details are verified. Candidate would receive an acknowledgement email once the process is finished.
