About IIT Ropar

Indian Institute of Ropar, IIT Ropar, was established in 2008, in Rupnagar, Punjab. It is one of the top IITs in India. IIT Ropar offers a variety of courses across different levels which include Undergraduate Programs like B.E. / B.Tech, B.Sc., and B.Arch., Postgraduate Programs like M.E./M.Tech, M.Sc., MBA, and Ph.D. programs across specializations including Engineering, Architecture, Sciences, Humanities, and Social Sciences. The Ministry of Education (MoE) has released the NIRF ranking 2025. As per the rankings, Indian Institute of Technology Ropar (IIT Ropar) has achieved an NIRF rank of 47 in the Research Institutions category along with an NIRF rank of 32 in the engineering category. The institute has also achieved an NIRF score of 56 in the overall category and 59.66 in the engineering category. 

Admission for MTech for this year has closed, GATE applications for 2026 will start from August 2025 and the exam will be conducted in February 2026. For BTech admissions, JoSAA counseling is almost at its conclusion, the registered candidates can check their cutoff from the official site. According to the placement report 2024-25, the average salary package offered to students was around 22.92 LPA, while the highest package recorded was 55 LPA. The placement percentage for the 2024 season was 73.49% for UG students and 55.94% for PG students. 

The IIT Ropar courses are offered at UG, PG and PhD levels in the fields of engineering and science. The degrees candidates can choose from are B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc and PhD. B.Tech is the flagship programme. The IIT Ropar fees range from Rs 4,235 to Rs 1 lakh per semester. The fees will differ depending on the degree level and the student category.

IIT Ropar course admissions are determined by valid entrance exam scores along with candidates' previous academic merit. The entrance exam scores accepted for admission to the institute are JEE Advanced, GATE and IIT JAM. Candidates seeking admission to the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar have to apply via the exam portal. Candidates must also meet the IIT Ropar cut off list to secure admission.

IIT Ropar spans over a 500-acre state-of-the-art campus and has modern facilities. In addition, it has various Hi-Tech labs that allow young minds to foster and grow. It hosts an incredible library that contains over 10,000 books and 17,000 online journals. IIT Ropar faculty consists of 150+ distinguished professors and scientists in 11 academic departments.

IIT Ropar FAQs

What is the IIT Ropar NIRF Ranking?

The MHRD authorised the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), which was introduced in 2015 by the Honourable Minister of Human Resource Development. This ranking framework provides a mechanism for classifying educational institutions/ universities across the nation. One of the recent IITs founded by the Indian Government is the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, which is situated in the Punjab. IIT Ropar has effectively earned a spot among India's best engineering institutes despite being new. It also received a ranking from the prestigious organisation, NIRF. The Indian Institute of Technology Ropar NIRF Ranking 2023, is 33rd with a score of 56.16, in the Overall category. In the NIRF 2023 Engineering category, the institute secured the 22nd rank, with a score of 60.51.

How is the IIT Ropar placements?

The IIT Ropar placements are top notch. The institute has a Career Development and Placement Cell that actively organises practical training for the students, in addition to helping final-year students find jobs. 80.85% of the B.Tech batch was placed, and 45.45% of the Dual Degree (Mechanical Engineering) batch was placed, according to IIT Ropar placements 2023. The IIT Ropar highest package offered to the students was Rs 55 LPA, while the median salary package recorded was Rs 16.62 LPA. The IIT Ropar average package offered to the students was Rs 19.85 LPA. The Dual Degree batch secured an average salary package of Rs 22.49 LPA.

How to apply for IIT JAM 2024, for IIT Ropar admissions?

The IIT JAM exam is required for admission to the M.Sc programmes offered at IIT Ropar. The conducting body, IIT Madras, released the application form on the official website (jam.iitm.ac.in). Candidates with 55% aggregate marks in Bachelor’s degree are eligible to take the exam. The exam date is February 11, 2024. The applications for the IIT JAM exam are done online. Candidates have to register on the JOAPS portal by entering their name, mobile number, valid email address and password. The process required for IIT Ropar MSc admission is given below: Visit jam.iitm.ac.in. Then, on the homepage, click on the ‘Candidate Portal (JOAPS).’  Fill out the necessary details to finish the IIT JAM registration. Log in using your unique login credentials and fill out the application form. Upload the documents required. Make the payment of the registration fees. Then, submit the application form.

What are the top companies that hire students during the IIT Ropar recruitment process?

Over the years, the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar students have managed to secure placements in some of the most reputed companies and organisations in India and abroad. Through the IIT Ropar placements, several B.Tech students secured job opportunities in sectors like Information Technology, Customer & Client Service, etc. Some of the top recruiting companies that hired students from IIT Ropar campus placements were Texas Instruments, Phone Pe, Goldman Sachs, Infosys, Zomato, Samsung, Zomato, Oracle, Paytm, Wipro, Google, Amazon, Flipkart, Airtel, Deloitte, Honeywell, Reliance Jio, Microsoft, etc. The IIT Ropar placement package offered to the students ranges from Rs 16.62 LPA to Rs 55 LPA.

What is the IIT Ropar fee structure?

The Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, in recent years, has emerged as one of the most prestigious IITs. The courses here are offered at UG, PG and PhD levels. Popular for its B.Tech course, which is its flagship programme, and the primary undergraduate courses offered here. It also offers other courses as well. The other courses include M.Tech, M.Sc and PhD. IIT Ropar has made strides in every area, including education, research, job placements and infrastructure. The institute’s NIRF ratings also significantly raised to their maximum level. The IIT Ropar fees structure for the courses offered ranges from Rs 2,500 to Rs 1 lakh (semester basis). The fees will differentiate on the basis of the degree level opted for and also on the duration of the programme.

What is the IIT Ropar admission criteria?

The IIT Ropar admission requirements are different for the UG and PG programmes offered here. Overall both the academic merit from the last qualifying examination and valid entrance exam scores are taken into consideration for admission. For B.Tech, candidates must have passed 10+2 with at least 75% with PCM as compulsory subjects. Candidates must have a valid JEE Advanced score as well. They will have to go through the JoSAA counselling process to secure admission to IIT Ropar. For M.Tech, candidates must have a B.Tech/ B.E degree with at least 60% marks in aggregate in a relevant discipline. A GATE score is mandatory for admission. For M.Sc courses, a Bachelor’s degree in a relevant discipline along with valid JAM scores is necessary. For IIT Ropar PhD admissions, a GATE, UGC NET, CSIR NET or DBT is required.

Are scholarships available at IIT Ropar?

Yes, scholarships are offered to the students at IIT Ropar. B.Tech students can apply for merit-based scholarships. Around 25% of the students get to opt the scholarships. It is a Merit-cum-Means Scholarship. For General category students, Rs 1000 per month along with zero tuition fee is provided. The eligibility criteria for this IIT Ropar scholarship is All India Rank (AIR) in the JEE. The scholarships are renewed each year if the student satisfies the requirements. They must maintain a 6.0 or higher CGPA, the earned credits must be at least 20 times the number of registered semesters and the preceding semester's SGPA must have been 6.0 or higher. These will evaluate the IIT Ropar scholarship continuance.

Are IIT Ropar PhD courses available?

The Indian Institute of Technology Ropar offers a variety of specialisations in engineering and science fields under the PhD programme. Both full time and part time PhD courses are available in the institute. The PhD courses offered at IIT Ropar are spread across various departments namely Biomedical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Metallurgical & Materials Engineering, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Humanities & Social Sciences. For admission, candidates have to meet the eligibility criteria and then go through the written test and interview conducted by the institute itself for the IIT Ropar PhD admission process. As per the regulations of the Government of India, reservations are available to candidates who are SC/ ST/ OBC-NCL/ EWS/ PwD.

When was IIT Ropar established?

The Indian Institute of Technology Ropar was established in 2008, under its mentor institution, IIT Delhi. The first batch was the academic year 2008-09. The Societies Registration Act of 1860 observed the inaugural registration of IIT Ropar on July 29, 2009. Before that, the laying of the cornerstone ceremony was held on February 24, 2009. The Institute of Technology Act (Amendment) Act 2012 (No. 34 of 2012), a Parliamentary Act, was subsequently passed to create the Institute. It the year 2018, it moved to its permanent campus. The campus was 500 acres, situated at Birla Seed Farms in Rupnagar's lush countryside along the Satluj River. IIT Ropar, then as an institute also received a 5 Star GRIHA (Green grade for Integrated Habitat Assessment) grade, one of the top recognitions for green buildings.

What are the courses offered at IIT Ropar?

The state of Punjab is home to the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar. It is one of the more recent IITs and has become one of the top universities for engineering. It offers a total of nine specialisations namely  Artificial Intelligence & Data Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Engineering Physics, Metallurgical & Materials Engineering, Mathematics & Computing, Mechanical Engineering and Electrical Engineering. The other courses offered are M.Sc, M.Tech and PhD. There is also an integrated B.Tech+M.Tech programme offered here.

What is the requirement for IIT Ropar PhD admission?

To secure admission in the IIT Ropar PhD course offered here candidates need to have a postgraduate degree in the relevant discipline and must qualify the CSIR NET/ UGC NET/ DBT/ GATE examinations. On the basis of their success in the written test and PI administered by the institute, candidates at IIT Ropar are shortlisted for the PhD programme. The IIT Ropar PhD admission is done in the following modes namely: Regular Mode: Candidates with an MTech/ ME degree with a minimum of 60% marks or 6.5 CGPA out of 10 from a recognised institute/ university. Direct Mode: Candidates must have passed with a BTech/ BE degree with at least a CGPA of 8 out of 10.  External Mode & Part Time Mode: Candidates must have passed MTech/ ME with 60% marks along with 2 years of work experience, at least in a relevant field. Lastly, candidates with a Bachelor’s or Master’s from any IITs with a CGPA of 8.0 or more out of 10, will be exempted from the JRF/ UGC-NET/ GATE exam requirement for IIT Ropar PhD admission.

IIT Ropar Scholarships

All the students who have secured a seat at IIT Ropar are eligible for all the government schemes and scholarships, irrespective of their reservation or category.

Below is a list of IIT Ropar scholarships:

IIT Ropar B.Tech Scholarships

Institute Merit-cum-Means (MCM) Scholarships 

IIT Ropar offers Merit-cum-Means scholarships to 25% of undergraduate students in engineering and technology. The present value of Merit-cum-Means scholarship is Rs. 1000 per month for general category students and the recipient is exempted from paying the tuition fee. 4-year B.Tech. students are eligible to receive Merit-cum-Means scholarship at the time of joining the Institute. The criterion of merit for the first year is the All India Rank (AIR) in the JEE.

Institute Merit Prizes and Certificates

IIT Ropar offers merit prizes and certificates to the top 7% of the students of each 4-year B.Tech. Program for the 1st  and 2nd semesters. The value of the merit prize is Rs. 2500.

Institute Free Studentship

IIT Ropar offers free studentship to 10% of the students on the basis of means alone. The recipient is exempted from paying the tuition fee. Students who are not eligible for the award of the MCM Scholarship on the basis of their parent's income will not be considered eligible for the award of the Free Studentship Scholarship. 

Scholarship provision for Students of SC/ST Category

Tuition fee exemption is admissible to all SC/ST students irrespective of their parent's income. IIT Ropar offers a scholarship of Rs. 300/- per month and exemption from paying room rent of the hostel, only to those SC/ST students whose parent’s income does not exceed the limit prescribed by the Government of India from time to time for the award of Merit-cum-Means scholarship. The SC/ST students can also opt for free mess facilities (basic menu) and Rs. 250/- per month as a pocket allowance in lieu of the amount of the scholarship.

IIT Ropar M.Tech Scholarships 

Teaching Assistantship

B.E./B.Tech./BS/B.Des and GATE/GPAT qualified students enrolled in the IIT Ropar M.Tech courses can avail themselves of the institution's Teaching Assistantship. The allowance is Rs. 12,400/-p.m. (Both 1st and 2nd year and 5th year of Dual Degree Programmes. 

IIT Ropar M.Sc Scholarships 

Merit-cum-Means scholarship to M.Sc. Students

The M.Sc. students are eligible for Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (MCM) in the form of tuition fee waiver and monthly pocket allowance. The students are exempted from paying tuition fees and will further receive a pocket allowance of Rs. 1000/- per month. M.Sc. students will be eligible to receive MCM in the first semester based on the All India Rank in IIT JAM.
