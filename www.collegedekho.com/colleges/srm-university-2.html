About SRM Chennai

SRM Institute of Science and Technology, formerly known as the SRM University was established in 1985. It has a total of 8 campuses, and one such campus is located in Kattankulathur, Chennai. SRM University is approved by the UGC and AICTE. The Ministry of Education (MoE) released the NIRF rankings for 2025 on September 4, 2025. As per the rankings, S.R.M. Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) has achieved an NIRF rank of 14 in the Engineering category with a score of 65.83. Additionally, it secured rank 10 in the Pharmacy category with a score of Pharmacy, rank 18 in Medical with a score of 63.22.

It is accredited by NBA, IET Engineering, the Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET, the Computing Accreditation Commission of ABET, and the NAAC with an A++ Grade. SRM University campus is spread over 250+ acres of land comprising 42 blocks and provides education to 50,000+ students. The 250+ acres campus has facilities such as classrooms, spacious labs, libraries, a knowledge centre, an AC-equipped auditorium, prayer halls, high-speed wi-fi, bookstores, cafeterias, etc.

SRM University courses are offered at graduate, postgraduate, diploma, PG diploma, and doctoral levels. With a total of 3200+ academicians, the university offers 41 courses such as BTech, BCom, BArch, BDS, BCA, MTech, MDS, MSc, MHA, MArch, etc. SRM University admissions are offered to all its courses based on entrance exam scores as well as merit for other programs. In 2024, Rs 52 LPA was the highest package provided during the SRM University placements. Around 60 organisations and businesses, including BITS Pilani Goa and Toyo University in Japan, have partnered with SRM Chennai.

SRM Chennai Admission Process 2024

  • Candidates must first meet the eligibility criteria set for the course, and apply, appear and qualify for the entrance exam, if any.
  • Next, candidates have to register themselves with their email ID and mobile number on the university’s official portal.
  • After the registration, candidates need to fill up the SRMIST application form, upload the necessary documents, and pay the SRM University application fees.
  • All the necessary details regarding the admission will be communicated to the candidates through their registered email ID and contact details.
  • A preliminary screening will be done based on the candidate's application and documents submitted for shortlisting.
  • Shortlisted candidates will then attend the e-counselling process, wherein they have to choose their desired programme, get their documents verified and submit the tuition and other requisite fees.
  • Lastly, candidates will receive a Provisional Allotment Letter (PAL), which will mark the end of the SRMIST admission process.

SRM University Chennai Choice Based Credit Systems 2026

SRM University Chennai has a total of 231 programmes in which Choice Based Credit Systems (CBCS) has been implemented. Amongst these 231 programmes 106 programmes have been introduced recently. Implementation of CBCS at SRM University Chennai focuses on student-centric education rather than teacher-centric education.

Benefits of CBCS at SRM University Chennai

It helps students to choose inter-disciplinary, intra-disciplinary courses along with the skill oriented papers. Following the CBCS system also ensures that the global education standard is met and students get a lot of exposure. They can have a unique combination of subjects such as Physics along with Economics.

Minors and Majors Offered by SRM University Chennai

In order to give flexibility in the course curriculum and help students seek more interdisciplinary programmes, SRM University Chennai gives full freedom in choosing the programme path and this approach adopted by the university is called ‘Inter-Disciplinary Experiential Active Learning’ (IDEAL). Major and minor is a part of IDEAL and was introduced by the university in the year 2019. 160 credits are required for the majors on the other hand 172-178 credits are needed for minors. The university offers a total of 18 engineering minor programmes whreas it offers 3 non engineering minor programmes.

SRM University Chennai FAQs

Is the SRM Chennai campus infrastructure and facility good for students?

Yes, the SRM University Campus infrastructure and facilities are good for students as the campus is clean, green, and uses less energy. Numerous more convenient amenities, including cafeterias, dining options, prayer rooms, bookstores, ATMs, gyms, and more, are available on the SRM University campus.

Does SRM Chennai offer scholarships to students?

Yes, SRM University offers scholarships to students along with fee waivers. The SRM IST scholarship is contingent on continuous outstanding performance following the standards that the institution typically establishes. The different types of scholarships mainly include Merit Scholarship, Founder's Scholarship, and Rank Based Scholarship.

What is the highest package offered during SRM University Chennai placement 2024?

The highest package offered during the SRM University placement 2024 was Rs 52 LPA; while the average package offered was Rs 7.50 LPA. In addition, until April 13, 2024, more than 853 companies attended the Chennai campus to place scholars, and more than 5,176 of those organisations made offers to the 2024 batch.

Is the SRM University cutoff for 2024 released?

No, the SRM University cutoff for 2024 is not released, however, it is expected that students will need to secure a rank up to 9000 for the CSE branch at SRMJEEE. For branches like ECE and IT, they will be required to secure a rank of up to 19,000 and 12,000, respectively.

Are the SRM Chennai rankings for 2025 out?

Yes, the SRM University rankings for 2025 are out, which are declared by the QS World University Rankings. As per the results, SRM Kattankulathur ranks 1001-1200 among the Top Global Universities, 162 among the Asia University Rankings (Overall), and 17 among the Private Universities in India.

What is the SRM University Chennai admission process?

The SRM Chennai admission process varies from one course to another as well as depending upon the course level. Students should follow the steps outlined below. Step 1: Students must visit the university’s official website and apply for admission to their desired course provided they satisfy the required eligibility criteria. Step 2: They must take the necessary university-level or national entrance exams. Step 3: Candidates must opt for counselling to confirm their admission.

What are the top courses at SRM University?

Some of the top courses at SRM Chennai are BA, BBA, BCA, BCom, BPharm, BSc, BTech, MA, MBA, MCA, MCh, MD, MSc, MTech, etc. This reputable university, which is situated in Kattankulathur, Chennai, also provides candidates with online and remote learning options. SRM University is well-known for its premier BTech and MBA programmes. The SRMJEEE UG results are used to determine admission to the BTech programme.

What is SRM University full form?

SRM University full form is SRM Institute of Science and Technology, which was established in 1985, and the other SRM institutions followed suit from 1992 to 1997. With its six faculties — Medicine & Health Sciences, Science & Humanities, Law, Management, Engineering & Technology, and Agricultural Sciences — the institution offers a broad range of undergraduate, graduate, and doctorate degrees.
