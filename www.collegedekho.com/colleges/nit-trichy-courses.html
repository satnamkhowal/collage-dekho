NIT Trichy Courses and Fees 2026

NIT Trichy Courses and Fees 2026 offer a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programs that help students build strong academic and career foundations. The institute offers programs like B.Tech, B.Arch, B.Sc. B.Ed., M.Tech, M.Sc, M.A, MCA, MBA, M.S. by Research, and Ph.D., each with clear eligibility criteria and required national-level entrance exams. The NIT Trichy Fees 2026 are designed to be budget friendly for students while maintaining high academic quality of education. Specifically, the B.Tech fee is ₹6,52,100 and the B.Arch fee is ₹8,13,900 for the full program. Postgraduate courses like M.Tech and M.Sc have lower fees, making higher studies more affordable. Professional programs such as MCA and MBA also offer good value with updated learning and industry-focused training. Research programs, including M.S. and Ph.D., have moderate fees to support long-term academic work. Overall, NIT Trichy provides well-structured courses and transparent fees suitable for students aiming for quality of technical education.

NIT Trichy Courses FAQs

What types of courses does NIT Trichy offer?

At the UG level, NIT Trichy offers B.Tech, B.Arch and BSc+BEd, while at the PG level, NIT Trichy offers M.Tech, MSc, MCA, MBA, M.Arch, MA, MS (by Research) & PhD at Doctorate level.

Which courses are popular at NIT Trichy?

The most popular NIT Trichy courses include the B.Tech, B.Arch, M.Tech, MS, MSc and MCA programmes.

Does NIT Trichy accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at NIT Trichy. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, B.Arch and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is NIT Trichy easy to get into?

Admission to NIT Trichy is highly competitive. Selection is based on valid scores in national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, GATE, CAT, NIMCET, CUET-PG, and IIT JAM. For some programmes, the institute also conducts its own tests and interviews. Candidates must meet the cutoff and complete the counselling process to secure admission.

Is NIT Trichy expensive?

The annual cost for UG Program range from ₹5,52,100-₹8,13,900, while for PG program ranges from ₹1,24,800-₹3,35,250 & PhD program costs at ₹2,77,100.

What is the annual fee of NIT Trichy?

The annual NIT Trichy fees fall between ₹1,24,800-₹8,13,900. Depending on the degree level, duration, and area of specialisation, the fees differ for various programmes. The annual cost for UG Program range from ₹5,52,100-₹8,13,900, while for PG program ranges from ₹1,24,800-₹3,35,250 & PhD program costs at ₹2,77,100.
