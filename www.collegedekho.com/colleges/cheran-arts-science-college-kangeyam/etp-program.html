Cheran Arts & Science College Job Ready Degree

If you wish to get the best out of your academics and placements, you can have a look at Cheran Arts & Science College job ready courses. The college has introduced 5 market-driven programmes at the UG level and 2 at the PG level. These programmes offered are designed to provide industry related knowledge and skills for students and to prepare them for real-time industrial practices. To get admission in these industry-oriented courses, candidates must meet the eligibility criteria which is to have a minimum 50% marks in class 12th from any recognised board from any stream.  Hence, if you have a budget of Rs 15,000 to Rs 80,000 annually for your academics, then BBA and BSc at UG, and MBA courses at PG by Cheran Arts & Science College are apt for you!

Cheran Arts & Science College Job Ready "Assured" Courses 2024 - Admissions

For industry relevant programmes offered by Cheran Arts & Science College, the admission is done directly on the basis of merit in the qualifying exam.
