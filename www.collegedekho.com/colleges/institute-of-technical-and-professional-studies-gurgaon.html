About Institute of Technical and Professional Studies

Institute of Technical and Professional Studies is situated in Gurgaon in Haryana state of India. Established in 2009, ITPS is a Private college. The college is accredited by UGC. Institute of Technical and Professional Studies offers 11 courses across 4 streams namely Engineering, Arts, Media and Mass Communication, Science..Popular degrees offered at Institute of Technical and Professional Studies include B.Tech, M.Tech, MA, Masters, MSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Institute of Technical and Professional Studies is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Institute of Technical and Professional Studies, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Institute of Technical and Professional Studies is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
