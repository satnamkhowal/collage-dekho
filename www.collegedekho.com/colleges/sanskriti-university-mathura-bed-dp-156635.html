About Sanskriti University B.Ed

Sanskriti University B.Ed is a 2 Years year program. To pursue a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) degree at Sanskriti University Mathura, candidates must fulfill certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their undergraduate degree from a recognized university with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks. The degree should be in any discipline related to Arts, Science, or Commerce. Additionally, candidates must have studied Sanskrit as a subject in their 10+2 or graduation. They should also clear any entrance examination conducted by the university or state government. Meeting these criteria will enable aspiring educators to embark on their journey towards obtaining a B.Ed. degree from Sanskriti University Mathura. The annual fee for B.Ed is INR 60,000. Sanskriti University B.Ed Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Ed at Sanskriti University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Sanskriti University B.Ed admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Sanskriti University B.Ed Application Process

Sanskriti University B.Ed application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Ed course at Sanskriti University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Sanskriti University B.Ed application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Sanskriti University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Sanskriti University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Sanskriti University B.Ed application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Sanskriti University B.Ed application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Sanskriti University B.Ed Application Form.

Documents Required for Sanskriti University B.Ed Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
