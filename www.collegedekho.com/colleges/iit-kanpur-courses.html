IIT Kanpur, the BTech program costs around ₹9.5 lakh for 4 years (tuition ₹8.2 lakh + other charges ₹1.3 lakh), MTech is ₹90,000 for 2 years (tuition ₹22,000), MBA totals ₹4.5 lakh, PhD is ₹1.05 lakh for 5 years, and MSc costs ₹34,000, based on the June 2026 fee structure for 2027 admissions. IIT Kanpur Courses FAQs

What is the seat intake for IIT Kanpur MTech Civil Engineering 2026?

The MTech programs at IIT Kanpur admit approximately 45 students for a 2-year duration, with a total fee of Rs 84,000. This includes Rs 21,000 for the first semester for General category hostellers, covering tuition, hostel, and related charges. Students opting for the dual MTech-PhD track are required to pay an additional Rs 25,000, supporting extended research and academic resources. The programs provide rigorous training in advanced engineering disciplines, supported by state-of-the-art labs and faculty mentorship. Financial provisions, along with GATE-based scholarships or assistantships, make the program accessible while encouraging high-quality research engagement.

What waivers apply to IIT Kanpur courses 2026 for SC/ST/PH?

IIT Kanpur provides extensive financial support for low-income students, offering 100% tuition exemption across UG, PG, and PhD programs, which translates to a saving of Rs 8 lakh for BTech students. Additionally, eligible students are exempted from seat rent charges, which normally amount to Rs 4,000 per semester, further reducing the financial burden. Other benefits include a 2/3rd reduction in Other Educational Benefits (OEB), covering expenses like laboratory and academic resources. These measures ensure that meritorious students from economically weaker backgrounds can pursue quality education at IIT Kanpur without financial constraints, promoting inclusive access to premier programs.

What is the total fee for IIT Kanpur BTech courses 2026?

The total fee for IIT Kanpur’s 4-year BTech program is approximately Rs 9.19 lakh, which includes Rs 8 lakh tuition fee spread across eight semesters (Rs 2 lakh per year) and Rs 1.19 lakh towards additional charges such as registration, hostel, and caution deposit. For students belonging to SC, ST, or PwD categories, the tuition fee is fully waived, reducing the overall payable amount to around Rs 1.31 lakh for the entire course. The fee structure remains consistent across BTech specializations, as per the institute’s latest financial and academic guidelines for the 2026–27 session.

How many seats are available for BTech Computer Science at IIT Kanpur 2026?

IIT Kanpur’s BTech Computer Science program offers 120 seats through JEE Advanced for the 2027 batch. The total program fee is approximately Rs 9.19 lakh, which includes tuition fees of Rs 8 lakh spread across eight semesters and additional charges such as registration, hostel, and caution deposit. For General category hostellers, the first-semester cost amounts to around Rs 1.55 lakh, covering all initial academic and residential expenses. The structure aligns with IIT Kanpur’s official fee schedule for the 2026–27 academic session, ensuring transparency and uniformity across all undergraduate BTech disciplines.

What are IIT Kanpur MTech fees 2026 for Aerospace Engineering?

The MTech program at IIT Kanpur costs a total of Rs 84,000 for two years, which includes Rs 20,000 tuition fees distributed over four semesters. The program admits 35 students per specialization, selected primarily through GATE scores. Candidates with Teaching Assistant (TA) or Research Assistant (RA) positions receive a full tuition fee waiver and are eligible for a GATE fellowship stipend of Rs 12,400 per month throughout the duration of the program. This structure ensures financial support for meritorious students while promoting high-quality research and technical advancement within the institute’s postgraduate framework.

What is the seat intake for IIT Kanpur MBA 2026?

The MBA program at IIT Kanpur offers 150 seats for its 2-year duration, designed to develop strong analytical, managerial, and leadership skills. The total program fee is Rs 4.2 lakh, which includes an annual tuition of Rs 1.05 lakh along with other academic and administrative charges. To encourage merit-based performance, the institute provides a 50% assistantship or tuition fee waiver to the top 50% of students based on their academic excellence and overall performance each semester. This structure ensures both affordability and motivation for high achievers pursuing management education at IIT Kanpur.

How much do IIT Kanpur MSc courses cost in 2026?

The MSc programs at IIT Kanpur, including disciplines such as Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Statistics, cost a total of Rs 32,000 for the 2-year duration. This includes a tuition fee of Rs 12,000 spread over four semesters, with the remaining amount covering academic and hostel-related charges. Each program offers around 55 seats, and admission is based on IIT-JAM scores as per national-level merit lists. The fee structure is highly subsidized, making it affordable for meritorious students pursuing advanced scientific education and research across core disciplines at one of India’s leading institutes of higher learning.

What is the PhD fee structure at IIT Kanpur 2026 for Computer Science?

The PhD programs at IIT Kanpur cost a total of Rs 1.01 lakh for five years, which includes Rs 50,000 in tuition fees spread across ten semesters. Each department typically offers around 35 seats, with highly qualified candidates admitted through GATE, CSIR-NET, or JRF. The first-semester fee for General category hostellers is approximately Rs 19,995, covering tuition, hostel, and other institutional charges. All admitted PhD scholars receive a full tuition waiver and are eligible for a monthly fellowship under GATE or JRF schemes, making the program both academically rigorous and financially supported for full-time researchers.

What are the seat intakes for IIT Kanpur BS programs 2026?

The BS programs at IIT Kanpur in Economics, Physics, and Statistics & Data Science follow the same fee structure as BTech programs, totaling Rs 9.19 lakh for four years, including Rs 8 lakh tuition and additional institutional charges. The seat intake for these programs is 70 for BS Economics, 80 for BS Physics, and 60 for BS Statistics & Data Science. Admissions are through JEE Advanced followed by JoSAA counseling. These programs blend theoretical foundations with practical research exposure, preparing students for careers in academia, analytics, and interdisciplinary scientific or economic research fields globally.

What is the total fee for IIT Kanpur online MTech Data Science 2026?

The MTech in Data Science & Business Analytics at IIT Kanpur (Online Mode) costs Rs 6 lakh for a 3-year duration and offers 150 seats. Conducted through a Coursera partnership, the program allows professionals and graduates to pursue advanced technical training without the need for GATE. Instead, admission is based on an institute-level entrance test and interview. The fee is fully refundable before the cohort’s start date, providing flexibility for applicants. The curriculum emphasizes AI, machine learning, data visualization, and business analytics, equipping learners with both theoretical and practical industry-oriented expertise.
