About Thapar University

Thapar Institute of Engineering and Management was established in 1956 in Patiala. The National Accreditation and Assessment Council (NAAC) accredited the university with an “A” grade. As per the NIRF 2025 ranking, Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET) has achieved an NIRF rank of 42 in the Research Institutions category along with an NIRF rank of 29 in the Engineering category. Thapar University has also achieved NIRF scores of 47.59 and 60.97 in each category respectively. 

Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology also known as TIET Patiala offers UG, PG, and PhD courses in streams of engineering, management, sciences, and arts. Admission to these courses will be done based on CAT, MAT, XAT, and JEE scores. The Thapar University fees range from INR 54K to INR 25L, depending on the Thapar University courses. The university placement rate hovers in between 75-89% for the past 7 years. In 2024, the highest package offered to the students was INR 1.23 CR, which is also the highest package so far achieved in the TIET Patiala since its establishment. With 250 acres of land, TIET Patiala owns multiple academic schools and a well-equipped campus. The campus has modern and upgraded facilities for students including spacious classrooms, libraries, hostels, auditoriums etc.

Thapar University FAQs

What is the application process and fee for admission to Thapar University in 2026?
Candidates must visit the official Thapar University admission portal, register online, fill the application form with personal and academic details, upload required documents (like 10+2/graduation marksheets, entrance scorecard, photo, signature, and category certificate if applicable), and pay the non-refundable application fee of Rs 1,000 or Rs 1,500 (depending on the programme) via debit card or net banking. After payment, submit the form to complete the process.
What is the tentative admission timeline for Thapar University courses in 2026?
The key dates for 2026 admissions are: Application form release in January 2026, last date to apply in May 2026, counselling and merit list preparation in June–July 2026, and admission offers along with fee payment in July–August 2026.
What are the key eligibility criteria and entrance exams required for admission to major courses at Thapar University in 2026?
Eligibility varies by programme: B.Tech requires 10+2 with 50% marks and a good JEE/GMAT score; B.Sc and BBA need 10+2 with 70% aggregate plus the Thapar University Test; M.Tech requires 50% in graduation and GATE (preferred); M.Sc needs 55% at graduation; MCA requires 55% in relevant graduation; MA needs 50% in graduation; PhD requires 55% in post-graduation. The university also conducts its own entrance test for certain UG programmes.

How are the expected JEE Main 2026 cutoffs projected to change for core engineering branches at Thapar University, and what does this mean for applicants?
The cutoffs are expected to tighten by 5–8% compared to previous years due to rising competition. What JEE Main percentile range is likely required to secure admission to high-demand branches like CSE and ECE at Thapar University in 2026?

What are the expected JEE Main 2026 closing ranks for Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Thapar University for General candidates?

Thapar University FAQs

What are the key features of the library and classrooms at Thapar University?
The university has the Nava Nalanda Central Library, which is WiFi-enabled and holds over 1,06,000 books, theses, and journals, along with 4,500 audio-visual items. It features four reading halls with a total seating capacity of 350. Classrooms are fully air-conditioned, spacious, and equipped with modern teaching aids to enhance the learning experience.
What sports and recreational facilities are available on the Thapar University campus?
The campus provides a wide range of sports and recreational facilities, including badminton and basketball courts, cricket and football grounds, tennis courts, running/jogging tracks, swimming pools, recreation rooms, and an indoor gym. These facilities support both outdoor and indoor activities for students.
Where is the Thapar University campus located, and what are the key hostel facilities available?
Thapar University has its campus in Patiala and Dera Bassi areas of Punjab. The campus offers 14 hostels (9 for boys and 5 for girls) spread across a spacious area. These hostels are two-storied and equipped with modern technology to provide comfortable accommodation for students.

Thapar University FAQs

What are the eligibility and continuation requirements for Merit Cum Means Scholarships at Thapar University?
Both Merit Cum Means Scholarships (Normal and Special) are available for students whose gross family income is less than INR 5 lakhs. Students must maintain a CGPA of more than 7.0 each academic year to continue receiving the scholarship. The Special category additionally requires a recommendation from the Dean of Student Affairs (DoSA) and approval from a committee headed by the Dean of the institution.
Who is eligible for Merit Scholarship III at Thapar University?
Merit Scholarship III is awarded to the first 10% of admitted students based on their performance in the Thapar Institute Entrance Test. Additionally, candidates must have scored more than 85% aggregate marks in 10+2.
What are the eligibility criteria for Merit Scholarships I and II at Thapar University?
For Merit Scholarship I, students must have more than 95% aggregate marks in 10+2 and rank in the top 25 based on JEE Main scores (irrespective of branch). For Merit Scholarship II, students need more than 90% aggregate marks in 10+2 and must be among the next 30 students based on JEE Main rank (irrespective of branch).
What are the different types of scholarships offered by Thapar University, and how are they categorized?
Thapar University offers five types of scholarships: three merit-based (Merit Scholarships I, II, and III) and two merit-cum-means scholarships (Merit Cum Means Scholarship – Normal and Merit Cum Means Scholarship – Special). These scholarships are designed to support academically talented students and those from economically weaker backgrounds.
