About Times Pro (TP), Chandigarh

A business college established in 2013, Times Pro Chandigarh is an AICTE accredited college.  Located in Chandigarh, they provide industry-relevant courses that use modern teaching methods and technologies to build the student's knowledge in that particular field. 

Times Pro Chandigarh courses are provided in 23 programmes in four categories such as engineering, management, information technology, commerce, and banking.  The most popular degrees they offer include MBA, PGD, MMS, PGDM and Diploma.

These courses focus on critical economic drivers where demand is expected to grow substantially for courses like supply and custom design. They provide industry engagement, using a methodology based on the student’s perspective.

Times Pro Chandigarh admissions aim to bring intelligent and hard-working students from all backgrounds. The admissions are done based on their overall academic merit and valid entrance exam scores from CAT/ MAT/ XAT/ CMAT/ GMAT/ SNAP/ NMAT/ ATMA/ KMAT exams. 

The Times Pro Chandigarh application form is designed in a way that highlights every student’s individuality and suggests what course choices will work for them. Students can pursue degree programs in Business and Management, Marketing, Computing and Analytics, Web Development, Design, SAP, Logistics, BFSI Training and Hotel Management at the college.

Times Pro's domain expertise and experience in skill development are well-known, particularly in the areas of Banking and Financial Services, Digital Marketing, SAP Training, International Pathway Programs for foreign study, and Study Abroad Consultancy.

Times Pro is a multi-award-winning professional and vocational learning sub-brand of Times Professional Learning, India's largest and most varied media conglomerate's education effort. Times Pro represents the Education 4.0 revolution in learning: learner-centric, industry-relevant, and technology-supported, emphasizing employability.

Over 500 alumni have graduated from the Times Pro Chandigarh. The MBA program has seen over 100 students and placed them in reputed companies both in India and abroad.

The Times Pro Chandigarh placement cell bridge the gap between academics and industry.  They have a  nationwide network of expertise and corporate partners.

They have placed over 100 students with the highest package of Rs 5 lakhs per annum and the average package of Rs 2 lakhs per annum. Some of the Times Pro Chandigarh top recruiters include Kotak Mahindra Bank, Beekay Industries, TCS, Infosys, Jana Bank, HDFC, Intel, Oppo, etc.

Times Pro (TP), Chandigarh Courses and Admission 2026

The general Times Pro Chandigarh application fee for the form is Rs 1,000.  The payment can be made offline as well. Candidates applying online must upload a scanned colour photograph alongside the Online Application along with payment via credit or debit cards (Master Card/ Visa).

Through the official website, candidates can complete the application process for Times Pro Chandigarh admissions. Candidates must fulfil the eligibility requirements and perform well in the selection process to secure admission at this institute.

Times Pro Chandigarh admissions are done based on their overall academic merit and valid entrance exam scores from CAT/ MAT/ XAT/ CMAT/ GMAT/ SNAP/ NMAT/ ATMA/ KMAT exams. The Times Pro Chandigarh fees range from Rs Rs 85,000 - 3.85 lakh.

Times Pro Chandigarh Eligibility Criteria for 2024

For applying at Times Pro Chandigarh, candidates must meet the given eligibility criteria:

  • For pursuing a UG course, candidates must have a valid score in their  10+2 qualifying examination from a recognized board.
  • For pursuing an MBA/ MMS course, candidates must have an overall 50% in 10+2  and Graduation. 
  • A valid score in CAT/ MAT/ XAT/ CMAT/ GMAT/ SNAP/ NMAT/ ATMA/KMAT exam is required, as the selection will depend on this. 
  • For pursuing a PGDM course, a 50% overall in 10+2 and Graduation is required, along with a  valid score in CAT/ MAT/ XAT/ CMAT/ GMAT/ SNAP/ NMAT/ ATMA/KMAT exam.
  • Candidates who qualified for the TAP test (Times Admission Process test) with a minimum of 50% aggregate are also eligible.
  • Graduates with or without professional experience and Final-year grads are also eligible to apply.

Times Pro Chandigarh Application Process 2026

Every year, the admissions for UG and PG courses at Times Pro Chandigarh begin in February. Candidates who are interested in a particular course can apply online.

  • Class 12 scores are important for qualifying the eligibility.
  • Candidates must submit an online application on the official website.
  • Selection is merit-based along with the consideration of scores from the entrance examinations.
  • The Times Pro Chandigarh application form for all courses is available online at their website. 
  • Candidates for the MBA/ MMS/ PGDM programme must be less than or equal to 26 years old at the start of the programme.

How to Fill Times Pro Chandigarh Application Form?

  • For admissions at Times Pro Chandigarh, candidates can apply through the official website.
  • Click on “Apply Now”.
  • Register on the application portal and fill in all the details. 
  • Upload the necessary documents.
  • Pay the Times Pro Chandigarh application fee of Rs 1000 online via Net Banking, Credit Card, or Debit Card.
  • Finalize the application process by submitting it and taking a printout of it for future reference.
