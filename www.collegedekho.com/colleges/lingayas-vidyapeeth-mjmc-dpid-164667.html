About Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC

Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC is a 2 Years year program. To be eligible for the M.J.M.C. (Master of Journalism and Mass Communication) degree at Lingaya's Vidyapeeth Faridabad, applicants must meet the following criteria. Candidates should have completed their undergraduate degree in any discipline from a recognized university, with a minimum aggregate score of 50%. Additionally, they must have a keen interest in journalism, mass communication, and related fields. Proficiency in English is essential, as it is the medium of instruction. Applicants will also need to clear the entrance examination conducted by the university, followed by a personal interview. Prior work experience or relevant internships in journalism or media organizations will be an added advantage. The annual fee for MJMC is INR 93,500. Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC Admission 2026

Admission to the MJMC at Lingaya's Vidyapeeth is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC Application Process

Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MJMC course at Lingaya's Vidyapeeth have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Lingaya's Vidyapeeth and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Lingaya's Vidyapeeth. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC Application Form.

Documents Required for Lingaya's Vidyapeeth MJMC Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
