About IIT Delhi Admission 2026

IIT Delhi admission 2026 for B.Tech is based on JEE Advanced 2026 scores followed by JoSAA counselling. The JEE Advanced 2026 exam is scheduled for May 17, 2026, and the counselling process will begin in June 2026. The total seat intake for B.Tech at IIT Delhi is around 1,239 (based on 2025 data). For B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, the IIT Delhi JEE Advanced cutoff 2025 closed at AIR 126 for the General category in Round 6.

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi also offers postgraduate and doctoral programs like M.Tech, M.Sc, MBA, M.Des, MS (Research), MPP, MA, and Ph.D. Admission to M.Tech is based on GATE 2026 scores followed by COAP counselling, while M.Sc admissions are conducted through IIT JAM 2026 and CCMN counselling. MBA admission is based on CAT 2025 scores followed by a personal interview conducted by the Department of Management Studies. Admission to the Ph.D. program requires GATE/UGC-NET/CSIR-NET scores or a Research Aptitude Test, followed by an interview conducted by the institute.

IIT Delhi B.Tech Admission Process 2026

The admission process for B.Tech at Indian Institute of Technology Delhi is as follows:

Step 1: Qualify for JEE Main & JEE Advanced

  • Register and appear for JEE Main 2026 (Session 1 or 2). 
  • Secure a rank within the top 2.5 lakh candidates to become eligible for JEE Advanced.
  • Apply for JEE Advanced 2026 and appear for the exam scheduled on May 17, 2026.

Step 2: Check Results

  • Check JEE Advanced 2026 results (expected by end of May or early June 2026).

Step 3: Register for JoSAA Counselling

  • Visit the official JoSAA portal (josaa.nic.in) and register using your JEE Advanced credentials.

Step 4: Choice Filling

  • Fill in your preferred choices, including IIT Delhi and desired B.Tech branches, in order of preference.

Step 5: Seat Allotment Process

  • Participate in JoSAA counselling rounds (up to 6 rounds). Seats are allotted based on rank, category, and preferences.

Step 6: Seat Acceptance Fee Payment

  • Pay the JoSAA seat acceptance fee through online modes such as UPI, debit/credit card, or net banking.

Step 7: Document Verification & Reporting to Institute

  • Complete online document verification on the JoSAA portal.
  • Report to IIT Delhi for physical document verification and final fee payment within the given deadline.

Step 10: Admission Confirmation

  • After successful verification and fee payment, your seat will be confirmed. 
  • Attend orientation and begin classes in July 2026.

Practical Tip:
For General category candidates, IIT Delhi CSE typically closes around AIR 120–130, while Mathematics & Computing closes around AIR 200–300. Lock your preferences carefully during JoSAA counselling, as changes after Round 3 can impact your chances of securing higher-preference branches.

IIT Delhi Application Process 2026

IIT Delhi application process can be completed online for UG, PG, & PhD admissions. For B.Tech admission, candidates have to register on josaa.nic.in and select IIT Delhi as one of the preferred institutes. For PG programs like M.Tech, M.Sc, & M.Des, candidates need to directly apply via the offiical portal of IITD. However, they need to register separately for counselling (COAP for M.Tech, IIT JAM for M.Sc, and CEED for M.Des).

IIT Delhi BTech Application Process 2026 

The IIT Delhi BTech admission process 2026 is conducted through JoSAA counselling after JEE Advanced qualification. There is no separate application form for IIT Delhi BTech.

  • Visit josaa.nic.in and register using JEE Advanced 2026 roll number and JEE Main application number.
  • Fill in personal details such as name, category, and contact information.
  • Enter academic and exam details including JEE Advanced score and rank.
  • Fill and lock choices of IIT Delhi BTech programmes in order of preference.
  • Once the seat is alloted, pay seat acceptance fee online.
  • Upload and verify documents online through JoSAA portal.

IIT Delhi PG/PhD Application Process 2026

IIT Delhi PG and PhD admissions are done through the official IIT Delhi admission portal: ecampus.iitd.ac.in/PGADM/login. Candidates must complete the application form online and upload required documents before payment and submission.

  • Visit ecampus.iitd.ac.in/PGADM/login (official IIT Delhi PG and PhD admission portal).
  • Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to create login credentials.
  • Log in and fill personal details such as name, date of birth, category, and nationality.
  • Enter academic details including UG/PG qualifications, marks, institution name, and year of passing.
  • Enter entrance exam details such as GATE / JAM / CAT / NET / CEED scores as applicable.
  • For PhD, upload research proposal or Statement of Purpose (SOP) and select research area.
  • Select department, programme, and specialisation carefully from dropdown options.
  • Upload required documents in prescribed format.
  • Pay the application fee online using UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking.
  • Review the form and submit it. Download and save the confirmation receipt for future reference.

IIT Delhi Admission FAQs

What is the IIT Delhi admission criteria for MBA?

The admission criteria for MBA at IIT Delhi include a minimum of 60% in their Bachelor’s degree. After this, candidates must clear the CAT exam with a good percentile since the institute releases its cutoff based on the CAT exam for MBA admission.

Can I get IIT Delhi admission without JEE?

The admission to B.Tech courses at IIT Delhi is strictly based on the JEE scores. For various other courses, the eligibility criteria differ at the institute. For M.Tech, the GATE scores are needed, for MBA, the CAT scores, and, for M.Sc, the JAM scores. Thus, the courses where candidates can get admission without JEE are M.Tech, MBA, PhD, M.Sc, B.Des, and M.Des.

From where can I access the IIT Delhi admission portal?

Candidates can access the admission portal of IIT Delhi through the official website iitd.ac.in.

What is the IIT Delhi admission criteria for B.Tech?

To secure admission to the B.Tech course at the institute, candidates must have a good JEE Rank. Based on the JEE ranks, the institute releases its cutoff list. This may be followed by a personal interview round.
