About IIT Delhi Placements 2026

IIT Delhi placements 2025–26 are ongoing and will continue until May 2026. So far, the institute has released placement data updated till December 2025. According to the report, a total of 1,275 offers, including PPOs, have been made, with over 1,140 students successfully placed. More than 300 PPOs have already been secured this year, reflecting a 33% increase compared to 2025. Additionally, 35 international offers have been made in 2026. Leading recruiters such as Amazon, Accenture, Google, Goldman Sachs, and American Express participated in the placement drive.

As per the IIT Delhi NIRF Report 2025, the median package for the 4-year undergraduate program stood at INR 19 LPA, while the 5-year undergraduate program recorded a median package of INR 24.3 LPA. For M.Tech graduates, the median package was INR 15.59 LPA. As per the IIT Delhi MBA 2023–2025 Final Placement Report, the highest package stood at INR 43.55 LPA, while the average package was INR 22.53 LPA. The median package was recorded at INR 22.51 LPA.

IIT Delhi NIRF Placement Report 2025

As per the IIT Delhi NIRF Report 2025, the median package for UG 4-year programs stood at INR 19.08 LPA with 66% students placed. UG 5-year programs recorded a higher median package of INR 24.3 LPA with 80% placements. For PG 2-year engineering programs, the median package reached INR 15.59 LPA with 47% students placed. Meanwhile, PG 2-year management programs recorded a median package of INR 22.05 LPA with a strong placement rate of 96%.

IIT Delhi Placements FAQs

What is the highest package in IIT Delhi for BTech?

As per the IIT Delhi placement report for 2023, the highest package (domestic) for BTech courses stood at Rs 1 crore per annum, whereas highest package (international) was Rs 2.5 crore per annum. A total of 1,150 unique offers were provided during IIT Delhi placements 2023.

Which branch of IIT Delhi has the best placement record?

The management branch at IIT Delhi has the best placement record. For the second consecutive year, the institute achieved 100% placement for the MBA programme. IIT Delhi median package for MBA in 2023 was Rs 24.45 lakh per annum.

Who are the top recruiters of IIT Delhi?

During IIT Delhi placements companies from national and international markets participate and hire students. Some of the renowned recruiters of Indian Institute of Technology Delhi are Google, Microsoft, Gartner, Copgemini, Amazon, ICICI Bank, Yes Bank, Adani, Wipro, TCS etc. It must be noted that IIT Delhi top recruiters vary for engineering and management domains.

What is the Placement Percentage in Delhi ?

IIT Delhi placements 2024 are underway and the placement percentage will be released once placements are concluded in May 2024. However, in the year 2023, IIT Delhi placement for engineering courses was 84.94%. On the other hand, the institute achieved 100% placements for the Master of Business Administration (MBA) course.

How was the placements at IIT Delhi in 2023?

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi has an excellent placement record. The institute has a dedicated placement cell known as Office of Career Services (OCS). It is responsible for handling all placement activities. In 2023, IIT Delhi MBA placement percentage was 100% and for engineering courses it was 84.94%. The highest international and domestic package in 2023 was Rs 2.5 crore per annum and Rs 2 crore per annum, respectively.

What is the average package offered at IIT Delhi?

IIT Delhi average package 2024 is yet to be released by the institute. As per the 2023 placement report, the average package offered to BTech students was Rs 17.60 LPA. On the other hand, IIT Delhi MBA average package 2023 was Rs 25.82 LPA.

What is the highest package for Btech in IIT Delhi?

The highest package offered during IIT Delhi placements 2023 was Rs 2.5 crore per annum for BTech. A total of 1300 job offers were made to engineering students in 2023. Some of the prominent recruiters of Indian Institute of Technology Delhi are Accenture, Microsoft, Goldman Sachs, IBM, Dell etc.

How many companies visit IIT Delhi placements?

As per the IIT Delhi placements stats for the year 2023, as many as 500 recruiters participated in the placement drive. Some of the prominent recruiters of the institute are Google, Amazon, Capgemini, Microsoft, KPMG etc. They regularly participate in the placement drive and offer jobs with handsome packages to students.

What is the highest package in IIT Delhi for MBA?

In the year 2023, IIT Delhi highest package for the MBA course was Rs 41,13,000 per annum. In 2022 and 2023, the institute has achieved 100% placements for the Master of Business Administration course. The highest package for MBA in 2022 was Rs 36,47,000 per annum.

Does IIT Delhi have 100% placement?

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi has 100% placements for the MBA course. In 2022 and 2023, it achieved 100% placements for MBA. For engineering courses, IIT Delhi placement percentage was 84.94% and 82% in 2023 and 2022 respectively.

Is IIT Delhi good for placements?

IIT Delhi is one of the best colleges when it comes to placements. Every year, the institute achieves more than 80% placements for engineering and 100% placements for MBA courses. The highest package of IIT Delhi in 2023 stood at Rs 2.5 crore per annum.
