About Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University

Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University is situated in Kolkata in West Bengal state of India. Established in 1955, FCET is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University offers 91 courses across 9 streams namely Engineering, IT, Media and Mass Communication, Pharmacy, Education..Popular degrees offered at Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University include B.Tech, BA, BSc, B.Pharm, B.Arch..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Faculty Council of Engineering & Technology - Jadavpur University is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.

R
Rupsa •  Nov-15-2024

Dear Student,

Unfortunately, diploma students from Bihar or any other state with 10th marks are not eligible for WBJEE exam. This is because the minimum WBJEE 2025 eligibility criteria requires that all students (from West Bengal or other states) must have completed their 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as main subjects to get admission to B.Tech degree.
