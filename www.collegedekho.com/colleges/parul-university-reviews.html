Parul University Overall Rating

Overall I had enrolled in B.Tech CSE AIML at Parul University in 2024 batch, currently in the 2nd year, from so far I can comprehend that the academic faculties, resources such as the labs and access to a few paid tools(ex. canva) is provide to students who are interested in that specialisation. The specialised labs at Piet targets core knowledge and better projects to sincere students. When I got here, the fees for my course was 149k/Year and they made me pay in two parts, provisional fees and sem fees. The admission process in my case was smooth, as I was from outside state and my counsellor did all the process hassle free. I didn't come to university for any other extra formalities. I also received a 30% tuition fees waiver upon my previous results for first year. I chose this University because the overall expense of accommodation and fees was equivalent to only the tuition fees at my state. To have a different experience with the same expense is why I chose.

Placement One of my friend is an intern at the placement cell department, and according to the information there are many reputed companies that keep coming to Parul for placements, with higher packages, definitely not all get place, but the department makes sure that deserving candidates grab the opportunity.

Infrastructure Campus is well equipped with all the basic amenities and showcases good infrastructure as compared to other private universities. The campus has high-tech labs, decent libraries, and amenities like gym, food courts, swimming pool, super martket, etc.

Faculty Faculties are supportive and encouraging. My department, Engineering faculties are quite good with good amount of knowledge and expertise in the respective field. Once I had faced a dispute with one of my faculties, and I reported it to my HOD and the issue was considered and resolved, so student's problems are heard here, unlike some people saying otherwise.

Hostel Hostel is decent with all the basic facilities. The WiFi works fine but its not 24X7. Mess Food is a hit-and-miss. Hostel housekeeping is provided but its average.

Here’s a short and balanced overall review of Parul University:

Overall Parul University offers a beautiful and well-equipped campus with modern classrooms, high-tech labs, and strong academic infrastructure. The university hosts regular cultural events, international programs, and supports innovation and entrepreneurship. Hostel life is comfortable with good facilities, and students get exposure through workshops, internships, and placements. It’s a great place for holistic development and a vibrant student experience

Placement Parul University is a strong placement choice for ambitious students who proactively engage with training, internships, and academics while adhering to attendance norms. Those seeking uniformly high packages across every department may need to weigh the trade-offs carefully.

Infrastructure Parul University has excellent infrastructure with a sprawling, green campus spread over 150+ acres. It features modern classrooms, fully equipped laboratories, libraries, auditoriums, sports complexes, and even a multi-specialty hospital. Hostels, food courts, Wi-Fi, and other amenities are well-maintained, providing students with a comfortable and resource-rich environment.

Faculty Parul University’s faculty features a mix of high-achieving, research-active educators and entry-level instructors, especially in less-established departments. While official accolades and many student reviews praise the teaching staff, other real-world reports highlight inconsistent classroom effectiveness and gaps in pedagogy. For those looking to choose Parul, it’s wise to explore faculty profiles in your specific department—through alumni or peer contact—to assess teaching quality before committing.

Hostel Parul University hostels are well-maintained and offer a safe, clean, and comfortable living environment. Rooms are available in various sharing options with basic furnishings, Wi-Fi, laundry, and regular cleaning services. The campus provides vegetarian meals, 24/7 security, and easy access to university facilities. Hostel life also helps students build strong friendships and be part of a vibrant student community.

A Well-Rounded College Experience with Good Placements and Campus Life

Overall My overall experience at Parul University has been positive. The campus offers a good balance between academics and extracurriculars. The BCA program is practical and industry-focused, and the faculty is supportive and knowledgeable. Placements are decent with many reputed companies visiting. Hostel life, events, and campus activities make student life engaging. There’s scope for improvement in curriculum updates and mess food, but overall, it’s a value-for-money experience.

Placement Parul University offers good placement support for BCA students. Around 70% of students get placed, with average packages around ₹3.5–4 LPA. Companies like TCS, Wipro, Juspay, and Flipkart visit the campus. Internships are also offered by tech startups. Students with better coding and communication skills receive stronger placement offers.

Infrastructure Parul University offers well-maintained infrastructure for BCA students. The classrooms are equipped with smart boards, and the computer labs have updated systems with reliable Wi-Fi. The library is spacious with both physical and digital resources. Hostel facilities are clean, and the campus has 24/7 medical support, sports grounds, and active recreational spaces.

Faculty The faculty at Parul University is well-qualified and approachable. Most professors explain concepts clearly and encourage doubt-solving and project-based learning. They use real-world examples during lectures and are supportive during assignments and placements. A few could improve their teaching methods, but overall, the academic support is strong and consistent.

Hostel The hostel facilities at Parul University are well-organized and safe. Rooms are clean, with proper ventilation and regular maintenance. Wi-Fi, laundry, and water facilities are available. The mess food is hygienic, though taste-wise it's average. Security is strict with fixed in-times and 24/7 surveillance. Overall, it’s a comfortable stay for students.
