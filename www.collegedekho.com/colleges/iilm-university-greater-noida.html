About IILM University Greater Noida

IILM University Greater Noida is a private university located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The university offers 50+ undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in Management, Engineering, Computer Applications, Law, Liberal Arts, Communication, and Sciences.

Popular courses at IILM Greater Noida include B.Tech, BBA, MBA/PGDM, BCA, BA LLB, BBA LLB, MCA, M.Tech, LLM, MA, and MSc. Admissions are based on entrance exams such as JEE Main, CUET, CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, CLAT, and LSAT, depending on the programme.

IILM University Greater Noida course fees range from INR 2 lakh to INR 12.4 lakh for the complete programme duration. The university also offers merit-based scholarships on tuition fees for eligible students.

IILM University Greater Noida Admission 2026

IILM University Greater Noida admission is open for undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. Admission is offered based on JEE Main, CUET, CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, CLAT, or LSAT scores, depending on the course. Candidates can also apply through Class 12 or graduation merit, followed by a personal interview.

How to Apply for IILM University Greater Noida Admission 2026?

Candidates can apply for IILM University Greater Noida admission through the university's online application portal. The application process is simple and can be completed in a few steps.

  • Visit the official IILM admission portal and click on "Apply Now".
  • Register using your name, mobile number, and email ID.
  • Fill in your personal, academic, and contact details.
  • Upload the required documents, including photographs and academic certificates.
  • Submit entrance exam scores, if applicable.
  • Pay the INR 1,000 application fee through Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI.
  • Submit the application form and download the confirmation page for future reference.

IILM University Greater Noida FAQs

Is a hostel available at IILM University Greater Noida?

IILM University Greater Noida hostel facility is offered on a first come first serve basis. All of the hostel rooms have been built to allow enough movement space, as well as enough air and light. For security purposes, a warden is always available to monitor and help students and the management of hostels. There is a 24-hour water and electricity supply available. Along with that RO water purifier, geyzer, gym and mess facility are available for students. The sports facility is also available at each hostel. There is a game room, study room and service area to let students feel at home and all the necessary amenities are provided for a comfortable stay.

What facilities does IILM University Greater Noida offer?

IILM University Greater Noida facilities include all the necessary things students would need during their stay at the university. Along with academics, the university takes good care of students through its facilities. The university's classrooms, laboratories, and library are in good condition. Modern teaching aids are available in the classrooms. To assist students' academic needs, the libraries include a large collection of books and other materials. The university contains a sports facility that includes a badminton court, a basketball ground and a cricket ground. There is also a cafeteria, gym, hospital, and convenience store on IILM University Greater Noida campus. The cafeteria offers a range of fresh and healthful meals at reasonable prices.

Is a scholarship available at IILM University Greater Noida?

IILM University Greater Noida scholarships are offered to meritorious and deserving students. The university has bifurcated the scholarship details based on scholarships for 10+2 and scholarships after graduation. Students can check the details of scholarships available at the official website. The IILM University Greater Noida scholarship is offered based on CUET scores, JEE Main scores, CLAT scores, scholarships for Haryana domicile students and more. The students will be given scholarships on a pro-rata basis subject to the marks secured in the entrance examination. Note that, one student will be entitled to get only a single scholarship at a time. If a student falls into two or more categories, then only one category shall be allotted to the student.

What is the process for IILM University Greater Noida course admission?

IILM University Greater Noida admission process is online and students can apply online from the official website. However, students are suggested to check and verify the eligibility criteria for their desired course beforehand. Once the eligibility criteria are satisfied, students can fill out the online application form from the website. The application fee of IILM University Greater Noida shall be paid in online mode and then the academic details, as well as the personal details, shall be filled in the application form. Once the documents are verified, the admission is confirmed and the admission letter is communicated to the students.

Triparna Choudhury •  Jul-22-2024

Yes, you can take admission in the B.Tech in CSE programme at IILM University Greater Noida based on your 12th marks. To be eligible, you must have passed your 10+2 level examination with a minimum of 50% marks, with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects and one optional subject from Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, or Technical Vocational. For SC/ST/reserved category candidates, the minimum required mark is 45%. The university will consider the best of three subjects, with the third subject being the one in which you scored the highest. If you meet these criteria, you will be eligible to apply for B.Tech CSE at IILM University Greater Noida.
