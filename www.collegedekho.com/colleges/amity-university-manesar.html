About Amity University Manesar

Amity University Gurgaon courses are offered at UG, PG and PhD levels in a variety of disciplines. Some of the popular degrees include B.Tech, B.Pharma, B.Sc, MBA, etc. The Amity University Manesar fees range from Rs 50,000 to Rs 4.60 lakh per year (approx). It may vary depending on the specialisations and degree level as well.

The placement data released for the year 2023, showed that a total of 423 companies visited, comprising 266 new recruiters and 157 repeat recruiters. The salary packages as per the placement data of 2022 are provided. The Amity University Manesar placement packages offered to the students range from Rs 5 LPA to as high as Rs 45 LPA. 

Amity University Manesar admissions are based on entrance tests administered by the university and also national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, GATE, CAT, MAT, etc.

Amity University Manesar is located in Gurgaon Haryana and is a private university, accredited by NAAC with an A grade. The campus is spread across 110 acres of land. The courses are recognized by the UGC. The campus also has a diverse student population with applicants from 15 countries. It was established in 2010 as a part of the Amity Group of Institutions.

Amity University Manesar Application Process 2025

The following are the steps that the applicants must follow to apply for the Amity University Gurugram admission process.

  • The candidate needs to register on the entrance exam conducting body's official website.
  • The candidate has three options for submitting their application: online, by download, or in person at the university office.
  • Go to the university's official website.
  • Then, select Apply Now to begin the online application process.
  • Complete the Amity University Manesar application form. 
  • Include a soft copy of your Class 10 report card and a current colour passport-sized photo.
  • The Amity University Manesar application fees can be paid using a credit or debit card.
  • Pay the fees for the downloaded form via demand draft made payable to Delhi/New Delhi and made out to "Amity Form."
  • The fee for application is Rs 1,100.
  • The application form must be forwarded to or submitted to:
  • Amity University Haryana Campus, Amity Education Valley, Manesar, Gurgaon, Haryana-122413.

Amity University Manesar FAQs

Are hostels provided at Amity University Manesar?

Yes, comfortable and well facilitated hostels are provided for the Amity University Manesar students. The hostels are located on campus and have an intake of up to 2100. The rooms are provided in a Double and Triple Sharing basis. On campus, there are several stringent security measures in place, such as the newest IP cameras, fire warning systems, biometric readers, 24-hour guards and patrolling units. The Amity University Manesar hostel fees structure for AC rooms is Rs 1.10 lakh and for Non-AC rooms, it is Rs 74,000.

What is the Amity University Gurgaon rankings?

Many organisations have recognised Amity University Gurugram and ranked it for its excellence in the education field. Below are the rankings received by the university. It was ranked 94th in the University category, 99 in Engineering, 81 in Management and 23 in Law category by NIRF Ranking 2023. It was placed in the rank band of 101-150 by the NIRF Innovation Ranking 2023. Amity University Gurgaon ranking also includes it being ranked 5th overall amongst the top universities in India and 1st in Life on Land by THE Impact Rankings 2023.

What is the Amity University Manesar address?

The Amity University Manesar campus is located in Gurgaon, Haryana on a picturesque, 110-acre of land. The campus is situated in close proximity to Gurgaon, one of India's largest corporate hubs. It is only a 30-minute drive from the IGI Airport. The location is very suitable and is well connected. The complete address of the university is Amity Education Valley Gurugram (Manesar), Haryana - 122 413. The campus also has a 5-acre Herbal Park and 200+ labs among other facilities.

What is Amity University Gurgaon BSc Nursing fees?

The B.Sc Nursing programme offered at Amity University Manesar is four years long and divided into eight semesters. To secure admission in this programme, candidates must have passed the 10+2 qualifying examination from a recognised board with at least 45% marks. At the 10+2 level candidates must have Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as main subjects. The Amity University Manesar BSc Nursing fees structure Rs 73,000. The fees are for the first year non sponsored semester.

What is the Amity University Manesar admission process?

The application process is straightforward. The following are the steps applicants need to do in order to apply for Amity University Manesar admission.  Visit the official website (amity.edu/gurugram). Click on the ‘Apply Now’ button. Fill out the Amity University Manesar application form. Submit the application form online. Upload a soft copy of the class 10 mark sheet. Upload a recent colour passport-size photograph. Make the fee payment online via Credit or Debit Card. The Amity University Manesar application fees is Rs 1,100.

What is the Amity University Gurgaon MA fees?

The Amity University Manesar MA courses are offered in a variety of specialisations. The programme duration is two years. Admission is done on the basis of the merit and entrance test. Candidates must have a Graduation Degree in a relevant field with at least 50% marks. The Amity University Manesar MA fees structure for the following programmes is: MA English: Rs 47,000. MA Economics: Rs 45,000. MA Journalism & Mass Communication: Rs 1 lakh. MA Political Science: Rs 47,000. MA Counselling Psychology: Rs 37,000. MA Applied Psychology: Rs 37,000.

How is the placements according to the Amity University Manesar placement report?

Amity University Manesar has provided the placement data 2023 on the official website, indicating that 423 organisations participated in the Amity University Manesar placements. 291 National Companies and 132 Fortune 500+ MNCs participated. 266 fresh recruits and 157 returning recruiters participated also. The IT&ITES, Ed-Tech, and Services sectors accounted for the majority of the organisations that participated in the Amity University Manesar placements. The salary package 2023 was not yet provided, however, the highest package reported was Rs 45 LPA, while the average package was Rs 5 LPA, based on the placement data 2022.

What is the Amity University Gurgaon MBA fees?

The Amity University Manesar MBA courses are of two years duration. It offers specialisations in  Retail, Finance, IB, Marketing & Sales, HR, E-Commerce and IT. Candidates who passed with a Graduation Degree with at least 50% marks are eligible. The Amity University Gurgaon MBA fees structure is Rs 2.04 lakh. The other MBA courses provided here are Business Analytics, Banking & Finance, Sustainability Management, Hospital & Healthcare Management, Executive, International and Executive MBA (Sustainability Management).

Is Amity University Manesar UGC recognised?

Yes, Amity University Manesar is recognised by the University of Grants Commission (UGC). Established as a State Private University by Act (No.10 of 2010) of the State Legislature of Haryana, the university is authorised by section 22 of the UGC Act 1956 to confer degrees as prescribed by the UGC. The Amity University Manesar, Gurgaon is also a member of AIU, approved by the Rehabilitation Council of India, PCI and COA. The university is also accredited by the ACBSP USA, ASIC UK, Indian Nursing Council and IET UK.

What is the Amity University Gurgaon fees for BTech in CSE?

The university offers a four year long B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering programme which is divided into eight semesters. Candidates with 60% aggregate marks at the 10+2 level with subjects PCM/B/CS/Electronics/Biotech/IT/ Informatics Practices/ Technical Vocational Subject/ Agriculture/ Engineering Graphics/ Business Studies/ Entrepreneurship are eligible. The  Amity University Gurgaon BTech CSE fees is Rs 1.40 lakh. The other B.Tech CSE programmes include International (4 years), 3 Continent (4 years) and B.Tech+MBA (5 years).

What is the requirement for Amity University Manesar PhD admission?

The Amity University Manesar PhD courses are provided in full-time and part-time modes. The basic admission requirement is that candidates must possess a Masters's Degree (min. 2 years)/ M.Phil in a relevant discipline with at least 55% marks or equivalent grade. For the Amity University PhD admission, candidates have to take a written test and PI. Candidates who have cleared an All India National Level Competitive Exams such as CSIR/ ICAR (ASRB)/ NET of UGC/ GPAT / ICMR/ GATE/ DST - INSPIRE will be exempted from the written test and will directly be able to proceed to the PI session.
