About Global Research Institute of Pharmacy

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy is a private institute founded in 2017. The institute is located in Haryana, India. GRIP is approved by Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) under section on 12 & Department of Technical Education (DTE) Govt. of Haryana and is affiliated to Pandit Bhagwat Dayal Sharma University of Health Science, Rohtak as well as Haryana State Board of Technical Education (HSBTE), Panchkula and is rated amongst top pharmacy Institute in Haryana.

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy offer courses like B.Pharmacy and D.Pharmacy. GRIP admissions are considered based on the class 12 merit and HSTES.

GRIP has placed over 1500 students in the previous academic year with the highest package of Rs 11 lakh per annum and the average package of Rs 3 lakh per annum. There are a number of top companies that visit for recruiting students such as  Ben Pharmaceuticals, Mc Dill Laboratories, Radicals Pharma Ltd., Ron and Baker, Satiate Research & Anatech Pvt. Ltd. The college has recruited well-qualified and scholarly faculties to impart quality education and training.

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy Courses and Admissions 2026

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy offers UG and diploma courses in pharmacy. The popular GRIP courses include BPharmacy and DPharmacy, Admissions are based on the merit of qualifying examination. For UG and diploma courses class 12 scores are required. Admission in D. Pharmacy and B. Pharmacy courses are made on the basis of qualifying examination through online off-campus counseling by the Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES). Global Research Institute of Pharmacy fees range from Rs 55,200 to Rs 72,250 annually. GRIP courses are offered in a regular mode.

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

State-of-the-art infrastructure and an environment-friendly campus await students at the university. 

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy Campus

The college campus is spread over 11 acres and is located 5km from the Radaur bus stand. It is well-planned with flowerbeds, pathways, and plantations amidst the hustle and bustle of the city. A magnificent building houses the college cafeteria, labs, library, and library.

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy Infrastructure

  • A rich, green landscape embraces the Global Research Institute of Pharmacy Yamunanagar campus. 
  • Boys and girls stay in separate hostels, which are fully insulated and provide all basic amenities. Colleges and hostels offer backup power supplies and internet access.
  • During the summers, the lecture rooms of the institute provide a better atmosphere for the students. The rooms are airy, illuminated, with an ergonomic seat and whiteboards, and furnished with curtains for maintaining a cooler atmosphere.
  • The institution has a well-equipped and spacious library with subscriptions to international and national journals and magazines, as well as thousands of books pertaining to Pharmaceuticals and related fields. Students have access to a book bank. E-books are also available with this facility.

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy Facilities

  • Campus students have access to a full LAN network and Wi-Fi. Eight computer labs equipped with around 250 PCs running a variety of software are available.
  • Several language lab stations, equipped with computers, headphone jacks, and the latest software, are available to the students so they can improve their communication skills.
  • Students can gain a number of important skills through multimedia labs, such as public speaking, group discussions, debates, and presentation skills.
  • Seminar rooms include multimedia projectors, audio equipment, and amplifiers, as well as state-of-the-art technology for academic and cultural events. 
  • The institute has an air-conditioned conference room that can accommodate 50 participants at a time for meetings/conferences and is a fully-furnished and spacious seminar hall with the capacity to accommodate 300 students at once.
  • For students' convenience, the college has a fully equipped LAN and Wi-Fi network. These GRIP hostel facilities are available in the hostel (Boys & Girls) 24/7.
  • An institute museum offers details about all the fields of Pharmacy and displays various crude drugs and popular patent medicines inappropriate display cabinets, along with a library counter.
  • A variety of human body organs, as well as crude drug preparation samples, can be found in the Museum.
  • The university has established separate pharmaceutics laboratories containing different machines used in the pharmaceutical industry as part of the formulation, production quality control, and packaging processes.
  • GRIP has a herb garden with over 100 different kinds of herbs demonstrating their medicinal properties. By studying herbal medicine, students increase their knowledge of traditional medicine.
  • A full range of state-of-the-art analytical instruments such as spectrophotometers, polarimeters, and colourimeters is available to the students, and they are provided hands-on training.

Global Research Institute of Pharmacy Scholarships

The institute provides multiple scholarships to the students. The scholarship is provided on the basis of merit scores of students:

  • Mata Savitri Devi Merit Scholarship:

Students occupying a position from 1 to 5 in the university shall be given this scholarship. In addition to a merit certificate, a cash award of Rs 5100 shall be paid (one time).

  • Mata Savitri Devi Beti Bachao Beti Padhao Merit Scholarship:

Girls will be given a scholarship of Rs 5100 until the end of the academic year. The scholarship will be given on the college's annual day, and a merit certificate will be given to each recipient.

  • Lala Prem Chand Merit Scholarship:

Students who excel in university examinations at least 85% of the time, branch-wise/year-wise, will be eligible to receive this scholarship. This scholarship is worth R 5100 and comes along with a merit certificate.

  • Lala Prem Chand Merit Scholarship To Top Position Holder:

The scholarship will be given to students who score at least 85% marks in the qualifying examination. This scholarship will be for Rs 5100  and shall be accompanied by a merit certificate.

  • Lala Prem Chand Special Meritorious Competition Scholarship:

Scholarships are awarded to highly meritorious applicants who appear and pass a competitive exam such as the GATE examination, the IAS/IPS examination, the IFS examination, or the HES examination. The amount of the award is Rs 11,000 with a certificate to that effect.

  • Lala Sumat Parshad All Round Personality Development Scholarship:

On the annual day of college, this scholarship will be presented to the Best Student in the school showing all-around development of personality. The award amount is Rs 11,000 (one time) along with a merit certificate.

  • Lala Sumat Parshad Best Project Award:

Students preparing the Best Project shall be awarded this scholarship, which is worth Rs 11,000 (one time), along with a merit certificate.
