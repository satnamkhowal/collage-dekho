About LPU PhD

LPU PhD is a 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for a Ph.D. degree at Lovely Professional University, applicants must have a Master's degree in a relevant field with a minimum of 55% marks or equivalent grade. They must also have a valid score in the entrance test conducted by the university. The annual fee for PhD is INR 1,00,000 - 2,40,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for LPU PhD admission are given below:

LPU PhD Fees

LPU PhD fees is 1,00,000 INR (Yearly) and goes up to 2,40,000 INR (Yearly) depending on the specialization. The average fees for the PhD course is 1,20,727 INR (Yearly). LPU PhD admission 2025 will be based on LPUNEST.
