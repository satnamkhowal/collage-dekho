IIT Kanpur Admission FAQs

What is the withdrawal policy for IIT Kanpur admission 2026?

IIT Kanpur admissions 2026, PG applicants who withdraw before December 13, 2026, face a Rs 1,000 deduction from the application fee, while those withdrawing after January 15, 2027, are eligible for a full refund. For UG programs, if any seats remain unfilled after the regular JoSAA rounds, spot rounds are conducted in August 2026 to fill these vacancies. These policies ensure fairness and flexibility in the admission process, allowing candidates to plan accordingly, while optimizing seat utilization across undergraduate and postgraduate programs at IIT Kanpur for the 2026–27 academic year.

What is the selection weightage for IIT Kanpur MBA 2026?

IIT Kanpur MBA admissions, the selection process for 147 seats is based on a weighted system: 50% from CAT scores, 30% from personal interview (PI) and group discussion (GD), and 20% from academics and overall profile. The incoming batch is projected to have approximately 85% candidates with engineering backgrounds, reflecting the institute’s strong STEM focus. The average work experience of selected students is expected to be around 24 months, based on trends from the 2025–27 admissions cycles. This combination ensures a highly competitive, academically strong, and industry-ready MBA cohort at IIT Kanpur.

What is IIT Kanpur admission 2026 for foreign students?

At IIT Kanpur, a portion of undergraduate seats is reserved for international applicants through the DASA scheme, accounting for 10% of total UG seats, with admission based on JEE Main scores. For postgraduate programs, 5 seats per specialization are available for international students via JAM (for MSc) or OIR (for MTech/PhD), providing global access to IIT Kanpur’s advanced programs. Additionally, candidates holding OCI or PIO status are treated as General category applicants for admissions through JoSAA, ensuring they compete on equal footing with Indian applicants in UG seat allocation. This promotes both diversity and merit-based selection.

How to apply for IIT Kanpur MSc admission 2026?

IIT Kanpur MSc admissions 2026, candidates must secure a JAM 2026 rank, for example, under 98 in Physics Round 3 (General category). Admission is conducted via CCMN registration in June–July 2026, covering a total of 220 seats across four MSc programs including Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Statistics. Applicants must have a Bachelor’s degree with at least 60% marks to be eligible. The application fee is Rs 500, applicable to all General/OBC/EWS candidates, with reduced fees for reserved categories. Selection is merit-based, ensuring highly qualified candidates are admitted to IIT Kanpur’s rigorous postgraduate science programs.

How many seats for BSc Physics at IIT Kanpur 2026?

IIT Kanpur BSc Physics 2026 admissions, there are a total of 80 seats, distributed as 32 General, 22 OBC, 12 SC, 6 ST, 8 EWS, and 4 PwD. Admission is through JoSAA counseling based on JEE Advanced 2026 ranks, with the General category cutoff at 5,691. Additionally, 20% of the seats are reserved for female candidates, promoting gender diversity in the program. This ensures a balanced and merit-based selection process. Candidates meeting the eligibility criteria and rank requirements can secure admission into this four-year undergraduate program in Physics at IIT Kanpur.

What is the application fee for IIT Kanpur PG admission 2026?

The application fees for IIT Kanpur 2026 admissions vary by program and category. For postgraduate programs (MTech/MSc/PhD) applied via pgadmission.iitk.ac.in, the fee is Rs 500 for General, OBC, and EWS candidates, while SC, ST, and PwD applicants pay Rs 250. For the MBA program, the application fee is Rs 2,000 for General candidates, with a reduced fee of Rs 1,000 for reserved categories. All fees are non-refundable, regardless of the outcome of the application. These structured charges help manage the administrative process for multiple programs and ensure equitable access for all applicants.

What are the PhD admission 2026 dates at IIT Kanpur?

IIT Kanpur PhD admissions 2027, applications are open from October 21 to November 10, 2026 for the January 2027 intake, with interviews scheduled in December 2026. The programs offer over 500 seats across 18 departments, including engineering, science, and interdisciplinary research areas. Eligibility requires candidates to have a Master’s degree with at least 60% marks or a BTech/B.E. with 70% and a valid GATE or UGC-NET score. Selection is based on academic merit, research aptitude, and performance in interviews. The intake ensures highly qualified candidates join IIT Kanpur’s advanced research programs.

When is the application window for IIT Kanpur MBA admission 2026?

IIT Kanpur MBA admissions 2026, applications are open from December 30, 2026, to February 5, 2027 through mbaadmission.iitk.ac.in. Applicants should have a Mathematics background, ensuring proficiency in quantitative and analytical skills required for management studies. Shortlisted candidates are called for interviews and further selection processes in January 2027. The program offers 147 MBA seats, combining rigorous academics, industry exposure, and leadership training at IIT Kanpur.

How many seats are available for MTech programs at IIT Kanpur 2026?

IIT Kanpur MTech admissions 2026, a total of 800 seats are available across 25 specializations, filled based on GATE 2026 scores. Minimum qualifying scores vary by branch, such as 500 for Aerospace Engineering (AE paper) and 650 for Computer Science (CS paper). Candidates may apply through COAP registration for seat allocation. These criteria ensure merit-based selection while accommodating professional candidates, maintaining both academic excellence and industry collaboration across IIT Kanpur’s postgraduate engineering programs.

What is the eligibility for IIT Kanpur BTech admission 2026?

IIT Kanpur BTech admission 2026, candidates must have a minimum 75% aggregate in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects.
