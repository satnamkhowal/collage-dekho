About SRM University Delhi-NCR

SRM University Delhi-NCR, Sonepat is a private university located in Rajiv Gandhi Education City, Rai, Sonepat, Haryana. The university was established in 2013 and is recognised by UGC and accredited by NAAC. It is also approved by AICTE, BCI, AIU, and DSIR. As per IIRF Ranking 2024, the university is ranked 75 under the B.Tech category.

The campus spans 48 acres and offers UG and PG programmes in Engineering, Law, Management, Science, and Arts. UG annual fees range from INR 55,000 to INR 3,00,000. PG annual fees range from INR 60,000 to INR 1,75,000. Admissions are based on SRMJEEE, JEE Main, CLAT, GATE, CAT, or SRMHCAT depending on the programme.

Campus facilities include a library, air-conditioned auditorium with 1,000-person capacity, computer labs, boys and girls hostels, sports grounds, healthcare centre, and transportation services. Hostel fee is INR 1,50,000 per year and mess fee is INR 85,000 per year.

In the 2025 placement season, the university recorded 2,024+ placements with 925+ companies visiting the campus. The highest package was INR 50 LPA offered by Emory Healthcare. The median B.Tech package was INR 5.50 LPA and median MBA package was INR 6.37 LPA. Top recruiters include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, Cognizant, IBM, and EY.

The university offers CUET-based scholarships ranging from 10% to 100% based on percentile scores.

SRM University Sonepat Admission 2026

SRM University Delhi-NCR, Sonepat 2026 admissions are based on SRMJEEE, JEE Main, CLAT, GATE, CAT, or merit, depending on the course. SRMJEEE Phase 2 exam is scheduled June 10–15, 2026. SRMHCAT-based counselling for BBA, BCA, MBA, and PG courses is ongoing.

SRM University Sonepat Admission Process 2026

Admission at SRM University Delhi-NCR, Sonepat follows a different process for each faculty. B.Tech is entrance-exam-driven; law is CLAT-based; MBA involves an exam and a personal interview; and most B.Sc./B.A./BCA programmes are merit-based.

B.Tech Admission Process

  • Step 1: Register for SRMJEEE at applications.srmist.edu.in or appear for JEE Main. The application fee for SRMJEEE is INR 1,400 per phase (non-refundable).
  • Step 2: Download your SRMJEEE scorecard after results. Phase 1 result was declared on May 5, 2026. Phase 2 exam is scheduled June 10–15, 2026.
  • Step 3: Wait for the university counselling notification. Shortlisted candidates are called for counselling based on their SRMJEEE/JEE Main rank.
  • Step 4: Attend counselling either online or at the campus. Choose your B.Tech specialisation based on rank and seat availability.
  • Step 5: Complete document verification and pay the first-year tuition fee to confirm your seat.

Law Admission Process (B.A.LL.B. / B.B.A.LL.B. / LL.B.)

  • Step 1: Register for CLAT 2026 at consortiumofnlus.ac.in, or appear for AILET, LSAT India, or SRMHCAT.
  • Step 2: After CLAT merit list release, indicate your choice for SRM University Delhi-NCR during counselling. The 5th merit list releases on May 15, 2026.
  • Step 3: Pay the seat confirmation fee by the deadline mentioned in the merit list notification.
  • Step 4: Report to the university with original documents for verification.
  • Step 5: Pay the full first-semester/first-year tuition fee to complete admission.

MBA Admission Process

  • Step 1: Obtain a valid score in CAT 2025, XAT 2026, MAT, CMAT, NMAT, GMAT, or register for SRMHCAT at srmuniversity.ac.in.
  • Step 2: Apply online at admissions.srmuniversity.ac.in with your scorecard and graduation documents.
  • Step 3: Shortlisted candidates are called for a Personal Interview (PI) conducted by the university.
  • Step 4: Selection is based on entrance exam score, PI performance, and past academic record.
  • Step 5: Pay the first-year MBA fee of INR 1,75,000 to confirm your seat.

M.Tech Admission Process 

  • Step 1: Obtain a valid GATE 2026 score in the relevant paper, or register for SRMHCAT.
  • Step 2: Apply online through the official portal and upload your B.Tech degree and GATE scorecard.
  • Step 3: Attend the counselling round. Selection is based on GATE score, academic record, and other criteria.
  • Step 4: Complete document verification and pay INR 1,00,000 to confirm admission.

BBA / BCA / B.Sc. / B.A. Admission Process

  • Step 1: Register for SRMHCAT at srmuniversity.ac.in, or apply on merit basis directly through the admissions portal.
  • Step 2: The university releases a merit list or shortlist based on SRMHCAT score and Class XII marks.
  • Step 3: Report to the campus for document verification within the notified dates.
  • Step 4: Pay the applicable annual fee to finalise admission.

SRM University Sonepat Admission Required Documents 2026

Candidates must carry original documents and one set of self-attested photocopies at the time of counselling and final admission. The documents required are:

  • Class X (Secondary) marksheet and pass certificate
  • Class XII (Senior Secondary) marksheet and pass certificate
  • Entrance exam scorecard (SRMJEEE / JEE Main / CLAT / GATE / CAT / SRMHCAT as applicable)
  • Graduation degree and marksheets (for PG programmes)
  • Migration certificate from the last board/university attended
  • Transfer certificate from the last institution
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS, if applicable)
  • Haryana domicile certificate (mandatory for Haryana Domicile Scholarship)

  • Aadhaar card or any valid government-issued photo ID
  • Allotment letter / seat acceptance confirmation (for CLAT/SRMJEEE counselling)
  • Character certificate from the last institution
