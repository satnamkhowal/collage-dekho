Most Commonly Question Asked

Do Chandigarh University fees increase every year for students?
Your core tuition fee generally remains locked as per the fee structure of your admission year. However, hostel and accommodation charges face a standard nominal increase of INR 2,000 annually to account for food inflation and maintenance.
How can I reduce my fee at Chandigarh University?
The main way to reduce fees at Chandigarh University is through the CUCET scholarship. Scoring 90–100 in CUCET (Phase 1, October to May) gets you a 100% tuition waiver for most UG and PG programmes. Even a score of 40–50 fetches a 15% waiver in Phase 1. Phase 2 (June to August) has slightly lower scholarship percentages for the same score range. Beyond CUCET, CU offers: the Kalpana Chawla scholarship for girl students, scholarships for defence personnel children and Kargil war heroes' wards, sibling discounts for students whose siblings are already enrolled, and scholarships for national-level sports achievers. JEE Main applicants with ranks below 50,000 get direct admission without needing to appear for CUCET and are eligible for corresponding scholarship slabs. Total scholarships worth INR 200 Crore are offered each year for the Punjab campus.
6. Is Chandigarh University fees worth it compared to placement outcomes?
For BE CSE students, the placement outcomes at CU look favourable relative to the fee. The 2025 placement season saw 10,000+ offers, with over 1,300 companies participating. The highest domestic package was INR 54.75 LPA and the international package reached INR 1.7 Crore. The average package for BTech students ranges from INR 10 LPA to INR 15 LPA for placed students, and 300+ companies offered packages above INR 7 LPA. At a total fee of INR 12.24 Lakhs for BE CSE (without scholarship), the payback period on fees is well under a year at average package levels. That said, placement rates vary by specialisation — CSE and CS-related branches see strong demand, while Civil and Mechanical students have fewer offers.

What is the total fee for BTech at Chandigarh University?
The total fee for BTech (BE) at Chandigarh University for the 4-year programme ranges from INR 8.00 Lakhs to INR 14.84 Lakhs depending on the specialisation you choose. Are studio and material costs included in B.Arch fees?
The B.Arch tuition fee covers academic instruction and studio access. However, students must independently fund their drafting equipment, model-making materials, and mandatory architectural site visits, which are essential components of the five-year professional degree program at Chandigarh University.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Does Chandigarh University charge fees differently for girls and boys?
Chandigarh University does not charge different tuition fees for girls and boys — the academic fee structure is the same across genders. The difference exists only in hostel fees. AC hostel options for girls are available only in 4-seater rooms (INR 1,65,000/year), while boys have access to 5-seater, 3-seater, 2-seater, and 1-seater AC rooms with fees from INR 1,50,000 to INR 2,20,000. Non-AC hostel options are available for both boys and girls in 5-seater, 4-seater, and 3-seater rooms. Single-room (1-seater) hostels, both AC and non-AC, are available only for boys. CU also has a Kalpana Chawla scholarship specifically for girl students that provides additional fee waivers on tuition. The university's women's cell helpline (9877457238) is operational 24x7 on campus.

Most Commonly Question Asked

What is the hostel fee at Chandigarh University per year?
Chandigarh University hostel fees for national students per year range from INR 1,20,000 (5-seater non-AC, the most affordable option) to INR 2,20,000 (1-seater AC, the most premium). The fee is all-inclusive covering accommodation, electricity, water, Wi-Fi, and gym access. Mess charges, covering three meals daily and evening snacks are charged separately at approximately INR 30,000 to INR 35,000 per year. So the effective annual cost of staying at CU's hostel is around INR 1,50,000 to INR 2,55,000 depending on your room type. Girls only have AC options in 4-seater rooms (INR 1,65,000). Single-room options (1-seater, both AC and non-AC) are available only for boys. The university has 16,000+ rooms across separate male and female hostels. Hostel booking is first-come-first-served after admission confirmation.
Is the mess food fee included in CU hostel charges?
No, the standard hostel fee only covers your accommodation, electricity, water, internet, and basic campus amenities. Students must pay separate mess charges, which typically range between INR 30,000 to INR 35,000 annually for daily meals.

Most Commonly Question Asked

What is the fee refund policy at Chandigarh University?
Chandigarh University follows UGC fee refund norms. If you withdraw before your course starts, you get a full refund minus a processing charge of INR 1,000. If you withdraw within 15 days of course commencement, the deduction is still only INR 1,000. Between 16 and 30 days, you get 80% of your tuition back. Between 31 and 60 days, you get 50% back. After 60 days from the start of the course, no tuition refund is given. Hostel and mess fees are refunded on a pro-rata basis. The refundable security deposit (INR 2,000) is returned when you complete the programme. To initiate a refund, submit your request online at cuchd.in/refund/ with your University Account Number, date of birth, and registered mobile. The submission date on the portal is considered as your official application date for refund processing.
How much scholarship reduces Chandigarh University total course fees amount?
Chandigarh University scholarships can reduce fees from 15% up to 100% based on CUCET scores. Higher scores give better fee waivers. This helps many students study at Chandigarh University with much lower overall course cost.
Do Chandigarh University scholarships continue for the entire course?
Yes, but the renewal is not automatic. To maintain your CUCET or merit-based scholarship in subsequent years, you must secure a minimum CGPA of 7.5 and ensure you have no active backlogs or failing grades.

How to Pay Chandigarh University Fees?

Chandigarh University fees can be paid online through the student portal at students.cuchd.in. The fee payment portal is active at the start of each semester. Students can also pay via the Bajaj Finserv BBPS platform. Here's the step-by-step process:

  1. Visit the official student portal of Chandigarh University (students.cuchd.in)
  2. Log in using your student University Account Number and registered mobile number.
  3. Click on the "Fees" tab in the student dashboard.
  4. Select the current semester and the fee type (tuition, hostel, etc.).
  5. Choose your payment method: Net Banking, Credit Card, Debit Card, or UPI.
  6. Enter payment details, complete the transaction, and download your receipt.

For offline payments, students can visit the CU accounts office on campus. Education loan support is also available through the official page at cuchd.in/admissions/education-loan.php. CU has tie-ups with multiple banks for student loans covering up to 100% of the course fee.

Which banks provide education loans for Chandigarh University admissions?
Chandigarh University has official tie-ups with several major national banks, including Punjab National Bank and Indian Overseas Bank. They offer collateral-free student education loans up to INR 4 Lakhs with faster processing times for verified enrolled students.
Can I pay Chandigarh University tuition fees in installments?
The university generally requires students to pay the full semester tuition fee upfront before the term begins. However, you can manage these costs by utilizing zero-cost EMI options or formal student education loans through partnered banks.
What is the late fee penalty at Chandigarh University?
Chandigarh University imposes a daily or weekly late fee fine if semester dues are not cleared by the official deadline. Consistent failure to pay may result in blocked student portal access or the withholding of admit cards.
