About GCE Gurgaon

Gurgaon College of Engineering is situated in Gurgaon in Haryana state of India. Established in 2008, GCE is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak and accredited by AICTE. Gurgaon College of Engineering offers 7 courses across 1 streams namely Engineering..Popular degrees offered at Gurgaon College of Engineering include B.Tech, M.Tech..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Gurgaon College of Engineering is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Gurgaon College of Engineering, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Gurgaon College of Engineering is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
