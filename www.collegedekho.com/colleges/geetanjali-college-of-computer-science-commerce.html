About Geetanjali Group of Colleges

Geetanjali Group of Colleges, Rajkot, was founded in 1999 as a private educational institution to provide higher education to students interested in pursuing careers in management, science, and computer applications. Shailesh M. Jani is in charge of the Geetanjali Group of Colleges, Rajkot (Chairman). The teachers, students, administration, and management of Geetanjali Group of Colleges foster a culture of collaboration, trust, mutual respect, care for others, hard work, social value, personal development, and honesty. As a result of this culture, students can interact with the business sector and the general public with similar efficiency.

GGC Rajkot has built a unique education centre called "Geetanjali college of computer science & commerce" connected with Saurashtra University and recognised by the Gujarat government to meet worldwide demands. It Invites and welcomes all students to a unique centre of excellence to impart world-class education and training in business management, information technology, and commerce. 

This institution is committed to implementing the principle of "Total Quality Management" and pursuing our mission of providing high-quality education by utilising resources such as experienced faculty, integrated education methodology, and a large library with recent publications and management classics. This would emphasise to pupils that education is the key to unlocking the door to success. All students are approaching the golden years of college life with hopes of success and accomplishment.

Geetanjali Group of Colleges Courses and Admission 2026

Candidates may learn more about the admission procedure on the official website of the Geetanjali Group of Colleges, Rajkot. By checking in to the admission microsite, candidates may keep track of the progress of their application forms. Candidates are asked to participate in the selection process, which may include an entrance exam, a group discussion, or a personal interview, after they have been approved. If an applicant is selected, they will get a digital admission letter that must be presented to the institution. Admissions for UG and PG courses at the Geetanjali Group of Colleges, Rajkot begin in April each year. Candidates interested in a specific course can apply online at the official website.

  • According to the college's guidelines, there are a limited number of seats available. The ACPC or their respective board will set the application deadline.
  • Candidates will be chosen for the final round based on their performance in the group activity (GA)/discussion (GD) and personal interview (PI).

This is how students must fill out the Geetanjali Group of Colleges, Rajkot applications.

  • You must visit the official website of Geetanjali Group of Colleges.
  • You must provide basic information such as your name, address, phone number, and email address.
  • A soft copy of the Class 12 certificate should be uploaded.
  • Enter all grades up to UG level for admission to PG programmes.
  • Once you've double-checked all of the information, click Submit.
  • This college will release its cutoff and merit list for courses in management, commerce, science, law, education, and computer application programmes.
  • Geetanjali Group Of College (GGP) departments and colleges for which cutoffs will be calculated depending on reservation categories and seat availability. 

Geetanjali Group Of Colleges Rajkot Eligibility Criteria 

  • Geetanjali Group of Colleges admissions is done gradually, based on the merit gained in the certified test.
  • Students must have a cumulative grade point average of 45- 48 per cent in UG subjects in Class 12 or equivalent examinations. 
  • A bachelor's degree in a relevant field is required for PG courses.

Geetanjali Group Of Colleges Rajkot Entrance Exams

There is no entrance exam, and admission is based on qualifying exam results. Online applications are accepted by the Geetanjali Group of Colleges, Rajkot. Visit the official website for more information. Here is a list including the entrance Exams for Geetanjali Group of Colleges, Rajkot.

  • There is no entrance exam for admission at B.Com, B.Sc, BBA and BCA courses. Selection to these courses is done based on merit.
  • There is no entrance exam for admission to M.Com and M.Sc. The selection is done based on merit only. 
  • There is no entrance exam for admission to PGD and B.Ed. The selection process is based on merit. 
  • There is no entrance exam for admission to PGDCA. Selection to these courses is done based on merit.
