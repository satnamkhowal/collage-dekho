About NIMS University Jaipur

NIMS University is a private university located in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and was established in 2008 by Prof Dr Balvir S. Tomar under the NIMS University Act. The Government of India recognises NIMS University as per the provisions of section 2(f) of the University Grants Commissions Act (UGC), 1956. 

NIMS University, Jaipur has also received approvals from UGC, NMC, DCI, PCI, BCI, INC, AICTE, COA, AIU, RNC, RPC,Council of Architecture (COA) and AIU. This university is also accredited with the NAAC garde. It has received many awards for its quality teaching and research-oriented disciplines. To mention a few, NIMS University has been awarded the "Best University" in India by CEGR (2021), "Best Institute for Health Care Services in India by SPGHTN, India (2020)", "Best University for Skill Development by ASSOCHAM, New Delhi (2018).

NIMS University  offers 450+ courses include several undergraduate, postgraduate and PhD programmes in engineering, medical, dental, management, commerce, pharmacy, architecture, humanities, law etc. Some of the popular courses at NIMS University, Jaipur, are B.Tech, MBBS, BBA, B.Pharm, M.Tech, MBA, etc. NIMS University admissions are conducted on the basis of entrance exams like NEET UG/ JEE Mains/ GATE/ MAT/ CAT/ CLAT / GPAT and marks secured in the qualifying examinations. The university also conducts its in-house entrance examination for some courses.

Regarding the NIMS University placements, over 45000+ students have obtained offers in more than 2000+ companies. The highest domestic package secured is Rs 60 LPA, Highest international package is Rs 1.2 Crore. Google, Wipro, Aditya Birla Group, Fortis, Amazon, Mahindra, HDFC Bank, etc., are some of the top recruiters that have offered placements to NIMS University, Jaipur students. NIMS University, Jaipur boasts a sprawling 250-acre campus, surrounded by the Aravali hills. Classrooms, libraries, food courts, ATMs, grocery stores, hostels, medical facilities, and 24*7 security on campus are some of the facilities available at NIMS University.

NIMS University Jaipur Admission 2026

NIMS University Jaipur admission process 2026 is conducted online, including registration, application submission, entrance exam scores or NIMSEE, counselling, document verification, and seat allotment. Candidates must meet eligibility criteria and confirm admission by paying the required fee within the deadline.

The university accepts national exams like JEE Main, NEET UG/PG, CAT, GATE, and CLAT, with key dates from January to November 2026. Counselling, merit list, admission confirmation, and academic session are scheduled between June and August 2026.

NIMS University Jaipur Admission Process 2026

The NIMS University application process 2026 is being held online. Candidates can follow the steps below and proceed accordingly -

Process Details
Check EligibilityVerify minimum eligibility for the selected course. NEET is mandatory for medical programmes.
RegistrationApply online through the official admission portal using mobile number and email ID.
Application FormFill personal and academic details. Upload required documents (Class 10 & 12 marksheets, photo, ID proof).

Merit List & CounsellingCandidates are shortlisted based on merit and called for counselling (online/offline).

NIMS University Jaipur Campus

NIMS Jaipur campus spans 250 acres on the Jaipur–Delhi Highway (NH-11C). It has classrooms, advanced labs, and a central library with 62,000+ books. Separate hostels for boys and girls have 24/7 security. Sports facilities include a swimming pool, courts, and athletic tracks. A 1,400+ bed hospital provides on-campus medical care. Food court, ATM, post office, laundry, and transport services are available on campus.

Classrooms NIMS University classrooms are purpose-built for focused learning. Each room is equipped with modern audio-visual tools, projectors, and comfortable seating. The layout encourages interaction between students and faculty. Classes are designed to support both theory and practical discussions across all programmes.

Library NIMS Central Library spans 2,500 sq. m. and houses over 62,000 books, 288 print journals, and dedicated section libraries for each college and faculty. Students have access to e-books, e-journals, and back volumes of research publications. The library is fully air-conditioned with Wi-Fi facility. It serves as a primary resource centre for academic and research needs across all disciplines.

Labs NIMS University provides state-of-the-art laboratories across medical, science, engineering, and technology disciplines. Each lab is equipped with advanced instruments and tools for hands-on training. Students get direct exposure to real-world equipment from the early years of their programme. Lab sessions run alongside theory classes to reinforce practical knowledge and skills.

Tech Invent & Events NIMS University hosts technology events, innovation challenges, and prototype competitions for students across streams. These events give students a platform to build, test, and showcase their ideas beyond the classroom. Participation is open to all students regardless of their programme. It creates an environment where creativity and problem-solving are practised, not just taught.

Hostel & Fitness Centre NIMS University provides separate hostel accommodation for boys and girls with 24/7 security and CCTV surveillance. Each hostel block has basic amenities including Wi-Fi, common rooms, and laundry services. An on-campus fitness centre is available for students within the residential zone. The hostel environment is designed to support both academic focus and personal well-being.

Sports & Adventure NIMS University has indoor and outdoor sports facilities on campus including a swimming pool, basketball and tennis courts, badminton courts, athletic tracks, and a fitness centre. Students can participate in inter-college and inter-university sports competitions. The university supports student athletes with proper coaching and infrastructure. Sports activities are open to all students as part of campus life.

Clubs & Societies NIMS University has multiple student-run clubs and societies covering areas like technology, arts, literature, social work, and entrepreneurship. These clubs give students a space to pursue interests outside academics and develop leadership skills. Regular meetings, workshops, and inter-college events are organised by each club. Membership is open to all enrolled students across programmes.

Cultural Programs NIMS University organises cultural festivals, performances, and events throughout the academic year. These programmes celebrate regional and national traditions, giving students from different states a platform to represent their culture. Events include music, dance, theatre, and themed celebrations. Cultural programmes are open to all students and are a regular part of campus life.

Bollywood Celebrities NIMS University invites Bollywood celebrities and performers for live shows, annual fests, and special campus events. These events are organised as part of the university's annual cultural calendar. Past events have featured known artists and performers from the Indian entertainment industry. These occasions add to the overall student experience on campus.

ATM and Post Office NIMS University campus has an ATM and a post office within its premises for day-to-day student needs. The ATM provides easy access to banking services without leaving the campus. The post office handles document dispatch, courier, and postal services. These facilities save students time and reduce the need to travel off-campus for basic tasks.

Transport Facility NIMS University provides transport services for students commuting from Jaipur city and nearby areas. The campus is located on the Jaipur–Delhi Highway (NH-11C), making it accessible by road. University buses cover key routes connecting the campus to the city. Students can also use private transport with good road connectivity to the campus.

Laundry Services On-campus laundry services are available for hostel students at NIMS University. Students can drop off and collect their clothes without leaving the campus. The service is designed to reduce the daily workload of residential students. It is one of several support services provided within the student residential zone.

Shopping Mall / Grocery Store NIMS University campus has a grocery and shopping facility for students' daily needs. Students can purchase stationery, toiletries, food items, and other essentials without going off-campus. This reduces dependence on outside markets for routine purchases. The facility is located within the campus for easy access to all residential students.

Mess NIMS University provides a mess facility for hostel students with meals served three times a day. The menu is planned to provide nutritious and hygienic food to students. Separate mess facilities are available for boys and girls hostels. Meal timings are fixed and aligned with the academic schedule of the university.

Food Court / Restaurants NIMS University has a food court and restaurant options on campus including Le Gabriel restaurant and multiple food joints. Students can choose from a variety of cuisines and meal options throughout the day. The food court serves as a common social space for students between classes. Over six food options are available within the campus premises.

NIMS Hospital & Emergency Facility NIMS University has a 1,400+ bed multispecialty hospital on campus that also serves as an emergency care centre for students and staff. Medical services are available 24/7 for any health-related needs. The hospital is NABH and NABL accredited, ensuring quality healthcare standards. Students of medical programmes also use the hospital for clinical training and rotations.

Campus Surveillance NIMS University campus is covered by CCTV cameras across all key areas including hostels, academic blocks, and entry and exit points. Security personnel are deployed round the clock to monitor campus activity. The surveillance system is part of the university's broader safety and student welfare framework. Both boys and girls hostels have dedicated security arrangements in place.
