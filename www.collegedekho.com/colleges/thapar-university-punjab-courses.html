Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology Courses 2026

Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET), part of Thapar University, offers a wide range of courses at UG, PG, and doctoral levels across various disciplines such as engineering, management, sciences, and liberal arts. At the UG level, TIET offers popular courses like B.E. / B.Tech, B.A., B.Sc., and BBA. While at the PG level, courses like M.E. / M.Tech, MBA, M.Sc., and MCA are offered. The institute also offers integrated programs like B.E. - MBA and PhD programs in various fields. The annual TIET fees range from Rs 4.7 lakhs to Rs 19.6 lakhs depending on the type and level of courses.

Thapar University FAQs

Which undergraduate programmes at Thapar University require 70% aggregate in 10+2 for admission?
BBA, BSc, BCom, and BA programmes require a minimum 70% aggregate in 10+2 in any stream for admission.
What is the eligibility criterion for BTech/BE programmes at Thapar University?
Candidates must have a minimum 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) or Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) or technical vocational subjects in 10+2 (55% for SC/ST candidates).
Which BTech specialisations at Thapar University have the highest fee of INR 25 lakhs?
The BTech specialisations with fees of INR 25 lakhs are: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Computer Engineering, Electronics and Computer Engineering, and Data Science & Artificial Intelligence.
What is the fee range for undergraduate programmes at Thapar University?
The fees for undergraduate programmes range from INR 4.70 lakhs to INR 25 lakhs for the entire programme duration.
How many specialisations does Thapar University offer across its undergraduate programmes?
Thapar University offers a total of 32 specialisations across five undergraduate disciplines: BTech (17 specialisations), BBA, BA, BSc, and BCom.

Thapar University FAQs

What are the eligibility criteria for admission to Thapar University’s IEP 2+2 programme?
Candidates must have at least 60% aggregate in 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) or Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB for Bioengineering). Admission is based on JEE Main or NEET percentile through counselling.
Which partner universities are available under Thapar University’s 2+2 International Engineering Programme (IEP) and what are the corresponding programmes?
The partner universities and programmes include: University of Queensland (Australia): Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil EngineeringUniversity of Toledo (USA): Bioengineering, Computer Science and Engineering/Computer Science University of New South Wales (Australia): Electrical Engineering, Quantum Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering, Chemical EngineeringTrinity College Dublin (Ireland): Computer Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering
What is the fee structure for Thapar University's International Engineering Programme (IEP) for 2025?
The fees for the IEP 2+2 programme mirror the standard BTech rates at TIET, starting at Rs. 358,500 per semester plus a one-time admission fee of Rs. 30,000. These fees apply only for the first two years spent at Thapar Institute.

Thapar University FAQs

What is the eligibility criterion for ME/MTech programmes at Thapar University?
Candidates must have at least 60% marks in their graduation (BE/BTech or equivalent). For SC/ST candidates, the minimum required marks are 55%.
In which discipline is the MA programme offered at Thapar University, and what are its specialisations?
The MA programme is offered only in Psychology under the Thapar School of Liberal Arts and Sciences (TSLAS). It has three specialisations: General Psychology, Clinical Psychology, and Counselling Psychology (also referred to as I/O Psychology in some contexts). The programme is available in both 1-year and 2-year durations.
What are the different postgraduate (PG) courses offered by Thapar University (TIET)?
Thapar University offers five regular postgraduate programmes: ME/MTech, MCA, MSc, MA, and MSc (Biotechnology, Mathematics & Computing, Chemistry, Physics).

Thapar University FAQs

What is the duration of the PhD programme at TIET?
The minimum duration is 3 years and the maximum duration is 5 years for the PhD programme.
What are the eligibility criteria for admission to PhD at TIET?
Candidates must have a minimum of 60% marks or a CGPA of 6.00 on a 10-point scale in their Master’s degree. For candidates with a BTech degree, a minimum CGPA of 7.50 or 75% marks is required. Additionally, many specialisations require GATE or CSIR-UGC NET qualification.
What is the fee structure for PhD programmes at TIET?
The fees for the regular PhD programme range from Rs. 54,000 to Rs. 90,000 per year, depending on the specialisation. For the part-time PhD programme, the total fees are Rs. 3 lakhs.
