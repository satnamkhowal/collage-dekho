About Amity University Noida BSc

Amity University Noida BSc is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Sc. (Bachelor of Science) degree at Amity University, Noida, candidates must fulfill the following eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 examination from a recognized board with Science stream, including Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology, securing a minimum aggregate score of 55%. Additionally, candidates should have a valid score in the Amity Joint Entrance Examination (AJEE) or any other national/state-level entrance exam. Admissions are also granted based on merit, subject to availability of seats. Aspiring students must meet these requirements to embark on a rewarding academic journey in the field of science at Amity University, Noida. The annual fee for BSc is INR 53,000 - 5,04,000.
