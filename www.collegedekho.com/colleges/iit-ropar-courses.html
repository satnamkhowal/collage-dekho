IIT Ropar Courses and Fees 2026

IIT Ropar offers a total of over 42 courses at Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Doctoral levels. These courses are available in various disciplines such as Engineering, Science, and Technology. At the UG level, IIT Ropar provides popular courses like B.E. / B.Tech, B.Sc., and B.Arch. While at the PG level, courses like M.E./M.Tech, M.Sc., and MBA are offered. The university also provides Ph.D. programs in various specializations.

The annual fees at IIT Ropar range from approximately ₹60,000 to ₹8.5 lakh, depending on the type and level of the course.

Ankita Sarkar •  Jun-14-2023

Hello Arya,

The JEE Main 2023 exam results are taken into consideration for admission to the B.Tech programmes at the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar. The JEE Advanced Result 2023 release date is June 18, 2023. It will be released by IIT Guwahati on the official website (jeeadv.ac.in). To view their results, candidates must log in using their login credentials (registration ID and password).  For the JEE Advanced 2023 exam, 80+ points are regarded as good, whereas the minimum passing criteria is 70 marks. The JEE Advanced exam will only be available to the top 2,50,000 JEE Main exam takers. To check the IIT JEE result 2023 candidates can follow the procedure below:

  • Go to jeeadv.ac.in, the official website.
  • Select the 'IIT JEE Advanced Result Link' link.
  • Your Roll number, birthdate, and phone number should be entered in the corresponding fields.
  • The "I'm not a robot" checkbox should be selected.
  • When you click the "Submit" button, the JEE Advanced Result 2023 will be shown on the screen.
  • Take a printout of the result by downloading it for future use.

IIT Ropar Courses FAQs

What types of courses does IIT Ropar offer?

At the UG level, IIT Ropar offers B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech , etc, while at the PG level, IIT Ropar offers M.Tech, MSc, MS, etc.

Which courses are popular at IIT Ropar?

The most popular IIT Ropar courses include the B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, M.Tech, MSc, MS and PhD programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MSc, MS , etc. IIT Ropar also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does IIT Ropar accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at IIT Ropar. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is IIT Ropar easy to get into?

Admission in IIT Ropar courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the IIT Ropar Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include GATE, COAP, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, UGC NET, CSIR NET, JEST, IIT JAM etc.
