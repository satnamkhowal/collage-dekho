About IET Ropar

College of Architecture IET is situated in Ropar in Punjab state of India. Established in 2004, IET is a Private college. The college is accredited by Government of Punjab, AICTE. College of Architecture IET offers 11 courses across 3 streams namely Engineering, Medical, IT..Popular degrees offered at College of Architecture IET include B.Arch, B.Tech, M.Arch, Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, College of Architecture IET is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at College of Architecture IET, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at College of Architecture IET is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
