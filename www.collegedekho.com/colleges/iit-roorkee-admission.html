IIT Roorkee Admission 2026

IIT Roorkee admission is primarily based on national-level entrance examinations and merit in the qualifying exam. For undergraduate programs such as BTech, admission is granted through the JEE Advanced score, after qualifying JEE Main. For postgraduate programs like MTech, candidates are admitted based on their GATE score, while MSc admissions are done through the IIT JAM score.

IIT Roorkee also offers MBA programmes, where admission is based on CAT/ MAT/ CMAT scores followed by group discussions and personal interviews. For PhD programs, candidates are selected based on GATE/ NET scores and an interview process conducted by the institute. Some Frequently Asked Questions about IIT Roorkee Admission:

1. How is IIT Roorkee for MBA?
IIT Roorkee offers MBA through its Department of Management Studies (DoMS), known for strong academic curriculum, industry exposure, and good placements in consulting, IT, and analytics.

2. How is IIT Roorkee paper?
IIT Roorkee conducts exams like JEE Advanced, GATE, CAT, and its own semester exams; the papers are designed to be concept-oriented, testing analytical and problem-solving skills.

3. IIT Roorkee without GATE?
For M.Tech, GATE is mandatory; however, IIT Roorkee also admits sponsored candidates, foreign nationals, and through self-financed categories without GATE.

4. IIT Roorkee without JEE?
B.Tech admissions require JEE Advanced, but you can join IIT Roorkee without JEE through M.Tech (GATE), MBA (CAT), MSc (IIT JAM), and PhD programs.

5. IIT Roorkee MBA without CAT?
CAT is the primary exam for MBA admissions; only international students can apply without CAT using GMAT scores.
