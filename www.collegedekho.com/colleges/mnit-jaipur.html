About MNIT Jaipur

Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur, also known as MNIT Jaipur, is a public institute that was established in the year 1963. The institute is jointly managed by the Government of India and the Government of Rajasthan. It is approved by AICTE and has been accredited by the NBA. As per NIRF 2025, MNIT Jaipur has been ranked 42nd in the Engineering category and 62nd in the Management category. Apart from this, the institute has also signed MoUs with various institutes such as Stevens Institute of Technology, North Dakota University, University of Greenwich London, University of Saskatchewan, Deakin University, etc.

MNIT Jaipur courses are offered at the UG and PG levels. There are a total of 7 programmes offered in various streams such as engineering, management, and science. Some of the popular MNIT programmes include BTech and BArch at the UG level and MBA, MSc, MTech, etc. at the PG level. The average MNIT Jaipur fees range from Rs 15,000 to Rs 5,00,000 depending on the courses. Admission to these courses is based on entrance exams as well as merit. The institute accepts JEE Main for BTech/ BArch admission, GATE for MTech admission, IIT JAM for MSc, and XAT/ MAT for MBA admission.

MNIT Jaipur placements are managed by its placement cell every year. According to MNIT Jaipur placements 2024, the highest and average packages offered were Rs 64 LPA and Rs 11.58 LPA, respectively.  Some of the top recruiters include D E Shaw, Adobe, JPMC, Arcesium, Texas, Salesforce, Atlassian, Apple, etc.

FAQs About MNIT Jaipur

Q1: What is MNIT Jaipur and what is its institute status?
MNIT Jaipur is a public technical institute established in 1963 as Malaviya Regional Engineering College and upgraded to NIT status on 26 June 2002. It was declared an “Institute of National Importance” under the NIT Act on 15 August 2007.

Q2: Where is MNIT Jaipur’s campus and what is its size?
MNIT Jaipur is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan, on a lush campus of about 317 acres. It is situated at Jawah Nehru Marg, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur, accessible from the city and close to airport & business district.

Q3: What is the mission and vision of the institute?
MNIT Jaipur’s vision is to create a centre for imparting technical education of international standards and conduct research at the cutting edge of technology. Its mission emphasises quality engineering education, research and innovation, entrepreneurship and moulding students with integrity and ethics.

Q4: What is the ranking or reputation of MNIT Jaipur?
According to its official website, MNIT Jaipur is ranked 42nd nationally in NIRF 2025 (Engineering) category. It is part of India’s network of NITs and has the status of Institute of National Importance, carrying significant reputation.

Q5: What kinds of departments and academic scope does MNIT Jaipur cover?
MNIT Jaipur offers programmes across engineering, sciences, architecture, management, environment & energy, design/humanities. Over the years it has grown from two engineering branches to 14+ departments plus a School of Management and various research centres.

Q6: How is the campus infrastructure and student life at MNIT Jaipur?
MNIT Jaipur campus is green, with departmental buildings, hostels, lecture theatres, library with digital services, sports facilities. There are about 12 hostels (10 for boys, 2 for girls), WiFi/LAN in rooms, and vibrant student culture with tech and cultural festivals.

MNIT Jaipur Admission 2026

MNIT Jaipur admission is done either on the basis of entrance exams or merit in the qualifying exam. The institute accepts JEE Main paper 1 for BTech and JEE Main paper 2 for BArch admission. Other entrance exams accepted for MNIT Jaipur admission include GATE for MTech, XAT/ MAT for MBA, IIT JAM for MSc, etc. For courses like MPlan, admission is based on merit in the qualifying exam. MNIT Jaipur Admission 2026

MNIT Jaipur admissions are based on national-level entrance examinations. Students seeking admission at MNIT Jaipur must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the institute and appear for the entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for MNIT Jaipur admissions:

BTech: Candidates seeking admission to BTech must appear for the JEE Main and participate in the JoSAA Counselling for the seat allotment process.

BArch: Candidates seeking admission to BArch must appear for the JEE Main Paper 2 and participate in the JoSAA Counselling for the seat allotment process.

MTech: Candidates seeking admission to MTech must appear for the GATE and participate in the CCMT Counselling for the seat allotment process.

MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the CAT/XAT/CMAT/MAT/GMAT and participate in the Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) rounds conducted by MNIT Jaipur.

FAQs About MNIT Jaipur Courses and Admission

Q1: What undergraduate courses (UG) are offered at MNIT Jaipur?
MNIT Jaipur offers B.Tech courses in disciplines such as Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Metallurgical & Materials Engineering. Additionally, a 5-year B.Arch programme is offered through the Architecture & Planning department.

Q2: What postgraduate (PG) and doctoral courses does MNIT Jaipur provide?
The institute offers M.Tech (various specialisations), M.Plan (Urban Planning), M.Sc in Applied Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) and MBA programmes. For doctoral studies (Ph.D.), the institute admits eligible candidates in engineering, science and allied areas.

Q3: What is the eligibility criteria for courses at MNIT Jaipur?
For UG B.Tech programmes: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics is required, along with performance in the entrance exam (e.g., JEE Main) for allotment. For PG programmes (M.Tech/M.Plan): typically a Bachelor’s degree (B.E/B.Tech) in relevant area with minimum aggregate marks (e.g., 60% for general category) and a valid exam like GATE for admission.

Q4: What is the admission process for undergraduate B.Tech/B.Arch at MNIT Jaipur?
Admission to UG B.Tech/B.Arch programmes at MNIT Jaipur is via the national entrance exam JEE Main followed by counselling through Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) and CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board). Candidates must register, participate in counselling, select preferences, and meet eligibility criteria.

Q5: What is the admission process for postgraduate M.Tech/M.Plan/M.Sc at MNIT Jaipur?
For M.Tech/M.Plan programmes, admission is through the centralised counselling process of Centralised Counselling for M.Tech./M.Plan. (CCMT) for full-time seats. For M.Sc programmes, admissions are via the national exam IIT JAM followed by counselling via CCMN (Centralised Common Counselling for M.Sc).

FAQs About MNIT Jaipur Placements

Q1: What is the placement performance and average salary package at MNIT Jaipur?
According to Careers360, MNIT Jaipur’s median salary for B.Tech in 2024 was about Rs 11 LPA. Other sources show average placement package around Rs 9.10 LPA, median around Rs 8.50 LPA in a recent batch.

Q2: What is the highest salary package recorded at MNIT Jaipur?
For UG B.Tech courses in recent years, the highest package has gone up to around Rs 64 LPA.

Q3: How many companies and what kind of recruiters come to MNIT Jaipur?
The placement statistics indicate more than 250 companies visited the campus in recent years. Top recruiters include multinational companies and major Indian firms across IT, core engineering, finance, consulting sectors.

Q4: What is the placement rate (percentage of students placed) at MNIT Jaipur?
For the UG B.Tech programme, the overall placement rate was about 81.51% in a recent batch.Different branches have different placement rates; e.g., Electrical Engineering branch had ~90.29% in a past year.

Q5: Does MNIT Jaipur offer internships or training as part of placements?
Yes. The institute has a centralised Training & Placement (T&P) cell that manages internships and placement drives. Additionally, students are encouraged to do summer internships, live projects and industry liaison which bolster placement readiness.

Q6: Are placement outcomes different by branch at MNIT Jaipur?
Yes. Some branches like Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) tend to command higher average packages (~Rs 19.33 LPA in 2024 for CSE) according to data. Core branches like Civil, Metallurgical may have lower average/median packages comparatively. Branch-wise statistics vary.

FAQs About MNIT Jaipur Cutoff

Q1: What is the cutoff rank or closing rank for B.Tech admission at MNIT Jaipur via JEE Main?
For the General AIQ category in 2025, the closing rank for B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering was 5,995 in the last round. For other core branches like Civil, Mechanical etc., the maximum closing rank was around 37,637 in last round for General category.

Q2: How does the home-state quota affect the cutoff at MNIT Jaipur?
Under Home State (HS) quota for Rajasthan, the cutoff closing rank was 6,638 for CSE in 2025 for General category, whereas the All India quota (AIQ) was 5,995. Thus, the HS quota has slightly higher (i.e., numerically lower rank requirement) competitiveness in many cases.

Q3: What is the cutoff for postgraduate programmes (M.Tech/M.Plan) at MNIT Jaipur?
For M.Tech via GATE/CCMT 2025, the cutoff for General category had GATE score around 681 for some programmes. Similarly, for M.Sc via CCMN/JAM, closing ranks for Applied Mathematics in General AI quota was 954 in 2025.

Q4: What trends are observed in cutoff ranks at MNIT Jaipur?
The cutoff for popular branches like CSE remains most competitive and tends to require ranks <10,000 in AIQ. For other branches the closing rank can stretch to 30,000 + depending on category/quota. Additionally, cutoffs vary each year depending on seat availability, number of applicants, difficulty of JEE Main etc.

Q5: Where can I check the detailed branch-wise and category-wise cutoff for MNIT Jaipur?
Detailed branch-wise, category-wise opening and closing ranks are published on portals like Shiksha, Careers360, IQuanta and the institute website. For example, Shiksha lists that closing ranks in 2025 ranged from 4,748 (Round 1 for CSE) to 38,727 (various branches) for General category

Q6: Does MNIT Jaipur have a minimum percentile cutoff or only rank-based?
Admission cutoffs are expressed in terms of JEE Main All India Rank (AIR) or closing ranks; percentiles may be used indirectly (via JEE Main percentile criteria) but the institute commonly cites rank openings & closings.

FAQs About MNIT Jaipur Scholarships

Q1: What scholarship schemes are available at MNIT Jaipur for undergraduate students?
MNIT Jaipur offers merit-and-means based scholarships to UG students. For example, the Swami Dayanand Charitable Educational Foundation scholarship is for students from families earning < Rs 6 lakh p.a. who secured minimum 65% in 12th and renewal requires CGPA > 6.5. Amounts range Rs 15,000 to Rs 50,000 per annum.

Q2: What are the eligibility criteria for scholarships at MNIT Jaipur?
Eligibility typically depends on family income limits, academic performance (e.g., 60–65% in qualifying exam or CGPA minimum), and category (SC/ST/OBC/General). For example, SC/ST/PC students with income less than Rs 1.5 lakh p.a. are eligible for certain schemes.

Q3: Are there scholarships for postgraduate students at MNIT Jaipur?
Yes. For full-time M.Tech/M.Plan students admitted via GATE, there are institute assistantships (stipend) and other merit/need based scholarships.

Q4: Does MNIT Jaipur support economically weaker students with tuition fee waivers or concessions?
Yes. For example, for B.Tech programmes, students from families with annual income below Rs 1 lakh may be exempt from tuition fees; those with income between Rs 1-5 lakh may pay reduced tuition fee (e.g., Rs 20,834 per semester) in recent data.

Q5: How can a student apply for scholarships at MNIT Jaipur and when?
The institute invites applications (typically July/August) through the Dean (Student Welfare) office; the standing committee reviews and recommends awards. Scholarships are awarded after approvals, and students must fulfil performance & conduct criteria.

Q6: Are there external scholarships (government/private) that MNIT Jaipur students can avail?
Yes. Students at MNIT Jaipur can avail various central sector and private scholarships (e.g., ONGC Scholarship, EIL Scholarship, Shraman Foundation Scholarship) based on category/income/branch criteria.
