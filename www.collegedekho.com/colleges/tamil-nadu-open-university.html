About Tamil Nadu Open University

Tamil Nadu Open University was established in the year 2002 by the State Government of Tamil Nadu to promote ‘Open Learning and Distance Education Systems’ in the education system. The sole objective of the TNOU is to strengthen and introduce degree, diploma, and certificate courses that can fulfil candidates' employment needs and, at the same time, contribute to the economy of the state and country. To enhance employability opportunities by upgrading knowledge, training, and skills in the context of innovations, research, and discovery in all fields of human endeavours. The overall mission is to transform an individual so that they will be able to attain the heights of success and satisfaction. 

Tamil Nadu Open University courses are accessible to everyone who wants to pursue higher education. Courses at TNOU Chennai are classified as UG, PG, diploma, and certificate courses. Interested candidates can seek TNOU admission annually. Also, the Tamil Nadu Open University scholarships offer a couple of scholarships for students from various categories. 

The TNOU has grown widely in the past 16 years. It currently offers 218 programmes across various disciplines and skills through short-term certificate courses up to the postgraduate level. It runs 13 academic schools, 5 support divisions, 8 regional centres, and 2 constituent communities, and a chain of networks with 536 centres, including 68 learner support centres.

The Tamil Nadu Open University is recognised by the Union Grants Commission, Distance Education Bureau, National Council for Teacher Education and Rehabilitation Council of India. The university has signed MoUs with various institutes at the national and international levels. It is in collaboration with the Commonwealth of Learning (COL), the Asian Association of Open Universities (AAOU), and the International Council for Open and Distance Education (ICDE). It has signed an MoU with the Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, which resulted in offering 16 agriculture-related programmes at a distance through the Krishi Vigyan Kendra of the TNAU, to target 4000 unemployed youth in 2016-2017.

It is the only institute in the state that has signed an MoU with the National Skill Development Council (NSDC) and has made its vocational programmes in collaboration with the National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF). The TNOU placement cell also works throughout the year to provide better employment facilities to the final year degree candidates. The alumni of TNOU are placed among the top market players in the country and are growing.

Tamil Nadu Open University Courses and Admission 2026

The admission procedure of Tamil Nadu Open University is set simple to avail the benefit of this course program to all the deserving candidates. It admits the students in two cycles. One is similar to other universities, the students are admitted in the Academic year (1st July-30 June), and the second cycle begins from 1st January to 31st December. With the growing advancement in technology & taking the benefit of this development, the TNOU admission process is fully held online.  

The students interested in TNOU admission can complete the procedure from anywhere within the given period just with the help internet and supporting devices. Students in need of any clarity can approach the Regional Centres. The institute has set up twelve Regional Centres throughout Tamil Nadu to assist the admission to Tamil Nadu Open University. The Tamil Nadu Open University, Chennai, admission invites application into various UG Programmes, PG, Diploma, Advanced vocational Diploma programs, Vocational Diploma, Certificate, and Short-term programs. 

Students can visit the admission portal of TNOU and are advised to check the prospectus and other eligibility details such as minimum age, educational qualification, and other experience thoroughly before applying. The mode of fee payment for all the programs is online through various payment methods. For the admission fee, Registration fee, Examination fee, provisional certificate, and convocation fee, a demand draft will be accepted in the name of Tamil Nadu Open University drawn from any nationalized bank.

After applying, the admission section will analyze it in detail. Those who get selected will receive the ID card at their given address. An enrolment number will be allotted, which is will used for further purposes. The academic TNOU Counseling classes will be conducted in Learner Support Centre, and students can collect more details by visiting the college website or contacting the concerned authority.

The admission section will allot the centre available at nearby locations if the applicant has not opted for a learner support centre while applying. The e-prospectus for admission at Tamil Nadu Open University is available on the college’s official website under the tap Admission. The admission starts from:

Tamil Nadu Open University FAQs

What is the TNOU fees for BA in English?

TNOU BA in English fees stands at Rs 3,500, Rs 3,300, and Rs 3,300 for the first, second, and third year respectively. The duration of the course is three years.

What is the address of Tamil Nadu Open University?

TNOU address is No 577, Anna Salai, Saidapet, Chennai - 600 015. Candidates can also contact on 044-24306600

What is the eligibility criteria for TNOU UG courses?

Candidates must be Class 12th qualified or complete 12th from National Institute of Open Schooling or 3 years diploma from DOTE.

What are the amenities offered by Tamil Nadu Open University?

TNOU campus offers its students all basic and modern facilities, such as a library, health centre, mother care room, auditorium, and a help desk.

What is the TNOU admission procedure 2023?

Visit the TNOU website (www.tnou.ac.in) and click the menu ‘online admission’ at the top. On clicking the above menu, you must register yourself and fill out the application details. On clicking the ‘New Registration button, the login screen will appear. You can log in and see the application status later.

How can I apply at TNOU?

Candidates seeking admission at Tamil Nadu Open University may visit the university's official website and fill out the application form 2023. Candidate must keep all the necessary documents that will be required to upload, such as JPEG file of Class 12 and graduation mark sheet, scanned copy of passport size photograph, etc.

What are the courses offered at TNOU?

TNOU course list includes UG, PG, PG diploma, diploma, advanced vocational diploma programmes, vocational diploma programmes, certificate programme, and short-term programmes.

Is Tamil Nadu open university a good university?

TNOU is considered a top State University in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. The university is recognised by Union Grants Commission- Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB), National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) and All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). TNOU has been accorded the 12B status by the UGC.

When was the college , TNOU , Chennai established ?

TNOU , Chennai was established in the year 2002.

Is TNOU , Chennai a government college?

Tamil Nadu Open University Scholarships

Scholarships greatly relieve students who discontinue their education only due to financial crisis. The Tamil Nadu Open University Scholarship programme mainly includes Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department for SC/ST students and Higher Education Department for differently-abled students. The SC/ST candidate who are unemployed and if their parent's annual income is within Rs 100000 are eligible for reimbursement of non-refundable fees.

Students should apply for the scholarship with the prescribed application form with the documents such as savings bank account number, Name of Bank, Branch, etc.

Another TNOU Scholarship is for differently-abled students. They are eligible for exemption in Tuition fees. To get more information on Tamil Nadu Open University Scholarship 2023, students may contact the university authorities.
