About C. U. Shah College of Engineering & Technology

C. U. Shah College of Engineering & Technology is situated in Gandhinagar in Gujarat state of India. The college is accredited by AICTE. C. U. Shah College of Engineering & Technology offers 15 courses across 2 streams namely Engineering, Management..Popular degrees offered at C. U. Shah College of Engineering & Technology include B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, MAM..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, C. U. Shah College of Engineering & Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at C. U. Shah College of Engineering & Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at C. U. Shah College of Engineering & Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
