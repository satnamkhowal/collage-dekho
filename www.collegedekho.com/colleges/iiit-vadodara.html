About Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Vadodara

Indian Institute of Information Technology, Vadodara, also known as IIITV, is a government institute established in the year 2013. Located in Vadodara, Gujarat, the institute is an Institute of National Importance. The institute is approved by the UGC and AICTE. IIIT Vadodara offers a total of 3 courses in engineering and technology streams. Some of the popular IIIT Vadodara programmes include B.Tech. etc. For admission to its courses, IIIT Vadodara accepts JEE Main score. IIIT Vadodara fee for its courses ranges from Rs 8 Lakhs (minimum fee) to Rs 10 Lakhs (maximum fee) depending on the type of course.

IIIT Vadodara offers a total of 3 courses at the UG levels. The institute offers B.Tech courses at the undergraduate level. The college provides admission on the basis of entrance exam. The national level entrance exams required for admission to the IIIT Vadodara include JEE Main for B.Tech and GATE for MTech followed by JoSAA and CCMT counselling. MTech admissions are closed for this year, BTech admissions are still ongoing, interested candidates can check the official site of JoSAA for the same. 

The college has a placement cell for the training and placement of students. As per previous years IIIT Vadodara placement statistics, the highest package offered was 40 LPA and the average package offered was 12 LPA. Its campus has modern facilities and infrastructure including library, hostels, sports facilities, computer lab etc. for the holistic development of the students.
