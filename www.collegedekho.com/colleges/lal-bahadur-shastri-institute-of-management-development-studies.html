About Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute of Management and Development Studies

Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute of Management and Development Studies, also known as LBSIMDS Lucknow, established in 1982 in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. The institute is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), affiliated with Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), and recognized by several other regulatory bodies. It is a private institute that offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in management and computer applications. The institute offers MBA, BBA, BCA, MCA and several other programme specializations such as Business Administration, Computer Applications and other related fields. The total fee is Rs 1,60,900 for MBA, Rs 1,62,450 for BBA, Rs 1,62,450 for BCA and Rs 1,60,900 for MCA.

For Lal Bahadur Shastri Group of Institutions BBA admission, candidates must pass Class 12th with minimum 50% aggregate marks in any discipline from a recognized board or qualify through CUET-UG examination, while admission to the MBA programme requires a valid score in CAT/MAT/CUET-PG along with a Bachelor’s degree with minimum 50% aggregate marks. The admission process is conducted online through the institute portal which includes national-level exams such as CAT/MAT/CUET or merit-based shortlisting, followed by counselling, written test and interview (if required), document verification, and fee payment. The Lal Bahadur Shastri Group of Institutions placements in 2024 show the highest package offered was Rs 11 LPA, while the average package was Rs 5.25 LPA.

Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute of Management and Development Studies Admission Process 2026

Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute of Management and Development Studies admission process 2026 involves multiple steps, including registration, application, and selection rounds. Check the steps below to understand the complete process.

Step 1: Check Eligibility

  • UG: Candidates must have passed 10+2 with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in any discipline from a recognized board.
  • PG: Candidates must have passed their Bachelor’s degree with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in any discipline from a recognized university or institution.

Step 2: Online Registration
Candidates must visit the official website and register using a valid email ID and mobile number.

Step 3: Fill the Application Form
Complete the online application form with the required personal and academic details. Upload scanned copies of the photograph, signature, and relevant marksheets.

Step 4: Shortlisting

  • UG: Candidates are shortlisted for admission based on their performance in CUET-UG or through merit in the qualifying examination.
  • PG: Candidates are shortlisted based on national-level entrance examinations such as CAT / MAT / CUET-PG or through merit in the qualifying examination.

Step 5: Selection Process
Shortlisted candidates are required to participate in the counselling process, followed by a written test and interview (if required) conducted by Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute of Management and Development Studies.

Step 6: Document Verification
Candidates must submit and verify original documents, including Class 10th, Class 12th or Graduation marksheets, ID proof, and category certificate (if applicable).

Step 7: Fee Payment
After document verification, shortlisted candidates are required to pay the prescribed admission fee within the given deadline to confirm their seat.
