About Grant Medical College

Grant Medical College , also known as Grant is a Medical College, established in 1845. Located in Mumbai. The college is affiliated to Maharashtra University of Health Sciences and recognized by the NMC.

Grant Medical College offers a wide range of various programs in the streams of Public. MBBS, MD, MS are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Grant Medical College are based on NEET Scores. The college accepts NEET UG for MBBS, NEET PG for MD/MS, NEET SS for DM/M.Ch.

How do I take admission in Grant govt medical college for paramedical course in OTT

S
Samiksha Rautela •  Jun-20-2025

Dear Student, 

To get admission in the Grant Medical College Paramedical course in OTT, students must pass 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English. The general category students must secure 50% marks, while the reserved category students must secure 40% marks for admissions. The Grant Medical College Paramedical course in OTT, selections will be based on merit based on students' 10+2 scores, and no entrance exam will be required for admissions.
