About IIM Bangalore

Indian Institute of Management Bangalore, or IIM Bangalore, was established in 1973 by the government of India. IIM Bangalore is located on Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru, India. The college is accredited by the European Quality Improvement System (EQUIS). NIRF Ranking 2025 positioned IIM Bangalore at the 2nd position in the Management category with a composite score of 81.56. 

IIM Bangalore courses are offered at undergraduate, postgraduate, certificate, and doctoral levels. The IIM Bangalore course list includes MBA, PGPBA, PGPEM, EPGP, PhD, BBA and certificate programmes. For IIM Bangalore admissions to MBA and PGPBA, CAT scores are considered. As per the IIM Bangalore admission process for PGPEM, candidates must have valid scores in the CAT/GMAT/GRE/IIMB Test, along with a minimum of 6 years of experience. For IIM Bangalore EPGP admissions, valid scores in the GMAT or GRE are mandatory. Candidates who wish to pursue PhD, have to appear GMAT/GRE/CAT GATE/JRF (NET)/IIMB Test to get admission at IIM Bangalore.

IIM Bangalore's placement process starts from the final year of the degree so the students get familiar with the process by the end of the year. IIM Bangalore has achieved 100% placements for its 2025 batch, with an average package of Rs 35.92 LPA and a strong presence of top recruiters across various sectors. Some top recruiters participating in the IIM Bangalore placement drive every year are Accenture, Flipkart, Microsoft, Infosys, Myntra, Axis Bank, Syntel, Bajaj Auto, etc.

IIM Bangalore Courses and Admission 2026

IIM Bangalore offers seven programmes for 2026, enrolling 1,200 students from a three-year online BBA to a five-year PhD. The flagship MBA (formerly PGP) features global immersion, while the Executive MBA (EPGP) targets professionals with over 100 electives. The MBA in Business Analytics (PGPBA), in collaboration with IIM Calcutta, focuses on AI-driven analytics for 50 students.

  • PGP MBA: Two years, CAT-based, 600 seats, includes INR 2 lakhs summer internship stipends.
  • EPGP: One-year residential, GMAT entry, 140 seats, ranked 7th globally for immersion.
  • BBA DBE: Three years online, CUET 70th percentile, 500 seats, partners with Flipkart. Below, the details of the IIM Bangalore fees for all the courses along with the eligibility criteria are tabulated.

  • Candidates have to first submit the online application form of entrance exam. 
  • Candidates have to download the admit card of the respective entrance exam from the official website. 
  • After appearing for the exam, candidates have to meet the IIM Bangalore cutoff.
  • Qualified candidates will be eligible for GD-PI and WAT conducted by IIM Bangalore.
  • Final selection of candidates will be made on the basis of overall performance in both the the qualifying examination and the entrance test.

IIM Bangalore Academic and Industry Collaborations

IIMB-SMU MoU

IIMB signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) in management research and education with Singapore Management University (SMU). The MoU will facilitate joint research and publication by faculty of SMU and IIMB, the organisation of research seminars and workshops, and support for research projects in research centres at both institutions in areas of mutual interest.

  • SMU and IIMB also intend to invite doctoral students and junior faculty to participate in Summer Institutes / Winter Institutes to be hosted or co-hosted by the institutions.
  • The MOU also provides for the exploration of joint executive education, faculty exchange, and graduate student mobility between the two institutions.

IIMB-University of Exeter MoU

  • University of Exeter Business School formalised an MoU with IIM Bangalore and reviewed the progress of ongoing research and education exchange in areas of mutual interest, which include corporate governance and corporate social responsibility (CSR), service management, entrepreneurship and innovation, sustainability, economics, and health care.
  • Both will work together in the areas of collaborative research, bilateral faculty and student mobility, mutual exchange of information and experiential learning, participation in publications, seminars, conferences, lectures, and related activities.

Harvard Business Publishing and Richard Ivey School of Business

  • IIM Bangalore is the first school in India to enter into an agreement with Harvard Business Publishing for the distribution of teaching cases globally. With this, IIMB joins a select group of B-schools such as Stanford Graduate School of Business, Kellogg Graduate School of Management, and the Darden School of Business.
  • IIMB has partnered with the Richard Ivey School of Business and the University of Western Ontario to co-brand and distribute IIMB-authored cases on a global basis.

IIMB-IISC MoU

IISC Bangalore and IIM Bangalore have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to formalise academic exchange and collaboration between the two institutes.

  • The MOU focuses on two goals: to have closer academic collaborations between the two institutes and to initiate Technology and Innovation Management activities related to science-based intellectual property generated at IISC Bangalore and co-created between the two institutes.
