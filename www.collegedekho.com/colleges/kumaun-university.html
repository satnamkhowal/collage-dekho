About Kumaun University

Kumaun University Nainital, established in 1973 through the merger of DSB Government PG College and Almora Government PG College, stands as Uttarakhand's premier residential-cum-affiliating institution under UGC Section 12-B, enrolling 1,50,000 students yearly across three campuses and 82 affiliates while delivering 116 specialized programs in eight faculties like forestry and biotechnology—its NAAC A accreditation and QS India ranking of 81-85 make it a top pick for 2026 admits chasing merit-based entry with cutoffs as low as 72% for BA Economics. This overview draws from the 2025-26 session data to detail exact rankings, infrastructure perks like the 160-acre Bhimtal campus with Himalayan Museum access, and scholarship paths covering full waivers for SC/ST families under ₹2 Lakhs income, empowering you to weigh options against peers like HNB Garhwal University before applying via kuntl.net in January.
