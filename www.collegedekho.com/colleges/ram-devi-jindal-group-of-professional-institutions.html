About Ram Devi Jindal Group Of Professional Institutions

Ram Devi Jindal Group Of Professional Institutions is situated in Mohali in Punjab state of India. Established in 2009, RDJGPI is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. Ram Devi Jindal Group Of Professional Institutions offers 10 courses across 2 streams namely Engineering, Management..Popular degrees offered at Ram Devi Jindal Group Of Professional Institutions include B.Tech, BBA, MBA, Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Ram Devi Jindal Group Of Professional Institutions is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Ram Devi Jindal Group Of Professional Institutions, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Ram Devi Jindal Group Of Professional Institutions is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
