About Sanskriti University

Sanskriti University was established in 2016 as a private university in Mathura. It is recognised by UGC and is also a member of AIU. The university has received approval from BCI, RCI, PCI and NCTE. The Sanskriti University courses are offered through 16 separate schools of specialisation. The programmes offered are from the fields of management, commerce, engineering, education, science and more. Students can check the Sanskriti University course and fees details from this page.  

The overall Sanskriti University placement percentage is 89 percent including 100 percent placement for diploma, BBA, MBA, and M.Tech courses. Further, the Sanskriti University ranking in India by NIRF ranking 2023 was among the top 101-150 institutes in the innovation area. MBA admission to Sanskriti University is done through the CAT exam. The other entrance exams accepted by Sanskriti University are CUET/ JEE Main/ NEET UG/ XAT and NMAT. The university uses a problem-based learning method and case study method to train their students. 

Sanskriti University has collaborated with international universities like the University of Cambridge and Help University for Faculty Exchange, Lecture Exchange, and Student Exchange programmes. The university also offers campus placements for students and in 2023 the highest package offered was Rs 54 LPA. In addition to placements, the university helps improve the employability rate of students provide international certifications and internship opportunities.

Sanskriti University Cut Off

Sanskriti University cutoff is not released for any courses as admissions are done based on merit with the candidate's performance on the entrance exam and previous academic achievements being taken into account. The university holds all the rights to accept or reject a candidate based on their academics and scores. While calling candidates for counselling on the Sanskriti University campus, the university evaluates their performance and creates a merit list. As the Sanskriti University cut off is not made available, students are then informed about their selection and then they have to complete the admission formalities respectively.

Sanskriti University FAQs

When was Sanskriti University established?

Sanskriti University was established in the year 2016 to offer high-quality education in the state of Uttar Pradesh. Sanskriti University is a 'Centre of Excellence' that was developed with the intention of instilling moral, cultural, and ethical qualities in students. The university is a leading educational institution that is situated in the holy land of Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, and has established itself over time as an acclaimed educational provider with a curriculum that emphasises on holistic learning and increasing skills in students.

Is Sanskriti University a private or government university?

Sanskriti University is one of the top private universities in Uttar Pradesh offering good education in several disciplines. The Sanskriti University faculties are extremely supportive and offer academic as well as life consultation to students. The teachers are well qualified and hold PhD degrees in their respective fields. Students at Sanskriti University are offered all the necessary amenities and the campus is located in a good and peaceful location. The university not only focuses on academics but also focus on physical activities through sports events from time to time.

How good is Sanskriti University?

Sanskriti University is a good university considering the infrastructure, placement and academics. Sanskriti University is located on NH-2 Chhata in Mathura. The university's cutting-edge infrastructure and architectural quality help it make a powerful first impression. The university was founded with the goal of imparting "Excellence in Life" to its students. Among the several universities in Uttar Pradesh, Sanskriti University has created a name for itself. transportation facilities, classroom facilities, library, medical and sports room, hostels, conference rooms and other important facilities to make student's stay comfortable.

What courses does Sanskriti University offer?

Sanskriti University courses include several programmes in diploma, UG, PG and Ph.D. management, engineering, commerce, education, science, para-medical, indian medicine, agriculture, and arts are among the many programmes available to students. Aside from classroom instruction, students gain additional knowledge through dedicated and well-equipped laboratories, industry tours, and co-curricular activities, among other things. The emphasis is on kindling young minds to develop and focus on research-based projects. Students can also use the library, which offers a large range of textbooks, journals, and reference materials.

Is there any entrance exam for Sanskriti University admission?

Sanskriti University Mathura, offers diploma, UG, PG, integrated courses, and doctoral programmes in a variety of fields. Students willing to take admission to Sanskriti University have to qualify the entrance examinations. For Sanskriti University MBA admissions, CAT/XAT/NMAT/MAT scores are considered for admission. On the other hand, CUET UG scores are also considered for admission to several UG programmes. For BAMS admission in Sanskriti University, NEET UG scores are taken into consideration. Moreover, Admission to the B. Tech programme is based on the valid marks obtained in the JEE Mains entrance examination.

Does Sanskriti University offer scholarships?

Yes, Sanskriti University scholarships are awarded to deserving students based on their performance in qualifying/competitive examinations. There are more than 10 types of scholarships which are offered on a merit basis including CUET scholarships, SUNAT scholarships, MBA programme scholarships, defence quota scholarships, girls quota scholarships and more. Only one scholarship scheme will be available for admission at any given time. To continue receiving the scholarship and its percentage, the student must maintain a minimum grade as defined by the Sanskriti University each year, with no back papers. The academic performance of a candidate shall be the sole criterion for scholarship continuance. Every academic year, the scholarship qualifications may vary.

What is SUNAT? How to apply for it?

Sanskriti University National Aptitude Test which is also known as SUNAT is a scholarship aptitude test conducted by Sanskriti University Mathura. It is a merit-based scholarship entrance test to offer financial assistance to students with good scores and economically weak backgrounds. Students with 90 percent or above marks in SUNAT are eligible for up to 100 percent scholarship and fee waiver in the first year tuition fees. The SUNAT registration process is online and the examination is conducted in the month of May every year. Further, students having marks between 85 to 89 percent in SUNAT are eligible to get a scholarship up to 30 percent of the annual tuition fees.

How many constituent schools are there at Sanskriti University?

There are 18 constituent schools at Sanskriti University. Each school is responsible for handling their domain-wise subjects and information related to them. The constituent schools are in the following fields: Engineering and IT Agriculture Tourism and Hospitality Rehabilitation Medical and Allied Sciences Basic and Applied Sciences Yoga and Naturopathy Law and Legal Studies  University Polytechnic Nursing  Management and Commerce Education Fashion Designing Ayurvedic Medical College and Hospitals Pharmacy and Research Centre  Nursing and more.

What is the rank of Sanskriti University in India?

Sanskriti University ranking has been released by several ranking bodies for the academic session 2023-24. To check the ranking of Sanskriti University, students can check the below pointers:  Sanskriti University's School of Engineering has been ranked among the top 5 engineering colleges in UP by Outlook Magazine 2023.  The School of Tourism and Hospitality has been ranked third in UP and 20th in India by Outlook Magazine in 2023.  School of Management & Commerce has been ranked 1st In U.P. & 4th In India by India Today Magazine 2023.  Intellectual Property India has ranked Sanskriti University as the top university with the highest number of patents submitted.  Sanskriti University has been ranked 8th In Multidisciplinary Emerging Universities By The Week.

Does Sanskriti University have sports facilities?

Yes, Sanskriti University gives equal importance to students’s academics as well as physical health. The university feels that ensuring student engagement in various sports is vitally necessary for 360-degree personality development. The university is fully aware of the value of physical activity and sports as leisure for students. Sports facilities on campus include badminton, volleyball, football, basketball and cricket, among others. Students are offered the opportunity to play both outdoor and indoor games after class. Every weekend, inter-college tournaments on different games are held according to the calendar of events. The annual sports meet is held where students are appreciated for their sports achievements.

Sanskriti University Scholarships

Sanskriti University scholarships are awarded on the basis of their performance on the qualifying examinations; the university awards scholarships to deserving students at the time of admission. To continue receiving the scholarship and its percentage, the student must maintain the minimum standard of performance set by the university each year without any back papers. Candidates seeking a 100% Sanskriti University scholarship can achieve 95 percentile or more in the CUET UG exam. The following Sanskiriti University scholarships are offered to students: 

  • Sanskriti University B.Tech Scholarships - Students can get 25% to 90% scholarship depending on the score obtained in the qualifying exam.
  • Sanskriti University M.Tech Scholarships - Get 10% scholarships on first-year tuition fees if you are GATE qualified. 
  • Sanskriti University MBA Scholarships - Students can claim between 20% to 95% scholarship depending on their score in CAT and MAT exams.
  • Sanskriti University CUET Scholarships - Students with 50 to 95 percentile CUET scores can get between 20% to 100% tuition fee waiver for the first year.
  • Sanskriti University Aptitude Test Scholarships - The top three students in the Aptitude Test get 15% to 25% scholarship in the first year fees.
  • Sanskriti University Defence Quota Scholarships - Students whose parents are in the defence forces of India can get 20% scholarships in the first year fees.
