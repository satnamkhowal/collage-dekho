LPU Campus

LPU campus is regarded as one of the best campuses among private universities in India. Similarly, LPU’s infrastructure is one of the biggest and best in the world. The campus of LPU is surrounded by trees and gardens that make teaching and learning environment more comfortable. The pollution-free and healthy environment at LPU campus is other major highlights of the campus..

The LPU central library is one of the major attractions of LPU. The library has lakhs of books in various subjects, and it helps students to clarify their subject related doubts. The library also has several magazines, journals, newspapers etc so that students spend their recreation time by updating themselves with the latest happenings.

LPU has labs for all the required courses that impart practical knowledge to students. The Chemistry Laboratory and MAC Lab are the major highlights across all labs prevalent on LPU campus. Aviation Lab is the supreme lab on LPU campus that offers outstanding practical experience to the students of Airlines course.

LPU has a separate division or building for research activities. Scholars, faculty, and scientists involved in constant research activities in different areas of specialisation. The major aim of the research centre is to make valuable contributions to the country.

As far as sports facilities are concerned, the campus is bestowed by all major sports facilities. There is a separate football field, cricket ground, boxing ring, and taekwondo for interested students. Apart from these, there are separate areas for all major sports, athletics etc. The B.P.Ed/ M.P.Ed and other physical education students of LPU host several sports events/ competitions on the campus.

As LPU has students from various states and countries, cultural events play a vital role. LPU has one of the largest indoor auditoriums in the country – Shanti Devi Mittal Auditorium for various official, academic, and cultural activities. The Open Air Theatre at LPU is one of the favourite spots for students to hang out during non-academic hours. Baldev Raj Mittal auditorium is the third spot at LPU for hosting formal and informal events.

Recreation facilities at LPU are top notch. The campus has a Roller Skating Rink that is often busy during exam breaks. The Snooker Longue, In-House Bowing Complex and  Fitness Edge offer required entertainment to students so that they are relieved from exam/ academic stress.

Apart from the above, University Mall and Cafe Coffee Day are the other top recreation spots on the campus. Students can buy the required things from university mall. For banking, students need not go out of the campus, as LPU has 5 banks and 55 ATMs on its campus.

LPU has more than 300 food kiosks that cook unique varieties of food for minimal costs. African Food Cafe is one of the major highlights while there is a specific sweets store that offers all kinds of Punjabi sweets.

LPU has fully secured student residence facility. The separate hotel facility is provided to boys and girls. The hostels are offered to students on a sharing basis (two or three mostly) and single basis (as per the needs of students subject to availability). The fees for different categories of hostel facility will be different. The hostel has all the facilities such as convenience store, hospital, and in-house laundry. Students can use bicycles for an easy commute on the campus.

LPU Hostel Fees

LPU hostel fees 2026-27 range from INR 70,000 per year for a 5/6/7/8-seater air-cooled standard room (with Early Decision Benefit) to INR 2,00,000 per year for a single-seater AC studio apartment (without Early Decision Benefit). The hostel fee covers fully furnished rooms (bed with mattress, study table, chair, almirah), attached washroom with geyser, 24×7 security with CCTV surveillance, fire safety systems, electricity backup, Wi-Fi, and access to common amenities like study room, TV room, lifts, purified water, and playground. Free electricity units (250 to 1,000 per session, depending on room type) are included; usage beyond limits incurs extra charges. Mess fees are charged separately and range from INR 40,000 to INR 56,000 per year depending on the meal plan. The hostel fee, mess, laundry, swimming pool, gym and indoor facilities are all managed by Pro Bricks (the University's residential services partner).

Lovely Professional University offers a 10% lower hostel fee under the Early Decision Benefit (EDB) for students who book before 15th May 2026. Without EDB (after this date), each room category is INR 10,000 higher per year. Boys and girls have separate hostel buildings on LPU campus, and LPU's residential capacity covers over 25,000 students.

LPU Mess Fees 2026-27

Mess fees at Lovely Professional University range from INR 42,000 per year for a two-meal Standard plan to INR 58,000 per year for the Ala Carte four-meal plan. Mess Plan is optional — students can either avail the University's mess plan or manage meals independently. Plans are offered in two categories: Standard (fixed meal timings with Breakfast, Lunch, Tea Snacks, and/or Dinner) and Ala Carte (choice of cuisines per meal). The full mess fee structure is below.

LPU Campus FAQs

What are the various facilities available on campus at LPU , Phagwara ?

LPU , Phagwara offers various facilities to its students including Math Lab, Academic Zone, A/C, Auditorium, ATM and more.

How far is the LPU campus from the railway station?

The Jalandhar City Railway Station is at a distance of about 19 kilometres from the LPU campus. It takes approximately 25 minutes to reach the campus from the station by road.

What are the facilities at the campus of LPU?

The LPU campus is modern and equipped with all the latest facilities. It has laboratories, cafeteria, a central library, sports complexes, recreational facilities, ATMs, banks, etc. The Universal Mall and Cafe Coffee Day are the major recreational areas of the campus. Apart from that, there are more than 300 food kiosks that cook unique varieties of food at minimal prices.

What is the hostel fees of LPU?

Three kinds of accommodation are offered at LPU - Apartments, Standard Rooms, and Dormitories. The fees for each hostel differs from others. The hostel fee also depends on if the room is a single seater, double seater, triple seater, or quadruple seater. The apartments cost in the range of Rs. 1,68,00 to Rs. 78,000 per annum. Students staying in standard rooms can choose between an air conditioned room and a non air conditioned room, the fee of the former being more. These rooms start from Rs. 1,08,000 per annum and the minimum fee for these rooms is Rs. 54,000 annually. Dormitories cost between Rs. 44,000 to Rs. 34,000 on an annual basis.

What is the total area of the LPU campus?

The campus of LPU is spread over a total area of about 600 acres.

Does LPU provide students with hostel accommodation?

Yes, students can avail hostel accommodation at LPU. Separate hostel buildings are established for boys and girls on the university premises.

Are there any sports facilities available at LPU?

Yes, LPU has many playgrounds assigned for different sports on its campus. There is a football field, a cricket pitch, a boxing ring, and a taekwondo area among some of the common sports grounds in the LPU campus.
