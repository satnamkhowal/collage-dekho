About Guru Ram Dass Educational Society

Guru Ram Dass Group of Colleges Bhatinda, Malout was established in 2002 under the Guru Ram Dass Educational Society. The institution is affiliated with Maharaja Ranjit Singh Punjab Technical University Bathinda (MRSPTU), Punjab Nurses Registration Council, Mohali, Panjab University Chandigarh, and, Indian Nursing Council Delhi. Guru Ram Dass Group of Colleges courses are offered in streams like B.Pharm, D.Pharm, B.Sc, and B.Ed, among others. Admission to the courses at the institution is merit-based.

Guru Ram Dass Educational Society has a ragging-free campus located in Malout, Punjab, and has transport facilities. The Guru Ram Dass Group of Colleges hostel is separate for boys and girls. Other infrastructural facilities of the institution include a dedicated admission cell, education loan facility, library, cafeteria, etc. At present, the institution has 10,500+ students, 250+ teachers, and 360+ staff members. The Training and Placement cell of the institution trains students in placement activities. Guru Ram Dass Group of Colleges placements are done in reputed firms like Cipla, Natco, Sun Pharma, and, Abbott, to name a few.
