About Ranchi University

Ranchi University is a public state university located at Shahid Chowk, Ranchi, Jharkhand, established on July 12, 1960. Its campus spans 65 acres in Ranchi. RU is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 12B, accredited by NAAC with a B++ grade, and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). The university currently has 23 postgraduate departments, 19 constituent colleges, and 47 affiliated colleges. 

Ranchi University (RU) offers UG, PG, M.Phil., doctoral, vocational, and self-financing programmes across streams, including Arts, Science, Commerce, Engineering, Law, Medicine, Education, Management, and Journalism. Flagship programmes include MBBS, BA, B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., MBA, MCA, and LL.B. Ranchi University course admission to UG programmes is through CUET UG 2026, while PG admissions go through CUET PG 2026, conducted by NTA. Professional courses require scores in JEE Main, NEET, CAT, GATE, and MAT. All applicants must register on the official Jharkhand Chancellor Portal (universities.jharkhand.gov.in) to apply for UG and PG courses at Ranchi University, Ranchi.

Ranchi University placement 2025 report has not been published yet, but as per the NIRF report 2025 and official placement report 2024, a total of 44 students have been placed, with the highest package of INR 7.25 LPA and average package of INR 4.68 LPA. The top recruiters were HDFC Bank, Concentrix, Learning Routes, Reliance Retail, and others.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Does Ranchi University allow pursuing two degree courses simultaneously?
Following recent UGC guidelines, Ranchi University allows students to pursue two academic programs simultaneously, provided one is a regular full-time campus degree and the other is pursued through open/distance learning (like RU’s distance education directorate).
Can I change my affiliated college after the first year?
Yes, university rules permit inter-college migration after the first year, provided both principals issue a No Objection Certificate (NOC) and there is a vacant seat in your desired college for the exact same course.

Ranchi University Admission 2026

Ranchi University admission 2026 is open for the UG, PG, and PhD course the admission process conducted through the Chancellor's Portal (jharkhanduniversities.nic.in) for both UG and PG programmes. UG admissions for BA, B.Sc., and B.Com. are based on CUET UG 2026 scores. Students without CUET may get admission based on Class 12 marks (if seats remain). 

Students with valid CUET scores are given preference in merit lists. For professional UG courses (B.Tech., MBBS, B.Sc. Nursing), scores in national-level entrance exams — JEE Main/JCECE for B.Tech. and NEET UG for MBBS and Nursing — are mandatory. Ranchi University PG admission uses CUET PG 2026 scores for MA, M.Sc., and M.Com.; professional PG courses use CAT/MAT for MBA, GATE for M.Tech., and NEET PG for MD/MS. PhD admission requires students to appear in the Ranchi University PhD Entrance Test. UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, JRF, or GATE-qualified candidates are exempt from the written exam. Ranchi University Admission Process 2026

The steps below show how a student can apply for the Ranchi University admission for UG, PG, and PhD courses. Students must apply through the chancellor portal Ranchi, as an important part of admission.

  • Visit the official university website ranchiuniversity.ac.in, and navigate to the Admissions section.
  • Select your course type (UG, PG, Vocational, or PhD) and check the relevant admission notification.
  • Register and apply on the Chancellor's Portal (jharkhanduniversities.nic.in). Application fee: INR 500 (General/OBC) and INR 400 (SC/ST).
  • Appear for the relevant entrance exam — CUET UG/PG (NTA), JEE Main, NEET, CAT, GATE, or the university's own entrance test, as applicable.
  • Check the merit list or cut-off list published on the Chancellor's Portal.
  • Report to the allotted college/department for document verification within the stipulated dates.
  • Pay the admission fee and complete enrollment.

Ranchi University Examination 2026

Ranchi University conducts semester and annual examinations for its various Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), Vocational, and Professional courses throughout the year. The university manages all examination-related activities, including form fill-ups, admit card issuance, and result declarations, entirely through its dedicated online portals.

How to Access the Ranchi University Examination Portal?

For all exam needs, students will primarily use the official Ranchi University Examination Portal (exam.ranchiuniversity.co.in).

1. Downloading Your Ranchi University Admit Card

  • Visit the RU Examination Portal.
  • Click on the Admit Card section from the home page.
  • Select your specific Course (e.g., FYUGP, MBBS, BBA.LL.B.) and Semester/Year.
  • Enter your University Registration Number / Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  • Click "Get Admit Card" to download and print your hall ticket.

2. Checking Exam Datesheets & Notices

  • Visit the Ranchi University official website (ranchiuniversity.ac.in).
  • Navigate to the Notice For Students or Examination Programme/Centre section to download the latest official PDF datesheets.

3. Filling Out Ranchi University Examination Forms

  • You must create a student login on the Ranchi University examination portal using your University Registration Number and Name (exactly as it appears on your 10th board certificate).
  • Once logged in, you can verify your details, pay your Ranchi University exam fee online, and submit your Ranchi University exam form for the semester.

Ranchi University Results 2026

Ranchi University results are published on the official examination portal at exam.ranchiuniversity.co.in. Results for UG (BA/B.Sc./B.Com. FYUGP and CBCS), PG (MA/M.Sc./M.Com.), professional courses (MBBS, BDS, B.Sc. Nursing, B.Ed.), and research programmes are uploaded semester-wise. Students can also download marksheets and apply for original degree (OD) certificates from the same portal.

How to Check Ranchi University Results 2026?

To check the Ranchi University result for the semester, follow the given instructions:

  • Go to the official examination result portal: exam.ranchiuniversity.co.in/result.
  • Select your course type from the dropdown (BA/B.Sc./B.Com./PG/Professional/Vocational).
  • Enter your Roll Number in the designated field.
  • Click 'Submit.' Your result marksheet will load in PDF format.
  • Download or print the result for your records.

Student Life at Ranchi University

Ranchi University campus is spread over an area of 71 acres at Ranchi, Jharkhand. Library, Laboratories, Hostels for both Girls and Boys, IT infrastructure, Auditorium, Sports Complex, Cafeteria and more are some of the infrastructural facilities at the campus of Ranchi University. These facilities are updated as per the modern requirements to provide education to the students. 

Campus life

The campus life at the university is very happening. The canteen is one of the busiest places on campus. We can get a variety of cuisines here that are prepared hygienically every day. The central library of the university has more than 90000 textbooks, 10000 reference books, and nearly 350 theses for PhD. There is also a fully operational INFLIBNET facility (connected through a dedicated lease line to INFLIBNET Centre, Ahmadabad) which makes the library a happening place. 

Sports life

The sports complex of Ranchi University comprises of basketball court, volleyball court, ground to play football and cricket and more. The students participate in various competitions at zonal, district, state and national levels regularly. 

Hostel life

There are 2 boys' hostels and 1 girl’s hostel at the Ranchi University. The capacity of the boy’s hostels is 210 and the capacity of girl’s hostel is 60. The rooms are available for single and triple sharing. Residential facilities for the faculty members and non-teaching members are also available at the university.

Is LLB course offered in a regular mode at Ranchi University?

A
Anmol Arora •  Oct-14-2024

Dear Student,

Yes, LLB courses are offered in a regular mode at Ranchi University under the UG degree of BA LLB, which is an integrated course providing knowledge of both arts as well as law subjects simultaneously. The course is offered for a duration of five years, wherein the student has to complete both core and elective subjects along with practicals and internships. The minimum eligibility criteria to apply for the course is completion of 10+2 with minimum of 50% marks in any stream from a recognised board of school education. If you’re planning to pursue law from Ranchi University, then you have to study in a regular mode. However, there are many colleges situated in Jharkhand that do offer LLB course in a distance mode. These include Jharkhand State Open University Ranchi, Chotanagpur Law College Ranchi, Vinoba Bhave University Jharkhand etc. Students must also note that distance LLB course is not valid according to BCI regulations in order to practice law in India.
