About Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech

Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech is a 3 Years year program. To be eligible for the B.Tech degree program at Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science, Kolkata, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. They must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in their qualifying examination. Additionally, candidates must have a valid score in national level engineering entrance exams such as JEE Main or WBJEE. Meeting these criteria will qualify candidates for admission to the prestigious B.Tech program at Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science, Kolkata. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 3,42,500. Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech Application Process

Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Indian Institute of Aeronautical Science B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
