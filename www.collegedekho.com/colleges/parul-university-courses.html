About Parul University Courses 2026

Parul University courses list includes 200+ programs, including UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate, PhD, integrated, and online programs. Courses like B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Pharm, B.Des, MBBS, BAMS, and BHMS are offered at the UG level, while MBA, M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc, MA, M.Com, and LLM are offered at the PG level. Parul University fees range from INR 78,000 to INR 33.75 Lakhs for the complete duration across various programs.

Parul University offers full-time academic programs across Engineering, Medicine, Pharmacy, Management, Law, Design, Science, Arts, and Social Sciences, along with research-focused PhD programs and flexible online degrees. Admissions are based on merit and entrance exams such as JEE Main, GUJCET, NEET, GATE, CAT/MAT/CMAT, NATA, and PU-DAT, depending on the program.

Parul University Courses FAQs

What are the Parul University courses after Class 12th?

Parul University offers several UG courses after Class 12th. Some of the popular courses include B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Sc, BA, B.Com, B.Pharm, B.Des, B.Arch, BHMS, BAMS, BHMCT, MBBS, BSW, B.Lib and BVA.

What are the specialisations offered with the MBA programme?

Parul University offers several specialisations in the MBA programme namely agriculture management, banking and financial services, digital marketing and sales management, entrepreneur and innovation development, family-managed business management, etc.

Is Parul University good for B.Tech?

Parul University has a good reputation for its engineering education and exposure. It provides one of the highest placements in Gujarat for B.Tech. The highest package offered is Rs 23 LPA and the average package ranges between Rs 2.5 LPA to 5 LPA.

What is the cost of an MBA course at Parul University?

The Parul University fees for MBA is Rs 81,550 per annum (tuition fees). The eligibility criteria for MBA admission is to obtain 50% marks in graduation and qualify for CAT, MAT or CMAT exams.

What are the best courses offered at Parul University?

Some of the best courses offered at Parul University are B.Tech, MBA, M.Tech, BBA, BBA+MBA, B.Des, B.Sc, M.Sc and LLB, GNM etc. At the Diploma level, students can choose among a Diploma in Engineering, a D.Pharm, and PhD and post-doctoral courses.

Does Parul University offer online or short-term courses?

Yes, Parul University offers 10 online courses and various short-term digital courses. The Parul University online courses include BBA, BCA, MBA, MCA, M.Com, M.Sc, MA and MSW. The duration of these courses varies from 2 to 3 years depending on the level. Moreover, the Parul University short-term courses are offered virtually/online around the year. What types of courses does Parul University offer?

Parul University offers a wide variety of courses at different levels. The Parul University courses list includes undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, doctoral, post-doctoral, dual degree, honours, Industry-embedded, pathway and global programmes. Apart from these, online courses and continuing education courses are also available at Parul University. In total, Parul University offers 450+ courses to choose from.

Can I pay my Parul University fee online?

Yes, Parul University fee payment can be done online through the official website. Students can visit the official payment portal (paruluniversity.ac.in/pay-fees) and choose the fee type. Parul University fees can be paid for the tuition, hostel, new registration, uniform, etc. Choose the desired option and click on the ‘Pay Now’ button to complete the payment.

What are the Parul University courses after 10th?

Parul University offers a wide range of Diploma courses after Class 10th. Some of them are a Diploma in Engineering, a D.Pharm, DHMCT, a Diploma in Product Design, a Diploma in Interior and Furniture Design, a Diploma in Fashion Design and Technology, a Diploma in Paramedical and Health Science, a Diploma in Animal Husbandry and Diploma in architecture

Which courses are popular at Parul University?

B.Tech, MBA, BBA, BCA and MCA are some of the popular courses offered at Parul University. At the PG level, MBBS, M.Tech and M.Pharm are also sought-after programmes. At the UG level, other trendy courses at Parul University are B.Com, LLB, B.Pharm, B.Des and B.Arch. Parul University Diploma courses including Diploma in Engineering (Polytechnic), D.Pharm, ANM and GNM are also well-known.

What is the difficulty level of the PU CUET exam?

Parul University courses admission to various undergraduate and postgraduate courses is offered through the CUET examination. The difficulty level of the CUET exam is considered moderate for an average student. However, it varies depending on the preparation level of the students.

How to get a free seat in Parul University?

To get a free seat at Parul University, the student should be eligible for a 100% scholarship offered by the university. The university offers various scholarships such as cultural scholarships, Sports scholarships, etc. If a student qualifies for the eligibility criteria for any of the scholarships, then he/she can get a 100% concession on the Parul University fees.

Does Parul University accept 12th marks?

Yes, the primary eligibility to get admission to Parul University UG courses is to pass Class 12. The university offers admission to various UG courses based on the marks obtained in Class 12th only. Such courses are BCA, BBA, B.Com (Hons.), B.Sc, BA, B.Pharm, B.Lib, BVA, etc. The students will not be required to take any entrance examination and will be offered admission based on their performance in Class 12.

Is Parul University easy to get?

Getting admission to Parul University depends totally on the eligibility criteria and selection process. For example, admission to the B.Tech programme at Parul University is based on GUJCET or JEE Main scores. Students who are not able to qualify for B.Tech eligibility may find it difficult to get in. However, admission to other courses such as BCA, BBA, B.Com, B.Sc, BA, MA, M.Com, and MCA require minimum qualification.

Is Parul University very expensive?

Parul University is neither too expensive nor too affordable but somewhere in between. However, students belonging to low economic groups may find it difficult to afford the fees for most courses. The fee structure of Parul University for UG courses ranges from Rs 32,000 per year (B.Lib) to Rs 20,81,162 per year (MBBS). Parul University fees 2024 for PG range from Rs 20,000 per year (M.Lib-external) to Rs 4,28,000 per year (MS).

What is the annual fee of Parul University?

The annual fee structure of Parul University varies according to the level and type of course. For instance, at the UG level, Parul University fees for B.Tech ranges from Rs 86,000 to Rs 1,78,000 per year whereas the fee structure for BCA, BBA and B.Com ranges between Rs 68,850 to Rs 1,16,900. Similarly, at the PG level, Parul University MBA fees is Rs 1,66,000 per year and fees for M.Tech is Rs 1,25,250 per annum.
