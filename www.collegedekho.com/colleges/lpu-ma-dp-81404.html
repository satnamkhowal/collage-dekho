About LPU MA

LPU MA is a 2 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the M.A. (Master of Arts) degree at Lovely Professional University, applicants must have a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university. The annual fee for MA is INR 20,000 - 2,80,000. LPU MA Admission 2026

Admission to the MA at LPU is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the LPU MA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

LPU MA Application Process

LPU MA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MA course at LPU have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill LPU MA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of LPU and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of LPU. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the LPU MA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the LPU MA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of LPU MA Application Form.

Documents Required for LPU MA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
