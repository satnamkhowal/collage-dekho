About CSIR-Central Electrochemical Research Institute

CSIR-Central Electrochemical Research Institute is a premier research institution located in Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu. The institute was established in 1948. CSIR-Central Electrochemical Research Institute has been recognised by Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR) and is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an A++ grade. Electrochemical Energy Systems, Corrosion Science and Engineering, Electrochemical Sensors and Biosensors, Electrochemical Materials Science, and Electrochemical Power Sources are some of CSIR-Central Electrochemical Research Institute facilities.CSIR-Central Electrochemical Research Institute courses are offered at UG levels. CSIR-Central Electrochemical Research Institute admission is based on merit & entrance exam. The college accepts TNEA for admission. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include Exide Batteries, Shell, Luminous Batteries, Amara Raja Batteries, TVS motors, Hyundai, Sterlite, GE, NOCIL, Bloom Energy, DuPont, Okaya Batteries, HBL, Corrtech and Eveready in addition to others.

Cecri Karaikudi collage admission at CECRI

R
Rajeshwari De •  Oct-09-2023

To get admission to the CSIR-Central Electrochemical Research Institute, you must first complete the following steps:

  • Examine the eligibility requirements. CECRI admits students to a variety of research programmes, including the Ph.D., Integrated Ph.D., and Postdoctoral Fellowship. Because the eligibility conditions for each programme differ, it is critical to carefully review the webpage.
  • Fill out an online application. All CECRI research programmes include an online application process. The application URL can be found on the CECRI website.
  • Submit the necessary documentation. In addition to the online application form, you must provide the following documents: a resume, a cover letter, transcripts of all academic records, a copy of your GATE/NET score (if applicable), and two letters of recommendation.
  • Participate in the interview. If your application is chosen for further consideration, you will be contacted for an interview. The interview will take place at CECRI in Karaikudi.
  • Take admission. Students are admitted to CECRI's numerous research programmes based on their performance in the interview and their academic records.
