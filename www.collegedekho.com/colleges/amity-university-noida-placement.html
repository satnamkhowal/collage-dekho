About Amity University Noida Placement 2025

Amity University Noida placement 2025 report has been released. The highest package recorded during the campus placements reached INR 75 LPA. Over 800 recruiters participated and extended more than 6,000 job offers to students. B.Tech students secured the highest package of INR 35.9 LPA, with an average package of INR 9.6 LPA and a median package of INR 6 LPA. 

MBA placement report is yet to be released by Amity Noida for the 2025 batch. The highest and average package offered to the MBA Class of 2024 was INR 26.30 LPA and INR 7.35 LPA, respectively. Leading recruiters at Amity University Noida placements 2025 included major companies such as Amazon, Palo Alto Networks, Google, Cisco, IBM, TCS, Samsung, Nokia, KPMG, and Cognizant.
