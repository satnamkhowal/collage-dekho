About IIT Roorkee

Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, also known as IIT Roorkee, is a public institute established in the year 1847. Located in Roorkee, Uttarakhand, the institute is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is also approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). IIT Roorkee is also accredited by NAAC and approved by AICTE. IIT Roorkee recognized 7th Overall Category ranking by NIRF 2025. As per NIRF 2025, IIT Roorkee has been ranked 6th in the Engineering category and 1st in the Architecture category.

IIT Roorkee offers a total of 15 courses at the UG, PG, and PhD levels. At the UG level, institute offers courses like BTech, BSc, BDes, etc. Meanwhile, at the PG level, courses like MBA, MSc, MTech, etc. are offered. These courses are offered in various specialisations. The institute provides admission on the basis of merit and entrance exam scores. The entrance exams required for admission to the IIT Roorkee include JEE Advanced for BTech, IIT JAM for MSc, GATE for MTech, etc.

The institute has a placement cell for the training and placement of students. As per previous years' IIT Roorkee placement statistics, the highest package offered was Rs 1.5 CPA, and the average package offered was Rs 34.05 LPA. Its campus has modern facilities and infrastructure, including separate hostels for boys and girls, an auditorium, smart classrooms, library, laboratories, gym, etc. for the holistic development of the students.

IIT Roorkee Application Process 2025

Candidates can fill out the IIT Roorkee application form in online or offline modes. The admission form can be downloaded from the college website. The step-by-step process to fill out IIT Roorkee application form is explained below:

Step 1: Visit the official website at iitr.ac.in. Go to the “Admissions” section and click on the “Application Form” link.

Step 2: Download the application form. Save the PDF form on your device and take a printout.

Step 3: Fill in the personal details. Enter your full name, date of birth, gender, contact number, email ID, and address as per your official documents.

Step 4: Fill in academic qualifications. Provide information about your qualifying exam, including board name, year of passing, marks obtained, and subjects.

Step 5: Attach required documents. Photocopy the necessary documents, such as:

  • Class 10 & 12 mark sheets
  • Admit card/ rank card of the respective exam
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile certificate
  • Passport-sized photographs

Step 6: Submit the application.

Step 7: Keep a copy for future reference.

What is the registration process for JoSAA counseling for IIT Roorkee admission?

Candidates need to register on the JoSAA website using their JEE Main or JEE Advanced application number and password. After registration, they need to fill in their choices of IIT Roorkee and courses based on their preferences.

What was the last date of JoSAA counselling 2023 for IITR admission?

The last date of JoSAA counselling 2023 was July 26, 2023. Students willing to take admission to IIT Roorkee were required to attend the counselling session before the deadline. For any reason, if they skipped the counselling their seat were allotted to the student under their name on the list.

Is JoSAA counselling free for IIT Roorkee admission?

No, JoSAA counselling is not free. JEE Advanced qualified students have to pay the fees for the JoSAA counselling. The JoSAA counselling fees is Rs. 35,000 for the general category and 15,000 for SC, ST, GEN-PwD, GEN-EWS-PwD, OBC-NCL-PwD, SC-PwD, or ST-PwD category.

What was JoSAA counselling 2023 start date for IITR admission?

IIT Roorkee JoSAA counselling registration started on June 19, 2023. Students who were qualified were able to register and participate in the counselling process.

How can I check my JEE Advanced result for admission in IIT Roorkee?

To check your JEE Advanced result, you can follow these steps: Visit the official website of JEE Advanced or the result portal specified by the organising IIT. Look for the result link or tab on the website's homepage. Click on the result link and enter your JEE Advanced roll number, date of birth, and other required details. After entering the necessary information, click on the submit button. Your JEE Advanced result will be displayed on the screen, showing your All India Rank (AIR), subject-wise scores, total marks, and qualifying status. Take a printout or screenshot of the result for future reference.

Mohit Saxena, the Co-founder of InMobi, is an IIT Roorkee alumnus.
