About Dayanand Brajendra Swarup College

Dayanand Brajendra Swarup College is situated in Kanpur in Uttar Pradesh state of India. Established in 1959, is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University and accredited by UGC, NAAC, National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). Dayanand Brajendra Swarup College offers 24 courses across 4 streams namely Arts, Vocational, Science, IT..Popular degrees offered at Dayanand Brajendra Swarup College include BSc, BA, MA, MSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Dayanand Brajendra Swarup College is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Dayanand Brajendra Swarup College, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Dayanand Brajendra Swarup College is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
