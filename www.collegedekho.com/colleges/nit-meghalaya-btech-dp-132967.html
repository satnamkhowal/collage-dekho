About NIT Meghalaya BTech

NIT Meghalaya BTech is a 4-years program offered in 5 specialisations. To pursue a BTech (Bachelor of Technology) degree at NIT Meghalaya Shillong, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. They should have successfully completed their 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects from a recognized board. Additionally, they must secure a minimum aggregate score of 75% (65% for SC/ST/PwD candidates) in the qualifying examination. Aspirants are required to appear for the JEE Main entrance examination and obtain a valid rank. Admissions to the BTech program at NIT Meghalaya Shillong are based on the JEE Main scores and subsequent rounds of counselling conducted by the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA). The annual fee for BTech is INR 1,25,000. NIT Meghalaya BTech Highlights

NIT Meghalaya BTech is a four-year, full-time undergraduate engineering programme offered in a regular mode across core specialisations such as civil engineering, mechanical engineering, computer science and engineering, electrical engineering, and electronics and communication engineering. Admission to the programme is based on performance in the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main, and candidates must meet the prescribed eligibility criteria set by the institute and the counselling authority. The selection criteria for the BTech at this institute is to have a valid JEE Main 2026 score. The entire admission process is carried out online, from application and counselling registration to seat allotment, through the official portals.

NIT Meghalaya BTech Fee Structure 2026

NIT Meghalaya BTech fee structure covers tuition and support charges for the full programme, with waivers for SC/ST/PwD and low-income students on tuition. NIT Meghalaya BTech fees are INR 891,150 for the entire duration of 4 years, covering everything. New students pay one-time fees of INR 5,150 for an ID card, degree fee, student welfare fund, and alumni charge, along with caution deposits at entry, while semester charges include fees regarding exams, enrolment, library, lab, internet, hostel establishment, hostel, mess, and student activity.

  • Students need to pay an INR 10,000 caution deposit, halved equally for the institute and the hostel. Both the caution deposits are refundable after the course completion.
  • While the caution deposit is refundable, none of the other one-time fees are refundable.
  • A tuition fee waiver of 66% is available for the general, OBC, and EWS students with an annual family income of between INR 100,000 and INR 500,000, while for the SC, ST, PWD, and students with an annual fee income of below INR 100,000, the tuition fee is completely waived.

NIT Meghalaya B.Tech Eligibility Criteria 2026

NIT Meghalaya BTech eligibility criteria for the 2026 admission requires candidates to pass Class 12 from a recognised board with physics, chemistry, and mathematics as main subjects. Students must score at least 75% aggregate marks overall and 75% in PCM (65% for SC/ST/PwD categories). A valid JEE Main 2026 score is a must for admission to the BTech programme, with an age limit of 25 years for the general, OBC, and EWS candidates. SC, ST, and PwD candidates get a 5-year relaxation in the JEE Main eligibility.

NIT Meghalaya BTech Admission Process 2026

NIT Meghalaya BTech admission process for 2026 starts with JEE Main registration, followed by JoSAA choice filling for seat allocation. Candidates register online through the official portals and upload required documents during verification.

Steps to Apply for NIT Meghalaya BTech in 2026

Step 2: Appear for JEE Main

The candidates must appear for the JEE Main 2026 in the scheduled January and April 2026 sessions.

Step 3: Register on JoSAA Portal

After the result, the candidates must log in to the JoSAA counselling portal using their JEE Main 2026 credentials.

Step 4: Fill Choices

Then the candidates must take part in all 6 counselling rounds, select and arrange NIT Meghalaya BTech branches in order of preference and lock the choices within the prescribed counselling deadline. Usually it’s in the 2nd or 3rd week of July 2026.

Step 5: Pay Partial Fees

After the 1st round of seat allotment, the candidates must submit the seat acceptance fee of INR 30,000 for the General, OBC-NCL, and EWS categories, while for the SC, ST, and PWD, it's 15,000. The candidates need to pay this fee only once after the 1st time allotment. If a candidate is later upgraded to a better seat in another counselling round, they do not pay the seat acceptance fee again.

The process will progress with result declarations for each session, followed by the opening of the JoSAA portal in July 2026 for registration and choice filling. Candidates must track these dates carefully, as all key admission activities—including examinations, counselling, and seat allocation—will be conducted strictly as per the scheduled timeline.

NIT Meghalaya BTech Application Fees 2026

NIT Meghalaya BTech application fees include JEE Main registration and JoSAA seat acceptance charges. General male candidates pay INR 1,000 for JEE Main, while general females pay INR 800 and reserved categories INR 500. The JoSAA seat acceptance fee stands at INR 30,000 for general/OBC/EWS males and INR 15,000 for females and all SC/ST/PwD. The seat acceptance fee includes a JoSAA processing charge of INR 5,000, and the remainder is adjusted against the total admission fee at the institute.
