About Chandigarh University BJMC

Chandigarh University BJMC is a 3 Years year program. Chandigarh University requires applicants for the Bachelor of Journalism and Mass Communication degree to have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate from a recognized board. The annual fee for BJMC is INR 1,58,000. Chandigarh University BJMC Admission 2026

Admission to the BJMC at Chandigarh University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Chandigarh University BJMC admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Chandigarh University BJMC Application Process

Chandigarh University BJMC application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BJMC course at Chandigarh University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Chandigarh University BJMC application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Chandigarh University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Chandigarh University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Chandigarh University BJMC application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Chandigarh University BJMC application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Chandigarh University BJMC Application Form.

Documents Required for Chandigarh University BJMC Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

FAQs about Chandigarh University BJMC

What is the duration of the BJMC course at Chandigarh University?

The BJMC course at Chandigarh University is offered for a duration of 3 years. The course is split into 6 semesters and the course curriculum is also divided in a semester-wise manner. The course fee for each semester is Rs 67,500.

How to apply for Chandigarh University BJMC admission?

To apply for admission to BJMC at CU, visit the official website cucet.cuchd.in. Register for the CUCET exam and appear for the online exam on the selected date and time. Once you obtain te results of the exam, submit the same for the further shortlisting process.

Are there any scholarships for BJMC course at Chandigarh University?

Yes, the university provides Chandigarh University BJMC scholarship on various criteria. The CUCET-based scholarships offers up to 100% fee concession on the entire fee. Apart from this, scholarships are also awarded for sports category, merit-basis, wards of defence personnel category, etc.

What are the career opportunities after pursuing Chandigarh University BJMC?

The scope of career is vast after pursuing the BJMC course from CU. Students can build careers in fields like TV Production, Print Media, Public Relations, Publishers and Writers, Brand Management, as well as Content Writers.

Is Chandigarh University good for BJMC Course?

The Chandigarh University BJMC is among its most popular courses. The university conducts visits to media houses and radio stations during the course tenure. Moreover, the rankings of CU for its BJMC course are 5th, 10th, and, 12th by India Today, Outlook I-Care Rankings, and, The Week, respectively, for the Journalism and Mass Communication categories.

Is Chandigarh University journalism fees high?

The Chandigarh University BJMC fees for the entire course duration of 3 years is Rs 4,05,000. Apart from this the miscellaneous fee charges include Rs 2,000 as the examination fee and Rs 2,000 as the one-time security deposit. The fee is average as compared to various other private universities.

Is the Chandigarh University BJMC admission open for 2023?

Yes, the admissions for BJMC for the 2023 batch at Chandigarh University are currently open. Admission will be based on the CUCET. The Registration for CUCET Phase 2 started on May 30, 2023, and will end on August 30, 2023. Moreover, the exam is being held from May 30, 2023, and will end on August 31, 2023.

Is Chandigarh University BJMC good?

The Bachelor of Journalism and Mass Communication (BJMC) course at Chandigarh University is one of the most popular UG courses. It is offered for a duration of 3 years and the course fee is Rs 4,05,000. The rankings of Chandigarh University for BJMC are 12th by The Week in the Mass Communication Colleges in India, 10th by Outlook I-Care Rankings in the Top Mass Communication private Colleges of India category, and, 5th by India Today in the Top Private Mass Communication Colleges of North India category.

What are the top recruiters for Chandigarh University BJMC placement?

For BJMC placement at Chandigarh University, some of the most reputed media and journalism firms hire from the university. The top recruiters include  Aaj Tak, India TV, Big FM, News 18 India, and, PTC News.

What is the Chandigarh University BJMC fees?

The BJMC fees is Rs 4,05,000 for the entire duration of 3 years. Along with this tuition fee component, candidates are also required to pay Rs 2,000 as the examination fee and Rs 2,000 as the security deposit. The security deposit is one-time.
