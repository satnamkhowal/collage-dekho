DPG Institute of Technology & Management FAQs

What is the fees for BCA at DPG Institute of Technology and Management Gurugram?

The Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) programme is of three years duration, that is six semesters. Due to the need for IT specialists, the demand for this course has grown exponentially. BCA graduates from DPGITM secure job roles as system administrators, junior programmers, web developers, system engineers, etc. IT companies like Infosys, IBM, Wipro, Oracle, etc. hire students. The DPG Institute of Technology and Management BCA fees structure is Rs 55,000 per annum.

Are scholarships available at DPGITM?

Yes, scholarships are available for the meritorious and deserving students of DPGITM Gurugram. Scholarships are awarded at the conclusion of the semester. The merit based scholarships include: Rs 15,000 scholarship for students with 90% in 10+2 or rank less than 10000 (Boys and Girls). Rs 10,000 scholarship for students with 80% in 10+2 or rank less than 30000 (Boys and Girls). Rs 5,000 scholarship for students with 70% in 10+2 or rank less than 50000 (Only Girls).

What are the documents necessary for DPG Institute of Technology and Management course admissions?

There is certain documentation that needs to be provided by the students for verification purposes. The documents required during the time of DPG Institute of Technology and Management admission are as follows: Class 10 Marksheet. Class 12 Marksheet. Graduation Marksheet (for PG admission). Entrance Exam Card (JEE Main/ GATE). Caste Certificate (if any). Income Certificate of Parents/ Guardian. Character Certificate. Certificate in respect of Physically Handicapped candidates (if any). Anti-Ragging Undertaking by Students and Parents. Medical Fitness Certificate.

Is DPG Institute of Technology and Management AICTE approved?

Yes, DPG Institute of Technology and Management Gurugram is approved by the All India Council of Technical Education (AICTE), Ministry of HRD, Govt. of India. It also has approval from the Director of Technical Education (DTE), Govt. of Haryana. Located in the heart of Cyber Hub, the institute is affiliated to MDU University. It has a 100% ragging free environment and a mentoring system to support the students. Public transportation connects the campus to all areas of the city with ease.

What is the DPG Institute of Technology and Management admission process?

The applications for the DPGITM admission process require students to complete and submit the online application form. Here is the steps for the procedure: Visit the institute's official webpage at dpgitm.ac.in. Select the "Apply Online" option located at the top of the webpage. It will show the online application form. Complete the application form by providing the necessary information. Then, select "Submit." This will conclude the DPGITM application process.

How is the DPG Institute of Technology and Management placement?

The placements at the institute are good with numerous companies taking part in the placement drives conducted by the DPGITM placement cell. 90 students were placed through the on-campus placement, according to the placement data for 2023. The cell also guarantees 100% assistance regarding recruitment activities. The DPGITM highest package recorded was Rs 5 LPA and the lowest was Rs 1.8 LPA. What is the DPG Institute of Technology and Management MTech CSE fees?

The DPG Institute of Technology and Management MTech course is offered in two specialisations namely CSE and ECE. The Computer Science and Engineering programme is more popular among students. The course has an intake of 36 students. Admission is granted on the basis of the GATE exam result. Other than BE and B.Tech graduates, MCA and M.Sc (Computer Science/IT/Software/ Maths/ Physics) students are also eligible. The DPG Institute of Technology and Management MTech CSE fees structure is Rs 88,000 per year.

What is the eligibility criteria for BBA and BCA at DPGITM Gurgaon?

The DPG Institute of Technology and Management admission criteria for the BBA and BCA courses is the same. Both are three year long courses which is divided into six semesters. The course fees is Rs 55,000 per annum for both BBA and BCA. To secure admission, candidates must have passed the 10+2 qualifying examination from a recognised board with at least 45% marks in aggregate. The final selection is done on a merit basis from the marks obtained in the 10+2 qualifying examination.

Is DPGITM government or private?

The DPG Institute of Technology and Management was established in 2011, under the DPG Group. It is a private institute and is affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU) Rohtak. The institute is located in Gurgaon, Haryana. It is approved by the All India Council of Technical Education (AICTE), Ministry of HRD, Govt. of India; Director of Technical Education (DTE), Govt. of Haryana. The institute offers UG and PG courses with an intake of around 300 students in total. The degrees are B.Tech, BBA, BCA and M.Tech.

What are the DPG Institute of Technology and Management courses offered?

There are both undergraduate and postgraduate courses offered at the DPG Institute of Technology and Management Gurugram. The UG degrees are B.Tech, BBA and BCA. At the PG level, M.Tech degree is provided. The B.Tech course has seven specialisations namely AI&ML, CSE, Data Science, ECE, EE, ME and CE. The BTech fees is Rs 89,000 per annum. The BBA and BCA course fees is Rs 55,000 per annum. The M.Tech course has two specialisations namely CSE and ECE. The M.Tech fees is Rs 88,000 per annum.

What is the DPG Institute of Technology and Management BTech CSE fees?

DPGITM offers a four year long B.Tech programme in seven specialisations. One of the most popular of them is Computer Science and Engineering. Due to its career prospects after graduation, many students tend to opt for this programme. The DPG Institute of Technology and Management BTech CSE course has an intake of 60 students. Admission is given on the basis of the JEE Main marks. The DPG Institute of Technology and Management BTech CSE fees structure is Rs 89,500 per year.

DPG Institute of Technology & Management Scholarships

The institute offers scholarships to its meritorious students. The DPGITM scholarships are provided to the students based on their extraordinary academic performance and financial background. The eligibility criteria must be met in order to avail of the scholarship. 

The DPG Institute of Technology and Management scholarships are provided in the following three categories:

  • 90% in 10+2 or rank less than 10000 offers Rs 15,000 scholarship for boys and girls.
  • 80% % in 10+2 or rank less than 30000 offers Rs 10,000 scholarships for boys and girls.
  • 70% in 10+2 or rank less than 50000 offers Rs 5,000 scholarship for girls only

Apart from this, DPGITM Gurgaon also provides rewards to the students who secure ranks in the semester exams.

  • The student who secures the first position is provided with a laptop.
  • The student securing the second position is provided with a tablet.
  • The student securing the third position is provided Rs 5000 as a reward.

Scholarships are provided over a time period at the semester's end via DD or cheque. The cheque is issued only in the name of the student. Also, in order to be eligible for the scholarship, students must have at least 85% attendance in each of the semesters.

Scholarship Test: DPG Institute of Technology and Management conducts a scholarship test for the students, and the scholarship for the B.Tech program is given as follows: 

  • The top 10 students are provided free-of-cost education
  • A scholarship of Rs 10,000 is given to the students who secure passing marks in the examination.
  • All the girls who take admission at DPG Institute of Technology and Management Gurgaon are given free laptops.

Education Loan: DPGITM Gurugram offers education loans to students. It has collaborated with various banks to provide financial help to the students.

Indian nationality students can avail of loans from any bank to pursue UG or PG courses in engineering. The applicant must have admission to a UGC-recognized college or university.
