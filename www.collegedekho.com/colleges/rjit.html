About Rustamji Institute of Technology

Rustamji Institute of Technology is situated in Gwalior in Madhya Pradesh state of India. Established in 1999, RJIT is a Private UnAided college. The college is affiliated with UNIVERSITY INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, RGPV, BHOPAL and accredited by AICTE. Rustamji Institute of Technology offers 20 courses across 2 streams namely Engineering, IT..Popular degrees offered at Rustamji Institute of Technology include B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Rustamji Institute of Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Rustamji Institute of Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Rustamji Institute of Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.

Rustamji Institute of Technology specialties

  • Region : Strategic Paramilitary Location - Situated within the expansive BSF Academy Tekanpur, ensuring a highly secure and disciplined academic environment. - Strategically located on the Gwalior-Jhansi National Highway (NH-75), providing connectivity to major industrial hubs.
  • Fees : Low Cost Education - Rs 1.58 - 3.15 L (Total Tuition Fees for B.Tech) - Highly subsidized fee structure for wards of BSF and CAPF personnel.
  • ROI : Excellent Value - High return on investment due to low tuition costs paired with consistent placements in the IT and Automotive sectors. - Access to specialized BSF technical resources and workshops at no additional cost.
  • Specializations : Automobile Engineering Flagship - First institute in North-Central India to offer specialized B.Tech and M.Tech in Automobile Engineering. - Features the Audi Service Training Lab (VG-TAP) and a dedicated Future Mobility Lab for hands-on learning.
  • Placements : Automotive & IT Sector Focus - Strong recruitment ties with top automotive firms like Mahindra, Audi, and TVS Motor. - Consistent placement record with major IT services companies including TCS, Wipro, and Infosys.
