About IIT Kharagpur

IIT Kharagpur, established in 1951 as India's first IIT on a 2,100-acre campus in West Bengal, holds NAAC A++ accreditation and serves over 13,000 students through 19 departments. In NIRF 2025 rankings, it secured 5th in Engineering, 6th Overall, and 5th in Research, with QS World 2026 at 222 globally. For students eyeing 2026 admission, B.Tech entry needs JEE Advanced ranks under 11,111 for General in the least competitive branches, while placements in 2025 delivered 1,811 offers with an average Rs 24 LPA for B.Tech.

IIT Kharagpur Fees Structure 2026

IIT Kharagpur fees for 2026 use 2025-26 rates, with semester-wise tuition Rs 1,00,000 for BTech General, waived for SC/ST/PwD.

  • BTech: Rs 1,00,000 tuition/sem + Rs 19,650 other fees/sem; total first year Rs 2,55,000.
  • MTech: Rs 5,000 tuition/sem + Rs 6,050 other; total Rs 45,000/program.
  • MBA: Rs 2,17,500/year + other; total Rs 4,35,000.
  • Hostel: Rs 39,460/sem, including mess Rs 14,500.
  • PhD: Rs 15,000-25,000/year.

Income waivers: Full for <Rs 1 lakh, 2/3 remission for Rs 1-5 lakh.

IIT Kharagpur Admission Process 2026

IIT Kharagpur admission 2026 opens with JEE Main registration closing November 27, 2025, for B.Tech; GATE for M.Tech in February 2026.

  • B.Tech: Via JEE Advanced + JoSAA; eligibility 75% Class 12 PCM.
  • MTech: GATE + COAP; BTech 60%.
  • MBA: CAT (Nov 30, 2025) + GD/PI; graduation 60%.
  • MSc: IIT JAM (Feb 2026) + JOAP.
  • PhD: GATE/NET + interview; applications March 2026 for Autumn.

New 2026: Talent-based BTech seats without JEE ranks for Olympiad winners.
