About Vidyasagar University

Vidyasagar University is a public state university that was established in the year 1981. Located in Midnapore, West Bengal, the university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is also approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and a member of AIU. Vidyasagar University is also accredited by NAAC B grade. As per NIRF 2024, Vidyasagar University has been ranked under 151-200 band in the University category.

Vidyasagar University offers a total of 5 courses at the PG and doctorate levels. At the PG level, university offers courses like MBA, MSc, MTech, MA, and MCA. Meanwhile, PhD course at the university is offered in Chemistry and History streams. The PG courses are also offered in various specialisations. The university provides admission on the basis of merit and entrance exam scores. The entrance exams required for admission to the Vidyasagar University include CAT/ MAT/ CMAT for MBA, NIMCET for MCA, GATE/ UGC NET for PhD, etc. 

The university has a placement cell for the training and placement of students. As per previous years' Vidyasagar University placement statistics, the highest package offered was Rs 8.50 LPA, and the average package offered was Rs 4 LPA. Its campus has modern facilities and infrastructure, including separate hostels for boys and girls, an auditorium, smart classrooms, library, laboratories, gym, etc. for the holistic development of the students.
