About JECRC University

JECRC University, established in 2012 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, is a private university recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited by NAAC. It operates under the JECRC Foundation (established in 2000) and spans a 3 million sq. ft. campus at Vidhani, Jaipur. The university offers 100+ programmes across 11 schools in engineering, management, computer applications, law, design, sciences, humanities, hospitality, and allied health sciences. The university offers courses such as BTech, BCA, BBA, BCom, BA LLB / BSc LLB / BBA LLB, MBA, MTech, MCA, and several others. The total fee ranges from INR 7,00,000 to INR 13,00,000 for BTech, INR 5,00,000 to INR 6,00,000 for MBA, INR 4,50,000 to INR 7,20,000 for BCA, and INR 2,15,000 for PhD programmes.

For JECRC University Jaipur BTech admission, candidates must pass Class 12 with a minimum of 60% marks in Physics, Mathematics, and English, with admission based on JEE Main 2026 or CUET-UG 2026 scores. MBA admission requires a Bachelor’s degree with at least 60% marks along with a valid CAT/MAT/XAT score followed by GD and PI. Law admissions are based on CLAT 2026 or CUET-UG scores along with a Personal Interview, while PhD admissions require a Master’s degree with a minimum of 55% marks followed by an entrance test and interview. The admission process is conducted online through the university portal and includes application submission, merit list preparation based on entrance exams or qualifying marks, physical counselling, document verification, seat allotment, and fee payment for seat confirmation.

JECRC University Rajasthan placements 2025 recorded the highest package of INR 33 LPA and an average package of around INR 6 LPA, with 2,230 placement offers made by 140+ companies. Top recruiters include Amazon, Adobe, TCS, Accenture, Deloitte, HPE, Bosch, L&T, Nestlé, and Google across sectors such as IT, consulting, engineering, FMCG, and others. The university reported 11,470 internships in 2024–25, with 61% of internships being paid and stipends ranging from INR 5,000 to INR 60,000. The university was ranked 6th in India by Internshala among 600+ colleges.

JECRC University Campus Life & Facilities

JECRC University campus spans 3 million sq. ft. at Ramchandrapura Industrial Area, Vidhani, Jaipur. The campus houses academic blocks named after Indian scientists, a fully air-conditioned 18,000 sq. ft. food court, on-campus hostels, a library, community radio station, and a dedicated innovation Makerspace.

P sidhu •  Oct-29-2025
Yes, you can definitely study MBA at Lovely Professional University (LPU), and the university offers several scholarships to support deserving students.
