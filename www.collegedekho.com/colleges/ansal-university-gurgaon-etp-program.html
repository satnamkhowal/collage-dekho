Sushant University Job Ready Degree

If you are looking to kick start your professional career then Sushant University brings you the Job Ready “Assured” courses. These courses will help students in learning job-oriented skills and familiarise them with rapidly changing industry practices. Job Ready courses at Sushant University are B.Des in UX (IXP) and M.Des in UX (IXP). The university conducts entrance exams and personal interviews for admission in BDes and MDes courses. The annual fees for B.Des in UX (IXP) is Rs 1,98,000.

Sushant University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions

Sushant University conducts an entrance test and a personal interview for admission in “Assured” courses. The university conducts B.Design Aptitude Test (BDAT) and M.Design Aptitude Test (MDAT) for admission in BDes and MDes courses respectively.
