IIT Gandhinagar Placement Rate 2025

Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar placement rate has fluctuated considerably over the last five years, with no two consecutive years recording the same direction of change. The rate peaked in 2023 at 94.44% before declining in both 2024 and 2025.

  • The placement rate of BTech in 2025 stood at 71.89%, with 133 students placed out of 185 registered across all six BTech branches.
  • The placement rate of MTech in 2025 was 42.98%, with 49 students placed out of 114 registered across eight MTech and BTech+MTech branches.
  • Among BTech branches, the CSE branch recorded the highest placement rate at 86.05%, while the MSE branch recorded the lowest at 40% in 2025.
  • The overall placement rate in 2025 dropped by 21.52% compared to 2024, making it the largest single-year decline in the five-year period from 2021 to 2025.

IIT Gandhinagar Placement Rate 2025 FAQs

How many students at IIT Gandhinagar opted out of the 2025 placement drive?
In the 2025 placement drive, out of 239 enrolled BTech students, 54 did not register for placements, accounting for roughly 22.6% of the total BTech batch. Similarly, out of 169 enrolled MTech students, 55 did not register for the drive. This means that a considerable share of students across both programmes chose to pursue higher education, research, or other opportunities instead of campus placements, which is a common pattern at premier engineering institutes like IIT Gandhinagar, especially when the institute is known for its research excellence.
Does a lower placement rate at IIT Gandhinagar mean fewer companies visited the campus?
Not necessarily. A lower placement rate can reflect multiple factors beyond the number of companies visiting campus. These include a higher number of students registering for the drive, students declining offers to pursue higher education, or companies reducing their hiring intake in a given year. In 2025, 299 students registered across BTech and MTech programmes, which is a larger pool compared to previous years. A larger registered base with similar or fewer offers naturally results in a lower placement rate percentage.
Does the IIT Gandhinagar placement rate include students who received PPOs?
Yes, students who receive pre-placement offers at IIT Gandhinagar are counted within the overall placement figures. A PPO is treated as a confirmed offer and the student is marked as placed without participating in the regular placement drive. This means the published placement rate includes both students placed through the regular drive and those who secured offers through internships. Students who accept a PPO are not eligible to sit for further companies during the official placement season.

IIT Gandhinagar BTech Placement 2025 FAQs

Is it mandatory for all BTech students to participate in the IIT Gandhinagar placements?
No, participation in the IIT Gandhinagar placement drive is not mandatory for BTech students. Students who wish to pursue higher education, research, or entrepreneurship may opt out of the placement process. In the 2025 placement drive, out of 239 enrolled BTech students, only 185 registered for placements, indicating that a portion of students chose alternative paths. The Career Development Services cell supports both placement-seeking students and those pursuing postgraduate admissions or other opportunities.
Can BTech students at IIT Gandhinagar apply for jobs outside their branch during placements?
Yes, BTech students at IIT Gandhinagar are allowed to apply for roles outside their core discipline during the campus placement drive. Many students from branches such as mechanical engineering, chemical engineering, and civil engineering regularly receive offers from the IT and software sector, which accounted for 30.9% of total placements in 2025. The Career Development Services cell does not restrict students to branch-specific companies, allowing them to apply based on their skills and interests.
Can BTech students at IIT Gandhinagar sit for another company after securing a job in campus placements?
As per the IIT Gandhinagar placement policy, students who receive an offer during the placement drive are generally not permitted to sit for another company once they have accepted an offer. The Career Development Services cell follows a one-offer policy to ensure fair opportunities for all registered students. Students are advised to make a careful decision before accepting any offer, as withdrawing from an accepted offer is not permitted under the placement guidelines.

IIT Gandhinagar MTech Placement 2025 FAQs

Do IIT Gandhinagar MTech placements include opportunities in core engineering companies?

Organisations such as Texas Instruments, GAIL, BPCL, IOCL, JSW Steel, Larsen and Toubro, and Thermax have participated in placement drives at IIT Gandhinagar. Can IIT Gandhinagar MTech students work in the IT sector after graduation?
Yes, IIT Gandhinagar MTech students can and do secure positions in the IT and software sectors after graduation. Yes, IIT Gandhinagar MTech placements include research-orientated roles alongside industry positions. IIT Gandhinagar Placement Trend FAQs

How has the placement scenario of IIT Gandhinagar changed in the last five years?
The IIT Gandhinagar placement scenario has seen significant changes between 2021 and 2025. The average package rose from INR 11.09 LPA in 2021 to INR 19.34 LPA in 2023 before declining to INR 13.07 LPA in 2025. The placement rate peaked at 94.44% in 2023 and dropped to 60.87% in 2025. The median package followed a similar pattern, rising to INR 14.87 LPA in 2023 before settling at INR 12.24 LPA in 2025, reflecting broader shifts in industry hiring patterns.
Do IIT Gandhinagar placement trends differ between BTech and MTech programmes?
Yes, IIT Gandhinagar placement trends differ considerably between BTech and MTech programmes. In 2025, the BTech placement rate was 71.89% compared to 42.98% for MTech. The BTech average package ranged from INR 9.62 LPA to INR 23.88 LPA, while the MTech average ranged from INR 4.8 LPA to INR 16.73 LPA. BTech students consistently attract more recruiters and higher offers, particularly from the IT and software sectors, while MTech placements are more concentrated in core engineering and research-orientated roles.
What factors influence IIT Gandhinagar placement trends?
Several factors influence IIT Gandhinagar placement trends, including overall economic conditions, industry hiring cycles, the number of students registered for the drive, and the mix of branches in a given batch. The placement rate dropped from 94.44% in 2023 to 60.87% in 2025, reflecting broader changes in hiring across the IT and engineering sectors. The average package is also affected by the ratio of BTech to MTech students placed, as BTech branches consistently record higher average packages than MTech branches.

IIT Gandhinagar Top Recruiters 2025 FAQs

What types of roles do companies offer during IIT Gandhinagar placements?
Companies visiting IIT Gandhinagar during the placement drive offer a wide range of roles across disciplines. Common roles include software engineer, data analyst, product manager, research and development engineer, core engineering roles in mechanical, civil, and chemical domains, financial analyst, and management consultant. The IT and software sector, which accounted for 30.9% of placements in 2025, primarily offers software development and data-related roles, while core engineering companies offer design, operations, and project management positions.
Do startups participate in IIT Gandhinagar placements?
Yes, startups have participated in the IIT Gandhinagar placement drive alongside established companies. The Career Development Services cell invites companies from a wide range of sectors, including early-stage and growth-stage startups. Companies such as Cred have previously recruited from IIT Gandhinagar during the placement drive. The institute encourages participation from diverse organisations to give students a broader set of career options beyond traditional large-scale recruiters in engineering and technology.
How do companies participate in IIT Gandhinagar placements as recruiters?
Companies interested in recruiting from IIT Gandhinagar can register through the Career Development Services cell by contacting the placement office directly. The CDS team coordinates with companies on job descriptions, eligibility criteria, interview schedules, and campus visit logistics. Recruiters can reach out via the official IIT Gandhinagar website at iitgn.ac.in or through the placement office email. The institute welcomes participation from companies across all sectors, including IT, core engineering, finance, consulting, energy, and research organisations.

IIT Gandhinagar Placement Cell and Training Support

IIT Gandhinagar placement cell operates under the name Career Development Services (CDS). It is responsible for running all aspects of the placement drive, from registering students to inviting recruiters and conducting pre-placement training. Students register for the placement drive through the official CDS placement portal once the placement season is announced. The selection process at most companies visiting IITGN involves a written test or aptitude round, followed by a group discussion or technical interaction and a final HR interview round.

The CDS conducts regular orientation sessions, skill development workshops, resume-building workshops, mock interview sessions, and career counselling. These sessions are held across both semesters to ensure students are ready well before the placement drive begins. MTech full-time students at IIT Gandhinagar are also eligible to apply for a monthly scholarship of INR 12,400 as per the official website.

IIT Gandhinagar Placements FAQs

Are internship opportunities linked to final placements at IIT Gandhinagar?

Yes, internship opportunities at IIT Gandhinagar are directly linked to final placements in several cases. Students who perform well during their internships are often extended pre-placement offers by the same company, allowing them to secure a job before the official placement drive begins. The Career Development Services cell actively facilitates internship placements with reputed organisations across sectors. Accepting a PPO means the student does not participate in the regular placement drive, making internships a significant pathway to final placement at IIT Gandhinagar.

Do IIT Gandhinagar students receive international job offers?

Yes, IIT Gandhinagar students have received international job offers during the placement drive. Global companies such as D E Shaw, Goldman Sachs, Google, Amazon, Rakuten, and Salesforce have recruited from the institute across recent placement seasons. Some of these offers include international postings or roles with global teams. However, the institute does not separately publish the count of international offers in its publicly available placement data. Students interested in international roles are advised to check directly with the Career Development Services cell for the latest information.

Are students at IIT Gandhinagar allowed to pursue higher studies instead of campus placements?

Yes, students at IIT Gandhinagar are fully allowed to pursue higher studies instead of participating in campus placements. Many students choose to apply for postgraduate programmes, doctoral research, or competitive examinations such as GATE and CAT. In the 2025 placement drive, 54 out of 239 enrolled BTech students did not register for placements, indicating that a notable share opted for higher education or other pathways. The institute supports both placement-bound and research-bound students through dedicated academic and career guidance.

Do IIT Gandhinagar students receive pre-placement offers (PPOs)?

Yes, IIT Gandhinagar students are eligible to receive pre-placement offers based on their internship performance. Students who complete internships with companies during their penultimate year may receive a PPO from the same organisation before the official placement drive begins. PPOs are counted as confirmed placements, and students who accept a PPO do not participate in the regular placement drive. The Career Development Services cell facilitates internship opportunities that can lead to PPOs across a range of sectors.

When does the IIT Gandhinagar placement season usually begin?

The IIT Gandhinagar placement season runs in two phases. Phase 1 typically begins in December, when major recruiters, particularly from the IT and software sectors, conduct their hiring drives. Phase 2 is held between April and May for students who did not receive offers in Phase 1 or for companies that prefer to recruit later in the academic year. Students register for the placement drive through the Career Development Services portal before Phase 1 begins
