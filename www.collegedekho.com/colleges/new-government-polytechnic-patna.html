About NGP Patna

New Government Polytechnic is situated in Patna in Bihar state of India. Established in 2003, NGP is a Government college. The college is affiliated with VNSGU Surat, Gujarat University and accredited by AICTE. New Government Polytechnic offers 6 courses across 1 streams namely Engineering..Popular degrees offered at New Government Polytechnic include Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, New Government Polytechnic is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at New Government Polytechnic, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at New Government Polytechnic is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.

S
Shivani •  Jul-11-2025

The main documents required at the time of admission to New Government Polytechnic Patna are DCECE 2025 admit card, DCECE 2025 rank card, class 10 marksheet and certificate, address proof of Bihar, caste certificate as per requirement, valid ID proof, scanned passport size photograph and signature, and medical examination report. These are the major list of documents that are required during the admission process. In case the authorities request any other document, the students need to submit that document. Students should keep a few copies of these documents along with them in case more than one copy is required.
