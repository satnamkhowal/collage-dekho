NIT Warangal Placement Overview 2025

The National Institute of Technology (NIT) Warangal has established itself as a leading institution for engineering education, with a strong focus on student placements. As per the placement statistics for 2025, the highest package offered was INR 88 LPA, while the average package stood at INR 17.29 LPA and the median package at INR 12.6 LPA. The placement session saw over 1,400 students placed, achieving an 82% placement rate. Notable recruiters included TCS, Blend360, Axis Bank, Adecco, Genpact, and L&T.

Some Frequently Asked Questions about NIT Warangal:

1. What is the highest placement at NIT Warangal?

The highest placement package offered at NIT Warangal is INR 88 LPA.

2. Is NIT Warangal a tier 1 college?

Yes, NIT Warangal is a Tier 1 engineering college in India. It is one of the top National Institutes of Technology (NITs) in the country.

3. Does Microsoft visit NIT Warangal?

Yes, Microsoft is a regular recruiter at NIT Warangal, visiting the campus for placements to hire students for its various roles. 

4. How many companies visit NIT Warangal?

150 to 270 companies visit the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Warangal campus each year for recruitment, though the exact number can vary annually

5. Does NIT give 100% placement?

No, NITs do not guarantee 100% placement for all students or all branches, though some individual NITs and specific branches, like Computer Science Engineering, do report 100% placement rates.

NIT Warangal Placement Comparison 2025 vs 2023

Can I get scholarship at NIT Warangal?

D
Diksha Nautiyal •  Jun-25-2020

Dear student, 

This depends on whether or not you can meet the eligibility criteria for scholarships provided at NIT Warangal. To avail merit-based scholarships, you must have scored over 9.1 CGPA in the first semester of your programme. You JEE Main rank also determines your eligibility. The college offers scholarships to students with JEE Main rank equal to or less than 2,000. You can also get a scholarship based on your total annual family income. It must also be notified that the institute does not award more than one scholarship to one student even if the candidate is eligible for more than one scholarships.

NIT Warangal Placements FAQs

How many companies visit NIT Warangal?

Over 270 companies including top MNCs, banks, and other corporates visit NIT Warangal Placement every year. Some of the most visited NIT Warangal Placement companies are  Microsoft, BOSCH, Amazon, Accenture, Margon Stanley, etc.

What is the placement percentage at NIT Warangal?

As per the NIT Warangal Placement record 2024, 76% of students were placed. This was slightly less than 2023 when 82% NIT Warangal Placement percentage was achieved.

What is the average package in NIT Warangal for MTech?

As per the NIT Warangal placement report 2024, the average package offered for the MTech programme was Rs 26.24 LPA. This package was offered to the MTech CSE programme. For MTech Mechanical, the average package offered was Rs 10.30 LPA.

What is the minimum package in NIT Warangal?

The minimum package offered in NIT Warangal is Rs 10 LPA. Moreover, the average package offered during 2024 placement was Rs 15.6 LPA with the median package of Rs 12.5 LPA. NIT Warangal placement rate in 2024 is 76% overall.

Is NIT Warangal good for placements?

Yes, NIT Warangal is good for placements. According to NIT Warangal placement report 2024, more than 1128 offers were made from top companies like Microsoft, BOSCH, Amazon, Accenture, Margon Stanley, etc. The highest package offered was Rs 88 LPA. This shows that NIT Warangal has very good placements.

Which branch of NIT Warangal has best placements record?

The BTech CSE branch of NIT Warangal has excellent placement records. For BTech CSE, the highest package offered was Rs 88 LPA in 2024. This package was offered to Abdul Samad from BTech CSE by L&T. So BTech CSE branch at NIT Warangal is very good for placements.

What is the highest package for Btech in NIT Warangal?

The highest package offered for the BTech programme was Rs 88 LPA. This package was offered to Abdul Samad from BTech CSE by L&T. This package was the same as 2023 also when the highest package offered for BTech was Rs 88 LPA.

Who are the top recruiters of NIT Warangal?

NIT Warangal Placement companies include top MNCs, banks, and other corporates. Some of the most visited NIT Warangal Placement companies are Microsoft, BOSCH, Amazon, Accenture, Margon Stanley, etc.
