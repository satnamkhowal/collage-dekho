Jagran Lakecity University Job Ready Degree

If you are looking for courses which will train you according to the current job market then Jagran Lakecity University brings you job ready courses. This university offers four assured courses for its students alongside conventional courses. The job ready programmes will help students learn about the latest industry practices, and grow their job-relevant skills rapidly. Jagran Lakecity University assured courses are B.Tech in Data Science (IXP), B.Des in UX (IXP), M.Des in UX (IXP), B.Tech in AIML (IXP), and BCA in Data Science (IXP). Jagran Lakecity University fees for job ready courses range from Rs 85,000 - Rs1,80,000 per year. Students need to achieve 50% marks in the qualifying exam to become eligible for admission to these assured courses. The university also offers scholarships to meritorious students based on their performance in the qualifying or the entrance exam. Interested candidates can check the table below for more details on the job ready courses by Jagran Lakecity University and their fees.

Jagran Lakecity University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

For Jagran Lakecity University job ready “Assured” courses, the admission process involves candidates passing the required entrance exams and then attending the personal interview round. For example, BTech courses require candidates to qualify for Jagran Lakecity University Entrance Test (JLUET) or JEE Main. For BDes, students need to pass the NID Design Aptitude Test exam.
