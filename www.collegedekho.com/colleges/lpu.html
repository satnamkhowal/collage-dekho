About Lovely Professional University (LPU)

Lovely Professional University (LPU) is a private university located in Phagwara, Punjab, on the Jalandhar-Delhi G.T. Road. Also known as LPU University or Lovely University Punjab, it was established in 2005. LPU is a NAAC A++ accredited university and is also recognised as a Category 1 University with Graded Autonomy by the UGC. The university offers 150+ programmes across UG, PG, Doctoral, and Diploma levels in engineering, management, pharmacy, law, agriculture, design, hotel management, and more.

LPU fees range from INR 50,000 to INR 4.50 Lakhs per semester for most of the flagship courses. For LPU admission 2026, candidates must qualify the LPUNEST (Lovely Professional University National Entrance and Scholarship Test). The university also accepts scores of national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, CAT, MAT, CUET, NATA, CLAT, and GATE.

In the NIRF 2025 rankings, LPU University ranked 31st in the University category and 49th Overall. Lovely Professional University is also ranked 5th in India — among both government and private universities, including IITs and NITs by Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings 2026. In the 2024-25 placement season, 2,225+ recruiters hired LPU students with 6,000+ offers from Fortune 500 companies. The highest international package crossed INR 3 Crore per annum, and the highest domestic package stood at INR 62.72 LPA.
