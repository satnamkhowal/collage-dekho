About Raffles University

Raffles University is a private university located in Neemrana, Rajasthan. The institute was established in 2011. Raffles University has been recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is accredited by NAAC. As per NIRF Ranking 2023, Raffles University has been ranked at 67th under the ‘University’ category. Facilities such as a modern library, advanced laboratories, spacious classrooms, and vibrant extracurricular activities are some of Raffles University's facilities.
Raffles University courses are offered at undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include BBA, LLB, B.Tech, MBA, and LLM. Raffles University admission is based on merit and entrance exams. The college accepts CAT, MAT, and CLAT scores for admission. Regarding Raffles University placements, the highest package offered in 2023 was Rs 12 LPA, and the average package offered was Rs 4.5 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include Infosys, TCS, and ICICI Bank.

Here are some reasons why Raffles University stands out as a top choice for students:

  • Diverse Academic Programs: Offers 50+ programs across undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma levels in arts, science, and commerce.

  • State-of-the-Art Infrastructure: A 75-acre campus with facilities like tech-enabled classrooms, well-equipped labs, hostels, and sports amenities.
  • Strong Placement Record: Placement rate of 85%, with the highest package of ₹12 LPA per annum.
  • Global Exposure: Collaborations with institutions like Edinburgh Napier University for research and internships.
  • Holistic Development: Focus on extracurricular activities through various clubs, societies, and events.

Raffles University Admissions: 2025

Raffles University admission to its courses is done on the basis of merit and entrance exams. The college accepts CAT for MBA, CLAT for LLB, and merit in the qualifying exam for undergraduate courses. Candidates must also ensure that they meet the eligibility criteria for the respective courses before applying for Raffles University admission.

Raffles University Admission Process: 2025

  1. Eligibility Check: Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria for their chosen program.
  2. Application Form: Fill out the application form available on the official website.
  3. Entrance Exam: Appear for CAT/CLAT on the scheduled date, if applicable.
  4. Document Submission: Submit required documents like mark sheets, ID proof, and entrance exam scorecards.
  5. Counseling Process: Participate in counseling sessions as scheduled.
  6. Fee Payment: Complete the admission by paying the necessary fees.
