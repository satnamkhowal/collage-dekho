About Adesh Institute Of Technology (AIT), Mohali

Adesh Institute Of Technology is situated in Mohali in Punjab state of India. Established in 2014, is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Punjab Technical University and accredited by AICTE. Adesh Institute Of Technology offers 10 courses across 4 streams namely Engineering, IT, Commerce and Banking, Management..Popular degrees offered at Adesh Institute Of Technology include B.Tech, BCA, B.Com, BBA, M.Tech..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Adesh Institute Of Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Adesh Institute Of Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Adesh Institute Of Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
