Lingaya's Vidyapeeth

Overall A wide variety of programs with a strong emphasis on academic and extracurricular balance are available at Lingaya's Vidyapeeth. The campus has state-of-the-art labs and libraries. But occasionally the administration moves slowly, and the hostel's amenities might use some work. Placements are respectable, and many students land positions in reputable businesses.

Placement Strong placement cell at Lingaya's Vidyapeeth draws in businesses like TCS and Wipro. The typical package costs between INR 3 and 4 LPA. However, there is room for development in the infrastructure, and some of the faculty members lack enthusiasm. There are many cultural events on campus, making campus life lively.

Infrastructure With advanced classrooms and well-equipped labs, Lingaya's Vidyapeeth provides a well-rounded academic experience. Although there is a lot going on on campus, the dorms could use better upkeep. The large library can accommodate a wide range of study requirements. Students value the helpful instructors.

Faculty The faculty of Lingaya's Vidyapeeth is mixed. Some lecturers are incredibly informed and committed, providing insightful analysis and useful advice. Some faculty members, meanwhile, still need to work on their engagement and instructional strategies. The university offers a helpful atmosphere overall, however its faculty development initiatives may need some improvement.

Hostel The well-maintained hostel of Lingaya's Vidyapeeth in Faridabad features wide accommodations and standard facilities. Although it might need more variety, the mess food is decent. The dependable Wi-Fi helps with academic requirements. There aren't many leisure options, though. All in all, a wise option for engineering students looking for a healthy way of life.

How can I get admission in lingyas University

S
Saniya Pahwa •  Sep-14-2023

Dear Rashi

You can apply for admission to Lingaya's Vidyapeeth if you meet the basic eligibility criteria for your desired course. The application process can be carried out in online as well as offline modes. In order to apply online, all you have to do is visit the official website of the university and fill out the application form available in the admission section. After uploading the needed documents and paying the fee, your form will be submitted. Further, for Lingaya's Vidyapeeth admission in the offline mode, you can visit the campus and fill out the form in person and submit it. Once you have successfully applied, you must appear for the Lingaya's Vidyapeeth Entrance Test (LET). If shortlisted, you will be called for further counselling and admission rounds. 

Hope this helps!

Fashion designing is among the highest paying and popularly growing field pursued by many creative minds. LPU, or Lovely Professional University, offers a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Fashion Design program at its School of Fashion Design. The program is designed to provide students with a comprehensive education in the field of fashion design, including design principles, textile science, garment construction, and business aspects of the industry. The program is delivered through a combination of classroom instruction, studio work, and practical experience in the form of internships and industry projects. Students will also have the opportunity to participate in fashion shows, design competitions, and other events to showcase their work and gain exposure to the fashion industry.The curriculum of the program is regularly updated to keep pace with the changing trends and technologies in the fashion industry, and the faculty members are experienced professionals who bring industry knowledge and expertise to the classroom. Overall, pursuing fashion design at LPU can be a great choice for those who are passionate about the field and want to acquire the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in the industry.
