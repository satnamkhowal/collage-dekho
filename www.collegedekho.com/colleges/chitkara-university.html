About Chitkara University

Chitkara University Punjab, also known as Chitkara University, established in 2010 in Punjab. The university is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited with A+ grade by NAAC. It's professional programmes are also approved by AICTE, PCI, NCTE, INC, COA and NCHMCT. It is a private university that offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes in various fields including engineering, management, pharmacy, design, health sciences, and more. The university offers BE, MBA, BPharm, BDes, BArch, MPharm, PhD and several other programmes in specializations such as Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Pharmaceutical Management, and other related fields. The total fee is Rs 7.3-19 Lakhs for BE, Rs 3.3-9 Lakhs for MBA, Rs 4.2 Lakhs for PhD and Rs 4-12 Lakhs for other UG/PG programmes.

For Chitkara University BE admission, candidates must pass 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics securing at least 60% aggregate marks (70% without JEE Main) and qualify JEE Main, while admission to the MBA programme requires a valid CAT/MAT/XAT/NMAT/CMAT score along with a relevant bachelor's degree with 50% marks. The PhD admissions accept candidates with GATE, UGC-NET, or GPAT qualifications, followed by a written test and interview. The admission process is conducted online through the university's portal for all programs, followed by merit list preparation, personal interview/group discussion for selected courses, seat allotment, fee payment, and document verification. The Chitkara University placements in 2025 shows the highest package offered was Rs 1.1 Crore, while the average package of Rs 10 LPA.

Chitkara University FAQs

What are the career opportunities after B.Sc Nursing (Basic)?

After completing B.Sc Nursing (Basic), graduates can work as registered nurses, ICU nurses, paediatric nurses, nurse educators, or nursing officers in hospitals and healthcare centres. The degree also allows them to pursue M.Sc Nursing or specialised diploma programmes for career advancement.

Is work experience mandatory for MBA admission at Chitkara University?

Work experience is not mandatory for MBA admission at Chitkara University. However, some specialised MBA programmes may prefer candidates with relevant work experience. Fresh graduates with strong academic records and valid entrance exam scores are also eligible.

Are internship opportunities available for all engineering branches at Chitkara University?

Yes, Chitkara University offers internship opportunities across all engineering branches, including CSE, Civil, Mechanical, ECE, Mechatronics, etc. Each branch receives dedicated internship support aligned with industry requirements.

Can engineering students pursue an MBA after engineering to move into management roles?

Yes, many engineering graduates pursue an MBA after engineering in fields such as Technology Management, Business Analytics, or General Management to move into leadership and management roles across industries.

What is the campus size of Chitkara University in acres?

Chitkara University campus covers 70 acres and includes modern academic buildings, laboratories, and library facilities. The campus also offers hostels, sports infrastructure, and essential student amenities.

Can students apply for an education loan to pay tuition fees at Chitkara University?

Yes, students can apply for an education loan to pay tuition fees at Chitkara University. The loan application must be initiated by the student, and the university provides all required documents and certificates to support the education loan process with banks or financial institutions.

Does Chitkara University provide fee waivers or financial assistance for economically weaker students?

Chitkara University does not currently offer specific fee waivers for economically weaker students. However, the university follows a transparent fee structure to ensure access to quality education at a reasonable cost.

Do global or international engineering programmes at Chitkara University have a separate fee structure?

Yes, global engineering programmes at Chitkara University, offered in collaboration with Deakin University and Arizona State University, have a separate fee structure. The fee covers international credits and academic transfer costs.

Chitkara University Campus Life & Facilities

Chitkara University offers a modern and student-friendly campus designed for academic learning, comfortable living, and daily student needs. The campus provides everything needed for a comfortable and productive student life:

Academic & Learning Environment

  • The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes in Engineering, Pharmacy, Management, Hospitality, Design, Health Sciences and other disciplines.
  • Classes are conducted in spacious amphitheatre-style rooms equipped with modern audio-visual teaching systems.
  • Laboratories are well-equipped with tools such as MATLAB, microprocessor kits, VLSI/CAD tools, embedded systems, and CAD–CAM workshops to support practical learning.
  • High-performance computer labs and cloud platforms are available round the clock, supported by campus-wide broadband and Wi-Fi connectivity.
  • The libraries provide reading rooms, discussion areas, and a large collection of over 57,800 books and journals, along with access to e-books and e-journals.

Campus Infrastructure & Amenities

  • A centrally located auditorium with seating for over 1,500 people is used for academic programmes and cultural events.
  • The campus has food courts and cafeterias that serve meals and snacks throughout the day.
  • Branches of PNB and UCO Bank, along with ATM facilities, are available within the campus.
  • High-speed internet and wireless connectivity are available across academic and common areas.

Accommodation & Campus Life

  • Separate hostels are provided for male and female students, supervised by wardens and secured round the clock.
  • Hostel facilities include Wi-Fi, telephone access, common rooms with television, and indoor games.
  • Each hostel has its own dining hall that serves balanced meals.
  • Recreational lounges and regular activities help students interact and build friendships.
  • The campus has a diverse student community from different parts of India and abroad.

Health, Safety & Student Support

  • The university has tie-ups with hospitals for medical emergencies, and students are covered under a medical insurance plan.
  • Free and confidential counselling services are available for academic and personal support.
  • The campus maintains security through gated entry, CCTV monitoring, and 24-hour security staff.

Clubs, Events & Extracurricular Activities

  • Student clubs such as EBUZZ, MATRIX, SEAGULLS, BIT AND BYTES, MECHSTEIN, ELECRAMA and C2S2 organise workshops, competitions and other activities.
  • Annual events include Pratibimbh, Praudyogikii, Algorythm, Techelone, Gameeks, C-Champ, the T20 Cup and the University Sports Meet.
  • The campus also has a radio station and innovation facilities such as the Jio Games Innovation Centre to support learning in gaming, AI and technology.

Sports & Recreation

  • Outdoor facilities include an athletics track and grounds for cricket, football, hockey, baseball and handball, along with courts for tennis, basketball, volleyball, kabaddi and kho-kho.
  • Indoor facilities include badminton, table tennis, chess, carrom, boxing, gymnasiums and fitness centres. A swimming pool and multigym facilities are also available.
  • An annual Sports Week is organised with competitions in volleyball, basketball, pickleball, powerlifting, badminton and other games.

Transport & Location

  • The university operates bus services connecting the campus with Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, Zirakpur, Derabassi, Patiala, Ambala and Ludhiana.
  • The campus is located on the Chandigarh–Patiala National Highway (NH-64) near Rajpura, around 30 km from Chandigarh, ensuring convenient road access.
