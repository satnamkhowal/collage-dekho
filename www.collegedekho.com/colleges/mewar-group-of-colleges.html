About Mewar University

Mewar University is a private university established in the year 2009. The university is promoted under the aegis of the Mewar Education Society (MES). The Mewar University is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC), the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), the All India Council of Technical Education (AICTE), the Bar Council of India (BCI), and the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). It is also accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a B grade. This university is also a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) and the Indian Association of Physiotherapists (IAP). Mewar University is also RNC approved.  Some popular Mewar University courses are B.Tech, B.Tech, BBA, BCA, MBA, M.Tech, and MCA  All these courses are offered under 11 distinctive academic faculties at the university. For Mewar University admission the university accepts various entrance exams such as CUET, JEE Main, GATE, CLAT, CAT, etc.

The Mewar University campus is spread across 30 acres and is located in Chittorgarh, Rajasthan. The campus has all the required facilities such as separate hostels for boys and girls, an auditorium, a central library, a hospital with 200 beds, guest houses, experienced faculty, and many others. The teachers at this university take Interactive Lectures and teach case studies with the help of Audio Visual Aids. Mewar University has workshops and labs for practical exposure of students. The university organises Quiz & Competitions for its students. Audio-video lectures from world-renowned academicians, professors & scientists are arranged for students of this university. Students studying at Mewar University get the opportunity for self-study with the help of study groups and online discussions. In addition, students get on-the-job exposure & projects in live scenarios to deepen their knowledge about their field. The university offers excellent placement opportunities to its students via the Students & Corporate Counselling. Before the campus placements start the university runs personality development classes for students. During Mewar University placements 2023, the average package recorded was Rs 4.20 lakh per annum.

How to do Mewar University mark sheet verification?

For Mewar University mark sheet verification students need to send all semester mark sheets to ascoe@mewaruniversity.org and call 180070307373(4) for any inquiry. Students can also use the Right to Information (RTI) form to verify the mark sheet provided by this university.

Is Mewar University NAAC accredited?

Yes, Mewar University is a National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) accredited university with a B grade. The NAAC accreditation was granted in 2018.

How are the facilities in Mewar University?

The Mewar University facilities are excellent. The facility list of this university includes a moot court, a library, hostel buildings, a canteen, a hospital, banking facilities, a music room, a business lab, and more. What is the rank of Mewar University?

Mewar University ranking in the India Today ranking 2023 was 215th out of 246 in India for the B.Tech course, 61st out of 70 in India for law programmes, and 150th out of 170 in India for the BCA course. The IIRF rankings 2023 ranked Mewar University 47th out of 171 in India in overall category.

Why should I choose Mewar University?

Mewar University is a good choice for students because the university has all the required infrastructure to provide proper education to the students. The university also has a dedicated placement cell. In 2023, the average package of this university was Rs 4,20,000 per annum.

What campus facilities does Mewar University offer?

Can I apply to Mewar University through LSAT India?

Yes, Mewar University offers admission to its law programmes through LSAT India. Generally, LSAT India is conducted twice a year - January and May. The exam assesses the critical thinking abilities of a student through three types of multiple-choice questions. The duration of the LSAT India exam is 2 hours and 30 minutes. There are four sections on the exam - analytical reasoning, logical reasoning (1), logical reasoning (2), and reading comprehension. The test is generally proctored remotely using AI-assisted technology.

What scholarships are available at Mewar University?

The Mewar University scholarship list includes a wide range of scholarships, including various merit scholarships and special scholarships. Students who have passed Class 12 with 90% are exempted from paying 75% of their tuition fee. The university also offers scholarships based on CUET. For example, students who have scored 95.1% and above in CUET are eligible for a 100% scholarship in academic fees. There are also special Mewar University scholarships available for students. General students from economically weaker sections having income less than Rs 1 lakh per annum with 75% or more marks are eligible for a 50% scholarship in tuition fees.

Does Mewar University conduct any entrance examinations?

No, Mewar University does not conduct any entrance examination at the university level. The university is participating university in the CUET exam eco-system. What that means is that the university accepts CUET UG and CUET PG scores for the Mewar University selection process. Students can get admission to courses such as B.Tech, BBA, B.Com, B.Sc, BA, MBA, M.Tech, M.Com, and many others based on the CUET scores. On the other hand, the university also accepts other national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, GATE, CAT, MAT, LSAT, and others.

How can I apply to Mewar University Chittorgarh?

Candidates must fill out the Mewar University application form with all required information and send it to the university via post with all mentioned documents attached. They can also fill out the online application form and complete the online Mewar University application process to skip the hassle of the offline application submission process.

Does Mewar University accept CUET scores?

Yes, the university accepts both CUET UG and CUET PG scores for Mewar University UG and PG admissions, respectively. The university conducts separate CUET UG and CUET PG counselling processes for candidates based on their marks obtained in the CUET UG or CUET PG exams. Students can apply for courses such as BBA, BCA, BA, and B.Sc, among others through CUET UG. On the other hand, CUET PG scores are used for admission to courses such as M.Tech, MA, M.Com, MBA, and many others.
