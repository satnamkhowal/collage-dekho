About PIC Jaipur

Poddar International College admission 2023 is open for all the courses. PIC Jaipur courses are offered at undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and certificate levels in the discipline of arts, commerce, science, IT,  management, design and paramedical. Students can fill out the PIC Jaipur application form through the official admission portal of the institute. They will be required to fulfil the Poddar International College admission criteria for the course they wish to apply for. The selection and eligibility criteria for admission vary according to the course. The institute provides direct admission to the majority of the courses. However, PIC Jaipur PGDM admission is based on the candidate’s performance in the last qualifying examination and any management entrance test. The institute also conducts Poddar Common Aptitude Test (PCAT) for admission to the PGDM programme in case the candidate does not hold any valid scorecard of any management test. 


Poddar International College (PIC Jaipur) is one of the premier institutions of the Poddar Group of Institutions Jaipur and it is affiliated with the University of Rajasthan. It is accredited by National Accreditation and Assessment Council (NAAC). PIC Jaipur awards list includes the ‘Best Upcoming Higher Education Institute of Rajasthan’ award by ASSOCHAM, Ministry of Human Resource Development, New Delhi and the ‘Best Private College in Rajasthan Award 2019’ by ICCI, New Delhi. Poddar International College ranked among the top positions in Rajasthan by the India Today Nielsen survey for its excellence in varied fields. 

PIC Jaipur not only offers the best learning environment but also provides practical education to the students along with good internship and placement opportunities. The highest package offered at Poddar International College placement was Rs 14 LPA. Some of the top recruiters at PIC Jaipur placements are Aditya Birla Capital, Infosys, Amazon, Indigo and Zycus. The institute also offers merit-based scholarships. Students with Class 12 marks from 60% to 100% get benefitted from the Poddar International College scholarship scheme. Up to 20% scholarship is granted to the students who qualify for it. Also, BPL card holders, state-level sports achievers, siblings of past or existing students and current students taking admission to PG courses are also eligible for PIC Jaipur scholarships.

PIC Jaipur Courses and Admission 2026

PIC Jaipur admission 2023 is open for all the courses. Interested candidates can apply through the official admission portal of PIC Jaipur. PGDM and MBA are the two most popular programmes offered for PIC Jaipur PG admission 2023. Selection is based on the student’s performance in qualifying and any management entrance examination. However, students not having a valid scorecard of any national or state-level management entrance test can appear for Poddar Combined Aptitude Test (PCAT) for PIC Jaipur PGDM admission 2023. 

Students can also apply for various PG courses such as MCA, MA, M.Com, M.Sc and M.Voc. PIC Jaipur selection process for these courses is based on merit. Poddar International College UG admissions are offered to courses such as BBA, BA, B.Sc, B.Com, BCA, and B.Voc. Admission to these UG and PG courses is based on a first come first serve basis and merit. Poddar International College form fees are Rs 1,000 for PGDM and MCA and Rs 700 for all other courses. 

Go through the online application process given below for PIC Jaipur admission 2023. 

Poddar International College Application Process

Step 1: Visit the official website or admission portal of PIC Jaipur

Step 2: Click on the ‘Admission 2023’ link. 

Step 3: Fill out the PIC Jaipur application form with the required details

Step 4: Upload the required documents and pay the application fee. 

Step 5: Submit the application.
