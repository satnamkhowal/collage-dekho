Application Process

  1. Registration: Visit saarthi.dtu.ac.in or jacdelhi.admissions.nic.in (for B.Tech). Create an account with email/phone.
  2. Fill Form: Enter academic details, upload documents (10th/12th marksheets, entrance scorecard, photo, signature, category certificate).
  3. Pay Fee: ₹1,000–₹1,500 (online via net banking/credit/debit card). Non-refundable.
  4. Submit: Download confirmation. Apply for up to 2 programs.
  5. Counseling/Selection: Shortlisting based on entrance scores; followed by online counseling (JAC for B.Tech, DTU portal for others). Document verification and fee payment confirm admission.

DTU Admission FAQs

Does DTU offer direct admission?

DTU does not offer direct admission for all courses. Admission to most programs at DTU is based on merit, determined through entrance exams like JEE Main, GATE, CAT, etc., followed by a counseling process. However, for programmes like BA, you can get direct admission based on merit in the qualifying exam.

Is it good to take admission in DTU?

Yes, Delhi Technological University is a good university to take admission to. The university offers 8 courses at UG, PG, and PhD levels. Since the university takes admission after evaluating the performance of candidates in the entrance exam, it shows the authenticity of education at the university. Hence, you can consider taking admission to the university.

What is the last to apply for admission at DTU?

The last to apply for admission at DTU depends on the respective entrance exams. However, usually the admission is conducted until the last week of August. But that also depends on the date of the respective exam counselling process.

Does DTU conduct its own entrance exam for admission?

No, DTU does not conduct its own entrance exam for regular course admission. However, for BTech lateral entry admission, the university conducts the DTU LEET entrance exam. However, for other programmes, admission is based on central level entrance exams like JEE Main for BTech, UCEED for BDes, GATE for MTech, CAT/ MAT for MBA, etc.

Does DTU accept JEE Main for admission?

Yes, Delhi Technological University accepts JEE Main for admission to BTech programmes. Candidates need to appear for the exam and get a valid score in order to be eligible for admission.

Does DTU accept CAT for admission?

Yes, Delhi Technological University accepts CAT for admission to MBA programmes. Candidates need to appear for the exam and get a valid score in order to be eligible for admission.

Can I get a scholarship at DTU at the time of admission?

DTU offers various scholarships based on merit, financial need, and other criteria. Some scholarships are awarded during the admission process itself, while others require fulfilling certain academic conditions in the first year.

Can I join DTU after the 10th?

Sorry but DTU does not offer diploma programmes where you can get admission directly after the 10th. At the lowest level, the university offers UG level programmes. For the UG courses, candidates need to pass the 12th exam as the minimum DTU admission eligibility criteria.

What is the application fee for the DTU admission?

For DTU, the application form can be filled out either in online or offline mode. For online application form, the application fee is Rs 1500 and for offline application form, the application fee is Rs 1500 as well. DTU application fee can be paid in online mode as well.

Which Branch is best to get admission at DTU?

Both engineering and management branches are best to get admission to DTU. One of the students from BTech CSE got a package of Rs 64.23 LPA in placements last year. Also, many students got packages above 10 LPA from the engineering and management branches. Hence, both of these are good.

What percentage is required to get admission to DTU?

The percentage required to get admission to DTU depends on the course you wish to pursue. For example for BTech, you need 50% aggregate in your 12th while for BBA, you need a 45% aggregate minimum. However, you should aim for 45% to 55% aggregate to be eligible for DTU admission.

Is it compulsory to live in the hostel at DTU or book a hostel at the time of admission?

No, it is not compulsory to live in a hostel at DTU. You have the option to stay in a hostel or find your own accommodation outside the campus. However, booking a hostel at the time of admission is highly recommended, especially if you are new to Delhi. Hostel accommodation can be limited, and getting a room might be challenging later on.
