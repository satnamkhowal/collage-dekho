About Adhunik Group of Institutions (ACE), Ghaziabad

Adhunik Group of Institutions is located in Ghaziabad in the state of Uttar Pradesh. It was established in the year 2005. AGI is a private college that is accredited by AICTE. Adhunik Group of Institutions offers a d diverse range of courses across multiple streams. For example IT, B.Tech, B.Sc, BCA, BPT, MPT, BBA, MBA, and much more. 

At Adhunik Group of Institutions, the focus is given to co-curricular activities since it has a widespread infrastructure for numerous extra-circular activities. Adhunik Group of Institutions also has a lot of international and national collaborations. Moreover, placement at Adhunik Group of Institutions is diversified from course to course. The AGI group consists of 70+ teachers, 17+ courses, 50+ awards, and 17000+ students.

Based on my personal experience, I can confidently say that LPU is dedicated to providing the best possible placements for its students. The placement process consists of multiple phases, and students must successfully qualify each one. The faculty at LPU is also commendable. They are highly supportive and their level of support depends on how well students integrate with them. In my own experience at LPU, we had some initial issues with a faculty member during our second year. However, by the end of my graduation, that faculty member had become our best mentor and supporter. It is worth noting that LPU provides opportunities to both experienced and inexperienced faculty members. If a faculty member does not perform well, they are asked to leave LPU.
