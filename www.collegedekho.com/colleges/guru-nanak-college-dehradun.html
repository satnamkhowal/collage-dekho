About Guru Nanak College, Dehradun

Guru Nanak College, Dehradun (GNC Dehradun) is a well-established private institution founded in 2002, known for its strong academic focus on pharmacy, paramedical sciences, nursing, healthcare, management, and applied sciences. Affiliated with reputed universities such as HNB Garhwal Central University, HNB Medical Education University, SDSUU, UTU, and UBTER, the college operates under approvals from major regulatory bodies including UGC, AICTE, PCI, Paramedical Council of India, and the Indian Nursing Council, ensuring quality and compliance across programs.

Over the years, GNC Dehradun has emerged as one of the most respected pharmaceutical and paramedical colleges in Dehradun, supported by modern laboratories, industry-aligned curricula, and experienced faculty. The college emphasizes practical learning, research exposure, ethical values, and career readiness. Its collaboration with Career Buddy Club (CBC) India and Dubai enhances career mentoring and placement support, while partnerships like India Accelerator encourage innovation and entrepreneurship among students.

The campus offers a holistic learning environment with smart classrooms, digital resources, fully furnished hostels, sports facilities, student clubs, and incubation support. With consistent placement outcomes, affordable fee structures, and a student-centric admission process, Guru Nanak College, Dehradun continues to prepare skilled professionals ready to meet industry and societal demands.
