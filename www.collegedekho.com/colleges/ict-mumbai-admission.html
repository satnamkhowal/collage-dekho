ICT Mumbai Admission Process for BTech

Admission process for BTech at ICT Mumbai involves registering on Samarth portal, submitting JEE Main/MHT CET scores, and participating in CAP counseling by DTE Maharashtra:

  • Eligibility: 50% HSC with Physics, Chemistry, Math; no age limit.
  • Selection: 70% seats JEE Main merit, 30% MHT CET.
  • Documents: SSC/HSC marksheets, entrance scorecard, domicile for Maharashtra quota.

ICT Mumbai M.Pharm Admission 2026

M.Pharm admission at ICT Mumbai 2026 requires GPAT qualification, B.Pharm with 60%, and online application from March, followed by merit-based selection in June:

  • Intake: 18 seats per specialization like Pharmaceutics.

  • Counseling: Institute-level for vacant seats post-CAP.
