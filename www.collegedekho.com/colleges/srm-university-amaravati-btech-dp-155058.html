SRM University Andhra Pradesh BTech

SRM University Andhra Pradesh BTech program equips students with skills in core engineering fields through a 4-year full-time curriculum focused on practical projects and industry tools. Admission requires clearing SRMJEEE with at least 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics from 10+2, while yearly fees range from INR 2,50,000 for branches like Civil Engineering to INR 3,60,000 for Computer Science Engineering. Specialisations such as Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in CSE open doors to high-demand roles, supported by 100% placement records where graduates secure offers from firms like Oracle and IBM at up to INR 46.37 LPA. This setup ensures hands-on learning from semester one, blending theory with labs to build problem-solving for real-world tech challenges.

SRM University Andhra Pradesh BTech Eligibility Criteria

  • Eligibility: Class 12 with 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (or Computer Science instead of Chemistry).
  • Relaxation: 55% for SC/ST candidates.

Note: Mathematics is compulsory at the higher level; NRI/OCI applicants must submit equivalent qualifications.

SRM University Andhra Pradesh BTech Admission Process 2026

  • Register online at srmap.edu.in with personal and academic details, paying INR 1,400 fee.
  • Appear for SRMJEEE, a 2.5-hour online test with 125 MCQs on Physics (35), Chemistry (35), Math (40), and English/Aptitude (15).
  • Receive a rank based on score; top ranks secure preferred branches like CSE.
  • Attend counselling sessions for seat allotment, where SRMJEEE rank determines cutoff—expected closing ranks for CSE around 8,000-9,000.
  • Verify documents and pay fees to confirm seat; AP EAMCET scores accepted as an alternative for state quota. This merit-driven process ensures fair access, with over 4200 offers from past cycles guiding selections.

How to Apply for SRM University Andhra Pradesh BTech Admission 2026

  • Visit the official website srmap.edu.in and open the UG BTech application section.
  • Create an account using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  • Fill in personal details such as name, DOB, address, and contact info accurately.
  • Enter Class 10 and Class 12 marks, ensuring PCM subjects are selected correctly.
  • Upload scanned copies of required documents (mark sheets, photo, signature).
  • Choose up to five BTech specialisations, prioritising options like CSE, AI/ML if preferred.
  • Pay the ₹1,400 non-refundable application fee via card, UPI, or net banking.
  • Book your SRMJEEE exam slot, noting the remote-proctored format.
  • Submit the form and download the application confirmation for reference.
  • Track application status through your login dashboard.
  • If shortlisted, attend counselling online or on campus with original documents.
  • Confirm admission by paying the first-semester fee within the deadline.

SRM University Andhra Pradesh BTech Syllabus 2026

The SRM University Andhra Pradesh BTech syllabus integrates theory, labs, and projects across eight semesters, emphasising application in real scenarios.

  • Semester 1-2: Basics like Engineering Mathematics, Physics for Engineers, Programming in C, and Workshop Practice to build computational thinking.
  • Semester 3-4: Branch-specific cores—Data Structures for CSE, Circuit Theory for ECE, Thermodynamics for Mechanical—plus Environmental Science.
  • Semester 5-6: Advanced topics such as Operating Systems, Digital Signal Processing, Fluid Mechanics, with electives in AI or VLSI.
  • Semester 7-8: Capstone projects, internships, and Professional Ethics, culminating in industry-ready skills like cloud computing or sustainable design.
