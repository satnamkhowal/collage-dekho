About G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology BSc

G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology BSc is a 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for the BSc degree program at G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology in Chennai, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education with a minimum aggregate of 50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Additionally, candidates must have a valid score in entrance exams such as JEE Main or any other relevant entrance exam approved by the institute. Candidates with a diploma in engineering or equivalent are also eligible to apply. It is important for candidates to meet the eligibility criteria set by the institute to be considered for admission to the BSc program in Marine Sciences and Technology. The annual fee for BSc is INR 35,000 - 2,50,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for G.K.M. G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology BSc Application Process

G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology BSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BSc course at G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology BSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology BSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology BSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of G.K.M. Institute of Marine Sciences and Technology BSc Application Form.
