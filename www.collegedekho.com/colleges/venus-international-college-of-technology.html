About VICT Gandhinagar

Venus International College of Technology is situated in Gandhinagar in Gujarat state of India. Established in 2009, VICT is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Gujarat Technological University and accredited by AICTE. Venus International College of Technology offers 11 courses across 1 streams namely Engineering..Popular degrees offered at Venus International College of Technology include B.Tech, M.Tech..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Venus International College of Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Venus International College of Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Venus International College of Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
