About Vel Tech University

Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology, popularly known as Vel Tech University, is a prominent private, Deemed-to-be University that was established in 1997. It holds official UGC & AICTE approval and is accredited by NAAC and NBA. Emphasizing its engineering focus, the institution secured the 87th position in the NIRF Engineering Category in 2025 with NIRF score of 47.43 and is recognized as the first Indian member of the CDIO Initiative. The university campus is expansive, spread across 100 acres, offering a comprehensive learning environment.

Vel Tech offers a wide range of academic programs, including UG (B.Tech, BBA, B.Com, Integrated Law) & PG (M.Tech, MBA, M.Sc) degrees. Admission eligibility for major programs like B.Tech typically requires completing the 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects and a minimum aggregate of 50%. The university accepts scores from multiple national entrance examinations, including JEE Mains, CLAT, CAT, and others, alongside its institutional test, the VTUEEE.

Course fee structures vary, with B.Tech. annual fees ranging from ₹2,90,000 to ₹3,60,000. Significantly, Vel Tech offers substantial tuition fee scholarships, including a 100% award for the MPC (Mathematics, Physics & Chemistry) topper from each State/UT. Other tiers offer up to 75% coverage based on high MPC aggregate scores or top VTUEEE ranks, requiring a minimum 60% MPC eligibility. Placements are excellent, with the 2025 recruitment drive reaching a top salary of ₹50 LPA and an average package of ₹10 LPA. These figures highlight the university's strong commitment to quality education and high career outcomes.

Vel Tech University Admission Process 2026

Vel tech university admission process for 2026 requires online registration, fee payment, and VTUEEE/National Exam scores. For the next step in the Vel tech university admission journey, selection involves Micro Presentation/GD, Personal Interview, and a profile evaluation, after these processes selected candidate will get offer letter.

  • Online Registration: The candidate must first register on the official Vel Tech University admission portal.
  • Form Submission: Complete the detailed online application form, ensuring all academic, personal, and contact information is accurate.
  • Application Fee Payment: Pay the non-refundable application processing fee to finalize the submission.
  • Entrance Exam Score Submission (VTUEEE / National Exam): Applicants must submit valid scores from recognized national entrance examinations (e.g., JEE) or appear for the VTUEEE (Vel Tech Undergraduate Engineering Entrance Examination), depending on the program.
  • Micro Presentation / Group Discussion (GD): Shortlisted candidates may be required to participate in a Micro Presentation or Group Discussion to assess communication and analytical skills.
  • Personal Interview (PI): Candidates proceed to a mandatory Personal Interview (PI) round, where their motivation, subject knowledge, and suitability for the program are evaluated.
  • Profile Evaluation: A comprehensive evaluation of the candidate's complete profile, including academic records, entrance exam scores, and performance in the GD/PI rounds, is conducted.
  • Receive Offer Letter: Candidates who successfully clear all rounds are issued an official Offer Letter for admission.
  • Pay Confirmation Fee: The admitted candidate must pay the confirmation fee within the given deadline to secure their seat in the program.
  • Document Verification: The final step involves the physical verification of all necessary original documents (academic mark sheets, transfer certificates, identity proofs, etc.) to confirm eligibility.

Vel Tech University FAQs

Does Vel Tech University offer scholarships?

Ans Yes. It's true that Vel Tech University offers a range of scholarships to students enrolled in graduate and undergraduate programmes.

How is the infrastructure at Vel Tech University?

Ans Vel Tech University has more than 22 lakh square metres of built-up space spread throughout its 100-acre campus, which is home to state-of-the-art infrastructure. This offers plenty of room for educational structures, research centres, hostels, and leisure areas. The university has state-of-the-art labs for many academic fields, such as computer science, science, and engineering. Modern hardware and software are frequently installed in these labs to give students practical learning opportunities. It features multiple libraries that include substantial holdings of books, periodicals, and electronic resources from a range of academic fields. These libraries offer a calm and cosy setting for research and study.

Is Vel Tech University a government university?

Ans No, Vel Tech University is not a government university. It is also known as Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology. It is a private considered university, meaning that under Section 3 of the University Grants Commission (UGC) Act, 1956, the Indian government has bestowed upon it the status of a university. This means that it is not sponsored by the government and has more autonomy in choosing its own curriculum and fees. It also grants it some of the same advantages as a government university, such as the power to offer degrees.

Ans Numerous undergraduate and postgraduate programmes are offered at Vel Tech University, with a focus on engineering, management, media technology, and law. The university is well-equipped with libraries, nice dorms, and well-equipped labs. This may support easy access to resources and a supportive learning atmosphere.

Vel Tech University B.Tech. Scholarship

Vel Tech B.Tech. Scholarships offer up to 100% tuition coverage, rewarding high scores in MPC(Mathematics, Physics & Chemistry) having minimum 60% or top VTUEEE ranks. The university applies a "Best Score Policy" to ensure students receive the highest possible scholarship percentage.

Scholarship Eligibility and Award Details

  • Minimum Eligibility: A minimum aggregate of 60% in MPC is required to be eligible for availing any of the above scholarships.
  • Best Score Policy: Candidates can avail the scholarship calculated based on the MPC Aggregate or the VTUEEE Ranking, whichever results in the higher percentage of scholarship.
  • Historical Impact: Since 2009, Vel Tech has awarded over 9500 scholarships worth approximately Rs. 35 Crore.
