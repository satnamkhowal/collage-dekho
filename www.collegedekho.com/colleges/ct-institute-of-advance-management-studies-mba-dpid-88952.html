About CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA

CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA is a 2 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue an M.B.A. (Master of Business Administration) degree at CT Institute of Advanced Management Studies in Jalandhar, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their undergraduate degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum aggregate score of 50%. Additionally, they must have a valid score in any of the national level management entrance exams such as CAT, MAT, XAT, or CMAT. Candidates with work experience will be given preference during the selection process. The final selection is based on the candidate's performance in the entrance exam, group discussion, personal interview, and academic record. The annual fee for MBA is INR 35,800 - 89,630. CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA Admission 2026

Admission to the MBA at CT Institute of Advance Management Studies is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA Application Process

CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MBA course at CT Institute of Advance Management Studies have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of CT Institute of Advance Management Studies and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of CT Institute of Advance Management Studies. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA Application Form.

Documents Required for CT Institute of Advance Management Studies MBA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
