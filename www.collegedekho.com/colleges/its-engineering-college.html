About ITS Engineering College, Greater Noida

ITS Greater Noida is one of the 10 best private engineering colleges in North India, the college is affiliated with Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU). It is also NAAC and NBA accredited, and AICTE approved.

ITS Engineering College (ITSEC) was established in its first campus in Mohan Nagar, Ghaziabad, in 1995 and the Greater Noida campus in 2006. Comprising private engineering and management schools this college is a part of the ITS Education Group and was founded by Dr Bhure Lal (IAS Retd.), Chairman of the Board of Governors. The college’smission is to promote technical proficiency by using new and effective learning techniques. The great educational heritage spans 29 years and is carried on.

The ITS Engineering College courses are offered at both UG and PG levels. Its Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) programme offers specialisations in Mechanical, Civil, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Electronics and Communication and Electrical and Electronics. The institute has 2000 alumni who make up a strong and resourceful network.

ITSEC Greater Noida admissions are based on the candidate's previous academic merit and the marks obtained in the respective entranced exams. The MBA selection will be based on CAT/MAT scores and for B.Tech, JEE Main scores are necessary. The college works with the vision of providing students with value-based education while focusing on academic excellence.

The college collaborates closely with the dedicated training and development team, which prepares students for ITS Engineering College Greater Noida placement from the first year onwards by providing job-oriented personality development training and aptitude and soft skills. Students have received job offers from 350+ recruiters from various industries in the placements on campus. The ITS Engineering College Highest placement package offered has been INR 49 LPA.

The ITS Engineering College faculty includes eminent academicians and renowned professionals, and the college has a robust corporate network that assists students in finding relevant opportunities. The college also strives to provide healthy learning environments and opportunities for holistic development to its students. The institute has always worked to become the greatest engineering college in AKTU and the top engineering college in Greater Noida. The ITSEC Greater Noida campus life promotes learning outside of the classroom. The learning environment is conducive to student development and complements the current educational scenario. It caters to all of the needs of students that are essential for their study, growth, and comfort, including a library, auditorium, cafeteria, sports facilities, hostel facilities, and transportation.

ITS Greater Noida Placements 2025

ITS Greater Noida Scholarships

The scholarships offered by the institute are both merit and means based. They ensure every student gets the proper opportunity to pursue higher students in their desired field. Hence to provide some financial assistance to deserving students, ITS Greater Noida came up with several scholarship schemes for students to avail of. There are three types of ITS Engineering College scholarships available for the candidates, these are:

  • B.Tech and MBA: These ITSEC scholarships are based on a student's previous academic record and semester-by-semester performance. 
  • Special Scholarship: Scholarships are also available for dependents of esteemed Armed/Ex-Armed forces personnel at ITS Engineering College. Scholarships are also available for students who excel in sports and the arts.
  • Defence Scholarship: This ITS Engineering College scholarship depends upon the Serving personnel/veterans/ retired personnel and the previous year of the result.
