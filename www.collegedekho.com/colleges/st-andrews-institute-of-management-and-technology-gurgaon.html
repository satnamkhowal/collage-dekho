About St Andrews Institute of Technology and Management

St. Andrews Institute of Technology and Management (SAITM), founded in 2014, is a leading private institute offering excellence in technical and professional education., located in Gurgaon, Haryana, within the Delhi NCR region. Affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU), Rohtak, and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), the institute is committed to delivering quality education across engineering, management, pharmacy, architecture, and computer applications.

Spread across a sprawling 22-acre green campus, SAITM offers a modern and student-friendly learning environment. The campus is equipped with advanced laboratories, a well-stocked library, seminar halls, conference rooms, sports facilities, and separate hostels for boys and girls. Centrally air-conditioned classrooms with smart teaching aids enhance the overall academic experience.

SAITM boasts a dedicated team of experienced faculty members and industry professionals who focus on academic excellence, practical exposure, and mentorship. The institute places strong emphasis on industry interaction, skill development, and hands-on training. Through an active placement cell, students benefit from regular training programs, internships, and campus recruitment drives with reputed organizations.

Overall, SAITM provides a balanced ecosystem that supports academic growth, professional readiness, and holistic student development, attracting learners from across India.

St Andrews Institute of Technology and Management Gurgaon Admission Process 2026

The St Andrews Institute of Technology and Management Admission process involves submitting an application, shortlisting, attending counselling with required documents, confirming the seat by paying the deposit, and completing the semester fee payment. Timely submission of all fees and documents is essential to secure admission.

ProcessDetails
ApplicationFill out the admission form online or offline and pay the application fee of INR 500.
ShortlistingEligible candidates are shortlisted based on the submitted form and eligibility. Shortlisted candidates receive notification for counselling. 
Counselling & Document VerificationAttend counselling on the allotted date with original documents for verification (10th, 12th/Diploma, Character, Migration, photos, etc.). 
Seat ConfirmationPay INR 10,000 (via DD or online) to confirm your seat. After payment, semester fees must be deposited within 15 days of confirmation. 
Semester Fee PaymentPay the first semester fees before the deadline. Failure to pay cancels the admission.

What is the fee for MBA program and placement ratio at St. Andrews Institute of Technology and Management?

A
Aarushi Jain •  May-08-2025

Dear Student, 

If you are thinking about the MBA program at St. Andrews Institute of Technology and Management (SAITM) Gurgaon, the overall cost of the MBA program is INR 2,64,000 for the two-year duration. Also, if you decide to stay in the hostel, the cost will be INR 1,15,000, making the overall cost around INR 3.75 lakh. SAITM also provides a Global MBA program at a higher fee of INR 3.5 lakh for two years, without hostel fees. When it comes to placements, SAITM has a good record. The average placement is between 80-90%, and some of the top recruiters are Amazon, Tech Mahindra, Wipro, and TCS. The average salary package offered to MBA passouts is INR 4 LPA, and the highest package offered is up to INR 12 LPA. The institution also offers value-added certifications, overseas exposure trips, and exclusive placement assistance to boost your career opportunities. Overall, SAITM presents a complete MBA program with competitive tuition and excellent placement assistance, rendering it a feasible solution for future management experts.
