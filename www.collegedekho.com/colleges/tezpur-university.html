About Tezpur University

Tezpur University, a central university established in 1994 by an Act of Parliament, is located at Napaam in Tezpur, Sonitpur district of Assam. Spread across 262 acres of green campus, the university hosts 21 departments and 12 centres offering over 70 academic programs in engineering, sciences, humanities, social sciences, and management.

Ranked 79 in NIRF 2025 (University Category), Tezpur University has emerged as a leading higher education institution in Northeast India. The university emphasizes interdisciplinary learning, cutting-edge research, and industry partnerships, ensuring its graduates are well-prepared for professional and academic careers.

For engineering aspirants, the B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) includes AI and emerging technology modules, while the MBA program is uniquely recognized for its focus on rural management and sustainable business practices. The M.Sc programs offer modern research opportunities in physics, chemistry, nanoscience, and environmental sciences.

The campus provides state-of-the-art facilities, including advanced laboratories, digital classrooms, hostels, central library, and IT services with Wi-Fi access. With its serene location in Assam and connectivity to Tezpur town and nearby Saloni Airport, the university combines academic excellence with a peaceful learning environment.

In placements 2025, Tezpur University achieved an 80% placement rate, with the highest package at INR 37 LPA and an average package of INR 6 LPA, particularly strong for CSE graduates who secured an 8 LPA average. Leading recruiters include Amazon, Accenture, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and Oil India.

For students seeking 2026 admissions, Tezpur University accepts scores from JEE Main, CAT, CUET, MAT, XAT, CMAT, and its own TUEE depending on the program. With low semester fees (B.Tech at INR 15,000), national scholarships, and SC/ST tuition waivers, it remains one of India’s most affordable yet prestigious institutions.

FAQ about the Tezpur University

Where to find the TU Tezpur B.Tech cutoff?

Students can find the TU Tezpur B.Tech cutoff on the JoSAA website (josaa.nic.in). The cutoff can also be found on our website. B.Tech cutoff for Tezpur University is based on JEE Main rank. The JoSAA website releases the B.Tech cutoff in the form of opening and closing ranks. Moreover, the cutoff is further divided into various seat types like Open, EWS, OBC-NCL, SC and ST.

When can I apply for admission to TU Tezpur?

You need to apply for admission to TU Tezpur after the Tezpur University Entrance Examination (TUEE) results are declared for PG course admission. For UG course admission you must apply after the CUET results are declared. In short, students need to apply once the required entrance exam has been concluded.

What is the fees for the B.Ed course at Tezpur University?

The fees for the B.Ed course at Tezpur University is Rs 43,486 per year. This is a two-year programme divided into four semesters. B.Ed is offered under the Department of Education at Tezpur University. To get admission to B.Ed you need to have a graduation degree with 55% marks and pass the TUEE PG exam.

Can I get admission to B.Tech without JoSAA or CSAB counselling?

Yes, you can get admission to B.Tech without JoSAA or CSAB counselling. This is possible only if you belong to one of India’s North-Eastern states. 60% of the B.Tech seats at Tezpur University are reserved for such students. In this case, you need to pass the B.Tech Screening cum Selection Cell at Tezpur University (BSSC-TU) to get admission.

Does Tezpur University have a mess?

Yes, Tezpur University does have a mess facility for its students. The mess food is available at all the men and women’s hostel buildings. The university charges a fee of Rs 19,200 per year for the mess facility. Students can choose to pay the mess fees on a monthly basis.

Is Tezpur University ragging free?

Yes, Tezpur University is ragging free. The university campus is protected by the Assam Prohibition of Ragging Act, 1999. According to this act, ragging is strictly prohibited and if found guilty, the offender will be charged a fine of Rs 10,000 and will be imprisoned for a duration of six months. In addition, TU Tezpur also has the Hostel Level Anti Ragging Cell (HLARC) in all their hostels to stop any raging attempts made inside the hostel buildings. Other than this, the university also has the Anti Ragging Committee (ARC), the Anti Ragging Squad (ARS), and the Departmental Anti Ragging Cell (DARC).

What is the Tezpur University address?

Tezpur University is located in Tezpur, Assam. The Tezpur University address is Tezpur University, Napaam, Tezpur, Sonitpur, Assam, India, pin - 784028. This is also the postal address of the university. Students can reach the university by air and land. The nearest railway station is at Guwahati. Moreover, the nearest airport for this university is Lokapriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport, located in Guwahati, Assam.

Can I get a scholarship at Tezpur University?

Yes, you can get a scholarship at Tezpur University. Before applying for a scholarship, you should first check for the eligibility criteria. If you meet the eligibility criteria, then you can head over to the National Scholarship Portal and fill out the application form. Tezpur University offers various scholarships for students in need and students with merit. The scholarship amount will depend on which scholarship you are applying for. Remember that you can get only one scholarship for the entire duration of your course.

How many departments are there at TU Tezpur?

There are 28 departments at TU Tezpur. These departments are under four schools of study. The names of these schools are the School of Engineering, the School of Humanities and Social Sciences, the School of Management Sciences, and the School of Sciences. The names of the 28 departments under these schools are the Department of Civil Engineering, the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, the Department of Design, the Department of Business Administration, the Department of Chemical Sciences, and 23 more.

What is the Tezpur University entrance exam 2023 called?

The Tezpur University entrance exam 2023 is called TUEE. This entrance exam is conducted by the university itself. TUEE is a national examination. Other than the Tezpur University Entrance Exam (TUEE), the university also accepts other entrance exams like GATE, JEE Main, CEED, CUET, CAT, MAT, XAT, ATMA, GMAT, CMAT, and more. After qualifying for the entrance exam applicable to your course, you need to attend the counselling process conducted by the university.

What is the rank of TU Tezpur?

The rank of TU Tezpur is released by various government and private bodies. According to the NIRF ranking 2023, Tezpur University ranked 69th in the university category, 93rd in the management category, in the 101-150 rank band for the overall ranking, and in the 151-200 rank band for the engineering category. Besides that, QS Asia University Ranking ranked the university in the 351-400 rank band in 2023. Moreover, Times Higher Education Ranking 2023 ranked TU Tezpur 1201th out of 1500 universities in the world.

How much percentile is required for Tezpur University admission?

The percentile which is required for Tezpur University admission varies from one course to another. If you look at the TU Tezpur eligibility criteria for a popular course like B.Tech, it requires candidates to pass Class 12 with a minimum of 60% marks. Similarly, for the M.Com course at TU Tezpur, you will need to have passed B.Com with a minimum of 50% marks. Moreover, the minimum required percentage for admission to PhD courses at Tezpur University is 55% in the post graduation exam. Along with the eligible percentage, students also have to pass the required entrance exam for the selected course.

What is the highest package of Tezpur University?

The highest package of Tezpur University in 2023, according to various sources on the internet, is Rs 13,00,000 per annum for the MBA course. Aspiring candidates should know that the NIRF report 2023 mentions the 2022 placement data of Tezpur University. According to the NIRF report 2023, the median package for B.Tech in 2022 was Rs 4,25,000 per annum. Similarly, for the MBA course in 2022, the median package was Rs 5,11,139 per year.

Is Tezpur University under CUET?

Yes, Tezpur University is under CUET. The TU Tezpur is a participating university in the Common University Entrance Test (CUET), which is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). The university accepts CUET UG scores to provide admission to various undergraduate courses offered by various departments. Students who pass CUET UG can apply for admission through the Tezpur University online application form.

Is Tezpur University good or bad?

Deciding whether Tezpur University is good or bad depends on various factors such as the quality of teaching faculty, placement facilities, facilities offered to students, types of courses offered, and much more. As for the teaching faculty, the professors and assistant professors are mostly PhD holders. TU Tezpur placement cell offers placement assistance to all the final year students. The university also offers hostel accommodation, medical facilities, and more.

What is the jee cutoff for food engineering?

D
Dewesh Nandan Prasad •  May-06-2025

Dear Student, 

Tezpur University (Assam) does not offer a B.Tech in Food Engineering—it provides only an M.Tech in Food Engineering & Technology, which requires a GATE score (Food Tech/Agricultural Engg.) or performance in the university's entrance exam. Since it’s a central university, JEE Main scores are not applicable for this programme. For B.Tech in Food Tech, consider NITs (JEE Main rank ~50,000–100,000) or state/private universities like SHUATS Allahabad (60–70 percentile in JEE Main). If targeting Tezpur University, focus on GATE for M.Tech admission
