About Shri Ram Institute

Shri Ram Institute is a private institution and was founded in 1997 by Sachin Garg. It is situated in the Niranjanpur area of Dehradun, Uttarakhand. The institute has a serene campus spread across 25 acres of land. Shri Ram Institute is approved by the AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) and the MHRD (Ministry of Human Resource Development). It is accredited by the UGC (University Grants Commission) and is affiliated with Shri Dev Suman Uttarakhand University.

The instituteprovides more than 10 courses in management, hospitality, teaching and various certification courses. Some of the popular Shri Ram Institute courses are B.Com, BBA, MHM, Diploma, certificate courses, etc. It has more than 80 teachers and the faculty constantly guides and supports the students. The mentor-mentee ratio is 1:20, which ensures adequate attention to every student. 

The Shri Ram Institute campus maintains a welcoming and sustainable atmosphere. It has a state-of-the-art infrastructure and facilities. The campus is strictly Ragging-Free and safe. There are separate hostels for boys and girls, sports grounds, libraries, cafeterias, and 24/7 Wi-Fi. The Shri Ram Institute placements are a success each year and the highest package goes up to Rs 25 lakh per annum. Some of the top companies that visit the campus for recruitment are Taj, Oberoi, Radisson, Hyatt, Lodhi, Jaypee Hotels, Amazon, Phonepe, IDBI Bank, ITC, etc.

Shri Ram Institute Courses and Admission 2026

  • Online Admission Process

Candidates who want to secure admission at Shri Ram Institute can apply through their official website. Candidates would be required to create an account first and fill in all the mandatory information. Once done, the candidate would have access to the application form, which they need to fill out and attach all the mandatory documents. While submitting the form, the candidate needs to pay a nominal fee of Rs 1,000, which can be paid via credit/debit cards or through UPI. Once submitted, the candidate would receive an acknowledgement mail as well.

  • Offline Admission Process

Candidates participating in the Shri Ram Institute admissions offline can visit the admission cell directly. The candidate would be given an admission form which they need to fill out with all the required details. Candidates can clear any questions or doubts about a specific course at the admission cell itself. While submitting the form, the candidate needs to pay a nominal fee of Rs 1,000, which can be paid via credit/debit cards, net banking or UPI. Once submitted, the admission cell would give a receipt of acknowledgement to the candidate.

Shri Ram Institute Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The campus is built on more than 25 acres of land and has beautiful weather conditions throughout the year. Latest and high–quality infrastructure is installed all over the campus, whether it is for classroom usage or any other purpose. The projectors, computers and electronic accessories are of exceptionally high configuration providing the best user experience.

Gym Facility

  • The Shri Ram Institute gym facility is advanced and equipped with the latest machines for students' physical development and fitness. The Gym equipment is maintained and sent for repair regularly.

Canteen and mess 

  • Mess with a good seating area, and the token system saves time and makes it easier for the cooking staff to manage orders and deliver them systematically.
  • The restaurant is an entirely new and unique way of hoteling that themanagement has come up with; it is a mix of hotel and mess that provides a combination of continental and Indian food but comes at a higher cost than Mess.
  • Indoor gaming options with many sports equipment installed in the sports are where students usually gather around after the daily schedule.
  • Freshwater is available all over the campus to ensure that basic hygiene is maintained.

Transportation

  • Shri Ram Institute transportation facility covers almost all of Dehradun and comes at as low as Rs 6,000 to Rs 25,000 per annum depending on the choice of route and distance from the campus.
  • The transport facility is available 100% of the time as there are extra busses parked on the campus in case of emergencies or breakdown of other busses.

Library

  • Students can borrow books from the library and return them as per their requirements. However, one needs to create a library card before carrying the books outside of the library area, and certain rules are imposed to ensure everyone gets access to the books.
  • Students can also read books out of their syllabus without any objections. The library has a huge collection of books and volumes from various authors explaining multiple ways to solve the same problems, broadening students' mindsets and teaching them brainstorming methodologies.

Hostel Facility

  • Shri Ram Institute offers a perfect hostel stay option considering a significant portion of enrollment is done outside of Dehradun. A separate boy and girls' hostel is available at the campus, which functions as an independent unit. The annual fee for the hostel is Rs 35,090 for both genders.
  • Good laundry service, fresh water and good food service can be found at the hostels.
