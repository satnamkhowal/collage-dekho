NIT Rourkela Placements 2025

The placements for NIT Rourkela are managed by its Career Development Cell (CDC) every year. The Career Development Centre (CDC) at NIT Rourkela manages all placement and internship activities, fostering strong industry connections to ensure robust career opportunities for students. Headed by a senior faculty member and supported by a student placement committee, the CDC collaborates with various industrial groups and research organizations to facilitate smooth recruitment drives. The cell organizes pre-placement talks, aptitude tests, group discussions, and interviews, while also providing training in soft skills, logical reasoning, and technical competencies to prepare students for professional success. As per NIT Rourkela's latest placement report, the highest package offered during placements was INR 1.2 crore per annum, while the average package stood at INR 13.43 LPA to INR 14.57 LPA depending on the course. The placement rate was almost 99%, with 24 students receiving packages above INR 50 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visited NIT Rourkela for placements included Apple, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Wipro, Deloitte.

NIT Rourkela Placement Statistics

According to NIT Rourkela placement 2024, a total of 342 companies visited for placements, and more than 1300 offers were made. The highest package offered during 2024 placements is Rs 83.60 LPA for the BTech branch and Rs 52.89 LPA for the MTech branch. Moreover, the average package offered during 2024 placements is Rs 12.89 LPA overall and Rs 14.05 LPA for BTech CSE. Some of the top NIT Rourkela recruiters that visited for placements included Google, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, Deloitte, BPCL, Wipro, GAIL, BEL, etc.

Hi, for admission to NIT Rourkela's Integrated B.Sc. + M.Sc. Mathematics program, your score in the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main is the main determinant. The amount of candidates, available seats, and general exam difficulty are some of the variables that can affect the precise percentile needed each year.

Closing ranks from the previous year: The closing rankings from prior years can give a broad indication of the necessary performance, even though the precise percentiles are not made public:

  • The Integrated B.Sc. + M.Sc. Mathematics program's finishing rank for the 2023 academic year was roughly 83,330.
  • The closing ranks for the 2024 academic year were reported to range between 23,934 and 62,446.

Estimating the Required Percentile:

The total number of candidates and the distribution of scores both influence JEE Main rankings. Given that the 2024 closing ranks were between 24,000 and 62,000, aiming for a rank in this range would increase your chances of acceptance. Typically, a ranking in the top 25,000 is considered competitive for this program.

Note: The cutoff ranks and admission criteria are subject to change every year.
