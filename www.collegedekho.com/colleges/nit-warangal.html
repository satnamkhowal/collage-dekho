About NIT Warangal

Established in 1959 as the Regional Engineering College (REC) Warangal, NIT Warangal was upgraded to a National Institute of Technology in 2002. Located in Warangal, Telangana, it is recognized as an Institute of National Importance by the Government of India, approved by AICTE, accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC, and recognized by the UGC. NIT Warangal ranks 21st in the Engineering category and 53rd in the Overall category by NIRF 2024, with a 100th rank in the Management category. It also holds a 351–400 rank in Asia by Times Higher Education 2025 and 1001–1200 globally.

The institute offers over 80 programs across 13 academic departments in Engineering, Sciences, Management, and Humanities. Popular UG programs include B.Tech and Integrated M.Sc, while PG programs include M.Tech, MBA, M.Sc, and Ph.D. Admissions are based on entrance exams: JEE Main for B.Tech, GATE for M.Tech, IIT JAM for M.Sc, and CAT/MAT/CMAT for MBA, managed through centralized counseling like JoSAA, CCMT, and CCMN. The institute is ranked 28 in Engineering by NIRF 2025. The Centre for Career Planning and Development (CCPD) drives placements, with the 2024-25 placement drive reporting a highest package of INR 64.3 LPA and an average of INR 15.6 LPA for B.Tech, with 631+ UG students placed across 150–180 companies like Microsoft, Amazon, and Atlassian.

NIT Warangal Campus

NIT Warangal’s 256-acre campus is designed for academic excellence, research, and holistic student development.

  • Central Library: The Central Library, spread across 4,000 sq. m, houses 1.87 lakh books, back volumes, standards, technical pamphlets, CD-ROMs, video cassettes, e-books, and 1,400+ journals. It offers digital access to IEEE, Springer, Elsevier, and other databases, with a seating capacity of 500+ and 24/7 access during exams. Recent upgrades include expanded e-resources and a renovated reading area.
  • Hostels: The campus has 22 hostel blocks (20 for boys, 2 for girls), accommodating over 3,000 students. Options include single, double, and triple occupancy rooms with Wi-Fi, LAN, common rooms, TVs, indoor games, and mess facilities. Mess options include private (IFC A, B, C) and government messes (Mess 1, 2, 3), with IFC B recommended for quality and proximity to hostels. Hostel fees for first-year B.Tech are INR 43,600 (boys) and INR 9,900–14,400 (girls).
  • Indoor and Outdoor Sports Facilities: Facilities include a floodlit cricket/football stadium, athletic track, tennis courts, basketball courts, volleyball courts, badminton courts, and an indoor complex for table tennis, chess, and carrom. A modern gymnasium with two instructors supports fitness needs.
  • Canteen: The campus features multiple dining options, including IFC messes (South Indian, North Indian, mixed) and government messes (vegetarian and non-vegetarian). IFC messes offer better breakfast quality, while non-vegetarian messes serve meals six days a week. A food court near hostels provides additional dining options.
  • Shopping Centre: A shopping complex near hostels offers groceries, stationery, and daily essentials, catering to students and faculty.
  • Guest Houses: Two guest houses (AC and non-AC) with 30+ rooms accommodate visitors, faculty, and conference delegates, equipped with telephones and TVs.
  • Health Centre: A 24/7 medical center with three doctors, an Apollo Pharmacy, and ambulance services provides consultations, diagnostics, and emergency care, with tie-ups to nearby hospitals.
  • IT Infrastructure: A campus-wide network with 10,000+ nodes connects academic blocks, labs, hostels, and offices, offering 5 Gbps bandwidth. Smart classrooms feature projectors and audio-visual aids, and labs include specialized facilities for CSE, mechanical, and civil engineering.
  • Free Wi-Fi: High-speed Wi-Fi is available across academic areas, hostels, and the library, with 10 Mbps LAN in hostels and labs, accessible via a captive portal.
  • Auditorium: The Bhubaneswar Behera Auditorium (1,000+ seats) and seminar halls host conferences, cultural events, and symposiums, supported by advanced audio-visual technology.

NIT Warangal Scholarships

There are many scholarship programs offered by the institute, as listed below

Institute Merit Scholarship:

  • As to the title of the scholarship, it is clear that the scholarship is offered based on merits scored by the students in their last qualifying examination. 
  • To be eligible for the Merit Scholarship, a student must have scored a minimum CGPA of 9.1 in each semester. 
  • The institute will process the Scholarship award directly into the bank account once the candidate matches the eligibility and completes the documentation. 

MHRD Scholarship:

  • The MHRD Scholarship is offered under the MHRD scholarship guidelines with no reservation or caste criteria for the students of NIT Warangal.
  • To be eligible for the MHRD Scholarship, a candidate must have a JEE Main rank of less than 2000.

TS E-Pass Scholarship: 

  • The TS EPass Scholarship is specifically designated to the students of the OBC/EWS/SC/ST category of Telangana state only. 
  • Students having an annual family income of 1 lakh or less than 1 lakh are eligible to apply for this scholarship.
