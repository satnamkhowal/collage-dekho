About Jamia Millia Islamia Courses 2026

Jamia Millia Islamia courses list includes 300+ programmes across 11 faculties and 40+ departments. Students can pursue UG, PG, PhD, Diploma, PG Diploma, and Certificate courses. Courses like BA (Hons.), B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Des are offered at the UG level, while MBA, MA, M.Sc, MCA are offered at the PG level. For 2026-27, JMI has introduced 30 new programs including specialised M.Tech, MBA, and interdisciplinary courses. Jamia Millia Islamia fees range from INR 20,350 for MA to INR 6,12,700 for B.Tech (Self-Financed) for the complete duration. 

JMI offers both regular and self-financed courses across Engineering, Law, Arts, Sciences, Management, Fine Arts, Education, Dentistry, and Architecture fields. The university accepts JEE Main for B.Tech, NATA for B.Arch, NEET for BDS, CUET for BA (Hons.)/B.Sc, and CUET PG for MA. JMI also offers executive MBA programs and distance learning courses for working professionals and students who cannot attend regular classes.

Jamia Millia Islamia Courses FAQs

Can I pay the JMI course fees in installments?

Jamia Millia Islamia may allow fee installments for certain programs. Check Jamia Millia Islamia’s finance or admissions office for installment options, schedules, and bank arrangements for your course.

Does JMI offer distance education courses?

Jamia Millia Islamia offers some distance and online programs through designated centres. Check Jamia Millia Islamia’s distance education portal for eligibility, course list, and admission details for 2026.

What is the hostel fee in Jamia Millia Islamia?

Jamia Millia Islamia hostel fees are modest compared to private hostels. Fees depend on room type and mess plan—check Jamia Millia Islamia hostel office or website for current hostel charges.

What is the fee for B.Tech CSE in JMI?

Jamia Millia Islamia B.Tech CSE fee is set by the university and is lower than many private colleges. Check Jamia Millia Islamia’s official fee structure for the exact, updated tuition and semester charges.

Is Jamia Millia Islamia expensive?

Jamia Millia Islamia is a public central university with relatively affordable fees compared to private colleges. Costs vary by program; review Jamia Millia Islamia’s fee schedule for program-wise details.

Does Jamia Millia Islamia offer online or short-term courses?

Yes, Jamia Millia Islamia Centre For Distance And Online Education offers online and distance education. Certificate in Information Technology, BCom, MA English, MBA to name a few are some of the Jamia Millia Islamia online courses.

What types of courses does Jamia Millia Islamia offer?

Jamia Millia Islamia offers diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral courses in full-time and part-time mode. The university also offers integrated and PG diploma courses.

Can I pay my Jamia Millia Islamia fee online?

Yes, candidates selected for Jamia Millia Islamia courses admission can pay the fees via online mode. They can visit the university online fee payment portal to pay the fees.

What are the Jamia Millia Islamia courses after 10th?

The university offers a total of 20 courses after 10th. A few of them are Diploma in Modern Persian, Diploma in Civil Engineering, Diploma in Elementary Education etc. The duration of diploma courses is 1 year.

Which courses are popular at Jamia Millia Islamia University?

Some of the popular courses offered by Jamia Millia Islamia are Bachelor of Technology (BTech), Bachelor of Architecture (BArch), Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS), Master of Business Administration (MBA), Master of Commerce (MCom) etc. The duration of Jamia Millia Islamia courses ranges from 1 year to 6 years.

What is the difficulty level of the Jamia Millia Islamia exam?

The difficulty level of the JMI Entrance Exam is moderate. Candidates just need to check the syllabus and cover all topics to clear the exam and get admission in Jamia Millia Islamia courses.

How to get a free seat in Jamia Millia Islamia?

Admission seekers cannot get a free seat in Jamia Millia Islamia. However, they can get a scholarship based on their family income or academic performance. The scholarships provide tuition fee waiver to students.

Does Jamia Millia Islamia accept 12th marks?

Yes, admission in Jamia Millia Islamia courses offered at diploma and UG levels are done based on marks obtained in Class 12 and entrance exam conducted by the university.

Is Jamia Millia Islamia easy to get?

For admission in Jamia Millia Islamia candidates are required to qualify the JMI Entrance Exam or obtain a good score in JEE Main/NEET BDS/CUET/NATA.

What is the top package of Jamia Millia Islamia?

The highest package offered to BTech students at Jamia Millia Islamia during the 2023 placements was between Rs 20 LPA to Rs 25 LPA. On the other hand, top package offered to MBA students was Rs 25 LPA.

Is Jamia Millia Islamia very expensive?

Jamia Millia Islamia is an affordable university.

What is the annual fee of Jamia Millia Islamia?

The annual fees of Jamia Millia Islamia range from Rs 6,700 - Rs 1,50,000 (depending on the level of degree). The university offers a total of 33 degrees in various disciplines at diploma, UG, PG and doctoral levels.

Can JMI Delhi scholarships be availed?

Yes, JMI Delhi scholarships are provided to some deserving students. Both merit-based and need-based scholarships are provided.

What are the Jamia Millia Islamia fees payment guidelines?

Jamia Millia Islamia fees can be paid through net banking, debit or credit cards of any bank. The mode of payment is online.

What are the facilities offered at Jamia Millia Islamia hostels?

Jamia Millia Islamia hostels provide facilities like 24x7 wifi, 24 hours of running water and electricity, well furnished rooms with bed, chair & almirah, regularly cleaned bathrooms and 24 hours security.

What are Jamia Millia Islamia hostel fees?

Jamia Millia Islamia hostel fees are INR 6,400 for boys and INR 5,500 for girls.

What are JMI Delhi B.Tech fees and their offered specializations?

JMI Delhi fees for B.Tech is INR 64,000 for 4 years. The specializations it offers include Computer Science, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics and Communication and Civil Engineering.

How much does the Jamia Millia Islamia BDS course cost?

Jamia Millia Islamia BDS (Bachelor of Dental Surgery) course costs INR 1.55 Lakh for 5 years. Candidates will be selected on the score they obtain in NEET entrance exam.

What are JMI Delhi popular courses and fee details?

JMI Delhi popular courses and fee details include : B.Tech with INR 64,000 for 4 years, M.Tech with INR 36,700 for 2 years and MBA with INR 2.10 Lakh for 2 years.

What is the entrance exam required to get admission in Jamia Millia Islamia MBA course?

The entrance exam required to get admission in the Jamia Millia Islamia MBA course is the JMI entrance exam. Candidates need a valid score in this for the selection process.
