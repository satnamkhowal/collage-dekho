About Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology

SVCET admission for the academic session 2024-25 will begin soon. Admissions at SVCET are offered to graduates and postgraduates based on the entrance examination. The college accepts Maharashtra Common Entrance Test, popularly known as MHT CET for admission to various engineering and management courses. The SVCET admission process and couselling is conducted by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), of the Maharashtra State Government. 

Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology course list include four UG and PG courses including B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA and Diploma in Engineering. The college offers various specialisations with technical courses such as computer engineering, artificial intelligence and machine learning, artificial intelligence and data science, electronics and telecommunication engineering, mechanical engineering and engineering design. Students can choose any of the courses based on the SVCET eligibility criteria. 

Sahyadri Valley College of Engineering & Technology is a popular private college located in Pune, Maharashtra. The institute offers good placement opportunities in global companies such as Accenture, Bajaj, Genpact, Kellog, JP Morgan, Aircel, Lo’oreal, and L&T.The SVCET average salary package ranges between Rs 2 to Rs 5.5 Lakh per annum.  Moreover, the college offers decent infrastructure and facilities to students.
