About Jaipur National University MBA

Jaipur National University MBA is a 2 Years program offered in multiple specializations. Jaipur National University requires applicants for the M.B.A. degree to have a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks. Additionally, applicants must have a valid score in CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT/ATMA/GMAT. The annual fee for MBA is INR 1,39,100 - 2,04,600. Jaipur National University MBA Admission 2026

Admission to the MBA at Jaipur National University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Jaipur National University MBA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Jaipur National University MBA Application Process

Jaipur National University MBA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the MBA course at Jaipur National University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Jaipur National University MBA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Jaipur National University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Jaipur National University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Jaipur National University MBA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Jaipur National University MBA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Jaipur National University MBA Application Form.

Documents Required for Jaipur National University MBA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
