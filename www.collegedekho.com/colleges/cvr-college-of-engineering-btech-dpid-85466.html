About CVR College of Engineering B.Tech

CVR College of Engineering B.Tech is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at CVR College of Engineering Hyderabad, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Firstly, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they should have secured a minimum aggregate score of 45% in the qualifying examination. For candidates belonging to reserved categories, a relaxation of 5% is provided. Furthermore, candidates must qualify in the Engineering, Agriculture, and Medical Common Entrance Test (EAMCET) conducted by the Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TSCHE) to be considered for admission. Meeting these eligibility requirements ensures a strong foundation for students to embark on their journey towards a B.Tech. degree at CVR College of Engineering Hyderabad. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 1,15,000. CVR College of Engineering B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at CVR College of Engineering is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the CVR College of Engineering B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

CVR College of Engineering B.Tech Application Process

CVR College of Engineering B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at CVR College of Engineering have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill CVR College of Engineering B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of CVR College of Engineering and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of CVR College of Engineering. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the CVR College of Engineering B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the CVR College of Engineering B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of CVR College of Engineering B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for CVR College of Engineering B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
