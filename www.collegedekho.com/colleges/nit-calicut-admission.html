NIT Calicut admission 2026

National Institute of Technology Calicut (NIT Calicut or NITC), established in 1961 in Kozhikode, Kerala, is a premier public technical university and an Institute of National Importance. Accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC, it ranks 21st in Engineering and 2nd in Architecture by NIRF 2025. Located on a 296-acre campus along the Calicut-Mukkam route, NITC offers Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Doctoral programs in Engineering, Sciences, Architecture, and Management through 15 departments. Admissions are entrance-based, requiring JEE Main for B.Tech/B.Arch, GATE for M.Tech/M.Plan, IIT JAM for M.Sc, NIMCET for MCA, CAT for MBA, and NET/JRF/GATE for Ph.D. Applications are primarily online via exam portals or nitc.ac.in. Below are the details for NIT Calicut Admission 2026, including eligibility, selection criteria, application process, and key dates.

NIT Calicut Admission Process 2026

Admission to NIT Calicut in 2026 is done through national-level entrance exams. Students who want to join B.Tech or B.Arch courses need to clear the JEE Main exam, while those applying for M.Tech or M.Plan must qualify through GATE. The counseling process is organized by different bodies — JoSAA for B.Tech and B.Arch (June–August), CCMT for M.Tech and M.Plan (May–August), CCMN for M.Sc (May–July), and NIMCET for MCA (June–July). MBA and Ph.D. admissions are handled directly by the institute from January to December. Final-year students can also apply, but they must submit their final marks by September 30, 2026, to confirm admission.

Key steps include exam qualification, portal registration, choice filling, seat allotment, partial fee payment (Rs 35,000 for JoSAA), document verification at reporting centers, and spot rounds for vacancies. Category reservations follow 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 27% OBC, 10% EWS, with supernumerary for females (5-10% extra) and PwD (5%).

NIT Calicut Admission FAQs

What scholarships can EWS students apply for in NIT Calicut admission 2026?

NIT Calicut offers Merit-cum-Means scholarships for students from economically weaker backgrounds. Candidates with an annual family income below Rs 1 lakh are eligible for full tuition remission—for example, a B.Tech student can receive a waiver of Rs 62,500 per semester. This financial aid ensures that deserving students can pursue quality technical education without monetary constraints. Interested students must apply through the institute’s official portal after securing admission, with the application deadline in September 2026. The scholarship supports inclusivity and equal opportunity, allowing meritorious students to focus on academics and skill development without financial stress.

What are the NIT Calicut spot admission dates for 2026?

Spot admissions at NIT Calicut for 2026 are scheduled from August 20 to 25, covering both undergraduate and postgraduate programs. For UG courses like B.Tech, the process is conducted through CSAB, while PG programs such as M.Tech and M.Plan follow CCMT procedures. These spot rounds occur after regular counseling and seat allotment, filling 5–10% of seats that remain vacant across various programs. Eligible candidates can apply to secure admission in remaining seats, ensuring maximum utilization of available capacity. The process provides an additional opportunity for students who missed initial counseling rounds or wish to explore alternative branches and specializations.

When is the PhD winter intake deadline at NIT Calicut for 2026?

NIT Calicut PhD admissions for the 2026 winter intake are scheduled for December 15, 2026, with applications accepted through the institute portal at research.nitc.ac.in. Eligibility requires a Master’s degree with at least 60% marks (6.5 CGPA) and a valid GATE or UGC-NET score, or qualification through the institute’s own test. Each department offers 10–15 seats, catering to full-time, part-time, and self-sponsored candidates. Selection is based on a written test followed by a personal interview, ensuring rigorous assessment of research aptitude. This process enables aspiring researchers to pursue doctoral studies across NIT Calicut’s 15 academic departments.

What seats are available for NIT Calicut MCA 2026?

NIT Calicut MCA admissions for 2026 are conducted through NIMCET, with the exam scheduled for June 8, 2026. Counseling takes place from June to July, offering 50 regular seats. Additionally, 10 self-sponsored seats are available at Rs 35,000 per semester. Eligibility requires candidates to have a BCA or BSc degree with Mathematics at the 10+2 or undergraduate level and a minimum aggregate of 60% (55% for SC/ST). The application fee for regular seats is Rs 1,500. This structured process ensures merit-based selection, providing students with opportunities to pursue advanced studies in computer applications and career-ready skills in IT and software domains.

What is the NIT Calicut MBA application process for 2026?

For NIT Calicut MBA admission 2026, candidates must have valid CAT 2025 scores (exam held on November 24, 2025). Applications open in January 2026, followed by Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) rounds scheduled from March 15 to 20, 2026. The program offers 75 regular seats and 15 sponsored seats for industry or academic nominees. Eligibility requires a Bachelor’s degree with at least 60% marks (55% for SC/ST candidates). The application fee is Rs 1,000. Admission is based on a 70:30 weightage of CAT score and GD/PI performance, ensuring a holistic selection process.

What is the timeline for NIT Calicut MSc admission 2026?

NIT Calicut M.Sc admissions for 2026 begin with the IIT JAM exam scheduled on February 9, 2026. Eligible candidates must register for CCMN counseling between May 20 and 30, 2026. A total of 75 seats are available, distributed equally across Chemistry, Mathematics, and Physics (25 seats each). The main counseling rounds take place from May to July, during which candidates can select and lock their preferred specializations. For any vacant seats after these rounds, spot admissions are conducted from August 20 to 25. This structured process ensures fair allocation and smooth admission for aspirants seeking postgraduate science programs.

How many seats are there in NIT Calicut MTech programs for 2026?

NIT Calicut offers over 800 M.Tech seats across 26 specializations, including Computer Science & Engineering (50 seats) and Structural Engineering (25 seats). Additionally, 20% extra seats are available for self-sponsored candidates in each program. Eligibility for admission requires a B.Tech or BE degree with a minimum of 60% marks (55% for SC/ST candidates) and a valid GATE score in the relevant paper. Admission is conducted through the CCMT counseling process, ensuring fair allocation. These programs provide advanced technical knowledge, research opportunities, and industry-oriented learning, preparing graduates for high-level careers in engineering, R&D, and academia.

When is the expected JoSAA registration for NIT Calicut BTech 2026?

B.Tech admissions at NIT Calicut for 2026 are scheduled from June 10 to 18, following the release of JEE Main results in April. Candidates must register on the JoSAA portal, fill and lock their choices, and participate in six counseling rounds, which continue until August. For seats remaining vacant after these rounds, CSAB conducts spot admissions to ensure maximum enrollment. This structured timeline allows candidates to secure seats in their preferred branches while providing multiple opportunities through successive rounds, including provisional admission for final-year students, ensuring a fair and transparent selection process for NIT Calicut’s undergraduate engineering programs.

What eligibility criteria apply for NIT Calicut BTech admission 2026?

For B.Tech admission at NIT Calicut in 2026, candidates must have scored at least 75% in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (65% for SC/ST), and must qualify JEE Main Paper 1. Final-year Class 12 students can apply provisionally, provided they submit their marks by the specified deadline. The application fee through the JoSAA portal is Rs 900 for male candidates and Rs 450 for female candidates. This eligibility ensures that applicants meet both academic and competitive exam standards, allowing NIT Calicut to select meritorious students for its undergraduate engineering programs across all branches.

What is the NIT Calicut BTech seat allocation for 2026?

NIT Calicut offers a total of 1,042 B.Tech seats across 10 branches, equally divided between home state (Kerala) and other state candidates, with 521 seats each. The allocation includes supernumerary seats for female candidates and students from Lakshadweep, promoting diversity and inclusivity. Among the branches, Computer Science & Engineering is the most sought-after, with 183 seats, of which 89 are reserved for home state students and 94 for other state candidates. This balanced distribution ensures fair access for local and national applicants while maintaining gender and regional representation across all engineering disciplines at the institute.
