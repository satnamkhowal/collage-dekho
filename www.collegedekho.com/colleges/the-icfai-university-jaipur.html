About The ICFAI University (ICFAI-J), Jaipur

The ICFAI University, Jaipur is a Private University located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. The institute was established in 2011. The ICFAI University, Jaipur has been recognised  University Grants Commission (UGC), Bar Council of India (BCI), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). As per IIRF Ranking 2024, The ICFAI University, Jaipur has been ranked at 19 under the overall category. well-eq laboratories, workshops, a cafeteria, student welfare amenities, and sports grounds are some of The ICFAI University, Jaipur facilities.

The ICFAI University, Jaipur courses are offered at undergraduate and postgraduate program levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include  B.Tech, BBA, B.Com (Hons.), BCA, MBA, and LL.B. The ICFAI University, Jaipur admission is based on merit and entrance examinations. The college accepts CUET, CAT & MAT for admission. Regarding The ICFAI University, Jaipur placements, the highest package offered in 2024 was  20.5 LPA and the average package offered was 7.3 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include CS, Accenture, Flipkart, JK Cement, Avalara Technologies, Varun Beverages, ICICI Home Finance, Acxiom Consulting, DeltaX, Reliance Retail Ltd.
