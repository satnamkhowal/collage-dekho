About ABESEC

Established in 2000, ABES Engineering College (ABESEC), Ghaziabad, is a private AICTE-approved institution affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, with NAAC A-grade and NBA accreditations. Spread across a 14.16-acre campus, it houses modern academic blocks, 50+ IoT-enabled labs, high-speed Wi-Fi, and separate boys’ and girls’ hostels with biometric security. The college offers B.Tech (Regular, Working Professional, Lateral Entry), M.Tech, and MCA programs, emphasizing emerging tech fields like AI, ML, and Data Science, with flexible payment and merit-based scholarships. 

ABES maintains a dual admission track—85% via UPTAC JEE Main counseling and 15% through direct merit-based entry, ensuring transparency and equity. Placements in 2025 saw 562 recruiters, including Atlassian, Microsoft, Amazon, TCS, and Infosys, yielding 78.5% placement rate, median package Rs. 4.50 LPA, and international roles. ABES ranks NIRF 201–300 (Engineering 2025) and #12 among AKTU affiliates, with notable achievements in robotics and research output.

ABESEC Admission Process 2026

ABES Engineering College’s admission procedure for 2026 combines merit-based and rank-based entry to ensure transparency and fairness:

UPTAC Counseling (85% seats): Based on JEE Main 2026 ranks (minimum 10th percentile). Candidates must register at uptac.admissions.nic.in, fill choices, attend virtual counseling, pay a ₹20,000 seat lock fee, and report with original documents. Registration fee is ₹1,000, with counseling in June–July 2026.

Direct Admission (15% seats): For merit-based candidates, considering JEE/10+2 marks and interview performance. Apply via ABES ERP portal, upload marks, pay pre-enrollment fee (₹500–₹2,300 based on category), and attend the interview. Timeline is May–July 2026.
