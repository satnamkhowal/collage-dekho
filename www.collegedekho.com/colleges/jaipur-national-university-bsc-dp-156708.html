About Jaipur National University BSc

Jaipur National University BSc is a 2 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for admission to Jaipur National University, applicants must have passed 10+2 in the science stream with Mathematics/ Biology/ Agriculture/ Biotechnology, or 10+2 in any stream for B.Sc in Fire and Industrial Safety/ Hospitality Management & Hostel Administration. For B.Sc Agriculture/ Horticulture, applicants must have a minimum of 50% marks in 10+2 (Science/ Agriculture). Selection is based on merit and personal interviews, or an entrance test and personal interview round for B.Sc Agriculture/ Horticulture. The annual fee for BSc is INR 37,400 - 5,00,098. Jaipur National University BSc Admission 2026

Admission to the BSc at Jaipur National University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Jaipur National University BSc admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Jaipur National University BSc Application Process

Jaipur National University BSc application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BSc course at Jaipur National University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Jaipur National University BSc application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Jaipur National University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Jaipur National University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Jaipur National University BSc application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Jaipur National University BSc application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Jaipur National University BSc Application Form.

Documents Required for Jaipur National University BSc Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

Overall Jaipur University offers a balanced blend of academic excellence and a vibrant campus life. The faculty members are knowledgeable and supportive, often going the extra mile to help students grasp concepts clearly. The university provides a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses, along with well-equipped labs and libraries that support in-depth learning and research. The campus is clean, green, and well-maintained, creating a positive atmosphere for learning. Placement opportunities are decent, especially in fields like engineering, management, and computer science, with several reputed companies visiting the campus. Cultural events, seminars, and extracurricular activities are regularly organized, helping students develop soft skills and confidence. Hostel facilities are satisfactory, with basic amenities and a secure environment. However, some areas like administrative services and internet connectivity could be improved. Overall, Jaipur University is a good choice for students looking for quality education with holistic development in a well-structured academy

Placement Jaipur University provides satisfactory placement opportunities, especially for students in fields like engineering, management, computer applications, and commerce. The university has a dedicated placement cell that works to connect students with recruiters and prepare them for interviews through training sessions, mock interviews, and resume-building workshops. Several reputed companies visit the campus during placement drives, including TCS, Wipro, Infosys, HCL, and Byju’s, among others. The average package ranges between ₹3 to ₹5 LPA, with a few students getting higher packages depending on their skills and performance.

Infrastructure Jaipur University boasts a modern and student-friendly infrastructure that supports both academic and extracurricular development. The campus is spread over a large area with well-planned buildings, lush green surroundings, and a peaceful learning environment. Classrooms are spacious, well-lit, and equipped with smart boards and projectors for interactive learning. The university has advanced laboratories for science, engineering, and medical students, stocked with the latest equipment and tools. The central library is a highlight — fully air-conditioned, digitalized, and filled with a vast collection of books, journals, and e-resources. Wi-Fi is available across the campus, though connectivity can sometimes be inconsistent. The university also offers a well-maintained sports complex, auditorium, seminar halls, and common areas that support co-curricular growth. Hostel facilities are adequate, with clean rooms, basic furniture, and a hygienic mess. Overall, the infrastructure at Jaipur University is well-developed, providing a comfortable and productive environment for student.

Faculty The faculty at Jaipur University is one of its strongest assets. Most professors are well-qualified, holding PhDs or other advanced degrees in their respective fields. They are not only knowledgeable but also approachable and supportive, always ready to clear doubts and guide students both academically and personally. Teaching methods are a mix of traditional and modern approaches, with the use of smart classrooms, presentations, and project-based learning. The faculty regularly updates the curriculum to keep it industry-relevant, especially in technical and professional courses like engineering, management, and computer science.

Hostel The hostel facilities at Jaipur University are decent and cater well to the basic needs of students. Separate hostels are available for boys and girls, with proper security arrangements like CCTV surveillance and wardens on duty 24/7. Rooms are generally shared (double or triple occupancy), clean, and come with essential furniture like beds, study tables, and cupboards. The mess serves vegetarian meals with a fixed weekly menu. While the food is hygienic, the taste may feel repetitive at times. Drinking water and hot water facilities are available, along with regular cleaning and laundry services. Wi-Fi is provided in the hostel, though speed and stability can vary. Common rooms with TV, indoor games, and reading areas add to the convenience. Strict hostel timings and rules ensure discipline but may feel restrictive to some students. Overall, Jaipur University’s hostels provide a safe and comfortable environment, especially for outstation students adjusting to campus life.

Overall The B.Sc. (Hons.) Mathematics program at Jaipur National University offers a strong foundational understanding of pure and applied mathematics. The curriculum is well-structured and in line with UGC guidelines, covering essential topics like algebra, calculus, linear algebra, real analysis, numerical methods, and differential equations. Advanced elective options allow students to explore topics like mathematical modeling or statistics. The faculty is generally supportive, knowledgeable, and qualified, though some lectures may be theory-heavy with less real-world application.

Placement Placements for pure sciences like Mathematics can be limited compared to professional programs. However, the university’s Career Development Cell provides support for internships, entrance exam coaching (like JAM, GATE), and higher studies counseling. Students typically pursue further studies in mathematics, statistics, data science, or go into teaching, research, or government exams.

Infrastructure The university campus is well-maintained with good classroom infrastructure, clean surroundings, and decent access to academic resources. The mathematics department has functional labs and a well-stocked library. Wi-Fi and computer labs are available for research and project work. However, the university can improve in offering more interactive tools and smart classrooms.

Faculty Most faculty members in the Mathematics department hold PhDs or postgraduates from reputed universities and have teaching experience. The teaching is theory-focused, covering core subjects like calculus, algebra, real analysis, statistics, and linear algebra thoroughly.
