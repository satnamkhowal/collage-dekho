About Jagannath University Jaipur

Jagannath University Jaipur was established in 2008 under an Act of the Rajasthan State Legislature (Act No. 19 of 2008) as a private university in Jaipur, Rajasthan. It is promoted by the Jagan Nath Gupta Memorial Educational Society. The university is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). Moreover, it has been accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an ‘A’ grade.  From 2022, a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). The University has also acquired a distinctive status in agriculture education having been accredited by ICAR for 5 years with its B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture program.

The Jagannath University Jaipur courses list includes a wide range of UG, PG, diploma, and PG diploma courses in the fields of engineering, management, architecture, agriculture, design, paramedical sciences, education, law, and many others. The university has a total of seven academic faculties to oversee all its academic activities. There are also separate departments for research and vocational studies. Jagannath University scholarship is also offered to students based on their merit and need. The university also offers scholarships for students belonging to specific categories like SC/ST, OBC, PWD, etc.

The Jagannath University Jaipur campus offers both academic and recreational facilities to its students for optimal development. The list of facilities at Jagannath University Jaipur includes a library, various departmental laboratories, seminar halls, an auditorium, a cafeteria, a yoga and meditation centre, and many others. The university also offers promising placement opportunities to its students. For example, Jagannath University placements 2024 had a median salary of Rs 4 lakh per annum with the highest package going up to Rs. 12 lakh annum.

Jagannath University Jaipur Admission 2026

Jagannath University Jaipur admission 2026 follows a 6-step online process — registration, application, entrance exam, interview/GD, document verification, and fee payment — managed through jagannathuniversity.org/admissions/apply. The university accepts JUEE 2026 (held on February 01) along with JEE Main, CUET (UG/PG), NEET, CAT, MAT, CMAT, GATE, CLAT, PTET, and Rajasthan JET, depending on the course. The application fee is INR 500 for UG, PG, and Diploma courses and INR 1,200 for PhD, payable via Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI. Required documents include Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, graduation/post-graduation mark sheets (for PG/PhD), entrance exam scorecard, Aadhaar, category certificate (if applicable), and passport-size photographs.

Jagannath University Jaipur Admission Process 2026

Jagannath University Jaipur admission process 2026 is a 6-step pipeline — register, apply, appear for the entrance exam, attend interview/GD, complete document verification, and pay the admission fee. The exact entrance exam differs by course (JUEE for in-house admission; CUET, JEE Main, NEET, CAT, CLAT for national-route admission).

The university follows a single online portal for all programs. Once an applicant clears the eligibility check and a valid entrance score is uploaded, the admission cell schedules an interview (and a GD for MBA). Below is the step-by-step admission process for 2026-27.

  • Step 1 — Choose your program: Visit jagannathuniversity.org/admissions/apply and pick the course from the dropdown. The portal lists 60+ programs across 7 faculties.
  • Step 2 — Register and fill the application: Enter personal, contact, 10th, 12th, graduation (if applicable), and entrance exam details. Upload scanned documents.
  • Step 3 — Pay the application fee: Pay INR 500 online for UG, PG, and Diploma courses. PhD applicants pay INR 1,200. Modes: Net Banking / Debit Card / Credit Card / UPI.
  • Step 4 — Appear for the entrance exam: Attempt the relevant exam — JUEE 2026 (Feb 01), JEE Main, CUET (UG/PG), CAT/MAT/CMAT, NEET, CLAT, GATE, PTET, or Rajasthan JET. JUEE is offline; venue is the Sitapura campus.
  • Step 5 — Interview / Group Discussion: UG and PG applicants attend a personal interview. MBA applicants attend a GD followed by PI. NEET-based BPT/BNYS admissions skip the interview after qualifying.
  • Step 6 — Document verification & fee payment: Selected candidates report at the Sitapura campus admission cell with original documents. Pay the first-semester fee within 7 days of the allotment letter to confirm the seat.

B.Tech, BBA, BCA, and B.Com admissions follow the CUET/JEE/JUEE-led route — flagship programs accept multiple national exam scores. B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture admission goes through Rajasthan JET or ICAR. MBA admission requires a CAT/MAT/CMAT scorecard or JUEE plus mandatory GD-PI. PhD admission is open across 7 disciplines based on UGC PhD Regulations 2022 — minimum 55% in Master's plus Entrance Test and Interview.

How to Apply for Jagannath University Jaipur Admission 2026 ?

Jagannath University Jaipur application process 2026 is fully online via jagannathuniversity.org/admissions/apply. Applications can also be downloaded from the website and submitted offline at the Sitapura campus admission cell with a Demand Draft of the application fee.

Below is the step-by-step application process for Jagannath University Jaipur admission 2026. The same portal handles all UG, PG, Diploma, and PhD applications.

  • Open the official admission portal at jagannathuniversity.org/admissions/apply (mention only — do not hyperlink).
  • Click on 'Apply Now (Online)' on the homepage banner that reads Admissions 2026-27.
  • Select your program from the dropdown — for example, B.Tech Computer Science Engineering or MBA (General-Dual Specialisation).
  • Fill in personal details — Name, Gender, Father's & Mother's Name, Aadhaar Number, Category (General / SC / ST / OBC / EWS / Minority / PH), Date of Birth, and Nationality.
  • Add contact details — Email, Mobile Number, Full Address, City, State, and Postal Code.
  • Enter academic details — 10th, 12th, Graduation, Post-Graduation, and any other qualifying exam (Institute, Subjects, Year, Total Marks, Marks Obtained, Percentage).
  • Upload scanned copies of mark sheets, ID proof, photograph, signature, and entrance exam scorecard.
  • Pay the application fee online — INR 500 for UG/PG/Diploma; INR 1,200 for PhD.
  • Submit the form and download the confirmation receipt for future reference.
  • Track entrance exam (JUEE 2026 / JEE / CUET / CAT) and counselling notifications on the same portal dashboard.

Required documents at the time of upload include 10th and 12th mark sheets, graduation mark sheet (for PG), category certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar, recent passport-size photograph, signature image, and the entrance exam admit card and scorecard.

Jagannath University Jaipur FAQs

How can we get admission to Jagannath University Jaipur?

The Jagannath University Jaipur selection process has two steps - an entrance test and a personal interview. For MBA admissions at Jagannath University, there is also a group discussion round. The university accepts various national-level entrance exams, depending on the course of your choice. It also conducts a separate university-level entrance called JUEE for admissions. Students who have cleared the prescribed entrance exams need to participate in the interview round for final admissions.

Does Jagannath University Jaipur accept CUET scores?

Yes, Jagannath University accepts CUET scores along with the scores of other popular national-level entrance exams. Some of the programmes in which the university provides admission through CUET UG are B.Tech, BCA, B.Des, B.Sc Agriculture, BBA, B.Com, BHM, and many others. The university also has a separate portal - https://www.jagannathuniversity.org/cuet.php - for students who want to apply through CUET scores.

What is the placement package of Jagannath University Jaipur?

As a private university, Jagannath University Jaipur puts extra emphasis on its placement opportunities. The university has not released the Jagannath University Jaipur placement report 2023 yet. However, we can get an idea about its previous years’ placement package from its NIRF report 2023. In 2022, the three-year UG students received the JU Jaipur median package of 3.05 lakh per annum, while the median package was Rs 4.15 lakh per annum for the two-year PG students.

What are the best courses offered at Jagannath University Jaipur?

Jagannath University Jaipur courses include courses at various levels such as UG, PG, diploma, PG diploma, and PhD. The courses at the university are offered in various disciplines such as engineering, management, law, agriculture, design, architecture, journalism, humanities, sciences, and many others. Some of the best courses at Jagannath University are B.Tech, MBA, M.Tech, diploma in engineering, B.Sc Agriculture, B.Des, M.Plan, MCA, B.Com, BA LLB, and many others.

Is Jagannath University good?

As a private university, Jagannath University Jaipur has a reputation in the region. The university offers various job-orientated professional as well as degree programmes at UG, PG, diploma, PG diploma, and PhD levels in a multitude of disciplines. At the same time, it focuses on effective learning, skill development, and industry exposure. Furthermore, the Jagannath University Jaipur placement record is proof of its excellence in the field of higher education.

What is the rank of Jagannath University Jaipur?

When it comes to Jagannath University ranking, the university regularly participates in the NIRF ranking process. At the same time, it has been ranked by several independent ranking bodies. For example, EduRank Rankings 2023 has ranked Jagannath University 11 out of 24 universities in Jaipur. At the same time, it has been placed at 37th position among 70 universities in Rajasthan. It has also been ranked 605 in India as per the EduRank 2023 Rankings.

Is Jagannath University government or private?

Jagannath University Jaipur is a private university in Jaipur, Rajasthan. This university operated under the aegis of the Jagan Nath Gupta Memorial Educational Society. However, the university is approved by several government higher education regulatory bodies such as the University Grants Commission (UGC), the Bar Council of India (BCI), the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), the Council of Architecture (COA), the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE).

When was Jagannath University Jaipur established?

Jagannath University Jaipur, a private university in Jaipur, was founded in 2008. The university was established by an Act of the Rajasthan State Legislature (Act No. 19 of 2008). Moreover, it is promoted by the Jagan Nath Gupta Memorial Educational Society. Since its inception, Jagannath University Jaipur has been aimed at providing quality education through the adoption of an industry-oriented curriculum, the latest teaching methodology, and the recruitment of the best faculty members.

Does Jagannath University provide scholarships?

At Jagannath University Jaipur, students can avail themselves of various scholarships to aid their higher education. The list of Jagannath University Jaipur scholarships includes various merit-based scholarships. It also offers scholarships to SC, ST, OBC, and PwD students. At the same time, the university offers special scholarships to all those students who have unfortunately lost an earning member in the family due to COVID. These special Jagannath University scholarships are offered in the form of a fee waiver of up to 100% based on the specified eligibility and merit. The university also has a Girlchild Scholarship Scheme.

What are some of the best facilities available at Jagannath University?

The university offers a holistic learning environment to its students through the Jagannath University Jaipur campus facilities. The main campus is spread across 85 acres and is home to various facilities and amenities. The list of facilities at Jagannath University includes spacious classrooms, a library, multiple laboratories, seminar halls, an auditorium, a gymnasium, a yoga and meditation centre, transport facilities, and sports facilities, among others.
