IIT Kharagpur Placements Latest Update

VGSOM IIT Kharagpur reported strong placements for the 2024–26 batch, recording the highest package of Rs 44.73 LPA. The average and median salaries stood at Rs 21.24 LPA and Rs 20.35 LPA, respectively. A total of 143 students were placed during the placement season, reflecting consistent hiring and competitive compensation across leading recruiters.

IIT Kharagpur Placements 2025

IIT Kharagpur placements 2025 achieved more than 1800 offers in the 2024-25 session through the Career Development Centre. The highest package reached Rs 2.14 Cr, with 1085 undergraduate students and 393 M.Tech students placed in roles across core engineering, IT, finance, and analytics sectors.

Over 400 companies visited, making 25 international offers. Students from B.Tech, dual degree, and MSc programs received 1811 total offers. Top recruiters included Apple, Capital One, DE Shaw, Google, Microsoft, Quantbox, Databricks, and Qualcomm.

IIT Kharagpur Placements FAQs

How many companies visit IIT Kharagpur?

In IIT Kharagpur placements 2024, 61 companies have visited the campus so far. During the 2023 placement season, more than 300 companies participated in the recruitment process. As the placement process for 2024 is still ongoing, the number of companies may increase. Some of the top companies that visit the campus include Goldman Sachs, Google, American Express, Microsoft, IBM, Morgan Stanley, Flipkart, and others.

What is the placement percentage at IIT Kharagpur?

IT Kharagpur placement percentage is very high, often exceeding 90% for most programmes. The exact percentage can vary each year based on the number of graduating students, the number of recruiters and the performance of the students. The placement percentage of B.Tech in computer science engineering in 2023 was 92.97%.

What is the highest package in IIT Kharagpur for B.Tech CSE?

For B.Tech CSE students at IIT Kharagpur, the highest package offered in 2024 is Rs 1 crore per annum. In 2023, the highest package for B.Tech CSE graduates was Rs 2.5 crore per annum. There is a strong demand for CSE graduates at top companies and MNCs.

What is the minimum package in IIT Kharagpur?

The minimum package at IIT Kharagpur varies depending on the programme and the year. As of recent placements, the minimum domestic package offered ranges from Rs 8-10 LPA.

Does IIT Kharagpur have 100% placement?

IIT Kharagpur placement percentage usually exceeds 90% in many programmes, but the exact percentage can vary each year. The placement percentage depends on factors such as the course opted by the candidate, individual student performance and the number of recruiters.

What is the highest package for B.Tech in IIT Kharagpur?

In 2024, the highest domestic package for B.Tech students at IIT Kharagpur is Rs 1 crore per annum. In comparison, the highest domestic package for B.Tech students in 2023 was Rs 2.68 crore per annum.

Which branch of IIT Kharagpur has the best placement record?

The Computer Science and Engineering branch at IIT Kharagpur is known for having the best placement record. CSE graduates frequently receive the highest salary offers and are highly sought after by leading technology companies and MNCs. In IIT Kharagpur placement of 2024 some of the CSE students received a salary packages as high as Rs 1 crore per annum and in 2023 the highest package was Rs 2.68 crore per annum.

Is IIT Kharagpur good for placements?

Yes, IIT Kharagpur is renowned for its strong placement record. The institute consistently attracts top recruiters from various industries, including technology, finance, consulting, and engineering. In the IIT Kharagpur placement, more than 700 job offers were made, with several students securing offers with the highest packages of Rs 1 crore per annum. The IIT Kharagpur placements 2023 were good, as the highest package recorded was the highest in the previous three years. The highest package in 2023 was Rs 2.68 crore per annum.

Who are the top recruiters of IIT Kharagpur?

Top recruiters at IIT Kharagpur include various MNCs, leading tech firms, and prominent financial institutions. Some of the top recruiters are Goldman Sachs, Google, American Express, Microsoft, IBM, Morgan Stanley, Flipkart, and others.
