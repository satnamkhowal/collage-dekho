About Amity University Noida Admission 2026

Amity University Noida admission 2026 is open for undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs. The university accepts applications online through its official portal at noida.amity.edu, with an application fee of INR 1,500 (non-refundable). Admissions to most courses are based on merit in the qualifying exam, followed by a personal interview. However, the university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores such as CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, and CMAT for MBA, and NATA for B.Arch.

At Amity University Noida, admissions are offered under three categories: Non-Sponsored, Sponsored, and Company Sponsored. The university also offers Fast Track Admission & scholarships to students scoring 90-95% or above in CBSE/ISC Class XII. Scholarships of up to 100% are offered on the academic fee for the first year, based on academic performance. Candidates with a high percentile in JEE Main/CUET/NEET/CLAT/CAT are also eligible for scholarships.

Amity University Noida Admission Process 2026

The Amity University Noida admission process 2026 includes registering on the official website, filling out the application form, paying the application fee, appearing for an entrance exam (if required), and attending a personal interview. 

  • Step 1: Visit noida.amity.edu and click 'Admissions'. Fill out the online Application Form and pay INR 1,500 (non-refundable).
  • Step 2: Upload the original class 10th mark sheet and latest passport-size photograph. Foreign students must also upload academic transcripts and a UGC Equivalence Certificate.
  • Step 3: The form is processed within 2–3 working days. Track your status on the Admission Microsite at portal.amity.edu (login with the registered mobile number).
  • Step 4: Shortlisted candidates receive a notification on the admissions microsite, by email, and via SMS to select a date for the Personal Interview (PI).
  • Step 5: Appear for the respective entrance exam or program-specific evaluation such as Creative Test, Physical Test, or subject-based assessment (if applicable). 
  • Step 6: Attend the Personal Interview process on the specified date.
  • Step 7: If selected, candidates will receive an admission letter on the admissions microsite, which includes fee details, commencement date, venue, hostel allotment (if applied), and scholarship information.
  • Step 8: Pay the fees using the payment links on the microsite and download the fee receipt confirmation. Admission is confirmed only after successful fee payment and document verification.
  • Step 9: Report on the commencement date with all original documents for verification. Your Amizone Login ID and Password are provided after fee payment.

Important Points for Amity University Admission Process

  • Candidates applying for MBA programs can submit scores of national-level entrance exams such as CAT, MAT, XAT, GMAT, or CMAT. However, these are not compulsory, as admission can also be granted based on merit and PI.
  • For B.Arch admission, candidates must qualify NATA, as it is a mandatory requirement as per national guidelines.
  • Amity University Noida also offers Fast Track Admission for students scoring 95% or above in CBSE/ISC Class 12, allowing them to skip the regular admission selection process (subject to eligibility).
  • Fast Track Admission is not available for Architecture, Design, Pharmacy, Bachelor of Physiotherapy, Education, Fine Arts, Fashion, Law, and Physical Education programs.
  • Scholarships of up to 100% on the academic fee (first year) are available for high-performing students based on Class 12 marks or valid scores in exams like JEE Main, CUET, NEET, and CLAT.

How to Apply to Amity University Noida

Amity University Noida’s application process for 2026 is fully online, requiring candidates to submit forms via the official admission portal with a Rs. 1,500 fee. The process is streamlined, with status updates within 2-3 days, leading to interview scheduling or direct admission for eligible high scorers. Follow these steps to apply seamlessly.

  • Register Online: Visit the Amity University admission portal, create an account, and fill out the application form with accurate academic and personal details.
  • Pay Application Fee: Submit Rs. 1,500 via net banking, UPI, or card; non-refundable even if eligibility is unmet.
  • Upload Documents: Attach Class X marksheet, recent passport-size photo, and additional documents like NATA/CLAT scores if applicable.
  • Track Status: Log into the microsite to check shortlisting within 2-3 days; schedule interviews or receive provisional offer letters.
  • Confirm Enrollment: Accept offers by paying the Rs. 20,000 academic security deposit and submitting the remaining documents by the specified deadline.

Amity University (AIHC), Noida FAQs

Can I take direct admission in Amity University Noida?

Yes, candidates can take direct admission to select seats for B.Tech courses if they have 90 percentile or above. Direct admission to certain UG and PG programs are also provided based on CUET percentile (above 90) or aggregate marks of 80% or above in class 12th.

What are some of the popular UG courses offered at Amity University Noida?

Some of the popular UG courses offered at Amity University Noida are: B.Tech, B.Sc, BBA, B.Com, B.Pharm, etc.

What is the admission criteria for entry into PG courses at Amity University Noida?

Amity University Noida admission process is either merit-based or via entrance exams, although the requirements differ depending on the course to be taken. Candidates must possess a Bachelor’s degree in any field from a recognized University with not less than 50% marks. Depending on the programme, the selection process may include entrance exams, interviews, and/or portfolio submissions. Amity alumni are given direct admission to many postgraduate courses without any entrance test.

Is Amity University Noida’s application process offline or online?

Amity University Noida application process is done completely online and candidate’s must visit the university’s microsite to get themselves registered and complete the online application process and pay the registration fee of INR 1500 to complete the initial part of the admission process for their respective courses.
