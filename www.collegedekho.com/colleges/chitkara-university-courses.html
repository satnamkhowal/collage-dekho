Chitkara University Courses 2026 Overview

Chikara University courses are offered at the undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctorate levels. At the undergraduate level, B.E. programmes are available in Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and other disciplines. The Chikara University Punjab campus offers around 100 courses across UG, PG, and doctoral levels. The institute also provides MBA, MPharma and several other programs at PG level and PhD programs at Doctorate level. The Chikara University fees range from Rs 2.50 lakhs to Rs 19 lakhs as a total fees depend on the program selected by the student.

Chitkara University Courses FAQs

What paramedical courses are offered at Chitkara University?

Chitkara University offers paramedical courses such as BMLS (Medical Laboratory Science), B.AOTT (Anaesthesia and Operation Theatre Technology), BMRIT (Medical Radiology and Imaging Technology), BPA (Physician Associate), and other paramedical programmes. The total fee for these four-year courses ranges from Rs 4.40 lakhs to Rs 5.20 lakhs.

Does Chitkara University offer nursing courses for undergraduate and postgraduate students?

Yes, Chitkara University offers nursing programmes such as BSc Nursing (Basic) for 4 years with a total fee of Rs 6 lakhs, Post Basic BSc Nursing for 2 years at Rs 1.80 lakhs, and MSc Nursing for 2 years at Rs 2 lakhs. To be eligible, candidates must have secured at least 50% marks in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

What is the online application process for admission to Chitkara University courses?

To apply for Chitkara University courses online, visit the official admission portal and complete the online application form. Upload the required documents, including Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, photograph, and relevant entrance exam scores. Pay the application fee and submit the form. Applicants can track their application status through the admission portal.

Does Chitkara University offer lateral entry admission to the BE programme?

Yes, Chitkara University offers lateral entry admission to the BE programme in the second year for diploma holders. Candidates must have completed a three-year diploma after Class 10 with at least 60% aggregate marks from a recognised board to be eligible.

Does Chitkara University offer admission to the BCA programme for candidates who have completed diploma?

Yes, Chitkara University offers BCA admission to candidates who have completed a three-year diploma. However, lateral entry admission is generally available only for BE programmes. For the BCA programme, diploma holders must take admission in the first year and complete the full three-year course as per university guidelines.
