About IEM Kolkata

Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata or IEM Kolkata is situated in Kolkata, West Bengal. It was founded in 1989. It is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with the grade ‘A’. IEM Kolkata is one of the best engineering and management colleges in West Bengal. IEM Kolkata departments like CSE, IT, ECE, MBA, and EE are accredited by the NBA. IEM Kolkata has won the title of the “Jewel of the East” by Telegraph. The college has been ranked the 3rd best engineering college in West Bengal and 79th in India by NIRF. It also won the title of “Picture Perfect” by ABP Group. The Best Institute of India in Star News Award was won by IEM Kolkata.

IEM Kolkata courses are affiliated to the Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT). IEM Kolkata’s courses include BBA, MBA, B.Tech, BCA, M.Tech, and PGDM. Various specialisations are offered for these courses. IEM Kolkata admission to these courses is based on different entrance exams. For some courses, the college conducts interviews as well. The IEM Kolkata fee structure is affordable. The teaching pedagogy of this institute is great because several of the students are toppers in the MAKAUT semester exams.

Institute of Engineering and Management Kolkata offers various facilities such as separate hostels for men and women, a well-stocked library, sports facilities, an auditorium etc. IEM Kolkata placements are exceptionally good. Every year, numerous reputed companies visit the campus during the campus placement drives. The top recruiters here are TCS, Wipro, Accenture, Google, Deloitte, IBM, Microsoft etc. Students of IEM Kolkata also receive multiple job offers during campus placements.

IEM Kolkata FAQs

Which entrance exams are accepted by IEM Kolkata?

IEM Kolkata accepted some entrance exams for admission to different courses. The entrance exams that are accepted here are JEE Main, WBJEE, JEMAT, CAT etc. The cut offs based on the scores of these exams guide the admission process. WBJEE/IEMJEE is accepted for B.Tech admission, and CAT for MBA admission.

Does IEM Kolkata have placement opportunities?

IEM Kolkata arranges placement drives where eminent recruiters visit the campus to recruit the students. The students get placement opportunities from them. Companies like Amazon, Deloitte, TCS, IBM, and Accenture visit the campus every year during the campus placement drives. The salary packages offered to the students are also lucrative.

Does IEM Kolkata have scholarships?

The Institute of Engineering and Management offers scholarships to meritorious students. The scholarships are offered to B.Tech and MBA students. There are different scholarship schemes. There is a special scholarship scheme called Pragati which is disbursed to eligible female students. Another scholarship named Saksham is dedicated to students who have disabilities. The amount of the scholarship depends on the marks the student has obtained.

Which facilities are offered by IEM Kolkata?

IEM Kolkata offers excellent facilities to the students and staff. These facilities include hostel facilities, a mess, sports facilities, a library, a cafeteria, modern labs, medical facilities etc. Besides these, the students have medical insurance here. The students can freely take part in the sports activities. There is free wi-fi available and transportation facilities.

What about the IEM Kolkata B.Tech cut-off?

IEM Kolkata B.Tech cut-off is based on WBJEE. For B.Tech admission, IEM Kolkata accepts WBJEE scores. This cut off determines the IEM Kolkata admission process. IEM Kolkata B.Tech cut off 2023 for WBJEE counseling round 1, 2, and 3 is out. B.Tech CSE (Artificial Intelligence) opening rank is 2496 and closing rank is 5609 for the open category.

How to take admission to the IEM Kolkata?

IEM Kolkata admission is mainly merit-based. On the other hand, IEM Kolkata admission to some courses is conducted on the basis of the cut-off of some entrance exams. For B.Tech admission, WBJEE ranks are required. The students have to make sure that they have obtained the required marks/percentage for the specific course. If the student meets the eligibility criteria, he or she can apply through the official website of the university.

What about the IEM Kolkata ranking 2023?

IEM Kolkata has ranked excellently in the past few years. In NIRF rankings 2023, IEM Kolkata has ranked 169th (in engineering). It has proved its excellence in academics and has competed well with the other reputed universities in India. In India Today 2023 rankings, it has secured 53rd position out of 246 for B.Tech.

Is IEM Kolkata recognised by UGC?

The IEM Kolkata is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is also recognised by the All-India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). It has received an ‘A’ grade from NAAC. It is a reliable institute teaching many popular UG, PG engineering and management courses. It is one of the leading engineering and management institutes in India.

Overall, IEM Kolkata is a good choice for those who are looking for an excellent learning environement, good placement opportunities, and good student facilities.

Dear Student, 

The MAKAUT CET 2025 exam was conducted on June 8, 2025, and the result was announced on June 28, 2025. You can expect the MAKAUT CET 2025 counselling schedule and choice filling to commence in July or August 2025. Seat allotment is then likely to be done in August 2025. The West Bengal University of Technology, also referred to as Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), typically announces the counselling schedule a few weeks after announcing the CET results. Usually, the counselling schedule is released on the official MAKAUT website (makautwb.ac.in) and the special counselling portal.

After publishing the notice, it will contain step-by-step instructions for registration, choice filling, seat allotment, and document verification. It's always best to check the official website frequently or subscribe to their updates so you don't miss any important announcements.
