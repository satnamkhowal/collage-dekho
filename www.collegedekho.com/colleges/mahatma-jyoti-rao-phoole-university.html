About Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University

Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University, also known as MJRPU, is a private university established in 2009. Located in Jaipur, Rajasthan, the Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University campus spans over 100 acres. The university is affiliated to itself and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), Bar Council of India (BCI), and National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE). Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University is ranked 775th in India by NIRF under University in 2025 and 139th for B.Tech by IIRF in 2025.

Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and doctorate programs in the streams of Engineering, Pharmacy, Management, Law, Agriculture, Arts, Commerce, Science, and Education. B.Tech, MBA, B.Pharm, and LL.B. are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University are based on merit and entrance examinations. The university accepts JET for B.Tech, CLAT for LL.B., CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT for MBA, and the university’s entrance exam for Ph.D.

For placements, Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University placement rate is approximately 70%. During the placement drive of 2024, the highest salary package offered was INR 20 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 3.5 LPA.

Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University Admission 2025

Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University admissions are based on merit in the qualifying examination and national/state-level entrance examinations. Students seeking admission at Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the university and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for Mahatma Jyoti Rao Phoole University admissions:

  • B.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech must appear for the JET/REAP/JEE Main and participate in the REAP Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT and participate in the university’s counselling process for the seat allotment process.
  • LL.B.: Candidates seeking admission to LL.B. must appear for the CLAT and participate in the university’s counselling process for the seat allotment process.
  • B.Sc (Hons.) Agriculture: Candidates seeking admission to B.Sc (Hons.) Agriculture must appear for the Rajasthan JET and participate in the JET Counselling for the seat allotment process.
