About GNA University

GNA University is a private university located in Phagwara, Punjab. The institute was established in 2014. GNA University has been recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).As per the NIRF Ranking 2024, GNA University has been ranked at 201-300 under the Engineering category. Other notable rankings include its performance in India Today and The Week rankings for various programs like MBA and B.Tech.Some of the key facilities at GNA University include state-of-the-art classrooms, modern laboratories, a large library, hostels, sports facilities, and placement support services.GNA University offers courses at Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Diploma levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the university include:

  • B.Tech (Engineering)
  • MBA (Business Administration)
  • M.Tech (Engineering)
  • BBA (Business Administration)
  • BCA (Computer Applications)
  • Ph.D. programs in various fields.

Admission to GNA University is based on merit or entrance exams, depending on the course. The university accepts entrance exams like CAT, MAT, XAT, GATE, and university-specific exams for various programs.Regarding placements, the highest package offered in 2023 was ₹12 LPA, and the average package offered was ₹3.5 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Cognizant, and HCL.

FAQs about GNA UNiversity-

Does GNA University offer scholarships?

Yes, GNA University offers several scholarships for eligible students. Even in cases when students might otherwise face financial obstacles, financial aid enables them and their families to pursue their education. Tuition fees are covered by this financial assistance for education. Meritorious students, children of defence personnel, single girls, children of GNA Group employees, and top-performing athletes in sports are all eligible for various sorts of financial aid. The GNA University scholarships are offered on a percentage basis subject to the degree of contingency. Further, the authorities also offer GNAU scholarships for JEE Main and MBA scores up to 100%. Moreover, the GNA University sibling scholarship is another financial aid granted to the students whose brother or sister is currently studying in the university.

How is it to study at GNA University?

GNA University is known to provide the best college experience to its students. As the university has been recently established, its infrastructure is modern and well-maintained. Students get to learn the latest pedagogy which is relevant in academics and classroom learning is always made fun at GNA University. The variety of courses and streams offered at the university makes the campus quite diverse and students get the chance to interact with people of different backgrounds, thus making it a culturally rich learning experience as well.

How good is GNA University ranking?

GNA University ranking is provided by various ranking bodies such as The Week, India Today, IIRF, Outlook and more. However, for the academic year 2023, the GNA University NIRF ranking has not been made available. GNA University overall IIRF ranking was 159 out of 171 in 2023, down from 143 out of 161 in 2022. Moreover, GNAU Phagwara has been ranked at 4371 out of 5830 universities in Asia. Further, In the R World Institutional Rankings 2023, GNA University achieved a GOLD Band (A GRADE).

How good is the B.Tech course at GNA University?

B.Tech is among the top courses offered by GNA University. GNA University B.Tech courses are offered in 7 disciplines including B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering, B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering, B.Tech in Electronics and Computer Engineering and more. The average B.Tech fees at GNA University is Rs 85,500 per semester. Moreover, B.Tech courses attract the maximum number of recruiters during the campus placement drive. As per the B.Tech placement records, companies like Infosys, TCS, L&T and Databricks hired many students to be a part of their esteemed organisation.

Is GNA University good for an MBA course?

Yes, the GNA University MBA course is among the top courses of the university. About 80% of MBA students are placed each year at a number of top organisations in India and abroad including HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Amazon and more. The scope of doing an MBA has drastically increased over the years because of placement opportunities overseas. Hence, students who pursue an MBA from GNAU Phagwara generally get placed at good organisations. The GNA University MBA fees range between Rs 75,000 to Rs 87,500 per semester. Further, for MBA students, GNA University scholarships are offered based on CAT scores.

Is GNA University good for placement?

GNA University placement is managed by a team of professionals who are a part of the GNAU placement cell. The placement cell organises all the placement-related activities for recruitment and internships. Right from the beginning, students are provided with pre-placement talks, webinars, seminars, group discussions, and personal interviews to groom students and make them placement-ready. The placement cell also has successfully maintained 500+ corporate associations for their students. Further, students are willing to pursue government exams are guided by the university for better preparations. Hence, it can be said that the placement at GNA University is good and the future of the students is safe at GNAU Phagwara.

Does GNA University have any anti-ragging policy?

The anti-ragging policy at GNA University is highly strong, and any student found in violation faces severe consequences. Senior faculty members who are on the university's anti-ragging committee determine the rules and regulations governing the policy and address any matters pertaining to this topic. Therefore, it is strictly forbidden for the students to engage in any form of ragging. Forms of ragging: Displaying boisterous, disorderly behaviour, teasing, excitement through harsh or rude attitudes or handling, including disruptive, unruly activities that cause or are likely to cause discomfort, unnecessary stress, physical harm, or psychological harm, or that incite apprehension in a new student.

What is GNAU fee structure?

GNA University fee structure varies for each course. Moreover, for some courses, the fees vary subject to the discipline. For example, the MBA in Finance fees is Rs 83,700 per semester. On the other hand, the MBA in Financial Services and Insurance fees is Rs 75,000 per semester. Students are provided with the complete GNA University course and fee details on this page. Students must note that the fees mentioned on the website are merely a tuition fee and students are required to pay other miscellaneous fees over and above these tuition fees. To support students with economically weaker backgrounds, students are offered various financial assistance on the basis of merit. Eligible students can apply for the scholarships and finance their tuition fees.

How is the hostel at GNA University?

GNA University hostels are equipped with all the necessary amenities like a water cooler, bed, fully-ventilated rooms, bed, mattresses, almirah, study table, geyzer, clean premises, clean washroom and quality food at the mess. The students are provided with a neat and clean environment at the hostels. Further, each hostel has a warden who takes care of their students and helps them whenever needed. Students have a fixed in and out time at GNA University hostels. The hostels are available for both boys and girls students. The GNAU Phagwara hostel fees is Rs 40,000 per student/ per semester and the hostels are located on campus for all students.

Is GNA University government or private?

GNA University is a privately owned university situated in Phagwara, Punjab. GNAU Phagwara is among the top private universities in north India. It was established in 2014 and it is UGC-approved and has received NAAC and PCI accreditation. The GNA University address is Sri Hargobindgarh, Phagwara Hoshiarpur Road, Punjab. The university believes in promoting universal education that equips each student to meet challenges, succeed at them, and be successful in all spheres of life. Its cutting-edge facilities, first-rate placement assistance, and committed instructors make it the most sought-after location for both academics and athletics.
