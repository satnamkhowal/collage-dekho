About Amity University Bengaluru

Amity University Bengaluru is a top private university. It focuses on creativity and innovation. The university grooms leaders who are skilled professionals and kind people. It blends modern learning with old values. Established in 2018 under Karnataka Act No. 16. Degrees are recognised by UGC for jobs and studies worldwide.

The campus sits in Bengaluru, Karnataka. It offers over 102 programmes. UG courses include BTech in CSE, BCom Hons, BSc Computer Science, and BA LLB. PG options have MBA in HR and Finance, MTech in Engineering. PhD programmes cover sciences and management.

Admissions need Class 12 with 55% for UG. Graduation with 50% for PG. Exams like JEE Main or CAT help for some courses. Fees range from INR 120K yearly for BA to INR 5.26L for MBA first year. Placements are strong with top companies like Amazon and Deloitte visiting. All eligible students get chances in management and tech. With a 98% placement rate, the median package stood at INR 12 LPA in 2025. Scholarships give up to 100% aid for high scorers above 95% in Class 12.
