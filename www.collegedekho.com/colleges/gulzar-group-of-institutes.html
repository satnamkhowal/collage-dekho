Gulzar Group of Institutes Admission Process 2025

Every year, the Gulzar Group of Institutes admissions for UG and PG courses begin in March. Candidates who are interested in a particular course can apply online. 

  • GGI Ludhiana B.Tech admission is based on the JEE Main, CET, or other state-level entrance exams.
  • Admission to the BHMCT programme is based on PUTHAT scores.
  • Aside from B.Tech and BHMCT, admission to UG courses is based on Class 12 results.
  • Class 12 results determine admission to GGI Ludhiana integrated courses.
  • Lateral entry admissions are also available for the B.Tech programme. The eligibility is a diploma after grade 10, i.e., 10+3. Candidates should have a minimum 50% aggregate in their diploma course.

Gulzar Group of Institutes Application Process 2025

GGI Online Admission Process

Candidates who want to secure Gulzar Group of Institutes Ludhiana admissions can apply through their official website. Candidates would be required to create an account first and then fill out all the mandatory information. Once done the candidate would have access to the application form, which they need to fill out and attach all the mandatory documents. While submitting the form the candidate needs to pay a nominal fee of Rs 1000, which can be paid via credit/debit cards or through UPI. Once submitting the GGI application form the candidate would receive an acknowledgement mail as well.

GGI Offline Admission Process

Candidates seeking admission at Gulzar Group of Institutes can also take admission via offline mode by visiting the admission cell directly. The candidate would be given an admission form which they need to fill out with all the required details. Candidates can clear any questions or doubts about a specific course at the Gulzar Group of Institutes admission cell itself. At the same time, submitting the form the candidate needs to pay a nominal fee of Rs 1000, which can be paid via credit/debit cards, net banking or through UPI. Once submitted, the admission cell would give a receipt of acknowledgement to the candidate.

Gulzar Group of Institutes Cutoff

Gulzar Group of Institutes cutoff is the minimum score required for admission in courses offered by the institute. Note that, for admission to courses other than BTech and MBA, a merit list is prepared. The Gulzar Group of Institutes merit list consist of names and details of the students selected for admission at the institute. The GGI cut off is affected by several factors such as the number of students who appeared for the entrance exam (in case of courses where entrance is required), the number of students who applied for the courses, the number of seats available at the institute and more. The Gulzar Group of Institutes BTech cut off is affected by the toughness level of JEE Main exam. Similarly, the GGI MBA cutoff is affected by entrance exams such as CAT/XAT and MAT.

How to get admission in hostel at Gulzar Group of Institutes?

Gulzar Group of Institutes hostel facilities are offered on a first come first serve basis. The seat intake of each hostel is limited hence the institute releases the Gulzar Group of Institutes hostel allotment list on the official website including the names and details of the students. The students are required to pay the hostel fee in the month of May every year with a booking amount of Rs 25,000. This ensures that students gain a spot in the hostel. Note that, the booking amount is adjusted in the annual hostel fees The hostels are clean and plenty of space is available in each room to offer comfort to the students. For the security of the students, a guard and watchman along with helpers are available 24/7 at the hostel.
