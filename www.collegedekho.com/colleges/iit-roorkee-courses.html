IIT Roorkee Courses and Fees 2026

IIT Roorkee fee for its courses ranges from Rs 8,980 to Rs 2,00,000, depending on the type of course. IIT Roorkee offers a total of 15 courses at Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), Dual Degree, and Doctoral (PhD) levels across streams like Engineering, Sciences, Management, Architecture, and Design. At the UG level, courses such as BTech, BArch, BDes, and Dual Degree (BTech + MTech) in various specialisations are offered. At the PG level, IIT Roorkee offers MTech, MBA, MSc, MDes, MArch, and Executive programmes. 

The MBA programme at IIT Roorkee is offered in various specialisations such as Finance, Marketing, Operations, Information Technology, and Human Resource Management, and MSc course is offered in specialisations like Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Some Frequently Asked Questions about IIT Roorkee Courses:

1. What is IIT Roorkee fees?

IIT Roorkee fee for its courses ranges from Rs 8,980 to Rs 2,00,000. The actual fee also depends on the type and duration of course.

2. Which branch of IIT Roorkee is best?

IIT Roorkee is best known for its Engineering branch. It is highly reputed for Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Architecture specialisations.

IIT Roorkee’s Department of Management Studies (DoMS) offers an MBA with specialisations in Finance, Marketing, HR, IT, and Operations. It combines IIT’s technical edge with management education and has good industry exposure and placement records.

4. How is IIT Roorkee Electrical Engineering?

Electrical Engineering at IIT Roorkee is among the top choices, with modern labs, strong research, and expert faculty. The branch has good placement opportunities in core industries, research, and IT sectors.

IIT Roorkee Courses FAQs

What types of courses does IIT Roorkee offer?

At the UG level, IIT Roorkee offers B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Tech, B.Arch, BSc+MSc, B.Tech+MBA , etc, while at the PG level, IIT Roorkee offers M.Tech, MSc, PGD, M.Arch, MBA, M.Plan, etc.

Which courses are popular at IIT Roorkee?

The most popular IIT Roorkee courses include the B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Tech, B.Arch, BSc+MSc, B.Tech+MBA and M.Tech programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Tech, B.Arch, BSc+MSc, B.Tech+MBA, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MSc, PGD, M.Arch, MBA, M.Plan , etc. IIT Roorkee also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does IIT Roorkee accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at IIT Roorkee. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech+M.Tech, B.Tech, B.Arch, BSc+MSc, B.Tech+MBA and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is IIT Roorkee easy to get into?

Admission in IIT Roorkee courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the IIT Roorkee Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include JEE Main, JEE Advanced, IIT JAM etc.

Does the IIT Roorkee (IITR) have an LLB programme?

What is IIT Roorkee hostel fees?

IIT Roorkee hostel fees for 2 living share and single basis, only for girls. The basic payment all the students have to make include the Mess fees and Hostel Security Deposit fees is INR 44,000 for female and INR 42,000 for male.

What is IIT Roorkee M.Tech in Mechanical & Industrial Engineering course duration ?

What are the payment methods to submit IIT Roorkee (IITR) fees?

The payment methods to submit IIT Roorkee fees are through - cash, demand draft (DD), bank transfer, netbanking and credit or debit card. You can avail both online and offline methods.

What is IIT Roorkee (IITR) MBA fees?

IIT Roorkee (IITR) fees for MBA is INR 8.28 Lakhs for a two year course. It is divided into four semesters, candidates with 50% in their 10+2 are eligible to apply.

Can IIT Roorkee fees be paid in cash?

Yes, at the IIT Roorkee fees can be paid in cash or via Demand Draft (DD).

What is IIT Roorkee (IITR) B.Tech fees?
