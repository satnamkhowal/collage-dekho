About IISC Bangalore

The Indian Institute of Science (IISC) Bangalore was established in 1909 and is the best research institute in India. It spans over an area of 440 acres in the city of Bangalore. At the UG level, IISc Bangalore offers popular courses like BSc. (Research) and BE / BTech. While at the PG level, courses like M.E. / MTech, MSc., MDes, and M.Mgt are offered. The institute also offers integrated programs like M.Sc. + Ph.D. and PhD programs in various fields. 

IISC is ranked among the top 100 institutions by the QS World rankings and Times Higher Education Ranking, receiving a rank of 100 In QS World Rankings and 99 in the engineering category of Times Higher Education Ranking. Also, it is ranked 1 in the NIRF ranking 2025. IISC Bangalore fees for UG courses range from 75,800 - 8.36 Lakhs and for PG courses it ranges between 43,400 - 5.18 Lakhs. Admissions to IISC Bangalore takes place through national level exams like JEE Main /Advanced or KVPY for BS, or GATE, JAM, JEST, CEED or UGC NET for MSc. GATE applications for this year have already been closed, for next year the applications will open in the last week of August with the exam being conducted in February 2026. JoSAA counselling based on JEE Mains/ Advanced scores is still ongoing. Candidates can check the official website of JoSAA to see their results.

IISc Bangalore has a separate placement cell known as the Office of Career and Counselling and Placement (OCCAP). The placements at IISc are highly sought-after, with many top companies offering great job offers to its students. The institute has a strong industry collaboration, with several reputed organizations offering internships, research collaborations, and recruitment opportunities. The IISc Bangalore placement cell reaches out to companies to invite them for final placements and handles all the placement activities. The placement cell also offers internship opportunities for students. The highest package offered at IISC was 86 LPA, while the average package stood at 28 LPA. The top recruiters of the university include Amazon, Bajaj, Finserv, Cisco, Pwc, Mahindra, Siemens etc.

IISc Bangalore Admission Process 2025

  • As per the IISc Bangalore admission process, candidates have to appear for JEE Advanced/IISER IAT for B.Tech course, GATE for M.Tech course, CAT/GMAT/GATE for MMS course and CEED for M.Des course.
  • Applications are made via the online admissions portal admissions.iisc.ac.in.
  • To register, select the option “New user to register click here” on the admissions portal and provide your name, date of birth, and email address.
  • Start filling out your application form by submitting the documents and paying the required application fee.
  • After logging into the portal, you will arrive at the “Application Status” page. Here, you can view both completed and incomplete applications.
  • To begin a new application, click on “Apply for another Programme” located in the “My Application” panel on the left side of the page.
  • Candidates meeting the IISc Bangalore cutoff will be eligible for the counselling process.
  • After the seat allotment, candidates have to pay the IISc Bangalore fees and get their documents verified.

IISc Placements

Registrations for IISc Bangalore placements begin after July every year. Campus placement opportunities are available to all IISc students, and registration is mandatory for those interested in placements through OCCaP. Pre-placement talks occur between August and September, with interviews scheduled from October onwards. As per the latest IISc Bangalore placement reports, the highest package offered was Rs 86 LPA and the medial package was Rs 28 LPA. Some of the top companies that participate in the IISc Bangalore placements are IBM, City Bank, Myntra, Tata Motors, Toshiba, Bajaj, Airtel, Intel, Mahindra, and Dell.

IISC Bangalore FAQs

Is IISc Bangalore better than IIT?

Recognised as an Institution of Eminence (IoE) by the Government of India, IISc consistently ranks among the top Indian universities in world university rankings. According to the NIRF Rankings 2023, IISc Bangalore is ranked #1 in the university category, surpassing all the prestigious IITs.

Is there any scholarship in IISc Bangalore?

IISc Bangalore provides scholarships/fellowships to students on the date of their joining. The amount of these scholarships vary according to the course chosen. The award value typically ranges from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 42,000.

What is the NAAC grade of IISc Bangalore?

Founded in 1909, IISc Bangalore is recognized by the UGC (University Grants Commission) and accredited by NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council). It holds a NAAC score of Grade AA+ (3.67).

IISc Bangalore is one of the top research universities in India, ranked at #211 in the QS World University Rankings of 2025. IISc Bangalore Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

IISc Bangalore has a diverse campus spanning 440 acres in Bengaluru, the heart of India's technology industry, known for aerospace, electronics, and information technology. In 2009, during its centenary, IISc expanded with a new campus in Challakere taluk, Chitradurga district, Karnataka, covering 1,500 acres. The university offers various infrastructural facilities to the students as per their convenience. The eateries at the campus are open from 8 A.M to 10.15 P.M.

IISc Bangalore Facilities

  • Hostels - IISc Bangalore campus provides separate hostel blocks for male and female students, accommodating over 3,000 students. Married students can choose from single or double-room apartments. Each hostel has various amenities such as a small indoor cricket ground, hallways, areas for cultural activities, and a bonfire zone. The rooms are well-equipped with big spacious balconies.
  • Gymkhana - IISc Bangalore has a Gymkhana that offers various facilities for indoor sports, outdoor activities, dance, music, a recreational library, and more, such as swimming pool, gym, kung fu classes, and a badminton court.
  • Health Centre - The Health Centre at the Indian Institute of Science, serves the staff and all students. It includes 2 emergency day care beds and 10 general beds for routine medical cases. Additionally, there is an isolation ward with 2 beds dedicated for  infectious diseases.

The application form is available from May 1 to June 6, 2025. The application fee is Rs 500 for General/OBC/EWS and Rs 250 for SC/ST/PwD.

The total fees for the first year are around Rs 30,200 for General students. This includes tuition, lab, library, and other fees.
