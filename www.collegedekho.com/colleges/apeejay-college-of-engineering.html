About Apeejay College of Engineering (ACE), Gurgaon

The Apeejay College of Engineering is situated in Gurgaon in the Haryana state of India. The college was established in 1997 with the accreditation of Government of Haryana, DTE & AICTE. The college is affiliated with Maharishi Dayanand University, Rohtak. The Apeejay College, Gurgaon provides 4 courses in Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.). The campus of the ACE campus is spread over 23 Acres. In which two Academic Block, Library, Labs, Sports etc. are available in the college premises. In the athletes, the college had won 21 medals in Haryana State level Track and Field events held in February 2002. The College also holds the Annual Sports & track events to promote students for the state level competitions.
