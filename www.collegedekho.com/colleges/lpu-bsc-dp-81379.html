About LPU BSc

LPU BSc is a 2 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for a B.Sc. degree at Lovely Professional University, applicants must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board with a minimum of 45% to 60 % marks in aggregate depending on the chosen specialisation. The annual fee for BSc is INR 1,00,000 - 7,00,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for LPU BSc admission are given below:

LPU BSc Fees

LPU BSc fees is Rs 1,00,000 and goes up to Rs 7,00,000 Annually depending on the specialization. LPU BSc admission 2026 will be based on UPCATET, UCEED, LPUNEST, CUET.
