About K.R. Mangalam University BPT

K.R. Mangalam University BPT is a 4 Years year program. To be eligible for the Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) degree at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurgaon, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in the qualifying examination. Candidates must also clear any entrance exams or interviews conducted by the university for admission to the BPT program. Meeting these eligibility criteria ensures that students have the necessary academic background and skills to succeed in the rigorous curriculum of the BPT degree at K.R. Mangalam University. The annual fee for BPT is INR 1,58,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for K.R. Mangalam University BPT admission are given below:

K.R. Mangalam University BPT Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) degree at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurgaon, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as compulsory subjects. Additionally, they must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in the qualifying examination. Candidates must also clear any entrance exams or interviews conducted by the university for admission to the BPT program. Meeting these eligibility criteria ensures that students have the necessary academic background and skills to succeed in the rigorous curriculum of the BPT degree at K.R. Mangalam University.

K.R. Mangalam University BPT Admission 2026

Admission to the BPT at K.R. Mangalam University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the K.R. Mangalam University BPT admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

K.R. Mangalam University BPT Application Process

K.R. Mangalam University BPT application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BPT course at K.R. Mangalam University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill K.R. Mangalam University BPT application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of K.R. Mangalam University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of K.R. Mangalam University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the K.R. Mangalam University BPT application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the K.R. Mangalam University BPT application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of K.R. Mangalam University BPT Application Form.

Documents Required for K.R. Mangalam University BPT Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
