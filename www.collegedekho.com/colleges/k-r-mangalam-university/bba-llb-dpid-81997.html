About K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB

K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB is a 5 Years year program. To be eligible for the BBA+LLB degree program at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurgaon, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Additionally, applicants must have a valid score in CLAT or any other national-level law entrance exam. It is also essential for students to possess strong communication skills, critical thinking abilities, and a keen interest in pursuing a career in the field of business and law. Meeting these criteria will ensure that students are well-prepared to excel in the rigorous academic environment of K.R. Mangalam University's BBA+LLB program. The annual fee for BBA+LLB is INR 1,85,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for K.R. K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the BBA+LLB degree program at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurgaon, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Additionally, applicants must have a valid score in CLAT or any other national-level law entrance exam. It is also essential for students to possess strong communication skills, critical thinking abilities, and a keen interest in pursuing a career in the field of business and law. Meeting these criteria will ensure that students are well-prepared to excel in the rigorous academic environment of K.R. Mangalam University's BBA+LLB program.

K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB Admission 2026

Admission to the BBA+LLB at K.R. Mangalam University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB Application Process

K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BBA+LLB course at K.R. Mangalam University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of K.R. Mangalam University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of K.R. Mangalam University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB Application Form.

Documents Required for K.R. Mangalam University BBA+LLB Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
