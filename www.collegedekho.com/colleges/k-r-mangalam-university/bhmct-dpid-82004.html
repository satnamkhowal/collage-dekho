About K.R. Mangalam University BHMCT

K.R. Mangalam University BHMCT is a 4 Years year program. To be eligible for the Bachelor of Hotel Management and Catering Technology (BHMCT) degree at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurgaon, candidates must have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Additionally, candidates must have a valid score in the university's entrance exam or any other national-level entrance exam approved by the university. It is also essential for candidates to have a strong interest in the hospitality industry and possess good communication skills. Meeting these criteria will ensure that students are well-prepared to excel in the BHMCT program at K.R. Mangalam University. The annual fee for BHMCT is INR 1,95,000. About the Course

The Bachelor in Hotel Management and Catering Technology is a four-year undergraduate program designed to equip students with the essential skills for a successful career in the hospitality industry. The course blends classroom learning with practical training, preparing students for roles in hotels, restaurants, and various service sectors worldwide.

Students gain expertise in areas such as food production, beverage service, front office operations, housekeeping, and overall hotel management. The program also features guest lectures from industry experts, international learning opportunities, paid internships, and entrepreneurship support for those interested in starting their own hospitality ventures.

Admission to the BHMCT at K.R. Mangalam University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the K.R. Mangalam University BHMCT admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

K.R. Mangalam University BHMCT Application Process 

K.R. Mangalam University BHMCT application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BHMCT course at K.R. Mangalam University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill K.R. Mangalam University BHMCT Application form:

  • Start your Application: Begin your application process at admissions.krmangalam.edu.in
  • Complete Payment and Submit Application: Pay the required application fee and submit your completed application.
  • Take the Entrance Test or Submit Valid Scores: Take the K.R. Mangalam University Entrance Examination (KREE) or submit valid standardised test scores (if applicable).
  • Attend the Faculty-Led Interview: Participate in a personal interaction session with our esteemed faculty.
  • Receive Your Admission Offer: Upon successful completion of the interview, you will receive your admission offer letter.
  • Enroll and Start Your Journey: Complete the enrollment process and get ready to embark on your educational journey with us.

K.R. Mangalam University BHMCT Course Career Options

Graduates of the BHMCT program have a wide range of career opportunities in the dynamic hospitality industry. The knowledge and practical skills gained throughout the course prepare students for roles in hotels, restaurants, event management, and other service sectors. Curious about the earning potential in hotel management? Below are five popular career paths along with their average salaries:

Food and Beverage Manager

  • What they do: Oversee the food and beverage operations in hotels or restaurants. Their responsibilities include menu planning, supervising orders, staff training, and ensuring seamless daily operations.
  • Average Salary: ₹4 – ₹6 LPA (with potential for higher earnings as experience grows)

Operations Manager

  • What they do: Manage the day-to-day operations of a hotel or restaurant, including staff supervision, budgeting, and ensuring services are delivered efficiently and on schedule.
  • Average Salary: ₹5 – ₹7 LPA

Customer Relationship Manager

  • What they do: Develop and nurture positive connections with guests or clients, focusing on delivering outstanding service to ensure customer satisfaction and loyalty.
  • Average Salary: ₹3.5 – ₹5.5 LPA

Event Manager

  • What they do: Coordinate and manage events such as weddings, conferences, and corporate meetings, ensuring smooth setups and a great experience for all attendees.
  • Average Salary: ₹4 – ₹6 LPA (with freelancers and business owners often earning more)

Housekeeping Manager

  • What they do: Ensure that guest rooms and public spaces in hotels or resorts are clean, organized, and well-maintained. They also supervise housekeeping and laundry teams.
  • Average Salary: ₹3 – ₹5 LPA

These are just a few career options—BHMCT graduates can also find opportunities in airlines, cruise ships, resorts, or even launch their own businesses.

Please note: The salaries mentioned are approximate figures collected from various online sources and may vary.
