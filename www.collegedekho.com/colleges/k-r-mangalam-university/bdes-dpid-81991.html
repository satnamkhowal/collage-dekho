About K.R. Mangalam University B.Des

K.R. Mangalam University B.Des is a 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a Bachelor of Design (B.Des.) degree at K.R. Mangalam University Gurgaon, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate score of 50%. Additionally, they should have studied Arts, Science, or Commerce as their main subjects. Admissions are based on merit, and candidates may be required to appear for a design aptitude test followed by a personal interview. This program offers a comprehensive curriculum that equips students with the necessary skills and knowledge in various design disciplines, making it an excellent choice for those aspiring to excel in the field of design. The annual fee for B.Des is INR 2,10,000 - 2,40,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for K.R. Mangalam University B.Des admission are given below:

K.R. Mangalam University B.Des Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Des at K.R. Mangalam University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the K.R. Mangalam University B.Des admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

K.R. Mangalam University B.Des Application Process

K.R. Mangalam University B.Des application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Des course at K.R. Mangalam University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill K.R. Mangalam University B.Des application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of K.R. Mangalam University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of K.R. Mangalam University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the K.R. Mangalam University B.Des application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the K.R. Mangalam University B.Des application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of K.R. Mangalam University B.Des Application Form.

Documents Required for K.R. Mangalam University B.Des Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

Overall My overall experience at KR Mangalam has been quite positive. The college provides a good learning environment with supportive faculty and decent academic structure. There are regular activities, workshops, and events that help in personal and professional growth. While there is still scope for improvement in certain areas, the exposure and opportunities given to students make it a good place to study and build skills.

Placement The placement opportunities at the college are decent, with several companies visiting the campus every year. The placement cell is supportive and organizes training sessions, workshops, and mock interviews to prepare students. While top-tier company opportunities can be limited, students who actively work on their skills and portfolios have good chances of getting placed. More industry tie-ups and higher package offers can further improve the placement experience.

Infrastructure The infrastructure of the college is well-maintained and modern. Classrooms are spacious and equipped with basic facilities. The campus is clean, with good seating areas and greenery. Labs and studios are functional, especially for design students. However, some areas like Wi-Fi connectivity and maintenance can be improved for a better overall experience.

Faculty The faculty at the college is knowledgeable and supportive. Most teachers are approachable and help students with doubts and guidance related to academics and projects. They focus on both theoretical and practical learning, which is helpful for skill development. However, teaching methods of some faculty members can be more interactive and updated to match industry standards. Overall, the faculty contributes positively to student growth.

Hostel The hostel facilities are decent and provide a comfortable living environment for students. Rooms are manageable and basic amenities like water, electricity, and security are properly maintained. The mess food is average but hygienic. Wardens are supportive and ensure discipline. However, improvements can be made in maintenance, Wi-Fi connectivity, and recreational facilities to enhance the overall hostel experience.
