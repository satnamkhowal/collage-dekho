K.R. Mangalam University Placements 2025

K.R. Mangalam University has designed its curriculum in such a way that it makes the students industry-ready through placements. KRMU’s “Campus to Connect” initiative is a mutually rewarding and sustainable relationship between esteemed multinational companies and institutions across the industries. This “Connect” helps the students at the university to seize great job opportunities in leading companies. It also is beneficial for corporates as they get a chance to select from a rich pool of talented students to work for their companies. 

Every year many recruiters (some new and some regular) from various sectors visit the KRMU campus. Along with Placements, the university organizes various events such as - The Corporate Talk Series, Guest Lectures, Internships, Live Projects, and Case-Study Competitions to enable the students to actively engage themselves with the corporate world.

Almost around 800+ recruiters have visited the campus of K.R. Mangalam University, and 2000+ offers have been made to date. The highest package offered is Rs 56.6 LPA per annum and up to 92% of the students have been placed. 

KRMU arranges HR Conclaves and symposiums for the students to allow them to interact with industry experts. This helps them gain insight and make important decisions about their professional carrier. 

K.R. Mangalam University placements and training team train the students to feel more confident during the interview process and help them in training, skill development, personality development, and communication skills. It also helps in enhancing the skills like resume writing, workplace etiquette, and stress management skills.

K.R. Mangalam University alumni state that the training and placement cell helped them in getting good opportunities to work with some of the top companies. The dedication of the faculty members towards the guidance for their bright professional careers is commendable. 

Various activities conducted by the Training and Placement Cell of the university are:

  • Training - A Memorandum of Understanding has been signed with many renowned companies and institutions in diverse sectors to help the learners apply the practical skills to tackle real-life problems.
  • Projects - Project-based learning helps the students complete their research projects and apply innovative solutions to fix industry-specific problems. This helps in ensuring the project’s relevance and helps the students get familiar with the existing requirements of the industries.
  • Corporate Life - In-house training is organized for the students to understand the hierarchical structure of the companies. Mentors will help the students to acquire hands-on experience with job responsibilities. This makes the students industry-ready by applying theoretical and technical skills. 

The director of the KRMU placements and career development centre currently is Mr. Shivkant Sharma. He has over 22 years of experience in training and placement activities. If we look back towards the glorious past, he has the presence of his ex students in FMCG, IT, Tyre, Retail, Big 4, Banking, Consulting, Automobile, Electronic etc. industries.

K.R. Mangalam University Placements 2025

One of the main motives behind starting the placement and training cell at K R Mangalam University Gurugram is to make students ready for the industry and professional world by boosting their confidence and helping them identify their dream careers. The placement and training cell conducts mock interviews to train students. This process helps students understand what to expect during the recruitment drive and helps them identify their improvement areas. The placement cell informs the students beforehand regarding the companies which will be visiting the campus so that students are well prepared.

If any student has failed the final year, the company which hired the candidate will reject the offer. The KRMU placement and training team prepares students well in advance in various courses ranging from group discussion to one-on-one personal interviews.

The main pointers regarding the activities included in the K.R. Mangalam University placement process are:

  • The placement team will hold personal interactions with the candidates
  • Standard Resume formats will be shared with the students to aid them in developing one for themselves
  • The placement team trains the students to face the recruitment process by organizing Group discussions and having mock interview sessions.
  • They enable the MNCs and HR Agencies to visit the campus for providing the students with placement opportunities

K.R. Mangalam University Placement Policy

Various placement activities are often organized by the Corporate Resource Centre (CRC) at K R Mangalam University. This helps in generating abundant placement opportunities for the students eligible for placements. CRC often conducts several counselling and training programs to enroot academic excellence. These programs help the graduates to acquire the required and necessary skills for lucrative job offers.

The training and Placement cell invites corporate recruiters and HR agencies every year to the campus to offer the dream jobs to the students with attractive packages.

The main highlights of the KRMU placement policy are:

  • Recruitment drives will be organized by the Corporate Resource Centre only for the students of K R Mangalam University. 
  • The students can register themselves for campus placements as early as the commencement of the final year of their respective programs.
  • The placement drive will start in September every year.
  • Orientation and the placement policy for all the registered students will be organized at the beginning of academic sessions. This will help in providing insights into the policies and guidelines of the CRC. Introduction to the placement team will be organized during the beginning of the academic session.

K.R. Mangalam University Gurugram Placement Cell 

The placement office at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurugram, helps the students from resume writing to group discussions which also covers a one-on-one interview with the placement team. The placement and training officer ensures that the students can maintain their calm under pressure situations. Often, the interviewers create hypothetical situations before the students to evaluate their problem-solving and analytical skills under pressure situations.

The Training and Placement cell at K.R. Mangalam University aids campus recruitment by providing a common platform for job seekers. It hosts organizations across the geographies of many industries to participate in the placement process. The T&P cell not only assists students in landing jobs in relevant organizations but also assists the students in career planning and final placement preparation. 

The training and placement cell of KRMU coordinates with the senior executives of reputable organizations to develop effective communication linkages with several significant industrial and professional groups.

The training and placement cell is putting in every effort to match the students’ career goals with the professional needs of these organizations. KRMU provides a platform for students looking for jobs to connect with potential employers on and off-campus. T&P cell is responsible for establishing the year's recruitment program, inviting employers for pre-placement talks on campus, final placement, and completing the process.

Some of how the KRMU Gurugram placement cell aids the students are:

  • It provides career counselling through expert lectures by senior corporate executives, consultants, and soft skill development consultants.
  • The cell also helps develop a student's personality and soft skills, such as resume writing, and communication ability, to prepare them for recruiting.
  • The training and placement cell has been working on developing and finding innovative solutions on how to keep up with the drastic changes in technology, management methodology, and human resources. 
  • The cell works on providing employment opportunities to the students in the public as well as private sectors.

K.R. Mangalam University Placements FAQs

Does SBAS, KR Mangalam University have any provisions for the preparation of Civil Services?

Yes, the School of Basic and Applied Science (SBAS) provides IIT JAM Coaching and the Induction for Civil Service preparation to all of the undergraduate students. This facility by SBAS makes it special and sets it apart from its competitors. The students who are interested to build their careers in Civil Services will be hugely benefitted by studying at KRMU.

What can I do after completing the B Pharma course from KR Mangalam University?

B Pharma is a job-oriented course in which students are trained in diverse aspects of the pharmaceutical industry to ensure the best pharmacy career options are available to the B pharma graduates. Some of the career options are drug inspector, health inspector, medical underwriter, pathological lab scientist, chemical technician, and many more.

What are the top companies hiring BHMCT degree holders from KR Mangalam University, Gurgaon?

They are required in many areas that can be directly and indirectly associated with Hotel Management and Catering. Some of the areas where Bachelor of Hotel Management and Catering Technology get jobs are Oberoi Groups of hotels, ITC Group of Hotels, Welcome Heritage Groups of Hotels, and many other top hotels and catering services.

What are the career options after completing a Bachelor of Science (Honours) Interior Design degree from KR Mangalam University?

After obtaining a Bachelor of Science (Honours) Interior Design degree, you would have multiple career options to choose from. You could build your career as an Interior and Spatial Designer, Production Designer, Exhibition Designer, or an Architectural lighting experts or Lighting designer. You could also take up higher studies and get deeper into academics, or work for the government sector.

Does the K.R. Mangalam University provide sufficient placement opportunities to its students?

Yes, the K.R. Mangalam University provides an abundance of placement opportunities to its students once they complete their studies. The university has tie-ups with several industry giants and well-known enterprises like HCL, CPA Global, Oyo, Paytm, Magic Meters, News On Dot. BookMyForex, Indiabulls, Husys, LawRato, and Airtel.

What is the entry-level salary package for BCA with specialization in AI and Data Science graduates?

The average entry-level salary for BCA with specialization in AI and Data Science graduate may range from INR 5,00,000 to INR 8,00,000.

What are the career options after completing a Master of Computer Applications degree from KR Mangalam University?

After a Master of Computer Applications degree, you can go into academics. You can also take up job roles like that of a Full Stack Web Developer, Mobile Application Developer, Software Engineer, Systems Architect, Machine Learning Engineer, etc. This is a very lucrative field and you can find endless kinds of job opportunities in this area. You can also find a high-paying job through these opportunities.

Are there any internship/ placements opportunities for CSE students at KR Mangalam University?

Yes, the college has signed many Mou's with large and small scale IT industry for providing students with a platform to gain skills and train them according to the dynamic environment so that they can adopt new skills.
