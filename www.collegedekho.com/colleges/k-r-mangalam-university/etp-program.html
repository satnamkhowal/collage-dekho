K. R. Mangalam University Job Ready Degree

One of the biggest concerns for students today is placement after completing a degree. K. R. Mangalam University addresses this by offering industry-focused, job-ready programmes designed in collaboration with industry partners. 

The university offers specialised programmes in B.Tech and B.Des with academic support from ImaginXP: 

  • B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (UX/UI)  
  • B.Des in UX/UI & Interaction Design  
  • B.Des in Game Design & Animation  

These programmes are structured to align with current industry standards and ensure students are job-ready from day one. As per the latest fee structure, these programmes fall in the range of approximately Rs 2,60,000 to Rs 2,70,000 annually. 

Additionally, all these programmes are now offered as 4-year degree programmes (Hons./Hons. with Research), providing deeper academic and practical exposure.

K. R. Mangalam University Job Ready Degree 2026 – Admissions 

Admission to these programmes is based on entrance exam performance and eligibility criteria set by the university. 

  • For B.Tech, candidates can apply through JEE Main / CUET or university criteria  
  • For B.Des, candidates may be evaluated based on design aptitude/entrance criteria as applicable  

Candidates must meet the eligibility requirements and follow the admission process prescribed by the university. 

K. R. Mangalam University Job Ready Placements – Average CTC 

  • B.Des in UX/UI & Interaction Design (IXP): Average starting salary around Rs 12 LPA  
  • B.Des in Game Design & Animation (IXP): Average starting salary around Rs 12 LPA  
  • B.Tech in CSE (UX/UI) (IXP): Average starting salary ranges between Rs 12 LPA – Rs 15 LPA
