About K.R. Mangalam University PhD

K.R. Mangalam University PhD is a 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To be eligible for a PhD degree at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurgaon, candidates must have a Master's degree in a relevant field from a recognized university with a minimum of 55% marks or an equivalent CGPA. Additionally, candidates must pass the university's entrance examination and interview process. Prior research experience and publications in reputed journals will be considered favorably during the selection process. Candidates with a strong academic background, research aptitude, and a passion for contributing to their field of study are encouraged to apply for the PhD program at K.R. Mangalam University. The annual fee for PhD is INR 1,20,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for K.R. K.R. Mangalam University PhD Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a PhD degree at K.R. Mangalam University, Gurgaon, candidates must have a Master's degree in a relevant field from a recognized university with a minimum of 55% marks or an equivalent CGPA. Additionally, candidates must pass the university's entrance examination and interview process. Prior research experience and publications in reputed journals will be considered favorably during the selection process. Candidates with a strong academic background, research aptitude, and a passion for contributing to their field of study are encouraged to apply for the PhD program at K.R. Mangalam University.

K.R. Mangalam University PhD Admission 2026

Admission to the PhD at K.R. Mangalam University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the K.R. Mangalam University PhD admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

K.R. Mangalam University PhD Application Process

K.R. Mangalam University PhD application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the PhD course at K.R. Mangalam University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill K.R. Mangalam University PhD application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of K.R. Mangalam University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of K.R. Mangalam University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the K.R. Mangalam University PhD application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the K.R. Mangalam University PhD application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of K.R. Mangalam University PhD Application Form.

Documents Required for K.R. Mangalam University PhD Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Mark sheet of graduation
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
