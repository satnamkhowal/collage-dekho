K.R. Mangalam University Admission 2026

K.R. Mangalam University admission will be done on the basis of various entrance exams like JEE Main, CLAT, NATA, UPSEE, UGAT, CAT, MAT, etc. Apart from this, the university also conducts its own admission test for which students need to fill the K.R. Mangalam University application form through the site admissions.krmangalam.edu.in.  K.R. Mangalam is also accepting the Central Universities Entrance Test (CUET) for admission to its UG and PG courses. CUET is a common entrance examination that enables admission to many central, private, and deemed universities.

K.R. Mangalam University Gurugram Eligibility Criteria 2026

K.R. Mangalam University course admission requires the applicants to qualify for the entrance examinations for an undergraduate degree, and diplomas. For Postgraduate degrees, the candidates who are applying should hold a valid bachelor’s degree or an equivalent from a state or central recognized university with at least 50% aggregate marks.

KRMU courses include bachelor's and master's degrees, diplomas, and doctoral courses across hundreds of subjects. Read on to know more about how a student can get admission to KRMU Gurugram.

K.R. Mangalam University Gurugram Selection Criteria 2026

Like any other University, even K.R. Mangalam University Gurugram has a set of specific selection criteria that a student should fulfil to be eligible for admissions. Here are some of the main pointers for eligibility:

  • 12th Marks: 

Candidates who have passed the 10+2 examinations from a state or central recognized boards like CBSE, ISC, IB or Cambridge are qualified for admission. . Along with 10+2 scores, the candidates should have a scorecard of any of the following entrance examinations such as AIEEE /AISEEE /UPSEE /UGAT /CAT /MAT /SAT /GMAT /ENAT /EMAT.  For several courses, just the passing certificate of 10+2 examination becomes a qualifying criterion.

  • Graduation Marks: 

Candidates who wish to apply for Postgraduate courses should have passed graduation with relevant streams from a recognized university with at least 50% aggregate marks. They have to fill out the application form and pay the fees. 

  • Other Methods:

The candidate must ensure meeting the required entrance examination cut-offs for certain courses, etc. In addition, when they fill out the application form for admission at KRMU, a test link will be sent to the registered email ID. On completion of the test, there will be personal interviews with one of the faculty members. 

K.R. Mangalam University Official Admission Portal

To ease the application procedure, K.R. Mangalam University has put up a virtual assistant “Prodigy” to help the candidates with admission queries. The admission portal of KRMU is very user-friendly. Interested candidates must fill out an application form on the official website. 

Before applying for any course at KRMU, candidates are suggested to read all guidelines of K.R. Mangalam University admissions. The portal will ask for basic details, such as personal information, education details, address, etc. The applicants should provide scanned copies of the required documents, provide the payment details, and pay the application fee. 

K.R. Mangalam University Application Process for Admission 2026

Admissions for the academic year 2024-2026 are now open at K.R. Mangalam University. The admission process can vary from course to course. For some courses, admissions are granted based on the scores of entrance exams, for some admission is granted based on merit (marks in the last qualifying exams).

The K.R. Mangalam University admission process for the courses is very crisp and simple:

  • Candidates should go to the official website of KRMU Gurugram.
  • Candidates should click on the “Apply Now” link on the home page. Alternatively, they can select the highlighted “APPLY NOW 2026” Tab at the top right of the home page.
  • Candidates must register first by providing Contact details like a mobile number and an email address. The credentials for login will be sent to your provided mobile number.
  • Email ID once registered can’t be changed under any circumstances and will be used for all the correspondences.
  • To fill out the application form next, the candidates should log in to their accounts. They can log in either via mobile number and password or they can log in via mobile number and OTP.
  • Fill out the application form by providing the requested details, uploading the documents, making the payment, and uploading the payment proof.

To ensure quick responses, K.R. Mangalam University has Query Management System. To use Query Management System, one should follow the following steps:

  • Registering and verifying the email ID
  • Click on the link “Any Queries? Ask us” on the candidate’s dashboard.
  • Submit your query by selecting the appropriate category.

How to Get Admission to K.R. Mangalam University Gurugram (KRMU) for various courses in 2026?

The K.R. Mangalam University has started the application process for the January session admission to any course for the academic year 2026. Students satisfying the KRMU eligibility criteria will be selected for admission. 

The applicants must validate their phone number and email id before applying for any of the courses at K.R. Mangalam University. All the important information and updates regarding admission and the grievance are sent by the university over an email id and mobile number.

Candidates must follow the given steps to apply for K.R. Mangalam University Admission 2026.

  • Visit the official website of K.R. Mangalam University
  • Click on the admission tab
  • Click on the Freshers/ first-year admission or lateral entry/ promoter admission tab as per your preference
  • Click on the 'Apply Now 2026' section and choose the course of your preference 
  • Fill out the KRMU University Admission application form
  • Fill in all the requested and mandatory fields asked for admission accurately (Name, Communication Address, Subject Combination, etc.)
  • Upload the scanned copy of the passport size photo and signature in the prescribed format.
  • Review all the details meticulously before submission and finally submit the application form.

K.R. Mangalam University Admission Process 2026 

  • After submitting the application form, candidates must pay the application fee of ₹1,000 through online modes such as internet banking, UPI, credit/debit card, or other available payment gateways. This fee is non-refundable.  
  • Candidates are advised to download and keep a copy of the application form and fee receipt for future reference, including registration details. 
  • Once the application is reviewed, eligible candidates may be invited for the next stage of the selection process, which may include an entrance test and/or personal interview, depending on the programme.  
  • Candidates must attend the interview or assessment as per the scheduled time slot.  

Upon selection, candidates are required to:  

  • Submit all necessary documents  
  • Pay a registration fee of ₹25,000 (adjustable in the programme fee)  
  • Pay a refundable security deposit of ₹10,000  

For hostel applicants:  

  • Hostel Security Deposit: ₹20,000 (refundable)  
  • Hostel Fee: Approximately ₹1,72,000 per annum (may vary based on room type and facilities)  

Additional optional charges:  

  • Transport Fee: Approximately ₹54,000 per annum  
  • Shuttle Service Fee: Approximately ₹27,000 per annum

K.R. Mangalam University Admission FAQs

What is the application fee of KRMU Gurgaon?

The application fee of KRMU Gurgaon is INR 900. The application fee once submitted will not be refunded.

What is the selection process of KR Mangalam University?

Candidates will be selected on the basis of their performance in bachelor's degree/ 12th, entrance examination. The college also conduct a personal interview for selection.

Is there a lateral entry option available in the Engineering courses at SOET, K.R. Mangalam University?

Yes, admissions can be granted in the 2nd year of an Engineering degree programme, if the student has completed a 10+3 Diploma in Engineering or a 12+2 Diploma in Engineering recognised by the Central/State Government. After B.Sc. (P/C/M), the student can also take an admission in the 2nd year of Engineering. A minimum of 50% marks is required in all of the cases.

Can I apply for admissions at KR Mangalam University in offline mode?

Yes, you can apply for admission at KR Mangalam University, Gurgaon in offline mode. You can download the application form from the college website or you can collect it from the college campus. You can then fill in all the mandatory fields in the application form and submit it along with the application fee and required documents.

How can I apply for admissions at KRMU Gurgaon?

You can apply for admissions at KRMU Gurgaon by filling out the application form available on the official website of the college. You have to enter your name, email address and other details in the application form. It should be noted that you have to submit the application form before the deadline or you will not be considered for admissions.

What documents are required for admissions at KR Mangalam University?

Some of the documents are required for admissions at KR Mangalam University are 10th mark sheet, 12th mark sheet, medical certificate, character certificate and photo ID proof.

Why pursue BCA with specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at KRMU?

BCA with specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science helps students to learn new skills and techniques like database management systems, Statistics and Probability, rapid innovation, etc which can help them to make a career in Artificial Intelligence. KRMU conducts industrial visits for students to have a better understanding of the practical and theoretical knowledge also KRMU has joined hands with SAMATRIX and IBM to provide a deep understanding of all the important aspects of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

What are the pre-requisite skills for computer science and engineering professionals?
