About K.R. Mangalam University BBA

K.R. Mangalam University BBA is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a Bachelor of Business Administration (B.B.A.) degree at K.R. Mangalam University Gurgaon, applicants must meet certain eligibility criteria. Candidates must have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate score of 50%. They should have studied English as a compulsory subject. Additionally, they must have appeared for any of the national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main, SAT, or ACT. Admissions are granted based on merit, followed by a personal interview. The university also considers extracurricular activities, leadership qualities, and communication skills during the selection process. Meeting these eligibility requirements ensures a smooth admission process for aspiring students seeking a B.B.A. degree at K.R. Mangalam University Gurgaon. The annual fee for BBA is INR 1,85,000 - 2,05,000. More details about eligibility criteria, fees, admission process, selection criteria, application fees, how to apply, and important dates for K.R. K.R. Mangalam University BBA Admission 2026

Admission to the BBA at K.R. Mangalam University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the K.R. Mangalam University BBA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

K.R. Mangalam University BBA Application Process

K.R. Mangalam University BBA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BBA course at K.R. Mangalam University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill K.R. Mangalam University BBA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of K.R. Mangalam University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of K.R. Mangalam University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the K.R. Mangalam University BBA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the K.R. Mangalam University BBA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of K.R. Mangalam University BBA Application Form.

Documents Required for K.R. Mangalam University BBA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
