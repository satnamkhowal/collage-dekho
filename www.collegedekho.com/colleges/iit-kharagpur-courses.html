IIT Kharagpur LLB 2026

Spanning 3 years, the IIT Kharagpur LLB offers 1 specialisation, and the curriculum is designed to equip engineers, scientists, and professionals with legal expertise in patents, copyrights, and technology transfer through moot courts, internships, and research seminars. Admission is merit-based via a dedicated entrance test, requiring a first-class graduation. The syllabus includes doctrinal analysis, case laws, and policy drafting across six semesters, gaining exposure to IP tribunals and international conventions. The IIT Kharagpur LLB fees are INR 750,400 for 3 years, with waivers for SC/ST students and scholarships for top performers.

  • Students learn through a mix of core legal foundations and specialised IP coursework, supported by guest sessions from legal practitioners and industry experts connected to RGSOIPL.

  • The curriculum covers subjects such as patent law, technology transfer, IP transactions, and emerging areas of intellectual property.
