About Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA

Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA is a 3 Months - 3 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a Bachelor of Business Administration (B.B.A.) degree at Chitkara Business School, Rajpura Rajpura, candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Interested applicants should have successfully completed their higher secondary education (10+2) or its equivalent from a recognized board or university. They should have studied English as a compulsory subject and achieved a minimum aggregate score of 50% in their qualifying examination. Additionally, candidates may need to appear for entrance exams such as the Chitkara University Test or other national-level entrance exams. Meeting these requirements ensures that students have a strong foundation and academic aptitude to excel in the B.B.A. program at Chitkara Business School. The annual fee for BBA is INR 1,10,000 - 3,90,000. Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA Admission 2026

Admission to the BBA at Chitkara Business School, Rajpura is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA Application Process

Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the BBA course at Chitkara Business School, Rajpura have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Chitkara Business School, Rajpura and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Chitkara Business School, Rajpura. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA Application Form.

Documents Required for Chitkara Business School, Rajpura BBA Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
