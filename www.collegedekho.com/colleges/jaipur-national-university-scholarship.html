Jaipur National University Scholarships 2026 Overview

Jaipur National University offers financial assistance and scholarships covering up to 30% of tuition fees across various undergraduate and postgraduate programs. These awards are primarily based on academic merit in qualifying examinations (Class12th or Graduation), scores in national level entrance exams such as JEE, CUET, CAT, CLAT and etc. Additionally, the university provides specialized scholarships fo national-level, state-level and district-level sports person, as well as scholarships for children of defence personnel. To apply, students must submit relevant scorecards or category-specific documentation to the Admission Cell during the enrolment process.

Jaipur National University Early Bird Registration Policy 2026-27

The Early Bird Registration (EBR) Scheme 2026-27 at Jaipur National University is designed to encourage early admissions by offering tuition fee benefits to students who confirm their admission and pay the full yearly fee within the specified timeline. Under this scheme, students can receive up to 25% tuition fee waiver depending on the admission phase and payment date.

JNU Early Bird Registration Phases and Benefits 2026

Students who pay the full first-year fee within the given deadline are eligible for EBR benefits. The percentage of fee waiver depends on the phase of admission.

PhaseAdmission Window1st Semester Fee Due DateEBR Benefit on 1st Year Tuition Fee
Phase INovember, 2025 – March 15, 2026March 31, 202625%
Phase IIMarch 16 – May 31, 2026June 15, 202620%
Phase IIIJune 1 – June 30, 2026July 15, 202618%
Phase IVJuly 1 – July 31, 2026August 15, 202615%

Note: The EBR benefit is applicable only when the complete first-year fee is paid within the specified deadline of the respective phase.

General Conditions for JNU EBR Scheme

Students must follow the below conditions to receive the Early Bird Registration benefit:

Minimum Token Fee

  • A minimum token fee of Rs 15,000 is required to confirm admission.

Exception:

  • The minimum token fee condition does not apply to Nursing, Paramedical, and Allied Health Science courses.

Fee Calculation

  • EBR benefits are calculated only on the tuition fee.
  • Other charges such as registration, hostel, transport, or examination fees are not included.

Phase-Based Benefit Rule

  • If a student takes admission in an early phase but pays the full fee later, the EBR benefit will be adjusted according to the phase in which the payment is completed.

Example

  • Admission taken in Phase I
  • Fee paid in Phase IV

The student will receive 15% benefit (Phase IV benefit) instead of 25%.

Programs Not Eligible for Early Bird Registration (EBR)

The Early Bird Registration benefit is not applicable for the following programs:

  • All Paramedical Programs (except M.Sc. Forensic Science)
  • All Education Programs (except B.Sc Psychology and BA Psychology)
  • B.Tech / BCA / MCA Specializations such as Cyber Security and Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Nursing Programs
  • Pharmacy Programs
  • B.Sc Agriculture
  • All Collaborative Programs
  • All Diploma Programs (except Diploma in Fashion Design)
  • PhD Programs

JNU Jaipur Early Bird Registration EBR Scholarship FAQs

Is the EBR policy different for international students at JNU Jaipur?
Yes, international students at Jaipur National University usually follow a different fee structure, which may be quoted in US Dollars. The Early Bird Registration (EBR) scheme is mainly designed for domestic applicants. However, international candidates can contact the university’s International Admissions Cell for details about possible early admission benefits or regional scholarships that may apply to them.
Can I pay in installments and still get EBR?
No, the Early Bird Registration (EBR) benefit at Jaipur National University is usually available only when the full first-year tuition fee is paid within the specified deadline. If students choose to pay their fees in installments, they may not be eligible for the EBR discount. The scheme is designed to reward applicants who complete the full fee payment early during the admission process.
Can I combine the EBR discount with a merit scholarship?
Jaipur National University usually allows students to avail only one major scholarship or fee waiver at a time. If a student qualifies for both the EBR discount and a merit-based scholarship, the university generally grants the higher benefit. However, certain additional concessions, such as sibling or alumni discounts, may sometimes be combined. Students should confirm the exact scholarship policy with the JNU Jaipur admission cell.

JNU Jaipur Merit Scholarship Eligibility FAQs

Can lateral entry students apply for merit scholarships at JNU Jaipur?
Yes, lateral entry students at Jaipur National University are eligible to apply for merit-based scholarships. These students are admitted directly into the second year of their programme. In such cases, the scholarship eligibility is usually determined based on the percentage obtained in the diploma or qualifying examination. Applicants must submit their final diploma transcripts during the admission process to confirm eligibility for the scholarship.
Is the merit scholarship automatically renewed every year at JNU Jaipur?
No, the merit scholarship at Jaipur National University is usually awarded for the first year based on the student’s admission scores. To continue receiving the scholarship in later years, students must apply for renewal. The renewal generally requires maintaining good academic performance, often with a minimum CGPA such as 7.5 or higher, and having no academic backlogs or disciplinary issues during the academic year.

JNU Jaipur Progression Scholarship Eligibility FAQs

Is the 10% progression waiver available for second PG programme?
The progression scholarship at Jaipur National University is mainly offered to students moving from their first undergraduate degree to their first postgraduate programme. If a student has already completed one postgraduate course and plans to pursue a second PG programme, the 10% tuition fee waiver may not apply automatically. The scholarship usually prioritises students progressing directly from UG to PG within the university.
Are collaborative programmes eligible for progression scholarship at JNU Jaipur?
Generally, collaborative or partnership programmes at Jaipur National University may not be eligible for the progression scholarship. This waiver usually applies to the university’s regular full-time degree programmes. Students moving from one JNU Jaipur programme to another within the university may receive the benefit. However, those enrolling in collaborative or twinning programmes should confirm eligibility with the university’s admission cell before applying.
Is progression scholarship available for diploma to UG students?
Yes, Jaipur National University offers a progression scholarship for students moving from a diploma programme to an undergraduate degree. Students who complete their diploma at JNU Jaipur and continue into a related bachelor’s programme without taking a gap year may receive a 10% tuition fee waiver. This scholarship encourages students to continue their education at the university while supporting their academic progression.

JNU Jaipur Special Scholarships FAQs

Does the 15% special scholarship cover lab and kit fees?
No, the 15% special scholarship at Jaipur National University usually applies only to the core tuition fee. Additional charges such as laboratory fees, professional kits, equipment costs, hostel fees, and examination fees are not included in this waiver. Students enrolled in practical-based programmes like Hotel Management or Fashion Design must pay these extra charges separately as per the university’s standard fee structure.
Are BHMCT students eligible for special scholarships at JNU Jaipur?
Yes, students enrolled in the Bachelor of Hotel Management and Catering Technology (BHMCT) programme at Jaipur National University may apply for the Special Scholarship for Hospitality, Medical, and Community Tourism (SHMCT). This scholarship provides a 15% tuition fee concession for eligible candidates. It is specifically offered to students of the School of Hotel Management and is granted after verification of the applicant’s academic documents and certificates during admission.
Is there a scholarship for female design students at JNU Jaipur?
Yes, Jaipur National University offers a Women Empowerment Scholarship for female students enrolling in the Bachelor of Design (B.Des) programme at the School of Fashion Design. This scholarship provides a 15% tuition fee waiver to eligible candidates. To receive the benefit, students must submit the required original documents for verification during the admission process. The initiative encourages more women to pursue careers in creative and design fields.

JNU Jaipur Defence and Martyr Scholarship FAQs

Can grandchildren claim the 10% martyr scholarship at JNU Jaipur?
No, the 10% martyr scholarship at Jaipur National University is usually available only to the direct dependents of defence martyrs. This includes their spouse or children. Grandchildren are generally not covered under the standard eligibility criteria for this scholarship. However, such students may still apply for other financial aid options, such as merit-based scholarships or Early Bird Registration benefits, if they meet the required conditions.
What documents are required for defence scholarship verification?
Applicants claiming the defence scholarship at Jaipur National University must submit valid proof of their parent’s service. Children of retired defence personnel typically need to provide a copy of the Discharge Book or an Ex-Servicemen Identity Card issued by the Zila Sainik Board. In addition, students must submit their Aadhaar card and previous academic mark sheets for identity verification and scholarship approval.
Does defence scholarship apply to CAPF families at JNU Jaipur?
Yes, Jaipur National University generally extends its defence or uniformed services scholarship to families of Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF). This includes personnel from forces such as the BSF, CRPF, and CISF. Eligible candidates, usually children of these personnel, may receive a 5% tuition fee waiver. The scholarship recognises the service and contribution of defence and paramilitary personnel in maintaining national security.

JNU Jaipur Sibling Scholarship FAQs

Can three siblings receive the sibling scholarship at JNU Jaipur?
At Jaipur National University, the sibling scholarship usually applies when two siblings study at the university. The younger sibling may receive a 5% tuition fee waiver. If a third sibling takes admission, the scholarship is generally limited to two siblings only. This means the benefit may not extend to all three siblings under the standard scholarship policy.

JNU Jaipur Sports Scholarship FAQs

What happens if a sports scholarship student gets injured?
If a sports scholarship student at Jaipur National University suffers a verified injury, the university may allow the scholarship to continue for the academic year. The student must submit a certified medical report from a recognised hospital to the university’s sports department. During recovery, the student may still be expected to remain involved in team activities, such as strategy planning or assisting coaches.
Are indoor sports like chess eligible for scholarships at JNU Jaipur?
Jaipur National University mainly offers sports scholarships for recognized national, Olympic, and inter-university sports. However, indoor games such as chess or table tennis may also be considered in certain cases. Students with strong achievements at the state or national level can apply by submitting valid federation certificates. The university sports committee reviews these records to decide eligibility for a tuition fee waiver.
Is a physical trial required for sports scholarship at JNU Jaipur?
Yes, Jaipur National University may require applicants to attend a physical trial for the sports scholarship. Along with submitting sports certificates, candidates may need to demonstrate their skills and fitness during the trial. The university’s sports committee evaluates performance to determine eligibility. This process helps ensure that selected students can actively represent the university in sports competitions and contribute to future achievements.

JNU Jaipur Entrance Exam-Based Scholarships FAQs

Can NEET score help get scholarships for BSc Nursing or BPT?
Yes, a high NEET score may help students receive merit-based scholarships for programmes like BSc Nursing or Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) at Jaipur National University. Although NEET is mainly used for MBBS admissions, strong NEET performance can also be considered as a merit indicator. Based on the score, eligible students may qualify for merit scholarships that offer up to 30% tuition fee waiver.
Does JNU Jaipur offer GPAT stipend for MPharm students?
Yes, Jaipur National University provides the GPAT stipend to eligible students admitted to the MPharm programme. Candidates with a valid GPAT score may receive a monthly stipend of around Rs 12,400, as per the guidelines of the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and AICTE. This government-supported benefit is available only if the student is not receiving any other financial assistance.
What scholarship is available for GATE-qualified MTech students?
GATE-qualified students taking admission to MTech programmes at Jaipur National University may receive financial support from the university. This can include a stipend or a tuition fee concession based on their GATE score. In some cases, these students may also be given priority for research or teaching assistantship opportunities in the School of Engineering.

Jaipur National University Scholarship FAQs

Can I apply for state government scholarships at JNU Jaipur?

Yes, students at Jaipur National University (JNU Jaipur) can apply for various state and central government scholarships. Many of these scholarships are available through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). The university administration assists students with document verification and application procedures when required. These schemes are especially beneficial for students from SC, ST, or OBC categories who may qualify for additional government-funded financial support.

What is the JNU Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (JEST)?

The JNU Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (JEST) is an online entrance exam conducted by Jaipur National University (JNU Jaipur). The test allows students to compete for scholarships while applying for admission to various undergraduate programmes. Based on their performance, candidates may receive scholarships that can cover a significant portion of their tuition fees. JEST provides students an additional opportunity to secure financial support beyond their board exam results.

Does JNU Jaipur provide financial aid for student's research projects?

Yes, Jaipur National University offers financial support for selected student's research projects, especially in technical and science programmes. The university may provide grants or fellowships to help students cover expenses related to research activities. In some cases, students who qualify national exams such as NET or JRF may also receive research assistantship opportunities. These initiatives encourage students to participate in academic research and innovation during their studies.

Will my scholarship continue if I change my course at JNU Jaipur?

If a student changes their programme at Jaipur National University, the scholarship may be reviewed again. Scholarships are often linked to a specific course or its fee structure. Because of this, the scholarship amount may change or may no longer apply after switching programmes. Students are advised to consult the JNU Jaipur scholarship or admission office before changing their course.

Can I get a scholarship based on my MAT score?

Yes, Jaipur National University offers MBA scholarships based on scores in entrance exams such as MAT, CAT, XAT, or CMAT. Students with high percentile scores may receive financial benefits on their MBA tuition fees. For example, candidates scoring above 80 percentile in MAT may be eligible for a scholarship of around Rs 30,000. Students can submit their best MAT scorecard during the admission process.
