About Galgotias University

The Galgotias University was established by the government of Uttar Pradesh in 2011 and is approved by the UGC, AIU, Bar Council of India (BCI), AICTE and Council of Architecture. The institution is accredited by the NBA for its academic excellence, placement & research. It has also been awarded the prestigious A+ grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). Recognised as one of the top-ranking institutes in the UP and NCR region, the Galgotias University campus is spread over 52 acres of land, with 180+ student laboratories, and classrooms centrally air-conditioned with LCD projectors. As per NIRF 2025 rankings, Galgotias University Greater Noida is ranked 36th in Law and 55th in Pharmacy.

Galgotias University offers a diverse range of innovative programmes in architecture, research, commerce, management, engineering, and more. The degrees include BSc (Hons), B Tech, BBA, BA (Hons), B Des, BA LLB, B Pharm, BPT, MBA, MCA, MA, LLM, MTech, BEd, LLB, and many more. Galgotias University admissions are usually merit-based but they also consider valid scores of certain entrance exams like JEE Main, UPSEE, NATA, etc. Regarding the Galgotias University placement drive 2024, the highest package offered for MBA and BTech placements was Rs 7.87 LPA and Rs 39 LPA, respectively. Some of the top recruiters who visited the campus were Deloitte, Microsoft, American Express, Capillary Technologies, Amazon, etc.

Galgotias University Application Process 2025

For the Galgotias University application process, candidates have to fill out the application form online. An application fee of Rs 1,200 will be required during the application. The following is a step-wise Galgotias University application process:

  • First, candidates must visit the official website of the university.
  • Click on 'Apply Now' on the home page of the portal.
  • Register with your basic details to generate the login credentials.
  • Once the credentials are generated, log in with it and fill out the application form.
  • Upload the required documents and pay the Galgotias University application fee.
  • Then submit the application form to conclude the process. After this, you will receive an email/ SMS for your admission confirmation.

Galgotias University Scholarships

The Galgotias University scholarships are offered to deserving candidates who either have a very good academic performance or candidates belonging to a financially weaker background to support their higher education. The scholarships are offered in three types: Merit-based, Merit-cum-Means based and Program based.

Galgotias University Greater Noida Scholarships — Merit-Based 

Galgotias University scholarships are offered to meritorious candidates based on their academic performance in qualifying examinations prescribed by the university, like 10+2 or graduation, rank/ score in All India/ State Level Entrance/ Aptitude Test and candidates possessing exemplary skills in sports, performing arts or literature.

  • 100% tuition fee waiver for toppers of all Boards and universities.
  • 50% tuition fee waiver as a scholarship for students securing up to 4000th rank in the IIT-JEE.
  • 50% tuition fee waiver as a scholarship for students securing up to 100th rank in the AIEEE.
  • 50% tuition fee waiver as a scholarship for students securing up to 100th rank in the UPSEE.
  • 25% tuition fee waiver as a scholarship for students securing 93% aggregate/ PCM and above in class 12 or 80% marks in graduation.
  • 20% tuition fee waiver as a scholarship for students securing up to 5000th rank in AIEEE or students scoring at least 70%tile in CAT or above 700 marks in MAT.
  • 50% tuition fee waiver as a scholarship for students with gold or silver medals at the national level/state level in sports or who have some exemplary achievements to their credit in the area of performing arts or literature.

Galgotias University Greater Noida Scholarships — Merit-Cum-Means

Scholarships are also available for economically weak or differently-abled students, on a case-to-case basis, depending on their performance in the qualifying examination.

Galgotias University Scholarships — Programme Based

To encourage students to achieve higher performance during their studies in an academic programme, the university institutes merit scholarships for them in recognition of their academic achievements in the concerneprogrammesms of study. These scholarships are awarded every year from the second year onwards, after the declaration of the results, for one year.
