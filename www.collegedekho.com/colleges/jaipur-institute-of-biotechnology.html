About Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology

Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology is situated in Jaipur in Rajasthan state of India. The college is accredited by AICTE. Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology offers 2 courses across 1 streams namely Science..Popular degrees offered at Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology include BSc, MSc..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Jaipur Institute of Biotechnology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations.
