About Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village

Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village, Gandhi Nagar, was established in 2009 to promote professional education in Gujarat and particularly in and around the Ahmedabad-Gandhi Nagar region. The trust and educational institute under the umbrella of Bapu GKV was managed and promoted under the guidance of Chairman Shri. Shankersinh Vaghela Bapu was a popular leader known for his pioneering efforts for social development.

The Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village, spread across 40 acres of land at Vasan, Gandhinagar, provides an ideal environment for the students to engage in academic activities. The Institutes here have world-class infrastructure that facilitates excellence in teaching, research, consulting, and other professional activities. Located in Gujarat's high-tech capital, Bapu GKV is in close proximity to some of the leading corporate houses in the country, ranging from information technology to consumer product companies, giving it the added advantage of integrating classroom knowledge with practical experience. Institute aims to achieve excellence through partnerships with industries and leading academic institutions in Gujarat.

Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

  • They have large theatre-style classrooms that are centrally air-conditioned and provide adequate comfort for students to concentrate on their studies.
  • The state-of-the-art auditorium at BGKVserves as a common ground for students, faculty, and guest speakers for regular interfaces, conferences, seminars, and cultural events.
  • Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village provides well-equipped laboratories for students to conduct experiments for better understanding and application of theory to cultivate knowledgeable professionals and thought leaders.

Other Facilities at Gujarat Knowledge Village 

  • Within the premises of Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village, there are two large hostels, one for boys and one for girls. The rooms are apartment-style, with bedrooms sharing a lounge and bathroom. All rooms have internet access to help students with their classwork. Each student has their bed, mattress, pillow, pillow covers and sheets, cupboards, and a study table with a chair. Because each bedroom has a double bed, each apartment has six occupants.
  • The cafeteria is spacious, well-lit, and well-ventilated, with menus designed by a team of professional dieticians to ensure a well-balanced diet.
  • Students have access to medical services 24 hours a day, seven days a week. The infirmary is located within the hostel to allow students easy access to medical care. The trained medical staff is always on call to handle any emergency or major medical problem. There is also an ambulance on campus that can transport you to better medical care, as well as a first-aid station that is open 24 hours a day, seven days.

Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village Scholarships

All students who qualify for Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village, scholarships (after 10+2) can opt for Fast Track admission. They are not required to appear for the admission selection process, and their admission is determined solely based on their application form.

Direct admission is not accepted for postgraduate programs.

Bapu Gujarat Knowledge Village scholarship facilities for UG and PG students help the candidates with financial assistance and reward academic performances. The scholarships are given below:

UG Scholarship:

  1. 100% Scholarship is awarded to students who have secured a minimum 93% aggregate in class 12th of CBSE/ISC/State Boards.
  2. 50% Scholarship is awarded to students who have secured a minimum 88% aggregate in class 12th of CBSE/ISC/State Boards.
  3. 25% Scholarship is awarded to students who have secured a minimum 70% aggregate in class 12th of CBSE/ISC/State Boards.

MBA Scholarship

  1. 100% Scholarship is awarded to students who have secured a minimum 90 percentile in CAT/MAT or 650 minimum in GMAT.
  2. 50% Scholarship is awarded to students who have scored a minimum 85 percentile in CAT/MAT or 600 minimum in GMAT.
  3. 25% Scholarship is awarded to students who have achieved a minimum 75 percentile in CAT/MAT or 450 minimum in GMAT

PG (Non-MBA) Scholarship

  1. 100% Scholarship is given to students with a minimum 93% aggregate in class 12thof CBSE/ISC/State Boards and 80% in Graduation.
  2. 50% Scholarship is given to students with a minimum of 88% aggregate in class 12th of CBSE/ISC/State Boards and 75% in Graduation.

B.E at Bapu GKV

The Bachelor of Engineering programme at Bapu GKV is a four-year engineering programme with a 240-student yearly intake. It offers six courses: information technology, computer engineering, mechanical engineering, civil engineering, electrical engineering, and electronics and communication engineering.

M.E at Bapu GKV

M.E. at Bapu GKV is a two-year postgraduate engineering programme with an annual enrollment of 96 students in four different courses. The goal of Bapu GKV is to provide M.E students with human resources in the form of technical professionals who contribute significantly to national and international economic and moral development.

B.Pharma at Bapu GKV

It is an undergraduate degree of 4-years. B.pharma at Bapu GKV provides educational opportunities for those interested in pursuing a career in pharmaceutical sciences in order to increase the quality of education and research, as well as the healthcare system.

M.Pharma at Bapu GKV

It is a two-year post-graduate programme. Bapu GKV M. Pharm degree trains students for both theoretical and practical parts of work, guaranteeing that they can understand and cope with work in the real world.

B.Sc at Bapu GKV

The B.Sc. degree is a three-year undergraduate degree programme in science. The Bapu GKV B.Sc course is the foundation of science and includes courses such as physics, chemistry, biology, nursing, and mathematics.

B.Com at Bapu GKV

B.Com is a three-year undergraduate degree programme. B.Com at Bapu GKV is aimed to give students a broad variety of managerial skills and knowledge in areas such as finance, accounting, taxation, and management.
