Jaipur National University Conducts Industrial Visit for B.Tech Students

Jaipur National University, renowned for its innovative and industry-oriented approach to education, recently organised an industrial visit for its B.Tech in Food Technology students. The visit was named "Tour To Bakery" and aimed to provide students with hands-on industry experience.

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell & JNU Jaipur School of Engineering and Technology Department of Food Technology organised the industrial visit and was open only to 2nd, 4th, and 6th-semester students. The industrial visit occurred on Jan 27, 2023, from 12 PM to 3:30 PM and was held at Tulsyan Foods PVT LTD (Company Name: Calorie) located at G1-552 Road Number 12, Sitapura Industrial Area, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302022.

This industrial visit is an excellent opportunity for B.Tech in Food Technology students to gain practical knowledge and experience, and it will surely aid them in their future careers. The university is committed to providing its students with an education that is both theoretical and practical, and this industrial visit is a step in that direction.

Jaipur National University 12th Convocation Registration Open for Batches 2020-21, 2021-22 and MBBS Students

Jaipur National University (JNU) is excited to announce that registration for their 12th convocation ceremony is now open. The event, set to take place on February 11, 2023, will be held at the JNU Medical Grounds. This convocation ceremony is open to students from the batches 2020-21, 2021-22 and for MBBS students.

The last date for registration is January 31, 2023, and students can find the registration form on the university's official website. The registration process is straightforward, and students will have three options, depending on which applies to them.

To fill out the JNU Jaipur 12th Convocation form, students will need to have the following information ready: ERP ID, Name, Gender, Name of the Programme, Mobile Number, Email ID, Number of Accompanying Guests, Address, Whatsapp Number, Enrolment Number, Fathers Name, and Accommodation Required (on nominal payment).

It is important to note that the accommodation is provided on nominal payment. This will be on a first-come, first-serve basis. Students are encouraged to register to secure their spot at the convocation ceremony as soon as possible.

The JNU Jaipur 12th Convocation promises to be an unforgettable event that marks a significant milestone in the students' lives. It is a time to celebrate their achievements and look forward to the future. The university looks forward to welcoming all students and their guests on February 11, 2023, at the JNU Medical Grounds.

Jaipur National University To Hold International Law Moot Court Competition

The 9th Professor V.S. Mani Memorial International Law Moot Court Competition- 2023 will be held at Jaipur National University's Seedling School of Law and Governance in Rajasthan. The “JNU Jaipur Moot Court Competition” will be conducted between February 23 to 35, 2023. The location for the competition will be Seedling School of Law & Governance, Jaipur National University, Main Campus, Jaipur, Agra By-pass, Near New RTO Office, Jagatpura, Jaipur Rajasthan-302017.

How To Participate In The Jaipur National University To Hold International Law Moot Court Competition

Each team must complete the registration process within the time frame specified. The registration process consists of two steps: filling out the registration form and paying the registration money. Registration will be available till January 17, 2023. (11.59 PM IST). The registration form is available for download below. Along with the complete registration form, teams must provide a genuine letter issued by the authorised authority of their college or university.

Download the registration form by clicking here. 

Registration Fees Payment Details

The registration fee for Indian teams is Rs 4,000/-. As a registration fee, all other Teams must pay US $ 100 or its equivalent in Euro, minus any service charge (if applicable). Indian teams must pay by online payment or demand draft. All other teams must pay via online payment/bank transfer. The registration form includes instructions on how to do so. All registration-related processes should be completed by January 17, 2023. In this aspect, the Director's judgement is definitive.

Ground Rules of The Competition 

  • The Competition is open to all students who are enrolled in regular mode undergraduate Law course (5 years integrated Law study/3 Year L.L.B Course) or its equivalent offered by any recognised institution/college/university in the world. 
  • No institution/college/university may enter more than one team in the competition. 
  • The Competition has a limit of 50 teams, and teams will be chosen on a first-come, first-served basis. 
  • Each Team must have a minimum of two and a maximum of three members. In a Team of two members, both members must be identified as Speakers.
  • Any additional team member or team coach will not be properly acknowledged and will not be eligible for a certificate from the University.

The Jaipur National University To Hold International Law Moot Court Competition Awards

  • The winning team will receive a trophy as well as a cash reward of Rs 27000.
  • The winning team will receive a trophy and a cash reward of Rs 18000.
  • The Best Speaker/Advocate will receive a Plaque as well as a monetary prize of 6000.
  • The Best Written Submission/Memorial award comes with a Plaque and a cash incentive of Rs 5000. 
  • The Best Researcher will receive a Plaque and a monetary prize of Rs 4000.

Jaipur National University Organised “Youth Dialogue” on National Youth Day

The National Service Scheme (NSS) unit of Jaipur National University organised the “Youth Dialogue” programme. It was organised on the auspicious occasion of National Youth Day and the birth anniversary of Swami Vivekananda.

Under the supervision of the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, the National Service Scheme (NSS) is an extension of activities to the higher education system to orient the student youth to community service while they are studying in educational institutions.

Jaipur National University NSS unit organised the Youth Dialogue on January 12, 2023, and the timing was between 11 AM and 1 PM. The venue selected for the Youth Dialogue programme was the engineering auditorium, Jaipur National University's main campus. The programme's main goal was to provide a platform for the youth to share their perspectives and ideas to create a brighter future for all. 

Jaipur National University NSS unit Youth Dialogue programme was organised in collaboration with “Samajik Yuva Sangathan Sansthan” and “Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India”.

How & Where To Apply For Jaipur National University PhD Admission 2023

JNU Jaipur PhD Admission Process 2023

Step 1: For admission to the PhD programme offered by JNU Jaipur, students first need to make sure that they are eligible for the programme. 

Step 2: Fill out the JNU Jaipur entrance test application form from the official website. 

Step 3: Appear for the entrance test on January 18, 2023. You must appear for the personal interview round if you pass the entrance exam. 

Step 4: If you are selected, then pay the admission fee to secure admission.

JNU Jaipur PhD Eligibility Criteria 2023

Students must have passed their graduation exam with 55% or more marks. Moreover, they also need to pass the JNU Jaipur entrance exam.

JNU Jaipur PhD Selection Process 2023

Once an eligible student applies for the JNU Jaipur PhD admission, they need to clear the personal interview round to finalise their admission.
