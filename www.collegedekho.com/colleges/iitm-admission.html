Admission to IIT Madras for its undergraduate engineering programmes is a tough race that is decided by the Joint Entrance Examination. In 2026 session aspirants must first appear for the JEE Main, whose second phase registration opens in February. Only those who qualify JEE Main will be allowed to appear in the tougher JEE Advanced, the only examination that counts for admission to any B.Tech programme at the college. The last leg of the process is an admission counselling conducted under the auspices of the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) after the announcement of JEE Advanced results. IIT Madras Admission Process 2026

IIT Madras admission 2026 is offered to various undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral courses in engineering, sciences, humanities, and management. The admission process at IIT Madras is completely entrance exam-based and follows national-level examinations conducted by different authorities. Candidates must meet the required eligibility criteria and secure valid scores in the respective entrance exams to be considered for admission.

Some of the major entrance exams accepted for IIT Madras admission include JEE Advanced for BTech, JAM for MSc, GATE for MTech, CAT for MBA, and national-level tests followed by interviews for PhD admissions. Seat allocation for most technical programs is done through centralized counseling processes such as JoSAA, COAP, and CCMN.

Eligible candidates need to apply through the respective examination portals and then participate in counseling or seat allocation rounds. Once allotted a seat, candidates must complete document verification and pay the admission fee to confirm their seats.

At the undergraduate level, IIT Madras offers BTech and BS programs in various engineering and science disciplines. Admission is based solely on JEE Advanced rank. Candidates must first qualify for JEE Main to become eligible for JEE Advanced. Final seat allotment is done through JoSAA counseling.

IIT Madras Admission Process for UG Courses

The step by step admission process for IIT Madras admission is given below for UG courses:

  • Qualify for JEE Main examination accepted for BTech admission.
  • Appear for JEE Advanced (mandatory for IITs admission).
  • Register for JoSAA counseling.
  • Fill in the choices of IIT Madras and preferred programs.
  • Participate in seat allotment rounds.
  • Accept the allotted seat.
  • Report to the institute for document verification.
