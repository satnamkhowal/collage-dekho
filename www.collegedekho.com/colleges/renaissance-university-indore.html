Renaissance University FAQs

Is Renaissance University a government college?

No, Renaissance University Indore is not a government college. It is a private university established in 2007 in Indore. Renaissance University Indore is a self-financed institution, meaning it receives no direct funding from the government. It is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and other relevant authorities.

Does Renaissance University provide scholarships?

As per the university's official website, there is no information about the scholarship. However, according to their past academic records, candidates can apply for a loan scheme or avail of some special benefits.

How is the infrastructure of Renaissance University?

Renaissance University has a state-of-the-art infrastructure, including a swimming pool, basketball, volleyball court, transportation facilities, gymnasium, table tennis, snooker, in-campus call centre, wifi, and canteen.

Why should I choose Renaissance University?

Renaissance University is among the most prestigious universities in Indore and, in association with Harvard Business School. The university has a strong alumni network of around 60000+ graduates around the globe and highly skilled faculty members.

Does UGC recognise Renaissance University?

The university holds recognition from the University Grants Commission. It has also collaborated with numerous international and domestic institutions, such as Meiosis College (Cyprus) and Varna University of Management. The university has more than 200 faculty members across all the schools.

What is the Renaissance University application process?

Visit the Renaissance University official website to begin the online admissions process and locate the programme you want to apply for. You can then fill out the online application form, upload the required documents, and pay the application fee online. Detailed instructions for the application process can be found on the admission page. If candidate want to apply offline, then go to the Renaissance University's campus along with application fees and documents such as id proof, Class 12 and Class 10 mark sheet, a bachelor degree if applying for PG programmes.

What law programmes are offered by Renaissance University?

Law programmes are offered under the School of Law at Renaissance University in undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels. There are a total of six law programmes, i.e. LLB, LLM Corporate Law, LLM Constitutional Law, LLM IPR Law, LLM Criminal Law, and PhD in Law. All the law courses are approved by the Bar Council of India. The fee varies in the range of Rs 40,000- Rs 80,000 per annum.

What documents shall I carry during offline admission at the Renaissance campus?

The specific documents you need to carry for offline admission at the Renaissance campus can vary depending on the programme you are applying for. Some of the common documents include identity proof, passport-size photographs, letter of recommendation, caste certificate, Class 12 and Class 10 mark sheet, bachelor's degree (if applying for a master’s degree), and entrance exam scores if applicable.

How does the placement cell of Renaissance University offer assistance to its students?

The placement cell at Renaissance University offers comprehensive assistance to its students in their pursuit of career opportunities. This assistance includes career counselling, job search, resume building, mock personal interviews, personality development, networking, soft skills training, and alumni support. Training and Placement Officer ensure that every student finds a job opportunity based on their qualifications and academic records.

Where is Renaissance University located?

Renaissance University campus address is Gram Revti, Behind Aurobindo Hospital Sanwer-Ujjain Road, Indore (M.P.) - 452015. Whereas Renaissance University office is located at 201-202, HeadQuarter Building, IDA Plot no. 54, Satya Sai Square, Indore (M.P.) - 452010. The university is accessible by all means of transport and has direct connectivity by air and railway.

How is the Renaissance University campus life?

Renaissance University is known for its vibrant campus life and its commitment to student development. The university offers a variety of extracurricular activities and clubs for students to participate in. It also organises cultural fests, international workshops, various events, and introduction ceremonies for the holistic development of students.

What programmes are offered by Renaissance University?

Renaissance University offers more than 30 programmes in various disciplines such as business administration, paramedical, design, law, computer science, humanities, social sciences, media and mass communication, and nursing. There are 12 schools running under Renaissance University, i.e., management, law, agriculture, social science, fashion, engineering, nursing, science, journalism, library science, and performing arts. All the programmes are recognised by regulatory bodies such as Union Grants Commission, Bar Council of India, Pharmacy Council of India, etc.

How much does Renaissance University MBA cost?

Renaissance University MBA fees vary depending on the specialisation the candidate chooses. The two-year MBA programme fees range from Rs 82,000 - Rs 2,89,000 per year. MBA offers three specialisations: MBA +PGP in Business Analytics, MBA in Hospital Administration, and MBA with Dual Specialisation. MBA by Renaissance is a cutting-edge programme that covers finance, marketing, HR, and business analytics.

How can I apply at Renaissance University for the UG programme?

To apply for an undergraduate programme at Renaissance University, you may visit the official university website to access the online application portal. Once there, select the UG program of your choice and carefully review the admission requirements, including academic qualifications and any entrance exam scores, if applicable. Complete the online application form by providing accurate personal and academic information, and ensure you upload all required documents, such as transcripts, certificates, and identification. Pay the prescribed application fee and submit the application form.

What is the Renaissance University admission eligibility?

For Renaissance University admission to undergraduate programmes students must have passed Class 12 from a recognised board with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Students who have appeared for or are planning to appear for the Pearson Undergraduate Entrance Exam can also apply for admission. For PG admissions, candidates must have a bachelor’s degree in any discipline with a minimum aggregate score of 50% (for SC/ST/OBC 45%) and a minimum duration of 3 years from a recognised University.

Is Renaissance University AICTE and UGC approved?

Renaissance University was established in 2017 in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, as a private university. The university is recognised by the Univesity Grants Commission (UGC) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). In addition, the university is also recognised by the Bar Council of India (BCI), the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI), and Madhya Pradesh Private University Regulatory Commission (MPPURC).

Is Renaissance a good university?

Renaissance University is a good university that offers students a well-rounded educational experience. The university strongly focuses on academics, research, placements, and student development. RU has a highly qualified faculty and state-of-the-art facilities. The university has a dedicated placement cell that works with students to help them find suitable jobs after graduating from the university.
