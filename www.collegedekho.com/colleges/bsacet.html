About BSACET Mathura

B.S.A. College of Engineering & Technology is situated in Mathura in Uttar Pradesh state of India. Established in 1997, BSACET is a Private college. The college is accredited by AICTE. B.S.A. College of Engineering & Technology offers 21 courses across 4 streams namely Engineering, Management, IT, Pharmacy..Popular degrees offered at B.S.A. College of Engineering & Technology include B.Tech, MBA, M.Tech, MCA, Diploma..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, B.S.A. College of Engineering & Technology is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at B.S.A. College of Engineering & Technology, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at B.S.A. College of Engineering & Technology is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
