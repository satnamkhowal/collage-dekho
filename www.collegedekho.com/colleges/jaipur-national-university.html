About Jaipur National University

Jaipur National University, also known as JNU Jaipur, established in 2007 in Jaipur, Rajasthan. The institute is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC), AICTE, National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), BCI, INC and several other regulatory bodies. It is a private university that offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and doctoral programmes in engineering, management, pharmacy, law, nursing, hospitality, media, and other fields. The institute offers BTech, MBA, BPharm, BA LLB/BBA LLB, MTech, LLM, PhD and several other programmes in specializations such as Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Cyber Security, and other related fields. The total fee is Rs 5,18,400 – Rs 7,01,360 for BTech, Rs 2,78,200 – Rs 4,38,000 for MBA, Rs 3,46,500 – Rs 3,93,000 for PhD and Rs 99,645 for LLM.

For JNU Jaipur BTech admission, candidates must pass Class 12th with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with minimum 45% marks and may qualify JEE Main or JNU JEST, while admission to the MBA programme requires a valid score in CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT along with a Bachelor’s degree with minimum 50% marks. The PhD admissions accept candidates with a Master’s degree and JNU JEST score, followed by a personal interview. The admission process is conducted online through the university portal with JNU JEST, national-level exams (JEE Main, NEET, CAT, CLAT etc.), counselling, seat allotment, fee payment, and document verification. The JNU Jaipur placements in 2025 show the highest package offered was Rs 27 LPA, while the average package was Rs 5.43 LPA.

Jaipur National University Admission Process 2026

Step 1: Online Registration
Visit the official website and register using a valid email ID and mobile number.

Step 2: Fill the Application Form
Complete the online application form with the required personal and academic details. Upload scanned copies of the photograph, signature, and relevant marksheets.

Step 3: Appear for Entrance Exam (if applicable)
Candidates must appear for the relevant entrance exam depending on the course, such as JEE Main, CAT, MAT, CMAT, CLAT, or JNU JEST.

Step 4: Result and Shortlisting
Candidates are shortlisted based on entrance exam scores or merit in the qualifying examination, depending on the program.

Step 5: Counselling
Shortlisted candidates participate in the counselling conducted by Jaipur National University.

Step 6: Document Verification
Candidates must submit and verify original documents, including Class 10th and Class 12th marksheets, ID proof, and category certificate (if applicable).

Step 7: Fee Payment
After verification, candidates must pay the admission fee online to confirm their seat.

Jaipur National University FAQs

Can I opt for distance education at Jaipur National University?

Yes, you can opt for distance education at Jaipur National University with the help of the School of Distance Education and Learning (SODEL). SODEL at JNU Jaipur was first established in 2009. The university offers distance education courses like MA, MBA, MCA, M.Sc, BA, BBA, BCA, B.Com, and B.Sc. The fee range for these courses is Rs 7,500 to Rs 12,500 per semester. The degree obtained from SODEL at JNU Jaipur is completely valid as it is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). SODEL is also approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and the Distance Education Bureau (DEC).

Does Jaipur National University offer scholarships for its students?

Yes, the Jaipur National University does offer scholarships for its students. Students of UG courses can claim up to 100% scholarships by performing well in the Jaipur National University Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (JNU JEST). Other than that the university also offers other scholarships, like merit-based scholarships for students who have scored 75% marks or more in their respective subjects. Additionally, students can also get help from the JNU Jaipur to secure easy education loans from the nationalised banks in India.

Is JNU Jaipur ragging free?

Yes, JNU Jaipur is completely ragging free. The Jaipur National University has a strict anti-ragging committee which follows government norms and stops any ragging attempts inside or outside the university. Those who are caught ragging or are in support of ragging are punished by the anti ragging committee of the university. The hostel of this university is also safe and ragging is completely prohibited in all forms. Students can repost to the anti ragging committee if they witness or hear about any ragging inside or outside the campus.

What is Jaipur National University famous for?

The  Jaipur National University is famous for its courses like B.Tech, M.Tech, BCA, MBBS, MBA, MCA, and others. Other than that, the university is also famous for its placements. What are the accreditations and recognitions of Jaipur National University?

Jaipur National University is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a B++ grade. The university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). With UGC recognition, Jaipur National University becomes eligible to receive funds provided by the UGC for the development of the university.

Does Jaipur National University offer a hostel?

Jaipur National University does offer hostel accommodations for its students. Jaipur National University hostels have AC rooms, warden supervision, transportation facilities, laundry, and more. Jaipur National University hostel is located on the campus.
