About FMS Delhi

Faculty of Management Studies, Delhi, also known as FMS Delhi, is a public university, established in 1954. Located in New Delhi, Delhi, the FMS Delhi campus is part of the University of Delhi’s North Campus. The college is affiliated to the University of Delhi and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an 'A' grade. FMS Delhi is ranked 2nd by IIRF under the Best Government B-School category in 2024 and 7th by MBAUniverse.com among top B-schools in India in 2024.

FMS Delhi offers a wide range of postgraduate and doctorate programs in the streams of Management. MBA, Executive MBA, MBA Executive (Health Care Administration), and PhD are some of the popular courses. Admissions at FMS Delhi are based on entrance examinations. The college accepts CAT for MBA, and academic scores with interviews for Executive MBA and MBA Executive (Health Care Administration).

For placements, FMS Delhi placement rate is 100%. During the placement drive of 2023, the highest salary package offered was INR 123 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 34.1 LPA.

FMS Delhi Admission 2025

FMS Delhi admissions are based on national-level entrance examinations and interviews. Students seeking admission at FMS Delhi must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the college and appear for the entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for FMS Delhi admissions:

MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the CAT and participate in the FMS Delhi counselling process, including SOP discussion, extempore, and personal interview for seat allotment.

Executive MBA: Admissions to Executive MBA are based on academic scores, discussion on executive experience & SOP, extempore, and personal interview. Candidates must live/work in Delhi-NCR for mandatory physical attendance.

MBA Executive (Health Care Administration): Admissions to MBA Executive (Health Care Administration) are based on academic scores, discussion on executive experience & SOP, extempore, and personal interview. Candidates must live/work in Delhi-NCR for mandatory physical attendance.

PhD: Admissions to PhD are based on a master’s degree with 55% marks and performance in the interview.

FMS Delhi Campus Life & Facilities

The FMS Delhi campus has everything that a student needs for their overall development. The campus is situated in New Delhi and is one of India's most popular business schools. It looks after the students' comfort and the teaching and non-teaching staff of the college. The campus provides the best environment for the students along with advanced infrastructure. 

Some of the FMS facilities provided to the students are as follows. 

  • Library – It has a central library. The library holds more than 50,000 books required for educational and recreational purposes. Along with the books, the college also has various national and international journals, CDs, and other study materials. The library facilities include books on various topics, newspapers, magazines, periodicals and other reading materials The topics include current affairs, management, commerce, banking, accounting, and many others. Apart from this, the Faculty of Management Studies students also have access to a special library. They have access to the Ratan Tata Library. This library contains more than 20,000 books in various fields. 
     
  • Hostel – It provides lodging and boarding and proper accommodation. The FMS hostel facilities are available for both male and female students. The hostels are neat and clean, and the staff always maintain proper hygiene. It is filled with all the necessary things required for healthy living. Clean water 24x7, a recreation room, a common room, laundry services, a mess, a surveillance system and security services are some of the Faculty of Management Studies hostel facilities. According to the student's preference, they are also provided single-sharing or double-sharing rooms. 
  • Sports Complex – It also has a sports complex for students. It has a basketball court, badminton court, and tennis court. In addition, the college also has big playgrounds where the students can freely play football and cricket. Along with outdoor sports, the FMS Delhi sports facilities also include indoor games with the opportunity of playing carrom, chess, table tennis, etc. 
     
  • Computer Labs – The computer labs are well-equipped with updated computers. The computer labs also have lab assistants who are there to help the students in case of any problems.
  • Cafeteria – The university campus has a cafeteria that offers food from many cuisines. The food that is provided here is made in hygienic kitchens, and the staff here also maintain proper hygiene. 
  • Gym – The Faculty of Management Studies, has the facility of an on-campus gym. The students who are interested in availing of this facility will have to talk to the gym in charge of the college. 
  • Medical Facilities – Medical facilities are also provided by this college. In case of any emergency, the students are provided with proper first aid. This helps in buying some time so that the patient can be taken to the hospital. For not-so-serious problems, the students are treated in the private hospital of the college. 
     
  • Wi-Fi Campus – The campus of the FMS has Wi-Fi connectivity. All the students and staff of the college can avail this facility. 
  • Auditorium – The auditorium has the capacity to hold a huge number of people. All the important workshops and seminars are held in this auditorium. 
     
  • Parking Facility – The college also provides a parking facility. This is to ensure that the students and other college members do not have to face any trouble regarding parking their vehicles. 
     
  • Convenience Store – The FMS campus has some department stores that facilitate daily shopping.
