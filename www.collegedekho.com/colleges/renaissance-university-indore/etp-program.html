Renaissance University Job Ready Degree

Keeping in mind the industrial demand and needs, Renaissance University has introduced four Job ready courses. These courses are specially designed to prepare students for the professional world. In these courses both theoretical and practical knowledge is offered to students. The “Assured” courses at Renaissance University are B.Tech in FSD (IXP), BBA in Digital Marketing (IXP), BCA in Full Stack Development  (IXP) and PGDM in Digital Marketing (IXP). The annual fees for BTech course is Rs 1,20,000 whereas for BCA course candidates need to pay Rs 70,000 per annum. Admission seekers need to pass Class 12 from with aggregate 50% marks to be eligible for admission in the Job Ready “Assured” course at Renaissance University.
