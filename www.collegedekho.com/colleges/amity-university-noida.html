About Amity University Noida

Amity University Noida is a private university founded in 2005. Located in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, Amity Noida is recognised by UGC, AICTE, and is accredited by NAAC with an A+ grade. In NIRF 2025 rankings, Amity University Noida was ranked 37th and 49th in Engineering and Management categories. The university offers 600+ programs, including UG, PG, PhD, Diploma, Certificate, Dual-Degree courses, and more.

MBA, BBA, B.Tech, LLB, BA LLB are some of the popular courses offered by Amity Noida. The university offers admissions based on merit in qualifying exam & personal interview. However, national entrance exam scores like CAT/MAT/XAT/GMAT/NMAT are also accepted for MBA admissions. Scholarships up to 100% and fast track admissions for high performing students are also available at Amity University Noida.

In terms of placements, overall highest package at Amity Noida was INR 85 LPA in 2025. The highest package for B.Tech stood at 35.9 LPA, while average and median packages were INR 9.6 LPA and INR 6 LPA, respectively. For the MBA class of 2024, the highest & average packages offered by the university were INR 26.30 LPA and INR 7.35 LPA, respectively.

Amity University Noida Admission 2026

Amity University Noida admission 2026 is open for undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs. Admissions at Amity Noida are based on merit in the qualifying examination, followed by a Personal Interview (PI). Applications are accepted online through the official portal (noida.amity.edu). Students need to pay a non-refundable application fee of INR 1,500.

Amity University Noida also accepts entrance exam scores like CAT, XAT, MAT, GMAT, and CMAT for MBA admissions. For B.Arch, a valid NATA score is mandatory. Admissions at Amity Noida are offered under three categories: Non-Sponsored, Sponsored, and Company Sponsored. 

Fast Track Admission is also available at Amity Noida for students scoring 90–95%+ in CBSE/ISC Class XII, along with scholarships of up to 100% on the first-year academic fee based on merit or entrance exam performance. The university also accepts JEE Main, CUET, NEET, & CLAT for scholarship purposes.

Amity University Noida Admission Process 2026

The step by step process for admissions at Amity University Noida is given below: 

  • Step 1: Register online on the official Amity admission portal (noida.amity.edu) by filling out the application form and paying the INR 1,500 application fee.
  • Step 2: Upload required documents including Class 10th/12th mark sheets and photograph.
  • Step 3: Wait for application review and shortlisting (typically within 2–3 working days).
  • Step 4: Appear for the selection process, which may include a Personal Interview (PI) and/or program-specific test (if applicable).
  • Step 5: Check admission status on the admission microsite and download the offer letter if selected.
  • Step 6: Confirm admission by paying the required fee online.
  • Step 7: Report to campus with original documents for verification and complete admission formalities.

Amity University Noida FAQs

Are there international opportunities at Amity University Noida?

Amity University Noida offers international exchange programs, tie-ups, and study-abroad options for students. Check Amity Noida global partnerships and exchange programs for exact opportunities.

What is the placement record for B.Tech at Amity University Noida?

Amity University Noida B.Tech placement record varies by branch and year. Top branches get strong recruiter interest; check Amity Noida placement reports for branch-wise placement statistics and averages.

Does Amity University Noida offer distance learning?

Amity University Noida offers distance and online programs under Amity Directorate of Online Education. Check Amity Noida’s distance learning portal for available courses and admission rules.

What are the hostel facilities at Amity University Noida?

Amity University Noida hostels provide furnished rooms, mess, Wi-Fi, security, and recreational spaces. Options include shared and single rooms; check Amity Noida hostel details for fees and rules.

What is the admission process for Amity University Noida in 2026?

For Amity University Noida 2026 admissions, apply online via Amity portal, choose course, submit documents, and appear for Amity entrance test or use national exam scores where accepted.

Is Amity University Noida expensive?

Amity University is considered costly compared to other universities in India. The yearly tuition fees for bachelor's degree programs are between Rs 42,500 and Rs 95,000 for non-sponsored students. For sponsored/ NRI students it can be as high as Rs 1,11,000. The university also asks for more money because of hostel expenses (Rs 45,000), safety deposit (Rs 5,000) and other small costs. In total, the expense for an education from Amity University is not insignificant.

What is the ranking of Amity University Noida?

Amity University Noida, a private university in India, is ranked 1001-1200 on the QS World University Rankings 2025. It holds the position of 53 (overall category) and 35 for government universities category according to National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) 2023. Furthermore, the university has been given an "A+" grade from the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).

What is the Amity University Noida JEE Main cutoff percentile for direct admission cum scholarship into B.Tech programs?

For getting direct admission cum scholarship into B.Tech programs at Amity University Noida through JEE Main, you need to have these cutoff percentiles:100% scholarship for JEE Main percentile between 97-10075% scholarship for JEE Main percentile between 94-96.950% scholarship for JEE Main percentile between 90-93.9

Does Amity University Noida provide 100% placement?

Amity University Noida being one of the renowned universities in the country offers a good placement facility with approximately 60-70% students getting placed through campus interviews. The university has a very good record of placements with more than 11000 offers for the 2023 batch placement and the major recruiting companies include IBM, Google, Microsoft, and Amazon, etc. But they do not guarantee placements at 100% because placements depend on several factors such as the confidence level, grades achieved, and the current employment opportunities.

Can I get a scholarship in Amity University Noida?

Amity University Noida gives many scholarships to its excellent students. The university offers scholarships ranging from 50% to 100% based on academics and sports achievements among other criteria. Scholarships are given for undergraduate and postgraduate programs which include B.Tech, BA LLB and more. The procedure of eligibility and application is different for each scholarship, therefore students who are interested must consult the university's scholarship policy for detailed information.

What are the facilities at Amity University Noida?

Amity University Noida has many facilities, such as different hostels for boys and girls inside the campus, a big sports area of 15 acres where you can find a swimming pool, squash courts, tennis courts and shooting area. There are also cafeterias, food courts, departmental stores with a bookshop; gymnasiums; laundry services along with underground parking space. The university provides medical help too - cultural clubs exist here as well as festivals or events take place from time to time at this location.
