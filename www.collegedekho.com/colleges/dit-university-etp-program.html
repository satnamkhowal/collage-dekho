DIT University Job Ready Degree

Getting a job right after you graduate is a dream come true for many students. If you are also dreaming the same then you should get admission to one of the job ready courses offered by DIT University. This university offers one UG and one PG course which are job-centric. These assured courses at DIT University are designed in a way which teaches you with the latest course material and assures that you gain job-relevant skills. The DIT University job ready courses are B.Des in UX (IXP), and M.Des in UX (IXP). The fees for these courses range from Rs 1,68,000 to Rs 1,98,000 per year. Meritorious students can also claim merit-based scholarships offered by DIT University. This scholarship is offered to 10% of the students of each programme. Through merit-based scholarships, students can get a discount between 10%-30% on the tuition fee. DIT University also offers other scholarships like scholarships for girl children, students from Uttarakhand & other Himalayan states, scholarships for children of defence personnel, etc. Interested applicants can find the fee, eligibility, and duration details of DIT University job ready courses below.

DIT University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

For admission to DIT University job ready courses, applicants only need to meet the eligibility criteria, there is no need for an entrance exam. Students can fill out the application form, submit the required documents, and pay the fee to get admission.
