Cochin University of Science and Technology is a state government university. The college was established in 1971 and was originally known as University of Cochin and later reorganised as Cochin University of Science and Technology. It is located in Kochi in the state of Kerala. CUSAT particularly emphasises postgraduate studies and research in the fields of applied science, technology, industry, and commerce.

The university has been re-accredited by NAAC with an A grade and is also recognised by UGC. Additionally, more than 70% of the departments of CUSAT Kochi have won research projects that have been awarded by national agencies like ICMR, DRDO, DAE, and ISRO. It is the only university that has been publishing a high-quality newsletter in English for the past 20 years highlighting all-important university activities. The Cochin University of Science and Technology also provides recognition to several well-established premier research institutions in the country and the Southern Naval Command of the Indian Navy.

Cochin University of Science and Technology Kochi has some prominent centers of study such as the Advanced Centre for Atmospheric Radar Research (ACAAR), Centre for Science in Society (C-SiS), and Inter-University Centre for IPR Studies (IUCIPRS). CUSAT faculty has some of the most experienced and talented members for providing holistic education to their students.

CUSAT Kochi, at present, has a total of 27 departments of study and research offering graduate and postgraduate courses in areas of engineering, science, technology, humanities, law & management.

CUSAT Kochi courses and fees structure is designed to promote graduation and post-graduation studies in applied sciences, Technology, industry, commerce, management, and social sciences The institution is also home to many advanced Centers aiming at conducting advanced research in various fields. CUSAT offers UG courses like B.Tech, LLB and PG courses such as M.Tech, M.Phil, M.Sc, MCA, MSc, and Ph.D. in different specialisations.

Cochin University of Science and Technology admission in UG courses is based on the minimum qualifying marks in 10+2 examination and marks secured in CUSAT Common Admission Test. In order to be eligible for PG courses at CUSAT, candidates need to have good academic records in graduation to get admission. Selection in PG courses will be based on the scores of various entrance examinations such as GATE, CMAT/ CAT for M.Tech and MBA respectively. Admission to PG courses is also based on Department Admission Test (DAT). In addition, for getting admission in Ph.D. at CUSAT, candidates must hold a Master’s degree from a recognised university with minimum qualifying marks.

CUSAT placement cell works toward building strong and mutually beneficial relations between the corporate and students. The placement record for the university has been impressive with multiple recruiters coming forward to select the students for diverse roles and profiles at attractive compensation packages.

Cochin University of Science and Technology Courses and Admission 2026

Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), Kochi admission 2022 is open now for UG and some PG courses. Candidates looking for Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), Kochi UG courses admission must appear for the Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), Kochi entrance exam conducted by the university itself.
Online registration for CAT (accepted for all undergraduate and post graduate courses excluding M.Tech, MBA, Diploma and PhD) started from 8 th February, 2022 and remained open up to 25th of March, 2022.

  • The students can apply for M.Tech course 2022 by April 21st , 2022.
  • The students can apply for MBA course 2022 by April 25st , 2022.
  • The students can download admit cards for the CUSAT CAT exam from May 1st to May 14th , 2022.
  • CUSAT CAT (PG) Entrance Exam dates will be 14th and 15th May, 2022.
  • CUSAT CAT (UG) Entrance Exam dates will be 22nd , 23rd and 24th of June, 2022.
  • IMU-CET 2022 and CUSAT CAT (B.Tech) exams will be held on 29th May, 2022.

Getting admitted to the CUSAT Kochi undergraduate courses such as BA, BSc, and Certificate of Proficiency (COP) courses is done purely based on merit proven in the performance in CUSAT entrance exam and other accepted exams. On the other hand, the admission to PG courses at CUSAT Kochi is based on the candidate's performance in Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), Kochi Entrance Examination, CAT / MAT / KMAT / GATE and viva voce. The application form for Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), Kochi PG admission was opened from the last week of April. Registrations for admission to the M.Tech and MBA programmes at the University were closed on April 31st , 2022.

Cochin University of Science and Technology Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

The 180-acre campus of CUSAT Kochi has everything a student will need for their overall development. The campus is located in Kochi, the capital city of Kerala. The CUSAT Kochi provides the students with the best environment for learning and the most advanced infrastructure for professional growth. The CUSAT Kochi campus has several schools and special centers, student, faculty, and residential staff quarters, a healthcare center that is operational 24 hours, and an administration block. In addition, the campus is well equipped with a sports ground for indoor and outdoor
games, a gym, ATM, library, mess, and shopping facilities.

  • Hostel – Cochin University of Science and Technology CUSAT Kochi hostel is very much spacious and well-furnished having of a large dining hall. It is to be noted that admission to Cochin University of Science and Technology CUSAT Kochi doesn't ensure guaranteed hostel accommodation due to the limited capacity of 600 males and 700 females in the hostels. Cochin University of Science and Technology CUSAT Kochi offers accommodation to the applicants based on different factors.
  • Gym - Gymnasium is part of CUSAT Kochi campus facilities. Students can take care of their health and fitness by making use of this gymnasium facility provided by the CUSAT Kochi. The gym is well equipped with exercise and training equipment. The gym helps the students to stay fit and healthy.
  • Sports – The CUSAT Kochi campus has outdoor and indoor games facilities for students.

The Campus Cafe remains open from about 8.00 A.M. in the morning to 8.00 pm. at night on all working days even including Saturdays. A very wide variety of South Indian and North Indian dishes are also available there. During lunchtime, the meals are available there at a very nominal price.

Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), Kochi Application Process 2026

CUSAT Kochi application process usually starts in April of every year with the announcement of the application form for the MBA and M.Tech programmes. The selection criteria for different courses are different in the University and for different courses. The application process for CUSAT Kochi entrance exam is completely online. To complete the COCHIN UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (CUSAT), KOCHI application form, candidates need to follow the below-mentioned steps:

  • First of all, the interested students need to visit the official admission portal of CUSAT Kochi.
  • They need to go to the admission page of the official website of CUSAT Kochi.
  • Then they need to click on the “New Registration” tab to register themselves for new admission.
  • They will be asked to fill out a form with their respective personal details and set a password that they will need to remember and thus they will get registered successfully.
  • They should read the instructions carefully and click to start the registration procedure.
  • They need to select the concerned boxes to opt for any special advantage.
  • They will have to upload a recent photograph of theirs, whose file size must not exceed 500 kb (dimensions 150 px x 180 px)
  • They will have to upload a scanned signature of theirs, whose file size must not exceed 100 kb (dimensions 130 px x 57 px), in .jpg, .jpeg, .png formats.
  • Students need to select the programmes or the combination of some programmes from the given dropdown list very carefully.
  • Next, the students need to select their latest acquired qualifications. Based on that selection of the qualification by the student, a list of available programmes at the University will be dropped down in front of them.
  • They need to click on the “Confirm Course” button.
  • After confirming that, the respective test codes will be displayed in front of them, from which they need to select the test code specific for the very course they want to get admitted in.
  • Then they need to click on the “Confirm Test” button.
  • Then they need to submit all the course details.
  • After submitting, they will be displayed a short preview of what they have put in so that they can make any changes or corrections needed and save those.
  • After confirming the preview section, application fee is needed to be paid via online mode of transaction. Online payment can be made through any Credit/ Debit Card or Net banking of almost any Bank. At this step, the students may be able to check the test centre availability. The whole application process will be considered to be complete only when successful payment of application fees will be done.
  • Candidates are suggested to keep the receipt of the fee paid safe as proof for any further need where they have to prove that they have applied.
  • After the successful completion of the payment procedure, the list of examination centres which will be available will be displayed. Candidates need to select one of the test centres among those where seats are available for them and will also be convenient.
  • CUSAT Kochi entrance exam (CUSAT-CAT) is a computer-based test, which is a must to qualify to be eligible for the admission procedure. After qualifying for the examination, candidates will be invited for viva-voce by the COCHIN UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (CUSAT), KOCHI admission committee . The selection of candidates for CUSAT KOCHI admission will be done based on their overall performance in the last qualifying examination, COCHIN UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (CUSAT), CUSAT-CAT marks, and Viva-voce marks.
