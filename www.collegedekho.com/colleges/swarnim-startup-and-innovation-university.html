About Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University

Swarrnim Startup & Innovation University (SSIU), established in 2017 in Gandhinagar, Gujarat, is India's first startup-focused private university, approved under the Gujarat Private University Act. Ranked among India's top 50, it operates as a hub for multidisciplinary programs across 13 constituent colleges, offering 106 courses in engineering, architecture, design, science, management, and more. Recognized by UGC, AICTE, and other bodies, SSIU emphasizes hands-on training, value-added certifications, Apple labs, and robust startup incubation with over 100 innovative ideas supported. Boasting international collaborations with institutions like Birmingham City University and a 75-acre lush campus with hostels, sports facilities, and scholarships, it fosters employability and global opportunities for students from 20+ states and abroad.

Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University Application Process 2025

A candidate can apply online to Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University, Gandhinagar. There is direct admission available for some courses, and for others, the candidates need to go through counselling rounds based on entrance test marks, PI and profile-making rounds. 

Step 1: Applicants interested in getting admission to Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University, are required to visit the website of SSIU and click on the admissions tab. Then they need to click on ‘Apply Now’, in which case they would be redirected to the SSIU application page. If one has any queries, they can request a callback using the chat box or can contact the admission cell directly by using the phone number mentioned on the website on the top left. 

Step 2: On clicking the ‘Apply Now’, the applicant needs to enter the contact details and their name, email address, state, city, mobile number, a course in which they are interested, and other required details. 

Step 3: a valid email id is mandatory for continuing with the application process. The applicant needs to verify their email address by entering the OTP. To verify the email address, click on the ‘verify now’ button and then an OTP will be sent to the mail, which needs to be entered for verification. 

Step 4: This email id shall be used for communication purposes throughout the admission process, and it cannot be changed. If the candidate has mistyped the email (before verification), then he/she can go back and change it.

Step 5: Once the email verification is completed, the candidate can proceed to the application page. Here, the applicant needs to enter their details such as father’s name, mother’s name and other family details. 

Step 6: The aspirant needs to enter their communication details like parent’s contact number, address, etc. Then academic information needs to be entered. This needs to be done properly. Any error in this section can lead to the candidate’s disqualification as eligibility criteria are checked via the academic information entered by the candidate. The academic information includes Class 10 passing marks and year, Class 12 passing marks and year, details of the school, etc.

Step 7: After this is done, the candidate needs to upload their mark sheets of Class 10 and Class 12, Aadhar Card, Character Certificate, Migration Certificate, Admit Card of Class 10, and Transfer Certificate with a photograph of the candidate. 

Step 8: Lastly, the candidate must pay the non-refundable application fees. One can pay through Gpay, UPI, net banking, credit card, bank transfer, debit card, and others. After that, the applicant would require to go to the merchant site where the payment needs to be completed.

Step 9: Download the application form once the payment is done and keep it safe for future use. It must be noted that the application form will only be considered submitted once payment is made.

Step 10: There are specific courses such as MBA, which require PI round and some other courses like Architecture which require portfolio making and some tests for which the candidates would be called after the application process is over. The student needs to keep checking the dashboard and their mail from which the entrance test details could be downloaded.

Step 11: After the selection process, the students would get an email about whether they were selected. The selection letter will be sent directly to the student if they fulfil the eligibility criteria for those who don’t need to give the entrance test. 

Step 12: Once the selection letter is mailed, the applicant needs to go to the institute and then the document verification process is carried out.

Along with the application letter, the candidate needs to carry their other academic documents, which they have uploaded on the SSIU admission portal. After the verification, the candidate needs to pay the course fees.

FAQs about Swarrnim Startup And Innovation University

Are the B.Tech courses of Swarnim Startup and Innovation University recognised?

Yes, the Swarnim Startup & Innovation University B.Tech courses are recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). Since the UGC has granted recognition to SSIU under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, this means that SSIU is eligible to receive financial assistance and that its B.Tech as well as other degrees are also recognised by the UGC.

Does Swarnim Startup and Innovation University have its own individual entrance examination?

SSIU does not conduct its own entrance exam. Instead, the university accepts some state level and national level entrance exams for selecting candidates for the programmes that it offers including GUJCET, GATE, CAT, CMAT, NATA and JEE Main. The specific entrance examination that a student needs to take will depend on the programme they are applying for.

What is the strength of the faculty of Swarnim Startup and Innovation University?

The total faculty strength of Swarnim Startup and Innovation University is more than 85. These are learned professionals who are experts in their respective areas and have significant experience in teaching. The faculty members are highly qualified and experienced, with many of them holding doctorates from top universities in India and abroad.

Does Swarnim Startup and Innovation University have a management quota?

Yes, Swarnim Startup and Innovation University has a management quota for admission to its undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. The management quota is reserved for students who have scored well in the qualifying examination, but who do not meet the minimum cut-off marks for open competition. The management quota is usually around 25% of the total seats in each programme. The exact number of seats in the management quota varies from programme to programme.

In which city is Swarnim Startup and Innovation University located?

Swarrnim Startup and Innovation University is located in the village of Bhoyan Rathod, in Gandhinagar district, Gujarat, India. The university is about 12 kilometres from Gandhinagar, the state capital. The SSIU campus is spread over an area of 110 acres and it has state-of-the-art facilities, including laboratories, libraries, auditoriums, and hostels.

Is Swarnim Startup and Innovation University private or government?

Swarnim Startup & Innovation University (SSIU), a private university established under the Gujarat Private Universities Act, 2017 by the Gaya Prasad Jain Charitable Trust through the Government of Gujarat. SSIU is a non-profit organisation that is committed to providing quality education to students. The university offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, technology, management, science, and humanities.
