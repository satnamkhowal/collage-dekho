About IIT Bombay Courses 2026

IIT Bombay offers 80+ courses across undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral, and executive levels in engineering, science, design, management, and humanities. At the UG level, IIT Bombay offers BTech, BS, BDes, and dual-degree programmes. At the PG level, the institute offers MTech, MBA, MSc, MDes, MS by Research, MPP, and several integrated dual-degree programmes. IMumbai IT Bombay also offers PhD programs in Engineering and Management, which last 3-5 years. IIT Bombay fees 2026 range from INR 1.13 to INR 15.15 Lakhs. The IIT Bombay fees for 4 years BTech are INR 8.59 Lakhs.

The Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IITB) has launched new courses: MTech in Climate and Sustainability and MTech in Manufacturing Processes, Systems, and Automation. IIT Bombay also offers professional certificate programmes in Cybersecurity and Software Development through the IITB Trust Lab.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Can international students apply for courses at IIT Bombay?
IIT Bombay allows international students through special admission channels. They can apply via DASA or ICCR schemes depending on the course. Eligibility and entrance requirements may differ for foreign applicants.
Can students pursue research projects during IIT Bombay courses?
IIT Bombay encourages students to take part in research projects. Many departments offer guided research opportunities, helping students gain practical exposure and prepare for higher studies or research-based careers.
Does IIT Bombay allow semester exchange programs during BTech?
Yes, IIT Bombay maintains active Memorandums of Understanding with numerous top-tier international universities. Undergraduate and postgraduate students can apply for a semester exchange abroad, provided they meet the strict CGPA criteria and secure departmental approval beforehand.

Most Commonly Question Asked

What is IIT Bombay policy for the academic performance evaluation system?
IIT Bombay follows a CGPA-based evaluation system. Students are graded on exams, assignments, and projects. Continuous assessment at IIT Bombay helps track academic progress and encourages consistent performance throughout the course.
Does IIT Bombay offer direct PhD after BTech option?
IIT Bombay offers direct PhD for students with high CGPA, usually 8.0 or above from IITs. These students can skip master’s and apply directly through departmental selection process.
Can non-engineering students apply for IIT Bombay courses?
IIT Bombay allows non-engineering students for courses like MBA, MDes, MA, and MPP. These programs accept graduates from different backgrounds based on entrance exams and eligibility criteria.

IIT Bombay BTech fees is INR 73,000 to 91,000, which covers only the non-tuition semester charges and one-time admission fees over the course duration.

IIT Bombay UG Courses 2026

IIT Bombay offers 4 UG courses in BTech, BS, BDes, and BTech + MTech Dual Degree. The BTech in IIT Bombay is offered in 11 specialisations, and the BS in 4 disciplines. All IITB UG programmes are for 4 years except the Dual Degree, which spans 5 years.

IIT Bombay BS Course 2026

IIT Bombay BS course is a 4-year programme offered in 4 disciplines: Chemistry, Economics, Mathematics, and Applied Geophysics. IITB BS admission is through JEE Advanced and JoSAA. Students completing the BS programme can choose to continue for a 5th year to earn an MSc degree.

IIT Bombay BDes Course 2026

IIT Bombay BDes is a 4-year programme offered through the IDC School of Design in the specialisation like Industrial Design, Visual Communication, Interaction Design, Animation Design, and Vehicle Design. IIT Bombay also offers a 5-year integrated BDes + MDes dual degree

The BDes course in IIT Bombay requires a UCEED (Undergraduate Common Entrance Exam for Design) score, conducted by IIT Bombay, while IIT Bombay does not offer direct admission into a 5-year integrated BDes + MDes program from the start. At the end of their third year, top-performing students can opt to convert their 4-year degree into a 5-year Dual Degree (BDes + MDes).

Most Commonly Question Asked

What is IIT Bombay seat intake for BTech branches?
IIT Bombay offers around 1,100+ BTech seats across 11 branches. Total intake increases with supernumerary seats. Seat distribution varies by branch, with Computer Science and Electrical having higher competition.
Can I change my BTech branch at IIT Bombay?
Yes, IIT Bombay allows branch changes after the first year based purely on your two-semester CGPA. However, it is highly competitive, and strict seat matrix limits apply, meaning only the top-performing students usually secure their preferred branch shift.
Does IIT Bombay allow semester exchange programs during BTech?
Yes, IIT Bombay maintains active Memorandums of Understanding with numerous top-tier international universities. Undergraduate and postgraduate students can apply for a semester exchange abroad, provided they meet the strict CGPA criteria and secure departmental approval beforehand.

IIT Bombay PG Courses 2026

IIT Bombay PG courses are offered in Engineering & Science, Design, Management and Humanities & Policy. The IITB PG course list includes MTech, MSc, MS Research, MPP, MA Research, MDes, MDP, MBA, and MA + PhD. 

PG courses IITB admission is based on discipline-specific national entrance exams, like GATE, IIT JAM, CAT, or CEED, followed by a written test and/or interview at the institute level.

IIT Bombay MSc Course 2026

IIT Bombay MSc is a 2-year course offered in 7 specialisations. During the MSc programme, top-performing students may convert into the PhD programme as per Senate-approved eligibility criteria. The MSc + PhD dual degree is offered in 3 additional specialisations, Environmental Science & Engineering, Operations Research, and Energy Science & Engineering, for students who wish to continue directly into doctoral research. MSc students admitted with a TA fellowship pay INR 17,750 per semester and receive a monthly stipend of INR 12,400.

IIT Bombay MBA Course 2026 (SJMSOM)

The MBA course at IIT Bombay spans 4 semesters and is offered through the Shailesh J. Mehta School of Management (SJMSOM). MBA courses in IIT Bombay include 23 core courses, covering Finance, Marketing, Operations, Strategy, and Human Resources. IIT Bombay admission for MBA is through the CAT score, followed by a Personal Interview.

 IIT Bombay MDes Course 2026

The IIT Bombay MDes at the IDC School of Design is a 2-year programme in Industrial Design, Interaction Design, Visual Communication, and Animation Design. An MDes by Research option is also available for candidates seeking a research-focused design degree.

IIT Bombay MS by Research, MPP, and Other PG Programmes 2026

IIT Bombay MS by Research is available in Computer Science & Engineering, Data Science and AI (under CMInDS), and Healthcare Informatics (under KCDH). These are 2–3 year research-intensive programmes. 

IIT Bombay MPP (Master's in Public Policy) is a 2-year programme under the Centre for Policy Studies, focusing on governance and public administration. MDP (Master's in Development Practice) is a 2-year transdisciplinary programme under CTARA for candidates interested in rural technology and sustainable development.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Can I convert my IIT Bombay MTech into a PhD?
Yes, exceptionally performing first-year MTech students at IIT Bombay can apply to convert their program into a Dual Degree (MTech + PhD). Approval depends on securing a high CGPA, a strong research proposal, and passing a rigorous departmental interview panel.

IIT Bombay PhD Course 2026

IIT Bombay PhD course is offered across 20+ departments. It runs for a minimum of 3 years. The PhD program is broadly divided into two phases: Coursework (Phase 1) and Research Work (Phase 2). PhD scholars under TA receive INR 37,000/month for the first 2 years, rising to INR 42,000/month; under RA student gets an INR 42,000/month, rising to INR 47,000/month.

 IIT Bombay PhD selection is through a written test and/or interview by individual departments. GATE, UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, JEST, NBHM, or DST-INSPIRE fellowship are typical entry requirements.

IIT Bombay Online Courses 2026

IIT Bombay online courses 2026 include a wide range of options spanning from free short-term courses to advanced postgraduate diplomas. Online courses by IIT Bombay are delivered through various official channels, including the government’s NPTEL/SWAYAM portal, international platforms like Coursera and edX (via IITBombayX), and its own Continuing Education Programme (CEP). Below is a list of online courses in IIT Bombay

  • NPTEL & SWAYAM (Free MOOCs with Paid Certification): These are government-supported platforms where IIT Bombay offers many courses. Learning is completely free, but if a student wants an IIT Bombay online courses with a certificate, they need to pay around INR 1,000 and take a proctored exam.
  • Coursera & edX (Global Certifications): IITBombay also provides courses through IITBombayX on these global platforms. Students can study course content for free, but certificates and graded assignments require payment.
  • e-Postgraduate Diplomas (e-PGD via CEP): These are fully paid advanced online programs by IITB for working professionals. These typically run for 12 to 18 months, require a bachelor's degree (and sometimes a qualifying GATE score or work experience), and include mentorship and structured learning. Admissions and eligibility criteria are managed directly through the official IIT Bombay CEP portal (cep.iitb.ac.in)

Most Commonly Question Asked

Are IIT Bombay online course students eligible for placements?
No, students enrolled in NPTEL, Coursera, or CEP online programs do not get access to IIT Bombay’s central placement cell. Campus placement services are strictly reserved for full-time, regular students admitted to standard undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral campus courses.

IIT Bombay Course for Working Professionals 2026

IIT Bombay offers several programmes for experienced professionals outside the regular full-time campus programmes:

  • Sponsored MTech (Part-time): Working professionals from reputed organizations can pursue MTech as a part-time student (3 years), with classes scheduled from 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM. No financial assistantship is provided to sponsored candidates. A GATE score or 2 years of relevant experience is required for most disciplines (except CSE, which mandates GATE for all).
  • Professional Certificate Programmes (IITB Trust Lab): IIT Bombay launched two professional certificate programmes in 2025 — Professional Certificate in Cybersecurity and Professional Certificate in Software Development. These IIT Bombay courses online are short-term, industry-focused programmes open to graduates and working professionals. Offered online/blended.
  • NPTEL / Swayam Online Course: IIT Bombay is a key contributor to the NPTEL platform, offering 200+ free online courses in engineering, science, management, and humanities. These courses are open to anyone globally and offer certification upon passing proctored exams.
  • CEP / Continuing Education Programmes: IIT Bombay's Dean (Educational Outreach) organizes short, intensive courses and seminars for practising engineers and academics throughout the year. These are not degree programmes but offer professional development certificates.

Overall I completed my M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Bombay (2023–2025). Overall experience was great. About 95–98% of our batch got placement, with packages between ₹15 LPA and ₹46 LPA. Big-tech companies coming for recruitments included Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and NVIDIA. The department had great infrastructure—Wi-Fi smart classrooms, well-furnished labs, and 24/7 library facilities. The teachers were extremely knowledgeable and helpful, and the curriculum was industry-oriented, like AI, systems, and advanced-level computing subjects. The exams were fairly tough, and the passing percentage was approximately 95%. The total course fee was approximately ₹3.2–₹3.3 lakh. IIT Bombay Courses FAQs

What types of courses does IIT Bombay offer?

At the UG level, IIT Bombay offers B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc, B.Des+M.Des, B.Des, BSc+MSc , etc, while at the PG level, IIT Bombay offers M.Tech, MSc, M.Des, M.Phil, MBA, M.Tech+PhD, MSc+PhD, MS (Surgery), etc.

Which courses are popular at IIT Bombay?

The most popular IIT Bombay courses include the B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc, B.Des+M.Des, B.Des and BSc+MSc programmes. UG courses also include B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc, B.Des+M.Des, B.Des, BSc+MSc, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.Tech, MSc, M.Des, M.Phil, MBA, M.Tech+PhD, MSc+PhD, MS (Surgery) , etc. IIT Bombay also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does IIT Bombay accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at IIT Bombay. Admission to programmes such as B.Tech, B.Tech+M.Tech, BSc, B.Des+M.Des, B.Des, BSc+MSc and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is IIT Bombay easy to get into?

Admission in IIT Bombay courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The college conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the IIT Bombay Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include UCEED, MHT-CET, COAP, CEED, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, GATE, IIT JAM etc.

The annual cost of undergraduate programmes ranges from Rs 27000 for BSc+MSc to Rs 1000000 for B.Tech+M.Tech. The annual IIT Bombay fee structure for PG programmes is between Rs 15000 for MSc to Rs 200000 for MBA.
