About Government Engineering College (GEC), Gandhinagar

Government Engineering College, Gandhinagar is a public engineering college located in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. The institute was established in 2004.  Government Engineering College, Gandhinagar has been recognized by AICTE  and is accredited by Gujarat Technological University. Library, Laboratory, and Hostel are some of the Government Engineering College, Gandhinagar facilities. Government Engineering College, Gandhinagar courses are offered at Level of courses such as UG, and PG levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include B.tech and M.Tech. Government Engineering College, Gandhinagar admission is based on the entrance exam. The college accepts JEE Main / GUJ CET and GATE /Gujarat PGCET for admission.
