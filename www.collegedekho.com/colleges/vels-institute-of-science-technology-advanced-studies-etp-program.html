VELS University Job Ready Degree

The job market is expanding rapidly and with that the demand for technologically skilled employees. How can one identify the required skills and effectively develop them while pursuing a degree? One of the many gateways is the Job Ready Degree offered at VELS University Chennai. The course curriculum is designed in a way that will help candidates gain real-time practical experiences and insightful knowledge making them ready to outperform in the competitive world. Those who are interested can take admission in BBA in Digital Marketing (IXP) offered at VELS University. The course duration is three years and the fee is Rs 45,000 per semester.

VELS University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

Admission in the “Assured” programme available at VELS University Chennai is based on the aggregate score of candidates' performance in the qualifying examination and personal interview rounds conducted by the university. There is no specific entrance exam for VELS University job ready degree admission.
