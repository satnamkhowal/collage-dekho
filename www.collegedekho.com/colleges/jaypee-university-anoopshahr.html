Jaypee University (JUA) is a private university, located in Anoopshahr, Uttar Pradesh. Established in 2014, this university was established by the Government of Uttar Pradesh under UP Act No. 8 of 2014. JEE Mains scores are used to determine admission to the university's B.Tech. programme. Admission to doctoral programmes is based on the UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, GATE, SLET, or JUAT. The university has a dedicated placement cell that trains and mentors incoming graduates on how to build a successful career. Check Jaypee University (JUA) Admission Process.

The university provides UG programmes in a variety of specialisations, including B.Tech., B.Com., BA, BBA, and B.Sc. The university believes in offering quality education through UG-level courses in the fields of Engineering & Technology, Arts, Commerce, Management and Science. Jaypee University, Anoopshahr Courses and Admission 2026

The modes of Jaypee University (JUA) course admissions 2023 are online and offline. Admission to various courses is based on entrance exams and merit. At the UG level, Jaypee University Anoopshahr courses offer four-year full-time B.Tech and, three-year full-time BBA, B.Com BA and B.Sc courses to students. The full-time B.Tech course is offered in around 12 specializations. The courses offered by Jaypee University Anoopshahr and their respective eligibility and Jaypee University (JUA) fees are provided on this page for complete information.

Jaypee University (JUA) offers admission to undergraduate programmes. Jaypee University Anoopshahr admission to UG courses is based on JEE Main exam or on the basis of merit. Jaypee University (JUA) admission to UG courses offers B. Tech in 5 specializations with an intake of 330 seats. For admission into B.Tech, a candidate must have got a minimum aggregate of 60% in the 10+2 examination with Physics and Mathematics as the main subjects and one of the Chemistry/Computer Science/Informatics Practices/Multimedia & Web Technology/Engineering Drawing/Engineering Graphics/English. The university also offers B.Tech Lateral Entry admission for which the candidate must pass in BE/B.Tech first year or B.Sc. degree or Diploma in Engineering with a minimum aggregate of 60% marks. Jaypee University Anoopshahr Admission to B.Tech Lateral is done on the basis of JUAT.
