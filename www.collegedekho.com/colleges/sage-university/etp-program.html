Sage University Bhopal Job Ready Degree

Sage University Bhopal offers a unique blend of conventional and job-oriented degree programmes. These courses are curated to equip students with the skills that are in high demand in today's evolving job industry. If you're looking for a course that not only provides a degree but also enhances your employability, then Sage University Bhopal has a range of options for you. With an annual fee ranging from Rs 50,000 for undergraduate programmes to Rs 65,000 for postgraduate degrees, the university offers eight job ready courses like BBA in Financial Management and Banking Investment & Insurance, MBA in the same specialisations, BCA in Animation, B.Des in Visual Communication, and B.Com in Banking & Finance and International Business & Finance.

Sage University Bhopal Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

Sage University Bhopal offers direct admission for these job ready “Assured” courses. Students are shirlisted based on their academic merit in the qualifying examination.  Candidates just have to fill out the Sage University Bhopal application form and pay the fees.
