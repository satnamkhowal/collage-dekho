About Chandigarh University Ranking

Chandigarh University ranks 526th globally in the QS World University Rankings 2027, released on June 18, 2026. The university rose 49 places from approximately 575 in 2026. It ranks 13th in India and 2nd among private universities. Its overall QS score is 31.1, and its Academic Reputation score is 24.9. CU has ranked in 1% of the Top Universities in India and 2% in the Top Universities Globally by the QS World University Ranking 2027.

Chandigarh University also performed well in India's National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) 2025, released by the Ministry of Education. As per the NIRF ranking 2025, Chandigarh University has achieved 19 ranks in Best Universities in India.

In the Engineering category, it has been ranked 31st, while the Pharmacy category stands at 15th position, and the Management category at 32nd position. The University has also been placed in 34th position for the Research Institutions category and 14th among the Best Institutions for Architecture & Planning in India, with a score of 59.70 in the NIRF 2025 ranking.

Chandigarh University NIRF Rankings 2025

Chandigarh University has secured strong rankings across 6 categories in NIRF 2025, released by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The CU Mohali ranks 19th among Universities, 31st in Engineering, 15th in Pharmacy, 14th in Architecture and Planning, and 32nd in Management, with an overall national rank of 32nd. 

These results place CU among the few private universities in India to appear in the top 35 across five different NIRF categories in the same year.

Chandigarh University NIRF Ranking Trend (2022-25)

Chandigarh University has climbed steadily across every NIRF category over four consecutive editions.
