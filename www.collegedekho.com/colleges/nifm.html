About National Institute of Financial Markets (NIFM), Delhi

National Institute of Financial Markets, Delhi is situated in Delhi in Delhi state of India. was established in 2008. National Institute of Financial Markets, Delhi offers 6 courses across 4 streams namely Commerce and Banking, Management, IT, Education..Popular degrees offered at National Institute of Financial Markets, Delhi include B.Ed, Diploma, Certificate..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, National Institute of Financial Markets, Delhi is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at National Institute of Financial Markets, Delhi, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at National Institute of Financial Markets, Delhi is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
