NIT Rourkela Eligibility and Selection Process

For admission to the MBA programme at NIT Rourkela, candidates must hold a BBA degree or an equivalent bachelor’s qualification with at least 50% marks in aggregate. The selection process is based on performance in national-level management entrance exams such as CAT, XAT, or MAT. For the MA programme, applicants are required to have a bachelor’s degree in any discipline from a recognised university, with a minimum aggregate of 55% marks. Selection for the MA course involves qualifying through exams like GATE and participating in the centralised CCMT counselling process. Candidates can get an insight for the detailed eligibility criteria for NIT Rourkela courses before applying for admission. NIT Rourkela Application Process 2026

Candidates can fill out the NIT Rourkela application form in online or offline modes. The admission form can be downloaded from the university website. The application fee for NIT Rourkela is Rs 1,500 for General/ OBC/ EWS candidates, which needs to be paid along with the admission form.

How to Apply for NIT Rourkela 2025?

The NIT Rourkela application process is completely online. Candidates need to follow the steps mentioned below to complete the NIT Rourkela application process. 

  • Step 1: Register on the official admission portal of NIT Rourkela with correct personal details, including name, email ID, mobile no, state, gender, and password
  • Step 2: Fill in the NIT Rourkela application form.
  • Step 3: Apply for the required entrance exam.
  • Step 4: Candidates are required to book a slot with their preferred date and time to appear for the relevant entrance exam.

NIT Rourkela Documents Required for Admission

  • Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets and certificates
  • JEE Main/GATE/IIT JAM/CAT scorecard (as applicable)
  • Character certificate and school leaving/transfer certificate
  • Category certificate (if applicable)
  • Three passport-size photographs
  • Valid proof of residence
  • Aadhaar card of the student
  • Anti-ragging affidavit signed by the student and parents
  • Income certificate (for fee remission, if applicable)
