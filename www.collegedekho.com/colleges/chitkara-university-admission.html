Chitkara University Admission 2026 Overview

Chitkara University admission 2026 is ongoing for its UG, PG, and Doctorate programs in various fields including engineering, management, design, health sciences, and more. The university primarily offers B.E. programs at the undergraduate level, with admissions based on national-level entrance exams or merit.

Admissions to B.E. programs accept JEE Main scores, followed by university-level counselling and personal interview. For B.Arch programs, admissions are based on NATA or JEE Main Paper 2 scores. For MBA programs, admissions are based on CAT/MAT/XAT/NMAT/CMAT scores, followed by group discussion and personal interview or institute-level processes. The university also offers Ph.D. programs, with admissions involving university entrance tests/interviews or national exams like GATE/GPAT/UGC-NET.

For B.E. admission 2026, candidates must have passed 10+2 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as main subjects, securing at least 60% aggregate marks with valid JEE Main score or 70% without JEE Main score.

Chitkara University Admission Process 2026

Chitkara University admission process 2026 is conducted online through centralised counselling. Admission is based on national level entrance exams, with JEE Main for BE, NATA for BArch, UCEED for BDesign, CAT for MBA and many other entrance exams for several other courses. The process is completed online in multiple phases, including online registration, choice filling, seat allotment, fee payment and document verification. Only the final reporting and institute registration are done offline at Chitkara University after seat confirmation

Chitkara University UG Admission Process 2026

The below are the steps provided through which a candidate can apply for admission at Chitkara University undergraduate courses. Check the complete steps below:

Step 1: Check Eligibility Criteria

  • Bachelor of Engineering (BE):
    • Candidate must pass 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects.
    • A minimum of 60% marks is required with a valid JEE Main score.
    • A minimum of 70% marks is required without a JEE Main score.
  • Bachelor of Architecture (BArch):
    • Candidate must pass 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics or 10+3 Diploma with Mathematics and at least have 45% aggregate marks.
    • Candidate must have a valid NATA score.
  • BDes/BFA: Candidate must pass 10+2 with a minimum aggregate of 50% and having a valid UCEED score.
  • BPT: Candidate must pass 10+2 with a minimum aggregate of 50% and having a valid NEET score.
  • BA BEd (ITEP): Candidate must pass 10+2 with a minimum aggregate of 50% and having a valid NCET score.
  • Candidates must meet the minimum academic requirements prescribed by the counselling authority.

Step 2: Register for Counselling

  • Some individual institute or state-level counselling authorities conduct the counselling process.
  • Registration must be completed within the deadline.

Step 3: Choice Filling and Locking

  • Candidates need to select Chitkara University and preferred UG course and specialisations.
  • Review and lock choices carefully before the deadline.

Step 4: Seat Allotment

Seats are allotted through multiple counselling rounds based on:

  • Entrance exam rank/score
  • Category and reservation norms
  • Choice preferences
  • Seat availability

Candidates may accept, upgrade, or withdraw as per counselling rules.

Step 5: Seat Acceptance Fee Payment

  • Pay the prescribed seat acceptance fee online to confirm the allotted seat.

Step 6: Online Document Verification

Upload and verify required documents through the counselling portal, such as:

  • Entrance exam scorecard
  • Academic mark sheets and certificates
  • Category certificate (if applicable)
  • Identity proof

Step 7 Reporting to Chitkara University

  • After successful verification, report to the institute for final admission formalities.

Step 8: Institute Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation

  • Pay the remaining institute fees and complete registration to confirm the admission.

Chitkara University Admission FAQs

What are the admission criteria for engineering courses at Chitkara University?

Admission to engineering courses at Chitkara University requires passing 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, securing at least 60% aggregate marks and a valid JEE Main 2026 score. Alternatively, 70% marks in 10+2 without JEE may qualify. The process involves online application and merit-based counselling.

Can I get admission in BE course at Chitkara University with 59% in Class 12 and JEE Main rank 3879?

No, admission to engineering courses at Chitkara University requires passing Class 12 with PCM, securing at least 60% aggregate marks and a valid JEE Main 2026 score. Alternatively, 70% marks in Class 12 without JEE Main may also get admission.

Is it possible to apply for admission to a programme at Chitkara University before the qualifying entrance exam results are declared?

Yes, candidates can apply for admission to Chitkara University before the declaration of the qualifying examination results for programmes where admission is based on merit. However, for courses that require specific eligibility criteria, candidates must submit proof of passing the qualifying examination with the required marks at the time of counselling or final admission.

Is it possible to receive the Chitkara University application form by post?

Yes, candidates can obtain the Chitkara University application form by post by sending a Demand Draft (DD) of the prescribed amount to the university.

Does a candidate need to submit separate application forms to apply for more than one programme at Chitkara University?

Programmes at Chitkara University are organised into different groups. A single application form is sufficient for courses within the same group. However, candidates must submit separate application forms for courses that fall under different groups.

Can a candidate appoint a nominee to attend the Chitkara University counselling process on their behalf if they are unable to be present?

No, it is mandatory for the candidate to attend the Chitkara University counselling session. A nominee cannot attend the counselling process on the candidate’s behalf.

Does Chitkara University offer admission through a management quota, reservation quota, or donation quota?

No, Chitkara University admissions are strictly based on merit and eligibility criteria. The university does not offer admission through any management quota, reservation quota, donation seats, or payment seats.

How does Chitkara University conduct the admission process for international students?

Chitkara University international student admission process is conducted through an online portal. Applicants must have equivalent qualifications, such as 10+2 for undergraduate programmes. Admission is based on academic merit, and entrance exams are not required. The process includes document verification, proof of English proficiency (IELTS or TOEFL), and visa assistance. Merit-based scholarships are available for eligible students.

Does Chitkara University have its own entrance exam?

Chitkara University conducts its own entrance exam, known as CUDAT (Chitkara University Design Aptitude Test), for admissions to design programs like B.Des and BFA. For most other courses, it accepts national-level exams such as JEE Main, NATA, NEET, CAT and other.
