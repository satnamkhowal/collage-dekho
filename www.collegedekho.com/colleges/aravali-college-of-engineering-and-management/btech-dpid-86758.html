About Aravali College of Engineering and Management (ACEM), Faridabad B.Tech

Aravali College of Engineering and Management (ACEM), Faridabad B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Aravali College of Engineering and Management Faridabad, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants should have completed their 10+2 education from a recognized board with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Computer Science as an additional subject. Additionally, candidates must have obtained a minimum aggregate score of 45% (40% for reserved categories) in the qualifying examination. Admissions are granted based on the candidate's performance in national-level entrance exams such as JEE Main or Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) conducted exams. Meeting these eligibility requirements ensures a strong foundation for students to pursue their B.Tech. degree at Aravali College of Engineering and Management Faridabad. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 55,000 - 2,20,000.
