About GIFT Autonomous Bhubaneswar

GIFT Autonomous Bhubaneswar is a private autonomous institute in Bhubaneswar, established in 2007, GIFT Autonomous Bhubaneswar campus spans over 48 acres. The college is affiliated to BPUT, Rourkela and SCTE & VT and recognized by the AICTE, New Delhi. GIFT Autonomous Bhubaneswar is ranked 201-250 band under Engineering category by NIRF under Engineering and Management in 2023. NIRF ranking: 201-250 band under Engineering category

GIFT Autonomous Bhubaneswar offers a wide range of Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Doctoral programs in the streams of Engineering and Management. B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at GIFT Autonomous Bhubaneswar are based on Entrance exams and merit. The college accepts JEE Main, OJEE for B.Tech, GATE, OJEE for M.Tech, OJEE for Diploma. For placements, GIFT Autonomous Bhubaneswar placement rate is 94.3%%. During the placement drive of 2024, the highest salary package offered was INR Rs. 12 LPA LPA and the average package stood at INR Rs. 7.6 LPA LPA.
