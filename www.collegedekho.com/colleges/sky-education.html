About Sky Education

Sky Education is situated in New Delhi in Delhi state of India. was established in 2012. Sky Education offers 4 courses across 4 streams namely Engineering, Management, Law, Education..Popular degrees offered at Sky Education include B.Ed, B.Tech, LLB, MBA..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Sky Education is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Sky Education, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Sky Education is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
