About NIT Jalandhar B.Tech

NIT Jalandhar B.Tech is a 4 - 6 Years program offered in multiple specializations. The eligibility criteria for B.Tech. at Dr B R Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar is that the candidate must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination with a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate of Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. The candidate must also have a valid score in JEE Main. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 56,000. NIT Jalandhar B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at NIT Jalandhar is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the NIT Jalandhar B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

NIT Jalandhar B.Tech Application Process

NIT Jalandhar B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at NIT Jalandhar have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill NIT Jalandhar B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of NIT Jalandhar and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of NIT Jalandhar. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the NIT Jalandhar B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the NIT Jalandhar B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of NIT Jalandhar B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for NIT Jalandhar B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

NIT Jalandhar information

Overall NIT Jalandhar is a well-known engineering college in India. It has a big and beautiful campus with modern classrooms, well-equipped labs, a large library, and good hostel facilities. The college offers different courses in engineering and science for students. There are many activities like cultural and technical fests, sports, and clubs for students to enjoy and learn new things. The placement opportunities are good, with many big companies like Microsoft, Amazon, and Infosys hiring students. However, some students feel that hostel facilities and placements for non-computer branches can be improved. Overall, NIT Jalandhar is a great place for students who want to study engineering and build a bright future.

Placement The placement of some branches like computer science , information technology and electronic and communication engineering departments are very good. Fir mechanical engineering department the placement is 100 % but for other core branches the placement is about 40 -50 % only.

Infrastructure Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology (NIT) Jalandhar has a well-developed infrastructure that supports academic, research, and extracurricular activities. The campus spans over 154 acres, featuring modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, research centers, and a vast library with digital resources. It has separate hostels for boys and girls, a sports complex, gymnasium, health center, and auditoriums for events. The institute also boasts advanced computing facilities, Wi-Fi-enabled buildings, and eco-friendly initiatives like solar power and rainwater harvesting. Additionally, industry collaboration centers and incubation labs promote innovation and entrepreneurship among students.

Hostel HOSTELS There are 10 boys hostel and three girls hostel at NIT Jalandhar. Boys hostel are named as bh1 bh2 bh3 bh4 bh6 bh7 and MBH a block b block and f block The girls hostel are named as GH 1 gh2 and MGH For 1st year boys three hostess are allotted and the rest hostels are for senior students For 1st year girls GH 1 and GH 2 are allotted and the MGH is for senior year girls The hostess are well maintained with good sanitation and facility
