About Chandigarh University

Chandigarh University (CU) is a private university in Mohali, Punjab, established in 2012. The university holds NAAC A+ accreditation and is recognised by the UGC. Also, it is the first private university in India to earn ABET accreditation for nine engineering programmes. CU offers 200+ courses across UG, PG, and doctoral levels in engineering, management, pharmacy, law, and more, with annual fees ranging from INR 84,000 to INR 3.97 Lakhs. Chandigarh University conducts admissions primarily through its own entrance test, CUCET (Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test), held in two phases. 

In the QS World University Rankings 2027, Chandigarh University is ranked 526th globally and 2nd among Indian private universities, making it one of the fastest-rising private universities in India over the past five years. In NIRF 2025, Chandigarh University secured the 19th position in the University category.

CU's placement record is one of the strongest in North India. In 2025, over 10,000+ job offers were made by 1,300+ companies, with the highest domestic package at INR 59.9 LPA and the highest international package at INR 1.7 Crore offered to BTech students.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Does Chandigarh University accept lateral entry or student migration?
Yes, Chandigarh University accepts lateral entry directly into the second year for programs like BTech and BPharm. For migration, students must provide a No Objection Certificate (NOC) and detailed transcripts, subject to credit mapping by the university.
Does Chandigarh University offer UGC-approved online or distance degree programs?
Yes, through its Institute of Distance and Online Learning (CU-IDOL), the university offers fully UGC-DEB approved online degrees, including MBA, MCA, BBA, and MA. These remote programs operate independently from the regular full-time campus degree courses.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Can I pursue a minor degree along with BTech?
Yes, Chandigarh University follows a flexible Choice Based Credit System (CBCS). This allows engineering students to take open electives and earn a minor degree in entirely different fields like Psychology, Management, or Design.
Does Chandigarh University offer BTech with AI and ML?
Yes, CU offers specialized BTech CSE programs in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, and Data Science. These programs are designed with industry partners like IBM and Microsoft, though the tuition fees are slightly higher.
Is Chandigarh University's BTech degree recognized for foreign jobs?
Absolutely. Chandigarh University is the first private university in India with ABET accreditation for nine engineering programs. This international recognition ensures your BTech degree is globally accepted by top tech employers and universities for higher education abroad.

Chandigarh University Admission 2026

Chandigarh University admissions are primarily based on its own online entrance test, CUCET (Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test), which is conducted in remote-proctored mode. CUCET is s held in two different phases, namely CUCET-I and CUCET-II. It is mandatory for admission to B.E (engineering), MBA, Pharmacy, LLM, and Law Programmes. The university also accepts scores from national-level tests such as JEE Main, GATE, CAT, MAT, NATA, and other relevant examinations. Admissions at Chandigarh University are conducted in both online and offline modes. The complete admission process of Chandigarh University is as follows:

Chandigarh University Admission Process 2026

Chandigarh University Admission goes through various processes starting from the application process to seat allotment. The step-by-step process of admission for Chandigarh University is given below:

  • Register on CUCET portal at cucet.cuchd.in by paying INR 1,000 application fee.
  • Choose your preferred exam date and slot from the calendar available on the portal.
  • Appear for CUCET online from home with a laptop, webcam, and live invigilator support.
  • You will receive results and a scholarship offer based on CUCET percentile simultaneously on your registered email.
  • Visit the university campus or complete the online process to select a programme and submit documents.
  • Pay the admission fee and complete the enrolment formalities.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Can I attempt CUCET multiple times to improve scores?
Yes, you can attempt CUCET a second time if you are unsatisfied with your initial Phase 1 score. However, you must pay the INR 1,000 application fee again, and the highest score among your attempts will determine your scholarship.
Can I get direct admission in BTech without CUCET?
No, for core programs like BTech, MBA, and Pharmacy, appearing for CUCET is mandatory. Direct admission is only possible for some non-technical BA or BSc courses, but you will miss out on major scholarship benefits.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Does CU provide paid internships during the pre-final year?
Yes, CU has a dedicated Department of Career Planning and Development (DCPD) that helps students secure paid internships by their 6th semester. Top recruiters like Amazon and Microsoft often offer pre-placement offers (PPOs) post-internship.
Do core engineering branches get good placements at CU?
Yes, core branches perform exceptionally well. Over 3,750 engineering students were placed in recent drives, with major industry recruiters like L&T, TVS, and Honda hiring heavily from Mechanical, Civil, and Electrical domains at highly competitive salary packages.
Which BTech branch records the highest placements at CU?
Computer Science Engineering (CSE) and its specializations consistently record the highest placements. In 2025, the peak domestic package of INR 59.9 LPA and the international package of INR 1.7 Crore were both secured by BTech CSE students.

Chandigarh University sets cutoffs for 2025 admissions using multiple entrances like JEE Main for BTech, CUCET for MBA, MCA, BBA, and most UG/PG streams, plus NCHMCT JEE for hospitality, keeping thresholds low to welcome applicants with 50% eligibility in prior qualifications. 

JEE Main's CSE closing rank eased to 63,416 for general category, making tech entry more feasible, while CUCET's 30% minimum score qualifies all for counselling, with 90%+ unlocking full scholarships.

Chandigarh University FAQs

What documents are necessary for admission at Chandigarh University?

The documents required for admission to Chandigarh University are marksheets from classes 10, 12, graduation, postgraduation (as required), proof of DOB, caste certificate, character certificate and transfer certificate.

What exam is accepted for MBA admission at Chandigarh University?

For MBA admission at Chandigarh University, candidates must have passed with a graduation degree from a recognised university. The entrance exam scores accepted are CUCET/ CAT/ MAT/ ATMA/ XAT.

Is admission possible at Chandigarh University without an entrance test?

To secure admission to Chandigarh University, candidates have to first meet the eligibility criteria for their chosen course and then take the CUCET exam conducted by the university itself. The exam is mandatory for admission, however, for some courses such as BE or MBA, national-level entrance exam scores are considered such as JEE Main or CAT.

Are the Chandigarh University courses UGC approved?

Yes, the courses offered at Chandigarh University are recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). The university awards degrees per Section 22(1) of the UGC Act, 1956 and is recognised by UGC under Section 2(f).

How to pay the fees online for Chandigarh University?

The online fee payment at Chandigarh University can be done by Payment Gateway choices which include Net Banking and Credit/ Debit Cards. Within 72 hours, the status of the fee paid online will be updated.

How to get admission to BPharm at Chandigarh University?

To secure admission in the B.Pharm programme at Chandigarh University, one must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology as main subjects with a 50% aggregate and a valid CUCET score.

Who are the top recruiters during Chandigarh University placements?

Chandigarh University was the 1st university in India to hold the Limca Book of Records for the Highest Number of Companies in 1 academic year. Some of the top recruiting companies include Cognizant, Sapient, IBM, Mindtree, SAP Labs, VMWare, Hitachi, Practo Technologies, Amazon, Microsoft, John Deere, Schindler, HP, Deloitte, etc.

What are the Chandigarh University campus facilities?

The campus of Chandigarh University has modern facilities for students. The 250 acres campus has individual boys and girls hostels, 14 well stocked libraries, spacious auditoriums, cafeteria, playground for outdoor sports, wifi, medical centre, well equipped laboratories etc. Chandigarh University campus facilities allow students to gain both practical and theoretical knowledge.

Which Chandigarh University courses are for medical students?

The university has a University Institute of Applied Health Science that offers a medical stream programme. Medical courses at Chandigarh University are B.Sc Nutrition and Dietetics, Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT), B.Sc Medical Lab Technology, M.Sc Forensic Science, M.Sc Medical Lab Technology etc. The university provides merit based admission in medical courses.

What is the NIRF Ranking 2025 for Chandigarh University?

Chandigarh University has shown strong upward momentum in the NIRF Ranking 2025, securing the 19th position among Indian universities. This rise reflects the university’s expanding research output and academic performance. In QS Asia 2025, the university stands at 120th, making it one of the highest-ranked private institutions in India. From a category perspective, the university demonstrates competitive strength with 31st rank in Engineering and 32nd in Management under NIRF 2025. It recorded a 60.46 score in Engineering and 59.40 in Management, supported by an impressive 73.83 score in the Teaching, Learning & Resources (TLR) parameter. Additionally, 515 global collaborations further enhance its academic and research ecosystem.

Yes, the university has an association for its alumni. Is Chandigarh University private or government?

Chandigarh University Mohali is a private university established in the year 2012. It is one of the youngest private universities in India to get an ‘A+’ grade accreditation by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). It is also accredited by the NBA and approved by the UGC, NCTE, AICTE, PCI, and AIU. Furthermore, the university has secured a good rank in the NIRF ranking 2023.

What is the Chandigarh University entrance exam?

For admission to CU, the university conducts the Chandigarh University Common Entrance Test (CUCET). Apart from this, exams like JEE Main, GATE, NATA, CAT, MAT, XAT, and NMAT are also accepted for Chandigarh University admission.

Chandigarh University Campus and Facilities

Chandigarh University's main campus is a 238-acre self-contained township in Gharuan, on the NH-05 Chandigarh-Ludhiana Highway, Mohali. The campus includes separate academic blocks for each department, residential zones, sports complexes, and a medical facility, all connected by Wi-Fi 24/7. Below mentioned are some of the Chandigarh University campus facilities:

  • Libraries: 13 libraries on campus — one central library plus 12 department-level libraries.
  • Hostels: Separate hostels for boys and girls with furnished rooms, 2-seater to single-seater options. Mess serves fresh food daily; the mess fee is included in the hostel package. Hostel fee range: INR 1.00 Lakh to INR 2.14 Lakhs per annum, depending on room type.
  • Sports: Cricket ground, football ground, basketball courts, volleyball courts, badminton courts, tennis courts, boxing arena, a full gym with qualified instructors, and a swimming pool.
  • Labs: Industry-collaborated labs with IBM, TCS, SAP, Intel, and Microsoft hardware/software ecosystems for hands-on learning.
  • Medical: On-campus medical centre with free first aid, free routine checkups for nursing students, and discounted services for all students.
  • Fests and Events: Annual cultural fest (CU Fest), tech fests, MUN (IGNITE MUN), national and international conferences. Past performers and speakers include Diljit Dosanjh, Neha Kakkar, Harry Sandhu, Akshay Kumar, and Katrina Kaif.
  • Transport: Bus services are available from Chandigarh and Kharar, the campus is 2 km from Kharar Bus Stand and 18 km from Chandigarh Airport.

Does CU campus have guest house facilities for parents?
Yes, Chandigarh University has an on-campus guest house. Parents or guardians visiting students can book a stay for a limited duration, subject to prior approval and room availability, by applying through the student's CUIMS portal.
Are AC rooms available in Chandigarh University boys' hostels?
Yes, the university offers both AC and Non-AC hostel rooms across single, double, and multi-seater configurations. AC rooms cost significantly more and are allotted on a strict first-come, first-served basis during the online admission process.
Can day scholars use the gym and sports facilities?
Yes, day scholars have full access to campus sports complexes, libraries, and gymnasiums. However, specific facilities like the swimming pool or professional gym training might require a nominal separate registration or membership fee for all students.

Can I apply for an education loan without CUCET?
You must first secure your admission seat, which generally requires clearing CUCET for major courses. Once you receive your confirmed admission letter, you can apply for collateral-free education loans through CU's banking partners like PNB.
How can I maintain my 100% CUCET scholarship annually?
To renew your 100% CUCET scholarship every year, you must strictly maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.5 across all semesters. Failing to meet this academic requirement or receiving any backlogs will result in the immediate cancellation of your fee waiver.
