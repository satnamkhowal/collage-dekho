IIT BHU Courses 2026

IIT BHU Courses 2026 encompass 13 four-year BTech programs totaling 1,589 seats allocated through JoSAA counseling post-JEE Advanced, with dual-degree options in 14 branches adding 250 spots for research-focused paths. Entry-level fees for the 2025-26 session—applicable to 2026 admits—start at Rs 21,600 for SC/ST/PwD students covering one-time, deposits, and first-semester charges, escalating to Rs 1,21,600 for General category families earning over Rs 5 lakhs, inclusive of Rs 1,00,000 tuition but exempt for low-income brackets under Rs 1 lakh. This structure supports 300+ merit-cum-means scholarships, waiving up to full tuition for 25% of undergraduates, ensuring accessibility amid Varanasi's historic campus fostering 92% placement rates in tech and core sectors.

Postgraduate offerings span 18 MTech specializations with 450 seats via COAP/GATE, plus PhD in 15 disciplines enrolling 250 annually across departments, all under the same fee slab with Rs 500 medical insurance added in odd semesters. If targeting CSE BTech, expect 75% Class 12 PCM eligibility and ranks under 1,500; dive into specifics below to align your JEE prep with exact intakes and costs for a seamless 2026 application.

IIT BHU Courses FAQs

What do students say about IIT BHU courses and fees value in 2025-26?

Students call it highly affordable and valuable. A CSE student said, “Rs 1.2 lakh first year gave access to world-class labs and full tuition waiver for my family income — best ROI with 92% placement.” A Civil MTech graduate shared, “GATE entry at around Rs 50000/year funded international fieldwork; the low fee and strong curriculum directly led to Rs 20 LPA offers.” Most praise the sliding-scale fees and abundant scholarships.

What reservation policy applies to IIT BHU seats in 2026?

UG seats follow 27% OBC-NCL, 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 10% EWS, and 20% female supernumerary quota. A 5% horizontal PwD reservation applies across all categories. PG seats have 5% PwD horizontal reservation. PhD seats are department-specific but give preference to reserved categories and JRF holders.

Does IIT BHU offer credit transfer from online courses in 2026?

Yes, up to 20% credits can be transferred from NPTEL/SWAYAM MOOCs (over 500 courses). Access is free; certification exam costs Rs 1000. Popular courses include Data Structures, Machine Learning, Renewable Energy, and Ethics in Technology. These credits work well for electives or dual-degree thesis enhancement and require approval from the Dean of Academic Affairs.

How many PhD programs and departments does IIT BHU have in 2026?

IIT BHU offers PhD in 15 departments: Architecture Planning & Design, Ceramic Engineering, Chemical Engineering & Technology, Civil Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Humanistic Studies, Mechanical & Industrial Management, Metallurgical Engineering, Mining Engineering, Pharmaceutical Engineering & Technology, Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematical Sciences. JRF-qualified candidates receive full fee waiver and Rs 31000 monthly assistantship.

What are the eligibility criteria for IIT BHU BTech 2026 admission?

Candidates need 75% aggregate in Class 12 PCM (65% for SC/ST/PwD) or top 20 percentile, must qualify JEE Advanced (top 250000 ranks in JEE Main), be born on/after 1 October 2001, and have maximum two attempts. Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics are compulsory. The same criteria apply to all BTech and dual-degree branches; final selection is through JoSAA counseling.

Which MTech specializations are available at IIT BHU in 2026?

IIT BHU offers 32 MTech/MPharm specializations: Biochemical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Ceramic Engineering, Chemical Engineering, six Civil branches (Structural, Geotechnical, Hydraulics, Environmental, Transportation, Engineering Geosciences), three Electrical (Power Systems, Machines & Drives, Control & Power Electronics), four Electronics (Microwave, Digital Techniques, Microelectronics, Communication Systems), three Mechanical (Machine Design, Heat Power, Production), two Metallurgical (Extractive, Alloy), three Mining (Environment, Rock Mechanics, Planning), Industrial Management, Materials Science & Tech, Systems Engineering, and four MPharm streams (Pharmaceutics, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmacology, Pharmacognosy).

What is the total seat intake across all programs at IIT BHU in 2026?

Total annual intake is 2594 seats: 1894 UG seats (BTech + dual degrees + BArch), 450 PG seats (MTech + MPharm), and 250 PhD seats. UG includes 20% female supernumerary quota; standard reservations are 27% OBC, 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 10% EWS, and 5% PwD horizontal across categories.

Which BTech-MTech dual-degree programs are offered at IIT BHU in 2026?

IIT BHU offers 14 five-year dual-degree programs: Ceramic Engineering & M.Tech., Computer Science & Engineering & M.Tech., Civil Engineering & M.Tech., Electrical Engineering & M.Tech. in Power Electronics, Mechanical Engineering & M.Tech., Metallurgical Engineering & M.Tech., Mining Engineering & M.Tech., Biochemical & Biotechnology, Bioengineering & Biomedical Technology, Materials Science & Technology, Pharmaceutical Engineering & Technology, Industrial Chemistry, Mathematics & Computing, and Engineering Physics. These award both degrees with a mandatory research thesis.

How does the fee structure work for IIT BHU UG courses in 2026?

Fees follow the 2025-26 income-based slab. General/OBC/EWS above Rs 5 lakh family income pay Rs 121600 first year (includes Rs 100000 tuition). Income Rs 1-5 lakh pay Rs 54933, below Rs 1 lakh pay Rs 21600 with full tuition waiver. SC/ST/PwD pay only Rs 21600 irrespective of income. Mess advance is Rs 16500 per semester and hostel rent Rs 300 (hostel rent waived for reserved categories). More than 300 merit-cum-means scholarships fully waive tuition for eligible students.

What BTech courses are available at IIT BHU for 2026 admissions?

IIT BHU offers 15 BTech branches in 2026: Ceramic Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Metallurgical Engineering, Mining Engineering, Pharmaceutical Engineering and Technology, Industrial Chemistry, Engineering Physics, Mathematics and Computing, Biochemical Engineering, and Bioengineering. All are 4-year programs with 160 credits, starting with common first-year sciences and moving to branch-specific labs and electives like AI, sustainable mining, and biomedical applications.
