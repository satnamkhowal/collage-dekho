About SOA Bhubaneswar

Siksha 'O' Anusandhan has been ranked 1001-1200 in QS World Rankings 2023. Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA), formerly Siksha 'O' Anusandhan University, is a private deemed-to-be university located at Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. The university is composed of nine degree-granting schools and colleges and has a student body of around 10,000. Many of SOA's programs are nationally accredited, including engineering, medicine, pharmacy, business, nursing, biotechnology, science, humanities, environment, nanotechnology, materials science, agriculture and law. 

SOA is a NAAC accredited Deemed to be University situated in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. NAAC as accredited it with an A++ grade (3rd cycle). SOA was granted greater autonomy (graded autonomy) by the UGC in 2018, one of the few to be granted so in India. The Ministry of Education (MoE) has released the NIRF ranking 2025. As per the rankings, Siksha O Anusandhan has achieved an NIRF rank of 49 in the Research Institutions category along with an NIRF rank of 22 in the Engineering category. The institute has also achieved NIRF scores of 45.12 and 62.58 in each category respectively. In terms of category wise rankings, the Siksha O Anusandhan NIRF ranking management 2025 is on 66 position with a score of 50.88. The college has also achieved NIRF 15 rank in the Medical category with a score of 63.73. For the Dental category, Siksha O Anusandhan achieved the 9 NIRF rank with a score of 63.12.
