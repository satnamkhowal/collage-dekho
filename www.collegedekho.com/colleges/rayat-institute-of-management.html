About Rayat Institute of Management

Rayat Institute of Management is situated in Ropar in Punjab state of India. Established in 2003, RIM is a Private college. The college is affiliated with Punjab Technical University and accredited by AICTE. Rayat Institute of Management offers 7 courses across 3 streams namely Commerce and Banking, Management, IT..Popular degrees offered at Rayat Institute of Management include MBA, BBA, BCA, B.Com..Besides a robust teaching pedagogy, Rayat Institute of Management is also a leader in research and innovation.Focus is given to activities beyond academics at Rayat Institute of Management, which is evident from its infrastructure, extracurricular activities and national & international collaborations. The placement at Rayat Institute of Management is varied, with recruitment options both in corporates and public sector as well as entrepreneurship.
