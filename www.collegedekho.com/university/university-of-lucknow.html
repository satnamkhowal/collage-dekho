About Lucknow University

The University of Lucknow, also known as LU, is a public state university established in 1920. Located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, the Lucknow University campus spans over 223 acres. The university is affiliated with its own governance and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), AICTE, ACU, AIU, and DEC, with an A++ grade from NAAC. Lucknow University is ranked 97th in the University category by NIRF 2024 and has been marked for the first time globally in the Times Higher Education Ranking 2024.

Lucknow University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs in streams such as Arts, Science, Commerce, Engineering, Law, Management, and Fine Arts. B.Tech, B.Sc, BCA, and MBA are some of the popular courses. Admissions at Lucknow University are based on merit in the qualifying examination and entrance examinations. The university accepts JEE Main for B.Tech, CUET UG for undergraduate programs like BA, B.Com, and B.Pharm, CUET PG for postgraduate programs like MCA and MBA, and NEET UG for B.Sc Nursing.

For placements, Lucknow University’s placement rate is approximately 70% for undergraduate programs, as per the NIRF 2024 report. During the placement drive of 2023, the highest salary package offered was INR 24.80 LPA by ONGC, and the median package stood at INR 7.99 LPA for UG 3-year programs and INR 7.50 LPA for UG 4-year programs.

Lucknow University Admission 2025

Lucknow University admissions are based on merit in the qualifying examination and national/state-level entrance examinations, followed by counseling or personal interviews for certain courses. Students seeking admission at Lucknow University must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the university and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for Lucknow University admissions:

  • B.Tech: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech must appear for JEE Main and participate in UPTAC counseling for seat allotment.
  • B.Sc, BCA, BBA, B.Com, BA: Admissions to these programs are based on CUET UG or the Undergraduate Entrance Test (UGET) conducted by the university, followed by a Personal Interview where applicable.
  • B.Sc Nursing: Candidates must appear for NEET UG and the ABVMU entrance test, followed by counseling.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the Lucknow University Management Entrance Test (LUMET) or CAT, followed by a Personal Interview.
  • MCA: Admissions are based on CUET PG or the Postgraduate Entrance Test (PGET) conducted by the university.

Lucknow University FAQs

Why Lucknow University is so popular?

Lucknow University is popular for several reasons. It is one of the oldest public state universities in India. It enjoys a strong reputation in terms of academic excellence and providing a holistic educational environment to its students. Lucknow University has produced famous politicians, poets, journalists, novelists, and scientists, such as Shankar Dayal Sharma, Ex-President of India and author and novelist Nitya Prakash.

Is Lucknow University good or not?

Lucknow University is generally considered good for studies. It offers a variety of courses under streams like arts, engineering, law, commerce, management, and pharmacy. Admissions at Lucknow University are entrance exams attracting high-quality students. Placements are also decent and opportunities for internships are provided to the students. More importantly, Lucknow University fees is highly affordable and it provides scholarships as well.

How to check Lucknow University results?

To check the results of Lucknow University, you can visit the official website of the university. Also, the results for different semesters can be found at https://result.lkouniv.ac.in/Results/. Here, you can access the results for various examinations, including odd and even semester results for different years. Input course name, subject name, roll number, and date of birth to check the Lucknow University result.

How to download the Lucknow University admit card for exams?

To download the Lucknow University admit card for exams, you can follow the steps given below:Visit the official website of Lucknow University, https://www.lkouniv.ac.in/.Click on the ‘Download Admit Card’ link on the home page. Enter your roll number/LURN number, and captcha for the semester/entrance exam. Click the submit button.  You can also visit the Lucknow University exam portal, https://exam.lkouniv.ac.in/apps and follow the same steps to download the admit card.

How to fill out Lucknow University examination forms?

To fill out the Lucknow University examination forms, you can follow the steps given below:Visit the official exam portal of Lucknow University, https://exam.lkouniv.ac.in/apps. Log in by entering the username, LURN/roll number, password and captcha. Fill out the examination form with the required details, including personal information, educational qualifications, and examination details. Upload the required documents, including a passport-sized photograph and signature.Pay the examination fee through the available payment options.Submit the examination form and take a printout of the same for future reference

What entrance examinations are conducted by Lucknow University?

Lucknow University conducts multiple entrance examinations for admission to undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and doctoral programmes. Undergraduate Entrance Test (UGET) is held for admission to UG courses such as BA, B.Com, BCA and B.Sc. For admission to PG courses, Lucknow University conducts Post Graduate Entrance Test (PGET). Moreover, Lucknow University Management Entrance Test (LUMET) is organised for admission to MBA programme. Similarly, entrance exams are held for admission to PhD and Diploma courses.

What are the objectives of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of Lucknow University?

The main objective of IQAC of Lucknow University is to develop a quality system for conscious consistent and catalytic programmed action. Another objective is to improve the academic and administrative performance of the departments. To promote measures for best practices and internalization of quality culture.

What basic services and facilities does Lucknow University campus offer?

Lucknow University offers all the essential amenities for the comfort of the students, including sports, a health and medical centre, a canteen, a placement office, a counselling and guidance office, a hostel, a computer centre, a guest house, and many other things.

What is the total number of departments in Lucknow University?

There are a total of 10 departments that provide a wide range of courses in various subject areas. Arts, Commerce, Education, Fine Arts, Law, Science, Engineering & Technology, Yoga & Alternative Medicine, Ayurveda & Unani are among the departments.

At what levels Lucknow University courses are offered?

Courses at the Lucknow University are offered at the diploma, undergraduate, and graduate levels. In its courses, UL offers a wide range of specialisations.

Does the Lucknow University has any educational collaborations?

Yes. There are numerous partnerships and educational collaborations at Lucknow University. It has agreements in place with CSIR-IITR, NIFT Rae Bareli, ISKCON, NCL (Coal India), and other organisations. Some of the international collaborations of Lucknow University are with the Thai Global Business Administration Technological College Thailand and Quest International College Nepal.

How many UG courses are offered by Lucknow University?

A total of 15 courses are offered by the Lucknow University. BA, B.Com, B.Tech, B.Sc, LLB, BBA, and BCA are some of the courses available at the UG level at Lucknow University.

What recognition has Lucknow University received?

University of Lucknow University is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is the only state university of Uttar Pradesh to be awarded ‘Category-I’ university status by the UGC for excellence in teaching and research. Additionally, the university has been accredited with an A++ grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).

Is Lucknow University a government or private institution?

The Lucknow University is a state university with its main campus in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. It is one of India's oldest government-owned institutes of higher learning and was established in the year 1920.
