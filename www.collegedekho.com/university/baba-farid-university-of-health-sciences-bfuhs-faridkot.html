                  BFUHS Overview

                  Baba Farid University of Health Sciences is conducting the Punjab state counselling for NEET. The authorities will release the round 2 provisional counselling results on August 25, 2023. Students will further be able to check the final counselling results for BFUHS admissions to MBBS and BDS courses on August 28, 2023. Students who will be selected on the basis of Punjab NEET counselling results will have to report to the allotted college between August 29 to September 4, 2023.

                  For Baba Farid University of Health Sciences UG admission 2023, the round 2 choice filling last date was August 21, 2023. Before that, the authorities released the BFUHS merit list for round 2 on August 16, 2023. To offer students the opportunity to check the seat vacancy after round 1 counselling, the authorities released the round 2 seat matrix on August 10, 2023.

                  For Baba Farid University of Health Sciences admission 2023 to the MBBS and BDS courses, the NEET UG exam scores are considered. The NEET 2023 cut off for BFUHS admission was 720-130 (GEN/ EWS), 129-104 (SC/ ST/ OBC) and 129-115 (GEN/ EWS - PwD) categories. Candidates have to meet the Baba Farid University of Health Sciences cut off 2023 to secure admission to the MBBS and BDS courses. The NEET PG counselling is conducted for admission to the MD, MS and PG Diploma courses. There is the university’s own entrance exam which is considered for certain programmes along with NEET scores. Merit-based admissions are granted for a few courses as well. The other exams accepted are PMNET, PPMET and PHDET.

                  BFUHS, one of the 13 universities of Health Sciences in India, has 155 associated colleges comprising 146 affiliated and 9 constituent colleges. The BFUHS courses are offered at both UG and PG levels in the subject areas of dental sciences, medical sciences, physiotherapy and nursing sciences. Other than these, certificates, diplomas, PG diplomas and PhD programmes are also available here. The Baba Farid University of Health Sciences fees range from Rs 19,500 to Rs 5.85 lakh depending on the degree level opted for. Some of the popular courses often opted by the aspirants are MBBA, BDS, B.Pharm, B.Sc and MD.

                  The Baba Farid University of Health Sciences placements are handled by a dedicated training and career guidance centre. By offering placement assistance, the university aids students in landing top employment in corporate firms and organisations that are relevant to their specialities. The cell also conducted numerous seminars, internships, industry visits, guest lectures, presentations, etc. to prepare the students for the challenging and demanding work culture. The BFUHS placement salary offered typically ranges from Rs 2 LPA to Rs 5 LPA. Companies like Cipla, St Stephens Hospital, Sun Pharmaceuticals, Medanta Medicity and many others recruit students for both full-time and internship opportunities.

                  The five-acre BFUHS campus is well-stocked with amenities like a library, hostel, cafeteria, auditorium, sports complex, labs, etc. to cater to students' academic and holistic needs. The BFUHS facility for laboratories includes modern equipment with automated instruments and has dedicated rooms for biochemistry, medical, pathology, microbiology, anatomy, immunology, haematology and Cardiac Cath labs.

                  Baba Farid University of Health Sciences (BFUHS) was established in 1998 under an Act passed by the Legislature of the State of Punjab. Situated in Faridkot, Punjab, the university is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), SAARC and the University Grants Commission (UGC), among others. Currently, the university serves as the American Heart Association's international training facility. In addition, BFUHS has four faculties and numerous component colleges. The university's four faculties are as follows: Faculty of Medical Sciences, Faculty of Nursing Sciences, Faculty of Dental Sciences and Faculty of Physiotherapy.

                  BFUHS Scholarships

                  The Baba Farid University of Health Sciences scholarships are provided to candidates that are eligible for admission but are unable to pay the fees due to the financial crisis. As a result, the university has partnered with the state government to assist these students. BFUHS offers several scholarship opportunities. To receive the benefits of Baba Farid University of Health Sciences scholarships, one must pass the scholarship test.

                  There are several scholarship programs for students. The different types of scholarship schemes mentioned on the BFUHS official website are:

                  • The scholarship is also given by the Government of Punjab, Department of Medical Education and Research. The details of scholarships which are regulated by the Government of Punjab, Department of Medical Education and Research, are provided on the website. Important letters and notices are also provided on this website.
                  • Another scheme is the BFUHS Post Matric Scholarship Scheme, the details of which are provided on the official website. Such scholarships are provided to SC /BC students. The letter from the Welfare Department for fees exception of such students is provided on the official website of the University.
                  • Post Matric Scholarship System for minority students is also provided. The details of various scholarships provided by the Government of India or the Government of Punjab are also provided on the University website. 
                  • SC category of candidates is entitled to a scholarship from the Department of Welfare, Government of Punjab, if they fulfil their terms and conditions. SC candidates with a family annual income from all sources of less than Rs 2.5 lakh are eligible for a post-matric scholarship under the guidelines of the Government of India and the Punjab Government. The respective candidates will have to produce an income declaration by self-employed parents or guardians, stating definite income from all sources by way of an affidavit on judicial stamp paper.
                  • Employed parents or guardians must get an income certificate from their employer and provide a declaration by way of an affidavit on non-judicial stamp paper for any additional income from any other sources.
                  • The respective colleges will claim the fee of such candidates from the Director, Research and Medical Education, Punjab, as per the procedure available on their website. It is the responsibility of the concerned candidates to ensure that the college has filled out his/her application form for the scholarship on time.

                  The Baba Farid University of Health Sciences campus is located in Faridkot, Punjab. The campus covers a 30.6836° N latitude region and a 74.7348° E longitude region and is spread across 5 acres of land. Environment plays a crucial role in developing a good learning atmosphere. The BFUHS infrastructure is also an important factor based on which students rate a college. A good environment can generate positive thoughts and a healthy mindset which is very much important for a student’s overall development. Thus, the BFUHS campus assures us to provide a friendly environment with all the high-tech facilities for student growth.

                  The sole mission of the institute is to create an intellectual, academic and physical environment conducive to the free flow of ideas and exchange of information between this university and other universities of health science in the country and open a window for the members of the health sector such as health planners, health managers, biomedical, social scientists and educator in health science of the country.

                  HSLIBNET

                  This is one of the major initiatives taken by Baba Farid University of Health Science for the welfare of the students. The HSLIBNET is a Health Sciences Library Network of Punjab State, where learning is possible through a networking concept. It is a concept where all the college libraries working under this university are networked to promote the culture of cooperative procurement and resource sharing. The need for information resources in the educational sector is high, but depending on expensive books and journals was not feasible and building a comprehensive collection at every college was unaffordable. 

                  To resolve this crisis, the Resource Sharing Consortium for Learning Materials was implemented. This helped to maximize the utilization of resources among students, faculties, and researchers and minimized the cost of acquisition and maintenance of learning materials. The main areas of the consortium are E-journal subscription, Union catalogues, and the Digital Library of Health Sciences. 

                  With the Implement of HSLIBNET in the BFUHS infrastructure following points have been attained :

                  • This enables access to learning materials by everyone without any discrimination at high discounted rates.
                  • The students can access the information at any time from anywhere with the help of desktop 365 days a year.
                  • Digital access to information saves printing costs and makes management easy.
                  • At the same time, multiple users can access the material. It provides access to the current content and also backfiles of the subscribed content.

                  The other BFUHS facilities available are Guest House, Ayurvedic Clinical Research Centre, Lecture Theatre, Common Room, and Demonstration Room.
