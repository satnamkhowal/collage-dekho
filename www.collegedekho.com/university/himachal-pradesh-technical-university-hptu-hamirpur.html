About HPTU

Himachal Pradesh Technical University, also known as HPTU, is a state government university established in 2010. Located in Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh, the HPTU campus spans over 2 acres. The university is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). HPTU is ranked in the 101-150 band by NIRF under the University category in 2024 and 22nd by India Today among all India universities in 2023.

HPTU offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programs in streams such as Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Science, Hospitality, and IT. Popular courses include BTech, MTech, MBA, MCA, and BPharma. Admissions at HPTU are based on merit in qualifying examinations or entrance examinations. The university accepts JEE Main and HPCET for BTech, GATE for MTech, CAT/MAT/CMAT/HPCET for MBA, HPCET for MCA, and HPCET for BPharm.

For placements, HPTU’s placement rate is approximately 80% (based on available data). During the placement drive of 2024, the highest salary package offered was INR 3.27 LPA, and the average package stood at INR 3 LPA.

HPTU Admission 2025

HPTU admissions are based on national-level, state-level, or merit-based criteria, depending on the program. Students seeking admission at HPTU must fulfill the eligibility criteria and appear for the relevant entrance examination or qualify based on merit. The admission process for key courses is as follows:

  • BTech: Candidates must appear for JEE Main or HPCET and participate in centralized counseling for seat allotment.
  • MTech: Candidates must appear for GATE or HPCET and participate in counseling for seat allotment.
  • MBA: Candidates must appear for CAT, MAT, CMAT, or HPCET, followed by university counseling.
  • MCA: Candidates must appear for HPCET, with seats filled based on HPCET scores or merit in graduation if seats remain vacant.
  • BPharma: Candidates must appear for HPCET, with selection based on HPCET or 10+2 merit if seats are vacant.
  • BBA/B.Sc: Admissions are merit-based, based on marks in the qualifying examination.
