Sigma University Job Ready Degree

Sigma University is offering full-time job ready programmes for candidates to acquire new market-integrated skills that are in high demand. These courses will not only help students gain qualitative knowledge to stand out in the competitive world but also attract good placements in roles such as Digital Marketing Strategist, Brand Manager (Digital Marketing), Full Stack Developer, Product Manager(Tech), etc after the completion of the course. A total of four such “Assured” programmes are available in Sigma University, namely, MBA in Digital Marketing (IXP), BBA in Digital Marketing (IXP), BCA in Full Stack Development (IXP) and B.Tech in Full Stack Development (IXP). Admission is based on merit and the annual fees range between Rs 85,000 - Rs 1,00,000 per year.

Sigma University Job Ready Courses 2024 - Admissions 

Interested candidates can directly take admission in the job ready “Assured” courses offered at SIGMA University. The only criterion is to meet the eligibility criteria set for the course opted. There are no specific entrance exams for admission in these courses.
