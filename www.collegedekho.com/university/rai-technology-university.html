About Rai Technology University

Rai Technology University is a private university located in Doddaballapur, Bangalore Urban, Karnataka, established in 2014. It is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and offers a variety of programs in Engineering, Management, Science, and Computer Applications. 

Rai Technology University offers 15+ courses at the UG and PG levels, with popular programs such as B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Sc, MBA, MCA, M.Sc, and M.Tech. The fee structure at Rai Technology University ranges from Rs. 60,000 to Rs. 3,00,000 per year, depending on the program and specialisation. Rai Technology University admissions 2026 are based on the national and state-level entrance exams like KCET, COMEDK, JEE Main, GATE, KMAT, PGCET and other exams.

For the 2025 placement season, 50+ companies visited the campus. The highest package offered was Rs. 16 LPA, while the average package was Rs. 8 LPA. Some of the top recruiters included TCS, Accenture, Amazon, IBM, Wipro, HDFC Bank, Capgemini, Tech Manhindra.

Rai Technology University Admissions 2026

Rai Technology University admissions 2026 are ongoing. Rai Technology University admissions are based on the national and state-level entrance exams like KCET, COMEDK, JEE Main, GATE, KMAT, PGCET and other exams. Below is the Rai Technology University eligibility criteria and Important admission dates: 

Undergraduate Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 60% aggregate in 10+2 or equivalent from a recognised board
  • Mathematics and Physics as compulsory subjects for Engineering programs
  • Valid JEE/KCET/COMEDK scores preferred but not mandatory
  • Direct admission based on merit for top performers

Postgraduate Eligibility Criteria

  • Bachelor's degree with a minimum 55% aggregate from a recognised university
  • Valid GATE/PGCET scores for M.Tech programs
  • Valid CAT/MAT/CMAT/KMAT scores for MBA programs
  • Work experience preferred for certain specialisations

Doctoral Eligibility Criteria

  • Master's degree with a minimum 60% aggregate in the relevant discipline
  • Valid NET/SLET/GATE qualification preferred
  • Research proposal submission
  • Interview performance

International Students Eligibility Criteria

  • Equivalent qualification as required for Indian students
  • English proficiency test scores (TOEFL/IELTS)
  • Valid passport and student visa
  • Medical fitness certificate
