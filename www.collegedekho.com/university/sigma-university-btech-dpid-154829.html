About Sigma University B.Tech

Sigma University B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Sigma University Vadodara, aspiring students must meet certain eligibility criteria. Firstly, candidates should have completed their higher secondary education or its equivalent, with a minimum aggregate score of 50% in subjects like Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Additionally, they must have qualified in a relevant entrance examination, such as JEE Main or GUJCET, as per the university's guidelines. It is crucial for applicants to fulfill the age criteria set by the university. Furthermore, candidates from reserved categories may be eligible for certain relaxations in terms of minimum scores. Admissions to the B.Tech. program at Sigma University Vadodara are highly competitive, and meeting these eligibility requirements is essential for securing a seat in this esteemed institution. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 75,200 - 1,09,300. Sigma University B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at Sigma University is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the Sigma University B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

Sigma University B.Tech Application Process

Sigma University B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at Sigma University have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill Sigma University B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of Sigma University and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of Sigma University. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the Sigma University B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the Sigma University B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of Sigma University B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for Sigma University B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
