About Anna University Chennai

Anna University is a public state university established on September 4, 1978, in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. It holds NAAC A++ accreditation, UGC recognition, and AICTE approval. In NIRF 2025, Anna University ranked 20th in Engineering and 2nd in the State University category. The campus spans over 189 acres.

Anna University offers 80+ UG, PG, and PhD level courses. Anna University flagship course BTech admission happens through TNEA counselling based on Class 12 marks; no separate entrance exam is needed. PG admissions use TANCET scores for MBA/MCA. MTech requires a GATE/CEETA-PG score.

Anna University reported a 58.5% placement rate in 2025, with 1,792 of 3,062 students placed. Anna University Guindy highest package reached INR 53 LPA for BTech CSE. Median salaries were INR 7.5 LPA for UG students and INR 6.0 LPA for PG students. Major recruiters included Amazon, Apple, and Bosch, with placements coordinated by CUIC.

Most Commonly Question Asked

What is the official website of Anna University?
The official website of Anna University is www.annauniv.edu. For admissions specifically, students should visit cfa.annauniv.edu (Centre for Admissions). Fee payment and academic services are managed at the e-Governance portal: www.auegov.ac.in. The TNEA counselling portal for BTech admissions is tneaonline.org — this is managed by the Directorate of Technical Education (DoTE), Tamil Nadu, not Anna University directly. Students applying for PG programmes like ME/MTech under CEAP should visit the CEAP web portal linked from the CFA site.
What is Anna University's NAAC grade?
Anna University holds NAAC A++ accreditation — the highest grade awarded by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council. A++ indicates that the university meets the highest standards in academic quality, infrastructure, research output, student outcomes, and governance, as assessed during the most recent NAAC peer team visit. Additionally, Anna University is NBA-accredited for its engineering programmes (CEG campus departments). The NAAC A++ grade, combined with UGC recognition and AICTE approval, confirms Anna University's legitimacy and quality for students choosing it over private alternatives.

Most Commonly Question Asked

What is the difference between a regular and self-supporting BTech at Anna University?
Regular programmes at Anna University are funded by the Tamil Nadu government and carry the standard INR 1.38 Lakh fee. Self-supporting programmes at constituent and affiliated colleges are privately funded — fees can range from INR 1.38 Lakhs to INR 4.35 Lakhs depending on the college. Both follow the same Anna University curriculum and examination system.
Does Anna University offer BTech in AI and Data Science?
Yes. Anna University's CEG campus offers B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering (AI and Machine Learning), and the MIT campus offers emerging specialisations including AI & Data Science. Admission is through TNEA counselling based on Class 12 PCM normalised marks. The 2025 cutoff for these branches at CEG was among the highest, often closing above 197 for the OC category.

Anna University Admission Process 2026

Anna University admission process for the Tamil Nadu BE/BTech students is entirely merit-based, without any separate entrance examination. Here is how the Anna University in Chennai admission process works:

  • Register on the TNEA official portal at tneaonline.org and complete the certificate upload.
  • Wait for the TNEA rank list, which is released based on normalised Class 12 PCM marks out of 200.
  • Fill and lock your choice of college and branch during the choice-filling window.
  • Check the provisional seat allotment and confirm your allotment within the deadline.
  • Download the allotment order and pay the initial seat confirmation fee.
  • Register separately on the Anna University e-Governance portal at auegov.ac.in to upload documents and pay semester fees online.
  • Report to the allotted campus (CEG, MIT, ACT, or SAP) on the specified admission date with original documents.

For PG programmes: ME/MTech admission is through CEAP (Common Engineering Admission Procedure) using GATE or CEETA-PG scores. MBA admission requires TANCET, CAT, or MAT scores followed by TANCET counselling conducted by the Directorate of Technical Education (DoTE), Chennai.

Most Commonly Question Asked

Is there a direct admission process for NRI students at Anna University?
Yes. Anna University has an NRI/CIWGC/FN (Children of Indian Workers in Gulf Countries / Foreign Nationals) category for UG admissions. These students apply separately through the Centre for Admissions. Eligibility, fee structure, and documents required for NRI category students differ from Tamil Nadu quota students — refer to cfa.annauniv.edu for the official circular.
Does Anna University accept GATE scores for MTech admission?
Yes. Anna University accepts GATE scores for admission to ME/MTech programmes through CEAP (Common Engineering Admission Procedure). GATE-qualified candidates are eligible for the GATE category seats at university departments, regional campuses, and constituent colleges. Candidates who don't have valid GATE scores can apply through the CEETA-PG (Common Engineering Entrance Test for Admissions to PG) category. For MBA, the accepted exams are TANCET, CAT, and MAT. The TANCET exam is conducted by Anna University and is valid for 1 year from the date of results.
Is Anna University BTech admission possible for students from other states?
Yes. Anna University accepts students from outside Tamil Nadu through the Other State quota, limited to specific seat counts in each department. Admission applications for 2026-27 were open from March 16, 2026, with a deadline of May 22, 2026, via cfa.annauniv.edu. Eligibility requires passing Class 12 with relevant subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics for engineering programmes) from a recognised board. Admission is merit-based. Other State students pay an additional TNEA counselling fee of INR 5,000 at the time of admission, over and above the standard semester fee. There is no management or donation quota at Anna University's university departments.

Anna University Distance and Online Education

How to Check Anna University Results 2026?

Students must follow the steps mentioned belowed steps to check the Anna University Chennai result:

  • Open your browser and go to the official result portal: coe1.annauniv.edu or aucoe.annauniv.edu
  • On the homepage, click the result link for your exam session (e.g., "Results for November/December 2025 Examinations — UG/PG/PhD")
  • Select your Regulation year (e.g., R-2021, R-2025)
  • Enter your 12-digit Register Number (printed on Anna University Hall Ticket)
  • Enter your Date of Birth for verification
  • Complete the CAPTCHA if prompted
  • Your result will appear as a provisional marksheet — download and save it as a PDF immediately

The portal receives extremely high traffic on the result day. If the site is slow, try accessing it before 8 AM or after 10 PM. The result is also accessible through the Anna University mobile app, available on both Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

CGPA Calculation: CGPA is the cumulative credit-weighted average of all semester GPAs. Anna University uses the formula — Percentage = CGPA × 10 — for conversion purposes (e.g., CGPA of 8.2 = 82%). A CGPA above 8.5 is generally considered competitive for campus placements and postgraduate admissions.

What RA and WHRS Mean in Anna University Results

RA (Re-Appear): The student did not clear the minimum passing marks in that subject. They must reappear in the next available supplementary/arrear examination to clear the backlog. Arrear exams are conducted alongside the regular semester exams — students can clear backlogs without repeating the full year.

WHRS (Withheld Result): The result is administratively held. Common reasons include pending exam fees, incomplete internal assessment submissions, or document discrepancies. Students should contact their college exam cell with their Register Number to resolve this.

Anna University Revaluation Process

Students dissatisfied with their marks can request revaluation. The process works in two stages:

Step 1 — Photocopy First: Apply for a photocopy of your answer script through your college exam cell. Anna University Revaluation fee is approximately INR 300 per subject.

Step 2 — Revaluation Application: After reviewing the photocopy, if discrepancies are found, submit the revaluation application within the announced deadline. Revaluation is done by a senior faculty member not involved in the original evaluation.

Revaluation Result Timeline: Typically 2–4 weeks from the application deadline. If marks change after revaluation, the updated score is reflected in a revised marksheet, distributed through the college.

(Source: Anna University Controller of Examinations — coe1.annauniv.edu; Official Notifications)
