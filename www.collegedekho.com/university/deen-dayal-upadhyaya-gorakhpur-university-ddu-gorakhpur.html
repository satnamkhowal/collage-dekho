About DDU Gorakhpur

Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gorakhpur University, also known as DDUGU, is a government university established in 1957. Located in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, the DDU campus spans over 190.90 acres. The university is affiliated with itself as a teaching and residential-cum-affiliating university and is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Bar Council of India (BCI). DDUGU is accredited with an A++ grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a 3.78 CGPA and is ranked 24th in the college category by the NIRF in 2024. It also holds the 240th rank in the QS World University Rankings (Southern Asia).

DDUGU offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and doctorate programs in streams such as Engineering, Management, Arts, Commerce, Science, Law, and Education. BTech, BSc, MBA, and LLB are some of the popular courses. Admissions at DDUGU are based on merit in the qualifying examination or entrance examinations, depending on the course. The university accepts JEE Main for BTech, DDUGU Entrance Test for BA, BSc, BCom, MA, MSc, MCom, and GATE for MTech.

For placements, DDUGU has a placement rate of 85%–90% for its BTech programs. During the placement drive of 2023, the highest salary package offered was INR 36 LPA, and the median package stood at INR 5.84 LPA for PG (2-year) students and INR 3.24 LPA for UG (3-year) students.

DDU Admission 2025

DDUGU admissions are based on merit in the qualifying examination or entrance examinations, depending on the course. Students seeking admission at DDUGU must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the university and appear for the relevant entrance examination. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for DDUGU admissions:

  • BTech: Candidates seeking admission to BTech must appear for the JEE Main and participate in the UPTAC Counselling for the seat allotment process.
  • BSc: Admissions to BSc are based on the DDUGU Entrance Test.
  • BA: Admissions to BA are based on the DDUGU Entrance Test.
  • MBA: Candidates seeking admission to MBA must appear for the DDUGU Entrance Test or CAT and participate in the university’s counselling process.

DDU FAQs

Where can I find the DDU GKP examination schedule?

A schedule has been established at Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gorakhpur University for each and every subject and its subfields. On the DDU Gorakhpur University's official website, prospective and present students can access their specific time tables for the courses they have chosen.

Where can students discover the DDU GKP test schedule?

For each subject and area of specialisation, DDU GKP has a schedule. On the university's official website, prospective and present students can see their separate schedules for the courses they have chosen.

What is the highest package that DDU GKP has to offer?

The DDU GKP placement cell is renowned for offering students a variety of employment alternatives. The cell is active and gives students the chance to connect with leading national and international businesses in a variety of fields. The largest package available to DDU students is INR 5 lacs, which is sizable and indicative of the calibre of instruction and training students get at the institution. The institution places a strong emphasis on giving its students real-world experience and exposure in the pertinent subjects so that they are prepared for the workforce.

What areas of study for a Ph.D. does DDU GKP provide?

The DDU GKP University provides Ph.D. programmes in a variety of subjects, including geography, political science, economics, management, english, hindi, psychology, and visual arts.

What criteria do the UG programmes at DDU GKP have for admission?

Applicants to DDU GKP's UG programmes are chosen depending on how well they performed in the Class 12 qualifying test. However, DDU GKP also takes UPSEE results into account. To determine whether they have qualified according to the merit list or not, candidates who wish to be admitted to DDU GKP may wish to consult the UPSEE cut off list.

Is attendance in the UG counselling procedure for DDU GKP required?

For admission to its undergraduate (UG) programmes, DDU GKP, like many other institutions in India, has made the counselling procedure a requirement. Students must adhere to a set of rules and regulations during this online process in order to be admitted into the chosen course. They could lose their chance to be admitted if they don't accomplish it.

At DDU GKP, how many seats are available?

The number of programmes provided by DDU GKP is over 72, and they include a wide range of subjects including the arts, business, science, engineering, education, law, and medical. The University offers a total of approximately 10,000 places to students through its affiliation with 341 institutions. Students have a fantastic opportunity to pursue their ideal occupations and succeed in their academic objectives thanks to the enormous number of seats the university is offering.

What is the DDU GKP admissions procedure for undergraduate programmes?

There are a few phases in the admissions process for UG programmes at DDU GKP, including completing the application form, taking the entrance test, and attending the counselling session. Students must read the eligibility requirements and submit their applications before the deadline. Students are contacted for counselling depending on their merit and the availability of seats once the entrance exam results are released.

What are the DDU GKP University courses offered?

To meet the various academic demands of the students, DDU GKP provides a broad variety of courses. The institution offers undergraduate, graduate, and doctorate programmes in a range of fields, including the arts, sciences, business, law, engineering, and medicine. The institution offers a number of well-liked courses, including B.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Ed, M.Tech, M.Pharm, MBA, MCA, and many more. The courses are intended to give students in-depth academic and practical information as well as practical experience.
