Want to know more about Indus University?

About Indus University

Indus University is a private university,  located in Ahmedabad Gujarat. The institute was established in 2012. Indus has been recognised by UGC, and AICTE and is accredited by NAAC. As per IIRF Ranking 2024, Indus University has been ranked 107 out of 160 colleges in India. Hostel, Entertainment & Sports Facilities, Transport Facilities, Library Facilities, Classrooms & Labs Facilities, Canteen Facilities, Seminar Hall Facilities, and Auditorium Facilities are some of  Indus University facilities.

Indus University courses are offered at  UG, PG, PhD levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the college include Btech, Aviation, Management, Design, and Architecture. Indus University admission is based on merit and the Entrance exam. The college accepts JEE, Mains, NATA, GUJCET, GATE, CAT, MAT, etc. for admission. Regarding Indus University placements, the highest package offered in #2023 was 30 LPA and the average package offered was 6.8 LPA. Some of the top recruiters who visit the campus for placements include ICICI Bank, Siemens, IndusInd Bank, TCS, Wipro, Decathlon, Capgemini, Godrej, Deloitte, Cognizant, ICICI Bank, Adani Gas and many more.

Indus University FAQs

Is Indus University a good choice for pursuing BTech?

The BTech programme at Indus University has shown promising outcomes. For instance, during the Indus University placements 2023, the highest package offered to the students was Rs 30 LPA.

What is the size of the Indus University campus?

The campus of Indus University spans across 16 acres. It is designed to provide a serene and knowledge-driven environment for students, surrounded by nature and located away from the bustling city of Ahmedabad.

What is the ranking of Indus University?

According to the IIRF University Rankings 2022, Indus University is ranked 101st across India and stands as the 11th state university of Gujarat among the top universities in the country.

Is Indus University a private or a government institution?

Indus University is a private university which was formerly known as Indus Institute of Technology and Engineering. The university was established in 2012 and is situated in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

Is Indus University approved by UGC?

Yes, Indus University, It received university status and recognition from the University Grants Commission (UGC) in 2012. Additionally, it is also approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

How can I check my rank in GUJCET for Indus University admission 2023?

candidates can check the  rank secured in GUJCET 2023 by following the process given below. 1)Go to the official website at www.gseb.org.2)In a new tab, the login page will appear.3)Then enter the six-digit seat number in the provided field and press the submit button. 4)The GUJCET scores will be displayed on the screen in PDF format.

How does the Indus University Placement cell prepare their students before attending the interview and during the courses?

Indus University Scholarships

Indus University offers a variety of scholarships to support its students financially. These scholarships are based on merit, need, and extracurricular activities. 

  • The Merit-Based scholarship is available for all programmes except BE and is applicable only for the first semester. From the second semester onwards, it is awarded each semester to position holders based on CGPA (minimum 3.75). The scholarship amount varies based on the percentage achieved by the student in their 12th/UG exams.
  • The Foundation for Better Life (FBL) Scholarship is a need-based scholarship offered only on the university's recommendation. This Indus University scholarship programme aims to support students who need financial assistance.
  • The Extracurricular Activities Scholarship is awarded to domestic and state-level winners in extracurricular activities. This Indus University scholarship is only applicable for the first semester and provides a 100% fee waiver in tuition fees. All these scholarships are subject to meeting the eligibility criteria set for each scholarship.
