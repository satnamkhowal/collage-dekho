About AKTU

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, also known as AKTU or UPTU (Uttar Pradesh Technical University), is a public collegiate university established in 2000. Located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, AKTU spans over 50 acres and is an affiliating university with over 700 colleges and institutes. It is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), accredited by NAAC, and approved by AICTE, PCI, and COA. AKTU is ranked 68 in the Engineering category and 101-150 in the University category by NIRF 2024.

AKTU offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across streams like Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Architecture, Design, and IT. Popular courses include BTech, MTech, BPharma, BArch, MBA, and MCA. Admissions are primarily entrance-based, accepting exams like JEE Main, CUET UG, CUET PG, NATA, CAT, MAT, CMAT, and UPCET. The university also conducts counseling through UPTAC for seat allotment.

In 2024, AKTU achieved a placement rate of approximately 75-80%, with over 13,898 students placed. The highest package was INR 49 LPA (offered to a student from IET Lucknow, an AKTU-affiliated institute), and the average package ranged between INR 4-6 LPA.

AKTU Admission 2025

AKTU admissions are entrance-based, with seats allocated through UPTAC counseling. Candidates must meet eligibility criteria and appear for relevant exams like JEE Main, CUET UG/PG, NATA, CAT, MAT, CMAT, or GATE. The admission process for key courses is as follows:

  • BTech: Appear for JEE Main or CUET UG, followed by UPTAC counseling.
  • MTech: Appear for GATE or CUET PG, followed by CCMT or UPTAC counseling.
  • BPharma/BArch/BHMCT: Appear for CUET UG or NATA (for BArch), followed by UPTAC counseling.
  • MBA/MCA: Appear for CAT/MAT/CMAT/CUET PG (MBA) or CUET PG (MCA), followed by UPTAC counseling.
  • PhD: Appear for AKTU’s PhD Entrance Test (scheduled for December 20, 2024, for Category B candidates) and interview.

AKTU FAQs

What is the AKTU BTech admission process?

AKTU Lucknow conducts the admission process for the four-year-long B.Tech course by taking the JEE Main scores into consideration, followed by UPTAC counselling. For B.Tech, candidates must have passed their 10+2 qualifying exam from a recognised board with at least 45% marks in aggregate with physics, mathematics and chemistry as main subjects. Students who belong to ST/ ST categories will be given a relaxation of 5%. According to State Govt. norms, AKTU accepts 20% of total sanctioned candidates based on JEE Main.

Does AKTU grant direct admission?

AKTU Lucknow grants direct admission to B.Tech and B.Pharmacy lateral entry candidates. Candidates who passed the CUET UG test are eligible to register online for AKTU direct admission. Before the deadline, pharmacy diploma graduates must register online for counselling. No direct admission to the second year for B.Arch., BHMCT, BFAD, BFA, B. Des, B. Voc, MBA or MCA programmes are given. Candidates who passed their qualifying examinations in a state other than UP and whose parents do not live in UP are ineligible for direct admission to the B.Tech/ B.Pharm second-year programme.

Being one of the top universities in India, AKTU during their campus placements is visited by numerous reputed companies and organisations from a variety of sectors such as IT, FMGC, marketing, banking, engineering, pharmaceuticals and many more.
