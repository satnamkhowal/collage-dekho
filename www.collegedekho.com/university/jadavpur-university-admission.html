Jadavpur University Admission FAQs

What are the eligibility to apply for admission at Jadavpur University?

The Jadavpur University eligibility criteria vary for each course. For UG courses, candidates must have passed 10+2 from a recognised board. For PG courses, a relevant undergraduate degree is required. Additionally, qualifying in an entrance test, whether at the national level, state level, or conducted by the university itself, is a mandatory part of the eligibility criteria.

What are the documents required to apply for Jadavpur University admission?

The documents required for Jadavpur University admission include the admit card and rank card for WBJEE, mark sheets and passing certificates for 10th and 12th standards, a mark sheet for Madhyamik or equivalent examination, and mark sheet for HS (10+2) or equivalent examination. If applicable, the final mark sheet for the relevant diploma course is also required.

Can I get direct admission in Jadavpur University?

Direct admission to Jadavpur University is not offered. The university follows a merit-based admission process that involves entrance exams and counselling. For Jadavpur University admissions, candidates have to appear in state-level, national level or entrance examination conducted by the university to be eligible.

How can I get admission in Jadavpur University?

For Jadavpur University admission 2024, candidates need to register online on the official website, fill out the application form, upload the necessary documents, and pay the application fee. The selection is based on entrance exams like WBJEE and JEE Main for UG courses like BE and GATE for PG courses like ME. For other courses, admission is based on merit obtained in entrance exam conducted by the university.
