About MMYVVDDE

Maharishi Mahesh Yogi Vedic Vishwavidyalaya (MMYVV), also known as MMYVV, is a private university, established in 1995 under the Madhya Pradesh Government Act. Located in Katni, Madhya Pradesh, the MMYVV campus spans over 50 acres. The university is affiliated with itself as a statutory university and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). MMYVV is ranked 42nd in India and 4th among state universities in Madhya Pradesh by IIRF University Rankings 2023.

MMYVV offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and doctoral programs in the streams of Vedic Science, Arts, Commerce, Computer Applications, Education, Social Work, and Yoga. B.A, B.Com, BCA, BSW, M.A, M.Com, MCA, and PhD are some of the popular courses. Admissions at MMYVV are based on merit in the qualifying examination for UG and PG programs, with the Doctoral Entrance Test (DET) required for PhD programs. The university accepts CBSE 12th and MPBSE 12th for UG admissions and DET for PhD admissions.

For placements, MMYVV’s placement rate is not explicitly quantified in available data, but the university has a dedicated placement cell that facilitates job opportunities. During the placement drive of 2023, specific salary packages were not disclosed, but students with over 75% marks are prioritized for internal job opportunities within the Maharishi network.

MMYVV Admission 2025

MMYVV admissions are primarily merit-based for undergraduate and postgraduate programs, with the Doctoral Entrance Test (DET) required for PhD programs. Students seeking admission at MMYVV must fulfill the eligibility criteria set by the university and submit applications online or offline. Interested students should follow the below-mentioned criteria for MMYVV admissions:

  • B.A, B.Com, BCA, BSW, B.Ed: Admissions to these courses are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination (10+2 for UG, bachelor’s degree for B.Ed).
  • M.A, M.Com, M.Sc, MCA: Admissions to these programs are based on marks secured in the qualifying examination (bachelor’s degree).
  • PhD: Candidates seeking admission to PhD must appear for the Doctoral Entrance Test (DET) conducted by MMYVV and participate in an interview for seat allotment.

Applications are submitted via www.mmyvv.com or by post to the university’s address in Katni, requiring academic documents, a demand draft of INR 1,000 payable at Jabalpur, and certificates like domicile and Aadhar. The application process typically starts in February 2025.
