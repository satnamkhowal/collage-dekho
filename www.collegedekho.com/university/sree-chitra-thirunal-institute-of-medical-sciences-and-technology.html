About SCTIMST

Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology is a public medical college and research institute located in Thiruvananthapuram. SCTIMST was established in 1973 by the Government of Kerala. According to NIRF 2025 Ranking, Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Thiruvananthapuram has secured 17th rank in the Medical category. SCTIMST courses are offered at the undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels in various fields of medicine, including Cardiology, Neurology, Neuroimaging and Interventional Neuroradiology, Cardiovascular and Thoracic Surgery, Neurosurgery, Medical Laboratory Technology, and Biomedical Technology. Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Thiruvananthapuram admissions to the courses are based on entrance examinations. For Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology admission to the M.Tech programme, candidates have to apply, appear and qualify for GATE. 

There is a placement cell at Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology that handles all the placement-related activities. It ensures that students are properly trained for the SCTIMST placement drive organised every year. According to the placement report of 2021, PG students who had opted for a two-year course were offered a median salary of Rs 510000. Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology campus offers a wide range of facilities to the students, including, a library, hostels, medical facilities, sports facilities, wifi facilities, transportation facilities, a placement cell, and research facilities.

Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Courses and Admission 2026

Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Thiruvananthapuram admissions are offered to various postgraduate courses in medical specialties and medical technology. SCTIMST admission to the postgraduate courses is based on the performance of the candidates in the entrance examination conducted by the institute. To be eligible for admission to the postgraduate courses in medical specialties, candidates must have a Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) degree from a recognized university and must have completed their compulsory rotating internship. For SCTIMST admission to postgraduate courses in medical technology, candidates must have a bachelor's degree in the relevant field from a recognised university. SCTIMST admission to the MD programme is based on the scores of NEET-PG followed by a merit list declared by the institute. 

Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Thiruvananthapuram Admission Process 2024

  • Candidates must check the Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Thiruvananthapuram eligibility criteria for the course they wish to apply for. 
  • Candidates who meet the SCTIMST eligibility criteria can apply for admission by completing the online application form.
  • For postgraduate courses in medical specialties, candidates must have an MBBS degree from a recognized university and have completed their compulsory rotating internship.
  • Candidates can apply online through the SCTIMST website during the admission period.
  • The interview is conducted to evaluate the candidate's communication skills, subject knowledge, and aptitude for research.
  • The final selection of candidates is based on the performance in the entrance examination and interview. 
  • Candidates who are selected will be informed through the SCTIMST website and will have to complete the admission process by submitting the required documents and paying the admission fee.

Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Thiruvananthapuram Application Process 2024

  • Candidates must visit the official website of SCTIMST. 
  • Candidates must check the academic section and click on register or login button.
  • Candidates must register with their personal details. 
  • Candidates must login and fill the SCTIMST application form with the desired course. 
  • Candidates must pay the required application fee. (General candidates application fees are Rs 1,000 and for SC/ST candidates fees is Rs 800).
  • Candidates must submit the form and take a hard copy for future reference.

Documents Required

  • Mark sheets and certificates of qualifying examination
  • Certificate of registration with the Medical Council of India (for medical specialties)
  • Caste/Community certificate (if applicable)
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Passport-sized photograph

Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Scholarships

Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology Thiruvananthapuram Kerala offers various scholarships and financial assistance programs to eligible students pursuing postgraduate courses in medical specialties and medical technology. Here are some of the SCTIMST scholarships offered:

  1. SCTIMST Research Fellowship: This fellowship is awarded to students pursuing research in various fields of medical sciences and technology. The fellowship provides a stipend, contingency grant, and other benefits.
  2. External Scholarships: SCTIMST encourages students to apply for external scholarships offered by various organizations and government bodies, such as the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), Department of Science and Technology (DST), and University Grants Commission (UGC).
