About Invertis University Bareilly

Invertis University is a NAAC B++ accredited private university established in 1998 in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, and granted university status in 2010 under UP Act No. 5 of 2010. The university is recognised by UGC under Section 22 of the UGC Act, 1956, and holds approvals from UGC, PCI, BCI, NCTE, AISHE, NCC, NSS, IIC, and the Ministry of Education. It is also a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) and holds four ISO certifications covering quality, environmental, information security, and energy management systems. The NAAC B++ accreditation carries a CGPA of 2.85 and is valid till March 2030. The university is a participating institution in NIRF and is located on the Bareilly-Lucknow National Highway (NH-24), Rajau Paraspur, Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh – 243123.

Invertis University offers 80-plus programmes across UG, PG, integrated, diploma, lateral entry, and Ph.D. levels in engineering, pharmacy, computer applications, management, commerce, science, law, media, design, agriculture, and education. B.Tech admission is accepted through JEE Main and IUCET, while MBA admission is based on CAT, MAT, CUET-PG, and IUCET scores. Total fees for UG programmes range from INR 60,000 to INR 6,00,000, and PG programme fees range from INR 60,000 to INR 3,60,000. The application fee is INR 750 for all categories.

The university's Corporate Resource Centre managed placements for 750-plus students in 2025-26, with 650-plus companies visiting campus. The highest package recorded was INR 41.17 LPA, the average salary was INR 5.87 LPA, and the highest international package stood at INR 16.22 LPA. Top recruiters include Amazon, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, Oracle, and Cognizant, among others.

The 70-plus acre residential campus includes six academic blocks, 120 smart classrooms, 16 computer labs with 1,300-plus systems, 2 libraries, research labs supported by DST, DBT, and SERB, a moot court, forensic and psychology labs, an auditorium with 1,200-plus seating, and a Smart Lab developed with HP and Intel. Campus facilities include separate hostels for boys and girls with a combined capacity of 2,000-plus students, a fleet of 60-plus buses covering 18 routes, and a healthcare tie-up with Clara Swain Mission Hospital. Scholarships of up to 100% of tuition fee are available across 8 schemes covering merit, JEE Main rank, CUET/CAT/MAT/IUCET percentile, CLAT rank, sports, achievements, and defence personnel categories.

Invertis University Admission 2026-27

Invertis University admission 2026-27 is open for UG, PG, diploma, and Ph.D. programmes. The application fee is INR 750 for all categories. Applications are accepted online at invertisuniversity.ac.in and offline at the Bareilly campus. Admission is based on qualifying exam marks or entrance scores from JEE Main, CLAT, GATE, CAT, CUET-PG, or IUCET.

Invertis University Admission Process 2026-27

Admission to Invertis University programs follows a streamlined process combining online/offline application, merit or entrance score evaluation, and document verification. Here is the step-by-step process:

  • Visit invertisuniversity.ac.in and navigate to the Admissions section to access the application form.
  • Fill out the application form with personal, academic, and contact details. Select the program and specialization.
  • Upload required documents: 10th and 12th marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, Aadhaar card, and passport-size photograph.
  • Pay the application fee of INR 750 online via the university payment gateway (net banking, debit/credit card, UPI) or submit offline at the admissions office.
  • The university reviews applications and shortlists candidates based on qualifying exam marks and/or entrance exam scores.
  • Shortlisted candidates are called for document verification and seat confirmation (GD-PI for MBA where applicable).
  • Pay the first-year tuition fee and confirm the seat to secure admission for the July/August 2026 session.

How to Apply for Invertis University Admission 2026 ?

Both online and offline application modes are available. Online applications are submitted through the official website; offline forms can be obtained from and submitted to the admissions office on campus at Bareilly-Lucknow NH-24, Rajau Paraspur, Bareilly - 243123.

  • Visit www.invertisuniversity.ac.in and click on 'Apply Now' or 'Admissions 2026'.
  • Select the program level (UG, PG, Diploma, Ph.D.) and choose your preferred program.
  • Register using your email address and mobile number. An OTP-based verification may be required.
  • Fill the application form with personal details, educational qualifications, and entrance exam scores.
  • Upload scanned copies of required documents in the specified format and size.
  • Pay the application fee of INR 750 via net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI through the secure payment gateway.
  • Submit the form and download the acknowledgment slip with your application number for future reference.

How to fill the Invertis University application form?

The application form of Invertis University can be filled in online mode. Students have to visit the official website and click on the ‘Apply Now’ tab to register themselves. After properly submitting the form, students will receive a form submission email. The email contains a link that can be used to download the completed application form. After submitting the application form, students cannot change the information. As a result, students must double-check the information they enter before submitting the form online. They can, however, contact the helpdesk or mail the details that need to be rectified to the admissions desk.

Is Invertis University Bareilly a government university?

No, Invertis University is a NAAC-accredited private university situated in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India. The university was established in 1998 with an aim to offer quality education to students. The IU offers several undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral courses in the fields of management, law, commerce, pharmacy, computer applications, journalism & mass communication, science, technology and engineering. The university has received approvals from the University Grants Commission (UGC), National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), Association of Indian Universities (AIU), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), Bar Council of India (BCI), National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) And All India Council of Technical Education (AICTE).

What are the Invertis University hostel fees for AC and Non-AC rooms?

The Invertis University hostel fees differ based on the AC and Non-AC facilities, and also based on the room type, be it single occupancy, double occupancy or multiple occupancies. The hostel facilities are provided to both boys and girls. The hostel fees for AC rooms are Rs 1.25 lakh per year for single occupancy and Rs 1 lakh per year for double occupancy. The hostel fees for Non-AC rooms are Rs 85,000 per year for single occupancy and Rs 65,000 per year for double occupancy. The triple or quadruple occupancy rooms are all Non-AC and the annual fees are Rs 55,000.

How many hostels are there at the Invertis University campus?

Invertis University offers hostel accommodation to both male and female students. There are a total of three girls’ hostels and four boys’ hostels. The hostels for female students are Kaveri, Bhagirati and Godavari. The hostels for the male students are Shivalik, Himalaya, Nilgiri and Himgiri. Invertis University Campus Facilities

Invertis University facilities include six academic blocks, 120 smart classrooms, 16 computer labs, 2 libraries, a moot court, research and forensic labs, a 70-plus acre green campus, separate hostels, sports facilities, 60-plus buses, and Clara Swain Mission Hospital tie-up.

Invertis University Academic Facilities

Invertis University campus features six academic blocks, 120 smart classrooms, 16 computer labs, research labs, a moot court, forensic and psychology laboratories, language lab, 2 libraries, an auditorium with 1,200-plus seating, and 4 seminar halls supporting academic and research activities.

Academic Blocks

Invertis University has six academic blocks (AB-1 to AB-6) housing air-conditioned lecture halls, libraries, science laboratories, computer labs, language labs, seminar halls, and conference halls. Each block also includes faculty cabins, HOD offices, and specialized facilities such as smart labs, research labs, moot courts, and forensic labs.

Smart Classrooms

The university has 120 smart classrooms equipped with smart boards and multimedia projectors, with most being air-conditioned. Classrooms are spacious and well-ventilated, and faculty members are provided laptops and ICT equipment to support modern teaching methods.

Laboratories

Invertis University maintains advanced laboratories across departments, equipped with modern technology and equipment from established manufacturers. These labs support hands-on learning across engineering, science, and computer science disciplines.

Smart Lab

The Smart Lab is developed in collaboration with HP and Intel, equipped with high-end PCs and software for computing, data analytics, and artificial intelligence. It supports project-based learning across Engineering, Management, and Computer Science streams.

E-Library

The E-Library has over 100 computer systems providing access to global databases, research journals, and digital resources. It supports students and researchers in accessing academic content electronically.

Computer Labs

The university operates 16 computer labs with more than 1,300 configured computers and 10 high-end servers, including DELL PowerEdge, IBM X Series, and HP Proliant systems. All systems are connected to broadband internet with relevant academic software installed.

Research Labs

Research laboratories at Invertis University are supported by funding agencies including DST, DBT, and SERB. The labs cover fire testing, composite testing, advanced computation, and construction material research, enabling interdisciplinary scientific investigation.

Workshops

The university's workshops provide students with technical resources to work with different materials and techniques. These workshops form part of the academic curriculum, combining theoretical learning with hands-on technical training.

Forensic Labs

The Forensic Science Lab allows students to study and apply forensic science principles to real-world cases. The lab is equipped with specialized equipment to support hands-on learning and research in forensic techniques.

Language Lab

The Language Lab uses Orell Talk corporate software for language learning across listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. The software is based on the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and includes personality development modules.

Psychology Laboratory

Established in 2018 and brought under the Psychology Department in 2022, the laboratory provides facilities for personality testing, aptitude testing, and psychological assessments for undergraduate and postgraduate students.

Centre of Excellence

The Centre of Excellence for Sustainable Materials operates under the Department of Civil Engineering through an MoU with UltraTech Cement. It provides students and faculty access to technologies focused on sustainable construction practices.

Moot Court

The moot court allows law students to practice legal document preparation, negotiation, and oral arguments. It simulates real-life arbitration and appellate court proceedings, with sessions judged by professors or guest academicians.

Library

Invertis University has 2 libraries stocked with textbooks, reference materials, and other publications. The libraries hold subscriptions to national and international journals and provide online resources through DelNet, INDEST, and EBSCO memberships.

Auditorium

The university auditorium has a seating capacity of 1,200-plus and is equipped with audio-visual equipment for academic and cultural events. It has hosted speakers and performances over the years.

Seminar Halls

The university has 4 seminar halls, each with seating for 300 students, used for workshops, seminars, and Faculty Development Programmes. Additionally, 3 conference halls are available for managerial and academic meetings.

Invertis University Campus Facilities

Invertis University campus spans 70-plus acres with separate hostels, two cafeterias, sports facilities, a 60-plus bus fleet covering 18 routes, parking for 100-plus vehicles, a Health Centre, and tie-up with Clara Swain Mission Hospital, along with student medical insurance coverage.

Lush Green Campus

Invertis University spans over 70 acres of green campus with a variety of trees and well-maintained gardens. The campus follows eco-friendly practices including solar heating, rainwater harvesting, paper recycling, and waste management initiatives.

Hostel Facilities

The university provides separate hostels for boys and girls with single and double-occupancy AC and non-AC rooms. Rooms are furnished with beds, study tables, and balconies, while meals are served in Annapurna, an air-conditioned common mess. Hostels also include common rooms, sports courts, a gymnasium, and guest rooms for visiting parents.

Cafeteria

Invertis University has two large cafeterias with seating both indoors and outdoors. The campus also has INVERtea, a tea stall located within the campus landscaping where students can relax and socialise.

Sports

The university has two large playgrounds, one supporting athletics, cricket, football, hockey, volleyball, basketball, and kho-kho, and the other dedicated to lawn tennis. Indoor and outdoor badminton courts and gymnasiums are also available, along with separate sports facilities within the hostels.

Transportation Facilities

Invertis University operates a fleet of more than 60 buses covering 18 routes and connecting over 42 locations around Bareilly. Authorised security personnel are deployed on buses, and bus timings are synchronised with university schedules. Buses are also used for excursions, industry visits, and sports events.

Parking

The university provides parking for more than 100 four-wheelers, along with designated bus parking spaces. Parking areas are monitored by trained personnel and over 40 CCTV cameras for round-the-clock surveillance.

Healthcare Facilities

The University Health Centre provides first-aid care and a 24-hour ambulance facility, with on-call doctors available for students. Invertis University has a joint venture with Clara Swain Mission Hospital to provide free treatment to students under its medical insurance policy.

Mission Hospital

Clara Swain Mission Hospital is a 350-bedded multi-specialty hospital in Bareilly providing primary healthcare and round-the-clock emergency medical services to the university. The hospital partners with the university for health awareness programmes, blood donation camps, and on-campus doctor visits.

Medical Insurance

Invertis University provides medical insurance to all students under its Student's Education and Health Protection Policy. The policy covers pre-hospitalization and post-hospitalization expenses to protect students and their families from financial strain.

What is the Invertis University ERP?

P
P sidhu •  Jul-31-2024
At Lovely Professional University (LPU), the University Management System (UMS) serves as a comprehensive ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) platform, streamlining various administrative and academic processes. UMS integrates functionalities such as academic management, where students can handle course registration, timetable management, and attendance tracking. It also manages examination schedules, result declarations, and grading, ensuring transparency and efficiency. Financial services, including fee payments and financial aid processing, are seamlessly handled through the system. UMS supports campus life with features for hostel management, library services, and extracurricular activities. Additionally, it facilitates communication through notifications and announcements and provides secure access to personal and academic records. Overall, LPU's UMS enhances operational efficiency and improves the overall educational experience by centralizing and simplifying administrative tasks.
