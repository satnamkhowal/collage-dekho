About DAIICT

DAIICT has started the admission process for courses including BTech and MTech programmes. JEE Main 2024 session 2 registration is ongoing till March 2, 2024, and the exam will be held between April 4 - April 15, 2024, for BTech admission at the institute. Further, for MTech admissions, GATE 2024 answer key for all sets will be released on February 21, 2024.

Established in 2001, Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology is a private institution located in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. The institute is named after the late Dhirubhai Ambani who was the founder of the Reliance Group. Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology is also known as DA-IICT which is a deemed university under the Gujarat Legislature Act. In 2017, DA-IICT was accredited with ‘A’ Grade from National Assessment and Accreditation Council, Government of India.

In 2009, Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology became a part of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). DA-IICT is located on 50 acres of land in Gandhinagar. Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology is spread over 50 acres of land in Gandhinagar.  

Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology provides undergraduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D programmes to the students. DAIICT admission for all courses is based on the scores obtained in the entrance examination. Aspiring students can register for admission directly on the college's official website. DAIICT Gandhinagar offers a total of 16 courses which includes B.E/B.Tech, M.E/M.Tech, M.Sc, M.Des Communication Design, Ph.D and more.

DAIICT FAQs

What is the mode of education at DAIICT Gandhinagar?

DAIICT Gandhinagar stands for Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology. DAIICT Gandhinagar courses at undergraduate, postgraduate & doctoral levels are provided in full-time offline mode. The university does not offer online courses or distance learning programmes.

Is there any entrance exam required for Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology PG admission?

Yes, candidates for PG courses are required to take an entrance exam followed by the counseling process. Aspirants have to appear for the entrance exam either conducted by the university or national entrance exam, then apply online via the university's official website. DAIICT Gandhinagar PG admissions are offered to the students on the basis of entrance exam scores.

What is the total number of seats available for the DAIICT Gandhinagar B.Tech Gujarat category?

Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology have seats reserved as per the categories. Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology B.Tech programme has 195 seats available for the Gujarat category.

Does DAIICT Gandhinagar offer admissions to the NRI students?

Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology admissions are offered to the national as well as international students based on the entrance exam scores. Yes, Non Resident Indian (NRI) students can enrol in Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology if they have a valid SAT score.

What are the specialisations available through DAIICT Gandhinagar M.Tech (ICT) programme?

DAIICT Gandhinagar M.Tech is a two year long programme which is offered in a variety of disciplines. Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology provides M.Tech (ICT) programmes with specialisations in Machine Learning, Software Systems, VLSI, and Embedded Systems.

Does DAIICT offer distance learning courses to the students?

Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology courses are available in full-time offline mode only. DAIICT Gandhinagar does not offer any distance learning courses to the students currently. Candidates wanting to pursue offline courses can apply online by visiting the official website of the institute.

What is the selection criteria for DAIICT Gandhinagar M.Sc programme?

Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology offers M.Sc programmes in various disciplines. The course duration of M.Sc is 2 years. DAIICT Gandhinagar M.Sc admission is offered on the basis of entrance exam scores followed by the personal interview.

How do I get into Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology?

DAIICT Gandhinagar admissions are offered on the basis of entrance exam scores. Students seeking admission to Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology have to appear for the Gujarat Common Entrance Test or JEE Main. Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) and Common Entrance Examination for Design (CEED) are required for M.Tech and M.Des students. Candidates interested in the M.Sc programme can also take the DA-IICT entrance test.
