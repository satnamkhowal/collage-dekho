Chaitanya Deemed to be University Hyderabad Job Ready Degree

If you are more focused on getting a job than adding one more degree to your educational qualifications then Chaitanya Deemed to be University Hyderabad has added four job-ready courses to its courses list. Out of the four courses, three are UG courses and one is a PG course. These courses are designed to help you learn the required skills and acquire knowledge which will make you job ready when you graduate. These assured courses at Chaitanya Deemed to be University Hyderabad are offered at an annual fee which ranges from Rs 80,000 to Rs 1,50,000. Chaitanya Deemed to be University Hyderabad job ready courses are BBA in Fintech (IXP), BBA in Digital Marketing (IXP), BCA in Full Stack Development (IXP), MCA in Full Stack Development (IXP), and B.Tech in AIML (IXP). Students can also claim up to 100% scholarships based on merit when applying for these courses. Scholarships are offered based on the performance of the candidate in the previous qualifying exam like class 12 and graduation. Interested students can check the table below for Chaitanya Deemed to be University Hyderabad job ready courses.

Chaitanya Deemed to be University Hyderabad Job Ready “Assured” Courses 2024 - Admissions 

There are no entrance examinations required for the job-ready courses offered by Chaitanya Deemed to be University Hyderabad. Eligible students will get direct admission to these courses. Interested students can fill out the application form and pay the enrollment fee to get admission to their desired course.
