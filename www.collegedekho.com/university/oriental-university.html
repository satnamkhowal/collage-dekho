About Oriental University Indore

Oriental University is a private university located in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. The university was established in the year 2011 by Devi Shakuntala Thakral Charitable Foundation as the first private university in Indore. Oriental University Indore is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and certified by International Organization for Standardization (ISO). It is also approved by the Pharmaceutical Council of India (PCI), Bar Council of India (BCI), MP Private University Regulatory Commission Bhopal (MPPURC),  All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), AIU and National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE). As per IIRF Ranking 2023, Oriental University Indore has been ranked 38th position out of 182 colleges in India.

Oriental University Indore courses are offered at diploma, UG, PG, and PhD levels. The university Indore offers 58 courses across 10 streams: engineering, pharmacy, management, commerce, science, law, computer application, humanities, agriculture, and education. Some of the popular degrees being offered by Oriental University Indore are B.Tech, B.Sc, BBA, B.Com, B.Ed. Oriental University Indore admission is done either on the basis of entrance exams such as JEE Main/ CAT/ CLAT/ MAT/ UCEED/ CET/ PAT/ others or merit in the qualifying exams. 

The university offers scholarships to students based on academic records and financial background such as merit-based scholarships, aspire scholarships, sports quota scholarships, etc. The major highlights of Oriental University Indore are tabulated below:

Oriental University Indore Application Process 2026

Applicants seeking admission to the Diploma/UG/PG programs at Oriental University must register on the official Oriental University admission portal. The complete admission process is based on the respective entrance examinations i.e., JEE Main/GATE/GPAT/CLAT/MAT/LSAT India. Oriental University admission procedure 2026 will be conducted online and offline. Below are the details of the same.

Online Application

  • Go to the Oriental University website and click on the Admission tab.
  • Read the instructions about the admission process carefully.
  • Register yourself on the portal with your mobile number.
  • After Registration, enter the mobile number and academic session details
  • After Successfully login in, Candidates are required to complete all the relevant sections of the application form. The system will not allow the incomplete form to be submitted.
  • Once the student has been submitted the form, application will redirect you to the account section (online/offline) through the head and the student shall be able to pay the fee via various payment modes.
  • Post application, candidate can also avail the various merit scholarships based on their qualifying percentage or entrance rank.
  • To avail the provisional admission or merit scholarships, students must verify all the documents.
  • After verifying the eligibility and documents, candidate can pay the semester fees via UPI/Debit/Credit/Net banking/Challan.
  • At last, candidate can collect/download their Admission Slip.
