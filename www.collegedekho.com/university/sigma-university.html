About Sigma University

Sigma University was established in 2003 as a private university in Vadodara, Gujarat. The university is spread over 26.7 lakh square feet with an up-to-date infrastructure.  Sigma University is affiliated with Gujarat Technical University (GTU) and Shri Govind Guru University (SGGU). The university holds more than 100 patents in its name and is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Government of Gujarat, All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), Indian Nursing Council (INC), Gujarat Nursing Council (GNC), and Gujarat State Council for Physiotherapy (GSCPT). 

Sigma University offers 27 courses at the UG, PG, diploma, and doctoral levels in multiple specialisations, including science, engineering, pharmacy, commerce & management, pharmacy, etc. There are a total of 14 departments at this university. Sigma University Job Ready Degree list includes MBA, BBA, BCA and B.Tech courses. The university conducts entrance exams for PhD, while the university accepts entrance exam scores for technical programmes like B.Tech and MBA. The placement rate of Sigma University is over 85%. More than 10,000 placements have been successfully done at the university, and the highest package being offered was Rs 60 LPA to an engineering student. In addition, the university has also signed 40+ MoUs and partnered with renowned institutions and colleges across the country. A few of the MoUs are Zydex Industries P. Ltd, the Edunet Foundation for IBM SkillBuild, Ajeenkya D. Y. Patil University, Pune, D. Y. Patil University, Pune, NIMS University, Jaipur, Sanskriti University, Mathura, UP, LNCT University, Bhopal, etc. The university offers merit and sports based scholarships.

Sigma University FAQs

Is Sigma University UGC approved?

Yes, Sigma University is UGC approved. It is a private university located in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. It was established in 2002 and is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) of India. Sigma University offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in engineering, technology, management, and business. The university has a strong placement record and its students have been placed at top companies such as Amazon, Deloitte, and Microsoft.

What courses does Sigma University offer?

Sigma University offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs in engineering, technology, management, and business. Some of the popular Sigma University courses are MBA, B.Tech, B.Sc, BBA, BCA, B.Des, ANM, B.Com, B.Pharm, M.Tech, M.Sc, MCA, M.Pharm, MSW, Diploma, D.Voc, D.Pharma and PhD.

Is Sigma University accredited?

Yes, Sigma University is accredited by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), the Gujarat State Council for Physiotherapy (GSCPT), the Indian Nursing Council (INC) and the Gujarat Nursing Council (GNC). Gujarat Technical University (GTU) and Shri Govind Guru University (SGGU) are both affiliated with Sigma University. It has the endorsement of the, and the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). Each year, this institute enrols more than 10,000 new students.

Where is Sigma University located?

Sigma University is located in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. The exact address of the university is Bakrol, Ajwa-Nimeta Road, Waghodia, Vadodara, Gujarat 390019. Students can visit the university campus to checkout the facilities or apply for offline admission. An application form must be fully filled out by candidates. Candidates with queries or uncertainties can seek clarification from the admissions cell. A notice of receipt of the information will be sent to the candidate once the information has been submitted to the admissions cell at Sigma University.

What are the minimum marks I need to get into Sigma University?

The minimum grades required for admission to Sigma University vary depending on the program and level of study. However, in general, students must have a minimum of 50% marks in their previous academic examinations such as class 12, class 10 and graduation, to be eligible for admission. Candidates applying for admission to the MBA programme must have a valid score in CAT, MAT or CMAT exams. Whereas admission to the BE/ B.Tech programme must have a valid JEE Mains score.

What are the campus facilities at Sigma University?

The Sigma University campus has a wide range of facilities, including an amphitheatre, football ground, guest house, cafeteria, and auditorium. The amphitheatre is a unique hang-out place and creativity hub for students. The football ground is state-of-the-art and has hosted many prestigious tournaments. The guest house is an outstanding and totally out-of-the-ordinary architectural and engineering feat. The cafeteria offers a wide variety of tasty snacks at reasonable rates. The three auditoriums have modern audio-visual and other facilities.

What are the hostel rooms like at Sigma University?

Sigma University offers air-conditioned dorm rooms with comfortable beds and study spaces. The dorms also have common facilities such as a mess hall, laundry, and recreation room. The hostel fees for AC rooms is Rs 97,000 for girls and Rs 70,000 for boys, while the Non AC rooms cost Rs 67,000 for girls and Rs 40,000 for boys per year.

What are my chances of getting a job after graduating from Sigma University?

The Sigma University placement percentage was 85% last year, which means there is a pretty good chance of getting a job. Getting meaningful industry exposure is only one of the numerous benefits of attending a big university like Sigma University. The Sigma University placement process includes grooming sessions, and other perks that might eventually entice students to be hired by companies that enable them to lay a stronger foundation for the future. The placement cell offers instruction on everything, including writing resumes and conducting interviews.

How can I get admission to Sigma University?

Since the Corona epidemic started, the admissions process for Sigma University has been moved online. The applicant should go to the institute's official website, click ‘Apply Now’ and then choose the course they want to enrol in before filling out the application form with all the necessary information. The candidate must pay an application fee of Rs 1,000 in order to be accepted. Once the application has been submitted, the candidate will receive an email and SMS acknowledging receipt.

Sigma University Campus Facilities

Sigma University provides many facilities to make the life of students easier in and around the campus. Given below is the list of such facilities 

  • Virtual Lab in Collaboration with IIT Bombay: Experience hands-on learning with cutting-edge experiments and simulations facilitated by IIT Bombay, bridging the gap between theory and practice.
  • Swimming Pool with Full Time Swimming Coach: Dive into a well-maintained swimming pool with professional coaching available full-time, ensuring students swim safely and improve their skills.
  • Gym with Modern Equipment: Stay fit and healthy with a fully equipped gym that houses the latest fitness equipment and machines to cater to all your workout needs.
  • Library with Over 70,000+ Books: A haven for book lovers, the library offers an extensive collection of over 70,000 books across various genres and disciplines.
  • Guest House with AC Rooms for Guests: Comfortable and air-conditioned guest house facilities for visitors, providing a pleasant stay experience within the campus.
  • Bank Branch in Campus along with ATM: Convenient banking services with an on-campus bank branch and ATM, ensuring easy access to financial services for students and staff.
  • Transportation through 20+ Buses, 10+ Mini Buses, and 50+ Vans: Reliable and extensive transportation services covering a wide range of routes to and from the campus.
  • Air-Conditioned Auditoriums with Combined Seating Capacity of 600+: Spacious and air-conditioned auditoriums capable of accommodating over 600 individuals, perfect for events, seminars, and lectures.
  • Food Court Serving Hygienic Food to Students: A variety of healthy and hygienic food options served at the food court, catering to the diverse tastes of students.
  • Hostels for Boys and Girls with Hygienic Food and AC Accommodation: Comfortable hostel facilities offering air-conditioned rooms and nutritious meals, ensuring a conducive living environment.
  • Hi-Tech Workshop with Modern Machines like CNC, Lathe, etc.: State-of-the-art workshop equipped with advanced machinery such as CNC, Lathe, and more, supporting practical learning and projects.
  • 3D Printers for Students' Learning: Access to 3D printers, enabling students to bring their creative and innovative designs to life.
  • Open Air Amphitheater Spread Over 35,000+ Sq Ft: A vast open-air amphitheater providing an ideal venue for cultural events, performances, and gatherings.
  • Sport Ground for Various Outdoor Games with Lighting Facility: A well-maintained sports ground with lighting facilities, promoting various outdoor games and physical activities.
  • Mauryan Sport Academy for Students with Full-Time Coach: A dedicated sports academy with full-time coaching available, focusing on the overall athletic development of students.

Sigma University Scholarships

Sigma University offers several scholarships that the students can easily avail themselves of:

  • The university offers merit-based scholarships for the eligible students and the scholarship covers 50% of annual tuition fees.
  • Students can have sports-based scholarships, where being a state-level player can cover 50% of annual tuition fees and for national-level players, 100% of annual tuition fees are covered.
  • Students can apply for a state scholarship if they belong to a minority caste.
  • With the Sigma University scholarship, BE students will be able to pay Rs 40,000 per year, which will be the lowest fees currently available for this course during this COVID-19 Pandemic.
  • Scholarships in the M.Pharm course are available at Sigma Institute of Pharmacy for up to Rs 57,000.
  • Students can pay fees in instalments, and the university offers various payment plans.
