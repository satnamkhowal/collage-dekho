JU B.Tech 2022: Real Campus Insights

Overall Jadavpur University (JU), established in 1955, is a premier state university renowned for its academic rigor and vibrant campus culture. Ranked **#12 in Engineering** by NIRF 2024 and **721–730 globally** by QS 2025, JU excels in research, innovation, and industry collaboration. The B.Tech program is highly competitive, with admissions via **WBJEE** and a strong emphasis on theoretical and practical learning. The university’s **Grade ‘A’ NAAC accreditation** and partnerships with global institutions underscore its academic credibility. Campus life blends intellectual freedom with cultural activism, fostering holistic development. While infrastructure is functional, some facilities like labs and hostels need modernization. Placements for core engineering branches (CSE, ECE) are stellar, but niche departments like Printing Engineering lag behind. Overall, JU offers unmatched value for money, especially for in-state students.

Faculty JU’s faculty comprises **PhD holders from IITs, IISc, and global universities**, known for their research contributions. Reviews highlight supportive mentors in core engineering (CSE, Mechanical) but uneven engagement in smaller departments. Guest lectures from industry experts and collaborations with bodies like AICTE enrich learning. However, attendance is not enforced, leading to self-driven study cultures. While teaching quality is praised, some outdated syllabi in niche courses need revision.

Dear Student,

Counselling for PG Engineering admission at Jadavpur University is conducted by the Faculty of Engineering Technology (FET). The counseling process depends on the course you are applying for. For M.Tech, M.E and M.Arch programs, admission is based on your GATE score (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering). If you have a valid GATE score, you can participate in the counseling process to apply for these courses. In this process, after you receive your GATE result, you need to register for counselling, where seats are allocated based on your GATE rank and chosen program. Jadavpur University considers GATE scores as the main selection criteria for these engineering courses.

Jadavpur University Courses FAQs

What types of courses does Jadavpur University offer?

At the UG level, Jadavpur University offers B.E., B.Tech, B.Ed, BBA, BA, BSc, B.Arch, B.Pharm, B.Com, B.Lib , etc, while at the PG level, Jadavpur University offers M.E., M.Tech, MBA, MA, MSc, MCA, M.Arch, M.Pharm, M.Com, M.Lib, etc.

Which courses are popular at Jadavpur University?

The most popular Jadavpur University courses include the B.E., B.Tech, B.Ed, BBA, BA and BSc programmes. UG courses also include B.E., B.Tech, B.Ed, BBA, BA, BSc, B.Arch, B.Pharm, B.Com, B.Lib, etc. Some of the popular PG courses include M.E., M.Tech, MBA, MA, MSc, MCA, M.Arch, M.Pharm, M.Com, M.Lib , etc. Jadavpur University also offers several certificate and diploma courses.

Does Jadavpur University accept 12th marks?

Yes, passing class 12 is a prerequisite for enrollment in UG courses at Jadavpur University. Admission to programmes such as B.E., B.Tech, B.Ed, BBA, BA, BSc, B.Arch, B.Pharm, B.Com, B.Lib and so on is determined by the grades/ marks received in Class 12 qualifying exam.

Is Jadavpur University easy to get into?

Admission in Jadavpur University courses are based on valid entrance exam scores. The university conducts its own entrance test to grant admission in Ph.D, PG and UG courses. Candidates must apply for admission and take the Jadavpur University Entrance Exam followed by a personal interview session, to secure admission. The university also accepts national-level entrance exam scores for admission which include WBJEE, JEE Main, GATE etc.

Is Jadavpur University very expensive?

The annual cost of undergraduate programmes ranges from Rs 20840.0 for B.Pharm to Rs 170000.0 for BBA. The annual Jadavpur University fee structure for PG programmes is between Rs 9600.0 for M.Tech to Rs 155000.0 for MBA.

What is the annual fee of Jadavpur University?

The annual Jadavpur University fees fall between Rs 9600 to Rs 170000. Depending on the degree level, duration, and area of specialisation, the fees differ for various programmes. Jadavpur University courses at the Ph.D, PG and UG levels are available.Around 53 courses are available under different degree levels; some of the well known ones are B.E., B.Tech, B.Ed, BBA, BA, BSc, and so on.

How much does Jadavpur University cost?

Jadavpur is considered rather affordable than other universities in Kolkata. The fee structure for most of the courses is low. However, it totally depends on the course you opt for. The fees for different courses range from Rs 5 K to 8 L. Apart from course fees, the charges for the hostel, examination, mess, etc are not high. You can also avail of various scholarships as well.

What UG courses does Jadavpur University offer?

Jadavpur University offers 39 UG courses. The course list includes B.Tech, B.Arch, BA, B.Sc, B.P.Ed, and B.L.I.Sc, etc. The fee structure for JU UG courses ranges from Rs 10K and 5 L.

Are distance-learning courses offered at Jadavpur?

Jadavpur University does not offer distance learning courses so far. However, the university is planning for distance and web-based courses.

What courses does Jadavpur University offer?

Jadavpur University offers 39 undergraduate courses, 57 postgraduate courses, 14 M.Phil and PhD programmes and 8 PG Diploma courses. There are also 3 other courses such as D.Sc, D.Litt, etc. These courses are offered in the disciplines of technology, science, arts and management.

What is the fees for the Jadavpur B.Sc programme and eligibility criteria?

The fees for the Jadavpur University B.Sc programme range from Rs 1.51 L to 4.13 L. The university increases or decreases the fee structure in the new academic year. Students who have passed Class 12 with atleast 50% marks can apply for admission to the B.Sc programme.

What is the fees and qualification for the Jadavpur University M.Sc course?

The fees for the JU M.Sc programme range from Rs 48 K to 1.57 L and the eligibility criteria for admission to the M.Sc programme is to have a graduate degree with 60% marks. Relaxation to category students is available.
