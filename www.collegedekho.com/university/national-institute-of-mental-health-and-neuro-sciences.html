About NIMHANS Bangalore

NIMHANS Bangalore or National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences Bangalore was established in the year 1974. It is a public (deemed) institute, situated in Bangalore Karnataka. The institute has been recognised by UGC. There are 26 departments at NIMHANS Bangalore, including departments of biostatistics, biophysics, and human genetics. There are 16 centres of excellence at the National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences, some of which include the Advanced Centre for Ayurveda, the Centre for Public Health, the Library and Information Center, the Center for Addiction Medicine, and the NIMHANS Digital Academy. As per NIRF Ranking 2025, the institute has been ranked 7th in the medical category.

The NIMHANS Bangalore courses are offered at both postgraduate and doctoral levels. Some of the popular courses offered by the institute are MD in Psychiatry, M.Ch in Neurosurgery, M.Sc Psychiatric Nursing, DM in Neuropathology, and MD in Ayurveda Manovigyan Evam Manasa Roga among others. All the courses are offered in full-time, regular mode to interested candidates. The NIMHANS Bangalore admission to all the courses is offered on the basis of an online entrance test and marks scored in the last qualifying exam. But for admission to MD in Psychiatry course, candidates have to appear in the INI-CET entrance exam conducted by AIIMS Delhi.

The NIMHANS Bangalore campus is spread over an area of more than 135 acres and offers various facilities to interested candidates. The students of the institute have access to a wide range of facilities on campus such as a gym, girls as well as boys hostels, laboratories, a sports complex, transportation facilities, banking options, a full-fledged library, a canteen, and others. The NIMHANS Bangalore placements are conducted by a dedicated placement cell every year to allow graduating students to secure employment of their choice before even the completion of their education. As per the NIRF report 2022, 92 UG and PG students were placed during the NIMHANS Bangalore placements 2021. In 2021, the National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences offered the students of UG (3-year) and UG (4-year) courses with a  median salary package of Rs 6 Lakhs per annum and Rs 7.20 Lakhs per annum, respectively.

NIMHANS Bangalore Courses and Admission 2026

NIMHANS admission is based on entrance exams. The institute accepts NEET scores for admission. NTA has published the NEET UG result 2024. NIMHANS Bangalore courses are offered to interested candidates in postgraduate and doctoral studies. All the courses are offered at NIMHANS Bangalore in the streams of medicine, surgery and applied medicine. Some of the popular courses offered at NIMHANS Bangalore are M.Sc Biostatistics, M.Sc Psychiatric Nursing, MPH, M.Phil, PhD etc are available for admission. The super specialisation courses such as DM Addiction Psychiatry, DM Neuropathology, DM in Neuroanesthesia, and M.Ch Neurosurgery are also available at the institute. Students must fulfil the NIMHANS Bangalore eligibility criteria in order to submit an application for admission. They also have to appear in an online entrance test (except INI CET for MD in Psychiatry) which is conducted by NIMHANS Bangalore for admission to various UG & PG courses. 

It should be noted that the eligibility requirements for NIMHANS Bangalore courses vary from course to course and depend on the course a student wants to enrol in. As a result, the level of the course, stream of specialisation, and fees of NIMHANS Bangalore courses are all relevant when determining the eligibility requirements for a given course. Courses at NIMHANS Bangalore are only available in regular, full-time mode. The NIMHANS Bangalore fees for the courses range between Rs 34,200 and Rs 2,11,000.

NIMHANS Bangalore Fee Payment Guidelines

NIMHANS Bangalore fees for various offered courses are accepted in both online and offline modes from registered students. Both online and offline payment options are included below.

  • Payment with a debit or credit card
  • Through online banking
  • Through UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
  • Depositing fees to the institute's bank account in form of cash or cheque
  • Demand Draft with the institute's name on it

The NIMHANS Bangalore admission is offered in a variety of PG and PhD courses within many specialisations in the medical stream. The only full-time courses offered at the UG level are the B.Sc and certificate courses. The NIMHANS Bangalore PG admission is available for the courses such as MD, M.Sc, MPH, DM, M.Ch, and PG Diploma, while the doctoral level courses available at the institute are M.Phil and PhD. The candidates have to appear in the online entrance test and must have passed the last qualifying exam with the required minimum marks. After fulfilling the minimum NIMHANS Bangalore admission criteria, candidates have to fill out the application form and pay the required application fee.

NIMHANS Bangalore M.Sc/MD/MPH Eligibility Criteria 2024

  • The candidates must have an MBBS degree from a reputable university or institute.
  • The Medical Council of India (MCI) or any State Medical Council registration must be valid and produced by th candidates.
  • A minimum of 55% marks overall in their MBBS programme is required for NIMHANS Bangalore PG admission. For those who fall within the SC/ST/OBC (Non-creamy Layer) groups, a minimum overall score of 50% is needed.
  • Applicants must show up for the online entrance test administered by NIMHANS Bangalore. The entrance exam, which consists of multiple-choice questions, examines the expertise of the applicant in relevant specialisations for admission to NIMHANS Bangalore.

NIMHANS Bangalore PhD Eligibility Criteria 2024

  • The applicant must have earned a master's degree in a relevant discipline from an accredited university or college with a minimum score of 55%. Candidates from the SC/ST/OBC (non-creamy layer) and PwD categories are given a 5% relaxation.
  • The institute administers a written entrance exam as the first step in the NIMHANS Bangalore selection process. 100 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) based on the candidate's master's degree syllabus make up the test. The test takes 90 minutes to complete. The examination will be taken online.
  • Those applicants who pass the written exam will be contacted for an interview. The interview panel will consist of subject experts who will evaluate the candidate's expertise, research skills, and course eligibility whose decision is binding for NIMHANS Bangalore admission.
  • In accordance with government laws, a specific number of seats are set aside for applicants who fall under the SC, ST, OBC (non-creamy layer), and PwD categories.

NIMHANS Bangalore Application Process 2024

  • Step 1: The initial step of the NIMHANS Bangalore application process is to visit the NIMHANS Bangalore official website i.e. www.nimhans.ac.in.
  • Step 2: To look at the list of courses the institute offers, candidates should go to the "Education" section of the homepage.
  • Step 3: Once the candidate has chosen the course they want to get admission to, click on the “course” link to learn more about the requirements, course fees, admission procedure, etc.
  • Step 4: The majority of NIMHANS Bangalore courses include an online application process. On the official website, you must register and generate a login ID and password.
  • Step 5: Fill out the application form after registering with all the necessary information. Make sure you correctly fill out all the essential fields on the application form and attach all the required documents.
  • Step 6: Once the candidates have completed the application form, they must pay the nominal NIMHANS Bangalore application fee online via the website.
  • Step 7: Online application forms must be submitted after the application cost has been paid. Obtain a printout of the application and store it securely for future use.
  • Step 8: NIMHANS Bangalore will release the admit card for the entrance exam following the conclusion of the application process of NIMHANS Bangalore. Candidates must print out the admit card that they downloaded for future reference.

NIMHANS Bangalore FAQs

Is NIMHANS Bangalore a government or private institution?

The National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences Bangalore is a public institute, situated in Bangalore, Karnataka. In December 1974, NIMHANS was founded.  NIMHANS provides a variety of undergraduate, diploma, postgraduate, postgraduate diploma, master's, post-doctoral fellowship, super speciality medical courses and PhD courses in a number of specialisations. It is an autonomous institute.

Are there any MOUs between NIMHANS Bangalore and other organisations?

Yes. NIMHANS Bangalore has MOUs with several reputed universities and institutes such as Columbia University, University of Florida, Johns Hopkins University.

What is the mode of application at NIMHANS Bangalore?

The application mode for the NIMHANS Bangalore is completely online.

What are the offered courses at NIMHANS Bangalore?

NIMHANS Bangalore courses are offered in a variety of UG, PG, Super Speciality, and PhD-level courses in a range of specialisations, including M.Phil, M.Ch, MSc, MD, MPH, and PhD.

Who all are eligible for NIMHANS Bangalore INI CET counselling?

Applicants who qualify for the INI CET entrance exam by scoring the nominal cut off are eligible for the counselling process. AIIMS Delhi releases a merit list of provisionally eligible candidates for the seat allocation process at the institute.

What is the admission process for NIMHANS Bangalore DM courses?

Candidates must have to appear for an online entrance exam administered by NIMHANS Bangalore to be considered for admissions to DM courses.

What is the eligibility criteria for NIMHANS Bangalore M.D. Psychiatry admissions?

Candidates must have MBBS or DPM degree from an institution recognised by the Medical Council of India to be eligible for M.D. Psychiatry admission at NIMHANS Bangalore.

Does NIMHANS Bangalore offers any part-time or distance education courses

The answer is that all courses are taught in full-time mode at the institute. Private practice in completely prohibited. The candidates are not allowed to get admission in any other part-time or correspondence courses.

In which sector, the largest share of NIMHANS Bangalore alumni are employed?

The largest share of NIMHANS alumni are working in the sector of healthcare services (34%), which is followed by Research (16%) and Education (16%).

What was the median salary package offered at NIMHANS placements 2021 for UG (4-year) courses?

As per the NIRF report 2022, the median salary package offered to the students during the NIMHANS Bangalore placement drive 2021 was Rs 7.20 Lakhs per annum.

What was the median salary package offered at NIMHANS placements 2021 for UG (3-year) courses?

The NIRF report 2022 states that the median package given during placement in 2021 was Rs 6 Lakhs per annum. The placement record has been a perfect 100% over the last 3 years.

NIMHANS Bangalore Campus Facilities and Infrastructure

Several amenities are available to students at the NIMHANS Bangalore campus. A library, recreational facilities, medical facilities, IT infrastructure, and numerous other amenities are available at NIMHANS Bangalore. Residential facilities are available for both male and female students at the National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences in Bangalore. These hostels are equipped with all the necessities. The campus library is equipped with all the academic tools required to support students' academic work. Facilities at the NIMHANS Bangalore campus additionally include a gym, an auditorium, a medical centre, etc. The school offers its students access to medical facilities.

  • Boy's Hostel: Boys can stay in a separate hostel at the campus of NIMHANS Bangalore. The institute's administration building is not far from the NIMHANS Bangalore boys hostel. The boys' hostel comprises 4 hostels, 18 cubicles, and 81 single occupancy rooms.
  • Girl's Hostel: The college includes a unique residence hall for female students called Cauvery Hostel. 179 trainees can stay in the hostel's 110 single and double occupancy rooms. The majority of the rooms include bathrooms. Facilities at the girl's hostel of NIMHANS Bangalore include a mess, a study room, housekeeping, hot water, etc. Moreover, the institute has a Kabini hostel complex that was officially opened on August 18, 2012. Rooms in this hostel are available on a single occupancy basis. Kabini Hostel complex has six floors, consisting of 32 rooms. In total, the hostel complex has 192 rooms.
  • Medical facilities: Students, faculty, and employees who need medical care can go to the medical centre at the National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences in Bangalore.
  • Library: A well-stocked library is available at NIMHANS Bangalore to meet both staff members' and students' informational needs.
  • Sports complex: Students at the National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences Bangalore campus have access to a variety of sporting facilities.
  • IT Infrastructure: Bangalore's National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences' ICT infrastructure provides all required IT-related services.
  • Cafeteria: Students, professors, and staff can get healthful cuisine from the cafeteria at the campus of NIMHANS Bangalore.
