by which counsseling process i have to apply for counselling

R
Rupsa •  Nov-15-2024

Dear Student,

Counselling for PG Engineering admission at Jadavpur University is conducted by the Faculty of Engineering Technology (FET). The counseling process depends on the course you are applying for. For M.Tech, M.E and M.Arch programs, admission is based on your GATE score (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering). If you have a valid GATE score, you can participate in the counseling process to apply for these courses. In this process, after you receive your GATE result, you need to register for counselling, where seats are allocated based on your GATE rank and chosen program. Jadavpur University considers GATE scores as the main selection criteria for these engineering courses.

Jadavpur University Placements FAQs

Does Jadavpur University have 100% placement?

Yes, Jadavpur University does offer 100% placements for some of the courses. Overall the placement percentage of this university stays between 90-95%. For example, in 2024, departments like BTech in Instrumentation Engineering, Mechanical, and Construction Engineering achieved 100% placements. While the overall Jadavpur University placement percentage stood at 90.91% in 2024.

What is the highest package in Jadavpur University for BTech in CSE?

Jadavpur University placement CSE in 2024 has been phenomenal as now 97% of the students have been placed. Also, the highest package in 2024 for BTech in CSE is Rs 57 LPA. Three students have received the highest package in BTech in CSE by Google.

What is the placement percentage at Jadavpur University?

Jadavpur University's placement percentage for 2024 is 90.91%. This university's placement percentage in 2023 was 95%, and in 2022, it was recorded at 84%.

Which branch of Jadavpur University has the best placement record?

BTech in IT has the highest placement package of Rs 1.40 CPA and has a 96% placement percentage in 2024. In terms of placement percentage, the BTech in Instrumentation Engineering, BTech in Mechanical, and BTech in Construction Engineering have achieved 100% placements in 2024.

Who are the top recruiters of Jadavpur University?

Jadavpur University top recruiters for placements are a mix of MNCs, unicorns and startups. During the Jadavpur University placements in 2024, the top recruiters were Google,  DE Shaw India, Texas Industries, HSBC, Microsoft, Schrodinger, Samsung, City Bank, and others. The highest accepted package in 2024 was offered by Google.

What is the highest package for BTech in Jadavpur University?

Jadavpur University highest package in 2024 is Rs 1.40 CPA, but the BTech in IT student who got the package refused it and joined another company for a Rs 25 LPA package. Therefore, the highest package accepted by students in 2024 is Rs 57 LPA which was offered by Google. A total of three students got the Rs 57 LPA package from Google.

Is Jadavpur University good for placements?

Jadavpur University is good for placements. In 2024, 830 out of 913 students were placed in the engineering branch. The placement percentage in 2024 was recorded at 90.91%.
