About DAIICT B.Tech

DAIICT B.Tech is a 3 - 4 Years program offered in multiple specializations. To pursue a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) degree at Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information & Communication Technology (DA-IICT) in Gandhinagar, aspiring candidates must meet certain eligibility criteria. Applicants must have completed their 10+2 examination from a recognized board with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry as core subjects. They should have secured a minimum aggregate score of 45% in these subjects. Additionally, candidates must have qualified in a national-level entrance examination, such as JEE (Main) or GUJCET, and have a valid scorecard. Meeting these requirements ensures that students have a strong foundation in the essential subjects and are well-prepared to pursue a B.Tech. degree at DA-IICT. The annual fee for B.Tech is INR 6,88,000. DAIICT B.Tech Admission 2026

Admission to the B.Tech at DAIICT is subject to meeting the eligibility criteria. All shortlisted candidates have to complete the DAIICT B.Tech admission process by getting their documents verified and paying the admission fee.

DAIICT B.Tech Application Process

DAIICT B.Tech application form has to be submitted online by visiting the official website. Candidates aspiring to take admission to the B.Tech course at DAIICT have to first check and meet the eligibility criteria and then proceed further with the application process.

Steps to fill DAIICT B.Tech application form:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of DAIICT and click on ‘apply online’.
  • Step 2: Now, you will be redirected to the admission page of DAIICT. Complete the registration process and create a profile.
  • Step 3: Fill in all details available in the DAIICT B.Tech application form.
  • Step 4: Now pay the DAIICT B.Tech application fee and click on submit button.
  • Step 5: After the submission, keep a hard copy of DAIICT B.Tech Application Form.

Documents Required for DAIICT B.Tech Admission:

  • Marks sheet of class 10th standard
  • Marks sheet of class 12th standard
  • Transfer certificate (if required)
  • Character certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
