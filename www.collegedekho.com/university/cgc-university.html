About CGC University Mohali

CGC University, Mohali, established in 2025, proudly carries forward the legacy of the Chandigarh Group of Colleges (CGC)—a trusted name in higher education with over 25 years of excellence. Formerly known as CGC University, founded in 2012, the institution has grown into a full-fledged university, marking an important milestone in its academic journey. CGC University, Mohali is an autonomous institution and is not associated with or affiliated to Chandigarh University (CU) in any manner.

Built for the ambitions of the Gen Z learner, CGC University is a modern, AI-enabled campus where innovation, technology, and experiential learning come together to prepare students for the future. The university features world-class infrastructure, smart classrooms, high-tech laboratories, digital libraries, research centers, innovation hubs, sports facilities, and comfortable hostels—creating an environment that supports both academic and personal growth.

Staying true to the CGC legacy, the university offers globally relevant programs that combine strong theoretical knowledge with real-world experience. Partnerships with leading organizations such as EY, IBM, and OS. Sci help design industry-aligned curricula that prepare students for careers across a wide range of fields.

As CGC University, the institution earned several national recognitions that continue to define CGC University, Mohali today. With an A+ grade from NAAC, collaborations with 300+ international partners across 70+ countries and five continents, and awards such as Excellence in Delivering Quality Education 2023 and Iconic College of the Year 2023, the university has established itself as a center of quality education. In 2025, it was ranked 174th among engineering colleges by India Today, within 201–300 in the NIRF engineering category, and #101–125 in management, highlighting its consistent performance.

Research and innovation are key strengths of the university. More than 2,000 patents have been filed, with 608+ published, supported by a INR 15 crore+ annual research budget and 1,800+ research papers authored by faculty and students. The on-campus incubation center, VentureNest, supports aspiring entrepreneurs through mentorship, funding, and networking opportunities. To date, it has successfully incubated 70+ startups and signed 53 Memorandums of Understanding across 17 states and Union Territories.

With over 100 undergraduate and postgraduate programs across disciplines such as Engineering, Management, Law, Paramedical Sciences, Allied Health Sciences, Pharmacy, Design, Physical Education & Sports, and Ph.D. programs, CGC University offers diverse pathways to match every student’s ambition.

CGC University Mohali showcased outstanding placement outcomes with a highest package of INR 1 crore, over 12,000+ placement offers, and 15,000+ students placed by 1,500+ recruiters. Top companies offered high-value roles across multiple salary bands, reflecting strong industry demand and career opportunities.

Beyond academics, students enjoy a lively and inclusive campus life filled with clubs, cultural events, sports, and activities that encourage creativity and leadership. CGC University, Mohali, stands as a place where innovation meets opportunity, continuing a proud legacy while shaping the next generation of thinkers, innovators, and leaders.

CGC University Mohali Admission 2026

CGC University Mohali admission 2026 is fully online with a non-refundable application fee of INR 1,000. CGCUET Stage 2 registration closes on September 12, 2026, with classes commencing in September 2026 and final admission round on September 15, 2026.

CGC University Mohali Application Process 2026–2027

The CGC University Mohali application process for 2026–2027 is completely online. Candidates can complete their application by following these steps:

  • Visit the Official Website – Access the university’s official portal to begin the application.
  • Fill the Online Form – Complete the admission form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.
  • Upload Required Documents – Submit all necessary documents as specified in the form.
  • Pay the Application Fee – The non-refundable application fee of Rs 1,000 must be paid online.
  • Submit the Application – Ensure the form is submitted after completing all required fields and document uploads.
  • Receive Confirmation – After successful payment and submission, applicants will receive a confirmation email from the university confirming completion of the process.

For Admission Deatils, Visit CGC University Admission.

CGC University Mohali Admission Timeline 2026

CGC University Admission 2026 follows a structured, stage-wise process with rolling applications, CGCUET-based merit, and time-bound deadlines. Early registration ensures better scholarship opportunities, while timely exam participation, document verification, and fee payment are essential to secure seats before the final admission round.

Capgemini–CGC University Industry Training Collaboration 2026

Special Financial Aid Scholarship Policy

The CGC University Mohali Special Financial Aid Scholarship is designed to support students from Bihar, West Bengal, Jharkhand, and all Northeastern states. Eligible students receive INR 8,000 per semester as financial assistance, which can be availed along with the CGCUET scholarship and an additional 10% Early Bird scholarship. To continue receiving this scholarship along with other benefits, students are required to meet the following eligibility criteria:
