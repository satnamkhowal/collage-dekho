Bachelor of Design

The Bachelor of Design is also referred to as B. Des or B. Design is an undergraduate academic degree in the field of Design. The B.Des program cultivates creative problem-solvers ready to shape the future of design. Students develop a robust portfolio through hands-on projects, guided by industry experts who bring real-world insights to the classroom.
