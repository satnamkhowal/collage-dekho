Top BBA Colleges in Delhi (Feb 2023): Ranking, Fees, Placements, Admission

Updated on - Sep 21, 2026 05:27 AM IST

There are about 121 that offers B.B.A. (Bachelor of Business Administration) courses in Delhi. Out of these 5 are government colleges and 86 are privately owned. Some of the top B.B.A. (Bachelor of Business Administration) colleges in Delhi are Jamia Millia Islamia, GGSIPU, Jagan Institute of Management Studies, Jamia Hamdard, NDIM Delhi, DTU, Maharaja Agrasen Institute of Technology, Vivekananda Institute of Professional Studies, Maharaja Surajmal Institute, Tecnia Institute of Advanced Studies. The admission criteria for these colleges is to pass in the qualifying examination or appear for the entrance examination. Only top scoring candidates are offered admission to these top colleges. The fees for top B.B.A. (Bachelor of Business Administration) colleges in Delhi range from INR 6,000 - INR 8,16,290.
